A FAIR MINER

Miss Nellie Othman, a tall, handsome, dark-eyed lady of twenty-six, is
attempting to become to Arizona what “Queen Midas has been to Australia.
She has a natural instinct for’ discovering gold, and she has developed
and Strengthened it by careful study with her brother, who is a
practical miner. She pushes out to the newest camps, gives her judgment
upon the probabilities of the various mines, backs her judgment with her
money, and is seldom mistaken. The miners are superstitious and believe
that Miss Cashman—whose name is itself a god omen brings them luck and
they are willing to give her a share of any mine which see approves,
knowing that its price will be at once advanced by her indorsement
whether the mine turns out richly or otherwise. Migg Cashman has been
for nine years in thig business, seems well educated and ladylike, and
is thoroughly respected by the rough men with whom she trusts herself as
the only woman in their camp.

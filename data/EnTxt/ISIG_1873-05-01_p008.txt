NOTICE is hereby given that the Superintendent-General of Education will
hold periodical Examinations of

Teachers, and of Candidates for employment as Teachers, of Schools in
connection with the Department of Public Educa­tion ; and Certificates
will be granted to qualified persons of either sex.

The regulations regarding the Elementary Teacher’s Cer­tificate are as
follows:—

Elementary Teacher's Certificate.

I.—Candidates for this Certificate, qualifying the holder for the charge
of an Undenominational Public School of the Third Class, or of a Mission
School, will be examined in

1.  The English Language: Reading, Dictation, and Grammar.

2.  Arithmetic : including Vulgar and Decimal Fractions.

3.  Descriptive Geography.

4.  Handwriting.

5.  School management.

6.  The Dutch Language, at the option of each Candidate.

II.—Those who distinguish themselves in the Examination will have the
words “ with honours" marked on their Certi­ficates.

III.—The first Examination will be held in Cape Town on Friday and
Saturday, 4th and 5th April, 1873; and Candi­dates are required to send
in their names, with age and testi­mony to good character, on or before
1st March next.

The possession of an Elementary Teacher’s Certificate, or of some higher
and duly recognized Certificate, will in future be indispensable to
those seeking employment in Schools connected with this Department.

Arrangements will be made for holding similar Examinations in other
places besides Cape Town.

  LANGHAM DALE, Superintendent-General of Education.

Education Office, Cape Town, 1st January, 1873.

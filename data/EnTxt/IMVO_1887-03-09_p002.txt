Native Educational Association.

  THE Special Jubilee Committee appointed
  by the Native Educational Association

  at its last session in Grahamstown in Jan-
  uary last, will meet at Brownlee Station
  together with the Kaffrarian Native Jubilee
  Committee on Friday, the 18th instant, at
  6 30 p.m. The Special Committee are :—
  The Revs. E. Makiwane, W. B. Rubusana,
  P, J. Mzimba. Messrs. J. Tengo-Jabavu,
  Peter Tyamzashe, and B. Sakuba.

  BEN SAKUBA,
  Secretary N.E.A.

  King William’s Town, March 9, 1887.

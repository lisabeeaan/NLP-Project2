We now take the opportunity of thanking the Conference for having sent
us a committee to remove the mis- : understandings that existed amongst
us as a community; misunderstandings that threatened the peace of our
church. When we were hoping that everything was ami­cably settled,
several articles appeared in the official organ of our church ; to wit,
the South African Methodist :—vide issues tor July 16th, 23rd, and 30th.
From these articles it appears, amongst other things, that the
Government has made a promise to the authorities of our Society to
survey the location known as Tsomo and Mkwinti, and to give title deeds
to the Society. As the Committee never mentioned this to us, we should
be very glad if you could give us the report of the Committee and put
before us all correspondence that has passed between the Government and
the authorities of our Society re- Tsomo lands.

Our fervent prayer is that God may bless your labour abundantly.

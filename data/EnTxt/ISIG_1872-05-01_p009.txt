SIGNS OF NATIVE PROGRESS.

WE are glad to be able to state that the notice of the Civil
Commissioner of Alice on the subject of native Title Deeds and arrears
of Quit Rent, have already produced the sum of nearly £500. It speaks
well for the circumstances and loyalty of the people that a simple
notice and a few words of explanation from the Commissioner should lead
to so satisfactory a result.

We have been informed that the Fingoes in the Transkei have guaranteed
£100 to any medical man who will take up his residence among them. This,
of course, is entirely distinct from the fees arising from his practice.

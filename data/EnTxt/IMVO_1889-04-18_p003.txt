CARL F. PAPER NO A. LUCK.

  In a county court near London recently ’there was a trial to determine
  the ownership of a dog. The Judge couldn’t make out from the evidence
  which claimant was the real owner, so he made one stand on each side,
  while the officer held the dog in the middle of the room. Then he told
  them both to whistle and the officer to release the dog at the same
  moment. When this was done the dog bolted through the open door. “Call
  the next case” was all the comment the Judge made, although the
  litigants stormed.

  “I don’t see why you should sneer at my engagement ring,” said a fair
  girl with a flush of indignation on her cheek as she faced a rival
  belle; “it’s a good deal prettier than the one you wore three years
  ago,and haven’t worn since.” “No, dear,” replied her friend, with a
  cool far-away look, “not prettier, but quite as pretty. It is the same
  ring.”

The sun never sets except in the west, but a hen will set anywhere. This
shows how far more liberal-minded than the sun a hen is.

NATIVE OPINION

  Our Third Year

  With this number we have
  the satisfaction of announcing that
  we enter on our THIRD YEAR’S exis-
  tence as a Kafir Newspaper. Native
  Opinion having been first published
  on the 3rd November, 1884.

  We have once more to thank our
  supporters and friends, and to solicit
  a hearty continuance of their
  esteemed aid. We are amply
  rewarded to hear that this journal
  is now a power in the land. We
  have not spared energy and labour
  to make it a power for good both to
  our people and to their neighbours
  of European extraction. It has
  been our endeavour fearlessly and
  boldly yet temperately withal to
  grapple with the varied and various
  obstacles that lie in the path to a
  good understanding between the
  different races that Providence has
  destined to live side by side in
  South Africa. How far we have

succeeded in this task we are not in
a position to say. It is enough,
however, to be assured, as we have
again and again been assured that,
the Imvo has carved for itself a
place among the vehicles of public
Opinion in this country.

We therefore make another

  strong appeal for increased support
  by all lovers of light and sweetness
  within and without the Cape Colony,
  promising on our part to do all we
  can to repay it.

  Raising once more our Literary
  Banner aloft, we once more cry—

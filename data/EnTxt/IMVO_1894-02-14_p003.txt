THE ALIWAL FIASCO.

THE outcome of the Aliwal § North election has been that a safe seat for
the enlightened party has been given over as a present to the Bond. In
the list Parliament Aliwal North was well and creditably represented by
two very able gentlemen, Mr. SAUFER and Mr. E G. ORSMOND ; the former an
old member of South African repute; the latter made himself a name as a
popular politi­cian, not lacking.in debating power, and had the credit
of defeating the Government on the Coal Question last Session, They both
belonged to the party that made justice to Natives also the main plank
of their platform. But the proverbial vaulting ambition caught hold of
Mr. AV. C ORSMOND. and he made a determined bid for Parliament, even
trimming his soils to catch votes—now coquetting with the Bond, then
declaring himself a supporter of Mr. RHODES’S al­though his whole past
showed him a consistent supporter of Mr SAUER’S and of the able
gentlemen who have, always been associated with the senior member for
Aliwal North. No sooner did Mr. AV.

ORSMOND make his bull-like rush at the Parliamentary gate, than Mr.
DAVID DE WET and the Bond saw their chance, and profited accordingly ;
the result of the Poll , in Aliwal North standing—Mr. SAUER 63G; Mr. DE
WET 520; Mr. E. G. ORSMOND 492, and Mr. W. C. ORSMOND following as a bad
fourth with 262. This is what we feared all along, viz., that those who
were engineering Mr. WALTER ORSMOND’S candidature were making an
egregious mistake be­cause while their man had no chance whatsoever he
would take away from his abler cousin just enough votes to allow the
Bond­man to get in. For this untoward result the Herschel people have
themselves also to thank. Had they presented a solid front and not
encouraged Mr. W. C. ORS­MOND by consenting to be split, they could have
gained their end— which is to secure a colleague for for Mr. SAUER who
would back him in his life-long endeavours to get just and good
government for the Natives of this country. The man who has prevented
our people from getting this is Mr. W. C. ORSMOND ; and they should
remem­ber this for all time. Mr. W. ORSMOND, moreover, has done this in
shameless preference of his own interests to those of the people. He did
it too when grave questions of Native land settlement are trembling in
the balance, knowing full well that Mr. E. G. ORSMOND was in entire
sympathy with Mr. SAUER, who in turn has the interest of the Natives at
heart in this matter ; while Mr. DE WET with his party will do all they
can to prevent a settlement that will be satisfactory to the Natives. We
should be only too glad to be dis­appointed in respect of our opinion as
to the attitude of Mr. RHODES and the Bond on this land settle­ment :
but when the Prime Minister hints at keeping the Native reserves intact
for five years, and for that period bartering the franchise rights of
the people ; and when, moreover, his chief lieutenant Sir G. SPRIGG is
alto­gether against confirming Natives in their own even for that
ridicu­lously short period, every Native must be filled with the gravest
apprehensions for the future ; and those friends of ours among the
Europeans who seem to feel sore because Natives have not supported
men—like Mr. SAMPSON V. and D., and AV. ORSMOND—who professed themselves
one with the Party which believes in carrying out a land policy so
disastrous to the Natives, must recognize that our people could not help
themselves.

NOTICE.

THE Transkeian Mutual Impro-
vement Society will hold its

Quarterly meeting at Butterworth
on Friday, 26 March, 1886. The
business of the evening will be (1)
An Essay on “ The housing of the
Natives ” by Mr. Geo. Pamla ; (2)
A Debate on the question “ Is it
right for educated young men to
marry uneducated girls?” Aff:
Mr. W. F. Bassi; Neg: Mr. Jer.
Kuzane (8) A Dialogue by Messrs.
P. Rozani and J. Kuzane. The
choirs will be led by Messrs. E.
Mamba and C. Veldtman. The
meeting will begin at Sunset.
New members will be received.

EDUCATION BOARD NOTICE.

A YOUNG MAN left Lovedale a year ago, on a temporary

Certificate, to undertake a school in the Transkei, and

allowed that School to dwindle away from his inefficiency. He came here
the other day, professing to have left his situation owing to the
smallness of the salary, and wished a recommen­dation to a Church of
England Missionary, who is in want of a Teacher. On the ground just
stated, this was refused, but it has since been ascertained that there
were more serious grounds for refusing a Certificate. It appears that he
had been charged by one of the headmen with a serious moral offence, and
that he was escaping from justice.

This is a case in point, to shew how guarded Missionaries should be, in
receiving young men on their own profession of coming from Lovedale,
without enquiry made here as to their antecedents, or without their
presenting a proper Certificate.

A. SMITH, Secretary of the Education Board.

NOTICE is hereby given that the Superintendent-General
of Education will hold periodical Examinations of

Teachers, and of Candidates for employment as Teachers, of
Schools in connection with the Department of Public Educa-
tion ; and Certificates will be granted to qualified persons of
either sex.

The regulations regarding the Elementary Teacher’s Cer-
tificate are as follows:—

Elementary Teacher's Certificate.

1.  .—Candidates for this Certificate, qualifying the holder
    for the charge of an Undenominational Public School of the
    Third Class, or of a Mission School, will be examined in

1.  The English Language: Reading, Dictation, and
      Grammar.

2.  Arithmetic: including Vulgar and Decimal Fractions.

3.  Descriptive Geography.

4.  Handwriting-

5.  School management.

6.  The Dutch Language, at the option of each Candidate.

II. .—Those who distinguish themselves in the Examination
    will have the words “ with honours" marked on their Certi-
    ficates.

III. .—The first Examination will be held in Cape Town on
     Friday and Saturday, 4th and 5th April, 1873; and Candi-
     dates are required to send in their names, with age and testi-
     mony to good character, on or before 1st March next.

The possession of an Elementary Teacher’s Certificate, or of
some higher and duly recognized Certificate, will in future be
indispensable to those seeking employment in Schools connected
with this Department.

Arrangements will be made for holding similar Examinations
in other places besides Cape Town-

  LANGHAM DALE,
  Superintendent-General of Education.

Education Office, Cape Town, 1st January, 1873.

NOTE.—Due notice will he given of the time and places fixed
for Examination in the Eastern Province.

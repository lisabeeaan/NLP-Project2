NOTICE.

THE Resident Magistrate of Victoria East in conjunction with such of the
members as attend, will under the provisions of Act No. 10, of 1860,
hold a meeting of the Licensing Board in the Court room, at Alice, on
WEDNESDAY the 20th day of December, 1871, at 10 o’Clock a. m., for the
purpose of granting or otherwise the undermentioned application, viz.,
Richard Austen, to keep an Hotel and Tap, at Cathcart Vale, during the
quarter ending 31st March, 1871.

  PERCY NIGHTINGALE, Resident Magistrate.

  Printed and Published at the Lovedale Missionary Institution Press,
  about the first of each month.

I.  Language, literature and history : (1) The English Language and its
    history. (2) The history of England and modern Discriptive
    Geegaphy. (3) The Latin language as specified in the list of
    subjects for matriculation. (4) Modern languages—Dutch, French,
    German, Kaffir, Sesutu. The candidates will be examined in any one
    of these languages which he may select.

II. Mathematics : (1) Arithmetic, (2) Elementary Algebra, as far as, and
    including quadratic equations. (3) Plane Geometry—the first four
    books of Euclid with deductions from them.

III. Physical Science—viz.’either (1) Chemistry, or (2) Geology, or (3)
     Light and Heat, or (4) Electricity and Magnetism, or (5) Botany,
     or (6) Zoology.

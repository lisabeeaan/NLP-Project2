VACCINATION,

MISSIONARY CONFERENCE.

Division of Victoria East.

  CASES of Small-pox having occurred on board the Royal Mail Steamers
  Norse-
  man and Northam, now placed in Quarantine in Table Bay, the inhab-

tants will perceive the necessity of taking precautionary measures to
guard
against the spread of this disease by using their best endeavours to
induce
a more extended practice of Vaccination and re-vaccination, the
observance of
cleanliness and the adoption of such sanitary measures as are best
calculated to
reduce the risk of contracting disease.

For greater security, re-vaccination on the part of those previously
vaccinated
is considered to be highly desirable.

It is therefore hereby notified, that until further notice, the District
Surgeon
will be in attendance at the Public Offices, Alice every Tuesday between
the hours
of 10 and 12 O’clock a.m. for the purpose of vaccinating or
re-vaccinating persons
of all ages gratis, and I have to urge on the public, the necessity of
systematically
returning at the proper time and place, the successfully vaccinated
children,
in order, to secure a constant and regular supply of reliable lymph.

  Acting Civil Commissioner.

THE NINTH ANNUAL MISSIONARY CONFERENCE of Missionaries labour­

ing on the Frontier will be held in the Council Room of the Town Hall,
King William’s Town on Wednesday the 12th July.

Proceedings to commence at 10 o’Clock.

Secretary.

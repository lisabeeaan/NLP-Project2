  Government House, Cape Town.

Sir,

I am directed by His Excellency the Governor to ac­knowledge the receipt
of your letter of the 8th May last, and to for­ward, in reply, for the
information of the Committee and of the Natives whom they represent, a
copy of the despatch which His Excellency has received from the
Secretary of State for the Colonies, con­veying the thanks of Her
Majesty the Queen for their sympathy and congratulations, and an
expression of Her Majesty’s gratification at the assurance of their
loyalty and good wishes.

  I am, Sir,

  Your obedient servant,

GRAHAM BOWER,

SECRETARY OF STATE TO GOVERNOR, CAPE OF GOOD HOPE.

Copy] Downing Street,

Sir,

I have the honour to inform you that the Address, enclosed in your
Despatch No. 183 of the 15th of May, has been laid before the Queen and
that Her Majesty thanks the Natives of South Africa who signed it for
their sympathy and congratulations on Her escape on the oc­casion of the
recent attempt on Her life, and is much gratified by the assurance of
their loyalty and good wishes.

  I have, &c.,

UMPATISWA WA KOMKULU, KWI RULUNELI YE CAPE OF GOOD HOPE.

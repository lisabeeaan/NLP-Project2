[FROM OUR CORRESPONDENT].

MOUNT FRERE, December 22, 1888.—The fight that took place on Friday
morning resulted in the death of two Pondos, one Griqua, and two of
Mbali’s men. Mbali’s cattle that crossed the border were captured by
Tsibiyana, a Baca chief, and driven to the Rode, and were afterwards
seized by the Magistrate of Mount Ayliff. Umdlangaso demands four
hundred head of cattle for the release of Mbali. Umdlangaso is expected
to attack Siyoyo’s people.

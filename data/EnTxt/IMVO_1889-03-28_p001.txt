  A CONTRADICTION.

IT has been reported by some unknown authority that I am about to leave the good work in which 1 have been engaged for so many years. I beg emphatically to contradict the statement, and to say that when —from a breakdown in health, or some other cause—I am likely to give up a work which has been so honoured and blessed of God in time past, I shall be sure to give due notice to the friends of our work, and of the girls. Till then the report may be considered untrue. I state this clearly, because it has caused some trouble and anxiety to the parents and friends of the girls under our care.

     J. E. DEXTER.

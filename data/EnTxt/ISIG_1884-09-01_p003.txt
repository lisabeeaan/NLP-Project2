Uyayiqondana into oyitetayo mfo ka Sicina? If you didn’t answer this
letter ; I will answer you quite presently. Because is the first time I
hear a native say that the (chieftainship) must be destroyed to natives.
God created the rulers in the world to be the rulers of the world. If
you say that the chieftainship must be deprive from N. C. we could lose
everything we possessed. Uxolo manene ndingowenu futi.—ROBT. MADLIWA,
Native Teacher, Blackfountain School, 29 July, 1884.

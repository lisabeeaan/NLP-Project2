MISSIONARY CONFERENCE.

  HE TENTH ANNUAL CONFERENCE of Missionaries of various Denomi-
  nations will (D.V) be held at King William’s Town on the 10th day of

July, in the Council Chamber, proceedings to begin at 10 o’clock a.m.
CHARLES BROWNLEE, ESQ., in the Chair.

BUSINESS.

1. Various Papers read at previous Meetings to be finally disposed of.

2. Paper on the action of the Missionary Conference, by the REV. DE.
STEWART.

3. Paper on the advisability of Government introducing a measure for
Compul-
sory Education, by the REV. RICHARD BIRT.

Instead of the Annual Missionary Meeting in the Evening, a Social
Meeting
will be held.—See Advertisements in the King William’s Town papers.

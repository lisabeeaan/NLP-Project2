STOLEN!

  ONE BROWN GELDING, about five

  years old, with a small star on forehead and
  small white specks all over body. Indistinct
  wound mark above left fore fetlock, mark of
  wound underneath root of tongue, small
  white hairs on back. ONE BROWN MARE
  about six years, with a small star on fore-
  head, hindquarters rather turned in. Both
  horses in very good condition when stolen,
  Anyone recovering the same or giving such
  information as will lead to their recovery
  and the apprehension of the thief or thieves,
  will be rewarded by METU F. TSALA, of
  Balasi Station.

  Town Office, King William’s Town,
  August 28th, 1886.

Messrs. A. T. Bell

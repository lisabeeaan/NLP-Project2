“Light in the darkness sailor day is at hand,

See o’er the foaming billow’s fair heavens land—

  Drear was the voyage sailor day almost o’er, Safe in the life boat
  sailor pull for the shore.

CHORUS

Pull for the shore, sailor pull for the shore,

  Heed not the rolling wave but bend to the oar; Safe in the life boat
  sailor cling to self no more, Leave the poor old stranded wreck and
  pull for the shore.”

Black Fountain School, 30 June, 1884.

POUND NOTICE,

Division of Victoria East.

NOTICE is hereby given that I have with the advice and consent of the
Divisional Council of Victoria East, established a Pound at Binfield
Park, Upper Tyumie, and have appointed Mr. George Humphreys to be
Poundmaster thereof.

Acting Civil Commissioner.

Printed and Published at the Lovedale Missionary Institution Press,
about the first of each month.

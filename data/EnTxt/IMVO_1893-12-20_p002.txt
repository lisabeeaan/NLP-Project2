KING WILLIAMS TOWN.

WANTED.—From the 1st January, 1891,
IT at Mount Arthur Native Institution,
a first class certificated Native Teacher.

  Salary : £52 per annum, bouse and garden
  provided.

  None but experienced Teachers having
  good credentials need apply.

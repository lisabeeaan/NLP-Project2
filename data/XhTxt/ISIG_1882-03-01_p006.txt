1.  Abakatali luhlobo lwe Titshala abazitumela ezikolweni zetu. Ndiya
    kukunika umzekelo omhle kunene, kuko i Institution ekutiwa yi Heald
    Town apo amadodana nentombi afundiselwa ubu Titshala akumashumi
    amahlanu anomvo omkulu. Kweli nani kuko amadoda­na nentombi asiwa
    ngumnqweno selemadala, kuze kubonakale ukuba akanakwenza nto,
    intloko sezi ngamatye. Aze lomatye akutshwe kutiwe makaye kufundisa
    abantwana betu. Aze lamatye amane uku landeleliswa ngoku
    landeleliswa etabata sikolo sinye baze bati abantwana oyise abazizi
    alam abangena kuba tumela ezi kolweni ezikulu (Seminaries)
    bangabinto ngapezu kwama qaba angazange angene ezi kolweni, kanti
    oyise ba batala iminyaka emashumi mabini, leyo ke yi average number
    xa umntu anaba ntwana abane mhlaumbi abatandatu. Abanye mhlaumbi
    balahlekwe ngamashumi amane nama hlanu e ponti nge mfundo engelo
    ncedo lwanto kuye naku mntwana wake. Umfundisi yena akateni so lange
    isikolo siko.

2.  Umntu one gunya lokuncedisa ukuba imali ye Titshala irolwe ngum
    Fundisi, nalapa akateni so lange ipuma imali yoku xasa yena, i
    Titshala mayizibonele ngokwayo, kuba ngelinye icala nom Fundisi
    kobonakala eyi nkatazo ebantwini, mhlaumbi kobonakala eyake imali
    ingafinyeleli xa abantu abakataza nge mali yenye indoda.

3.  Njenga bongameli abawufezi umsebenzi wabo, abezi mhlana zivalwayo
    izikolo kuza kukutaza abazali nabantwana babo. Ngati bati “vula zi
    buqe.” Iyinto yezi denge nje zinga boni siya kufumane sizi kataze.

4.  Akuko nomahlulo owenziwayo ezi Titshaleni ukuxela ukuma kwazo, ukuze
    imfundo inyanyekelwe luhlanga. Nokuba uyise womntwana uzamile ngako
    konke anako ukumfundisa kakuhle, aku katalelekile aba Fundisi bona
    baya kuyi buqisa dizeni linye. Lento ayikutazi luhlanga. Kautabate
    um Fundisi omnyama yindoda enosapo olukulu ifuna olu sapo lwayo
    ilufundisile ayinako kuba yancama konke okwe lizwe yabengisa yonke
    imfuyo yayo kuba ifuna ukunceda uhlanga lwa kowayo. Lendoda ipila
    ngama shumi asixenxe nasi bozo ngonyaka kungengokuba uhlanga lwa
    kowayo aluna kuyinceda. Ngani, ngenxa yokuba icebo lika Rev. E.
    Makiwane alitatyatwa kuba liya kuvusa izidenge aze amagwangqa
    apulukane nezisulu zawo. Litinina lona elo cebo? “Kunge kumana
    kusenziwa intlanganiso ezitile kuboniswa abantu abantsundu indlela
    yoku xasa kwabo umsebe­nzi,” &c.

Elam mna icebo nditi: kunjanina kwakulingwa iqinga lokuba uhla­nga
lungabi yiyo inzuzo nje kodwa lube lona lunga ncedakali: —

Ngecala lezi kolo lonto ilula kakulu, kunjanina kwaku landelwa le ndlela
yoku patwa kwe English Undenominational Schools.

1.  Kuti apo kuko umzi (Location) kwenziwe intlanganiso yabo bonke
    abantu.

2.  Kunikwe isaziso secawa ezintatu ukuze abantu babe nako uku bako
    bonke.

3.  Emveni kokuba kuvunyelwene ukuba kumiswe i Undenomina­tional School.
    Kufuneka kucelwe ukuba bati bonke abavumayo ba­nyule aba pati,
    bab'ale amagama abo ukunq'ina ukuba bayavuma uku batala imali ye
    Titshala, indlu yayo, indlu yesikolo. &c.

4.  Abapati baya kuwu bamba lomsebenzi iminyaka emitatu. Inani labo
    lingabi ngapantsi kwa madoda amahlanu ane ngqondo ze mfundo.

5.  Umongameli (Chairman) we ntlanganiso leyo uya kubala amagama aba
    pati besi kolo eso kunye nawo wonke umsebenzi obusenziwa kulo
    ntlanganiso awutumele ku Dr. Dale, Superintendent Genera of
    Education, Cape Town.

6.  Abapati emveni kokuba amagama abo evunyiwe ngu Rulumente baya kuti
    bavumelane nendawo eziya kuti zifunwe ngu Rulumente. Baze bazifunele
    ngo kwabo i Titshala benze nemi miso yesi kolo (Regulations.)

7.  Aba pati benze imiteto yoku patwa kwe ntlanganiso zabo, ezi ngena
    ngama xesha atile amisiweyo. Begcina ingxelo yezinto ezenziwa kwezo
    ntlanganiso.

8.  Abapati babe negunya lokwenza intlanganiso kanye ngonyaka yabo bonke
    abantu ababe vumelene ngokumiswa kwesikolo. Baze babacazele ngoku
    citwa kwe mali zesikolo. &c.

9.  Ukuze kuti kwakubako ituba elenzekayo ngakuba pati bezi kolo ngokuti
    mhlaumbi omnye angene ematyaleni, mhlaumbi afuduke, mhlaumbi abube,
    babuye bahlanganisane abantu banyule omnye endaweni yake.

Niya bona ke abapati bakumana besiya esikolweni bekangela ukuhambiseka
kwaso. Niyabona maninzi amadodana ako wetu ane mfu­ndo entle, encomekayo
angawuvumiyo lomsebenzi ngenxa yezimbandezelo ndizixelayo. Lamadodana
ange luncedo olukulu. Uyabona e Heald Town indawo efundisa i Titshala,
abantu bakulekwa esi singeni, uti umntu xa aqalayo uku tungulula
akutshwe, lonto ayilu ncedi nento uhlanga olumnyama. Masi ngabi yi
nzuzo, bati aba Fundisi betu, ne Titshala zetu zibe zicaka nje kodwa
kanti imali zibatalwa kwa siti. Malunga bubi uhlanga sikangele. Ndim,

I GRAMMAR YESI XOSA.

Nakuzo zonke i article kunjalo, nokuba ngu isi, nokuba ngu izi, nokuba
ngu aba, nokuba nguwupina, kuzo zonke i article kubako igunyana
lesikumbuzo ekuteteni eliqondisayo ukuba kusatetwa ngalanto eyayi
kankanyiwe eyayi ne article yayo etile, njengoku nje ke,

Isi denge siti entliziyweni yaso akuko Tixo.

Izi tya zam zifile, zigqityiwe ngabantwana, bapikele ukudlala ngazo.

Kuko enye into kanjako eyenziwayo ngazo ezi article. Kubako izwi li
linye elifakwa imipini ngemipini, lize liguquke litete enye into eyodwa
xa lifakiweyo omnye umpini.

  Um koba ungumti omde, lo siti tina yi yellowwood tree, isi koba ligama
  lendawo ezitile ezimile imikoba.

Maninzi ke amazwi ahlukayo nge article ukuze izwili linye libe nokubizwa
izinto ngezinto.

Kungale ndawo ye article enditi inteto yama Xosa yahlukile kakulu kwe
yama Ngesi. Ngokwesi Ngesi i article yintwana encinane; ngo a. an. the.
kube kupela. Kanti ngesi Xosa yonke inteto ukuze icace, inokuba
ilungeleliswe ngalamazwi mafutshane abati abanye zi prefix, kodwa kum
ngati ziyinto nye ne article yama Ngesi.

Ukuze ndibone ukuba niqondile mani bale ezi leteni zenu lamazwi nize
niwabekele i article ezi lunge nawo.

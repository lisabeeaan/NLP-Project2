ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1881.

EZIVELA KUBA BALELANI.

IMALI EZIBATALELWE ISIGIDIMI NGO MAY, JUNE,

Incwadi ezi sukuba zibalelwa ukufakwa e Sigidimini, kufuneka u Mhleli
yena elazi igama lo mtumeli ncwadi, nakuba lingayi ku shicilelwa ukuba
umninilo akalifuni ukuba laziwe luninzi.

Siyazisa kanjako ukuba asilitabateli pezu kwetu ityala lenteto zaba
balelani beta, asitsho nokuti siyavumelana nazo zonke incwadi zabo
esizishicilelayo.

NKOSI—Ndingavuya kakulu u Mr. Jonas J. Mcanyangwa eke wandicacisela
ukuba ababantu “babekwe izandla” bali 16 ngaba hedeni abaguquke kulo
Tyalike intsha na? Lento ndibuzayo incwadi yake ibalwe nge 17th June,
uti ke kuyo, le Tyalike iqalwe nge 21st April 1881. Ngati ishumi eline
sitandatu, ukuba sisiqamo sale Tyalike intsha kuba hedeni kwezo ntsuku
zima shumi mahlanu anesixenxe, yintsikelelo enkulu. Ukuba ababantu
baguquke mininye baza babekwa izandla, angafanela u Mr. J. J. M. nabo
wayenabo ukuti ubone into angazanga ayibone. Nam ndingati ndive into
endingazange ndiyive apa e Afrika.

Ndifuna ukwazi kanjalo ukuba lo David Mitchell litiwenina elokulwa
ityala lake. Kuko indawo yokungacaci kulomcimbi ka David Mitchell. Ikwa
nguye na lo ulwileyo waza weba?

Umntu xa abalela e Sigidimini makazi ukuba lipepa le ngwevu
zohlanga—ayicacise inteto, ati ukuba kuko ilizwi angena kulingenisa
ngesi Xosa alilungise ke nge si Ngesi. Eli liti perish neliti parish
ateta izinto ngezinto. Eli liti Chaplin belifanele ukuti Chaplain.

Kanjako uku kukumalisa kuku zaliswa liratshi eli kutiwa sisono sokuqala.

  Ndim

  E. M. Mngunikazi. Port Elizabeth.

  July 14 1881.

“INTSIKA YE TEMBA.”

Le Tempile isaqubela pambili nakuba bebaninzi abazintshaba zayo.
Amalungu ayo aku 40, kweloqela amadoda ngama 21, odade li 19. Asinto
ihlala ibako ukuti inani lodade libe ngapantsi kwelamadoda. Kuba
yimivuyo ngakumbi e Tempileni lakunyuka inani lama dodana, kuba inkoliso
ngama dodana la atyebisa abagcini benkanti. Siyatemba ukuba liyasondela
ixesha eyakuviwa ngabo bonke abakulo mandla wase Uitenhage imita
yokuzola epuma kule Ntsika Yetemba. Sesike sayibona into enokwenziwa yi
Tempile e Bayi nalapa.

Kwiminyaka elishumi edluleyo Amalawo ase Uitenhage ebe kwisimo esipantsi
ngenxa yokusela, kwapola ne ramente, kwafa nezikula, kwati kumaxa
engekuba kuya tandazwa alabiko ixesha ngenxa yamatyala oku xabana
kwabantu ekufuneka kumana kutetwa wona. Wafika u Rev. H. Kayser wenza
umsebenzi omkulu wokuhamba evuselela ezindlwini, etshatisa usapo lwama
kolwa olushweshileyo. Yonke lomizamo yake yanceda kancinane, wada
wayicana eyona ndawo ibutataka kuyo le remente. Kanti iremente ite
bukuqu etywaleni. Waqala ukuzigquta-gquta indlu zamakolwa angacebi
akuyibaqa ibotile yotywala imi etafileni—ayitululele pantsi. Wati kanti
uyasinda umzi. Namhla ke kubuye imihla emibi kuhlanga olumnyama ama Xosa
nama Tshaka. I Lawo alisabonakali pambi kwe nkanti. I Lawo line Tempile
yalo ekuma 200 amalungu; line Benefit Society enamakulu amatatu £300 e
Bankini; line Burial Club ene £100 e Bankini. Isiqala sesisiq'amo
kwakutshwa ibotile endlwini yatululelwa pantsi.

Eyokuqala itempile yaba ntsundu yema ixeshana elifutshane yafa nge 1877.
“Intsika yetemba” ise mnyaka mnye imi, ukuhamba kwayo kuquba kancinane
bamana bengena ngabanye. Lupawu oluhle olu. Umti okaulezayo ukukula
kwawo owomeleli.

Abapati bale Tempile ngu Rev. Samuel Ntsiko, G. T. T., no Isaac Wm.
Wauchope G. S.

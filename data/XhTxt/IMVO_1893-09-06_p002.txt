Bandla ngokuwuquba kakubi umsebenzi. Lipalele kakulu ixesha, amandla
abantu atululelwe into ezingezizo zakulonto, ngendlela ebange ukuba
wonke ade eve ete-futu ngumsindo. Imbangi yoku sakuyipanda siyifumana
ibubutatasholo bamanene la atabate izitulo zabalauli kwakuba kupume o
Messrs. SAUER, MERRIMAN no
INNES. Awangawo amadoda abuchopo buqinileyo nobuhlambulukileyo
njengalamadoda apumileyo. Ewe, angaba anave u Mr. RHODES ; kodwa elonene
belingazanga lavelela ekupateni uchuku lwemicimbi enjenge yokuqutywa kwe
Palamente. Linene elibaluleke kwinto ezibanzi njengezokumbiwa kwe
daimani, nokutabata amazwe akude njengela kwa Mzilikazi. Ngalamatuba
ugaxaza ngokoyikekayo ukuhamba kwawo umsebenzi. U Sir GORDON, noko
ayinkabi endala ubengazanga wayindoda emi ngezinto ezipuma kwe yayo
ingqondo. Ngapambili ube sakwayama ngo Sir THOMAS UPINGTON, namhla
uyedwa, kuba ingamadoda ayengenamanqaku avela nawo kakade la ango.
Messrs. LAING,
SCHREINER no FOLOSI. Yinto ke ekuti eliqela lakusondeza into e Bandla
isuke ibe yeyokugxwalwa ngentsini zizilumko ezitatu ezipumileyo
nazezinye ezikunye nazo ezinje ngo Messrs. HAY, JONES, SOLOMON, TAMPLIN,
CAPT. BRABANT, nabanye; oti ke umsebenzi ungabi nabani
uwukokelayo. Nento abayisondezayo abalauli ifike yenziwe intlekisa

ABALIMI NEMPAHLA.

ENYE yezinto ezixakileyo yinkatazo yokutyana kokutya. Abalimi bakala
kodwa ngenkatazo yempahla emasimini. Elabo bati : “Siti sakulima kuqala
asuke amavila azigalele emasimini impahla.
Ingqolowa asisayilimi,—nehabile, neralasi—zonke izinto ezilinywa ebusika
; ngenxa yokuba amavila
alima umbona yedwa asuke anga
zigcini inkomo.”

  Abanye benjenje esabo isililo—
  “ Liti libalele ilanga, kungeko nento ekubonakala ukuba kungalinywa
  yona, basuke bafumane barwele ababantu bakuteleyo, kuze kuti kwa
  kungeka pumi nto—kungeko ’nto iya kupuma
  ngenxa yelanga—baqale babambe
  inkomo zakunyatela kulomagada.”
  Zinkulu ke zombini ezonto. Icebo ke linye kubo bonke, leli :—Biyelani
  amasimi ngocingo. Asililo icebo elitsha eli. Licebo elaqalwa kwa
  ngawokuqala amapepa, emva ko NONGQAWUSE, nanamhla lisafuneka. Ngalo
  lonke ixesha ekusabiywa ngenkwenkwe akuko nqubo iyakwenziwa kwizilimo
  zobusika. Yintonina yona imbangi yokuba lombona alinywe yedwa kanti
  uyakumela ukutyiwa yedwa; imali zerafu ziya kupuma kuye yedwa; ingubo
  zokwambata zipuma kuye yedwa, imali zesikolo neminikelo yetyalike
  zipuma kuye yedwa? (Enye into enkulu epuma kwa kulombona Yindlala.)
  Akuko nto yimbi ibopelele abantu bakowetu kulo Uya kukungabiyeli
  amasimi. Asiyikuze sibe ngabantu ngokulima umbona yedwa, zize izinto
  ezinokusinceda xa sukuba imvula zingananga ehlotyeni zingalinywa
  ngenxa yokuba siyoyika ukukatazwa yimpahla.

  Malunga nesikalo saba banjelwa
  impahla, okunene kona abantu bayabulalana. Kwati kwafika olutshaba
  lwenkumbi nje kuko abantu abaxolela ukulala ezindle ukuze babambe
  impahla yabanye kwezondiza zenkumbi. Baguxene tu kwezinye indawo
  ngokubambelana. Kuko abangeka kangelani kakuhle nangoku. Intango
  zintlobo mbini kona. Umlimi ubiya nge Sikiti, umfuyi ubiya
  ngenkwenkwe. Ezonto zombini azipilisi, kuba azina ngqolowa.
  Makahlangane amadoda ngecebo lokubiya ngocingo, ukuze kube nokulinywa
  amaxa onke ngexesha
  kungeko kuti omnyo alinde omnye.

KUMA TEMPILE.

Kwincwadi yam yokuqala ndinike ama- cebo amabini pezu kwenkatazo
ezimbini. Kweyokuqala, engezimilo ezingcolileyo knina Tempile, ndite—“
Mabakutshelwe ikomiti (ibametshi, bohlwuywe ngokuhlazisa umhlambi.”
Kweyesibini, yokwamkela amalungu e Ramente odwa e Tempileni, ndite: “
Vtilani amasango ma Tempile, bangene bonke, nize nibaqeqeshe.

Ndingena namhla ekuchazeni umbuzo oti: “Ukwapula idinga ebu-Tempileni
kukutinina?” Litinina idinga lobu Tempile ?

I. Idinga lobu-Tempile lintlantlu mbi­ni. Kuqala, butywala; okwesibini,
sisimilo somntu esisimbi, Idinga ngotywala lidinga izinto ezisixenxe :

(1) Ukungabenzi utywala,

(2) Ukungabutengi.

(3) Ukungabutengisi.

(4) Ukungabuseli.

(5) Ukungapi ngabo.

(6) Ukungabangi ukuba kupiwe ngabo,

(6) Ukungabangi ukuba kuselwe.

Idinga ngesimilo somntu kukuba Azipate ngohlobo lokuba umsebenzi wobu
Tempile nemfunchso zabo zidunyiswe nga­bo bonke abawaziyo”

Kusemhlotsheni ke ukuba umntu owapule enye yezindawo zisixenxe zoluhlu
lokuqala wapule idinga, nokuba umntu ometshayo (umpimisi) wapula uluhlu
lwesibini lwedinga lake kuba uhlazisa umhlambi,

II. Izohlwayo o Tempileni zizipina? (1) Kuko ukuhlutwa indawo yobugosa
ukuba ulilo. (2) Kuko ukumiswa emalungelweni okungena i Tempile ade
atembise ukuhamba ngolunye uhlobo. (Kuxa ke ihambo yake yakuba ilandwe
yi Komiti, ifunyenwe engemsulwa ezizi- ndlweni zokungcola nakuba
engadanga ati bhutyu sonweni sibalaseleyo). (3) Kukunqunyulwa alahlelwe
pandle aze ati rnhla abuyayo angene njengomntu owapule idinga. (4)
Kukutyiwa imali, (5) Kukucela kwi Ndlu Enkulu ukuba imhlute amawonga
atile, (Esike sisibheno kwabatile abanamawonga abawazuza kwi Ndlu
Enkulu).

Ukuba i Komiti ziyawuqonda umsebe­nzi wazo zininzi inkatazo
ezingazilungisayo. Okwangoku ide ityefeke yonke intlanganiso ngesimilo
esibi somntu opambili, mhlaimbi oligosa, ibe Komiti ikohliwe ukuba
ingena pina. Oku kubangwa kukuba kuko uluvo ezikomitini ezimiyo
(standing committee)—eyabange- niswa, eyabaroxi, neyabapuli dinga—ukuba
maziwuziselwe umsebenzi, kanti oufuneka ukuba ziwufune, ziwulande
ksukuba ungenamsizi.

Ukutsho nditeta lento, noko abakade beba zikomiti njengam bondiva :—Kuti
maxa wambi kubeko umingimingi lokuba umzalwana otile nokuba ngudade
wenze into etile. Ilungu le Komiti lakuyiva into enjalo yindawo yalo
ukuyilanda pakati kwamalungu ide ivele itetwe. Indlebe ze Komiti
mazibaze—nokuba izuze igama lokuba nguntaminani ludumo olo kuyo—bako
ababulalayo, mababeko nabapilisayo ontaminani.

Kumcimbi obalulekileyo wetyala loku- metsha nezinye izinto zokungcola
ine lungelo i Tempile ukumisa ikomiti ebalulekileyo (special committee)
ngokubalula abatile kumalungu-abantu abakulu —abazalwana ukuba yindoda,
odade uku­ba yinkazana lowo weyeleyo. Xa nditi abametshi mabakutshelwe i
Komiti ndi­teta i sjiccial committee. Emva kokuba iwupicotile umcimbi
mayiyibhale ingxelo yayo, ize ibekwe pambi kwe Executive ye Tempile. (I
Executive liqumru lalamagosa :—i D.G.T.T.; D.G.S,; C.T.; P.C.T.; V.T.;
Chaplain ; Secretary ; Treasurer) ize yandule ukuya e Tempileni.

ISAZISO SE RULUMENTE—Sama 601, 1882.

  Ofisi yo Mpatiswa Micimbi ye Koloni,

31 May, 1882.

INGXELO NGE ZILIMO.

INGCAMANGO zabo bonke ekusingiselwe kubo, ziya celwa ukuba zike
ziqwalasele le nteto kugqitywe kuyo Yindlu Ebekekileyo yama Bunga {House
of Assembly), ekuvunyelwene ngayo ngolwesi Bini, 9 May, 1882, eyile:— “
Ukuba kubekwe pezu kwe Tafile ingxelo yonyaka, etelekisa ihambiselo
pambili yezi Limo, ne Mfuyo, nezi Nyiti

zalo Mhlaba. Nalendlu yama Bunga, icinga ukuba de zibeko izilungiselelo
ekufuneka ziko xa esi sigqibo siza kuhanjiswa, indawo ejongwe siso
yofezwa ngokwaneleyo, yingxelo engati inikwe Izibonda ibandakanywe nenye
engavela kweminye iminombo anayo u Rulumente.”

2.  . Ukuze ke abantu, ekungati exesheni bacelwe malungana nesi Sigqibo,
    bayazi imo yengxelo engati icelwe, icelelwa ilungelo labemi beli
    lizwe, kucingelwa ukuba kufanelekile ukwazisa, kwangoku, ukuze
    iqondwe eyona nto ifunwayo, ukuma koku balwa kwamapepa ekucingelwa
    ukuwasebenzisa, kuze kuqukwe amanani nge zinto esezi cazu-cazululwe
    malungana nase kuqaleni kule nteto.

3.  Ukuhlutisa umnqweno we Parlamente kungena kwenzekanje, ngapandle
    koncediso oluntliziyo ’nyelwabemi beli lizwe, u Mpatiswa Micimbi
    yeli lizwe utemba ukuba imizamo ye Rulumente, eyondele ekuqukeni
    ingxelo, ezifezekileyo nezi tembekileyo, zoku hambisela pambili
    kweli lizwe, kwizi limo, nemfuyo, nemi sebenzi enje ngokumba
    izinyiti, yoti ixaswe ngomoya owaneleyo woncedisano.

  J. C. MOLTENO,

U Mpati Micimbi yeli Lizwe.

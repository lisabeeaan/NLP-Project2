MNUMZANA omhle Mhleli ne-Sigidimi sabantsundu ndifakele lamazwi
kwelopepa lomhlambi wama Afrika, ndihlaba umkosi kubemi belilizwe
kumacala ngamacala, kumadoda, kubafazi, kumankazana, notandazo
kubakonzi, nakubakonzikazi bokumkani wonapakade.

Ningati cwaka bazalwana nodade, i Afrika ayivumi ukulisela iyeza
losindiso, ayivumi nokusondela apo likandwa kona inqwememile, nditi
inqwememile ayisondeli kufupi neyeza losindiso. Nabakufupi kulo abavumi
kulisela. Nabekukumbuleka ukuba bawasele amanzi obomi, kulusizi ukuba
nditi inxenye ayiwaselanga kude kupele inxano lemvelo, hai! Ngamandla
aloqaqaqa abahlakuli bakohlwe kukuyicokisa intsimi ye Nkosi, kufanelana
sesizibonakala kakuhle izitombo, kuti mhla kubizwa igama lomtshato ncama
kumhla koti kusitiwa gelekeqe ngapaya ibibune kwetu intsimi, kanti koti
nokuba kwenziwe yimbi imvuselelo zobengezela elixa ungekabiko umtshato.
Ukubako komtshato kufana nokubalela kwelanga kuntsimi yolwalwa. Ulwaluko
lukulakazi olukulu kwakufika ixa lokwindla, boziti bululu ibulukwe,
bashiye nesimnari, zekuti bakupila batengelwe ezintsha bonke. Musa
ukulindela uburamente ababebubo, kofika abantu aba- ngati abazange
balufumane uguquko. Ezonto zombini ziya kupendu- Iwa ngubanina ngomhla
we Nkosi ? Boba msulwana abazali bonyana nentombi ? Anditeti ngolobolo
abati sisonka ngenxa yokubawai nokunqena ukusebenza, nomnqweno wokunga
bungati ubutyebi obu buxelise icibi likupa ityuwa baze bonasebe mane
besenza amaviyo-viyo amakwelo behlanganisela ezintlanti bengabilanga,
ati nosebenze wabila umnqweno wemvela yoninakulu angavumi ukwenela
zimpahla zokupila kwake, afune nezenzuzo esisulu. Ndihlaba umkosi yeyele
i Afrika zihlobo zayo. Putumani! putumani! Ungafana umzi unododana
oluwele esandlenl. Unamadoda awazana nobedesho.

James Mjila.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 4, 1871.

INKUMBI! INKUMBI!!

KWELE DIAMOND.

Asikuko nokuba ezizilwana ziwungene- le Umnquba zenza inkatazo enkulu.
Zise ngumqikela nje, zisahamba pantsi, zisuka zibe yimisinga ukubekisa
emlanjeni. Zo- ngezelela kwizitukutezi zempilo yase Mnqubeni kuba
zinganeli ukuwugubungela umhlaba, koko zingena nase zitenteni nase
zinqweleni, nakwezinye indawo zokuhlala ngokuqanqalaza. Zipikele
ukuhlala nabembi ezindlwini zabo, xa kulalwayo naxa kudliwayo. Ziti xa
kudliwayo ziniane ukuqakaqakata zingena kwinto yonke, kunqabile ukuba
ude ugqibe ukudla ungakange uzidle ezinye. Iba zizo ezincamla iswekile
yomntu, zixumele ekofini yake, zitsibele nase mafuteni, ziti isitebe
senyama zisenze sibe bala limbi. Ibike izimpukane zodwa ezibe zikataza
xa kudliwayo, kodwa zona inkumbi zongezelele ububi obungapezulu, obude
bukumbuze izibeto ze Egypt (Jipete). Ziti zakuba zinganelanga kukudla
kwabembi zitye zigqoboze iminxuma ezitenteni; zizitsheqatsheqe zizenze
amajacu ingubo zabo esezisonakala kade; uti oshiye ingutyana yake alike
iseyizele ziziroba. Siyakuvuma ukuba kunokungxengelelwa, kodwa into
eyenziwa zizo inokumangalisa kunentsomi. Ute omnye wabembi, U-Mr.
Connolly, eye kutiyela intlanzi, wati ngenxa yobushushu wayixbma
ezingcongolweni ibatyi yake. Wati ke xa aza kuyinxiba wafika omnye
umkono nenxa- lenye yomhlana webatyi ungaseko, udliwe zinkumbi ezo. Zide
zati xa ziyidlayo “ Sibonwa yinina betu! ” Omnye kwa kubembi, ute eye
kuquba wati xa asesi zibeni waqonda seleraulwe zinkumbi sezikwela
nasezintloko—ngokuba ziti ngokunxamela pambili zitsibele nase manzini.
Kude kuti ukuhamba kwazo pezu kwendlu kunge kukuna kwemvula. Ixala
labembi abanehabile alitetwa, kuba kurolwa isheleni ngesitungu. Omnye
ongumtengisi walapo, selede wawuyeka umsebenzi wake ukuba aye kugxota
aba batshabalalisi ehabileni yake, ngesiziba sibotshelelwe entongeni;
akancedanga Into noko. Ungaqondake nawe ukuba kuxakekile xa amadoda
amakulu ase Rini, nase Bayi, nase Kapa, ade abe seloko emi kulomse-
benzi wenkumbi. Asikuko ke nokuba sikulu esisibeto Emnqubeni. Akulapake
kodwa apo kunjalo. Indoda evela E- Kamastoni iti kulo lonke ilizwe
ecande kulo akuko apo zingekoyo.

Kwasekukangelekeni kwazo zibonakala ukuba ngaba tshabalalisi;
nokonakalisa kwazo kuyoyikeka. Kungaba okwenene kuko nto zidalelwe yona,
kodwa uncedo Iwazo alwaziwa, kanti incitakalo eziye-

nzayo isekuhleni. Izihlobo zetu ezimgama zingamangaliswa zakuva
ukuhlakazeka kwenkohlakalo etile ngenxa yenkumbi, kuseloko zati zafika
akuzanga kubeko zituko zigqita ezi zase Mnqubeni ngoku.

Sosiqukumbela esisililo setu ngokuni- balisela ngempuku eyabonwa
yintokazi etile, isidla inkumbi ngase tenteni yayo. Ide yaba yinto
elondolozwayo, ikalitshiselwa kulomsebenzi wayo. Yaquba ngokunjalo ke
intsuku zontatu, ihluta kuku­dla engahlal’ikudla.

Yati ukutyeba nokuguda oku Iwati uvuyo lwayo lwada lwakupatelela nase
mseleni; umnqweno wayo ungowokuti, Zanga azingeze zapela ! Kodwa ke ide
yati kanti idle kunene, yahluta ngokugqitileyo. Iteke kwakuba njalo
yagcakamela ilanga pezu kwetyeya yalala, kanti kumhla iza kuhlelwa
lilishwa. Kufike indoda yalomfazi yayibona, ayabi nakunyamezela ukuka
ibe ikangelene nempuku, yasel’isuka iyiti xamfu ngesandla yayibulala.
Bafanele ukuyililela lempuku abase Mnqubeni; [nani nina niyifunda
lembali yayo ngesixosa ngenibalilisa.]

AMATYALA ABEKO EDIKENI.

[PAMBI KOSESIKUNDHLENI SE MANTYI ONGU P. A. MADEE, ESQ.]

Thursday, ngo 2 December.

U-Zipo enetyala lokuba ete wabeta umfazi ngokufuna ukumbulala. Latetwa
elotyala kwafanela ukuba lilinde umgwebi. Kwabonakala entetweni yomfazi
ukuba kute mfuleni utile kwa Gaga, U-Zipo waqubisana nomfazi wake,
wambuza ukuba uye ngelikabanina kowabo, engavunyelwanga nguye nje;
waselembetake ngoswazi emwisa pantsi ehlala pezu kwake erola isitshetshe
emsika emqaleni efuna ukumbulala.

Ute yena U-Dr. Palmer ukulixela inxeba, linzulu lilide, esiti ngati yena
sesinye isandhla esisike elinxeba kuba kambe iti yona indoda ngumfazi
ozisike ngokwake, ngunawe nawe onjalo.

Liteke elotyala lalungiswa ngumkangeli wamatyala, ukuba I-Mantyi
iligwebe wazi- swake U-Zipo pambi kwe Mantyi nge 12 December.

Yatike I-Mantyi ubunqina obukoyo kwe lityala bobomfazi yedwa, obunge na
kuqiniswa ngabumbi, busweliswa amandhla ngokuti kungafunyanwa gazi
endodeni, kwati kanjalo ubunq'ina bo Tyala no Jacob, bayinceda indoda
leyo—yaselinikwa ityala lokubeta umfazi ngoswazi yasinda kwelokumsika
emqaleni ngesitshetshe yagwetywa ukuba iblaule iponti iti

ukuba ayinayo ibeveki mbini etolongweni isebenza kalukuni.

Friday, ngo 8 December.

U-Mene wamangalela U-Nduluka uyise womfazi wake, ngenxa yokuba ete aku-
baleka umfazi lowo ka Mene waya ku yise, wati yena wamkusela, ke U-Mene
umbiza amashumi omabini eponti ngendhleko abenayo.

Yatike I-Mantyi ibucokisile ubunqina belityala, kusekuhleni ukuba U-Mene
watshata nentombi ka Nduluka ngokuse- mtetweni, nokokuba yona yemka
kwindoda yayo ingeyiyo intan do yayo, yaya kuhlala ku Nduluka, akuko
mntu unamandhla okukusela umfazi wenye indoda, ewe ndiyalwazi utando
lomzali kumtwana, endingeti ndimdele kanye usonkazana, kodwake nangona
kunjalo wona umteto awuvumeli ukuba akuselwe umfazi womnye umntu
ngomnye, ebengatike usonkazana ukutintela londawo asike amvalele
ngapandle kwendlu yake. Itshoke yagweba ngelokuti makahlaule usonkazana
isheleni ibenye kwandawonye nemali efunwa ematyaleni, yabake iyonke
lomali £2 11s. 6d.

Ngolwe 19 ku December, kwakuziswe amakwenkwana amabini U-Cacab no Cikwa
ematyaleni E-Dikeni, ngokuba ibokwe ka Mr. William Els wakwakona.
Bafunyenwe benetyala, bagwetyelwa ukuba babetwe imivumbo elishumi
elinamihlanu emnye; kwatiwa ke mababuyele koma wabo.

Kukutshwe incwadi yokubamba U-Mfu- nzana igqira lemfengu yase Nqushwa,
ngenxa yokudlisa abantu Emxelo, kwafa batatu. Babuya ezibini abatatu.

INDABA NGOCINGO.

UMKOMBE ekutiwa yi Norseman ufike E-Cape Town ngomgqibelo ngolwa 31 ku
December. Uze nendaba zokuti I-Europe yonke ipambane, zibonakal’
ukungati izizwe zakona zonke ziza kulwa.

I-Russia iseyite izakuwapula umnqopiso owawenziwe ukuze kuxolwe
ngomnyaka we 1856; iseyilungiselela imfazwe kolu- lwandle kutiwa yi
Black Sea.

U-Gortschakoff, itshawe lase Russia, seletumele ilizwi kwinkosi yase
Turkey eliti uyakuwapula umnqopiso obekisele kwinani nobukulu bemikombe
enokuvunyelwa ukuba ingene kolo lwandle.

I-England, ne Austria, ne Italy, ne Turkey zihlangene ngeliti
azinakuyivumela I-Russia ukuba yenze oko.

Isizwe esingati siyayivumela londawo yi Prussia. Kungati kuko
ukuyivumela nase America kweli kutiwa yi United States.

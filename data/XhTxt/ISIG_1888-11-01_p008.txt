ngokuba kangela kutwa “ uzakudla isonka ” isonka sipuma emblabeni,
akutshiwongo ukutwa “ sebenza imali wena uyeke umhlaba ” kutwa “ lima
umhlaba udle ngokonwaba ” into zonke zilungile ukuba zibeko azideleki
nazo, kodwa umhlaba uwulime udle ngokonwaba, ungabi libadubadu ngenxa
yezinye izinto, kanti zizona zineinane kunokulima umhlaba. Beke
ndayikankanya lonto yabo batshoyo ukuti umhlaba awuna msebenzi into
enomsebenzi yimali; ndaza ndati lonto kukulahleka, kukuzany’iminwe nje
ngo mntwana ndingati mna musa ukuyilahla nayo imali ngenxa yokulima, hai
ukuba uyindoda lima kuqala wandule ukutata inqwelo yako uyokufuna imali.
Na- ngona ndinyanzelekile kangaka pezu kokulima andimdeli naye lowo
ofuna imali—nenkomo—ngokuba ezizinto ziyasebenzisana zonke; musa
ukulahla umhlaba ke ngenxa yemali; njengokuba nalapa e-Skapu sendibone
abantu abaninzi abalahle imihlaba yabo ebangeniselayo bnya kulahla
isimbo sabo ngo Poyiyana! Manditi kwakona indoda enomhlaba nase Kafile
iwuhlaulele ne tayitile ziqiniswe, ezobuzwe nezeziqu ukuba konke oko
kunokwenzeka; ikakulu ndirunele eku- beni ezindawo zimbini ziqondwc
ngumzi.

a. Umhlaba ulinywe kakulu intlobo zonke zezityalo, ezonto zenziwe
ngabanini mihlaba, ukuze imisebenzi ilungelelane kakuhle ngokute tse.
nendlala nditeta nje izakubopa inkutalo isaqala ukungena ekaya.

b. Ingaba kukangelwe kakuhle ukuba imihlaba seyihlaulelwe, ukuba
amntyala emihlaba yako onke ahlaulwe msinya ngokuba lonto yonisela,
umntu emhlabeni wake uzakubuyela ngaku Rulumeni uknba intlaulo ilityclwe

IMPENDULO KU AKANAXESHA.

Inteto ka “ Akanaxesha” ayifake kwi Mvo ka4th Oct. ayiqondakali- Kuqala
ndifuna ukupendula umbuzo awusingise ka mhleli welopepa- —uti
kulungilena ukubulela ku-Tixo ngokukululwa kwe Nkosi ? Kulungilena
ukubulela ku-Mdali into eyenziwe ngu Rulumente? Kulungilena ukunikela
izinto zika Kesare ku-Tixo ? Nditi ukupe- ndula into ezenziwa ngu Tixo
uzenza ngabantu, u-Rulumente okulule. Inkosi u tanjiswe ngu Tixo. U
Rulumente yinja ka Tixo akananto ilungileyo angayenza ngokwake, yinto
ekohlakeleyo yodwa angalatiswe Tixo umntu. Umsebenzi wenceba ungo
ka-Tixo yedwa wokuxolela aboni ekufuneka nabenifana naye: Kanti umntu
akanako ngokwake engapiwanga, ukuba u Rulumente ebengati atakazele elako
ebengaba unje ngo Herode owatyiwa zimpetu, nawe ukuba umoya wako uwu-
singisela kulondawo unje ngambangela mpetu.

U Rulumente ufanele ukubulelwa ngoxolo alwenzileyo ukuvumela- na no
mteto oti xolelani ukuze nixolelwe, ibe ngoko into ka Kesare inikwe
kuye, abeke eyifumene u-Tixo eyake ? Ubeke ngokoke umpa- ngile yona.
Kanti nawe uti wakupiwa into uti enkosi, kanti usaya kusa isandla ebunzi
uti Tixo.

Utsho wati lombali akakolwa ukuba zonke inkululo zababanjwa zenziwa yi
Nkosi akakolwa ukuba inkwenkwana eyadubula abantwana baka Haihai
yakululwa ngu Tixo kwanamasela ekufika kungabiko bungqina bumhlope
bezenzo bazo bakululwe. Kuqondwa nganina ukuba Inkosi zikululwe ngu Tixo
?

Impendulo nditi mna kuse kuhleni zona zikululwe ngu Tixo kuba zona ku
ngqineka ukuba zingaba vukeli baka Rulumente nazo azilandulanga nanamhla
azilanduli zagwetywa yi Jaji mhlope. Suka omandla makulu wasombulula
isandla sika Rulumente wanenceba suka no Rulumente wavuma ngapandle
kweqinga. Kwahlukile ngoko- okwabantwana baka Haihai, namasela akululwa
ngokuswEleka kobu- nqina obucacileyo.

Lo wona ngumsebenzi wofefe wenceba wokubulelwa kanye. Utsho ati kungati
ke kwabulelwa ezityalikeni bati abantsundu ezi nkosi za- dlelwa indlala
waza u-Tixo wazikulula ngokuba msulwa kwazo ngati oku kubulela
kuyakubonisa ukuba okukubulela u Rulumente waye posisile.

Ndiyampikisa nalapo, ngosavelapina lomntu untsundu ungatshoyo kodwa
akusiqashina ? Litenina wena lento ungalibekanga igama lako ? Woyika
ntonina lento umane uteta ele kwetyolo ? Kunga- tiwa selehleli esazi
wonke umntu ontsundu ukuba inkosi inetyala kuleyo ilwa nayo nokuba
kungasipina nasipina isizatu sekude kwa- qutyiswana akuseko nteto tina
ibibulawa ema-Ngesini iyabanjwwa abantu abanenceba kuba beno Tixo. Kodwa
akutshitshisi elicebona ? Nangalendawo uti kwati u Wesile no Tshatshi.

J. MASINGATA,

Regu, Middle Drift, 13th October 1888.

UKUNGEHLI NOMSINGA.

NKOSI—Lento ikukuti umlambo uzele, ukukulisa izigodo, ewe, into- yonke,
kanti noko kuko ubani ongawukataleleyo. Kuvakala, ukuba wauzele umlambo
kwa Mdange, isina imvula yo Nkanunu, nabanye, yana wahamba
ezintilini—ngalomhla. Iti Imvo kwemka wonk- umntu, nditetanje abakaziwa
apo lomlambo waba lahla kona, nokuba wabasampela elwandle—kodwa
masitembe ukuba noko umke nabonjalo lomlambo, zona izidumbu zofunyanwa,
bate ababe raxwe njekodwa ngamanzi, babetwe ngentlabati nangemizi
bawahlanze lomanzi, bawat-shibiziswe.

Ukungehli nomsinga bubudoda ukungabi naku cengwacengwa kwen- doda,
nokuhlala ezwini layo lonto injaio ite Imvo ukuyibiza “ kukufela
Enyanisweni.“ Wenjenjalo lomfo ka Rubusana, no A. H. Maci.

Baninzi abantu ongaba qeshayo ngesheleni ngemini ukuba kukutshwe or
kudutyulwe kubo, njengama Valanti nomajoni, njalonjalo abantu
abaqeshiweyo, beqeshelwe ukubulawa bavume nabo ukubulawa nge- sheleni
ngemini.

Mhlayimbi kulomavalanti akuko nalinye elingavumayo ukuba li- bengeze
eluntwini uluvo Lwalo ngani ? Tu lento kutiwa yi moral courage.
Unesibindi mfondini wakwa Mdange, ukuti izinonopu o-Tshambuluka, o-Egan
no-Jay njalonjalo, ude ubonakale uyiti nqi- ntamo, usindwa zizito,
uhlale, ufele unga shukunyiswanto ezweni lako—Ngamana wenjenjalo futi
walekele ukwenjenjalo—ukuze ngomso kwaziwa ngamakowenu, ukuba xa
ungayingeniyo into, ayilungile— zengoko balinge ukukulandela, nokufuna
izwi lako ukuba utinina kulento.

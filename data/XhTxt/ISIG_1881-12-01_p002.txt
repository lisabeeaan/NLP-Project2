IMPAWANA.

BEKUKE kwatiwa u Mr. H. M. Stanley, owaka wahambela empakatini we
Africa, ukuya kuwu hlola, obuye wabuyela kufupi nje, uyafa kakubi
ngangokuba kungabiko temba. Baninzi abovuya ukuva ukuba upilile omelele
nje gokwapambili.

ZAFA intshayi! E Cape Town, umpati we nqwelana zololiwe ufake isaziso
sokuba, wonke ofunyenwe etshaya pakati kwe nqwelo, ze treni ngapandle
kwendawo ezi miselweyo wodliwa iponti zombini. Kwaba hamba indlela, ze
bafike bavutywe nentshayi kuloliwe, lomteto wofana netamsanqa elihla
pezulu.

YIN’ LE! Sada sayi fumana ibaliwe into esihlala siyi bona, noko singa
xeleli mntu ngayo. Elinye lama pepa apesheya axoxa ngoku hamba kwe
Lizwi, libuza lombuzo: Yintonina engenziwayo ukuze umlisela uye
etyalikeni? Liti elinye: Zamela ukuba amantombazana aye, mhlobo
ongcwele, zamela ukuba amantombazana aye.” Kaniyi kanyele lonto sibone,
madodana.

TINA kute sakuteta ngemfundo, kwi Sigidimi sika Nove­mber, sisiti
lemfundo udodana lumka lunayo ezikolweni ayanele, seva intsebelele
zokuti sino vela ngempumelelo yaba fana nomtinjana obungene uviwo Iuka
September. Kwe lanamhla ipepa kofunyanwa incwadi ka W. P. wase Graham’s
Town, ogalela kwa kwintento yetu; singati lombaleli wetu uteta
ekanyiselwa kukwazi okukulu ngezi kolo nemfundo. Inteto yake ifanelwe
kukwetyiswa ngabo bonke abacinga ngokufundisa intsapo yabo. Masitembe
ukuba akuko wobuya ati ngumona owabanga ukuba sibale ngohlobo esabala
ngalo, emva kwencwadi ka W. P., ebonisa ukuba asisiti sodwa ababona
ingozi emfundweni yododana lwako wetu njengokuba injalo namhla.

OMNYE waba baleli bama pepa alapa e Koloni opesheya uti, ebala nge 13,
October, ukusuka kolu iveki ezimbini, kobako isidlo esikulu, e London,
esonzelwa u Mr. Sprigg. Aba menyiweyo ngaba:—Sir B. Frere, Lord
Brabourne, Sir Donald Currie, M.P. Hon. John Laing, M. L. A., Mr.
Hubbard, M.P. Mr. L A. Vintcent, M. L. A., nabanye. Inteto awoyenza apo
u Mr. Sprigg soyilandela kakulu.

NGENENE akuko unga porofetayo ukuba beku ngaba yintonina uku cuma kwe
Sigidimi ukuba bonke abamkeli baso bebe ngaba nale ntliziyo yale ndodana
iti, xa itumela imali yayo ngezi Stamps :—“Mandisi batale esam Isigidimi
noko’lisekude ixesha laso kum, ngomteto lowo nifuna kurolwe kuqala imali
ukuze situnyelwe. Nantso ke lomali Nkosi ndiyikupa ngovuyo 5s.—Mawuhambe
umsebenzi weli pepa kabini ngenyanga. Ndingavuya bayirole bonke abantu,
ungaqumbi nkosi.”

AMA bedengu kubonakala ukuba ayanda. I Bulu abati ngu Oelsfe wabe hamba
ne nqwelo zake ku mandla wese Uitenhage. Zite xa zikululwe, kwafika
igqalashwana lo nondala, lam hlohla-hlohla ngendatyana ezingeni kwada
kwalalwa. Kute ebusuku kwavakala inkwenkwe ye Bulu isiti ityeya ehlala
nemali ihambile. Kwa hlatywa kwa oko emkondweni, kwakusa yasetyenziswa
intambo yocingo. Wabanjwa e Rini, kubonakala ukuba weza nge train. Imali
awabe ebaleke nayo yabiyi £103. Indoda kufuneka ivule amehlo kwezi mini.

BANINZI abo vumelana no Mr. W. W. Nkomo kulenteto: “Ndifumana umbuzo ka
Mr. Mqibisa ulungile. Yonke lenteto mayigqitywe ngelilizwi u Satana
usapume ngamnye umlenze ezintliziyweni zetu. Aba bantu bezakum
ngezambato zegusha kanti ngapakati bazingcuka eziqwengayo, kumbula
esositeto.” Ngombuzo wako, mzalwana, wokuba, uku lobolisa kulungilena ku
Makristu, singati, abobantu ubabuzayo wena abayazi ukuba yintonina yona
elulobolo, yintonina engelulo. De kube kude kwatsola into oluyiyo, siti
kuwe musukuti waku tshiswa ngu mqa wako ude uvutele ngomlomo.

IPEPA lase Natal eligama liyi Witness liti amajoni abese Transvaal nabe
kwelo, aya qala uku nduluka. Izinto azinakude zihlale zole tu xa lama
doda aseko, nakuba ilizwe liba noku papa okutile xa kona. Xa apungulwayo
ama joni akanakuba anga ncotuki u Sir Evelyn Wood kunye nawo. Kobako
usizi ngenxa yoku, ube yi Ruluneli ebakolisayo abantu, hleze kufike enye
engasokuba nayo lenkoliso.

“IMI mangaliso ayisoze ipele!” Lamazwi afike ezi ngcingeni zetu emva
koku funda incwadi ka Heer P. J. Joubert Umpati Mikosi yase Transvaal
eyibalela i Rulumente yase Transvaal apo ateta ngokuti, kokwake uku
cinga, alusoze lubeko uxolo olumiyo kwa Zulu engadange wabuyiselwa u
Cetywayo, wamiswa endaweni yake yobu Mtan’ Omhle. I Volksraad—i
Parlamente ye Transvaal, ivumelene no Mr. Joubert. Aku ncedi nto tina
ukuba sihanahanise, sibona ukuba lamazwi ka Mr. Joubert anyanisekile.

Abafundisi ababeko ngaba—ngo Revds. R. Birt, wase Peelton ; W. B.
Rubusana, Peelton; J. Harper, King William’s Town; D. Doig Young,
Tembuland ; W. J. B. Moir, Lovedale; J. Webber, Stellenbosch; H.
Gutache, King William’s Town; Bryce Ross, Pirio; J. D. Bon, Secretary,
King William’s Town; R. Lamplough, Mount Coke; J. Smith Spencer,
Queenstown; W. Stuart, Burns­hill ; G. Weaver, Mount Coke; J. Davidson,
Mbulu; J. P. Jooste, Macleantown ; J. Gordon, King William’s Town; J. B.
Yekele, King William’s Town ; R. Stumbles, Newlands; J. Ferguson, East
London ; R. Barr, East London; W. Girdwood, Tutura; W. Leith, Somerset

AMANQAKU.

Kugqitywene ngokuba ibalwe ivakaliswe indawo yosizi oluviwa
yintlanganiso yabafundisi ngenxa yokububa kuka Mr. J. J. Irvine obe
ngumxasi womsebenzi wabafundisi igidi, ebanonelele nabantsundu kwano
msebenzi we ngqungqutela yentlanganiso yabafundisi bamahlelo onke.

INCWADI ZESI XOSA.

Ite i Secretary ayipumelelanga kuwo lomsebenzi, ayifuma- nanga nto
iyileyo.

INGXOXO NGE TAYITILE.

Kuxoxwe ngetayitile zabantu base zikolweni. U Rulumente ufune ukwazi
ukuba zifunwa ngabo bonke na abantu, nokuba ngabaninzi na abazifunayo
kunabanye, nokuba i komiti iqinisilena ukuba ezitayitile zeziqu
zingaligugu na ebantwini, kwanokuba ucando nezinye izinto ezine ndleko,
i komiti leyo iti ezondleko zingatiwani na.

Rev. J. Harper, ute i Secretary iyicacise kakuhle lento, waye kananjalo
eyivuyele lendawo yokuba isingatiwe indawo yokwenza ukuba kube lula
indlela yaxa umntu ontsundu atenga isikonkwane somnye. Utsho wati
kokwake ukwazi umzi ontsundu awuwomele wonke kwezi tayitile zeziqu, kuko
abasankontele kwezobuzwe. Okunye ke lendawo ati u Rulumente kuko
itayitile ezi 4,900 ezingananzwe mntu, lonto ixela ukuba masime ngenyawo
tina bafundisi ukuba mabazitabate abantu bazo.

Rev. R. Birt ute yena imbangi ebangele ukuba zityesheIwe nje
zingatatyatwa kusekubeni zite seziko njalo ezi tayitile, suke u
Rulumente wabuyekelela, ekubeni ngelete xa zikoyo nje zabizelwa
abaninizo kwa oko. Utsho wati uyatemba ukuba njengoko aba kunjalo, u
Rulumente unayo londawo ukuba mayenzeke.

I Secretary ibuze ukuti kuko bani wazi sizatu na soku- ngatatyatwa kwezo
tayitile, kuba nanku u Rulumente ebapendula ngale ndawo yetayitile
ezingatatyatwa mntu.

Rev. R. Stumbles ute ezimali zifunekayo mhla kuputunywe itayitile
kwanezokucanda zingayibanga lonto ukuba ibe njalo.

Rev. R. Lamplough ute yena into ayibonayo apo iqina likona yile yokuba
kunzima ukufumaneka kwemali kwabantsundu ngoku, imali ayiko. Ute
ukubonisa oko walinganisela ngemihlaba ebinamatyala erafu yabantu bake,
ate u Rulumente, angati umntu erole £2 12s. awufumane. Inxenye yasel’
iziposa kulomihlaba, yasinelwa ayabako yona imali. Lonto ke yalata
indawo yokungabi nakuzi- nceda kwabo. Ugqibele ngendawo eti ulusizi yena
kunje- njenje kuba ikakulu imbangi yoku kungokuba abantu abantsundu bate
ukuncedwa kwabo bade bagqitiswa. Yiyo lento kunje.

Rev. B. Ross ute ukuba kuyaku yekwa kuqubeke ngoluhlobo kuyakonakala
ngakumbi. Makupunywe egusheni. Kunjenjenje akuko umsulwa kuyo lento
kwisonakalo sayo. Nabafundisi, na Rulumente, nabantu bantsundu, yonke
lonto isingelwe pakati kwelipundu lesonakalo. Utsho wati yena ubona
ukuba ababantu kunjenje benziwa kukuba bafunde ukuba namatyala
(izikweliti) ekude kwangati baquba ukurweba, kwade kwangati lento yokuti
umntu ahlale enesikwelite, kukuba uhleli umte qaku ngoko, uhleli unaye.
Ngokotende oludala Iwase ndaleni bebengangeni zikweli- teni zanto.
Ezinto zixakileyo mazijongwe ebusweni kanye, baye bona abafundisi
bemelwe kukubabonisa abantsundu ukuba bafanele ukuzayama bazimele
ngokwabo, bazazi

imfanelo zabo, nendawo ezibameleyo. Ungene ngamandla kwindawo yetayitile
zeziqu, bati kungenjalo bafumane ezobuzwe. Uqube nezinye ke indawo
zobuciko.

Rev. J. Smith Spencer, ute yena lengqungqutela yalentla- nganiso
yabafundisi ingeyimeli lendawo yezitayitile zeziqu bayikontse. Utsho
wati ukuba bekuqinisekile ukuba kuyakuti nokuba kukwiminyaka emashumi
mabini abantu. abantsundu banamaname kubemi base Sikotilani ebumeni
babo, beku ngebinani make bona bafunde kwizintu ezenzekayo, nakuba yona
oko kunxusana angetsho ukuti kuse kunoncedo olungangokuba kungaqutywa
ngalo ngokomteto. Kuba xa kuko itayitile zeziqu kukuti ke oko elowo
uyakutana qwe no Rulumente ngokunyolulekileyo engoko yena okwake
angaboniyo ukuba lentlanganiso yabafundisi inganika isihlahla kulonto
okwangoku.

IZIQIBI ZOTYWALA

I Secretary ifunde ipepa elide kunene malunga nalomcimbi, elisingete
nengxelo ze mantyi ezinje ngo Messrs Chalmers no Dick, ngohlobo
ekuqubeke ngalo pakati kwabantsundu malunga noselo kusoloko kwavingcwa
ikantini kumabala atile esitili sase Qonce.

INGXOXO.

Kubeko ingxoxo enkulu ngalondawo ebonisa uncedo olukulu olwenzekileyo
ngokuvalwa kwezo kantini, nobunqina obuninzi obukoyo bokutyebisa
lonyaniso nomnqwenowokunga amabala anjalo angongezwa. Kungenwe nakuselo
lotywala babantsundu kuyo longxoxo kwangokunjalo.

Kuleswe ipepa elivela kulondlu inkulu ya Bazili benene, ebulela
ingqungqutela yentlanyaniso yabafundisi ngendawo yokuba yayikontsela
kwade kwakuko lento yokupungulwa kotywala bomlungu, nokukontsela
ukumiswa kwamaqela abazili ezikolweni zabo. Kanjalo wona Amatempile aya
lugxwagxusha uselo lotywala babantsundu nalo.

ILUNGELO LOKUVOTA.

Kuvakaliswe ukuba, nangani ixa selihambile lalomcimbi, lentlanganiso
ayinakuyiyeka ide igqite lento inkulu kangaka ingenzi nto kuyo.

Kushukunyiswe indawo eti—Lentlanganiso iva ububi mgenxa yale Bill iya
kuwahluta amalungelo asel’ enemi- nyaka exanyulwa ngabantsundu bewapete
kakuhle, indawo leyo engayi kuba mnandi ezintliziyweni zaba ntsundu.
Engati le Bill ike yemiswa yaba ngumteto, ibe lentlanganiso iya kubalela
ku Mntan’ omhle, ukuze angayivumeli ayinike isihlahla sokuba ibe
ngumteto omiyo.

Rev. R. Birt, ubuzile wati, ngokutini ?

Rev. B. Ross, ute ngokuba kuxelwe mhlope ukuba ukutiwa, abantsundu baya
kulaulwa nje ngohlanga olupetweyo olungapantsi kwetu, kanti wona umteto
awunakete licalula amabala.

Rev. R. Birt ingati kanti londawo asiyona lululuvo Iwe Palamente.

Rev. B. Ross kungaba kubetene indlebe kambe, kodwa ke yona inqayi ingena
ngentlontlo.

Rev. J. Harper ute yena lendawo akayiboneli kuti yenza nto malunga ne
Constitution ekubeni ilungelo lokuvota emakabe nalo umntu ontsundu
ingamhluti nje. Kodwa ke kona uyavuma ukuti kungati kwenziwe ikete,
kubeko ingxakeko ezininzi.

Rev. R. Birt okwake yena ukubona le Bill ayicaluli bani, mayibe
igqwetiwe ukuxelwa.

Rev. J. D. Don le Bill iyakuguzula abantsundu abebenga- bavoti iminyaka
emininzi, kuba kuqala bekukangelwa nendawo yedlelo emntwini, ke namhla
londawo ayiko, ke ngoko kubonakala ukuba abona baninzi bayakupuma
ekuvoteni.

Rev. W. B. Rubusana ute kambe ukuba kutiwa lento isenziwa nje, kuhluzwa
i register, ziko ke indlela ezinye esekwenziwe ngazo ngapambili zaze
zapumelela kakuhle. Umntu ongenalungelo lokuvota ube kutshwa pambi kwe
mantyi. U Rulumente akadanga atsho ukuti londlela ayincedanga.

Rev. R. Birt unge angake eve eyona nto kuyakonakala yona ukuba le Bill
kumi ngayo.

Rev. B. Ross ute le Bill ingonakalisa ngokukonwa kwi- zitili ezinje
ngezo Rev. E. J. Barrett. Ukuba umntu upantsi kwe tayitile yobuzwe,
akanakuba na erf, ke namhla idlelo alibalelwa nto ; utsho wati, kutiwa
le Bill iceba okusetunzini, baye nabapambili e Palamente bebambene
ngeyona nto yalata yona. Lendawo yona iti—Abantu

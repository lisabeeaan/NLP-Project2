8 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, FEBRUARY 16, 1884.

Kwizibonda nezinye izi ma-mhlaba ezingabanyuli
kwimizi ngemizi yase Victoria East. U

WILLIAM YOUNG

ucela ivoti zenu ukuba abe lilungu lase Victoria
East lentlanganiso yokwenza amasiko.

1.  Kuba ngumhlobo ngendawo yokuba imali yokuncedisa imfundo inikwe
    nakwabamnyama kwangangokuba inikwa nakwabanye.

2.  Kuba eyakumela ukuba abantsundu nabanye bafumane iziqiniselo
    kwimihlaba abafanele ukuzifumana kuyo.

3.  Kuba izikalazo zabamnyama nabanye ezifuneka ziye e Palamente eya
    kuzingenisa (ukuba ube ngomnye wabatunyelwayo.)

4.  Kuba eya kuxasa yonke imiteto emitsha emiswayo yokunceda indidi
    zonke ezimele le Koloni.

KE, abanyuli abamnyama lumkani, ningakolwa yiyo yonke inteto eniyivayo.
Lamazwi ashicile- lelewe ukuze nibuze xa engazaliswanga.

Bonke abamnyama abangabavoti mabayiqiqe lento, ze ngolwesi- HLANU 22
FEBRUARY, 1884 ninyule u

WILLIAM YOUNG

ukuba ayekuba lilungu lembuto emisa amasiko,
emele isiqingata sase Victoria East.

Elipepa lishicilelwa E-Lovedale Institution ekuqalekeni napakati
kwenyanga.

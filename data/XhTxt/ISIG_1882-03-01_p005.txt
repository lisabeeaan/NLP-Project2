King William’s Town, January 2, 1882.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI.—

Camagu nganto nye mnumzetu, ndifakele elopepa apo, ndifuna ndipiwa
impendulo ngamanene akowetu, izima’mhlaba zona ndicela utywala into
obuyiyo bonke, obom Lungu nobom Xosa buyintonina? into obufaka yona
emtwini, yingqondo, bulumko, butyebi, lukolo, yintsikelelo, yintonina
into zininzi? Kanindi vise zingqondi ndifuna ukuva into obuyiyo zingaka
nje. Ezi zinto ndizi balileyo, eyona nto bulunge nayo yiyipina? Kuba
lento nditshoyo mzi wakowetu, utywala bom Lungu nobom Xosa bumenza umntu
ukuba atande ukuhamba exwaya imfazwe. Nganina? ngokutuka, nge
ntshwabulo, nokunyelisa umntu kungeko nto ayenzileyo; atukwe, anyeliswe,
abetwe, kungeko nto enze yona. Mhlayimbi uve kusitiwa yeyazipina le?
Lonto ke ngumntu ayisiyo nkomo, lokutiwa yeyazipina; mhlayimbi ufika uko
apo nguye ongenayo, uyangena umve sesiti yityewana yapi le? Iyawa ngum
Xosanyana mhlayimbi iyawa yi Mfengu, benjanenjalo ke, umve esiti i—soti
nkinti nge nduku le, yintoni nale. Kulapo isisusa sento sizakubakona,
uzakutinike umtukwa? Uza kulwa nomtukwa lo nganina? Ngokuba akanaso
isizatu afela sona. Mhlayimbi u betwe kakulu ngu lamntu ub’ umtuka,
kanti yimfazwe ke lonto uvunduvundu imfazwe yotywala. Mhlayimbi
kuxatyanwe ngokuxoxa oku endlwini kule ibe ino tywala, kuba wofika
bexela amahobohobo endlini ati omnye ngapa kwelekwele, lo ungapa ikwangu
kwelekwele, kuza kuxabana babini ngapa balwe, bati abaya bangapaya
bengazi nto balwa ngayo aba banga hluli, lo abetwe yimfesane yalo wako
wabo ubetwayo, suka kulile intonga apa kuf’ amadoda kube kubi. Kanti
kulapo nje bekuziwe kuselwa la masi bati nga masi.

Kupina ukulunga apo mfo wakwa Xosa? Sipina isimilo apo mfondini wakwa
Xosa? Bupina ubulumko bako mfondini wakwa Xosa? Yeha! zimkile putumani
induku kwa mnqayi bafo ba masi. Yeha! Zimkile itiki zenu nomqomboti.
Nabo befutafuteka zituko emqombotini, ngu msindo ema Xambeni, ngu msindo
wanina woku xakanisa abamipefumlo icinga okuyelele esikweni, kupina
ukulunga kwako bra­ndy ndini? Nawe mqomboti ndini? Yitsho ka yise.

WALTER KOBE.

Tyinira, December 22, 1881.

Umntu uya kuti akufunda lenteto ati fanukuba lendodana ayonwabile apo
ikona fanukuba okunye aziko ezi zinto ateta ngazo apo akona. Mandityumbe
le ndawo yodwa okunye yindawo emnandi kakulu indikolisa kakulu sendiza
kuyenza ikaya lam eliqinisekileyo. Nditeta nge pesheya kwe Nciba
(generally ndiyaliquka). Molweni ma jentlman ase Colony, lanto beni
fudula niyiva kusitiwa pesheya kwe Nciba kuko izinto ezintle ezi gqite
ezase Colony ncamani hlalani pantsi bafuna ukuba nize kule ntshabalalo.
Lawo magabugab'u engca yejojo ako kambe. Abafundi beli pepa ba pesheya
kwe Nciba bakuti likakashotwana, liyakayakana lapina, elifike ninina eli
liteta lento. “ Not so fast good gentlemen,” kuhle Akwaba bendimcelile u
Mr. Joseph H Mazamisa ukuba ake anicazele eli pesheya kwe Nciba kwandlu
zesikolo zingo nomtyedwana, ziko ezilungileyo e Butterworth, Tyinira
Toleni, Tsomo, Hlobo, &c. &c. inqugwalana ezi dilikayo, (in some places)
Apo wova kusitiwa o Titshala ngama h'ilih'ili ngono twal’ impahlana bate
sa kulo lonke ibe kodwa kungeko ne kaya elilungileyo abalilungilelela
ama badubadu o Titshala ukuze baze kuba gxeka kakuhle, befezile ngelabo
icala. Mn’ apa ndonwabile nemi hlaba yam yalile. E Tolen kwa Rev. R.
Ross, kuhle kanye uti usati tu e Sautana ubizwe lihlati le miti
etyaliweyo ngowapesheya kolwandle (over the blessed isle; home of the
brave and free.) Akwaba ibiyi Capital yapesheya kwe Nciba. Inene lase
Koloni kelalala endlwini yomnye u Titsala ekutiwa isemzini omkulu.
Umniniwo lomzi umgxeki we Colony ne contents zayo. Liti elonene
andilalanga zintakumba nazi ncukutu, engxushungxushwin, Lomzi ungapaya
kwe Butterworth. Nam ndake ndabuza ukuba ipina indlu yo Titshala ndi
hambele nge holide, kwatiwa nantsiya seyilala ihangu. Umniniyo ulala
komkulu, ndamangala kuba lilihlazo ukulala endlwini yabantu
abatshatileyo. Lo Titshala ndibala nje sele nyanga 3 wemkayo, wemka nge
10 days holiday engabaxelelanga nokuba xelela. Asi kuko nokuba wakolisa
umntu owati: U mame! U mame i Xego limazinyo amdaka! liyityela nina inca
lingumntana! Ndeza pesheya kwe Nciba entshabalalweni apo kubuliswa nonge
Nkosi, apo kubuliswa onyana bokwel’emtini! apo kubuliswa onyana bovuka

bezonwaya! apo kubuliswa onyana bovuka alimpempete—kunjalokanye,
ukuzipakamisa kuka vuk’ endleleni. Mandiba kumbuze lento inyeyokuba tina
sisinga e Lutukela (Tugela River) sakuti sakupumela lomlambo sikulule,
kuti emva kwe dinala siti sonke okanye kuqale mna Hip! Hip! Hurrah! Hip!
Hip! Hurrah! hure zimi ngonina, sikahlele kwezo ntlanga inkosi
bakuzimela ovuk’ endleleni, A Dla­mini! Jama omhle! Sandlulube! ! ! M.
N. G.

Idutywa Reserve, 17 January, 1882.

NKOSI YAM MCOKELI,—Ndicela indawo kwelipepa lako. Kaunditele lanzi lama
zwana. Iya buleleka kanye i Grammar ka Rev. Mr. E. J. Barret. Nendawo
ekankanywe ngu Mr. A. Gontshi ebifudula ikankanywa. Iyavuyeleka ukuba
inga ingakaulezeka. Indawana elusizana yeyokuba amakowetu ayikangela
kancinane lendawo yokulesesha incwadi ngencwadi nokuba zezesi Xosa
mhlayimbi zesi Ngesi kanti kulapo ukona umtombo wokuqonda, umntu lo
uzifundela ngokwake ukuze aqonde ngakumbi. Mna ndiva abafundisiweyo
bemane besiti ayiko imisebenzi efanele bona inqabile, kanti hayi
akunjalo ngabo bajonge ngacalanye abajiki bakangele ukuba nabo banako
ukungenisa imali ngapandle kokuba baziqeshise. (Ngekubako indodana
ibenye yokuqala umsebenzi. Make ndipeze Nkosi ukuze ukauleze.) Esako
isicaka somonde D. BULUBE.

Heald Town, January 10, 1882.

NKOSI YAM MCOKELI,—

Ndincede undifakele lamazwana kweli pepa lidumileyo lama nene. Ndifuna
ukubonisa amazwana atile, ngakuba baleli beli pepa, into esifanele ukuba
siyenze. Masikangele izinto zokunyusa uhlanga, sibale zona; ukwalatisa
uhlanga ukuba luzamele kuzo; sibonise izinto ezitshonisa uhlanga, ezilu
b'ekisa ezantsi singa bali izinto ezingena kulu nceda. Nangu umbuzo wam
ku Ntam’ Engenakala. Xa ikala zikululweyo zalahlwa lonto yoluncedana
uhlanga? Kodwa mna kum akubonakali ukuba uku kululwa kwe kala
kungalunyusa uhlanga, akufuneki ukuba sigqibe ixesha kulonto asizizo
ikala ezibange ukuba abantu banga sebenzi, ukuba uteta ngoku sebenza
ubutyebi abantu bangasebenza nokuba bazi nxibile ikala maninzi amadoda
namadodana ezi dolopeni nasezi lokeshoni anganxibi zikala noko
akasebenzi, enziwa yintoni na ke? Mna nditi enziwa bubuvila, abanye
benziwa kukuhiliza kuko izinto eziba hilizisayo ezi nditi mna masi
bonise nangezinto ezi hlisela abantu ezantsi. Amanye amadoda namadodana
ungafika ebaleka emva ko tywala, kukupela amadodana ezilokeshoni
ungafika ehlanganisana amaqela ngama qela kuvunywa kusitiwa ku fundwa
amaculo unguwona msebenzi wabo imini ngemini, ungafika udodona lubaleka
emva kwemi tshato kukupela luvela kuma cala ngamacala kusiyiwa kufeketwa
ngengoma zokubonga opezu konke, ongumniki wama tamsanqa, ongumnyusi
wabantu, kusenziwa nezinye izinto endingena kuzingena ngoku. Nanzoke
izinto ezingena kulunyusa uhlanga ziyalutshonisa ukuba lutshabalale.
Ukuba ipepa lesi Gidimi beli banzi bendiya kubonisa ngaba tshonileyo
ngenxa yezi zinto zinge zizo ikala ezitoba uhlanga, asikuko ukulahlwa
kwe zivato zomzimba okunga nyusa uhlanga, into enganyusa uhlanga
yimfundo kutike eku fundeni kwa bantu bazi tobe bafune ubulumko bokwazi
belinga ukuzishiya izinto zasemva zobunyama bezolulela kwizinto
zapambili nje ngelizwi lom Postile u Paulusi kuma Filipi (iii. 13.)
Kanjalo ezi mbalini siyeva ukuba lamadoda amhlope ekuqaleni aye njengati
aye sambata iminweba yenyamakazi, aye qaba izinto ezi bomvu, kwafika
ilizwi lika Tixo, kwafika imfundo, bazishiya zonke izinto ezindala baza
bakobu bulumko banabo namhlanje. Ndim,

A. RADAS

UKUZIMELA KWA BAMNYAMA.

Ndiyatemba ukuba lifikile ixesha lokuba abamnyama balinge ukuzimela kwi
zinto ezitile. Anditsho ukuti banokuzimela kuyo yonke into. Kodwa
zininzi into abanga zimela kuzo ekuti ngobu denge noku ngazi
bazipatelwe, zezi ngaze zande, zihlume, kuba azipetwe ngabaninizo.
Indawo yokuqala endibekisa kuyo, enkulu, engu mtombo wokunyuka
kohlanga—Zizikolo zangapandle, anditeti nge Seminaries kuba abafikeleli
bonke abamnyama kanjalo azixaswa ngabo. Lento yezikolo zetu iyabonwana
sizwe esimnyama. Izikolo zetu zimi ngolu hlobo, zibatalelwa ngu
Rulumente amashumi amatatu eponti ngonyaka elindele ukuba abantu
abamnyama babatale ekwa mayela apo.

Zezo nganyelwe ngaba Fundisi abamhlope, ibandla elingateni nento kunyuka
kohlanga olumnyama, abongameli ngegama kupela, bezikolo zetu. Nditsho
nganina.—

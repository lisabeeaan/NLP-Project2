  Jaji, lenkwenkwe, asele zakumana ukona njeke angabetwa yabetwana? Kuya
  bonakala ukuba abantu abasenakuyitintela into embi nasezikolweni
  akasatetwa amatyala entombi.

  Uyatshitsha umzi. Ndixolele ndingo wako umfundi.

  MHLELI wo Sigidimi siwamkele umpanga wokubuna kuka MM. Chalmers

  Mandenze kufupi. Lonke usapo olondliwe pantsi kwemfundiso zabo
  alutusiwe, kuba ubeti obunayo komnye useko na u Dyifolo kuba ligama
  abaqele Iona kwimihla yabo. Ke ukugula kwake bekunzima kuti. Isikungo
  setu besisiti—Yanga i-Nkosi ingasindulula isicakakazi sayo side siye
  ekupumleni kwaso.

  Simazela utando Iwake emihleni yake kubantu aze kusebenza kubo
  nobunzulu bomxelo wake ukunga bangasindiswa.

  Nje ngokuba ekubizweni kuka Mr. Chalmers yi Nkosi yake ite yakuti
  kuye-kwanele.” Wasala yena ebizwa zihlobo zake Pesheya wama.

  ngala kwapela. U Mrs. ChaImers ngeliti—ufanele yena ukufela kwapakati
  kwabantu ababezele bona, kwanje ngom fundisi; wancatywa nalo utando
  lwake ngokunje.

  Masendisiti kuba bubalisiwe kade ubomi bake savuya kakulu ekubaliseni
  kwepepa lako. Nangu umsebenzi wokugqibela awawenzela tina. Wati
  engumhlobokazi ukumiswa kwalomzi uyi Henderson ngu Rev. J. A. Chalmers
  wasibasela ixilongo, elitenge nge £7 ngati kwabako ishumi ngapandle,
  akatsho ukuti ndingumhlobokazi elitumela u Mngcangatelo. Asinakulibala
  izenzo zake Ukububa kwake kusikumbuza iminyaka yakudala yase Gwali.

  Hai ukungabuyi ngamva kwenu minyaka ndini yakudala. Kodwa kululungile,
  kuba luhlobo ateta ngalo u Somandla nagawe ncwaba.

Mayibongwe inkosi u Somandla yakuteta nati ngoluhlobo.

NIKANI MANTSAYI.

Henderson.

NKOSI Mhleli kwixesha elidluleyo, ndifunde into eninzi ngentla-
ganiso zabantsundu, ngokutumela e (England) izitunywa, ngomteto
wenkululeko (Franchise question), ndingavuya okokuba unokuti
kwelopepa lako ufake ezizibakalana. Nditemba okokuba ezizibakalana
aziyikulahlekana nendawo zazo. Kunje ngokuba wena usazi, okokuba
into ezifunekayo kwaba bavoti (voters) zezokuba abe nendlu exabiso
elingamashumi amabini anesihlanu eponti £25—mhlaumbi amkele
amashumi amahlanu eponti £50—ngonyaka, nokuba amashumi mabini
anesihlanu eponti £25—kunye nokutya nokuhlala nokulala ngonyaka
Ke kubonakala okokuba uninzi Iwa bantsundu lucinga okokuba
amalungelo abo apulwe; nokuba imilomo yabo itungiwe, bengenaku-
bekisa lizwi labo ku Rulumente welilizwe.

Mna ke ndifike ndiyikangele ngolunye uhlobo kanye lengxoxo.
Ezam izimvo zingafike zahluke kanye kunento ecingwayo luninzi;
kuba inkoliso ifike yakangela ngokuti amalungelo ayo ahlutiwe;
kodwa mna ndicinga okokuba akunjalo. U Rulumente ute roqo unika

imivuzo yemali kwizikolo, esenzela ukuqubela abuntsundu emfundweni
nasekukanyeni, ekungeko matandabuzo kulonto, ngoko ke wenze into
enkulu yobulungisa, Xa kunjalo ke, yini na etintela indoda
entsundu enomonde ukuba ingalinganisi a Mangesi izakele indlu entle-
yokulala? Isiqu sam ndazala iqela labantwana abantsundu abafundi-
siweyo kakuhle, abafunde namasiko abamhlope, nempato zabo, kodwa
ekubonakaleni ngapandle izindlu zabo, zifike zahluke kancinane kwi-
ndlu zamaqaba, nezinye zingahlukani kanye; sendifike ndibone
ndakungena ngapakati, ndandule ndibone okokuba andiko na endlwini
yeqaba kodwa ndisendlwini yomntu ontsundu olundileyo. Xa kunjalo
nje ndifike ndibone okokuba u Rulumente wenze lomteto wenkululeko,
(Registration Bill) ukunika abantsundu ubanengqondo inkutazo,
yokulandela a Beluugu ekwakeni nase mpatweni. Ukuba imali yale

Ntumelo e England ibihlanganiselwa izakiwo, ibiya kuti ngapandle
kwamatandubuzo okunceda iqela elitile lubantsundu, babalwe babe

nevoti (voters) ekubeni kutunyelwe ubabini nokuba batatu, ngomci-
mbi mhlaumbi oyakuti ungubi naziqamo.

Ndivile ukuba imalunga kumakulu amhlanu eponti £500, efunekayo
ngalentumelo pesheya. Ke elinani ukuba liyabiwa pakati kwabanye
abantsundu ngezakiwo, lingufeza amashumi amabini ezindlu
-ezimashumi amabini anesihlanu eponti inye £25 (20 houses at £25) abo
ngatike, lomashumi amabini abalwe babe nevoti. Okokuba intumelo
pesheya ite ayapumelela emalingeni ayo, elinani lingako lingabalahle-
kela. Ndingati ngoko ndibakumbuze abantsundu ngapambi kokubo
batumele pesheya ukuba bagqale lenteto kutetwa ngayo apa, njengoka
bacinga eyabo. Bayakuti ngokukangela kumacala amabini, babenako

'ukuqonda ngokucacileyo ukuba lilipina icala elona lakwenziwa,

Ndingu

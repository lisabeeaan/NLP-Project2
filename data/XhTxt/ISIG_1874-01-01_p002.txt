IMFAZWE YASE NATAL.

INDAWO engamandla neyona ipumeleleyo kwindaba zase Natal, zokupela kuka
No­vember kude kube pakati ku December, ibe ikukulandela
U-Langalibalele. Kwe- lokugqibela ipepa letu sasixelile ukuba ubalekele
kwintaba ze Drakensberg. Ute ke kona walandelwa ngumkosi wama Ngesi,
odibene nabantsundu abangakuwo aba 1,500. Kweka Adam Koko impi kwakupume
amadoda ase wakeni; noko abuye engekabanjwa yena.

Ukuqala kokuqondwa komkondo wake kwaba ngomhla wa 22 ka November. Kwati
ngawo kwabanjwa intlola zake ezimbini, zazake ukusindisa obazo ubom,
zamxela ukuba use mantla E-Gqili namadoda asekulwini; nokuba omnye
umkosi wake okuma 300 amadoda amlandela ngase mva. Walandela ke umkosi
wase Natal opantsi ko Captain Allison no Captain Hawkins.

Kwati xa kunjalo kwako inkosana abati

ngu Putili ekubonakalayo ukuba inendawo zokumnceda U-Langalibalele. Yati
ngoko yanikwa umteto wokuba izinikele kuma Ngesi zonke izixobo zayo,
yadliwa nenkomo ezikumawaka asibozo nasitoba. Yati noko ike yanga
iyatandabuza yabuya yazinikela izixobo zayo; imipu yamakulu mabini. Emva
koko akubuyanga kubonakale nkatazo kwabo bantu baka Putili.

Kute ukwenzelwa ukuze kungabiko ndlela yakusinda ku Langalibalele,
kwakutshwa amapolisa amahashe alapa E-Koloni, abantsundu nabamhlope,
ukuba enyuse I-Gqili. Ute U-Mr. Griffiths obewapete wahlanganisa neqela
la Besutu.

Ngomhla we 9 ka December kwavakale ukuba U-Langalibalele uyakuba kwa
Molappo ngomhla we shumi elinesinye. U-Molappo yenye yenkosana za Besutu
ezipantsi kwama Ngesi, ate U-Langaliba­lele wacinga ukuba angazuza
indawo kuyo, kuba etshatiselene nayo. Ute ke ngoko U-Mr. Griffiths waya
kona kwa Molappo. Akufika ucele ku Molappo ukuba akupe amadoda aya
kumnceda, wamxelela ukuba koti xa kufumanekileyo ukuba uncedana na
Mahlubi, ahlutwe zo­nke izixobo anazo, aze kanjalo ohlwaywe ngokunzima.
U-Molappo akabanga senza ntozinde kuba bekungaseko xesha lokutandabuza.
Ute wawakupa amadoda nonyana bake ababini. Okwenene ngomhla we 11 ka
December, kungekabi mzuzu mkulu efikile U-Mr. Griffiths, babone
egaleleka U-Langalibalele. Ute esiti qata wabe selehlangatyezwa
ngu­mkosi wama Ngesi. Ubehamba nabantu abamashumi asibozo, amadoda
emfazwe. U-Mr. Griffiths unike umteto wokuba mabazibeke pantsi izixobo
zabo, bazinikele ukuba babe ngaba banjwa. Akayi vumanga londawo
U-Langalibalele, waza ke U-Mr. Griffiths wayalela amapolisa ukuba
alunge; wati ku Besutu mabamele bu- cala, ukuze abe sesitubeni yedwa
U-La­ngalibalele. Kwakuba njalo uqondile ukuba akuseko luncedo, waza ke

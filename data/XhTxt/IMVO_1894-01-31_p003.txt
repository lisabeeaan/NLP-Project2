Umnyuli uti;—Tina apa umzi uyapitizela ucela icebo kuwe Mhleli, mayela
neyona ndoda emasiyivote. Amadoda aselugqatsweni apa matatu, nanga
amagama awo: Orpen, van Rensburg no Dr. Smartt. U Mr. Orpen andisayikuba
sateta nto ngaye kuba umzi sewumazi wonke, ukuba ungumhlobo wabantsundu,
nosimeleyo kumalungelo etu e Palamente. Inteto awayenza kwi Palamente
egqitileyo yanele ukuba simqonde ukuba ungumhlobo wetu, ke ngoko
sifanele ukuba yena simvotele ngapandle kwamatandabuzo. Ngokumalunga
namalungu amabini asondezwa yi Bhonti, u van Rensburg no Dr. Smartt,
umzi uyapitizela wama Bhulu, amanye ama Bhulu ati afuna u van Rensburg,
amanye ati akamfuni, afuna u Dr. Smartt. U Van Rensburg yindoda esiyazi
kakuhle uku­ba ungumchasi wabantsundu ; ngobhalo lwamagama lo 1887
wayese ofisini e Dordrecht, echasa amagama abantsundu ukuba angaugeniswa
emqulwini wabavoti. Wada wafuna igqweta lokumncedisa ekuchaseni amagama
abantsundu. Ndiyatemba okokuba into zo Kalipa, Pelem, njalo-njalo,
zokumbula mhla kwakusiliwa elodabi. Bada aba Tembu bazifunela igqweta u
Mr. O’Brien indoda eyasoloko isilwela kude kube nanamhla. Namhlanje
lendoda ingu Van Rensburg ize kuti, icela ivoti zetu, iti masiyivotele.
Uti ke umzi, nxayipina okuya wawusiti masibe ngo Tung’umlomo, Bati ke
aba tembu tina asikabinazo ivoti, zachaswa nguwe pambi kwemantyi, Kuko
into ongummangaliso kwakona emangalise kwa aba Tembu, ukuba ati amadoda
abezibiza ukuba zizihlobo zabo, namhla­nje masijike silahle umhlobo wetu
u sibinze ku Van Rensburg utshaba nwetu, ivoti. Sicala ke icebo kuba
sicmga ukuba elidabi lipakati kwamalungu e Bhonti odwa, u Van Rensburg
no Dr. Smartt; ke ngoko besimele ukuba ezetu sizibinze kumhlobo wetu
omdala u Orpen: singangeni kucuku lwasemzini, kuba hleze siti
ngokwenjenjalo sibe si ya kushiya umhlobo wetu eludakeni, abe ngoko
ungapandle kwe Palamente; ze kungene amalungu e Bhonti amabini. Olunyulo
Iwanamhlanje lufana nolwamhla kwanyulwa u Orpen no Van Rens­burg, ama
Bhulu ezisa amalungu amabini lomini u Van Rensburg no Coetzee. Wati wena
Mhleli, maze sizipose ivoti zetu ku mhlobo wetu u Mr. Orpen, okwenene
senza njalo ; waba u Mr. Orpen upumelele. Ngo Dr. Smartt ndingati ngaye,
yena uzixela mhlope ukuba usondezwa yi Bhonti elugqatsweni, ke ngoko
akafuni voti yamntu umnyama; angati omvotelayo azi ukuba uvotela i
Bhonti, ehleli isaziwa ukuba ilutshaba lwetu. Umbizo ke ngulo:
kuyimfanelona ukuba umzi uvotele u Van Rensburg? njengokuba
ezibonakalalisile ngemisebenzi nezenzo zake okokuba ulutshaba lomntu
ontsundu. Kuko abamhlope ebesiba zizihlobo zetu, babini abasijikayo
kwisitete setu, ngokuti masivotele u Van Rensburg, selede lamadoda
abamba entama umzi, ngokuti masivotele u Van Rensburg, okanye sipose
ibenye ku Van Rensburg, ibenye ku Orpen, saye sisazi ukuba
ngokwenjenjalo siyakuba silahla umhlo­bo wetu u Mr. Orpen ze kupumelele
amalungu amabini ama Bhulu. Nditi umntu ontsundu ovotele u Van Rensburg
aze azi ukuba uzinqumla umqala ngokwake. Umzi mawuwe wonke ne lungu lawo
elidala u Mr, Orpen kwa njengamhlamnene, de abe unesizatu ukuze
simlahle. Utinina wena Mhleli? [Kakade.—EDITOR.]

INTO EZISA INTSHABALALO.

MHLELI,—Ngendiba andikukatazi nkosi yam, kusoloko ndikatazwa yinto
endiyibona imana ukwenzeka futi kumzi wakowetu. Nantsi lendawo
indikatazayo: kukungatenjwa ngumzi kwabahlobo abaziwayo bawo, ngokukodwa
ngelixesha lonyulo lwamalungu e Palamente lomoya uzibonakalisa
ngokuqaqambileyo. Bad la ngokuti bakuti abahlobo bomzi, nantsi indoda
emanikangele yona eyakuncedisana nabahlobo benu, kakade usuke umzi
uqabalake wahlukane: into ke leyo eya kuzisa intshabalalo emzini, kuba
iyakubangela ukuba kuugene intshaba ezisicudisayo. Kwisiqingata se
Bhofolo no Mpofu lomoya uzibonakalisa ngokuti bakuti abahlobo betu
bambelelani ku Mr, Hutton, kubeko abadidizelela ko Painter. E Komani
ndipaula kuko ababambelele ko Folosi no Lotyisi. Kwakona eba Tenjini
inxenye isabambelele ko Samp­son no Faku, elixa abahlobo bomzi kakade
kwezombuso besondeze u Mr. Molteno. Mzindini wakowetu, make sibatembe
abahlobo betu abase Palamente kwakunye ne Mvo ZABANTSUNDU ezi mana
ukusikokelela kwimigaqo yabo bahlobo. Mawalahlwe ngomanyano ngu­mzi
amadoda anamabalabala azele zizitembiso kanti efike e Palamente
awakuzipakamisa izandla kunye nabahlobo betu. Ndiyapinda, nditi,
mabatenjwe, balandelwe, lamkelwe icebo labo abaziwa beneziganeko
zokulwela umzi. Hlala kuhle nkosi yam Mhleli uchume, wande, zide ziti
indwadubana zakowetu zifumane intlalo yobubele ngokuziietelela kwako.

IPOSI YOMHLELI.

MNUMZETU,—Nceda undipe ituba kulomvaba yomzi. Ngetyala lokunukwa
ebelipambi kwe mantyi elibamba e Lady Frere, kwakumangalelene umntu no
mninawa ; isimangalo, uti omnye ubizwe ngobugqwira, kwatiwa uyatakata.
Uti ummangalelwa mna ndite, ningezi nje bantwana baka bawo sixhele
lenkomo ihlatywe ligqira, niyandibulala nanina? Kutsalelwene
emagqwoteni, ngamacala omabini, laba liyatetwa ityala. Indawo ezibe
ngainandla kukuchaza isiko lokuvumisa nelokuba xa igqira lihlabe idini
kuko ukunukwa kotile na kulonto; apikisene amacala, lisiti elinye
igqweta kuko, lisitsho lisiti libona kwincwadi zabachazi masiko esi
Xhosa. Elinye icala lommangalelwa lide lacela isibonda esingu Zote
unyana we nkosana ote yena, kungahlatywa idini sukuba kungeko mntu
unukiweyo, idini alidibani nokunukwa komntu. Ite ke ukugweba imantyi,
pambi kwam akuko bungqina bokuba undimangelele ubizwe ngobugqwira.
Kanjalo xi ebenukiwo ebengatinina ukuya kwa oko axe onwabe kwindlu
yommangalelwa; kwaye kubonakala mhlope ukuba njengentsebenzo yenqubo

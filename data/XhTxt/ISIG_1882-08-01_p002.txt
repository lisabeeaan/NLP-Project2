E EGYPT.—Malungana noku qala kuka July kuqaleke imfazwe e Egypt pakati
kwama Ngesi nama soldati apantsi kwe nkosana yakona engu Arabi Pasha. U
Arabi lo yi nkosana yama Joni kupela, nakuba engu mutwana we gazi.
Itshawe lona lase Egypt ngu Twefik, oyi Khedive (ukumkani). U Arabi
uvukele ulaulo luka Twefik, wabe u Twe­fik etembele kuma Ngesi nama
French, kuba ingawo awa boleke ulaulo lwase Egypt into eninzi ye mali.
Kute xa sekuza kuliwa ama French aroxa ngo mva. yaqalwa ngama Ngesi
imfazwe ke ngoko, ngoku minxela isi xeko esiyi Alexandria. Mva nje ama
French abuye agalela ku Mlu­ngu. Akuka liwa dabi likulu noko, kuba kute
xa beli lindelwe wabaleka u Arabi. Ngenxa yokunga vumelani ne Government
ka Mr. Gladstone malunga nehambiso yayo nemfazwe e Egypt apo u Mr. John
Bright upumile ekubeni lilungu layo.

IMFAZWE ZAKWA MZIMKULU.

Ngenxa yobuninzi bengxelo ezivela kwesi sipitipiti akulula ukuzivisisa
ka­kuhle indaba ezimana ukufika; lendawo iranelwa nangamanye amapepa.
Inteto ebalulekileyo kukuba ama Ndebele avu- telwa njengomququ pambi
komoya. Ngomhla we 3 November umkosi opetwe ngu Colonel Goold Adams
uqubisene nempi ebipetwe ngu Gambo (abati ngu- myeni wakwa Lobengula);
emva kwe dabi elishushu agxotiwe ama Ndebele etshise enye inqwelo
yabamhlope atimba nezipani ezine zenkabi, kwabulawa ama 60
kuwo—kwabainhlope kufe 8 i Netevu nabelungu ababini, kwangxwelerwa i 11.

ope­twe ngu Dr. Jameson kubulewo

wakwa Bulawayo abate befika abelungu kwaye sekulaula amalangatye odwa,
Kuvakala ukuba u Dr. Jameson utumele ilizwi Lokugqibela ku Lobengula
lokuba makabuye azokwenza isitembiso sokuba uyakubapata kakuhle

Palamente yase Ngilane u Mr. Labouchere ushukumise indawo yokuba

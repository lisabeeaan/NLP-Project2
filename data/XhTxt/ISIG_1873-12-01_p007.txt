ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1873. 7

BZAPESHEYA.

INKOSI YASE PERSIA ngoku iselibuye yagoduka. Kuvakala ukuba inxamele
ukungenisa ezweni layo izinto ezininzi ezintsha, nokuguqula imiteto
etile ebiko ngapambili.

UNYANA KA VICTORIA, omncinane kutiwa uya kutshata ngo January ozayo
E-St. Petersburg ikomkulu lase Russia.

ITYALA LIKA TICHBORNE lisatetwa. Lomfo ubanga ilifa usafuna amanqina
okubonisa ukuba uyindli lifa okwenene.

U-Dr. LIVINGSTONE kuko amareokuba usingisa ngakolunxweme lungase
ntshona-langa E-Africa.

U-SIB SAMVEL BAKER owaye nyuse I-Nile enxamele ukuncipisa umsebenzi
wokutengisa amakoboka ngoku ubuyele ekaya E-England.

IMFAZWE YASE ASHANTEE.—Kutiwa iyalungi- selelwa kunene E-England. Okwezi
njanga ama Ashantee ebengekenzinto ngenxa yokuba ezi zinyanga zemvula.
Anemikosi emitatu; emibini iselekumhlaba wa Mangesi, omnye ubuse
ngakomkulu.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

AMANZI-ABOMVU.—Esisifo sisenamandla kakulu ezinkomeni E-Natal.

INKUMBI ziyanconywa E-Burghersdorp, nakuwo onke lomacala.

UCINGO.—I-Government yase Free State ivumile ukuba ucingo lunyatele
emhlabeni wayo.

U-PBESIDENT BURGER wase Transvaal kutiwa asikuko nokuba uyafa.

UBUSELA.—Kubonakala ukuba bukulile ubusela ngoku, bayakala kumacalana
onke.

INDLELA ZENQWELO ZOMLILO.—Kusand’ ukufi- ka imikombe emine evela pesheya
itwele izinto zokwenza indlela zenqwelo zomlilo.

OZIBULELEYO.—Umfo abati ngu Chapman uzi- dubule ngokwake kufupi nase
Maritzburg. Asikaziwa isizatu sokuba enjenjalo.

OWENZAKELEYO.—E-Rini umntu omhlope we- nzakaliswe yinkomo ngangokude
kungabiko temba lokuba wobuya apile.

KWELE DIAMOND liya lincipa ngokuncipa inani labantu, ngenxa yamagqiza
emkayo ukuya kwele Golide.

UMCANCATO.—Seluqaliwe ukwenziwa umcanca- to okulomlambo kutiwa yi
Bushmans River, opakati kwe Bayi ne Rini.

INKOMO ZOKUXELA ngoku ixabiso lazo linyukile. Enye E-Qonce ide yadla £17
10, noko ibe inkulu ityebile.

KWELA BATEMBU.—Kuvakala ukuba U-Gwa- diso ubaxalisile aba Tembu
ngokubabela impahla zabo. Ezisindayo zezase sikolweni kupela.

INGOZI YENQWELO.—Umfo otile ontsundu usand’ ukunyatelwa yinqwelo
esifubeni xa angase Bothashill. Ibete wafa kwangoko.

E-MONTI umntu ontsundu owayenikwe ityala lokubulala umfazi wake ukululwe
ebubanjweni ngokuti kungabiko nyaniso zisemhlotsheni zokumalamanisa
netyala lake.

OBILEYO.—Omnye wababalelani betu uti, Hau linikwe unyaka etolongweni
yase Cradock ngoku- suke litengise ihashe, nesali, netuma, ezompahla
zizezenkosi yalo.

UMBANE.—E-Humansdorp ngokunye koku ku- duduma kube kusand’ ukubako,
umbane ubete wabulala inkomo ezilitoba. Kuko nomfazi wase Ncwazi obetwe
nguwo noko ungambulalanga.

E-FORT BEAUFORT kuko umfo ontsundu osand’ ukubanjwa, enikwa ityala
lokubulala. Kube kute inani labantsun du lamana ukutukana lingati
liyadlala lada lasuka lalwa, kwazake kwako obulawayo.

OBULELE OMNYE.—Umfo abati ngu Andries wase Beaufort West usand’
ukubulawa ; kutanda- tyuzelwa ukuba ubulewe sicaka abesebenza kunye
naso.

OLUNYWE YINYOKA.—Umntu ontsundu usand’ ukulunywa yinyoka E-Bayi, wafa
kwangalo mini. Kutiwa asikuko nokuba zininzi inyoka kulomacala nonyaka
nje.

INQAKAQA.—Abantu esasite bane nqakaqa E- Wynberg ngase Kapa, kuvakala
ukuba bayapila bonke. Baza kubehle bacitakale emnqubeni wabo ukuba akuko
bambi ibahlileyo.

INTENGO.—E-Burghersdorp, emhlabeni ka Mr. J. Smit kuya kutengiswa ngo 14
ka January oza­yo ; igusha ezi 1,000, amahashe ali 14, inkomo ezi 10,
nezinye izinto.

ABA VELA PESHEYA.—Kulindeleke ukuba kufike amawaka amatatu (3000) abantu
abamhlope abavela pesheya, bafike apa pambi kwe Kristmas. Bazela ukuza
kusebenza ekumbiweni kwendlela neminye imisebenzi.

AMAHASHE ENTENGO.—E-Queenstown ngomhla we 9 ka December, kuya kutengiswa
umhlambi omkulu wamahashe avela kwelipezulu. Kobako namahashekazi amahle
angamashumi amabini anesihlanu.

E-NATAL umfo abati ngu Mr. Carpenter, umgcini we Hotel, usand’ ukudliwa
£10, ngokusuke atengise utywala kumntu ontsundu. Woti ukuba ubuye
wayipinda lonto mhlaumbi abe setolongweni esebenza nzima inyanga
ezintandatu.

INTENGO E-SOMERSET.—Ngomhla 20 ka Janu­ary ozayo kuya kutengiswa igusha
ezi 2,200, iseyibokwe ezi 600, inkomo ezi 100, izipani zenkabi ezi 20,
amahashekazi amane, amahashe enqwelana amabini, nezinye izinto ezingati
ziziswe ngalo mini.

E-BAYI kusand’ ukubako inxabano pakati kwa Besutu nama Mfengu. Iqale
ngokuti I-Mfengu etile ku Msutu, wena “ uyinkwenkwe.” Ite lontwana
incinane kangako yabangela ukuba kude kupume into eninzi kumacala
omabini. Ukuba kungacedi amadindala akwazeki ebe kuya kuba kuko.

UKUBALELA KWELANGA.—Abantu bebelilango- kubalela kwelanga kumacalana
onke E-Koloni. Asikuko nokuba ibe ninzi imfuyo efileyo ngenxa yelanga.
Kwiremente ezitile kube kuko imitandazo yokucela imvula ku Somandla, ke
uyipendule lomitandazo. Kufike imvula etandekayo. Ixesha lokulima
alikadluli kwezinye izinto, kengoko akuko ungayi kubulela. Intlotyana
zonke zokudla zinqabe kanye ngoku. Apa E- Dikeni inxowa yomgubo izi £3;
kwezinye indawo ingapezulu.

ELUSUTU.—Kutiwa abantsundu kona baya beyiqonda ngakumbi imfundo,
nokuyitanda kwabo kuyakula. Ekuviweni kwe Institution yase Morijah,
okube kuko ngo 30 ka September, baba baninzi abantsundu abeza kupulapula
kuko ne nkosi U-Letsei. Abafundisi bakona balila kwangesi sikalazo
sikoyo nalapa, sokuba abazali ababa yeki xesha laneleyo abantwana, ukuze
bazuze imfundo enobom. Mhlaumbi yopela lonto abantu bakuyiqonda into
eyiyo imfundo.

EMAMPONDWENI.—Kuvakala ukuba U-Ndamasi akavumanga ukuza ku Mr. Orpen,
ukuza kupendulela ivenkile yomntu omhlope eyahlutwa im­pahla yatshiswa
ngabantu baka Ndamasi, oko babe siyakulwa no Mhlontlo U-Ndamasi uti ufna
ukubuya aye kumngenela U-Mhlontlo. noko selezinikele kuma Ngesi. U-Mr.
Orpen ufumene imvumelo yokumohlwaya U-Ndamasi ngezo zizatu eyizuzaku Sir
Henry Barkly. Bekuse kucelwe no Adam Koko ukuze ancedise watembisa ukuba
wokupa amadoda ama 3000. Akukabiko nto yenziweyo kodwa amadoda lawo
selehleli elungile.

U-MANKORANA, inkosi yama Batlapins, ucele uncedo ku Sir Henry Barkly,
ukuba ancedise kumfo abati ngu Smith, obanjwe emhlabeni ka Mankorana
ngama Bulu. Inkosi zalomacala zilinga ngako konke ezinako ukuwatintela
ama Bulu ase Transvaal, ukuba angamane ehluta umhlaba wabanye abantu;
zilangazelela ukuba ude ucandwe kakuhle umda ekukade kupikiswene ngawo.

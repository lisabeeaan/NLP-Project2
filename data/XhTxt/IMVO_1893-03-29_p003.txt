AMANANI EZINTO.

  Kuleveki igqitileyo nengapaya Imantyi yase Monti ibinomsebenzi
  ongemncinane wokupanda isizekabani sengozi kaloliwe yckugaxeleka
  kwetreni pezu kwenye ku Xaxazele (Greytown). Isigqibo sikamantyi
  sisekwe pezu kocingo lwenkosi yesitishi sase Bhaza u Mr. Joseph Ryan,
  oluti, Umyalelo wakutshwa ndim kwi treni u 31 ukuba iye e Greytown apo
  iyakuyigqita kona engu 2. Ndite kanti ngengozi ndiyayilibala into
  yokuba u 302 onyukayo akakafiki e Toise River.” Ute ke u Mr. Fletcher
  itreni awayindulula ngoluhlobo yayitwele abaha- mbi abaninzi, Pantsi
  kwemeko zonke aka- cingi ukuba lencazo yanele, ngokoke ufu. mana ukuba
  Umlauli-Stishini u Joseph Ryan unetyala lokuwutyeshela
  ngokupindapindi- weyo umsebenzi, nokuba ukungqubana kwetteni, okubange
  ukufa komfo ongu Bukani kubangwe lolutyesho lugqitileyo luka Joseph
  Ryan. Ngoko ute makabanjwe ngetyala lokuwupata umsebenzi ngendlela
  yokuba kufe umntu. Ebanjiwe umelwe u Mr. Ryan.

  Biyavuya ukuqonda ngencwadi esisand’. ukuzamkela kubalesi be Mvo
  ZABANTSUNDU abapesheya kwe Nciba, ukuba baqalile ngoku kona
  ukubazelela ubuxabiso bepepa ; oko kukuti bazakufana nabamhlope kanye
  abangena kuquba nayipina ibizinisi (ngoku- kodwa eyobulimi neyokufuya)
  bengena pepa libahambelayo. Sitsho kuba ezincwadi sizifumeneyo zilila
  ngokuba awahlokonyi- swa kwi Mvo amaxabiso oboya, zayo zicela ukuba
  amane edweliswa zonke iveki. Oko ke siqale ukukwenza kweleveki
  egqitileyo, saye siya kuzingisa ukukwenza. Siyababu- lela abahlobo
  abasikumbuzileyo ngento eli- lungelo labo nelepepa, banga abangedinwe
  kukwenjenjalo. Umzi ubusel’ ufun’ ukuti udliwe zivenkile ngokuba
  ungagqibi kunazi amanani kwelo lipesheya kwe Nciba. Wavuseni ke,
  alibone ilungelo lawo epepeni, namavukaba asengapandle kokuxhasa um
  sebenzi wokunceda uhlanga.

  Kuko umbhaleli ose ba Tenjini osibhalele ngamadoda amabini, awabalule
  nangama gama, esiwaziyo ukuba azibandiba apo eba Tenjini. Uvakalisa
  lomhlobo ukuba abuyele amasiko obuhedeni ngokusuka alobole, alo-
  bolise. Akulungilo ukubatyila ngamagama abo bahlobo. Silusizi nati
  ngokubuya umva kwabo. Kaloku aba Tembu aba bakanyi. Belweyo bona
  bebete bahluma, balungelwa ngokubamba imfundiso zika Wana. Enye yazo
  yaye iyaleze elisiko ukuba lingakanka- nywa pakati kwabakanyiselweyo,
  ekuteke kwalunga. Siva ububi njengalomhlobo wetu nkwa ngum Tembuke
  ukuba zilahlwa ezomfundiso, kubuyelwa kwimisebenzi epu- tileyo, kuba
  ke sisazi ukuba akuko kulunga kuyakuveliswa yilonto.

AMAFAMA NGE GLEN GREY-

Kwingxelo zentlanganiso yamafama endawo ngendawo ebise Rini sitabata
lengxoxo

U Mr. J. W. Garrett wase Glen Grey ute intlanganiso mayivumela- ne
ngokuba abantsundu bomandla we Glen Grey banikwe iziqiniselo (itaitile)
zemihlaba yabo. Lomcimbi ucukumisa abantu aba 40,000 kulomandla, waye
udale ubushushu boluvo kwelilizwe. Bayicela lentlanganiso namhla ukuba
yamkele, iyenze eyayo ingxelo ye Komi- shoni yase Glen Grey eyavumelana
ngamoya mnye ngemfanelo yebango la- bamnyama kulomhlaba wabo. Ukuba
lento ibinokwenziwa lengxokozelo inga- basebenzi ibingapelayo.
Abantsundu bayakunyanzeleka ukuba bapile kukubila kwamabunzi abo.
Ukunikwa kwetaitile nokuqiniselwa kwabantsundu kwimizi eyakuba
selulaulweni-lwe Bhodi kunga- ncipisa ubusela.

U Mr. J. K. Stretton (Dordrecht) use- kele, esiti kuyimfanelo kanye
ukuba aba bantu bayifumanele itaitile imihlaba yabo.

U Mr. T. A. King (Monti) ute uvuyiswe kakulu kukuba u Mr. Garrett
awungenise lomcimbi kulontlanganiso. Bekungalu- nga ngapezulu ukuba
abantsundu bonke abakulungiseleleyo ukutabata itaitile ba- zinikwe
msinya. Umhlaba okoyo ngoku ngowanelele ukuba uxase abantsundu base Glen
Grey. Ukuba bayazinikwa itaitile abaninzi abangevumi ukuba ku­beko
amanqenera angena mihlaba ukuba afumane ahlale. I Komishoni ikupe icebo
lokuba bangavunyelwa abantsundu ukuba batengise ngemihlaba de kupele
iminyaka emine. Lo ngumgqaliselo ofanelekileyo, kokwake ukubona.

U Mr. H. T. Bait (Dordrecht) ute uli- fakela impakata icebo ukuba ze
itaitile ezinikiweyo zingabi nakutengisa de ibe yiminyaka emashumi
mabini. [Izikalo : Hai, hai.] Ukuba banokuvumela abate- ngi ukuba
bayokupanga abantu imihlaba ayingepele nenkatazo ekoyo.

U Mr. B. H. Pringle (Bedford) ute uyavumelana nalenteto isand’ ukutetwa
kanye. Ngapandle kokuba kubeko imvu- melwano ezenziwayo, abantsundu baya
kubehle bahlutwe imihlaba yabo babe yinto ete-sa kule Koloni,

U Mr. J. McGregor (Xalanga) ute yena uyazi ukuba kezanikwa itaitile
abamnya- ma kungaba nzima ukuzitengisa kunoko bacinga ngako abanye. Yena
uxhasa inteto ka Mr. Garrett. Itaitile zesiqu zazitenjisiwe
kwabantsundu, kodwa ngo- ku kungxamele ukuvelela “Onozikaka- na”
(izatifikiti), abaneliswa zizo bafuna itaitile ababezitenjiswe ngu
Bulumeni.

U Mr. Geo. Gray (Qumra) ute eli linya- telo lokuqala lokupelisa
lengxokozelo ingabasebenzi. Yena ucinga ukuba u Bulumeni akanamfanelo
yokuyidukisa lento ukuba abantsundu bazinikwe itaitile, abangewavumeli
amadungudwa- no emihlabeni yabo. Uyabazi abamnya- ma enokutsho ukuti
baya kwenjenjalo. Uyayichasa indawo yokwenziwa kwemi- gqaliselo
yentengiso etaitileni ukuba abantsundu babalelwa ukuba bayalingana nabo
mabangapatwa ngokwahlokileyo. Msan’ ukuti ngenye imini yindoda ma-
kavote, ngenye niti ungubhebhi maka- kuselwe. Bonke mababe kumgangato
ofanayo, nokuba bamhlope nokuba ba- ntsundu, enyuke ogqita omnye, eble
ongena kuma ngokwake.

U Mr. W. D. Snyman (Barkly East) ute waka wafumana ituba lokuyixoxa
lenda- wo kwintlanganiso ye Bhonti, yena uya- temba ukuba elicebo liya
kupunyeleliswa. Ute abona bahlobo be Netevu ngabo bati mazinikwe
itaitile zazo. Kuko uluvo lokuba abarwebi baya kuzipanga itaitile, kodwa
yena uyazi okokuba ngabona bazise intlambuluko pakati kwazo. U- kuba
ebengeko u Mr. Walter Orsmond into eninzi ngeyifile e Herschel. I Nete-
vu kanye bezingena kufumana nto ku- barwebi kuba bezingena nto
yokubhatala. Mazazi ukuba zingasengozini itaitile zazo ukuba zingena
ematyaleni.

U Mr. Hall (Bedford) ubongoze intla- nganiso ukuba yamkele indawo yokuba
itaitile zingatengisi. Ngapandle kwale- ndawo unoloyiko ukuba elicebo
aliyiku- pumelela. Inene elake lati nyakenye i etevu azikaqubeli pambili
ngokude zibe nevoti elingana neyetu, alingezi apa liti kulento mazipatwe
ngokufanayo kanye nabamhlope kulomcimbi.

U Mr. Win. Bogers (Cathcart) ute aba­ntsundu base Glen Grey zityakala
ezi- ngazinto ngexabiso letaitile kunenyanga. Uyakuxasa elicebo
lihlonyelwayo.

U Mr. Tweedie (Cathcart) ute de babe abantsundu balifundile ixabiso
lomhlaba abafanele kuvunyelwa ukuba batengise. Ufakel’ eliti;
Mazingatengiswa itaitile zazo de zifike engqondweni yokulazi ixabiso
ezifanelwe kukuyitengisa ngalo.

Emva kokupumla ixeshana elincinane ibuyeyaqutywa lengxoxo.

U Mr. W. J. Warren (ilungu le Pala- mente) uqube wati abantsundu
bayalazi ixabiso letaitile, baye bepapele indawo ezingamalungelo abo.
Uyakolwa ukuba abamnyama bangati bezinikiwe itaitile zabo bonze konke
ukuba bangalahlekwa zizo. Ngokumayela nalendawana iteta ngokungatengisi,
ute yena ingabasisenzo socalulo esidandalazileyo. Ke zenziwa ukuba
zingatengisi isiqamo siyakuba kukuba abantu babe zinto ezidolileyo
ezingena msebenzi. Yena uyakuxhasa icebo lokuqala kodwa angatanda
ukubona umgqaliselo wokuba kungatengiswa nge taitile iminyaka emihlanu
ze ke emva koko u Bulumeni avume.

U Mr. Hockly, M.L.A.. (Bedford) ute uvumelana nenteto ka Mr. Tweedie.
Kwintlanganiso eyandulelayo imvume- lwano yayingokunikwa kwe taitile,
ko­dwa ingxako yengokuba zibe nalomgqa- liselo. Ingxaki ziya zikula
leminyaka ngeminyaka njengokuba kumana uku- bako abantwana ngabantwana
nje, kwaye kuyakuba yinkatazo ukuxela ukuba nguwupina emfaneleyo
itaitile. Akakayibeni (pofu eyiqonda imfanelo yazo kulomhlaba) into
yokuba bafike bamise imigqaliselo yabo ezitaitileni, abantu ngokwabo
mabazicelele ukuba batintelwe ekuzitengiseni itaitile. U Mr. Snyman
utete ngeqela elifanelekileyo labarwebi, kodwa kuko kulamadoda azi- milo
zipantsi kangangokuba amyeke etyaleni umntu ze afumane itaitile. Ye- na
akangemisi xesha, kodwa angati ma- zingaguqulwa ngapandle kwemvume ka
Rulumeni. Okwangoku indawo kakuba abantu bengena kuwagxota amanqenera
yeyona kanye ibabulalayo. U Rulumeni bangamtemba ukuba emva kokucinga
angayazi eyona nto iyimfanelo mayelana nokutengisa nokungatengisi.

Icebo lika Mr. Garrett lilungiswe nge- lika Mr. Tweedie lamkelwe.

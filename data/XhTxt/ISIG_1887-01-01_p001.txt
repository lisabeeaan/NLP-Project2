Vol. XVII, LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, JANUARY 1, 1887. No. 210.

IZAZISO.

NGE NTLAULO YE “SIGIDIMI.”

  MAGAMA afunyenwe esemva nge ntlaulo ye
  “Sigidimi” ekupeleni kuka December anqunyulwe amapepa awo ukususela ku
  January, 1887, nje nge saziso senyanga edluleyo. Abasukuba botu-
  mela incwadi ngomcimbi we “Sigidimi”, nokuba
  yimali makube ngegama lika Dr. James Stewart,
  Lovedale, Alice.

NGE MALI YE MFUNDO.

  UYAZISWA kuyo yonk’ into ukuba akuko bani uyakwamkelwa engezanga nayo
  imali yenxaso yemfundo yake. Ke ngoko bonke abazali mabapaule esi
  saziso, ati otumela umntwana wake amtumele kunye nokozo olo
  lufunekayo, angasayikwamkela lungeko.

  NGO KUVULWA KWEZIKOLO.

  SUKU lokuvulwa kwezikolo ngumhla wamashumi
  omabini anesixenxe ku January (27) ngolwe Sine evekini. Abaqalayo
  ukuzisa abantwana mabapaule olosuku, kuba lolokuviwa kwabasafikayo.

NGOM-GQIBELO, JANUARY 1,1887.

INGXELO YOMNYAKA WE 1886 YASE
LOVEDALE INSTITUTION.

NGOMHLA we 15 ku December kuleswe ingxelo yomnyaka ngu Rev. Dr. STEWART,
U Mongameli walomzi. Wayilesa. pambi kwentlanganiso edla ngokubako
iminyaka yonke, yokuvalwa kwezikolo okwexeshana. Kute kuba bekuko
undwendwe olubalulekileyo u Dr. CAMERON wase Kapa, yaba nguye obekwe
esihlalweni. Usukile u Rev. Dr. STEWART walesa ingxelo yomnyaka,
wenjenje ukuqala kwake—Kwiminyaka ngeminyaka iquba nje, kuya kunqaba
ngokunqaba ukwenza ingxelo ezalisekileyo, kwanale yezi Seshoni zombini
zalomnyaka ikwanjalo. Kantike kwanayo le igixiwe, kuba isingele pakati
yonke imilibo-libo yezipa- luka, nezamashishini nezantoni ezilunge
kulomzi, ezose zibonakala emapepeni ngokuzeleyo. Singati ukuba lomnya-
ka siwuncomile, ekuqubeni kwemisebenzi yawo, mhlayimbi kubeko
abangayitabati kamnandi londawo inge ayina nyaniso. Kantike kona,
zimbalwa i Institution nokuba zezalupina uhlobo ezingade zincome
ukuqubela pambili komsebenzi wazo ngendlela zonke, ekubeni nekutiwa
zicunyelwe ngumsebenzi wazo sezifane zazixolisa ngale ndawo yokuba
zisamise inyawo zazo noko sekunje. Ke ngoko, ingxelo esiya kuyenza iya
kuyelelana neyanyakenye eyayi nendawo yokuba xa kuya kuba nje,
kubonakala ukuba endaweni yokuwutwabulula umsebenzi, kuya kufuneka
unyiniwe, wabutyazwa, oko kungebe nani-nani. Kubonakala mhlope ukuba
kunokuba kutwatyulelwe kwezintsha indawo ezingandisa kwa indleko,
okunye, nokuze sibe nako ukuquba kwangoluhlobo siqube ngalo lube
nezindleko, kuqala kufuneka sifumene ingqiniseko evela kumacala amabini
eyakuba luncedo kuti.

  Kuqala kufuneka sincediswe kulo msebenzi ngabantu abantsundu,
  esikolwayo ukuba ngabantu aba nemvume yokunceda, koko bakohliwe kule
  minyaka kune yangapambili ukuyi kupa yonke imali yokuxasa imfundo
  yosapo Iwabo olufunda kulomzi. Kanjalo kufuneka sincedwa nayi
  Palamente yale Koloni, oko ke kukuti kuyole Koloni. Ukuba i Palamente
  iyaluhluta uncedo eb’ilwenzile lokuxasa imfundiso yabantu abantsundu
  ebapeteyo, asikuko oko, ukutsho ukuti obo bunzima mabutwalwe ngabanye
  abantu, kantike kunjalonje, yi Koloni leyo kanye kwakunye nabantsundu
  abafundiselwayo apa kulomzi wase Lovedale. Ekubeni ke kungaqondeki
  eyonanto kusakubayiyo, ngenxa yokungaqiniseki kwezinto, kuyakude
  kufuneke kuncitshiswe amanani abeza emfundweni. Nangapezu koko.
  Siyakude siye kuqala kwase zantsi esisekweni, siwunyine kanyeumsebenzi
  wase kaya apa—

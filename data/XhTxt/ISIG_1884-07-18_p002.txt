ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA JULY 18, 1884

Kuyakutaza ukupaula umoya wododana ukupapamela ukungena kuzo zonke
ingxoxo ezipatelele ekuhambiseleni pambili kohlanga. Lentlanganiso
yokugqibela ibe nomsebenzi wokunyula abapati abatsha bentlanganiso ngale
minyaka mibini izayo. Okwenyanisoke imgungxule u Mr. Shaw. Abapati
msebenzi ngelixesha leminyaka emibini ezayo bemi ngoluhlobo:— President
: Rev. E. MAKIWANE, Macfarlan ; Vice-President : Mr. J. TENGO- JABAVU,
Lovedale ; Secretary: Mr. BEN. SAKUBA, Perksdale; Assistant Secretary:
Mr. JAMES J. BOVULA, Roxeni; Treasurer: Mr. J. K. BOKWE, Lovedale;
Committee: Messrs J. SHAW, Fort Beaufort; J. W. GAWLER, St. Matthew’s;
Chief EBEN. MHLAMBISO, Matole ; NICHOLAS BOVULA, Gillton.

Asinasituba namhla esingangenisa kuso indaba eziyintloko zentlanganiso
yabafundisi ebise Qonce ekuqalekeni kwenyanga. Kwipepa elizayo sengaba
nokwenjenjalo. Okwanamhla kwanele ukuti le yokugqibela ibe yenye
yentlanganiso ezinexabiso ezaka zako zelo bandla. Inani labafundisi
ebeliko belinobom, bonke bona aba Pesheya kwe Nciba bebekolise ngokubako
kwanabo bamele i Qonce. Pakati kwemicimbi ekangelweyo ebingowemihlaba
yezikolo, okucedululwa kungeka fezwa ngendawo yokuba u Rulumeni
esakangela, nengxelo yegqugula elalinyulwe ukucikida eyemfundo yaba
ntsundu imicimbi, nengxelo yabaguquli be-Zibalo, nezinye ingxoxo pezu
kwamapepa awa leswayo.

ELA BAROLONG.

Ngomhla we 11 July ucingo luzise oludaba lusuka e Bloemfontein: U
Sepinare Moroko udutyulwe emva kokuzikusela ixesha elide kumzana
okwakwelo lake. Kutiwa ngati wayesilwa neqela la Besutu elipetwe
yinkosana engu Mrolong abati ngu Bogatsu. Ubukulu bomzi wake butshile.
Abafazi abancinane nentsapo ka Sepinare batshabalele. Unyana wake omkulu
[u Joel?] ungumbanjwa ka Bogatsu. Ngayo lemini kutiwa bekusaliwa.
Umongameli we Free State usinge kwela Barolong. Kuvakala ukuba
kuzakubehle kusondezwe inkanunu ezimbini namadoda a 500.

Emva koludaba kufike olunye ngo Mgqibelo lokuba u Samuel Moroko ungene e
Thabanchu na Besutu aba 2000. U Sepinare selencwatyiwe. U zinquml’
umqala ngokwake. Umfazi nosapo lusapilile. Bonke abamhlope basendaweni
enqabileyo.

Nge Cawa (13 July) kufike olucingo: Umongameli we Free State ugaleleke e
Thaba Nchu nomkosi ngo Mgqibelo. Ute u Samuel Moroko makapume apo emzini
kwa oko, ukuba akavumi seleya kuwenza umhlaba wama Bulu lowo wa
Barolong. Utimbisile u Samuel. U Sir John Brand ufunde isaziso sakomkulu
wamisa ilapu lobukosi bama Bulu wasiqinisekisa isaziso ngokudubula
inkanunu katatu. Akukova ukwenjenjalo uyalele u Samuel ukuba mapume
ngapambi kokuba ilanga lipume ngo Mvulo, ukuze angadutyulwa, ati kanjalo
akulule u Joel Sepinare am’bambileyo. Walile kuba esiti u Joel ubanjelwe
ityala. Kutiwa kuko iqela la Besutu elisinga apo e Thaba Nchu.

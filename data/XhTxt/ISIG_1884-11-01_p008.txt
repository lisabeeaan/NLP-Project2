  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA NOVEMBER, 1, 1884.

UKUBUYA KUKA CAPTAIN BLYTH.

INTLAULELO YE “SIGIDIMI.”

Siyavuyisana nabemi bapesheya kwe Nciba ngokubuyelwa kwabo ngu Capt.
Blyth, umhlobo wako konke okulungileyo ab’ekukutaza ngezenzo, ngexesha
lolaulo lwake ebumantyini ba Pesheya kwe Nciba. Nantso incwadi
esiyifumana ibika ukufika kwake. Ngokungapezu koku, zite zonke imantyi
zambalela incwadi zimbnlisa, zibulela ukufika kwake. Oko kukuti akuko
ungavuyiyo, ngapandle kwaboyika ikatsi ngezigqito zabo, ngokubuya kwale
ndoda idumileyo. Iti incwadi:—

Mhleli we-Sigidimi Samaxosa ndifuna ukwenza mbalwa kwimbonakalo ebiko e
Butterworth ngolwesi-Hlanu olutile kwegqitileyo inyanga. Bekufike
izikulu ezibini apa, Umpatiswa Micimbi yaba Ntsundu u Mr. De Wet ne
nkosi u Captain Blyth. Owokuqala akahlangatyezwanga kuba engaxelanga. U
Mantyi yena ufike sekumi isikolo sase Gcuwa ecaleni lendlela, sadnma
katatu sacula iculo le Nkosazana u Victoria, sasipetwe yindodana
eqabukileyo u George Pamla, waposa amazwi amnandi umfo ka Blyth. Ugqite
apo wafika e Butterworth sekulinde igubu lakwa Captain Veldtman,
endingexeli nto ngalo mna kuba ndandisondele kufupi kakulu kulo,
ndikwanovalo lokuba azi loquba kuhlena? Emveni kokumvumela umnumzana
lowo ele Nkosazana u Queen Victoria, saduma katatu. Yateta amazwi
anokukutaza izikolo into ka Blyth yati : “ Ndibuyile nje mna ndisekwanje
ngoko; ndisazela umsebenzi wokukutaza ugqoboko nemfundo pakati
kwentlanga ezintsundu za Pesheya kwe Nciba.” Ndapaula into entle kakulu
ukubona ukuteta ngoknbukana kwe nkosi ezintsundu, zona eziya kwakuse
kudume ukuba zimangalelana e Kapa. Kuqonde wonke umntu ukuba namhla
akuko unonxa komnye.

Butterworth, October 8, 1884.'

OBUBILEYO.

U Mrs. Galela ufike apa ngo 23rd July. Xa ndaye ndimana ndimncomela
ubumnandi beli lizwe, ndisiti liyi Kanana yase Mamfengwini—wati kuseko i
Kanana na esemhlabeni ? mna nditanda kupela ilizwi lika Tixo. Iqabane
lake ibiyinkosikazi ka Mr. John Mbombela —okwa ngunina wake. Ngenye
imini way’esiya naye entlanganisweni wabnza ukuba apina amancwaba alapa,
ndifuna ukuwazi,—yati yona ukumpendula soze siye ngenye imini. Ngenye
imini ehamba no Mrs. Levi P. Mbange bekapana, babemana, beteta ngezi
zinto zivakala emaMpondweni nangalomaca.a, esiti: “ Andingi ndingayibona
lomini yokuvukelana kwentlanga, bayavuya abafileyo befele e Nkosini.”
Ngenye imini ndaye ndimncomela ukucuma kwezityalo kweli lizwe, wati: “
Nokuba sezi zine izikwebu eludizeni, asinto lonto ; wati E Somerset
bekuko zonke intlotyana zemiti, kanti noko akuko lizwe libi njengalo.”
Ngazwinye ubehlala esiti : “ Akuko apo kumnandi kulo lonke—inye kupela
into endiyifunayo li Lizwi lika Tixo.” Ezi zinto tina saye singaziqondi
ublobo awaye ziteta ngalo, kuba umntu wokuvela akaziqondi izinto zase
Moyeni ngap ndle kwalowo osel’ezityilelwe. Ugule intsuku ezilishumi
elinantatu wemka ngolwe 14. Nge yokuqala iveki way’emana ehamba nje pofu
engalele efane wanokungapili, efuna ilamuni, situme intombazana iye
kuzifuna e Toleni. Ndite ngom-Gqibelo ndaturnela. e Bofolo nakwa.
Gaga,—kanti u Tixo uza kusuke enze into emfutshane, esaba ngalila
sim’bongoza u Tixo, sisiti: “ Yini ukuti umntu efika wenze oku — uze
seukwenza ngalonyaka uzayo, hai wala u Tixo. Oyi! Imisebenzi yako
yeyokutelwa nqa—ayinakupengnlulwa, igqita konke ukufikelelwa. Wada naye
u Dear wati: “ Aninakuyitini intando ka Tixo.” Inkosikazi ka Mr. John
Mbombela, u ma u Mityi. yatwala umsebenzi ayengeze enze ngapezu koko no
ma ondizalavo, nozala no Dear lowo, wada wati u Dear ize ndibalele ekaya
e Bofolo ndazise u ma. Inkosi, u Mr. Solomon X. Zazela yenza umsebenzi
ab’engenze ngapezu koko no mkuluwa warn. Ebelala kuyo londlu emonga,
pambi kokuba apume umpefumlo wati makamsingate. Imali yomkoba yapuma
kuye. Inkabi yegusha wayixelela abantu ngomhla wokuncwaba. Wati u Dear:
‘ Azi ukuba u nkosi ndiya kumnika ntonina ngokundihlamba kwake umqala
lemihla.” U Mr. Simon Gqiba wenza umsebenzi oinkulu ongenziwe zizo zonke
izihlobo zam. Walala kona naye wamonga, wamgona. U Mr. Levi P. Mbange
wenze umsebenzi omkulu kunene abam’balwa abangawenzayo— ukundiyela e
Gcuwa (Butterworth) kabini ngehashe lake esiya kutumela ingcingo e
Koloni ngendleko zake. Oko kukuti ihashe lake ndingamniki mali
yokulidlisa—naye ndingamniki mali yokuba aze atye akufika kona. Yanga i
Nkosi ingasikumbula esisenzo sababantu ngalomini. Esi senzo
ndiyakusibala ngentsimbi—okanye ngosiba lwentsimbi. Ute u Mrs. Galela. “
Kuko umntu ondigalela ngeyeza, ke ngoko sendinje ngomnye wabapilileyo.”
Wandikumbuza lendawo: “Akuko balm na e Giliyade okanye itsori, akuko
gqirana apo?” Kwakuxa intliziyo zimandla—wat ho zati zisihla inyembezi
saye singati siyonwatyiswa, wati : ‘‘ Ukufa andikoyiki kwinto zeli lizwe
akuko nanye endisayikataleleyo, nawe andilusizi ukuba ndikushiya, Ixesha
lifikile tshayelani apa endlwini nindivase.” Etshilo wati: “Kuko umntu
ozayo, nguye lo osandibambileyo.” Kanti unyanisile nanku u dade wake
inkosikazi kamkuluwa wam.—Yatsho yawacima amehlo ayo lonzwakazi. Oyi!
hai okwam mna ndisasele! Azi ukuba ndiya kububa nje ngaye na?
Osezinyembezini,

MLINDAZWE N. GALELA.

Tyinira School, Fingoland.

ISAZ1SO.

NJENGOKUBA ndisiva ukuba kuko udaba olulahlekisayo lokuba ndiwuyekile
umsebenzi wam wokumela
amatyala, Iwaye oludaba lundenzakalisa emsebenzini.
Ndivazisa kuzo zonke izihlobo zam e Mamfengwini kwe
lika Blyth ukuba i Ofisi yam ndiya kuyivula e Ngqamakwe
nge nqn, nge 1st ka October, 1884, lo uzayo. Enditemba
ukuba izihlobo zam ezise Tsomo, nase Ngqamakwe zondixasa
kwanjengoko kwangapambili. Zonke incwadi zebizinesi
yam ese Mamfengwini zotunyelwa e Ngqamakwe. Incwadi zezihlobo,
nebizinesi ekuma Ngqika zotunyelwa apa
ekaya. Iveki yokuqala neyokugqibela enyangeni ndobako

nge nqu e Ngqamakwe.

Aloeville,

Kentani District.

12 September, 1884.

ANDREW GONTSHI.

EZAMAYEZA

No. II.

UKOHLOKOHLO OLUYI “HOOPING COUGH.”

Kupantse ukungabiko mhla ekunganyangwayo mntana kulendlu yama yeza e
Lovedale onolu kohlo-kohlo ade ome, ahlanze umntana. Kwaziswa omnye
esele qauka, wabuba kwakamsinyane befikile naye. Lomntwana waye selene
veki esifa esekaya engazanga kucelelwa mfisi wada umntana wamayela
ekuqaukeni.

Nje ngokuba kubonakala ukuba olu kohlo-kohlo (Hoop­ing Cough) luya
lusanda, sifuna ukuzinika izihlobo zetu ezi Ntsundu amazwi ambalwa
okuziluleka.

1.  Ningabayeki abantwana abanolu kohlo-kohlo bahamba- hambe pandle ude
    unqume umbete wakusasa, kanjalo bangapumi pandle emva kwokutshona
    kwelanga. Ukuba liyabanda mhlaumbi liyana bangapumi kanye.

2.  Banxibeni ingubo ezishushu, oko kukuti, banxibeni ihempe ze flannel
    nokuba bangama kwenkwe mhlaimbi bazintombi. Umntana ohamba ze
    nguyena kanye use ngozini nje ngokuba sesike sabona kule’Ndlu yama
    Yeza. Siya temba ukuba bonke abantu abakolwayo aba Ntsundu, abaya
    kuyifunda lendawo, boziyala izihlobo zabo ezi hamba ze ukuba ziba
    nxibe abantwana bazo aba kohlelayo ihempe ze flannel.

3.  Ukuba umntana uba nepika emva kwokuba ekohlele wada woma, lowo ngele
    ngavunyelwa ukuhamba pandle, ngekubekwa imiqa (poultices) eshushu
    futi esifubeni sake nje ngokuba ukupefumla kwake kubonisa ukuba
    asilulo kohlo-kohlo lodwa abanjwe lulo, kuko nesinye isifo eso
    ngezelelekileyo esi fubeni sake.

4.  Musa ukulibazisa ude umkuhlane ubenzima kumntana wako ukuze uye
    kucela umfisi, yiya kwaoko uzuze umfisi neqinga omawenze ngalo.
    Ungati mhlaumbi ungapulukani nesheleni enye mhlaumbi ezi mbini
    ngokungayi kucela mfisi, kodwa unga pulukana nomntwana wako.

  JANE E. WATERSTON.

EZE PARLAMENTE.

ABAFUNDI betu bofumana kwesi Sigidimi ingxoxo ze Parlamente noko singa
zitabatanga zonke. Eyona ngxoxo inkulu etabate iveki ezi mbini ivulwe
ngu Mr. Scanlen, esiti lamadoda angu Rulumente ngoku apetwe ngu Mr.
Sprigg

afanelwe ukukutshwa elulaulweni. Kute ngosukulwa 28 ku April kwaku
tatyatwa i “vote,” oko kukuti kwakumiswa izandla kwafunyanwa ama 34
angecala lika Mr. Scanlen, nama 37 angecala lika Mr. Sprigg. Isizatu
sokuba oyise ngesitatu yena kukwongezelelwa e Parlamente amadoda amane
ase Diamond Fields ate ama 3 avota ngecala lake; oko kukuti inkoliso
yama lungu e Parlamente angabemi bale Koloni asele casene ngoku. nolaulo
luka Mr. Sprigg neqela lake.

Kofunyanwa kanjako pakati kwenteto ukuqokela kuka Mr. Upington obefudula
engomnye wolaulo, inteto yake ukuba “Abantu abamnyama bazintshaba zaba
mhlope ngemvelo” ude wati uyazizonda ukuba engazange atsho kubalingane
bake ukuba abafundisi mabagxotwe e Lusutu pambi kwale mfazwe. Bova
kanjalo abafundi betu amatyala ate u Mr. Sprigg wabanika wona abafundisi
base Lusutu kubabebesenza intlanganiso zemitandazo ngelixesha lemfazwe.

Siqinisekile ukuba lude lwe nziwa uxolo, lofunyanwa ngempembelelo yaba
fundisi base Lusutu, nokuba seleteta ntonina yena u Mr. Sprigg neqela
lake, emoyeni abanawo wokucasana nokusebenza kwa lomadoda.

Kovakala ngocingo lwe 6 ku May, emveni kokuba sesi yibalile lenteto
indaba zokuba lamadoda abe yi Rulumente abukuqiwe. Fanel’ kuba kuya
kufakwa u Mr. SCANLEN endaweni ka Mr. Sprigg, yobu Premier, oko kukuti
intloko ye Rulumente; ahambe kunye nokuba li Gqweta Lakomkulu Attorney
General; yabe eyobu Colonial Secre­tary iya kunikwa u Mr. MOLTENO ; ize
ibe kwa ngu Mr. MERRIMAN, endaweni ka Mr. Laing yokuba yi Commissioner
of Crown Lands and Public Works, oko kukuti umpatiswa wento zonke
ezibekele emihlatyeni, neza kiwo; u Mr. HUTTON, endaweni ka Mr. Pearson
umgcini we mali; u Mr. SAUER, endaweni ka Mr. Ayliff umpatiswa micimbi
yaba Ntsundu.

Kuya kuba luvuyo olukulu kuso sonke isizwe sakowetu esi Ntsundu, sakuva
ukuba lamadoda apumile elu laulweni; nakuba singe kabi nakutsho ukuti
loba njanina olwaba bangenayo namhla. Siqinisekile kodwa ukuba aliyi
kudlula ngobubi nobu tshaba obubandezelayo ngakuti ba Ntsundu kolwaba
babukuqiweyo namhla.

Pesheya kwe Nciba nokuba kukuyipina indawo E-South Africa. Indawo
ebihlangene ngayo ibe ikukunikela ku Dr. Stewart imali ebifuneka ngenxa
yale Institution iza kuba lisebe lese Lovedale. Amamfengu arole £1,400,

kunoku, mhlaumbi sinike namagama abarolileyo.

UMNYAKA OMDALA NOMTSHA.

NGOKU sifikile ekuqalekeni komnyaka omtsha. Omnye ufikile wabuya wemka
nawo amatamsanqa awo, nako konke obe ufuna ukusinika kona. Amatuba
okwenza okulungileyo, nesite sawamatela, adlule kunye nawo.
Owanqwenelayo akasenakubuye awufumane ; esikuposisileyo kuwo
asisenakubuya sikulungise. Umnyaka we 1873 ngoku unje ngencwani
evaliweyo, ate lowo nalowo wabala akutandayo kuyo, nokuba kulungile
nokuba kukohlakele. Oko esikubalileyo kuyo asisenakukucima ngoku kuba
loncwadi iselivaliwe. Into engesiyenza kukucinga esikubalileyo, siti
ukuba kuko esingakutandiyo ukuze ngemini enkulu eziyakunqikwa ngazo
zonke ezmcwadana, sicele lowo unokusicimela ukuba enjenjalo kuseko
ixesha. Ukusilelwa kwetu kunyaka odluleyo kube kufanele ukusenza ukuze
ngozayo sizame ngakumbi. Sifanele ukukutazwa kokungaba sikufezile.

Kambe sibuye sapiwa omnye, omtsha. Masiu- bulele ngentliziyo ezeleyo.
Usisipo so Mdali. Wona unje ngencwadi engekabalwa esemsulwa emapepa
amhlope. Nje ngazo zonke izipo zake awunacapaza. Unikelwe kuti ukuba
sibale kuwo incingane zetu, namazwi, nezenzo. Masiti ke sonke sizibuze
ukuba mna kwelam ipepa ndiya kubala ntonina? Ndiyakusonakalisa na
esisipo sinje ukuba sihle kwaso? Zininzi izinto ozitembisayo lomnyaka,
maninzi amatamsanqa oza nawo. Umnqweno wetu ke ngowokuti banga bonke
abaleseshi betu, nabo bonke abantsundu, bangalungelwa ngulo mnyaka;
banga bangacunyelwa, baze bahambele pambili entweni zonke.

I-ANNIVERSARI

Intlanganiso yomnyaka elunge ne London Mis­sionary Society ibe ise
Uitenhage ngomhla we 15 ka December. Abantu bebebaninzi kakulu, kuba
bebezele nokuza kwamkela U-Rev. Mr. Paterson, umfundisi wabo obesele
neminyaka emibini enesiqingata, ehambele E-England ngenxa yokungapili.
Indlu izale kwati tu ngabamhlope nabantsundu, bada abanye ababi nandawo
yokuhlala.

Kute kwakuba intlanganiso ivulwe ngomtandazo, isihlalo satatyatwa ngu
E-Philpott Esq., Imantyi yakona. Uteke lowo usesihlalweni wabekisa
amazwi okuba iremente ka Rev. Mr. Paterson ifanele ukuvuya kuba namhla
ebuyile umfundisi wayo, kanjalo efika enokomelela nokupila. Ubize U-Rev.
H. Kayser obebambele U-Rev. Mr. Pater­son ngexesha lokungabiko kwake
ukuba aleseshe ipepa elibonisa isimo somsebenzi.

U-Rev. H. Kayser usuke waxela ukuhambiseka komsebenzi kuloremente. Uti
ngalomnyaka upelayo landile inani labeza etyalikeni. Lonto ibangelwe
kukuba amadikoni enenyameko enkulu yokukangela abantu, nokuba abaninzi
befungele ukubuyeka utywala. Badla ngokuti abasukuba beshiya inkantini
beze kweka Tixo indlu. Inani labashenxiselwe ecaleni erementeni ngenxa
yezizatu ezitile lishumi. E-Janseville kuko ityalike entsha eyakiweyo.
Inani labantu abangene eremente­ni linga 94 xa kuqukwa nezikolo
zangapandle. Amabuzwa alikulu elinamanci asixenxe. Eziziqamo ke
ziveliswe kukuti abantu beremente namodikoni bancedisane nomfundisi,
bangamyekeli ukuba asebenze yedwa.

Imali ehlanganisiweyo ngomnyaka E-Uitenhage izi £212 11s. 8d., eti
yakuhlanganiswa ne £32 6s. ezazisalele kongapambili umnyaka ibe £244
17s. 8d. Ecitiweyo xa sekuqukwa ne £160 zokubatalela umfundisi ibe
£22113s. 5d., ke ngoko eseleyo izi £23 14s. 3d. Imali efunyenwe
kwizikolo zangapandle ibe £100, necitiweyo kuzo yaba kwangako.

Emva kokulesesha isimo somsebenzi U-Rev. Mr. Kayser ute ke namhla
umsebenzi uwunika kwasezandleni zika Mr. Paterson, evuya ukuba ebuya
enokomelela nokupila.

U-Rev. Mr. McIntosh usuke wateta ikakulu ngokuba zonke iremente nawo
onke amalungu azo, ebefanele ukuti lowo nalowo abesisikanyiso apo akona,
abe ngononcedo notandekayo ngendlela zonke. Makungalityalwa ukuba
sinqongwe sisitokotoko sobunyama, esimelweyo ukuba siti sakukufumana
ukukanya kwe gospel sikwandise.

Kute emva kokuba kwenziwe inkongozelo U-Rev. H. Kayser waposa amazwi nge
Sibulu ne Sixosa, kwazake kwabizwa isitamsanqeliso kwapunywa. Kute xa
kupunywayo yaba intombazana ye Mfengu ibeta ipiano, uhlobo olutile
lwayo.

IQELA LABANTSUNDU LOKUNCEDANA.

E-Rini kuko iqinga elihle kunene eliyiliweyo, eliyakuti ukuba
linempumelelo livelise ukulunga okukulu kwabantsundu. Kufunwa ukumiswa “
I- qela Labantsundu Lokuncedena.” Iqalwe lonto ngomhla wa 31 ka August
1873. Abasebenga malungu alo bangamashumi amatatu anesitatu. Uti
olilungu amane ukurola imalana encinane ngenyanga, aze ke amelwe
kukuncedwa xa afayo, nendleko zokuncwatywa zikangelwe leloqela xa
abubileyo, mhlaumbi kubube umfazi wake. Uti ukuba uhlelwe yingozi
mhlaumbi wapuka in gain nokuba ngumlenze afumane ukuncedwa ngento
azifunayo ezinje ngomayeza. Yoti yakwanda imali yelo Qela, ibenobom
kanye into ekuncediswa ngayo. Enye yendawo ezitandekaya ngokubako kwalo,
yeyokuba liyilwe ngabantsundu. Abamhlope abangeneyo ukuba babe
ngamalungu babini. Langa lingazuza ukukutazwa ngabantsu­ndu nabamhlope.
Kwanga kanjalo ungati lomzekelo walo unje ukulunga, ulandelwe kwezinye
indawo ngabantsundu. Anga angamaninzi amaqela ayakubangela ukuba
kuhlalelwane ngoxolo, nobuhlobo, kuncedwane ezinxakekweni, kutenjwane
omnye nomnye.

KIMBERLEY.

(NGU DAYIMANI.)

NGAHLAMB’ EZANTSI KWAKE.-Andi- tandabuzi ukuba, ukuba ubelapa u “ Go-
zo-Qonqa” wabhala ngoluhlobo abhale ngalo nge Bhai ne Rini, ubeyakupepa
izigweba, ewe, nokusinekelwa ngabapokeli kulemityino yalomzi ngokwenza
indaba ezinjalo. Um’bhali walamacapaza ulupata usiba engakululekile
ngenxa yomoya ombi okoyo kwinxenye yomzi eziva gwenxa indaba epepeni.
Ndingabeka icapaza libe linye ukuqondisa le ndawo obusobini balinge
konke ukuzinga oka Kumalo ukuba amangalele lombhaleli ngokuba besiti
ungcolise igama lake ngokubhengeza isiganeko sake—esesiviwe ngumzi—njalo
ke. Into emayiqondwe ke sukuba ngokwenza indaba epepeni andinyeke
kuhlazisa bani ngapaya kwento zamehlo ngexesha endipete usiba—imbi lento
mzindini wenyushu ; kuba

NGOMNQAY’ ULAMBILENA? — Nokuba ihla kangapina ngonyaka dasinekisi
no­kuba ngubanina ongasakukolwa yilento ndizakuyikankanya namhlanje
iqhela nje. Irwada lento yokuba ikhaba elibukwa kangaka ngumzi—lase
posini—lisuke umkondo wendlavini wokupatela iminqayi ligangqelane
ezindlwini ebusuku ibe ngamadididi nenduma [O, shame!—EDITOR.] ngencuku
ekungatiwa zezingendawo. Nokuba abenzi balento abayiqondi ububi bayo
bona, ilibala elibi nebala entloko emntwini ngapaya kokuba ilihlazo
kulomsebenzi bakuwo ka Rulumente; mabangati ababona bexengaxenga
ngezizatu zabo mhlaumbi okanye bediniwe ngumsebenzi bangxamele ukonisela
abasawutandayo ngokwenza ezizaqunge zingeni; okunye kwakubo kuko
abangafuniyo ukuba intloko zabo zivatiswe ngenduma ngapandle kwencaza
elulamileyo. Nyani nyani ikhaba malahlukane nezizipendu zoda zifane
nomntu otshixa isitali selimkile ihashe.

BABABHEBHETA. — Ingaba yinyaniso na? Bati umfundisi omhlope we Lutheran
kwenye yezi lokishini zalapa, wale mpela ukubopa amaqina emitshato
emibini etyalikeni yake, ngesizatu sokuba abaninisiganeko benxibe nzulu
ngokugqite yena mhla watshata ; lomfu- ndisi akanelanga kukwala
ukuyibopa lemitshato kodwa, akavumanga nokuba imigcobo yenzelwe kulo
lokishini akuyo, into leyo ede yalanyulwa ngonegunya. Nokuba lisiko
lalotyalike na ukubanjalo, ayingemangalise lento koqondayo ukuba
abafundisi belihlelo bakolwa egqwagqwe nje umntu ontsundu emfundweni,
kwanele kubo umntu ontsundu ekwazi ukufunda i Bhaibhile qha.

IMITSHATO.—Nakuba iko inteto yokuba imitshato inqabile kulomzi—watsho
nomfundisi ngomnye umhla — inyatna ike yafuna ukubaninzi ngezintsuku ;
imi­tshato ibe mitatu, emibini iyeyase No. 3 Location (leyaliwe
ngumfundisi) ; omnye ingoka Mr. Sulelo e No. 2 Location— ziziganeko
zaleveki ezo. Ndawo kungatiwa igqitwa nazi lokishini ezi ngokuba semva
ngeziziganeko, yile kunokutiwa kulapo amaninzi kona ama Koloni—i Malay
Camp; imitshato yalempi kungatiwa ikolise ukuba se gantolo, esingaziyo
nokuba ingaba ngo “vula ngezantsi” ebuhlanti kusinina lento kunjale;
ngati nomfundisi wayeteta ukuba ebona udodana luluninzi kaugaka uko
nomtinjana, pofu kungashukuuywa ngakwelicala.

lakube kulaula utando lwe sesman; kuba

LUTANDO LONA! — Ayiqondeki lento yokuba amagwangqa abonakalise umoya
wokuyichasa lempi yakwa Ntshatshongo (ama China), kanti ngo “sibali”
bawo. Nditeta ukuba mna ngolwesi-Bini olu lugqitileyo egantolo imantyi
ibibandakanya omnye u Ntshatshongo nobhelukazi lomlungukazi wases’ apa e
Beaconsfield, yangumtshato omhle. Nakuba amagwa­ngqa ezibeka izizatu
zokuyichasa kwawo lempi, sipambili esi sokuba idyarelene nawo
ezibhizinisini, kuba izite gxwa ivenkilana zayo kulamagumbana onke omzi,
woba ngajikela ugapa sewuqubisana nobuso Iuka Ntshatshongo — ugo “ so
lingeva yeza ” ke mfundini lamadoda ebhizinisi apa.

O KENKEBE.—Ezona nkenenkene kwe zikoyo ngaba bamane bekataza abanye
ngokuboleka amapepa, oti wakubuza ukuba lipina elake ati, “ bendifudula
ndilamkela koko wasuka wati u Jabavu, ndase ndiliyeka”—into leyo efana
neyendoda eti yakutsarwa ngumqa iseliwukalala, lakuyambata ipango ibuye
ikumbule kwakuwo. Namhlanje ke Messrs. Magogotyandini ipepa liziswa
ezimpumlweni zenu; oke waza kundikataza ngakumbi mna ndiyakumbhengeza ;
ngokutshetsha too. Nalo ke !—Kunjalo ukufa kwalo nkosi.

pezu ko Kafile xa ahleli esihlalweni—ongena nceba—wakupika ! Ungamqonda
kukungahlalwa zizicaka—esihlala inyanga iba ngummangaliso—wakupika.

Amatyala alindele i Jaji alibatu—awo- kubulala asesibinini.

Asiyo mitshato apa, yinkungu nelanga, apo ungafika ngezo mini amaledi
namanene beqakata, bexhuma, ingu two by two —kubetwa isitampu—eko lo
ubeta ifleti, iqalwa kwalisati capa kude kube yenye imini, ukuvuma kona
kuhleliwe endlwini—wakupika.

Inkumbi zidlule apa kweziveki—into ezibete samnyama isibhakabhaka—kwawa

yindoda kulombuso wasa mlungwini. Nditi ipina imigudu yetu yokuzamana

yonke imilambo nemifula emiwe ngu mzi ingeniswe kwelitamsanqa. Ngxatshi
batwali bohlanga.

IWESILE.

Kwintlanganiso yabamhlope beli Bandla e Bhai ngeveki egqitileyo kutetwe
ngokushushu ngokwakiwa kwetyalike entsha e “ Hilini ” eyakufikelela kwi
£5000 exabisweni, esekubonakala ukuba into leyo inokwenziwa kuba
sesifunyenwe nesiza.

U Mr. Mzimba, umfundisi wase Love­dale, Free Church, ucelwe ukuba aye
kuncedisa umsebenzi eramenteni ka Mr. Ross ose Toleni, ngokuba esifa
umfundisi walondawo. Uhambe nabadala aba­bini beramente yase Lovedale, u
Mr. Pato Marhawu wase Sheshegu, no Mr. Metu Gingxa wakwa Gqumahashe.
Ku­ko ke neqela labahlabeli lalo ramente yase Lovedale, elipetwe ngabafo
baka Bokwe—u Mr. Candlish Bokwe, no Mr. J. K. Bokwe. Babe ke bepete no
hadi oludlalwa ngu Mr. J. K. Bokwe. Kute ezindleleni apo inqwelo imi
kona bakutsho ngengoma nohadi kwabeta bazishiye abantu inqwelo zabo
bapulapule ka­kulu. Bazokwenza imvuselelo nemitandazo kwi Bandla
letyalike yase Toleni. Inkonzo zibe mnandi kakulu nge Sabata. Yati
ingoma etyalikeni nge Sabata ye 20 September, yayolisa kakulu
kwabalusizi.

U Rev. R. Ross ubamkele ngobubele obukulu abantwana beramente yase
Lovedale ebifudula ipetwe nguye. Wabaxhelela unkonka, into enkulu
yenkabi yebokwe emazinyo asibhozo, encum yesifuba ilukuni. Singasaziteti
ezinye indlela zobubele, nezenziwa ngu Mrs. Ross, umfundisikazi. Yaye
ititshala u Mr. S. Mpondo nenkosikazi yake bezindlezana ezi ububele,
kumasi kuntoni.

Intshumayelo bezilungiselelwe ukuba kwa Ndabakazi ngo Mvulo kwesika
Faleni Ngwabeni. Kusasa kufike ilizwi eliti lonkosi yeso sizwe sakwa
Ndabakazi ibhubhile. Siyeke enchwabeni; safika umzi umkulu, upelele
kakulu. Amado­da nabafazi bekumakulu amatatu. Yati i coffin yenziwa
yantle ngamadoda antsundu akwa Ndabakazi. Umsebenzi wokunchwaba
uhanjiswe ngu Mr. Mzimba ; saye isililo sentsapo sisikulu, silusizi
kanye. Kuba lomfo wakwa Maduna ube nobubele kwinkedama namaxhego
akowabo. Namhla ke ezonkedama lizi- fele. Ngumfo obegcine imfundo, awada
wayiyela e Lovedale Institution, waza nakuba engahlalanga xesha kuba
ebesele nendlu, epete isizwe, ingxakeko zase kaya zabangela ukuba abehle
agoduke. Ute kodwa wayinyamekela imfundo, waluncedo olukulu kumfundisi
ngokuncedisa ukwaka indlu yesikolo efulelwe ngezinki, nokuquba abantwana
ukuba bafunde, kunye nabazali ukuba baxase imfundo. Namhla kubatandi
bemfundo lomsebenzi usele ungati nwunabani. Ubhubha namhla intombi yake
enkulu, nomfana wake bese Simnareni, e Blythswood, baye abancinane
abantwana befu- nda bonke. Ibite lendoda yazigcina kakulu esimilweni
ngokuti ingaseli tywalabomlungu nobabantu abantsundu; nakuba ebengemntu
weramente, ube nyamekole izinto zaso tyalikeni, engumngeni tyalike.

Kuleveki ke intlanganiso ziyakuba se Toleni, kwa Mgodla, emantloko e
Tyinirs, nase Ngqutu. Ngolwesi Ne evekini siya e Ceru mhlaumbi nase
Mtonjeni. Kwiveki ezayo nge Sabata siya e Cegcuwana, e Ndakana, nase
Ngqamakwe esezantsi. Ngolwesi Illanu siye e Blythswood Institution, apo
iqela labahlabeli liyakuvuma ingoma ngokubalulekileyo ngokuhlwa.

Ekubuyeni ukuba kubonakele ituba nemvume kobako utnvumo e Ncemera
(Peelton) nase Qonce. Sinduluka apa ngo Mvulo. 5th October. Siyakuba se
Ncemera nge 8th, e Qonce nge 9th.

IFANDESI LEMIHLABA E DODOLORO.— Imihlaba enexesha isezazisweni kwipepa
le Mvo ukuba ingqeshiso yayo iyakutengisa—oko kuyakuqutywa ngalo
Mgqi­belo (30 October) ngexesha leshumi. A- maxabiso ayo mabini—elinye
(a) lelokuqesha isituba sonyaka omnye, (b) elesibini leloyakuwutabatela
iminyaka emitatu. Lemihlaba izifama ezasala kwifandesi ngenx’
engapambili ngokuti abatengi baugafikeleli kwi “ upset ” ka Rulumeni,
asenokuti ozivayo ayitenge, nangapandle kwefandesi, ngeloxabiso
lingumgqaliselo.

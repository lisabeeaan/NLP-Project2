ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MAY 1, 1875.

UMDITSHWA inkosana yama Mpondomise udliwe inkomo 30 ngokusuke anuke
umntu, ngoku selepantsi kwa Mangesi. Enye inkosana idliwe 40 enkomo.

IZICAKAKAZI—I-Government yase Victoria E- Australia itumele kweyalapa
ukuba itunyelwe izicakakazi. ezintsundu ukuba zinokufumaneka. Asikuko
nokuba ziya funeka kona.

AMADODA atile ase Mangesini abetunyelwe E-China ate angenelwa liqela
lamadoda ekuta- ndatyuzelwa ukuba libe lisuswa yinkosi yase Burmah. Kulo
madoda kuko ebuleweyo.

E-SHAWBURY kuko umfazi ofike evela E-Ma- mpondweni, oti usinde
selegqityelwa kusitiwa uyatakata. Kutiwa ubeselepelelwe ngamandla
ngangokude akohlwe kuhamba, kwezinye indawo apumelele kuhamba ngezandla.

E-UITENHAGE kuko umfazi womlungu obulewe lilawu esiya E-Bayi ehamba ne
nkwenkwana yake. Inkwenkwana ifunyenwe izimele etyolweni. Kuko umntu
obanjiweyo ngokuranelwa, ekufunyenwe kuye inwele ezifana nezalomntu
ubuleweyo.

E-QONCE—Ngokumalunga nencwadi yomnye wababalelani betu, owake wakankanya
ukuba I- mantyi yase Qonce isabekwe nxamnye emsebenzini wayo, kuba kuko
imali etile ekungaqondakaliyo ukucitwa kwayo, sinovuyo ukuti kude
kwaqondakala ukuba, lemantyi ikoyo ngoku U- Mr Innes, akanatyala
kulonto. Ngoku uselequ- ba umsebenzi wake kwanje ngokwapambili,

INDABA—Abanye abaleseshi betu bangaba mhlaumbi abazi ukuba “Indaba,”
ipepa ebelishi- cilelwa apa E-Lovedale pambi kokuba kubeko Isigidimi,
linokufunyanwa nangoku libotshiwe lenziwa umqulu omnye. Sibona kona kuko
namapepa abalwe ngu Rev Tiyo Soga, esikolwa ukuba baninzi abangatanda
ukuwafunda. Itengiswa ngesheleni ezintlanu xa iselingumqulu.

LANGALIBALELE—Lenkosi yama Hlubi kubo- nakala ukuba iza kupelela
ekuhlaleni kule koloni yetu. Sesike sakukankanya ukuba U-Lord Carnarvon
ute makapume E-Robben Island afunelwe indawo apa, kulunyukelwe ukuba
angabuyeli E-Natal. Izipata mandla zale yetu ikoloni ziza kungenisa
eparlamente ukuba mayivunywe londawo. Asikazike ukuba yotinina yona
iparlamente.

E-ZANZIBAR—Inkosana yama Arab ebisand’u- kuvukela izipata mandla ite
ngomhla 18 ka January yangenelwa yimikombe yokulwa ye Nkosazana,
ebicelwe yinkosi yase Zanzibar ukuba iye kuncedisa. Kwelama Arab icala
kubulewe amadoda amashumi mabini, kwanxwelerwa amashumi amahlanu,
yabanjwa nenkosana yawo. Kwelinye icala akubangako noko amnye
owenzakalayo. Lonkosana ibanjiweyo, isiwe esiqitini sase Pembe esisentla
kwesase Zanzibar.

KUBADESESHI BETU—Site namhlanje ngoku- nxamela ukunga singazishicilela
zonke incwadi ezivela kubabalelani, ezinye zizezenyanga ezidluleyo, sade
saswela nendawo yokuke senze awetu amazwi, noko zikoyo izinto ezininzi
esinqwenela ukuteta ngazo. Ezinye sesoti sizikankanye kule izayo
inyanga. Kulungile nokuke sisitele ngenye imini siyeke kutete abanye.
Kanjalo yinto esivuyisa kakulu ukufumana indaba kubanini Sigidimi,
kuhlanga oluntsundu naba- sukuba benqwenela ukunyuka kwalo. Incwadi ka
Mrs. Adonisi sonika indawo ezitile zayo kwelizayo.

KUTE EMNYAMEMI (Alexandria) umbanjwa wo- nilungu wakutshwa etolongweni
ixesha lake linge- kapeli ngenxa yesifo ebenaso, kwatiwa makemke
kwangolosuku aye e-Hospital yasebayi (kwakungo suku 12 ku April) wasika
wanxila, wabuye wabanjwa wafakwa etolongweni kwangobo busuku! kwati
kwakusa wabeta elucangweni ukuba azo

kuvulelwa, yavula ke indoda ehlala kona ; wati ufuna amanzi, wampa U-W.
N. Somgesi wabuya wamvalela eyardini kwati kanti ukwela elutangweni
wabeta ngapandle wabaleka, wabonwa ngabanye ababanjwa bekude naye,
baxela, wasukelwa wafunyanwa selebaleke imayile ezintatu.

OZELWE NGUNYANA WAKE—Kute pesheya kwintlanganiso etile yentsapo zesikula
se Cawa, umfo obesand’ukuguquka wasuka wati. “Asikuko nokuba ndiya
mbulela U-Tixo kunye nesikolo senu se Cawa. Nimbonanje lonyana wam
uhleli emva kwam ungubawo ngokwase Moyeni. Ute ukundizala kwake,
wandibona ndinxilile ndihamba ndituka wati ke, bawo yintoni na le
uyenzayo, umfundisi wam wesikolo se Cawa ubesiti kuti esikolweni, “
Bonke abanxilayo nabatukayo abanandawo ezulwini.” Ate lamazwi
ukundingena kwawo entliziyweni, kanti ngofefe Iuka Tixo sendiya kusuke
ndincedeke, ndiziyeke ezondlela zikohlakeleyo. Nditi ke ngoko unyana
wam, ungubawo ngokomoya.”

AMANZABOMVU—Elinye lamapepa angase Gqili liti esisifo sesingene kule
koloni. Umfo otile abati ngu Forester wase Peddie obeye E-Win­burg ute
kanti ebuya nje uselesiza naso. Kwesi situba sase Queenstown ne Aliwal
North ubeselefelwe lishumi elinesine lenkabi. Siya temba ukuba izipata
mandla ziya kubukangela msinya ubunyaniso balonto ziwuqinise umteto
wokuba inkomo zingahambelani nezamazwe aneso sifo. Ukuba side sanda kule
koloni ingaba lilishwa elikulu kanye. Umsebenzi ungebi nakuhamba ngenxa
yokusweleka kwenkabi. Abapila kuquba inqwelo, bangafumane bakohlane nazo
; namana- ni ezinto zonke angenyuka. Ngazwinye ikoloni ingabuyela emva
ezintweni zokukanya.

E-QONCE liti ipepa lakona abantu abaye ku- ncwatywa emancwabeni akona
nge 1875 bafike 162. Kwelinani abantwana babantsundu babe 19 ; kubantu
abakulu amadoda 14, amankazana 8; bebonke abantsundu 41. Kwabamhlope
amakwenkwe 34, intombazana 17, amadoda 42 abafazi 23 bebonke 116.
Kubonakala ukuba elabelungu lingapezulu kude kwelabantsundu, nokuba
kanjalo amadoda afa ngapezu kwamankazana pakati kwabamhlope nabantsundu.
Ngomnyaka we 1872 inani labancwatywa kona lalipele 99, abamhlope 64,
abantsundu 35. Ngowe 1873 inani lenyuka leza 128 bebonke: abamhlope 88,
abantsundu 40. Kubonakala ke ngoko ukuba leminyaka mitatu inani liya
linyuka ngokunyuka,

ONTSUNDU WABESIKOLWENI NAQEBA—Kuko umfo abati ngu Ferdinand Vogt,
I-German, obalele kwelinye lamapepa adumileyo ase Germany. Kwincwadi
yake yomhla 29 ka January 1875 uteta ngokuti amaqaba alunge ngapezu
kwabase sikolweni. Uti abamana ukutumela imali yokuncedisa abafundisi
apa kulunge ukuba bangayiciti, ayifuneki nganto. Abafundisi balapa uti
zizityebi ezingetande ukwananelana indawo namanene ase Germany. Ingubo
ezitunyelwa ukuza kwabelwa abantu, apa zifike zitengisiwe ngamanani
amakulu. Abafundisi bahleli njengenkosana ; zinto ezinikwe imihlaba
emikulu yi Govern­ment. Omnye usand’ukutenga umhlaba nge £1,000.
Ngazwinye singati uti abafundisi abalapa ngabakohlisi into ezibonayo
abantsundu ngapezu kokubalungisa.

UKUTYALWA KOMGAMTREE-Abanye bokumbula ukuba sake sati ukuwukankanya
kwetu lomti ubufanele ukukutazwa utyalwe kunene kuba apo wonjiwe njalo
ube luncedo olukulu. E-Algeria ilizwe elisemantla E-Africa ute wakuba
mninzi wayipelisa kanye icesine kwezinye indawo. Namhla kumiwe nalapo
abantu bebesoyika ukuhambela. Olunye uncedo lomgum silubona E-Jipete.
Kule minyaka ilishumi idluleyo I-Government yakona ityale imiti yomgum
efike 20,000,000. Isiqamo soko sesokuba namhla apo imvula ibinconywa xa

ifika kubekanye ngomnyaka namhlabati ibako kakulu, ngokukodwa ebusika.
Noko ke kodwa yona londawo yokuna futi kwemvula iveliswa zintlobo zonke
zemiti xa imininzi ngokwaneleyo. Yinto engaluncedo leyo ukuba abagauli
bemiti baya kuhlala beyikumbula.

ISITEMBU.—Abaleseshi betu bokumbula igama lika Sir Samuel Baker esasite
ubeze kuhlola amazwe apakati E-Africa. Sasike sakankanya kanjalo ukuba
ute akubuyela ekaya wabala incwabi yehambo yake. Loncwadike
isand’ukushicilelwa. Pakati kwezinto ateta ngazo ukankanya nesitembu ati
ngaso.—“ Isitembu E-Africa, siyingcambu yenkohlakalo ezininzi.
Ngumonakalisi wezimilo zabantu. Abafazi bakona ngenxa yaso, banobudenge
obulusizi, obubangela ukuba lingabi nakuhluma ilizwe. Intlalo yalapo
kuko isitembu ayinqweneleki nangento. Kulapo lakele kona ikwele,
nokutiyana, nomona, nokuzondana, nokuvimbana. Bati ke abantwana baya
kula nje sebefana nabazali, bezizinto ezibandayo, ezikangele okwazo
kodwa, abahanahanisi; amakwenkwe engenamnqweno wokuti ndanga ndingafela
ilizwe lakowetu ; intombi zikula zingamanqenera, zinga- fundile.”

E-NATAL—I-Governor ebikona ngapambili U- Sir Benjamin Pine unduluke
ukugoduka, ukuba ekutshiwe ebukosini ngomhla 4 ka April. Endaweni yake
kufike U-Sir Garret Wolseley, lomfo sasite kukutshwe yena ukuya
kulungisa ama Ashantee oko ayefuna ukupakamisa intlokwana ngaku Mangesi.
Siyazi sonke ukuba akatabatanga xesha ukuwabonisa indawo yawo. Kubapati
bemikosi yase England akuko namnye opambi kwake ngelixesha langoku,
ngokukalipa, ngamaqinga okulwa nokukwazi ukupata umkosi. Yinto ekade
isinda kwamakulu amacebetshu ike ; ngamhla wumbi icolwe sclingati ifile.
Azikaqondakali kakuhle ke indawo atunyelwe ukuba eze kuzenza apa.
Kubonakala ukungati uze kulungisa ukupatwa kwabantsundu balokoloni,
nezinye indawana ezingaqondakaliyo napakati kwabamhlope. Ufike apo
E-Natal ngomhla 30 ka March. Abantu bakona bamamkele ngovuyo olukulu
noko bate babuqubuliseka. Ngoku ubesafuna ukuke ayi hambahambe I-Natal
azibonele ngokwake into eyiyo, nento eyisweleyo, azivele izikalazo zabo.
Ngentsukwana ezimbalwa silindele ukuva owona msebenzi atunyelwe ukuza
kuwenz’a. Siyatemba kanjalo ukuba ngoku nabantsundu bakona baya kuba
nokufumana lamalungelo esinawo tina balapa, baze ke bakauleze ukunyuka.

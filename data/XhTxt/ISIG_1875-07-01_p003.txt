Makahlale umkuluwa wake nelolifa lake, axamleke yilomisebenzi
ayisebenzayo kowabo apo, makalale yena aligcine elo xego awugcine lomzi,
abambe elogama lika yise. Kona ukuba ebesahleli ekaya bekuyakumsiza
ntoni oko, ebeyakuhlala epantsi komkuluwa wake nje, nalempahla anayo
namhla ingaze ibe sesandleni sake. Okukona kulungileyo koku, okukona
wobufumana kamsinya ubuntu nobukosi kungoku; angaba ubeya kuzibulalela
ni ngokuhlala ehleli engumntwana makakaye apo atanda kona. Woba nezicaka
nje kwelo- zwe aya kulo,eziyakuzakumbusa,zibusele inkomo; wongenanje
kwezimpahla axele akulamba, atengise ngenkomo akuxakwa yinto emxakileyo;
kunga- bakonakele ntoni ngokukwenjenje kwake? Hai kukona kulungileyo
namhla. Umka enentliziyo enjalo ke lomfana kungaseko bani amaziyo
emhlabeni apa.

Elilizwe likude kutetwa lona apa, lihlabati eli nenkazimlo zalo,
yintliziyo le, nenkanuko zayo, yimisebenzi le ikohlakeleyo atandayo
umntu uku- sukelana nayo, yimikwa le ingendawo apikelene umntu ukwenza
yona lemihla. Umntu nokuba ngubani wapi osele zinikele kwezonto,
ngoselem. mele mgama u-Tixo, ngosele sezweni elikude, kuba u-Tixo
akavumani nezonto.

Lomfana ebesimka nje kowabo ebesiti ndiya kude apo ndingasokubonwa ngu
bawo, ubezimela uyise, ezimela impato yake, ezimela intando yake. Nokuba
wenza ntoni wena, nokuba uhambele pi, nokuba usebenza pi, ubusa bani,
musa ukuke ulilibale elilizwi liti “ Wena Tixo uyandibona.” Hlala
ulibeke pambi kwako, nokuba selukude nabazali bako, nekowenu, ukoma Bayi
usebenza kona, ukozindlela zakwa Rulumente usapuka kona, ukwele Daiman
usimba kona, ukwelegolide uhlamba luhlalu kona, usema Bulwini usalusa
gusha kona, usezidolopini upeka kona, musa ukuke uyilibale lendawo
yokuba u-Tixo uyakubona, akuko ndawo angekoyo yena kuyo, akuko msebenzi
uwenzayo angawuboniyo yena, akuko zwi ulitetayo wena angaliviyo yena,
akuko bukolwane ubutandayo angabuqondiyo yena. Into inye kwapela
angavumelani nayo u-Tixo engenakusondelelana naye sisono esi sodwa.
Sililizwe elikude naye sona, si- butshaba obukulu kuye sona. Musa ke
ukungena kuso, pambuka, musa ukuyihamba indlela yezikohlakali, cezela
kude kuyo kuba kulapo u-Tixo angekoyo, apo angena kungena kona,
yintshabalalo, lilishwa elingapele ndawo. Musa ukuzipalaza intsikelelo
onazo, musa ukudlala ngazo, mhlonele u-Tixo onguyihlo, uyibambe yonke
imiteto yake,

ungaxelisi yena lomfana osuke wawalahla onke amatamsanqa, wafuduka
kowabo waya ezweni eli- kude.

UMBONISO WA PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

UMBONISO wesibini pesheya kwe Nciba ubuko ngo 25 ka May, e-Nqamakwe kwa
Captain Blyth. Abantu ababeze kubonela baqala ukufika kwangosuku
olungapambili; abanye abamhlope beza nosapo Iwabo ; kuko nabavela
kwimigama enje nge Queenstown. Indawana ete yasixakaniso ibe kukuna kwe
mvula. Kute ngo Mvulo yamana ukuna, yaza nangemini yomboniso yama- na
ukufika izipango.

Ngolwe Sibini evekini, mhla ngomboniso, baqa- la kwanini ukufika, bevela
kumacala ngamacala. Ute tu u-Feltman mayela nexa lesitoba, elandelwa
likulu lamadoda atsho ngezifanayo onke. Yena ubetsho ngengubo azipiwe yi
Governor oko wayese Kapa. Kute ngeleshumi ixesha kwafika u-Rili, inkosi
yama Gcaleka, ehamba namadoda akumashumi amahlanu, kunye nemantyi yakona
u-Mr Ayliff. Enye inkosi ebiko kwezivela mgama ngu Ndarala, inkosi yaba
Tembu, no Mr. Eynn Imantyi yakona. Bekuko kanjalo neqela lamadoda avela
kwa Matanzima.

Kute pambi kokuba kuqalwe umsebenzi woku- kangela impahla kwake
kwadunywa katatu yimpi yonke, kudunyelwa Inkosazana u-Victoria. Emva
koko bapumile abagwebi ukuya kukangela izinto abamiselwe ukuze bahlule
kuzo, kute kuzo zonke kwanyulwa omhlope nontsundu. U-Rili no Ndarala
bate nabo bafakwa enanini labagwebi —U-Rili emiswe no W. R. O. Eynn Esq.
ati ukumbiza ngumntana wake.

Izinto ezibe ziziswe embonisweni azinakucuku- swa bani zonke. Bezilapa
inkomo, amahashe, inkabi, igusha, ibokwe, ihangu, izinja, umbona,
amazimba, itapile, izonka, imigubo, izihlangu, into ezikandiweyo, nemiti
ecweliweyo, nezinye izinto.

Bakuwugqiba umsebenzi wabo abagwebi ku- hlanganiswene ndawonye ukuba
kuze kwamkelwe amabaso. Abangekankanywe bonke abawafumeneyo ; aba
balandelayo ngabafumene azintloko—

Emva kokwabiwa kwamabaso ute u-Captain Blyth waposa amazwana ambalwa
ewabekisa ku Mamfengu, ewabonisa ukulunga komboniso, nokuba ubufanele
ukuvuselela ekutini bonke bazamele izinto ezicokisekileyo : nokuba
kanjalo i-Government iyayi tanda kanye lento bayenzayo yomboniso, ide
yarola £50.; Enye indawo wati uyatemba ukuba ngozayo umnyaka kobako
elendlu ekupe ezinye, nelomhlaba obiyelwe ngokudlule eminye, nezinye
izinto abazenza emakaya abo, ezibonisa ukuhambela pambili. Ubekise
umbulelo kwabebevela mgama abancedisileyo ngokubako kwabo nangokuvuma
ukuba ngabagwebi.

Emva kwake kusuke u-Feltman waposa amazwana okubulelela Amamfengu kubo
bonke ababonisa ukuwakatalela, nabade bahamba imigama emide ukuza kulo
mboniso.

Noko ubungafikanga kowokuqala kubonakele ukuba kuhanjelwa pambili. Akuko
umke esiti ndifumane ndazixamla ukuza apa. Abaqale kulo ukubako bemke
bengalibali, bemangalisiwe. Ite enye indawo ewenze mandi yangababeti
begubu bakwa Feltman, abebefike bafaniselana ngento entle. Nompati wabo
ubekwangumfana ontsundu. Badlale kamnandi kanye, noko angekabi maninzi
amaculo abawaziyo.

Kanjalo kuyabuleleka kakulu ukubako kwe nkosi ezinje ngo Rili no
Ndarala: kutembeka ukuba kovelisa izinto ezininzi ezinkulu.

Utandekile umsebenzi walomini, waqondisa imizamo nempumelelo ka Captain
Blyth pakati kwabantu apakati kwabo, nokuvuma kwabo izinto
ezifanelekileyo.

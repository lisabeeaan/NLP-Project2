UMTETO ONGOKUCOKISWA KWAMAKAYA.

Womnyaka we 1883.

  BANTU balatiswa ngesi saziso kvvezi ziqendu Zomteto ongoku Cokiswa
  kwamakaya wonyaka we 1883: —

Isiqendu sa XLVI. Nokuba ngubani omangalayo ukuba kungene endlwini yake
igosa lopeteyo kulo mandla akuwo, nokuba lelogunyazisiweyo ngombalo welo
gosa, nabani ongxabalaza endleleni, otukayo, odubulayo, mhlaumbi
owenzakalisayo ngenteto elo gosa mhlaumbi lomntu, xa ahambisa izimiselo
zalo mteto, woti, efunyenwe enetyala amelwe sisohlwa- yo
esinjengokuhlaula iponti zoshumi, ati ukuba akahlauli afakwe
entolongweni asebenze nzima mhlaumbi, ixesha elinge- ngapaya kwenyanga
zontatu.

Isiqendu sa XLVII. Ngexesha ekoyo ingqakaqa isipatamandla sinokumenza
nabani opakati kwemida yegunya laso ukuba anike ubungqina bokuba ubani
lowo waqapula kakuhle, ze ukuba ubani lowo ute akabi nabo ubungqina
bokuba waqatshulwa aze kanjako amangale ukuba aqatshulwe, woba sicenge,
enetyala ngazo zombini ezindawo sokuba ahlaule, ngokwala ngakunye,
iponti zombini, ati ukuba akanakuhlaula amelwe yintolongo ixesha
elingengapezulu kwentsuku ezisixenxe.

ALEX. STEWART.

Kwi Ofisi ye Mantyi, Imantyi.

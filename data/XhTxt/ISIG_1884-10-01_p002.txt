Kuko izinto ezintatu ezisenze buhlungu kunene sakuzibona esiyaleza ukuti

Umnenga asasibaliselwe ngawo kwelenyanga efileyo siva ukuba ama Slamsi
agqibile ukuwuqina. Amafuta awo azalise imipanda ema 42, e gallon zima,
52 umnye um’panda. Ewonke amafuta zi gallon ezimawaka mabini anekulu
linamashumi osibozo anane (2,184). Inye i gallon idle 5s., ngoko ke
amafuta odwa angenise £546. Imbovu zomnenga (whale-bone) zitengiswe uge
£120. lyonke imali engenileyo yi £666. Inkunzi ike yabonwa, kodwa ibuye
yemka yabalahleka abazingeli. Ikatazela kulandawo yahlukana kuyo nosapo
lwayo.

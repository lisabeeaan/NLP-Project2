ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 2, 1883.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

INGQAKAQA.—Kutiwa ayikanqumki ngapaya kwe Natal e Delagoa Bay.

AMATEMPILE.—Intlanganiso yomnyaka yeli bandla ibise Rini ngo 22 March
ngokuhlwa.

KOBESATETA NGOKUYA E DAYIMANI.—Kutiwa kwelo ngenxa yokunqaba komsebenzi
inkita iyacitakala.

INGOZI.—Unyana ka Mr. Ferreira, ngase (Kromme Biver) pakati,
wenzakaliswe ngumkuluwa wake ngompu ngengozi kodwa.

UTYELELO.—Liti elase Kapa elonene libekekileyo lingu Mr. J. X. Merriman,
losinga ekaya e Europe ngo August, ngotyelelo kodwa.

EZASEMA MPONDWENI.—Amampondo ngoku ngati ano- kuzola, noko ama Xesibe
wona ngati akakolisiwe sisimo sawo esingenakuzola.

I TUNXE.—Kugqitywe kwelokuba abemi balomandla bafudukele Pesheya kwe
Nciba emva kokuba bevune ukudla abakulimileyo nonyaka nje.

U MR. SCANLEN.—Eliuene libete elucingweni, lixela u- kungabi
nakufikelela kwalo e Kimberly, ngenxa yokuba intlanganiso ye parlamente
selikufupi.

IMVULA.—Ngenxa yemvula ebeziko, ukuvulwa kwendlela kaloliwe pakati kwe
Cradock ne Middleburg, kuyekelelwe, oko kukuti, kushenxiselwe kwi 2 ka
April.

AMANZI ABOMVU.—Esi sifo asikawungeneli owaseba Tenjini, noko abantu
bonabasenkatazweni yokungavunyelwa ukuba batwale imitwalo eya kwezinye
indawo.

KWINTLANGANISO yozilo ebise Rini, yeb'axa labazili, elilapa e-Lovedale
lino Bev. P. J. Mzimba no Mr. J. K. Bokwe abazizijoli zalo. Lomanene ke
anduluka ngo 28 March.

INTLANGANISO YAMAFAMA.—Kwintlanganiso yalamadoda ebise Cathcart (kwa
Daliwe) ebecela ukuba u Rulumente abuye enze inguquko kwindawo
abeyitetile eyiyeyokuba kuugabiko kuhambelana nga nqwelo kulomandla
wakona nowangase Qonce.

EZEMALI—Umgcini mali wemicimbi yololiwe wase Natal ugwetyelwe iminyaka
emitatu esebenza nzima ngokucita imali leyo. Nohlobo Iwengxelo Iwencwadi
azipeteyo alwanelisi.

EZASE-LUSUTU.—Kutiwa abe Sutu abayiqondi kanye indawo yokuba banikelwe
ngo Blayi, (Capt. Blyth), ukuba ibe nguye otabata indawo ka Mr. Orpen;
noko Iona elika Masupa lingade liti tya.

IMVULA.—Kubeko isipangokazi esikulu kunene ngase Murraysburg, esitsho
wati lomlambo kutiwa yi (Buffel’s Biver) wazala ngokukulu kanye, oteke
wona wenzela abantu inxwalekokazi ezinkulu kunene.

U PROFESSOR HOFMEYR, kuvakala ukuba wenze isicelo, esingisa kubongameli
besikula esi sikulu kutiwa yi Theological Seminary yase Stellenbosch,
ukuba ake avunyelwe ukuba anqumke ukushumayela ngoku.

ISISA.—Indoda epantsi ko Lord Derby ivakalisa ingcinga ka Rulumente
eyiyeyokuba unxamele ukungenisa u Mankoroane no Montsioa pantsi kwake,
aze njengoko enjenjalo kwezinye inkosana ezipantsi kwake abamkelise
intwana.

AMANZI ABOMVU.—Kusand’ukuvakaliswa kwa Rulumente ukuba ngenxa yobuninzi
besi sifo e Dayimani, lunqunyulwe uhambelwano ngenqwelo pakati kwalo ne
Qonce. Sona i Sinqenqe sikululwe ngoku kuba sinqumkile isifo kona.

EZE PARLAMENTE.—U Mr. E. C. Wright, M.L.A., obe lilungu le Parlamente
lase Rini, siva ukuba upumile. Indawo yake ke yozaliswa ngu Mr. Jos.
Gush, sikolwa kananjalo ukuba amafama omxasa. Ganyana limbi siva ukuba
lingeniswa kunye nelo lake lelika Mr. A. Douglas. Kolu nyulo luzayo siva
ukuba u Mr. Douglas ngomnye wabaya kuzimisa pambi kwabanyuli base Dikeni
ukuba angeniswe e Parlamente.

AMA BULU.—Kuvakala ukuba ayakauleza kanye ukwenza umsele e Spitzkop.
Kutiwa ama Swazi selete atelela kuwo, kubonakala ukuba nezinye inkosana
zika Mapoko, ziza kwenjenjalo. Amaxosa wona abambene odwa e
Zoutpansberg.

UBUSELA.—U De Beer, u Mhleli weli pepa kutiwa yi “Adelaide Sphoenix"
ubanjiwe e Cradock, kutiwa ubile. Lo De Beer ikwa ngulowa ubeke
engumshumayeli we Vangeli yoxolo ku Besutu e Middleburg, ote emva koko
waba ngumteteleli ematyaleni e Cradock.

UNYULO LWE BISHOP.—Kunyulwe u Bishop Webb obe ngu Bishop e Bloemfontein
ukuba abe ngu Bishop wase Bini endaweni ka mfi u Bishop Merriman.
Sitandaza ukuba ama Tshatshi antsundu afumane kuye indoda
entliziy’ifanayo neka Dr. Merriman, esisamlilelayo nanamhla.

EZOMHLABA WABA TEMBU.—Eliqela lababantu bafumana bengena behlala
ngapandle kwemvumelo kabani, lenze inkatazo enkulu kunene e Xalanga,
ngokokude i Mantyi yelo u Mr. Cumming, anikwe igunya lokuba asebenzise
amapolisa ama Bulu ase Dordrecht. Asintw’izi mfuduka ezisinga kwelaba
Tembu.

IMPAHLA KA RULUMENTE.—Impahla yakwa Rulumente yotengiswa e Mtata
ngezintsuku zilandelayo ngo 11 April, 11 July, nango 10 October;
ngapandle kokuba kube kwako nto iyiyimbi ivakaliswayo. Esi saziso
sikutshwe egameni lika Major Elliot. Zezako kambe ezondaba mfundi ose
pesheya kwe Nciba.

AMARE EMFAZWE.—Kuxelwa ezokuba Indlu Yegqugula lapesheya, emveni kokuba
ike yenza izwi kwi Ruluneli yeli letu, yoti kwaoko yenze amalinga
okukusela ama Bechuana kuma Bulu. Uti u Lord Derby yena kufanele ukuba
ngoku e London kwenziwe inyatela ngakuma Bulu, utike amadoda ayi 2000
oba nako ukulikusela kuma Bulu elama Bechuana.

IZIHELEGU.—Oka Swaartbooy, u Kivit, uke wonakalelwa kakubi ngale nyanga
ifileyo. Amakwenkwana amabini ake afunda kwesi sikula, abukuqeka ne
skotsh-kari, enye yenzakala kakubana, enye yabusinda. Kute nge Cawa kwa
kule veki ihle ngayo lengozi, lenkwenkwana ibisindile yatunywa amahashe,
ete yakuwabamba yakwela nenye. Apale lukuni amahashe, yawa ; ihashe
layinyatela emhlatini lamroboza. Siyavuya ukuva ukuba ziyatembisa nokoke
ingozi ezo.

INCWADI ZABA ƁALELI-

INDABA EZILUNGILEYO E-SWAZINI.

NKOSI YAM.—Kawundifakele lamazwana ambalwa epeponi lamanene Ndicinga
ukuba into eninzi ayazi ukuba kweli lakowetu u Tixo usele zukiswa
endaweni zonke ngoku, akuseko ndawo ingasena mfundisi. Amawaka waka
abantu abafuna u Kristu sitetanje, nabafundisi abo enditeta ngabo
asingabo abafundisi njengokuba kunjalo apo e Koloni nase Natal.
Ngabafundisi abafumane bazenza abafundisi ngenxa yotando Iuka Tixo.
Ngamana u Tixo angatumela abafundisi bavule izikolo ezipilileyo nakweli
njengase Koloni. Ngobanina apo abafuna ukusebenzela i Nkosi? Nanku
namhla amasango evulekile kanye. Anditi niyakolwana kukuba xa imfama
ikokela imfama zolimazana? Bafundisa pina bonke abakade befunda e
Lovedale, Healdtown, nae Grahamstown? Litinina ilizwi?—

“ Hambisani ezindaba

  Ziye kuwo wonke umhlaba Bulelani bantu nonke Zibulele into zonke.”

Tandazani kwi Nkosi yesivuno itumele abasebenzi esivunweni sayo.

Ndingomnye wabasebenzi

M. MOKONE.

Pretoria, Transvaal, Janry. 22, 1883.

Gaga School, March 8, 1883.

MR. MHLELI.—Ndifakele lamazwana ambalwa. Yinina lento nitwele amehlo
kangaka emva kwemali ninyuka lemihla yonke. Nite zisheleui ezintatu,
savuma, sati siseva elo seva sekusitiwa zine savuma. Ngoku siva
sekusitiwa lishumi. Besisiva kusitiwa ema Gqunukwebeni nase Ngqushwa,
akuko mntu usitatayo Isigidimi. Botinina abantu ukupumela kwezizinto
zonke? U Rulumente unyukile naye kwelake icala. Ilanga linyukile.
Bekulungile ukubiza isitandatu isixenxe kotunyelwa ngesitampu. Uxolo
mnumzetu. Owako futi

MFAZO N. GALELA.

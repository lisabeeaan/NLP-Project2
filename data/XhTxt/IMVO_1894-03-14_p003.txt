Ngelase Mampondweni.

UKUNGAFIKI NGEXESHA.

Inkosi u Sigcau ayifikanga ngexesha entlanganisweni ebibizelwe kuyo ngu
Meja e Mfundisweni. Lento ite ayanelisa kanye.

INTSUKU.

Intlanganiso yama Mpondo no Meja ibise Mfundisweni ngolwesi-Tatu lwe-
gqitileyo. Ama Mpondo anikwe intsuku ezilishumi ukuba agqibe kwagqiba
kulo mayelana nesigitshimi esibekiswe kuwo.

UNCEDO.

U Mhlangaso ubalele kunia Rulumeni ukuvakalisa ukuba yena nempi yake
uzilungiselele ukumnceda ukubantba u Sigcau. Kuvakala ukuba usamkele eso
sipo u Rulumeni, engati seyixotyiswa impi ka Mhlangaso.

U SIGCAU NESIGITSHIMI.

E Kokstad banenkolo yokuba ngati u Sigcau akasakwenza mpendulo intle
kwisigitshimi sika Meja. Ngokoke imikosi yamaqakamba iyalelwe ukuba
isondezele kwelama Mpondo, emo kufupi nomda, apo ayakuti ngo 21 March
anikwe izwi lokuba angene.

U MEJI NO SIGCAU.

Kwintlanganiso ebise Mfundisweni ngomhla we 9 kule imiyo, u Meji
umxelele u Sigcau ukuba baka babonana okuya yena wayehamba ne Ruluneli
kwelozwe. Ngeloxa wayenguinntu nje kodwa, ke namhlanje ungu Mtunywa we
Nkosazana oze kumbikela udaba olubalulekileyo angazange alufunjatiswe.
Ube nomnqweno wokudibana no Sigcau njengomhlobo, nanjengokuba ka bebona
nabo upakati kwabo engaxalabile nakancinane, engenalo ne rola. U Meji
uyixelele inkosi leyo ukuba kuko abantu abamane ukumpulula kakuhle
bembonga ngohlobo olungabangela ukuba nengwevu engapezu kwake iti hili
intloko. Ekubekweni kw’ake esihlalweni wayemneinane ka­kulu ukuba agqale
ababezihlobo zake, nabo babesebenzela amalungelo abo kupela. U Meji emva
koku umxelele u Sigcau ukuba ilizwe lake liyakungeniswa kwelakwa
Rulumeni, nangona imigaqo eliyakungena pantsi kwayo engayikankanyanga.
Utsolise indawo yokuba izinja zakomkulu zemfazwe sezikululwe, kwaye
kukapukapu ukazifunza kunokuziroxisa pambi kokuyiqwenga inyamakazi.
Yinto elula ukutshisa ingca, naxa sete ubani walumeka akuko waziyo apo
uyakucima kona umlilo. Zimbini kupela indawo ezivulelekileyo ku Sigcau:
kukuba azitobe ngoxolo, enye yekokelela emlilweni. Ukuba uzinikele
ngokuzolileyo, amabango ake ayakuqwalaselwa kakuhle. Woqonda naye ukuba
ekumnikeni kwake elicebo akalingi ndlela zakumbexesha nakumoyikisa. U
Meji uqoshelise ngokuteta ngemfazwe yakwa Mzilikazi nokoyiswa kweso
sizwe ngomkosana ongepi ngemipu apa edubula utoto; ekwati ngeveki
ezintandatu oyiswa ama Ndebele no Kumkani wawo, u Lobengula, wafela
ematyolweni. Emva kwalenteto u Meji unikele u Sigcau inewadi evela kwi
Ruluneli amcele ukuba aqwalasele into etetwa yiyo ngayo yonke inyameko.

IBHUNGA NO BOKLENI.

Kwangalomhla kugaleleke u Bokleni, unyana ka Nqwiliso, ehamba nepakati
elikulu, Emfundisweni, babunga no Sigcau. Bagqibe ekubeni babe nentla-
nganiso ekumkeni komhla no Meja ukuze u Sigcau abe netuba lokupendula
incwadi leyo. U Sigcau ucela isituba sentsuku ezilishumi pambi kokuba
apendule. Kuko inteto yokuba usaza kutumela abatunywa e Kapa, okanye kwi
Nkosazana. Ukuba unikele impendulo enganelisiyo, u Meja uya kumnika
isituba seveki ukuba apendule. Amampondo abese Mfundiswweni abe malunga
namakulu amabini engaxobile, ezolile, ayacinga.

UKUGODUKA KWE RULUNELI.

Ukuwelakuka Sir Henry Loch oseke kwavakaliswa kufun’ ukuba nendawt
yokungati akangebe sabuya. Oku kuqondekele kwinteto ayenze ekubekwen:
kwelitye kwityalike etile e Kapa, ate angabuya okanye angabuyi. Kwakubon
ukuba yenze amanqaku alolohlobo : Ruluneli ipepa eliyi Star lase
Johannesburg livakalise ukuba kwezase Bhotwe e London indawo selisaziwa
negama 1o mnumzana ozakutabata indawo ka Six Henry Loch, engati Dike
ungekapel: unyaka. Kutiwa iyakuba ngumfoongqondo ivileyo, kuba
kukangelwe intc ezinkulu eziyakwenzeka kukuma kweli lizwe ngeminyaka
emihlanu ezayo. Asazi ke. Kodwa angaba akaligqibanga ixesha lake u Sir
Henry Loch, kuba i Ruluneli zalatelwa iminyaka emitandatu, yena ke
wafika ekupeleni kuka 1889, esiti ingabi makabe uyaroxiswa ngenxa
yezimfazwe abonakala ukuba uwungenisa kuzo umbuso wapesheya. Ngapambili
waqala watanda imfazwe n Sir Bartie Frere, isipelo saba kukuroxiswa
sekona- kele. Ngoku no Sir Henry Loch ungene kwakulowo mkondo; emakube
kukwa yilonto ngaye.

3 IMVO ZABANTSUNDU (NATIVE OPINION). MAY 28, 1891

U Mr, Warren, M.L.A., e Pirie.

Ngokwesaziso ebesenxiwe, eli nene elingummeli we Qonce kwi bandla le
Palamente, libeko e Pirie, pantsi-kwa-Mahlati, ngo 21 May, ukuhlangana
nepiko labanyuli balo labantsundu. Iqale ngo 11 30 kusasa intlanganiso
ebeke u Mr. Peter Qwela kwindawo yckongameleyo.

U MR. TENGO JABAVU ubalule into ekungayo, wavakalisa usizi oluviwe ngu
Hon. J. L. Bradfield, otumele ngocingo, ukuba amacebo ake okuza
onakaliswe zimvula ezinkulu, etemba ukuba intlanganiso iyakumvela. Yena
(utetayo) uyavuya abe akafikanga u Mr. Bradfield kuba ubeyakuwa uluvo
akufumana bengeko abantu akubon’ ukuba ubexamtekile ukusuka kude e
Dordrecht ukuza kubo. Abantu abahambe imigama uknza entlanganisweni
kupela ngamatsha-ntliziyo—u Mr. Njikelana, naye. Zinto ezinjengezi
ezingqina nkuba abakazilungeli intlanganiso zafuti. Mayelana no mnyulwa
okoyo—aknnaninani kuba engomnye wabo.

Umongameli ncele ilungu elibekekileyo labo ukuba liqube elinga
lingakubhekisa, emva kwalo nabo bobhekisa abanako kuye.

U MR. WARREN ute nlapa ngedinga awaye ewubeke ngalo nmzi ngexesha ekwaye
kulungiselelwa unynlo. Bokumbula nkuba oko wayete naninina ababonileyo
abanyuli ukuba banokudibana, baza bamnqwenela ukaba abeko, uyakwenza
konke ukuba abeko. Mayelana nexesha ebizwe ngalo lentlanganiso angaqanla
lendawo, Ukuba bekunokwenzeka bekungalungayo zingakandanisananga
nokudibana kwe Palamente intlanganiso zabo, isituba seveki ezimbini
pambi kokuhlala kwe Palamente Bingamlungela. Namhla ke, njengokuba
esendleleni (kufuneka esukela inqanawa yangomso e Monti) useleyakwenza
amanqaku asentloko ngezinto ezibonakala kuye zipatelele kubo ete zapambi
kwe Bandla le Palamente; ukuba kungaba kuko nngaba akazikankanyanga
uyakuvuya ukuziva nga bo abanike ingxelo anako ngazo. Emva kokuba
bemnyulile bate befika e Bandla u Rulumeni owayepete oko wabeka pambi
kwe Bandla icebo le Pasi Engqongqo. Wawenza owake umgudwana, wokuteta
nokuvota, ngendlela yokuncedisana nabo babewuchasile lowomteto. Nide ke
natuma e Kapa abatunywa abayakudibana namadoda ekulandelwa wona ngamanye
apo e Palamente, imigudu ke eyenziwayo, kudibene neyenziwa ngabatunywa
benu, yabanga ukuba ade awuroxise lowomteto u Rulumeni. Ukutalatela
kweloxesha kwati xum ke mayelana nento ezibalula abantsundu. Kuye kwaya
wada wapuma lowo Rulumeni, kwamiswa wumbi. Ekumisweni kwalo mtsha
angabalula indawo ati yena ililungelo elikulu labantu abantsundu.
Ukumiswa kwake kute kwako amanene amabini abalulekileyo ngobuhlobo bawo
ngakontsundu angeniswayo ebapakatini bobu Rulumeni. Utete ngo Mr. Sauer
no Mr. Innes. Lonto yamvuyisa kunene. Nabo mabahlale besamele ukuba
lomanene ekulomabhunga apambili nje akuko nto imbi iyakufumana imwele
ontsundu. Kwimiteto eyakukangelwa yi Palamente njengoknba izakuhlala
angabalula owotywala. Indawo emandla kuwo yeti ukutengiswa
nokungalengiswa kotywala makuyekelwe ekutandeni kwaba ntu ngokwabo. Yena
uyavisisana kunene nalonto; ukuba abantu bangatyikityeliswa ngotywala
bengabufuni, Ngokupatelele kwi mfundo into aziyo ukuba bashushu ngayo
angati aknko nto inokwenziwa ngabo njengamalungu e Palamente. Bafike
bona banike imali ku Mpatiswa-Mfundo ze ke yena azilaule izikolo
ngokubona; ukuba kodwa banendawo abangampatise zona uyakuwenza amatuba
ukuzaneka kubapatiswa iziroro zabo. Ubeselete akasakuba bambezela
seleyakwenza amabala engwe, uyakuvuya ukuva abangakutetayo ngelabo
icebo.

u MR. p. QWELA (Umongameli) uvuyiswe yindawo yotywala. Utsho wati yonke
into elungileyo eyenziwa kweli lizwe yonakaliswa yinto enye—utywala.
Kuko ntsomi eti kwakulima indoda nomfazi, kuti kwakusa bofika kupume
utyani, bangayazi ne ndawo abebesebenze kuyo. Kanti yintaka eyenza
lonto. Iti kwakuhlwa ifike iti utyani balentsimi mabuhlume. Ide indoda
yalalela yayifumana, yayibulala. Lentaka ke ngoku bobutywala—bomlungu
nobabantsundu.

U MR. NJIKELANA ute banovuyo uku mbona umtunywa wabo eko pakati kwabo.
Bamazi engeko ukuba ngumhlobo, ekuti ke nokuba abavanga nanteto
bavuyiswe kubona obobungwevu bendevu nobuhlope bobuso, abe ngakumbi
owolohlobo umhlobo. Bahleli bengamroreli nganto. Uyaleze bukala
ngotywala esiti, kuko indawo engaqondwa kakuhle ngabamhlope, xa bona
bantsundu bateta ngotywala, basuka ngati bateta ngobomlungu bodwa. Unga
lingaqonda ilungu ukuba bachase utywala bonke. U Rulumeni xa abenzela
iqinga ngotywala makavelele lemitayi, nentotjane inkatazo yabo ikwa
nakwezonto. Imfundo—makaqonde ukuba bashushu ngayo bafuna amacebo angayo
omelele kunoku. Ugqibe ngendawo abanga bona e Debe bangacandelwa.

U MR. JIM VANTYI ute indawo yokuyekelwa kubavoti iyakonakalisa ngakumbi
kubo, kuba umzi awuko evotini, kunjalonje nalo ukoyo uhleli
ungatembekile kuba , ubiwa nakule voti, nangoku ’ banesiroro sabantu
abamagama abiweyo akutshwa. Ukankanye indawo yokuba bona apo e Pirie
babetenjiswe ngokongezelelwa i akile ezine kwisitandatu esingabancediyo.
Ngemfundo ute mabafundiswe namashishini.

U MR. WARREN upendule wati ngokupatelele kwindawo yokuyekelwa kotywala
kubavoti uyayiva indawo yokuba bunokwanda. Icebo elivakalayo lenziwe
kwipepa le Mvo ukuba abarafi bonke kwindawo ezina, bantsundu babe
ngabavoti ; into inye eyoyikekayo mayelana nelicebo kukuba bangati
bengenise into ezininzi ezingamalungelo abantsundu upumelele nzima
umteto e Palamente, balahlekwe kwanayilendawana ivumela abavoti ukuba
bayilaule intengiso yotywala. Ngendawo yamasimi ukongezelelwa, bangati
bamnike incwadi yedinga ababelenzelwe ayizame.

U MR. ALEX. MABONA ungenise indawo yobuyekeyeke bomteto ongokudliwa
kwamatshivela awonakalisa abantwana babantu.

U MR WARREN njengonozala uyayivisisa lento, ute nakuba engababeke
ngadinga — akamntu wenza madinga apo angasakufeza — woyikankanya lendawo
kopatiswe isiko Embusweni.

U MR. NJIKELANA yakupela ingxoxo uvakalise indawo yokuba umzi
ungekaroreli nto enqubeni yomnyulwa wabo abavuyayo ukumbona.

U MR. WARREN ubulele amazwi amnandi awenziweyo ngaye wati, ubengavuyayo
kubeko ipepa elenziwayo lento abazikankanyileyo kuba engangi angatembela
ekuzikumbuleni ngentloko. Uyakuhlala elinga ukusebenza ngendlela yokuba
bamtembe. Ekubuyeni e Bandla into efunekayo kukumxelela apo
bangahlangana kona kupela.

Yaba iyahlukana intlanganiso ebi " yeyozolo,”

Ingxelo ngobalo lwabantu e Transvaal zibonisa ukuba abantu abamhlope
kwelo ba 119,128, kwaba amadoda a 66,498, amankazana 52,630. E Rautini
bamayelana 22,150 abamhlope. Akuvakali kubalwa kwe Netevu, emakube
kukuba kusitiwa zizidalwa (schepsele) abantsundu ngama Bhulu. Kakade
amawetu ebeyakutsho kude le kulamanani.

COLESBERG. — Into ekoyo tina apa, utsho umbhaleli, ngumhlambi omkulu
wenkumbi. Ezingama andizanga ndizibone, ndazigqibela ngo 1873
zingeninzi, namhla lento yangoku isimangalisile sonke. Kunjalonje aku
Msutu aku Lawu kupela wobona zanekiwe zityiwa ngabanumzana. Lento
yenkumbi ngati ingu nongqause, sesisiva ukuba kuko nabelungu
abasebezitya; bati zimnandi abaninizo kambe.

UMTSHATO OBALULEKILEYO. — Kunconywa ubuhle bomtshato obuse Mount Arthur.
Ubalisa omnye umbhaleli uti: Ukuyola komtshato ka Mr. Maloni Xayimpi ne
nenekazi u Miss L. Noboza lase Mount Arthur, umtshato ozolileyo
obekekileyo opetwe kakuhle ngumfundisi wesosikalo nabantu bake. Ete
nemantyi yase Lady Frere yenza inteto emandi ngenxa yokuba ababantu
bobabini iyabazi kumaxesha angapambili ababezipete kakuhle, yatsho yati
inenekazi elo iminyaka mitandatu lipantsi kwake lizipete kakuhle kuyo
yonke lominyaka, kengoko ulusizi kunye nentsapo yake. No Mr. M. Xayimpi
uneminyaka engapezulu yena waye engumntu ozipete kakuhle, angavuya betu
bazama okokuzipata nase ndlwini yabo, watsho wayeka apo.

ISIHELEGU SOKA GWIJI.

NKOSI MHLEEI,—Kaundisize ngesituba
kwelopepa lako, izwi linye ngeso sihelegu sokutshiswa komzi ka Madikana
Gwiji. Lomfo wakwa Qumbu uti ungu “Pantsi-Komti” uhlab’ ekangele
ukuzibiza. Uti kambe yena ubuzela oka
Majeke. suke enze amambamfambamfa omntu otunga amapondo.

Unanina u Majeke akuti tyititi emhlotsheni azipenduiele. Umboneli
unyanisile ukuti lomali, obobomi bunzima akubo u Madikane bumangele.
Kanti na umntu kwa Rulumente ungumbulali
wokusikwa na ngokusuko azifunele ifama engapetwe sibonda, nama Mpondo
kanti akangegxekwe bani, kakade wona akazibizi ngakuba lusapo lokukanya.

Ndipela ngombulelo ke Nkosi Mhleli
ngesituba salomazwi.—Owako otobileyo,
UM-KOLONI.

E Qonce, 15th May, 1891.

NGERAMENTE YASE GILLTON.

NKOSI MHLELI OBEKEKILEYO,—Ndikubongoza ukuba undipe indawo kwelope­pa
lomzi. Ndindululwa yinteto ka Rev. II. Kayser, eyibhekisa ku Rev. E.
Makiwane esiti, upange iramente ka yise engowelinye ihlelo. Mna
ndimbongoza ngentobeko u Maneli ndisiti, sicaka Sopezulu toba igazi, xa
ulitobile igazi wobona ukuba ubengafanele ukuyibhala lencwadi. Kuqala
uti, yiramente ka yise, unyanisile ke, leramente ngabantu asinkomo, into
engenangqondo yona ungangena uyiqube ihambe; umntu unemvo zake, xa
efikile u Maneli kwiramente ka yise, ibifanele ukuti yona ize ku Rev. E.
Makiwane ibulele ugcino abeyigcine ngalo, ibulise iti, namhla ufikile
ubawo wetu, namhla siyagoduka. U Mr. Makiwane akangeze abambe mntu
ekugodukeni kwake. Xa bali sisacopile nalapa ngokwabo ipina indawo
yokupanga. Into efanele u Ma­neli kukuti kubantu bakayise ndinone
ngantonina ramente ka bawo, angaqumbeli u Mr. Makiwane. Xa ababantu
bebengabaka Mr. Makiwane beku Mr. Kayser bezitandele bona ubengeva
njanina xa kubhalwa inteto eti upanga iramente ka yise. Kulungile
ukucinga umntu avelele zonke inkalo; xa u Maneli ebeke wafika kwingcinga
ukuba ababantu ndibapete njanina lento namhla bawushiyayo umzi wakowabo,
akangeze alwe namntu. Ndinga u Maneli angatoba igazi, acinge ukuba bona
bafundisi bazizitya zonyulo; litinina ilizwe elichase Ilizwi xa libona
abafnndisi bexubushana emapepeni, into ebifanele ukupelela apo ibitetwa

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1884. 7

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

IBOLA E NATAL.—Ishumi elina-nye lase Kearsney, emazantsi o Tukela,
elalidlala nelase Stanger, lenza isikora esi-3 sisonke nge innings
yesibini!

IVATALA ENKULU, isand’ ukufunyanwa kwintsimi ye Mantyi yase Humansdorp,
ubukulu bayo, sitat’ ubunzima kutiwa ibe ziponti ezimashumi mahlanu
anesitoba.

IMVULA.—Apa sisand’ ukutyelelwa ngemvula ezimnandi, nakuba zifika okunye
ukutya kona sekugqibele ukutsha, sinetemba ukuba kwindawo zamva,
nasenceni zobaluncedo.

ABATSHELE ENDLWINI.—Elase Monti ipepa libika ukufa kwamadoda amabini
antsundu atshe nendlu. Kubonakala ukuba amadoda la ebelele, ateke
esotuka kwaye kungaseko ndlela.

INDAWO ENTSHA ekusand’ ukufunyanwa i golide kuyo kusemantla e New
Zealand e Australia. Ifunyenwe ngum Maori ongowabemi bokuqala belozwe
obehamba hamba eyifuna.

UKUSWELEKA KOMSEBENZI, kubange ukuba abantu basingise kwamanye amazwe,
ukuya kufuna umsebenzi. Sisitsho nje siva ukuba yimirozo amadoda amblope
omsebenzi asinge e Australia ukuya kufuna umsebenzi.

IGOLIDE YASE TRANSVAAL.—Okuvakalayo ngezeli kukuba kuya kubonakala
ngakumbi ngakumbi ukuba iko igolide kweli. Okwangoku ingati ixabiso
elifunyanwa ngamanene atile ancedisanayo liku £510 ngemini.

I ANNIVERSARY E HEALD TOWN.—Lomgcobo wonyaka weskolo secawa kuvakala
ukuba uyakuba ngosuku lwe 11 nolwe 12 lo May. Le yenye yembuto ezimnandi
zonyaka kwintsapo yezikolo ze Cawa kwindawo ngendawo.

INGOZI EHLUNGISAYO.—Pakati ko March (15) kwafika ngocingo ezokuba e
Virginia (America) kudubule ngengozi iruluwa kwindawo ekumbiwa kuyo
amalahle, abantu ababe- semnxumeni belikulu elinamanci omahlanu,
ekucingelwa ukuba batshabalele bonke.

UBUKUMKANI BAKWA ZULU.—Itsha ntliziyo abati ngu Mapela, elidume ngokuba
laka labulala umfundisi kwa Zu­lu, uzenze ugqada-m’bekweni ungxamele
isihlalo sika Cetywayo esifuna ngegazi. Yinene ukumkani ufile kwa Zulu ;
life ngokunjalo negunya nokuzola.—The Natalian, (8 March.)

EZASE LUSUTU.—I Rulumente yase Free State ifumene ngo 18 March ezindaba
zivela ku Ferreira omele umda walomzi umelene ne Leribe:—U Joel (yinto
kam Lambo enci le) no Matella babuyile e Tlotsi, woyisiwe ngumkuluwe
wake u Jonathan, Kufe amadoda amashumi matatu macala omabini. Into
eninzi yamahashe ka Joel ifile.

INTLANGANISO YO TITSHALA YASE HEALD TOWN.—Ama- lungu alentlanganiso
alatiswa kumhlati wezaziso apo ofuma- na udaba lokuba intlanganiso
ebimiselwe ukuba ngeholide yentsuku ezilishumi (Easter Holidays)
ayisakubako. Ishe- nxiselwe kwiholide zo September lo uzayo. Olona suku
ingati ibeko ngalo lovakaliswa ngalo maxesha.

ULAULO LWE NKOSAZANA E LUSUTU.—Ngolwe-Sibini (18 March) i Ruluneli u
Genl. The Hon Sir Leicester Smythe yakupa isaziso sakomkulu esibika
ukuba Inkosazana iqalile ukupata e Lusutu, nokuba imisa u Colonel M.
James Clarke ukuba sisandla sayo kwelozwe. Imiteto iya kumiselwa yi
Ruluneli epeteyo xa isazeke ivela kwinkosazana.

ODUBULE INKOSIKAZI.—Ngase Dayimani kuko indoda abati ngu Stanley,
edubule umkayo. Isizatu asikaviwa, kuba abantu ngobusuku bamvulo utile
babese mdanisweni, kute kuyasa kwasa imdubula, yam’pa amanxeba amabini
ate waqauka ngelesibini, Ite yafunyanwa indoda le yakufunwa ngamapolisa
ngacala limbi, ete yona ibisiya kuzinikela ku mapolisa ngokwayo.

U CAPTAIN BLYTH, lipelile nje ixesha lake e Lusutu undulukile kwelo
ukusinga kwa Pesheya kwe Nciba endaweni yake. Siyatemba ukuba abebekade
bemlilela bokoliseka namhla. Pambi kokumka kwake e Maseru kubeko
intlanganiso enkulu abati li ‘ Pitso,’ ate wamkelwa kuyo u Colonel
Clarke onyulwe ngu Mntan’ Omhle. Yonke imicimbi yayo ihambiseke
ngokuzola.

IDLAKA ELIKULU.—Ekuzeni kupelako January walo nyaka kubube kwelama
Jalimani u Herr Lasker omnye wezinxiba-mxaka zelo. Ngapandle kwabane
wonga ababeko kwidlaka lake, ati amapepa kwabako 20,000 abantu abamsa
kwindawo yake yopumlo ukulindela umhla wokupela. Sizindlela ukuba
elinani lingangabo bonke abantu abanga funyanwa e Bayi nase Rini
bequkiwe.

UQWITELA oloyikekayo Iwabako ngo 27 ku Jan. kwiziqiti zelama Ngesi
Iwenza ilahleko nenxwaleko embi kwabase- zinqanaweni, nakwabasemhlabeni.
Kwatshabalala into eninzi yabantu. E London into eninzi yabantu
yenzakala yenzakaliswa kukuwelwa zizindlu. E Wales oluqwitela
Iwadibanisa neliqwa elikulu. E Scotland laba lityobo imiti eyapukayo
emiyezweni etyelwe zikulu zelo. Inqanawa ezapukayo yinto eninzi ezazi
kwimizi emi pezu kwe St. Georges’ Channel, pakati kwazo ngumkombe abati
yi Juno, ne Rhiwabon, ne Lauderdale nezinye.

UKUTENGISA NGEMIPU.—U Greaves, Reid no Golding amagwangqa amatatu
awabanjwa etengisa ngemipu ngapandle kwelungelo kwelama Mpondo bapendule
palnbi kwe jaji e Qouce ngenyanga efileyo bagwetyelwa ukuba bagqibe
iminyaka emitatu etolongweni. La madoda asimagxagxa kowawo, ngamadoda
anazo indyebo zehlabati abefuna kodwa ukurweba ngemipu le. Umqondisi
mteto owayewanyanyatela ngu Mr. Frames BA., B.Sc., indodana esand’
ukufika kweli ivela emfundweni Pesheya. Abakuseli ibingo Messrs Richard
Solomon no Francis Brown.

IBALI ELIBUNTSOMI.—Ibali elibuntsomira liti: Kute ngamini itile umntu
nenkosikazi bamana becinga ngalento ibutywala uhlobo ebatobe ngalo,
basebegqiba kwelokuba bake babuyeke. Bayeke bazibandakanya kwinani lama
qwela, bate kodwa babuzisa indawo yokuba abangeyitatina intwana ye
whiskey njengeyeza, hayi kutiwe akuko salelo kulondawo. Ekugodukeni
kuvunyelwene ngelokuba kutatwe ibotile ye yeza ukuze lihlale likufupi.
Kute ngamhla utile yati indoda ukunxanelwa utywala oku yaxela into
zigeza, yaselizilalisa isiti, ibekisa kwinkosikazi, ndinesisu kaundipe
iglasi, omnye, Yo! akuseko nto ndibe ndisifa nam sendilifincile iyeza.
Yeka kupululu inyati kwa oko, sapila isisu.

ISENZO SOKUKOHLAKALA.—Kwifama engase Burghersdorp kuko into embi kunene
eyenziwe yinkwenkwe entsundu. Lenkwenkwe yati ngamini itile yatunywa
yinkosi yayo ukuba ise amahashe ayo kwenye ifamo. Ivumile inkwenkwe,
koko ite inkosi yeka kuba sekuhlwile. Kute ngokuhlwa yawaqubula
inkwenkwe nantso, ngapandle kwezwi kumniniwo, kute kusasa yagaleleka
isiti iwasile, nakuba ite pambi kokuwasa yalifumana linqunyulwe indlebe
estalini, itsho yazirola indlebe enxoweni, kuyiwe e stalini akubangako
mkondo wagazi, namahashe abonwa ekwakufupi nomzi; atike bani uyibonileyo
lento yena amahashe la uwabone kukwelwe kuwo ngamakwenkwe amabini
antsundu. Kuyiwe kwenye inkwenkwe leyo, kufikwe ingubo zayo okwenene
zinegazi, ekubonakala ukuba esi senzo senziwe ngawo.

INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

NIFA NGANINA?

NKOSI YAM MHLELI.—Kaundincede undifakele lamazwana, ndifuna ukubekisa
kusapo Iwabantu bakowetu. Ndinala m’buzo. Kunganina ukuba abantu batande
ukufa ? Inkosi yazifunga yati: “ Ndihleli nje anditandi ukufa komoni.
Ayivuyiswa kukufa kwabakohlakeleyo, koko itanda ukuba baguquke badle
ubomi bapile. Kodwa abantu abavumi bapikele ukufa, kunganina ? Kukona
ongandixelelayo? Kuba ndibona abantu bepikele ukuzibulala ngotywala
babelungu nango Mqomboti. Bekwenziwe umteto apa e Bloemfontein ukuba
zivalwe izindlu zotywala, bangabufumani abamnyama. Into engummangaliso
ungababona benxilile ungazi ukuba babufumana pina. Kaloku uti wakubuza
kutiwe bangena ngasemva. Asikukona ukufuna ukufa oko ?

Enye into sisitampo endingati mna ku danisa, lento yenziwa ngama-
ntombazana nabafana ebusuku, lentoke imbi ayilufanele ulutsha Iwase
sikolweni. Ezinto zonke kukutanda ukufa, ukufa ngakuye u Tixo.
Malutandazelwe uhlanga Iwakowetu ngala matuba nangezinve izonakalo
ezininzi ezibonakalayo. Ungadinwa nkosi Mhleli ndifuna ukuteta
ngentombazana ete yakuba igqoboka kwatiwa iyafa yabizelwa amagqira,
bunjalo ubumnyama. Kwatiwa inerabia, eso sisifo ekutiwa sivela
ngokwenziwa ngabanye. Kodwa yona ayivumanga, yoyisa ngerele lakwa Tixo,
yabaxelela ukuba ililiswa kukuziva ukuba ingumoni ine tyala lezono.
Mandingagqibi ndawo, sala nkosi undixolele.

  GERT MBONGWE. Bloemfontein, March 13, 1884.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 3, 1871.

U-JEREMIAH WOODLAND

Uyakutengisa into eninzi yemfuyo eskiti sase Cradock
ngo Mgqibelo ngolwe 10 ku March.

Angati nangoku oye nemiti yenqwelo, nokuba ziplanga,
nokuba ngamazimba, nokuba ngumbona,

Afike kuko iqela lemazi zenkomo ezintle kunene namahashekazi.

Ayakuti ke ukuba akahlange ananiswe otengiswa
nawo ngolwe 18 ku Murch.

Sekuhleli kuko iqela lempahla zeskiti ezilinde
ukutengiswa ngalomini.

U-J. MILLS,

Umhlanganiseli wabanamatyala,

Nemali uyayibuta nemicimbi yabantsundu
uyayiquba ezindlwini zamatyala;

Ezise DIKENI nase SINQENQENI
nase MPOFU.

EBIZAKE INANI ELINGEPI.

  J. MCGILVRAY,

  WAGONS, CARTS, AND SPIDERS,
  PAINTED AND TRIMMED.

EDIKENI NASE-MKUBISO,

U-J. B. TEMLETT,

  Uhlala enempahla ezitengisayo ezilungi-
  leyo, ezinje ngentlobontlobo zengubo;

  iswekile nekofu; intlobontlobo zekomityi
  nepleti; ipuluwa, nepeki, nentlobontlobo.
  zezitya. Ingafunyanwa nenkoliso yezinto
  ezifanele indlu.

  Izivato zokutshata nezinye intlobo zezi
  vato. Nazo zonke intlobo zezilimo zinga
  tengwa.

  Kwanamayezana okupilisa zonke izifo,
  nabo bonke ubulwelwe; ukuba alikdbi lixa
  lokuba umntu afe.

U-A. T. GOODCHILD,
Ngumteteleli wamatyala entlobo
zonke E-DIKENI

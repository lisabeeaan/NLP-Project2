  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1872.

U-DR. LIVINGSTONE kuvakala ukuba usapilile
E-Ujiji. Ufunyenwe ngu Mr. Stanley wase Ame-
rica owayepumele ukuya kumfuna. Ngoku ba-
hleli ndawonye.

E-BEAUFORT WEST izindlu zamadoda antsundu
amabini. U-Carl Christian no Japhtha Karl ziqe-
kezwe ngamasela, kweka Christian kwebiwa £84,
kweka Karl kwebiwa £36. Akukabiko mntu
ubanjiweyo ngenxa yoko.

PESHEYA KWE NCIBA, kuvakala ukuba lomda
womhlaba bebemana ukulwa ngawo U-Ngangeli-
zwe no Matanzima ngoku ulungisiwe. kwintlanga-
niso ebezikuyo ezonkosi zombini, kunye no Mr.
James Ayliff no Mr. Grant.

ABAXONYIWEYO.—E-Natal kwitolongo yase
Durban, kusand’ ukuxonywa Amaxosa amabini
ngenxa yokubulala. Bate besiya kuxonywa ba-
bengati abakatali; wati omnye wabo—ukuba kwa-
kuko abanye bokuba ndibabulale ndandiya kuba-
bulala.

UKUTSHA KWAMAHASHE.—Kwele Diamondi
ate Amaxosa amabini atshisa isitali sika Mr. Otto,
kwatsha namahashe ake amahle amabini. Loma-
xosa ngoku asetolongweni, ati ebenxilile, ukuye-
nza kwawo lonto.

OZIBULELEYO.—E-Dikeni, Um-Irish otile abati
ngu James Glancy, obesebenza ku Mr. McBrian,
uzibulele ngokuzidubula nge pistoli yenkosi yake.
Ufe kwaoko. Isizatu sokuzibulala kwake asika-
zeki kakuhle.

OHLATYWE NGOMKONTO.—U-Mr J. R. Thom-
son, okangela isikolo sokufundisa ama Xosa E-Lu-
jilo kwesika Kona unyana ka Maqoma, uhlatywe
ngomkonto. Ubete weva inxokozelo endlwini
ngokuhlwa, wapuma epete intonga ukuya kuka-
ngela ukuba kutenina. Ufikeke kuko amadoda
amane enxamele ukungena, ate akumbona abaleka;
wawasukela ; ati akuqonda ukuba akanampu agu-
qula, enye yambeta uduma entloko. Ubalekile aku
bona ukuba aza kumti ngunga onke, wati xa ange-
nayo endlwini yamhlaba ngomkonto emqolo;
abaleka ke emva koko. Zisalingwa indlela zokuba
abanjwe. Kulindelekile ukuba no Kona ancedise
kuba into ihla ekaya kuye, ke ngoko umkondo
usemnyango wendlu yake.

U-Rev. MR. LESLIE wakwa Mankazana. unyu-
lelwe ukuba ayekuba ngumfundisi kwa Rili enda-
weni ka Mr. Soga.

UMBONISO WEKOTON E-Qonce uyakuba ngom-
hla we 9 ka August. Imali eyakucitelwa emaba-
sweni iyakuba zi £100.

IGOLIDE.—Kuvakala ukuba baninzi nakwele
Diamondi abaya kwele Golide, ngasentla kweli-
xwe eliyi Transvaal.

I-DIAMONDI.—E-Bayi, ngomhla we 10 ka June
kwakuziswe entengweni idiamondi ezi 3,532.
Ezaba nokutengiswa zaba 3,348.

UKUBUBA KUKA SIR WALTER CURRIE.—U-Sir
W. Currie obengumlawuli wama Polisa ubube
E-Oatland ngomhla we 11 ka June. Ubudala
ubeminyaka ima 54.

UMTIRARA owayete kwinyanga ezi 18 ezidlu-
leyo, waqekeza itolongo yase Bedford, wabaleka,
ubuye wabanjwa. Ngoku usetolongweni yase
Qonce. Ufunyenwe eneqela le diamondi.

OZIDLISILEYO.—Intombazana ebudala bumi-
nyaka ili 12, ka Jacobus de Wet wase Cradock,
isand’ ukuzibulala ngokusuke itabate iyeza elingo-
zi, inkulu ekutiwa yi strychnine.

ILAHLEKELO ENKULU.—Kule mvula ibisand’
ukubako U-Mr. John Atherstone wangase Rini,
upulukene nenciniba esezinkulu ezili 12. E-Coles-
berg bekusand’ ukutiwa iyakuti inye irolelwe
iponti ezi £40.

OBULEWEYO.—Umzimba we Mfengu etile ufu-
nyenwe ngase Zwart Ruggens, ekubonakalayo
ukuba ibulewe ngumntu. Kuko Ummantatee
osetolongweni ngenxa yoku’ranelwa.

INDLEKO ZEMVULA.—Pesheya kwe Nciba uku-
dla kutshabalele kakulu yilemvula ibisand’ uku-
bako. Siva ukuba nakwezinye indawo kunjalo.
Kuko abemkelwe zizita zombona nabemkelwe
zezamazimba. Ngase Queenstown kubonwe umzi-
mba womlungu usihla nomlambo ; nangoku awu-
kafunyanwa.

AMANDLA OVALO !—Umntu otile obeselemi-
nyaka ili 16 enetyala le 3s. 2d. kumhlobo wake
ose England, usand’ ukuya kwiposi engase Qonce
eyekufuna indlela zokuba elotyala lihlaulwe. Uti
ubengasacingi nokucinga ngalo, koko kute ngabu-
suku butile wapupa lomntu anetyala kuye emso-
ngela ngokuti uyakuhlelwa bububi ukuba akali-
hlauli.

UMBONISO WE KOTON.—E-Rini kuya kubako
umboniso we koton ngomhla wa 31 ka July.
Amabaso ayakunikwa ayakuma ngoluhlobo—Elo-
kuqala loba £25 ; linikwe okoton ingengapantsi
kwe ponti ezi 1,000 lbs. xa icokisiweyo, iti ukuba
ayikacokiswa ingabi ngapantsi kwe 3,000 lbs.
Elesibini loba £15, linikwe okoton ingengapantsi
kwe 750 lbs. xa icokisiweyo, iti ukuba ayikacokiswa
ingabi ngapantsi kwe 2,250 lbs. Elesitatu loba
£10, linikwe okoton ingengapantsi kwe 500 lbs.
xa icokisiweyo, iti ukuba ayikacokiswa ingabi nga-
pantsi kwe 1,500 lbs.

IZIQAMO ZEPEPA LENDABA.—Inkosana etile
yama Mfengu Pesheya kwe Nciba ebingabanga
namatuba okufunda ebuncinaneni bayo, yasitaba-
ta Isigidimi Samaxosa ingakwazi njalo ukufunda.
Yaqala ngokufunda amagama, yada yakwazi uku-
lesesha amazwi amafutshane, nokulesesha indaba
zezinto ngezinto. Yaba nokunxama ngakumbi
kwakuvela amapepa ayeteta ngemfazwe ya peshe-
ya kolwandle, yada yaba nokuzixela kwabanye
ezondaba izifundileyo incokole ngazo. Oku
kuveliswe kukuti umntu omiswe yi Govemmente
kona abanyamekele abantu abantsundu.

INGWE EZIMBINI zibulewe ngase Mgqwakwebe ngumfo ontsundu, ute wazitiyela
ngompu ngase- nkabini yenkomo ebifile.

INCINIBA.—Inciniba ezisitoba, ezibe zinyanga ntatu ubudala zite
ukutengwa kwazo, inye yazi £12.

INGOZI EMBI.—E-Bayi ute umfo ongu Ward ukuvela kwake emsebenzini weza
enxilile. Ute esamana ukoja inyama ingubo yake yalumekeka emlilweni
akabi namntu umncedayo wada umlilo weza kuma ngesifuba. Abanye abantu
abenabo bebekwanxilile. Ngoku usegqireni lomlungu.

INTENGO YEZINTO EMARKENI,
NGECAWA EDLULEYO.

itapile 5s., ihabi- le 4s. 1d. irasi 5s. 10d. ; iflara yenkuni 37s.

E-CRADOCK. — Nge 100lbs;—Umbona 10s. 6d. umgubo 15s. 5d., itapile 5s.
3d., ihabile 5s. iflara yenkuni 43s.

E-SOMERSET EAST.—Nge 100lb :—umbona 8s. umgubo 11s. 3d., itapile 7s.
6d., irasi 5s. ; ihabile £2. nge 100 lezitungu.

E-RINI.—Nge 100ft ;—Umbona 12s., umgubo 12s. amazimba 10s., ingqolowa
8s. irasi 9s. ihabile 11s.; iflara yenkuni 60s.

  UMVUZO WE £10.

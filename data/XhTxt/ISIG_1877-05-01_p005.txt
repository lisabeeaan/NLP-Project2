HADI-WASELUHLANGENI.

Fort Beaufort, March 15,1877.

NKOSI—Mhleli we Sigidimi ndingatanda uke undi- fakele lamazwana ambalwa.
Namhlanje kube kuko umsebenzi, wokubekwa izandla pezu kwabo sebefu- mene
upau lwe Nkosi; ngu Mongameli u-Bishop wehlelo le Church yase England.
Umsebenzi ubenemini emnandi kwangena kuqala, umtendeleko, wa- wuqutywa
ngu Rev. Mr. Turpin wase St. Philips e- Rini, oyena uyi Chaplain ka
Bishop encediswa ngumfundisi walapa, U-Bishop ingomnye wabamkelia
mtendeleko. Abebesamkela esosidlo singcwele bakumashumi amabini
ngapandle ko Bishop no Rev. Turpin nomfundisi walapa.

Sakuba isidlo esingcwele sigqityiwe kupunywe ixe- shana elifutshane
yabeta intsimbi yomsebenzi woku­bekwa izandla, ngeli ixesha u-Bishop
wangena kwezo- bukosi bake ingubo. Ukuya etyalikeni wakokela umfundisi
walapa, elandelwa ngu Rev. Turpin emva kwake ngu Bishop. Kuhanjiswe
iculo. Lifeziwe, u-Mnyakama uleseshe indawo yokuqala emisiweyo
yombedesho wokubekwa izandla. Kwalandela ngokulesesha u-Rev. Turpin
indawo nentandazwana ezimisiweyo, u-Bishop ezipinda ngesi ngesi.

U-Bishop uhambise amazwi amnandi, ancomekayo navisisekayo kubo bonke
abebeko, ngakumbi nanga- kumbi kwabanamhla bazuza isipiwo somoya Oyi-
ngcwele; ingu Rev. Turpin obekumshela. Umsebenzi wonke uhambiseke
ngokuncomekayo, esitemba ukuba bonke ababeko, nabo bebeze kubekwa
izandla, bohla- la bewakumbula amazwi, nembekisiso abe singiselwa kuyo
ngu Bishop. Namhla abebesamkela lomsebenzi bebe lishumi linesibini;
yanga inkosi ingamane iwu- sikelela umsebenzi wayo ngezicaka
zayo—kwindawo ngendawo. Liti igama layo libe nokunqweneleka ngapezu kwe
golide ne silivere. Siti isandla sayo sibe pezu kwesicakakazi sayo,
u-Victoria ote ngokuxasa izwi layo, sabafumana abafundisi
abasishumayezileyo indlela zoxolo nosindiso olungunapakade. Emva
komsebenzi u-Mongameli u-Bishop ute wawuhambahamba umzi, wawubuka,
waboniswa nendawo eluncedo olukulu kakulu yamanzi, esandu ukwenziwa emva
kwendlu yetyalike esibulela kakulu ngokukankanya o-Jesse Shaw Esq. wase
Beaufort, no Rev. W. Turpin umtyeleli wasekayapa abasincedisileyo ngawo
lomsebenzi. Ipelekelelwe Ibishop yayakufiki- swa e-Fort Beaufort.

Fort Beaufort, March 16, 1877.

Nkosi yam Mhleli we Sigidimi Sama Xosa. Kau ndinyamezele ngokundifakela
lamazwana. Ingxoxo inkulu ngeli gama lokuba “ Tixo.” Nditi ngalo
masingazikatazi tina lutsha lwakwaxosa ; ngokuba alisayikuze liyekwe:
liyaziwa ukuba ligama esibiza ngalo lowo usidalileyo. Enye into
masingakangeli ngokunyukanyukana nelavela kubo kuqala. Sifumana izizwe
ezifundileyo, ukuba kuyinto enkulu ukulanda ingcambu yegama nokuba
lilizwi; kungekuko ukucukuceza into ebelifudula liyiteta. Ke eligama
lokuba “Tixo” nditi namhla kuti alinakuyekwa, kuba omdala nomncinane,
ekunquleni, nase kuteteni ungafika lileli gama “ Tixo” aziwa ngalo
Umdali. Ewe abafundileyo bangamazi ngamanye. Into enokude ince­de
kwindawo ezinjengezi, yintlanganiso, yamadoda afundileyo, ndawonye
nalangazelela ukwazi, ibeko iminyaka yonke : kube kuqutyelwa, kwincwadi
yesi- xosa esicacileyo, ukuze ibe lilifalikafa lenteto yesixosa
kwizizukulwana ngezizukulwana.

WODELUKOLWA.

Rabula Mission Station, April 26, 1877.

NKOSI YAM.—Ndifuna ukufakelwa amazana amba­lwa, malungana nenteto
yomnumzana otile ka July 1876. Igama lake nditi mna Ngutetinene,
lomzalwa- na uteta ngokupambana kwabafundisi, into okunene ebangela
ukudeleka, nokutyafa komsebenzi ka Tixo. Esigidimini apa nimana niteta
ngekazi, nisiti, kutengisa abanye, kuqeshisa, abanye sisono. Qondake apo
impambano ihla kona, makazeke unantsi kwimvaba etile mhlayimbi
engalobolisiyo, lo wetile yena makalobolise, umloboli makutiwe unetyala
kowabo, ade akutshwe nase rementeni, aze umlobolisi angabinalo
kweyakowabo, asiyompambano na leyo ? Umfu­ndisi wasekutini makafike
ekutini kubaheyideni, ashumayele ukufa nokuvuka kwenkosi makeve
owasekutini. Yekake ukulwa kwake! unantsi lo asingowakowetu, musani
ukwamkela onantsi aba ngamanantsi; asingawo manantsi, imfundiso yabo
ayifani neyetu tina manantsi. Yintoninake leyo? Ngamhla otile wecawa
ndake ndakushumayela kumaqaba ase- kutini, kute ndakugqiba abuza ukuti
aba Tixo banga- pina ? ndite nitsho nganina ? ate sitsho kuba lemihla
simelwe ngamanantsi ati manokuti nakufika apa niza kushumayela
sinigxote, batshoke bati kum kanti ke tina asibafuni kanye, sifuna nina
tina, ngokoke tina ngoku sidiniwe yilento, asisayazi neyona iyiyo.
Ndi­te ke mna ukuwapendula, makowetu nina ninetamsanqa elikulu, kuba
nipakelwa izitya zegospel kwezindlwana zonke, musani ukugxota mntu
unizisela ezindaba, basahleli ekufeni, yena u-Tixo mnye kwapela, qondani
lonto yodwa.

Tina zizwe zintsundu sonke ibifudula yonke into engumfundisi iyeyetu,
kungeko ongumnantsi. Namhla ke akunjalo. Omnye umfundisi ngawo lonyaka
udlu- leyo wapantsukufa zingqele egxotwa ngemvula, izele nemilambo,
wagxotwa ngabantu kuba engowasekutini, imvaba ekukuti ke leyo, engeyiyo
etile. Yintonina ke leyo?

Utsho ati ke lomfo makowetu, tina luhlanga sonke masingayingeni lento.
Nam ndiqokela kwelitshoyo. Masingapambani, singama Kristu nje namhla
singum- kosi wankosinye, singamatwalandwe apezulu, simelwe sonke
kukufunzela landwe yasempumeni ngamxelo mnye.

Masibambane ngengalo njengabantu abazalelwa ngumlamba bona banikana
amandla ngokubambana. Nguduntsuduntsu wanina lo makowetu nakwelipepa
lendaba, sipina isidima seyonanto nilinga ukugxota ngayo abagondozimayo,
lamatunzokuhlwa aseyintshe- la zobuheyideni kuni. Xanditshoyo ke
makowetu nditi masazane, sibekane sivane nakwesi Sigidimi,

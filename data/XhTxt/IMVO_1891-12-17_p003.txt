Extra to “Imvo Zabantsundu,” December 17th, 1891.

Pilisa

KA Bangalisa

  HOLMES-ROBERTS

Umciza Omkulu wase Afrika.

PILISA : Lelona liyintloko, ekucokiseni igazi.

MANGALISA : Upilisa isifuba, nokuba Sesinjanina.

  UKUBEKA pambi komzi lemiciza ikankanywa ngasentla, abenzi bayo, banya-
  nisekile, okokuba yolungela indidi zonke zabantu ukuba izincede,
  kwezizifo zikankanyiweyo. Yinto eyazeka kunene, kubo bonke abemi base
  Afrika, ukuba eli- lizwe linengcambu,, ekungekuko ukungxengelela
  ukuti, zingcambu ezingummanga- liso. Ukulunga kwazo kwaziwa yinxenye
  kwabantsundu, kuxa kupela, ubulumko, nengqondo yamangesi bufikelele
  ekubeni buwazi, ukuze kuzaliseke ukulunga noku- pilisa kwazo.

  Abaninzi kuwenza bafuna ukwazisa ukuba lamayeza enziwa ngengcambu zase
  Afrika, nakuba, kokokuqala ebikwa pambi komzi, seleneminyaka
  esetyenziswa, epilisa ubulwelwe, obungangeminyaka elishumi.

  Kungokutenjwa kwawo lento abekwa pambi komzi, ongaba unobobulwelwe
  bukankanyiweyo.

  Sekuyiminyaka emininzi ete lemiciza, yalingwa, yasetyenziswa pantsi,
  ko- kongameli, kwa-Magqira, Okuninzi okutetwayo ngawo kube Sekuqondeni
  kwa Magqira lawo.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 1, 1887. 77

IMIPANGA.

UKUBUBA KOMKA NTIBANE MZIMBA.

E-Regu ngo-Mvulo ngomhla we 13 ku September kulonyaka, pakati kwe yure
yesitandatu neye sixenxe kwakutshona ilanga, kubube u Leah Silokazana
intombi ka Dube, obendele ku Ntibane Mzimba. Akagulanga lomntu. Kuse nge
Cawa edumbe iqeba necala lomqala, kwapangwa emayezeni kwaoko ngunyana
wake opakati. Lite litshona wabe umkuhlane selukulile. Akwalalwa, kwasa
umntu seledumbe wonke umqala, ukufa kumandundu. Kwatsitywa kwelomlungu
igqira, lamkangela lamva, lashiya awalo amayeza, kanti ke akusokunceda
nto.

Ebeyintombi yesibini kokwabo ku Dube kwindlu yase kunene, unyana emnye,
intombi zintlanu zizonke. Abafazi ku Dube bebebane. U Dube uzalwa ngu
Gwebu unyana ka Dlomo inkosi yase Mahlubini. Lomfikazi uzelwe malunga
nemfazwe ka Hintsa nge 1835. Bate abazali bayakukonza emlungwini, nje
ngenkoliso yama Mfengu eyangena emlungwini mhla yemka kwa Hintsa.
Basebenza e Somerset, waza lo Leah xa ayintombazana elula wapata
abantwana bomlungu apo emane ekapa intombazana yomlungukazi wake xa iya
esikuleni aze yena amane efundiswa ngumlungukazi ngokuhlwa. Ude ke waba
nako
ukulesa incwadi ya Maxosa, waba nokusiva isingesi kancinane, sati sona
isi Bulu waba nako ukusiteta. Ute wenda emncinane xa akwi shumi
elinesixenxe iminyaka kutelekelelwa. Waye uyise enenkomo kakulu. Wendela
e Sheshegu kunyana ka Mzimba, u Ntibane, ikupela kwake kuyise; wendela
kumzi okwa mkulu nangenkomo nebokwe. Kwatshatwa ngokuzidla ngamacala
omabini, Iwalukulu uduli, wamkulu nomdudo. Waye ungumzi wembola kubomvu
nenkuko, nentsika, nezitya. Yati inkosikazi yake mhla wenda lo Leah
yambulisa ngencwadi, yamnika i Bible
ya Mangesi, wahamba nazo naye incwadana zake nesipeli neculo. Kute
kwakuhamba inyanga ezitile lomntu endile, yasuka indoda yake yakubona
ezincwadi yanqwenela ukufunda. Woyika umfazi ukumfundisa ekuhleni kuba
yena ungumtshakazi kusemzini. Wamane emfundisa ngokuhlwa ngomlilo
wamaceba omtati, emfundisa isipeli sama-Xosa. Ute akusazi wamfundisa
eyamaculo. Ite indoda kwakuba semaculweni yangenwa lilizwi, yancoma
ukuba mnandi kwamaculo. Ute umfazi kuko engapezulu kwale yamaculo
ngokubamnandi uze uke uzamele ukufumana yona, leyo yi Testamente.

Ide yafunyanwa i Testamente e Nxikwebe ngokufunwa yindoda, yati
yakuyifunda, yaselitanda ukuti ngomhla we Sabata isuke e Sheshegu iye
kuyingena icawa e Nxikwebe. Kwehla imfazwe ka Mlanjeni, ati ama Mfengu
nabo bonke abangecala lika Rulumente, basabela e Dikeni bangqonga Iona.
Eloxa ke akukabiko mntu uti uyakolwa kwabobantu bobabini. Edikeni
bafumene intshumayelo zabafundisi, bangena ityalike, bazinikela
elizwini, kunye nabanye ababeze ngentsabo Edikeni apa.

Lentokazi ka Dube ke langena ngalomfundiso yayo kulomzi wakwa Mzimba,
ngokufundisa indoda yayo. Yaza londoda yalinyamekela kakulu. Ibisuka
indoda leyo e Sheshegu ngolwe Sihlanu iveki ngeveki, ize kufunda e
Lovedale kubafundisi. Kantike awona mandla iyakuwabonakalalisa okutanda
imfundo kubantwana. Abantu abasekoyo awabaptizwa nabo mhleni wangeniswa
eramenteni ngu Ntlonga Mtshemla ongomdala Emxelo, kwibandla lase
Lovedale, no Tsewu Konongo ongomdala kwibandla lase Burnshill, no
Christian Ngqika, ose ofisini Enyara kumadoda. Kumankazana ngu Lumka C.
Ngqika.
Kwabangasekoyo ngu N. Mzimba indoda yake, ngu Filita Bokwe, no Mityi
Mpinda. Ngalomini kanjalo kwa baptizwa abantwana babo ababini. Owama
zibulo u Rev. P. J. Mzimba, nosana olwalekela lowo, intombi eyendele ku
Mr. J. Msikinya ongumfundisi e Gqili. Esingati ke iminyaka imashumi
matatu elilungu leramente kwi Free Church. Ibiyintokazi enceda indoda
yayo kuyo yonke imisebenzi ngokuzolileyo. Etetisa abantwana babo ukuba
bamhlonele bamve uyise. Kuzo zonke indawo abate bahambe bebakuzo,
ibingumbuki wendwendwe, umncedi wolusizana nosweleyo. Eyada indoda
yakwa-Gcaleka (pesheya kwe Nciba) yambulela ngamazwi ati—
“ Wanga u Tixo okunika lentliziyo intle kangaka inenceba komaziyo
nakongamaziyo onje ngam nje, umfo wesinye isizwe, angakutamsanqela, iti
inzalo yako isikeleleke, uraulwe ngamatamsanqa apo ukona imihla yonke,
amashwa akwelele.” Watsho eliqaba, wanga uteta iziprofite-

sho. Lomntu ubetanda abantu bonke, naye enjalo ebantwini.
Ubengenalucalulo, Iwantlanga. Ubuninzi babantu bebude bungawuqondi lomzi
wakwa Mzimba ukuba ngama-Xosa ngama-Mfengu sinina, kuba zonke ezontlanga
ubungafika zonwabe kona, yaye nenteto kungeko nomkwa lo wokuba asingawo
ma-Xosa. Ubezele onyana abasixenxe, baseko abahlanu, ababini abaseko,
babube bebancinane. Intombi bezintlanu, zintatu ezisekoyo, ezimbini
aziseko.

Ushiye intsizana zabazukulwana abebesondliwa nguye, into leyo
eyongezelele usizi ngakumbi kusapo nakwizihlobo abezondlela.

Incwaba lake laba likulu. Ayeko amaqaba kanobom ebonakala ukuba alusizi
kunene kukupulukwa ngumhlobo abevana naye kangaka, nangumntu obesisibane
esikufupi kakulu kuwo, ebesiwakanyisela ngemikwa nenteto zake, naxa
ateta nawo ngelizwi lika Tixo. Kwabako neqela lempi yakwa Zibi, nelakwa
Gaga, nelase Sheshegu, kwa nabase Dikeni.

Encwabeni kutete umfundisi wase Rwarwa u Mr. Ma- kiwane. Pakati kwamazwi
amakulu awatete xa abekisa ko- nyana wati—“ Nina bakwa Mzimba nishiywe
netamsanqa elikulu nelifa elikulu. Anditeti elenkomo nomhlaba, ezonto
ndiyazibona kodwa zincinane, nditeta ilifa lobukumkani buka Tixo
,elishiywe nani, ngeziyalo ezihle, ngomzekelo wesimilo esihle kubazali
benu. Ize ke elolifa likulu ka ngaka niligcine.” Kutandaze u Mr. Stuart
umfundisi wase Mkubiso ngamazwi amnandi kakulu okubopa intliziyo
ezopayo. Wababika ngamagama ngabanye onyana kwindawo abakuzo pambi ko
Mdali wezulu nomhlaba ; wazitandazela ngazinye intombi kwindawo ezikuzo.
Esiti— Zanga indaba zalompanga zingezotusi zizipapatekise intliziyo
zezontombi.

“ Banoyolo abafi, abo bafela e Nkosini kutabatela apa ; ewe, utsho u
Moya ukuba bapumle ekubulalekeni kwabo.”

  Ishiye intsizi intombi ka Dube, Kubantwana bayo, nakwizizalwane;
  Kubantu ngabantu, bendawo ngendawo, Kwibandla lakwabo,
  namany’amahlelo.

  Lomnt’ubesazeka, kwintlang’ezininzi. Lomnt’ubebekiwe zindidi ngendidi.
  Kulila iqaba, kulila gqoboka, Kulila ma Mfengu, kulila nom-Xosa.

  Kub’ukuba njalo ubezizwe zonke. Elunge kuhlanga, olo Iwama Kristu
  Olungenam-Xosa, olungena Mfengu,— Lusingele kulo, intlangana zonke.

  Zonk’ezo ziganga, zifelwe ngunina, Unina we ndlodlo, wazo indla-mhlaba
  Wazondla ngelizwi, nangaw’umtandazo Wondla ngeziyalo ze zwi lo
  Mongamo.

  Taruni zihlobo, taruni lusapo Sulan’inyembezi, uhleli unyoko.
  Ukumbul’ekaya, ap’akon’u Yesu INKOSI yoxolo ay’nyule kwa mzuzu.

  Way’nyula esemtsha, way’nyula es’emhle. Umhlab’usenguwo,
  umhlab’uyolile.

  Wabe ngumntwan’omhle, enguma tebenqa. Wenda kwa Radebe, kunyana ka
  Mzimba.

Ushiye inkwitshi ngemfundiso zake-

Ze zwi lika Tixo kusap’aluzele.

  Taruni! ma-Dlomo, nani ma-Radebe Intombi ka Dube, ishiye yenzile.

  ANNIE SPELMAN.

Kute kwasekutwaseni kuka September kwabuba u Annie Spelman, intombi yase
Zinqayi, yeramente ka Rev. P. J. Mzimba. Lentombi ibisifa ixesha elitile
yaye noko izibika isazihambela. Kute kwakuquba ixesha, yalala pantsi
kaloku yasindeka emzimbeni, nakuba empefumlweni yahlala ibambelele ku
Msindisi wayo eyamnyula kwange-mini zokupila kwayo. Yancwatywa
ngumfundisi wayo u Rev. P. J. Mzimba. Lontombi ib’itomalalisile kunene
eburamenteni bayo yaye iluvuyo Iwabazali bayo. Ishiye usizi
nokukumbuleka kubo nakwiramente yakowayo, kuba kubazali ib’iluncedo
olukulu ngendlela ezininzi. Lusizi olukulu kulomzi wakowayo nangenxa
yokuba
ibingumntwana wokuqala wamazibulo kubazali.

Lento siwukhetayo owakwa Rulumeni umbuso ukuba siwupikamise ngelituba
senziwa kukuba sinomnye umbuso esi- wujongileyo, onqubo, mayelana
nabebala, singade sigqibe ukuyiqonda. Siteta owemfundo. Imfundo kukudala
nje yaqalwayo pakati kwetu kuseko indawo ezinjenge Lovedale kwizikolo
zetu ezi- kulu ezibona ukuba abebala, nakwaku­beni zikade zifundisa,
abakafanelwa zindawo zokuba bade babe zititshala kuzo. Ibilixesha
emkumbulweni wetu ukuba namhla kube sekuko amadodana avileyo amatatu
namane azinkonde apa e Lovedale efundisa udodana Iwakowawo, ukuze afike
u Smiti kwindawo ezipambili eziqukumbela imfundo egqiba. Kude kubeyile
mini yanamhla nje akubona- kali kuko nenye indodana eseyike yada
yatenjwa ngangokuba inikelwe isihla- lo ekupatoni ulutsha olufundayo
kulowo mzi wakowetu omkulu wemfundo. Sebe- baninzi esibeva bebuza ukuba
lento ibangwa kukuba bengeko na asebefunde ngokwanelele ukuba batabate
ezindawo; nokuba kucekiswa kwebala?

Kanti-ke into le iyaligqiba ukulihleba ibala kumzi ongapandle.

KIMBERLEY.

(NGU DAYIMANI.)

ITIMITI YOMBULISO.—Umhla we 21 December, 1891 (ngokuhlwa) awusokube- hle
ulityalwe ngabebeko kulembuto imnandi, ebeyenziwe e Malay Camp lusapo
Iwesikolo, luyenzela ititshala etandwayo nebekwe kunene kulomzi, u Mr.
Philip J. Mayeza. Isizekabani soku u Mr. Mayeza utabata iholide yenyanga
ezintatu ukutyelela kwelakowabo, e Ma- mpondweni. Indlu yama Wesile
izele ngokute yala ngabantu, isihlalo sikwelwe ngu Rev. J. Conjwa; zaye
izinto zokuyo- lisa umhla: iluluhlu Iwexilihlelo zosapo Iwesikolo
(ezipumelele ngokuncomeka kakulu), uqolwane ne keki, izizipitshi nokutya
kwendlebe—ingoma ye choir ne yosapo. Ncama, akuko tuba lakuchaza inteto
eziwileyo ukuncoma isimilo, into- beko, ewe, nokuwuquba kakuhle
umse­benzi wake u Mr. Mayeza. Kumanene azandla zikululekileyo ukuya
engxoweni kulomcimbi kuvele u Mr. W. Rubushe (owaziwa ngokuba yi
domkrara kuma- hlelo) wenza into zamehlo; kwaza ezi- ntetweni kwasamela
into ka Mazamisa, u Joseph, otsho ngenteto engqakamba ecubhule
intlanganiso ngentsini; yati into engcokocileyo kwezinye yakukusi- lela
kwe choir ekwenzeni imfanelo yayo nayo kumpati wayo ngalomhla nakuba ite
“ better late than never,” yowulande- lisa owayo umbuliso—sobona ke. U
Mr. no Mrs. Mayeza banduluke izolo (22nd inst.) emva kwedinala ukusinga
e Bhai, zaye zipitizela ngokwenyosi izihlobo zake estishi ukubulisa
nokubona ukumka kwe “ ntanda ” ipelekwa yiminqwenelelo eshushu
yehambo-mnandi-ntle nenkosi- kazi yake ngabo bonke abamaziyo. Aba- nye
amalinga abo amahle ababhatala kwabesahamba ngenyawo zombini!

NGOLWESI-TATU, JUNE 1, 1887.

IMBALI YABAFUNDI SASE LOVEDALE.

KUKO incwadi eshicilelweyo e Lovedale Institution, epete amagama abo
bonke abake bako apa, nabasekoyo, kuseloko savulwayo nge 1841 esi sikula
sikulu sase Dikeni. Ishwa- nkatele yonk’ into naba ngamankazana
abafundiswa apa. Ipete ukwalatisa wonke ubani imisebenzi ebipetwe ngabo
bantu—abangasekoyo, nabasayipeteyo abasekoyo—nendawo ababekuzo nabase
kuzo ngoku. Ikumbuza nabase belibalekile emixelweni yabaninzi, ibonisa
nomqulu womsebenzi owenziweyo siso esi sikolo. Ibonisa ukuba lilonke
inani elake lafundiswa apa, nelisafundayo, ngamawaka matatu, anamakulu
akwa matutu, anomvo osisibozo (3,308). Kweli nani amawaka amabini
(2,000) ngumzi ontsundu.

Kumzuzu yaqalwayo ukulungiswa lencwadi, kodwa isizatu sokuba ikutshwe
ngoku yingozi ecopele umzi ontsundu ngelinga elenziwayo ukuncitshiswa
kwemali yokunceda imfundo yabantsundu kuba kutiwa—“ Abayisebenzisi
imfundo, bati bakupuma ezi Seminareni babuyele ekuqabeni imbola nezinye
impau zobu heyideni; ngoko mayincitshiswe imali ngu Rulumente encedisa
ukufundisa bona.” Kula mawakamabini, sinovuyo ukuti abafumaneke ukuba
okwenene bayaqaba batandatu, noko kute ngesizatu sokuba hleze mhlaumbi
kube kuko abangaziwayo kubalwe ishumi elinesihlanu inani laba buyele
kwase buhedenini. Ubuninzi busemashishinini neminye imisebenzi enoncedo.
Lomanani sowacwangcisa kwesizayo Isigidimi.

UBOMI NO LAULO LUKA VITOLIYA
UKUMKANIKAZI.

UBUNTWANA BAKE.

KWIMINYAKA emashumi matandatu anesibozo edluleyo, kwazalwa usana e Botwe
lase Kensington, ekute ngeloxa lati ilizwe alwalucingela nto kakulu,
nakuba intliziyo zoyise nonina zazigcoba zona kuba zazi nobubele. Uyise
waye yi DUKE yase KENT, ingunyana wesine wo Kumkani owaye laula
ngeloxesha u GEORGE we III. Indlu yama Tshawe yona yayi vuyisiwe
kukuzalwa kwalo mntwana, waye uyis’omkulu u Kumkani enga kungatiwa ngu
GEORGIANA igama lake. Uyise yena wayenga kungatiwa ngu ELIZABETH, kuba
ib’iligama elitandeka kunene ku Mangesi; kodwa uyisekazi obeyakubamba
yakufa inkosi, eb’ekutiwa yena yi PRINCE REGENT, wapikela ukuba kutiwa
ngu ALEXANDRINA, abe ukwenjiwa njalo efuziswa i Emperor yase Russia.
Igama lokuba “ VITOLIYA” lelika nina, kwabonakala ukuba elo gama
liyanda, nokuba londawo ib’ise- semixelweni. Kodwa kuke kwema eli lokuba
ngu DRINA (umteketiso) ngalo lonke ixa lobuncinane bake. Nguye

owaqalayo ukuqatshulelwa ingqakaqa kulondlu yama Tshawe, ekute emva
koko, wasiwa e Sidmouth ngunina noyise, elunxwemeni Iwase Devon. Kulapo
Iwasinda cebetshu olosana. Kute ndodana itile iye kuzingela intaka yati
ngokudubula ngapandle kwentelekelelo suke uhlwaya Iwangena ezifestileni
zalondlu yakulo “ DRINA,” zegqita kufupi entloko yake, epetwe ngumondli
wake. Kwakufupi emva koko, uyise wayeye kuwaka wabuya enete kunene, wati
engeka zikululi nezongubo zimanzi, akakatsho ukuya kunxiba ezinye
engaqalanga ngokuya kukangela ukuba u DRINA usapile kakuhle na. Kweloxa
way’esel’eminyakaisibozo ubudala, esel’ emazi uyise akuba embona.
Wayonwabile waza uyise wahlala umzuzwana, kanti sel’engenwe kakulu
yingqele awabuba ngenxa yayo. Zati kanti ingubo zikululwe sekusemva.
Wangenwa yingqele enamandla, yabusela nasemipungeni, waza u DRINA waba
yinkedama, unina waba ngumhlolokazi.

U VITOLIYA kwakufuneka efundiswe kakuhle nangokwase nkonzweni. Uyise
ubeyinyamekele yonke imigaqo yokutanda umntu, ukuze kube lilungelo
ebantwini. Ibe litamsanqa nendawo yokuba umhlolokazi wake lowo
ebengemntu utanda kubalasela embutweni, ubanele kukuhlala kwindawo ete
nxwe yase Botwe lase Kensington namantombazana ake omabini,—kuba obu
ib’iseyibobesibini ubuhlolokazi, intombazana yomzi wokuqala yayi ngu
FEODORA, inobudala obukwiminyaka emibini nemitatu kuno DRINA.

Ute u VITOLIYA napambi kokuba abe nokuzilesela i Bayibile ngokwake,
wamana unina emlesela imihla yonke, emfundisa nemitandazo eguqile kunye
naye. Oko kwenje- njalo emqeqeshela endleleni zobu Krestu obu nangoku
asabaluleke ngabo nanamhla.

Okwenene kona u DRINA ubengakolisi kuba selugcinwe- ni Iwezicaka.
Bekungeko sidlo sakusasa sidlelwa kuwindlu yokondlela usapo kulomzi,
ukudla kwake, bekusisonka, ubisi, neziqamo, ubekunikwa kusasa ngo eight
o’clock kusasa, esecaleni lonina. Bekusiti ehlotyeni, iti ibulakufesi
itiwe dwe eqiyeni yasendlwini, malungana ne fesitile, apo
bebenokupulapula ingonyana ezivunywa zintaka, bamane bezikangele pakati
kwamasebe emiti, okunye bate te ngetyatyambo ezibe zibaraule.

Ibehle lentombazana yaqela imigcotyana eyeyekileyo ekute xa
sel'isexabisweni lokuba ifundiswe kwabonakala inyamekelo yayo iseku
wakeni nasekukweleni kwinqwelwana encinane, ejikeleza lo miyezo yase
Kensington, nokuti mhlaumbi imane idlala emagumbini apa, nase mtyini we
Botwe elo. Lomntwana ebangavunyelwa kakulu ukuba abone inqubo yase zi
ofisini.

Kwaye kuko soldati lidala ligama lingu HILLMAN, ebe- lifumana i pension
kuyise, abete walibeka nomfazi walo kwindlu eb’ikufupi ne Botwe elo,
ebekuti xa kuyakubonwa abobantu ngunina nomfundisikazi obefundisa yena,
(u DRINA) ibe yinto eyole kunene kuye. U HILLMAN waye nentombi eb’
ingumlwelwe, yaye inkosazana le iyitatanda kakulu, ngakumbi ke kona
ngesizatu sokuba bekungeko ntanga zake zininzi abe nokudlala nazo.

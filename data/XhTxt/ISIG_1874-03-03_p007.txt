IZINTO NGEZINTO.

KWA RILI.—I-German elaligalele U-Rilingamanzi lidliwe iponti ezilishumi
yi Mantyi yakona.

IMVULA pesheya kwe Nciba kutiwa ibe ininzi kanye ngale nyanga ifileyo.
Bekunzima kakulu ukuwela imilambo.

KWELE DIAMOND kuko umteto wokuba ati umntu osukuba etenge idiamondi
ebiweyo akatswe kunye nesela elo atenge kulo.

AMALAHLE.—Ipepa lase UITENHAGE liti kuko amalahle afunyenwe kulo
Mahlulo, ekubonakala kungati ayakuba noncedo.

E-QUEENSTOWN kuko inkwenkwana ekufikwe ifele emanzini, kuqondakala ukuba
yoyiswe butywala ngapezu kwamanzi.

E-MONTI ute umfo obesand’ ukupuma etolo- ngweni, waya kuwela epontini
enxilile, wazuzana nokunyatelwa yinqwelo abewela nayo, wafa.

E-UITENHAGE ngo 20 ka March I-mantyi ya­kona iya kuqeshisa ngemihlaba ye
Government, ekulo Mahlulo, kwaba ngawu funela okonyaka omnye.

ABEMI BASE CLANWILLIAM batumele ipepa lokucela I-Government ukuze incede
ingake im- tumele U-Mr. Meurant ukuba aye kuba yimantyi pakati kwabo.

INTLANGANISO YABAFUNDISI iyakuhlangana E-Qonce ngomhla we sine
nowesihlanu enyangeni. Intlanganiso ivulekile kubobonke abafuna
ukupulapula.

PESHEYA KWE NCIBA kusand’ ukulwa impi ka Smith Mhala no Sigidi. Isizatu
sokokulwa beku- xabene abatile ngesicuba. Kweka Mhala kubulewe amadoda
amatatu, kwanxwelerwa mabini.

E-MONTI umfo otile obanjwe esiba imbodlela yotywala unikwe iveki
ezimbini etolongweni, aya- vunywa imali yake xa afuna ukubatala.
Uyakuliqonda kanye ixabiso lembodlela ebiweyo.

E-BAYI kuko ontsundu ofakwe etolongweni inyanga ezintatu esebenza
kanzima, ngokusuke abete inkosi yake kuba ibimgcine emsebenzini
obunxamisekile lada ladlula ixesha lomsebenzi.

U-MAJOR MALAN obeke ngapambili eyinkosana yamasoldati usabuyele
kwakwelilizwe. Uzele umsebenzi wokushumayela kwabantsundu, okwe- xesha
elitile. Uzakuba pesheya kwe Nciba.

INGOZI EMBI.—Umfo abati ngu Patterson ute esaka indlu etile E-Rini,
lamngena ilanga ngangokude ayotywe, awe umgama ozinyawo ezi 20, apuke
imbambo ezintatu, kuko nezinye indawo enzakele kuzo.

E-TRANSVAAL kutiwa kuko umfo osand’ ukuba- leka kona, ogama lingu Herman
Jepper, ubengu- mgcini mpahla yenkedama nabahlolokazi. Ube kulomali
abeyigcina iponti ezi £7,02818s. Lomntu usafunwa.

UKUBULALA.—Kule veki idluleyo kuko amadoda amabini alike equtywa eziswa
etolongweni, enikwa ityala lokuba ate ngo December axabana nenye indoda
enxilile, ayibeta yada yafa. Noko abukabiko ubunqina obaneleyo.

U-MR. ORPEN, lomfo uzakupata ama Mfengu kumhlaba omtsha kunconywa ukuba
uyafa kakulu.

U-MR. HOGARTH, umkangeli wezikolo ukula macala angase Qonce. Uzakude aye
kufika nase Monti napesheya kwe Nciba, apo ayakuva abantu abafuna
iziqiniselo zokufundisa kwizikolo ezibatalelwa yi Government. Usuku
lokuviwa luxelwe kwipepa lezaziso.

ODUTYULWEYO.—Elinye lamapepa ase Qonce litiumlimi abati ngu Gert usand’
ukudutyulwa ye- nyeindoda ngengozi. Umfazi walomntu udutyuIweyo ube bona
naye ukuhla kwalo ngozi. Ute esiya kufika apo iwele kona yaba iselifile.
Kusapengululwa zizipata mandla.

INQWELO YEPOSI yase Middelburg ite xa iwela umlanjana ongenisa E-Nxuba
yatatyatwa ngumsinga. Umntu wayo usinde macebetshu. Inkwenkwe yake
namahashe amatatu imke kwapela. Incwadi zide zaya kungena E-Nxuba. Enye
inxowa yazo icolwe E-Cradock; ezinye nangoku zisalahlekile.

AMASELA E-DIKENI ate ngo Mgqibelo ngo 21 February aqekeza, ivenkile
yakwa Baker ebusuku, emka nengubo, nemali. Akakabanjwa, kodwa siyatemba
ukuba aya kufunyanwa azuze umvuzo wawo. E-Fort Beaufort ate kwangalo
mini eba iqela legusha. Sikolwa ukuba nangoku azikafunyanwa.

KWELE GOLIDE kutiwa isafumaneka kakuhle. lyabuyiselela kanye. Baninzi
abayayo kona besuka E-Natal. Kuko abazakuya kuqala ipepa lendaba, kuba
ama Ngesi akasenakupumelela ngapandle kwalo. Selide lapantse ukuba
njengokudla namanzi, zona zinto singenakupila ngapandle kwazo.

KWELE DIAMOND kusand’ ukubako inqwitela ezinkulu nezicoto zemvula. Bati
ziliqela izindlu ezimke namanzi. Zite zona ezibe zakiwe ngezitena
ezingatshiswanga zatshabalala. Indawo zokumba ezinye aziyikuba
nakusetyenzwa kude kube ziveki ezitile. Abantu bazo sebehamba befuna
umsebenzi kwezinye indawo.

OBULELE OMNYE.—Ipepa lase Uitenhage liti kuko Ilau elisand ukubulala
umfazi walo ngohlobo olubi. Libe lipikele ukumbeta ngenduku; wati noko
ake wancedwa, kanti liya kubuya limbuyele kwakushenxa abantu. Limbete
wada wafa. Bobabini, umfazi nendoda, bebevela enkantini? Buyasebenza
kanye utywala kwabantsundu.

UKUNXANYELWA KWEMIPU.— Elinye lama­pepa ase Kapa liti—ama Damara, na
Malau, nama Ovampo, nezinye izizwe ezipakati kubonakala ukuba
ziyinxamele iruluwa nemipu. Ngalenyanga idluleyo imipu etunyelwe
E-Walwich Bay, isuka E-Koloni ibe 600, iruluwa yaziponti ezimawaka
mabini anekulu (2,1001bs).

I-COOMASSIE, kutiwa ngaba sand’ ukuya kufupi nayo, asinguwo mzi
unqatyiswe kangangoko kube kuvakala njalo. Inani lezindlu Iona okwenene
likulu, kodwa noko zinqukuva ezakiwe ngodaka, ezinqongwe lutango
olushinyeneyo loqalo, nemiti. Into ebanga ukuba woyikeke linani lentloko
zabantu ababulawayo, ezenziwe imfumba ecaleni lomzi, kube kusitiwa uya
vatiswa. Bati abatinjwe emfazweni intloko zabo kuvatiswe ngazo umzi.

OBELINGA UKUZIBULALA.—Kute ngase Bayi umfana otile walinga ukuzidubula
ngokwake. Ikaya lake lomntu lise mantla ne kwezana kwe sika Mabandla,
E-Tyume. Bekusekunomzuzu eno- kupambana intloko. Isizatu sokuba
azidubule uti ucapukela ukuba seleyinto esisihiba, ungake ngoko
angaselesifa kube kanye. Abeli cala, abake bambona, bati imbumbulu,
ifike yagwengula emqaleni, watshiswa kakulu yiruluwa esifubeni. Lompu
azidubule ngawo ube ewuboleke komnye umntu, wati uyakudubula inkau.
Ngoku use ndlwini yamayeza E-Bavi.

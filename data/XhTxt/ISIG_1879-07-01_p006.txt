Port Elizabeth 28th May 1879.

Nkosi yam Mhleli Wendaba. Ndiyakubongoza uku- ba undi tele gxwa
lemigcana kwelopepa lamakowetu Besinoviwo lwentsapo e-Wesile kwesisikolo
somfo ka Mvambo, ngokuhlwa komhla 27 kulenyanga ; intla- nganiso ingene
emva kwesitandatu, isihlalo sinikwe umfo wase Diamond U-Mr. J. Zuma.
Umpati sihlalo ke utete amazwi amafutshane, okubulela ugcino Lomdali;
ute esitsho wabezincoma ubuyilo, esiti akakulungiselelanga ukutabata
indawo enje ngale abekwe kuyo, utsho wazibiza ngelokuba uyimpara yase
Diamond.

Kubeko amadodana amatatu anamaqela awo ate ayiyolisa intlanganiso
ngemvumo yamaculo esi Ngesi apetwe ngu Jacob Morley, no Josiah Mzozoyana
no Jacob Williams eyesine yangumfo ka Mvambo i-Teacher, ete yona
yatabata awa Belungu nawa Maxosa. Amaculo alomanene omane abe kumashumi
mabini anesibozo (28) equkiwe onke, evunywe kamnandi onke, ngokokude
inxenye ilibale ukuba lusapo, lwabantu abamnyama olu lwenza uselesele
olunje.

Imfundo yabantwana iqutywe ngofihlelo kwezama- ngesi nezamaxosa,
kwizilungiselelo zezibalo kusuke abantwana abakumashumi matatu. (30).

Cwecweni Station June, 3,1879.

Mhlekazi omhle kaundifakele lamazwana ambalwa kwelopepa lezikulu
zakowetu. Besinentlanganiso yomnyaka apa ngomhla 19 ku May. Sasimeme
nemantyi yetu ukuba ize kutata isihlalo yaqutyulwa sisifo ayabi
safikake, sasesitatwa ngumnini zikolo u-Rev. P. Hargreaves, andinamlomo
koko ndakubona ngalomini. Wamisa into ezintatu umpati sihlalo ekufanele
ukubulelwa zona, konamandla onke. Eyokuqala yimfazwe kuba nakuba
ibinkulu kangaka ayifikelelanga kwelicala. Eyesibini yindlala ukukutshwa
pakati kwesizwe. Eyesitatu lontlanganiso ifike kusand’ ukubako
imvuselelo enkulu, kufanele ke ukuba kunikwe imbulelo kwi Nkulu yenkulu.
Mandiwayeke amazwi nditi, ucwayito olunjalo ngati andizange ndilubone.
Bada batintwa kwatiwa pezani basuka benza into yanye ukunikela iminikelo
bencokola kamnandi bengaqutywa. Kwazibula umzukulwana warn u-J. Impey
wakupa inxowa yombona kwangati kufunziwe. Sendingati ngamafupi
emfutshane impahla irolwe kwaoko enje ngegusha nebokwe ibe 39,
etenjisiweyo 4, inkomo zimbini, inxowa zamazimba nombona amashumi 27.
Imali imayela namashumi £30 3s 6d. Andilazi elepulanga inani andikalazi
elengobozi nezikotile. Kunjalo apa Eba- tenjinii. Into ka Gubanca
u-Mhala ukupe £4, kanti noko abantwana bake bakupe inxowa ezi 2. Yawati
into ka Zihlangu yahlabanisela nge £3, ngapandle kwabantwana bayo omnye
5s omnye 10s omnye 5s. Yacapuka inkwenkwe yelipesheya yarola £5. Into ka
Mabangula 15a, lowo ukupe inkabi yinto ka Cokazi nelinye inene la kwa
Caba u-Jafuta' Beliko nexego elidala lapesheya ko Tukela elimayela
kwiminyaka 80 ne 90 u-Abraham Pamla umntu ongummangaliso zihlobo zam ;
izinto ezipuma emlonyeni wake zingummangaliso.

J. MJILA.

ubala ukuti, isifo sama nzabomvu sesifikile kona. Uti, “ Ndinosizi ukuti

ISELA ELIMHLOPE.—Elinye lamapepa ase Natal liti, Emapoliseni kuke
kwafika umfo ontsundu, umqubi wenqwelo ekala ngokuti ubelwe iponti
ezilikulu elinamashumi amatandatu, lomali yabiwa ngumntu omhlope, umfo
wase Kapa. Lemali ebeyi fumene ngetoro ezintatu. Uze kuqondakala ukuba
ukolukulu usizi. Amapolisa ahlabe ngomkondo amfumana umnene, seletsho
ngezintsha ingubo, impahla zake zitwelwe ngomnye umntu, iyinto iqiya
ezimhlope, isepa ezimavumba amnandi, incaza nebanti namaqosha egolide.
Ambambile amapolisa afika ziseko iponti ezilikulu elinamshumi matatu.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1880.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

ABANTSUNDU UKUSEBENZA KWABO.—Ontsundu osand’ ukuvela kwele diamond
uti:—Kwenye indawo apo kwaku funyenwe idiamond, kwaye kuko aba Ntsundu
aba tenge imihlaba, basuke kodwa aba sebenzakuyo lomihlaba yabo, banela
ukuba bamane ukuya kusebenzela abamhlope, imihlaba (claim) yabo. Bate
emzuzwini abamhlope baza kutenga lemihlaba kwaba Ntsundu. Omnye utengwa
ngenkabi ezine, omnye ngesipani, wumbi nge mali. Bate aba Mhlope, bayi
sebenza lemihlaba. Ngoku sebe tyeba abanye kona, nge diamond
abazifumanayo.

E-MADAGASCAR.—Inkosazana yesi siqiti ivelise i-Ntshumayelo ene- ndawo
ezinkulu. Kwezinye kutiwa ; “ Ndiya bulela kakulu ku Tixo, ngokuba
indaba ezilungileyo zika Yesu Kristu, zifikile kweli lizwe, lobu kumkani
bam, ezo benza abantu bam ukuba baqonde, bamazi u- Tixo, bafumane ubom
bapakade. Ngenxa ke yokuselo, endi lufumeneyo ku Tixo, endilu bonayo
ukuba lulungile, ubu kumkani bam ndibunikele ku Tixo.” Kwenye indawo kwa
lelwa ukwenziwa, noku tengiswa, nokuselwa ko tywala. Kuko nemiteto
esingisele e-Tyalikeni, noku hanjiswa kakuhle kom sebenzi kona, neminye
eyala nge Sabata ; kuko nomnye wokucasa izilinganiso zenkohliso.

OYENA upete ngoku esona sikula sikulu, sidumileyo, kwezama Silamsi
(Mohammedan) inkonzo, e-Cairo, ngum Yodi olahle eyakowabo inkonzo
watabata eyama Silamsi. Igama lake ngu Abbasi unyana wom Israel odume
kunene. Ngexesha lokuguquka kwake, engena kweyama Silamsi, bafumane be
p'atshalala abantu. Esisihlalo akuso wasifumana kwangomnyaka we 1871.
Abafundi (students) abafunda pantsi kwake ngamawaka alishumi (10,000),
abafundisi (professors) abafundisa pantsi kwake ngamakulu omatatu (300),
imali ayamkela ngomnyaka ziponti ezingama £2,500. Ngapandle koku, indlu
yake inje nge ye tshawe.

E-EBOMANGA.—Ngolwama 20, ka November, 1839, kwesi siqiti sase Eromanga,
kwa bulawa umfundisi, u-John Williams, watyiwa nga- bantu ba kona.
Nabanye ababuleweyo kona, bayi hlwayele londawo ngegazi labo, kodwa
lifikile ngoku ixesha loku vuna, kweso siqiti. Lomsebenzi umnandi, woku
vuna unikelwe ku Rev H. A. Robertson. Kwi ngxelo yake yokugqibela uti:
Kuko aba ngama 25 aba fundisayo, kusafuneka nabanye. Kulo mnyaka,
kubapatizwe abantu abakulu abali 13, nabantwana aba 5. Abantu bakona ba
kuma 2,500 ; kweli nani kungatiwa bakuma 500 aba pantsi kwe lizwi lika
Tixo. Sekuko ezinye incwadi ze zibalo eziguqulelwe kwinteto yeso siqiti;
kwa ne ncwadi yamaculo iko.

U-CINGO oluliamba ngapantsi kolwandle. Ngomvulo ngosuku Iwama 29 ku
December, Iwa vulwa ucingo olusuka e-Natal, luya kuhlangana e-Aden,
noluvela e-London. Ngoku sesi nokutumela ngocingo kuwo onke amazwe
apesheya ko Iwandle. Ngemini yoku vulwa kwalo, u-Kumkanikazi u-Victoria
utumele ku Sir Bartie Frere, i-Ruluneli ye koloni, evuyisana naye,
ngokuba ugqityiwe lomsebenzi wokuhlanganiswa kwe Koloni ne England.

OZELWEYO.

E-Rini, ngomhla we 11 ku February, kubeleke inkosikazi ka Pulani
Langa.—Inkwenkwe.

OBUBILEYO.

Sigidimi.

NGOLWESI-HLANU, FEBRUARY 1, 1884.

IZIZWE EZINGAPANDLE KWE KOLONI.

ENYE yengxoxo ezipokeleyo kweli letu kuyile mihla ingo- kuba indawo
ezinjengoma Pesheya kwe Nciba,—ama Mfengu naba Tembu—ngazwinye zonke
izizwe ezingapaya kwemida ye Koloni maziti, ngecala lempato, zibuyiselwe
kwi NKOSAZANA. Inxenye yemfama zakowetu yoti mhlai- mbi, siteta ntonina
ukutsho; bekuke kwaninina abeke akalaula pezu kwetu u MNTAN’ OMHLE ?
Abatshoyo singati babuza befanele, kuba sitemba ukuba abazauga baziswa
mhla u Rulumeni ose London, pesheya kolwandle, wamkela isicelo sabantu
beli lizwe ngo 1872 sokuba bazilaule ngo- kwabo ze olo Iwa Pesheya lube
situnzi kodwa esongame leyo. Abantu beli basenza isicelo eso ngomlomo
wabo oyi Palamente. Yiminyaka elishumi linamnye namhla yati ke i Koloni
nemihlaba epatelele kuyo yapantsi kwe- mpato yolaulo olubotwe lise Kapa
kwapela. Indlela oluqutywa ngayo olu laulo icazwe mhlope entetweni
yomnye wababalelani owenza umtyangampo kubantu bakowabo. Inqubo yolaulo
Iwase Kapa ayicedululayo lombali yeya- neleyo, inene, ukwenza isitulu
esingumntu ontsundu ukuba sibuze ukuba babinina o Rulumeni ? Kuba kaloku
namhla kuvelela eli qela ze lihambise ngokobuntu—ngoku- zola, kanti
ngomso kuza kungena idlokova, libovuye liba- dube abantu, licite
amadinga afanelekileyo abemiselwe ngabo bebelandulela. Lempato
ifana-nqwa nalo mdlalo wengqalutye ekatywa kodwa ukudlalwa kwayo—le ati
ama Ngesi yi “ foot-ball.” Kwabaluqondayo olu laulo akuko nto imnandi
injengalo ekuqutyweni kwalo ; alwahlukile ku gungxuka-ndinyuke
wamakwenkwe. Longqondi, elotshawe lifanelekileyo u Mr. SAUL SOLOMON
labehle labona ukuba lenqubo ati ngokukumsha ama Ngesi yi Responsible
Government, ayisoze iqondwe, ibakolise abamnyama: waseleyibeka ke
umnumzetu into leyo pambi kwe Palamente esiti, nakuba sekunqabile
ukubakupa abamnyama base Koloni kwizaqunge zolu laulo u
Gungxuka-ndinyuke (Responsible Government,) bona betu abangapaya kwe
mida ye Koloni mabasebekutshwa kweli ziko lishushu lale mpato. Esi
sicelo usenze u Mr. SAUL SOLOMON ngo 1881, unge usenza kwintlanganiso
yamaramba azizitulu ngenxa yokuswela kwake ongamnika indlebe. Ngomnyaka
we 1881 kwakulelixa imfazwe ya Besutu yayingena bani unga- sixelayo
isipelo sayo. Ekuhambeni kwexesha ide yafika inxolazwe, ikauleziswa ngu
Mr. SCANLEN, kwati kuba a Besutu bebesebefumene ukuba hleliwenje
sekumzuzu bapatwa yi Koloni, abavuma ukuba sahlala pantsi kwayo bafuna
bebuyele kwa Pesheya. Le Palamente yokugqibela iyivumele lonto—okanye
isininzi samalungu ayo. Laxola ke e Lusutu. Lixolile, ngokuba benikelwe
Pesheya, luti ulaulo olonganyelwe ngu Mr. SCANLEN aba Tembu nama Mfengu
mabanikelwe nabo kwangokunjalo; i Koloni ezi ingabi saba namcimbi
nezizwe ezingapaya kwemida yayo. Yenye yendawo arutana ngazo amadoda la
anyulwayo; awakwa Mr. SPRIGG ahlanz’ okwayizolo yinto enjengokuba ezi
ndawo zibuyiselwe ku Rulumeni wase London, awakwa Mr. SCANLEN abonisa
ngendlela ezininzi imfanelo yoko. Ngoku kwenjenje sigqwatulela umbuzo
esifuna siwubekise

kwizizwe zakowetu ezingapaya kwemida yetu, ongulo— Zinyuleleni namhla
oyena Rulumeni ninga ningapantsi kwake—ngobotwe lise Kapa (Ugungxuka)
ngobotwe lise London sinina ? Leli ixesha ke lokuba nenze eyenu imvo
—into le isaxoxwa nje, hleze de igqitywe ingxoxo owenu umxelo ungaziwa
bani apo ukona.

IMPAWANA.

Kesinihlebele mhlambi mtsha. Ningaze niye Engqushwa ningazazi kakuhle
izibongo zakowenu. Engqushwa kulapo u Radebe, no Mti, no Dlamini, no
Maduna esapatelwe apa. Apo ixesha elide lisacitelwa ekupandeni umnombo
avela kuwo unantsi nonantsi. Umzalwana wetu onexabiso uke wasityilela
inyaniso enkulu xa sibuza ukuba yintonina le ibanga ukuba ne Titshala
eya apo Engqushwa sisuka sicime msinyane isibane sayo sokukanya xa ifike
kwelo—-lento isuke iseyifana nalo mhlambi ukedame ebunyameni isazeke
itunyelwe ukuba iye kuwukanyisela. Ute, “ Yabona,” ingati inguwe nje,
usafika, uti wakungena endlwini libe ixegokazi selikubuza, “ Konje,
ushiy’ ukuba ngumni, Tata ? ” Ze ukuba akulihlutisanga kwakowokuqala
umhla elihlangana naye liyabuza, “ Isitutshana esi, konje ngumni ? ” Ngu
“ Madun’-Edakeni.” “ Tshono.” Uyeva wena Titshala ngomso, “ Yeboke
Maduna, wena wakulo Jiyana, njalo njalo.” Bosololo i Titshala ixele
imifuno ipekiwe. Buma indlebe inge yinkomo isonwaywa. Ze ke ngoku ihlale
icebetshele izibongo. Le ke yinto eyenziwa nguye wonke okulomzi.
Ziyapuma ke zonke izinto ezipatelele emfu- ndweni; ze nayo igutyungelwe
bobo bunyama bugubungele abantu. Sicimile ke isibane sayo. Linjalo ibali
lehambiso yododana olufundileyo kwi Ngqushwa emnyama.

Intlanganiso ye Mbumba Yamanyama ekwakumiselwe ukuba ibese Cradock ngo 4
January siva ukuba ayibangako. Imbangi kukuba akabangako amalungu
awayenyulwe zintlanganiso ezingamasebe ngapandle kwamabini ase Bayi.
Lento, kokwetu ukubona iwagxekile amabandla e Mbumba. Ingati kuti u
Joseph Ngaba oligosa layo e Bayi wabehle wakauleza ukutsha- bisa ibandla
lo Manyano nge Mfundo, exumisa, ezukisa noku- zukisa i Mbumba ngenyanga
ezadlulayo. Into emayo yinto eqalwa ngentobeko nokweyeka. Xa umntu afuna
ukuma pezu kwendlu akaqali ngecala elingentla ukunyuka, uqala ezantsi.
Imbumba ipange ngokwenza ingxolo ngokuqanana nezinye imbuto efike
seziko, usilelelo lwayo e Cradock belufuneka lusi- fundo kumalungu ayo.
Xa sisenjenje ukubala asibali ngami- hlali yoti, “ Tshotsho ! Tshotsho!
amehlo etu abonile.” Sino- sizi ukuba yehlelwe sesi siwo i Mbumba
kwangelinga lokuqala. Nto kodwa siyohlwayayo, nesayohlwaya kwamhla
mnene, yi “ bumptuous spirit ” [umoya wokuzingca] eyaqalwa ngawo
ngabaseki bayo. Singavuya sisiva ingxelo egqitiseleyo ngeha- mbiso yayo.

Kungovuyo olukulu esifumana ukuba ihlebo esaka salivaka- lisa kwezi
Mpawana lifuna ukuhloba kungoku. Kule ncwadi siyihlomelayo ka Rev. Mr.
Magaba sigxeka ukusweleka kwento enye—ukungavakaliswa kaloku kwelona
nani lifunwa yikomiti ukuze yenze oko ijonge kuko. Pantsi kwezi zimeko
sibona tina ukuba wumbi anganikela idobilityi ezilishumi endaweni
yesheleni ezilishumi, ngendawo yokuba engazi ukuba lishumi lesheleni
lishumi leponti sinina elona xabiso kukangelwe kulo. Ibinga ke noko i
Reverend Secretary ingayicaza lendawo okanye kolahleka, kulahlekise,
kulahlekiswe isininzi. Siyihlo- mela ngovuyo lencwadi:—

NKOSI MHLELI,—Ndincede undifakele lomcimbi kwelo pepa lendaba.
Njengokuba abalesi be Sigidimi baka baviswa kancinane kwi Mpawana nangu
Poyiyana. Kusezingcingeni zekaba elafundiswa ngu Mr. Baker, odume ukuba
ngowase Nxukwebe, ukubonakalisa uluvo Iwabo ngakuye, ngokunikwa nto
itile ezandleni zake. Ndixolele Mr. Mhleli ndingazikupi izimvo zam
kwangoku, koko mandiqube into ecingiweyo kupela. Abo olu luvo Iwafika
kubo kuqala, bade bapume- lela ukwenza intlanganiswana ete yagqiba
kwelokuti lento mayenziwe. Ize kwamkelwe kubo bonke abaya kutanda
ukufaka izandla, into aba- ngayirolayo. Ke wonke ofuna ukudibana
nentsapo ka Mr. Baker angancedisa. Imali ese iko zi £2 5s. Umamkeli wayo
ngu Rev. G. Kakaza ose Heald Town. Kunqweneleka ukuba abafuna ukurola
baze benjenjalo pambi kolokuqala usuku ku April, ukuze ngexesha le
Holiday ka April ibe se iko. Inganikelwa nokuba kukuwupina umfundisi
ontsundu wase Wesile, ngabangakwaziyo ukuyitumela ku Mr. Kakaza nokuba
kukuwupina u Teacher onokutembeka.—EBEN. MAGABA, Secretary of
Committee,—Grahamstown, 21 January, 1884.

    Esi sompefumlo wetu,
    Silivil’ ilizwi lako

3.  Sindulule sinoxolo,
    Sindulule ngobubele;

      Lisiqubele pambili,

elinga- sentla kwele Nyassa nge mayile ezikwi 210. Kwi Cape Monthly
Magazine sifumana imbali yokuqaleka kwaso nokuba sekwenziwe ntonina

apakati e-Africa baya kuyitanda. Ezizikolo zonke sibe singa singahlala
siyazi imbali yazo ukuze abaziqubayo sibe nokubanceda, siti ukuba
asibatumeli mali sibabike emitandazweni. Sinikake kulendawo imbali
yesikolo esiya kuba se Tanganyika, silandela umbali oyibalisa kwelo pepa
sesili kankanyile.

Bamangwato ngakwele Diamond ukuba aye e-Zanzii bar aye kuhlola eyona
ndlela kufanele ukuhanjwa ngayo, nokuba ziya kutinina impahla ukutwalwa.
U-Mr. Price lowo waya e-Zanzibar wahlala kona iveki ezintlanu
elungiselela ihambo yake. Izinto ayezilungisa waya kuzifezela kumzi
abati yi Sadani, Akulunga wanduluka ngomhla 10 ka June 1876 namadoda
amashumi matatu nenkabi zenkomo ezine zitsala inqwelana encinane
nondlebende. Wahamba wada wakufika kwa Mpwapwa indawo enemayile ezi 200
ukusuka elunxwemeni. Waqonda ukuba uligqibile elona lizwe linezixakaniso
ezininzi sekungaba lula ukuyi hamba ngalonqwelana indlela esaseleyo.
Wabuya wagoduka ke ngoko wayakubika kubafundisi abebemtumile ukuba
izixakeko ezikoyo endleleni zinokoyiswa ngunmtu onomonde oqondayo.
Wabuya esiti akakolwa ukuba kulondlela siko esisibau sino- buhlungu
ezinkomeni ekutiwa yi Tsetse. Inkabi aye- hambe nazo wabuya wagoduka
nazo zisapile kakuhle. Ubunzima obufunyanwa ngabahamba e-Africa naye
ubufumene, obunje ngokunqaba kwabatwali bempa-

ngabatwali abangxamele ukubaleka, kwanokubizwa “ihongo.” Le “hongo”
yimali ebizwa zinkosana kumhambi odlula emhlabeni wazo. Maxa wambi

Lomlilo waqala ngolwesi - Tatu seli- tshonile ilanga. Kwati zakulila
intsimbi zomlilo, wawuhlaba umntu umkosi esiti umgxuma wase De Beer’s

ngobobusuku inani asingelazi si- ngasuke siti yayi ngamawaka.

UKUCINYWA KOMLILO.

wusitsha endleleni yokuqala ka Sikolipa- ti, ebebengati ukuba abantu aba
ukuba bayasinda banyuke ngayo. Kulingwe kwakanye ukumnyusa usikolipati,

inxalenye yendlela seyitshile waqauka wabuyela kwasezantsi, bafa bona
abo belungu ba- bekwele kuye. Kude kwasa ngolwesi- Ne kungekabonwa
namnye umntu kwa- bavalelweyo ezantsi.

UKUPUMA KWABOKUQALA.

Kuqale ngexesha lesixenxe ukubonwa umntu oinnye wangapantsi. Kwaye se-

UKUSINDA KWABEBENGAPANTSI.

Uyibalisa umntu ibe mbi imbali yoku- sinda kwabo, kuba bekungeko,
nandlela yokusinda xa kungaba kwehle into enje- ngale ihlileyo. Into
elileyo nangoku ya- bantu ayikaziwa kwabantsundu naba- mhlope.

INDLELA YOKUPUMA.

Zile zakuba zivaliwe zonke indlela ngumlilo, nonisi, nazizidumbu
zabafileyo wasele umntu ehamba pezu kwomnye ofileyo, bakwelana njalo
kungaseko owazi owakowabo, baye ke bonke ngoku beku- mbula indlela
endala eyayingakunjulwa, kuba yavalwa mzuzu, lentunja ivulwe ngumntu
ontsundu laba liyasinda ke iqela elikulu elati lakuvela, kwaba luvuyo
kumntu wonke owayeko, banyuka nzima.

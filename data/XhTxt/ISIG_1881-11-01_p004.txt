ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1881.

NGENCWADI ezininzi zaba balelani ezenze umtya omnye ukufika kuti,
siqonda ukuba izikolo kwela ngase mpuma langa, koma pesheya kwe Nciba
nakoma Queen’s Town ziyatshonela kuba zingangenwa bantwana. Enye into
ezitsobisayo izikolo kukungalungi kwezindlu, ze afike Umkangeli zikolo
(Inspector) azivale. Baninzi, befanele, abaliliswa sesisimose zinto.
Omnye u Jeremiya, ozamana nesililo ngenxa yoku ngu Mr. David Thomas
Malasi, wase Frans Burg. Pakati koku bomboloza kwalo liti elinene, “Kawu
vule zibuqe kade nimane nisiti elipepa lipepa elifileyo. Namhla mna
lindoyisile. Nditi mna, namhla inkomo ebikade yeyele undo ka Gaba
uyifumanele umvalo womtati nakuba efile usateta, uti yibani yimbumba
yama nyama. Nango ke namhla umvalo womtati. Manditi ngokufutshane
mabandla esiqingata sase Queen’s Town, nashiywa zezinye iziqingata
ngokwenza amalungelo emfundo. Manditi ukuze niqonde nina zibonda esi
siqingata sizele zi Teacher ezimiswe ngu Rulumente ukuba zifundise
intsapo zenu, kodwa anibatumeli abantwana esikolweni”. Okwenyaniso i
Hewu lidumile ngalento; lidumile kanjako ngobu mpatalala bododana
lwakona. Imbanga yeyokuba abazali abayi tumeli intsapo ezi kolweni
kanti.

GXEBE bati abanye uhlanga oluntsundu lwenyukile? Asi saku zama
ukuwupendula lombuzo. Wonke otandayo angake azifundele elibalana.
Litsha; libanga udano noko. Kumadodana apa, ngesiko lakwa Mlungu
nelakuti kwa Xosa, lento kusakutiwa yintombi, ngokwakwa Mlungu yi ledi,
ayiwi pantsi kunge mangaliso oko, kuba isidalwa esibutataka
nesibukekayo, esibeta imbuto yabantu ibe netyuwa. He, mayela ekupeleni
kwe nyanga engu September bekuko intlanganiso yo Titshala e Heald Town
yehlelo lika “Dyan Wesile.” Kuluvuyo ukuti lentlanganiso ibe ne gqiza
elimnandi lamadodana nomtinjana, ohambisa lomsebenzi wokufundisa usapo
lwakwa Xosa nolwase Mbo, kwindawo-ngendawo. Lite inenekazi elitile,
elimhlope ekuzaleni luvuyo kwentliziyo yalo, ngokubona esi sibonakalo
s’entyantyambo ze South Africa, lamema lo mlisela nomtinjana ukuba uye
“kutshisa imiqala” yawo ngama gqabi apa bati yi Ti. Uqalile ukusondezela
kufupi umhlambi ngentsimbi ezintlanu zokumka komhla. Sehla apo ke
isibonakalo abanga sokusilibala msinya abacubekileyo, ababe sibonele.
Intoni! Zati ke wena i Titshala, zabeta zacezela paya kwi ledi; zangati
azizange zizibone nokuzibon’ oku. Ngokufupi, zati intombi lomini, kwi
Titshala, zangati ngabantu apa zekusakutiwa bane lepere. Nokuhlala
endlwini, batsho ababeko, zahlala kweyazo zodwa itafile, emgama, ukusuka
kwe yomtinjana, wawunga ngesiya situba sasipakati ko Lazarosi ne sityebi
sase mzekelisweni. Ngokwama Ngesi kwaku funeka ziwaxuba-xubile ama ledi.
Kodwa, hayi zingezizo ezi Titshala zingcwele, zingafuni ukudyoba izivato
zazo ngokupata ngokucubekileyo umtinjana. Wamangala u Mlungu. Hleliwe
nje nakwi Titshala umoya wokuhamba ngobu reletyo ne ledi, kwaba
bantsundu usekona?

INGOZI ELUSIZI.

KWAKU kweli xa Isigidimi sale nyanga efileyo sesi shicilelwe, ekwafika
indaba zokuntywiliswa e Cape Town kwe ntombazana yomhlobo wetu u Mr.
Saul Solomon, u Miss Maggie Solomon ne ntombi emhlope ebikangela
olosapo, egama lingu Miss Burton. Izimeko zezi. U Miss Burton wa puma
nentsapo ukumka komhla esiya kubetwa ngumoya; kule ntsapo aye matatu
amakwenkwana iyintombazana enye. Usinge ngase damini ekutiwa yi Sea
Point Reservoir, apo alike wahlala wabe efunda; abantwana bona bedlala
kufupi. Kuze kuye inkwenkwana u Master Saul Solomon, nelokuti u dade
wetu u Maggie uyaquba. Ikaulezile intombazana, yafika esanabalaza pezu
kwamanzi; ilinge ukumnika umntwana intonga ukuba abambelele, akwanceda
luto. Itumele omnye umntwana ekaya elixa yona iziposa emanzini. Ufike
umntwana ingu Mr. Saul Solomon ote kwa oko wenza into emsinya ukuya
kona, wafika, sebe tshone bo babini. Zide zakutshwa izidumbu emzuzwini.
Egameni lendidi ezimnyama, eziteta kweli lizwe ngelipepa siyamkuza, u
Mr. Solomon no Mrs Solomon kule lahleko yabo elusizi kanye. Ngamana u
Sombawo wama pakade ababope ngonqwemesha lwanga pezulu.

INAMBA.—Lenyoka ifunyenwe izisonge pezu kwe sihlalo sika Mr. Frost wase
Donqaba, umzana afika eme kuwo u loliwe. Ubude bebu zinyawo ezi ntatu.

UMCANCATO WASE ADELAIDE.—Ipepa lase Nyara, Bed­ford Advertiser liva
ukuba lomcancato unga fezwa ukwakiwa ngapambi kokuba lonyaka upele,
ukuba kuseloko kutiwe ngcumbu kusakiwa. Baninzi abaya kuxoliswa yile nto
kuba i Kobonqaba eli kade lilixala kubantu abaya ngapakati.

Umboniso wento zonke e Bay i.

U Mr. Brister obeyi Mayor yase Bayi nyakenye ube nentlanganiso yabantu
base Bayi abantsundu ngokuhlwa kolwe Sitatu 26th August. Ubaxelele ukuba
ekupeleni kwalomnyaka nge 10th Decem­ber kuyakubako umboniso omkulu kwi-
sakiwo esitsha semaliki yezilimo noboya. Ute umboniso onje waqalwa e
London ngo 1st May, 1851, naye wayeko kuwo, nakomnye owawuse America
emva kwa lowo. Lemiboniso idla ngokujika ingqo- ndo zabantu ekucingeni
ngemfazwe izi- zise ekunyamekeleni amashishini, noku sebenza. Kuti
ngokubutelwa ndawonye kwezinto ezenziwa kumazwe ngamazwe kwande ubuncibi
nokwazi kwabantu bamashishini, kanjako kuvele into eninzi yamashishini
ebifudula ingaziwa wande umsebenzi, zincitshiswe indlala nokuhi- liza.
Ute uyatemba ukuba nakweli lizwe le miboniso yoba nesiqamo esinjalo,
nga- kumbi 10 Mboniso uya kuba se-Bayi. Wona lo unendawo emandla kweyake
ingqondo—lendawo yokuba kuvuleke ituba lokuba umntu ontsundu abonise
into enokwenziwa nguye ngokutumela embonisweni nokuba yintonina anoku-
yenza ukuze ibonwe. Ute kanjako uyazi ukuba abantu abantsundu bane ncibi
ezikwaziyo ukwenza ingobozi nenkuko nezitya ngeminxeba nemizi ne njica,
kuko nabenzi bentonga zokusimelela ezintle nenqawa. Zonke ezonto zingaba
nentengo. Nina ke bantu base Bayi ani- nayo imizi neminxeba, kodwa
lobonwa iqinga lokuba niyifumane—noko ke balelani izihlobo zenu. Ute
kanjako u Rulumente uyitobile imali yakwa Loliwe Mgokuba ati oza
embonisweni akwele nge 5s. ukusuka kumgama ongango pakati kwe Bayi ne
Rini—abuye kwa ngalo tikiti. Ati oza nempahla yomboniso akweliswe kunye
mempahla yake ze. U Mr. Brister ugqibe ngeliti mabaze ba- ziswe bonke
ngezi ndawo. Zonke impa- hla ezite zabukeka zotengiswa baze abenzi bazo
mhlaimbi bacelwe ngabatengi bazo ukuba benze ezinye. Yena okwake ute
angalusizi ute lomboniso waba yinto yomlungu yedwa, ngokuba uqinisekile
ukuba into enye entle eyenziwe ngumntu omnyama ingabanga ukuba kuguquke
into eninzi yabelungu eluveni Iwabo lokumkangelela pantsi ontsundu.
Uvuye kakulu akuva ngo cingo okokuba kuko into eninzi yempahla yomboniso
eya kuvela e Lovedale. Kugqitywe ekubeni kubeko enye intlanganiso nge 3
Septem­ber ekuya kumiswa kuyo i committee eya kulungiselela abaneihpahla
yombo- niso.

Impahla yomboniso iyakufuneka ukuba ifinyelele yonke malunga noku- pela
kuka November,

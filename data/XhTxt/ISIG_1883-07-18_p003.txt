ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 18, 1883.

Abo bazinoneleleyo ingxoxo eziqubeka e Parlamente, mababe sebekupaule,
njengati, ukukutala noku papama balongcwele inkulu u Mr. Frederic
Schermbrucker. Ngenye imini apa wanika isaziso ngengxoxo zosixenxe awaye
yakuzixoxa. Pakati kwezi ngxoxo enye yayiyakuba pezu ko mnumzana u
Feltman. Lifikile ixesha lokuxoxa ngeli tshawe, ngama futshane wenjenje
u Mr. Sauer ukupendula : “ U Feltman wayeze ngomcimbi we ngqungqutela
yama Wesile. Xa wabekoke wadibana no Mpatiswa Mihlaba naye, ngedinga
elalenziwe ngexesha lika Mr. Sprigg, lokumvuza ngomhlaba ngenxa
yentsebenzo yake esebenzela u Rulumente. Kwintlangano awaye nayo yena no
Feltman bagabadela entetweni bada bangena nakwengemicimbi yolaulo
kwelama-Mfengu. Akungebe namsebenzi mni ukuvakalisa oko wakutetayo,
kodwa i-Rulumente yakutakazela, yatonyalaliswa kukufumana imvo zika
Feltman.” Ngale nteto wambeka isiziba emlonyeni.

Yinto efanelwe kukangelwa kwangoku nguwo wonke umzi omnyama le yokuba
abo bebekade besitetelela bayatshona. U Mr. Stretch waya elupumlweni
Iwake ngonyaka odlulileyo. Ngalo nyaka ngu Bishop Colenso. Namhla
sisabelo setu esibuhlungu ukwazisa ukuba u Mr. Saul Solomon akasakubako
kule Parlament. Usinge Pesheya ngenx’ enokungapili. Ipepa lama Bulu
(Volksblad lase Kapa, elisesimeni sokuteta ngokuqinisekileyo ngo Mr.
Solomon, liti kunokwenzeka ukuba akasakubuya ahlale e Parlamente.
Asinguwo umnqweno wetu ukuvakalisa imvo zetu ngelifu elimdaka elipambi
kwetu bamnyama elihlalele ukusigubungela kamsinyane uvalwe umlomo ka Mr.
Solomon. Kodwa siti wonke ubefanele ukuzibinqa isinqe elungiselela elo
xesha lingqele.

Zingaba mhlayimbi intlanganiso zolutsha zoba nento eziyi- fezayo,
kwicala lokuzalisa umkenkenene wesituba esikoyo ngoku kwelela kwala
matshawe alungileyo : kodwa xa sisangenwe ngama kwele—olumanyano
lunyelisa oluya, noluya olu, asiboni kakuhle ukulunga okuya kubako
Udodana olumnyama oluyiqondayo inqubo yama Ngesi njengoko injalo aluko
kanye. Kumakulu amalungu entlanganiso ye-Mbumba, asixelela ngawo u Mr.
Joseph Ngaba, kwingxelo ebonakala komnye umhlati, asinantandabuzo ukuti
mnye babini kupela abantu abangayilwayo imfazwe yamazwi xa ike yako
pakati kwentshaba ezimhlope nomanyano olo. Enye inkita leya ingesaba
ikupe ulwimi. Oku kunokutetwa ngokufanayo, mhlayimbi, ngeyo Manyano nge
Mfundo. Kanti ke ikwele elibonakala kwingxelo ka Mr, Ngaba likulu.
Ingaba mhlayimbi u Mr. Ngaba akayazi inteto apa yama Ngesi eteta
ngokuti—abuse the attorney on the other side. Ukuba kunjalo unabo o Mr.
Wauchope nabanye aba profite, angeva ngabo. Bekungalungayo ite iyileyo
ntlanganiso naleyo, ngelixesha ingekabiko intliziyo yomanyano, yasebenza
ngokubona kwayo : yayekwa into enjengaleyo yenziwe ngu Mr. Ngaba, esiti
yeyobu ntwana—yokuhlahlambela inyanga enaso isonka sake esine botolo
ezandleni.

U Mr. John X. Merriman ubuye eluhambeni Iwake ngolwe- Sine. I Rulumente
ye Nkosazana ivurnile ukuba Abesutu bangene pantsi kolaulo Iwayo, bapume
kolwe Koloni. Akukaziwa eyona nto kuya kugqitywa kuyo yinto eninzi e
Parlament. U Mr. Scanlen selete makuvunyelwane kwelokuba u Sutu
lubuyiselwe kwi Nkosazana, abanye ngati baya kuyicasa into leyo.
Sozixonxa indlebe ukuze sive oko kuya kugqitywa kuko.

Hayi, nakuba seloko senze umtyangampo ngento ezininzi ezibonisa
ukuhleleleka kohlanga, ziko zona izinto ezikolisa umxelo. Ngomhla we 10
July u Mr. Daniel Gezani, ofunda e Stellenbosch, wabemelwe kufunda ipepa
(deliver a lecture) kumadodana amhlope ase Cape Town. Ingxoxo yake
yayiya kuba yile : “ The Native Question from a Native’s Point of View,”
Impato yaba mnyama. Ipepa lase Kapa (Cape Times) libika ngosizi ukuba u
Mr. Gezani engasakulifunda ipepa elo ngokuba engapilanga ; linqwena
kanjako ukuba wanga u Mr. Gezani nge elifunde isahlangene i Parlament.
Ukuba impilo embi ibimvumele, isihlalo besiya kugcinwa ngu Mr. Saul
Solomon.

U-Miss Ngqina liledi lase Nyara; u Mr. Jacob Morley linene lase Bayi.
Ngenyanga ezitile ezidlulileyo uMr. Morley wacela u Miss Ngqina ukuba
ibe nguye “ oyakwabelana naye ubumandi nobubi ngexesha apila ngalo.”
Uvumile omnye. Kute kusisa ngomnye umhla wa omnye engasamfuni omnye.
Lonto yabehle yafakwa epepeni ngu Miss Ngqina yaya ku Mr. Morley.
Siyifunda kakuhle ingxelo yamapepa, ngati u Miss uti ngu Morley
obengasamfuni. U Moli uvelisa incwadi ebo- nisa ukuba ngu Ngqina
omalileyo yena ubesamfuna, wabizake impahla yake, nomnye wabiza eyake.
Ude u Ngqina waya ematyaleni. Ziviwe inyaniso zamacala omabini, umantyi
ugwebele isoka, zonke indleko zawa pezu kwentombi. Kuno- kwenzeka ukuba
intombi yayilikaba isoka ukuba ize kubona ukuba isatandwana lilo.
Waciteka wona umcimbi. Silungenisa nje oludaba asingi siugenza manqaku
pezu kwalo, kuba asibantu ukuranelwa, masitule betu.

Ama Mfengu ase Zeleni—ngesisiti into eninzi yawo ifudu selwe kwa Gcaleka
ngu Rulumente. Imbangi yoko kukuxina na. Ependula u Mr. Irvine ute u Mr.
Sauer, eli nyatelo ucetyiswe ngo Mantyi base Qonce ukuba u Rulumente
alitabate. Ama Mfengu wona akali ukufuduka, ngazwinye, ngawo atandileyo.
Abasicasileyo esi senzo se Rulumente bati ama Mfengu nama Ngqika akana
kuhlalelana; koseloko kuko udushe. Ingxoxo pezu kwalo mcimbi iseza.

Ukubonile oka Jabavu, u John walo mlibo. Ngo February kulo nyaka yaba
ngumsebenzi wetu ukubonisa ukuba akanguye u Sir Tengo, namhla sifumana
esenziwa isikwayile (Esquire) yimambane ya pesheya kwe Nciba. Sicelwa
ngu Mr. Tengo-Jabavu ngesiqu ukuba sivakalise uluvo Iwake malungana noku
kankanywa kwake ngu Mr Theodore Ndwandwa owati ungu “J. Tengo-Jabavu
Esq.” Uti akagqibi kuyiqonda eyona nto ibangela ukuba ingxame kangaka
impi yakowabo ukumnika imbeko angekazi nqweneli. Uti iminya- ka yake
isengapantsi nakweka A,B,C. Akakafaki saziso sokufuna undofa naye.
Ezindawo zibonisa ukuba ingati i Nkosi ivuma ake abe nemihla
kwelihlabati. Ucinga ngokuba kuko nto inkulu angayenzela abantu bakowabo
ke kweso situba. Ekungati ke eyenzile lifike ke ixa lokuba anikwe onke
amagama: anganeli kuba ngu “ Esq.” no “ Sir ” kupela, kodwa emva kwegama
lake kubeko no V.C., G.C.B., D.C.L., LL.D., G.C.M.G., K.G., njalo njalo,
njalo njalo, njalo njalo!

Kwingxelo yake ku Mpatiswa Micimbi yaba mnyama, u Mr John Hemming,
imantyi yase Queenstown ibika inyaniso ekwanqisa kunene, yokuba ama
polisa ase Batenjini abukali njengonomeva, ekubambeni. Lamapolisa
akolise pgokuba ngama Mfengu. Udunkunku Iwabantu ke e Batenjini ngaba
Tembu. Oluhlobo lobukali ke selubonakala luyimfazwe ipela. Imfengu
isilwa no Mtembu. Tina asicingi njengo Mr. Hem­ming. Eyona nto yeyokuba
yonke into elipolisa elimnyama lubengu olu ngoburalarume ngakomnye umntu
omnyama. Tina bantu bamnyama sifana nqwa nezilwanyane ezaka zako kwelama
Irish abati zi Kilkenny Gats (Imbodla zelase Kilikani,) ezidume kulo
lonke elifundileyo ngokubulalana zodwa. Wonke umhambi omnyama, kususela
kumfundisi oqina limhlope kuse kumosuli wezixatula, akuko nto
ayixalabele njengomntu omnyama onxiba i Kodi emnyama nomnqwazana
olatshana limhlope. Utyelelo pesheya kwe Nciba ngapandle kwe pasi
lungamfundisa into entsha kongayaziyo into leyo. Kusasweleke amadodana
atile amnyama awoze ake ahambe, abafumane ngeqinga aba Judas, ze “inkala
ixinge etyeni.” Njengokuba kunjeke e Batenjini, ngati u Rulumeni
uzakuwancipisa amapolisa ama Mfengu lawo.

Lentlanganiso yokugqibela ibise Bofolo yayiwa libinza eli- mnandi
lamalungu nababefuna bengamalungu. Udodana luqale ukufika ngentsimbi
ezilishumi linanye; yaza ayabi nakuqalwa intlanganiso ngapambi ko 2.30.
Pakati kwexesha lokufika nokuhlangana, iqela elifanelekileyo lododana
lwakowetu Iwahlala esidlweni sedinala kwindlu ka Mr. Ferguson. Uxasiwe
umzimba kume u Mr. J. W. Gawler wacela umhlambi ukuba uselele kwimpilo
entle ye “ Nkosazana,” ate wayixasa ngenteto emnandi ehlekisayo,
kupendule u Mr. Ferguson ngokwake. U Mr. Tengo-Jabavu ungenise impilo ye
Ruluneli u Sir Hercules Robinson ; kupendule u Mr. Ferguson kwakona. U
Mr. Paul Xiniwe ucele ukuba kuselelwe kwimpilo yolaulo luka Mr. Scanlen,
eselwe ngovuyo.. U Mr. J. K. Bokwe ungenise impilo ka Mr. Ferguson
umlungiseleli webandla : kupe- u Mr. Ferguson. Tina sakoliswa kanye si
Ngesi sekaba, no Mr. Feaguson waba njalo. Samqonda kodwa ukuba ubengazi
ukuba kuko kungaka kwezi ntlokwana zimnyama. Ikaba alibuyanga nganeno
kokumtyela inxwaleko zomntu omnyama ofundileyo zokuba engamkelwa
ezihotele. Ute yena sazeke bengacingi ukuba kuko nto ikoyo kumntu
omnyama, kodwa namhla nokuba ngubanina ofikayo owelo qela wofumana konke
akufunayo. Libakala elo.

Sikufupi nenyaniso xasiti incwadi ka Mr James Pelem ebonakala kwi bala
laba baleli iyakubanga amaqela amabini kubafundi be Sigidimi. Bako
abayakuti ngeingafakwanga: beko nabaya kuvuya ukuba ifakiwe. Impi eninzi
kubafundi betu mayibe seyi budinwa yingxoxo ka M.P. Beside sacinga ukuba
sazise ukuba inqumke. Ndawana ibange ukuba siyipe ituba yile yokuba,
napakati kwamagwangqa imfundo yeyona nto ubambene ngayo umzi. Kuko
abayakuti incwadi ka Mr. Pelem yengafanelekileyo. Tina sicinga ngolunye
uhlobo. Wonke oyigqalileyo lengxoxo, nokuba ngovelana no M.P. & Co. or
no Wauchope & Co., akasakurana ngokuba u Mr. Pelem ezekele ezantsi
ekuteteni. Isigidimi sisiqonda tina simele izinto ezinjalo—ukuvakalisa
imvo ngemvo ezipakati kwezifuba zamadoda. Asitandabuzi imvo zika Mr.
Pelem zinento eninzi evelana nazo ; kwanjengokuba sikolwa ukuba zikwa
nesininzi esizicasileyo. Kulungileke ite yaziwa into leyo. He, kunjalo
njeke sicinga ukuba akusakubako woti “ sayama ngasekugxekeni.”

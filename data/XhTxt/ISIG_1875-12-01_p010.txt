E-GRAAFF-RIENET kuko umtwana we Bulu abati ngu Maria Bosch obulewe
yintombazana ekuvakala ukuba yeye Lawo. Bekuxatyenwe ngengubo zopopi,
baza ke oyise baka Maria bakumka lentombazana ye Lawo yayibizela endle
enye kungati kuyiwa kudlalwa. Bate bakusitela wabanjwa u-Maria
warwitshwa ngeqiya ebisemqaleni, wabetwa. Ukale ngokuti musa
ukundibulala, omuye wati ukupendula ukuba ndikuyekile uya kundixela
kuyihlo aze andibete ; watsho ke wamrwitsha wada wafa. Lontombazana
ngoku isetolongweni. Ubuso bayo bune mida apo irwentshwe kona yenye
intombazana xa yenza imizamo yokuba isinde ekubujisweni ngumbulali wayo.
Ubudala balo mbulali ukwi 15 ne 16 iminyaka.

UMFO OTILE obala ngabantsundu ekwele Diamond uti, “Kuse kubonakele ukuba
umntu ontsundu utanda utywala nokutuka ngapezu kobu Kristu. Kananjalo
ubucopo bake abuyilungele nemfundo kuba iti nentwana encinane anokude
ayiqode ibe yiyona imtshabalalisayo. Ngubanina ongaziyo ukuba ofundileyo
uyamgqita osesebuqabeni ngokutuka nangokusela nokukohlisa nokuxoka,
nokungenwa zizifo? Kubonakala kum ukuba bamelwe kukutshabalala kude
kungabi sabako nengeambu yabo. Indlela yokubanceda kukukumbulela ukuba
badalwa banganeno komhlope ngokomelela komzimba nokukalipa kobucopo
nokuzinza kwesimilo.”

UKUNYUKA KWABANTSUNDU—Uti umhleli we- pepa elitile lase Bayi ukupendula
inteto yomfo wase Diamond ngokunyuka kwabantsundu. “ Intshaba
zabantsundu kulekoloni zinkantini, nabafo abanteto inje ngale yowase
Diamond. Ukonana umntu ongati eye ezikolweni abuye engabonanga ukuba
ilizwi lika Tixo lakungena entliziyweni lisebenza usindiso nokuba ibala
lomntu linjanina? Kule koloni ukuba uye ezityalikeni nasazitoreni
ungabona ukuhambela pambili kwabantsundu. Nakuba beko abanye ababuya
ngomva elukolweni siyazi ukuba uninzi'alungede lufaniswe nokufaniswa
namaqaba. Alusangeni ekutukeni,ekuseleni, ekukoliliseni nase kuxokeni
koko luzamela ukupila intlalo engcwele.”

KWA NGANGELIZWE.—Indaba zokugqibela ezi- vela kulomacala zezokuba
u-Ngangelizwe nabantu bake bazinikele ukuba babe pantsi kolaulo Iwa
Mangesi. Umntu wonke obeyilandela imbali yokuhlala kwalonkosi nokupata
kwayo abantu uya kuvuya ukuva ukuba namhla apumile esandleni sayo
amandla okufeketa ngabantu, ngokukodwa ukudlala ngabafazi bayo. Noko
ebengumfo ekuvakala ukuba unemfundwana, ngendlela ezininzi ube
sisixakaniso ekuhambiseleni pambili kwesizwe sake. Besingazanga
sanokonwaba, engapeli amare emfazwe. Isenzo sake sokugqibela sibe
sesokubulala u-Nonxokozelo intombi ka Sarili. Ngalonto udliwe inkomo
ezimakulu mahlanu. Kuya tembeka ke ukuba ngoku aza kuba pantsi kwa
Mangesi ziya kupela incuku ezinjalo, no Sarili akululeke ukuba acinge
ngokuhluma kwabantu bake endaweni yokumana ekatazwa ngu Ngangelizwe,

KWA SECHELI.—U-Secheli yenye yenkosana ezisazipeteyo ezikufupi ne
Transvaal Republic. Ute kwezinyanga zisand’ ukudlula wapambana nenye
yenkosana ezipantsi kwake wati xa aya kuyohlwaya yamxobela. Ite
yakuhlangana imikosi wagxotwa oka Secheli yaza ke lonto yabanga

ukuba adelwe ngakumbi, kutelele nabanye ngase- lutshabeni Iwake. Nangoku
ke lomfazwe isaha- mbiseka pakati kwake nalo nkosana. Ipikele ukuti
akanakumana eyixelela into zonke kanti ubengenjenjalo kuyise wayo.
Kwelinye idabi kubulewe abantu 55 kwelika Secheli icala kwabaleka
nomkosi wake. Ute umfo obewupete lomkosi ubalekiswa kukubona ukuba
lonkosana ize kuncedwa zizitixwana ezitatu, eziti amaxa onke zakudubula
kufe umntu, ize zona zingafi nokuba sezidutyulwa lishumi labantu. Kute
ke emva kwelodabi bake baya kunyangwa ligqira abantu baka Secheli ukuze
bangabaleki. Kulomacala kusalaula ubunyama nobudenge.

EZEMFUNDO.—U-Mongameli wezikula ezipantsi ko Rulumente uyazisa ukuba
uviwo lwe Titshere lobako emva kwenyanga ezintandatu kuyo yonke
iminyaka. Bonke ke abafuna ukuba zi Titshere kuyanikwa kubo iziqiniselo
zokuba misela kubo ubu Titshere. Bonke abazilungisela ukungena kulo olo
luviwo mabagqale ezi mpau:— 1. Elandelayo imini yoviwo yoba ngolwe
Sibini ngosuku lwe 11 ku April 1876 nange mini ezilandelayo. 2.
Ukulungiselelwa uviwo kobase Kapa, e-Rini, e-Qonce na pesheya kwe Nciba
kwa Captain Blyth. 3. Bonke abafuna ukunge- na kulo uviwo mabatumele ku
Dr. Dale e-Kapa amagama abo kunye nobudala babo ne zimilo zabo ngapambi
kolokuqala ku March 1876, baxele kanjako indawo abaya kungena kuzo
eluviweni.

UKUBUBAKUKA KAMA.—Elinye ipepa lendaba lamangesi liti ngaye, “Lenkosi
yamaxosa ingu Mkristu ibube xa iminyaka 76 ngobudala. U- Kama ngomnye
weziqamo zokuqala zemisebenzi yama Wesile. Wabaptizwa ngu Rev. William
Shaw e-Wesleyville kwakamsinya emva kokumiswa kwesosikolo nge 1824.
Ukusuka kweloxesha kusoloko wapila impilo engenasizatu, yo Mkristu
kanye. Ngemfazwe ze 1835, 1846 ne 1851 yena nesizwe sake wahlala
ekolekile ekoloni etembekile ku Mangesi. Kusekuhleni ukuba okukutembeka
kwake kwakubangelwa bubukristu bake. Abo bamaziyo u-Kama bayamvuma
bonke. Ubengumfo ozolileyo onika imbeko kwabanye, inene elingu Mkristu,
elite ke lazifumanela ukubekwa nokutandwa ngabo bonke abebeke basondele
kuye. Ubube njengoko ebehleli ngako, wamkela e-Nkosini. Isizwe sake
ngoku siza kupatwa ngu William Shaw Kama umfo okwa ngu Mkristu naye,
ompembelelo iselinexesha isebenza okulungileyo pakati kwabantu bake.”

E-BATENJINI.—Iqela elikulu lama Polisa apetwe litshawe elingu Bowker,
ohamba kunye no Mr. Wright belicanda pakati kwelase Batenjini, lihamba
lihlangana nenkosi ezitile zakona eziti zona zitanda ukuba pantsi
kwepiko lika Rulumente.’Enye yezonkosi egama lingu Bacela izise amadoda
amakulu alishumi elinantlanu, isiti ifuna ukuba abe pantsi komlungu aze
abe ne Mantyi yawo eya kuwakangela. Enye into ehlileyo apa lelinye kuma
polisa eli fanise ihashe lalo ku Mtembu. Elihashe lalahleka e-Qonce
kweli Polisa. Ite yaku picotwa lonto kwabonakala ukuba lo Mtembu
walizuza ku Mpondo owayedliwa ngenxa yobusela. Usukeke u-Mtembu wasele
mnika umnini hashe ihashe lake ze! Elinye kanjako ipolisa linyatelwe
yinqwelo xa kuwelwa u-Mbashe yalicola uzwane. Lite kodwa igqira laseli
lususa olo zwane, ke ngoku lomntu uyapila. Lomkosi ngoku usinge e-Mtata,
apo wohlangana kona ne Nkosi u-Ndamas ize ipeliswe lemfazwe ikoyo pakati
kwaba Tembu nama Mpondo.

OFUNAYO.

Bazalwana ndicela amehlo kuni nonke. Ngomhla 16 ka November
ndilahlekelwe lihashe e-Xesi lakwa Kama. Impau zalo zezi:—kulendlebe
yase kohlo lisikwe kwindawana ezimbini, ite yakubona ukuba itiwe gqiwu
pakati kwabuya kwako indawana encinane ngapantsi, ubuso balo bute
cokocoko ngamabala amhlope, libambe ubumpemvu; litokazana elifutshanana,
elikuminyaka emine ubudala, itshoba lalo belisand’ ukusikwa noko libe
lingenziwanga lifutshane kakulu. Umntu oya kulandisa umkondo walo
wofumana iponti eneshumi.

PETER DINGISWAYO.

INTENGISO.

Ngomhla 2 ka December, e-Three Vley kuya kutengiswa izipani zenkabi
ezisitoba,—ezinkabi zilishumi elinesitandatu sisinye.

Ngo 9 ka December kuya kutengiswa e-Fort Beaufort umhlaba ekutiwa yi
“Howes Post” ose Ncwenxa, imayile 15 ukusuka e-Beaufort, ubukulu bawo
uziakile 4,000.

Ngo 10 ka December kuya kutengiswa imihlatyana yeziza eli 112 kulendawo
kuzakwakiwa umzi omtsha kuyo kutiwa yi “Paterson,” kufupi ne Nqweba.

Nge 14 ka December e-Whittlesea kuya kute- ngiswa umhlaba obungoka
Beeton.

Ngomhla 5 ka January 1876 ngase Aliwal North, kulendawo kutiwa kuse
lukalweni, kuya kutengiswa umhlaba omkulu onedlelo elihle: nomnye
umhlatyana okwakufupi nalowo onezindlu, inkomo 90, amahashe 12, igusha
629, ibokwe 50, ibokwa, nenqwelana zamahashe, inxowa zamazimba 20,
ezenqolowa 10, ezombona 10, ezekalika 5.

ABABUBILEYO.

E-Rini ngo 5 ka November kububc u-James Pemba.

INTLANGANISO YE TITSHERE.

ITITSHERE zalo Mandla wase Dikeni zimema bonke abazi titshere,
nabawunyamekeleyo umsebenzi wokufundiswa kwentsapo, ukuze babeko
kwintlanganiso eyayubako e-Dikeni apa ngolwe Sitatu ngo 12 ku January
1876. Yeyokukangela ukuba kuyanqweneleka akunqweneleki kusinina ukuba
kumana ukubako intlanganiso ye Titshere kanye ngonmyaka.

D. SIHAWU.

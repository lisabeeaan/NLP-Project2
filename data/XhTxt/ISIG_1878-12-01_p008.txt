  Fort White, February 17, 1877.

Ku Mr. J. Shaw, Fort Beaufort.—

  Nkosi etandekayoi—Umntu otile wandiyalela ukuze ndike ndilinge iyeza
  lako loxaxazo mhla ndandipetwe kakubi sisisu. Landinceda kwakamsinyane
  kakulu ndaza ndagqiba kwe lokuba ndiya kuka ndililinge kwinkwenkwana
  yam ebingazange ipile soloko yazalwayo. Noko amagqira ndibe ndihamba
  kuwo ubesuke umntwana ancedeke intsuku ezimbini nokuba zintatu.
  Ndilinge elako iyeza. Ndite ndakumnika okwesibini ndabon’ ukuba
  uyancedeka. Ito ipela ibotile waba selepilile kwakanye umntwana. Ngoku
  sekupele inyanga ezilishumi elinesibini noko asikazanga simbuyele
  isifo. Ndingakankanya ukuba nabanye abamelwana bam endite ndabayalela
  eliyeza lako balilinga kwababo abantwana, nabo bancedekile. Akuko
  namnye ongati elazi umsebenzi walo ahlale ngapandle kwalo endlwini
  yake. Mna andike ndihlale ndingenalo.

  Ndingowako ongu

  J. THOMAS.

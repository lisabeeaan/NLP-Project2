Ngo 20th June, 1889, senzelwe imvumo emnandi kunene yi Choral Society yesi kolo sase Mgwali, upetwe ligqadagqada elikhutele kunene into ka Ncapayi ititshala yakona. Lifikile ixesha isihlalo sitatyatwe yimantyi yalapa. Siwabalula amaculo singakankanya eliculwe ngu Miss Bevu “O Gently Breathe,” namaculo aculwe ngu Miss Mangcu—‘‘Gentle Annie,” no “ How blissful is the meeting,” wawacula ngendlela etsho kwaluma izisu. Siyinambitile ingqumshela eyenziwe ngu Mr. Sonqishe. Kwinto ebingalindelwe nangubani kwi sikolo sase Mgwali, wonke otanda uhlanga ebengenakuba angawuboni ngokubuka umsebenzi wenkutalo ka Mr. Ncapayi ngendlela usapo lwase Mgwali oluvume ngayo. Ngenxa yokuvuma kwake okumnandi u Miss Mangcu upiwe ibhaso ngu Miss M. S. Nqadini, omnye wamanenekazi ale dolopu yase Cumakala. Pambili Mgwali!

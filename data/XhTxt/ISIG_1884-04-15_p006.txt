owonganyelwe zi Archdeacon ezintsundu o Johnson no Crowther usahamba

Onitsha ahamba ngenkululeko ukuya kuvakalisa ilizwi kwimizi emelene
nawo. Ecelwe ukuba aye kushumayela komnye wale mizi, u Arch­deacon
Johnson wafumana i 1500 lilindele ukumpulapula. Inqanawa ezihamba

UYINTONINA UMTANDAZO?

UYINTONINA umtandazo ? Asiko kuvala amehlo nje, kudibanisa izandla, kuma
njengokuba kusenjiwanjalo etyalikeni, mhlaumbi eskolweni, kuguqa
njengokuba kusenjiwanjalo ekaya, kutetwe amazwi atile. Oko asiko
kutandaza. Ndaka ndatyeIwa ngomntu “ obesakutandaza kodwa engatandazi.”
Lonto iteta ntonina ? Yayilixego, elati kwasebusaneni alaza liye kulala
ngapandle kokuzifihlela umtandazo elaufundiswa ngunina, oti,—

Xa ndilala ebusuku,

U Krest’ egcin’ umpefumlo ; Ndibuba pambi kokusa, Nkosi wutsalele kuwe.

Kanti akazanga watandaza namnye umtandazo wenyaniso. Ukufihlela amazwi
okutandaza asiko kutandaza. Ukuba ubani ubeya kuza kuwe atete amazwi
atile angawaqondiyo naye; ukuba ebeyakubonakala engayiqiqi into
ayitetayo; ukuba umane ebekabeka xa atetayo engati akateti nawe; ukuba
ubeya kozela esateta nawe ; ukuba ubeya kumka kuwe ngapambi kokuyiva
nempendulo yako—ubungatinina ngalomntu ? Kanti yinto eyenziwa luninzi
leyo ku Tixo! baze bati lonto ngumtandazo. Uyintonina ke umtandazo ?

BUBAMBE UBOM OBUNGUNAPAKADE.

NGE Cawa yomhla we 6 ku April u Rev. Frederick Mason wase Natal wenza
intshumayelo ebukali e Nxukwebe kuwo lamazwi ewakupa kweye 2 ku Timote
vi. 12. Ubonise ukuba ubom obu yinto enexabiso elingaziwa bani. Akuko
mntu uyaziyo. Idlula ubutyebi, amagunya, imihlali, yonke into esiyaziyo.
Ngelinye ixesha wati eshumayela kubantu abangamaqaba eteta ngabo ubom,
yavakala inkosi yalomzi abeshumayela kuwo isiti—Mfundisi kausinike
ivangeli eyakubanga ukuba sipile unapakade apa emhlabeni ukuze tina
sikolwe. Wati yena ukupendula akanayo ivangeli enjalo, engena
kubatembisa ukuba angabanika, kodwa unokubanika egqitiseleyo— le yobom
bapakade bese zulwini. Mababambe u Kristu baya kupila kweli lizwe
nakwelo lizayo. Mabalahle zonke ezinye izinto; bam’bambe ngezandla
ezize. Ngenye imfazwe eyaka yako Emampondweni u Ndawana itshawe wafa
ebambelele etshobeni lenkomo. Bati abantu bakuti makashiye inkomo asabe
yena akavuma. Walahlekwa bubom u Ndawana kuba wayenyule ukububamba
ngezandla ezizele inkomo. Abanga bangabambelela ku Kristu mabalahle
zonke izinto ezisezandleni. Ubalise awaka wakubona komnye umzi awaye
ngumfundisi kuwo. Kwana isipango wazala umfulana okufupi. Omnye wabantu
besikolo wayengecala elingapaya. Ute kuba eyindadi wazilantsha, udade
umganyana amoyisa amanzi. Yek’oko ukusinga ezantsi kwindawo ezinamawa.
Kute sebencamile wafika kwindawo eyayinegatya lomti lijinga pezu
komlambo wabambelela kulo wasinda. Ukuba umntu lowo wayepete nto
ngesandla ngewayetshonile. Kukwanjalo kuti kweli lizwe simka nomsinga
wezono ukuba asibambeleli kwi- sebe elingu Yesu elijinga pezu kwetu siya
kutshabalala kupele.

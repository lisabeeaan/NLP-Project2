Ngomhla we 25th December, 1891, be- kuqubisene i Millioner C.C. yase
Bur­ghersdorp ne Look out C.C. yase Aliwal. Kwaqala i M. C.C.
ukuhlanganisela imiti yapuma ne 89 emva kwe yure ezimbini nesiqingata.
Bayifaka i L. O. C.C. yapu­ma ne 25 ngesiqingatana se yure. Yacela i
Capt. ye Millioner kwi Capt. ye Look Out ukuba bapinde bangene, kuba
nange- siko bebefanele ukupinda kuba i score sabo sisezantsi. Yasuke
yangxola yonke i Look Out kwatsho kwako ne echo e Town yenza isipitipiti
ekubonakele oko- kuba akusenakubuye kudlalwe. Kute kweso sidubantini
ndeva into ka Mbelle i Captain ye Millioner seyisiti, “ March on boys to
the station,” kwati dhu uku- singa kwaloliwe, yaba ke iyapela ibhola.
Ibingalilungelo elikulu ukuba i club ezintsundu bezingalinga ukudlala
ngemi- teto yebhola.

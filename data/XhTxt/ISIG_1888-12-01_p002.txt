ufanele ukwenza uqilima ubumbane kuba ubuninzi bawo
ubulewe ngu Matunga, kokukona uxela isonka sentsipo,
sona singahlanganiyo. Kolu nyulo sipaule indawo ezimbini
ezibonisa lempangalasane. E-Qonce u-Rev. C. PAMLA wenze
eyake, into wabe umzi uteta nto yimbi. Amashumi ama-
tandatu anesine emke ke no-TSHAMBULUKA. Ukuba singa-

amazwi etu esiwabeka apa epepeni apolile kunento esiyicinga
entliziyweni. Imbangi yoku kukuba besinje uku-

okunje kuye. Ukuba eb’ecacile kwasc kuqaleni ngesinga-
kalazi, koko ubezifaka emkusaneni wokungati ungundilele
xa atetayo usebenzela utshaba.

UKUCOLACOLA.

IVOTI YABANTSUNDU.--Umzi ontsundu awuzange uyiqa- bukele ivoti
kangakanana. Ubete wawotusa wonke umzi omhlope. Ipepa lase Komani,
i-Queenstown Free Press, abantu abamnyama bavota into abayiqondayo
ngoku, lidlu- le ixesha lokutandelwa kwevoti yabo emnweni baze baman’
ukwalatiswa kusitiwa votela unantsi Iowa, baze bavume ukuvunyiswa noko
bangakoliswayo ngulo mntu.

EKUBENI esenjenjalo amanye amapepa ukuteta yona i- Alice Times isahleli
pezu kwentlamba. Iti kufuneka uku- qeshulwa iqela labavoti abantsundu
kumandla wase Qonce, likutshwe evotini kuba i-Nkanunu ingene
magcwizigcwizi. Lomzi umhlope wase Dikeni nowase Nqushwa tina namhla
siwuncame impela. Uqale ngokulahla iciko umfo ka Innes, uqokele
ngokulahla enye injengele ugxiba Iuka Tamplin. Endaweni yabo ungenise
u-Pope no Jongisongololo umxasi wentengiso yotywala. Eyona isimangalisa
ngokukodwa kukufumana nabantu abangamazila abanjengompati welo pepa
bepakati kwelo qela labaxasi botywala. Sisimanga lento yenziwa ngabantu,
abantu ababefuna ukuba u-Rulumente avale inkanti, ancipise uselo,
nabobantu benga Matempile, bajike bavotele umntu ocasene neminqweno
yabo, bemazi, bemva, bembona ngawabo amehlo. Wanyanisa oka Lose mhla
wati entlanganisweni, “ Abantu bayakupendula nga- malungelo abo.”

IKATSI E-PRETORIA.—Lento ikukutyakatya umfazi nge katsi e-Pretoria imbi.
Kule ifileyo, kubanjwe umfazi ontsundu kwi hotele etile engazipete
kakuhle. Ematyaleni ugwetywe ukuba ahlaule imali etile, mhlaumbi, ukuba
akanayo, akatswe imivumbo elishumi. Kute engekayenzi nemigudu yokufuna
lomali, wasel’ equtywa esiwa ekatsini. Lomivumbo ilishumi uyamkelisiwe.
Unga angapazamisa u-Rulumente wama Ngesi kolutyakatyo Iwabafazi lupele.
Kuqinisekile ukuba ama-Bulu avukwa vimvelo yawo endala.

MUSA UKUSHIYA INDLU YAKO NGOKUHLWA.—Lisiko elibi eli lokuti sekuhlwile
indoda ipume endlwini ishiye usapo lodwa. Enye intokazi ite ngokulinda
indoda yahlala kwada kwasebusuku, nobusuku obukabadakazi isozela. Ide
yangena ekambileni yakulala. Ifike sekuko oleleyo, yama- ngaliswa ukuba
ngubanina kanti, udiya akayanga ndawo, nanku elele.

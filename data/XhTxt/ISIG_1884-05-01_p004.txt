INTLANGANISO YABANGALUKILEYO.

Lentlanganiso imnandi kakulu yase Peelton ingene ngo 10 o’clock kusasa
16 April 1884. Afika amalungu onke ngento entle kunene, efake onke
ngasekunene upawu Iwentlanganiso iribbon eluhlaza malunga egxalabeni.
Ndite ndakuposa amehlo ndafika ezole onke ngati aya kuncwaba ehamba
ngamabini. Ivulwe intlanganiso ngomtandazo ngu Mr. Rubusana. Ute u
Mgcini-sihlalo obekekileyo wetu u Mr. P. Mcelu watata isihlalo. Emveni
koko usukile u Mr. Rubusana wati ngamazwi amnandi kunene watyila lento
ingummiselo wokwaluka. Umiso Iwayo no- kungafaneleki kwayo ngoku kuti,
nokulunga kwayo ngelo xesha; emana esalatisa elizwini indawo
ezingolwaluko, nokuyalelwa kwalo ngu Tixo kuma Jodi. Ezindawo uzingene
ngobushushu obukulu. Etyila ukuncola kolwaluko, nokungena kwamadodana
ezikolo kulo, nohlobo olumdaka ahamba ngalo nobulihlazo kwi ramente
zetu.

U Mr. MEYA QELA GINZA unyana wenkosi yakwa Mdange ute lento iyindoda ema
Xoseni kutiwa mayaluswe ukuze ikalipe kodwa kulemfazwe akuqondakalanga
njalo. Amagora ib’ingamakwenkwe ama Ngesi, la asalaulayo nanamhla.

U Mr. TOKWE omnye wabakokeli ute, Ndiyatakazela ngalento niyenzayo
madodana ilungile, pambili nibe banye.

U Mr. BOMBO HALIMAN umkokeli wetu omdala kakulu, ominyaka imashumi
matatu anesibozo enguye ute,—Ndiyi- ndoda endala, namhla kumnandi kum,
ize nomelele bonyana. Lode ligwetywe ilizwe beko aboni, koba kwanjalo
nakubaluki. Ningadinwa qinani.

U Mr. YEKELE (i teacher yase Qonce) owayesityelelele wati, Awu !
mabandla kuyatyutulwa ndifane ndasidenge, watsho wati ulwaluko lufanele
ukumiswa lububuhedeni obuzeleyo, lomeleza inkohlakalo. Watsho wati
ningaligcini elisiko e Peelton apa, yishumayeleni levangeli yokungaluki
nakwabanye.

Nabanye bateta kamnandi. Ivalwe intlanganiso ngomteto wo Mgcini-sihlalo
ngo 12 o’clock ngu Mr. Bombo Haliman ngomtandazo oshushu. Pakati
kwamanene abalulekileyo abeko sibone o Messrs Yekele, (K. W. Town) ; P.
Tyamzashe, (Mnqesha) ; Tshwete, (Gladstone) ; Dwashu, (Dorrington).
Amalungu entlanganiso abe ngamashumi 60 abeko. Kute kwakupunywa
etyalikeni kwawelwa kwayiwa e South Peelton apo izidlo zazilungiswe
kona. Kwatatwa iyure ezine ukutyiwa. Nto yabalulekayo ububi bayo
ngamadodana alenzileyo elisiko lincolileyo, kuba kwati
kwakusalungiselelwa intlanganiso aqala acela incwadi zokumka.
Kwangakumbi ngalomini kona kuba nomtinjana wawukwase ntlanganisweni, aye
wona engafunwa

nokubonwa. Lakutshona kwaxoxwa, kwati ngenxa yobumnandi bayo kwada kwasa
lapuma ilanga yapuma ke. Izinto ezaxoxwayo kuyo apo zazevifanele
ukutakazelwa sininzi. Yimini eyinkulu e Peelton leyo kubo bonke, ekute
ekupeleni yacitwa ngomtandazo. Sapuma sisiti— Huntshu ! baleka umke wena
Satana, kuba umke namadodana akowetu amaninzi kangaka ngelisiko

Peelton, 24 April, 1884.

INDABA ZASE LUSUTU.

Ipepa lendaba lase Lusutu liti onke amadodana akona afumana iziqiniselo
zakwa Rulumente ngo September ngoku axatisile emsebenzini. Amanye aqale
izikolo kwindawo ezintsha amanye avuse ezidala. Inani labantwana
liyanda. E-Koloyana, kummandla wase Leribe, ititshere yacela inkosana
ukuba zitumele abantwana. Enye yazo u-Mapeshoane uti yena angatanda
ukuba abantwana bake baluke ; kodwa uyakubuza kubo ukuba batanda.
kupina, kuko ukwaluka kuko ukuya esikolweni. Ubabizile okune- ne wati
mabanyule. Bate batanda isikolo. Bate abafana abebesebalukile bakuyiva
lonto babahleka onyana balenkosi bada babaleka baya kufuna umntu
wokubalusa. Uyivile lonto uyise waya kubafuna ababafana wada wabafumana
wababeta kunene. Abaleke lamakwenkwe aya kuzimela ezintabeni efuna ukuza
aye kwaluka akugoduka uyise. Akuyiva lonto uyise uye kumntu obewalusa
amakwenkwe wati xa ake wayenza lonto kwawake uya kumohlwaya. Aqondile
kaloku amakwenkwe ukuba anqatyiselwe, abuyela ekaya, ngoku ahamba
esikolweni. Siyambonga kanye u-Mapeshoane ngemigudu ayenzileyo
yokuwatintela lamakwenkwe.

Umsebenzi we Nkosi.—Amalungu e-Remente aba- mba pakati kwemizi yamaqaba
ati ngoku abantu bayeza kupulapula. Nokoke kuseko inkosana ezingavumiyo
ukuza kupulapula. Bangamawaka abahedeni abayaziyo into ezela yona u-Yesu
apa emhlabeni; kwindawo eninzi kubonakala kungati uti usacukumise
kwakanye u-Moya ka Tixo baze abantu bayazi ngokusindisayo inyaniso,
kunge kushukunyiswa amatambo omileyo. Kwindawo ngendawo abantu
bayaguquka, bayalivuyisa ibandla nezicaka zika Tixo.

Indaba zokugqibela ezivakeleyo ngabazalwana betu bama Swiss zezokuba
baqale isikolo kwakufupi nesi kutiwa yi Valdezia. Lakubavumela izulu
bafuna ukumisa esinye ku Tabana inkosana yaba Batsuethla. Kuvakala ukuba
ilanga libe libandezele kakulu kwelo cala. Abahedeni abakulu ama
Knobnosen ate kwakuba njalo aya kumba imizimba yabantu emangcwabeni
ahamba eyiposa emilanjeni nase mifuleni esiti kukona iya kuna imvula.
Ate akoyiswa ahlanganisana aya kumfundisi acela ukuba ayitandazele.
Solokoko, ngentsikelelo ka Tixo, abakazanga bayi- swele imvula.

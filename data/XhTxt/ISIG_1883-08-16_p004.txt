4 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 16, 1883.

IZIKOLO ZABAMNYAMA.

WAUCHOPE.

Nkosi Mhleli—U Mr. Pelem Sen. maze andixolele ukuba ndingampenduli.
Ndike ndafuna ukumpendula ndaswela impendulo etambileyo le kutiwa “ A
soft answer turneth away wrath.” (Prov. 15.1) Wati u Luther esingisa
kwizinto ze Pope: “ He snarls and snaps like a dog that fears his bone
will be taken from him.” Anditsho ke mna ku Mr. Pelem kuba
ndisenendawana yokumbeka esihlalweni se gentleman. Xa ndibalayo nditi
criticize i views ndisiyeke isimilo somntu in private. Kobam ubudenge
ndiba yeyona True journalism leyo.

Ndipendula u M. P. kuba endimema kwese 2 July. Namhla sibuyela emcimbini
Iowa ndandite makasuse lenteto ye Camp angene kuwo kuba inkulu. I
Proposal ka M. P. yeyokuba zishenxiswe i Mission Schools kumiswe i
Unde­nominational Public Schools (ukwenzela ubufutshane ndoti yi M. S.
ne U. P. S.) I M. S. uzidela ngezizatu ezibini.

1.  Abongameli bazo &c. 2. I teacher zazo. Ufike atyile kwi Education
    Manual yake aguqulele esi Ngesini i By- Laws and Regulations Sect II
    ye “ Nomination of Managers.” Yonke lonto andiyigxeki, noko
    ndingayincomiyo. Uwushiyile lornbuzo we practicability. Nguwo lo ke
    ndiza kungena kuwo kule amendment yam. Noko ati singofel’ enxoweni
    kw.eka July incwadi yake makakumbule ukuba siti isiteto sama Ngesi,
    “ A change is not always for the better,”—ati um Xosa, “ Ungalahli
    imbo yako ngo Poyiya- na,”—liti i Bulu, “ Beter zoeken en slechter
    vinden.”

Nantsi ke indawo endimisa kuyo inyawo. “ Le system ye Mission Schools
isilungele ngapezulu kweye V. P. S. kule ntlalo yetu sikuyo—alikafiki
ixesha esingaba nokuzipata i U. P. S. ngohlobo eziya kusebenza kakuhle
kuti njengo hlanga. Into efunekayo kukuba someleze i AL. S. nge
Com­mittee.”

Nditsho ngezi zizatu:—

1.  Isipo (grant) se U. P. S. sinikwa kwimimandla (Divi­sions)
    ngoluhlobo : Kwidolopu ezinkulu kunikwa i £200 yokuxasa i Teacher
    ezimbini; kwidolopana £50 to £75 kwi Teacher enye ; kwi stations
    kupela £30.

2.  Lemali ukukutshwa kwayo ikutshwa ngedinga le guarantors (vide “ Form
    of Guarantee ” attached to Sect V. of By- Laws and Regulations under
    provision of Educa­tion Act No. 13 of 1865). Ezi guarantors kunye ne
    guarantee zazo mazibe zeziya kumkolisa u Rulumente.

3.  Ngentlalo yetu site sa, ke ngoko asingebe nazazulu (centres)
    zokumisa i U. P. S. ye 1st ne 2nd class ene grant eyi £200
    kummandla, ne £50 or £75 kumzi.

4.  I U. P. S. esingamisa zona, oko kukuti, esingaba nako ukuzimisa,
    zeze 3rd class (schools at eligible stations among the agricultural
    population) lentlalo sikuyo ubuninzi betu.

5.  Kona ke apo kuko isigxeko esikulu sabantsundu, ako na amadoda angaba
    zi Managers ezinokwamkelwa yi Government ? Ako na amadoda kuti as a
    nation anokumela i £200 ukuze axase i Teachers ezimbini kunye nendlu
    ye principal Teacher, kanti nje sikala yi £30 le ? Ukuba ako
    awokumisa sibe sinye lungatinina ukuzuza amalungelo uninzi ?

6.  Kanti ke nakwindawo apo kungade kulingwe ukumiswa i U. P. S. kuko i
    deductions ezinokwenziwa. Zezi (a) imfundo ayikanonelelwa ngexabiso
    elifunekayo ; (b) Umoya we sectarianism unamandla angummangaliso.
    Um-Wesile nom-Tshatshi nom-Rabe yimihlambi eyalanyo; (c) Awukabi
    namandla umoya wokuba siluhlanga olunye— that is to say, sise ngama
    Xosa nama Mfengu nabe Sutu, asikabi zi Afrikanders ngomoya. (Imbumba
    izama oku) (d) Owona moya unamandla ngowobu Ramente ohlanganisa
    lempangalasane pantsi kwantungo ’nye. (The Mission Schools' system
    takes advantage of this influence of the respective churches over
    their adherents.)

Mandingene kwi Mission Schools :—

1.  Zimiselwe abo bangena mandla akumisa ngokwabo izi- kolo (vide
    article 1 Order B. of the Education Manual.) U Sombawo u Rulumente
    ngati wolula isandla ukupakamisa omtina.

2.  Ndohlala kumaqela amatatu azo—I grants zimi ngolu hlobo, njengoko
    zaziyo i Teachers:—

Class I. I grant yi £75 ko Teacher abatatu (i pupil Teacher ine grant
yayo eyodwa endingati ukuba ndiyaquka nditi yi £93 bebane.

Class II. Yi £30 kwi Teacher enye—iti ukuba inomncedisi ibe yi £45.

Class III. Kwezise macaleni £15 or £30 xa kude kwako umncedisi.

3.  Lemali ke inikwa i Ramente, ezinokuti ukuba zifuna ese 1st Class,
    zimise i Committee endaweni yokuqala, imise inani eliya kurolwa
    ngumzali, iti lomali iyi £75 or £93 inyuswe iye kwi £186 or £190. (E
    Graaff-Reinett ifudula ihlanganisa ne £200 ngomnyaka i Committee.) I
    Teacher ngoluhlobo inganikwa £100 kunye nendlu or ngapandle kwendlu.
    I assistant £50. Umtungisi £18. I pupil Teacher £18. (I Pupil
    Teacher ikwangomnye kumakwenkwe e 1st class eseyiqube pada yafika
    kwi Standard IV.)

4.  Ngomkwa onje ne £30 ye 2nd class inokunyuswa iye kwi £75. Nge 1877
    ndavula isikolo e Uitenhage se 2nd Class nge grant ye £30. Nge 1878
    ndacela umncedisi kwapuma kwa Rulumente i grant ye £15 yaba yi £45 i
    grant yodwa. Kwati ngokuhluma kwesikolo ndanikwa i £75 kwa Rulumente
    noko zimbini i Teachers—indim nenkosikazi yam—sabekwa kwi Class
    yokuqala ngu Mr. Lewis isikolo sam. Ndibonisa ukuqubela pambili
    kwale system ye M. S. Zifanele intshaba zetu bantsundu ukusimonela.
    Malunga ne U. P. S. akuko lungelo linje.

Mandiqokele kwasezwini lam nditi yeyona system isifa- neleyo le ye M. S.
Watsho no Mr. Hofmeyr kwi Congress yama Bulu eyi Africander Bond e
Cradock wati, “ Nani ma Bulu nise negama nje kungayo le system ye M. S.
kuba aninakumisa i U. P. 8. entlango.” Namhla siti kanye abacita
itamsanqa letu. Simemeza njenge Africa­nder Bond siti “ Susa! susa le
system ide imfundo iyise entlango apo kungeko Dolopu namanene anokuba zi
Guarantors.” Simshiya yedwa umncedi wetu u Dr. Dale simka neminyani
emitsha o Mr. Ross, abangasaziyo isimo setu, nezona influences
zinamandla kuti—abakangele kuba mntumnyama nje. Masondle sixume oko
sinako—sikuti IMPROVE. Mazipapame i Committee zincede bona aba bafundisi
kuyo le system—zincede zona ezi Teacher zimana zilila ngepango.

Siti Bazali, siti Makrestu ababanga ukuba zingahlumi izikolo zetu.
ASINGABO abafundisi abamhlope, siti. Ngani, siti aba bamana bezibeka
amabala i Teacher sisiti “ Mawo niti abantwana betu yobafundisa into ete
putu na le Mfengu, mhlaimbi lorn Xosanyana, mhlaimbi eli Lawo, mhlaimbi
le nkwenkwe. Ityafe i Committee zezi mpawu zinje. Ipele umonde i Teacher
in fact ife intliziyo, nantso iya kutolika ematyaleni. Abambelele ebunzi
umfundisi ancwine ngokuti—“ Somandla woyikumbula ninina i Tiyopiya ? ”
Xa sisindwa yinkumbi sozitinina ingcango kunye nemigubasi yase Gaza
ukuzitwalela entabeni ? Soqala sibe zicaka ezikolisekileyo kwinto
ezinkulu na, sandule ukuya kwezincinane? Hayi bantu bakowetu. “ Precept
must be upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon
line; here a little, and there a little.” (Is. 28.10).

Mr. Editor, ndixolele, ndiyeka ndingangenanga kwimili- nganiso
endiyitabate ekuhambiseni kwe U. P. Schools kwi Returns ze Inspector ne
Reports zo Dr. Dale no Mr. Ross malunga ne system ye Mission Schools.

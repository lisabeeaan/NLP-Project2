ISIGIDIMI SAMAX0SA, NOVEMBER 1, 1881.

UKU KUBEKISA U LOLIWE.—Ematyaleni e Jaji e Queen’s Town amadoda amabini
afakwe iminyaka emihlanu, anikwa imivimbo emashumi mabini anesi hlanu
ngokusuka abeke amatye emkondweni ka loliwe efuna uku mbukuqa.

E IRELAND.—Ama bedengu abambisa okoyikekayo kweli. Ibotwe lase Ireland i
Dublin ayingenele acita izindlu ezitile apanga ne venkile. Intlanganiso
zama Irish ekutiwa Land League kutiwe yi Rulumente yapesheya zingapandle
komteto.

OBULEWE KU MIWA.—U Mr. John Keevy, ifama kwisi qingata sase Somerset
East, wabetabata ibulakufesi xa kwati xa itambo emqaleni. Ukaulezelwe u
Dr. Clarke akwanceda nto. Ngokuhlwa kwalo mini ukufa kwazipelisa
intlungu zake.

I YEOMANRY.—Umkosi obubizwa ngeligama, obuseluko iminyaka emitatu ;
owamiswa ngu Mr. Sprigg,ucitiwe ngulo Rulumente mtsha. Kwi saziso
sokucitwa kwawo u Mhlekazi u Sir Hercules Robinson uyawubulela ngezenzo
zawo zokalipo.

INTLANGANISO YOKU BUTA.—Intlanganiso ye Ronaskap yabise Kamastone
ekuqaleni kuka October. Imali ehlanganisiweyo ibe £30 ehlauliweyo,
eyamadinga neye mpahla ezikutshiweyo, idlule kwi £10. U Mr. Jeoffrey
wabe sesi hlalweni, no Rev. E. Lamplough wabeko.

IMANTYI ZASE LUSUTU.—Kusatiwa ziyakuma ngolu hlobo imantyi e
Lusutu—Quithing, Major Clarke ; Mohali’s Hoek, Mr. Surmon ; Mafeteng ;
Cammandant Nettleton ; Maseru, Mr. Lavis. Akuka vakali nto ngoyakuba se
Thlotsi Heights.

U CETYWAYO.—Siva ukuba u Cetywayo usakuka aye e England emva kobu
busika, kuba kaloku busika ngoku Pesheya. Oku kuya kwake ku pendulwa
kwesicelo sake awayesi cetyiswe ngu mhlobo wake omkulu u Bishop Colenso
wase Natal.

ZAPELA I KUMKANI!—Kuko imigibe ebeyenzelwe ukubulala u Emmanuel u
Kumkani wase Italy. Kuse kubulawa lenkosi nje eyama Russia isandu’ kufa
no President Garfield ungcwatywe nge 21 ku September—zonke ezinkosi
zibulawa ngama xutantamo.

OLOLIWE E NATAL.—I Rulumente yase Natal ifuna indoda eyokupa imali yayo
ze kwenziwe indlela yenqwelo yomlilo, eyakusuka e Mgungu-ndlovu
(Maritzburg) lye e Mnambiti (Ladysmith;) umgama we mayile ezi 117.
Yohlaulwa yakugqitywa.

INTLANGANISO YOKU BUTA.—Ibandla lase Wesile beline ntlanganiso ye
Remente ekutiwa yi Ronaskapu e Cape Town. Esihlalweni yabi ngu Hon. C.
W. Hutton, Umpati Mali zakwa Rulumente. Kwaba tetayo ngu Eev. J. Smith-
Spencer.

U HON. T. C. SCANLEN.—Elinene lingu mongameli we Rulumente siva ukuba
lisazakuka libe se King William’s Town. Imbangi kukunga pili. Lohlangana
kona no Mpati-Micimbi yaba Ntsundu. Onke amalungu e Ruluneli ebefikela
ku J. J. Irvine, Esq., M.L.A.

INTAKA ENE XABISO.—Ama nene ase Kimberley ake aqubula imfakadolo zawo
aya kuzingela. Elinye kubo libe nokudubula idada lasendle, ekute laku
qangqululwa igila kwa funyanwa idiamond ezi mashumi mane. Kakade bezi
zintwana ezincinane.

AMA NXILA E BAYI.—Bekuko amadabi e Bayi kwiveki zombini zokuqala zale
nyanga ifileyo. Abebe bambene kutiwa ngabakwa Xosa naba Mbo. Kulomadabi
kubulewe kumacala omabini abantu abalishumi. Ingxwelera kutiwa zidlulile
ekulwini, ikostauli ebelite liya lamla libetiwe lamoshwa nangezi
tshetshe.

IRAMENTE entsundu yama Wesile yase Somerset East, ibe nentlanganiso yayo
ye ronaskapu nge 18 ku October. Yonganyelwa ngu C. L. Stretch, Esq.
owateta amazwi amnandi enxulumisa izinto zakudala neza namhla. U Rev. E.
Lones wafunda ingxelo, aza lamanene aposa amazwi:— Eev. W. Oates, Messrs
S. J. Robinson no C. Martin. Indlu yabi zele ngaba ntsundu benxibe
kakuhle.

IHAMBO YAMALUNGU E RULUMENTE.—U Hon. John X. Merriman emva kokuba ebese
Cawa nase Rini usinge e Kapa, akahlala xesha likulu nakona wasinga e
Kimberley Diamond Fields. Ubuye ngase Qonce apo bahlangene kona no Hon.
J. W. Sauer. Like labanjwa ngum kuhlane e Qonce elinene lokugqibela. Ngo
18 ku October sibe nokulibona apa e Lovedale elinene lingu Mr. Merriman
kunye nenkosikazi yalo.

U REV. OXLEY OXLAND.—Ekupumeni kwake ekubeni sisandlase Governor ema
Mpondweni siva ukuba u Mr. Oxland unyulelwe ekubeni ngumfundisi
wentlanganiso yebandla lase Tshatshi elikwa Adam Kok.

ISENZO ESIMDAKA.—Umfana wo Mlungu udubule udade wabo wazidubula naye
ngokwake ekayeni labo entilini ye Zwaart Kop, e Natal. Intloko yake
yenzakala mzuzu bepiti nobucopo. Ngalo mhla u Henry Gibson, ligama lake
elo, ufumane watabata ipistoli wadubula udade wabo emva kwandlebe; uye
wawa ngobuso. Ume ixesha elincinane endlwini apo, nosapo lwakowabo
lusakamise yilonto, wenza oluhlaza umtsi kuse mnyama njalo, wabaleka.
Aputuma amadodana, ati esahamba abona ngasidumbu sileleyo; akukanyisa
afika iyile ndodana, kanti izidubule. Umhla wokungcwatywa kwababantu
waba ngowosizi e Maritzburg.

UVUYO OLUKULU.

Komgha, September 20, 1881.

NKOSI MCOKELI we Sigidimi.—Ndincede undifakele lemigcana. Ututuzelwe
obelila uvusiwe obefile, ukutsho masibabulele onyana bama Skotshi
bazincamele tina zindwaduba zakwa Xosa. Tina e Qumra sine teacher
namhla, silinde umshumayeli ngoku, ziyafunda intsapo ezintsundu ngoku
nje ngezi mhlope. Kum ngati lipupa kuba kukade ndilindele ukubona into
elungileyo, kute xa kaloku ndincamayo ngomntu, wavela umninizinto zonke
watyapa umfundisi abanjwe yiyo lento imvusileyo u Somandla ukuba
asebenze e Qumra njengemini zaba postile, nezo Msindisi ngokwake,
intshutshiso zinentsikelelo uyavuka u Yehova axase inene yake watsho
apo. Okangele kuye akasayi kushiywa yedwa. Ndineminyaka esisixenxe
ndilapa, ngemini zokufika kwam kwakunzima, bebesitya ndibulela, balale
nditandaza, bati igqobokana maliye esikolweni; pofu bengena etyalikeni
noko, bati lingatinina ipolisa ukwenza ityalike, ibisitsho nenxenye yaba
mhlope bade bati abanye yeka, nditi bayi, asindim nditunyiwe, ukuba
ndimazise Umsindisi kwaba batengwa bake wabafelayo, ngoko ke ndimelwe
kukusebenza; bati ubatalwa ngubanina, nditi ndamkela ngesisa, ndipa
kwangaso, wondipa mhla ate peza yiza kwamkela. Namhla lomz sisikolo sika
Rev. Mr. R. Ross, we Free Church yase Sikotilani.

Imitshato mayikangelwe okunene ngati sibeka emva tina bangoku indywala,
izihlwele, ukugxekeka kweligama sibizwa ngalo; utinina lomzekeliso
womtshato, ungubanina uze na ngezake ingubo ; Yo ! yo !

Okwangoku ndise nexesha lokuya ngase kaya, e Rocksdale inyanga
ezintandatu; andoyiki nto ngokuba namhla umzi unomniniwo. U Mr. Ross
akayi kuwulibala, watsho u Msindisi dlisa izimvu zam.—Ndim

QEBEYI H. MEKENI.

Settingbourne, 20th October, 1881.

MCOKELI.—Kawusipe indawana, kwe lopepa Mhlekaz’ omhle lamanene. Akudinwa
wena ukuba upatiswe lomsebenzi wendaba.

Elanene labala ngomcimbi ka Edmund Sandile, siyalibulela kakulu. Ewe
utete into efanele ukuba itandazelwe kanye siti sonke midaka enoku
qonda, nokutelekelela. Siyakucela kanjalo ukuba uke unge, uyavuselela
ngenteto yako. Kuba uti umntu xa into ayipindayo alike abe noku kanyana
pezu kwalento wayeyitetile Njengokuti umntu xa atandazayo ave ukuba
wenza ngokunga pezulu akupinda.

Mr. Shady Mama, ungumfana esiku qondayo ukuba izinto zakwa Xosa
uzikangele kakulu, kuba uvuselela olwaluvo lwalandoda yabala kuqala
ngalomcimbi. Into esiyitetayo masitete ngokungena ntloni, ukuze akwazi
ukusiva Umntwana Omhle xa sitetanaye; kuba lomteto esihleli pantsi kwawo
wom Tan’Omhle, ngumteto oyolileyo. Baninzi abantu nanamhla nje
abangavumayo ukubala ama gama abo ukuba kuqondakele eyona ndlela
kufanelwe ukwenziwa ngayo.

Lofika ninina ixesha bazalwana ukuti sakuyikankanya into yenziwe
siyivakalise into esiyitetayo.

  Masipume egusheni kwizinto esiteta ngazo.—Ndingumzalwana wenu, W. C.
  MTOBA.

Ngosuku lwesitatu kuhlangenwe, kubeko intlanganiso- kazi yomzi eyole
kunene, yezidlo, imvumo, nenteto. Kulo- ntlanganiso kubaluleke kunene
inteto zabafundisi aba- ntsundu o Rev. Simon P. Sihlali no Rev. W. B.
Rubusana, ababalise ngomsebenzi abawupete kwindawo abavola kuzo.
Owokuqala uncomo amalinga enziwe li pesheya kwo Nciha apo akona ukuzama
ukuzinceda, nokukutalela ukuqutywa kwelizwi, ukuzakela ityaliko
nokuzizamola amaqinga empu- melelo : ecela ngoko uvelwano nabe njenjalo,
nokuzinyaswa kwabalinga ukuzinceda. U Rev. B. Rubusana ucele uncedo
lokutshitshiswa koselo oludambisa amandla nemigudu okuqutywa kwelizwi.
Apo yena asebenza kona kula macala ase Monti no Cumakala bazuzeka Tula
abapulapuli, koko wofika bezitshize kunene ngotywala.

Kwabelwe izikolo ngezikolo ze Union imali zokuncedisa umsebenzi wazo.
Kwamiswa abongatneli bonyaka ozayo, nabaya kumela yona e Jubilini ka
Rev. Richard Birt e- Ncemera, nezinye intwana-ntwana zokuqukunjelwa kwe
ntlanganiso yalo nyaka.

III. INGXELO ZENTLANGANISO EGQITIL1YO.

Kute kwaku picotwa imicimbi yentlanganiso egqitileyo— izinto
ezazinikelwe ezi Komitini, nezazi yeketiswe zuyeko- lwa le yanamhla
intlanganiso, kwavakaliswa uluvo lwe ntla- nganiso ngezi ndawo:

(1) Abatunywa ba Pesheya kwe Nciba.—Kwenziwe inteto ezimfupi ngamalungu,
ezibekiselele kwi mpato mbi ’efunya- nwe yile ntlanganiso kwi Pesheya
kwe Nciba. Intlanga- niso ibe moya mnye kuba ingazikatazi ngo moya-moya
ne nteto-nteto zabantu ngayo, mayiqube umsebenzi wayo ebiwuquba kade,
wokuluugiselela uhlanga lwakowayo oluntsundu, nowokulinga ukuba sibe yi
Mbumba ne Pesheya kwe Nciba. Incwadi ka Mr. Gontshi eyabonakala
emapepeni ilinga ukwenzakalisa u-Manyano mayi ngana- nzwa yile
ntlanganiso.

Kunyulwe, ikomiti engo Messrs. W. K. Ntsikana, Maki- wane, Boyce Mama,
no J. Knox Bokwe yokuyikangela lonto.

Ibuye necebo olute u-Manyano-nge-Mfundo lwalamkela nalo, lokuba
kutunyelwe Pesheya kwe Nciba lencwadi ilandelayo kuso zonke indawo ezine
ntlanganiso kona.

Incwadi yiyo le:

“ BAZALWANA :—Intlanganiso yo Manyano nge-Mfundo ita- bata elituba
ukuvakalisa ukuba ekuboneni kwayo kuya kuba lilungelo elikulu kuti sonke
bantsundu ukuba sibe nendlela zokuvana ngenteto ezitetwa ngati, nentlalo
esihleli ngayo.

“ Sibona ukuba kufunwe indlela yokuti nokuba asidibananga ngokuba sibe
yintlanganiso enye—ebesiya kutanda tina ukuba sibe yintlanganiso
enyo,—sibe nako kona ukuvana ngendawo ezipezu komzi wonke, ezinje
ngokuti, amadoda anje ngo Capt. Foltman kutiwe ngamaveza-ndlebe, anokuti
ukungena kulo mzi sikuwo ado atenge ngomali.

“ Sivelisa ukuti mhlaumbi kungaba liqinga elingasebenza ukuvana ukuti
kanye ngomnyaka, nokuba kungeminyaka emibini, kubeko abatunywa abavela
kwintlanganiso ezivuma ukuba sivane: Abatunywa abaya kuti nokuba
abanikwanga lungelo lokuxoxa into zonke ezipambi kwentlanganiso abaha-
mbela kuyo, bapulapule inteto, benze amazwi okubonisa noku- kutaza.

“Ukutumela kwetu eli lizwi kuxela ukuba siyavuya kuku- bako
kwentlanganiso ezinje ngezi ninazo, nokuba ukucunyelwa kwazo sikubalela
ukuba kokwetu nati.”

(2) Ukupononywa kwe Mfundo.—Pakati kwenteto eze- nziweyo kubalasele
indawo yokuba ingxoxo mayisekwe pezu ko Mteto ka Dr. Dale, wokuba
intsapo mayingaviwa ngum-Hloli—zikolo kwi Standard IV. xa isiqingata
sesi- kula singekona pakati kwe Standards. Kubonakele ukuba lo Mteto
wenziwe ngesizatu sokuba o-Titshala bafuudisa intsapo epambili yodwa
ngokufuna amanani atile kwibakala elipambili, luze usapo oluse zantsi
ngokukodwa olufunda ko “ A ” lugalelwe nje esikuleni ngokwegusha.

Ezinye i-Titshala ziti icala labazali maliqwalaselwe kakulu kuba
lentsapo ingahambisiyo emfundweui idla ngokuba yile bati bakuxakeka
abazali baxakekele kuyo; kanti noko balindele ukuba lube kwi klasi
ezipambili. Abazali ababeko entlanganisweni baceliwe ukuba ke batete.
Inteto zabo zibonakalise ukuba ityala liko macala omabini, kubazali
ngendawo yokuxakekela ebantwaneni, nako Titsha- la abangaluqubiyo usapo
emfundweni. iyeza lalento kukuba makukutazwe ikomiti zokukangela
izikolo. Isi- gqibo se ntlanganiso sibe seso kuba lo Mteto ka Dr. Dale
mawunga e'aswa.

Philip ukuba ongamele intlanganiso endaweni ka Mr. Mzimba.

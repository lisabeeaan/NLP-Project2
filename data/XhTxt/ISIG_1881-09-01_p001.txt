Vol XI. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1881. No. 132.

ISAZISO SOMSHICILELI.

Umshicileli we Sigidimi umema abalesi baso ukuba bapaule ezi zimiselo
ngezaziso.

1.  Isaziso esotabata ububanzi bepepa (Double column advertisement),
    kobizwa ngaso isheleni ezimbini nge inch (2s. per inch); ngomqolo
    omnye wepepa ixabiso loba yisheleni nge inch {single column 1s. per
    inch.)

N.B.—1. Izaziso zemibiko yaba bubileyo; nokuxela abazelweyo, naba
tshatileyo, zofakwa e Sigidimini kwakuba kubatelwe isheleni (1s.).

2.  Xa isaziso, afuna ositumeleyo sishicilelwe kabini mhlaimbi katatu
    njalo njalo, ngawo onke amaxesha esivelayo emva kwelo kuqala wokupa
    umntu idobilityi zontandatu (6d.)

II. Izaziso ekufuneke zingene ixesha lenyanga ezintandatu, mhlaimbi
ezilishumi lina mbini, kovunyelwana ngendawo ezitile ngumshicileli
nomntu lowo ufaka isaziso.

N.B.—Wonke otumela isaziso e Sigidimini makaxele amaxesha afuna eso
saziso sifakiwe, kuba ukuba ixesha alixelwanga soma simi ibe ihamba yona
imali. MSHICILELI.

Isigidimi Samaxosa.

“IMBUMBA YAMA NYAMA.”

IZE nibe yimbumba enjenge yama Nyama.” La ngamazwi ka NTSIKANA ngokwake,
amazwi avela encwabeni eli lele umfo ka GABA. Kula mazwi kuya bonakala
mhlope ukunyaniseka kwamazwi awa tetwa ngu Somakolwa u ABRA­HAM
kwisityebi esiya kutiwa zizazi igama laso yayi ngu DIVES wakudala, waye
bongoza ngesi rara u DIVES, esiti “O Bawo ABRAHAM seluke u tumele u
LAZAROSI lowo “kubazalwana bam mhlayimbi bokolwa nguye ngokuba “evuka
kwaba fileyo.” Waza wateta eliti u SOMAKOLWA “Bano MOSESI no ELIJA
mabeve bona, abasakukolwa nokuba kuvuke bani kwaba fileyo ukuya kuba
shumayeza.” Sibona tina abantu aba ntsundu befana naba hlobo aba baka
DIVES ababesebe zinjubaqa ngohlobo lokuba bangakolwa nalilizwi elivela
emancwabeni. Nga pambi kokuba sike siqubele pambili kuyakufaneleka ukuba
kesi kangelise ukuba yintonina lento ati lowo siti ngu mprofeti wohlanga
olu ntsundu “yimbumba yama Nyama.” Soba sisile ukuba siyakuti pambi kwe
ngwevu zohlanga lwakowetu, silinge uku caza into ayiyo ama Nyama, kuba
ezi ngwevu ziwazi njengoku kanya kwe langa lase mini ama Nyama lawo.
Saka sawabona ama Nyama; negaqa lawo siya lazi: Singati kodwa uku cazela
iqelana elitsba neli ncinane, eli salamkelayo eli pepa laba ntsundu:
Sisakuva kusitiwa ama Nyama la yinyamana apa isaku salela esikumbeni.
Bekusakuti ipalwe ngo koko ikandwe zeide ibe luqilima lwento. Asazi
nokuba asakudla ngokwa hlukaniswa, ngati kodwa akahlukani, yinto elukuni
ebambeneyo. Lamazwi avela kwabafileyo ashiywa—ngendlela ebanzi kwi zizwe
ezabe zimi kweli lakwa Xosa, kude kuye kwe lase Mbo. Ayebekiselele ku
Xosa matanci, wawadela, wawanyatela pantsi kwe nyawo, nento eye hlayo
emva koku yaziwa nangosebeleni. Kanti noko asateta. Tutu! Kaupulapule
elozwi litsho kancinane, li puma kwelo ngcwaba selamila ingca e Twatwa!
Litini?

—“Ize nebe yimbumba enjenge yama Nyama.” Lenze into enye, kade livakala,
lisavakala, lisaya kuvakala xa sonke tina sizukulwana esikoyo soba
sesine xesha pantsi kobandayo. “Ize nibe yimbumba enjenge yama Nyama.”
Ewe. Nokuba sesili ntywilisela emihlalini, nokuba sesi nxila emitayini,
esi siyalo sona simi.

Saka seva ibali ngento eyenziwa yi nkosi yase bunyameni nakuba ibali elo
beseli lidala pakati kwabe Lungu. Nge mfazwe yabe Sutu nama Bulu ase
Free State, ngoku xelelwa kwetu, baza hlula-hlula onyana baka MSHWESHWE;
omnye wakw'ela naba landeli bake kule ntaba, omnye kuleya, njalo njalo.
Ixego uyise ubahlanganisele ndawo nye, wanika owo kuqala umnqayi ukuba
awapule, wauti tyobo ngapandle kwe nkatazo; ubanike bonke ngabanye ngoku
njalo kusihla kwa lonto. Ute ngoku wayibandakanya, waqala kwakowo kuqala
akabi nakuyenza nto; wabanika bonke kwa ngokunjalo kusihla kwa lonto.
Ukupe ezi zifundo kwesi senzo; koba njalo nakuni xa intshaba zenu
nizilwa ngolu hlobo. Akahlalanga xesha lide emva koko wabuba. Kunanamhla
abe Sutu abazange bawalibale lomazwi. Ikwa yimbumba ka NTSIKANA le waye
fundisa isizwe sabe Sutu yona u MSHWESHWE.

Lama bali, madala, kanti noko matsha; siwakunjuzwa zinteto zika MR. SAUL
SOLOMON umhlobo waba mnyama, ote ubom bake bonke wabunikelela abantu aba
mnyama. Igama lika MR. SAUL SOLOMON laziwa kwi sitata sonke esi nomntu
one ntloko pezu kwa magxa ake. Icebo alinika abantu aba ntsundu ke ngoko
asintwana yoku feketa. Intlangano abe nazo zaba mnyama, ngelixa ebe
kweli lase ma Xosini singati zintlanu. Apa e Lovedale uhlangene namaqela
matatu; ngase Qonce namabini. Inteto ate wemisa kayo kakulu kula maqela,
nakuba enye ibiko yile, “wutobeleni umteto ukuba kuko into
enganikolisiyo, balelani ku “Rulumente, ze kuti ukuba impendulo yake
ayinikolisi “balelani kwi Parlamente, ze iti into egqibe kuyo
niyilulamele. Kodwa xa nizenzayo ezi zinto dibanani.” Siyikangele yonke
inteto ka MR. SOLOMON, ibiteta lonto.

Nanko ke amadoda amatatu eteta ntonye; siteta kambe amadoda atanda
uhlanga olumnyama. Ngumfo ka GABA uteta ngo manyano; nenkosi enengqondo
kunene eyayi ngu MSHWESHWE iteta lonto; nomhlobo onga tandatyuzwayo,
nozamela ukunyuka kwabantu bakowetu onjengo MR. SOLOMON uteta kwa
lonto—bonke, nabanye bateta ngo manyano. Izinto ezi ziswa kudibana
azingexelwe mntu nje ngokuba izinto ezimbi eziziswa kukwa hlukana
zingena kuxelwa mntu. Madoda akowetu—Maxosa, Bambo, nabanye, kanibaneke
lenteto. Nidibene ngoku. Uti ke u NTSIKANA yibani yimbumba yama Nyama.
Ningadanga nayilonto akuko temba lokuba noze nihlale kade, boti abo
banga ninga tshabalala bamane olunye uhlanga belu bulala ngolunye,
bayenze lonto zezide zombini intlanga zibube. Anga dwekesha
ongakataliyo, into eyinyaniso yeyokuba kuko oluya kutshabalala. Into
enje wonke otanda bepilile aba ntsundu wo ngenelwa lusizi akuyicinga
kakulu. Bekufuneka ngoku ete umntu ngamnye ngoku kodwa kwezintlanga
zimbini wayitabata

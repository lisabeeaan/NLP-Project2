ISIMEMEZO KWABANEVOTL

NKOSI YAM MHLELI wendaba, kaundifakele lemigca imbalwa kwelo- pepa
lamanene. Ndifuna ukubekisa izwi lesimemezo kumakowetu xnayela novoto
olulungiselelwayo ngezinyanga zonke, kuqale kulonyaka ugqitileyo kude
kuse kulemini ibonakala ukuba yeyokugqibela, olosu- ku lolwe 7 February
1884. Izinto ezibonakalayo ngoluvoto, kubona- kala ukuba inxenye
itengisa uhlanga lonke ngenxa yomtamo wesonka angati ubani awufumane
kulowo angamvotelayo. Okunene ndiyayivu- ma indawo yokuba inxenye
yabantu bakowetu bazinikela ivoti zabo kwintshaba zabo, bengazazi ukuba
zizo, kanti ke kuyimfanelo ngoku kumzi wakowetu ukucela uncedo kwabanye
abantsundu, kuba ndiya- temba ukuba kuyo yonke lemimandla inevoti kuko
amadodana antsu- ndu awafumanayo amapepa ama Ngesi, ati ke ngoko azive
izimvo zalo- madoda kuza kunyulwa kuwo ukusiwa e Palamente. Afanele
ukuti ke ngoko lawo angazi nto awacele lawo aziyo ake eve ngezimvo zama-
doda lawo, ngakumbi ngoku kutetwa ngokuhlutwa kwento ezininzi nje kuti
ezibe zingamalungelo etu. Sifanelwe kunyula indoda eya kusimela
ngapesheya paya, ingengawo amadoda ateta ngokuti iyapalazwa imali
ekutshelwa izikolo zabantsundu, ingengabo kanjalo abati ukuteta ngomntu
ontsundu makusondezwe iqamesi kuye, ingengabo abati ukuteta nangalendawo
yobupolisa, abantu abantsundu mabangayifumani kuba basuke babe neratshi,
bangxamele ukuzenza i Nkosi’ Ke makowetu apete ivoti zawo kumacala omane
maze nibambe zime, zingatengiswa ivoti ngenxa yesonka semini enye, kanti
lona uhlanga qlo lohlala ebukobokeni lomhla ulutengisayo, ewe kude kube
yimini engaziwayo yingqondo yomntu. Tina nganeno apa sozamela amadoda
anje ngo Mr. F. Dyer ukuwavotela, sitshonise utshaba Iwabantu aba-
ntsundu u Goold wase Qonce. Ndiyatshonela. Sova kambe mawetu
singasemoyeni ngomhla we 7th ka February enikwenzileyo.

Womnyaka we 1883.

  BANTU balatiswa ngesi saziso kwezi ziqendu. Zomteto
  ongoku Cokiswa kwamakaya wonyaka we 1883:—

  Isiqendu sa XLVI. Nokuba ngubani omangalayo ukuba
  kungene endlwini yake igosa lopeteyo kulo
  mandla akuwo, nokuba lelogunyazisiweyo
  ngombalo welo gosa, nabani ongxabalaza
  endleleni, otukayo, odubulayo, mblaumbi
  owenzakalisayo ngenteto elo gosa mhlaumbi
  lomntu, xa ahambisa izimiselo zalo mteto,
  woti, efunyenwe enetyala ameiwe sisohlwa-
  yo esinjengokublaula iponti zoshumi, ati
  ukuba akahlauli afakwe entolongweni
  asebenze nzima mblaumbi, ixesha elinge-
  ngapaya kwenyanga zontatu.

  Isiqendu sa XLVII. Ngexesha ekoyo ingqakaqa isipata-
  mandla sinokumenza nabani opakati kwe-
  mida yegunya laso ukuba anike ubungqina
  bokuba ubani lowo waqapula kakuhle, ze
  ukuba ubani lowo ute akabi nabo ubungqina
  bokuba waqatshulwa aze kanjako amangale
  ukuba aqatshulwe, woba sicenge, enetyala
  ngazo zombini ezindawo sokuba ahlaule,
  ngokwala ngakunye, iponti zombini, ati
  ukuba akanakublaula amelwe yintolongo
  ixesha elingengapezulu kwentsuku ezisixenxe

  ALEX. STEWART.
  Kwi Ofisi ye Mantyi, Imantyi.

Alice, 4 Dec., 1883.

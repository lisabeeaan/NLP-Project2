Vol XIV. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, NOVEMBER 1, 1884. No. 184.

Isigidimi Sama-Xosa

NGOM-GQIBELO, NOVEMBER 1, 1884.

“ ISIGIDIMI SAMA-XOSA.”

INYANGA engu October isikumbuza ukuba namhla singene kowe 15 umnyaka
wobudala betu. Akukazanga kubeko pepa lakwa Xosa elipumelele iminyaka
engako kuninzi oluseke lwalingwa. No “Vuk’ endleleni’’ abasebeke bavela
sesiko nje, bapelela emoyeni. Oko kutsho sibulela abahlobo abate bema
ngakuti ngokusixasa ngenkozo abapike ngokuzitumela iminyaka yonke,
sibacela ukuze bazingise ngokwenjenjalo. Kuko iqela elikulu kubafuncli
betu elisasibamba ishelenana ngazine, nangantlanu. Ewe, zingati
zidelekile ezomalana xa ingulowo nalowo, esiti kodwa sakuziquka zibe
kufupi nama £200 eponti, esilicela ke ngoko elo qelana ukuba lizitumele
msinya ukuze umsebenzi ungatinteleki.

Mhla satiywa igama ngo 1870, kwatiwa kufuziswa umsebenzi esasifunwa
ukuze sizinikele kuwo wokuba sibe Sigidimi Sakwa Xosa “sokumela indaba
yezikolo zonke, “ nelungelo yabemi beli lizwe.” Ubuvuvu bokux'umisa eli
iqela pezu kweliya, yaye ingenguwo umyalezo wobugitshimi betu, ngalo
lonke ixesha indawo yomnqweno wetu— “ ilungelo yabemi beli lizwe
”—lingapazanyiswayo. Asinakutsho ukuti oko sikuzalise njengoko
besingatandayo, ngokukodwa kule iminyaka yakutsha nje. Sizilungiselele
ke ngoko ukubuya nge qondo, ize sizizalise imfanelo ze Sigidimi
esikolekileyo, esokuteta oko sikutunyiweyo ngo sulungeko, nango
kungoyiki, xa konakalwayo; esoyibona ngotelekelelo ingozi ezungula umzi
wakowaso, nabemi belozwe, ize sibavuse ; ingozi ezinje ngo kupenukela
kokulahlekisayo ; inteto zocuku ; nobu vilikitshane obungeni.

NJE ngoko abafundi betu site komnye umhlati kwa kweli pepa, saba netuba
lokubenzela indaba malunga no “ Tyelelo e Kapa,” ngokukodwa mayelana
nezinto zo “Mboniso,” —singa ke ngoko singabuye siti kulo umhlati
sibavise ngento nati esiyifumana emapepeni ngohlobo ekutetwa ngalo nge
mpahla eyenzelwa lo mboniso ngabantsundu.

Ute u Dr. DALE, U Mongameli we Mfundo yale Koloni, kwinteto ayenze
ekubekweni kwe litye lesiseko, lendlu yesi kolo somtinjana e Wynberg,
nge 15 ku October, waquba wada weza kukankanya no “ Mboniso,” ob’uvalwe
ke nge pezolo. Akuba lapo wavakala eteta lamazwi— “ Uya kolwa ukuba
wonke oke waya e ‘ Mbonisweni ’ “ apo, wazibonela ngawake amehlo impahla
eyenziwe Indodana, no mtinjana ontsundu ofunda kwizikolo ezinje “ ngoma
Lovedale, oma Rini, kwanezinye, ukuba ’ntle “ kwayo, akanakuba abe
akafikelwanga yingcinga yokuba “isisifundo kuti—[eteta ke abamhlope
abab’ empulapula.] “ Ukuba xa angaba umntu ontsundu, erwada njalo,
enalomfundwana yake ingepi,—unokwenza izinto ezingade “ zibukwe kangaka
luluntu, makube kungaba kangakananina esingakulindela kwa betu onyana ne
ntombi, xa “ sibaqeqeshele kokulungileyo ! ” (Wavakala uduma umzi.)
Uqube ke wenjenjalo.

Sakuba siyifundile tina lenteto ka Dr. DALE sifumene inkutazo enkulu.
Sifumene kanjako nesifundo esitsha

esise sokuba akuko matanda nati ukuba site asema ndawonye, ukuba lute
udodana nentombi zohlanga lwa kowetu ezise mashishinini, nezise
mfundweni, zaqubela pambili ekwazini; singaya side sifikelele kwindawo
engapezulu le kunoko sinokucinga ngako tina okwa ngoku. Into kodwa
emayiqondwe, yeyokuba umzi ontsundu mauke wenze kulile ukuzibekela
endaweni yokuti ngoku sinokuzibiyela ubuhlanti betu,—shenxa wena “
MFUNDISI.” Xa abahlobo betu abamhlope besavuma ukusikokela, nokusibeka
endleleni eya kusiqeqeshela ebugciseni bokuzimela kamva, yimfanelo yake
wonke olunqwenelela impumelelo uhlanga olumnyama ukuba abakutaze abo “
bafundisi,” nemizi abayimisileyo, nabo bahlobo bavanayo nomsebenzi
owenziwa ngabo; bangabi zinto zokumane zinyhorwa nge ngqwiniba yinxenye,
zikatywa ngenyawo ngabanye abati bafundile kwabantsundu,—kanye aba
bakutyulwa ngonyamezelo nenkatazo enkulu ngabo bafundisi, nalo mizi
namhla bayinyhorayo.

Ngokubekisele kule ndawo, sibalatisa abafundi betu kwinteto
esibaguqulela kwi Christian Express yenyanga edluleyo, nge “ Mfundo
kwaba Mnyama.” Kuko inkolo pakati kwabanye abati bafundile kumzi
ontsundu, enx'amele ukulahlekisa amakowayo ngokuti imfundo iyehliswa
kuti belo bala, okanye kuyakohliswa ngaba bati balinga ukulunyusa ngayo
olo luhlanga. Masiti ukuzekelisa enye yezo ndawo zenjenjalo mhlaumbi yi
Lovedale. Linye esingapendula ngalo, leli Akuko nto injalo e Lovedale,
nokuba kungaba kunjanina kwezinye indawo. Lomzi uko nje ukolo ukunyusa
uhlanga olumnyama lwase Afrika; izakiwo namalinga akade esenziwa, nasa
xatalazelwa ukwenziwa nanamhla, anq'ina ukuba abo bateta ngokunjalo
babukuqa uninzi ukuba lulahleke; uninzi olungekezi kuzihlomlela
kulomalungelo alomzi, neminye efana nawo, abase bete bona ababukuqi abo,
bawaxamla lomalungelo; engoku bate ngcu kwindulana zokupakama kwabo
namhla besiti: “Iwu! pulapulani kuti apa nina! ” babe kodwa beyigushile
indawo yokuba—“TINA SESIWADLE KADE AWENKONAZANA.”

I MP A WAN A.

Inxenye, umhlaumbi inkoliso ingaba seyivile ngendawo yokuba i Pesheya
kwe Nciba elaba Tembu ne Griqualand East zitatyatelwe kwaselu laulweni
Iwe Koloni, into ke leyo esingaziyo ukuba boyitanda abaninzi abebekade
belangazelela ukubuyela elulaulweni lwe Nkosazana. Nto tina singati
mabasebeyenza abo kukuxolisa intliziyo, nokulinga ukuziqelanisa nolaulo
abakulo ngenxa zonke.

Lomlambo uli Pesheya kwe Nciba ngumlambo odume, novakala kakulu ngohlobo
ozibeke ngalo imantyi zawo. Namhla sinenye incwadana nakuba imfutshane,
esibalisela ngohlobo awamkelwa ngalo u Captain Blyth mhlezinikweni waya
kufika kwelo epuma pesheya. Njengoko nozifundela, kwangati lomhla
kungena i Ruluneli, apo ke sikuqonda kona ukubekwa nokutandwa kwe
mantyi, nase zimantyini u Capt. Blyth okade esenza ukulunga okukulu kuma
Mfengu apesheya kwe Nciba.

Ngokumayelana nenteto ekwincwadi yombaleli wetu u Lilazingasulwa,
singati tina okukokwetu asiyiboni, singayiva nokuyiva inteto yomzalwana
u Kakaza, onga yena kungasongwa izandla kumana kusitiwa—“ Yinina Nkosi ”
kungeko malinga enziwayo, into ke leyo ebubudenge, kuba lowo ungazamiyo
engasayi kuncedwa napakade. Nto iya kwenzeka kukusuka atshabalale
kwakuyo lontlalo ati yena unga angakutshwa kuyo. Ke ngoko siti asiboni
ukuba sifumane sizalise indawo ngencwadi ezivela kwelo nene ezingenanto
yona zibika yona; esinga ke ngoko anganeliswa u Kakaza yile yanamhla
incwadi.

E LUSUTU.

ULULO OLUBI E TWEEFONTEIN.

KUPUME nge 14 January 1881 mayela no 1 o’clock a.m. umkosi wamadoda ali
1,800 ene kanunu ezimbini upumake e kampini.

Ungekabi mgama mde ukusuka e kampini uqutyulwe kumacala onke nje ngokuba
uhamba, libubu labe Sutu elalincatame kwimimango engqongileyo. Kubulewe
kumkosi we Burghers amadoda asitoba.

Ulwe kakuhle umkosi ekutiwa yi C.M.R. onge wenzakele ukuba ubunge
njenjalongo.

Ulwe wonke umkosi, bekisa pambili elutshabeni, lwagxotwa kakubi.

Inani lilonke lababuleweyo li 13 lamadoda ndawonye nenkosi yomkosi
ekutiwa yi Commandant Erasmus womkosi wama Burghers ase Somerset East.
No Sergeant Major Hooper we 2nd C.M.Y. no Wallace wase Aliwal North.
Kubulewe abantsundu becala letu bababini kwa ngxwelerwa ama 20 abelungu.

Kubulewe umlungu ogama lingu Mr. Smith obe ngumrwebi e kampini apo,
ubulew'e enamanxeba ezikali amashumi ma 38.

Kuvakala ukuba wangxwelerwa u Mr. John Blaine kwa ngayo lemini
kwakusiliwa nabe Sutu. Kubuye kwavakala ukuba ubub'ile. Waye ngunyana
wamazibulo ka Mr. Blaine lo waziwa kakulu e Koloni.— Watchman.

E BATENJINI.

U COMMANDANT FROST uxela oku, ese Prentjesberg Jan. 23.—U Gecelo no
Mhlontlo inkosi zaba Tembu, nama pakati abo bazinikele izolo. Unyana ka
Stokwe uyakufika namhla nje, banezi xobo zabo nenkomo, baye besiti ama
hashe abo atshabalele onke ngenxa yokudiniswa ngumsebenzi. Abafazi baya
kupiwa ukudla bavunyelwe ukuba baye ku Matanzima. Abantu bebetintelwe
ngumlambo oyi Mooi River; baya kungena namhla. I patrol zipume emini
nangokuhlwa, zitimbe ama 400 enkomo zabulala i 16 lamadoda aselu
tshabeni.

INKATAZO ZE NDLELA

SISAND’ ukubaliselwa imbali ngobe sand’ uku hamba nenqwelo esinga
kulamacala ase ma Qonce. Uti:—Kwenye yetola ezi kulo ndlela, ute kuba
inqwelo zake bezimkokele, waye yena ekwele ngasemva kwazo, wati zakufika
kuyo

wapuma umgcini wayo wazi nqanda ukuba zime. Ukauleze wati makaziyeke uza
kuyi batala imali ye tola. Ute ngokuswela imali engumwangalala warola
iponti ehlangeneyo, wayinika umnini tola, ote kanti naye akanayo
itshintshi. Ufune umqubi nqwelo ukuba ashiye inqwelo ezi apa, ake aye
kufuna itshintshi. Omnye walile ukuyenza lonto, esiti nguwe obufanele
ukuba nayo. Kweso situba kufike ezinye inqwelo zomntu olelinye ibala
kanti naye ukwa kwesi simo, kodwa yena uyekwe wadlula. Umhlobo wetu
loyena esemi kwelilizwe, kutetwa kwalanto kuye, kongezwa ngeliti ukuba
akalumki woposana nayo neponti le. Ute omnye “ungandipanga kambe, kodwa
uyakuzibonela.” Ute ukupendula omnye wasel’ etuka emgxotela ngapandle ko
mnyango,—kwaba kukupela kwamazwi oxolo ke oko ngamacala omabini. Isipelo
ude umnini nqwelo wayi fumana iponti yake, koko ushiye ityatanga layo,
ukuba libe sibambiso sezo shelenana zintatu zetola

Efikile e Qonce uyokufuna ifulara, kwenye indlu, kutiwe ayiko eyabantu
abamnyama; kwenye aku pendulwanga ngamlomo, koko kutunyelwe isihlangu
ukuba siwenze lomsebenzi.

Udlulele e Aliwal North apo ati ubone ummangaliso. Nje ngokuba wemka apa
yena ene pass akayi sebenzisanga kwelozwe. Kodwa into ayibonileyo kukuba
umlungu apo kufuneka ehamba nge pass. Ute akubuzisa, kwatiwa lonto
ibangwe bubuninzi baba baleka emkosini; nga wake amehlo ubone ama 24
amakonxwa amhlope enze eso siposo!

UKUZIPATA E TYALIKENI.

NDIYA bulela ukuba usapo Iwakwa Xosa kubonakala ukuba luya yitanda indlu
ka Tixo. Bambani lusapo lwakowetu kuba kuyo yonke lento ininzi, akuko
igqite ukukonza u Tixo. Ebenyuswa endaleni umntu ngokuhamba futi komkulu
kanti woda apelele ebupakatini. Masihambele futi kwi komkulu lama
komkulu sasiya kuti nati kwa lapa kude kube kwelo lizayo, sizuze
inyweba.

Kodwa au! nazi indawana zintatu. 1. Ukungena emva kwe xesha etyalikeni.
2. Ukuti umntu xa angenayo abete kunene ngesihlangu etyityimba exela
umkweta. 3. Ukuti yakupuma ityalike ibe sisisi intshayi pambi kwendlu ka
Tixo. Zimbi ke zontatu ezondawana.

Kanize ngentsimbi yokuqala, iti ilila eyesibini nibe senishushu kade,
he, “se ni moyeni,” nje ngo Mpostile u Johane. Ati engena umfundisi luti
udodana ne ntombi ze kwayile lumamkele nge culo. Ibe ke indlu yona
seyizele, bonke behleli ngendawo zabo. Kaniti yakupuma niti runqu niye
emakaya, ati notya icuba azenzele kude paya ebugameni. Ndigqibe kani
yilungise kwango Mgqibelo imali yesitya ningamana nihlukuhla intloko.

Kuseko enye indawo—lendawo yokuba ulutsha lungazitengi incwadi zelizwi
lika Tixo ikwambi. Bambalwa abafana abane Bible, bambalwa naba nama
culo. Ityeya izele zingubo kodwa, indawo yencwadi ize. Tengani incwadi
msani ukulihilizela ilifa elingaka. ZITENGENI INCWADI.

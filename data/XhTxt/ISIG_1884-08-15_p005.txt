Thursday, 31 July, 1884—Namhlanje sive ezokuba Ama Slamsi afumenene
namanye amaqili malunga nokutengisa kwawo ngeminenga yawo. Ute u Mr.
Holmes, “Well I'll give you £150 for the two whales—if some one else
offers more let me know before you sell.” Aye ku Mr. Rous, wati, “I’ll
give £150 too.” Babuyele ku Holmes besiti, “Oh you can have them for
£150.” Ute “Why can’t you get more?’ Bati “Well we—oh not exactly
more”—but—unqumle u Holmes ngeliti, “Well in that case I do not feel
justified in offering more than £140.” Alle wereld, de Engelschman ze
maaters is dood—Staka-filah. (Liyekeni Ingesi ngamalahle anotutu).

IHAMBISO YABA NTSUNDU E-TRANSKEI.

NDIYAKUQALA pezulu kuba Tembu ndiye kuma elwandle kuma Ngqika. Ilizwe
laba Tembu bangase Queenstown ude uye kuma nge Tsomo, lilizwe elihle
kunene elizeleyo ngamanzi anokwenziwa imijelo, utabata Umnxe, i Guba,
Amacweba, u Tsembeyi, Izaqadala, Amacibi, ne Bolotwa kunye no Bengu ne
Seplan, angabona ukuba indawo zintle zizele ngamanzi anokwenziwa
imijelo. Kodwa ebusika lilizwe elibanda kunene, noko ililizwe elihle,
elilunga igusha kakulu nezinye impahla ehlotyeni. Manditi aba Tembu
bayalahlekelwa ngokukulu ebusika kukufa kwempahla ngamaqwa.

NGEZINDLU EZIFANELEKILEYO : Aba Tembu baqubela pambili kanye isikolo
sakwa Ndlovukazi (Lesseyton) singu mzekelo olungileyo. Indlu ye Titshala
yakona u (Mr. J. Z. Tantsi, isisivato esihle kona. Izindlu zamanene o
Messrs. P. Mahonga, S. Sigenu, K. M. Tshokotsha nezinye izidwangube zalo
macala zingumzekelo omhle kanye.

UKULIMA : Xa umntu aqukayo aba Tembu bangapambili ekulimeni ingqolowa,
ozihabile nezinye izinto. Kodwa ngabanye basekude ukuba babizwe ngokuba
ngabalimi, xa umntu akangela ukulunga kwelizwe labo ngamanzi, ngesekuko
abalimi abanokusebenzisa omashini. Aba Tembu basalimela ukudla,
abalimeli kutengisa.

UKUKWEBA : Aba Tembu basemva kanye ngokurweba ngati baya koyika kanye
ukurweba.

ILIZWI : Andiqondi ukuba kuko into endingayipaulayo ngokuhamba, kwelizwi
andikaboni andikeva nokuva ngagqoboko lutile lunokubalulwa ngecala laba
heyideni. Emveni kwazo zonke ezindawo ndizipaulayo ndigqiba ngelokuba,
aba Tembu bahambisa kakuhle kanye Inga kodwa kungabako kwa umoya
wokurweba kancinane kubo, batate indawo epakamileyo eluhlangeni. Indlu
yabalimi yabo e Cala iludumo eluhlangeni enditemba ukubona izinto
zinkulu eziya kucetywa zenziwe kona.

AMAMFENGU.

ILIZWE lama Mfengu ka Blyth lilizwe elihle, elinendawo ezingajikwa
imijelo, kodwa alilunge njenge laseba Tenjini. Amamfengu axinene kakulu
ngakuma, impahla emfutshane inge iba ilunga kanye, kodwa ngenxa
yokudlelana, nangenxa yamaqwa obusika iti ingapumeli kakuhle. Amamfengu
ayalahlekelwa kakulu kukufa kwempahla. Ndazi elinye inene elalahlekelwa
ngamashumi asitoba amatakane egusha ngobunye ubusika. Nakuba Amamfengu
ngemfuyo engapambili e Transkei apa, kodwa ngenxa yokuxinana impahla
ibeka emva kanye.

NGEZINDLU EZIFANELEKILEYO: Ndingati Amamfengu ayaqala ayazaka ngokukulu.

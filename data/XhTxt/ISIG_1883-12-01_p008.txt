ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1883.

INCAZO YEZIBALO.

MR. MHLELI we- Sigidimi.—Nkosi yam kaundincede undifakele lamazwana am
kwelipepa lamanene, ndifuna ukupendula u Mr. Bell B. Kota, ndicela ukuba
uzundinyamezele nkosi yam ngokuba ndiyakugqita umteto wako ngokwenza
amazwana amade.

Mr. B. B. Kota mzalwana wam uti wena kuko isimanga sokuteta kwentombi
ngelizwi lika-Tixo. Mnake andiyikangeli ngokuba isimanga ngokuba akabeko
amabala anjengala enziwa zezintombi. Abafazi base-Korinte, babetandaza
beshumayela, waza umpostile wateta indawo yanye yokuti: Akulungile ukuba
beme ngentloko xa baprofiteshayo, akatshongo ukuti bangamaxoki; wati
kodwa lihlazo ukuba umfazi eme ngentloko xa ashumayelayo. Kanjalo
ndiyivile indawo ondelatise ngayo ku-Timote, uzundive kakuhle ke
unyanisile ukuti kutiwa: “ Andivumeli umfazi ukuba afundise, nokuba abe
namandla pezu kwendoda.” Umpostile wayeteta ngesimo sentlalo semvelo
yomfazi esiti: “ makangapati igunya pezu kwendoda, ngokuba usisidalwa
esibutataka, ngokuba no-Satana wakangela wasibona esona sidalwa
sibutataka, angasikohlisa kalula, wayake kumfazi wamkohlisa.” Umpostile
waye ngavingceli umfazi ngakwizinto zika-Tixo zenkolo, wayesenzela ukuba
aze ati nokuba umfazi ude wakusihlalo sokufundisa, nokuteta kwabanye
abantu, aze angenyukwa ligunya, neratshi pezu kwamadoda. Waye ngamnqandi
umfazi nokuba ukuba aze ati nokuba wamkele amandla amqubayo angateti.
Hayi! Masilumkele ukuba singatinteli into elungileyo. Kanjalo uti
akuboni nto enditi yifunde ku-Joweli, naku-Zenzo. Uti u-Joweli uteta
ngo-Yesu, uti kumhla kwehla umoya oyingcwele, uti, uti umpostile u
Peter, kukuzaliseka kwalonto yatetwa ngu Joweli. Nditi unyanisile, kodwa
ke mna nditi: “ Sisiqalo eso sokufika kwalonto yatetwa ngu-Joweli.”
Ayikafiki ekufezekeni kwayo, zisamana ukufika nangezimini, inqubeko zayo
asazi nokuba akaqutywa yiyona lomantombazana, ukufezekiswa kwayo, u-Tixo
angasebenza ngendlela esingayaziyo, mhlaumbi kuhle umoya omkulu ngapezu
kwemini zabapostile kude kutete nomncinane, sisalindele izinto ezinkulu.
Kanjalo kuko intombi ezine zomvangeli ongu-Filipi (Philip)
ezazishumayela kuko nencwadi zabafazi e-Bayibileni. Eka Bute (Ruth), Eka
Esitere (Esther) zonke ezindawo zibonisa ukuba maku- ngatintelwa mntu,
kanjalo siti isitembiso: onyana nentombi bayaku- shumayela, kutiwa
izicaka nezicakakazi, amankazana, bayakushumayela kwakufika umoya
uzupendle kakuhle apo ungaboninto. U-Nongqause wabonakala kwase kuqaleni
ngemisebenzi yake ukuba ulixoki uze kubulala abantu, wati
makutshatyalaliswe inkomo, kulahlwe amazimba. Make ndime apo.

A. RADAS.

Heald Town, 3 November, 1883.

ABABUBILEYO BAYA EKAYA.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI we-Sigidimi ndincede undifakele lamazwana ambalwa
kwelopepa lamanene akowetu lendaba. Tina e Peelton apa sisezinyembezini
ngokumkelwa kwetu zizihlobo zetu ezibini: u Zuma- na Magabela obube nge
21st ka October lo, eshiya usapo oluninzi Iwake.—Kodwa siyabulela ukuba
eye kupumla. Site sisalilela lowo sahleIwa yenye into enkulu, ngokumka
komhlobo wetu, indodana entsha u John L. Lobese Nginza; obengomnye
wabatabata kwa Isigidimi esi. Lendodana yazalwa apa nge 1859 ngo June,
namhla ibikumnyaka wa 24 nenyanga ezintlanu. Ibingunyana omkulu ka
Lobese no Ellen Conta Nginza. Ebuncinaneni bake wafunda apa engumfana
opapile- yo, otembekileyo, nonika idinga lokuba uyakuba ngumntu
ofanelekile- yo. Ukulile wada wafika ngemfundo yake endaweni yokuba abe
ngumncedisi esikolweni apa pantsi kuka Mr. Ross Kota. Emveni kwexesha
elite uye kuba yi Teacher yosapo e Ntsikizini kwesinye sezikola zika
Rev. J. Harper. Ute emva kwexesha elitile efundisa kona wapuma waya
kufundisa Ezeleni kwesinye sizikolo zika Rev. B. Ross, ude nalapo wabuya
wapuma ngokungahlaulwa ngumzi, weza kuhlala ekaya. Lomfana ngumfana ote
ngexesha ebesekaya apa wawuquba umsebenzi ka Tixo ngokutembeka,
nangokomelela okukulu ; eququza, kunjalo njalo nguye yedwa kwintanga
zake obekulomsebenzi wokushumayela. Ute emva kwexesha elitile wemka apa
waya kufuna umsebenzi e-Monti; wafumana umsebenzi wokuhambisa incwadi
zabantu ezipuma kwi (Post Office) ndlu yeletali zabantu. Akahlalanga
xesha lide kona wahliwa sisifo, esati samgodusa weza ekaya. Efikile apa
uhleli inyanga enye enecesine, esiva ubuhlungu noko kubonakala uku- ba
ngumfo ka-Tixo. Ubungafika uye kumkuza kumfo opete i Bible yake efunda,
uti wakuteta naye akupendule ngamazwi entutuzelo. He lomfanake wanduluka
apa esiya ekaya ukuya kupumla ngomhla wesihlanu evekini, nge 26 ka
October. Sinovuyo kengoko ukuti ngaye akafile, ulele elinde ixesha
lokuvuswa kwake kunye nabanye. “ Lindani ngoko nitandaza.” Masishiye
ngeliti: Kufa wena akuketi —“ Omnis aetas mors communis esse.”

WALTER B. RUBUSANA.

Peelton, 30 Oct., 1883.

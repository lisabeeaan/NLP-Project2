Umhleli we Sigidimi, esingati ngelinye ngum Gcogeli, mhlaumbi yi Editor,
uquba umsebenzi onzima kweli pepa lama Xosa, ngapezu kwabazi Editors
zamapepa ama Ngesi. Bona babala besazi ukuba abapakeli ’nto zifa
yindlala, zingena kutya kumbi. Babala besiti “ wotya olambileyo, ayeke
ohlutiyo—wolitabata ipepa ovisisana nati, ati ongavisisani nati aliyeke
kuba amapepa ngo NINZI.” Bawupalaza njalo ke umoya wabo, bagaule ngapa,
bavune ngapa, bahlwayele i Roses ngapa, ameva ngapa —besazi ukuba woti
oxakene nomqa kwelabo aye kwi Argus nokuba yi Mercury nokuba yi “ Nkwe-
nkwezi ” ka Velem unomgogwana wetyalike yase Gqume. Akunjalo ke ku
Mhleli we Sigidimi. Yena ufana nomka “ Napakade ” into ka Buluneli xa
lontokazi yayimana ivuka ngesifingo ipekela imfebenge ezilambileyo
zempatalala zo Mhlakaza no Nongqause uwuca Iwe papa. Ezimpaza zayinqika
tu-tu-tu iminga, neqwa, namaqobo, nemisenge, nononca, nenongwe, kwada
kwavela Lawo lentaba lati, “ Kaselani kwa Chalisi he, ukuze sibe siko
nje isisalela sembewu ka Tshiwo. Kwakungaseko tambo linobukukuzo
kumatambo enkomo ezawa mhla kwaxelwa. Aye ngaseko amazimba eziseleni,
namasi ezimvabeni. Wati umntu—“ Huk! kwa Buluneli.” Nxatsho ke! Sinjalo
ke namhla Isigidimi—O Kwezi no Ndaba no Sibuto Samavo, bafa. Au ! kwada
kwafa ne Lawo lika Gqirashe u Mwesile—ofe nomtamo wemirumo aberunywe
ngayo. Simi sodwa ke Isigidimi. Simele ezimfebenge zimapango ate lambu ;
simele lanto iza ikemezela ivela e Dayimani isiti ndikumbule kwi:—

LOVEDALE.

Andinabani ngapandle kwake, sendinqenqele inceba yake - azi ndoba yinina
ukuba “ undipendule kalukuni ? ” U Buncatu uze esinxekenxeke exakwe
yimpangalasane yamanxila, aze ku Mhleli esiti makancede akalime Iwafa
uhlanga! U Mshumayeli uza etwele izandla entloko ambete intlakantlaka
ekala esiti, “Ngubanina okoliweyo yintshumayelo yetu? ” Nceda Mhleli—

‘Ndiswele imilomo

  Ayaba iliwaka Ibonga ngentlokoma Inceb’ engakanana.’

Nceda nkosi yam Mhleli! Umfana ofuna intombi u A. B. into ka C. uza ku
Mhleli emapika, efune intokazi esukileyo egadeni elusi, &c. 0 M. P., E.
Lamatye, Wauchope, & Co., no Gqub’ emswaneni o Hadi Waseluhlangeni,
benza ibubu pezu kwentloko yo Mhleli. He abanye beza bezinyidilili
bentywizisa besiti yo ! yo ! safa ngu Wauchope, yo ! safa ngu E.
Lamatye. Anxhenxheze u Mhleli—“Tutu tata, sokwenzela ngenyanga ezayo ngo
“ Pau.” Au ! sibala nina ? Kwanele sakuti lo Mhleli we Sigidimi unjenge
sikwabakazi esidala eti imbali sasihlala esihlangwini sinento eninzi
yosapo. Kutiwa besiba ngafunzela ngo wuca—“Ma ndongeze—Ma ndisafuna! ”
Ade umdunakazi atabate uswazi, aqumise ngalo esiti—“ Hambani niye
kulala—lalani.” Yeka ke ukuya kungena esihlangwini. Ukutsho oko ke,
asina kuhlutiswa sonke maxa onke. Iko imihla ayakuti ocela isonka atiwe
gelekeqe nge xuluba ezi ‘ Mpawaneni.’ Asideli noko. Asincami noko. Kuba
no Ngqika ebehamba ne nkonjane abehla ati minxi ngayo esiquleni sendoda,
kanti ulola impi yake ukuze abe nomqamelo mhla ilanga lipuma
entshonalanga. Unjalo u Mhleli we Sigidimi Sama-Xosa, ngu—

yahlangana i Uitenhage “ Good-Hope C.C.” vs. Uitenhage “ Ever-Ready
O.C.” Kumhlaba omhle kunene we “ Good-Hope.” Kwenziwa iqashiso labaza
kuqala bangene, lawela iqashiso i “ Ever-Ready ” yangena kuqala i
Good-Hope, yamelana nebola zika Edeross no Galim, akubanga mzuzu yapela
i 1st Innings ye Good-Hope nge 12. Yangena ke i Ever-Ready seyilindwe
kade ngu W Nkomo no W. Davis, kube mzuzwana bemi yapela eyabo i 1st
Innings nge 35 runs. Yapinda i Good-Hope yangena yahlanganisa i 29
yapela. Yangena kan jalo ne Ever-Ready yapela ihlanganise i 16, kodwa ke
noko uloyiso lwaba lolwe Ever-Ready nge 10 runs. Amagama abadlali

PAKATI KWA BASEBENZI NAMA NGESI ASE LOVEDALE.

Ingaba mhlayimbi inxalenye yabafundi betu ayazi ukuba ibola yinto
edlalwayo apa e Lovedale ngenx’ enokuba lite ipepa lipuma kona akwaze
kuke kubeko nomgca lo ngokumayela nayo, kwabe sekufuti singenisa
imidlalo yendawo ezite qelele. Kwabanjalo ke singati ibola yinto edlalwa
kunene apa e Lovedale. E Simnareni kodwa udonana luzahlule ndidi ne
—yeyama Ngesi, yeyabafundayo abantsundu (Oriental C. O.), yeya Malawo
(True Blue C. C.), yeyabasebenzayo (Brotherly Unity C. C.) Eyesihlanu
yeyase Location (Gaika’s Imperial O. C.) neyase Dikeni ke (Alice).
Imigqibelo le ke ezi club zabantsundu ziyankalana. Ngumnqweno wezi club
sizibaluleyo ngentla ukuke ziqubisane nezezinye indawo ezinje ngo
Mitwaku, Qonce, Rini, &c. ngemini yokuzalwa kwe Nkosazana (Queen’s
Birtday) ezayo (kuba kupela kwemini enenkululeko kwindawo zonke) ukuba
izipata-mandla zalomzi ziyavuma Sobuye sibe nenyaniso yalondawo kodwa.
Namhla singenisa umdlalo obe ulapa e Lovedale pakati kwe Brotherly Unity
neyama Ngesi alapa evangwe ngawe qela lesibini (Second Eleven) lase
Alice ngom-Gqibelo, February 23. Lomdlalo ke

Ndinosizi ukuti indimbane yamanenekazi namanene ayiyazi lento ikukufeza isitembiso. Nditsho kuba zininzi izitembiso ekade bezenza zebangazifezi. Uti wakubuza bati ukuba abatsho ukuti silibele baxele izixakeko ezingenakumtinte- la umntu ofuna ukugeina izwi lake.

Ndati mhla ndatumela amagama abatembisi be Society. Abanye ndiyazi ukuba banika amagama odwa ezabo iziqu azisoze zibeko. Kanti oko kuyinene. Kuwo onke amalinge esike sawenza ayo abanye abazange babonakale.

Emva koko abuya atembisa ukuba oke aye kubulisa u-Captain Blyth e-Ntlambe, ngosuku olwamiswayo, kwabako bababini qa yati yonke indambane yaxakeka yalibala. Bubunenena obo mhlaimbi bubunenekazina ?

Siyakohlwa nokuzibuza ezizinto kulama petshana esixosa angenamsebenzi kuba abanye tina apa bapakame kangangokuba bangabi nanto nalamapetshana. Kodwa batsho bengawatati nawama Ngesi. Inkoliso yoyika ukulahlekwa zipeni zayo, ifu- na ukuwafumana felefele. Kanti akuko nto imayo ngapandle kwendleko. Ndiyabulisa batembisi bakuti.

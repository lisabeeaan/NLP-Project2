LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, MARCH 1, 1875.

No. 54

Isigidimi Samaxosa.

ALEXANDER McDIARMID.

OMNYE wabafundisi abango Bawo uye ekulaleni kwake. U-Mr. McDiarmid,
ogama laziwayo ngabaninzi ubube ngolwe Sibini ngokuhlwa ngo 9 ka
February.

Wazalelwa E-Scotland ngo December 1801 waza weza kweli lase Africa
ngomnyaka we 1827, esiza “ engumfundisi wemisebenzi” nomdala weliqela
bekutiwa Liqela lase Glasgow lokutumela abafandisi (Glasgow Missionary
Society). Banduluka kwelakowabo bebane. U-Mr. Mc­Lachlan wabuya wabuyela
kwase Scotland engekabi naxesha likulu apa. U-Mr. Chal­mers, owaziwa
ngokuba ngumfundisi wase Gwali E-Tyume, seleminyaka itile abubayo, wemka
esemtsha, ubudala eyiminyaka 44 ; osekoyo kubo ngu Mr. Weir walapa
E-Lovedale, umfo owaziwayo ngokubekeka kwake osauqubayo umsebenzi
nangoku ngayo yonke intliziyo yake. Ixesha ke bekweli lizwe ababafundisi
singati ngabagabuli bezigcau, lise lipantse ukuba yiminyaka emashumi
mane anesibozo, ixesha abate ngalo bahlelwa zizinto ezini-nzi, babona
okuhle nokubi.

Ngomnyaka 1828, U-Mr. Ross ose Mgqakwebe ngoku, no Mr. McDiarmid, lo
simlilelayo namhla, bacelwa ukuba baye kumisa isikolo esitsha ekwatiwa
ukubizwa kwaso yi Balfour, pakati kwe sizwe sika Maqoma, oko sisese
Katala. Kwati ke kodwa kwehla inkatazo ezitile ezabangela ukude abantu
bashenxe kulo ndawo, neso sikolo siyekwe ngomnyaka 1829.

Ngomnyaka olandela lowo kwanyulwa U-Mr. Chalmers no Mr. McDiarmid ukuba
baye kumisa isikolo esitsha, kwa Sandile. Baya kukwenza okwenene ke oko
basibiza ngokuti yi Burnshill. Wablala kuso eso sikolo U-Mr. McDiarmid
ixesha elingapezu kweminyaka emashumi mabini,

into eke yampazamisa izimfazwe zodwa, eye 1835 neye 1846 neye 1851.
Ngalo lonke eloxesha lokuba kona kwake apo simfumana epete ukufundisa,
nokushumayela, nokwaka. Nguye owaye ngumkngeli nomaki wendonga zokuqala
zalomzi wase Lovedale ngomnyaka 1841.

Emva kwale mfazwe yokugqibela, ngo- mnyaka 1853 kwabuya kwamiswa esinye
isikolo esitsha, waza U-Mr. McDiarmid wanyulwa ukuba aye kusipata,
umsebenzi wokusikangela washiyelwa kuye yedwa. Uhlale ke kwesosikolo
kwade kwalusuku lokugqibela lokubako kwake apa emhlabeni. Uhliwe sisifo
xa kuteteka ngokuba enziwe umfundisi obekwe izandla.

Umsebenzi ka Mr. McDiarmid E-Mc- Farlane wabehla wane mpumelelo kwase
ntloko. Laba liqela elikulu elimvumayo Umsindisi, liyi boniswa inyaniso
yintshu- mayelo yake.

Zade kodwa zafika imini zembandezelo, ezixela ukuza kwamatinzi okuhlwa.
Kwati ngomnyaka 1871 we Jubili yase Burnshill wahliwa sisifo esamti
ngcumbu kwaba noko kubonakala ukuba siyinto. Sati noko si- mpeteyo
akavuma ukuwuyeka umsebenzi yade yapela iminyaka emibini. Kwati ngo
September 1873 yabuba ngequbuliso inko- sikazi yakwake. Wati kuba sase
simdlile kakulu isifo kwanzima kuye nokuya kufike lela encwabeni
lenkosikazi yakwake kwa- funeka ukuba ahambe exasiwe. Emva koko
akabuyanga abe nako ukuzihambela ngokwake. Kuse njalo kwaqezuka iqela
elikulu leremente yake laya E-Ndenxa, kwabonakala ngokungati uya citeka
um- sebenzi wake. Noko yena U-Mr. McDiar­mid wayiqonda ukuba lonto
yokumka kwa- bafudukayo ayingonakalise nto, kuba apo baya kona sebeza
kuhamba behlwayela imbeu elungileyo.

Ukusuka kwixesha lekristmas ye 1874 kude kube ngumhla wokumka kwake
ubeselesoloko elele elukukweni, iyinqaba ukuba azenzele into, kufuneka
ukuba ali- ndwe ebusuku nase mini. Bakwenza

oko abantwana bake ngentliziyo evumayo nenotando, bakufeza
ngokukolekileyo. Nge zokugqibela zobuko bake kweli lizwe kwa bonakala
ukuba iyehla ingqondo yake. Noko ke intliziyo yake ayiyekanga ukuba
kwaba bantu ebebapete. Nangesabata yake yoku- gqibela ekwakuko
umtendeleko kona, wamana ukubuza ukuba bebengakanani na abantu
etyalikeni. Isipelo sake saba luxolo novuyo ; ekwati naxa ungasekoyo
umpefumlo lwasalela uncumo ekubonakala ukuba ubenalo xa kutiwa kuye,
namhla sikufuna ukuba ugoduke uye eluvuywenilwe Nkosi yako, siya
kukulula kwintlungu zeli lizwe.

Ngesimilo, njengoko banqinelanayo bonke abamaziyo, ubengumfo okutalele
umsebenzi, okuziqonda kakulu izinto, ombedesho ingazenzisiyo. Usizi
abanalo bonke abazihlobo zake nabantu abebapete lubonisa ukuba ubebekiwe
kakulu. Siyakulilela ukupulukana naye. Noko ke siyazi ukuba yena
kumlungele apo akona ; asimmoneli ngokuya kwake ku Tixo wake.

Uye kuncwatywa lomfo ngolwe Sine ngomhla 11 ka February. Bekuko iqela
elikulu labamhlope abavela kwindawo ezimelene ne McFarlane nase Dikeni,
kuko kanjalo negqiza labantsundu abakoliweyo nabangakoliweyo. Ukuya
encwabeni umzimba ube utwelwe ngaba- dala bakona, abantsundu. Uye wala-
liswa ecaleni lowenkosikazi yakwake pakati kwamangcwaba abantu akade
esenza pakati kwabo. Intlalo yake ibiseloko yaba yeyokuzitoba
nokululama, ekubonakala ukuba tina isifundisa oku— Ukwenza intando ka
Tixo ngentliziyo epeleleyo, nokuba kungokusebenza ngezandla ebushushwini
bemini, nokuba kukulala elukukweni lokufa use zintlungwini, ungo-elelwe
ngamandla.

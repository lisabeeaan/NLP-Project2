ISINQINISO ESITSHA.

Fort White, February 17, 1877.

Ku Mr. J. Shaw, Fort Beaufort,—

Nkosi etandekayo.—Umntu otile wandiyale-
la ukuze ndike ndilinge iyeza lako loxaxazo
mhla ndandipetwe kakubi sisisu. Landinceda
kwakamsinyane kakulu ndaza ndagqiba kwe-
lokuba ndiya kuka ndililinge kwinkwenkwana
yam ebingazange ipile soloko yazalwayo.
Noko amagqira ndibe ndihamba kuwo ubesuke
umntwana ancedeke intsuku ezimbini nokuba
zintatu. Ndilinge elako iyeza. Ndite ndaku-
mnika okwesibini ndabon’ ukuba uyancedeka.
Ute ipela ibotile waba selepilile kwakanye
umntwana. Ngoku sekupele inyanga ezilishu-
mi elinesibini noko asikazanga simbuyele isifo.
Ndingakankanya ukuba nabanye abamelwana
bam endite ndabayalela eliyeza lako balilinga
kwababo abantwana, nabo bancedekile. Akuko
namnye ongati elazi umsebenzi walo ahlale
ngapandle kwalo endlwini yake. Mna andike
ndihlale ndingenalo.

  Ndingowako ongu

J. THOMAS.

LAMA Yeza atenjiwe ngokukulu ngumniniwo nanga- bo bawalingileyo
ngokuwaselela izifo enzelwe uku- nyanga zona. Azipilisa ezozifo
ngokufezekileyo noku msinya; asebenza ngoku yolileyo embilini womntu
ofayo, kuba exutywe nento ezingenzakalisiyo, nezi- ngena ntshabalaliso
ngapakati, ezifunyanwa kupela kwi miciza nemifuno yalo mhlaba, eyona
yazekayo usebenzo Iwayo ebuyezeni bayo; labe kanjako inani elibizwayo
ekutengweni kwawo lili ncinane.

The “ SURE CURE”—Elikupilisa kuqinisekileyo— Iyeza elikupilisa
kuqinisekileyo ekulunyweni zinyoka, nazo zonke izi nambuzane ezino
buhlungu.

“ SPECIFIC ”—Elona yeza—Iyeza elikupilisa ku- qinisekileyo ekuxaxazeni

“ AFRICANUM ”— Elamazinyo — Iyeza elikupilisa kuqinisekikyo kwi ntutumbo
yamazinyo.

“HERBAL TINCTURE”—Incindi yemifuno—Iyeza elikupilisa kuqinisekileyo kwi

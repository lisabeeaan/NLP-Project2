AMAGXABUZA.

Kusekufuti kuvakala isikalo sokuba Urulumente ubapete ngakunye ondilele
namagxabuza. Asikokwetu ukuhlanganisela Urulumente, nokoke banga bangati
abazama ilungelo yabanye xa banokulungileyo abakufezileyo banconywe.
Singa ke singenza amazwi atile ngendawo ezitunyelwe kwi Mantyi zalo
mandla, esikolwa ukuba baya kuzincoma bonke, nokuba ziya kuvelisa
ukulunga pakati kwabantsundu . Kute ngenxa yokuba maninzi kwamagxabuza
aligqibileyo ixesha lawo etolongweni, abuyela apa eza kucela umhlaba
wokuhlala, kwatunyelwa ilizwi e-Kapa lokubuza ukuba ayakutiwanina.
Impendulo iti, Urulumeni uza kubapa umhlaba ondilele, kodwa Amagxabuza
owufumana ngokuwusebenzela. Angena ekulweni eyalwa, angenisa indleko
enkulu, kwafa abantu ngawo, ngokoke makavune isiqamo sesenzo sa wo.
Urulumente akanakuwapata njengoko apata abamtobeleyo. Elolizwi siti libe
lifanele ukupuma ku Rulumente, angati ukuba anje onke amazwi ake avelise
ukulunga.

Kanjalo sive ukuba kute xa kuxoxwa nemantyi ngalenteto yokuba wonke
onendlu uza kufumana i-akile ezilishumi kwako oti, luya kutiwanina usapo
oluvelayo, umhlaba awukubanela bonke. Impendulo iti Urulumente
uyakucandela abanezindlu ngoku, uze umhlaba ufunyanwe zindlamafa zabo.
Abanye abantwana, xa bavayo ukuba umhlaba awubaneli kuya kufuneka ukuba
baye entsebenzweni. Esosigqibo siti tina siwenza ukuba uncomeke ngokunye
lomteto wokucandela abantsundu, kuba ukuba ukunikwa kwemihlaba kuya
kugqitywa ngaba bakoyo, siyazi ukuba abaninzi abanyukayo abakufumana
ndawo baze kengoko baye emsebenzini, bafunde inkutalo, nokonwabisa
nokuzuzisa komsebenzi.

E-KALK BAY.

E-Kalk Bay, kulamacala angase Kapa kuko isifo esibaxalisileyo abantu.
Lomzana sikuwo, bawuncoma ukuncola, ekungazekiyo nokubana eso sifo
singa. ba siveliswa kuko. Kodwa ukuza kuvela kwaso kube loluhlobo :
Ngomhla we 19 ku July kunduluke abalobi ababini beya kubambisa. Bate
besebotini babona intlanzi abati “ngumbulali” (kuba ibulala ezinye).
Bate bakusondela baqonda ukuba zimbini zidla ezinye. Into ebe
ngummangaliso ibe kukuba zingabaleki kanti zintlanzi ezidume
ngokundweba. Enkulu bahle bayi bulala kodwa yatshona ababinakuyifumana.
Eye sibini bayilandele bayi bulala bayisa elunxwemeni. Ite yakufika
baxelelana ngayo bonke ababambisi kuba yintlanzi enqabileyo ; wangulowo
wanga angake afumane intwana. Ite ifika iveki elandelayo lwaba uninzi
lwalo mzi seluke lwayiva, ise ide yakufika nakweminye imizi. Emva
kwentsu

ku ezintatu abuye ayikupa amanzi naleya ibe itshonile, nayoke yadliwa.
Abanye banela ukuba bagqibe ukudla bahliwa sisifo. Kute ngomhla we 23
kwako ogqitayo, ngomhla 27 kwagqita omnye, kwa dlula ntsuku ntatu
walandela omnye, ngomhla wa 31 bababini. Esisifo asibabambanga ngakunye,
kanjalo umntu uda aqauke kunga bonakalanga ukuba intlungu ziyahlaba.
Abanye ubesiti xa bancamileyo, babone esiti balulu,, adlamke okweyure
nangapezulwana bati xa babulelayo, kanti selekwimizuzwana yokugqibe la.
Abanye basuke banxanwe into engapeliyo abanye bangafuni manzi. Kuteke
naxa sekuke kwako abafayo, baba abanye besayidla inyama, kwada kwa miswa
umteto yi Mantyi yase Simons Town, wokuba ingabuye idliwe. Kekaloku
kubantu abahlinziweyo kusuke kungabonakali eyona nto ibabulalayo. Aba-
ntu abebesebefile ngomhla we 16 ku August, bebelishumi elinesitandatu,
abafa ngokuncamekileyo babini, abalele ngokunetemba belishumi
elinababini, Abanye bati kufe nezinja nekati nempuku ezike zafumana
lonyama. Into ke engummangaliso kuko konke oku kukuba lentlanzi ike
idliwe kungabiko nto ivelayo. Abanye bati mayibe ibinento ebulalayo ebe
iyidlile nayo ngokwayo.

AMAGQIRA ANTSUNDU

KUMHLELI WE “ SIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA.”

Shawbury, August, 12, 1879.

Nkosi ndiya cela, indawo kuwe yokuba ndenze a- mazwana ambalwa.

Balesi be Sigidimi kunimele ukuba nina eninalo ukanyiselo, ukuba nizame
ngako konke eninako uku- kanyisela abanye abantu bohlanga lwakowenu.
Abantu ekungabo ubumnyama buhlalayo, ngamagqira, ukukanya baya ku
timbela ngako konke abanako. Anditeti abavumisayo bodwa nditeta nala
anyangayo ngamayeza, La nyangayo wona angati esiqonda isifo enamayeza
aso, axele into yimbi, njengenunu, necanti, nemfene, nempaka, nezinye
izinto ezininzi, aze anga anyanga ezonto, kanti anyanga isifo
ngalamayeza aze lowo ufayo, kwa nabahlobo bake bakolwe bonke ukuba
kunyangwa ezonto eliti igqira zibanga isifo. Ezonkolo ziya batshonisa
abantu ebumnya- meni.

Nditi yazisani amayeza lahlani isiko elikoyo lokuba umntu akalixele
iyeza lake. Amayeza e-Africa apa maninzi, anamandla, anoncedo olukulu,
andikolwa ukuba adlulwa ngamayeza ezinye indawo. Bonisani amagqabi,
ukuze abantu babe nokuqonda ukuba into enyangwayo sisifo, asingawo
manunu namacanti, nezinye izinto ezinjalo.

Mandini kokele mna nangona ibala lam limhlope

Iyeza enditeta ngalo namhlanje sisidikili. Iyeza elikulu, elaziwayo
ngabantu abaninzi, belazela izinto ngezinto. Mna ke ndilazela izilonda,
nokuba sisilonda salupina uhlobo. Tabata ingcambu zaso, uzigcade uti
wakubona ukuba uzigcadile, uzisile, uwu dibanise umsizi namafuta
atambileyo, utambise amaxesha ngamaxesha. Uti usenjenjalo ube enye
ingcambo wayikanda wayigalela amanzi, aze umntu onesilonda amane
eselakancinane imihla yonke. E-sosilonda siyakupola.

Kuko umfana otile apa obenentloko embi kunga- tandeki nokumkangela,
kumana kupuma ubovu kuya yonke intloko, obe seleneminyaka elishumi
enjalo. Kube kukade kusiyiwa kumagqira kulingwe nakubamhlope abantu,
kungancedi luto. Ngenxa yamagqira antsundu kude kwafikwa esineni kuxelwa
inkomo. Ndamnyanga mna ngesidikili wapila nanamhlanje upilile. Enye
indoda ebi nomlenze oyibulalayo kakulu ise ikohlwa kuhamba igula kanye
yacela kum ndayipilisa ngesidikili kungeko yeza limbi. Lomlenze wayo ube
usisilonda esikulu esivuzayo ngelinye ixesha, sifuna ukoma ngelinye,
yaibe ibiza amagqira antsundu ati wona angena ezinkohlisweni zawo,
eli­de lati elinye fanel’ukuba ukuze ihle lento makabe umntu lowo
wanyatela emkondweni wecanti, lamnya- nga nga mayeza ecanti!!

Sendini Koxela hambani.

Ndingu
D. S. DAVIS.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

sokuba umntu wonke ofuya inja makayi rafele, arole ishumi lesheleni

nenciniba.

U-FELTMAN.—Abafundi betu bonke baya kuba nosizi ukuva ngendawo etetwa yi
Mercury, yokuba “U-Feldman ufumene ingozi embi kunene, wapuka umlenze
etangeni, ngokuwa kwenqwelo yamahashe abeyi quba enonyana wake. Ubesuka
kwa Blyth ukumhlela kwalo ngozi.”

E-PANAMA kuza kwenziwa umselekazi omkulu woku- hlanganisa i-Atlantic ne
Pacific. Kufunwa ukuba imikombe ibe nokuwelela Kwelinye icala le America
ngapandle kokuba ide ijikele ngase zantsi. Lomsebenzi unikelwe ezandleni
zika Lesseps umfo owenza I-Suez Canal.

U-MR. KEITH JOHNSON, opete elinye lalamaqela ahamba ehlola i-Africa
kuvakala ukuba ebesahamba kakuhle. Ebefike kumzi ose lunxwemeni lwelizwe
elingase miwe bantu, abeya kude ayi gqibe iveki ehamba entlango.
Abatwali bempahla zake bebengekamniki nkatazo. Into ebisambambezele
kulomzi kukufuna ukudla okuya kumanela ukuba acande lontlango ngapandle
kwembandezelo.

IZIKOLO ZAMA WESILE ezise Tonga ngoku zenza nangapezu kokuzixasa.
Kutshanje ute kona wonke umsebenzi wokuhambisa ilizwi wahlaulwa yimali
epuma kwiremente zalondawo, zada zahlaula nesixaso sabamhlope
abafundisi, kwaza noko kwako imali eninzi esalela ukuba iyakuquba ilizwi
kwezinye indawo Nezase Fiji iramente ziquba kakuhle kwicala lemali
nakuba zingekafiki kwezase Tonga.

abalunge no W. E. Stanford ukuba baza kubalwa kunye nemfuyo yabo. Enye

iziqiniselo. U-Mr. Stanford ufuna ukubakutaza abalimi namadoda aqubela
pambili.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 1, 1880.

Elesitatu—eloku gqibela ke, lelisa ziqala indawo ndawo zemfundo, bequba
ikakulu kulo woku lesa. Bona bafunda ku Royal Reader No. TV.

ISIKULA SENTOMBI.

Intombi, ukufundiswa kwazo zahlulwa zenziwa maqela matatu. Kwe loku
qala, kuko ezine ezi fumene isiqiniselo solo luviwo lubizwa ukuba lolwe
Sikulase Cape University. Kuko abanye aba fumene isiqiniselo sakwa
Rulumente. Ezinye ziquba imisebenzi nge misebenzi, eyenziwa ngumntu ongu
mfazi—kuko onje ngoku sika, noku tunga ingubo, noku hlamba, noku
strikisha. Emfundweni, intombi ziba kolisile aba fundisikazi bazo, nase
msebenzi ezinye zihambisa ngoku nobom.

IMISEBENZI.

Owokucwela (Carpenters).

Nguwona msebenzi utandwayo lo. Inani laba kulo msebenzi, linga pezu
kwenani laba funda amanye amashishini. Bane abali gqibileyo ixesha loku
botshwa kwabo, bapuma benesi qiniselo sabo—kodwa abane, bapume ngendlela
ezingalungileyo, kuko nomonye obubileyo;

Owoku zenza Inqwelo.

Umpati wawo, ufike pakati emnyakeni. Wawuke unge- nampati. Babini abali
gqibileyo kakuhle ixesha labo, bapuma bene ziqiniselo zabo. Inqwelo
ezenziweyo zintandatu, kuko ne zinye izinto ezenziweyo, ezinje ngo
zikiliva, kukandwe nezinto ezonakeleyo, ezinje ngo zipuluwa.

Owoku kanda Intsimbi.

Nawo ukwa kupe umntu oligqibileyo ixesha lake. Wapuma enesiqiniselo soku
kwazi ukusebenza. Apa kuko ukukanda intsimbi zenqwelo nemi sebenzi
enjalo, kude kube seku fakeni amahashe intsimbi.

Owoku Shicilela.

Mininzi imisebenzi eyenziwa apa, ku shicilelwa incwadi ngencwadi ezinje
nge zemfundo nezinye ezinjalo. Kuko enye yama Culo, ngokwesi Xosa; nenye
yama Ngesi, ibalisa ngeli lizwe lase Africa. Kubeko ke nawe ndaba
amapepa. Kufunwa apa, aba fana boku funda lomsebenzi. Indawo ngoku iko.
Babini aba ligqibileyo ngalo mnyaka ixesha labo, bapuma, sebe ngaba
nokuwazi lomsebenzi. Bobabini bawu fumene kwa oko umsebenzi.

Owoku Tunga Incwadi.

Babini aba kulo msebenzi. Omnye selenexesha. Sele noku zenza ngokwake
incwadi. Ezinye incwadi ezi tungwe apa, zili fumene ibaso, kwezinye
indawo apo bezi boniswa kona, izinto ezen ziwa kweli lizwe.

Izi Limo.

Kufunyenwe ngalo mnyaka, umbona okwi ngxowa ezi 780, Inqolowa engaba kwi
ngxowa ezi 300, nezinye izinto ezinje nge tapile, nembotyi, namatanga,
nehabile.

Ucinyo.

Oku tunyelweyo no kwamkelweyo ngalo mnyaka, kunga- neno koko mnyaka
ogqitileyo. Kubonakala ukuba naba Ntsundu aba tumelanga ngo cingo kakulu
nga lomnyaka.

UKUPUMA KWAMA HLOLA.—“Ama Mfengu alo mmandla wase Dikeni, kade ekala
ngokuti axinene, afuna umhlaba. Kute ngo Mgqibelo anentlanganiso kwa
mantyi. Ite imantyi yafunda incwadi ivela kwa Rulumente, isiti, kuko
umhlaba e-Gqili, kufupi ne Calvinia ne Frasersburg, angati ke ama Mfengu
atumele abantu, boku ke baye kuhlola ukuba unjanina. Ukuba abahloli
bakoliwe ngulo mhlaba, ama Mfengu owunikwa ngu Rulumente. Kufuneka kuko
nabaya kupuma e-Ngqushwa, nase Fort Beaufort. Asikuko nokuba inteto
zezibonda zibe mnandi, kuba ziyamva u-Rulumente. Abahloli ke bahlalele
ukunduluka. Kubonakala ukuba u-Rulumente unobubele, kanjako uyayisabela
into ebekiswa kuye ngaba Ntsundu.”—Alice Times.

U-MR. SPURGEON ETETA NGOKUSHUMAYELA
NGAPANDLE.

NGO October lo ugqitileyo, bekuko intlanganiso kulendlu yesikula ilunge
nalendlu yetyalike ekutiwa ukubizwa kwayo yi Metropolitan Tabernacle,
e-London. Beku hlangene amadoda aquba ilizwi kwi ndawana zanga pandle,
kunye ne zihlobo zawo, kupungwa itea.—Kute emva ko mtandazo, lo ubako
kade kulo tyalike ngemivulo yonke, usuke u-Rev. C. H. Spurgeon wenza
amazwi ateta ngoku shumayela ngapandle. Uqale ngokuti, amazwi ake aya
kuba mafutshane kuba enentlungu enkulu emzimbeni wake. Ute kuko into
abebefanele ukuhlala beyi lumkele abantu abangene kumsebenzi woku
shumayela indaba ezilungileyo. Yile yokuvana no Tixo. Ukuze bakwazi
ukuzi cukumisa intliziyo zabanye abantu, kufuneka bete bafana no Tixo.
Ukuze babe nokuvana no Tixo, kufuneka bete baba ngama- doda angcwele.
Bengengawo amadoda angcwele, nokuba sebe ziziteti kangakananina,
abasokuze bafumane ntsikelelo ka Tixo pezu komsebenzi wabo. Andikolwa
ukuba umntu angaya ku shumayela kubamelwane bake esitrateni, aze afumane
intsikelelo ka Tixo pezu koku shumayela kwake, ukuba lomntu uli
menemene. Ukuba u-Tixo akakabenzi ngcwele, mabavale imilomo bati cwaka,
ade abenze ngcwele. Enye indawo efunekayo, yile, yokuba umntu ofuna uku
shumayela, makabe nolulamo. Kute ngenye imini apa, wati umntu otile kum,
yena uya nditandazela ukuba ndizuze ukuzitoba. Ndite mna ukupendula “
Enkosi; ndiyatemba ukuba seluke wazitandazela oko nawe ngokwako.” Ute
lomntu “ Hayi, andimntu ulingekayo ngakwelo cala mna.” Ebantwini apa,
fan’ukuba oyena ubakupayo bonke nge ratshi, ngulowo uzingca ngokuti yena
akazipakamisile. Kuseloko bawayo omawokulu bokuqala, lento iratshi yaba
yimvelo kuti; sinalo sonke noko liti ukuzivelisa kwalo kuwumbi umntu
lizivelise ngendiela eyodwa. Uti wakufun’ukuti ukupumelela kancinane,
ube sewukukumala, uhamba apa liratshi. Imbangi yokuba u-Tixo, ati
inxenye yabantu angayi pumelelisi, ubona ukuba iratshi lingasuka
libagezise. Bangati kuke kweza iziqu ezikulu ezi ntshumayelweni zabo,
baxelele abafazi babo, kanti kukunyuka kwe ratshi oko. Abe selecota
u-Satana esiza kuti, “ Nantsi, kwowu! utshatshele.” Ke ngoko
abashumayeli mabaye ku tandaza. Batandaze amaxa onke, xa bashumayelayo,
xa baza kushumayela, naxeshi- kweni seleshumayele. Ukuba abatandazi
angasuke u-Tixo ati, “ andikuke ndiyi sikelele intshumayelo yako,
akundicelanga.” Enye indawo mabalindele ukuvuna. Bengafani nendoda eyati
andazi nokuba ndofika kuko into endingayivunayo na entsimini, make ndiye
kukangela. Mabafane nendoda eyaya entsimini isazi ukuba iya kufika
ivune; eyaya ine sekile egxalabeni layo, isiyela ukusika inqolowa, yaye
ilandelwa zinqwelo zokuyitwala. Ndiyakolwa ukuba zininzi intshumayelo
engeziba zilungile, koko zisuke ziswele lento mye, ukolo.

ISAZISO SAKWA RULUMENTE.

NO. 857, 1879.

  Sipuma kwi Office Yompatiswa Mihlaba Yakomkulu, nemicimbi yezizwe
  zonke, e- Cape of Good Hope.

August 8, 1879.

  GENGXELO yesenzo No. 14, somnyaka
  we 1878 kuyaziswa apa, kuyo yonke

into engumntu, okokuba iziqwenga zemihla-
ba yakomkulu ezikumandla wase Qonce
(King Wm’s Town) kweli fudula ilelakwa
Ngqika, ziyakutengiswa kwinto yonke pa-
mbi kwe Office Yemantyi yase Qonce nge-
xesha lakusasa (10 o’clock) Ngolwesine
ngomhla we 4 ku December, 1879, njengoko
sekuxelwe ngako kuso Isenzo, nange Saziso
sakwa Rulumente No. 668, esabalwa ngo-
mhla we 8 ku August 1878 sikelelwa into
yonke. Kanjako zotengiswa ezoziqwenga
ngeminqopiso ekoze kunqotshiswane ngayo
ngemini yentengiso.

Eso, neso, isiqwenga sotengiswa kumiswe
nenani esiyakurafelwa ngalo, ngonyaka. Ke
eyona ndoda ite yagalela kunamanye, yaza
kanjako ayabeta nganeno koko ifuneka nga-
ko irafu yomhlaba, iyakuba ngumnini weso
siqwenga kwamhlana ite yasitenga; yonke
ke into ete yatenga lomini, iyakunyanzelwa
okokuba iti kwalapo iyibatale irafu yomnya-
ka wokuqala, ize iti eyeminyaka emibini ela-
ndelayo yenze iziqiniselo pambi ko Mantyi
zokutembisa ukuba yoyirola lomali. Koti
kodwa ukuba ite indoda yati kwaoko yaseli-
rola kwi Mantyi irafu yeminyaka emibini,
ingabi sabala ziqiniselo. Indleko zokuca-
nda nokumisa ibakana, nendleko ze siqiniselo
(Title Deed) nokubekwa kwe Stamp, nemali
erolwa e-Offisini, ishumi elinambini lesheleni
eline tiki (12s 3d), zonke ezondleko ize zi-
batalwe ngumtengi mhlana awutengayo
umhlaba. Abatengi ke mabeze entengisweni
sebe zilungiselele indleko zokucanda umhla-
ba nokumisa ibakana; mabeze kanjalo ifa-
nele imali yokuwubatala umhlaba neyerafu.

Woti ke umtengi akukova ukubatala, aku-
zifumana iziqiniselo zokuba wayibatala irafu,
amkele isiqiniselo (title) somhlaba wake, ne-
se rafu kulomhlaba wake, esiya kuti ke eso
serafu ukuze sime kuye, aman’ ukusikulula
esibatalela inxalenye ngonyaka, njengoko
kuxeliweyo kwisiqendu (Section) se VI se-
sisenzo (Act).

Imifanekiso yalemihlaba, ukucandwa kwa-
yo, nendlela eyakutengiswa ngayo, kunye
nesaziso sakwa Rulumente No. 668, 1878
yofunyanwa kwi Offise Yomongameli Noca-
nda (Surveyor General) e-Kapu, nakwi
Mantyi zase Qonce, nase Monti, nakwa Ko-
mani (Queen’s Town) nase Bofolo (Fort
Beaufort) nakwa Daliwe (Cathcart.)

JOHN LAING.

Umpatiswa.

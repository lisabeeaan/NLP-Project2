Vol. V. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, DECEMBER 1, 1875. No. 63

Isigidimi Samaxosa.

AB ANTWANA ABADUTYULWEYO

E-GWALI.

NGOMHLA we 16 ka November kuhle e-Gwali into elusizi lungenakugqitywa
ngamazwi, yehla kumhlabakaMr. Murray. Ukwenzeka kwayo kube
loluhlobo.—Kute ukumka komhla ngalomini kwesuka kumhlaba ka Hayihayi
Maqubela—omelene nalowo ka Murray—amantombazana asixenxe ango Lejina,
Fuduka, Mkatemliti, Nongeve, Nomtiki, Nontengemntu, no Nomabangi.
Awokuqala amabini ebengaka Hayihayi. Asuke lamantomba- zana esiya kucela
ilamuni ezimuncu kwa Murray, kumlungu okangela lomhlaba, oqele ukuhlala
ebapa abantwana kuba esiti ziyabola. Bafike xa lomlungu apumayo endlwini
epete izembe eya kucanda inkuni, eza kubasela umlilo unyana ka Murray
abati ngu George, inkwenkwe ebudala buminyaka 16. Ute kanti naye usand’
ukufika evela e-Dikeni etunywe ngumkuluwa wake waza wagaleleka emanzi
kuba lalisina. Ezonkuni ke beziya kucandwa kulungiselelwa yena. Ute
akupuma lomgcini mhlaba ka Murray acela amantombazana, wati naye
akuwabona waseleyazi into ayizeleyo. Akawapendulanga noko kwa eko, waba
esiti makake agqibe ukucanda and’ ukuza kuwapa. Ujikele ecaleni kwendlu
apo bezikona inkuni basala abantwana bemi berozile ngase
mnyango—u-Lejina no Fuduka baka Hayihayi bepambili, emva kwabo engu
Mkatemliti ka Peter Mpanjukelwa wase Tolofiyeni kwesika Mabandla.
Kusenjalo uze kuti tu emnyango lomfana ka Murray wati kubo ndiza
kunidubula. Utsho wangena endlwini wapuma nompu wabuya wapinda ukuti
ndiza kunidubula. Enye yamantombazana u-Lejina ipendule ngeliti kunokuba
usidubule siyeke sigo- duke, ute akutsho yaselibasa lenkwenkwe

yazangeso sikoti sinye yababulala kwaoko abebepambili bobatatu,
yabanxwelera ababini, u-Nomabangi no Nongeve, bababini abasindileyo.
Lompu ubunxashwe uhlwaya olukulu. Ute akubadubula bake abantwana bawa
bonke, baza ke aba basindileyo nabanxweleriweyo bavuka babaleka. Lomfo
ubeye kucanda ute akuva isitonga wajika wakangela wabona sebefile
abatatu. Ubaleke waya ku George wati, “ Yintonina le uyenzileyo
akapendula omnye. Ubaleke u-Mr. Stroud—lomgcini mhlaba—waya kubika kwa
Mr. Amos umlungu okwakufupi. Kwangelo xesha kubeko umntu oza kubika kwa
Hayihayi. Aputume kwa- oko amadoda u-H. wabamba ihashe, waqala ke ngoko
waya kufika. Yakumbona lenkwenkwana ibalekile wayisukela wayibamba
ngesandla: engekazi ukuba yiyo le yenze oku, esaba ngu Mr. Stroud. Ute
xa esamana efuna u-Stroud pakati komzi eyite cu ngesandla inkwenkwe le
afika amanye amadoda ayitabata aya nayo kwa Hayihayi. U-Mr. Amos
uhlangene nayo endleleni ibanjwe ngengalo ngamadoda amabini, wati kuba
kungeko xesha wagqitela apo bebekona abafileyo abantwana. Akuba
eyibonile into eyenzekileyo ususe omnye wonyana baka Mr. Woest ukuba
lonkwenkwana ayise e-Dikeni. Uyifumene apo kwa Hayihayi isabanjwe
ngesandla ngamadoda amabini wemka ke nayo ukuyisa e-Dikeni.

Sibona ukuba kwipepa lase Fort Beaufort lamangesi kutiwa ababantwana
bebeye kuba esitiyeni waza u-George akuiri pumani abavuma, wada waya
kutabata umpu esiti uza kuboyikisa wati esawupete wakubeka, kanti umpu
uza kusuke udubule ngokukubeka. Sifuna ukubacacisela bonke ukuba lamazwi
elo- pepa ayayigqweta inyaniso. Ababantwa- na bebeye kucela. Kanjalo
badutyulwa bemi ebaleni bengeko esitiyeni. U-George lowo akakange atsho
nokukanye ukuti godukani; ukudubula kompu awenziwa-

nga kukukubeka, uwupakamisile wajolisa kubo. Bonke abasindileyo bati
bamvile xa awutyedayo.

Elotyala libe litetwa e-Dikeni ngo Mgqibelo ngomhla 20, Imantyi yagqiba
kwelokuba lonkwenkwe mayilinde u- Mgwebi.

Abantwana ababubileyo bancwatywe ngolwe Sine ngo 18, ngu Rev. Dr.
Stewart —ababini baka Hayihayi bancwatyelwa e-MacFarlane, omnye waya
kuncwatyelwa Etolofiyeni ngu Mr. Makiwane. Abanxweleriweyo kuvakala
ukuba bobabini base ndleleni yokupila.

Izihlobo ezimgama ziyacelwa ukuze seziwamkela nge Sigidimi lombiko,
zinga- lindeli kuti yinina ukuba zingabalelwa, kuba mhla kunje abazali
intliziyo zabo azinakucinga ngezinto ezininzi.

Ukuvelana esivelana ngako nabazali babantwana asinako ukukufeza ngamazwi
nangosiba. Intlungu yokupulukana nabantwana ngoluhlobo akuko simvayo
ukuba wake wayitwaliswa ngapambili. Kodwa ke indawo esinokuyenza kupela
kukubashiyela ezandleni zo Tixo wabo, umtutuzeli wentsizana ezinje
ngokuba benjalo ngalemini yanamhla.

IREMENTE YASE DIKENI.

IREMENTE yalo mzi, bokumbula abanye ukuba kuse kunexesha ingenamfundisi
ungowayo. Yati kwakumka u-Rev. James Robertson nge 1874— ukuya kwizizwe
ezimele i-Dutywa, yasala ikangelwe ngumfundisi opete ama Ngesi apa e-
Dikeni, u-Rev. W. J. B. Moir. Kute ke kuba le yabantsundu iyiremente
enkulu, ekufuneka inomfundisi omele yona yodwa kwaqondakala ukuba
kufanelekile ukuba mayimfumane.

Ngomhla 5 ka November 1875 kube kuko intlanganiso yokunyula umfundisi
engati imtande ukubi abe ngumkokeli wayo. Kute namhla kwako indawo
ezintatu ezibe zingazange zibeko ngapambili kwezi tyalike zilunge ne
Free Church, indawo

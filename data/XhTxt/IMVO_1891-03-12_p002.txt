U MR. DOLLEY ukuba apalaze uluvo lwake, ote uvuya ka­kulu ukuba
nentlanganiso na Bantsnndu base Bhayi. Wati Abantsundu base Bhayi
ngabona bantu bapambili kwizinto zolaulo kwesi sitili; kanjako bate
nasekuhambahambeni kwabo bada batika e Lovedale, apo bafike baxelelwa
ukuba oyena mntu upambili kwizinto zolaulo apo ngumntu ovela e Bhayi—u
Mr. Isaac Wauchope. Ute into yokuqala afuna ukuyibeka pambi
kwalentlanganiso, yincaso ayifumanayo kubaxhasi belungu lama Bhulu u
Bilingane (Mr. Bellingan), esona sizatu batika basibeke sokumchasa kwabo
sesokuba unomhlaba olungileyo e Nqweba awuyeko ukuba uhlalwe ngo Kafile,
bati ke ngeso sizatu abanakumvotela; kodwa yena akayiboni imposiso
ekuyekeni abantu abantsundu bahlale efameni yake. Ute into ayifunayo
kukuba situmele amadoda e Palamente aya kuwalwela amalungelo etu. Ukuba
situmele yena no Wilmot, no Brister, baya kuzatna konke abanako
ukungayivumeli imiteto erabaxa ngakontsundu. Kanjalo baya kulwela ukuba
utywala benzelwe iralu enzima (5s. per gallon) ukuze kuncipe olunxilo
lungaka. Ka­njalo uyakutetela indawo yokuba kuncitshiswe irafu yokutya
okuvela kwamanye amazwe ukuze kube tshipu, kudlule utywala.

Inteto yelinene ibe yeyanelisayo kaku­lu yengco emixhelweni yabantsundu,
ongati xa ilandwa yonke lizale ipepa.

Kubizwe

U MR. JAMES BRISTER, ote uyavuya ukubako kwintlanganiso yoluhlobo
ndawonye nabantsundu, nakuba enosizi ngetuba lowabo u Hon. A. Wil­mot
ongabanga nakufikelela kule ntla­nganiso ngesizatu sokuya ku Mboniso
wase Ngqushwa; kodwa ke nakuba engeko masingoyiki kuba bamoya mnye
ngobutatu babo, imvo zabo ziyafana. Lihambisile elinene lati—inkulu
imfuneko yokuba tina bantsundu masisebenzise ivoti yetu ngobulumko ukuze
i Palamente ingazaliswa ngamadoda aya kumisa imiteto yoburalarume
ngakuti, njengoko kubonakalayo okokuba ama Bhulu azama ukuba zonke
izihlalo zelobandla zibe zezawo. Aye ke ingengawo amadoda okutenjwa siti
mzi untsundu, nanjengokuba eve okokuba kwi ntlanganiso ebiko e Mnyameni
elinye i Bhulu elifuna isihlalo e Palamenteni (u Mr. Bellingan), lite
linga lona kungenziwa utango apo kufupi nolwandle, ize bonke o Kafile
besisitili baqutywe bafakwe kulo, kubekwe amapolisa amabini emasangweni
abagcine pakati apo bangayi ndawo. Wati ke umntu omvo zinjalo
ningamvotelana? “Bati bonke hayi.” Lite elinene, kwayena u Bilingane
lowo ute kwenye intlanganiso uya kuxhasa into yokuba irafu yezindlu
(House Duty) ivuswe njengokuba kuko umingimingi lokuba ingxowa
yakonikulu ifun’ ukuti iwute nje. Kodwa yena (Mr. Brister) akayiboni
nakancinane into yokuba kuvunjululwe into enjengaleyo waye eyazi
inkatazo yayo oko ibiko kubantu abantsundu, nohlobo ebibakataza maxa
ibizwayo. Ngoko ke ukuba uyile e Palamenteni uya kuyichasa ngamandla ake
onke into enjalo, esiti yena ucinga ngokuti irafu enzima etywaleni
inokuwavingca lomaroba engxowa ka Kulumente evuzayo ngapezu kokuba
kuyokuvuswa into eyinkatazo nendiniso kwabantsundu. Lihambise elinene
lati, enye into eliya kuyimela e Bandla yi Mfundo, Iisiti kuyo yonke
into enokwenzelwa umntu ontsundu ukuze abe ngopumeleleyo yimfundo, utsho
wati akuko salelo ukuba abantsundu nabo bangangeni kwintlanganiso
zabamhlope baxoxe, kuba bangabantu abanobuchopo obuliluzekileyo, into
inye kupela, kukuba bengena mfundo; utsho wati, nokuba kutiwa imali
ipelile yokuyixhasa imfundo, yena uya kumela indawo yokuba mayibolekwe
ngenxa yemfundo bafundiswe abantsundu njengabamhlope.

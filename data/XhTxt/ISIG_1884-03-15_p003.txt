Njengabanye bonke abantu abanewonga u General Gor­don asimfo utanda kuba
seziqwini, koko ngumfo otanda ukuba bucala. Wati nyakana wenziwa umpati
womkosi wase China, nakuba wawungemkosi upi, namkosi unanto yona
ubaluleke ngayo, wati yena wawuhluzahluza, wawenza ukuba uqonde imimiso
yake, ati naxa sukuba ete weyisa edabini, angawuvumeli ukuba uhamba
ubulala ngokungena nceba. Kwimfazwe eyayise Taipnig e China, uhlobo
lokuzipata kuka General Gordon, nokukalipa awakubonisayo, nolwoyiso
awalwenzayo zindawo ezo ezingalibalekiyo ebomini bale ndoda. Emva kwalo
mfazwe ke watunyelwa imbasa ebalulekileyo, nomvuzo owawuyi £10,000;
wayimangala kodwa yona imali, ngeliti ufuna kupela imalana engumvuzo
wendawo leyo ayipatisiweyo, nemalana ongeze kumadoda ake.

Enye indawo engqinela ubuhle besimilo sika General Gordon, kukuzipata
kwake xa waye ngumongameli mikosi e Gravesand, uhlobo awazipata ngalo
kumahlwempu, ngokukodwa utando Iwake kuba ntwana. Ubesiti asukuba
ebafumana beyele emigongxweni yamanzi abanyule, abahlambe, aye ahlale

izipitipiti. Uhlobo lokupola ahleli enako nangexesha lengozi, lubonakala

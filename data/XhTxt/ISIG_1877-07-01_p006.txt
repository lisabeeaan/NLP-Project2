UKUFUNDA KWABANTSUNDU.—Kwipepa langapa- mbili sibe sikankanye ukuba
imali yakwa Rulumeni erolelwa imfundo yabantsundu ikwi £20,000 ngo
mnyaka. Ingaba mhlaumbi ingapezulu nakoko kuba amanye amagama ezikolo
asiwagqibi, esite ke anjalo asawahalela kubo. Ukuba ke u-Rulumeni urola
£20,000 ukufundisa abantsundu ubefanele ukurola ngapezulu ukufundisa
abamhlope. Imfundo “ililifa,” esona sipo sinqabileyo anokusinikwa umntu
onqondo ilungeleleneyo omzimba upilileyo. Isizwe esinemfundo sisona siya
kulaula. Asitsho ukuti mabangafundiswa abantsundu, kodwa siti
mababahlaulele abantwana babo. U-Rulumeni urola £20,000 ukuba kufundiswe
abamhlope kanti bona babuye bahlaule nemali yabantwana. Isiqamo soko
kukuba aba- ntwana abamhlope abangena ezikolweni ezincedwa kwa Rulumeni
ba 184 kanti ababantsundu ba 2,857. E-Queenstown amakwenkwe amhlope aya
kwesakwa Rulumeni 148, abantsundu 1,292. Kube pesheya kwenciba emnye
omhlope kanti abantsundu ba 3,858. Lento ibonisa ukuba imfundo inikwa
kwabantsundu apo inganikwayo kwabamhlope. Lento ibangelwa kukunyameka
kwabafundisi nokuba imali ka Rulumeni ikwanela ukuyixasa ititshere
entsundu engafuni ndlu igqitiseleyo kwi nqukuva. Apa kungafuneka imali
eninzi ukumisa isikolo nokufuna ititshere ezifanelekileyo abati ke ngoko
abantwana bamahlwempu bazifumanele kwezinye indawo imfu- ndo.”— Cape
Mercury.

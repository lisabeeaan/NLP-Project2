Vol. XVII LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1887. No. 218.

Isigidimi Sama-Xosa.

NGOLWE-SINE, SEPTEMBER 1, 1887.

UTYWALA.

LENGXOXO ilandelayo, iteta ngo tywala, siyitabata kwi Christian Express.
Siyakuyinika ngokugixayo ukuze ibe mfutshane.

Kokumbuleka ukuba (itsho ke) besikunye nenkoliso yamapepa ase South
Africa ekucaseni utywala (brandy) ukuba bungavulelwa pesheya kwe Nciba,
saye sisiti lonto iyakufana nokubaqengqela ngefatyi, ukuba
zibantywilisele, zibatshabalalise. Kwabako indawo yokungati londawo
yokusiwa kwe brandi pesheya kwe Nciba ite gogololo, zanga ziyayekeka
nendawo ebekubanjwene ngazo ngayo okwexeshana. Kutekenqa kakulu,
kwamangaliseka yakuti intloko ka Rulumente ivakalise ukuba nendawana
ezizinkantini ebezivaliwe malunga nesitili sase Qonce ziza kuvulwa
kwakona. Yaye ke londawo yokuvalwa kwalo mabala atile, ibililinga
lokukangela ukuba kotekanina malunga nokuncipa nokungancipi koselo
pakati kwabantsundu. Yayinye kupela into ekalazekayo kwelolinga,
yayibubuncinane bezondawo zivaliweyo, into ke leyo ebingenako ukufeza
okukulu kwilinga elihle neliluncedo kangaka pofu, ete londawo yafana
nokuti igqira liti umntu esifa wonke yicesine, suke ukumnyanga kwalo,
lilibale kukuti cu ecikicaneni paya, ngesifo esesimgqibile. Ite lendawo
abejonge kuyo u Rulumente, kwanaleyo ka Mr. WOOD, eti yona mazongezwe
zandiswe izitili ezingena tywala, ezo ndawo mbini zicaseneyo zibuye
zavelisa ingxoxo engapele ndawo kwakona.

Ingxoxo ebiko pambi kwenkundla ye Palamente yefanele ukutiwa meqe
malunga nendawo eti mabavunyelwe ukuba bazinxale ngotywala ngangenani
abatanda ngalo, kwanendawo zokubohlwaya, xa kuvele indawo zokuba
bohlwaywe, besunduzelwe butywala obo kuzo, ize pofu kutiwe mabayekwe
benze apo batanda kona ngemali yabo. Yinto engumangaliso namhla le
yokuba abakontsela lendawo yotywala babe nje ukuvana kwabo ngayo
yokunika inyabesi yotywala kwabantsundu. Pofu kanti lona ilungelo
lokuvota liyanyinwa, ize ziti imali ezixasa imfundiso yabo ziguyelwe
pantsi, kwaye kunjalo nje bengenako ukuhamba ngokonwaba bangabuzwa pasi
ezindleleni, kude ngamanye amaxesha abanye babanjwe besiya ezityalikeni?
pofu singaze sike sive babanjwa bebesiya ezinkantini Ngecala lika
Rulumente yayingu Mr. HOFMEYR, ilungu. elibekekileyo lase Stellenbosch
no Sir GORDON SPRIGG Ngababuta onke amabibi ento-ngento ezingavani
nganto nalento kutetwa ngayo, ekude kwati nokude inge iroxiswa ngomva
lendawo, yaroxiswa pezu kwamazwi ati—“ Akuko sihlahla sokubonisa ukuba
unxile lukulile pakati kwa bantsundu, kanjalo akuko ndawo ityebisa ukuba
utywala buyagqitiswa enanini ukuselwa pakati kwamaqaba, nokuba
buyimbangeli ebangela ukuba bangenelwe ngamatyala entlobo zonke bona
ngoko kunezinye izizwe.”

Kwade kwatshiwo nokutiwa Abantsundu bona abafuni ukuba kubeko zinkanti
zivalwayo, nokutiwa uti u Rulumente akufun’ ukwenza okulungileyo
ebantwini angaxaswa bani. Elicebo lika Rulumente nabaxasi bake yayi
lelokuba lixoxwe ekubeni lalingelilo nelokwamkelwa, linjalonje
lingavakali ne yona nto liteta yona. Ukuba indawo ezipembelela uselo
azandisi lunxilo, maube lomteto ulungiselela ezompembelelo awuzifezi
indawo owenzelwe zona, awunasihlahla kwindawo omiselwe zona.
Ezingqondweni zomntu wonke, kukoleka ukuba ubunxila bahlala buyindlu
yamatyala (crime) kwaye kakade bekungena kuvela nto yimbi. Lendawo
yokuba ubunxila buyindlu yamatyala, inkolelo yayo, iya isanda ngokwanda,
kwanjengokuba indawo ezibupembelelavo zikwanjalo. Bute kuba buse nziwa
utywala (brandy) yaye ilahliwe nemiteto ebibiza imali ezitile kubenzi
babo (excise laws) baza ngoko o Rulumente bafumaneka bebuhlahlela
indlela zokuba yande intselo yabo, nokuba babangwe kukungacaci
kwenyaniso eziveliswayo zoncedo olubekoyo kwindawo ezivale inkanti,
nokuba kusuke kwacaca indawo elilungelo kubo kupela, abate bayikangela
bayipicota kwangapambili kokwenza ngoluhlobo benze ngalo.

Kodwa kona xa ute umcimbi lo wakangelwa wendeliselwa, zininzi zona
iziganeko ezityebisa ubunyaniso bokuba utywala bungumtshalalisi, nokuba
ezo ziganeko ziye kufunwa nakulipina icala. Singake sitabate uhlanga
olunga ma Lawo oluse South Africa olusuke Iweshwaka Iwapela ngenxa yabo,
kwa nama Indiya ase North America, awabangiswa kwabubo. Zozibini
ezozizwe zaye zingaba nini balomihlaba, (aboriginal races) zavelela
kona, noko zibangile zapela.

Make sitabate lamabalana avaliweyo esitili sangase Qonce, size sikangele
ukwanda korwebo lotywala olungeniswa kweli lizwe lase Africa ngokubanzi,
ngokukodwa e West Coast, yona into eyakuvela isaya kubanje ngezo zizwe
zabangayo.

Kodwa kona xa kukangelwa izizatu zoncedo olubekoyo ngokuvalwa kwamabala
atile esitili sase Qonce, zininzi ke zona, nezokubona ngamehlo, ukuba
ubunxila buncipile, ngamalazinge nasabonwayo luninzi Iwabantu. Baninzi
abangavelisa iziqonga zokoko, nabaseke bakwenza oko, nabahleli
sebecopele ukukwenza nokuba kude kwafuneka bede bafunga. Inteto yabo
ib’ingahamba ngohlobo oluti— Bekuti oko ub’ungeko lomteto wokuvalwa kwe
nkauti ezitile, bezihlala (inkanti) zitiwe ngunga zindimbane zezihlwele,
zamadoda nabafazi nabantwana, bonke bepants'ukuba ngababomvu. Azi u Mr.
HOFMEYR woyinqala na londawo ngokubekisele “ Kumaqaba ” aman’ukufunyanwa
ecalambe ezikoteni, ehamba iziqu, epants’ukuti onke afu- nyanwe
eziyakala ukunxila, esuke anje ngamaramnco aziralarume, ebangwa yi
brandy, ehamba ehlaba imikosi, exoxa ngencoko ezif’amanqe, nenteto
ezirwada zakwa Ntonjane. Yonke lonto yati gogololo wakumiswa lomteto
wokuvalwa kwe nkanti.

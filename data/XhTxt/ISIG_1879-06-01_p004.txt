Umlaz March 18, 1879.

Nkosi Mhleli we Sigidimi, yamkela nasisibongo sam, endibonga ngaso. Aba
Dlongwa. ngabesutu bakwa Sigonyela abacitwa ngu Mshweshwe ngemfazwe ye
1854, abanye baya e-Koloni abanye baya e-Natal.

U-Sigonyela waye nonyana abatatu, yati ukuba imfazwe ibacita u-Sigonyela
waya e-Koloni nabantu abaninzi, nendodana yake u-Lihana, nonyana bakwa
Sidlobongo, no Gabai. Wati u-Mr. Bunda waya e-Natal namadoda ake
omabini. Ufike u-Mr. Bunda e-Natal 1856 wabubeka pantsi ubukosi bake.
Wazi ukuba ubukosi bake butatyatwe ngu Mshweshwe. Wakonza ku Rulumente.

Ke ude wafa u-Mr. Bunda eyitobele yonke imiteto ka Rulumente, eyivuma
nokuyivuma ukuti ilungile imiteto yama Ngesi. Namhla afayo amlilela ama
Ngesi ati kufe indoda yamadoda. Kanti unyana wake osesihlalweni sake
sobukosi u-Hlubi unjalo. Nokuze kuma Bulu emncinane, ayambonga. Bonke
aba Dlongwa bayitobele imiteto ka Rulumente abangama kosana. Ke siyabona
nati ukuti aba Dlongwa bayitobele imiteto ka Rulumente, sibona mhla
wendaba ka Langalibalele 1873 aba Dlongwa basukela pe- zulu, kwakufika
ilizwi lika Rulumente. Eliti hlomani, bahloma masinya, bahamba pambili
kuyo yonke impi ka Rulumente, nokufa kwafa bona kuqala. Bafike basibenga
ngokutala, yaza yababonga inkosi yabo Colonel Durnford, yati, basebenza
kahle aba Dlongwa. Nanamhla kusenjalo jenga kwa Langalibalele nakwa
Zulu, basebenzile aba Dlongwa. Nakuyele yase Sandlwana, bayiqala kusasa
ngenkati yokudla aba Dlongwa. Zasala zonke izizwe ezimnyama ukuya ku
Zulu. Bayibamba aba Dlongwa laza latshona ilanga sisitsha besifa abantu
igazi lipalala njengamanzi. Bengavumi ukwahlukana no Rulumente aba
Dlongwa kunye nabase Edendale.

Kwaza kwafa inkosi yabo ebapeteyo u-Durnford, nomfo ka Somtseu George.

ENDIZIBONELE NGAMEHLO AM.

Natal March 14, 1879.

Ubuninzi bempi yama Zulu, ndiya kuvumela wena ubalisa ngemini enkulu
yakwa Sandlwana, ndim, owabesilwa lompi, ndingumrulumente ozalisekileyo,
ndiyilwela inkosi yam ngomxelo wam wonke, mandi- ngene endabeni. Sesuka
ngalomhla wama 23 ka December 1878, singamadoda ama 50 ase Edendale,
acelwe ngu Rulumente, ukuba aye kwa Zulu. Masendisiti sesuka saya
Emzinyati, sihlangene na Besutu, bakwa Mbunda, na Mangwe, ndite
ndakubona ubuninzi betu tina bakwele emahasheni, ndahleka ndedwa ndati
no Zulu namhla uya kuyifumana into yonke. Masendibekela entloko, nditi
safika ezintenteni apo isuke kona i-General ukuya kwa Matyana, sasipetwe
ngu Colonel Durnford, umfo obukali ekukalipeni. Kute ngolwesibini
sifikile kulendawo, wasiketa sama 26 ngaye u-Durnford, wati masixobe
siye kutimba ema Bulwini, singayazi kodwa into eyoniwe ngama Bulu,
sisukile ke, sazifaka endleleni inkabi zamahashe. Ihamba pambi kwetu
inkwenkwe emhlope, yapesheya. Kute kwakubona ukuba sikude kufupi apo
sisuke kona sibone ngehashe emva kwetu liza selilele pantsi, lifike
lamnika incwadi u-Durnford. Wati eyiyeka wayesiti masipenduke, sipindele
apo sisuka kona, siwanyatelise kalukuni ukuya kwase zintenteni, site
sifika yayisiti inkosi yetu yihlani niwakulule imi

ngaseko, nama Zulu emadolo anzima ukusondela kuti, site sakufika
ezintenteni ndibone inkosi yetu iselihamba pantsi, ubuso bayo
sebupendukile bubobunye, ndati wenziwa lusizi lokubona ezizidumba
zabafileyo izwi lake wati dubulani makwenkwe am ase Edendale watsho
kungaseko lusizo, ngokupela kwemisizi nembumbulu, sabutana ke sakuwela
Umzinyati. Mandipele ngelokuti, andikoyikisi mleseshi walencwadi, nditi
nakuba Uzulu esibulele kangakanje, sinetemba elikulu, lokuba Indlovukazi
yase London ukuba ekupeleni kwemihla iya kumnyatela Uzulu ukuba abe
ngapantsi kwenyawo zawo, Uzulu wabe yintonga yo Menzi wetu, abebeta
ngayo izizwe. Namhla umniniyo uyifaka emlilweni, lininzi igazi elim-
mangaleleyo elacitwa ngu Tshaka no Dingana no Mpande, no Cetywayo,
ndipelile.

Ngase Edendale e-Natal April 25, 1879.

Okwesibini ukusinda kuma Zulu. Kute ngalomhla wama 24 ka March 1879,
sesuka ngase Folozi, sacanda pakati kwezwe lakwa Zulu, singumkosi okwele
emahasheni we Nkosazana Umntanomhle, ngenye indlela Ukumkanikazi.
Sahamba siwela imilambo sada sakuwela Upongolo. Masendisiti ukwenza
kufutshane, saya kungena enqabeni ka Mbilini, safika apo salwa nabo
sazikupa ngamandla ozinkomo nozigusha, nozibokwe, siti maxa sibuyayo
sesiquba lemihlambi, wavela Uzulu ka Malandela, emva kwetu engamahlati
ubuninzi bake, satsho sonke ukuti sisindile, upants’ ukusivincela
kulanqaba ka Mbilini bekungayi kusinda noko amnye kuti. Lati ke igosa
elisipeteyo, masingalwi nabo ngokuba bebaninzi kunati, masizishiye
nenkomo sihambe. Ati amakafula asisayi kuzishiya tina, sahamba ke tina
asala ke wona eziquba. Wafika Uzulu kuwo. Wawabubisa onke lomakafula
abesezinkomeni. Sihambile ke tina saya kufika apo besiya kona salala.
Ndishiya indawo yokuti, ngalomhla sapuma ngawo enqabeni ka Mbilini Uzulu
wabulala ikulu kuma Bulu, sasinda tina. Mandize ke kulendawo yokuti
safika salala, manditi kanti Uzulu ulalisile emva kwetu. Kute kwakusa
sifikile, wavela Uzulu wemihla, emini emaqanda, wati ukufikakwake
wasifaka pakati, yateta inkwenkwe yapesheya, yati kokwenu ngoku bandla
lam, yasifunza. Site sakuti qolokoto, kwasekuqaleni njenge nkunzi
zenkomo, awubeka pantsi umhlope wakuti kunye namahashe omabini amabandla
ka Zulu. Ku­te kwesosituba xeshikweni sekumnyama kanye umntu emnye
pantsi. Lavutelwa ixilongo, lokuti masidedele umbayimbayi, samdedela,
yavakala imazi enomlomo yapesheya, yati isitsho yayiwisa into eninzi
pantsi yabantu, yayingenzeli luto kumabandla ka Zulu ka Mlandela. Bati
besiwa abanye babe abanye bekwe apezu kwabawileyo, besiya pambili,
besiti abanye uya vuya wena ulele apo, ati omnye egqita pezu komlingane
wake owileyo,. Ati kuye lala ntanga yetu, nam ndakulala pambi kwako.
Atsho enyanisile umfo wakwa Zulu. Sibambene ngalomhla lada laya pantsi
ilanga. Kwati enqabeni yamarwexu ako Nibe, Uzulu wazinqumbi ukufa kwake,
adubula apo alalapantsi. Kute emzuzwini Uzulu wavuma samquzula, samngena
ke semka naye, sambulala kwalusizi, sakupa imipefumlo emininzi
ezintenteni zayo, ngezwi labakulu ngalomhla, lada latshona sibagxota
sibabulala, wasigcina Umsindisi wemihla, njengoko wasigcina kwa
Sandlwana. Mandipele ngelokuti, kulusizi ukupuma impi, ungenaye
nowakowenu, ububi ndibufumana maxa imizalwana iketanayo, mna ndisala
ndedwa, ndabona ke ukuba andindedwa, Umsindisi warn undigcinile.

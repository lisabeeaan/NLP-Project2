INTENGO ZESIKITI.

E-WHITTLESEA—Ngomhla 5 ka May : Inkabi yenkomo elunga, indlebe yase
kunene itiwe pumpe, eyasekohlo yaxeliswa isisila senkonjane, impemvu.
Inkabi yenkomo empemvu emhlope indlebe nempumlo, indlebe yasekohlo itiwe
cipu,

ibalwe ngasekunene, iminyaka mihlanu.—Inkabi emhlope ete cokocoko
entloko nase ntanyeni, ebalwe ngesihlangu sehashe ngasekunene.— Inkabi
emnyama emhlope esiswini, umsila umhlope, indlebe yase kunene isipumpu
eyase kohlo ixelwe isisila senkonjane, amanqina asemva amhlope
omabini.—Inkabi emnyama, emhlope ngapantsi, emanqina angapambili
amhlope, umsila unqunyulwe, indlebe yasekohlo itiwe cipu, ibalwe H ngase

emnyama emhlope ngapantsi, indlebe yasekunene isipumpu eyase kohlo
ixelwe isisila senkonjane, inekolo enkulu.

UBALO LWE 1875.

Abafundi be Sigidimi bayalwazi olubalo— bantu beluko kulenyanga ifileyo
; kekaloku uti u Rulumente ufuna ukuba olu lubalo lube siseko sokumana
kubalwa izinto ezingabo abantu, ngokufuna ukwazi ukuba abantu bayanda
bayancipa kusinina.

Izinto eziyakubalwa ngabazelweyo ngabafile- yo, ngabatshatileyo,
ngabafikileyo bevela kwe- zinye indawo, nabafudukayo.—

Abantu belilizwe bangahlulwa babe maqela matatu xa kufunwa ukuba zibalwe
ezondawo :—

I.  Abantu abapetwe ngumfundisi walonkalo bazibiza ngayo, ngokwezinto
    zentlalo yokuba bengabantu—kutetwake obona buninzi babantu
    abamhlope, nabamnyama abangamakristu, nama Silamsi.

II. Iqela elinokukangelwa zizipatamandla ngokwezondawo—kutetwake
    ababantu baninzi bangenandawo imiyo, baqeshwe emlungwini, nabahleli
    emihlabeni yabelungu neqela labantu bentlanga zonke
    elingenakubalulwa, abasezidolo- pini, nangapandle.

III. Iqela elingenandlela yokukonza u Tixo, labeke lipantsi kolaulo Iwe
     mantyi kanye— kuqukwa intole ininzi yabantu abamnyama abahleli
     ezilalini.

Iqela lokuqala—Abafundisi bemvaba zonke bayakuti bamane ukubika
ngenyanga zonke inani labantu, Abazelweyo, Abafileyo, Nabatshatileyo
kwezo remente zipambi kwabo bangabali bambi. Angati umfundisi
wom-Wesile, nokuba ngowase Tshatshi, nokuba ngowama Yuda, mhlaumbi wama
Slamsi, abale abantu abangengabo abalomihlambi yabo. Lendawo kufuneka
ukuba ilunyukelwe ukuze angati kanti umntu ubalwe kabini nakatatu.

Iqela lesibini liya kubalwa zizibonda (Field Cornets) nenkosi zama
dindala. Maze balu- mke bangabali umntu ofanele ukubalwa ngum- fundisi.

Iqela lesitatu. Abeliqela boxelelwa ukuba batike ngokwabo nokuba kukwi
mantyi, noku­ba kukwi zibonda zalolali basukuba bekuyo— lonke inani
labazelweyo, nabafileyo nabatshatileyo—kulondawo bakuyo.

Kuya kufuneka ke ngoko ukuze bonke abantu baukalipele lomteto, ati
ofikayo aseleya kuzibika kwaoko kumfundisi nokuba kuse sibondeni nokuba
kukwimantyi—kube kwanjalo kosukuba efuna ukushenxela kwenye indawo.
Noqonda ke kulamazwi ukuba kufuneka ete wabikwa kwa oko osukuba ezelwe
mhlaumbi ebubile. Abaleseshi be Sigidimi baya celwa ukuze bayibike
kumntu wonke lendawo. Kufuneka ide yaziwa yaqondwa naleli bomvu iqaba.

IBANDLA ELIYI MBUMBA.

Kwi “SIGIDIMI” sokugqibela kwabonaka inteto yombaleli u “ SILWANGANGUBO”
esati ngokuswela isituba asayipendula kwa oko, sasesiyenza umbeko.
Lenteto yayi balelwe ukutetelela umanyano Iwekaba elimele imimandla
yangapakati ekutiwa yi Mbumba Yamanyama, kwinteto esayenza
ngentlanganiso. Umbali ube nendawana asibeka amabala amabi ngazo
malungana nawo wonke umoya wetu ngakwi Mbumba kwamhla yasekwa.
Kukuzikusela kwezi ntonga ke esibuyela kwakona kwakwi Mbumba.

U “SILWANGANGUBO” uti Imbumba “Ayifumani “[nkutazo] fair play kwi
Sigidimi; ” atsho aye kulinganisa ngemiqolo emibini esaka sayibala nge
Mbumba, engaba yayingeyiyo yokuncoma, ekukupela kwayo, kuba asikumbuli
nteto imbi saka sayenza ngayo. Lonteto yeti, “Siyazi ukuba kulo manani
abalwa yi Mbumba akuko “mntu (or bambalwa) unentloko yokupendula mhla
ati “u Rulumeni nenza ntoni?” Imbumba iminyaka mibini iko, ixoxa;
ingxelo zayo zonke, ezinye ezinde kunene, bezivakaliswa kwelipepa
ngapandle kwekwele. Ngazwiuye ixilongo layo beli leli pepa maxesha onke.
Kodwa namhla u “ SILWANGANGUBO ”—lom’bali, balesi, ngoyena “ Hambani ”
we Mbumba inteto yake ayina kufumane iyekwe nje—siti, u “ SILWANGANGUBO
” namhla utabata yonke lemigudu yetu yoku kolisa Imbumba, uyibeka
kwelinye icanci lesikali, kwelinye ubeka lentetwana sayenzayo, uti ke
emva kokuzitelekisa “ Asifumani fair play e Sigidimini ! ” Esi siteto, u
fair play masibe siteta ntoyimbi ekuqondeni kuka “SILWANGANGUBO”
kunaleyo ivisiswa luninzi. Sicinga tina ukuba Imbumba ifumene yonke i
fair play, nangapaya kwe fair play kwi Sigidimi.

Ndawo kodwa singazanga sayenza kukuvuma indumiso ze Mbumba. Nakulento
saxakwa kwa ngama lungu ayo. Kweli lase Maxoseni sasixwilwe sisandi
sokusekwa kwayo; sisacinga ukuba ingaba lomhlambi walanayo uzakubunjwa
ngo DEMETRIYO base Bayi nase Rafu nase Colesberg na. wotiwanina
ukwenjiwanjalo isinqe nentloko yawo sikweli lase Maxoseni nje? Sasimelwe
yile mibuzo, sisiti soka siyibone namhla lenyoka ingamakwele omzi
ontsundu, intloko ikweli lase Maxoseni, imsile use Mlungwini ngapakati
iyakubulawa ngokutyunyuzwa komsila. Siti sasisakanyiswe imilomo yile
mibuzo seva nge Mbumba, kwi Sigidimi so 18 July, 1883, isenza umemezo
“Umkulu Mbumba yama Afrika, I ” Amazwi ngawo la:

Igama lolu Manyano ngokwama Ngesi yi “South African Associa­tion.”
Yintlanganiso emiselwe ukuba ibe liziko lokuxoxa zonke izinto zokunyusa
uhlanga. Nokuba zeze Mfundo, nokuba zeze Ngqeqesho, nokuba zezo Laulo,
nokuba zezamatuba empumelelo, “nokuba zeze mihlaba (ukubandeza igada
lobawo ngokutenga amatuba kulemihla mibi). Amasiko, imikwa, nazo zonke
izinto zenveli ekubonakalayo ukuba ziyintintelo ekuhlumeni kohlanga ziya
kuxoxwa kuyo le Mbumba.

Zopuma kuyo le mbumba izicelo ezibeka e Botwe, nemitandazo yokutandazela
inkululo yenkosana ezemka nemisinga yemfazwe. Koti ukuqutywa kwezi zinto
zimiselwe i Committee zazo, ukuze zibe nokuqutywa ngenyameko. Yobako i
Committee yemfundo, neye miteto yolaulo, neyokwalusa amalungelo e
Locations, neyokugqala amalungelo aba voti (a committee to attend to the
Registration of voters). Yobako i Committee enkuluyentlanganisoyomnyaka
eziyakupakelakuyo zonke ezinye imicimbi, ukuze ilungiswe yintlanganiso
yomnyaka. Koti emizini yonke kwa kumiswa le Mbumba, kuyilwe i Committee
zokukangela imicimbi efanele ukungeniswa entlanganisweni yomnyaka. Le
yomnyaka ayikuma ndaweni nye, yoti ngomnye umnyaka ibe se Bayi, kanti
ngomnyeyoba se Cradock, e Grahamstown, e Qonce njalo njalo! Abo iya
kungabiko kubo botumela abatunywa babo (Delegates) bokubavela indaba,
nokuba yimilomo yabo. Sizama isiqalo somcimbi onje ke ngayo le Mbumba.
Sisiva ngendlebe ngati ziko intlanganiso eziyelene nale yetu.
Asizidelile, ukuba ziquba lomcimbi wetu sobamkela abatunywa baze,
kanjako imicimbi etunyelwa zizo kuti soyixoxa. Noko ke asiyi- boni into
exakayo ukuba zingangeni zonke pantsi kweli gama le Nzala (aborigines)
yeli lizwe ngokwe mvelo.

Ayaneliswanga kukuxela u SIMON wase Samariya ukuziteta yenjenje Imbumba,
ijike yangcikiva intlanganiso endala ezalwa yiyo. Yenjenje ukumtuka
unina :

Iko kanjako nale ibifudula ibizwa ngokuba yeyo Titshala, namhla kutiwa
yi “Native Educational Association.” Mkulu umsebenzi wayo, unjalo nje
ufana nentlaka yeliso ngobu delicate (au! lindixakile esi Xoseni. Kuko
abati ke nayo leyo inokuwuquba lomsebenzi wale yetu.

Siyiva singayivisisi londawo. Ngani? 1. Lentlanganiso yobangenisa
engozini o Titshala, kuba boxakwa bakungena kweze lizwe. 2. O dade
boxakwa kwakungenwa kwezo Laulo. 3. Umongameli ngumlungu woxakwa
kwakutetwa ngo Langalibalele, no Gonya, no Matanzima 4. Isingesi
sowaxaka amaqaba ohlanga. 5. U Dr. Dale worazula izivato zake ambate
intlakantlaka ahlale elu tutwini ukuba usapo lwake lupumile emadlelweni
amnandi emicimbi yemfundo, selu penu-penuka ematambekeni, nase mazeni
agqumayo e Politics. LE YETU yoba likuselo kuzo i Teachers, kuba zoxoxa,
zivote, zingaveliswa amagama azo kupela kwimicimbi yemfundo. Ngokunjalo
no Wauchope abafo be Civil Service bokuselwa kwayiyo.

Wonke onengqondo waqala ukuyidela kule misebenzi mibini Imbumba :
owokuzincoma, nowoku-Dyoba abanye ngodaka. Asitetanga nto noko, kuba
sasisazi ukuba kukapukapu ukuteta kanti ukwenza kunzima. Salindela
izenzo ke kwi Mbumba; azibonakalanga. Entweni zonyulo siyifumene inje
ngengdbngolo etyumkileyo. Saka safuna ukwayama ngayo ngalo maxesha e
Bayi, e Tinara, e Rafu, e Colesberg. Amagama amalungu e Palamente
awanyulwa zezindawo anele ukubonisa umsebenzi wayo. Ngokungeniswa
kwamagama encwadini yaba votiumncomi wayo u “ SILWANGANGUBO ”
usibalisela ngemisebenzi eyenziwa ngu Mr. FRANK MAKWENA e Bayi! Asivanto
nge Tinara nezinye izitili enembuto kuzo Imbumba. Siyoyika ukuba mabe
akukonto yenziwayo; sesitutuzelwa seso siteto sase Mlungwini siti, “No
news is good news.”

Kanti ke ezi asizona nto siyohlwaya ngazo kupela Imbumba. U “
SILWANGANGUBO ” usalatisa kum’miselo we Mbumba oti, “ Makume
intlanganiso kwindawo zonke “ apo kuko abantu abaliqela.” Lom’miselo
asiwuvisisi. Ingaba uteta ukuba ezamfama ziputaputayo zase Ngqushwa
mazibe nentlanganiso yazo zodwa na? Ukuba kunjalo ingaba Imbumba ayisoze
ibe noncedo, kwanjengokuba ingancedi Into to Oom SWANEPOEL no Tante
KETELBEK base Colesberg. Kusiza ntonina ukuhlanganisa intloko, nokuba zi
140 zabantu abangazi nento ngesiko lase Mlungwini. Abantu bafuna
intlanganiso eziya kuquka pakati kwazo amadodana akanyiselweyo ngokwento
zesiko lase Mlungwini ukuze kuxoxwe into enenkozo. Sivuya kunene
ukupaula ukuba umoya wododana lo Manyano utyekela kweli cala. Inkozo
zizenzo ezenzelwa wonke umzi (asizigxeki ezo zase Bayi; siya zincoma)
yeyona nto iyakubanga ukuba imixelo yabantu ikolwe kunemfumba zezitiloyi
zamazwi. Sifuna IZENZO ngezimini. Amazwi mawalandele kaba lakowetu.
Ningenzakali, fumanani inkutazo, mabandlandini e Mbumba.

AMA MFENGU ASE NGQUSHWA.

SINOSIZI ukuva ngo Mr. MARK MPAHLA, inene lase Ngqushwa elisesazulwini
se nkosi kwa NJOKWENI, libika ukupitizela kwabantu ngendawo ebangwe
yinto ebike yapambi kwe Palamente le isandukwahlukana. Umbaleli wetu
uti, “Intliziyo zabantu kweli zibuhlungu ngenxa “yento esiyitunyelweyo e
Sigidimini yokuba inkosi zase “Ngqushwa zivunyelwa ukumka ukuya Pesheya
kwe “Nciba. Ezi zindaba esizifumene epepeni lika July 18 “1884. Site xa
sivuyayo, sibulelayo, sabizwa edolopini “ yimantyi yase Ngqushwa, u Mr.
HODGES. Ute zakufika “inkosi ngo 31 July waliteta mhlope wati u
Rulumente “walile ukuba inkosi ziwele. Kutiwe bahleli kakuhle, “wati
utunyiwe ukuba axelele inkosi apo kugqitywe kona “ngu Rulumente.” Lento
ke ibete bonke bakamisa umlomo. Siva ukuba abantu bangqubngqubeka kwimvo
ngemvo; abanye bati makubuziswe kubafundisi, abanye bati makuyiwe
emagqweteni—nawo seleba biza esiti umzi umnye maurole iponti ayakuba
lungisela.

Akuseko situba singayingenisa kuso incwadi ka Mr. MPAHLA kweli pepa
lanamhla; sesikaulezela ukuyipendula ngamazwi ambalwa. Singa singacebisa
umzi lo wase Ngqushwa ukuba ubuyeke ubutyututyutu emcimbini onjengalo,
budla ngokonakalisa izinto ezinkulu. Imicimbi enje itandana isetyenzwe
ngentloko ezipolileyo. Nokoku-

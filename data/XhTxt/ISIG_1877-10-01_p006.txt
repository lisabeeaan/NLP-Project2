IZINTO NGEZINTO.

ababe betwe ngumbane. Indlu yake iraulwe yatshiswa naye wahlatywa
ngezikali wabulawa.—Alice Times.

ELINYE lamapepa ase Queenstown liti kusand’ ukulwa abantu baka Anta
nabaka Visi, isizatu kukuxabana kwabantwana. Kwelinye icala kubulewe
abantu abatatu, babaninzi abanenduma.

yokuhlekisa. Uti abanye abafundisi bafundisa imfundiso
eziposisekileyo—ezinje ngokushumayela Ilizwi kubahedeni!—Alice Times.

ELINYE lamapepa amangesi liti isoda xa yenziwe intlama yabekwa kwindawo
etshileyo asikuko nokuba iyanceda. Kufuneka ukuba ati umntu akuyi qaba
esilondeni ayigqume ngelapi elimanzi, aze aman’ ukulinyela side sipole.

UMFO abati ngu Paul unikwe inyanga ezintatu etolongweni ngokuba intsiba
zenciniba ezilishumi elinesitandatu e-Middleburg. Abafuyi benciniba
kulamacala ase Uitenhage bayakala kakulu ngokubiwa kwentsiba zabo
ngabantsundu.

NGENYANGA ka July abantsundu abafike kwele Diamond bevela kwezinye
indawo babe 2, 93. Beze kufuna umsebenzi. Isiqingata sabo ngabe Sutu.
Kubonakala ukuba inani labasebenzi liza kuba lininzi kakulu lide
lidlule. Abangafumani msebenzi kona baya kufika uwodwa kule koloni.

OBULEWEYO—Ngakwa Mr Finlayson e-Uitenhage kubuye kwabulawa omnye umntu.
Ngumzulu ekufikwe elele wahlatywa wafa kwalapo. Amapolisa afike kusitiwa
ubalekile umbulali weza ngase Uitenhage. Usafunwa ngawo nama Zulu amane.

AMAPEPA ase Bayi ati kulomacala kuke kwalwa Amazulu nama Xosa kwako
omnye obulawayo. Ku- de kwaya kulamla amapolisa, abamba abane abona
bebezintloko. Kutiwa kulungiselelwa ukuze ibuye ivuswe lonto ngomhla
wokuqala ka October.

KUMATYALA abetetwa e-Uitenhage pambi ko Mgwebi u-Denyssen bekuko nelika
Nekoni ekwakuke kwavakala ukuba ubulele Umlungu ngomhla 12 ka May.
Ebungqineni kubonakele ukuba wambulala besilwa kengoko abambadanga.
U-Mgwebi umnike iminyaka elishumi etolongweni esebenza nzima.

IRAFU YEZINJA.—Abagcini mzi wase Qonce bayazisa ukuba wonke umntu onenja
uyakuva kuyibika kwisandla salomzi, aze arole isheleni ezimbini ngenja
ukuyi rafela. Kufuneka kanjalo ukuba zonke zibe nento emqaleni ekubalwe
kuyo igama lomnini nja Engenalo elogama iya kubulawa ngendlela abaya
kuyi misela abapatimzi.

F-Rini kuko isiumbu somntwana ontsundu esi- funyenwe singcwatywe ngase
ndlwini. Ingcwaba libe lisidibi, sabonakala isidumbu ngokusuka sipandwe
yinja. Kubikwe kwizipata mandla. lati lakufika igqira labona ukuba
uzelwe epilile. Unina ufunyenwe wati akubuzwa wavuma ukuba umzele
epilile umntwana kodwa wafa kwakamsinya emva koko. Elotyala lisa
pengululwa.

U-MR BLENCOWE utumele kumapepa ase Natal incwadi ebonisa ukuba umkosi ka
Cetywayo ungama buto amashumi mabini anamane, oko kukuti ungamadoda
36.550. Kwelonani amawaka amatatu ngamaxego angasenamsebenzi mkulu.
Amawaka amane—lamabuto kutiwa ngama “panginyama,”—asengabantwana.
Amadoda aqinileyo ke ngoko okulwa akumawaka angamashumi amatatu.

U-K J. Ɓ. asazi ukuba waengabonanga na Esigidimini ukuba asiyi
kushicilela ncwadi ingabalwanga ngokuzeleyo igama lombali wayo. Incwadi
ezinjalo kade sisazisa ukuba aziyi kungena apa. Enye indawo isicelo
sabaguquli be Zibalo siti makati onesikalazo awaxele amazwi ati
akalunganga axele nendlela afanele ukubekwa ngayo. Incwadi ezingenayo
londawo, ezenza isigwebo zinganiki mazwi okulinganisela, asisayi
kuzishicilela.

E-NATAL kuko into esisixaki. Umlungu otile wave hamba kwelama Zulu
wabulawa sisicaka sake U-Cetywayo wasizisa e-Natal eso sicaka ukuba size
kohlwavwa ngemiteto yama Ngesi. Sati sakufika kwabonakala ukuba
asinakohlwayelwa umntu obulewe kwelama Zulu Sabuya ke sasiwa ku Cetywayo
ukuba aye kusohlwaya yena Usuke yena akayipendula londawo. Kekaloku
abamisimiteto base Natal bafun’ ukwazi ukuba ude wapelela pina lomntu,
nokuba kungenziwa nina.

IKUBA ELIHLABA IMIQELA EMINE.—E-Malmesbury ngase Kapa kusand’ukulingwa
ikuba lawenziwe ngu Mr. Howards elenza imiqela emine ngaxesha nye. Omnye
umfo obekona uti, “ ukuba kuza kulinywa umhlaba ongekalungiswa kakuhle,
ekunqabileyo ukuba ulinywe elona kuba lilungileyo lelenza umqela omnye;
kodwa ukuba intsimi ikumhlaba olungileyo, osekukafuti ulinywa, asikuko
nokuba lilungile elikuba lenza imiqela emine.

ABANTSUNDU E-KIMBERLEY.—Apakwele Diamond kuko iqela elikulu labantsundu
abangasa funi kubuyela emakaya. Sebemile apa, batshata abafazi
ezityalikeni, baka ityalike zokutshatela. Abantsundu xa sikangela ubuze
babo, singati banenyameko engapezu kweyomntu omhlope ekwakeni ityalike.
Banendlu ezimbini zokubedeshela e-Kimberley, inye e- Dutoits Pan.
Inkonzo zabo zihanjiswa ngokuzola okukulu. Ngenye isabata endandiyile
ndafika ndakuncoma ukuvuma kwabo kwanentshumayelo nemitandazo.—News.

E-NATAL.—U-Mr. Joyce omnye wabongameli aba- ntsundu e-Maritzburg e-Natal
uti ukubala ngabantsundu :—“ Kuko abantsundu abakumashumi amahlanu
abakulele kulomzi. Ababantu sebenokuwaqonda amasiko okukanya. Ngokoke
bayanxila bebe. Ontsundu xa angekafundi utembekile ulungile, kodwa uti
akuhlangana nabamhlope isimilo sake sibe yenye into. Amadoda abantsundu
akulo mzi angamawaka amatatu, amhlope aliwaka elinamakulu mane. No- koke
elona nani likulu lamanxila lelabamhlope. Kwezinyanga zintandatu
zidluleyo abamhlope ababe- nxilile babe 87, abantsundu 11.”

UMBALI otile oncwadi ikwelinye lamapepa ase Queenstown uti eteta
ngokumiselwa kuka Mr. P. Masiza ebufundisini, “ Kuya kufuneka ukuba
azinikwe izibabalo ezifuneka kulomsebenzi wobupristi— okwangoku uyedwa,
akuko wumbi untsundu unamandla obupostile okunika imitendeleko.”
Kubonakala ukuba lombali akazi ukuba baliqela abanye abantsundu
abangapandle kuka Mr. Masiza abanalo igunya lokunika imitendeleko,
abanje ngo Mr. Pamla no Mr. Lwana no Mr. Mzimba nabanye. Ukuba uteta
ukuti u-Mr. Masiza ukupela konokwenjenjalo kwelo- hlelo akulo ingavakala
lonto kodwa ukuti ukupela pakati kwabantsundu kukulahlckisa
abangakwaziyo ukuhambiseka komsebenzi we Nkosi pakati kwabant- sundu.
Abafundisi abantsundu ababekwe izandla bafikile kwishumi elinesitatu.
Kuma Wesile odwa base sibozweni. Kulusizi ukuba bengenga pezulu, nokoke
eso asisiso sizatu sokuba kutiwe mye kwapela onegunya lokunika
umtendeleko.

EZOKUGQIBELA ezivela kwa Zulu ziti kuko izizwana ezibini ezilwayo. Umfo
otile obala ngalonto uti: “ Kute ekuqaleni kuka August kwesuka esinye
isizwe sama Zulu saya kuvingcela esi bati sesase Sebeni esipetwe ngu
Assegai. Sifike satimba, abantu bengakumbulele nto. Bate bakuva ukuba
sezimkile yaputuma impi yazifumana zise sendleleni. Iqubisene imikosi
wagxotwa obuze kutimba, wabuya omnye wagoduka nenkomo zawo. Nokoke
kubonakala ukuba ayi kapeli lonto. U-Cetywayo akapenduli nto kuyo noko
ezo zizwe zozibini zibe zimcela ukuba alamle.” Enye indawo evakalayo
yeyokuba u- Somtseu ubeza kuya kucanda umda opakati kwakwa Zulu ne
Transvaal.

obala e-Willawmore uti, Asikuko nokuba kulusizi. Akangekolwe umntu

UKUHANJISWA KWELIZWI

NGONCEDO LUKA MR. GORDON.

(HONOURABLE H. J. GORDON.)

KUSAND’ ukufika E-Lovedale umfundisi
ongu Dalzell ovela E-Scotland ukuya
kugcina isikolo sika Gordon. Uhamba
nenkosikazi yake.

Indlela esiqaleke ngayo esi sikolo ibu-
yodwa. Ngonyaka we 1866, indoda
ebekekileyo U-Henry James Gordon

unyana wesibini wesikulu sase Aberdeen
(oku kukuti Lord Aberdeen) wali fumana
ilifa lake. Lemali yayi linani elikulu.
Kutiwa yayi ngama waka amasbumi
mahlanu eponti (£50,000).

Ekuqaleni U-Mr. Gordon waye nom-
qweno wokuya E-America ahlale pakati
kwama India awangabantu abamnyama
belozwe, abafana nenkoliso yabelilizwe
ngokuswela I-Gospel, nemfundo.

Wacinga ngokutenga umhlaba ake
umzi kona apo ilizwi nemfundo ziya
kufundiswa zityilwe. Wabuya kodwa
wakumbula ngela pesheya kwe Nciba.
Oko bekungekabiko Maxosa nama Mfengu.
Waselesiti uya kuza E-Africa ahlale
pakati kwabantu balapa. Kodwa ukufu-
duselwa kwabantu pesheya kwe Nciba
kwabanga ukuba ayilahle longcinga.
Kwati engekafumani qinga limbi wafa
ngebaqo. Waye lungisa umpu wake,
engawulumkele kakulu, waza ke wona
wamdubula wafa kwalapo, egumbini ebe-
hlala kulo E-Cambridge, apo waye funda
kona.

Nokoke izihlobo zake azivumanga
ukuba umnqweno wake utshabalale ngo-
kufa kwake. Zabeka bucala amawaka
omatandatu eponti (£6,000) okumisa isi-
kolo esiya kuba sikumbuzo sake. Kungo-
koke lomsebenzi uyakubizwa ngo Gordon.

UBUSELA BABAMNYAMA.

IZIKALAZO zabafuyi zezinyanga zmtandatu zidluleyo ngokubiwa kwe mpahla,
zibona- kele ubunyaniso bazo ngenani lamatyala okuba, abetetwa E-Rini
ngale nyanga ifileyo. Lomzekelo ukohlakeleyo ubonakala ungati uyasulela.
Baninzi abafuyi abangazanga babelwa gusha ababelweyo namhla.

Sibalisile kakulu kwela Maxosa icala leli pepa, ngokutetwa kwamatyala,
esitembayo ke ukuba into eyenziweyo kwabo bangena kwahlula pakati
kwempahla yabo neya bamelwana babo iyakunceda abaninzi. Baninzi
abagwetyelwe ikatsi— isohlwayo esoyikwa kunene. Ngokuhlokonyiswa kwezi
ndaba ayakwazeka elizweni lonke amagama abo bohlwaywe ngoluhlobo, baze
bati naxa bapumileyo etolongweni banga ncedi nto nokuba baya likanyela
elihlazo lekatsi.

Siyatemba kanjalo ukuba koqondakala, ukuba akubangako kuketa bala, kuba
eso- na sohlwayo sinzima—iminyaka esixenxe nomsebenzi onzima—sinikwe
komhlope. Abahlobo betu abamnyama mabaqonde ukuba akuko nto ibanga ukuba
bahlale becasene, omhlope nomnyama, nje ngalento yokuba.

E-Scotland kudala, umteto wokuba wa- wunzima ngango kude isohlwayo sobe
igusha sibe kukuxonywa. Kuyavuyeleka ukuba ilizwi lika Tixo
liyidambisile lomiteto yokuba, kuba ukuba belingafikanga ngebe baninzi
abahlala bexonywa kwelilizwe. Ukuhluma kwaba mhlope nabamnyama
kuhlangene ; kuya kuqutye- Iwa pambili kukuti bahlalelane ngoxolo. Onke
amatyala ahlekayo abanga ukuhla- lelana ngokunga tembani; aze kwaba
mabala ngamabala abange ukuhlalelana kakubi, nange ntiyano. Kuyi nyaniso
ukuba obela omhlope uya kumbela nomnyama. Singabala isibadubadu
senqenera esiyindoda ekwa se Dikeni apa, ete, isandukupuma etolongweni,
okokuba yayibe inani lenkomo namatole kumlungu ongumfuyi, yaselisiya
kuba ibokwe yama hlwempukazi amabini, ikupela kwayo— iyibela isikumba
esi sodwa, yayishiya inyama yonke ebuhlanti, ngapandle kwe- ntwana
encinane. Sikohlwe naligama esingalibiza ngalo imenemene elintliziyo
injalo.

Isigidimi Sama-Xosa

NGOLWESI-BINI, APRIL 1, 1884.

ISIKOLO SENTOMBI EMGWALI.

KWELI BALA singa namhla singa ngenisa ingxelo yomcimbi oya kwenza mnandi
umxelo womhlambi wakowetu. Lengxelo ke sibulela umhlobo omdala we
Sigidimi ngayo :

Umhla we 19 ka March, ube ungumhla obalulekileyo malunga nemicimbi
yokufunda kwentombi ezintsundu. Emgwali wakwa Ngqika kube kuvulwa
inzwakazi yendlu yokufundela intombi. Abavela gama bebeqale kwangemini
engapambili ukufika : kwaza ngomhla omkulu babonakala kwakusasa abavela
kumizana esemacaleni, abamhlope nabantsundu. Lize kufika ixa lokuvula
indlu kuse kuzele ngapandle; nalapo kupekwe kona. Ngexa leshumi elinanye
ibahlanganisele ndawonye intsimbi: baza badwela ngapandle kwendlu, usapo
Iwakona Iwahlaba ingoma yokuvula ucango, Iwatsho ngento emnandi
ebuhlungu. Uluvulile okunene umfundisi omkulu wakona u Rev. Mr. Cumming.
Lwavulwa lixego elikucwayita kubete kwayisulela yonke into ebikona,
Bangene abantwana kwigumbi elikulu, abati liya kuba lelokufundela usapo,
baza balandela nabantu. Bati bakulizalisa eligumbi u Mr. Cumming wabika
ukuba uza kuhamba nengcibi yalendlu, abakangelise amagumbi ayo abatanda
ukubona. Ube ngumrozo omde emva kwalo madoda, asitabata asisa kumagumbi
apezulu ; kuba lenzwakazi yendlu ine zintlu ezimbini zamagumbi. Sinyuke
sinyuka sada sakufika, sagqoba sipuma kumzi opeleleyo, into ebete abanye
beta ababa sazi nalapo bakona, nalapo asinga kona amadoda esibe
siwalandela. Siyalule sayalula sabona sise sikwezi pantsi izindlu, pofu
singehlanga ngendlela ebesinyuke ngayo. Kubuye kwahlanganiswana kwela
gumbi sibe sifikele kulo, waza kona u Mr. Cumming wavakalisa ukuba
namhla lendlu ivuliwe, kekaloku make kubulelwe ku Tixo osikeleleyo ukuze
kube kuyavuywa siti. Utsho watandaza obuhlungu, ebulela ukulunga kuka
Tixo, ukuhlumisa kwake umsebenzi owaqalwa ngaba ngasekoyo, ukuvumela
ukuba usapo Iwakwa Xosa malube nomzi onje ngalo siwubonayo. Emva koko
wenza inteto yokuvakalisa ukuzala kwentliziyo yake luvuyo analo ngale
ndlu ; ukuvuya kwaba fundisikazi bentombi nabantu bonke base Mgwali.
Kulandele u Tobi ngomtandazo, kwaza kwacelwa ukuba kuke kuyiwe ngase
zimbizeni ukuze sekuhlanganwa etyalikeni abantu behluti. Kucitakelwe
okunene, abanye baya ngase sikolweni sosapo esidala, abanye bafumana
okumnandana kwakweso sakiwo sitsha.

Emva kwe dinala ivakele ihlokoma kwakona intsimbi, wabutana umzi wonke
kulo tyalike inkulu kunene yase Mgwali eyakiwa ngumfo ka Soga. Babete
yazala, nakuba ingaxinananga. Sakukangela site kubafundisi be U. P.
balapo o Rev. Messrs Cumming no Stirling base Mgwali; u Dewar wase
Nkonkobe, Lundie wakwa Gcaleka, no Gezani ongati uza kuya kwa Pini,
ukuba iyamvumela impilo. Kwabe Free Church sibone o Revs. Bryce Ross
wapantsi kwamahlati, J. Rus­sell wase Monti, J. McDonald wase Dutywa, P.
J. Mzimba wase Love­dale, E. Makiwane wase Rwarwa no E. Tsewu osancedisa
kwa Pini. Kw abase Wesile sibone u Rev. J. Sikwebu wangase Bolo. Kwabase
German yayiyingwevu emfupi esingayazanga. Kuzima mhlaba siqonde Imantyi
yelozwe, u Mr. Wright, ingxwenga entliziyo icacileyo, no J. J. Irvine
Esq. wase Kubusi, umfo esinga angake abongwe ngu Hadi; no Feni into ka
Tyali inkosi yama Ngqika apesheya kwe Nciba. Ebeko namanye amanene ke
amhlope nantsundu ; kwanamakosikazi nama kosazana esingebe saquba magama
awo.

Emva kwe culo nomtandazo u Mr. Cumming ucele ukuba isihlalo sitatwe ngu
Mr. Irvine. Usitabatile okunene umfo lowo, umfo obubele bude bapumelela
nase busweni, into leyo ebangele ukuba atiyeke ko Shambuluka no Sprigg.
Usuke wati umnene ndiza apa intliziyo inovuyo nosizi. Ndibe ndicinga
ngemini zokuqalwa kwalo mzi, ndakumbula ukuba kanene abako apa namhla
abaseki bawo ; abona bantu ngeziba ziyapupuma intliziyo luvuyo ukuba
bebe wubona lomsebenzi. Mna ndazana nalo mzi usaqalwa ngo Soga no
Johnson no Miss Ogilvie. Indlwana ababekuzo zazidelekile, baquba noko,
balindela ukuba abalandelayo baya kufika babeke ngapambili. U Soga yena
waye ngomnye wetu, waza wazincama ezincamela nina nabantwana benu. Ubom
ba-

ke wabunikela ngenxa yelungelo lenu balapa namhlanje. Iyabongeka imizamo
ayenzayo ngenxa yenu. Wasebenza apa Emgwali kwada kwabonakala kuvela
lendlu yetyalike, inkulu, intle, yomeleleyo ; kwaza kwakubonakala ukuba
ukedamile umzi wakwa Gcaleka pesheya kwe Nciba wavuma ukusishiya isiqamo
semisebenzi yake apa aye kwaka omnye umzi kona. Wafika wabufinyeza ubom
bake kona ngokuzinikela kwake emsebenzini. Abantu balapa ubashiyele
umfuziselo omhle kunene. Igama lake bafanele ukuhlala belibulela. Omnye
wabantu abafinyeze ubom babo ngenxa yenu yinkosazana u Miss Ogilvie ode
wabuyela kwela pesheya kolwandle. Nalapo usasebenza, intliziyo yake
ilapa. Lomsebenzi ninawo namhlanje mkulu kanye. Into ebuhlungu kum kuba
ndiman’ ukubuza ukuba kanene kumiswa lendlu nje namhla kulo mhlaba wakwa
Ngqika, apina ama Ngqika? Ndiyavuya kunene ukubona inkosi u Feni; kodwa
sipina isizwe sakowabo ? Wati umfundisi u Soga akuti umhlaba mawucandwe,
asampulapula isizwe sakowabo. Ukuba sasilandele icebo lake, nanamhla
lomhlaba ngewuba ngowa ma Ngqika endaweni yokuba ngowa ma fama.
Ndivuyile ukuva ukuba niya zitabata iziqiniselo zemihlaba yenu nina
baseleyo. Namhla nimiselwe indlu entle kakulu. Bonke abantu baza
kukangela kuni ukuba niyintonina. Maze nikubonise ngemisebenzi
ukuyivuyela kwenu lendlu, ukubavuyela kwenu abafundisi abazincameleyo
ngenxa yenu, ukubulela kwenu ulaulo olulungileyo, namalungelo emfundo.
Maze nisigcine isimilo endihlala ndiqayisa ngaso apo nigxekwa kona,
esokuba akuko mkondo uke uye kungena kumhlaba wase sikolweni.

U Rev. J. F. CUMMING wavakalisa ukuba kwati kwakufika u Miss Mac-
Ritchie kwabonakala sisincinane isikolo ebekufundelwa kuso ; kwazake
kwayilwa lendlu. Abantu bapesheya kolwandle bati bona baya kurola
£2,500, ukuba abalapa barole £300. Savuma ke tina ukuba solilinga
elonani. Kute kwakubon’ ukuba iyaqutywa indlu kwavela umlilo owatshisa
impahla zokwaka, wema umsebenzi kwafuneka nemali engapezulu. Abantu
bapesheya bati bakuyiva lonto batumela £600. Ke kaloku tina kufuneka
sifeze amakulu amatatu. Nina bantu base Mgwali nibe nine gama elibi
lokuvimba. Mhla kwabekwa itye lokuseka elogama nalicita, abagxeki
nabavala imilomo. Lomini abamhlope barola £17 naza nina narola £62 ; e
Tunxe barola £32. Maze nanamhla nifuzise okwalo mini; zise ninzi izinto
ezifunekayo.

U Rev. P. J. MZIMBA ubulele ukuba isihlalo sipatwe ngumfo oyaziyo imbali
yalo mzi, wati yena akazi kuteta ngobuhle nokulunga kwemfundo. Into
enkulu esingayo namhla lityala lendlu. Kuluvuyo ukuva ukuba nina Mgwali
nilihlambile igama elibi. Kekaloku tina singumzi okolwa kukuti
yakulaliswa pantsi inkomo singaba salinda—uba ngulowo emke nesiqa, omnye
emke nesinye ubone ingaseko. Ngenani nto zakowetu, makupume inkomo.

U FENI, Inkosi, usuke wati, andikuba sabambezela seniwavile amazwi.
Namhla sikwindlu eyamkela umntu wonke, aze wonke atete. Nilivile ityala.
Inkosi yinto ebincedwa kade kwa Xosa. Ibisiti yakuvelelwa yinto
ihlanganise izipakati iliwise kuzo ityala layo, baze bonke abantu bati
masikulule inkosi etyaleni. Mna nakuba ndikubaqa oku kuvulwa kwendlu
sendiya kugxoga ngeponti ezimbini.

Emva koku babe yimirozo abeza kuposa iminikelo yabo, ngokukodwa
abantwana.

U Rev. E. MAKIWANE usuke wati yena unga angavakalisa ukuba namhla uze
kuzivuyela. Abanye beze kuvuyisana nabantu base Mgwali ukuba namhla
benesikolo esitsha esihle; abanye beze kuvuyisana nomzi wakwa Ngqika
ukuba unendlu enje ngale. Mna esisikolo ndize kusibanga ngetuba lokuba
ndintsundu. Akuni nokuba ndimi pina, akuni nokuba bati ndingumni na
abakolwa lucuku. Isikolo esi sesam, ukuvuya kwam ndivuyela into yakowetu
; mhla ndiza kuso andisokutsho ukuti ndiza kucelela, xa sonakalayo ndiya
kulila, xa sihlumayo ndivuye, ndingatsho ukuti ndililisa abanye abantu.
Ndiyabulela ukubona inkosi yezakowetu apa mhla sihlangene ngemfundo.
Imfundo le ukuba siyipapele yona siyakuba idabi silifezile lokupilwa
kwalo mhlaba. Abanye abantu basanxakamele amasiko amadala. Bawancoma
ukulunga, bancoma ukonwaba kwabantu ngezo mini. Bangaba banyanisile
kanti noko abasusi ubukulu bemfundo. Ebantwini ziko inzwakazi ezazintle
ongafika namhla zinemibimbi, ongafika kuvelele ezinye; ako amakalipa
angasoyikwa bani. Kuko into enjalo emasikweni ezizwe. Ako aye mahle
eluncedo kodwa namhla idlule imini yawo kwaza apa kwavelela imfundo,
esiya kuti sakuyilahla sibe siyazilahla ngokwetu. Kekaloku namhla size
kuvula indlu yentombi nje kumhla size kwenza into. Amadodana sibe
siposisa ukuwafundisa odwa, kuba engakwazi ukuhamba odwa awushiye
ngasemva umtinjana.

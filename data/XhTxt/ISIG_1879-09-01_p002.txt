Ama Bulu akakayeki ukuhlwayela indaba zobuxoki zokuba imikosi yetu

Koko kubonakala ukuba akancedi nto. U-Oham ubuye weza kwase Utrecht.

E-Edendale kube kwenziwe isidlo soku- gcobela imikosi yabantsundu
eyayinduluke kona ukuya kulwisa Inkosazana. Ebeko kuso no Mhlekazi u-Sir
Henry Bulwer. U-Sir Henry wenze amazwana amafutshane, wakuncoma
okwenziwe ngamadoda ase Edendale. Ute ukuzipata kwawo akunandawo
igxekekileyo. Ngamadoda ate akuba elungile aba ekalipile. Isekritari ye

emnandi kunene ngabahamba behlangana nako emfazweni. Batete kwalusizi
ngamadoda abe bapete auje ngo Colonel Burnford, no Captain George
Shepstone. Kwaba ngokunye bakucukumisa igama lenkosazana abapantsi
kwayo.

KWA MOROSI.

Umbaleli otile wepepa lase East London uti. Kuya kudlula iveki ngapandle
kokubonakala ukuba u-Morosi wenza nto yokuzinikela. Asigqibi nokuba
yintonina eyona angxamele yona. Intliziyo ziya capuka kukuba ake asinike
amatemba kanti uza kubuya awadanise ngoluhlobo. Nokoke siyazi ukuba soda
sifike isipelo, kuba akuko nto ingenasipelo.

EMAMPONDWENI.

Mampondweni ezisingisele ekutini Amampondo avukele Umlungu. Kude

lungekacaci kakuhle uhlobo lokuqaleka kwaso, nokuba Amampondo angene

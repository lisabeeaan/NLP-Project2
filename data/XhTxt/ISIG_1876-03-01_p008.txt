ISAZISO SAKOMKULU.

NGELIXESHA langoku asikuko nokuba bayafuneka abantu bokuza kusebenza
kwezindlela zenqwelo zomlilo ezenziwayo kwindawo ngendawo zekoloni.
U-Rulumente ke ufuna ukubakutaza abo bancedisayo ukufuna amadoda okuza
kusebenza. Uya kuti ke ngoko kosukuba etumele amadoda anokusebenza
angengapantsi kwamashumi amabini anesihlanu, avumayo ukuze asebenze
ixesha elingengapantsi kwenyanga ezintandatu efumana umvuzo omiyo kakade
—otumele amadoda anjalo
uya kuvuzwa ngoluhlobo:—

Ukuba aya kweyase Worcester ngase Kapa wonikwa £3 ngendoda inye
ayitumeleyo.

Ukuba kukweyase Uitenhage ibe £1 ngendoda.

Kweyase Bushmans River Poort (e-Qora) ibe 10s. ngendoda.

Amaqela amadoda afuna ukuya kweyase
Worcester aya kwamkelwa emkombeni e-
Monti. Umntu oya kuya kubanikela ezandleni zezipata-mandla apo e-Monti
wofumana iponti ngomntu emnye ongeneyo emkombeni.

Imali erolwayo ngemini kwabasebenzayo
2s. 6d. kunye nokudla ukuba bakwindlela
yase Worcester. Ukuba bakweyase Bushmans River Poort ibe 2s. 6d. kube
kupela.

Abaye kweya ngase Kapa boti ukuba
bahlale umnyaka kona mhla babuyayo bangabizwa mali ngokungeniswa
emkombeni.

JOHN X. MERRIMAN.

IZINQINISO EZIVUMELANA NOLONA LUPILISO “OLUQINISEKILEYO”

LUKA JESSE SHAW, LOKULUNYWA ZINYOKA, NEZINYE IZINTO.

  U-MR. MULLER wangase Fort Beaufort uti: “Iyeza lako Elikupilisa
  kuqinisekileyo lipilise inja yetu ebilunywe liramba; intloko yayo
  ibiselidumbe yaliqekele—noko yapila ngosuku olunye olunesiqingata.

  E-FORT BEAUFORT kuko ontsundu obelunywe yinyoka elunyaweni wadumba
  kunene, kanti noko uyakupila kwangalo mini lisagalelwe kwakanye
  eliyeza “Likupilisa Kuqinisekileyo.”

  Ngakwa CAPTAIN BLYTH pesheya kwe Nciba kwesika ZAZELA bekuko
  intonibazana ebilunywe luhlobo olutile lwe’ramba ilele, amanxeba ada
  amane kwindawo ngendawo zomzimba. Usike umzimba wayo wayingqindilili
  wabanda.
  Ilitabate kwakabini kwakupela eliyeza yapila.

  U- Mr. S. J. RADENMEYER, obala e-Clith Roe kufupi nase Rouxville,e-
  Orange Free State uti: “Zinkosi zam, asikuko nokuba kuluvuyo kum ukuba
  nokuninqinela ngoncedo lweyeza lako ‘ Elikupilisa Kuqinisekileyo.’
  Sekukafuti
  ndilisebenzisa mna nabamelwane bam. Ibotile selipantse ukupela. Ke
  akuko xesha like alanceda; Likufanele ukubizwa ngokuti Lelikupilisa
  Kuqinisekileyo.

  Abatengisi balo ngu J. Shaw, E-Fort Beaufort, nase Nxukwebe, nase
  Mpofu, nase Mxelo; no R. Stocks, no J. B. Temlett, base Dikeni;
  U-Whitcher
  no Dyer, kwa Qoboqobo nase Qonce; U-Hedding, E-Butterworth, pesheya
  kwe
  Nciba.

  JESSE SHAW.

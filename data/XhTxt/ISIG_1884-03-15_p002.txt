Mark Hope ” C.C. abanye kwakwi “ Fear Not ” C.C. Kwadlalwa ngo Mgqibelo
16 Feb., labe itafa lokudlalela lizele tu ngababoneli abantsundu
nabamhlope ; kuba i match le yayitembise ukuba yebukekayo ngenxa
yokomelela kwala macala omabini. Sati kanti singaqondi nje ukuba kuko
nani lingakanani apa elaka lalunga ne Lovedale, labonakala lingumqulu
omkulu ngokuza kutelela, nokunqwenelela impumelelo yesikolo salo,
kvvavuka namaxego. Emva kwe innings zokuqala zombini, kwabonakala ukuba
‘ inkunzi ’ i “ Fear Not ” seyiya kupumelela ngobunkunzi bayo. I batting
yezi innings ayibanga yebukekayo kumacala omabini. Kwi Lovedale ngu
Gallant yedwa owenza amanani amabini. Kwezesibini i Lovedale yadlala
ngokukululekileyo, isenza izihlandlu ezitile ezimnandi. U Makwena wenza
esona sikora sikulu salomini ngapandle kwemigudu emininzi. Yapela i
Lovedale ine 58 runs ne grand total ye 93 runs. Ingenile i “ Fear Not ”

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 1, 1872.

IGUSHA ziyafa kakulu ngakulamacala anezinta- ba Kutiwa zi Stormberg.
Zifikelwe sisifo esitsha, esiziqala ngokuzibamba ngomqala.

IKOTON—Umboniso wekoton owawuse Rini ngomhla wa 22 ka August watyila
indawo yokuba iyahluma ikoloni. Ikoton eyayize kuboniswa ya- yingaba
ziflara zenqwelo ezilishumi. Wayeko no Sandile, neqelana elalinaye,
wabonakala ukungati uyakutada konke akubonayo. Ukulinywa kwe koton
ngumsebenzi ongabanembuyiselo kwaba- ntsundu abakufupi nolwandle ukuba
bawutabatile. Ikoton le yinto ehleli ifuneka, engenanxamleke inkulu
nasekusetyenzweni kwayo. Umsebenzi usekucokiseni intsimi ukuba ingabi
nalukula ide iqine; uko kanjalo nasekuyi coleni. Ukuyicola oko
kungenziwa nangabantwana. Kuti kwezinye indawo umti wekoton ugqibe
iminyaka emitatu umana utwala. Apo kunjalo inkatazo iba ngomnyaka
wokuqala kube kupela. Asikabi nakulimisela kanye ixesha ongati utabate
lona apa E-South Africa, kodwa inkolo ekoyo yeyokuba ungati utwale
iminyaka yada yamibini unganembuyiselo.

AMASELA ABATWA—Abantu abantsundu abaku- fupi ne Natal abenzi milembelele
yokuteta xabeza kohlwaya amasela. Kuseko Abatwa abambalwa abahlala
kwintaba ze Quathlamba. Kute ke kweziveki zisand’ ukudlula kwako iqela
Labatwa eliyakungenela inkomo zika Sekoedwa umpakati ka Dumasi, beba
namahashe alishumi elinesixenxe. Ute noko ahle wawulanda umkondo
U-Sekoedwa, basinda Abatwa bayaezintabeni nezonto zibiweyo. Kute
ngolwesine usuku kulandwa, abantu baka Sekoedwa bafumana amahashe abo
amatandatu, ete akudinwa aselebulawa. Ite ke lonto yabakutaza, baza kwa
kamsinya emva koko bawa- bona amasela ehleli kwindawo apo wona ebesiti
anqabile. Bate bawarawula, emahlanu, kubonakala ukuba lendawo likaya
lawo, kuba bekuko nabafazi, nentombi inye, nentsana 'ezimbini. Ate
amadoda lawo aselengenelwa kwangokoko ; abafazi bebezakuyekwa koko
basuke ababini babatola abanini mpahla ngentolo ezidyojwe ngobuhlungu;
bati ke abo babini basebebulawa. Bate ukugoduka kwabanini mpahla
bagoduka namahashe amashumi mabini abawafumana kulondawo, awangawabo
kanye elishumi.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

U-A. T. PEPPETTO uti—Nam ndiyamvumela umzalwana U-P. Kalipa ngotywala.
Buyazonakalisa izinto ngoko mabupele. Kute ngalenyanga ifileyo Iqeya
labulala umkwekazi walo E-Aburidini ngenxa yotywala. Umsutu olapa
E-Graaff- Reinet abati ngu Jokalase ulahlekwe zi £7 10s.; waza emva koko
walahlekwa zi 9s. 6d. ngenxa yabo. Bubi ngenxa zonke. Unga Ukumkanikazi
wetu U-Victoria anganikwa amandla ngu Tixo ukuze ayiqetule lento. Izinto
ziyonakala, abantu bayagqibana. Elolawu libulele umkwekazi belisand’
ukuqekeza itolongo apa E-Graaff-Reinet.

U-JANUARY JACOBUS wase Rocky Mountain kufupi ne Tsomo Post ubala ngecuba
nejiki. Uti azilunganga ezonto zombini kuba zidla ngokutsalela
enkohlakalweni enkulu. Ukankanya indawo yomfazi ote esalelwa yindoda
ukuba angatshayi wati kanti uyaliba, waza akubaqwa ngamini itile walinga
ukuzimela ngobuxoki. Uti kanjalo akasiboni isizatu sokuba imilomo yetu
siyenze into zokupuma umsi. Uti ijiki alilungile kuba libangela ukuba
umntu enze into ebengeza ayicinga nokuyicinga xa angaselanga. Ngenxa
yokusela umntu ungenelwa ngamatyala angawaziyo nokuqala kwawo ; maxa
wambi umntu uqabuka selesetolongweni engazi ukuba utenina ukuze abe
kona; abo sebekwenze isiqelo boze mhlaumbi botuke Sebesesihokweni.

Kwele Diamondi, Du Toit’s Pan,

14th September, 1872.

Umbuzo kubafazi abamadoda akwele Diamondi ngulo—Incwadi amadoda
ayazitumela E-Koloni, kanti abafazi abazipenduli. Basuke bahlale
ngokungati abakatali. Lisiko linina ke elo? Lisiko elibi elo
lokwenjenjalo. Amehlo amadoda akwele Diamondi apa aselebomvu kukukangela
incwadi ezitunyelwa ngabafazi bawo. Ke bafazi bakowetu kukungena
kobulumko na oku? Ndaka ndeva izwi elatetwa ngumfundisi wase Dikeni
pakati kosapo lwesikolo esiti—tina Mangesi sixolele ukulahlekwa yimali
eninzi ngenxa yamapepa endaba zendawo ngendawo. Nitike ukuba kunjalo
zifuneka kangakananina indaba ezivela kumfazi. Nditike bafazi bakowetu
abase Koloni apa sisebenza ekufeni. Kuko enye indoda ebube ngo July. No-
wabalela nininake amadoda enu xa selesencwa- beni. Hayi ukuzeka intombi
zamazimba! Yintlalo yamaqaba-mbola leyo. Pendulani incwadi ezibalwa
ngamadoda enu, ningamane nilibele ku- kupikisana ngolobolo into
yobuheyideni. Gcinani imini epuma ngazo iposi.

  Kwele Diamondi,

Sept. 3rd, 1872.

Yenza kuhle, unganxami liza kupuma ilanga. Abahambi xa bahamba elizweni
abangalaziyo, bati noko bahlwelweyo, kubeko abamana ukuti ninga- nxami.
Uti noko omnye ati silahlekile abanve bati msukuputuzela. Ati omnye
akuti apa asinakulala akuko manzi, kanjalo ngumhlaba we Bulu bati
abanye, yeka ukuputuzela ; kube njaloke kudekukale nenkuku, kupume
nocanzibe, nezilimela nomakwezi, kude kusuke kupume nelanga emana
esitsho umfo ukuti ungatatazeli, lizakuti lakupuma libonise into zonke,
lityile apo akona amanzi, nomgama wendlu ye Bulu. Xa lingekapumi ilanga
aziqondakali izinto, nezilungileyo nezingalunganga. Ubusuku
buyazitintela ukuba zingaqondwa ngabangawaziyo umhlaba abakuwo;
kuyafaneleka ke ngoko akuti omnye omeleze ngelokuti msani ukuputuzela
yenzani kuhle.

Ukutsho kubonakala ngati zininzi izikalazo ezi- kalazwayo pakati
kwezikolo zabantsundu, bekala- zelana kwabodwa ngamasiko. Savelayo nje
Isigi- dimi ungaflka ezonanto zibalwe ngabantsundu— inkoliso ukuba
asizizo zonke—zizizikalazo. Ku­pela kukalazwa nge ngubo, ngemfundo,
ngokwaluka, ngotywala, ngomitshato, nezinye izinto eziyelele kwezo.
Yinina mabandla akowetu, yenzani kuhle. Usuke ati umntu kwakwisikolo
akuso ange selefuna into yokutumela Esigidimini; ati aku- bona intwana
angabisaceba ukutumela E-Dikeni. Kwaleka nezinte ebefanele ukuti
lowo-nalowo ayale ngazo kubantu akubo. Makowetu msani ukubuduzela.

Into ebifanele ukuba yenkulu kuzo zonke yi- mfundo. Yiyo yodwa enditi
mna ililanga elenza ukuba intombi izazi imfanelo zayo, umfana akwazi
ukuzipata, nomseli wotywala abazi into obuyiyo. Ukuba okwenene yile
eyona ndawo inkuli nditi msani ukubuduzela. Namhla zikamise imi- nyango
izindlu zezikolo zolutsha Iwabantsundu, amadodana namakwenkwana;
ndinovuyo ukuba ndongeze nditi nentombi. Nditi ke yenzani kuhle.

Ukuba namhla uya kuti wonke umntu ontsun- du azamele ukubatumela
esikolweni abantwana bake iminyaka emashumi mabini ezayo iya kuve- lisa
siqamo sinina pakati kwabantsundu? Kona ngoku akuko basebezinikelana
pakati kwetu, ukuba yonke impilo yabo bafundise abantu ilizwi elikulu
elivele ngapezulu. Ukuba kuse kunjalo yenzani kuhle. Abakona ocanzibe
abasebeqalile ukupuma, amadodana aselefundisa izikolo zetu, aselenconywa
nangabamhlope? Ukuba kunjalo yenza

kuhle. Mayike ibe yimfundo into enkulu neyokuqala, zoti kupakati ziqale
ukutyileka nezinye izinto, ziqondwe ngabantu befundile, beziqondela
bonke ngabanye.

Ewe siyabona ukuba ziko izinto emane zisihla kulutsha olufundileyo,
ezibanga ukuba inxenye ide iposise ngokuti imfundo ayilungile. Lonto
iyimpo- sisonokuba abanye bayayiteketisa, nditiyenzakuhle wena
ubuduzelayo. Yiyipina intsimi eyake ya- vunwa ingakange idliwe
ngoruxeshe, nangonom- yayi, nazinkomo? Yenza kuhle wena unamatata. Ukuba
kuyintando yake lowo usandla sibambe intlanga zonke nezinye
izinambuzane, okupa ilanga lake pezu kwazo zonke izidalwa,
kungangummangaliso na akulikupa ilanga lengqondo pakati kohlanga
oluntsundu. Yenza kuhle wena ubu- duzelayo.

Ndixolele Mhleli ukuba ndikubalele incwadi ende kangaka. Andihlale
ndikukataza mnumzetu kwangenxa yokuswela imfundo.

JOHN ZUMA.

  Peddie District, Pato’s Kop, 24th August, 1872.

BAZALWANA—Kuko into endiyikalazelayo kuni. Abantu bakuti ngabantu
abayivuma msinya into yakutetwa noko ngati ixakile ; kanti benziwa ku-
ba bengayi kuyilinga nakancinane. Ndmgatsho nditeta ngokuba lendawo
yolobolo abantu bako­wetu bayivumile ukuti abayi kuyenza, kodwa noko
iyenziwa ngabo bonke, ngabakokeli nabantu beremente ; nakuba ngoku
ibizwa ngegama elitsha kusitiwa kukupa. Anditsho ukuti asinto ikoyo
ukupa, anditsho nokuti yinto ekohlakeleyo. Hayi nditi okukupa sinako
kucala nye. Kupa umyeni yedwa, kanjalo kupiwa inani elixeliweyo. Ke
lento ikukulobola. Omnye upepa ukuze kungatiwa ulobole ; ati makubalwe
inani eliya kuziswa emva kokutshata. Kuze ke kuyiwe kumfundisi
kukanyelwe ukuba kuko inkomo eziza kurolwa. Kuti ukuba umyeni akaziroli
amenywe, umfazi atelekwe. Niyazike ukuba kutiwa “ningaxoki omtye
komnyekanjalo amaxoki aditywa kwabangenandawo pezulu. Kekaloku tina
ukuxoka sikwenza intwana encinane. Sinanina bantu bakowetu singati into
maxa singena kuyenza, sitsho? yinina lento sitandana nobuhanahanisi.
Endaweni yokuxoka masenze ngokusekuhleni; noko kulunge ukuba
kungalotyolwa kanye. Xa sixokayo masa- zi ukuba sixoka ku Tixo.

THOMAS NCAPAYI.

EZA PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

KWINDAWO ezishicilelweyo ngokutsho kuka Captain Blyth, no Rev. R. Ross,
no Mr. C. J. Levey, kubonakala ukuba Amamfengu akona ayakauleza
ukuhluma, ziyancomeka into azenzayo. Kubonakala ukuba kususela kumhla
wokuqala ka June we 1871, kude kube ngo 31 ka July we 1872, imali
ekutembiseke ukuba yozuzeka, yokulungisa nokwenza indlela kwelolizwe
lawo izi £810 10s. 3 1/2d. Ezuzeke kuma Mfengu odwa izi £697 8s. 3 1/2d.
Asikuko nokuba ezindaba zi- ronandi kubo bonke abazizihlobo zama Mfengu
; kuyabonakala ukuba ngabantu abalingayo ukuzi- nceda, nokukusebenzisa
ukwazi abanako.

Zonke inkosana zabantsundu ezibatalwayo yi Governmente zisand’
ukuxelelwa ukuba kulindelwe ukuba zilinge ukuwabamba amasela, zikupelise
ukuba. Ezinye ziti ziyazi ukuba kuyebiwa kakulu, kodwake azinamandla
okukupelisa. Inda- wo yokuba zifanelena ukubatalwa inkosana ezingenzinto
noko zibatalwayo seyike yakankanywa E-Parliamenteni yale Koloni.
Mhlaumbi yoxoxwa londawo yakubuye ihlangane, ukuba ubusela busengaka,
nokuba aziti inkosana zizamele ukubupelisa.

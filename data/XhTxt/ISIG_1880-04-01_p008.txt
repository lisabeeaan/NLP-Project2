ISINQINISO ESITSHA.

Fort White, February 17,1877.

Ku Mr. J. Shaw, Fort Beaufort,—

Nkosi etandekayo.—Umntu otile wandiyalela ukuze ndike ndilinge iyeza
lako loxaxazo mhla ndandipetwe kakubi sisisu. Landinceda kwakamsinyane
kakulu ndaza ndaqgiba kwe- lokuba ndiya kuka ndililinge kwinkwenkwana
yam ebinga- zange ipile soloko yazalwayo. Noko amagqira ndibe ndihamba
kuwo ubesuke umntwana ancedeke intsuku ezimbini nokuba zintatu. Ndilinge
elako iyeza. Ndite ndaku mnika okwesibini ndabon’ ukuba uyancedeka. Ite
ipela ibotile waba selepilile kwakanye umntwana. Ngoku sekupele inyanga
ezilishumi elinesibini noko asikazanga simbuyele isifo. Ndingakankanya
ukuba nabanye abamelwana bam endite ndabayalela eliyeza lako balilinga
kwababo abantwana, nabo bancedekile. Akuko namnye ongati elazi umsebenzi
walo ahlale ngapandle kwalo endlwini yake. Mna andike ndihlale
ndingenalo.

Ndingowako ongu

J. THOMAS.

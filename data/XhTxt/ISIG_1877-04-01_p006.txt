6 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1877. 

EZASE MATYALENI.
E-SOMERSET.

U-Boy Vaba obekutiwa ubile kubonakele ukuba ubetyolwa.

U-Valtyn Jawtyes ngokuhlaba u-Solomon Damaras unikwe iminyaka emibini
esebenza nzima.

U-James Austen ngokuba ucukwana lwempahla zendlu unikwe umnyaka esebenza
nzima.

U-John abati limbi ngu Gogomesh ngokuba ama- hashe amane ka Jack,
nasixenxe ka Mr. C. van de Wenter, nenkomo ezilishumi elinesine unikwe
imi­nyaka emine.

U-Bull Imfengu ehlala kufupi nase Somerset ibiziswe ngetyala lokubulala
abantwana bayo abatatu. Ilivumile nayo elotyala, yaza kwakubuzwa ukuba
inezwi na lokuzitetelela yaxela inkatazo zayo nomfazi wayo, eyamtshata
engumfazi wenye indoda. Emva kokutshata kwabo u-Bull wati lomfazi wake
makahlukane kube kanye nalandoda yake yokuqala, kuba nayo ihlala
kwakufupi. Lonto ke kwaba kusoloko ibanga impambano pakati kuka Bull
nalo mfazi. U-Mr. George Leppan ungene wangqina ukuba u- Bull ngumfo
olungileyo noko ati maxa wambi enze izinto ezisecaleni. U-Mr. Robb,
umgcinitolongo ungqine kwalonto naye. Isigwebo sibe sesokuba axonywe.

U- Wilhelmina Lange umfazi okwiminyaka 45 ngo- budala ubenikwe ityala
lokubulala usana lwake. Ku- bonakele ukuba ngumntu ongqondo ipambeneyo
kakade. Isigwebo sibe sesokuba asiwe endlwini yamageza e-Rini.

INGOZIYENQWELO.

Kute ngomhla 20 ka March kweza kudlula inqwelo yamaBulu e-Macfalane,
e-Tyume. Kute kuba beku- ngokuhlwa ayiposa indlela xa asentla komzi
wasesi- kolweni, yawa inqwelo yabagubungela ngapantsi bonke abebekuyo,
abafazi ababini nentombi, ezimbini nabantwana abasixenxe. Bafike kwaoko
abafazi bezindlu ezikufupi, baza njengesiko labafazi abantsu­ndu xa
babona umntu osebubini, bancedisa ngako konke abanako ukurola abantwana,
lwati usizi lwabo kwanga kwenzakalelwe izihlobo zabo. Ide inqwelo
yapakanyiswa ngenkabi kanti kuya kufunyanwa enye yazo intombi ebezise
nqweleni iseliqaukile, omnye wabafazi eruneke ingalo. Ufike xa kunjalo
umfundisi waseleyitabata yonke lompi ukuba iye kulala endlwini yake,
kunye nesidumba sofileyo. Omnye wabelungu abakufupi nesikolo ute akuya
kuyibikelwa londawo waputuma igqira e-Dikeni kwangobo busuku, bafika
ekuseni. Oberuneke ingalo limjojise amayezana alo walala laza ke
layilungisa engayiva nento ayenziwayo, wavuka iselise ndaweni yayo.
Ngemini elandelayo kufike abazali balentombi ifileyo nezinye izihlobo.
Kube lusizi kanye ukubabona, ku­ba lomntwana ebeselenexesha elide engeko
kubo ehla­la esikolweni. Kusihla lento nje ubesahambela ekaya ukuya
kubona abantu ezakubuya aye kwase sikolweni. Lide latshona ilanga
ungafikanga umkoba kwase kuyiwa kungcwatywa ngelandelayo imini.
Oberunekile uhlale kwamfundisi kwada kwangumvulo wemka noko engati
usingisela ekupileni. Asikuko nokuba siya velana nezihlobo zabahlelwe
bububi ngokukodwa abapulukene nomntwana wabo ngequbuliso elingaka.

KULUVUYO ukuva ukuba imvula ebeziko ngo Feb­ruary no March zikuvusile
ukudla kwinkoliso yendawo ezazilimile. Noko kungasayi kubako ndyebo,
intswelo ayisakubangangoko bekusoyikelwa. Kuko amacala okubalekela.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

Siyazisa ukuba asilitabateli pezu kwetu ityala lenteto zababalelani
betu, asitsho nokuti siyavumelana nazo zonke incwadi zabo
esizishicilelayo.

Kekaloku unina akasapile kakuhle ngokucinga ngaye. Samgqibela ekwele

JOHN MAZAMISA.

Mhleli we Sigidimi ndifakele lamazwana ambalwa Ndiyavuya kakulu ukuva
ukuba amadodxna abesukile apo e-Lovedale asinga kwilizwe lobumnyama
ecibini le Nyassa ukuba afikile kona. Kumnandi ukuva ekubeni efikile
kona uyaqaleka umsebenzi wokukanya. Manene akowetu nam bendilandelisa
ngemitandazwana yam ukunga ingabakapa Inkosi abantu bayo ekuhambeni
kwabo. Nokunga elolizwi lobom linga sebenza limke ubom kwezozizwe ezise
bumnyameni. Hambani nto zakowetu nilizalise ilizwi lenkosi eyalitetayo
kuba Postile entabeni ye Galali—wati linikiwe mna lonke igunya ezulwini
nasemhlabeni— Hambani ngoko niye kuzenza abafundi zonke intlanga.
Nibabapatizeshele egameni lo Yise nelo Nyana nelo Moya Oyingcwele.

Ndilapa e-Panmuae ndilinga ukuxelela abase mnyameni ngaye u-Msindisi
wetu u-Yesu Kristu. Malihambe elilizwi zide zimzukise lowo bezifanele
ukumzukisa zonke intlanga.

P. REBE.

Fort Beaufort, January 11, 1877.

Ndifuna ukuba nditelele kumzalwana u-B. Kwaza. Unyanisile ukuti
abafundisi mabayeke ukutshatisa amahilihili; nam undipangele ngalento.
Lomfana uteta ngaye akaqali ngalo amtshatileyo. Okunye wabaleka nambini
nangoku nantsi enye e-Nxukwebe inomntana wake. Unyanisile mna ndoyikela
ukuba abafundisi boza batshatise abantu abatshatileyo, ngo­kuba bako
nabafazi abashiye amadoda abo kwanama- doda ashiya abafazi bawo.

Nkosi yam uze ungakatazeki ndifuna ukupendula umfo wase Natal
ngokulobola esiti kulungile ukulobola. Nditi akulungile, yazi ngexesha
lama Izirayeli izinto zeloxesha lawo bezi lungile, yazi ukuba wati
Umsindisi akufika emhlabeni abantu bamela ukutabata abafazi ngabanye
kanti u-Solomon ebenamakulu asixenxe. Ukulobola kuqala bekulungile.
Ukuba wena unabafazi batatu ulahlekile elukolweni msa ukubuyisa abantu
endleleni; andazi nokuba ukoliwe kusinina mzalwane. Zininzi izinto
ngokufika ko Msindisi ezati azabanto.

W. DURANE.

Durban 21, 1877.

Kaundifakele Mhleli amazwana am. Kute ngomhla wamashumi amabini ka
February, ndayakupulapula aba Gwebi kwakutetwa ityala labafo base
Mkomanzi, beziswa yi Mantyi yakona engu Lukas. Kutiwa babulala umntu
kodwa bona baya kanyela, abo bafo amagama abo omnye ngu Balela omnye ngu
Mavusa. Bati aba Gwebi bafumana ityala ku Ba­lela ukuba afe ngelizwi
abati belwa ngomnye umhla, nabanye abafo, kwavela lowo mfo owafayo,
watshaya u-Balela entloko ngasemva, ute u-Balela akupendula ubuso,
lowomfo wabaleka ngamendu watshona egxeni, wati u-Balela soze sihlangane
belo. Kekaloku emveni kokulwa kwabo kwagqita inyanga zantatu kuko uxolo
pakati kwabo, wati lomfo owabeta u-Balela etunywe ngumlungu, wasuka
walahleka lomfo akaziwa nesidumbu sake kude kube namhlanje, noku­ba
wemka nemilambo akwaziwa mntu oko. Kepa ke kwati ngokunyamalala kwake
abakubo bakumbula ilizwi elatetwa ngu Balela lokuti soza sihlangane ba-
sebemmangalela u-Balela bati naba Gwebi bangena kulo. U-Mr. Dilisi
umgwebi omkulu obe yimantyi ngo 1876 e-Durban, uti kulomfo ityala
liyamkwela ;

kodwa okwam okusentlizyweni yam nditi mna tina bantu abantsundu base
Natal asinaye umbuzeli wetu ngokuba ngo 14 February 1877 bekungene
elomlungu waye dubule umntu ngompu kungeyiyo imfihlakalo wamti qwiti
lati ukugwetywa kwalo, wati umfo ka Somtseu okutiwa ngu Mr. W. Shepstone
nxa inkosi ifuna ukumgweba lomfo ukumnika ityala, wati abantu abantsundu
nina ma Ngesi anibazi banolaka (esitsho ngokuba emmele lomlungu) wasuka
wakululwa lomlungu akarola nento, nditsho itiki yodwa le pezu kokupalaza
kwake igazi lomntu. Kanjalo kwabuya kwangena elomnye umlungu, owadubula
ikuli ngompu lafa pambi kwake wamelwa ngu Escomb (omnye wabameli)
kwatiwa yi ngozi umpu upuncukile ezandleni zenjitiman yakululwa ke leyo
njenti ayenziwa nento nomyalo. Zonke ke ezinto ndizibonisa nina bakowetu
ndikulendaba, ka Balela ngokuba ngapandle kokucolwa kwesidumba kutiwa
into emnyama iyabulawa ngokuba ayinambuzeli. Nasemfundweni kutiwa
abafundisi yini ukuba bafundise izinto ezimnyama, banecala.

Alexandria January 15, 1877.

Kwipepa lako Nkosi yam Mhleli wendaba ndibone nendawana yamazwana
ka-John Hluba ngomtshato wase Tapolisi, asikuko nokuba ndiyamvela
intloni awaye nazo, ukuva amaculo okubedesha Inkosi epitikezwa nobubi.
Kuti bantu bafundisiweyo kuba zintloni wakufika apo umenywe kona uzubone
into zinga petwe ngendlela, zisuka ziyepina idyakane kulomacala ; ubu
dyakani bazo bupina.

Lutsha niti naku hlangana nivakale nisiti singamanene namanenekazi. Buyi
ntonina ubunene nebunenekazi, kauzi fune mfana nentombi. Nisengamaqaba
azeleyo yiyani ezikolweni nifunde izimilo. Abanye kwakona kulomtshato
ingaba bavela ezikolweni. Naleyo ndingayenzela izwi, ubona nje umvundla
lo wasendle ungaugcina ekaya ixesha elide kanti noko mhla waukupa uya
endle kwe minye, linjalo iqaba eli libanjwe nge ntambo lasiwa esikolweni
belisatanda indlela zalo, zeke lizi fune. Madodana nentombazana zakowetu
nditi nimalunga pina esimilweni sobunene nobunenekazi. Ndixolele mfo
wendaba ndifuna ukuqauqaula indawandawana kweli pepa lako.

Amahashe asikuko nokuba ayafa Emnyameni; aku­ko mntu unokunceda kupela
mnye opilise into eninzi.

Igqira lontsundu kuvakala ukuba kuko ibendengu ngasemnyameni nto apa
bati ngu Felem into eti umntu engekapili selibiza umlandu ; wahlala
ebona izikova emizini yabantu netsikizi; liyabagqiba.

JOHN PITYI.

IMFUNDO YENTOMBI.

Southwell, February, 1877.

Kuko into engumangaliso pakati kwabantu abantsundu, bafuna kodwa ukuba
abantwana babo bafunde, kanti noko abafuni ukuba intombi zifunde kakulu;
kuba bati intombi aziyi kwenza nto ngemfundo, kuba asimangesi; kodwa ke
kuyo kutinina ukude babe njenga mangesikazi, apo- sisengela kona. Iti
intombi yakuba inkulu iti mna andise nakuya esikolweni kuba sendingaka,
itsha ngokuva inteto yabazali, kanti yona ngokwayo ifana ne esile
ngokuba sidenge.

Ndake mna ndati ndisesimnareni e-Rini ndaya ngexesha lokupumla e-Dikeni
esikolweni sentombi,. ndiya kubona intombi engu Eliza Tsotso kona apo ;
ndafika kuko intombi ezinkulu nezinde zifanelekile kanye ; ndati ukuba
sonke siyakwenjenje lento kutiwa kukukanya (civilization) soyisukela;
kodwa ukuba siyakuti zisezi nkulu sezitwala iqiya sezi sambata ilokwe
ezinde ngasemva namapenya akusayikusilungela, siyakuba njenge siteto
esiti, “ Nakuba imfene ikulele pakati kwabantu kwasekuzalweni kwayo ayi-
sokuze ihambele pambili; kupela yakukululwa isuke ibuye ibe njengo yise
bakudala.”

Okokutsho makowetu nditi intombi mazifunde kude kube mhla zendayo,
zandule ukuti sigqibile.

JOHN HLABA.

NKOSI,—Noeda usifakele lemigcana im-
balwa kwelopepa le Mvo. Sibhekisa kwa-
bo banosizi ngati, bati sizimfenqemfenqe
ezisoloko zafayo yindlala, kuba kwelikaya
letu lentombi (Shaftesbury Home) siyavi-
njwa ukudla, sipile ngamat’ entamo, abati
ke abo bati ngelabo bayasisizela bada
bangenisa ezongcinga kubazali betu, zo-
kuba siyalamba. Nali ke eletu tina zinto-
mbl zase Shaftesbury Home:—

Lowo mhlaumbi abo batete lonto baya-
simonela, bafuna ukuba abazali betu ba-
capuke, baze bati kuti masipume baze
bona batumele ababo abahlobo mhlanmbi
abantwana; mhlaumbi bafuna ukubeka
ibala elibi ekayeni letu lokuqeqeshela
intombi, nokubeka isigxeko kwabasipe-
teyo. Sixelela bonke abahlobo betu nabo
bati banosizi ngati ukuba, sidla side
sihlute. Niyaxoka ke nina bazibendaba
nokuba na ngumazi wazo ukuti siyala-
mba, asilambi. Asinantloni ukuti noko-
kuba ngubanina makeze aze kuzibonela
ngokwake, anelise iliso lake ; tina asifuni
nto zibuxoki ngelikaya letu. Xa kunga-
kalazi tina asazi okokuba lamaququlura-
na ati ayasisizela ngobanina wona; kodwa
into esiyaziyo yile yokuba akuko unga-
yitetayo lonto pambi kwetu. Xa abazali
betu basibhalela incwadana besiti beva
ngo Mazindaba okokuba asihluti kulo-
mzi, xa bati kanjalo masibabhalele siba-
xelele ukuba kunjalo, nantsi ke impe-
ndulo yetu:—Asiyazi tina lonto kuba
siyahluta. Nina bazali betu pulapulani
kuti, ningaseli omoya enibevayo, nova
ngati. Masitshonele ke apo singagqibi
indawo epepeni lemz’ ontsundu.

[IVELA KU M’BHALELI]

25 Sept. 1886.

Ngomhla west xenxe kwatshatiswa kulomzi ngu Rev. W. Holford u Mr. Wesley
Wilson wase Bhofolo no Miss Holford, kwi tyalike yalapa. Kwaqalwa
ukutshatiswa ngentsimbi yeshumi elina- nye (11 o clock). Kwaye kuko
namanene amabini ase Rini o Messrs Walker esa- bapaulayo. Kwandlalwa i
carpet yesu- kela kwi front-stoop (wena uyaziyo i front­stoop yalomzi)
yakumiswa pambi kom- fundisi etyalikeni, ukuze banyatele pezu kwayo
abatshati bangagqutyelwa lutuli bangakutywa nangamatye, zona i flagi
zema kwangentsasa zaraqa umzi, yapa- nts’ukuzala indlu ngabantu, ukuza
kubo- nela. Kute xa akude kufutshane nom- nyango umtshakazi, ayitsho
ingoma ama- semnari ute akuti qingqi pambi komfu- ndisi ayiyeka ingoma,
kwaqutywa umse- benzi womdibaniso. Kute xa kupu- nywayo, ayitsho kwakona
ebuhlungu ingoma ama Semnari. Saye ke isaziso nkosi yam sokumema
simemelwa isidlo soqolwane sesiqutyiwe ngu Rev. Holford Sime ngoluhlobo
isimemo Abafundisi Abantsundu balapa kunye nezindlu zabo Amasemnari
kunye ne pupil Teacher Abashumayeli nabakokeli besiqingata esi, kunye
namalungu eramente azeleyo Kwapaulwa ke okokuba isidlo eso siya- kuqala
ngo 6 wokutshona kwelanga Okunene ibetiwe intsimbi kwangenwa ngabamenywa
ababesebe zilungiselele ukungena, zaye ne tini zoqolwane sezi- ngxangile
kunye namapetelo (wena wa- wuko ngokufakwe nkomo ngono Ngqawu- se).

kuqolwane babonakala bedenga, lwaye lona lusemi kwangokoko. Emva kwesi-

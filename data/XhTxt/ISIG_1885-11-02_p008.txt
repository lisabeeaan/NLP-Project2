  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 2, 1885.

  EZAKWA ZULU.

UKUJAMELANA KWAMA BULU NAMA ZULU.— Litsho ipepa lase Natal eliyi Times
ukuti kuza kubako isipitipiti pakati kwalo madoda ngenxa yokuti ama Bulu
asuke awuzaze wonke umhlaba wakwa Zulu, ezenzela amafa emihlaba
engacebisi nakubani, kuba kaloku afumane isisulu sombinza. Kutiwa
maninzi ngoku eliti liyaka indlu, kuse sel'idilizelwe pantsi nokuba
udonga Iwayo selupakamile. Elinye i Bulu lati lisakubela amasimi alo
afika ama Tshaka, ati kulo, malifuduke lisuke kulo mhlaba. Lite Iona
lomhlaba ngo wama Bulu. Ate ama Tshaka, akazi mhlaba wama Bulu wona, azi
oka Zulu; atsho ati kweli Bulu malikulule ezo nkabi, kuba ukuba ke labo
lahlaba enye ifola, ezo nkabi zalo aya kuzigwaxa zonke. Hayi i Bulu
lasel’isuka lizikulula, lincama, lizifak’amatole. Kunjalo nje akufu- nwa
nemisetyenzana. yawo, afane aman' epal' ezweni ukufuna abantu
okubaqesha, koko ayacekiswa, ecatshu- kelwa.

  INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

NKOSI YAM, Mgcogeli wendaba zamanene namanenekazi, kaunditwale
ngokundifakela lombiko kwelo pepa lenkosi zohlanga lwakowetu, lwase Mbo.
Ndibika umbiko wokububa kuka Mxaku Mbolekwa, indoda yamadoda ubube nge
19 September, 1885, Namhla usekaya, ekupumleni. Make ndikubalisele
kancinane ngalo ndoda. Londoda lb’ ingumkokeli apa e Toboyi, indoda eb’
iwukutalele umsebenzi woku- shumayela ilizwi le Nkosi yetu, u Somandla,
u Mdali we Zulu no Mhlaba.

Mandiqalele mhlenikweni wehliwa ngulo mkuhlane, wati eye kuka- ngela
igusha zake, (kuba yindoda eb’inegusha kakulu), wangenwa yingqele, wati
efika ekaya waye ekala nge ntloko, nomzimba wonke, zati zombini ezonto
zamkalisa kakulu, kwati kwakuba ntsuku kaloku wangenwa lukohlokoblo
nep'ika, zambandezela ezo nto ngokukulu, kwabonakala ukuba makufunwe
amayeza. Kwayiwa e Mgomanzi evenkileni, kwafikwa umnini venkile u Mr
Pearson engeko, inkosikazi yake yabala ngokwayo ukubalela ku Gqira. Baya
ke nale ncwadi, befikile wati u Gqira akanako ukumpa iyeza engamboni
lomntu ufayo. Bate bona Mkasena kutwalwa eze apa. Babuya ke abo bafana
bengenalo iyeza, bafika umkuhlane umkulu, kwasa betunywa kwakona komnye.
nanko e Ngculu, umfundisi wase Tshatshi ontsundu, abati ngu Mr.
Pumlwana. Befikile ke kuye wati uya kufika ngomso, ngom- Gqibelo, kuba
lwalulolwesi-Hlanu olo. Babuya ke abafana abo, bafik'umpefumlo
sewupumile. Wafika ke no Rev. Mr. Pumlwana ngom-Gqibelo, wafika
sekumbiwa incwaba.

Mandingene ke kumazwi awawatetayo xa waye sese lukukweni lokufa. Kwati
ngamini itile kwafika umfazi wase Wesile, wati kumfi lowo—“ kunjaui na
Xaba?” Wampendula ngeli lizwi—“Namhla ntokazi yakowetu kungani kambe,
ndisekeleni ngemitandazo, okwam ndiyemka.” Yatsho londoda yamadoda,
injengele yenjengele, nditeta elizwini lika Tixo. Umfo obeshumayela noko
abuhlungu, ati akana kunceda, inkabi ifela edyokweni. akuvumi ukuba
ayeke ukushumayela ngo Yesu u Nyana ka Tixo, owafela izono zetu, engena
tyala Yena. Emkile ke lomfazi kwafika ubanye beze kumbona, bafika
bati—‘‘Kunjani na Xaba? ” Wabapendula kwa ngokunjalo wati—“Namhla
zintokazi zako wetu kungani. ndisekeleni ngemitandazo, mna ndiyemka,”
watsho wavuma eli Culo la 206. Zemka ezo ntokazi zingarani nto. Naiiga
ke auiazwi awawatetayo xa waye selegoduka :—1 Mna ndiya hamba, ilizwi le
Nkosi maze lihambe, ndize kutatyatwa ekaya ngama- doda, akowetu ango
Rev. J. Mavayeni no Mntiyedwa, andiputumile." Watsho wati,—“ Imali
yom-Vangeli neka Teacher maze zipume.” Watsho wati,'— Lolwesingapi na
namhla !" Kwatiwa lolwesi-tatu. Wati,—“ Ngolwesi- Hlanu nize niputume
unyana wam u David e Semnareni, e Ngqamakwe." Ngokuhlwa kwafika
umfundisi, wati akuva ilizwi lomfundisi washukuma, kanti noko ngati
ebelele nje ubengekalali butongo. Wati umfundisi nanko esliukuma, yasuka
inkosikazi yake yamlungisa, wati ndivuse, yamvusa. Wabulisa ku mfundisi,
wabuza ukuba uvela pina mfundisi, wati omnye ndivela kushumayela e
Tsomo, wavuya kakulu akuva oko. wati malihambe ilizwi le nkosi u
Somandla Nizipate kakuhle neremente ze Nkosi u Somandla, “ Mna ndiya
hamba ” Watsho wakangela pezulu, wata- ndaza. wati,—“ Haleluya !
Haleluya! u Somandla." Watsho wati “ Mfundisi wohlanga nizjpate kakuhle
iremente, indlela esinga ezu- Iwini incinane. Okwenene ngolwesi-Hlanu
waputunywa unyana

  wake, kuloko wafika engaseko, selebubile. Walushiyu elusizini usapo
  lwake lomfo wohlanga. Mandibe ndiyapela apo mhlobo wohlanga oluntsundu
  lwase Mbo. Ndinibulisa ngotando nonke madoda akowetu,

  N. Gubesa MACWELA.

  Toboyi School, 30 Sept., 1885,

  NKOSI yam Mgcogeli wendaba zamanene namanenekazi, kawundifa- kele
  lombiko ndiwubikayo ku Mautshele onke api napi, okokuba inkosikazi ka
  Mr. Mzola Hoza ibube nge 28th September, 1885. lntokazi emke kakuhle,
  imke incokola nge Nkosi yayo u Mdali wezulu nomhlaba. Mandipinde
  ndikuxelele ngo Mr. M. Hoza. U Hoza lu yinkosi ya Mantshele apa e
  Toboyi Mission School.

  Mandingene kwezale ntokazi umfikazi, eyemka ngati sisakubuye siyibone
  apa emihlabeni, ngenxa yokusivuyisa kwayo. Nasi isifo esimgodusileyo,
  lihlaba nokohlokohlo, nomzimba wonke. Wasuka wudumba nemilenze le. lte
  ke lentokazi ngamini itile ilele elukukweni lokufa “ Kanene abantu
  banomnqweno wokuva into etetiweyo ngumntu xa umkayo kweli lizwe, kanti
  wona amakolwa ebefanele ukumtemba um Kristu ukuba ububele e Nkosini
  yake noko engateta- nga.” Yatsho lentokazi ukwenza intshayelelo.
  Yatsho yangena kumazwi esishiye nawo, yati: “ Ndinetemba nditembele e
  Nkosini yam u Yesu Krestu, u Nyana ku Tixo, itembu lam liselisondele,
  Inkosi

seyindikokele, Nantso, nantso empumalanga apo seyindilindele kona.” Kute
kusenjalo kwafika unyana wake oza pezu komkulu evela e Tabose,
Tembuland. Ute akufika wasitsho isililo. Unina ubuzile wati: “ Yinina
mntwana wam ulilela ntonina? ukuba bendisazi ukuba uyi kwenjenje
ngendingakubizanga. Ukuba unengqondo, musa ukulila, mna ndiya ekaya,
zililele okukokwako mntwana wam. Zonke izihlobo zam zezingalili,
ndiyagoduka mna ndiya ekaya eluyolweni Iwe Nkosi yam.” Kute ekuseui
kom-Gqibelo yati: “ Vukani nivuse nabantwana bam.” Bavukake, wati kubo :
“ Tandazani,” batandazake. bate bakugqiba wawutata ngokwake umtandazo,
wati: “ Uyi- ngcwele, uyingcwele, uyingewele Tixo ka Abraham, ka Isaac
no Jacob, Amen.” Ute, ' Vulani emnyango,” bavulileke. Ute kumninawekazi
wake it Madlamini, inkosikazi yomkokeli wase Tshatshi of England,
akukova ukumbiza wati: “ Inkosi iwutamsanqele umsebenzi wako wokundonga
yada yandiputuma, i Nkosi inawe ngenxa yomsebenzi wako.’’ Wati, “
Ndikupeleni pandle," bamkupileke, uhlale ixeshana wati, “ Upina unyana
wam omkulu u Ngqiniso M. Hoza? ” Ubiziweke, uteke akufika wati, "
Mntwana wam ndiyemka ndiyagoduka ndiya ekaya, i Nkosi yam seyindikokele
seyise Mpuma-langa indili- ndele kona.” Watsho wati, “ Ndingeniseni,”
bamngenisa. Ubaye wapinda wati, “ Mntwana wam Ngqiniso i Nkosi
iyitamsanqele indlu yam, uze uligcine elilifa ungalilahli mntwana wam.
Utsho watandaza wati, “ Uyingcwele, uyingcwele, uyingewele Amen.” Watsho
wati,

“ Ningakangeli ezintlungwini zenyama, okwenene yona inazo intlungu,
kanti wona umpefumlo wonwabile.” Wabulisa kuyo yonke iramente, naku
Mfundisi wake. Walala, kanti kukugoduka kwake.

Mandibe ndiyapelake makosi akowetu ase Mbo, ndibulisa kuni nonke.

  N. GUBESA MACWELA.

Toboyi, 10th October, 1885.

Vol. XVIII. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, JULY 2, 1888. No 227.

UKUBEKA ILITYE.

UMFI u REV. JOHN AITKEN CHALMERS

IMIPANGA ngemipanga, lingatsho ibali letu ngezi nyanga zimbini
zigqitileyo. Siqale ngokunc'waba ilulama unyana ka KOBOKA, ob’epete
indlu yamayeza e-Lovedale, ob’esiti enceda ubulwelwe beziqu, alipose
nelizwi lokususa intlungu zentliziyo ; ngoku ushiye isituba emsebenzini
wake akuko onokusivingca.

Kute inyembezi zingekomi, sehlelwa ngomnye umpanga, kuvuleke elinye
incwaba. Sibeke kulo omnye umntwana wase Lovedale unyana ka GQOBA
ob’esand’ “ ukubizwa ” ukuba aye kuba ngumfundisa kwesika Sikunyana. Sa-
shiywa yindoda ebe sisakangele izinto ezinkulu kuyo, sisiti usaya
kushicilela upau lwake pezu kwama kowabo.

Namhla sihlelwe ngumpanga oyigqitileyo eminye, inye- mbezi ziwa pezu
kwelinye incwaba, ngokuba namhla kusu- swe pakati kwetu itshawe,
umkokeli kwa Sirayeli. Sililela u Bev. JOHN AITKEN CHALMERS, umntwana
wasema Xoseni, unyana womnye wabanduleli abebeze nezwi lobomi kweli
lizwe, obenyatela emanyatelweni omzali obalulekileyo pa- kati
kwabakokeli ababezise ukanyiselo ebumnyameni bezizwe zama Xosa.

Yinkohla into esingayibalayo, ngokuba umyolelo yinto enkulu
engenakufumane igqitwe. Waye umfi lowo mhlana wabuba, wayolela
ngelokuti—“ Maze kungenziwa nteto ngaye ; maze kutetwe ngo YEHOVA,
i-Nkosi yake. ” Na- ngona likoyo elo, singa isikumbuzo sake asingetshoni
naye encwabeni. Nati asigqiti ezwini lake, kuba xa siteta ngomse- benzi
wake, abezamela i-Nkosi yake, asidumisi yena.

Umfi lowo, umbali welibali waqala ukumazi e-Gwali apo wazalelwa kona.
Waye eyinkwenkwana engcumbeke bubu- lwelwe kungeko temba ukuba iyakude
ibubone ubudoda. Kute emva kokububa kukayise, walangazelela ukufunqula
lomtwalo ub’upetwe nguyise, wati xa umkuluwe wake nodade wabo beza
kuwela ulwandle beya kufunda pesheya, wazama ngokubongoza kunina ukuba
naye ahambe, ayekuqeqeshelwa umsebenzi wobufundisi. Ute unina, nje ngo
HANA wakudala, wamncamela i-Nkosi yake, pofu kungeko temba lani ukuba
uyakubuya ambone. Akubanga mzuzu, abanga amatanda- buzo etu, ngokuba
i-Nkosi abeyikonza kwase buncinaneni bake, yay’imahlulele umsebenzi
omkulu kulomhlaba we Africa.

Ekuhambani kwexesha uye wabuya okunene, ewuxobele ngokufezekileyo
lomsebenzi abezinikele wona kwase bunci­naneni bake. Wafika epelekwa
ngumtshakazi okwa moya mnye naye, oshiye uyolo nokonwaba kwindlu kayise,
ukuba aze kusebenzisa umyeni wake kobu bugxwayiba base Maxoseni. Baya
kungena esikolweni sase Mgwali, kum- zalwana umfo ka SOGA, obe
ngumzalwana ngokupindeki- leyo ngenxa yomsebenzi wabo, bengonyana ba
Nkosi nye nangenxa yokuba oka SOGA engunyana ka somfi ngokuza- wa ko
MOYA.

Kwangonyaka wokuqala u Mr. CHALMERS wawezwa kumanzi anzulu osizi.
Usozimfesane wasusa pambi kwake ukukanya kwamehlo ake, wati umtshakazi
wake wamncwa- bela e-Mgwali apo, wabeteka, waqobokela elututwiui, wanga
akasayi kuba savuka. Noko akaroranga, wayitobela intonga ka YISE, wasel’
eba nendawo yokwenza owake umsebenzi nowalowo ubizelwe pezulu esawuqala.
Wenjiwanjalo ke ukucikidwa lomfana, yema yanq'ineka inkolo yake.

Umbalisi welibali uti usel’ elixego, imihla yake selifinyeze- kile.
Enkonzweni yake ende, kuninzi akubonileyo oku- yolileyo nokubi. Uti
akubeka ngasemva buvele bukanye ubudlelane bake no Rev. JOHN CHALMERS
nobo SOGA, nabanye abafundisi.

Uti: Kunjengezolo mhla umfi wagaleleka e-Gwali nomka- ke, saya
kubabulisa kona sibulela ukufika kwabo. Emva koko senza umjikelezo wezwe
sifuna iziza zezikolo, sati pesheya kwe Nciba saba lundwendwe lomkuluwe
wake e-Dutywa— sino Dr. STEWART, no Rev. B. Boss, wati umkuluwe ob’epete
elozwe, waba nentsuku ehamba nati sada saya ku- wela um-Bashe siya
kutyelela ku BILL. Inkonzo zelizwi ebezisenziwa kolo luhambo, zaba
zininzi, zinkulu; nanamhla amazwi eziyalo nemitandazo asematsha kuye.

Emva koko sazunguleza sihamba simfunela ibala lesikolo kwelakwa Ngqika,
yena etanda ukusebenza kwelozwe, ngo- kukodwa etanda pakati
kom-Ngcangatelo isizwe uyise awa- qala wapela, wancwatyelwa kuso.
Usawubona umhla abala- lisa pantsi kwemingakazi emikulu e-Tunxe behamba
noka SOGA, nabadala beremente yase Mgwali, onyana baka somfi; bavumelana
ngokuti umnquba mawugxunyekwe apa. Usayikumbula imitandazo nemibulelo
eyanyukela ku SOMA- NDLA ngobo busuku, ngenxa yesicaka Sake, nomsebenzi
waso.

Akubanga ntsuku ukwakiwa kweminquba, kwalandela indlu yetyalike neyo
mfundisi, simana simvelela ebuyedwe- ni bake ; besesimtanda
njengomninawa.

Sayibona imini yokuvulwa kwetyalike yase Tunxe, kuhlanganiswe abafundisi
bamahlelo ngamahlelo yati londlu inkulu njeyakandanisana kwasemnyango
kwade kwayaentla ngum-Ngcangatelo kwane nkosi zawo ; zipetwe ngu FENI no
OBA—izinto zika TYALI. Kumhla into ka SOGA yateta ngobuciko obungahlale
buviwa emhlabeni apa, xa inikela um-Ngcangatelo indlu yawo, iyaleza kuwo
unyana—umfu- ndisi wabo, oze kuma namhla esikundleni sika yise. Seva
ama- zwi obuciko omfundisi wase Bayi u-Rev. G. BENNY ; kwati kona
kwakuqoshelisa indodana ebesize kuyixasa, langa ilizwi lake kubeta
uhadi, ngamazwi entando nofefe, zabe- teka intliziyo ngalontlanganiso.
Apo into yehla kona kuxa kuvunywa iculo lika NTSIKANA kuhlatyelwa yinto
ka MNDAYI, uzwi-lixilongo; owati mhla wakolwa e-Gwali, za zizililo
em-Ngcangatelweni, kusitiwa ngubanina oyakusi- hlabelela namhla
emidudweni yetu ? Kute kwakufikwa kulamazwi—

Unyawo zako zinamanxeba Wena.

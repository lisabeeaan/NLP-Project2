Azi namhla wopeza na ukusela ! Ute akubuzwa ukuba uzibulalelaela nina
ngo Tywala watandaza lomtandazo—“ Ndibulela u Tixo ukuba ndise Bayi, apo
nditi ndisiwa kukoyiswa butywala babe sebenditwala bendigodusa, (uteba
ukusiwa e Nkongolweni) kuba ukuba bendise ndaweni ezingapandle
ngekukudala ndacolwa ndifile. Siwuhlomela lomtandazo ngokuti—O Brandy,
Brandy, ngubanina oya kusikulula kuwo amakamandela ako? Uqalekisiwe
umntu owangenisa lomtshabalalisi ezweni lakowetu. Mayiqalekiswe imini
ekwavulwa ngayo inkantini yokuqala kwa Xosa. Mayiqalekiswe indoda
eyatengisa ngomcepe wokuqala we Brandy kuzo izidenge zakowetu. U Tixo
makalekele ukwenjenjalo kubenzi bomteto owanika ilungelo lokuba
sibujiswe ngale mbubiso. Vuka Palamente! Vuka Palamente! Woda ube
nguwupina umhla wako—safana betu selifikile kuti ilizwi lika Tixo. Hayi
ilishwa letu! Pakamani ma Tempile! Binqani zintombi zakwa Xosa ezingama
Tempile—ngenani edyokweni nixwaye izidabane zenu nilwe nalo mpangi,
lombubisi, lomlilisi, lombulali wohlanga Iwakwa Xosa. Baroleni
emlilweni, ade no Booy Nzuzo abe nekaya pakati kwama Tempile.

zilizalisile.

ukuma ematyaleni ngendawo yokuba kusitiwa kulamhlaba ebewupiwe umise
izindlu ezininzi ngapandle kwemvumelo. Isigwebo sibe sesokuba arole i £5
nezindlu ezo zishenxiswe.

IZIMISELO ZE “ MBUMBA.”

NKOSI,—Ekubeni bebaninzi kumzi ontsundu abanga bangasiqonda isiseko se
Mbumba yabantsundu base South Africa ukuze babe nako ukuyiseka kwindawo
abakuzo, ndicela ngoko kuwe isituba sokuba ndifake ezi zimiselo
zomhlambi we “ Mbumba.”—I. W. W.

1.  Igama lalo mhlambi “YIMBUMBA YABANTSUNDU BASE SOUTH AFRICA.

2.  Umsebenzi wayo kukukangela nokuxoxa ngamalungelo ohlanga oluntsundu,
    namatuba empumelelo zalo, anje ngala:—

(а) Imiteto elukani yolaulo.

(b) Amalungelo emihlaba.

(c) Imfundo (kukutazwe ngakumbi neyesi Xosa ngokuguqulwa kwe- ncwadi
    zalo Nteto.)

(d) Ukukutaza uhlanga ukuba lupapamele ukuba ngaba voti.

(e) Ukukutaza ingxaso yepepa lohlanga, “ Isigidimi Sama-Xosa.”

(y) Ukugqala impato ye Municipality.

(y) Ukukutaza uhlanga ukuba luzilungiselele nge Mfundo, nge Zimilo
ezihle, nange Ntlumo engcubekweni, ukuze lufanelwe (1) Kukunyulwa kwi
Town Council, (2) Kukunyulelwa e Palamente.

(h) Ukukutaza ukulwa ngosiba, (1) Ngokutumela izicelo kwizi patamandla,
(2) Ngokuxoxa emapepeni xa kufunekayo.

(i) Ukuzama ukubulala umoya wokucasana nokucekisana pakati kwama hlelo e
Ramente, ngakumbi pakati kwezizwe.

(y) Konke okulilungelo lontsundu wase South Africa kunokuxoxwa kule “
Mbumba.

AMASEBE.

3.  Zomiswa ezi ntlanganiso kuzo zonke indawo apo kuko iqela labantu
    abapapamele ukuhluma kohlanga oluntsundu kungaketwa Bala.

4.  Amagosa oba yi Chairman, Secretary, Treasurer, ne Committee yamadoda
    alishumi elinambini. Emahlanu anako ukuwuquba umsebenzi,

5.  Ngapandle kwale miteto Intlanganiso zamasebe zinokuzenzela imiteto
    (Bye Laws) yokulaula ingxoxo zawo xa kuhlangenweyo.

6.  Imiteto eyenziwe zintlanganiso zamasebe, yokulaula ingxoxo,
    yotunyelwa i copy yayo entlanganisweni yomnyaka apo sukuba iyakuba
    kona, ukuze ikangelwe ngabatunywa bendawo ngendawo.

7.  Xa kuko onikwe ityala entlanganisweni ye Sebe, waza akaxoliswa
    sisigwebo sayo; ukuba ufuna ukub'ena makayazise i Secretary yesebe
    elo, eyakuti lomcimbi iwutumele kwi Secretary yentlanganiso
    yomnyaka, ukuze uxoxwe kona ngabezinye indawo. Nombeni atumele eyake
    incazo kwaku mbali wentlanganiso yomnyaka; ukuba unako, abeko naye.

8.  Imali eyakurolwa lilungu yomiswa zintlanganiso zamasebe ngokwazo
    ngokuyelene namandla amalungu. lyakuti kanjalo ukumiswa kwayo anikwe
    izaziso onke amalungu ukuba abeko ukuze kuqutywe nge vote yawo.

9.  Lemali umsebenzi wayo kukufuna izinto zokuquba umsebenzi, ezinje
    ngamapepa, nencwadi, nezibane, ndawonye nentlaulo yendleko
    zabatunywa.

10. Intlanganiso zamasebe zobako futi; kodwa kushiyelwa kuwo ukumisa
    amaxesha azo.

11. Ukuze kubeko ukuvana kuyo inqubo yemicimbi, ababali bama Sebe
    bayacelwa ukuba bamana ukumazisa umb'ali, nokuba yi Chairman ye
    Committee yentlanganiso yomnyaka.

12. Yonke imicimbi ecukumisa uhlanga lonke yoti yakuba ixoxiwe
    kwezamasebe, kwaza kwabonakala ukuba ingafanela ukushenxiselwa kwe
    Yomnyaka—oko kungenziwa nge Resolution eya kutunyelwa yi Secretary
    ye Sebe elo kweye Ntlanganiso Yomnyaka, kunye nengqibo (ukuba iko)
    ekugqitywe kuyo malunga nalo mcimbi, ukuze lomcimbi ufakwe
    emicimbini Yentlanganiso Yomnyaka eya kuxoxwa.

13. Imicimbi eya kuxoxwa mayingeniswe kwi Committee Yentlanganiso
    Yomnyaka pambi kosuku Iwayo, ukuze ibe nokupaulwa ngexesha
    elifanelekileyo.

INTLANGANISO YOMNYAKA.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1875.

Ngomhla 19 ka February kubuye kwako enye intlanganiso enkulu, engaso eso
sikolo. Isihlalo kuyo sibe sipetwe ngumfo abati ngu Lord Moncrieff,
inene elikulu E-Scotland. Into enkulu ebonakeleyo kule yokugqibela
intlanganiso kukuba ayanela amadoda amakulu angenayo ukuwuxasa
lomsebenzi. Banga ungangowenziwa luhlanga, ungabi ngoweqelana elitile
kube kupela. Kuvakele kanjalo ukuba imali epume E-Glasgow kodwa ke
iselikumawaka amatandatu. Inani lababuti bemali longezelelwe, waza no
Dr. S. wafakwa pakati, nokosikolwayo ukuba akayi kuba naxesba yena
kulomsebenzi. Kuko ke noko ezinye indawana ezilunge naso ekufuneka
zigqityiwe pambi kokuba anduluke ; kuba ukusekwa kwendawo enje ngale
ifunekayo asintwana incinane; ifuna umsebenzi omkulu nokulungiswa
kwentwanantwana ezinge- nakucazululwa. Abafo abaya kusimisa asikuko
nokuba sebenxamile. Baza ku- suka kwangezinyanga.

Asisenasituba namhlanje sokuxela ingcinga zetu ngeso sikolo. Uncedo
lwaso kumakowetu asikolwa ukuba lunokufezwa ngokutelekelelwa. Bonke
abalangazalela ukuqutywa kwelizwi, nokunyuka kwezizwe zakowetu (kuba
sibanye nabo bantu kuyiwa kubo) baya kuvuya ukuva ukuba okwenene kuza
kuma isikolo esinjalo sokugcina igama lalondoda. Kubo bonke ke, sicela
ukuze bangasilibali emitandazweni, bangawalibali amadoda asisebenzayo;
kuba engenakufeza nto engancedwa ngu Mpati opete into zonke.

IMFUNDO YABANTSUNDU.

ISIMO abakuso ngelixesha langoku abantsu- ndu asikuko nokuba sibaxakile
sibuhlungu kwabanqwenela ukubambela pambili kwa- bo. Singabakangele
nganto abambalwa ongati uqubisane nabo apa napaya, akukangela umntu
ufika isizwe sihleli, kubutwe ezinkundleni, kungazinikelwe emsebenzini
nase mfundweni, kanti kunjalo nje amatuba okusebenza maninzi kangaka.
Eyona nto ke engamandla nangapezu kwezinye kukuba bati nabake baya
emsebenzini bangavumi kuhlala, batande futi ukumana bepupumisa, besiti
basaya kupumla emakaya. Kunqabile ukufumana abantu abauzolelayo
umsebenzi. Uti nonconywayo ukuba uyautanda abe ngoya kugqiba inyanga apa
uve ngenye ekankanywa E-Bay, kanti, ngeyesitatu womva ekankanywa
kwelinye icala.

Indawo enjalo ke ayinakubatomalalisa ababanika imisebenzi, ibe ingelulo
ncedo nakwabantsundu. Kungoko ikangelwe nge- ntliziyo exalileyo ngabo
bonke abanga ku- ngenyukwa. Izizatu ezibangela ukuba kube njalo sikolwa
ukuba zininzi; kodwa indawo engumbuzo enkulu eyokuba akukondlela na
ingafunyanwayo enganceda ukuze kungabi nje.

Umfo otile obala kwipepa lenyanga lase Kapa uti indawo enkulu
kukufundiswa komsebenzi, uqalwe besibancinane abantu, uqalwe kwizikolo
zangapandle, ukuze ati ekula umntwana abe seleqelene nomsebenzi. Iqinga
alivelisayo uti kwezizikolo zikulu nokozize zangapandle ngekumiswa
umfundi- si wemisebenzi ongapandle kopete incwa- di. Lingati ke kuba
inani lezizikolo ati ngekuqalwa kuzo lilikulu, kufezwe ukumi- selwa
abafundisi abanjalo ngeminyaka emi- hlanu ; kumane ukumiswa babe 20 ngo-
myaka umnye. Ekuqaleni kungatandeka ukuba ipatwe ngomhlope indawo
enjalo; kuze emisebenzini kuqalwe ngobu karpenter nokutunga izihlangu.
Angati ke amakwenkwe nabafana ekubonwa ukukalipa kwabo kwizikolo
ezinjalo banyuselwe kwezingapezulu, bade ukuba baya quba banikwe
imisebenzi pantsi kwe Government, mhlaumbi bamiselwe ekubeni
ngabafundisi bemi- sebenzi—abantsundu bafundisane kwabodwa.

Kunganjalo ke nase zintombini, zifundi- swe ukusika, nokutunga, neminye
imise- tyenzana enjalo.

Ukumiswa kweqinga elinjalo kungafuna ukuba kurolelwe £15,000 ngomnyaka,
mhlaumbi nangapezulwana, ekungaqinise- kile ke nokuba ingaba
nokubuyiseleka yonke lomali.

Linjalo ke iqinga eliveliswa yilondoda ati liyakunceda kakulu
okukusweleka kwaba sebenzi bakala ngako bonke ngelixesha langoku.
Indawana esingayitetayo ngalo kukuba langa okwenene lingade libe nempu-
melelo. Lingavelisa ukulunga ngendlela ezininzi, ingeyiyo yodwa eyokuba
makube ko abasebenzi. Ngokumalunga nokufezwa kwelo qinga, sikolwa
bunyaniso bezwi lomfo esike samva esiti, into enkulu kufuneka ukuze
kufunwe amadoda awufaneleyo lomsebenzi nokuba ade aya kufunwa pesheya.
Linamandla elozwi; kuba bebaninzi nakwabamhlope abangaluhlisayo ngapezu
kokulunyusa uhlanga; bacite endaweni yokuhlanganisa. Akuko ungaziyo
ukuba asililo ibala yintliziyo into efezayo. Kekaloku indawo ezingaba
ngamandla kumadoda aza kufundisa kukuwazi umsebenzi, nentliziyo
enqwenayo ukunga bangenyuka ababantu bafundiswayo; abe ngumntu oba-

tandayo. Nokuba uyawazi xa ezele uku­fumana eloqosha angalamkeliswa
ngenyanga akuko nto iya kufezeka.

Enye indawo eyingozi ngoku kwabafu- ndayo, ngokukodwa kumadodana apo
siyi- bona futi, yile yokucinga ukuba uti umntu akufunda incwadi kube
sekulihlazo ukupata umsebenzi wezandla. Abanye kungafanelana
sekuxakekile, yinqabe ukuke ancede ungasike umbone efake izandla
ezinxoweni ete nkwa intshiyi ; nokuba selebona eqauka unina ngumsebenzi.
Lendawo sikolwa ukuba iveliswe kukungayazi kakuhle into eyiyo imfundo.
Ukubake kuya kuti kwase kususeleni kokufunda zipatwe kunye zombini
ezonto, kungancipa mhlaumbi kupele ukucinga ukuba imfundo iti umntu
makangasebenzi. Kananjalo sikubona sikubonile kwaba bafunde imisebenzi
ukuba zisike sezincola incwadi, angabi satanda ukukangela namapepa la
endaba wona odwa. Ikwayimposiso naleyo engati mhlaumbi itshatyalaliswe
ipeliswe kukuzi- quba zombini kwasekuqaleni ezo zinto. Ziko nezinye
izinto esingabuye sizikankanye e si­kolwa ukuba zingaveliswa liqinga
elinje ngeli lalo mfo.

ABANTSUNDU NOMSEBENZI.

ISIKALO esihlokoma kumacalana onke ngelixesha langoku, ewe esingati
soloko simi kuleminyaka isesitatwini idluleyo, sesokuba bazakuvela pina
abantu kokusebenza. Kuko isikalo esinjalo nje bazele abantu kwalapa,
kodwa abavumi kusebenza. Abanye bati makumiswe imiteto yokubanyanzela,
indawo leyo esi- ngeyi case nganto ukuba ayiyi kuketa bala,
iyakuwohlwaya ngokufanayo onke amavila. Imisebenzi itintelekile,
eyakomkulu neyabalimi neyabafuyi. Kuza kutiwanina? “Baza kuvela pina
abantu bokusebenza?” Baya kutiwanina ukuze bade basebenze aba bagqiba
imini yonke ngokuteta into ezingenamsebenzi; kanti mhlaumbi ngokuhlwa
banyeke ukuze badle ibokwana yosebenzayo?

Ezindawo asikuko nokuba zibanzi, azina- ko ke ngoko ukufezwa ukuxoxwa
kwisitu- tyana esingangesi sinaso. Sesoti ke ngoko sikankanye indawana
ibenye mhlaumbi zibe mbini.

Bate abanye ngokudinwa kukungavumi kusebenza kwabantu bezizigama bacinga
ukuba makusekusiyiwa kufunwa abasebenzi kumazwe apesheya. Okwenene
londawo kwade kwavunyelwana ngayo kwiparlame- nte ebipambi kwale
idluleyo kwakutshwa indoda yokuya kufuna abantu pesheya abati ngu Mr.
Fuller. Wawela kwaoko. Iziqa-

ABANTSUNDU NE RULUNELI.

ONKE amawetu avumelene ngendawo yokuba kwenzelwe indili efanelekileyo
ukuza ko Mhlekazi i Ruluneli. Kweleveki egqitileyo sati tupa ukuba,
nakuba iyakutyelela imizi emikulu etile, amawetu mawazamele into enye,
ukuba kubeko eyona ndawo angati ababone-abantu bakowetu belibinza.
Ubenga angababona bebubuqela abantu abazama, ukuhlambuluka abamnyama.
Obubuqela angedibane nabo xa beyakuba ngama gcudwana ayakuminyelwa
ngamaqela amakulu abamhlope. Yiyolento ke esiyiyaleza bukali indawo
yokuba impi engeyasekukanyeni —ezimisele ukuzama ubuntu pantsi kombuso
mayingakangeli ubude behambo. Kodwa mayijucele ukuba ize ibeko apa e
Qonce indawo eseyalatelwe ukuba bubonaliswe kona ubuqela bamawetu.

Masiwuqondise umzi. Abamhlope base Qonce abateni bona nokuba sivele
pambi ko Mhlekazi ukuze asibone ukuba siluhlanga siko. Ite yakubhekiswa
kubo lendawo ingokubonakaliswa kwetu yakondlozelwa kunene ngabanga
singebonakali siluhlanga. Pofu yona i Ruluneli seyivakalisile ngocingo
ukuba yodibana nezintsundu izihlwele ngentsasa yo- Mvulo 3 February.
Lencaso yentshaba ezikwabamhlope ibifanele ukubanga ikwele entliziyweni
yake wonke owakowetu ozidlayo ngobuntu nobuhlanga ukuba azimisele ukuba
woti enze konke ukuvala umlomo
wentshaba.

Umzi esiwukangeleyo ukuzakwenza isitonga sontsundu ngokuwo wonke
lomandla we Qonce, ne Monti, ne Cumakala, ne Ngqushwa ne Dike ne Komani,
naba Tembu, ne Transkei. Impi ebu Fundisi nengo Titshala neba Vangeli
ekwezindawo iyafuneka yona kunene. Singa ngenxa yohlanga, angababona
belibinza abantu abahlambulukileyo abantsundu, ukuze abe ebazi xa
benzelwa imiteto erabaxa
engabafaneleyo — abenezwi lokubhekisa kwi Nkosazana ayipateleyo. Amawetu
mawangemki nento yokuba eyakuba kufupi nemizi
etile. Makatumekelele ukuza kwenza indili nesihomo esiyakuba yimbasa ku
Sir HENRY LOCH yolutyelelo lwake kwelase Maxhoseni.

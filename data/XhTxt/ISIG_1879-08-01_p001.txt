LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, AUGUST 1, 1879.

No. 107.

Isigidimi Samaxosa

U-SANDILE.

IBALWE NGU HONOURABLE CHARLES
BROWNLEE.

{Kugqityelwa imbali ebikwi Sigidimi sika
(June)

WATI u-Sir H. Smith kuba etembele kudumo lwegama lake wayiguqula yanto
yimbi impato yabantsundu, zati zilungile pofu indawo azivelisileyo kanti
uyilibalele indawo yokuhluma kwabantsundu kwelixesha lipakati kwe 1835
ne 1846, akawenza amaqinga okuze zipumelele.

Amasoldati awaye kweli lizwe ekumisweni koxolo ancitshiswa kakulu,
kwaxotyiswa ama Xosa amakulu mahlanu enziwa umkosi wa- mapolisa.

Lomkosi wawuyilwe kakuhle waza wase- tyenzwa kakuhle. Asizange sibe nawo
umkosi ofike kuwo nanini ngokulanda nokupelisa ubiwo lwempahla,
ngokukutala nangokuzingisa emsebenzini.

Ukupulukana kwenkosi namandla ebezi- nawo, nokupulukana kwazo nenzuzo,
ngo- kusuka kwaliwe ukuba zihlaulise mhlaumbi zidle umntu, kanti
azinikwa mbuyekezo ngenxa yoko, kwavelisa umoya wolulo nencaso, —kwati
kwakuvela umprofeti kwa Ndlambe ogama lingu Mlanjeni, zati inkosi kuba
bezingakolwa yimpato zasebenza ngenteto zake zifuna ukuwahlokohlela
ebugwenxeni nasekulweni ama Xosa.

Kwiminyaka yokuqala yomitatu yokulaula kwetu ama Xosa izinto zaquba
ngohlobo olwanelisayo kanye. Kute kowesine umnyaka, kwakungena inteto
zika Mlanjeni, kwabonakala imiqondiso yentswela kululamela izipatamandla
nokuzicasa.

U-Sandile waya kumbona umprofeti wa- mkutaza wamomeleza ngendlela zonke.
Kwaqala kaloku ukuti u-Commandant Davis atintelwe ngabantu abaxolileyo
ekwenzeni imfanelo yake. U-Sandile wabizelwa e-Qonce kusitiwa makeze
kutyila ukuzipata kwake pambi ko Sir H. Smith, wati uyoyika, akeza.
Ndatunywa kuye, ndamxelela okokuba akasokwenziwa nto, wati yena
ukupeudula, wake weyeliselwa okunye, akangebuye aye ebizelweni, wala
kwapela ukuya kubonana ne Ruluneli. Noko u-Tyali namanye

amadoda ake apambili ayemzama akancedanga nto. U-Sir H. Smith uteke
uyamkupa ebukosini u-Sandile, kanti ebengenabukosi kade buvunyiweyo,
ebesiti kuzo zonke izinto atobele umpatiswa wemicimbi yakwa Ngqika.

Kwakuba kungafezi nto ukukupa ebukosini i-Ruluneli igqibe kwelokuba
ihlangane nenkosi zama Ngqika e-Fort Cox (e-Mkubiso). Zibeko kakulu
inkosi lomini zahlangana ne Ruluueli zineqela elikulu labantu bazo.
Bababaninzi abatetayo bevakalisa ukumtobela kwabo u-Rulumente, no Maqoma
oyena mntu wateta kakulu wahlaba kwakwelo zwi lokutobela u-Rulumente.

I-Ruluneli ifike yaxelela intlanganiso umvuzo oya kunikwa umntu obambe
u-Sandile, yati mayikutshwe imipu eyayipetwe ngu Tsolekile nabantu bake
lamini babexobele u-Commandant Davis. U-Sandile wayevuma ukuba imipu
mayikutshwe, nokuba yenye into ebizwayo kuye wayevuma ukuyenza, into
awayeyala kukubonakala pambi kwe Ruluneli kupela. Wala u-Maqoma ukuba
kubeko into eyenziwa ngama Xosa yokuxolisa, kuba wayeselelungiselela
ukuba kuliwe. Wayesiti, nge Yezembe bekusitiwa uyageza kuya kubonakala
namhla ukuba aka- gezi.

Ndingake ndifake izwana apa lokuti, u- Maqoma wayeyicasile kakulu
imfazwe Yeze- mbe, kwada kwafuneka ukuba abe bunyanzelwa ukufuduswa
kwake e-Bofolo, ukufa kwalo. Akubonakali nto ikukulwa wayenzayo ngalo
mfazwe kuba waye geza mhlaumbi ezigezisa. Wati xa kuqala ukutetwa
ezokuxola kwalo wabonakala egaleleka ekampini yetu engati upambene
ingqondo. Ude emzuzwini wasiwa e-Bayi, egcinwa kuba kubonwa engayeki
ukumana ukuhamba ezweni. Waye se Bayi apoke ukuqala kuka Sir H. Smith
ukuza emdeni.

Kusike kwema ngo Maqoma, ayakutshwa imipu, kwatiwa ku Sandile
makangagobi uya kuxaswa ngama Ngqika kwanezinye izizwe.

U-Sir H. Smith wati kuba ekolwe zezonteto zo Maqoma nabanye, zokuba bona
batobele u-Rulumente, wagqiba kwelokuba emke e-Fort Cox apo waye
qokelele umkosi omalunga kwiwaka lamadoda. Ubuye wabona ukuba makake
akupe umkosi ukweze i-Xesi; utumele amadoda akumakulu amahlanu ukuba
enjenjalo. Kute nge 24 ka December 1850 xa lomkosi ukwingxingwa

yentsunguzi enqabileyo (i-Booma Pass) awu- wela ama Xosa. Yenjenjalo
ukuqala kwayo imfazwe ye 1850 (eka Mlanjeni) yabako kwada kwafika
u-March 1853.

Ngalo mfazwe ama Xosa awonakalisa wonke lomandla wase mpumalanga ada aya
kuma nge Somerset, esiti kuzo zonke indawo ahamba kuzo, abulale abantu
kunene, atimbe impahla ehambayo abete atshayele, atshabalalise kuti tu
engahambiyo.

Ngegama kwakusitiwa ngu Sandile oyintloko kolo luvuko, kodwa imikosi
yayipetwe ngu Maqoma, ikwanguye ocinga amaqinga namacebo okulwa, ehleli
e-Mtontsi apo wahlala ixesha elingapezu kwenyanga ezilishumi
elinesibozo, simzama singenako ukumkupa.

Ekupeleni kwalemfazwe ama Ngqika agxotwa kweli lizwe lase Tyume ne Xesi
elino Matole nentaba ezinqabileyo, kwamiswa lendlela inkulu isinga
kwelipezulu ukuba ibe ngumda wawo ngase ntshonalanga, oko kukuti,
lendlela ipuma e-Qonce isiya kwa Komani avunyelwa ukuba ngase mpumalanga
kwalo ndlela ade aye kuma nge Nciba. Ate noko apantsi kwama Ngesi ama
Ngqika inkosi zanikelwa igunya lokubapata abantu bazo ngokwemiteto
yabantsundu.

U-Sandile wapulukana nesona siqendu sihle elizweni lake, kodwa
wayifumana into awayesilwela yona, ukuwapata ama Ngqika engapazanyiswa
ngandawo kwa Rulumente, kwada kwafika unyaka we 1856. Ngalonyaka u-Sir
G. Grey wafuna iqinga lokupelisa lento yokuti abantsundu behleli
emhlabeni wama Ngesi babe noko belaulwa zinkosi zabo ngokwesiko
labantsundu. Unqopisene nenkosi namadoda apambili ukuba uyakuza- mkelisa
imali etile ngomnyaka zize zona zincame lomalungelo zaziwanikwe nge
1853.

Ngomnyaka we 1856 kwaqala ukuxelwa kwenkomo. Ndakuva, ukuba lento yo No-
ngqause ayiqalwanga ngu Rili, yaqutywa yanyanzeliswa nguye. U-Maqoma no
Mha- la zazi zinkosi awaye sebenza ngazo nganeno apa u-Rili.

Ndandite kwasentloko u-Sandile neqela lamadoda apambili ndabahlula
abangena ekububiseni inkoino zabo namazimba abo. Endingaziyo nokuba
kungenxa yokona nokuba kungenxa yokuba u-Sandile ebendibikela konke
okwenziwayo engandifihleli nto, lento washiywayo u-Sandile ngu Rili
kwatenjwa

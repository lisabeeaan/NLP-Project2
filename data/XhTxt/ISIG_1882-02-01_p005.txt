U-Mdali kubonakala ukuba wabe misele ukuba abantsundu banga selitywala.
Sitsho ugale nto: into eninzi kula manene amhlope ngaba seli be ndywala,
kanti baninzi abangade baxolele uku nqumla elinye lama lungu abo
bekanyela ukuba iko into enjalo. Inyaniso yeyokuba basela ngobu ncatu.
Kuti bamnyama ayiko into enjalo; kupela iveki umntu esela ngoku
fihlisela, ze ngomso um’bone nge vukuvuku ledyasi sele ludwamba esi
trateni njenge hlungulu elifileyo. Lowo ulele apo yinto apa ifunde
kunene kanantsi, ibiti iyana nama jent’lmani aselayo: isiti bubutyakala
bama xego ukutetisa uselo. Namhla buyi hlazile bona utywala. Siti yinto
enqabileyo ukuba umntu omnyama alinganise ama Ngesi. Nangoku sinabo esi
cinga ngabo, naba magama ase ngqondweni zetu sibala nje, abati bahamba
emkondweni wamajeni Ima­ni baza beyela ekubeni babe ngama nxila ka Hiya.
Bahlazeka apo ke abantu abafundileyo. Iqinga esibanika lona leli: musa
ukuke ulinge uyicukumise iglasi nto ya kowetu; asi sidlo sa kowenu eso.
Akubonina sesigxekeze amagama oninzi lwakowenu nje, ngokuti siwangemse
ezonweni ezi ninzi?

Ngesimilo sayo sifumana ukuba impungutye apa ingumgcini we Tola ne Hotel
yase Botha’s Hill (pezu ko Mlomo,) asi “nkatazo” pezu kwaba mnyama
kodwa, nakuma gwangqa “isali yayo iya tyabula.” Kwati mhla yapata kubi
inene elidumileyo eli ntsundu, esingasakuli cakula igama lalo, elabe
lihamba kunye no mbali wezi “Mpawana,” sazi tutuzela ngeliti injalo kade
impato yama Ngesi ase Rini yomntu onelishwa lokwa mbata ibala elimnyama.
Site kodwa saku funda eli bali kwi pepa lase Rini eliyi Grocott’s Penny
Mail saqonda ukuba kanti lenkewu yi “njabav” indala yalo m’mango.

“Singazibiza ukuba singabane tyala,” itsho i Penny Mail, “ukuba sidlule
ngapandle kwe nteto, isenzo sobu ralarume somgcini Tola wase Botha’s
Hill, esi bekiselela kwi ngozi embi, ehlele u Mr. W. B. Shaw wesi
sixeko. Ukuba kuko ntwana iyinyaniso kwezi zinto zixelwayo, u Mr. Payne
umelwe yinto engapezulu kokwe nyanywa noku tshabiswa ngabantu. Kutiwa
lomntu wale nokuncedisa ngengubo yokugqubutela amalungu awa pukileyo eso
sialam sasicela uncedo. Ukuba u Mr. Payne uxokiwe, ukuba msulwa kwake
makaku hlokomise kakulu nabanzi, sasiya kuvuya; ukuba kodwa une tyala,
makeve ubunzima, tina sasingasayi kulila.”

Kuyo yonke lenteto siti tina Amen, makube njalo.

Nakuba ngati lenyaniso seyaba yeya kwa mkwekazi, soloko saba ngama
gqoboka nje, noko iseko. Siteta le yokuba kuqine, pakati kwetu bamnyama.
aba fazi emsebenzini, ibe indoda iyantaza kulo lali ikuyo, ilal’ivuka
nge sisu. Ngenxa yobubi bale hambiso yamadoda amnyama kuko esozeke siwa
kankanye nasesi, Gidimini apa ama gama awo, okwa ngoku siti makalumke.
Ndawo inkulu kodwa ibanga ukuba salatise kulento, ibambe uhlanga
lungenyuki, lityala ebelike latetwa yi mantyi yase Glasgow, pesheya.
Limi ngolu hlobo. Indoda “idlu’ moya,” imsbiye umfazi nonyana aba bini
beseba ncinane; oko kukuti yenza “umhlolokazi nenkedama ezino yise.”
Emva kwe minyaka e 20—galakaxa inkewu! ifike aba bantu seba tenga indlu
bayi hombisa ka bukekayo. Hayi bona, banga teti nto. Aqale umtakati
ukuzi pata njengo mninimzi. Makwenziwe lanto. Leya mayime ngolu
hlobo—hayi makuti apa. Lwale usapo. Yeka ukusinga ema tyaleni. Suke u
Sheriff Lees, umantyi wase Glasgow, ati“ Ngokomteto, imali, nezinto
umfazi azi fumana nge ntsebenzo yake, zezake ; asi zandoda. Eli tyala
ndicinga ukuba yinkatazo nje.” Unjalo umteto madoda ati aba fazi
besebenza abe dwekesha indaba ezi nge ze zakwanto!

Sir Honourable Gontshi!!! “Let us laugh and be fat!’ Asikolwa kodwa
ukuba u Mr Gontshi worn bulela u Mr Nxa­pala nakuba etywakanisele
wamdyoba ngamagama abekeke ngolu hlobo. Siyaqala tina ukuva ukuba kanti
nakwa Xosa inkosi bezi nika ama gama. Nxapala, ne sutor ultra crepidam
-—ngoku kumsha, “Yeka imicimbi yabanye.”

Ku bonakala ukuba uhlanga lusakumana uku kubeka endleleni yalo yoku
nyuka ngenxa yezinto ezimana uku bako eze nziwa yinxenye yalo nangabantu
aba ngabanye balo. Pakati kwezi zinto zenziwayo singa bala ishwangusha
eliziswe yimpi emnyama yo mandla wase Aliwal North. Bokumbula aba fundi
besi Gidimi ukuba sati kunyulo lwe ndoda ye Parlamente yoku ngena
endaweni ka Mr. J. M. Orpen, mabini amadoda eko balwa kuwo—ngu Mr.
Richard Dowling, oti yimpato ka Mr. Sprigg yaba mnyama akolwa yiyo; no
Mr. De Wet, ovumelana namadoda ati lo mpato yabi yeyoku capukisa
abantsundu—ayile Rulumente ilawulayo. Bate ke aba mnyama base Aliwal,
bese kondweni lomfundisi wabo babala ku Dowling, ongene ngo ncedo lwabo.
Aya buza ke lama doda angaba hlobo betu ukuba yinto eyaka yehlana ukuba
umntu apakamisele egunyeni utshaba lwake. Lombuzo ngobu buzwa siti.
Impendulo soyilinda kuma kowetu akweso siqingata sase Ali­wal, nawa
“votela” u Mr. Dowling. Ilungelo letu loku nyula ama doda e Parlamente
lise ngozini, kuba ezi zihiba zase Gqili “zibu tengisile ubuzibulo” bazo
nge nkobe.

ABAFUNDISI BASE WESILE E RINI.

Njengoko sa bikayo Isigidimi, intlanganiso yaba fundisi aba mnyama bama
Wesile uqale umsebenzi wayo ngomhla we 4 ku January, e Rini. Obeke azi
hambele ezi ntlanganiso into eyabi ngete yabonakala mhlope bubulula
benani lamadoda ahambisa lomsebenzi mhle. Buninzi ubuso obu qelekileyo
nobo nwabisayo obunga bonakalanga. Ama doda ama bini aka fikelelanga
ngenxa yokunga pili. Xa siti usizi nemigulo eyendeleyo ebonakeleyo,
ngamazwi nangoku bonakala kobuso bezi nkulu, sixela into eyaba mhlope
ngoku lusizi kwaba bese Graham’s Town ngexesha lentlanganiso. Asina
kungena kwi ngxoxo ezabe zipambi kwe ntlanganiso, kuba ezo bezi hanjiswa
ingcango zivaliwe; sesi ngazi kolisa ngezinto esizi bone nga pandle.
Ezike bezifezeke ngohlobo olo ezi fezeka ngalo izinto zelilizwe, nakuba
singeti akako ama “hlungulu” alila kuba enga koliswanga yindlela aba
lungiselelwe ngayo aba fundisi. Ayipambi kwetu noko ke lonto, sixela
into esiyi bonileyo ngapandle koko ngeza awetu amazwi.

Kwa kunye ne ntlanganiso, ibandla lama Wesile, lilungiselele isidlo
samanzi lawo ashushu bati yi “ti,” ne ntlanganiso leyo kutiwa yi Tea
meeting, kunye neye ngoma ekutiwa yi Con­cert. Lo mgidi ube ngolwesi
Hlanu, January 6. Eyokuqala ibise tyalikeni yaba ntsundu, ku nqabile
ukuti isidlo eso sibe sesi hambiseke kakuhle, ngendlela eza hanjiswa
ngayo izidlo. Umntu owake wangena kwi zidlo ezi njalo kwezinye indawo,
wabe ngeze atandabuza ukuti sasilihlazo kwabo basilungiselelayo, masiti,
nakwi bandla lama Wesile antsundu ase Rini. Ukuba ixesha le sidlo eso
lalibete lahlulwa kwele mvumo, kunokwenzeka ukuba ngesaba yenye into,
kodwa, njengoko kwaba njalo ukuzi hambisa kunye ezi ntlanganiso, kwaba
yihambiso eyabonisa ubutyakala obu ngu mangaliso. Bati abantu besa
lambele izinto ezimnandi, babizelwa kwi ndlukazi apa enkulu ekutiwa yi
Shaw Hall ukuba baye kupulapula imvumo. Kanye nge ntsimbi yesi bozo
londlu inkulu yaqala ukuzalisa kwaba ngumzuzu otile uku lungisa abantu.

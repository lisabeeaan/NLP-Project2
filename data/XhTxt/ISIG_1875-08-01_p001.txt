III.

INTLALO YAKE YOKUZIBUBISA.

Kungekabi zintsuku zininzi wahlanganisa into yonke unyana omnci wesuka
kwe- lakowabo waya ezweni elikude,wati kon'apo, wayicita impahla yake
ngentlalo zokububisa. Luka xv. 13.

Uyise walomfana akabekisanga zwi kunyana wake mhlana wasibizayo isahlu-
lompahla yake, akatetanga nto mhlana wafudukayo, akamputumanga akuba se-
lendulukile, eye wafika ezweni elikude akatumanga nasigitshimi sinye
ukuya ku- mvela indaba zalonyana wake nokumbuza ukuba uhleli njanina
kwelozwe akulo, kusekuhleni ke okokuba uyise ebenga- kolisiwe
ngulomsebenzi walomntwana wake, intliziyo yake ibuhlungu ngokokude
angabi nakumsonga lonyana wake. Mntwana wumbi ungageziyo ubeya kuqala
acinge ati, ndiyona andoni sinina lento ubuwo angawuvulanga umlomo wake
kum, lento kungafikiyo nasicaka sakowetu ukuza kundishumayeza nto
yakowetu nakundipa ndaba zasekaya? Mntwana wumbi unengqondo ubeya kubona
ukuba uyise umenzakalisile. Onguwumbi ebe- ya kutuma umntu ukuya
kumbulisela kuyise ambikele izinto ezonakeleyo kuseloko wemkayo kowabo.

Lonyana ebengagxotwanga nguyise, engoniwanga kowabo, uzimkele ngokwake
kowabo, uziyele ngokwake kwelozwe elikude, ke uyise akanakwenza mizamo
yokumguqula, akanakuya kumfuna. Akutandabuzeki ke okokuba uyise uqumbile
akakolisiwe bobubugeza balomntwana wake ; njengokuba unyana wake .

eye kuzimela kwelolizwe likude, naye uyise wasele zihlalela kwelake
angenzi mizamo yakumbuyisa yakumguqula unyana wake, Liti ilizwi lika
Tixo sipendule isidenge ngokobudenge baso. Wenjenjalo ke loyise
kulomntwana wake. Akuyibanga impahla yake usuke wamnika, akumka
umvumele, akufudukela ezweni elikude akupambananga zigitshimi endleleni
eziya kumputuma, wasuka wanga ngati uyise ute, lomntwana wam makaye
kuziqondela ngokwake kude apo ububi bokuhlalapakati kwabantu basemzini,
azitwalele ngokwake inxwaleko ezofika zimtshutshise kona.

Kwelozwe likude ufike wahlala kanjanina lomfana?

I. Indawo yokuqala yile, akabanga na- sikundla kwelozwe. Uye wafika
okunene wafika edlotyiswa yilomihlali yokuba engumfo okululekileyo
ongenampati. Fanel’ ukuba namhla ke woqala ake, abiye, ayaluse impahla
yake yande ; fanel’ ukuba enempahla engakanana wofike abonwe
ngabamelwane bake ukuba ulitanda nkomo elihamba nazo, ezifunela amadlelo
angamariwa; fanel’ ukuba wokutala alime kunene azinze, apelele kuye
amahlwempu ukuza kusebenza, bavukele kuye ababetwa ngozindlala, engumfo
obe- qele kuhluta nje fanel’ ukuba imizamo iyakuba yonke yokuti amasiko
akowabo abe kwangalawo awomzi wake. Fanel’ ukuba indyebo iyakuba yonke,
amasi abeko nokudla kubeko, nenyama ibeko, nemifuno yonke emnandi
ibeyonke. Seleke wabona nje ku yise uhlobo lokupatwa komzi, nolokupatwa
kwabantu kuya kuma kunjalo kulo wake, abasweleyo bopelela kuye apa,
batyebe, nenkedama zize kondliwa nguye apa, abe nomzi owovakala
ngobunzima bawo ukuba ngowakomkulu ngowesilumko, ngowendoda etanda
egcina abantu. Fanel’ ukuba ngumfo oyakulufumana udumo, abe nesizwe
asipeteyo kuba ngunyana we tshawe, owotanda ukuba umzi wake ube
kwangangalowa

woyise. Kufike kwanjalo na ke? Siba
siyawufundasiyawupengululalomzekeliso indawo ezinjalo asizifumani aziko
kanye nakuba ebehleli komkulu, ondliwe kona, ehlangana lemihla nengwevu
zakowabo ezingambalisela amavo, zimbonise uhlobo lokuzipata kwake,
nohlobo lokugcinwa komzi, nolokufuywa kwempahla, zimxelele indawo
abefanele ukwenza zona ukuze apume ebudengeni, zizonanto abengazibeke
ndlebe ezo. Ubulumko ebengabufumananga kwakowabo nje niti ebeya kutini
ubuganga ngamini nye kwelozwe likude? Umntu uqala kwa ese mncinane kwa
ese ngumntwana ukuzifunda izinto. Ufunda ukubapata abanye
ngokubahlonela, ubazuza ngokubanika imbeko. Ke, engamhlonelanga nje
uyise engampatanga kakuhle niti ubeya kwazi ngani ukuba nomzi obantu
ayakwazi ukubagcina. Ukulima akakufundanga, ukwalusa aka- kufundanga
kowabo, uyakukohlwa ke kukubela, nale mpahla emnye nje engena- bantu iya
kumpalala, elahlekileyo iyakuya kufunwa ngubani, ize leyo yasekaya
ikangelwe ngubani xa kuko engekoyo aye kuyifuna ezintabeni? Kungaseko
mntu nje amaziyo emhlabeni apa kona izicaka uya kuzifumana kumani eziya
kufika zim- sebenzele imisebenzi yake? Ke kwelazwe akulo akuko nto ifike
yalunga, akabanga naso isikundla, usuke waba lidungudwane elingateni
nakwaka nakuba nomzi walo, nakuba nabantu elibabusayo.

II. Ukuze ndingene kwelesibini, nditi kutiwa apa ufike wayicita impahla
yake, ayibiwanga masela, ayiqwengwanga ma ramncwa angozincuka nozingwe,
ayibu- banga sisifo ; usuke wayicita ngokwake. Akayanananga, akatenganga
mhlaba nga- yo, akafunisanga ngayo efuna oyiyimbi impahla engati
mhlaumbi iwulungelo lo- mhlaba akuwo kunaleyo abeze nayo; hai uyicitile
wadlala ngayo, yati ibininzi intle yamana ipela ngokupola ipoliswa
kwabo- bugaza bake. Ufunde ukunxila wati akuliva inxano limtshutshisa
wamana eziquba

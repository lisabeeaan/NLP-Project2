ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1. 1883.

IMPAWANA.

Mabube ubugqweta obu bunemali—sitsho kuba kusitiwa e Ngqushwa
selesesihlanwini.

E Qonce kwa Buluneli, (Brownlee Station) ngombla we 9 ka February indoda
etile ibonwe selizidubule endlwini yayo.

Intsimbi ezi zokucancata uloliwe opakati kwe Cradock ne Colesberg,
ukusuka e Colesberg selingumgama okwi mayile ezi 70.

U Rev. W. J. B. Moir, M.A., obexesha lide efundisa intsapo apa e
Lovedale, epete neramente yama Ngesi e Alice, unduluke ngo 13 Feb.,
etyelela e Scotland.

Uti u Mcokeli wepepa lase Monti, ubikelwe ezokuba kuko uhlobo Iwecesina
olungenele igusha zika Mr. Bowers, ngapa ngase Dilayibos, olutsho
Iwaqengqa amakulu amabini.

Umfo otile wakwa Hleke e Mgqwakwebe ubanjwe wafakwa entangeni e Qonce,
ngokusuka axabele omnye ngomhlakulo entloko. Umxatyelwa lo uhleli yada
yalolwe Sine ate ke waqawuka ngalo.

E Somerset East izitora zo Messrs C. Hannan, & Co., zisand’ ukuqekezwa
kwakuzo ezintsuku. Isela eli kubonakala ukuba belinxamele imali, koko
lisuke lafumana i 13s. 6d. nempahlana efan’ukuba ibingamelwa yi 30s.
kupela.

I Office ye Grocott’s Penny Mail, (lelinye lamapepa elo)— lendlu
iqekezwe ngolwesi Tatu olugqitileyo. Abaqekezi bayo kubonakala ukuba
bebeba bofumana imali koko badanile kuba bafumene isheleni enye enetiki
kupela.

Indawo yokuba Intlanganiso “Yembumba,” neyo Manyano Ngencubeko,
zimanywe, seke seva ngayo kwincwadi ete inxenye yamagunya e “ Mbumba ”
azibalela amanene esazana nawo apa. Into enkulu ngoku Lumanyano. Tina
siyavuya.

Kwi Christian Express yale nyanga u Dr. Dale ubale incwadi emnandi
kunene, akutaza naluleka kuyo amalungu eli bandla lintlanganiso
ibisandukubase Mkubiso. Anga amalungu angazi fumanela elipepa,
kwanjengokuba efanele ezifumanele onke amapepa ateta ngentlanganiso.

Kuvakala ukuba ngu Rev. Canon Woodrooffe, M.A., (Christ’s Church
Oxford), onyulelwe ekubeni ngumhloli zikolo Pesheya kwe Nciba. Lendawo
ixesha eli lonke ubuqutywa ngu Mr. Charles Clark umsebenzi wayo. U Canon
Woodrooffe okwangoku ngumfundisi webandla lase Tshatshi e Somerset East.

U Mr. John A. Bennie, obepeti isikolo sase Blythswood Pesheya kwe Nciba
ubuyele kwase Lovedale apa ukuza kuhlala. Abahlobo abaninzi baka Mr.
Bennie bavuya kakulu ukumbona kanjako pakati kwabo. Yinto eqelekileyo
kwintsapo ebefunda apa ukuva iteta ngemini ezimnandi oko abasebenzi
kulomzi, ibingo Mr. Bennie no Miss Waterstone. Umgulo wetu tina
ngowokuba ngamana loma xesha abuye ayifikele i Lovedale.

Ibandla elipete imicimbi yawo wonke Umandla wase Qonce lisicela ukuba
salatise kwizima-mhlaba zeso Siqingata, kwisaziso esibonakala kwelinye
ibala kweli pepa lanamhla nge Rafu entsha abati yi Road, Bate, elindelwe
ihlaulwe ngapambi kwe 23 March ngalo nyaka. Yinto esivuyisileyo le yeli
bandla, yokuti iti into ecetywayo ivakaliswe kwangapambili njengokuba
kunjalo kuma Ngesi. Xa kunje ke kuyaqala ukulunga.

I “ Poor Editor,”—Lenteto siyiguqule ku Grocott, sinokuti Amen kuyo. Uti
u Mhleli akuposisa epepeni lake, luti uluntu selumbiza ngokuti lixoki.
Kodwa uti umfo ongu’mi ndawo akwenza imposiso ingaze yaziwa bani
ngapandle kweqelana elitile lezihlobo, zize ngokukauleza zicela ukuba u
Mhleli angayifaki londawo. Kuze kuti akufa kwayena lomfo ati u Mhleli
acelwe ukuba axele yonke ingxelo entle ngaye, buzinyezwe bona ububi.
Makafe ke yena u Mhleli kusekutiwa loka liziqondele namhla ixoki.

Kusandukuvakala indaba zozibuleleyo e London. Kutiwa igqira elitile
elalinikwa ityala mfazi utile, esiti, belimgangqele, etyolwa nalikolwane
lake lisiti kuko nani litile lemali lingekoyo, ngokutengiswa kwento
ezitile eziyelene nomsebenzi wabo, lite ke eli nene lingena
kuzinyamezela ezi zinto lazibulala ngetyefu. Kubonakala ukuba umlingane
ubefumane wamti nca ngento angayaziyo, engxamele ukuyidla ngeqinga imali
yale ndoda. Ewe into le yona ibonakele ngolu hlobo eluntwini, abate bona
basebeyigqogqa indlu yalondoda ilimenemene, bebonisa ke uluvo Iwabo.

U Mr. Joseph M. Orpen, ogama like lapambi kwabantu belizwe ngokumayela
nemicimbi yase Lusutu, upumile kwindawo yokuba sisandla se Ruluneli
kwelozwe. Indawo leyo yamkelwe ngu Captain Blyth, C.M.G., imantyi enkulu
kwe lama Mfengu. Ukwahluka kwengqondo zabe Sutu nama Mfengu kunjengokuba
impumalanga injalo kwi ntshonalanga. Ekubeni kulula ukulaula ama Mfengu,
asintwana ukulaula abe Sutu kwisimo abangeniswa kuso lolo tshaba lukulu
Iwe Koloni u Mr. Sprigg. Singavuya u Captain Blyth epumelele kakuhle
ekupateni abe Sutu.

Namhla kumzi wakowetu kuko into eninzi yamadodana ezamela ukuzenzela
amagama. Mhlaimbi lamatuba angalu- ncedo kuwo—ukuzila indywala, ukugcina
imali, ukuteta inyaniso, ukubekeka, ukuncedisa abasweleyo—zonke ezi
zinto zibandakanyiwe esimilweni somntu zifeza ngaxesha nye konke
okungefezwe yiminyaka yemizamo yokufuna isimilo. Zomvulela amasango
empumelelo, embeko, okuxunyiswa nokutandwa. oluzame ukuwangena uninzi
alwapumelela. Nto ibange ukuba de sishumayele nje, sisibonakalo
esisibone ngo February nje sendodana enemfundo ete putu, eyabidume
ngesimilo, ete ngenx’enokudekisela elokuqala kulamacebo
siwakankanyileyo, yeyela etywaleni, eseyiti yakufika nakwababe
ngabahlobo bayo abakulu, nababuki bayo ikangelwe njengo dwebile ohamba
ngamanqina omabini.

Asazi nokuba soba sibengeza izinto zama Tempile—umhlambi osekutiwa ngowe
I.O.T.T.—xa siti ezintlanganisweni zawo kuke kubeko ixesha lokuncokola
ngezinto zokukutaza o Sister no Brother. Into eninzi yabafundi betu
yeyawo lomhlambi. Angati ke lamanqaku abe mnandi kumalungu. U Sir Garnet
Wolseley ngomnye wabapati mikosi abakulu bezi mini zanamhla. Nguye
owacima amalangatye emfazwe e Ashantee, iminyaka emininzi edlulileyo
namhla; nguye owabepete imikosi kwa Zulu ukuze lomfazwe ibehle ipele;
nguye owakaulezisa ukuzinikela kuka Sikukuni ; nguye opelise ngexeshana
elingekoyo imfazwe ebise Jeputa kulo nyaka udlulileyo. Eli roti ke
aliyipati nango cikicane indloloti ! U Cardinal Manning, intloko
yabafundisi base Roma Pesheya (England) uyingqina ngokumhlope lenyaniso
ngokwa- ke ukwazi. Kutiwa umkosi owoyise ama Jeputa kule mfazwe ka Arabi
ngowokuqala owatwala i-ti kumagogogo awo endaweni ‘ yesilo sika mhlola.’
Kutiwa amajeni ase Scotland alwa usizi, ngexesha apa i America yayifuna
amandla okuzipata ngokwayo, esidla imiqa kupela. Zonke ezi ziganeko
zibonisa ukuba umntu unokwenza ngokufaneleke ngapezulu umsebenzi wake
ngapandle kokuba ‘ azisuse intloni.’

Lento umntwana iti yakunyikilwa ngunina ivakale—“ Ma-na-na-na-na-na!
Ndigqibele ma, andisoze ndipinde! ” Net sou—injalo kanye indawo esimi
kuyo ngokumayelana ne Hewu ne Ngqushwa. Kanti ke umntwana utsho abuye
apinde. Lo Baba usiti ke, ngobude bunyawo ntandatu. Etetile utetile. Xa
sitshoyo ke sigqwatulela lenteto ivela kwinene esaziyo ukuba aliqutywa
ngumoya womona or wobutshaba ngakwi Ngqushwa xa libala ngoluhlobo
lulandelayo :—

Ubudenge base Ngqushwa, ibiyinteto yabantu ukuti xa kutiwa umntu sidenge
kutiwe ungu Mgcaleka, mhlaimbi uvela e Ngqushwa na? Kumadoda abeze
kutshata e Xesi evela e Ngqushwa enye imke ingatshatanga, yati enye
yatshata marixirixi, ngenxa yokungalungi kwamagama, kuba ute umfundisi
akuhlanganisa amagama wafumana isisikata. Zifike zati inkabi
zingamasoka, kanti zombini zabujelwa ngabafazi; enye yatshata ngokwama
krestu, enye ngokwaba hedeni, nezinye indawana zafumaneka zingalungile.
Enye kanye kubonakele ukuba ayisivatanga isivato somtshato, yagxotelwa
kwase Ngqushwa, kwatiwa yoselitshatela kona xa ite eso sikata
yasicombulula. Enye .nto eyapaulwa kwi Lady zakona, kukufukuta isimetyu
(isixaxa senqawa). Azi lamatili namahlazo obupantsi opuma ninina.
Ngqushwa ama Mfengu afikela kuwe ukuvela kwawo kwa Hintsa, uyenye
yendawo zokuqala elizwini nasekukanyeni, kanti kusate Iwale ubunyama.
Uti omnye umntu abantu bakona basa “tatshekile” kanye.

Pezu komhlaba akuko nto ibuhlungu njengokunyukwa yimpuku, mhlaimbi
lisele, nge bulukwe. Inene elisand’ukuhlelwa sesi sihelegu libonakele
lishwabulela owati amadoda makagxulushe ibulukwe endaweni yokuba nawo
abinqe. Umhlobo wombali wolu “ Pawana,” unduluka kweli esinga kwelanga “
pakati.” Ngokuhlwa ugaleleka entangeni kumzana abati li Kobonqaba. Ufika
abizele ihashe lake isitungu. Hayi aye asitabate. Cwaka entangeni.
Sekulelwe. Bantu bakoyo kufupi, ngamanene mabini amhlope, abefika nawo.
Akuviwanga ngani—kuviwe ngele hangu Iona uburara—Hi-i-i- i! What’s the
matter—umntu udliwa yini? Avakale umhlobo wetu ngeliqaqambayo—A snake
(Inyoka)! ! ! Where? Where? Where?—Uvakele esoma umnene (kuba ngoteta
isi Ngesi ngomxolo)—Inyuka ngentungo! Watsho seleyicudisa ngazo
zozibini. Bekumhlope kodwa ukuba ayinakunyukela ngentla kwamadolo, kuba
ngomnye wala manene anxiba onzonza, (ibulukwe ezite nyi emadolweni
zabanzi ezantsi.) Bate baku putaputa isiqu, akwabako nto yaka yayileya.
Ute akuyeka ukubikica umfomkulu — qata pantsi isele seliyintlama! Uposwe
ngesitukwana, ngokwesiko kakade u Nigala, wayekwa. Uhleka nje wena
mfundi, tina sesike seva futi ngokuti impuku namasele anyuke iziquluba
zamadoda ngapakati kwebulukwe, asinakukuncedisa kulo ntsini.

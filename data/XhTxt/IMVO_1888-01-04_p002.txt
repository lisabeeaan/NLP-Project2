EZASE MATYALENI ENGQUSHWA.

Inkosikazi ye Mantyi yase Ngqushwa imangalele intombi ka Booi Nginda,
ebi- sebenza kuyo ngezizatu ezitatu, (1) igile isitulo, (2) inetshiki,
(3) uti kulahleke i teaspoon ezimlpni, wati ke mayimke yakupela inyanga.
Yemka ke intombi yakupela inyanga leyo. Wati ngoku u Mr. Piers watumela
amapolisa ukuba ayibambe intombi ayifake entolongweni. Latetwa ityala,
akabiko unditnangele inkosikazi ka Mantyi, wesuka u Mr. Piers wangena
emkombeni endaweni yenkosi- kazi, watata isihlalo u Mr. Dorrington
isandla se Mantyi, wabuza ityala, u Mr. Piers wamisa ezindawo
zingasentla, wati utsho ke umkake.

Iti intombi ukupendula, Mr. Piers, wena nkosi akwazinto wawungeko. Wati
wena kum wakuva kusitiwa ndinetshiki, walwa, wati ndikohlakele,
ndinetshi- ki, ndifuza abafundisi bam base Wesile, wangxola.

Waligweba ityala u Mr. Dorrington engawevanga amazwi ka Mrs. Piers, wati
intombi mayirole £1 10 0, ukuba ayiko loinali Icawa ezintatu kwase
ntolongweni, wati inayibuyele ihlale inyanga yonke.

Wati u Nginda ku Mr. Piers ndirola enye intombi igqibe lenyanga, ngokuba
akuko luxolo pakati kwe nkosikazi no Agnes.—Wala u Mr. Piers.

Into esingayaziyo kukuba ligwetyelwe pezu kukabanina elityala ngu Mr.
Dorr- ington, ebengekonje u Mrs. Piers.

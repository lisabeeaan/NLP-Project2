elona bandla liliqubayo ilizwi, lelona lilungelwayo nakwinxaso zalenyama
nakwe zompefumlo. Njengokuba ibandla elise litsha kwi Lwandle
Ezisezantsi (Southern Seas) liliquba nje ilizwi e-New Guinea, kwaye
kubonakala okokuba liyanda kanye kulomsebenzikazi umkulu, maliti nalo
ibandlana lase South Africa lishumayele, likupe onyana nentombi baye
kushumayeza Amangoni angase ntshonalanga kwi Lake Nyassa; loti
lakwenjenjalo losikeleleka ngobomi, ekaya, ngohlobo elingazange
lilubone.

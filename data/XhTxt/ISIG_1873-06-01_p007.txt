JOHN MAHALEY.

Du Toit’s Pan, Diamond Fields.

Makowetu into yokuqala nditi hayi ukuba mna- ndi kwe Sigidimi, kuba
sifumana indaba zendawo ngendawo. Lendawo iyavuyisa ngokuba ibingento
sake sanayo, ili tamsanqa elikulu, esilipiwe Ngum- pi wento zonke.
Ukutsho ke nditi ayepina ama- dodana afundisiweyo lento angasitabatiyo
Isigidi­mi. Kuyinto ebukekayo kwabangafundanga maxa babona abafundileyo
belesesha. Boti nabo ba- nqwenele ukunga bangafundiswa, banqwenele
nokunga intsapo yabo bangayitumela esikolweni. Nditi kuni bafundi
niyakalazelwa ngabangafunda- nga. Bati imikwa yetu tina lusapo Iwemfundo
ayilungile; sikohlakele ngapezu kosapo lobudenge. Masiti ke ngoko
ekufundeni kwetu singa kulibali ukutandaza. Ndigqibela ngelokuti
masisitabate Isigidimi kakulu, sizekude sipume kabini ngenya- nga.

Imali esiyirolela sona asiyilahli, kanjalo asiyiyo eyokulilelwa kuba
incinane. Amanye amapepa ama Ngesi arolelwa £2 10s ngomnyaka. Ke ngoko
asiyiyo mali lento siyirolayo tina.

JOHN PHILIPS.

Port Elizabeth.

msani ukubuduzela Ilanga lipumile, kufanele ke ngoko ukuba sihambe kuhle
ekukanyeni, sipume emnyameni, siyeke ukutukana no kuzipakamisa. Siyeva

bazalwana nditi kunganina lento Isigidimi sezinyanga zintatu zidluleyo,
sifumana omnye esiti ndingu Mkristu, omnye esiti akunguye. Yintonina ke
leyo. Ngati kum kukubuduzela.

EMGWALI EBATENJINI.

Kute Emgwali kwafika U-Revd Davis umonga- meli wezizikolo zingelicala
linganeno kwenciba; kwabako intlanganiso ngo 17 ka April 1873 intla-
nganiso ebingalindelwe. Kwati kwa esandukufika kwenziwa umemo kubo bonke
abantu abazintloko zezi zikolo zipantsi ko Revd. P. Hargreaves kwati ke
bakuba befikile kwabako isikonzo etyalikeni kusasa. Sasipetwe ngu Revd.
Mr. Barrett umfundisi wase Gcuwa, sati sakuba sigqityiwe isikonzo wesuka
U-Revd. P. Hargreaves wazisa ebantwini ukuba kuyakubako intlanganiso
yabo bonke aba­ntu ngexesha leshumi linanye. Yabetwa ke intsimbi yangena
ngeloxesha, abati abantu bengena baye bengazi ukuba yintlanganiso
yentonina. Yati intlanganiso yanikwa emandleni emantyi yase Batenjini
U-Mr. Chalmers. Wesuka wema U-Revd P. Hargreaves kuqala waxela ukuba
bacinge bona elokuba kwakiwe I-Institution, kunjalo nje ke len­dawo
ndiyibekisa kuni ntlanganiso; watsho wati siyakulinda kumpati lo, wazake
wahlala pantsi. Wesuka U-Mr. Chalmers wati ndiyabulela ukuba ndibeko
pakati kwenu namhlanje wati, namleya- ndlu kungenwa kuyo ngabantwana,
ndiyayigxeka ayilungile kufuneka enye indlu entle; watsho wa- bonisa
uncedo Iwemfundo kumzimba nakumpefu- mlo; wati ke yena urola iponti
ezintlanu. Wacela ebantwini ukuba mabaze barole bafike kwelinani
alirolileyo. Wesuka wema U-Revd. Davis wati ndiyabulela kunene ukubona
abantu abaninzi nendlu entle kangaka eyakiweyo yimali yabo nayimali
yabanye abancedisi abamhlope, wati anditandabuzi ukuba nale yanamhlanje
niyakuyifeza, wa­tsho warola iponti ezintlanu. Wesuka U-Revd. P.
Hargreaves waxela utando Iwake kulomzi wa- kwa Hala, watsho wati,
kusoloko ndesuka pesheya E-England ndeza kulomzi, nokuba ndiya kwenye
indawo, intliziyo yam ilapa kulomzi wati urola ip­onti ezilishumi.
Wesuka U-Revd. E. Barrett umfundisi wase Gcuwa wawaleka amazwi okuncoma
imfudo ukulunga kwayo, wati abantwana zenibatumele esikolweni, onyana
benu ningabenzi ababexeshi benqwelo zenu. Ayepina ama Lawo, nabe Sutu
watsho warola iponti ezimbini. Andi- ngebi sacubacuba amazwi abo bonke,
kwakunye nemali yabo. Batete amazwi abo nabo amakulu ngokuvuma imfundo,
nokubulela lento yenziwanga bafundisi nokubakatalela kwabo kangaka,
njengo- kungati kuko nto bayakuyizuza kubo, kanti hayi akuko nto
bayakuyizuza. Barola kunene imali, endingasendiyidibanisa nditi ziponti
ezilikulu eli namashumi mabini anesihlanu. Abanye abantu abatembekileyo
bebengeko. Indlu le ke izakwakiwa kutiwa yopela ngamakulu amatatu
eponti. Akutandatyuzwa ukuba elonani liyakufezwa.

PAUL NKALA.

Indawo endifuna ukuteta yona makowetu yile yokuba ulwaluko olu lusisi
qalekiso pakati kwetu. Singayibona lonto ngamatuba amaninzi, anje
nangokukangeleka kwabakweta, okuhlala kundikii- mbuza umhlambo wesitunzi
sokufa owabonwa ngumhambi owaye siya kwisixeko sase Ziyone. Noko
ulwaluko olu lulisiko lohlanga ngesilucasa ngentliziyo yonke.

Elinye isiko engesisilwa nalo lelokulobola. Na- lo likwa sisi qalekiso,
esingavelisi nto ilungileyo. Abanye sebede bakuyeka ngenxa yomteto noku-
kanyiselwa yi Gospel. Kodwa abanye basamana ukuliquba ngamaqinga,
ngokuti bayacela, kube okukucela kwabo kubandezela nokuba umyeni aku­ko
nto anayo. Nalo ndawo masiyilumkele.

Kanjalo masikulumkele ukusela, noko kuko abati ezinto masingateti ngazo,
kanti ziyasigqiba. Nge- sivuya kakulu sakubona umntu eyeka ukulobola,
nokwaluka, notywala.

E-Free STATE kuko esinye isixakaniso esisand’ ukuhla. Nokumbula mhlaumbi
ukuba sasite kusand’ ukubako idabi pakati kwa Mabulu neqela la Basutu,
ekute kulo kwabulawa Abasutu ababini. Kekaloku I-Governor ite kuba abo
Basutu bengabantu ba Mangesi, yafuna ukuba zike zipengululwe izizatu
zokufa kwabo. Abapati mandla base Free State abavumanga ukukwenza oko,
bati isi- zatu sokuba bale, kukuba abo Basutu bebe twele imipu, into ke
leyo engavunyelwe kwelozwe. Ite kodwa I-Governor ayaneliswa yilompendulo
ke ngoku isatumele pesheya ukuya kubuza into engayenzayo.

INDLOVU EQELISWE EKAYA.—Indlovu yokuqala yezase South Africa ekulingwe
ukuba iqele ekaya selide yatamba. Abanye abaleseshi betu bayazi ukuba
E-Asia indlovu kututwa ngazo imitwalo enzima, nokuba kwi Africa
engasentla kwakusiliwa ngazo kudala. Kengoku enye iqeliswe ekaya E-
Zanzibar. Lidunana elihle, elikupakama k'usezi- nyawo ezintandatu ne
insh ezikwantandatu. Ubude bempondo busezinyawo ezimbini. Iselita- mbe
kangangokuba ikwelwe kalula kwizitilate zase Zanzibar. Iza kutunyelwa
E-Bombay, kuba ipiwe U-Sir P. E. Wodehouse, lo ube eyi Gover­nor yale
koloni. Ngoku uyi Governnor yesiqendu esitile somhlaba wase E-India.

IMINI YOKUZALWA KWE KUMKANIKAZI E- LOVEDALE.—Isiko lalomzi lelokuti
ngemini yoku­zalwa kwe Kumkanikazi, abafundi bapiwe iholide,
balungiselelwe nento zokuzigcobisa. Imini ezinjalo zidla ngokukunjulwa
naxa umntu selede wagoduka, ke singa bangati abake balapa, bahlale
beukumbula ngovuyo lomzi. Olona lusuku lokuzalwa kwe Kumkanikazi belungo
Mgqibelo, kwati ke kuba olo, lungelulo lusuku lungalungela ukuzigcobisa
kakulu, iholide yaseliba nge Friday. Kwakusasa ngalo mini kwayiwa
kwibala elihle ecaleni komlambo, kwadlalwa kunene ngabantu abatsha.
Ukuya kwabo kulondawo, bahamba befalile, be twele iHag, lidlala igubu.
Kwakukuninzi ukudla, Iwaza ke ulutsha Iwakupata ngemfanelo. Bekuko
kanjalo neqela labamhlope nabantsundu abebeze kubonela, bafika nabo
bangena emidlalweni. Ngokuhlwa kwadlalwa ibola zomlilo lada lalixesha
lokulala.

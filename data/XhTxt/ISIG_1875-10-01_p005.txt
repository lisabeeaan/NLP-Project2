ukuteta ngegama lika Yesu kubaheyideni.

Bazalwana abatandekayo nibe nifanele ukwazi ukuba iremente zalapa
elusutu zitumela kumacalana onke alapa, abaleseshi bezibalo abaya
kuvakalisa indaba ezilungileyo ze gospel. Kumacalana onke sibabona
abantu besiza ezinyaweni zika Yesu, ukuza kufuna kona ubom
obungunapakade. Kate ekuqutyweni ko baptizo ezikolweni zetu babaninzi
aboni abavuselelekileyo, esite ke samkelela eziklasini zetu abo ate
u-Yesu wabatsalela kuye. Umtandazo wentliziyo zetu ngowokuti wanga uMoya
wobomi angalifikelela lonke elilizwe, zide zidilikele pantsi indonga
zika Satana, onke amadolo azitobe pambi kuka Yesu.

Iremente zase Lusutu zicinga ukutumela uku- kanya kwe gospel kwizizwe
ezisese mnyameni. Zilungiselela ukutumela ku Banyayi, isizwe esi-
santywile ebunyameni betunzi lokufa, ukuze nawo ade akubone okukukanya
kungcwalisekileyo. Zihlobo ezitandekayo siyanibulela ngolo ncedo
enisitumele Iona. Siya temba ukuba yoti i-Nkosi, umnini msebenzi,
inizalise ngo Moya Oyingcwele, kuba itembise ukuti “ oncenceshelayo uya
kuncenceshelwa.” Siya vuya ukuva ukuba niza kubuya nisincede.

Ngokubekisele kwabaza kuya kuwupata lomse- benzi i-Nkosi iselite
yabalata, ke ngoku sitemba ukuba yoti ikulungise konke okumalunga
nalamadoda mane ayayo, ukuze awulungele lomsebenzi. Ize ningapezi
ukuwatandazela, ukuze niti nakuva ukuba aBanyayi enibe nizikanyezele
wona, azinikele ku Yesu, nibe nokuvuma ingoma zovuyo kunye nati,
simdumise lowo owabeka pantsi ubomi bake ngenxa yetu sonke.

Bazalwana, ukuba sibe sinamapiko njengentaka ngesike silinge ukuza
kunibona sitete nani. Kodwa ke nakuba singenakubonana, masizingise
emtandazweni, kuba ngalondlela sinokuhlangana. Yobuya ibeko indawo esoba
nokubonana kuyo.

Yamkelani umbuliso weremente zase Lusutu otunyelwa ngomlomo weyase

Rabula Mission Station, September 9, 1875.

  SIIILOBO sika Vazidlule, asikuko nokuba amazwi
  ako ngotywala, andihlabile ngenxa yobunyaniso
  bawo. Yabona! lonto injalo isandukuhla nati
  sisekonje kwelakwa Tato. Kute ukuya kuka Sa-
  rili ngoku babelwile lasila kunene lonke elo lakwa
  Tato, saye isaci salonto kusitiwa kusindlekwa
  umyeni lowo, ungu Sarili. Kwabanjanike yabuya
  ngokusenga into ka Hintsa, kwaye kulityelwe ngu
  lo rwaqelelo uteta ngaye. Kodwake utinina ngo
  Nrwele lo utshitshise yonke into. Yinkohla kalo-
  kunje nokushumayela kumaqaba, kuba umntu
  usagegezela nanamhla ngo Nrwele. Utinina lento
  ngati ucumise ujongwa lipela yedwa kantike wena
  usitinje Amamfengu acitwa butywala akunjalo,
  ade afika kwelizwe nati sada savela ingeko lento
  kuyiyo ngoku. Ufudula uke uvena kukalazwa
  into leminyaka mininzi kangaka seleko Amamfe-
  ngu nobutywala, afika nawo buko kade kwa Palo
  apa?

  Kuseloko kwagaleleka u-Nrwele kutshabalala
  izizwe. Lipina Ilawo? uyayibonana lento ange-
  ne kuyo ngoku um Xosa, uyehla ubeka ezantsi
  ngenxa yotywala bomlungu, asinguye ujongwali-
  pela ke lo ucitacitayo, ukupa lemipefumlo ingama-
  waka ngemini kumazwe ngamazwe, ngu Norwele.
  Amamfengu wona acitwa Yimfecane le ide yanqa-
  ndwa ngumlungu Emampondweni apa. Sazi nja-
  lo tina. Nditi mna kawubekise kumntu ongum-
  nini mhlaba u-Rulumente ngecala lezinkantini
  zigqibe lonke elilizwe, zihamba zitiyela lemipefu-
  mlo yezidenge, ukuba yena esuke abanike imvu-
  melo nje ababantu ayamkele imali abayirolela
  ukukupa nokutshabalalisa lemipefumlo ingaka,
  yena usebenzela lipina icala; kuba sikalanje tina
  ngento esizibonayo kwa nesizivayo ezimbi ngenxa
  yalomtshabalalisi ute tyindyilili, akakatali nento
  le nokuba kwenzeka ntonina kwabanye abantu,
  nokuba umsebenzi wo Mdali wake uyonakala,
  nokuba imipefumlo ipuma yonke lemihla ngendle-
  la zonke isiya kummangalela, akatini, uqonde
  inani lake lodwa lemali alingenisayo kwayekwa.

  Xanditshoyoke elokugqiba nditi pulapulani ka-
  mbe ma Palokazi nditsho kuni bono Wonda, nono
  Tonto nono Xina, unyanisile umhlobo wetu lo.
  Woba udlelwe yinike namhla. Pulapulani kambe
  Bambokazi nani Bolamtolo, Bomakoza, Bomnshe-
  le kuba wem’umntu ekuqanduleni ilitye akasava-
  kali uno Qandulilanga wobona kupela intokazi ka
  nantsi, ikwele etyeni izwe nezolo ukusindleka
  indoda yayo ngati uti ndoyika ukuti ndakuyeka
  kungabisatunywa ngakwa Tandokazi. Au! selu-
  ndixolela Mhleli nkosi yam ndive sendindabele
  ngenxa yokubona oq'aq'asibonele butywala.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1875.

Nditi zonke ezindawo nizifungeleyo zazifungelwe kwase kuqaleni nanamhla
zisafungelwa. Bahlobo bam niyazi ukuba into enkulu endodeni yinkazana.
Ukuba kuko umzalwana oti unyana wake makangaluki makafungele intombi
ukuba uyakuyinika umntu ongalukanga. Izeniti intombi ukuba isapule
isifungo soyise uyise angayitshatisi, ahgayiniki nto ngokusuka itandane
nomntu owalukileyo. Nditi mna kukona ayakuti umzali angatsho ukuti
kwedini, kongalukanga, nomzalikazi, kuba besazi ukuba boti bakuti
kwedini intombi zabo aziyi kwenda. Nentombi aziyikutsho ukuti kwedini
kuba zingayi kwenda zakutsho. Bonke abantu abayi kutsho ukuti kwedini xa
amakristu ezahlule azifungela intombi zawo. Abafuna ukuhambela pambili
mabahluke ngalo- ndawo.

BOOY KWINANA.

E-Natal, May 1, 1875.

Kuko umfo otile oxonywe ngo 1. ka May 1875. Kekaloku wabengunyana
womnumzana. Waenabafazi ababini. Ute ukuze abulale abantu umfazi wake
wamana ukuti ndipupe unantsi, emtsho ngegama lake. Ite indoda yake
yakuva oko, yati yo, kanti lendoda isele imtakatile umfazi wam ngomti,
kunganina amane ukupupa, imihla yonke ; wasele emzonda ngakumbi efuna
ukumbulala. Kute ngomnye umbla lendoda ibimana ukuputshwa ngumfazi
yatabata izembe ukuya kugaula kunye nomfana. Lendoda ibingumnini mfazi
yatabata isibam yalandela yafumana begaula yasel’ ibadubula ngesibam,
yabashiya yagoduka. Kute ngokuhlwa yabiza umfana wakowabo babopa
amahashe ukuya kubalahla ebusuku apo kungeko mntu. Bate bafunwa abobantu
abafunyanwa noko. Kute ukuze kubonakale kwabizwa ama polisa ukaza
kubamba amadoda esixeko eso. Kekaloku ate abanjwa onke. Ate akufika
ematyaleni alandula onke ngamanye ; kwati kamva kwabanjwa abafana bonke.
Kute kwakwenjiwa njalo wasele danduluka umfana ngelokuti, “Au”
sesibanjwanje singabulalanga mntu kutenina, ati amapolisa nguwupinake
obuleleyo, lomntu. Waza umfana waseleyihlakaza yonke lento, wati hambani
ndiye kunikombisa apo akona lomntu ofileyo. Amlandela amapolisa.
Bafunyanwa bese- bengimatambo omileyo, kanti yena obuleleyo, ababantu
usahambile. Ute nxa asele ebuya asel’ embamba amapolisa.

OWAKONA.

Cradock, June 14, 1875.

Ndipendula wena Mr Philip Qawe, nditi kuwe, cokisani ukuyifunda lento
yokulobola ukuze siqonde nati. Niti nina umntu uyatengwa njenge batyi,
nditi mna ungati ukuba utenge ibatyi yeponti ezintatu ubuye uyitengise
komnye umntu. Ndisitsho nje ke nditi kuwe ungati ukuba umfazi umlobole
ungabuye umendisele na kwenye indoda? ngokuba kaloku uti wena umntu
uyatengwa njenge batyi. Ungati ukuba uti manditengise komnye umntu ubone
selekowabo ku yise. Into etengiweyo ingatinina ukubuyela apo itengwe
kona? Umntu upa uyise nje kodwa.

EZOMTSHATO—Kwafika apa umntu evela ema- bulwini wafika ematyaleni esiti
uputuma umntwa- na wake ubaleke ne Lawu lizokutshata naye li-
ngamcelanga kum, andifuni ukuba atshate nalo. Ndisendixelelwa libulu
ukuba umntwana wako uyokutshata ne Lawu kuba ubengahlali kweli Bulu
ndihlala kulo mna ; kungoko ndilapanje, ndiputuma lomkosi: watsholomfo
wafika kodwa intombi ingekatshati, waya kumfundisi wati, mfundisi
andifuni ukuba atshate kuba ukuba utshatile iyakwenza lonto ukuba
abantwana badele abazali kuba bayabona ukuba bayavunyelwa bengekabi
nabudala babo, umfundisi wamtshatisa noko atshoyo uyise womntwana; wemka
uyise esongela kunene. P. J- PAKATI.

E-Bolotwa, June 29, 1875.

le Nkosi na- ngokwakiwa kwetyalike. Kusasa kwangena umte- ndeleko we

zokuncedisa izakiwo ezo. Abafundisi ababeko ngu Archdeacon Waters no
Rev. C. F. Patten no Rev. A. J. Newton. U- Rev. A. J. Newton washumayela
ngexesha lomtendeleko kamnandi kwati ngokuhlwa inkonzo yenziwa ngokwesi
Ngesi, U-Archdeacon Waters wa- shumayela kabukali kulamazwi, Omsindisi
ati, “ Ungu Petros, wena, ndandiyakulakela pezukweliwa ibandla lam
namasango ehadesi akayi kuloyisa.” Mateyu xvi. 18.

KUBAZALI BENTSAPO EZISE LOVE-DALE

  ABAZALI bentsapo ezilapa baya ku- njuzwa ukuba imali erolelwa uku-

funda nokuxaswa kwazo bayirole kwa isangena iseshoni. Umteto wokuba
oyirole kade, inyuke ngale ingeueyo iseshoni uya kuqina. Oya kuyirola
emva kuka August, womelwa ziponti ezintatu ngeseshoni endaweni yezimbini
ezine- shumi. Bonke abakukumbulelayo ukunyuka kwezinto, bokubona
ukufaneleka kwalo mteto, ngokukodwa bakuva ukuba imali eselityala
ngakubazali, yezidluleyo, ingapezu kwamakulu amatatu eponti.

engu Matomela ibe icele ukuba yenzelwe indlu
yetyalike. Ite iremente yakona yakupa £450
ngamini nye iyirolela ukuba kwakiwe londlu.
Kanjalo abantsundu balomacala babaxasa ngo-
kwabo abafundisi babo.

UMZEKELO OMHLE—U-Makololo, inkosi enkulu
yama Baralong isand’ ukumisa lomteto obufanele
ukubonelwa nazezinye inkosi. Uti:—“ Ekubeni
ukutengiswa kotywala kubantu bam, kungenisa
intshabalalo yabo mna ndiyamisa ngemvumelo
yamapakati am ukuba osukuba etengisa utywala
kulomhlaba wam, uyakudliwa imali engengapa-
ntsi kweponti, ingabi ngapezulu kwamashumi
amahlanu eponti, esiyakuti isiqingata sayo silu-
nge nomntu ohlakaze obetengisa.”

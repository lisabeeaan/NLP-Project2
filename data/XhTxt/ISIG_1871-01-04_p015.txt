ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 4, 1871.

15

Uti kodwa U-Bismarck, igosa lase Prussia, akuko ndawo kuvunyelwene ngayo
ne Russia ngokusingisele kulomnqopiso.

Umkosi omkulu wama French uwawele ama Prussia abese Orleans, wawakupa
kulomzi. Abuleweyo natinjiweyo kuma Prussia ngama waka alishumi. Lomkosi
usinga E-Paris ukuya kunceda esosixeko. Kutiwa sekunzima kuma German
ukuxasa imikosi yawo erawule esosixeko. I-Paris ayikadilizwa ngekanunu.

Ezinye indaba ezize nalomkombe zezinge diamond. Baninzi E-England
abasebe lungiselela idiamond. Omnye umkombe ekutiwa yi Westen Hope
sewufikile nabanye E-Cape Town. Kuko amare okuba lomkombe uqekeke xa uya
E-Natal.

I-Governor (iruluneli) engu Sir Henry Barkly, esesikundleni sale ibiko,
ifike nayo I-Norseman.

U-Dr. Livingstone, lomfondisi okade ehamba kwezizizwe ezisesazulwini se
Africa, apo kungazange kufike mlungu, seleneminyaka engaziwa apo akona,
ngoku ulindelwe imihla yonke ukuba afike E-Zanzibar.

KUBALESESHI ABANTSUNDU.

SIYAZISA kubaleseshi abantsundu ukuba soti siyishiyele indawo imitshato,
nokuzalwa kwabantwana, nokububa kwabantu, sibe silandela isiko lawa
Mangesi amapepa endaba. Abalitatayo elipepa bozifaka in- dawo ezinjalo
ngapandle kwe mali kunyaka wokuqala. Abanga litatiyo bobizwa isheleni
nge ntshumayezo inye abafuna ukuyi faka.

UKUNGENA KWESIKOLO SASE LOVE­DALE.—Esisikolo songena ngolwesibini
ngosuku Iwama 31 ku January. Bonke abazali bolutsha oluya kungeniswa,
nolwabo seluko kade, nokuba ngabamakwenkwe, nokuba ngabentombi, baya
menywa ukuba babeko entlanganisweni eya kwenziwa E-Lovedale apa. Yobako
ngexa leshumi elinesibini, emini enkulu. Abaza kungeniswa boqala ukuviwa
ngexa leshumi, emva kuhle kwexa lesitonga.

KWESE NTOMBI.—Kunqwenelwa ukunga kungabako kwesi sikolo, intombi ezi
funda imisebenzi enje nge yasendlwini, neyo kuhlamba, nokutrekisha,
nokusika nokutunga ingubo. Zoti ezo zifunda lemisebenzi zifundiswe
incwadi ngokuhlwa. Kunga kungaqalwa ngeshumi, zize zihlale iminyaka
emibini. Ngonyaka wokuqala ziya kuzuza isheleni zone ngenyanga. Nezi
zikoyo ziyayi funda lemisebenzi ibaliweyo, kodwa zifundiswa incwadi
ikakulu. Kufuneka ngoku eziya kuxatisa emsebenzini ngokukodwa.

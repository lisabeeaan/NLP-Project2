bangazifumana na inkomo. Bahlole ba- buya bengenzanga nto, bafike bawa

Inyam' amakwenkwe.

Kuko isifo sodakada (spleen) esite kuba sisoyikeka kakulu, sade
sabuhlonitshwa ukutiwa pa ngegama, kwangati soti sakwenjiwa njalo,
sibahle kakulu abantu ngakumbi. Lubende elona gama laso. Elike noko
asiqalo Iona, kukukwekwa nje kodwa okunje ngokuti umntu akuhliwa sisifo
esincolileyo kutiwe ukufihlwa, “Udliwengumlambo,” une Hashe elingwevu,
kube ukwenjiwa njalo kupungulwa ububi, nobuhlazo baso kuba kusaziwa
ukuba namhla lomntu, nokuba ngulomzi uyakuba nobunqambi nobu ncekevu
obutile kwabanye abantu. Baninzi ngoko abade benzakala baya kutshona
emancwabeni ngeso sifo sibi kunene kulityelwe kutiwa—Badliwe ngumlambo.

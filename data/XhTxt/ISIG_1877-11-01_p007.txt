ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1877

INDABA ZAPESHEYA KOLWANDLE.

ABANTU abakoliweyo e-Madagascar bangamawaka angamashumi asixenxe
(70,000).—Chrittian Week.

U-STANLEY uye kuyi puma kwicala lase ntshonalanga i-Africa, wafumana
ukuba lomlambo bati yi Lualaba mnye ne Congo.

E-CHINA kuya vuyisa kakulu ukuhamba komsebenzi we Nkosi. Omnye
wabafundisi bakona uti, “Ayasi vuyisa amatuba esiwafumanayo
okushumayela. Ezinye indawo zitumela izicelo zokuba masize
kuzishumayeza. Apo sihamba kona kuhlanganisana into eninzi yabaza
kupulapula, abati abanye bayamkele inyaniso.”

UCINGO oluvela e-Aden lubika ukwapuka komkombe ngase Guardafui. Lute
uqekeke okokuba ungabi sabanto. Abafundisi be London Missionary Society
bayazisa ngosizi ukuba kulomkombe bekuko abafundisi ababini nentsapo
yabo abebevela e- Madagascar betyelela e-England. U-Rev. G. Beveri­dge
no Mrs. Beveridge bafe kunye nonyana wabo nentombi. Omnye umfundisi
u-Rev. T. Rogers usinde ngokungummangaliso kunye nomntwa omnye wake,
nomnye ka Mr Beveridge. Kuleyo ka Mr Rogers indlu kufe inkosikazi nomnye
umntwana wabo nesicakakazi ebesihlala nomntwana.—Christian Week ELINYE
lamapepa apesheya liti e-Germany zininzi ngoku indawo ezinamahobe
okutwala incwadi. Uncedo Iwawo Iwabonakala ikakulu kwimfazwe eyayi
pakati kwe Germany ne France nge 1870. Bati abantu base Paris
bakuvalelwa, basebe man’ukutuma amahobe ukuya kubika kwizihlobo
ezingapandle komzi into abayiyo. Ke ngoku kulungiselelwa ukuze abe
nokutunywa lula xa kufunekayo. Kuko izindlu zawo kuyo yonke imizi
emikulu yase Germany. Ukuqeliswa aman’ ukutunywa izinto nangoku. Ku-
tiwa aselekwazi kanye ukupata incwadi afanelana esiya ndawonye
ahlukahlukane nokuba asuke xesha nye,—liti elinye lipapele pezulu amanye
abekude emacaleni. Akusuka apo atunywa kona akabuye ahilize endleleni.
Nokuba abona amanye akaze awakangela nokuwakangela. Maxa wambi aye
ayokufika apo aya kona seledinwe ngangokuba angabi nako nokudla. Xa
agqatswayo kutiwa ashiya amanye ngafukamayo, nasand’ ukuqandusela.

UBUKRISTU KWIZIQITI ZE PACIFIC.—U-Rev. Dr. Nesbit, owaba neminyaka
ekumashumi amatatu engumfundisi kwiziqiti ze Pacific, pambi kokububa
kwake, ute ezo ziqiti zazipantsi kobuhedeni kwiminyaka emashumi
matandatu edlulileyo kanti ngoku zinto yimbi. Namhla abantu bazo
abalivumileyo ilizwi ba 400,000. Kwezinye iziqiti bekumi izidla bantu
namhla ungebi sasicola. Eziziqiti kutiwa zezama Samoa abantu abakuzo
35,000. Abafundisi abamhlope baneminyaka emashumi matatu bekuzo. Ukufika
kwabo kona abemi bazo babe ngabahedeni, into ezingazi nto,
ezingenazincwadi. Namhla abantu bakona. balivume bonke Ilizwi. Akuko
mzar.a ungenasikolo. Ityalike bayihamba nge Sabata napakati kweveki.
Sebenayo incwadi ye Zibalo ngeyabo inteto, kwanezinye incwadi eziteta
ngelizwi lika Tixo. Barola £1000 ngomnyaka ukuba i-Gospel isiwe kubantu
abangekayiva. Banesikolo esikulu esikupa amadodana akumashumi amabini
aya kwi shumayela naya kufundisa. Lomadodana aya ku— ziqiti ze Pacific
ezingekafikelelwa kakuhle ngabafundisi. Imfundiso iyasikeleleka
kakulu.—Missionary Magazine.

u-Griffith ubeseleye wafika kufupi nehlati lika Manube. E-Ncazana
ufumene iqela elipetwe ngu Hook libe like lahlangana notshaba nge Sabata
ngomhla 21 ka October. Ama Gcaleka alinge ukubaraula noko agxotwa.
ashiya amadoda amashumi asixenxe ngase mva. Inani lengxwelera alaziwa.
Kwelama Ngesi icala kufe abamhlope ababini abebe- zalana, into zika Goss
ebezipete ama Mfengu. Kuma Mfengu kufe asitoba ingxwelera zamashumi
mabini. Ezinye kubonakala ukuba aziyi kupila. Kungxwele- rwe wamnye
omhlope. Kutinjwe inkomo 150, amahashe 50, negusha nebokwe eziliwaka.

Kuluvuyo ukubona enikwa knmacala onke ama- nani abenzakeleyo.

INTSHUMAYELO

Inkosi engu Sa-Rili akaqali ukuba anga yipulapule imiyalelo ka Rulumente
ati ke- ngoko azise imbandezelo pezu kwake kunye nabantu bake ama
Gcaleka. U-Rulumente kufuti emxolela kunye nabantu bake, emveni kwemigu
emininzi wabekwa endaweni eli- ngene intswelo zake neza bantu bake, apo
bebekululekile kuzo zonke inkatazo, noku pazanyiswa zintshaba nokuba
zezalupina uhlobo ; apo abantu ngebehleli ngokonwaba, wati no Sarili
wayiquba imihla yake ngoxolo.

Kodwa u-Sarili ebengakolwa, ecela futi ku Rulumente ukuba, atabate
imihlaba ya- bamelwana bake, esalelwa futi exelelwa ngo- kusekuhleni
ukuba makahlale ngoxolo naba- melwane bake, wamana ukugati uyavuma.

Ngexesha lenyanga ezintatu ezidhluleyo isipitipitana sahlisa ukulwa
pakati kwa Ma- Gcaleka nama Mfengu. Yaye iyintwana engeyagqitywa nge
ntlawulo. Kodwa u-Sarili umhlayimbi waeswele amandla, okubenza bagcine
uxolo abantu bake, gexesha ebekusa pendlwa inyaniso zi nkosi yesizatu
soko kuba kuliwe.

I-Ruluneli yaya emdeni ukuba izivele ngo kwayo imbangi yeso sipitipiti,
nokubayala ukuba bangalu pindi uhlobo olunjalo.

Yatingwatya kude kufupi nomzi ka Sarili, yababona bonke ababezile
ukumbona. U-Sa-Rili waziswa ngo kusekuhleni yimantyi u-Colonel Eustace,
indawo ehanjelwe yi Rulumente ete ke ngokwake waxela into etunywe yona
yi Ruluneli ku Sarili. Aka- myekanga ukuba aziqondele ngokwake wa-
mazisanangezihlobo nangamapakati ake, abe- siza kubona u-Ruluneli, kodwa
u-Sarili ngo- kwake akezanga, engatumanga nomyalezo kwi Ruluneli.

Into yokugqibela ukuze u-Sarili angabi nandlela yokusitela ngelokuti
waengaqondi into, awayeyiziselwe ngu Rulumente. Yabala yanikela imintyi
yonke into eyayimfunela yona i-Ruluneli.

U-Sa-Rili watumela nge mantyi yake u- Colonel Eustace, ukubulela amazwi
e-Rulu- neli, aka linga noko ukwenza ngotando Iuka Ruluneli, ukuxolisa
umhlaba.

I-Ruluneli isendleleni yeva ukuba umhlaba wake wangenelwa lutshaba,
nokupangwa, nokubulawa kwabantu bake, yimikosi yama Gcaleka.

U-Colonel Eustace isandla sake saqala ukuteta no Sarili embonisa,
wapendula ngelokuti akana kuba laula abantu bake, abangevayo ; akabonisa
ne migudu yokunce- disa u-Rulumente ukudambisa isipitipiti.

Okukumbi u-Colonel Eustace wayixelela inkosi ukuba ayi nakuba pata
abantu bayo. Imantyi yakwa Rulumente ayi nakuhlala

naye, u-Colonel Eustace wazikupa wati na- bantu bonke abaka Rulumente
maba kwelele e.Ofisini.

U-Sarili selete woyisiwe ngabantu bake, nokunqanda imisebenzi yabo emibi
abayipi- kele ukuyenza, Inkosi u-Griffith sekutiwa kuye mayingene
emhlabeni ka Sarili, ukuba atobe ngamandla, ukuba kuya bonakala, konke
akubonayo okucasene nokuteta kuka Rulumente, nokoniwa kwabantu bake aku-
lumkele, nenani limiswe nguye, elingahlaulwa gama-Gcaleka ukuhlaulela
abantu baka Ru- lumente abo niweyo, u-Sarili waebekwe engukumkani wama
Gcaleka, wa egciniwe ngokukulu zizipati zakwa Rulumenti, ukuba naye
akwazi ukupata abake abantu ngamasiko abo agcine ukupila kwabo,
nokuhlala ngo xolo nabamelwana babo.

Upulukiwe seso sipato ebepetwe ngaso ge xesha elingapambili, go fefe
Iuka Rulumenti ngo Kukutshwa kwobo bukosi no bu kulu boku pata, bonke
abantu ama-Gcaleka nabanye bayalwa belunyukiswa ngokukulu ukuba banga
mnyamekeli u Sarili njengati useyinkosi. Umhlaba wake utatyatiwe kuye,
iqinga lawo lobonwa ngu Rulemente nelizwi eliya kutetwa ngamapakati
E-Nkosikazi u-Victoria. Nenkosi ziyakudalwa ngu-Rulumente ezoku pata;
inkosi u- Griffith kutiwe kuyo makaye kuma umhlaba obufudula upetwe ngu
Sarili, alungisele ukuba namhla uzakuditywa nomhlaba wase-Koloni we
nkosikazi yapesheya kwolwandle; abantu bonke baka-Rulumente
abangampulapuliyo bayako hlwaywa ngokukulu.

Gomteto we Rulumente womhlaba wonke,
JOHN X. MERRIMAN,

Isandla sa.Kwomkulu.

ISAHLUKO SOKUQALA.

Ukuba kuko umxosa nokuba ngomnye umntu ohlala kule Coloni nokuba asikuyo
oyakuti kule Coloni anikele umyalezo Ku- mntu wale Coloni, nokuba
sisicelo esiyaku- banga ukuba ati lomxosa nokuba ngulomntu uwuvileyo
lomyalezo nesosicelo simbange ukuba ayishiye i-Koloni aye ngapaya komda,
ahambe eya kuhlangana nenkosi zaba ntsu- ndu, ukuya kuyinceda nokuya
Ezintlanga- nisweni zayo nokuba yeyasipina isizwe, lomxosa nokuba
nguwupina umntu ohambisa esosicelo nalomyalezo uyakuti ebanjiwe afakwe
etolongweni asebenze iminyaka emi- bini. Kengoko umntu ovela ngapaya
Komda wale Koloni engumxosa nokuba nguwupina umntu otunywe umcimbi
makabe nencwadi ayinikwe ngumhlekazi i-Ruluneli yelilizwe nokuba
ngumpati micimbi omkulu.

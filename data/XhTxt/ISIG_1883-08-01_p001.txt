NGOLWESITATU, AUGUST 1, 1883.

AMAKAYA AMA KRESTU.

UKUHLANJWA kwamakaya ama-Krestu kwaba yeyona nto afuna u Miss WATERSTON
ukuyiqononondisa kumalungu engqungqutela yaba Fundisi eyayise-Qonce
ekuqaleni kwenyanga efileyo. Kule ngxelo ipambi kwetu sifumana ukuba
wati egqiba ukulesa ipepa lake u Miss WATERSTON waselupuma wonke
umhlambi usinga, isininzi sawo, kwintlanganiso zama bandla abati
zi-Presbyteries. Akubanga ko ngxoxo pezu komcimbi ofaneleke
njengalo—into leyo ke iludanisile uninzi.

Kwipepa lake u Miss WATERSTON uqale ngokucitela komoya bobane inteto
eyandileyo kwabamhlope yokuba izicaka ezingamaqaba zigqitisele kwesazeke
zivela ezilalini zabantu abagqobokileyo; nezokuba igospel ifike yabenza
bonakala ngapezulu abantu abamnyama. Ubalise ngezinto azibone ngawake
ameblo e-Nyassa ngezicaka awayenazo zabantu abangazani negospel nangezo
anazo kweli eseziyamkele, ngokwazi kwake inteto enjalo yingcapukiso
yompefumlo. Ungene kanjako ekuboniseni ukungabi nyaniso kwenteto yokuba
intombi zamaqaba zinezimilo ngapezulu kwezase skolweni. Ute ekubeni yena
eligqira unawo amatuba amaninzi okubuvavanya ubunya- niso bezinto
esazeke zinjengezo. Pakati kwezinto emazi- gxotiswe emakayeni ama-Krestu
ubale indywala, ezingena- sabelo pakati kwendlela yokusinga pambili.
Amavumba emiqomboti endlwini yoti uzama intlalo yobu-Krestu
akanqwenelekile. Kwintlalo enjenge yasemaqabeni—ukwa. ka izindlu
ezingena mfanelo zomntu okanyiselweyo ubouisa ukwanda kwesifo, ngakumbi
kwintsana ezifumana into eninzi yemikuhlane. Wafumana yena e Nyassa into
eyandileyo ingamancwaba abantwana, nakweli uyakolwa ukuba kukwanjalo.
Umntwana yena osazeke ezelwe ebulwelwe akanakuba natuba lakupila efutwa,
edliswa amasi esemncinane, kungeko nkatalo inkulu yokumgcina efudumele.
Ubalise into eninzi yezifo ati abenetemba ngazo xa zikumakolwa ze ancame
xa zikwaba bomvu, ize kanti kuya kuba njalo kanye. Ngenyaniso ezininzi
ezinje ngezi u Miss WATERSTON ubonise ukuba okwesake isiqu, ukuba
akabonisanga ngangokude baqonde nabanye, ubu- Krestu abuzelanga
ukutshabalalisa omnyama njengoko kusitshiwo.

Akukova ukubonisa ngezindawo uze kowona mongo wepepa lake wati pakati
kwemviwo zemfundo epakamileyo nenqugwala kuko ilengalenga elikulu.
Umcimbi wepepa lake kukupakamisa elicala lingezantsi ukuba lize kweli
lingasentla. Ukalaze ukuba eyona mfundo yabamnyama ayihambi kunye
nemfundo enikwayo, kuba yena akavumi ukuba imfundo kukwazi ozi kemistri,
nozi fiziks, ne latine, ne lojik nama Grik, ingengakuba yena uzidelile
ezonto, kuba nge engeko kwindawo awayemi kuyo. Indoda nenkazana
efundileyo ingahlala engqukuveni okwexeshana kanti asikaya izimisele
ukuhlala kulo elo. Into yokuqala eyakumpakamisa umntu omnyama luxande
olunamagumbi alo. Ute gqaba-gqaba ezona nto ingena kwamkeleka ngenxa
yazo indlu engqukuva. Imisi yongqu-pantsi, engapumi ndawo ayinakuba
ingabangi izifo ezinjengozi konsamshoni. Kuko nqugwalana itile
engqondweni yake

angavuyayo eyibona isemalangatyeni ngenxa kanye yeso sizatu.
Ngamanqugwala la kanye abambe ukuba abantu abamnyama banganyuki. Luxande
oluneziko eliwukupayo umsi inyatelo lokuqala. U Miss WATERSTON ute bambi
bangati imali yokwenza ezi zindlu lyakuvela pina, ukubapendula ute imali
yalemitshato indleko zinkulu ivela pina? Imali yokwenza izidlo
zemitshato nengubo ezinqabileyo zemihla yemitshato ingacitwa
ngokugqitiselekileyo pezu kokuhlambulula ikaya. Yena wake wabona
umtshakazi kunye nomsakwabo benxiba ndlwininye, omnye watsho ngesilika
emhlope, omnye ngeluhlaza, kanti ke babepuma endlwini engqukuva. Pesheya
abantu abasweleyo ba- bengaxolayo betshatiswe ngengubo ezingenazo
indleko. Kulutsha kunqweneleka umoya oqondayo, kunqweneleka ukuba
lubambe itambo lokucoca amakaya nentlalo kuyekwe ingxam namaxolo ezidlo
zangapandle ezinendleko. Ukuba amakaya abakolwayo amele ukuba yityuwa
yehlabati masibe nezindlu zama-Krestu tanci.

Ngapandle kokucocwa kwezindlu into emayikangelwe ngamasiko abantu.
Amacuba angavuya eyekiwe. Imali engeba ihlaulela imfundo yentsapo
ipelela ecubeni. Enye into icuba eli eliqonda yena alibangi impilo
entle, lendawo kodwa usazama ukuyinq'ina. Elinye isiko kukucitela imali
ezingutyeni endaweni yokuba ilondolozwe e-Bankini. Owake umnqweno
ngowokuba i Banki zibeko kwizikolwana zonke, ati onesicaka asifundise
ukuhambisa kwe Banki. Esinye isiqelo angatanda sipelile kukunganoneleli
ukuzigcina engqeleni, ngokucuia ubusuku bonke, ukunxiba inqubo
ezimbopayo umntu ezinjengezitile ezinxitywa zintombi: ukuti izityudeni
ezimnyama zitande ukuhlala zizole tu singasebenzi nokuba kuhambe igazi.
Enye into emayikangelwe yile yemidlalo, nentlangano zemihlali. Ibinga
ezizinto zingangeniswa nakwabamnyama. “ Ogqups,” ne “Ion tennis,”
bawalungele amantombazana. Ukuba iramente ayizipapamele ezizinto ingwevu
enkulu u Satana lyaku- zinonelela yona, kuba ayilele. Ute umzekelo
wabamhlope ngokwabo maube ngofanelekileyo.

Eyetu imvo yeyokuba ezi zinto zonke zitetwa ngu Miss WATERSTON
zayekelelwa, zisayekelelwe, ngabafundisi. Liko ixesha awabe ngati ete
umfundisi, kwikolwa elimnyama. Wanantsi, tabata umtsi utsibele paya
enyangeni, ikolwa elimnyama lingayeki lingenzanga mizamo. Zonke ezi
zinto zimi endleleni yokucoceka kwamakaya ama Krestu namhla zizinto
ababengati bete mazenziwe abafundisi, ibe ngezenziwa kwakudala. Into
ebange oku kuhleleleka kungaka yile yokuba umfundisi atonyalaliswe
kukushumayela nge Cawa ze ngom-Vulo nangezinye intsuku zeveki axoliswe
Kukuhlala estedini sake ezibenca eyake intliziyo, angabafundisi abantu
izinto zentlalo.

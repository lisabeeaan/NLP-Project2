ICAWA E-TUNXE.

UMZEKELISO WONYANA OWALAHLA IKOWABO. (Ukucazwa komzekeliso we Nkosi yetu
ngu Bev John A. Chalmers).

Kekaloku wati, (U-Yesu) Umntu otile ubenonyana ababini, wati omci wabo
ku yise, Bawo ndinike isahlulo sempahla esilunge nam, waza wababela
impilo leyo. Luka xv. 11. 12.

Intaba azilingani zonke, ziko ezibeta le kwezinye ngobupakamo, ngobude
bazo, ezibonakala kude ekulihambeni kwako ilizwe, eliti capa kuzo ilanga
kwakusa, eligqibelela encotsheni yazo ukukanya xa litshonayo; eye Gqira
ye- njalo, eyakwa Xaxazele yenjalo, eye Gwali yekwanjalo, kweli letu
ilizwe.

Ukwanjalo ke nalomzekeliso watetwayo yi Nkosi yetu, uwodwa, wahlukile
kweminye, indawo zawo azinakufikelelwa mntu, zipakamile, zabanzi,
zanzulu, kunjalonje akuko mntu owoza azifince, note wake walinga
ukuzityila wovakala esiti, Andizigqibanga ndifumane ndanxwema ngapandle
andangena. Okukona uwu- leseshayo kokukona ungadinwayo nguwo, okukona
ucazwayo kokukona buvakalayo, butyilekayo ubumnandi bawo ; uyincasa
kancasa kowutandayo, ude uvakale usiti xa uzipindapindayo ukuzipulapula
inteto zawo “Akuzanga kutete mntu njengoko atete ngako lomntu.”

Kwakutenina ukuze Inkosi yetu iwutete? Wayecukunyiswe yindawo nina ukuze
awabeke lamazwi pambi kwabapulapuli bake? Ubeyawakalazelwa.
Bebeyawankuntuza abafarisi nababali besiti, wamkela aboni adle nabp.
Bebeyawa abawuqondi umcimbi abeze ngawo u-Yesu emhlabeni. Bebeyawati,
wenza nto nina lomntu lento apikelene nokubuta nezityakala lemihla,
ufuna ntonina kuzo, uzitande

nganina, usukelana nazo nganina. Una- ntonina lento angazani nati, tina
malu- ngisa tina zazi, tina bohlanga, kutenina lento afuna ubudlelane
bemiginwa siko nje tina manono. Esiti nje ulilungisa uzingenela ntonina
izindlu zezikohlakali, yinina azenze inqambi ngoku kudla kwake ukudla
kwazo?

Akenzanga mapike zinkani u-Yesu ukubapendula, usuke wateta lemizekeliso
siyibalelweyo kwesisiqendu, exela ke ukuba u-Tixo ngonenceba,
ngobafunayo ngobasindisayo abalahlekileyo, ngoba nemihlali encamisileyo
akubona umoni eguquka, elililela ityala lake. U-Tixo ngongena nqala,
ngotandayo yena ukuba bonke aboni nabapi nabalahleke njani,
nabazeyelisele kangakanani ukuba bavuye, babuyele kuye ; akuko mntu
amncamileyo yena; noko angungwe yimikosi yasezulwini engenakubalwa, noko
ahleliyo ebungcweleni bakona onwabile, lemihla uyabafuna, uyababongoza
aboni ukuba baguquke, igcobe ke intliziyo yake kube yimihlali ezulwini
akumbona umoni noselede wancanywa ngabanye ebuya, egoduka ezicelela
izono zake uxolo.

Utenina ke u-Yesu ukuzibeka kwake ezindawo pambi kwababantu barorayo?
Utete lomzekeliso wati, kwabe kuko mntu ubenonyana ababini, waza ke
omnci wa- bekisa ku Yise izwi eliti, ndinike isahlulo sempahla esilunge
nam.

I. Ke, mdawo yokuqala esiyibonayo apa yile yokuba lomfana, ubenalo
Ikowabo.

Ebengeyiyo inkedama, ebenaye u-Yise, ikaka lake, uyise ongambonelayo,
ame- nzelelele ezixakekweni zake, uyise ongamncedayo ematyaleni
angamhlelayo , ebengemfana uhleli pakati kwabantu basemzini abangazani
naye, wayengento icoliweyo yafumana yenzelwa usizi sityebi esibe
simcolile. Wayengatinjwanga ezimfazweni, wayengenguye umgqakwe otukwayo
ukuze ibe ihlala itutumba intliziyo yake, zigxeko ezipelela pezu kwake.
Hai. Ube ngumntwana wo-

mntu, umntaka nantsi, isityebi. Ebe- ngonelungelo, engehlwempu,
engaswelento ; ebenoyise omtandayo, omgcinileyo, omlungiselelayo.
Nantoni yakumhlela ebenaye umzali wake angati aye kuyibi- kalonto kuye,
ubehleli ekaya, ebuhlanti, kowabo, engoniwanto engumnumzana ose-
lenezicaka ezimkonzayo, intanga zake ezi- mbulisayo kuba ebengumntwana
wesidwa- ngube, ngenxa zonke ke ebenentlalo entle emnandi, eyonwabileyo,
engamnqwenisayo omnye umntu; ebengemntaka mfi, usizana olusebubini,
olulilela ububi bokubujelwa ngumzali usemncinane, ungekabina-
kuzixelengela. Hai ubunzima bobunkedama, bokuti ufelwe ngu Yihlo
usengumntwana ongenakuzisebenzela, ongenakuzifunela iq'inga lokuzixasa.
Lomfana yena ngongaluvanga ololusizi, obo bubi kuba nanku uyise ebeseko
esamxasa. Makabe nguyena mfana wonwabileyo ubulelayo lo, esenoyise nje
enamalungelo omzi wakowabo.

eliti ndinike isahlulo sempahla esilunge nam. Noko uyise ebenalonyana
bekunjengokungati akazalanga, kuba lomntwana wake ebeselengotsalele
zweni limbi, ebengasateni nayise, engasafuni kupatwa kulaulwa nguye,

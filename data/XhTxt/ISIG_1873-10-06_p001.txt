Isigidimi Samaxosa

UKUBUBA KUKA MAQOMA.

U-MAQOMA ububele E-Robben Island ngo 9 ka September. Ubudala bake kutiwa
ube kumashumi asibozo eminyaka. Mhlaumbi kungatandekaukuba sike sitete
amazwana ambalwa ngaye kuba eyiyona nkosi ya Maxosa ibe idumile
kuleminyaka imashumi mablanu edluleyo. U-Maqoma ubengunyana omkulu ka
Ngqika, noko oyena uvelele ubukosi engu Sandile kuba
engowesitandanekazi. Pakati konyana baka Ngqika akuko ubefike ku Maqoma
ngokuqonda nokwazi ukwenza izinto. Ngomnyaka we 1829, yena nabantu bake
bebemi E-Katala, apo ke bagxotwa ngo- kusuke bamane ukutimba impahla
yekoloni, nokungenela inkosi ya Batembu ebinobuhlobo.

Kubonakalaukubaimfazweyomnyakawe 1835 ekutiwa yeka Hintsa yabako ikakulu
ngenxa yake. Kwati emva kwalo mfazwe kwakubakumiswe elokuti umhlaba
opakati kwe Nxuba ne Nxwenxa maungamiwa, U- Maqoma weza kuma kufupi nase
Beaufort. Kude kwafika unyaka we 1846 engenanto eyona inkulu azamela
ukusebenzela uhlanga ngayo. Imfazo yalo mnyaka we 1846 yebla eyicasile
yena. Akubonakali ukuba kuko nto inkulu ayenzayo ngayo. Kwati noko
umkosi wa Mangesi nowa Maxosa useluke wadibana akemka E-Beaufort. Tati
noko I-Government ibiselide yanika umteto wokuba angezi ekoloni,
wazingisa yena ukuza, wada watatyatwa wasiwa E- Bayi. W ahlala kona wada
wayalelwa ngu Sir H. Smith ukuba abuyele kwase Maxoseni.

Ngale yokugqibela imfazwe, yomnyaka we 1850, ekutiwa yeka Mlanjeni,
oyena mntu ubeyintloko kwela Maxosa icala ube ngu Maqoma. Nguye
nowabangela uku­ba U-Sandile anga zipulapuli izinto ezi-

tetwa yi Government. Wati ezicasa ezo- ndawo waba esiti, “ ngedlulileyo
imfazwe bekusitiwa ndiyageza, kodwa ngale baya kubona ukuba andinjalo.”
Uhlobo alwa ngalo kumahlati ase Katala, nokuwangenela kwake Amangesi
Emtontsi kuyaziwa kwindawo zonke. Nalento lemfazwe yo­kugqibela yabande
kangaka kwaku nge­nxa yake.

Kanjalo ngomnyaka we 1856, sibona ukuba U-Maqoma wabengomnye wabapa-
mbili ukuxasa U-Mhlakaza ngokuxelwa kwe nkomo. Kwati kwakubonakala ukuba
unento ayaziyo ngokubulawa kuka Vusani wabanjwa; lati ityalalake
lakutetwa wagwetywa ukuba atunyelwe esiqitini iminyaka emashumi mabini.
U-Vusani wabadwa ngenxa yokuba engavumi ukuzixela ezake inkomo, kekaloku
emva kokufa kwake ezonkomo zatatyatwajngu Maqoma.

Kuminyaka emine edluleyo wakululwa wavunyelwa ukuba ezo kwase Maxoseni
ukuba uyakuzipata kakuhle. Wati esa- fika E-Maxoseni wati ku Mangqika
uzuze imvumelo yokubuyela kwase Mtontsi, ke ngoko bangamlandela
abatandayo. Baba baninzi abakolwayo yilonto, abati nokuze babe
abahambanga naye bacazelwa ngabafundisi ukuba ayiko into enjalo.
U-Maqoma yena wahamba wada waya kufika E-Fort Beaufort ehamba namapakati
ambalwa. Wati akufika kona wa-nqandwa yimantyi U-L. H. Meurant, Esq.
waxelelwa yi Governor ukuba woti xa abuye wayipinda lonto abuyiselwe
kwalapo abevela kona. Wati noko ayaliweyo ngoluhlobo wabuya waya E-Fort
Beaufort, wazake wapindiswa okwesibini ukutunye- lwa E-Robben Island,
apo asand’ ukububela kona. Ngoku kwesibini ubeye yedwa kuba umfazi wake
U-Katyi engavumanga ukuhamba naye.

Kuvakala ukuba ubube xa I-Govern­ment ngokukodwa U-Mr. Brownlee acinga
ngokumshenxisa E-Robben Island, amse kumlambo ekutiwa yi Kuil, avumele
ne- zihlobo zake ukuba ziye kumhlalisa.

Pakati kwa Maxosa bebembalwa aba- lingana naye ngobuciko bokuxoxa
izinto, namaqinga okuzenza. Ubengenamntu kwabakowabo ngokwazi ukulaula
umkosi; naye ngokwake ubekalipile. Emfazweni kona, nezinye inkosana zibe
zingeyiyo nto. Ukuba ubengumntu ozinikele ekunyuseni uhlanga Iwakowabo
namhlanje Amaxosa ngelengangene kangaka ekuseleni uty wala, ngele
luhlanga oluhambisileyo.

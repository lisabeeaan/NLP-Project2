nakalisa ngoko, ukuba engacingi ngokuti awu- bolekwe okwentsukwana,
ekubeni unikelwe wona nenzala yawo ngokwama xesha onke.

Ke kaloku ke, kute akuba emdala u Mpande epele amandla, ekubeni kanjalo
no Cetywayo ebeselebumisile obake ubukulu kwa Zulu, ete no Mtonga wabuya
wabaleka kwa Zulu apo uqalile ukupika u Cetywayo ukuba amabulu abepiwe
umhlaba, wafuna ukuwasusa, efuna ukuwunikela kwi Rulumente yase Natal
umhlaba lowo, kute kengokubakubangiswana ngawo, kwati e Natal apo,
waliwa umhlaba.

Kuza kuba minyaka mibini i Rulumente yase Natal isamkele isizwe
ebesipetwe e Transvaal, yati kunye nesizwe eso, yawamkela nomhlaba
obusesandleni saso, nako oko kwashunyayelwa ku Cetywayo; yena ke ebenako
ukupinda nebango elo, alise kwa kwi Rulumente yase Natal, apo
ebekewalisa kona, oko umhlabaubusapetweegunyenilaseTransvaal. U Cetywayo
akenzanga njalo, akalindelanga ukuba elo bango lake liconjululwe yi
Rulumente ebike yatsho akuti amaxesha ngamaxesha, ngati kwa Zulu kuko
ituba lokukalazela i Transvaal, ngenxa yokushenxisa aba kwa Zulu
emhlabeni wabo. Indawo ke ayenzileyo u Cetywayo, ute lamhlaba
kubangiswana ngawo, waya kuwuma ngempi, wawagxota amabulu eselengene kwi
Rulumente yamangesi, wati amabuto ake, wawakela imizi kude pakati
kwelizwe elibeli seli neminyaka emininzi lipetwe e Transvaal apo, limiwe
ngamabulu.

Ezindawo bezingalwelwayongu Rulumente ngenene, kute kodwa ngenxa
yokuzindla oku- sekukankanyiwe,nangokungafuni ukuba indawo ngati
igwetywa ingaqondwanga, ekubeni kuko nendawo ezilunge no Rulumente kute
ke ngezo zizatu, wati u Rulumente akayisunduza ngamandla leyompi yakwa
Zulu. Yati ne Ruluneli yase Natal yavelisa elokuti kungavela uncedo
ngelayo icala, lokwahlula elo bango lipakati kwaba kwa Zulu naba se
Transvaal, ngawo lowo mhlaba kubanjenwe ngawo.

Londawo ke yavunywa,yati i Ruluneli yase Natal yamisa abahluli,
bengamadoda awona anokwazi, enokuqonda okukulu, etembeke ngokukodwa
kumanene alo Rulumente ise Natal. Ayile lomadoda kulomda kubangiswana
ngawo, alindela ukuweva amacala omabini.

Amadoda abekutshiwe e Transvaal azivelisile izizatu zokubanga kwawo;
akupa ubunqina bokuziqinisa ezondawo. Abakwa Zulu bapele ngokuwangenisa
amabango abo, bongeza ngokuwagqitiselela kumazwe akude le, agqite nase
Balule umlambo ongazange umiwe nokumiwa ngaba kwa Zulu, bewubanga
ngetuba lokuba impi ka Tshaka neka Dingana, zake zafika kona, zazicita
izizwe

Kute ke kaloku akufika u Somtseu kwa Cetywayo apo, ukuze aye kumisa,
waqala ngokukupa ezindawo zilandelayo, wati ke yena u Cetywayo ngelake
icala wazivuma pambi kwa mabandla akwa Zulu.

Ukuqala Ukuba ukupalazwa kwegazi nga- pandle kwesizatu makunqumke.

Okwesibini, Ukuba kungabiko owakwa Zulu oyakugwetywa engaviwanga
ekuhleni, namanqina amtyolayo namnqinelayo nawo abuzwe ekuhleni, nokuba
lowo unetyala, abe nako ukubenela ku Cetywayo.

Okwesitatu ukuba kunga bulawa bani wakwa Zulu, ingazi inkosi ingavumanga
nokuvuma, ekubeni ukutetwa kwetyala elo bekuse kwenzekile, ete nalowo
unetyala eseleke wa- lifumana ituba lokubenela enkosini.

Okwesine ukuba kuze kuti nge zono ezilula, isigwebo sokufa singabiko,
simelwe kuhlaule- lwa.

Lamadinga ke u-Cetywayo wawamkela, wa- qinisa ku Rulumente wamangesi
naku mabandla akwa Zulu ukuba uyakuwa bamba awagcine lomadinga. Ke
kaloku ke akuko nalinye kulawo madinga ali bambileyo u Cetywayo.
Ukubulala okungaketileyo ngezono zonke, nanxa kungamiswanga siposo,
kuhambisile kwada kwagqita na koko bekuko ngexesha lika Mpande. Akubanga
ko ukutetwa ekuhleni kwetyala, nobefuna ukubenela enkosini akabanga
nako. Ngoko ke bonke abakwa Zulu bahleli bebuxalele ubomi babo, ngokuti
bangaqutyulwa babulawe, ziti impahla zabo zicitwe, zihlutwe elusatsheni
lwabo.

Kuyimfanelo yo Rulumente wamangesi lukubasiza abakwa Zulu, baze
baqiniselelwe indawo yokuba u Cetywayo abapate ngoku- lungelelene
namadinga abewamise mhlana watweswa ubukosi ngu Somtseu, lawo madinga
linani alirolele ukuxaswa nokuvunywa ngu Rulumente wase Natal, kunjalo
nje i Rulumente yondele ukugcina abayo abantu, ikangele nakwisizatu
sokuba waehlangene namabandla akwaZulu, ekumiseni u Cetywayo esikundleni
sika yise.

Kufika apa nje, kufanele ukuba kuke ku- buywe ngomva kancinane.

Ngemihla ka Mpande amabulu ase Trans-Vaal ebemi esahlukweni somhlaba,
osemantla o-Pongolo, umlambo obungemiwe, ungenamntu apo ngelo xesha.

Amabulu amisa elokuti, ngenxa yobubele awati u Mpande wabuzuza kuwo,
mhlana wa- baleka ku Dingana, nangenxa yokuba amabulu lawo ati Umtonga
ambuyisela kwa Zulu ebalekele emabulwini, ute lomhlaba ubugemiwe wapiwa
wonangu Cetywayo; kwatiwa ukuqiniswa kwalondawo, kwakutshwa inkomo
ngamabulu zasiwa ku Mpande waza wazamkela. Ati ke wona amabulu awuma
lowo mhlaba, aka izindlu, amisa imiti, alima amasimi, agalela imali yawo
emhlabeni apo, ebo-

ebezilimi elo lizwe, zatimba nenkomo zazo.

Abahluli abo bazipengulula ngokunyameka zonke izibango ezo, bati
ngokuqonda kwabo, ekuyilungiseni indawo leyo bebeyibuza, bakupa isigwebo
esiqinisiweyo ngu Mongameli omkulu nayi Ruluneli yase Natal, sati
nesigwebo eso sashunyayelwa kwabo babangisanayo.

Kwaza ke kwa oko, kwatiwa kwabakwa Zulu maze bangagqiti elu Pongolo, ngo
kuzakwaka imizi entla nalowo mlambo. Baze bati nokuba bana mabango
kulawo macala, bawangenise ku Rulumente, ukuze abuzwe aze ahlulwe.

Isigwebo sabahluli abo, sinikele kwabakwa Zulu imihlaba eyayipantsi
kwempato yamangesi, seloko abase Transvaal bangeneyo kuyo, nabantsundu
abebe kwanjalo bepantsi kwempato yamangesi, abangati kaloku baziketele
ekufudukeni bengenziwa luto nempahla yabo, baye kwelasemlungwini,
nasekuhlaleni kwelolizwe lahlulelwe KwaZulu, bapatwe KwaZulu, ngemiteto
yakona.

Kekaloku ke ifeziwe nje imbambano leyo, pakati ko Cetywayo ne Rulumente
yase Transvaal, kuse kusele okokuba i Rulumente yase mangesini ivelise
izizatu zoku misa impato elungileyo kwabo abebeke bengasemlungwini, be
kaloku bese ngabasecaleni lakwa Zulu, nokuba kuveliswe izizatu zokumisa
im. pato elungileyo ku Zulu wonke, nokuba ngoku u Cetywayo awabambe
awafeze amadinga abewenzile mhlana watweswa ubukosi; ukuze ke ifezeke
lendawo, iRulumente yamangesi imele ukuba ibize iziqiniso zexesha
elizayo kuye u Cetywayo; noko ke ezinjalo azinakuma ngoku u-Cetywayo
esahleli nemikosi engakanana yamabuto. Bonke ubufana nobudoda bakwa Zulu
butintelelwe ebubutweni, besalelwa ukuzeka nokuziqwebela imfuyo, baze
bati ngentando ka Cetywayo, banyanzelelwe ukwenza imiteto yake, bebulala
becita kungatetwanga tyala, kungavuswanga nokuvuswa, ngako oko ke,
babandezeleke kabuhlungu bona ngokwabo, babebona bezintonga
zokubandezela u Zulu wonke.

Lempato ilusizi olunzima, yapula amadinga abenqopisene ngawo u Cetywayo
ne Rulumenti yamangesi, kwa namabandla akwaZulu mhlana wahlaliswa
esihlalweni sika yise, exaswe ngabamele i Rulumenti.

Ke ngoko ke into yokuqala efunekayo ku Cetywayo, esingisa ekufezekeni
kwedinga lake lamhlana watweswa ubukosi, yeyokuba agece lempato yakaloku
yamabuto, amise impato ekungavunyelwana ngayo, kuhlangane ibandla
elikulu lakwa Zulu, ibe londawo ivumelekile kwabamele i Rulumenti
yamangesi.

Lempato yakaloku yentlanganisela yama- buto, ubuninzi bawo busalelwa
ukuzeka, mayipeliswe ingabiko, aze ati onke amadoda azizekele ngokutanda
kwawo, kube njengoko

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, FEBRUARY 1, 1879.

UKUBULAWA KUKA BISHOP PATTESON.

(EVELA KUMBALELANI.)

Incwadi ezivela E-New Zealand zifike nendaba zokubulawa kuka Bishop
Patteson no Mr. Atkin. Inqanawa eyayikwele kuyo ekutiwa yi Southern
Cross yayise ndaweni yokunqabisa inqanawa ekutiwa licweba lase Auckland
ngomhla wa 31st, ka October. Inkosi yenqanawa ye Bishop, U- Captain
Jacob, iti ukusixela isizatu sokubulawa kwayo,—“kusekuhleni ukuba kuko
inqanawa (yase Europe) eyaka yabapata kakubi abantu belozwe ngexesha
elingapambili kokufika kwetu, ngokuba bebe ngayi kuyibulala I-Bishop
leyo. Ibisiti yakuhambela kona iminyaka yonke ipe inkosana nabantu
amabaso, ihlale nexesha elikulu nabo emini elunxwemeni.”

Imbali yokubulawa kwe Bishop ngabantu bezo ziqiti ibaliswa yinkosana
yalomkombe yayikuwo iti.“ Kwati ngomhla 20 ka September kwa bonakala
izikitshana ekutiwa ngamapenyane, zingati zitandatu zipuma ngase
lunxwemeni, kumgama ongati zi mayile ezine ukusuka kwinqanawe leyo.
Zasondela, zati zakuba zi mayile ezimbini zema. Sati nqa kuba ngamaxa
angapambili ngezisondele imayile yanye. I-Bishop yati makutotywe iboti
(umkonjana omncinane) iye kubo. Sagqibela eloxa ukuyibona ihleli. Ngenxa
yokuhla kwamanzi iboti yakohlwa kukuhamba, yaza ke I-Bishop yakwela
ezikitshaneni yayangapesheya ihamba nenkosana ezimbini, U-Tanla no Motu.
Kute pambi kokuba I-Bishop ifike elu­nxwemeni abantu abasezikitshaneni
bayiwela iboti batola ngentolo ezininzi kubantu benqanawa pambi kokuba
babe nokusaba. U-Mr. Atkin (omnye wabebene Bishop) wabetwa emva
komkono.— U-Stephen watolwa ngentolo ezintandatu, olunye lusesifubeni,
U-John watolwa ecaleni. Iboti zakumbula enqanaweni ekutiwa yi Bessie
zakupa abenzakeleyo, amayeza bawapiwa ngu Mr. Brook. Ndati kwangoko
ndatumela inkosana engapantsi yenqanawa nabantu abahlanu, ukuya
kukangela

I-Bishop, umqubi engu Mr. Atkin, kuba bekungeko mntu kwabetu obeyazi
londawo. Ndati maze babete bucala ezintolweni bati ukuba bayibonile
I-Bishop bangasondeli. Lonke elixa inqanawa ekutiwa yi Bessie yayimi.
Kute ngexesha elincinane isukile iboti kwafika impetshana, ndaza
ndayisondeza inqanawa ukuze baxakwe ukuyiwela. Sabona besuka elunxwemeni
ezikitshaneni ezibini esinye basiyeka sazihambela, esinye sabuyela
elunxwemeni, iboti yaya kweso esasiyekwe sazi­hambela, saza sasifumana
kona isidumba se Bishop sisongelwe elukukweni.”

Omnye umfundisi we Bishop owabala ese Auck­land uti ukuxela uhlobo
eyafunyanwa ilulo I-Bishop—“ Umva wentloko wawungati ubetwe ngenduku,
ubuso bona bungenanto. Bakuyibulala, yonke impin dezelo yapela, basipata
kakuhle isidu­mba eso, basisongela elukukweni, babeka isebe lesundu
esifubeni sake linamaqina amahlanu. Kutandatyuzelwa ukuba amaqina lawo
ngumqondiso wokuxela ukuba ubomi bayo butatelwe “ utu”
okokukutiumhlauliso wobomibabantu bakona aba­hlanu, ekutiwa fan’ ukuba
babiwa ngabantu uku­ba bayekwenziwa amakoboka.”

I-Bishop yancwatywa elwandle ngu Mr. Brook ngomhla21; waza'U-Mr. Atkin
owayengxweleriwe wabuba ngomhla wa 27, kwati ngo 28 kwabuba
U-Stephen.—Umbedesho wokuncwaba wenziwa ngu Mr. Brook nge Simotu ne
Singesi.

I-ANNIVERSARI YASE ANNSHAW.

Ngomhla we 15 nowe 16 ka November, isidlo somnyaka, esilunge nesikolo
secawa sase Annshaw sibesiko apa senzelwe ilungelo lentsapo zabantsundu.
Bekuko nabanye abavela kwezinye izikolo ezilishumi elinantlanu. Bafika
ngokuhlwa ko Mgqibelo bevuma ingoma, bayake bonke ngendawo abebe
zilungiselelwe.

Kusasa nge Cawa nangokuhlwa U-Rev. Donald McLeod wase Bumshill
washumayela intshuma- mayelo ezinamandla zambini. Ukuviwa kosapo kwaqala
nge Cawa emva kwemini, kwada kwa ngu Mvulo ngokuhlwa. Ukuviwa oku ke
bekundawonye nokuvuma. Zonke into ebezise- nziwa zagqitywa ngexesha
leshumi ngokuhlwa.

Ngo Mvulo izulu lalingalungile kuko umoya nomkumezelo, kodwa kwabonakala
ukuba maku- hanjiswe ukuviwa kosapo. Umsebenzi wahambiseka ngempumelelo
enkulu. Ikakulu izifundo zezibalo ngokwa Mangesi nangokwama Xosa.

Ityalike yayixinene kunene ngangokude inxenye ime ngokuswela indawo
yokuhlala.

Kwintlanganiso yangokulilwa ko Mvulo U-Rev. Mr. Buckingham wase Aliwal
North no Rev. Gr. A. Rose bateta amazwana ambalwa ngokufutshane kwaza ke
kwavunywa iculo le Nkosazana, U-Rev. Mr. W. C. Holden wenza U-Eefe Iwe
Nkosi, kwapunywa.

Ukuviwa kwabafunda kulo Institution nokwabiwa kwamabaso alomnyaka we
1873, kuya kuba nge Wednesday ngo 17 ka December.

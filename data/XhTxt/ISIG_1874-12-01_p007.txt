ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1874.

IZINTO.

U-DR COLENSO useleye wafika pesheya.

E-ABERDEEN kuko umfazi ontsundu ozixome ngokwake.

E-GUATEMALA umzi otile utshatyalaliswe ku- zamazama komhlaba.

E-NATAL. kuko umfo ontsundu ozixomileyo kuba esuke wafelwa zinkabi.

ABAGUQULI bezibalo babuye bahlangana E-Qonce ukuquba umsebenzi wabo.

E-BUENOS AYRES umfo abati ngu Mitre umpati wemikosi, uvukele izipata
mandla.

U-MARSHAL BAZAINE, Kuvakala ukuba uza kuya kuhlala E-Spain apate imikosi
yakona.

U-MR SMITH, Umongameli Wemicimbi Yemi- hlaba Yakomkulu usahambele kula
macala.

INKUMBI kubonakala ukuba ziya kuxalisa nonyaka nje. Sezininzi indawo
abase belila ngazo.

ABANTU ABASEBENZA kwindlela yomlilo esuka E-Bayi bamakulu mabini
anamashumi matandatu anesine.

KUKO AMABE okuba U-Governor Pine uza kupuma ekupateni I-Natal. Ungati
woti noko angase nkosi ahlale kona kumhlaba awutengileyo.

AMAKOBOKA—I-Government yalapa ilungiselela ukuze abantu abafunyenwe
kumikombe eba amakoboka beze kule koloni okwexeshana elitile baze kuba
zizicaka kona.

E-TURKEY kuko iqela lamasodati elisuke langena etyalikeni, lambamba
umfundisi obeshumayela, kwako amadoda nabafazi abebeze kupulapula
elibanxwelerayo.

E-UNITED STATES zase America kuko isipitipiti esikoyo pakati kwabamhlope
nabantsundu Sekuke kwako abafayo kumacala omabini, ngokukodwa kumzi wase
New Orleans.

U-MR HOGARTH—Asikuko nokuba kuluvuyo ukuva engati U-Mr Hogarth uza
kubuya aze kuba ngu mkangeli wezikolo zala macala etu. Siyakuvuya
ngokungakumbi sakuqiniseka bunyaniso balonto.

UMSIZI WOKUBALA.—Umongameli weposi uti abantu ngebelumkela ukuba babale
nge inki emnyama xa batumela incwadi zabo, kuba ezinye intlobo ze inki
ziti zakufunyanwa bumanzi zingabi nakuleseshwa.

U-MR STANLEY kuvakala ukuba woti ukuba unokupumelela kakuhle apo aya
kona pakati E-Africa, abuye ingekapeli iminyaka emibini. Okokugqibela
esive ngaye ubese Zanzibar elungiselela ukunduluka.

OWENZAKALISWE YIMFUNDO—U-Stoffel, umfo ontsundu ofundileyo, ugwetywe ngu
Mr Justice Smith ukuba abe minyaka mitatu etolongweni esebenza kanzima,
ngenxa yokuba ebale incwadi ayenze ukungati ibalwe ngomnye umntu.

IKOFU—U-Mr Samuel Dredge wase Monti unesitiya sekofu kona. Nonyaka nje
imiti ekuso ikwikulu elinamanci mahlanu. Uti abatanda ukuba ihlume
lekoloni ngebe linga ukuyilima. Ikula kakuhle, ngokukodwa kufupi
nolwandle.

U-DR DALZELL, lomfundisi wayetunyelwe ukuza kumisa isikolo esiya kubizwa
ngo Gordon, usahambele pesheya. Kulindeleke ukuba woba nenyanga
ezintlanu nezintandatu kona. Uhambele ukulungisa imicimbi yeso sikolo
aza kusiqala.

UBUKOBOKA E-YEPUTA—Noko kuko umteto wokuba umsebenzi wokutengisa
amakoboka maupele E-Yeputa kuseko into eninzi ewuqubayo ngasese. Kuko
iqela elibanjwe liquba amakoboka 591. Zite izipata mandla zokubabamba
abo bantu, zabahluta lomakoboka, zati amadoda makaseleba ngamasodati
azo.

AMADODA ayetunywe E-Austria ukuba ahambe ehlola olu Iwandle lunqonge
inorth pole aselede abuya. Ati kufupi ne North pole kuko ilizwe
nakwezindawo kube kutandatyuzelwa ukuba lulwandle lodwa. Ngalo nyaka
uzayo kuya kubuya kutunywe amanya.

UMSEBENZI—Umfuyi otile ongase Rini uti unegusha ezimakulu asibozo
ezifuna ukucetywa, nezitungu zehabile ezimawaka mane ezifuna ukusikwa,
noko abantu anokubafumana ukuza kumsebenzela ngumcebi omnye, nomsiki
okwamnye, nabafazi ababini.

ABAFUNDISI BASE AMERICA E-NATAL— Ukuma kweremente elunge nabafundisi
base America kuloluhlobo—Abafundisi ababekwe izandla 11, amanenekazi
ancedisayo 16, abashumayeli abantsundu 20, ititshere ezintsundu 23,
isikolo esikulu samakwenkwe 1, esentombi 1, abahamba ezikolweni 878,
amalungu eremente 811, indlu zetyalike 11, ezezikolo 12.

UMFO OTILE obebalele omnye wabafunda apa ngolwe 5 ku November, uti “
Into enkulu apa E-Queenstown kukufakwabantu. Besincwaba into- mbazana yo
Msutu nge Cawa. Igama layo ngu Sabina. Abantu ababeko encwabeni
bebekumakulu amabini avayo. Omnye umntu oyindoda, U- mtembu, ubube
namhlanje; emva kokugula iveki ezintatu. Enye indawo kukubalela kwelanga
nokudula kokudla.”

E-LONDON,Kusand’ ukuhla ingozi embi kunene eyonakalise impahla. Kute
komnye wale mikombe imana ukunyusa umlambo, kwadubula iruluwa ebipakati,
wati umkombe obuyitwele wahamba inceba bafa abebekuwo. Konakele kanjalo
neminye ebikufupi, kwawa imicancato, ifestile babuvitiviti, babaninzi,
ekufuneka ukuba baye kufuna indawo zokulala kwezinye indawo.

ODUTYULWEYO—Ngase Bayi kuko umfo otile omhlope obetiyele ingulube
ngompu. Kute engeko yena, engaluxelelanga usapo Iwake apo ukona umpu,
unina waya ngakona. Ubelixegokazi eliminyaka 77. Kute emini wavakala
udubula waza unyana lowo waya kukangela ukuba kuko ngulube idutyulweyo
na, wafika unina selepolile, imbumbulu ingene entloko.

LANGALIBALELE—Umfo otile omhlope obeha- mbele E-Robben Island uti—Ndide
kona ndabona no Langalibalele nonyana wake. Ndifike besapilile kakuhle
noko bengati abonwabile kwelo kaya labo. Basand’ushenxiselwa kwindlu
entsha, eyona ilungiselelwe bona, kodwa abayitandi kanti le intsha
yiyona intle ngapezulu. Unyana unamazwana awaziyo nge Singesi, kodwa
indoda enkulu yona sizotane, into engatetiyo.

KWELE DIAMOND.—Umfo otile obala kwelozwe uti, Baninzi abantu abaqauka
yindlala apa: uninzi Iwabo luvela kumazwe apakati. Bafika apa bedlekile
kakulu yindlela nayindlala, bengenako ke ngoko nokuwenza umsebenzi. Kuti
ngelo tuba basweleke ababaqashayo. Uti noke wafumana umsebenzi abehle
angenwe sisifo akufumana ukudla, kuba kukade ahlukene nako, ize ke
inkosi yake imgxote. Intsizana ezinjalo asizizo nazo ngoku apa. Akuko
qinga bangancedwa ngalo na?

IGQIRA NOMPU—Umfo otile obala pesheya kwe Nciba uti; Apa kube kuko
intlanganiso ebibizwe ngomcinjana otile, kufunwa ukuba igqira lize
kunyanga imipu yalo mzi ukuze ibe nokucana ngemfazwe. Kute ke xa igqira
limana ukuyitabata ngaminye liyiposa pezulu, liyi yeka iwe emhlabeni,
likwitsa ngamayeza alo, kanti liza kude litabate nonxashiweyo. Lite
lakuwuposa pezulu wadubula xa uwayo. Yeka ke ukubaleka kwegqira
lakuwuva! Nditeta lisuke lalala pantsi akwabako unokubuye alibambe.
Kubuleleka ukuba kungeko wenzakeleyo.

E-QUEENSTOWN—Umfo obala ese Queenstown uti—Kuko Ilau elivumileyo ukuba
umfazi walo limbulele. Umxosa otile uzidize ukuba ubesand’ukubulala
omnye ngase Burgersdorp. Izingwe, nencuka, namaxwili azingenele igusha
kulo Mahlulo.

IPEPA elitile lase Kapa liti, U-Feltman usand’ ukutumela incwadi kumntu
otile apa ati kuyo, “ Ndiya kwazisa ukuba iharmonium (uhlobo olutile
lwepiano)ide yafika Usapo Iwam luya tanda kanye ukuyiva lento ivuma
endlwini yam. Bonke abake bayibona bati yelungileyo kanye. Enye indawo
amamfengu aza kusebenza apo selesuka apa. Apetwe ngumninawe wam
U-Jessie. Ndiya kolwa ukuba akufika apo woka eze kukubona kuba, ndandite
ungamamkela. Amamfengu ngoku akasaloyiki ulwandle. Sati sakufika apa
sawaxelela ukuba alunanto. Ngoku akasaukatalele umkombe.”

INTSHUTSHISO ZAMAKOLWA.—E-Turkey abantu abakolwayo basatshutshiawa
kakulu kwezinye indawo. I-Government yakona noko imise umteto wokuba
akuko mntu uya kutshutshiswa ngenxa yenkonzo yake, ayitabati nyameko
yokubakusela abakolwa ku Kristu. Omnye umfundisi wakona uti kuko ama
Kristu amatatu afunyenwe efundisa aza abanjwa, anxitywa amakamandela,
abetwa kunene, kwahlekiswa ngawo, aza emva koko agxotwa ezweni lawo.
Omnye umfo ute akwaziwa ukuba ungu Mkristu wabanjwa ngamapolisa amfaka
etolongweni, abatabata abantwana bake abanikela kumfundisi ongu
Mahomedan.

UMBULALI OMKULU—E-Free State umfo ontsundu abati ngu Jem, ubaleke
entolongweni. Wayefakwe kona ngetyala lokubulala inkwenkwana etile ngalo
May udluleyo. Wakuvuma ukuba uyibulele, wati kanjalo wambulala nomfazi
wake nentombi yake. Ute ukuze abaleke baba bebeye kusebenza ababanjwa
abo bepetwe lidindala elintsundu. Kwakufika ixesha lokupumla lisuke
elidindala lalala kuba belise linokubatemba. Abanye bemke baya kusela
kwasala batatu. Usuke lomfo unetyala lokubulala watabata ipeki,
wayihloma entloko yelidindala, yapantsa ukuya kupumela kwelinye icala,
wazake wabaleka. Kula ebenalo elinye lilandele labaleka, elinye laya
kuhlaba umkosi ekaya. Into emangalise abantu kukuba idindala elo like
lihlale ixeshana pambi ’ kokuba liqauke noko lenjiwe njalo. Lomfo yena
ulibuleleyo kutiwa ubekise ngase Diamond.

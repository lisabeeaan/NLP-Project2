ISIPANGO E AMERICA.—E United States kune kweziveki zidluleyo isipango
kazi esikulu, esidubule ekulahlekeni kobomi obuninzi. Umlambo oyi
Mississippi uzale ixesha elide, kwaza kwati kumandlana oyi Louisana
kwemka abantu aba 120, ababe kotame endlwini etile enkulu.

U MR. PATTLE. —Malunga nokupela kwe nyanga u March sesishicilelwe ese
nyanga edlulileyo isi Gidimi, ijuri eyabi pulapula ityala lake yamkulula
u Mr. Pattie. Ka­mbe kotiwa ezindaba zindala ngesingazi fakanga, unyaka
udlule wobonisa ukuba bezifanele ukuba zifakwe nokokunjalo.

zabafunde incwadi zama Ngesi, kuba ngumhlobo wabo bonke abazana
nencwadi.

obukulu obaka bafunyanwa buqokolo bomntu.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1887

  Isiko lendlu yokomkulu ib’ikukuvuka kwakusasa, nga- manye amaxesha
  yena u PRINCE ALBERT avuke kwangomso obomvu, abe sel’ekangela incwadi
  ezifikileyo nokuba zivela pina, pambi kokuvuka kwo Mntan’omhle.

  Pakati kwemisito eke yako kubeko ogama liyi Peace Festival,
  obungowokudibanisa yonke imisebenzi ye Nkutalo yazo zonko intlanga
  zehlabati. Lomsito wenzeka nge 1851. Kwiminyaka emibini emva koko,
  kwabako lamfazwe yase Crimea kwakusilwa ama Ngesi nama Russia,
  kwakusavana kakuhle ama French nama Ngesi oko.

  Kute ngenyanga engu January 1854 kwaqondakala ukuba liya likanye ukufa
  kuma Ngesi nama Russia, kwaye kuko umoya kwinxenye yabantu wokuti
  ngati u Kumkanikazi no PRINCE ALBERT banomoya wokuvelana nama Russia
  bevisana. Kwakuko ixala elikulu kumzi wonke ngenxa yalo mfazwe, ngonxa
  yokuba bekuzakuwelana indoga ezinkulu zombini, kubonakala ukuba
  kokwezitete namhla.

Kwaba lixapetshu elikulu kuyo yonke i England ukulu- ngiselela izinto
zokunceda abaya emfazweni. Iliwe ke lomfazwe e Sebastapool, ide yapela,
kwenziwa uxolo, yabuya imikosi. Kungekabi kade ibuyile kuvakele ukuba
ama solidati angama Indiya avukile, kuyaliwa nawama Ngesi.

U Kumkanikazi wayewavela usizi kakulu amasolidati ukuti esadiniwe evela
kwenye imfazwe bekusekufuneka egqitela kwenye. U Kumkanikazi ungumhlobo
omkulu ko matiloshe nakuma solidati.

Loma Indiya kwakuyiwe kuliwa nawo aye ngamawaka amakulu mabini
(200,000), aye ama Ngesi engamawaka amashumi mabini anamanci mane kupela
(24,000) kwaye kufuneka ukuba agcine akusele abantu abazizigidi
ezimakulu mabini (200,000,000), bawo onke amabala nezizwe.

Yaliwa kanjalo leyo imfazwe, oyiswa loma Indiya, basinda abantu ababeye
kuncedwa.

U Kumkanikazi lo, ubehle weva kamsinya ukuba ukuti buxe intlalo yake
kwindawo ezinje nge Windsor Castle nase Buckingham Palace apo wahlala
ejongene nemicimbi ya Komkulu akufani nokuba kwindawo ezingapandle
abenqwenela zona yena.

Kude ke exesheni, u PRINCE ALBERT waxelelwa ngu Sir ROBERT PEEL nge
Osborne Cottage, waza no Kumkanikazi wakolwa kunene yilondawo, ngenxa
yobufupi bayo elwandle, nokupakama kwayo ukubonisa kakuhle ilizwe.

Uti u Kumkanikazi xa abalela uninalume ‘ Kude kuti ukuyola oku ukuba
nendawo eyeyetu, ezolileyo, ete cwaka epolileyo.’

Kute kwakufudukelwa kulondawo, waba nako kaloku u Mntan’omhle ukubabona
kakuhle abantwana bake kunase London, kuba nokuba bebefundiswa ngu LADY
LYTTELTON wahlala unina engapumanga epungulelweni lokuhlala ondele zonke
izinto abazifundayo, nokuqonda kakuhle abantu abamane ukudibana nabo
abantwana bake.

Ide yalixesha kaloku lokwenda kwentombi enkulu, u PRINCESS ROYAL indawo
leyo engandulanga iqelwe ngu nina.

INKATAZO ZENDLU.

Nge 1861, kwakuko Umboniso Omkulu, kwaye kulixapetshu elikulu komkulu
apo lokulungiselela wona, waye u PRINCE ALBERT ezinikele kakulu
kulomsebenzi, nakuba besekubonakala ukuba amandla ayapela kuye. Kute ke
pakati ku March, kwafika indaba ezimbi ezibika unina we Nkosazana ukuba
asiko kufa, sekubonwa kubonwa.

Lomntu ube sel’enexesha esifa, yaye ne Nkosazana isel’ike yaya
kumlunguza kabini. Banduluka kwa oko u PRINCE ALBERT no Kumkanikazi
kwano PRINCESS ALICE (enye intombi). Befikile, bafika kuseko ukurbxoza
noko babukana, wafika wasanga isandla sonina noko wayengamqondi okwelo
xesha, ate akuti qabu wamnakana, noko seku njalo.

Kubuziwe kuma gqira, ate akuseko temba nto, kwasa eqauka ke kwakusa.

Waba nosizi olukulu u Kumkanikazi, koko bamtutuzela o PRINCE ALBERT,
kwano PRINCESS ALICE wade waxola.

Kuye kubonakala ukuba u PRINCE ALBERT uya etshona ngokutshona, egqitywa
sisifo sokutyatyamba kwamatambo, nawobuso.

Ngo December kona ube mandundu, bamane bemonga o PRINCESS ALICE ne
Nkosazana lonke ixesha abesapanyaza ngalo. Kute ngosuku Iwe 3 ku
December lowo, wenyuka kanye umkuhlane, lapela netemba kwinto yonke
engumntu. U PRINCE of WALES wayese Cambridge, wati ukubizwa wabizwa
ngosuku lokukuba koyise, ngolwe 14 ku December. Labuba elo Tshawe,
lishiya usizi nenyembezi kusapo, no mhlolokazi, nakuyo yonke i England,
nakwe sakowabo. Wavakala u Kumkanikazi esiti—“ Azi ngubanina namhla
oyakuti ‘ VICTORIA ’ ukundibiza?”

Kute emva kokububa kuka PRINCE ALBERT kwabuya kwehla enye into
eyamhlungisa kunene u Kumkanikazi. Kufike indaba zokububa kwento eninzi
yabantu kwindawo ekutiwa yi Hartley Colliery, Iwati usizi Iwake
Iwalukulu kwezo nkedama nabohlolokazi bashiywe zizihlobo zabo ngalo
ngozi.

Kubuye kwehla enye into embi kwakona nge 1878. Ite intombi yo
Kumkanikazi ab’esel’encedwa yiyo kwimicimbi yonke yakomkulu ebifudula
isetyenzwa nguyise, suke nayo yahliwa kukufa yabuba. Leyo ke ngu
PRINCESS ALICE. Wabalusizi kunene u Kumkanikazi, naso sonke isizwe sake.

Kute ngo March we 1884, kwehla enye into ekwalusizi ku Kumkanikazi.
Kuhliwe omnye wonyana bake kukufa obetandwa kakulu kokwabo, u PRINCE
LEOPOLD, obenobuIwelwe buka yise kanye yena. Wabubela ngapandle apo
wayeye kufuniswa koka umoya. Yinto leyo ekwabanjwa amenyo ngaleyo into
namhla kusitiwa u Kumkanikazi akayi kupumelela namhla.

INQUBELO PAMBILI YENTO ZONKE PANTSI KOLAULO

LWAKE.

Zininzi ke zona, ngapezu kwezo Kumkani no Kumkanikazi abake bakwela kulo
terone yase England. Singasesibalula ezitile kupela, ezinje ngo Cingo, o
Sitemele, i Mashini zokutunga, (Photography, ukwenziwa kwemifanekiso,)
ukungeniswa kwezitampu ze penny. Ucingo oluhamba pantsi kwamanzi.
Ukwanda kwe Mfundo, nokwe Lizwi kwizizwe ngezizwe, ukukululeka kwama
koboka entlanga ngentlanga. Ukwanda kwencwadi zelizwi nezinye ke izinto
ezinge qondwayo ngumzi ongafundanga nokuba besizi bekile zonke. Ulaulo
Iwake kuleminyaka imashumi mahlanu, lube licam kumntu wonke obepantsi
kwalo. Bekufanele ke ngoko ukuba ibe yimigcobo enje ngokuba kube njalo
nje ngale mihla ya 20 nama 21 ka June 1887, ebiyi Jubili yo Mntan’ Omhle
u VITOLIYA, U Kumkanikazi welase Mangesini, umlauli obekekileyo we
Indiya, nomkuseli we Afrika. Wanga u Tixo wezulu angake asiboleke eminye
iminyaka ake asilaule asipate kwakona.

LUMKANI MA LEDI.—Ati amapepa ite iledi itile pesheya kolwandle isiya
kutenga ezivenkileni, yahamba isipete ngesandla isipaji sayo. Iqubisene
nanene litile elifike latula nomnqwazi lati—“ Nkosikazi ndixolele lento
yokuyipata ngesandla inxowa yemali ayilungile, okunye isilingo
nakumasela. Kutiwake elonenekazi labulela, lasifaka epoketini isipaji
eso, bahlukana.” Lite lakufika evenkileni, Yini! asiko isipaji, kuteni
betu? Yaye intliziyo yalo sel’imi kula mntu. Liteke ekugodukeni laya e
Sitishi sika Loliwe ukuba liye kukwela, suke gwaqa kwa. nala mfo. Litule
libeka emxelweni, likohlwe yeyona nto malenze yona, kuko ukumbamba, libe
loyike ukuti kanene akuko bunqina, kukuzindla nje kodwa. Kute xa aza
kuhamba uloliwe lo likwele kuye elinenekazi, we tu ngefestile lo mfo
wati—“ Kuteni kaloku nkosikazi bendikutyele nje lento ngati akunaso noko
isipaji sako? Mandisibuyisele kwa kuwe.” Watsho wasiposa sesilikembeca
le tyabela elingenayo nento le. Kwaba kuyahlukana njalo kuba uloliwe
wayesel’ ebaleka emka. Yatsiba pantsi indodana leyo yahamba indlela zayo
ingasawoti.

Xalanga, Il November, 1882.

MHLELI,—Ngo September ogqitileyo u Rulumente utumele ibandla ekutiwa
yikomntyi ukuza kulungisa umhlaba wondiyalwa kulo mandla wase Xalanga
nowase Southey Ville, nokuza kucedulula nokucamanga amabanga ango
mhlaba. Wati u Mpatiswa Micimbi yaba Ntsundu entlanganisweni eyayi
kulomzi, kutolika u Mr. W. Stanford, Into eniyifunayo ezikomntyini
yibekiseni e Mantyini, sakolwa ngoku temba i Mantyi zetu.

Ikomntyi zenza umda pakati kwetu nama Bulu, sakolwa. Ndino sizi ukuti
emva koko asizanga sikolwe nokumnye wemisebenzi yayo. Imantyi zetu zonke
zimi ngambini ukusi lungiselela akuko tuba. Amanyatelo amabi e komntyi,
u Kosana yinkosana engalwanga unikwe 1000 morgen, u Gecelo undiyalwa
ufumene 2000 morgen. Ukumbulela kwabo ukuba elwe no Rulumente inga
zuziswa ngapezulu. Indawo yesibini abanye ondiyalwa banikwe amanxowa abo
yikomntyi. Indawo yesitatu lanto enditi yatetwa ngu Mr. Sauer yiyona nto
inga gcinwanga kanye. Indawo yesine amalungu ekomntyi aguquke azi Mantyi
zalo mhlaba yabeta lonto yawona umhlaba. Andika funi kubenca zinto kuba
kusatetwa ngomhlaba. Mandipele mhleli ngeliti ugqitise indaba ezo. Ndim
lowo,

  JOHN LAMTSATSA.

EZASE EDENDALE.

[NGUMBHALELI WETU.]

Kanti Mhleli abakowenu Amajili abazange bemke ne Mfecane ka Matiwana
ukuya kumfenguza. O Lusibalukulu abo basene Nkosi zabo kwakufupi ne
Edendale. 1. Nabo e Lovu (Richmond) pantsi kwenkosi ezinkulu o
Khukhulela no Bidla. 2. Nabo Emkomanzi pantsi ko Ramncwana inkosi
enkulu. 3. Nabo pesheya ko Mzimkulu pantsi ko Fodo, no Mzongwana unyana
ka Sidoyi. Kunconywa ukukalipa kwabo emkontweni ngemini zase Sandlwana.
Nangoku kusatiwa inkaui yabo ayijikwa mntu.

U Rev. J. J. Jabavu upumile kumteto wabamnyama. Yanga lonto
ingangumzekelo kwimpi efundileyo nesafundayo apa e Natal. Wati kum u Mr.
Hlatywako, omneinane, kungapunywa nje yenye impi entsha kulindwe
amakazi.

U Rev. W. G. Mtembu, unyana womfundisi walapa okwa Jonono ngoku, ubhale
ileta ebukali epikisa Umhleli we kanyiso xa ebesiti yena utywala
bukukudla, badalwa ngu Tixo. Uti ke u Mr. Mtembu nguwupina u Tixo
owadala utywala wabuhlisa ngengqayi ezulwini wabunikela abantu?
Kulombuzo utelelwa ngu Mr. Caluza wase Mpolweni. Mna nditi Inkanyiso
ayibakanyiseli abantu xa ibancomela umqomboti, iya bamfamekisa. Maube ke
upelile umsebenzi wayo eyayimiselwe wona. Kuko nokuba i Natal ipatwe nge
Responsible Govermnent. Asiyiva ukuba itinina ngezo, isuka iti qhapu
ngeze ngqongongqongo.

U Mr. Mordecai Ndaba ngu mntu omdala walapa e Edendale. Kute ke ngenxa
yokungapili kwabonakala ezinisela ukufudukela e Driefonteiu (e Mtshezi).
Lonto ibete umzi washukuma wonke ukumnqanda ngenxa yesimosake esihle
kubo bonke. Ngokubangu:—

  Xalanga elintsiba zinde Eligubungela bonke abalusizi. Akumfundisi,
  akukolwa Bonke kuye bayafana, Benziwe ngonamandla onke. Ngotanda
  u’melwane Njengoko azitandayo yena.

Makahambe ! noko sililayo w o fi k e awenze lomsebenzi nalapo ayakona.
Ngumxhasi omkulu wemfundo nankuya £1 Jafta unyana wake wokupela
(ngapandle kwentwana encinane) e Healdtown. Sisiva ne Edendale College
yapenjwa ngabo no Mr. Joshua Dlamini, ngumxhasi omkulu wo Lizwi,
ubengumshumayeli engenisa iramente ezimbini, ngumxhasi omkulu we Mvo
ZABANTSUNDU. Wanga angandlela ntle u Mr. Ndaba, kuba umka intliziyo zetu
zimhlope ngakuye.

Kuti nga Mgqibelo utile apa kuqubisane ngalomdlalo webhola ekatywayo i
Eagle Club yase Edendale College ne Mountain Club yase Zwaartkop College
kwayabantsundu, epatelwe u Rulumeni ngo Mr. Smith no Mr. Bryant Cele
owafutwa e Lovedale. Aina Zwaartkop nyadlala ngokomeleleyo, kodwa
awakabiyiyonto kwi Edendale. I Edendale yoyisa nge 4 goal to Nil. Mhle
umzi wo Mr. Cele, njengoko saka sanokuwubona xa siyokushumayela.

Kuti kwangalo Mgqibelo kanti u Mr. E. Msimang selebete kwanuka lutungayi
emzini. Wenze isikumbuzo somhla wokuzalwa konyana wake u Hananiab.
Nditeta ke uyazi nawe impi yakuti kokubheka pantsi kwempumlo yanto-
ntelana yabeta indlu yema ngembambo. Nditi mna wawumhle lomgcobo kuba
wenziwa emini kuko nabafundisi nabafana base Koliji. Lonto ayifani
nomdaniso wapesheya kwe Nciba. Ngenyeimini sizinto zontatu sasiwelela
ngase Cedara apo kufundisa u Mr. Jer. II. Langeni owayese Lovedale.
Lomfo samangaliswa ukumfumana esenoluya tando sambona sise ngama Bhoyisi
enalo xa sasihambele e Lovedale apo. Ngoku into endiyibona se-ibhaliwe
ebusweni bake yile : “ Xa ndingenalo utando, ndinje ngo simbali
ekencezayo.” Ungumshumayeli wase Wesile netitshala yakona. Waziwa njengo
mqeqeshi ofanelekileyo wabafana. Nangoku abafana abase Edendale College
abebepantsi koqeqesho lwake banezimilo ezibalulekileyo kunabanye. Nabo
bayashumayela kwimizi engapandle. As the teacher is, so are the
children.

  Ndiyavumelana mna no Mr. Jabes Mlife xa ati makubeko iklasi pakat
  kolutsha lwase Edendale. Maninzi ama fokotshela afanele ukuba yi 3rd
  Class kanye.

IDULI LE NKONJANE.—E Komani zake kunene e Town Hall; into engatandwayo i
ngabapati bomzi leyo. Bate ke bamisa umfo ukuba azichite izindlu
zezintaka ngomhlakulo omde. Zijokile zona ngokwaka, waye lomfo ebeke
kancinane ewuchita umsebenzi wazo. Kwakubon’ uku­ba kunjalo zibize
intlanganiso ezite ukusuka kuyozawuza, ziriwula lomonakalisi,
zimrweinpa. Ude wabaleka waqudalala pantsi kodonga Iwendlu: suka
zengweve pezu kwake intaka zimxhola. Kucolwe ishumi elinambini
ezityumkileyo kukusuke ayama ngodonga lwendlu xazimngungeleyo. Ayikaziwa
into eyakwenziwa yi Council okwangoku zisoyisile intaka.

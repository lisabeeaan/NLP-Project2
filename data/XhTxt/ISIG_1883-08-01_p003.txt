Intlanganiso yabafundisi ifumene idinga ku Rulumeni lokuba akasokwenza
into abesongela ukuyenza—ukutengisa nakubani otandayo imihlaba
esezikolweni esazeke ingahlaulelwanga i kwit rent ixesha elide.
Abafundisi bati imihlaba leyo mayifuuyanwe kwangabantu abahlala
kwasezikolweni apo. Linyatelo elikulu eli ilitabatileyo i Conference,
abebefanelwe kukulitakazela abamnyama.

eliyinceke leli bati lele Mpawana; sibahlebela abalesi singati elona
silitaudayo leli kutiwa Yintlaulelo Yesigidimi. Siliqwalasela elobala
kwelanamhla ipepa sibona ngentliziyo ezitomaleleyo indawana ezitile
esinga singazipaula. Asinayo nencinane intandabuzo xa siti ukubonakala
kwama ledi pakati Kwengwevu nododana olwamkela ipepa lohlanga, kongenisa
uvuyo kwintliziyo ezininzi. Abafundi bamabali entlanga bangaba
basebeyifumene indawo yokuba apo amaledi angafundiswanga, ahlanjululwa,
amadoda, abafazi, amadodana, amantombazana, intsapo—wonke lomhlambi uba
zidodo, izinto ezinqapeleyo ; kuba kaloku ukondla nokukulisa kuse
zandleni zonina. Ngxatshoke. E Sigidimini apa kulapo kuxoxwa kona
ngezinto zokunyusa uhlanga. Yinto entleke ukuba onina, nabaya kuba
ngonina bohlanga bayazi into efunekayo kubo ngokufunda indaba
ezinokufunyanwa esi Gidimini. Umtandazo wabahlobo bohlanga ke emini nase
busuku ngewuba ngoti : Au, akwaba onke amaledi ebengabatabati nabafundi
be Sigidimi njengala mane siwabonayo ! !

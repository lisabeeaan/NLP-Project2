III,—Ndifike kwilizwi lesitatu nditi elinene lifike lamsingisela ezantsi
lomfana ukumeyelisela entshabalalweni. Kuba lisuke latini? Alimyalanga,
alimbonisanga imfaneleko yake, alenzanga malinga okumgodusa, alitanga,
hamba ndikupeleke ndikuse kowenu, alitanga unjenje ufanele ukugoduka
ubuyele kwakuyihlo uye uzibike. Hayi, lisuke lati elinene, akuko mntu
unokuhlala apa engasebenzi, tina apa sisekeleze indyebo yasemhlabeni, ke
wonke umntu olapa makabe nayo into ayenzayo eyokwandisa umzi wam; nanko
emtuma ukuba aye esibayeni akupe ugxuba oluya Iwehangu. Uke wenqumama,
waselevakala umninimzi esiti ngomsindo, ngezwi elipakamileyo. Umele ni?
Akuva na nditi hamba uye kwalusa ihangu zam eziya. Waselesiya
kulomsebenzi atunywe wona. Asinto ilusizi ukubona umntwana womntu equba
ihangu esiya kuzalusa. Akwaba ebetunywe msebenzi wumbi, akwaba bekutiwe
maka- ye kwalusa inkomo, nokuba ebenikwe umsebenzi wenkwenkwe kwatiwa
maka- luse amatole, nokuba ngamatakane egu- sha, hayi kodwa into embi
yile yokude atunywe ukwalusa ihangu. Elinene be- libonakalisa ukuba
lomfana alimtembile lide liti nje makaluse into angazanga ayibone
kowabo. Oku akwenziwayo kuyangqina okokuba ubuntu bupelile akuseko
mntu’umtembe nganto. Lomnumzana umqetulela entshabalalweni ade amtume
ukwalusa iramncwa elimikwa incole kangaka. Upume nazo na ke? Uti
ebenokutini eselezinikele nje emandleni, elulaulweni lomnini wezihangu?
Nanko eziquba ehamba nazo, uselebonile naye okokuba uyakufa
engawubambanga lomsebenzi. Zaluswa nam- hla ngumfo ongazanga awupate
umsebenzi onjengalo. Kwalusizi, kwakubi, ukude ati umfana onekowabo
obengaba-

nawo amalungelo ukuba ugodukile, abo- nakale ehamba emva kwehangu.
Kufuduka kwake kowabo okubange oku, kulahla kwake uyise okuvelise oku,
kuyiketa kwake impahla yake emke nayo okumenze oku, kuyic'ita kwake
lompahl’ ake okumhlisele oku. Makahambe nezahangu zelonene ziqamo
zemikwa yake yobubedengu ezo. Kumhlana aqalayo ukuviswa ubunzima bemikwa
yake ekohlakeleyo. Asimfana ukuvana nento embi,. ehleli kowabo nokuba
ihangu ibiliramncwa elifuyiweyo ngunakanye kutiwe nguyise, mntwana quba
ihangu eziya uye kuzalusa. Kowabo ubeqele ukukangela inkomo namhlana
atandileyo ngokwake ukwalusa ; mhlenikweni wafudukayo kowabo wayequba
inkomo, namhla bupelile ubuntu etunywa nje lamaramncwa. Akamlulamelanga
uyise ekaya namhla uyabona naye ukuba akanakuligqita ilizwi lalenkosi
imtuma lomsebenzi. Ebenga- vumanga kupatwa kakuhle nguyise,. nanku
namhla engenakunceda akupatwa kangaka ade atunywe umsebenzi
ongatandekiyo. Ebengavumanga kuhlala ngokonwaba endlwini yakowabo, nanku
namhla egxotelwe ezindle paya, kusitiwa kuye hamba uye kwalusa ihangu
imini ipela. Ebengavumanga kuhlala noyise- nabazalwana bake, kwibotwe
lakowabo lakomkulu, nanku namhla kusitiwa nge- guuya hamba uye kuba
likolwane neha- ngu. Nanko eziquba engatyanganto- engumfana obeqele
ukuhluta wesuka wayicekisa indyebo yakowabo. Ukuba ebehleli ekaya ubuntu
ngebahlumayo, waya enyuka ngokunyuka, oku alapa nakuba esemzini
wesityebi uye enyatelelwa pantsi. Kowabo ebengunyana, apa ngumkonzi nje,
into yokutunywa imisebenzi eselibal’uqule abanye. Ubehle waqonda naye
ukuba inkonzo yalomzi inzima. Nalomnumzana akatshongo nokuti hamba ndiya
kukuvuza ngento etile,, ndiya kukunika imali etile, hayi, ubusela ukudla
lomfana kunamhla, uyaqauka yindlala, nokude awuvume lomsebenzi ukohlwe
liqinga enza ngalo ukuze ayizuze into yokudla, nokuba utunywe ntoni
useleyixolele kunokuba afe yindlala.

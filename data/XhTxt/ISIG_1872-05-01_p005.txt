LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, MAY 1, 1872.

No. 20.

I-CALCUTTA.

ISIXEKO ESI LIKOMKULU EASE INDIA.

INDIA.

LEMIFANEKISO ekwesi Sigidimi ibonisa isixeko se Calcutta (ukupinyiselwa
kutiwa Kalkatta), nomzi wama India.

I-Calcutta sisona sixeko sikulu kwezinye zelo lizwe kutiwa yi India.
Ubude base zimayile ezine, ububanzi zintatu. Abantu abakuso bangapezulu
kwamawaka angamakulu asixenxe (700,000). Simi kumlambo obanzi abati yi
Hooghly. Ububanzi bawo ngase Calcutta buyima- yile. Mininzi imikombe
emikulu emana inyuka isihla kulomlambo ukuzisa impa- kona.

Omnye umfanekiso ubonisa umzana wama India, apo intlalo yahlukileyo
kanye

kweyase Calcutta, isixeko esinobutyebi obukulu nesixineneyo ngabantu.
Kuyo yomibini imifanekiso kuko indlovu. Lonto ngokwayo ibonisa ukuba
kuko isahluko esikulu pakati kwe India ne Africa. E- India inkoliso
yabantu ihlumile, ifundile ; bate ke kwezinye izinto abazenzileyo
bayifundisa indlovu ukuba ibasebenzele nje ngehashi nenkabi kwelilizwe.
Nje ngoko nobona nani emifanekisweni, ifundiswa ukutwala imitwalo
nokutsala into ezinzima; kanti noko ungako umsebenzi wayo iti E-Africa,
xa izingelwayo kube kufunwa inyama nempondo zemixaka kupela.

I-India lilizwe elikulu noko lingebanzi ngange Africa. Abantu abakuyo
bapantse bazizigidi ezimakulu mabini (200,000,000).

Ilaulwa yi Governore etunyelwa nguku- mkanikazi wase England. Lo
Governore ilaula iminyaka etile, imali eyizuza ngonyaka ibe ziponti
ezimawaka amashumi mabini anantlanu (£25,000), singayibali indlu nezinye
intwana ezizuzayo. Iqukiwe yonke into ayizuza ngomnyaka umntu opete
lomsebenzi ibaziponti ezi £30,000.

I-Governore yokugqibela ibingu Lord Mayo. Usand’ ukubulawa ligeza, abati
ngu Sbere Ali. Akuzanga kubeko tyala linjengeli E-India lokubulala umntu
osihlalo sipakame kangaka.

Kwi Sigidimi esilandelayo sonibalisela ngalo elo lizwe nabantu balo,
nango- kubulawa kuka Lord Mayo.

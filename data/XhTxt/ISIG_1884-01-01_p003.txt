U RULUMENI NA BESUTU.

Inkosazana igqibe kwelokubamkelela esandleni sayo a Besutu ekubeni
leminyaka ilishumi linesine idlulileyo bebesepantsi kokupatwa lulaulo
olu lumi e Kapa. Lubamkele nje ulaulo iwe Nkosazana luyazi ukuba u
Masupa usengqukuva, ekubonakalayo ke ukuba seluzimisele ukumpata ngesiko
ukuba kude kwafuneka epetwe ngolohlobo. Ibamkele kwakona ibehle
yaluvakalisa uluvo Iwayo ngeyo- na ndlela baza kupatwa ngayo.—Kuko
umnumzana oza kuba ngamehlo nesandla serulumeni yapesheya: kumiselwe
kanjako ukuba kubeko iqela elimnandi lamapolisa amnyama. Oku kubuyiselwa
kwabo kulaulo Iwapesheya kuyinandipise yonke into enga u Sutu lungasinga
pambili lunoxolo, na Besutu ngokwabo batomalele kunene. Abahlobo
babamnyama banqwenela ukuba aba Tembu nama Mfengu manikelwe ezandleni
zenkosazana nawo. Intloya iya kucaca kwakuhlangana i Palamente.

KUNI MABANDLA E MBUMBA.

(SILWA-NGANGUBO.)

Mabandla akowetu. Endaleni ib’isiti indoda xa iyala ama- kwenkwe apuma
esutwini, inge iyawatuma—Iti “ Hi makwe- dini ke namhla ningamadoda; ize
nixalise abafazi bamanye amadoda; ize nixele igusha nenkomo zabanye
abantu; ize nibulale abantu; ize nibe zinto ezingateni namntu—nidele
oyihlo kuba ningamadoda nani namhla.” Kanti esitsho nje umfo uteta into
abafanele ukuzilumkela bangazenzi. Itsho ke i “ Nyashawe ” kunye nombali
we Mpau—Baveni ngolo hlobo. Umfo we Mpau uti kuni, condobezani, cokisani
ukuha- mba kwenu ninga dyuduzi, msani ukwenza itywabazo noyitusa
inyamakazi yenu. Ngazwi’nye ngeba uti umfo wopau, msan- ukutyeda
nidubule ninganxdshanga. Msani ukuqala ngokuti sifuna ukutumela u Bani
no Bani e Palamente kanti aninazo izixobo ze voti—He. Ningafumane ninike
umntu imincili kanti anikalungeli kulwa.

Uti umfo we Mpau, zilungiseni pambi kokuba nimeme umfo oya kumela
amalungelo enu—ukuze angadaniswa akufika engaxelelwanga. Mfondini we
Mpau'! ungabi akusifundi Isigidimi. Uyivile na inteto emnandi kunene ka
Mr. Mr. J. Tengo-Jabavu ?—apo ayala kona lamagqala sizama nawo kule
Mbumba, sifumana lempendulo ati ufumana bependula ngayo. Wenzani lento
uti masifane sidubule umoya. Siyeke sicokise. I hastara elo unalo yinto
ebulala umxelo onentumekelelo. Ako amadoda ayifaneleyo i Palamente—kodwa
pambi kokuba siwanandipise soke saneliswe linani esinalo kwi Voters’
List. Umfo ka Goezar yindoda apa, kodwa ipina i vote, kuba kaloku
asinakutemba vote ya Mlungu. Noka Mackay simfumane enetshanisi
engapezulu e Tinara kunase Bayi, kuba kaloku asinakudibanisa imimandla
emibini. Siyeva ngendlebe into ehambisekayo ngase Dikeni ngo Mr. J.,
kodwa nakuba into leyo intle, siyibuka, siyicelela intsikelelo,
asinakunceda ukuti baqale ngokubala amatole engekaqanduselwa, xa sitata
umpu. nga wencwadi ka Mr. Jabavu kwese 16 Nov. Ukuba ngenene mfo we Mpau
ufuna iqinga kwi Mbumba nali—Seka Imbumba Kuze kubeko umqulu wamadoda
azi Registered Voters oya kuba lilifa lentlanganiso (mhlaumbi
ingafunyanwa i copy ematyaleni) ukuze ihlale isaziwa impi ukuba
ingakananina. Yoba njenge Domesday Book, aya kubonakala kuyo amandla
pambi kokuba kuhlatywe umkosi. Kuzanywa umkosi yi Mbumba. Ngati ukabela
ezimvikweni mfo we Mpawana. Ngenyanga ezayo ndoka ndikutumele i list
yaba voti esinabo apa e Bayi kunye nenani lezinye intlanga. Wobona apo
ukuba asikagqibi ukuxoba. Hamba wenjenjalo nawe.

UKUVALWA KWESIKOLO E-LOVEDALE.

Upumlo Iwexesha lokupela konyaka lomlisela nomtijana ofunda kwesi sikolo
luqale ngo 19 December 1883. Ngalo- mhla kwabako ingqungqutela yabazali
nabahlobo bentsapo eyayize kuyiputuma nokuposa amehlo kumatiletile
okugqi- bela. Umhla lo waumkulu emabalini ase Lovedale. Ku- mqulu
wabasemzini ababalulekileyo sapaula u Rev. E. Solomon, wase Bedford,
Rev. James Read, wase Katala, Rev. John D. Don, wase Qonce, no
Honourable John Laing, wase Mpofu.

Isihlalo sagcinwa ngu Rev. Edward Solomon, owangeni- swa ngu Rev. Dr.
Stewart ote u Mr. Solomon ngumhlobo omdala we Lovedale. Ubuhlobo bake
ububonakalise nge- misebenzi enje ngokutumela amadodana ebandla lakowabo
ukuba aze kufunda e Lovedale, watsho walata ku Rev. G. Tyamzashe, no
Rev. J. van Rooyen, no Rev. T. S. van Rooyen, no Mr. George Williams,
omakabe usemacibini ompakati we Africa namhla esisa indaba ezilungileyo
kwi- mfama zelozwe.

Esitabatile isihlalo ucele u Rev. J. D. Don ukuba avule ngomtandazo.
Ekovile ukwenjenjalo u

REV. E. SOLOMON upose amazwi wati unexesha elide engumhloho wesikolo
sase Lovedale, kususela kwiminyaka emashumi mabini anesi- tandatu
edlulileyo oko wabeke weza nomkuluwa wake u Mr. Saul Solomon. Into
emkolisayo kukuba amasebe afundiswayo engawapaka- mileyo. Onyana bake
bafunda apa, okwesake isiqu ucinga ukuba indawo abazifikeleleyo namhla
zibangwe sisiseko esihle nempembelela zemini zobuntwana abazifumana apo
e Lovedale. Eyona nto iyintloko sikukufumana amabaso, yingqeqesho eyona
nto—ukuba ubani na- bani afumane indawo yake efanelekileyo pakati
kwebandla elikanyise- Iweyo. Intsapo emnyama mayibulele imigudu
yabahlobo babamnyama abase Palamente nabangekuyo. Intshaba zokunyuswa
kwabamnyama zifumana intonga kwizinto ezinjengokuba udodana olutile
lungaqubi ngokundileka. Bekunqweneleka ukuba amadodana abeneratshi nge-
ndawo ezipakamileyo azifumanayo ngenxa yemfundo. Akuko nto idluliseleyo
ekuncediseni ukuhambela pambili kwesi sikolo kunokuba amadodana abonise
ngentlalo yawo ixabiso lemfundo. Obona buciko buyakuncedisa ihambiselo
pambili yezikolo, nakuba intshaba zazo zininzi, bobenyaniso nezenzo—bona
bugqitile kobamazwi. Ukuba ubani usicaka makacikoze ngokutembeka
nokusebenza lukuni, ukuba uyititshala makangazifuyi asebenzele abo
apakati kwabo, ukuba ngu- mbali maka nyaniseke abeyiyo yonke into
elungileyo. Ngentlanganiso ezayo ye Palamente amalinga ayakwenziwa
ukuncipisa imali encedisa imfundo, bekufuneka ke izikolo zibonisile
ukuba imali ec'itelwa emfu- ndweni yabantsundu ayilahlwa. Abafundiswa
mabazi ncede bancede nabanye. Ukuze bafunde kakuhle mabanga buduzeli,
bazondelele bekutele—yiyoleyo into ekujongwe kuyo ngalemfundo
bayinikwayo. Ze konke kulungelelane mababe noloyiko lo Tixo, zaziya
kufaneleka izimilo zabo. Eyigqibile inteto yake u Mr. Solomon u cele u

REV. DE. STEWART ukuba afunde ingxelo yomnyaka etabate ixesha elide.
Ingxelo ibonisa ukuba akubangako sahluko sikulu ekumeni kwamanani xa
atelekiswa nawonyaka odlulileyo, nakuba ehlile kwindidi ezipambili
anyukile kwezisemva. Ngecala elisingisele engqobokweni lonyaka ube
namatamsanqa awo. Intlanganiso zemvu- selelo zo Rev. Dr. Somerville, no
Rev. Mr. no Mrs. Mountain zibe luncedo olukulu. Amalungu eramente 120;
abafuni 118; abangeni 51; abantwana 13. Emva kokuvelela ezindawo ingxelo
ingena ekukuseleni i Lovedale entetweni ekoyo pakati kwabatile yokuba
ifundisa aba- mnyama ukuteta isi Hebere nezi Grike nezi Latini.
Abafundisi bati akuko nyaniso kulo nteto, kuba ku 510 afunda e Lovedale
ba 5 abafu- nda isi Latini, kwaba akuko namnye omnyama osifundayo ;
batatu abafunda isi Grike bamhlope bonke, babini abafunda isi Hebere
nga- baza kuba ngabafundisi. Sona isikolo siyazama ukuzikulula ekufundi-
seni ezinteto.

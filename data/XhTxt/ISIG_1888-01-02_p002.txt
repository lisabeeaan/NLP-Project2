IMIGUDU YOBOMI.—Kuko ibali elilusizi elibonakele kwi- pepa lama Bulu i
Zuid Afrikaan. Liti yati ndoda itile pesheya iblala kumzana oyi Wallis,
indoda ebifudula isebenza emalableni, ete yakubona ukuba ayipumeleli
kona- yasingisa e America, yona yodwakuqala yake yalusbiya usa, po kwase
Wallis. Ite ifikile e America kwake kwaqubeka kakuble, yamane ibalelana
nomfazi wayo, yade yake yabuye- la kwa sekaya ixeshana. Ite ekupindeni
ukuya kwase America, yabamba nonyana bayo, kwati exesbeni yalandela
nenkosikazi. Ite ifikile kona, yafika umyeni wayo sele nto yimbi, kwaye
bekufanele ukuba njalo kuba ubeseF ezeke omnye umfazi. Wati ke noko
sekunjalo, wake wenza inkwalambesa yokungati usa mnonelele noko umkake
lowo. Ute ngomnye umbla wasela umoya nomkake lowo, benza uhanjana nje
kodwa, kanti umsa kumnxuma onzulu kune­ne. Kute kwakufika apo, wegweje
wanto yimbi we’ rutu ipistoli wati—“ Likangele uligqibelise nambla nje.”

AMABUTYE EMPILO. Uvakele esiti—Zinyulele ngokwako kwezinto zimbini, kuko
ukudutyulwa ngompu, kuko ukuzi- posa ngokwako kulo mnxuma ufe. Umfazi
unyule ukuzi- posa emnxunyeni. Etsbilo wati lo mfazi.—“Myeni wam, maze
ungandikangeli xa ndiziposa kweli liwa, maze ubekise paya ubuso bako.”
Indoda yatshikila okunene yamni- kela umva. Yavakal’ isiti le nkazana
entliziyweni yayo— Nankoke ungab’udlelwe yim”! Yazibuta into- kazi
yesuka ipela, yamti wolo ngamapango kunene, yamposa kulo nkemfu yelo
liwa waya kunqakaqe- ka ezantsi esel’ ete tyobotyobo tu, kwaba kuyapela,
yago- duka intokazi. Ifikile ekaya ifike yawa ngazo kubamelwa- ne,
saputunywa isidumbu eso sendodayo ekute emva kwe- ntsukwana ezitile,
yagoduka intokazi, yasingisa kwase Wallis apo yayivele kona. [Lumkelani
izitembu zinge- nisa isheshegu.—ED. Sigidimi

INDODA EBULEWE YINKAZANA.—Kuko umfo obulewe yinkazana e Victoria West.

kwaxa- bana, suke lomfazi wakupa ngebotile, watsbo qoro entloko kulomfo,
watsbo lwahlanza uselwa.

pesheya kolwandle kwela ma-French. Kutiwa lemipu itatisela into emgama

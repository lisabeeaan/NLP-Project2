I GOVAN BURSARY.

KUYA ziswa ukuba kuya kubako i Bursary ezi mbini, eyoku qala zi £10,
eyesi bini zi £5; zombini ke, zezo mnyaka omye. Ziya ku gqatselwa ngawo
lo umnyaka.

Izimiso zezi:—

1.  I Bursary ezi, zivulelwe udodana Iwama bala ouke, aba lukolo lulolwe
    Protestant, baze nange zinye indlela ba nyuleke.

2.  Aba Ngeni abaza ku viwa mababe sebe lu gqitile olu viwo loku
    ngeniswa, lwase Lovedale.

3.  Aba Ngeni maba vunywe kuqala yintlanganiso yaba Pati be Mali, na yi
    Education Board yase Lovedale.

4.  Aba Ngeni botumela ama gama abo ngepepa, kunye ne saziso sesi milo
    sabo esi kolekayo, ku Rev. J. D. Don, o gama libekwe apa, kungabi
    ngapantsi kwe nyanga, pambi kwe mini yo viwo.

5.  Uviwo luya kwenziwa ngoku bala ema pepeni; luya kuba se Lovedale nge
    veki yesi bini ka August, 1880.

6.  Aba zifumeneyo i Bursary baya ku funda e Lovedale, nge xa aba se
    nazo.

7.  Zinga hlutwa ezi Bursary ngo ku swela isimilo aba ba nazo. Aba gwebi
    balonto baya kuba, ngaba Gcini be Mali ne Education Board yase
    Lovedale.

Izinto zo Viwo.

(3.) Uku xela ngo kuma komhlaba (Descriptive Geo­graphy).

(4.) Imbali ye Great Britain, kususela kwi 1603 kude kuze kuba kweli
xesha.

8.  .) Ukuzazi izi Balo ; kweyo I. neye II. ka Samuel; ne Zenzo zaba
    Postile.

King Williams Town, February 23, 1880.

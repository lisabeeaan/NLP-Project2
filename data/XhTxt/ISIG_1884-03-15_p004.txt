  AMANGESI NONYULO LWABANTSUNDU

Lenteto ilandelayo siyiguqula kwipepa lase Rini i-Jouranl epantse
yawufumana umxelo wabantsundu ngento zovoto. Liti:—

Kaloku into ebesisayibone iqubeke mayelana nonyulo Iwase Qcnce,
ipengululwe, yaxelw’ intw’eyiyo lunyulo lwase Victoria East (Dikeni).
Abantsundu bakona bate ngenxa yokunganeliswa, ngamadoda ahleli nabo,
baselesiti ukuzilahla nanko beya kuzilahlela kowasemzini ovela kumgama
wangase ntshonalanga. U Mr. Advocate Innes impumelelo yake uyifumene
ngenxa yetemba abantsundu abanalo kuye, elilelokuba wobamela,
abakangelele amalungelo abo. Kuko intlanganiso yabantsundu apa,
kubonakala okokuba ama Xosa nama Mfengu umzekelo wama Bulu akatatanga
xesha ukuwulandela. Nokokuba na bona abantsundu besiqingata sase
Victoria East banentlanganiso enjenge yama Bulu asazi, kodwa baqube
kanye ngokohlobo lwama Bulu, kuba bete bahlangana ngokunyula u Mr. Innes
; saye ke sisiva ukuba u Mr. Johnson yena ubezilahlele ezandleni zama
Bulu nama Jelimani, ate ke amnyula waba ngowesibini. Ngazwinye singati
ama Ngesi wona namhla akanabani amnyule wapumelela, kwaye ke oko
kubangwe kukusuke angevani, nangapezulu kukusuka bangabi nabudlelane
nabamnyama. Sibona tina ukuba, ukuba bebete bahlalelana nabo, benza
ukuba babatembe ukuba ngamadoda anokwenza ukuba abamnyama babe nawo onke
amalungelo abafanelwe ngawo, ngebengadanga bacandise—nabo beya kufuna
indoda engabamelayo kwezinye indawo. Namhla fan’ ukuba isifundo
abasifundisiweyo abamhlope base Victoria East, abasayi kwandule
basilibale. Into namhla ebifanele ukwenziwa ngabamhlope kwindawo nge-

ndawo, kukulinga ukuhlalelana kakuhle nabamnyama, abapakati kwabo.
Esinye isiposo esenziwe ngabamhlope, yinto yokusuke liti elo iqelana
libe nowalo, elo nowalo, njalo-njalo, into ke leyo ebete ivoti zabo
zalusali. Lingani ke namhla bamhlope ukuvisisana nabantsundu abapakati
kwenu, ukuze ke bangacandacandi ukufuna amadoda avelana nabo, kodwa bade
banifumane nitembeke ngokwaneleyo kubo, baze ke batsale ngokwaneleyo
kuni.

U Mr. P. LUSASENI ute uyicase ngokukulu indawo ushukunyiswe ngu Mr.
Gontshi. Ute i Transkei imelwe ukuba iyixase i Koloni. Asingeti isininzi

Hofmeyr ungapela ngenxa yokuba i Koloni ipumelele esibenweni. Into
efunekayo yivoti eninzi yabantsuudu,
asizizo ingxande.

U Rev. J. SIKWEBU ute akaboni ngeniselo e Transkei iyakubako ngokuba
bona into abafuna ukuyibona yinqubelo pambili yohlanga. Ikoloni kudala
ivota akukonto iyenzayo isimbuku ngokokuvota. Bona nakuba betanda
ukuvelana ne Koloni kwinto zohlanga ezisakuba ziziroro, ukuba kuqala
bakangelele ilungelo le Transkei, ukuba ivoti imi njengoko ibinjalo
akwabiko mketo, abantu abambalwa abanokukanya bangagqunyelelwa yivoti
eninzi e Transkei. Nokuba e Koloni abalumkileyo bakuqondisa amaqaba
ivoti baninzi, bambalwa e Transkei: gako ke amaqaba zingazisulu nevoti
zawo ukusetyenziswa ngamabedengu angatanda ukungenisa utywala bo Mlungu
e Transkei. Ngako akaboni Lungelo ukuba i Transkei ixasa i Koloni
isibeno sayo.

U Mr. LAMBATA MGIDI wateta kabukali exasa u Mr.Gontshi esiti siyakutini
na ukubenela ivoti esingekayifumani nokuyifumana, umketo woxande
uyilungelo e Transkei, ufanele kanye umteto wezinto e Transkei. Elipike
lingene shushu imini yonke litabate iyure ezisixenxe. Lendawo ibekiwe
evotini, intlanganiso yonke
ngapandle kwamanene matandatu, abone ukuba i Transkei ayinakungena
esibenweni sase Koloni.

UNYULO.

U Mr. GONTSHI ungenise indawo yomntu omhlope omakanyulwe, waxela amagama
abazigqatsileyo abangaba—Col. Griffith, Messrs John Leach (Poplar Grove)
J. K. Jackson, Xalanga, S. J. Copeland, Barkly East, no Advocate
Tamplin, wase Grahams Town.

U Mr. Gontshi ute intlanganiso ingatembisi ukunyula nokuba ngubani na
kula manene ngapandle kokuba inenelo lide libale emapepeni imvo zalo
(Manifesto) ngendawo ezicukumisa abantsundu e Transkei—ize elonene libe
nokwanelisa imibuzo yabantsundu ngendawo abafuna bezenzelwe.
Intlanganiso yonke yaba zwi nye ekubeni kwenziwe njengoko u Mr. Gontshi
amisilevo.

  IZIBONDA NE TITSHALA.

U Mr. GONTSHI ufunde icebo le gqweta le Jaji u Mr Advocate Innes we
Supreme Court yase Cape Town ngendawo yokuba izibonda ne titshala zicaka
zika Rulumente kangangokuba zibepantsi ko Mteto we Service na?
Intlanganiso igqibe ngokuba kunyulwe o Messrs Mazamisa, A. Gontshi, no
J. Mvinjelwa ukuba babe ngabatunywa kwintlanganiso ka Tungumlomo e Qonce
ukungenisa uluvo Iwentlanganiso mayelana nesibeno.

U Mr. GONTSHI ubonise ukuba nge 4th ku October, kuyakubako intlanganiso
enkulu e Ntlambe, yazo zonke Imantyi ezipantsi kuka Captain Blyth, kunye
nenkosi nezibonda. Ute ngenxa yokutiywa kwale ntlanganiso, umelwe kukuba
aze abeko e Ntlambe, eve into eyakutetwa, ukuze ayise emapepeni, ngoko
ke akangebi nako ukuba se Ntlambe nge 4th October ize ibe se Qonce nge
6^(th) October. Intlanganiso iyibonile londawo, icitakele yaba iyapela.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1877.

ndisaninika izwi endilinikwe ngu Mhlekazi lokuti hlalani pantsi.

Site sonke sambulela ngento asixelele yona noko singenelanga kakuhle
kuba ebeqube inteto ye Governor kwakupela akasixelela eyake ingcingane
ngale mfazwe ipakati kwe Koloni nama Gcaleka. Tina sipantsi kwake sibe
siba uyakusiqubela ngokusemhlotsheni ezake ingcingane. Simbuzile ke
ngoko npendule ngeliti, “Andinako ukutsho ukuti kukangakanani na
ukuvelana kuka nantsi nonantsi nama Gcaleka, nokuba ngobanina
abangatanda ukutelela kuma Gcaleka balwe no Rulumeni. Mna okukwam
anditeni nezonto. Ndingumntu ka Rulumeni, ndiya kwenza into endiyi
yalelwa nguye. Ngoku kutiwe mandihlale pantsi, nditi hlalani nani. Akuko
nto iya kwenziwa ndim ecasene no Rulumeni, qinisekani yilonto.”

Kute kwakubuzwa ukuba ucinga ntonina ngabagcini venkile abebesand’
ukusaba, ute asikuko nokuba imqumbisile lonto yokumka kwabo. Bemka
kungeko sizatu ede wabaxelela abanye ukuba mabahlale lixolile, yena
akaboni ngozi. Ute “ Angaba usisidenge Umxosa olwa no Mlungu, into eyaka
imikombe yokuza imikosi yenze ololiwe nemipu nekanunu neruluwa
nembumbulu. Bangenzani ke abantu abapete izikali kwabapete imipu? Nditi
ngoko Umxosa olwa no Mlungu angaba usisidenge. Hambani niba- xelele
bonke ukuba u-Siwani usisicaka sika Rulumeni, ulinde into ayi xelelwa
nguye.”

Into asixelele yona u-Siwani kulo ntlanganiso ibingeyiyo ingcingane yalo
mini, yinto endihlala ndimva futi eyiteta. Ubemi kula mazwi nanyakenye
oko bekungati kuza kubako izipitipiti. Nangapandle kwalonteto nam
andiboni kuba kuko nto pakati kwabantu baka Siwani. Bonke baquba indlela
zabo njengokungati akuko mfazwe. Bonke bakala ngela- nga banxakamele
imvula, bafun’ukulima amasimi abo.—Watchman.

AMA GCALEKA.

U-Rev. Mr Rayner wase Qonce ngomhla 31 ka September ushumayele
intshumayo ayi pete ngalomazwi angama Gcaleka: —“Kuyi minyaka emashumi
mahlanu zafikayo izitunywa zomqamlezo kuma Gcaleka. Amaxesha amaninzi
u-Rev. Mr Shrewsbury waman’ ukuhambela emzini ka Hintsa emsela '
i-Gospel. Ngenyameko yaba profeti bakudala, wazamela ukubonisa ubukulu
buka Kristu kulo nkosi ingumhedeni; wayi tyala ngokuse mhlotsheni into
eya kuhla ukuba akaiytabati: wayi shumayela lonto nase bantwini, kwada
kwapantsukungasali namnye Umgcaleka onga shunyayezwanga. Ekuqaleni
Ilizwi lamkelwa ngendelo, abanye bahlekisa ngalo, kwaza kupakati
latiywa. kwada ngemfazwe ye 1835 watshiswa umzi wesikolo nendlu
yomfundisi neyetyalike. U-Hintsa ngobugeza bake wazidubulisa ngompu
womkoloni indawo yake yatatyatwa ngu Sarili. Umfundisi wabuya wabuyela
ukuya kuxelcla unyana indawo ezazisisiwe kuyise. U-Sarili naye wayala
i-Gospel wangena kwabaxela inkomo, into leyo eya bangela ukuba apulukane
nomhlaba wake. Ngenxa yobubele endingati mhlambi ye mposiso ka Rulumeni
sabuya sabuyiselwa kuye isiqendu selizwi lake. Wabuya ke waba hlanganisa
abantu bake wayi nkosi ezipeteyo. Libuye laza kushunyayelwa kuye Ilizwi
lika Tixo, namhla lasiwa ngu Mgqika okoliweyo. U-Tiyo Soga wayi bongoza
le nkosi ukuba mayinike intliziyo yayo ku Tixo ayavuma noko yanyula
ukuhlala ebuhedenini nesizwe sayo; namhla ke isohlwayo sifikile, yona
nesizwe sayo iya kutshabalala.

“Kude kube ngoku inkosi zelakwa Xosa azivumi zonke ukumamkela u-Kristu,
ate ke ngoko unyana opakamileyo wapangwa okwake, abantu bahleli
ebunyameni bobuhedeni. Bazalwana lonto ayinakuhlala ixesha elide.
Akubonakali ukuba kuko noko inye yazo eya kulamkela Ilizwi, kunjalo ke
zonke zimelwe kukutshabalala. Inkosi engu mhedeni ixaswa bubuhedeni bayo
ukuze ime. Iminyaka emashumi mahlanu yomsebenzi wabafundisi ayikenzi
ukuba kuguquke noko inye inkosi yo mhedeni ; kunamhlanje kukona zintiye
ngentiyo egqitileyo u- Kristu. Isipelo soko sinye. “ Uya kuba pula
ngentonga yesincete, ubaqekeze njengembiza yombumbi.” Amaculo II. 9.

“Sekuminyaka ndabonayo ukuba ezizizwe azisayi kumanga u-Nyana,’
kusolokoke ndalindelayo ukuba lento ikoyo namhlanje iza kufika.”

AMAGEZANA.

Kwelinye lamapepa ase Bayi kubonakala ukuba kuko isipukupukwana so
mlungu esenze ubugeza obunje ngobo Jan Nongcuba. Omnye walamanene bati
zi Alfred Volunteers ufike kumi Amaxosa asixenxe pantsi kwe veranda
yendlu ka Mr Payne. Ufike wabuza ukuba ningamani na bati bakuti singa
Maxosa waselerola incula yake ehlaba komnye abati ngu Matros, wenza umda
emagxeni. Umbambile u-Matros wamtyala waya kuwa pantsi. Uvuke apo
wahlaba omnye umxosa ebunzi wambeta wawa. Igama lalo Mxosa alaziwa.
U-Matros ute kwabanye ngebepanga lencula. Bate xa bazama oko wabaleka
waya kungena kwindlu ebuhotela ebikufupi. Amaxosa lawo ati akangefunge
ukuba angamqonda lomntu xa ambonileyo. Ipepa elibalisa lonto liyiquba
lize liti, “Asikuko nokuba kulusizi ukuba lomntu abe wenze into enjalo,
abapate ngohlobo olubi kangaka abantu besizwe esixoleleneyo naso (esika
Sandile), aqale ucuku obeluya kuti mhlaumbi lunge- nise into yonke
enkatazweni.”

Kulusizi kanye ukuba loma Xosa engabugcinanga ubuso balomntu, kuba ukuba
ubefumanekile kube kuya kubonakala ukuba nomhlope oqala izipitipiti uya
kufumana isohlwayo esinzima. Kube kuya kuqondakala ukuba umteto ka
Rulumeni ngowokuba bakuselwe bonke abahleli ngoxolo nokuba bangama Xosa
nokuba nga Besutu nokuba nagma Mfengu.

INDLALA YASE INDIA.

E-INDIA kuko ngelixesha langoku indlala abati iya kubulala nangapezu
kwale mfazwe ikoyo pakati kwe Russia ne Turkey. Elinye lamapepa apesheya
eliteta ngayo liti “apo imfazwe ibulala amawaka lendlala ibulala
amashumi amawaka. Nangoku amadoda nabafazi nabantwana abasebebulewe yiyo
bapants’ukuba ngangabase koloni. Basafa ke yonke lemihla. Abantu
abasebepila kukuncedwa ngu Rulumeni bakwizigidi ezibini (2,000,000).”
U-Ru- lumeni uya zama ngawo onke amalinga okuncedisa, nabanye abantu
abamhlope abase India nase England bazama ukuhlanganisa imali yokuya
kukulula.

OBEBALEKA ISENZO SAKE.

NGASE Uitenhage kuko Umzulu owaye bulele umntu kwiveki zokugqibela zika
September. Kufunwe kwafunwa kulo macala akwavakala mkondo noko. Ute
kanti umnene uselengene emkombeni wasinga e-Kapa. Kude kwavela umntu ote
umntu onjalo umke nomkombe. Amangesi amadala ahlabe elucingweni,
kwayalezwa kumapolisa ase Kapa ukuba avale amazibuko. Ute okunene ufika
umkombe lowo aseleuhlangabeza amapolisa, wati esonwabile umnene weva
sekusitiwa siya kubamba ubulele umntu. Liti ipolisa ebeliye kumbamba,
kute kwakutshiwo waguba kodwa umnene akapendula. Ute akubuzwa igama lake
wazenza into engasivayo isi Ngesi. Usiwe kwa Mantyi wafika wati kona
yena ubesilwa akabadanga mntu. Ubuye ke waziswa kwase Bayi, wagqitiswa
kona weziswa e-Uitenhage apo sikolwa ukuba usekona elinde ukutetwa
kwetyala lake. Ukuba ebengazi ngapambili fanukuba uyakwazi namhla ukuba
umntu akanakusishiya isenzo sake.

ngento zelizwi. Ndandite ndiya kuteta naye kwakusasa ngalo mini

tuba. Ndite ngentliziyo sesohlangana ngebrekfesi. Okunene ngeloxesha
ndimbuze ukuba uya kucingela na ukulungiselela umpefumlo wake. Ute
ukupendula, “Kanene wena uza kuba kusoloko uteta ngezi zinto. Sibe
kuhlangana utete ngonqulo, ungati akusacingi nganto yimbi.” Ndite mna, “
Ewe, kungokuba emhlabeni apa kungeko nto ingangosindiso lompefumlo; ke
inkolo ku Nyana ka Tixo kupela kwento enokonwabisa umntu, imxase ngeyure
yokufa, imnike itemba eliqinisekileyo loku-

ngena izulu.” Ute, “ Unyanisile, nam ndihlale ndinqwena ukunga ndinga
ligqoboka, koko ndisika ndikohlwe ukuba ndingatini na.” Ndirole
Itestamente Entsha yam ndambuz’ukuba uke eve na ukuba uyinto
elahlekileyo pambi ko Tixo. Ute akuvuma ndamfu- ndela lamazwi ati,
“Owati ngokwake izono zetu wazitwala ngomzimba wake emtini,” nala ati, “
Ngaye lowo kuxelwa kuni uxolelo Iwezono.” Emva koko ndenze umtandazo

Mount Coke, September 20, 1877.

Nkosi, Mhleli we Sigidimi Samaxosa, nceda undi- fakele lombiko epepeni
elo lako; ndibika ukububa komfundisi wosapo wasekayapa, u-Joseph Nyoka.
Ubube ngolwe 19 luka September ukumka komhla; ubehliwe yicesini
yamkaulezela, akalala xa lingakanani. Ngumfana owafunda ixeshana elitile
e-Dikeni waza waya kufundisa e-Pewuleni, ukusuka apo uye kufunda
e-Nxukwebe.

Umfundisi wosapo ngumntu osetubeni elibalulekileyo, elingeyekileyo, kuba
uyila isizukulwana into emasize sibeyiyo. Intsapo ezi kamva ngabaninimzi
nabanikazimzi, izama ukulungisa abafo nentokazi eziyakuma zipate ilizwe.
Tina ke apa namhla sipulukwe ngomnye wabobantu banjalo, ibingumfana
otanda umsebenzi wake ewenza ngenyameko yonke ; bebetonyakalalisiwe
abazali babantwana kakulu nguye kwabonakala oko nangendlela yokumonga xa
afayo nokumsa kwabo apo umzimba uyekulinda uvuko Iwabafileyo, ngomhla
wokupela ; ubelilungu leremente. Izihlobo zake mazamkele esisaziso.

Newlands, September 21, 1877.

Nkosi Mhleli ndifuna ukunga ungandinceda undifakele lamazwana am
ambalwa, kwezo zintlu zesi Gidimi sako zimnandi kunene, ndinga ndingake
ndibalule indawana ezingati zipambene, kwincwadi ka Rev. E. G. Warner
yosuku Iwe 18 ku July nasemtetweni we 18 ka 1864.

U-Mr Warner ucukumise indawo ebuhlungu kuti tina bantu bantsundu, le
yokwa biwa kwamafa ngo mteto we shumi elinesibozo kwi 1864. Lomteto uze
nobuhlungu kuti bantu bantsundu, kuba uze kutintela ukufezwa kwento
ezibe zifezwa ngenxa engapambili, uze kupambanisa abazalwana, uze kucita
nentlalo entle yobumelwane.

Akwaba u-Mr Warner ubete waku misa ukuteta kwake kulencwadi yake,
njengoko silwaziyo uhlobo lokuteta kwake. Yinina ukuba azitintele, nawe
Mhleli ufane uzitintcle ngesizatu sokungabi mkulu komfazi wokuqala nje
ngokungati litintelwa kukungabi njalo ilizwi lika Tixo nokungati
izipitipiti ezi zivela ngokunga binjalo! Inqu yokudalwa kwaba fazi
yiyona nto itenina kanene ezipitipitini ezi nase kutinteleni ilizwi lika
Tixo.

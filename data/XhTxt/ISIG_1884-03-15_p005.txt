UMHLATI WABA-XELENGI.

U GENERAL GORDON ngomnye wamadoda adume kunene asema Ngesini ezi mini.
Igama lake like lapakati kwemicimbi elukuni kunene eyayamene nempato
nolaulo Iwentlanga zabahedeni. Nguye owayepete imikosi ngemfazwe
enengozi eyake yakwe lama Tshayina ngo 1854 ; i Egypt ngelinye ixesba
yaxoliswa nguye. Abo banexesha lokuqwalasela ihambiso yemicimbi kwela
pesheya bayazi ukuba ulaulo lwase Egypt olonganyelwe lolo Iwe Nkosazana
lungene ezinkatazweni ekungacacileyo zingaba zezinjanina ingozi ezingati
zibeko zivele ngakuma Arab ase Soudan. Kule nkohla nesanga ke ude wonke
umzi welama Ngesi wenza umemezo usiti makutunyelwe u General Gordon,
igora elidla ngokupumelela apo uninzi luncamayo. Ikubalo lale mambane
namhla lifunyenwe. Pambi kokusinga kwalo e Soudan libalele incwadi
komnye wabafundisi bapesheya u Canon Wilberforce. Uti ecela umtandazo
kwibandla elonganyelwe seso sicaka se Nkosi:—Ndinganyula imitandazo yelo
bandlana lincinane elidibene endlwini yako namhla kunendyebo zase
Soudan. Nditandazeleni ukuze ndibe nokuzindila kwanoku kokelwa ngu Tixo,
ze kanjako nomoya wokudumzela utetiswe upume kum.” Ngelinye ixesba emva
koku ubalele kwakona esiti, “ Ize nenze imibulelo kwi- ntlanganiso yenu
elandelayo yomtandazo. Xa benditwelwe zintliziyo zalo Makrestu,
ndifumene ku Tixo itamsanqa lakwa Moya ebendilifuna, ke ngoku ndipumle
ngoxolo emsingeni wentando Yake.”

ELIBALI silitunyelwa ngu Rev. J. Mjila liyakuvusa imipefumlo emininzi.
Inqubo yo Pezukonke yaziwa ngu Mr. Mjila kunokuba ayikunjuzwe siti.

MHLEKAZI OMHLE,—Mhleli we Sigidimi samanene ndincede undifakele
lamazwana esililo kwelo pepa lamatshawe, ndifuna ukubikela wena Mhleli
nezihlobo zam zonke. Igugu lam litatiwe yi Nkosi ngomhla weshumi
elinesibozo ku February 1884. Nditeta umzukulwana omkulu ogama lingu
James Impey Mjila intloko yendlu yakwa Mjila. Isifo asimenzanga mzuzu or
asimpanga xesha, zihlobo zam. Kupela wenze intsuku ezilishumi
linesihlanu ; ukufa kwecesine kwakunamandla amakulu, kwayitata ingqondo
yomtana kanye, saxakeka, sakohlwa, sawa ngamadolo sam’bongoza unyana
onegunya wopezu konke. Walila wati kum Tatakulu, (kuba ebecitsho
ukundibiza kwake) andilili ngenxa yobuhlungu bokufa, hai kungenxa yezono
zam. Wati akutsho wavakala etandaza. Nanga amazwi omtandazo wake— “
Nyana ka Tixo ndixolele izono zam ezobuntwana nezobudala endingazaziyo.”
Akubanga kanye etandaza ngalo mtandazo zihlobo, kungapezu kwamaxesha
alishumi em’bongoza umkululi wetu ngalo mazwi. Kwati kwakuhamba intsuku
wavakala endibiza esiti Tatakulu nditandazele. Kwati emzuzwini wavakala
evuma iculo ngokwama Ngesi, wati akugqiba wavakala esiti u anti wake,
ehlelinje lomntu ubeliciko kangaka na ? Wavuma uninakulu wake wati ewe
utitshala ubekolwa kakulu nguye. Wavakala endibiza ubutana wam
otandekayo esiti Tatakulu : ndasabela, wati eliculo ndilivumayo lelase
zulwini. Usishiye ngom-Vulo kusasa, watandaza ubutana wam kanti kupuma
kompefumlo. Wati uninakulu engazinto akugqiba ukutandaza wamnika iti eba
yena uhleli, akayiginya ; wandivusa kuba ubusuku bonke bendingalalanga,
wati andikolwa ngulomntu : ndavuka msinyane ndapata ngesandla, ndeva
ukuba uyemka okunene, ndati masitandaze satandaza okunene sagqiba. Wemka
zihlobo zam onga angemki. Andingewagqibi amazwi ake, wati kuninakulu,
makulu, wasabela— andizange ndiye emtshatweni ebusuku. Wati ngelinye
ixesha kumyeni wodade wabo empa iyeza lama Xosa, befuna nokumfuta ukuba
abile, ‘ Nihleli nje ningabahedeni, nisenamasiko abahedeni,’ basuka
boyika bamyeka. Wati ngelinye ixesha ndikulule Tatakulu ndibambe, ndada
ndavuma ke zihlobo. Andinaxesha, ndiyoyika nomhlekazi woti ndenza into
ende. Ityeya yake yenziwa nge £4. Abancwabi baba likulu elinamashumi
omatatu anesibini, abafundisi babe batatu. Wateta amazwi amakulu
umfundisi omkulu u Rev. T. Chubb. Owona mmangaliso mkulu lutando
Iwamaboyisi, ebefika amagqiza ngamagqiza, wada wati yena oka Rev. S.
Mzamo wenza into endingayaziyo, ndingateti ngoka Mamba, hai utando
Iwentanga zake zihlobo zam. Umntana endingayaziyo into epakati kwake
waba mnye, waba ngosisi- hlobo esikufupi kanye wonyana wam ongumfundisi
kwa Baca. U James Impey Mjila wazalwa ngo 9 August, 1869.—JAMES MJILA,
Ecwecweni Station, February 25, 1884.

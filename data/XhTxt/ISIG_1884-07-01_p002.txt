2 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA JULY1, 1884

Ehleli endlwini yake yemicimbi u Mcokeli welipepa ufikelwe (ngo 25 June)
sikumbuzo sokuba kuko incwadi e Posini emelwe kukululwa ngepeni ezine.
Uvakele esiti iyawa ngu Magna Mzimasi ke lowo uzingise ngokumrafisa
ngoluhlobo. Eputumile, kwilishwa lake, ufike kunjalo okunene. Le krusi
kade itwelwe siti; bfikile ixesha lokuba sizibike. U David Magna Thomas
akaqali kusitwalisa nzima ngoluhlobo. Kanti kunganina konke oku? Lutyuka
Iwenteto ngomcimbi obufanele amadoda anokubala “in memoriam” (nguye
lowo) yedlaka “ Letshawe nezipakati zika Ngqika, Inkosi itshawe u
Sandile nompakati u Dukwana.” Bonke abafundi betu abacingayo bosivumela
xa siti lomcimbi ungapaya kwamagxana ka Magna. Siyilesile lencwadi
isidle kangaka njengezinye eseke zasidla zivela kwelo tyendyana sayiposa
kumnyazi wamapepa atshiswayo ngendawo yokungavisiseki kwayo. Silila
ngemali yetu epelayo.

Umhlambi omdala ose Gqili ongayanga ezikolweni utonyalalisiwe kunene
yimibuzo eyabanga impendulo esiqwala ka Mr. Augustus Bell ngezipatelele
ezintweni zonyulo e Aliwal North. Omnye walempi indala ofuna uhlanga
lulungiselelwe, noselezilungiselele ukuba bako abamkokelayo, wenjenje
ukubomboloza ngenx’ enokusweleka kokukanya e Herschel:—

Mhleli, ityala lokuyiloza kwabantu base Gqili silibeka kulempi ivela
ezikolweni; ayinantlanganiso zokukanya. Lomanene ndiwalandelisa nemizi
afunde kuyo—Rev. J. Mnyazi, Healdtown ; Mr. Johannes Kumalo, Healdtown ;
Mr. J. Gundwana, Healdtown ; Mr. S. Gawe, Grahamstown ; Mr. E. Ndobe,
Grahamstown ; no Mr. K. Kayo wase Tshatshi. Isibonda esingu Macwacuba
asipiwa manzi, isizatu kukuba ngecala lika Mr. Sauer; u Mr. Macwacuba
ukwenza oku xa into eninzi yezibonda yeyele ngelomkono ukulandela
igqweta (kuba kaloku lidume ngapezulu kunomdudo ka Mapasa, mhla ama
Gcaleka afunyanwa ngu Mlungu e Mbashe ashiya intabalala yenkomo asinda
ke.) Abambo apa balahla ubom ngenxa yeqelana lenkomo zika Zikilali
ezemka na Besutu.—OWAKONA, Aliwal North, June 5, 1884.

Siyatemba njengokuba kungeniswa amagama nje lamadodana abekwa ityala ngu
m’baleli wetu aya kuzizamazamisa atabate indawo yawo ekulungiselelweni
komzi.

Omnye womhlambi owaka wapantsi kwemfundiso ka Mr. G. Baker ose
Bensonvale namhla wenjenje ukusibalela :

MCOKELI.—Kaundibuzele ku Rev. Mr. Magaba ukuba yada yapelela pina lanto
awaka wayiti-tupa kwibandla elaka lapantsi kokuqeqesha kuka Mr. G. Baker
e Heald Town—yokuba kwenziwe um’bulelo ngentsebenzo yake entle
yokuncedisa ukuhambela pambili kwetu bamnyauia ngokunika imfundo
enexabiso kuninzi oluyisebenzisa kwindawo ngendawo zeli namhla. Baninzi
ababetonyalalisiwe ngamanyatelo awayetatyatwe yinxenye yempi ka Mr.
Baker engabafundisi kwintlanganiso yabo eyayise Rini. Lento yayibekiswa
kwabanxiba amaqina amhlope bodwa na? Ukuba kunjalo,—hayi ndiyayiqonda
imbangi yokuba ndingazanga ndabuya ndeva nto ngayo; ukuba akunjalo,
kutenina lento ibe andikafumani nyaniso zingapezulu kwezo ndazifunda
kwincwadi ka Mr. Magaba endandisiti mna iseyilento ama Ngesi ati yi
feeler kupela. Ingaba into entle kangaka ingayekelelwa iwe na likaba
elafumana okungaka kum’fo ka Beka? Ndawo ibanga ukuba ndide ndingene
ngegqudu kulo mdudo wababekekileyo (the reve­rend) ababesegquguleni lawo
lomcimbi e Rini kukuba ndive ukuba u Mr. Baker uyakubako kwintlanganiso
ye Titshara zase Wesile e Healdtown, ayi Secretary yayo, ngo September
ozayo. Ebingalixesha elifanelekileyo ke ukum’bonisa okupuma emibilinini
yetu ngomsebenzi wake. Ndixolele nkosi Mhleli— gxebe,
Mcokeli—ngolundelendele Iwenteto. Inga u Rev. Mr. Magaba
angandikaulezela kuba ndinga ndingabuye ndipendule kwangapambi ko
September. “Ndililitye” elinkumnkum noko u Q. X. R.

Siyavuya ukufumana u “ Q. X. R.” ewuvutela kwakona lomlilo ; ukulazi
kwetu tina ikaba lakowetu lifana kanye nomlilo wenkuni zomgxube ezimanzi
otandana nokuvutelwa futi-futi. Ungayeki ke “ Q. X. R.” woda uvute
lomlilo wako.

Sicelwa ngum’bali wale ncwadi ukuba simnike ituba lokuvelana no Magna
nge Sigidimi.

MR. D. MAGNA ndiyalillelana nawe mntakwetu. Woba unetamsanqa uke
wafumana ilungelo leso sicelo sako. Nditelekelelisa uyakufumana
izinyeliso nezitshabiso ngapezu kwezo useluzifumene kunokuba uxelelwe
igama lokuntaminanayo. Ubona nje amagama etu tina Zazulwana selezalise
elopepa lase Dikeni nje, sasuka sabuza igama lika Fikizolo, endaweni
yokupendulwa sadotyelelwa okunye. Ndoncoma itamsanqa lako ufumene
isicelo sako kwesi Sigidimi sala madoda. Impi le eninzi esigxelesheneyo
ingangeni kulento. sisililo sam no D. M. T. owenziwa esam. Ndingongakuwe
ngempato kwesi Sigidimi sabo,—THEODORE NDWANDWA, Butterworth,
Ezazulwana, 10 June, 1884.

Fan’uba um’baleli wetu akalazi isiko lase mapepeni ukuba osazeke ebale
akalibeka igama lake mangaziwa nangubani ngapandle ko Mcokeli. Hai kona
ku Mcokeli makalixele. No Mcokeli ke makangalixeli nakwinkosikazi yake
(ukuba uyavunyelwa ukuba abenayo). Kunjalo ukuqutywa kwamapepa.
Sasingena kulivakalisa ke igama lika “ Fikizolo,” no Magna akasoze
alifumane elika Mnqamlezile. Sinosizi kodwa ukufumana u Mr. Ndwandwa
ezibandakanya no Magna ukuya kwi Gave of Adullam.

Inga abo banako bangam’pendula um’baleli wetu kuba lento ateta ngayo
seyidube abaninzi.

Mhleli, ndincede undifakele lamazwana kwi “Mpawana.” Ndifuna ukubuza
ukuba lemali kutiwa yi Fees irolwa ngabazali kungenziwa ntonina ukuze
ipume kuba ayipumi. Ndicela iqinga kuyo yonke into engumntu. Ze kuti
pezu koko ititshala ingabi nako ukuxaswa ngabazali babantwana,
kubonakala umntu epila ngantonina ke xa kunjalo ?— J. K. G., Eluhlazeni,
June 7, 1884.

MHLELI WE Sigidimi kodwa ebantwini apa ungumntu onjani? Ukusuka undenze
into embi kangaka, ukundinqongoza igama lam e Sigidimini ngapandle
kwesizatu, kuba elam ixesha endandiyibuza lonto ka Fikizolo legqita
akuvumanga kuyicaza ngoluhlobo ; ndada ndadanela kweliti ongaba ufuna
ukuteta into nam ngalento masib'alelane ngencwadi, wayitakazela nawe
lonto endiyitshoyo. Ngoku ndibona e Sigidimini selingu Theodore
Ndwandwa—ndenze ntonina kaloku. Okunye upikele ukuti uyandizindla, zonke
ezizinto undizindla ngazo zibuxoki. Lonto imbi uyenzayo, ngati sakuba
singena Zigidimi tina. Kubonakala ukuba ute usakuba lapo waba amagama la
etu yimfeketo yako, nokuba wenzani xa ndingakuvumelanga msukubala igama
lam ngento ezindlwa nguwe. Ndiyakubongoza ndipeze ukundinqongoza.—T. N.,
Veldtman’s Location, 9 June, 1884

Ngete wakumbula noko um’baleli wetu ukuba amazwi alukuni akapuli
matambo. Sim’bone kusasa nje umhlobo wetu um’bali we “Mpawana” esapilile
engabiki nto emva kwe avalanche ayiqengqelwe ngu John William Edward
Archibald &c. Nkomo kwipepa lokugqibela, nasemva kwale ngati usakubuya
apile.

MHLELI,—Ndikucela ukuba uke ufake lamazwana ezi “ Mpawini ’ apo.
Ndiwabekisa kuwe Mr. D. Magna Thomas ; nditi kuwe nene lakowetu uzukuye
entlanganisweni yomanyano ngezinto zemfundo eyakuba se Bofolo ngo July
ozayo, apo uyakufumana kona intyilelo ngemibuzo yako elishumi. Kuba mna
ndakugqibela ulilungu noko ndingazange ndibe sakubona emva kwaleyo
yayise Mgqakwebe intlanganiso, ndingazange ndive nokuba wake walutumela
ukozo lokuxasa intlanganiso, kanti noko uti unga ingacuma yande
lentlanganiso. Ndenjenjalo ke mna Mnqamlezile ukukupendula. Igama elo
Iona lam aliteti nto kulengxoxo, ingeyiyo nendawo yako ukubuza amagama
abantu ude ube seungu Mhleli nawe ngokwako.—MNQAMLEZILE.

Kwingxelo zemicimbi yase Palamente zomhla we 17 June, sifumana ukuba u
Mr. Hockley (omele isitili sase Somerset) ubuze ukuba u Captain Hook,
Imantyi Enkulu Ebambileyo, uyifumene na imvumelo ku Rulumeni ukuba
batete nomnumnumzana u Feltman ngokwanana umhlaba omelene nesixeko sase
Gcuwa ngomnye omkulu ngapezu kwalowo okufupi nomnye umhlaba welo nene
kwa Centane. U Mr. J. A. de Wet (Umpatiswa Micimbi Yabantsundu) ute
ukupendula : U Feltman efumene ukuba enye yefam zake izaliswe kakulu
ngabantu besixeko sase Gcuwa wacela (ku Mr. Sauer) ukuba anikwe mhlaba

Ipepa lase Rini i Journal likalaza kakulu ngobulukuni bemibuzo ebuzwe
kwiqela ebelingene uviwo lokungeniswa kwibandla lemfundo epakamileyo
(Matriculation). Imibuzo eliyiririzela kakulu yeyepepa le English;
ebimininzi pezu kwenteto endala yama Ngesi. I Latin ne Greek kuvakala
ukuba nazo bezinzima, yati injalo nje yamide imibuzo. Ipepa le Algebra
belinqabile. Siwaqwalasele amanene abemiselwe ukuvavanya abebengene
imviwo asimangaliswa ukuva ukuba inxenye yemibuzo ibiqata. Ababemiselwe
ukuba zi Examiners ngala manene: Literature—John H. Brady, Esq., B.A. ;
Rev. J. Cameron, LL.D.; Rev. F. C. Kolbe, D.D.; Rev. Professor Marais ;
J. W. G. van Oordt, Esq., Ph. D.—Science—W. H. Finlay, Esq., B.A.,
F.R.A.S.; J. G. Gamble, Esq., M.A.; Professor MacOwan, B.A. ; R. P.
Solomon, Esq., M.A.; Hon. C. Abercrombie Smith, M.A.

Kwi mbumba yama Fama amhlope ase Qonce ebinentlanganiso ngo Jane 14
Emtonjeni (Kei Road), u Mr. Landrey uvakalise ukumangaliswa kwake kubona
kwipepa lezaziso zakomkulu lomhla we 2 May isaziso sokuba Umnumzetu
iRuluneli ecetyiswe ngamapakati ake umisele u Mr. William N. Seti,
um’bali wokuqala pantsi ko Mr. Dick, imantyi yase Tamara, ukuba abe
negunya lokunika ipasi kwabantsundu—neso saziso sibalwe ngapantsi u Sir
Thomas O. Scanlen. Ute elinyatelo lelobuyata. Imambane abati ngu Captain
Stevenson ite nokuba selefundisiwe omnyama ubengafanele kunikwa indawo
yegunya enje. U Mr. Cooper ute ukuba indawo leyo uyifanele u Mr. William
Seti akasiboni isizatu sokuba ubani aririze. U Mr. R. Warren uvumelene
no Mr. Cooper wati kodwa akabuboni ubulumko bokumpakamisa umntu
ebumpatalaleni ze umenze onegunya elizweni ekulaula esinye isizwe. U Mr.
John Landrey upete ngokuti makuvunyelwane ngenteto exela usizi
lwalontlanganiso ngenteto eyenziweyo, icela intlanganiso ngokululamileyo
ukuba ibe ngabamhlope bodwa amabamiselwe kwindawo ezinjengezi. Azi
itinina impi entsundu?

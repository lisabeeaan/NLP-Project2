Ncisininde, January 7, 1881.

KUVULWE apa ngolwe Sine ngomhla wama 27 ku January. Abaze kucela
ukungena babe mayela kuma 60 kubalwa abamnyama naba mhlope. Iqela
elikulu lelize emsebenzini ngokukodwa intombi. Sinosizi ukuti ababanga
naku tatyatwa bonke ngenxa yokusweleka kwe ndawo. Kwe Sentombi
kulindelwe u Mrs. MUIRHEAD ovela pesheya oza kongamela sona kunye ne
ntombi yake, eza kumnceda. Okwa ngoku usabanjelwe ngu Miss BARNLEY.

Ema shishinini basafuneka abanga tanda ukufundiswa uku Shicilela uku
Bop’ incwadi (Bookbinding), noku Kanda.

EMA MPONDWENI.

LEMFAZWE ingapele ndawo ide yeza kufika nalapa. Kubonakala ukuba
enzakele kakulu ama Mpondo kolu luhambo lwawo kutiwe angene ngalo kwe
lama Xesibe.

Kutiwa kubulewe ama 39, pakati kwabo kubulewe unyana ka Bekameva. Lo
nyana wenkosi engu Bekameva, uti u Mr. Oxland ib’ ingumfo otandwa kakulu
ngama Mpondo, nguyena ubeya kuhlala esikundleni sika Mqikela.
Bekulindeleke ukuba ama Mpondo abuye alwe. Kute cwaka okwa ngoku, nokuba
asa hlanganisana ama Mpondo pambi kokulwa. Utumele ilizwi u Mqikela ngo
Mr. Cameron exela ukuvuma kwake ukuzibuyisa inkomo zama Xesibe. Kum
kubonakala nje ngokuba ama Mpondomise etyunyuziwe nje, aye enga bonakali
kubuya alwe ama Mpondo, umkosi unga ncitshiswa kakulu. U Mdlangazo
unqande u Dabankulu ekupindezeleni ama Xesibe, awalwa nama Manci akubon’
ukuba aya vuma.

KUBA BALELANI.

NGEZINYANGA zombini zidlulileyo sifumene into eninzi yencwadi zivela
kuba balelani. Siya bulela ukubona umnqweno okoyo woku balela Isigidimi;
kodwa inkoliso yezi ncwadi zinde ngokugqitileyo, yabe inteto yazo
ikolise ukupinda-pinda nto nye, esiti ke ngoko sixakeke ukuzifaka xa
zinjalo. Sibacela aba balelani betu ukuba balinge ukuti inteto zabo aba
sukuba bezibalele Isigidimi, ukuba kuno kuncedwa, zingolulwa, koko
zicube indawo ezizi ntloko, ngamazwi ambalwa, nacacileyo.

Ku “MANXIWENI” e Rini.—Incwadi yako eteta ngemfundo yesi Ngesi e
Lovedale inendawo ezingena nyaniso, asina kuyishicilela ngoko, Abavumayo
ukuyifunda baya yifunda. Umntu emnye angalisa emanzini ihashe lake,
kanti noko sele ngama 20 amadoda elinyanzela ukuba lisele lingafuni
lona, ngu notshe.

“J. R.” e Kobonqaba.—Akulitumelanga igama lako elililo, funda Isaziso
eso sishicilelwa inyanga zonke, uve into elindelweyo kuba balelani.

“K. M. B.” e Tsomo Post.—Nawe sikwateta lamazwi sisand’ ukuwabekisa ku
J. R. wase Kobonqaba.

OKUCATSHULWE KWE ZABA BALELANI.

U J. K. MXHINA ependula incwadi ka Meshach Pelem uti:—“ Mna kweli cala
ndikulo (Wartburg) abantu bakowetu abanamoya wokunga u Mlungu angemka
kweli lizwe, koko bakalazela olu laulo lutsha. Kutabatela ku Nov. 1878,
abantsundu baya kala, yimiteto emisiweyo. Ndipikisa eli lizwi ati
‘udanile ukubona uhlanga lulableka ngoluhlobo amadoda ake axoxa nawo,
afundileyo nanga fundileyo, uwafumana uluvo lwawo lulunye lolokunga u
Mlungu angemka kweli lizwe.’ Unyanisile ngaba bavukele u Dr. Stewart
uyise wabo ubawo wetu sonke. Okunene indlela enjalo yabantu iya
kwantshabalalo nam bandidanisile kanye.”

U MELANI VELA JAMA uti:—Makuqwetywe uhlanga, oluya kuma lubekene,
luvana, lungabi ngo vuma zonke. Indoda yinto ezibuzelayo, asinto ifumane
ivume ukuvunyiswa yenye indoda, kuko lento kucitaciteke kwanje. Soda
sibe yinamba enkulu ninina tina eya kwoyikwa nazezinye intlanga?

U B, MYEDI,—ubalisa imbali yokupuma ekayeni labo e Mohali’s Hoek, noku
cita-citwa yimfazwe yase Lusutu emveni kokuba bebe sand’ ukunikela imipu
yabo; nokulwa okukulu abalwa ngako bayigxota impi yase Lusutu. Uncoma
imantyi yase Herschel, u Capt. Hook, ukulungela kwayo abantu ababe
sabele kuyo, ibapate bonke kakuhle nangokufanayo.

EZIVELA KUBA BALELANI.

Incwadi ezi sukuba zibalelwa ukufakwa e Sigidimini, kufuneka u Mhleli
yena elazi igama lo mtumeli ncwadi, nakuba lingayi ku shicilelwa ukuba
umninilo akalifuni ukuba laziwe luninzi.

Siyazisa kanjako ukuba asilitabateli pezu kwetu ityala lenteto zaba
balelani betu, asitsho nokuti siyavumelana nazo zonke incwadi zabo
esizishicilelayo.

ABA TINJIWEYO.

KUTE nge 27 Dec. 1880, kwangena amakulu amabini Amaqwati, inkosi yawo
ngu Singama, kubonakele seyi lusizikazi olukulu Iweso sijwili sokungena
kwentsapo no yise no nina kwabonakala ukuba esisikalo sokuti masilwe no
Rulumente tina bantsundu siseyisile. Make sifunde kwese Bayibile kuqala.
Pulapulani bahlobo abantsundu kanilande elinqina ukuba nande ukwenza
amagunya alomhlaba. Ngengqondo yam besinga singafunda i Bayibile namhla.
Sekusukelwa u Dalasile inkosi yama Qwati, no Siqungati umninawa ka
Ngangelizwe, u Ngangelizwe yena akalwi ungum Rulumente. Ayivuyelwe mntu
lento yokutshabalala kwesizwe sabantsundu, masifunde i Bayibile kuqala,
niyeva na ke matshawe aseluhlangeni?

Ndingowako umhlobo

R. S POSA.

Idutywa, 16 Dec. 1880.

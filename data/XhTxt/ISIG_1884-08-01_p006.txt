bangati beko abancedisi bamagqira abangafundiswanga ngokuzeleyo bona,
abangeti bape luncedo kubafundisi abamhlape abangamagqira poqo.

S akufika kumbuzo wesitatu iti iqale ivele inkobla. Lombuzo ngulo. “
Bakona na abafundayo abamfundo ipezulu nabazimilo zizezobu Krestu ongati
ubakupele ukufunda ubugqira ukuba isikolo sokubufundisela besingati
simiswe ? Kule mbuzo zibe kwaligqiza elimnandi impendulo. Ezona
zinintshi impendulo yayizeziqupayo zinobulula—“Hayi,” mhlaumbi “
Abakabiko okwangoku.” “ Andazi nokwamnye,” “ Ukuba ibingati yenziwe into
leyo ababini nabatatu bebengaba nokufumane- ka.”

Nakusinye nezibini sezikolo ezikulu sifumene oku kulandelayo:— “
Ndinamatandabuzo ukuba kuko abakoyo kwesi sikolo endingabakupa- yo. Emva
komnyaka nemibini ndiyakolwa ukuba bako ababini nabatatu abayakuti ituba
elinjalo balamkele ngemivuyo. Basengabaniwana ngoku.”

Impendulo yesinye isikolo yayiyile, “ Ndinabo ababini nabatatu abangaba
sebebulungele ngenyanga ezim’balwa kodwa andazi nokuba bakukatalele
ukuba ngamagqira.”

Omnye wabafundisi abadala beli lizwe okoyo ngoku kule ntlanga- niso,
uti: “Okwangoku andazi nokwamnye onokuba nako nomfundo inokumenza
abulungele. Ako amakwenkwe abukali akulayo.”

Omnye uti ndilusizi ukuti akuko ukoyo kulo ’mandla ndikuwo
endinokumtemba, nakuba ebefanele ukubako, ngeleko nokubako ku- ngengayo
impembelelo etshabalalisayo yoselo olunamandla olupakati kododana
olufumene eyona mfundo inkulu.”

Umfundisi okwidolopu enkulu uti: “ Ndinosizi ukuti okwangoku andazi
nakwamnye kulutsha nakusipi isikolo esipantsi kwam endinokumtemba.”

Singake sifake iziqendwana esizikupa kwelinye lalomapetshana ezo- bonisa
indlela yokupendulwa kwalo m’buzo uveliswe ngabatile.

1.  Ekuzazini enizazi ngako intlanga ezintsundu nicinga ukuba
    zingancedeka ngokukulu na okwangoku, nokuba msinya emva koku bete
    baneqela elintsundu elingamagqira nokuba lelingabancedisi bama-
    gqira ?

“ Kuko inxwaleko ezininzi pakati kwabantsundu ezibangwa kuku- sweleka
kwemiciza yokunyanga nendawana ezincinane. Abanayo indlela enenkoliseko
yokufuna imiciza, akuko temba kuhlobo lokunika amayeza oluloluyelele
kwiqela elitile lezanuse ezipesheya. Zizifo ezi- sakuba zixakile zodwa
eziti zitunyelwe kwigqira elimhlope. Abantsu- ndu batintelwa ngala
maxabiso abo makulu.”

2.  Angahle abe nako na kodwa lamadodana ukunika inkoliseko esebenzini
    elo nongani engalifundiswanga ngokute sululu, napantsi kwesikolo
    sokufundisela abantsundu ubugqira, nokuba na ke kokwenu ukucinga
    nicinga ukuba bangati kwangoku banikwe imfundiso ezeleyo enjengaleyo
    inikwayo kwelase kaya ?

“ Ingaba yinto etakazelekayo ukuba kubeko abantsundu abangamagqira
azalisekileyo, abangaba neziqiniselo zezikolo ezikulu ekutiwa zi Cape
University (ezo zikolo ke zezokuvavanya kupela, asizizo ezokufu.
ndisela), kodwa bangapina abebengalupumelelayo oluviwo, masiti kwisituba
seminyaka elishumi ? ”

Babini kupela abasebeke bapumelela uviwo ekutiwa lolwe Matricu­lation
pakati kwabantsundu, njengesiqamo sokufunda kwabo. Uku- tobeka
kwemfundo, eti idle ixesha e Lovedale nakwezinye izikolo ezinjalo
kwimfundo engeyiyo eyenteto-nteto ezininzi ngenye indlela kubangelwa
koku. Ukungavumelani okukoyo pakati kwale nteto ingeyiyo eyobutshaba
pofu, nomxelo wabafundisi ongowokuba ngaba. sakuba beyifanele indawo
bodwa abafanelekileyo, nokuba konke oku kupumle pezu kokuvelisa
abanjalo, oko kuteta ukuba ke uluvo loku- qala lurokubangelwa zingqondo
ezite zatyileka yimfundo, ekubeni olokugqibela lungenjalo.

Andinako ukuvumelana nalo ngcingane iti amadoda afundiswe nje kodwa
akanamsebenzi ade abe ate apumelela uviwo lokufumana iziqi- niselo
zobugqira. Ndingatanda ukwazi ukuba kunganina ukuba umntu ongqondo
irelerele, ndingateti ngemfundo, ukuba anqatyelwe kunyanga nendawana
ezincinane, nongani waka wayifumana imfundiso yazo kwigqira elitile ?
Kuko intsebenzo etile ebingati ilungele intlo. bo-ntlobo zezifo, nditeta
ukuba abo bangamagqira bangapihi nokunga. fani kwezifo bati ke ngoko
bangemki gama mni kwindlela abakolisa ukuyisebenzisa; osakuba ebambekile
nasisipi isifo, ngenyaniso ukolis’ ukuti abe nokuzuz’impilo ngendlela
elula ecingelweyo, nakuba ingati ingangqinelani nesifo esendeleyo, kwaye
ukulingwa kwesifo kulunge ngapezulu kunokuba kukangelwe ezibeleni zaso.

Kuko uluhlwa Iwezifo oluhla futi anako umntu onokwazi nengqondo
ukuzinyanga, ekubeni izityakala zingenako.

Ukuqala ubengati umfundisi otile owaziyo ngobugqira afundise iqela
elitile eliya kuba ngabancedisi, liqube ngokwemiyalelo yake, bakupuma
kuye bekungatiwa inxalenye yabo ingaratsha, ibambe emva kokucinga kwayo
kupela—akutandabuzeki oko, kodwa ke ekutini gqwiti ngoko- kuqala,
mhlaumbi msinyane, abantsundu abanokwazi bangabacasa, baqale kaloku
babetwe lipango bangangeniselwa nto bugqira babo,

ngelinye icala, ulwazi Iwemvelo (asinto ingaba nabo abantsundu, aba-
ngamagqira ngokwemvelo) olute Iwaqutyelwa pambili yingqeqesho enobom
nakukufundiswa ubugqira ngokukudlwana, abantsundu ba- ngabehle baqiqe,
bati ke abona balifaneleyo elo sebenzi baketwe njengokufaneleka
nokungafaneleki kwanjengokuba kukwanjalo nasekaya.

Ndicinga ukuba isikolo sobugqira singamiswa ngoluhlobo icanciswe ngalo
lemigca ilandelayo :—

Indlu yamayezu—Ukuhlala indimbane yabanezikuhlane ; nohlobo lotyelelo
Iwabo bafayo bangekude.

Amalinga ngamalungelo emipefumlo yabafayo ngokuteta nabo na-
ngokusebenzana neziqu zabo.

Igqiza labancedisi lifundiswe into zobugqira, ngakumbi indlela yokupatwa
kwesifo, babe pantsi komfundisi omhlope, nokuba kukusipina isikolo
sabafundisi—njengokuba amanani abizwayo ngamaveza kwabantsundu
kungakoleki ukuba anckuba makulu, isityakala nora- tshileyo bobehle
babaluleke ngokuti babe ngamafa epango. Abafu- ndisiweyo ngqe bona
bahlukile kuba bafuna umvuzo omkulu.

Aba bancedisi bangati izifo ezibanqabeleyo bazibike kwizindlu ezi. nkulu
zamayeza, mhlaumbi kwigqira elingu mfundisi, babatumele ukuba bayabona.

Ukuba amadodana anetalente ezilungileyo ebengati abe nokuvela ukuya
kwesi sikolo sokufundela ubugqira, ebengati akutazwe ngokuti azuziswe
kwa nemfundo epatelele ebugqireni, kodwa okwezifo ezibalu- lekileyo
ezinjalo akunqweneleki ukuba kwenziwe oko.

Bekungatetwayo no Rulumente ngendlela emakuhanjiswe ngayo mayela
namagqira antsundu, ukupuma baye kusebenza nomfundisi omhlope oligqira,
babufundiselwe kwelase Maxoseni ukuba akukona e Koloni.

Mayela nokumiswa kwesikolo sobugqira esiqinisekileyo, ukuba u Rulumente
we Koloni nezindlu zemfundo ezinkulu ziyaroxa kweli linga ngenx’
enokutozamela amagwangqa akubonakali ukuba singanako ukusebenzana
nabantsundu okwangoku.”

Zininzi impendulo ezivele kumadoda ekukade epakati kwabantsundu nazanayo
nabo ezitete kwangoluhlobo. Bonke aba balatisa koku, ukufundiswa
kwabantsundu abakufaneleyo ukuba ngabancedisi bama- gqira kaloku nje
ngokupatelele kumzi wobugqira onendlu enkulu nelula zamayeza
ezonganyelwe ligqira elimhlope; bati baziyekele bona izifo ezinqwenela
inyameko etile nokutembeka, ukuba zize zihanjiswe ngabo ekuhambisekeni
kweminyaka.

Esi sigqibo esingati mhlaumbi singabi siso eliyakuti elibandla livu-
melane naso kubonakala ngati sipumle pezu kwale mihlaba ilandelayo,

1.  Okokuba ingqondo ngokubanzi nesiganga semfundo esifikelelwe
    ngabantsundu beli lizwe azikupi iqela elikulu elibufaneleyo ubugqira
    obuzeleyo elingati lililungele ilinga elo lite lenziwa, kodwa
    kubonakala ngati nangayipina indlela bangakufanela ukuti
    baqeqeshelwe ubunce- disi endlwini yamayeza eyi Dispensary.

2.  Ukuba indlela zemfundo mhlaubi ukupatwa kwayo eseyinikiwe kungangoko
    yamkelwe ngako, kwanentumekelelo yabo kwintsebenzo elandela
    ukufundiswa kwabo; isikalazo esiqelekileyo sesokuba, oko sekute
    kwafundiswa akusetyenziswa ngokufanelekileyo.

3.  Okokuba zonke izigqibo ezifikalelweyo nokuba na zicatshulwe
    kwingxelo zasezi ofisini, nokuba zicatshulwe ezingcamangweni
    zabafundisi ngabanye, kubonakala ngati ziti imfundo yabantsuhdu
    kunqweneleka ike yahanjiselwa pambili ngendlela yokuqiniselwa
    kwempiio yabo ngayo nangapezulu kokuba kungati kungxalwe ingqo­ndo
    zabo ngento zobulumko kodwa; bafundiswe ukwenza imisebenzi elula ngo
    nkwintshano nange nzaliseko, nasekwenziweni kwayo kubeko inkoliseko,
    imfezeko, kuti ekuhambisekeni kwexesha iti enobukapukapu nengafumbi
    mtwalo watyala emagxeni enokwenziwa ngaba- fundisiweyo bodwa babe
    nokuyinikwa.

Lengcamango—iviwa ngokubanzi nangemihla yonke—emisa ezi zigqibo
ingoluhlobo, lokuba okwangoku kuyo yonke indawo efundisa ontsundu
umsebenzi, kangangokuba kungabiko sisalela ukangelo lomhlope Iwanele
ngokuquba kakuhle. Kufupi nayo yonke imizamo yontsundu kufuneka uncedo
lomhlope; nenkangelo engapeliyo ukuze lomizamo iqiniswe inikwe leyo
lungiseko egwetyelwa kukuhlala kade nobukapukapu.

Omnye uba nosizi kwanokucasana nesi sigqibo ; nangapandle kwe-
ntandabuzo abahlobo betu abantsundu abasitabati esisibonakalo sesimo
nexabiso lomsebenzi, kodwa lenyaniso yeqinileyo, yeyakoyisa omhlope
nomnyama ngokufanayo, ize isigqibo izixase. Lom’bekiso ukoyo wokuba
ulutsha oluntsundu lufundiswe ubugqira awumtsha kanye. Kwiminyaka
emininzi edlulileyo kuwo lomzi kanye ilinga lake lenziwa ngumongameli
ofanelekileyo we Grey Hospital, u Dr. Fitzgerald ; nakwakufupi nje ke
amalinga ake okuvuselela bona akazange abe namsebenzi kwangenxa yendawo
eseyixeliwe lelipepa ukuba ngumqa- tango.

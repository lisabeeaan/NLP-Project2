U-MAHLUBI WASE MBULU.

U-Mahlubi ibi ngumfazi womhedeni ohlala ezingxo- ndoreni zase Xolobe.
Waqala ukuliva ilizwi kwisituba seminyaka emibini enesiqingata edluleyo,
xa nda- ndihamba ndisbumayela ilizwi, waza walamkela, kodwa wayi fihla
lonto ngalo lonke ixesha abenako. Yati indoda yake yakuda ive ukulamkela
kwake yambeta kakulu yamtsalela ehlatini, yati yakumva etandaza yambeta
okunye, yambopa izandla yabacela abamelwane bayo ukuba bahlekise ngaye
abe yinto edeliweyo. Akakatalanga zezonto u-Mahlubi, wazingisa ekuyi
konzeni Inkosi ngokufihlakeleyo ixesha lomnyaka onesiqingata.
Kwakungavunywa nokuba aye Etyalikeni. Kwati ke ngamhla utile evela
ehlatini, kutandaza, ecela uncedo e-Nkosini, wangena endlwini ikona
indoda, yaza yati kuye : Ndiya bona ukuba ukupikelele ukuba ligqoboka,
andikuba sakupazamisa," ungaya apo unqwenela ukuya kona ngexe- sha
elibonwa nguwe. Wamangaliswa wawa ngamadolo ebulela Inkosi, nendoda yake
yaguqa ecaleni kwake. Wayede walufuinana uloyiso, uloyiso oluzu’ kileyo
nakuba lute cwaka. Ewe u-Mahlubi wayelwe idabi elikulu elinzima, kwada
koyisa yena. Watikaloku evuya waya ku Andries, umdala wakona, wamtyela
oku. Ukususela kulo mini waba ngomnye wa- bafuni, wada wamkelwa
erementeni.

Imini yokwamkelwa kwake yaba yimini enkulu kuti. Kwakuko nexego abati
ngu Mafa, ixego elise liyi mfama lisiqwala, ebeselingumfuni iminyaka
etile Ikoko belingena kufikelela etyalikeni. Kwati ngalo mini laziswa
ngenqwelo. Yaba yimini eyakuhlala ikumbuleka kuti sonke. Wayemi
u-Mahlubi zisabonakala impau azifumene ngokungqinela Inkosi u- Yesu,
ecaleni kwake kwakumi umfo ongu Yisaya, osazaliswe lutando lwe Nkosi
yake, kuko ke nexego elisiqwala, eliyi mfama, u-Mafa ominyaka ikumashu
Imi asibozo, amehlo ake ezaliswe zinyembezi. Bonke ke babemi pambi
kwentlanganiso yamawabo, bengqinela Inkosi u-Yesu ngokungafihlisiyo. Ewe
ukubona oko kwakuhlaba intliziyo zabo bonke abebe kokwabangela ukuba
utatwe ngoyolo umtendeleko wetu’ —Rev James Davidson.

KUMA QWELANA.

Ndinendaba ezimnandi ngelixesha, ngokuba itshivela elikulu kunene
u-Puffadder silibambile. Ndiya kukucazela konke ngokubanjwa kwake kuba
zindaba ezifanele ukwaziwa ezo. Yazi kodwa lendawo yokuba inkosi yetu
ebisipete u-Captain McTaggart wasishiya weza e-Colony. Isizatu sokuba
enjenjalonje asisazi, kodwa ngoku sesinenye inkosi umfo okupapa
enenyameko, u-Captain Alex. Maclean. Lomfo ke usipeteyo, ngumfo oke
aqonde kakuhle pambi kokuba abantu abangenise engozini. Ukwaligora
njengabazalana naye ababesilwa kwa Xosa.

U- Puffadder simbambe ngapandle kokukupa ne- ngxawana enye. Besipume
ipatulweni saya kufika ngakwi Kalihari Desert; site sakubona ukuba
silapo ngolweshumi usuku, seva zisiti intlola zetu ezimbini kesambona
u-Puffadder nabantu bake endle ezingela inyamakazi, masimcotele
simbambe. Kutiwe ngobobusuku, masixobe ize sicote ngobusuku. Malungake
nexesha lesitoba ebusuku site sakukova ukuwapa ukutya amahashe etu
sanduluka sikwele sonke singamakulu omabini anamashumi amahlanu.
Sawanikela imikala sifunzele ukuti ize kuti qipu ukusa sikona.
Siyekufika xa kusayo, safika seqingqi ngapantsi kodonga olubuntlabatira.
Sabaqubula ngangohlobo lokokude sebezinikela bengakupanga nezokuqala.
Sisuke samangaliswa, sadana.

Sitimbe ikulu lenkomo, neshumi lamahashe, ibo kwe, namakalu omabini
abantu abatinjiweyo. Yonkeke lonto siyiqube sayisa ekampini yetu e-Kaka-
mas, njengokuba ngoku sesiqala ukungena kwi Kalihari Desert. Ungxamele
ukugqitela pambili u-Maclean. Kesaya kufika nakwinkosi egobele umteto
u-Philander, ote wasamkela kakuhle wasixelela kanjalo okokuba
sisemkondweni ka Klaas Lucas, wasitembisa kanjako okokuba uya
kusincedisa ekubeni simbambe, ke sisukela elovotshovotsho lomnyani.
Kalokunje sesinamashumi matatu anesibozo intsuku singeko ekampini,
zincolile kanye ingubo.

Nge Cawa, ngomhla we 13 ka July sibuye sagaleleka ekampini, esiti yi
Bloemfontein, sifike nabatinjwa abangamashumi amahlanu anesitoba.
Ngesibambe ngapezu kwelinani, koko site kuba singamashumi mabini kupela
asaqubela pambili, sasesibuya naba. Siyakubuya siye kodwa kulondawo
sakubuya ekampini yetu e-Kakamas njengokubanje singamfumananga u-Klaas
Lucas omkulu, nenkomo, nabanye ababanjwa abangamashumi omabini anamnye
abebe- betelwe entlango.

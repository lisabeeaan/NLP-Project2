ABALESI betu sitemba ukuba bokumbula ukuba kwiminyaka embalwa edluleyo
Ibandla lase Depente latumela u Rev. T. BURANT PHILIP, B.A. e Lovedale
ukuba aye kuncedisa emsebenzini wokuqeqesha abafundela ubufundisi.
Lendawo yenziwa ngenxa yecebo ekwabonakala kulo ukuba lifuneka lanele
iqela labafundisi abantsundu base Rabe ukuze elohlelo lingasali ngasemva
ekuhambiseni i Gospile kweli lizwe. Eyile u Mr PHILIP e Dikeni
akavelanga ngokwaneleyo amadodana ase Rabe (Depente) afuna ubufundisi
nokuba kukuba ngaba vangeli. Unyamezele ke noko etemba ukuba ekuhambeni
kwexesha zode zivu leke amehlo i Ramente zabantsundu zitumele amadodana
ukuze uhambe umsebenzi we Nkosi. Lonke ixesha afumene ituba akayekanga
ukubika ezintlanganisweni zomanyano (Union) isizatu esibanga ukuba
angaveli amadodana. Ezo zizatu zezi :—

1.  Akuko nkutazo kubafundisi abantsundu, kuba bati aba bagqibileyo
    ukufunda bapumelela eluviweni, kufuneke beye kuba yimpahla emakaya,
    mhlaimbi beye kuba zi titshala, ngenxa yokuswela umse­benzi.

2.  Indawo zabo, apo bafanele ukusebenza kona zivingcwe ngaba- mhlope.

3.  I Ramente zase Rabe azikalilondi ixabiso , lokupatwa ngabafundisi
    bohlanga lwazo ziti ke ngoko naxa sukuba ziswele umfundisi dbashiye
    abambalwa abantsundu ibakpyo, zifune .abamhlope pesheva.

benxibe kakuhle kangako, kuba ndandingazange ngapambili ndiyibone into
enjalo. Ndada ndaya kufika kwindlu yomongameli wabembi bendlela. Ndafika
isekaya inkosikazi Kwada kwasemva kwedinara ndingekayi fumani
nebrekfesi. Ndacela kulenkosikazi ukuba indipe into edliwayo yati iza
kundipa. Kwakuba mzuzu ndabuya ndaya kucela, yati ukupendula lenkosikazi
ndicinga ukuba ndiza kufumana ukudla pambi kwabantu abamhlope na.
Ndalinda kwada kwangokuhlwa ndaza ndanikwa umgubo ukuba ndizipekele
ngokwam. Ndahlala kona iveki ezimbini.

Ngezo veki zimbini andizange ndiyibone inkosikazi yam nokukanye ihambela
komnye umntu, mhlaumbi ihanjelwa mhlaumbi isiya etyalikeni. Ndafika kona
kuko isicaka sisinye. Sasisuke kwakweliya qela limba indlela. Esisicaka
sehliwa sisifo semka. Ngalomini semka ndaya kusenga inkomo. Inkosikazi
yandimela ngase mva, ndati ndisenga nje yaba yona indibeta ngentonga.
Ubisi lwaselwa litole pambi kokuba ndiyibope enye inkomo. Ndagqiba
kwelokuba mandibope kwangobo busuku. Ndaya kwindoda yake apo kumbiwa
indlela. Ayindibuzanga nokuba ndigxotwa yinina yasuka yandibuza ixesha
endibe ndilihleli yandinika 2s 6d.

Andihlalanga xesha lide e-Natal, ndagqiba inyanga ezilishumi elinanye
kwakupela. Asele maninzi amaqabane am kona. Ngawo nandenza ukuba
ndicinge ngokuza kule koloni. Pambi kokuba ndibalise ngokuza kwam apa
make ndixele ngokuqala kwam ukuva kushunyayelwa Ilizwi le Nkosi. Ati
amaqabane am andirolela ukuba ndiye esikolweni sangokuhlwa nase
tyalikeni. Nokuza kwam kulekoloni ndahamba nalo madoda. Saya kuyicela ku
Somtsewu ipass e-Maritzburg, seza kucanda kwa Adam Kok nase Mampondweni
naku Mabaca.

Seza kugaleleka e-Qonce, kwanzima kanye ukufumana indawo yokulala
kubantu besi Station sika Brownlee. Sagqiba iveki ezimbini sifuna
umsebenzi singaufumani. Sasesidlulela e-Rini. Omnye wenani letu
akavumanga ukuba abe sabambela pambili. Mna ndafumana umsebenzi kumfama,
kwaza ekupeleni kwanyanga ezintatu kwatiwa upelile umsebenzi
ndingaliamba. Endandize nabo babesebecitakele abanye baya e-Bayi, abanye
e-Queenstown, omnye kwele Diamond. Ndati ndisableli kumfama ndaya
edolopini ngasabata itile. Umsebenzi wausanqabile e-Rini. Ndakupuma
kumfama ndaya kona apo e-Rini ndaqubisana nom nye wesasize nabo. Indoda
emhlope ehlala e-Bathurst yavuma ukundiqesha ndiye efameni yayo. Sati
kuba singatandi kwahlukana sahamba kunye nalowetu, noko ibindim ndedwa
otenjiswe umsebenzi. Sati xa sisendleleni sisiya efameni saceba ukuba
masizimele. Inkosi yam yayindinike xa sise Rini umhlakulo endiya
kusebenza ngawo yandibalela nencwadi. Ndandingatandi ukuba ndibaleke
nawo. Sahamba ke ngoko sada saya kufika ngokulilwa. Sapiwa ukudla
nendawo yokula- la kanti noko ebusuku siya kuvuka sihambe. Oko
sekuminyaka mihlanu kwenzekayo.

Ngobo busuku salala kwifama etile, isina kunene imvula, saza kusasa saya
e-Port Alfred. Safika unqabile umsebenzi. Kwakuko Mxosa utile owasenzela
ububele obukulu, wamini ukusipa ukudla. Naye ngokwake waengumsebenzi.
Ndade ndanenyanga ezimbini ndingenamsebenzi umiyo, lonke eloxesha
ndincedwa nguye. Omnye umfo owayengumkalabi wentloko zegusha wandiqesha
ukuba ndake indlu yake aze andinike 10s. Sancedisana nowetu ngayo,
yasitabata iveki enesiqingata. Sahlulelana ngeloshumi lesheleni. Emva
koko ndaya kuziqeshisa ku-

menzi wezitena, owayesaka idam. Akuwugqiba lowo umsebenzi wati uza kuya
e-Bathurst. Ndati mna andiyi kona makarole imali esendiyi sebenzele,
ishumi lesheleni elinesihlanu. Wati akayi kuyirola ukuba andihambanga
naye. Wada wati masiye kuyigqiba pambi kwe Mantyi lonto. Saya ke kona
kunye wangena ngapakati mna ndema ngapandle. Makabe waya kupuma kwelinye
icala kuba andizange ndibuye ndimbone. Emva koko sabuyela kwase Rini

iutsimi yake wasinka ipeni ezilitoba ngemini. Kwabako nabasicandela
isiqendu esitile haze ke basinike iponti.

KWIMIHLAMBI YABAZILI BENENE

Nov. 1,1876

Silinga ukunibikela ngamalungu alomhlambi asi shiyileyo tina apa e-Bayi
sashiywa ngu J. Kota wabubela e-Rini kuba wesuka apa esifa ehamba efuna
umoya, waye siya Emngqesha sambandezela Erini akabi nakugqita sakula
isifo ke wabuba ngo June 27, 1876. Saba lusizi kakulu kuba
singamcwabanga kuba ebengomnye wamagosa. Kodwa oko sakuyekela kulowo
ubonileyo okulungileyo nobemnyulele elongcwaba elise Rini. Ngokungapezu
koko wayete yena ngazo imini zentsasa wamamkela lo mkululi, waba
luvuyiso lwake ngemini zobunzima.

Kanjalo sashiywa ngomnye umzalwana obekwaligosa lalomhlambi wabazili
u-Mdingi Hlela, ubube ngo Oct 24,1876 yena ebelidyakana. Ewe wangcwatywa
ngokubekekileyo endingekakuboni okunjalo pakati kwabantsundu, kuba
kwapuma amalungu alomhlambi wamalawu nabantsundu onke Amangesi
alemihlambi mibini evete izivato zabazili, kwati ngasemva yangamalungu
kwati emva kwawo yayityeya, netyeya imali yayo ikufupi £5, kute emva
kwayo yangabantu abazihlobo zake, nje ngoko kuba njalokakade. Kanjalo
naye wafela ku Kristu ngoxolo kwanemivuyo endingenakuyixela, kuba inkosi
yaba ngowake umsimelelo, nakuba wemka ngapandle kosapo nendlu yake Nabo
kaloku abafezileyo uhambo lwabo. Ndibekisa kuni bazili benene, ndite
ningazili kodwa eyonanto enifanele ukuba ningayi libali pakamisani
isandla sokunene sobukristu nilangazelele ukwakelwa kuye kwanokwanda
kobukumkani bake.

yabo kude kube ngu mpefumlo ngo tywala, nazi ukuba umsebenzi lo ngoka

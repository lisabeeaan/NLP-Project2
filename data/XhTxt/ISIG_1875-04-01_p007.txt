IZINTO NGEZINTO.

IKOTON—Kuko amadoda atumele pesheya ko- Iwandle ibali zekoton 19,
ayilime kule koloni.

INKOSI U-SANDILE ubelapa Edikeni ngomhla 16 ku March. Siva ukuba ufuna
ukutenga umhlaba ka Mr. Temlett ose Mkubiso.

U-SOLANI, E-Bayi udliwe 5s., mhlaumbi ahlale intsuku ezimbini
etolongweni ngokusuke aqube ngeyure ekungavunyelwe kuquba ngazo nge
Cawa.

ISITSHETSHE—Umfo otile obehlatywe ngesi- tshetshe E-Graaff Reinet,
ngumzalwana wake ute noko ake wanga unika amatemba okupila wabuya wafa.

UBOYA E-BAYI—Ngomhla wa 21 ka March uboya obuhle buhlanjiwe bebusidla
Is. 5 1/2d. ngeponti. Obungahlanjiweyo 7 3/4d. : obeseibokwe 2s. 4d.

ISOHLWAYO SAMASELA—Kutiwa U-Ndamase ufike wamisa intsika ezimbini, ati
umntu ekutiwa ubile abotshelelwe kuzo, omnye umlenze usingiswe kwenye,
omnye uye kwenye, aze anekwe elangeni ade axele apo azifihle kona;
andule ke kuba akululwe adliwe.

INANI LABANTU E-BEAUFORT—Kufumaneke ukuba inani labantu kulomzi lifike
1,120. Kwelinani abamhlope 928, abantsundu 192. Ngokwetyalike bemi
ngoluhlobo. Abalunge e-Tshatshi 387 ; Amawesile 261; Amaroma 166 ; abe
Inde­pendent ne Presbyterians 227 ; abeyama Bulu 4; abezinye 75.

INGOZI YOMPU—Kute E-Uitenhage, inkwenkwana yomlungu ebudala buminyaka
mitandatu yamana idlala ngompu endlwini, kuko intombazana eminyaka 17
nehangu. Ite ke lenkwenkwana yakubeka ehangwini wadubuleka umpu,
imbumbulu yabeta entloko kulentombazana yayidlavula, yafa kwangoko.

EXALANGA—Umfo otile ontsundu usand’ ukufa kona ngoluhlobo. Kube kubulwa
ngenkomo zibiyelwe ngamahlahla. Umntu obeziquba ube zipatele isitolopu
esibopelele esihlahleni. Kute ke ekuqubeni kwake wabeta inkunzi
ebipakati kwezo nkomo isitolopo sabambelela etshobeni yasuka yatyoboza
yabaleka naye, wati ekululeka waba selefile.

KWELE DIAMOND asikuko nokuba imbi intlalo yakona ngoku. Abembi be
diamond abazitandi izip.ta mandla ezipezu kwabo ngoku. Balinga
ukuzenzela imiteto eyiyeyabo, ukuze kuti ukuba I-Government ifuna
ukunyanzela eyayo babambane ngezandla bonke bangayivumi. Kusand’ ukubako
intlanganiso kona abavumelene ukuze bangawupulapuli umteto omiswe yi
Government xa bangautandiyo.

UKUXABANA KWAMADINDALA.—Kute Emnya- meni (Alexandria) kwaxabana idindili
elingu W Somngesi nelingu John McLean, bexabana nga- babanjwa. Kute
kwatunywa U-Somngesi ukuba aye kutabata ababanjwa abane ku McLean, lowo,
etunywa ngu Mantyi. Ufike wala nabo U-Mc- Lean, banxolisana bada basana
ku Mantyi, lafika lase ligxotwa idindala elingu McLean, wati U- Mantyi
kukuteka nqa ukuba kufakwe izidenge emsebenzini we Government. Lemantyi
yona isand’ukufika, ifike ke seleko kade lomfo uzipete ngobuqaba obunje,
ngokuba umsebenzi wake kupela kukubakangela ukuba basebenze,
bangabaleki, akanamfanelo yokwala nabo befunwa ngu Mantyi. Akazi ukuba
ixalanga liti ukupapazela kwalo libeke pezulu kanti noko ukudla kwalo
liza kukufumana pantsi,

UBUSELA—Kute kule veki ipambi kwedluleyo indlu ka Mr. W. J. Muggleton
yangenelwa ngumlungu abati ngu William Feeney, engeko umninindlu. Ufike
umduna watabata ibatyi zambini ze £4 inye, nomnqwazi nezihlangu.
Enxoweni yenye ibatyi ufike kuko iponti zontlanu. Kute kwakubonwa ukuba
aziko ezimpahla kwase kuranelwa ku Feeney lowo, kuba ute akufumana
omtsha umnqwazi wawushiya owake. Apaleleke amapolisa, waya umkondo
wakuvakala kwa Moo­dy E-Ncera, apo ebeke wangenela ukutenga utywala. Ude
wakufunyanwa E-Mkubiso, umnene seletsole ngezingubo zibiweyo, kubonakala
ukuba akasaziva nento ayiyo. Kubuywe naye weza kufakwa etolongweni.

INKUMBA.—Umfo otile wapesheya uti ukubala nge nkumba—“Kubantu
abayikangelayo inkumba akuqondakali nokuba iyabonana, into eyazekayo
kuba iyakufumana lula ukudla kwayo. Ukutanda kwayo ukudla asikuko.
Amazinyo ayo afike kumawaka angamashumi amabini. Asiyiyo nento
ukunyamezela indlala. Kuko umfo owayesiti ifanelana uyivalele iminyaka
15, ipume isapilile. Kuko enye ekuqinisekileyo ukuba yavalelwa iminyaka
emine ingadli nto, yaza yakukutshwa yakufakwa emanzini yavuka yadla,
yapila iminyaka emibini emva koko. Kuko amazwe azidla kunene inkumba.
E-Italy lisiko lidala lakona ukwenjenjalo.

Abayiqelileyo abayazi into eyidluleyo ngokuba mnandi, Nase Paris liya
lisanda inani labayi-

dlayo. Kucitwa iponti ezikumakulu amahlanu leminyaka yonke ukuzitenga.
Nase England kuko indawo ezidliwayo kuzo. Inkumba ezi zizintlobo
ngentlobo, nemikwa yazo ayifani. E-America kuko intlobo eziti xa kuza
kubako imvula zibe zifuna indawo zokuzimela. Kuko uhlotyana oluti ukuba
imvula izakudlula msinya luhlale ngapezu kwamagqabi emiti, iti ukuba iya
kuquba ixesha luhlale ngapantsi kwawo. Olunye uhlobo luti lwakuva ukuba
iyeza luye kwindawo etyilekileyo ukuze imvula ipelele pezu kwayo. Into
engummangaliso kukuba ziyaye ziyive maxa wambi iseza, kube zintsuku
ezimbini pambi kokuba ine, ziti ezizimelayo zibe zizimela.”

ISICELO ESIHLE—Sivuya ukubona ukuba iqela elikulu lamadoda ase Bayi Jibe
litumele isicelo so- kuba kuncitshiswe inani lenkantini; besiti ukuba
kwenjiwe njalo nezicaka ziya kuwuquba kakuhle ngapezulu umsebenzi wazo,
ziya kuncipa impa- mbano, lande inani labahamba ityalike, uxaswe
umsebenzi ka Tixo benyuke abantu. Noko sibonayo kumapepa akona ukuba
abapumelelanga kweso sicelo sabo, siyatemba ukuba abasayi kuncama. Umntu
owakangeleyo Amangesi noyifundileyo imbali yawo uyakuqonda ukuba lento
apumelela kangaka kwizinto ezoyisa abanye abantu ati ahlangane aze
azingise. Siyatemba ukuba baya kwenjenjalo abantsundu base Bayi. Bekuko
kanjalo nebezitunyelwe ngabamhlope izicelo esinye sinamagama abafundisi
bamahlelo ngamahlelo abasixenxe O-Rev. Messrs. Edwards, Green­stock,
Goezaar, Lamplough, Kayser, Macintosh Brook.

KWELA MABOMVANA.—Umfo otile okwelozwe uti, “ Apa kuko umfo
osand’ukunukwa, kunqwenelwa inkomo zake. Ite indlu yomfo otile yalala
yonke sisifo, yaya kucela enkosini ukuba kungayiwana kuvunyiswa.
Ivumile, kuyiwe lati kuba kubonakala ukuba libe lise lixelelwe
kwangapambili, lase lisiti ababantu babulawa yimiti yalomfo, latsho
lingazange lambona nokumbona. Kute ngemini elandelayo inkosi yaya
kumlalela lomntu, ihamba ileqela lamapakati. Bambone esevenkileni eye
kutengisa imingqungu, yecuba, enabafazi bake bobabini. Bafune
ukmngqibela kwalapo besiti makambulule. Kwase kutinta inkwenkwana
yomlungu isiti akunakwenziwa into enjalo kwa Mlungu. Bayirolele lendoda
bucala, bayibopelela izandla nenyawo. Ite inkosi kuyo siti mbulula
kwangoku, yati yona ukupendula, incuma, Gxebe ude uvakale uteta ubuxoki
obungaka nje ubungena ndlela yimbina yokufumana inkomo zam. Uti
ndibendingade ndibulale abantu endingazange ndababona, ndibabulale
ndinempahla zam nosapo Iwam kungeko nto ndiyisweleyo? Ungandibulala
ukuba uyatanda, uzitabate nenkomo zam, wazi kodwa ukuba bonke ababulali
baya kupendula ebusweni buka Tixo, nawe eliga- zi lam uya kulipendulela
kona. Andoyiki nto ; ukuba U-Tixo umise ukuba ndibulawe nguwe,
andinakutini. Uyazi kodwa nawe ukuba ndim- sulwa. Kupela into
oyinxameleyo wena, nezincuka zikungqongileyo zinkomo zam. Kodwa ke ize
niti ndakufa mna ningalwenzi nto lona usapo Iwam, negama lam ize
niliyeke kude kube yimini eniya kupendula ngayo ebusweni buka Tixo. Ite
inkosi yakuva esenjenje ukuteta lomfo yabutandabuza, yabuya kodwa
yazomeleza intliziyo yavakala isiti, vala umlomo wako njandiri yatsho
yamxuzula ngentambo ebise mqaleni. Ihlale yezole tu lendoda kwaqondakala
ukuba ayoyiki nento, ayacela noxolo. Kubeko oti yide umbulule. Yati
ukupendula lendoda, Yidani nindenze into enindenza yona, lifikile kakade
ixesha lam. Ute akutsho lomfo inkosi yanika umteto wokuba makagqityelwe;
okwenene ke bamwela ngenduku bambulala. Emva koko kwanxanyelwa ukubanjwa
unyana, waba kodwa selebaleke nonina. Kube njalo ukucitakala kwalomzi
ngexeshana elifutshane.”

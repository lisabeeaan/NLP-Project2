ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1883.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

AMANZ’ABOMVU.—E Qonce kusand’ukubikwa, inkomo ekufumaneke ukuba ibulewe
ngamanz’abomvu.

INGQAKAQA.—Esi silo sibi kangaka sibuye sazibonakalisa kwelipezulu, e
Quillimane nakwezinye indawandawana.

IMVULA.—Hayi kanye kulo mandla wase Dikeni imvula isatambile ukuna.
Singavuya ngokugqitiseleyo ukuba kunjalo kulo louke.

OLUME OMNYE.—E Bayi into abati ngu Joseph Mayo, ibisiwe ematyaleni
ngokusuka ilume u Mlungu abati ngu John Webber, imqaule umlomo.

AMEHLO E QONCE E PALAMENTE.—Kuko isicelo esibalwayo sokucela u Mr. F.
Dyer ukuba aye kwindlu encinane yamabunga.—Cape Mercury.

UKUTSHA E TINARA.—Isitola sika Mr. Langford, Caledon Street, e Tinara,
sibujiswe ngumlilo nge Cawa 21 Oct. Kuko ulwandile lokuba sitshiswe
ngumntu ngabom.

E HEWU.—Ngencwadi ezivela kweli siva ukuba nakona imvula zike zana. Emta
bayalima; e Kamastone, nase Mcewula ine ukuba kupume ingca, ekulimeni
siqonda ukuba kuselukuni.

UKUMKA KWAMA JONI E KAPA.—Elase Kapa libika ukuba lusizi kwabakona,
ngenxa yokumka kwama Joni i 91 Begiment, ebiseyikupela kwama Joni
ebeseko e Koloni. Lomabandla kutiwa emke elilelwa kanye ngabakona.

INGQAKAQA KWAKONA.—Esi sifo soyikekayo sibikwa ukuba sizibonakalise e
Klersdorp indawana esendleleni evela kwi Bayi lase Delagoa eya e
Dayimani. Izipatamandla zase Kimberley sezixabashile ukuyitintela ukuba
ingafiki kwelo.

OZENZAKALISE NGOMPU.—Elase Tinara likankanya ingo- zi yendodana abati
ngu Coetze. Indodana le ngati ibipete umpu, ite ndaweni itile yawubeka
pantsi, yamana inqenqile ecaleni. Ekungazekiyo ke ukuba kutenina,
ucukumisekile umpu watsho wayivikiva inqwiniba.

E NGQUSHWA.—Inile imvula, ifike xa ozingqolowa bebe- sebeza kuncanywa.
Nokuba ezi limo zibuye zapila, isivuno asisakuba sikulu, kuba ke kuko
nendawo yokuba abalimanga bonke. Kakade (utsho umbaleli wepepa lase
Dikeni) ziyawa ’ne kakulu e Lwangcibeni.

UMLANDU NGOWANINA.—Siva ukuba ngase Bayi ite inkwenkwana yomlungu ihamba
nontsundu, behamba becola intyantyambo, kwafika umfo ontsundu, ote
enkwenkweni ndipe itiki, ulandule omnye. Okwesibini umxosa ukupe
ngenqindi pakati kwamehlo, waqokela ngenduku, washiy’ udwamba.

OFE NGEBAQO.—Indoda abati ngu Roberts yangase Bofolo, ite ihamba
eludinini lomlambo yatyibilika. Ekuweni ibeteke ngentloko kumatyana
abesezantsi elunxwemeni yahla yaqauka. Ekumkangakangeleni igqira, libone
ukuba imbangi yokufa kwake okumsinya kubangwe, ligazi elopele
ebucotsheni.

OWENZAKELEYO.—Ngase Kobonqaba kute xa o Messrs. Overton no Thomas,
bebeseluhambeni Iwabo, elinye lamahashe lakubeka, ekute ekukubekeni
kwalo yabukuqeka ikalityi, ebebe kuyo. U Mr. Overton obehleli ngapambili
utsibe ngapambili akwabako ngozi, ekumhlanzeni u Mr. Thomas itsho yena
waqoboka ingalo.

EZASEMA-MPONDWENI.—Amadoda awayenyulwe ngu Rulumeni ukuba acedulule
indawo engomda wake nama Mpondo agqiba kwelokumisa izikonkwane kwindawo
ezitile. Kona kwezimini bako batile belasema Mpondweni abazidonyuleyo.
Umqikela uyalele ukuba zibuye zimiswe, abadonyuli waza wabadla inkomo
ngantatu.

INGOZI YOMPU.—Ngapa ngase Badesi (Bathurst) nga mgqibelo utile, iqela
lamadodana akona laya kuzingela, kute ngebaqo kwavuka imbabala, ute
omnye akuti paku, wati nantso selimngene akatsho waseleyeka, kantike
mbabala yimbi, yenye yamadodana ekutiwa ngu Mr. Hayward. Ingozi leyo
ngati inkulu ngangokokuba kuncanywe.

OBEMANGALELWE.—Pakati kwamatyala abetetwa e Kapa, ngenye iveki kwabako
nelika Thomas. Sheppard owayeba- njelwe ukuba wayehamba esitalateni
evule imela. Ite i Mantyi ukugweba akuko tyala xa nje ebengasongele
mntu, engalwi nabani kananjalo. Nokoke ekundululweni kutiwe hamba,
uzungabuye uyivule imela yako estalateni nkabi.

E INDIA.—E India kusand’ukubonakala ummangaliso, ngamini itile lisuke
ilanga guququ laluhlaza kaca. Ama India abantu abanqula kunene iminyanya
boke bawanqe.

ISIQINGATA SASE DIKENI E PALAMENTE.—Kusatiwa amadoda aya kungena
kumnyadala wokufumana izihlalo e Palamente ukumela esi Sitili ngo
Messrs. Brown, Watson, Wm. Young, Nesbitt. Kweli qela ngu Mr. Young,
wase Cawa, obesakuba se Ngqushwa, osel’ efumene isicelo. Inkolo yake
entweni zolaulo usate uzakuhamba eyixela kwangazo ezimini.

IRONASKAPU E HEALD TOWN.—Intlanganiso zokubuta imali yokuhambisa ilizwi
zeli bandla zibe nge Cawa nangom-Vulo, Oct. 21, 22. Intshumayelo
ezayamene nomcimbi lowo zenziwa ngu Revd. Wm. Sargeant no Revd. Samuel
Ntsiko. Sifumana ukuba akuko saziso satunyelwayo kwimpi engapandle, nto
leyo ilisiko apo e Nxukwebe ngezi mini zabafundisi abatsha bodwa.

OBESIKWA YINDODA.—Ngase Rini kusand’ukubanjwa indoda etile ngokusika
umfazi ngesitshetshe. Izizatu azi- kaqondeki, kodwa into eyazekayo
yeyokuba indoda le ifike inxilile endlwini ngokuhlwa. Ite yakuba ifumene
into yokutya yaya yalala pantsi; kunini, ipapamile iqubule iboso yaya
kumfazi, ete imte nqo ngoqoqoqo yanxamela ukumsika ebusweni, intokazi
ukuzisindisa ihlanganise ngesandla, kantike kuyakuse kusenzakala umnwe.

ISIBONAKALO ENTLAMBE.—I Cape Mercury ibika eti kubonwe ngobedlula kwi
Ofisi ye Mantyi Enkulu ya Pesheya kwe Nciba Entlambe, pakati
kwalenyanga—Inkabi zamadoda zisopa intliziyo pandle kwe Ofisi zisala
ukusuka e Tutura apo zabe zifuduselwe kona zisuke Ezeleni, ukuya
e-Tsolo. Kutiwa u Captain Hook ude wasebenzisa amapolisa ngenxa
yokungxama kwamadoda ukungena e-Ofisini.

INKOSI EZISE KAPA.—Malungana nokupela kwale nyanga siyipeteyo, u
Rulumeni ugqibe kwelokukulula inxenye yenkosi ezise Kapa ezabanjwa ngezi
mfazwe-mfazwe beke zako. Iqela elinobom ke labanumzana ababanjwa kwezo
mfazwe bezisema Gqili lifunyanisiwe inkululeko yalo. Asiva nto kodwa
ngokukululwa kwezakwa Palo.—Daba lumbi siluvayo ngabo basekuvalelweni
lolokububa kuka Ngxito, igqira lika Sarili, kweso siqiti saka samiwa ngu
Langalibalele.

UKUTENGISWA KOTYWALA.—Liti elase Kapa, ngenxa yokuba bekuke kwako e
Palamente ingxoxo yokuba abantu abantsundu abakumgama okwi mayile
ezintlanu kufupi neminxuma le kumbiwa kuyo e Dayimani mabangatengiselwa
tywala; ate amanene anenkanti kwelo ukuvuyela ukuba lonto
ingapumelelanga, bonke abantsundu ngam-Gqibelo utile abamkelisa iblandi
ngesisa. Yeka ke kwanxilwa nga- ngokuba bati ababanjelwe ukunxila
edolopini kodwa ngalo mini bakumashumi asibozo anamnye.

OMNYE OWENZAKALISWE NGUMPU.—Ngamini itile unyana ka Rev. Edward Edwards,
umfundisi wama Wesile obese Stellenbosch, wapuma nabakuluwa bake besiya
kuzingela. Ute kwenye indawo wabona intaka, ute kuba indawo leyo
ebemikuyo eqonda ukuba ayimlungele watsiba kwindawana ebinamanzana, ute
ekutsibeni ukuze angabulaleki wamisa umpu ngelinye icala, ukuze abe
busimelela, ekutsibeni ijubile inqambu, watsho umpu wamisa kuye
entsuleni wemka nenyama kodwa, kanti ke noko uyakuhla aqauke.

EZA PESHEYA.—U-Mr. Gladstone, ipakati elikulu lo Mntan’Omhle,
lisand’ukubuya eluhambeni lokupumla Iwase Denmark.—Pakati kwezona nto
zingundaba-mlonyeni okwangoku, eyona iyinkahlukazi yengempambano epakati
kwabase France nabase China. Kupikiswene nge Tonquin. Ama French
angxamele ukuba ade aye kungena nakwelo Bayi, awavumi ama China ati
mawapele nge Anam apo akona ngoku. Akwazeki ukuba into leyo iyakupelela
eninina.—Ama Irish asiva nto ngawo ngati izinto kwelo zisate gogololo.

AMANTINDE.—Intsalela yesi sizwe nantso imele i Qonce ipetwe ngumnumzana
u Tshatshu. Sonke isitili esi sase Qonce besimelwe ngawo kwimini
zangapambili. Kute ngoku ngenxa yengxinano u Rulumente wafuna ukwazi
kuwo ukuba angavumana ukuya kuma Pesheya kwe Nciba. Entlanganisweni
ebisetyalikeni yawo (eka Rev. Mr. Harper) ngo 22 Oct., u Tshatshu
uvakalise umxelo wabantu bake ongowokuba abangalo elo lapesheya,
bangayamkela indawo kwanganeno kwe Nciba. Lomvo ke isatunyeIwe ku
Mpatiswa ngu Rev. John Harper, isitunywa se nkosi ku Mantinde.

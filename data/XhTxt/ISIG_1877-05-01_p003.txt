ase Transvaal ubale incwadi eyazisayo ukuba bonke abangayiroliyo irafu
baya kubalelwa ukuba bavukela izipata mandla, nosukuba esiti
mayingarolwa ngabanye anikwe ityala lofuna ukucita ilizwe. Zonke Imantyi
zakona kutiwa maziyi bute lorafu, ukuba ayibutwa kwakamsinya kuya kubako
intshabalalo yodwa kuba imali ipelile. Elinye lamapepa akona liti
Intshaba zelilizwe zisipahle kumacala onke, imali ipelile, nezicaka
eziqeshwe yi Rulumeni yetu Mabulu azisabatalwa, umntu wonke wenza into
ebonwa nguye, noko kuko kakulu ukwenza amatembiso bamba- lwa
abawazalisayo. U-Rulumeni akanamandla oku- zinceda abenze ukuba bahlaule
abantu. Sivakanjalo ukuba ama Swazi akavumi ukutoba kususela kwixesha
ekwakuyiwe kuliwa no Johannes.

Ngezinyanga zidluleyo ebe igusha ezimawaka mabini nenkomo ezimakulu
mahlanu, ngoku abanga isiqwenga esikulu somhlaba, asongela ukuba
ayakuwabeta ngezabokwe Amabulu angesukiyo kulomhlaba Kunjalonje Amazulu
asakangele ukuba iya kupelela pina indawo aze ngayo u-Somtseu, iti ukuba
ayipelelanga ndawo eze kusingenela.

Kuko abafundisi ababini abebebanjiwe ngenxa yo- kungavumi ukuza pambi
kwamadoda abezibiza ngokuti amiselwe ukupengulula into ezingobukoboka.
Bate bona abanakuza kunika bunqina ngezo nteto pambi kwalomadoda kuba
abawazi ukuba anikwe ngubanina igunya lokubuza abantu, kanjalo
abanakuhlakaza amagama abantu abebewahlebela izinto ukuze bapatwe kakubi
ngenxa yabo. Kwakuba njalo baye babanjwa bobabini abavuma noko
ukuyingena yonke lonto. Kuvakala ukuba babuye bakululwa bengenziwanga
nto.

Indaba ezifike ngocingo ngomhla 16 ka April ziti e-Pretoria kube
sekumiswe iflag yamangesi yaza ke ngoko i-Transvaal yatatyatelwa ukuba
ibe pantsi kwe Nkosazana. U-Burgers inkosi yama Bulu noko ati
akavumelani nayo lonto, ute makungaliwa. U Jorrison no Paul Kruger
amanye Amabulu baza kuya pesheya kolwandle ukuya kuvakalisa
kwizipatamandla zakona ukuba abevani nento eyenziwe ngu Somtseu.
U-Somtseu kutiwa noko angekayi biki kubo bonke indawo yokuba uyalitabata
elozwe uti akasenakulinda xesha. Kumadoda aya kuncedisa na nawo
ekulauleni kukankanyeka u-C. J. Juta oya kongamela izakiwo zakomkulu
namapolisa nento zemfazwe ; no Mr. A. S. Memich oyakuba ngomnye wa
Bagwebi; no Mr. P. J. Joubert oya kuba ngu Mpatiswa Wemicimbi
Yabantsundu. Amasoldati aye kuncedisa u-Somtseu kuvakala ukuba aseleye
kufika E-Pretoria. Akubonakali noko ukuba kuya kubako zipitipiti
zininzi.

Ezokugqibela ezivela e-Transvaal zezokuba u-Som- tseu uyitabatile ukuba
ibe pantsi kwa Mangesi. Akuvakali ukuba kuya kubako kucasa kukulu.
Ukuleswa kwesaziso soko ngomhla 13 ku April akubangako zipitipiti.
Amasoldati ebeseleye kungena komkulu. Kucelwe u-Advocate Kotze ukuba aye
kuba ngu Mgwebi omkulu wakona. Irafu ebekutiwe uyakupatwa nzima
ongayiroliyo ute u-Somtseu mabayiyeke bonke. Amanye Amabulu ati azakuya
kuzibika kwi Nkosazana ngelizwe lawo elitatyatwe ngamandla ngu Somtseu.

INTETO YOMBULALI U-J. BADENHORST.

ABALESI be Sigidimi bokumbula ukuba kwakuka- nkanywe kwesipambi kwesi
umfo obulele idindala elibe limquba. Uti ukuyibalisa kwake lombali:—

Sati sisand’ ukusuka e-Oudedag pambi kokuba sipambuke endleleni ndacinga
ukuba ndingatini na ukuza ndisinde ku Clarke elapolisa lalindiquba. Sati

sakufika apo yahluka kona indlela eya e-Burghersdorp ndacinga ukuba
mandimbulale. Sakupambuka endleleni yati intliziyo mandimbete
ngesitibila, Sasikwele sobabini sinxulumene noko yena waengati uvelile.
Ndambeta wawela pakati kwehashe abelikwele nabelinxulile. Ndehla nam
kwaoko. Wandipangela ngokuvuka wakulula umpu ekala ngeliti “ O Tixo,’’
wandibeta emagxeni ngesikanda sawo. Ndibuye ndapinda ndambeta entloko
ngesitibila. Uduma obeluse ntloko ndikolwa ukuba lwenziwe kukumbeta kwam
kwesibini. Ndabeta isitibila sapuka.

Ubuye wawa ekala ngeliti “ O Nkosi.” Akabuyanga apakame. Ndimbambe
ngomlenze ndamrolela emseleni. Sakufika kona ndimnqamle umqala
ngestishetshe. Nditabate amahashe ndanyuka nawo induli. Emva koko kuze
kudlula u-Gert Olivier ekwele. Ndasitela ngamatyolo ukuze angandiboni
U-Olivier lowo udlule kwakufupi nalapo besikona isidumba. Benditi mna
uyasibona kuba ubungenzulu umsele. Umpu nomnqwazi zibe zingapandle
komsele Emva koko kudlule inqwelana zamahashe zambini Zakugqita ndiye
emfuleni kuhlamba igazi ebelikum nase meleni yam. Emva koko ndiye
kufihla isali zam ematyolweni, ndaya kutabata umpu nomnqwazi we polisa.
Umnqwazi ndiwufihle esidulini, umpu ndaya kuwufaka etyolweni. Amahashe
ndiwatabatile ndakwela ndaya kuwela Igqili ngokuhlwa. Elopolisa u-Clarke
libe limnandi ukulibeta kwam. Ukudlula kwetu e-Oudedag ndibe ndim nike
ibrandi yepeni ezintandatu, nam ndayisela yakwangako. Umqala wake
ndiwusike kuba bendisiti mhlaumbi angabuye apile, ke ndibe ndisoyika
kakulu yinto ebendiyenzile. Ukumfaka kwam emseleni ubesashukuma.
Izihlangu zake ndizikulule ndifun’ ukuze ndizinxibe, ndati ko- dwa
ndakufumana zizincinane ndazilahla.

Lomfo abati ngu J. Badenhorst kuse kumiselwe yi Jaji ukuze axonywe.

ISAZISO KWI TITSHERE.

Umongameli wezikolo ezifumana uncedo kwa Ru- lumente uti uva ukuba kuko
ititshere ezike zingabiko esikolweni ngapandle kwemvumelo, maxa wambi
izikolo zazo zingazivuli ngosuku olumisiweyo. Uyazisa ke kwakona kubo
bonke ukuba xa ititshere ifunukuke ifumane ixesha mayicele kubapati
zikolo epantsi kwabo baze bakuyi vumela itumele isicelo kumongameli
wemicimbi yemfundu (u-Dr. Dale) kubeko nesiboniso sokuba iselivunyelwe
ngabapati zikolo. Kufuneka kanjalo ukuba ilixele ixesha efun’ ukungabiko
ngalo. Ukuba ititshere ite ayabiko esikolweni ingafumananga mvumelo ku
Dr. Dale, eso sikolo sayo siya kubalelwa ukuba sivaliwe, ayibambe imali
yake kuso u-Rulumente. Ukuba kuko isikolo ekunyanzeleke ukuba sike
sivalwe kufuneka aziswe kwaoko ngso Umongameli zikolo. Ukuze abazali
bazibone intsuku zokuvalwa nokuvulwa kwezikolo bayaziswa ukuba
kulomnyaka siwupeteyo izikolo ziya kuvulwa ngo 22 ka June: zivalwe ngo
16 ka July, zibuye zivalwe ngo 26 ka September; zivulwe ngowe 8 ka
October ; zivalwe ngo 21 ka December.

IZINTO EBATENJINI.

Siyavuya ukuva ukuba u-Mr. Levey usand’ ukwa- bela Abatembu
abebengazanga bayilima inqolowa ngapambili ingxowa ezimashumi mane ukuba
baye kuhlwayela. Abanye bakangezwe nge emele abanye banikwa ingxowa.
Inkosi ezinje ngo Matanzima no Ndarala no Siqungati no Falo no Mehlomane
nezinye, bonke bamkelisiwe. Lenqolowa inikwe kuba Tembu ngu Rulumeni
ukuba ancedise izilimo kulomacala.

Kuya kuti ekupeleni komnyaka bonke abamkelisiweyo beze kubika ukuba
bayitenina abebeyifumene.

Imijelo yamanzi eyenziwayo ngalomnyaka imashumi mabini anesihlanu.
U-Adam Tois no Barnabas Tois no Matiwana baka izindlu zamatye nezitena.
Umfo oliqaba abati ngu Malghas waka indlu yezitena wenza nomjelo oya
kubiza ikulu leponti (£100).

U-Rulumente utumele itikiti ezishicilelweyo eziya kunikwa kumalungu
Eqela lokukutaza into zokukanya kwintlanganiso elandelayo.

Elibandla lokukutaza into zokukanya liya kuvuya ukuba kuko abantsundu
nokuba ngabasipina isizwe nomlambo abaya kutumela amagama abo ukuba babe
ngamalungu babonakalise ukuvelana kwabo nomsebenzi walo.

U-Rulumeni utumele imbewu yentlobontlobo zemiti ekwixabiso elifike
kwiponti ezintlanu nayo iseyabelwe abantu base Batenjini.

UKUKULULWA KWAMAKRISTU

E-BONNY.

Kuya kuba luvuyo kubo bonke ukuva ukuba amakristu ayekonxiwe e-Bonny
(umzana ose mantla e-Africa) ade ayifumana inkululeko yawo. Kute
ngokucelelwu kwabo ngabamhlope zada zavuma ukubayeka inkosana. Indawo
elusizi kukuba zite noko zingaba- bulaliyo azisafuni ukuba bahlale
elizweni lazo. U- Rev. D. Crowther uti ngalonto “Mhla bakululwa mna no
Dr. Boyle sahlala nabo kwada kwase kumkeni kwelanga sisiva kwezi zicaka
ze Nkosi zitembekileyo ubunzima ezikade zibuva nokuxaswa ate u-Somandla
wazixasa ngako. Sileseshe kunye iculo lika David le 103 satandaza kunye,
simbulela u-Tixo ngokusikulula U- Captain Boler ubanike ihempe kuba
bafike besakonxiwe beze. Bayakuma ukuya e-Lagos. Kuseko ke noko
amakwenkwe amabini angamakoboka asagcinwe e-Warribo. U-Captain Boler
ndite ndakumxele- la wati makwenziwe imizamo yokuwakula nawo. Amagama
awo ngu Stephen no Abednego.”

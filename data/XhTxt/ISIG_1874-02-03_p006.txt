IZINTO NGEZINTO.

AM ALAHLE asand’ ukufunyanwa ngase Queens-
town kufupi nomzi.

ABANTU ABEMKE NOMLAMBO ngase Queenstown
ngezi mvula zokugqibela bade baba batatu.

IMASISI iyanconywa kakulu ebantwaneni kwin-
dawo ngendawo.

U-MR. MEURANT kuvakala ukuba uzakushenxi-
swa E-Beaufort asiwe E-Clanwilliam.

E-QONCE kwa Messrs. Peacock no Weir kusand’
ukuqekezwa isitola kwabiwa impahla ezitile.

UKUNYULWA kwamadoda aya kuba ngawe
parlamente kuyakuba ngomhla wesitoba ka Feb-
ruary.

IZIQAMO zemiti asikuko nokuba ziswelekile nga-
lomnyaka kulamacala anganeno, noko zinconywayo
kwawangase Kapa.

KWELA MAZULU kutiwa U-Cetywayo akavumi
ukuba abantu abeza kusebenza E-Natal bacande
emhlabeni wake.

E-CRADOCK kuko umsutu osand’ ukufakwa eto-
longweni ngokusuke abete umlumgu obengavumi
ukumnika incwadi.

E-BAYI kuko umntu ofunyenwe exonywe emtini
ngentsontelo selefile. Kutandatyuzelwa ukuba
uzibulele ngokwake.

AMA BATLAPIN. — Avumelene ko President
Burger ukuba abe pantsi kolaulo Iwe government
ye Transvaal.

U-HON. C. BROWNLEE, Umpatiswa wemicimbi
Yabantsundu asahambele kulamazwe anganeno.
Uyakude aye kufika nase Nomansland.

IMVULA ide yafika ekupeleni ilizwe liyatandeka,
inca iluhlaza, izilwanyana ziyadloba, akafanele na
umntu ukubulela?

ODUTYULWEYO.—Kuko umbanjwa odutyulweyo
kwele Diamond, elinga ukubaleka. Kuyatanda-
buzeka nokuba uya kupilana.

AMAPOLISA ayenyuke ayakweli pezulu, ngenxa
yesipitipiti sika Langalibalele ngoku ayabuya.
E-Lusutu kutiwa zonke izinto ziolile.

I-PARLAMENTE. — Into enkulu eyenziwayo
ngoku kwindawo ngendawo zekoloni kukunyula
amadoda azakuba ngawe parlamente.

F-KAPA kuko umfo ote akuxabana nomnye,
waselebalekela embodleleni yoty wala, wagena nayo
egumbini lake wayisela, kwasa eqauka.

UMLILO.— Umfo abati ngu De Klerk wase
Springbok kulamacala angase Rini utshelwe yin-
qoloAva abesand’ ukuyivuna. Ixabiso layo ibe izi
£700.

OHLABE UMFAZI.—E-Somerset East kuko umfo
ontsundu ofakwe etolongweni ngexa yokuhlaba
umfazi wake ngesitshetshe. Kubonakala ngati
akasayi kupila.

E-NXUBA ite Imfengu etile ilinga ukuwela ku-
lamazibuko avela ngase Rini ukuya E-Qonce ya-
pulukana nenkabi zayo ezintatu, basinda ebubini
nobayo ubom.

U-J. MUTLE omnye wababalelani betu uti
“ Asikuko nokuba iyancomeka ityalike yabantu
abantsundu yase Queenstown. Babe vatile ka-
kuhle kanye abantu mhla yavulwa.”

INANI LABANTU base Natal ngomnyaka we 1872
lalimi ngoluhlobo—Abamhlope 17,290; Amakuli
(abantu abavela E-India abeze ngomsebenzi)
5,068; abantsundu belilizwe 282,783.

OBILEYO.—Kwele Diamond kuko umfo ogwe-
tywe ukuba abetwe imivumbo ema 25, ahlale inya-
nga etolongweni, ngenxa yokuba ebe isigqubutelo
sendawo yokushumayelela etyalikeni.

E-DIKENI kute ngayo leveki kwako inqwelo
eziwela ezibukweni zitwele uboya, enye yawa
umbexeshi wasinda macebetshu. Ikakulu oku
kwezeke ngokuswela inyameko kwabazipeteyo.

  IMITI YOKULUMKISA abasukuba bewela I-Nxu-
  ba xa inamanzi, iza kumiswa kulamazibuko avela
  ngase Rini. lyakuqatywa ngamabala atile oku-
  bonisa ukuba ngoku akufanele kuwela mntu.

  ONTSUNDU OBULEWEYO.—Kute kwele Diamond
  kwaqubisana inani elitile labantsundu, lisilwa ngo-
  kudla. Kokokulwa omnye ubetwe entloke. Nge-
  nduku wafa kwa oko. Bahlanu abantu abafakwe
  etolongeni ngenxa yalonto.

  OBE IDIAMOND.—Kuko kwele Diamond umfo
  ote wabalekanesiqumasediamondzenkosi yake. Ute
  ke walandelwa wafunyanwa xa ase Bultfontein,
  ekobudala ubutongo, enaso eso siquma sazo kunye
  nenye imali. Ngoku use tolongweni.

  OSWELE INTLIZIYO.—E-Rini kuko umntu ofike
  watabata ihashe apo libe likulekwe kona, walifaka
  intsontelo emlonyeni walirolelo ngasebuhlanti
  besikiti, wafika kona walisika kunene emqaleni,
  waliyeka ukuba lihambe lisopa njalo. Yena aka-
  kafunyanwa.

  UMBONISO WASE QUEENSTOWN. — Kuvakala
  ukuba kuko abalimi nabafuyi abamhlope abati
  abiyikuba sauya lomboniso, ngokuba kusuke kwa-
  xutywa nabantsundu kuwo. Bati ukuba abantsu-
  ndu bayakutazwa ngebelungiselelwa ukuze ube
  wodwa owabo.

  IBARWEED, lomtana wonakalisa uboya begusha,
  asikuko nokuba iyanda. Bonke ngebencedisa
  ukuwutshabalalisa kuba abubi naxabiso uboya
  obungenelwe yimbewu yalomtana; ziti ingubo
  ezisukuba zenziwe ngoboya obunayo, kube nzima
  ukuzinxiba.

  E-DEBE kutiwa kuko umfazi obecele iyeza kwi-
  gqira lomtu omnyama, lati Iona kufuneka ukuke
  alaule ukuze limpilise eloyeza. Ulandule yena
  wati akuko nto angayilaulayo. Ude walinikwa
  eloyeza, kwatiwa makasele umhluzi akavuma.
  Kute kungekabi mzuze waqauka.

  EMATYALENI kube kuko netyala lomntu
  omhlope obe amahashe amabini. Ubesand’ uku-
  puma etolongweni. Ukuzitetela kwake ute ungu-
  mntu olihlwempu. kekaloku ubekohlwe yinto
  yokunika usapo. Isigwele sibe sesokuba abe ne-
  minyaka emine esebenza nzima.

  E-RINI izikolo zama Wesile ezilunge nakona
  kukokala ukuba indawo zokushumayelela zima 263
  ityalike 70, izikolo zemini 57, intsapo ezifundayo
  3,298, amalungu eremente abantsundu 4,243, aba-
  mhlope 1157. Inani lamalungu longezeleleke
  ngabantu aba 181 kulomnyaka udluleyo.

  EZEMFUNDO.—Kuvakala ukuba izikolo zase
  Lusutu zibekwe pantsi ko Mongameli opete ezale
  koloni, zize kanjalo zifumane uncedo kwi govern-
  ment. Kwi Institution ezakwakiwa kwelozwe
  kumiswe U-Mr. Nixon ukuze abe ngumfundisi
  omkulu wayo; abe kanjalo ngumkangeli wezikolo
  ezikulogama.

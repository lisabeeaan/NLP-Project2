INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

  IZWI LE-NKOSI E TSITSIKAMA.

  Humansdorp, St. Patricks Mission, October, 1883.

MHLELI WE Sigidimi—Kaundifakele lemidana andimntu ukatazayo ngokubala
futi. Ke Makrestu akowetu ndinesicelo endasicela ngo November 1882
somtandazo ngenxa yokuxaliswa ngama Bulu apa kule dolopu, ukucasa
imfundo yabantsundu ndawonye nenkolo yabo. Ke namhla siyanazisa ukuba
umsebenzi uhambiseka kakuhle kanye, kubonakala ukuba umtandazo wenu
waviwa kuba namhla umsebenzi wam uyanda kanye. Uke nomvi zikolo wako,
waye hamba no Rev. Llewellyn, B.A., umninimzi lo wase St. Patricks, umvi
wabulela. Umangaliso—aqala ama Bulu ukunditanda kude kube namhla, amane
ebuza inqubeko futi yomsebenzi, amane esiti kum, “ hoe gaat het mog met
jou school.” Luxolo olukulu ngoku ngakum, ininzi intsapo efundayo,
ikumashumi omatandatu (60) ekubeni yabe ingamashumi omatatu (30) kuqala.
Ke mandigqibe ngeliti sahluliwe isikolo setu ngoku—kuko esikulu sam nese
Infants esipetwe ngu Mrs. Nkomo. Pambili madodana okukanya, mandishiye
ndobuye ndinib'alele. Ndim JOHN WILLIAM NKOMO.

  YININA PESHEYA KWE-NCIBA!

NKOSI MHLELI.—Ndincede undifakele apo epepeni lamanene akowetu. Nditi
kuni bantu bapesheya kwe Nciba, nalo ihlazo, yinto embi kunene eyenziwe
ngumfazi wase-Tsomo, ngati ngumntu ofayo xa udwendwe Iufika kwake, kanti
akuva ukuba lomntu uxakekile wapila. Nditshiso lizwi lomhambi, uti libe
qata ilizwi akufika kwindawo kakuboleka inkabi, kanti ubuqata bake buya
engqeshweni, andazi nokuba ulilibelena elilizwi liti imbuko yaba semzini
ningayilibali kuma Hebere 13 chap. 1 vers. Neli um Samariya wamenzela
usizi umntu owawa pakati kwezi hange Luke chap. 10. vers. 33. Nditi
mabini lamahlazo amane evela apa. Sakaseva nokuti i Titshala zalapa
ziwudla ngamandla umti Kalonji. Enye into nanitanda imfundo kunene mzuzu
nditsho kuba zonke izibonda (Headmen) zinezikolo, kanti namhla xa usiya
kwinxalenye yezizikolo ungetsho ukuti iyatandwa imfundo, ngo- kuba
ungamangaliswa abantwana inani labo oko lingako, nemfundo kwi
examination egqitileyo, zaba mbalwa izikula ezinabantwana abagqita i
Standard II. Nditi kaniqwalasele bantu balapa ngati nibuya ngomva ngale
nqubo niquba ngayo. Ndinezikolo nam endaziyo ukuba zidala ndamangeliswa
xa zigqita u Standard I. lo. Imbangi yoku abantwana abaseko ezikolweni,
basetyenziswa emakaya. Papamani! Papamani bantu be Nciba! Uxolo Mhleli
ndiyaqala. Ndim

IMIZAMO YETU IYINTONINA?

  Cala, 3 Oct., 1883.

NKOSI MHLELI,—Kawundipe indawo kwelopepa lamanene. Imbangi yokubala kwam
yile:—Nditi ndakukangela kwelipepa lamanene ndibone imisebenzi emikulu
eqalwe ngabantsundu, enjengale : Imbumba, Intlanganiso Yotitshala, ne
Ntlanganiso Yabalimi (Agricultural Society), neminye imisebenzi
ebalulekileyo endingayivelisayo pambi kwenu; kodwa ke kuko indawo
endidanisayo ndakuyicinga pakati kwam. Lemizamo mikulu kangaka ingemvume
(Sanction) ka Rulumente na? Ukutsho manene nditeta lento ukuba mhlaumbi
“ sifute ” into kule ntlanganiso ye Mbumba kanti noko asinamandla
okuyigqitisa iye pambi kwe Palamente—ibe ke imizamo yetu nemigudu
iyintonina? Kanjalo singena mbalelwano sodwa nakunye no Rulumente
(corres­pondence) imizamo yetu iyintonina? Mandinicele nonke manene
ukuba niyicinge lento, ezintlanganiso mazitiwe approve ngu Rulumente,
zibe nemvisiswano zodwa, zibe nemvume yokuxoxa ngezinto zomhlaba
(Politics.) Lento singayenza lula sifumene ilungelo lokuzilaula, nditsho
ndivumelana ne Mantyi yase Cala eti kwi Blue Book yalo nyaka—" The more
self government we can give these people the better subjects will they
be. It is dangerous to destroy their cherished customs in despotic
manner. If we have patience to lead them, they will of themselves remove
many evils existing.” Ukuzilaula akuko kuti ngapandle kwezi ntlanganiso
; kunjengokuba kungeko ngapandle kwamandla eziwanikwe ngu Rulumente,
nemvisiswano zodwa, ingati enye itete enye into pambi kwe Palamente, oko
akunto.

Lomgcakazi singawenza lula ukuba siyatanda, bako abahlobo betu abamhlope
abangamalungu e Palamente abangayizamayo lento, nabazi Mantyi, bako o
Messrs. Irvine, Sauer, Solomon, Levey imantyi yase Batenjini, namanye
amadoda atanda uhlanga Iwakuti. Sifumane ilungelo elinje—ukuxoxa
ngemicimbi yomhlaba (Politics), kwezi ntlanganiso zetu, sibe
nemvisiswano no Rulumente abe nokusipendula sakumbalela. Bendingavuya
silifumene lonke ilungelo le Town Council, nokuba ngapezulu lento
singayenza lula. Manditi manene camangani lomgca, niwufute, niwumisele,
ndopinda ndiqokele kamsinyane ngalomgca, ndikupe ingcamango yam pambi
kwamanene endililungu kunye nawo.

UMTEMBU.

ISICELO KU RULUMENTE.

NKOSI MHLELI.—Indawo yokuqala nembangeli endiyakutatela kuyo yokubala
lencwadi endikucela ukuba uyifake kwelipepa lako, yincwadi yo Mpatiswa
Micimbi Yemfundo esiyamkeleyo, yafundwa pakati kwe Teachers
entlanganisweni yetu ebiko ngomhla wesi 3 ku October. Bezipelele
inkoliso yazo, ezingamadoda namanenekazi zesitili sase St. Marks
endisipeteyo ukususela e-Tsomo kude kuye nganeno kwentaba ze-Xalanga
e-Batenjini.

Indawo yesibini ndiyibone kweli pepa lako lidumeleyo, lale nyanga
ifileyo u October. Apo ucela amagosa nawezinye intlanganiso kunye
nabafundisi nezihlobo ukuba zincedise u Dr. Dale kwimizamo yake amele
tina bantsundu ngayo emfundweni. Unyanisile, ndiyawabulela amazwi ako.
Kuqalake lenewadi yeza iteta ngezikula ezi zitatu ezilandelayo: esakwa
Mfula ku Ndema isibonda salowomlambo, nesase Nqolose ku Maninjwa, nesase
Ngonyama ku Pitso. Isiti ukuba abantwana kule Quarter abafikanga kuma
shumi amabini anabantwana abahlanu imali yakwa Rulumente ayikutunyelwa
kuzo kwi quarter ezayo, ne teacher ezo zingabuye zizuze izikula
zakomkulu emva koko. Kanjako enye indawo yesibini, Siyakutinina
ukuncedisa u Dr. Dale? Kuba amandla akwakuye, nguye ongancedisa tina
ukuba senze isicelo setu ku Rulumente esifanele ukuba masisenze.
Amamfengu lusapo lukabanina, asilulona usapo Iuka Rulumente? Endingati
ukulubiza lutiwe adopted pakati kosapo Iuka Rulumente ama Ngesi. Ukuba
umteto ka Rulumente uyalunyanzela kwezinye izinto : njengokutatyatwa
kwemipu, namhla ayabulela ama Mfengu ukuba yatatyatwa; nanjengokukupa
imali zokwaka i (bridge) imicancato; nanjengokukupa imali zokusebenza
ozindlela kwelama Mfengu, benyanzelwa yimiteto yasezi Ofisini, bezenza
lula ezizinto. Sitike ebesifanele ukucela uncedo ku Dr. Dale okokuba
abekise ku Rulumente, kukutshwe umteto wokunyanzela ama Mfengu ase
Colony, nala ase Transkei, pesheya kwe Nciba ukuba atumele abantwana
ezikolweni. I Teachers zizakulahlekana nemisebenzi yazo, lingelilo
ityala lazo ikukuba abantu bengabatumeli abantwana ezikolweni. Kwezi
zikula azikankanyileyo Umpatiswa Micimbi Yemfundo, ndingahlomela
ezimbalwa, apo abantwana bebancinane kanye. Kwisikolo sakwa Tom Sijula,
ndafika ngenye imini besibozo kupela abantwana, nakwe sakwa Ntozini kwa
Hanga. Akungenxa yokuba kungeko bantwana kwezo zikula, i lowest weekly
at­tendance ingaba ngamashumi amahlanu, kungokuba abazali ababatumeli
abantwana, bati akuko mteto wokuba mabase abantwana babo ezikolweni.
Ngakumbi kubuye kwemka u Capt. Blyth nje izikolo zibuyele emva kwelama
Mfengu. Ukuba u Rulumente uyanyanzela kolunye usapo Iwake njengakuma
Ngesi pesheya, nala akweli lizwe, ndingavuya esenjenjalo nakuma Mfengu.
Ayikupa kuqala imipu ngomteto owabanyanzelayo kuba belusapo luka
Rulumente. Maba- nyanzelwe nangoku pambi kwezinye izizwe ukutumela
abantwana ezikolweni, ikwa yenye into enkulu abaya kubuye bambulele
ngayo u Rulumente. Ewe um Gcaleka, um Tembu, um-Mpondo, abangeqale
bawuqonde lomteto wokunyanzela emfundweni. Kodwa i Mfengu imelwe kukuba
iwuqonde kwanjengeminye eyayingati abayiqondi, abadange bayiqonde
yininake? Bona bamelwe ukunyanzelwa ukwaka izindlu ze Cawe zabo,
nezikula zabo, nokubatala i teacher zabo, baxase ngokondla nabafundisi
babo Omnye uyakuti akuva lenteto ati mandifuduke ndiye e-Mzinyati, omnye
elu-Tukela, omnye ndiye kwa-Mzilikazi, Kupina apo u Rulumente angalauli
kona ?

P. MASIZA.

St. Marks, 11 October, 1883.

OSHIYE INJINANA NGEMVA.

Mr. Editor.—Kautele jwaxa elizwana linye ndilibekise ko School masters. Kuko nto ndihlala ndibuzwa yona ngabantu besiti: “ Lomntu ufundileyo usakuba engazina ukuba ufundile, lento abozincoma kangaka adele onantsi nonantsi, ukuba bona zidenge? ” For instance —Uti u Mr. ofudukayo ahlanganise abazali nabantwana, to address them kuba emka that is good, ke uti endaweni yokwenza i address,, yokumka kwake, alike ati ndinosizi ukuba ndimka and kunjalo nje kube kusiza isidenge. Ndimka nje olusapo alusoze lufunde, kananjalo ndinosizi nangom Fundisi ukuba wotolikelwa ngubanina kusiza isidenge nje. Ndingati ke ngoku ubafunza a dry morsel of bread. Ke mna nditi inteto enje ipuma kumntu ongakumenteshiyo ukuteta kwake mhlaumbi kumntu ongenasifuba. Ke namhla ngenxa yoko kuteta kulomzi kusele impuza or injinina, nantso yona inkewu inyakaza imka umzi usele nokwekwe wonke uziqusha. Ndiba mna ofudukayo wenza isiqalo sokupozisa ububi abanabo ngetuba lokuba emka. Ndiba mna uncipisa usizi abaluvayo. Endaweni yoko umfo wakona ufike adibelele sorrow upon sorrow. Kaloku ngoku umzi uzakusala nentsizi ezimbini:—Olokuqala usizi lolokuba ebashiya, kanti ebebafundisela abantwana babo kakuhle kangaka &c. Olwesibini usizi abasala nalo uba kusiza isidenge, iqitala, into engenazo neziqiniselo. Nditi bo yiyona mfundo na leyo mawetu ? Sifike isialamdengi singazi ukuba sabekelwa izilaresitile, sifike umzi uziqusha ungagqibeki kuqondeka, mhlaumbi umzi lo ubunconywa ngembuko kowasemzini, sinalala singayifumani, kude kuyiwe kosiyazi, kufikiwe—Siyavuma mhloko, siyavu- ma &c. &c. Usiyazi kunjenje kungo sibanizashe. Baqale kaloku abanye hleliwenje kanti usibanizashe lo ligqwira? njalo njalo. Yiyona mpatwano yasemfundweni na leyo ? Ukuba bendinezwi elikulu bendiyakuti musani ukwenzelana imigibe emsebenzmi wenu mawetu. Abanye boti lonto yehla pina? Nibonanje e Cradock ngonyaka odluleyo yayimfazwe ngento enjalo. Mna nditi umzi wawufanele ukuba imfundiso, nokuya yayi yimfundiso egwenxa. Ndiba niti uhlanga maluzimele, nxa nisenjenje kwanina bapambili lotini? Mtiyi lumkela umgibe wako ungeyeli kwakuwo. Celani kuso Mandla upume umoya onjalo. Uxolo ngenteto ende. Ndim

G. Nqana. Middelburg, Sept. 17, 1883.

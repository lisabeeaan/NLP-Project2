U-HON. C. BROWNLEE KWA RILL

Ngomhla 4 ka March u-Hon. C. Brownlee ube nentlanganiso no Rili. Amadoda
abeko ebekwikulu elinamashumi amahlanu ; kuba ebete u-Rili waligxota
ikulu elize lixobile.

U-Mr. Brownlee uqale ngokuti: Namhla akuko nto ndiza kuyiteta, ndize
kunibona ukuba nisahlcli njanina kuba ndibe ndingeko ndiye pesheya.
Ukuba unento ongayitetayo ungayitsho ndikupendule.

Ute u-Rili ndivuyile ndakuva ukuba uyeza kuba bendibona izinto zingati
ziyapambana. Yinina ukuba uti akunanto yakuteta kanti nje ufika kuko
amapike?

U-Mr. Brownlee ute; ndibe ndisitsho kuba ezonto zamapike zidlule. Ewe
kuyinene ukuba amapolisa akweli lapesheya kwe Nciba, kodwa ke akezanga
kuwe. Eyona ndawo inkulu ayezele yona kwakukuba u-Menziwa waesiti woyika
ukubulawa ngu Ngangelizwe. Ubuze u-Rili ukuba waka waxelelwa na ukuba
azele lonto amapolisa, waza u-Mr. Brownlee wapendula ngeliti:
Kwakungafuneki ukuba ude ubikelwe kuba kwakungeko nto ifunwayo kuwe.
Wena wauselute lonto mayilungiswe ngu Rulumente, kwaza ke okunene
kwakutunyelwa abantu nguye bafumana eyinyaniso amazwi ako, intombi yako
ibulewe. Wena wati isigwebo sawo asikwanelisi, ndayiva lonto ndipeshea.y
Ndamangaliswa kukuba unganeliswa kanti kulandelwe isiko lamaxosa.
Uyawazi nawe amapakati abulawayo ngapandle kwako waza wena wahlaulisa
inkomo ezimashumi mabini. Lamadoda aye ngelatyala nawo njengalenkazana.
Ngoko kexa uti akwaneliswanga usuke u-Rulumente acinga ukuba kuko into
ongayipumiyo eyona uqubela kuyo. Ndimangalisiwe ukuba koku kunganeliswa
kwako kungene nabantu abebefanele ukukuvusa. Lento yokucasana no
Rulumente asikuko nokuba iyenzakalisa. Isahluko esikoyo pakati kwale
ndaba yentombi yako neyamanye amatyala abakoyo emhlabeni wako, kukuba
ababulawa apa bengenamteteleli. Utsho wakankanya amatyala afana neli
ahluke ngalendawo yokuba abantu bengenamteteleli.

Ute u-Rili into ayibuzayo kukuba kwakutiwe oya kungenisa inkatazo uya
kohlwaywa kakulu, uti ezinkomo zimakulu mabini sesisohlwayo sasitetwa
na? Ezonkomo andizivumanga ndada ndeva ukuba ngokwenjenjalo ndoba ndona
u-Rulumente. Nditi ke ukuba lento ibe yenziwe ndim ndibe ndiyakucitakala

U-SIHAWU NO PHILIP ROZANI.

E-MATYALENI ase Dikeni kwakutetwa umcimbi wentombi ka Sihawu ebudala
buli 17 iminyaka. Lentombi yayifilishwa ngu Philip Rozani wakwa Gaga obe
yi Titshere ycntsapo zase Sheshegu, apo ihlala kona intombi leyo. Wasuka
ke u-Philip Rozani lowo wayona lentombi waza akuba eyonile akavuma
ukuyizeka. Umcimbi ke ematyaleni ubungowokuba ahlaule amashumi amabini
eponti U-Philip lowo ke ute walivuma ityala elo, akenza nezwi
lokutaruzisa wafumana kodwa wati tyindyilili.

U-Mr. Muggleton umteteleli wentombi uvele ngoliti akuko nto yokuba
itetwe kuba kuko bunqina obaneleyo, watsho epete incwadi ezazibalelwe
intombi ngu Philip, ezinteto ibonisa ububedengu bake. Ute ke akuba
ewatetile lamazwi pambi ko Philip wacela isigwebo.

I-Mantyi ite nayo inendawo ezaziyo ngawo lomci. mbi, ezibanga umsindo
ukuba ntlakantlaka kwazo U-Philip Rozani ekulivumeni kwake ityala lake,
uzincedile kanye, into ke leyo asilusizi kakulu yiyo, kuba indoda enjalo
kufuneka ukuba ibengezwe.

Lo Philip ngumfana oqabukileyo ofunde kakulu, obenyuswe kwase
buncinaneni ngabafundisi base Lovedale, abate ke akwanela emfundweni
bambeka endaweni yomntu otembekileyo; ute ke ukubabuyisela izibele zabo
kuye, wenza usinga-lufilisho kule ntombi efuna ukuze ayihlise. Lentombi
ikwangomnyo wentsapo zesikula sake, kuloko ate kanti yona uyifundisa
ubumenemene. Ute ke akuba ekwenzile oku wakufeza, wasuka wayiti xibilili
k'ahla pantsi ngokupo lileyo, waza ke wamangala ukufeza idinga lake
lokuba wayete wotshata nayo, kuba kungenxa yelidinga lomtshato ede
lentombi yahlelwa yile ngozi.

Abafundisi ke basebesuka bemgxota u-Philip lowo emsebenzini wake,
kanjako kuyatenjwa ukuba u-Dr Stewart eziya kuboniswa yena ezonewadi
zika Philip wolinga amatuba okulicima igama lase Lovedale ahamba nalo
u-Philip, aze kanjako alinge ukwazisa uluntu isimilo sako.—Ukuba
lomcimbi ubutetwa kwamakulu amatyala ebeya kuhlauliswa ngokube kuya kuba
sisiyalo kwabaninzi.

Udliwe amashumi amabini eponti (£20).

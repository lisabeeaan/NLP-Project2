KUMAKRISTU ANTSUNDU ASE SOUTH
AFRICA.

BAZALWANA bam abatandekayo e-Nkosini u-Yesu.

Ruse kulixesha ndinomnqweno wokunga ndi- ngake ndibekise amazwana atile
kuni kwelipepa endaziyo ukuba baninzi abalileseshayo; kodwa soloko
ndafikayo kwelilizwe lenu ibisuke iti imisebenzi ese sandleni sam ukuba
mininzi ibete ndingabi nalo ixesha lokuke ndinibalele.

Ngokuke make ndinibalele. Kube kuko nto ndinga ndingayi teta, etetela
i-Nkosi yetu enkulu, u-Yesu Kristu, Umsindisi wetu; kanjalo ndinendaba
endaziyo ukuba ziya kunikolisa nakuziva.

Nozifumana ezondaba kule ncwadi ilandelayo evela kwiremente zase Lusutu
endandizityelelele ngo November no December kulomnyaka udluleyo.
Lencwadi itunyelwe kwiremente zama Mfengu ezipesheya kwe Nciba, kodwa ke
noko inelizwi elivela Enkosini, elitetwa kuzo zonke iremente
zabantsundu, endite ke ngoko ndacela ukuba ifakwe Esigidimini Samaxosa.

Ndinga ndinganazisa ukuba ngomhla 2 ka May kulo mnyaka, nge Sabata,
ndibe ndinentlanganiso emnandi kunene, kuhlangene Amakristu Amamfengu
amacala ngamacala. Ababazalwana babehlanganele ukuza kundibulela ukuba
ndibe ndipakati kwabo, beze kanjalo nokunditandazela pambi kokuba ndiye
kuqala isikolo esitsha ku Magcaleka kufupi Nombashe.

Yaba yintlanganiso emnandi kakulu. I-Nkosi yetu yaba pakati kwetu
njengoko yenjenjalo amaxa onke xa abantu bayo behlangene ngamoya mnye
nangotando. Ndazixelela ezozihlobo zam ezinga Mamfengu ukuba iremente
zase Lusutu ziza kutumela abashumayeli abantsundu kwizizwe ezingekazanga
ziyive i-Gospel ka Kristu ngapambili. Ndati ke kunganjanina site sane-
ntwana esiyinikelayo eya kuncedisa ikutaze aba- zalwana betu abase
Lusutu kulomsebenzi we Nkosi. Sati ke sahlanganisa imalana etile, ndayi
tumela nencwadi kwiremente ese Lusutu, ndikwenza oko egameni leremente
zakwela Mamfengu. Lencwadi ke ilandelayo ipendula leyo ndandiyitumele.

Bazalwana bam beremente zase Maxoseni, i- Nkosi yenze into enkulu
kwiremente zase South Africa ngokuvuselela eyase Lusutu ukuba itumele
abaya kunceda amakowabo, abaya kuwashiya amakaya abo imayile
ezingamakulu emva kwabo, baye kwazisa Igama ne Lizwi lika Tixo apo libe
lingaziwa.

Xa niva ngomsebenzi omkulu kangaka azizali luvuyo yinina intliziyo zenu?

Kum mna lento ivakala njengokungati lilizwi lika Tixo elibekiswa kwi
remente zabantsundu lisiti, “kaloku lifikile ixesha lokuba niyeke
ukukangela kwabamhlope ukuba baqube Ilizwi ukulisa kumakowenu, koko
vukani nize egameni nase mandleni e-Nkosi nilihambise kweli lizwe lenu.”

Okwangoku ndisakuke ndiyiyeke lendawo nakuba inkulu. Ndinicela ukuba
nicinge oku. Abantu base Africa abayi kulamkela msinya yinina elilizwi
losindiso xa baliselwa ngabakwa-

ntsundu abakolwayo lilo, kunokuba baliselwe nga- bamhlope abangabase
mzini, abahamba bengenabo abashumayeli abantsundu abangamanqina ka
Kristu.

Iremente zika Kristu ezise Europe nase America zinga i-Gospel
zingayitumela kweli lizwe lenu libanzi kunene; kodwa akuko bebefanele
ukuhamba ngapandle kwabashumayeli abavela kwiremente zabantsundu ezise
Africa.

Alisekude ixesha eniya kucelwa ngalo ukuba nitumele amadoda okuya
kuncedisa kulomsebenzi mkulu. Wakumbuleni amadinga e-Nkosi u-Yesu
Kristu, u-Tixo Omkulu, awanika kwabo abashiya amakaya, nemihlaba
nezihlobo ngenxa yake. Baninzi abamhlope abazishiyileyo ezonto ukuze
bazise igama lika Yesu ne Gospel yake kuni. Nani ngoku lungiselelani
ukwenjenjalo ukuze nilitwale elitamsanqa lingaka nilise kumakowenu asese
bumnyameni nase bubini. Nibe nifanele ukutandaza ngentliziyo
ezidandatekayo ukuba i- Nkosi iwavule amasango okungena abashumayeli
belizwi lake.

Ndiyanicela ukuze nikutandazele ngokukodwa okukutunyelwa kwelizwi
yiremente yase Lusutu, ukulitumela kwisizwe esikulu ekutiwa nga Banpayi
esingapaya kwe Limpopo. Ize kanjaloningakulibali emtandazweni
ukutunyelwa kwelizwi ecibini le Nyassa, ngoncedo luka Tixo ngu Dr.
Stewart.

Aninako ukupuma nonke ukuya kuba ngaba- shumayeli be Gospel; kodwa nonke
ningaba nako ukubanceda abayayo ngokubatandazela, nangokuti abanye
banikele imali.

Koba luvuyo olukulu kum ukutumela e-Lusutu uncedo oluvela kwezase
Maxoseni iremente. Asikuko nokuba luyafuneka uncedo kona, kuba iminyaka
isesixenxe lite laxola ilizwe e-Lusutu oko libe life iminyaka emitatu.
Lungaluvuyo kanye kum ukutumela uncedo engati i-Nkosi ivuselele
ezintliziyweni zenu ukuba nilutumele. Imali yokunceda kona iyakwamkelwa
ngabe Sigidimi Samaxosa, ukuba niyitumele kwanje ngokuba nitumela
eyokusibatalela.

Imitandazo yam inyuka imihla yonke, ukubika e-Nkosini iremente za
Magcaleka, Amangqika, Amamfengu, Abatembu, Amampondo, Amagrikwa, Amazulu
na Besutu, ndicela ukuba initobele amandla Omoya wayo Oyingcwele, nina
nonke bafundisi namalungu, ngokukodwa abashumayeli abantsundu ukuze niye
nisomelela ngokomelela ekumkonzeni, nilidumise igama lake kweli lizwe
lakowenu.

Umtandazo wam nowufumana kuma Efese I, 15—19.

Ndingumzalwana wenu kwi Nkosi u-Yesu.

C. H. MALAN.

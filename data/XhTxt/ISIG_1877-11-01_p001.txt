LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, NOVEMBER 1, 1877.

No. 86

Isigidimi Samaxosa

ISIPITIPITI SAPESHEYA KWE

NCIBA.

INDAWO ezingamandla zokuqubeka kwesi si- pitipiti ezenzeke kale nyanga
ifileyo akuyi kufuneka nganto ukuba sizingene kakulu. Nanamhla ke
asikatsho ukuti yimfazwe, kuba singayazi imfazwe ebako kungeko nkosi iyi
peteyo. Kwase kuqaleni kude kube namhlanje u-Sarili ute akafuni mfazwe
upulukwa ngabantu. Into ke eyenziwa ngabantu isaliwa yi nkosi kusazeka
kutiwe sisipitipiti.

Indawo ezinamandla zaso zezokuba kwati kwase mva kokupuma kwe Sigidimi
senyanga efileyo ama Gcaleka eza kungena emhlabeni ka Rulumeni omiwe
ngama Mfengu, kwaza ke kwafuneka ukuba amapolisa nama Mfengu ahlangane
ukuwukusela. Kwidabi ela- landelayo afe ngokulusizi ama Gcaleka kuba
kusitiwa kwafa abantu abama kulu mabini, kwelama Ngesi abavakalayo base
sihlanwini abamhlope. Elama Mfengu inani elafayo ngalo mini nangazo
zonke ezinye ezilandelayo ngapandle kombla 3, ekwafa isitandatu,
alixelwa, into ke leyo elusizi kuba indaba ezixelwa calanye mhlaumbi
ziveliswe isiqinga- ta azanelisi bani, zenza abantu ukuba banga- yamkeli
kakuhle nento exeliweyo. Abanye kambe bati lonto yokuba necala
elifihlwayo iveliswe zinkosana ezitile zama Mfengu. Ukuba kunjalo
okwenene asikuko nokuba ziposisile. Kodwa ukubuyela kwidabi lalomhla.
Kuvakala ukuba amapolisa aye likulu ama Gcaleka ekumawaka amatatu. Ati
ke noko amaninzi akafeza luto kuba abanye bepete ikanunu nalemipu
ingxashwa ngase mva. Ixesha ake anga aza kupumelela kwaba xa kwakwapuke
ikanunu, ekutiwa oko Imfengu nepolisa waba ngu “ owoshi- ywa,”kwade
kwalahlekwana kwase kubonwa- na nabanye ngemini elandelayo. Abanye ke
kodwa bati ukuyi xela lonto kwabaleka ama Mfengu odwa, amapolisa abuya
ngomva. Into engamandla ke kodwa yeyo kuba oko kwapuka kwe kanunu noko
kubuya ngomva akuwancedanga nto ama Gcaleka. Ati kamva noko asaman’
ukuza aba engase maninzi.

Kute ke kanjalo emva koko akuba ama Gcaleka enga sezi emhlabeni ka
Rulumeni, kwabonakala kaloku ukuba ngoku lifikile elika Rulumeni ixesha
lokuya kuyilwela indawo yokuwelwa komda wake nokubulawa kwabantu be
Nkosazana bezihlalele emhlabeni wayo. Kufuneke ngokukodwa ukuba makaye
kuzenzela lento kuba kaloku u-Sarili ete abantu bake bayamoyisa. Lite
lakuvakala elolizwi kule koloni ama Ngesi ada aya kunduluka nase Kapa
ukuza kuncedisa u-Rulumeni. Ate nangatabati mipu eze kulwa ayirola
ngovuyo imali yokuncedisa, ayirola engekatsho no Rulumeni ukuba
uyayifuna. Kubonakele ke nakumntwana omncinane ukuba ama Ngesi akakuze
ayekelane, ati akude nendawo enengozi abe ngawona kungati kutshiswe
izindlu zawo. Asiku- mbuze ingoma ebe siyi vuma oko sibe sise sikolweni
enamazwi ati:—

Sibambana ngezandla

Nje ngabafundayo.

Ukuba sibe sikolwa ukuba kuko umntu ontsundu ofun’ ukulwa nomlungu
ngesiba siti kuye makaze ube uyabona ukuba xa alwayo akulwi abapesheya
kwe Nciba bodwa naba kwezidolopu zise Maxoseni kube kupela, balwa bonke
ngangoko bangako ; ngesiba siti konjalo makangabali amasoldati odwa ati
angoyisa wona, makabale yonke into eyindoda, angapeli nangala alapa
kodwa, aye kuquka napesheya kolwandle, ngokuba nawo ukuba ebefuneka
ebeya kuza kulwa apa lula. Kodwa ke asiziteti ezonto ngokuba singakolwa
ukuba intlanga ezintsundu zifun’ ukulwa nomlungu, noko bebaninzi abati
kunjalo. Ukubonisa ukuba intlanga ezintsundu zinga zingamnceda umlungu
endaweni yokulwa naye nangoku u-Ngangelizwe upume namawaka amatatu
amadoda ake ukuza kulwa kwicala lama Ngesi. Abaka Matanzima Abatembu
angati ebafunza namhlanje u-Rulumeni basukele pezulu, ama Mpondo ka
Nqwiliso atumele ukuba maze u- Rulumeni awazise xa awafunekayo aya kuza
kunceda, u-Moni, inkosana yama Bomvane itumele ukuba yona ayiko ngaku
Magcaleka, yatumela nokuba makubuye Imantyi yayo nosapo Iwayo. Nakule
Koloni u-Sandile no Siwani nezinye inkosi zaba ntsundu akuko nento
eziyenzayo ngokuba u-Rulumeni ete hlalani pantsi. Yonke lonto nezinye
indawo esingenakuzingena, zibonisa ukuba umntu ontsundu akanantliziyo
yokulwa nomhlope.

Kunga ke kungahlala kunjalo—iti into enkulu ibe lilizwi lika Tixo
nemfundo.

Enye ke indawo eyenzeke kwakule ifileyo kukupuma kuka Mapasa nonyana
wake u- Xoxo ukuza kuma Ngesi. Sikolwa ukuba ngoku boti ukuba abako
e-Qonce ekaya babe bakwindawo ezikwakufupi nayo. Bati kakade bona
bebengavumi kwakude ukuba kuliwe babefuna uxolo, Sisakohliwe ukuyi teta
indaba yabo kuba kwakuvakele ekuqaleni engati u-Mapasa no Xoxo lowo
ngabapakati kwabapembeleli abamngenisa kwesi sixakeko nalenkatazo akuyo
namhla u-Sarili. Sitemba ukuba u-Rulumeni uya kubupengulula abucokise
kakuhle ubungqina obungabo ukuze ukuba okunene bebengabaqali ndaba
mhlaumbi bepembelela bangasindi ngokusulela u-Sarili—ukuze kanjalo bati
ukuba bamsulwa okwenene yaziwe lonto ngumntu wonke bangacingelwa ukuba
bangamapamba. Kuba ukuba bapambile akusakubako kuba- temba naxa bapantsi
ko Mlungu. Siti bona neqelana losapo Iwabo ngoku sebelapa e- Koloni.
Nalondawo ke iluvuyo kuba iyakubangela ukupela kwakamsinya kwesi
sipitipiti esikolwa ukuba bonke banga singa- pela kwangoku.

Indawo esiseza kuzikankanya zimbini kupela. Eyokuqala yenoyibona nani
kwi- ncwadi ye Governor u-Sir Bartie Frere ukuba namhla u-Sarili
ukutshiwe ebukosini umhlaba wake watatyatwa yi Nkosazana. Isizatu
sokukutshwa kwake sixeliwe kuyo, akusafuneki nganto ukuba sisingene.
Eye- sibini yeyokuba imikosi yama Ngesi iselide yakutshisa nekomkulu
lika Hili, lomzi ubungase ntla kufupi koka Sigcau. Mhla yatshiswa lomizi
kuvakala ukuba kwafa ama Gcaleka akumashumi amabini akwafa namnye
kwelama Ngesi icala. Indaba zokugqibela, ezolwe Sine ngo 18 ka October
zibike ukuba umkosi ka Rulumeni unduluke ukufunza kwa Rili umaqela mane,
abamhlope bemakulu asibozo, ama Mfengu engamawaka amatatu, Abatembu
beliwaka elinamakulu amahlanu. Singalindela ke ngoko ukuba isipelo asise
kude.

IGQIRAKAZI—Kute ngo 30 ka September xa ama Mfengu apetwe ngu Feltman
afunza kuma Gcaleka abulala negqirakazi, alidubula esifubeni. Ibiyi
nkazana ebudala 37 iminyaka, inxibe ingubo zamajoni ipete intonga.
Elinye iqela lama Gcaleka libe lipetwe yi mbongi, ete isabonga
yadutyulwa.

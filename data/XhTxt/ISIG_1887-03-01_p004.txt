ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1887.

OZIBULELEYO.—Umfana ogama lingu Botha wase
Middleburg e Transvaal wabujelwa ngumfazi wake ngo
May odluleyo. Ebubile ke umfazi wake akazange abena-
kututuzeleka u Botha, kangangokuba ade ati piti ingqondo.
Ute ke ngomhla wama shumi omabini kulenyanga ifileyo
watabata umpu wake engati uya kuzingela inqumeya.
Ute akuba nesituba esitile kususela endlwini yake wafaka
umtya encukumisweni yompu wawuti kata elunyaweni
wazidubula yati kata imbumbulu esifubeni yaza kupuma
pakati kwamagxa.

INGOZI EZIMBINI.—Ingozi ezimbini zihle kusapo Iuka Marais wase Stoepjes
esitilini sase Graaff-Reinet. Ute u Mrs. Marais akubon’ ukuba wandlala
inkuko endlwini yake yamana imkataza inkwenkwana yake eminyaka mitatu
ubudala bayo ngokumane ibaleka ijikeleza pambi kwonina, umtyalile
watyibilika waya kuwa, wapuka umlenze. Kute kwa ngalomini omnye unyana
wake obudala bulishumi elinesixenxe leminyaka, utabate umpu wake eya
kudubula. Akabuya ngokuhlwa, kute kusasa kwakufunwa safunyanwa isidumbu
sake pantsi komti esel’efile. Ngokukangela kulo mandla afele kuwo,
fanukuba ufunyenwe yinciniba kayise engevayo waya pantsi komti lowo
erwiqiliza pantsi, umpu nawo ewurwiqiliza umlomo ukangele kuye. Ite kata
imbumbulu pantsi kwebele yaza kupuma entloko ngasemva efanele ukuba
incukumiso ibanjwe lisebe wadubula umpu. Kubonakala ukuba warwiqiliza
isitutyana wabuba. Bayavelwa kakulu abo bapantsi kwobo bunzima.

UBOM ABUNATANGA NKEWU.—Kuvakala ngamapepa ukuba kuko umntu osinde ngo
Qamata kwapela kwelase Dayimani. Kutiwa kuko umfo ontsundu otobeke kwi
nkemfu yeliwa elibude buzinyawo ezimakulu mabini, umgama onje
ngokuxokelela ipulanga ezilishumi, ezibude buzinyawo zimashumi mabini.
Eye kufika ezantsi, wapuke ingalo kupela nokuntlaleka komzimba, kuba
evela emalengelengeni. Kutiwa ke uqubela ekupileni. Iti ke indoda
ebalileyo, azike ukuba ib’ingu Mlungu ange esindilena ?

LUMKANI BABEXESHI.—Kuvakala ngepepa lase Qonce eliyi Watchman ukuba kuko
umfo ontsundu odliwe iponti ezintlanu (£5) yimantyi yase Qumra, ati
engenazo ahlale inyanga etolongweni, ngokusuka ashiye izidumbu ezibini
zenkabi zenkomo ezibulewe ngu Manz'abomvu engazincwabanga.

INGOZI YEMFEKETO.—Kuvakala ukuba ite inkwenkwana etile, intwana etanda
kunene ukuhamb’ikwela, e Natal, yabona ivili yenqwelo yayanyiswe
ngendlu. Yasel’ isiya kukwela kuyo. .Suke yaqetuka, yaya kuwa pezu kwayo
lentwana, Iwaqekeka ukakayi, kwe tyum-tyum tu elinye icala, kwanelobuso,
yaba iyasweleka ngoko.

UMFANA ONOGEZO.—Kuvakala ukuba kuko umfana wase Natala ozidubuleyo wafa,
emva kokuba ehlabe imazi yenkomo kayise, kwane hashe, ange embulele
noyise ngokwake ukuba ub’emfumene ngexa abemfuna ngalo. Isizatu
esivakalayo sesentombi, esingaziyo ukuba ib’iluhlobo lunina kuye
lomfana, ite kanti ayizipete kakuhle; wati ke ngokuqumbela lonto wenza
ezizinto zikankanywe ngentl'apa.

OFUNYENWE NGAMANYANGAZA.—Kute nga Cawa itile e Dayimani kwafunyanwa
umntu esel’ eyoba enganeno koku qauka, enenduma ezintandatu, nenye
ingalo yapukile kubonakala ukuba lomntu ufunyenwe ngaka Ntsasana
ebusuku, amndilata ngentonga, lomntu ungontsundu. Ukufunyanwa kwake ube
ngenangqondo kanye. Kute ke kwakuxelwa emapoliseni aya kumtwala, amsa e
Sibedle- Iweni (Hospital).

I JUBILI! i JUBILI!! I JUBILI!!!—Kuvakala kumacalana onke ukuba i jubili
ye Nkosazana iyalungiselelwa. Kuvakala ukuba. indaba sel’idilikile
kwezinye indawo, yokukululwa kwabase bubanjweni. [Azike kuyakude
kushukunywe ninina kuwo lomcimbi, kulandelwe icebo elihle kunene lika
Rev. W. B. Rubusana lanyakenye liqutywe kanye-kona ngoku, malunga nabetu
abasebuba- njweni kule Koloni esikuyo nati.—-ED. SIGIDIMI.]

  ISIGEBENGA SE NKAU.—Ipepa lase Tinara lixela imbali
  embi kunene yokulunywa kwentombazana esicaka nomntana ka Mr. Cecil H.
  Buckland yinkau. Ababantu babehamba besiya emyezweni ukuya kuka
  amakiwane. Kute besahamba suke lakatya inkau ukubatsibela yamtsho
  qam-qam ukumluma umntwana lo emlenzeni yamenza amanxeba amaninzi. Site
  kwakuba njalo isicakazana samfunqulela pezulu umntwana. Asivanga
  ngani, suke inkau yapetukela kuso, yasiluma kunene entloko. Kute
  kwakuba njalo intokazi yamti qo ngobongwana u Soketye, waba kusuka
  ehlubuhlubula amazinyo, epata kukala esiti— Rwi-tsi-tsi-tsi
  rwi-tsi-tsi-tsi, yabamba intokazi, wabonakala sel’eyalula amehlo umf’
  omkulu waqauka kwapela. Wokulumkela u Soketye, yindoda ke lonto.
  Abatanda ukufuya lomanene ngebewagcina kunene, kuba anemfeketo
  ezininzi, ezimbi.

LENTO UTANDO YIMFAMA.—Ipepa lase Rini eliyi Grocott's -Penny Mail
libalisa imbali esintsompoti ngotando Iwendoda nomfazi abazimfene.
Lenjenje elopepa ukubalisa litabatela kwi Herald—Ute u Dr. Ensor xa
abalela kwelopepa—“ Site sakufika emantloko e Zwartkop Valley,
sagalelelwa ngumkosi oshushu kunene wemfene, sekusiti ngalamacalana
onke—Dyorom, dyorom, ngati yayi kukuza kusibuza ukuba sinikwe ngubanina
imvumelo yokulalisa kulondawo sasikuyo.”

UKUCITWA KWAZO.—Zicitwe kwa oko zizitonga ezabekiswa kuzo, kwasalela
imfen’induna nemfenekazi etile zombini, ekubonakala ukuba yayi ngu
Sonantsi no Nonantsi, izikulu zeso sitili. Kute kwakuba njalo yati
ndodana itile ingunyana ka Mr. Peek, yalala ngomhlana, yabamba itambo.
Ndzwanga, yatsho kwahlokoma namawa, wabonakala u Nonantsi
sel’eqikaqikeka pantsi.

OWONA MANGALISO.—Eyona nto isimanga yile yokuba inkunzi yemfene
ayiwotukanga nalompu neso sitonga kwanjengapambili, usuke umfomkulu
wamfunqula umkake ngengalo zake wake wamuna-munana naye, kubonakala
ukuba unosizi, emangalisiwe yilento ihlele unina ka Ntamekana kamsinyane
kangaka. Emva koko imfunqule (eso sidumbu kambe) yamposa ezantsi eweni,
ehlatini elintshinyela. Emva koko unyana ka Mr. Peek uhlile waya
kuyihlinza. Utike lowo ubalileyo naye usibonile isikumba sayo ngawake
amehlo.

ISANDLOZI SE NAMBA.—Kuko umfo obalisa ngenamba ake wayibona ngokwake,
ekungeneni kwe Tyityaba lingena e Nciba. Uti ute ngomnye umhla ebese
Aloeville kwa Mr. A. Gontshi, (Law-Agent), watata umpu wasinga e Nciba
emlanjeni. Ute akuti tu ezibukweni, wetu kwinto emehlo amaqakatye, ebuso
busivetyuma, bubi, yaye ingamboni yena, imane isidla ingcambu
zomncunuba, izonwabele, zaye izintlu ezi zimane zidlala paya kwelinye
ilizwe. Lomntu ke upete umpu. Unge angajolisa hayi kwala, oyike ukungati
angayiposa imbijele yona ngezintlu. Wayoyika kwapela ingamvanga nange
vumba, kuba umoya wawuvela ngakuyo yaye ingamboni. Zipina injengele
zibulale lo namba ibusulu bungako, zizuze imali ngesikumba nange mbasa
ukuba ayiwanga?

BAHAMBI LUMKELANI INCINIBA. Kuvakala kwakwelo pepa imbali elusizi kunene
ehle kwelama Bulu, ebalelwe kwipepa eliyi De- Volkstem, ngumbaleli wase
Utrecht. Uti—Kwakuko inqwelo yamahashe asibozo, eyayi nondwe- ndwe olu
ngumntu nomkake, nentombi zake zombini, no mbexeshi ke. Kute kwakufika
pi, batana gaga nenkunzi ye nciniba into esel’ilwe kade. Bavakele
besiti—Hoha emahasheni, ayesel’etwaxa ukutimla, nokubala ngendlebe,
igqusha nangamanqina. Kubonakele ukuba makehle umbexeshi ake ayipekuze.
Wehl’umame kwalapo, yasel'iziposa ipela kumbexeshi lowo, imane
izivutulula intsiba zayo, ipate kubeta emhlabeni ngelinye ipiko, isel’
igquma, iwate puhlu namehlo. Kute ukujika kwamahashe kwalumeza, atsho
yapenuka inqwelo yalala ngopote, benzakala kunene abebepakati
enqwelweni. U Mrs. Isaac Biernan wapuke ingalo, inqwelo yatyobeka tu,
elinye ihashe lawa larolwa ngamanye, le kutu-kutu tu, kwahamba kusala
izihlunu nase matyeni. Elinye lahamba lityotywa kwade kwafuneka
lidutyulwe ngokungabi namsebenzi, aye pofu onke enzakele. Siteta nje
umniniyo umangalelwe ukuba ahlaule zonke ezondleko zenziwe yilo nciniba
yake. Uya kunga ange ewubiyele ngocingo wonke umhlaba wake. .

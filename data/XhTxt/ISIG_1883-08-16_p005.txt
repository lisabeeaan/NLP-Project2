EZEMFAZWE YAKWA ZULU.

Kanye malunga nokuxola kwesi sipitipiti sase Transvaal, kute gqi enye
ingxabatshitshi kwa Zulu. Kusasa ngo Mgqibelo ngolwe 21 kule ifileyo e
Lundi kanye apo ku Cetywayo, kugaleleke impi ka Sibepu ngebaqo,
ngangokude impi ka Cetywayo ingabi nalo nelokumisa inyawo. Kutiwa u
Sibepu ube namakulu amatandatu pantsi kwake, xa u Cetywayo yena ebe
namawakawaka pantsi kwake. Ngati kute ekubalekeni wanxwelerwa u
Cetywayo. Ntsuku emva koku u Cetywayo ufunyenwe efile, eraulwe ligqiza
lenduna zake. Abafazi abatandatu benkosi leyo bate nabo benzakala, kuba
amadoda lawo ebesika ngapandle kwenceba. Amawakawaka amadoda ndawonye
nabafazi, asabele kulo mhlaba ubulunge kwa Bulumente. Ute kwaoko u
Sibepu emva kokuba exelele indoda emblope eyongamele elo, waselefunza ku
Mnyamana, obevingcele u Oham emiqolombeni, esiya kusindisa u Oham lowo.
Okuka Dabula- manzi yena obesabele emazantsi e Tugela uti okunene u
Cetywayo ufile. Ukuze u Sibepu enze oku nje kungenxa yokuba ebeve ukuba
u Cetywayo ucele uncedo kwinkosana yama Swazi lokuba kuze kuraulwa yena
(U Sibepu lowo) no Oham. Ngolu lulwo u Lundi no Dwengu zitshiselwe
pantsi ngu Sibepu, ati nalamabaso u Cetywayo wayewafumene e England
atshabalala kwangokunjalo. Isipelo saso sonke esisipitipiti sakwa Zulu
sibe kukufa kwenkosi ebisand’ ukubuyiselwa, ndawonye nokupalala okukulu
kwegazi. U John Dunn yena ubefuna ukuba lomhlaba uhle ungeniswe kulaulo
Iwase Natal, kuba hleze u Sibepu abe yingozi e Natal. Kodwa ke noko
akukabiko ngozi ihlela mntu umhlope kuso sonke esisipitipiti.

[Ezocingo Iwakutshanje ziteta ngoluhlobo.—U Cetywayo ukwa Bulumente
akafile. Inkosana ezimbini ebekusitiwa zifile, ziko kunye naye. Uti
omnye ongati yena ngobekona, kungaba ngummangaliso ukusinda kuka
Cetywayo, xa nje ebedutyulwe egxalabeni.]

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

OBULEWEYO.—U Miss Murphy inenekazi ebelihlala ngase Msunduzi e
Maritzburg, libulewe ngu Mxosa. Umxosa lowoke ubanjiwe.

PAKATI kwezocingo, ezivela e London, sifumana udaba olubika ukudlula
komfundisi ixego elingu Bev. Dr. Moffat, obesebenza kudala ku Besutu.

OBENYULWA.—U Mr. Jorrissen omncinane ubenyulelwa ukuba abe ligqweta e
Transvaal, uyimangele indawo leyo kwase kusingiswa ku Mr. Neckermann
wase Heidelburg.

ISIKELU sase Bofolo u Mr. W. J. Quin, simangalelwe ngetyala lokuba imali
ezitile nencwadi zebunga elipete imicimbi yalo dolopu. Lomcimbi
ulindiselwe i Jaji.

UNYULO.—Kwintlanganiso ebisandu’ ukubako yonga- nyelwe yi Bishop enkulu
kuqiniswe unyulo Iuka Dr. Webb okokuba yi Bishop yase Bini, no Mr. B. L.
Key ukuba abe yeyase Mzimkulu.

U-LANGALIBALELE.—U Sir Henry Bulwer uvakalise elokuba yena uyavumelana
nelokubuyiswa kuka Langalibalele, ukuba kodwa akayi kunikwa mandla
obukosi, nokungabi nasihlwele sabantu sihlala pantsi kwake ndawonye
naye, kananjalo angafumani mali ingapezulu kwe £50 ngonyaka, abe pantsi
ko Teteleku, kodwa avunyelwe ukubatata abafazi bake abasazigcinileyo.

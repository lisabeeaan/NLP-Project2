Isigidimi Samaxosa

INTSHAYELELO.

KWINDABA ze Sigidimi salenyanga, kuko into entle kunene, encomekayo,
eyenziwe ngala mabandla apesheya kwe Nciba kwelama Mfengu. Nanzo ke,
izinto zokunyusa uhlanga. Kuko inteto eti emlungwini “ umanyano yinqobo
” (Unity is strength.) Into eyenziwe ngala mabandla apesheya kwe Nciba,
yeyokufuzwa. Nilibona nje elakwa Mlungu liyi lento liyiyo namhla,
lumanyano. Nisiva kusitiwa, kwati, kwati u-Rulumente, asindoda inye,
zintloko ngentloko zamadoda, alike ayile into, avumelane ngayo isuke ime
inge lonto ivela mntwini mnye; kuba yenziwe ngomanyano. Aba Ntsundu
bezimpalala ezinjenje, kungokuba abavani, aluko umanyano kubo,
oluyinqobo yezinto. Mayibe ngumzekelo namhla lento yenziwe kwela pesheya
kwe Nciba. Kesibuye ngomva kancinane, sikangele ukufika kwawo lama
bandla pesheya kwe Nciba. Alika enqwa nezinye izizwe ezi Ntsundu,
engavani, lungeko umanyano, lowo, nalowo, ekangele okuko kwake kodwa.
Ate kuba enenkosi ebukali, angade ati obonelayo, nova ngendaba ati
ngumcinezeli, kanti wenziwa kwa bobu bume bokuswela kuqonda,
nakumanyana, ahla apuma kweso simo. Ite lenkosi imiswe ngu Bulumente
u-Captain Blyth, yaqo- nda ukuba, ukuba ayiwabonisi ama Mfengu ukuba
lento ilumanyano yinqobo yezinto, yoba ayiwufezanga umsebenzi wayo. Ade
awapesheya kwe Nciba ayiqonda nawo lonto. Abonakala esiba ngamagqiza
ngamagqiza eyila izinto. Ade kwezozinto aziyilileyo ngomanyano (emana
eboniswa yi nkosi yawo, nangamadoda anjengo Dr. Stewart wase Love­dale)
avumelana ukuba ake isikula (seminary) sokufundisa usapo Iwawo. Ayile,
aselesiwa ngazo, yaleyo yabeta ebuhlanti, nase ngxoweni ekupa imali
yokwaka. Asisenakuxela inani elarolwayo kuqala. Yabonakala isima inzwana
yesi- kula e-Ngqamakwe. Ongayaziyo wozibonela. Kanti lamabandla erola
kangakanje, akakagqibi. Senizifundela nani, nikangele into eyenziwa
lumanyano, entweni. Nowabona namagama amanene, arolileyo. Umlomo womntu,
nosiba, ne inki, ezonto azinakutyila ubukulu balento yenziwe
ngalamanene, ade ahlangana, avumelana, amanyana ngeyonanto iluncedo,
iyinzuzo, enyusa uhlanga.

Sipeta ngeliti, anga lamabandla, angahluma, asikeleleke kwimigudu
ayenzayo; wanga u-Tixo anganawo, abe luncedo kubazalwana babo abahleli
ebumnyameni. Zanga iziqamo eziya kuvela kweso sikula, zingazihle,
zivutwe, zibukeke ku Mdali, naku luntu lonke. Anga angakuta. zwa
nalamadoda amana ewavelela lamabandla apesheya kwe Nciba, ewomeleza,
ewancedisa. Umanyano yinqobo yezinto.

Inkosana zama Mfengu nabantu bazo, asikuko nokuba benze into entle
ngelilinga balenzileyo. Imali iyonke eroliweyo entlanganisweni ziponti
eziliwaka elinakulu linye (£1,100) ; iyonke ke imali eroliweyo ngoku
ngalomabandla ziponti ezi £4,500. Ngubani ke ongabuye ati abantu aba
Ntsundu abaqubeli ndaweni!

INTLANGANISO E-BLYTHSWOOD KWE LAMA
MFENGU.

Nge Tuesday, ngolwe 13th ku January, kwakuko kwi Institution yase
Blythswood, intlanganiso enkulu yama Mfengu, ene nkosi zawo.
Lentlanganiso ibi yeyo kucima ityala ebeli sasele, ngo kwakiwa kwe ndlu
yakona, ngokwe dinga awalenzayo ama Mfengu, kwi ntlanganiso yawo ngo
November. Kwaye kuko u-Captain Blyth, u-Mr. King, u-Mr. Gladwin, no Mr.
Paddle bezi Mantyi aba; no Mr. W. Brownlee, no Mr. Thomson. Abafundisi
ngo Rev. B. Boss no Dr. Stewart, no Dr. Girdwood. Kwaye kuko no Rev, B.
Barbour, no Mrs. Barbour, abasand’ ukuvela e-Scotland. no Mrs. Gladwin,
no Miss Garner namanye ama nenekazii Abantu ababe kona, bebe kuma kulu
asixenxe; kweli nan, kwaye kuko amakulu amahlanu ababe kwele, inkoliso
ivela kummandla ka Mr. Gladwin. Kwa kuhle kanye, xa ababakweli bagqitayo
pambi kwesakiwo eso, befalile, bekokelwe yi Union Jack, iflag yama
Ngesi, bebulisa u-Captain Blyth. Intlanganiso yaba Ntsundu, enjalo,
asinto idla ngokubako. Kwabonakala ngati amadoda anemihlali, nakuba kuko
ukubalela okungaka, nezinye izinto ezixalisayo. Kwahlanganwa kwi gumbi
elikulu, ela beta lazala, kanti into eninzi ibingapandle, ingena
kufumana ndawo, yanela ukufumana ukuza ku pulapula ngasezi festileni.

U-CAPTAIN BLYTH ute: Bafundisi, zi Mantyi, zi Nkosi zama Mfengu, nani
bantu nonke. Kwintlanganiso edlulileyo nabeka ityala pezu kwenu. Nacela
inyanga ukuba nilisuse. Namhla ke nihlanganele ukuzalisa idinga lenu.
Ngalontlanganiso nayiva yonke ingxelo ngalendlu. Neva ke ukuba umsebenzi
wayo awubambelele ezintliziyweni zabantu njengoko ubufanele. Nanengcinga
eti kuko indawo eposisekileyo. U-Dr. Stewart uniqinisekisile ngokunye
enyanisweni. Uvuse elutulini lomini i-Komiti yaba Ntsundu. Yema kwaoko
kwatiwa mayi sebenze. Esi sakiwo sesabantu belilizwe, seselungelo
yemipefumlo yabantu belilizwe, bonke abapesheya kwe Nciba. Yibani noluvo
loko- kuba yeyenu, yeyenu ukuyilungisa, ikwa nini abangayo- nakalisayo.
Lungafanelana lonke uhlanga olumhlope luze kuyimisa ngezandla zalo,
ayingemi. Mayingene ezintliziyweni zenu, nive emxelweni ukuba yeyenu.
Nize kufeza isitembiso senu nokunika eninako. Asiyiyo rafu le: asize
kuxoxa ngeyase matyaleni. Into erolwe yintliziyo evumayo, iyidlula
kaliwaka erolwe sisandla esirorayo nayintliziyo ebuhlungu. Isheleni
ezintlanu emntwini azenze kangakanani, kodwa yakuhlanganiselwa apa,
zisebenza okukulu zisusa ityala lalendawo. Aze no Dr. Stewart apunyuzwe
kumtwalokazi wetyala awutabatele kuye ungenguwo owake, ungowenu. Nite
ngentlanganiso edluleyo, u-Tixo unisikelele kulo msebenzi, lihlazo ukude
ube lomtwalo usahleli pezu kwake (Dr. Stewart). U-Dr. Stewart
akanakuhamba ezindawo etwele lendlu emhlana ngati lufudo. Ndiyazi ukuba
beninengxakeko: amaxesha mabi. Kodwa kumsebenzi wesizwe, bonke bafanele
ukutwala, bangawuyekeli kwabambalwa. Kukuti bonke bahlangane
ngokuncedisa ukuze kufezwe umsebenzi. Ndiyazi baninzi abangarolanga

Wesile emele eli lomzantsi we Afrika ekutiwa yi ‘ Conference,’ iyakuqala
e Rini ngo 15 kule nyanga kude kube sekupeleni kwayo. Intlangano
zabacedululi bengxoxwana ezinqabileyo (Committees) ziyakuba
kwangapambili. Kwipepa elipambi kwetu sibala nje elicwangcisa umsebenzi
wentlanganiso sifumana ukuba ikomiti eyaba abafundisi iyakuhlangana
ngolwesi-Tatu, April 9. Ngo April 10 kohlangana iqumru elicedulula
indawo ezipatelele kumhlaba wabafundisi; ngo April 11 abacedululi
benqubo yomsebenzi wentlanganiso zeziqingata, ngo Mgqibelo, April 12
abacedululi bemicimbi yemfundo; ngo Mvulo, April 14 abemicimbi yemali.
Intla­nganiso ezibalulekileyo ezipatelele kwi Conference ngu Mtandazo e
Commemoration Church (ityalike ebalulekileyo yama Wesile) ngo 15 April
ngo 12 emini. Ngolwe Sine April 17 intlanganiso ekongena nabani kwakulo
tyalike ngo 7 ngokuhlwa. Nge 2.30 nge Cawa 20 April, intlanganiso
yezikolo ze Cawa. Ngolwe Sibini 22 April, intlanganiso yeronoskapu ye ’
Conference ’ ngo 7 ngokuhlwa. Ngolwe Sitatu 23 ngo 7 ngokuhlwa
intlanganiso yokwamkelwa kwe ‘ Conference.’ Ngokuhlwa (7) ngo 25 isonka
sotandano kwityalike ese Fort England. Ngokuhlwa (7) ngo 28 kuyakubekwa
abafundisi abatile izandla e Commemoration. Intlanganiso yozilo e Shaw
Hall ngolwe Sibini April 29 ngexesha lesixenxe. Ngamana isitamsanqeliso
se Nkosi sapezu komsebenzi wentlanganiso.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1870.

UKUHAMBA ESIBAKABAKENI.

IPOSI EHAMBA NGE BALUNI.

Kwi Mbali Yomxosa nge England ekwi pepa lale nyanga ifileyo umhambi
owaye yibalisa wati, kwimi mangaliso ekoyo pesheya ubone ibaluni. Wati
ukuyixela kwake:— “Ndabona abantu benyuka esibakabakeni ngapezulu kwo
kozi. Anditsho ukuti babe namapiko, kodwa ndaba bona besemnyazini
benyuka. Kwaku botshelelwe kulomnyazi inxowa eyayizele ngumsi. Yanga
liselwa libekiswe umlomo ezantsi, waye umnyazi ujinga kuyo. Babe Meli
kulo mnyazi abantu beba bini. Yati yakuyekwa lenxowa yenyuka. Ndayi
kangela inyuka ada adinwa amehlo am, yaya ibancinane yada yanga
ngentaka.

Mhlaumbe kunga kuko abati bakuyi lesesha lendawo abakolwa yiyo, bati nje
ngompakati lowo, ibuxoki.

Sinityele ke nina banjalo, ngezi mini zanamMa incwadi zihamba nge baluni
xa zisuka E-Paris zisiya kwimizi esema- caleni ayo.

Abasi leseshayo Isigidimi Samaxosa bokumbula ukuba I-Paris iraulwe kuma
cala onke ngama Prussia anga 500,000, ke ngoko akuko unokupuma kona
ngendlela, yinqaba nokutunyelwa kwencwadi.

Kude kwati ngamini itile ku September U-Duruof wati uyakuzisa ezo ncwadi
E-Tours umzi ozi mayile ezili 120 ukusuka E-Paris. Yesuka lendoda yabeka
pezulu umgama ozi mayile ezimbini. Ingekapumi kuya pi ezindongeni zomzi,
ayibona ama Prussia, wawabona naye, ngexilongo lake lokukangela, xa
alungisa ikanunu nemipu, eza kumdubula. Amdubula okwenene. Kodwa ezo
mbumbulu zawo zapelela emoyeni, nakuba ezinye bezide zibuye kufupi. Wada
wase Mantes edu­tyulwa. Wawubona umsi pantsi kwake usuke welwale, waziva
nezitonga ezitsho kuwo.

Wakubona kakuhle ukuma kwemikosi yama Prussia. Abakwele emahasheni babe
like bawupahla lo uhamba ngenyawo umkosi.

Waya wehlela ngase Evreux umzi wase France, wamkelwa ngovuyo. Seleye
wafika nase Tours. Ube pete inxowa zontatu zencwadi zivela kwi duna
lemfazwe, nakwe zinye izikulu. Kwa kutiwe makaze azinike umpati walomzi,
mhlaumbi igosa lomkosi. Kwatiwa makaze azitsMse kunokuba zibo- nwe
ngomnye umntu.

Kuvakala ngoku ukuba leposi yase sibakabakeni iya kubako iveki zonke
E-Paris. Akuko kutandabuza ke ukuba wona ama Prussia afuna indlela
zokuyi tshabalalisa. Kungati noko kuya kuwa nqabela, ngapandle kokuba
abe nawo enze ezinye zokutintela ama French, kuba amaqinga omntu awapele
ndawo, ngoku- kodwa xa afuna ukubulala omnye.

U-Duruof unga angabuya abuyele kwa- se Paris. Usalinde ukuba umoya ube-
kise ngakona ukuvutela. Akubonakali noko ukuba uyakupumelela, ngokuba xa
aya kona angati epose umzi lowo kube sekupelile.

Lendoda yenze oku ubudala bayo ise 29 eminyaka.

Funda Isigidimi Samaxosa uve imima- ngaliso yelizwe.

UKUTETWA KWAMATYALA E-RINI

UMGWEBI ENGU MB. DWYER.

Ukutetwa kwamatyala kuqaleke ngolo 1 ku Nov., ngexa le shumi.

U-Mr. Tainton wafungiselwa ukubaabe yitoliki.

Abizwa ngamanye amakonxwa ukuba azitete- lele. Abavumayo ukuba banetyala
ngaba:—

UKUBIWA KWE NKOMO.

U-Tyeshane wase Philipton oweba inkomo ezi 13, zika Johannes Hendrick
Van de Veyver umfuyi wangase Sinqenqeni ngolwe 5 ku August.

Ugwetyelwe ukuba akatswe imivumbo enga 25 ahlale iminyaka emitatu
etolongweni, asebenze kanzima.

UBUGEBENGA.

U-Stephanas no Armoed no Joyce, abasebenzi base Rosendale banikwa ityala
lokuba bati ngolwe 17 ku Sept., bamwela U-Nxukumeshe wase Sheshegu
bamhlaba ngesitshetshe bamenza- kalisa entloko nase zingalweni, bambeta
ngenduku nangamatye, nangamanqindi, entloko, nasebusweni, nasezandleni,
nasemzimbeni.

U-Stephanus no Armoed bagwetywa, wakulu- leka U-Joyce. U-Armoed wakuvuma
nokuba ube nehashe.

Umgwebi wati akufuti kule Koloni ukuti aba- hambi badlwengulwe
ezindleleni. Kuya qondakala ukuba aba babanjwa bebepumele ubudlwengu.
Wati ku Armoed makaze abetwe imivumbo eli 15 ahlale unyaka etolongweni
asebenze kanzima. Kwatiwa ku Stephanus ityala lake lilula, kengoko
wagwetyelwa unyaka aze asebenze kanzima, angabetwa kodwa.

UKUBIWA KWE BOKWE.

U-Whitbooy wase Wilger Fontein E-Albany wanikwa ityala lokokuba weba
ngomhla wesi 7 ku July ibokwe ezi 3 nezintsu zebokwe ezintatu zika
William Johannes Goosen.

Wati umgwebi, ukuba ebese ngumfana ebeya kukatswa, kodwa kuba eyindoda
endala uya kusinda ekubetweni. Kodwa ke wohlala etolon- gweni asebenze
kanzima iminyaka emibini.

UKUBIWA KWE GUSHA.

U-Africander Jantyis umtwa wase Bourbon Fontein E-Albany wanikwa ityala
lokokuba ngo- kokuqala wati ngolwe 17 ku August weba igusha ka Gert
Johannes Nel umfuyi wase Bourbon Fountein. Nokuba ngokwesibini, weba
enye igusha ekwa yeka Nel ngolwama 26 ku August. Wagwetyelwa ke iminyaka
emibini, asebenze kanzima etolongweni, abetwe imivumbo ema 25.

UKUBIWA KWE HASHE.

U-Jack Gololo wanikwa ityala lokuba weba ngomhla we 10 ku Sept,
ihashekazi elingekaziwa umninilo.

U-Hook inkosi yama polisa wati kwakubuzwa kuye waxela ukuba umbanjwa
lowo wati ekuba- njweni kwake ulibe elohashe ngase Dikeni. Wa- gwetyelwa
iminyaka emibini aze asetyenziswe kanzima.

UKUBIWA KWE NKABI EZIMBINI.

I-Lawrence Bredenbach (umntu omhlope) wanikwa ityala lokuba ebe
E-Kereiga inkabi ezi 2 zika James William Carney ngolwe 11 ku Oct.

Umbanjwa lowo wazitandazela ukuba litotywe ityala lake wati kumhla
aqalayo ukuba, ulusizi ke kakulu. Wati umongameli wamatyala sezili 11
inkabi zenkomo ezilahlekileyo zika Mr Carney; zezi zombini kupela
ezifunyenweyo.

Umgwebi wamxelela umbanjwa ukuba wenze into elihlazo kakulu kanti
ungumntu omhlope: wati ke ebenokunga angake atyatyulwe, kodwa ke nje
ngokuba engati kumhla aqalayo ukuba netyala uya kunikwa iminyaka emibini
kube kupela, asebenze kanzima.

ISELA ELIDALA.

U- William Fredrick Morden (umntu omhlope) wanikwa ityala lokuba; kuba
weba ngolwa 30 ku July inkabi ezi 5 zika George Thomas Palmer umfuyi
E-Cypher Fontein. Nokuba ngokwe sibini, weba ngolo 1 ku August ihashe
lika John Thomas umgcini weposi wase Rini. Nokuba ngokwesitatu wati
ngolwe 4 ku Augnst waboleka ihashe, nesali, nomkala E-Blaauw Krantz
ukuba aye E-Rini, ebolekela ukuza azishiye ezonto ehoteleni; waza ke
kodwa wawapula lomnqopiso ngokuti enze intando zake ngazo, waza ke ngoko
waba ubile. Ngokwesine, okokuba weba inkabi zenkomo za 5 E-Featherstones
Kloof zika George Wood umfuyi wase Nxuba ngolwe 7 ku August.

Umbanjwa lowo wawavuma onke lomatyala, nokokuba kanjalo wake wase
tolongweni umnyaka E-Cradock. Waxelelwa ke ekugwetyweni kwake ukuba
akuko temba lokuba umntu onje ngaye angabuya abe ngumntu, ke ngoko
makatintelwe ngendlela zonke ukuze angabuye onakalise. Wagwetyelwa
iminyaka esi 7, esebenza kanzima.

UBUSELA.

U-Jan Jenjani umsebenzi wase Sinqenqeni wanikwa ityala lokuba wati
ngolwe 2 ku June weba E-Eeeuw Fontein ibokwe ezi 11 zika Christoffel
Andries Schoeman umlimi wase Leeuw Fontein. Nokuba ngokwesibini wati ngo
17 ku June weba E-Appies Draai izivato, nesidla, nomtya we watch (oku
kukuti intsimbi yamaxesha) nesitshe- tshe impahla zika Xekana wase
Appies Draai. Nokokuba wati ngokwesitatu kwangalomini weba E-Sinqenqeni
ihashe, nomkala, nencawa, nentabo ezimbini impahla zika Samuel Sydney
Mills um- limi wase Sinqenqeni. Nokuba ngokwesine wati ngolwe 9 ku June
weba ibokwe ezimbini zika Nohuza.

Wavuma umbanjwa lowo ukuba unetyala, engalikatalele elo tyala lake
kwanamanye anga- pambili.

Watike umgwebi kusemhlotsheni ukuba uleli- kulu isela.

Wati umbanjwa andinakunceda nkosi. Fanele ukuba ndibulewe. (Akutsho kwe
qara ukuhleka). Wabulela ukuba egwetyelwe iminyaka emihlanu aze asebenze
kanzima.

UKUBIWA KWE BOKWE.

U-Hendrik Bosak no Isaac Vincent banikwe ityala lokuba ibokwe ezine zika
Jan Jeremias Daniel Nell umlimi. Bagwetyelwa ukuba bazuze imivumbo ema
25 neminyaka emibini etolongweni basebenze kanzima.

UKUBIWA KWENKABI.

U-Jim wanikwa ityala lokuba inkabi ezi 3 E-Kobonqaba zika George Daniel
Diesel ngolwa 20 ku June.

Ute umgwebi, ukuba izohlwayo ekohlwaywa ngazo aziwatinteli amasela,
makohlwaywe ngezingapezulu, umbanjwa lo makabetwe imivumbo ema 25,
ahlale etolongweni iminyaka emibini esebenza kanzima.

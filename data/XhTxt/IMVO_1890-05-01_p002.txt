NGOLWESI-NE, MAY 1, 1890.

Umti Ungapezulu ko Ntsundu na?

  RULUMENTE, ngabapati
  mahlati, ubapete gadalala

abantu bakowetu kweli lase Maxhoseni.

Indawana enokutatyatwa uyingenise pantsi kwo Mteto wa Mahlati, kwabe
sekukancinane ukuba yonke into engumti ekulayo, ingafanelwa kugaulwa.
Ukwaka, ukubiya, kwanomlilo, — kufuneka uluti Abantu ngezizukulwana
ezininzi ezidluleyo bebe sebeqhele ukulufumana kumagcume namahlati
akufupi. Namhla bafika amahlati selezaliswe ngamapolisa apa angubo
ziluhlaza olufana nezikhwenene zehlati, asuke axhakamfule wonke opete
uluti, ibekoko ukumsa kwi Mantyi, ebehle imtumele entolongweni yoninzi.
Siya
kuvuma kona ukuba abantu bako wetu ’ke bafeketa ngamahlati ngamaxesha
adluleyo, nakwezinye indawo; kwanokuba, ngenxa yezizatu ezininzi,
ifanelwe ukugcinwa imiti enoncedo: kuloko u Rulumente lomsebenzi usuke
wawunikela amadoda aqonde into eqondwa ngawo kupela, suke lamagosa,
engakatali nto yilento ingumntu, amisa iqalekiso kuyo yonke into
engumti, netyolo, engayikatalele into yokuba umti otile wehlati
ungabizwa ngagama nye nomnye umti wangaselwandle, omnye unomsebensi,
omnye ungenawo ; yalelwe yomibini. Wakubuza ukuba
kuza kutiwanina ngokomlilo, usuka kulamadoda ube ngorharhayo. Yinto ke
leyo u Rulumente afanele

ukuyiqonda, ukuba angababulali abantu ngokwalela nemiti engena msebenzi.

Abantsundu base Ngqushwa siteta nje base bunzimeni, nendleko zamatyala.
Bate bona bakuva ukuba abapati bamahlati bayeza, basebebuta imali ukuze
’ke bave, balwele amalungelo abo. Lomcimbi ubungowokuqwalaselwa ngu
Mpatiswa-Micimbi ya Bantsundu ; koko akako ngokufa. Abanye abapati
babayeke abise Ngqushwa ukuba bazibonel; abanye badliwe, omnye elake
lisasingiswe ezi jajini Ngoko-mteto wa Mangesi asina kulingena elo
ityala. Kodwa singenza libe linye ngento ebangele ukuba i Mantyi
yenjenje. Wonke umlesi angaba uyazi ukuba Amamfengu angeniswa e Ngqushwa
ngo 1835, ngo 1837, nango 1845, kwenziwe iminqopiso kunye nawo, anikwa
lomhlaba unalamahlati. Amandla alo Mnqopiso asikabi nakuwaxela ngapambi
kokuhlala kwe Jaji ezayo. Ukuba iminqopiso ka Rulumente na Bantsundu
yinto yokuqwengwa xa atandayo, ingaba i Ngilane iteta ntonina ke pofu
kwelase Germany ne Portugal? Asikakolwa noko ukuba u Rulumente angaba
unike namnye waba Pati-Mahlati, noko aselengu Mtshutshisi Wamatyala,
igunya lokuba anyelise iminqopiso yabo nabantsundu njengoko e1inye ipepa
litshoyo, kuba kubantu bakowetu bona ilizwi lesicaka sika Rulumente
seyilelika Rulumente. Asiyiboni into ebangela ukuba kubeko ukutetwa
kwalamatyala, kuba angasuke ati kanti usayakugwetywa u Rulumente; ibe
yonke inkatazo ingeyingabangako ukuba kaloku bebeyiwe kakuhle abantu
base Ngqushwa, ingengawo kodwa amagosa anyelisa iminqopiso emikulu,
nakatalele ukuni olungenamsebenzi, kunesizwe sabantu.

Abamhlope Ngabantsundu.

IPEPA lase Kapa i Cape Times, lisixelela ngomoya okoyo wokuba ii
Rulumente ucinga ukucela i Palamente ukuba lipeliswe isebe lo
Mpatiswa-Wabantsundu kubapati bolaulo, ize indawo yake itatyatwe ngu
Mpatiswa-Micimbi yabalimi. Lengxelo ayisokutusa nabani ogqibeleleyo
ukwazi ngempato yeli lizwe, ngenxa yokuba elisebe belite ngamaxa
agqitileyo lasisikubekiso kunokuba luncedo kwimpato yamawetu. kule
mimandla besede kwako inkolo enkulu yokuba amacebo abapati babantsundu
abanokwazi okugqibeleleyo ngabo, asuke alahlelwe enkunkumeni, lonto ke
seyide yabangela ukuba kungabi salindelwa bulungisa bungaveliswa lelo
sebe.

LA ngamazwi e Re-Echo, elase Humansdorp, xa ibhekiselela ekuketweni
kwebala yi Palamente: “Siyasikankanya nesiduko sababatunywa babini (o
Messrs, van Minnen no Hawkins) kuba iyinto engumangaliso ukuba ati
amadoda amabini, angengawo nama Koloni ngokuzalwa, abe selezaliswe
yintiyo enkulu ngakontsundu. Sinokuyiqondisisa kakuhle yona into yokuba
ifama le Bhulu libe limchasile omnyama; kuba leyo iyiyona nto londliwa
ngayo. Kwakwintsuku ezidluleyo lafundiswa ukumkangela ontsundu ngohlobo
inkosi ekangela ngalo ikoboka. Kanti noko asizanga silive ifama nokuba
selixakaniseke ngalupina uhlobo, litetela ngendlela epantsi,
neyokumchasa kangaka ontsundu, njengako benjenjalo aba batunywa, saye ke
siqinisekile ukuba kumashumi amahlanu amalungu e Bondi akuko nalinye
eliya kuvumelana nezimvo zo van Minnen no
Hawkins. Asazi nento ngo van Minnen lo nokuba angaba lifama nokuba
uyintonina. U Hawkins yena ligqwetana nje kodwa, waye exelenga imali
eninzi kwabantsundu, kanye ababantu ke yena abagweba ngoluhlobo
lumasikizi.”

ITETA ngomfanekiso we Ruluneli, i Excalibur, elase Kapa, yenjenje: I
pepa le Mvo Zabantsundu, libhasele abalesi balo ngomfanekiso we Ruluneli
u Sir Henry Loch. Laye lisiti ‘Sanga esisenzo sitsha, nesokuqala kule
Koloni singancedisa ukunyhanyhatela uluvo lwentobelo-mbuso, nokuzinikela
okupeleleyo kwama wabo kulaulo olo Umhlekazi angumqondiso Womntan’ Omhle
u Fitoli.’ Ndinga ndigazisa u Sir Henry ukuba elipepa libhalwa, lihlelwe
kwali nene elintsundu elicingelwa ukuba lilipongoma lulaulo luka
Sipiligi. Eyona nyaniso ingenakupikiswa kukuba selenze ukulunga okukhulu
pakati komzi ontsundu, liwupakamisele kwingqondo yokuzipendulela
nokunyamekela izinto zombuso, napezukoko selidale nobuhlobo pakati
kwabamhlope nabantsundu bale Koloni. Ndinovuyo ukuti ngokungapezulu
ezonandawo ziyintloko kwelo pepa zibhalwa ngentobeko, nangomoya
opolileyo, nangobuciko obukulu, zayeke zibaluleke ngokuzala yimbeko
nangamaxa empikiswano enezibeko. Ndiyavuya ukongeza ukuba Imvo izimisele
ukwenza neminye imifanekiso xa kute kwako impumelelo. Leyo ke yoba yeya
Mangesi apakamileyo, awaziwa ngaba ntsundu abakanyiselweyo, nakuba
okwangoku esamana ukuvakala njengamapupa kubo.”

lokupata umntu ontsundu.

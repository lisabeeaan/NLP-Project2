ELITYALA litetwe ngolwe 16 ku November 1880. Lenje nje ke ukuma kwalo
:—Lo mfundiswa-shishini ungu Cornelius, walile uku wasebenza
amaxesha-xesha abengeko ngawo emsebenzini wake, yakupela iminyaka
yokubotshwa kwake. Kutiwe kuye pambi kokuba ayi fumane yonke imali yake
maka zalise idinga lake, alenzileyo kwi ncwadi ekutiwa yincwadi yama
xesha adlekileyo, apo abeke ne gama lake. Uyile ke kwa Mantyi,
wagwetywa, koko wabenela e Jajini, e Rini. Liti ke ipepa eliyi
Grrahamstown Journal, xa libalisa ngoku tetwa kwelo tyala:—Aku
tandatyuzelwanga ngaba Gwebi okokuba lomfana ubonise ubu menemene, ne
ntswela mbulelo ; umke emsebenzini wake ngapandle kwemvumelo, naxa
kungeko sizatu, ezenza ofayo, xa kubonakala kakuhle ukuba ebe pilile.
Ngapezu koko wafunga ngobu xoki, esiti ebenyanzelwe ukuba alibeke igama
lake encwadini apo. Ngoku futshane, i Institution yayi kohlisekile
kanti, mhlana yamamkela lo. Kwaluvuyo okokuba abagwebi bavumelene
ngeli—lokuba indawo ebe kujongwe kuyo nga- bapati be Institution ibi
yentle ne nobubele. Baliqukumbela elityala ngamazwi angancipisa iratshi
lake u Cornelius lowo, ukuba akalulo ugqwegqwelele. Aba pati base
Lovedale bagwetyiwe, ngendawo yokuba bengenzanga nje ngoko isiko limise
ngako, kuba ngebete, bamana ukumsa kwi mantyi ngama xesha onke abe
ngabangako ngawo emsebenzini, iz’ ibe yiyo eya kvnika umteto wokuba awa
sebenze lomaxesha adlekileyo.

Liya hambisa eli pepa liti:—Oku kumana ezikupa u Cornelius emsebenzini
wake; oku kuxela kwake nge ratshi nango buxoki, ukuba ebesiya kufuna
amayeza abengena kuwafumana e Institution ; obu bunqina bake bunje uku
ngabi nanyaniso ; oku kuwu zimela kwake umsebenzi abe ufanele, ngokuti
abalekele ezi ofisini; oku kuswela kanye kuye umbulelo ekupataneni kwake

ndoda epeleleyo ; oku konke kungumboniso odanisayo kanye kotanda

siveliswa koku fundiswa kwabamnya- ma amashishini, ngubanina ongatanda
ukunika umsebenzi kubantu abanemikwa enje?

U Mr. Justice Shippard kwi nteto ayenzileyo xa ateta ngeli tyala uti
:—Okunene isiqendu esitile somteto obekisele kwiminqopiso elunge nezi
caka, siti — Ixesha esite eso sicaka asabiko ngalo emsebenzini we nkosi
yaso, lino kongezwa kwela xa ekwa ku nqotshiswene ngalo—mhla mnene.
Kodwa ke esi siqendu siteta xa site eso sicaka, nokuba ngu Msebenzi
(Apprentice) sigwetywe kuqala yi mantyi, nge tyala lokunga biko

umteto awuna kuba nceda kweli tyala.

Le nteto yo benileyo, le iti unyanzelwe ukuba ali bale igama lake, ukuba
uyamkele lendawo yokuba uya kulise benza eli xesha libangwayo, elo xesha

zohlwayo bezi mfanele, wajik’ inqoboka kuba ncedi bake, aba pati be
Lovedale, wazi nceda wapumelela ngo mteto, xa apikisa indawo abe nqopise
ukuba uya kuyenza. I Love- dale Institution ipete ngoku ncomekayo, inika
ama lungelo angena kugqitywa, kwaba Ntsundu aba fundiswayo naba sebenzi
(apprentices). Ubu menemene obunje ngobu balo mutu ub'enileyo, buyi

ISIPELO BE NGXOXO TOKU HLUTWA KWE MIPU E LUSUTU.

BOKUMBULA aba leseshi betu ukuba kwi Sigidimi esidluleyo, sasiteta nge
ngxoxo enkulu eyabi vuswe ngu Mr. FULLER, ngoku fika azi case ezi
kalazela indawo ezi miswe ngu Bulumente ngoku malunga noku hlutwa kwe
ntonga e Lusutu. Tinto ke leyo ekude yapela iveki ku xoxwa ukuze ke ku
votwe ate u RULUMENTE wana 37 aba nge cala lake, waza u Mr. FULLER wana
28, kwa bonakala ke ukuba u RULUMENTE ugqite nga bantu abasi toba, 9.

Abecala lika Mr. FULLER, 28. ngaba:—

Aling, Bergh, De Smit, J. J. De Villiers, Rev. W. P. De Villiers,
Farmer, Fleming, Fuller, Goldschsmidt, Hofmeyr Irvine, Lewis, M. J.
Louw, T. Louw, Mackay, Manuel, Marais, Merriman, Molteno, Proctor,
Bensburg, Sauer, Solomon, Stigant, Scanlen, Vincent, Wilman.

Abe cala lika RULUMENTE 37:—

Auret, W. Ayliff, J. Ayliff, J. Barry, T. D. Barry, Blaine, Bradfield,
De Wet, Everitt, Frost, Goold, Hockley, Human, Joubert, Keyter,
Kirkwood, Laing, Leonard, Moodie, Myburgh, Orpen, Powell, Beitz, Beid,
Bobertson, Sichel, Sprigg, Te Water, Tennant, Timm, Upington, Walker,
Wood, S. C. Wright, E. C. Wright, Watermeyer, Van Zyl.

Okunene ke a Besutu baya ku hlutwa imipu. Kute kwi sicelo sa madoda abe
tunyiwe becela ukuba bapiwe ixesha pambi koku tatyatwa kwe ntonga, loku
shumayeza Inkosi nama pakati ngoku se mhlotsheni nangoku zalisekileyo
nge hambo yawo,—Siviwe eso sicelo kwatiwa nje ngokuba kwaku miswe umhla
wama 21 ku June ukuba kuni kelwe intonga ngawo, banikwe icawa ezi ntatu
ku susela kolwama 21 ku June, oko kukuti baya kuyi nikela imipu ngo suku
lwe 12 ku July.

  Bayi fundelwa ke ne mpendulo ye NKOSAZANA eyiyo le ilandelayo,

“DOWNING-STREET, 13 May, 1880.

“ Ku RULUNELI ongu Mhlekazi Sir H. BARTLE FRERE.

NKOSI,—Ndiya kwazisa ukuba ndiyi fumene incwadi yako, eyi No. 46, yomhla
wamashumi ma 20 ku February, ine sicelo esivela kwi Kkosi yabe Sutu u
Letsea eyi balele umntan’ omhle ongu Kumkanikazi, ecelela isizwe sake
abe Sutu ukuba singa hlutwa izixobo.

2.  Ndiya kwazisa ke ukuba esi sicelo sibekwe pambi kwo Kumkanikazi,
    ondiyalele ukuba ndixele ukuba uyamkele le tshumayezo omshumayeze
    yona nge migcobo le yokuba ngabantu aba petwe ngu Bulumente
    abadibeneyo nama Bulumente, ukuba ndikwa zise ukuba ukolisiwe u
    Kumka­nikazi sisenzo sika Letsea nabantu bake.

3.  Umntan’ omhle uya temba ukuba abantu bake aba ngabe Sutu, abantu
    abavela kunene kuma lungelo abo, aku-
    qonda indawo nokuma kweli cebo eli tatyatiweyo ngu Bu­lumente
    opantsi kwake ose Cape Town, ukuba banga cingi iukuti batotyelwa
    ezantsi ngeso senzo, kuti uku yikangela kwabo lento bayi kangele
    kakuhle ukuba ixela ukuba lifikile ixesha lokuba kuyo ihambiselo
    pambili yabe Sutu kwi misebenzi yoku kanya, baya celwa ukuba izenzo
    zabo mayela noku pata intonga bazi qelise uku nga pati ntonga nje
    ngokuba kunjalo kubantu aba pantsi ko Mntan’ omhle kuzo indawo ezi
    pantsi kwo laulo lwake ; nokuba bayi gqale kakuhle lendawo kwenziwa
    yona yeyo kuba kusela abe Sutu ngalo lonke ixesha loxolo abate
    bahluka ngalo kwe zinye izizwe zaba ntsundu base South Africa.

Ndingu,

(Signed) KIMBERLEY.”

Eyivile ke impendulo ye NKOSAZANA nesi gqibo se Parlamente u LETSEA
inkosi yase Lusutu wenze esi saziso, “ Isicelo setu simangelwe ngu
Mtanomhle ongu Kumka­nikazi ese ndlwini ya Matyala. Uti Ukumkanikazi
masi nikele intonga zetu Uti um tanomhle masi qiniseke ukuba uku
zinikela kwetu intonga ezi asi zincipisi, eyonanto kwenzelwa yona ku
londolozelwa tina vxolo. Umtanomhle uti uku nikelwa oku kwe ntonga
asiyinto yabe Sutu bodwa, yinto epantse ukuba kubo bonke aba pantsi
kwake. I Parlamente yase Kapu iya mvumela kulento. Nditi ke mna,
Bakwena, lento iseli gqityiwe kade. Akuko yimbi indlela esi ngenza
ngayo. Nditi ke xa nditshoyo qubani nje ngalomteto ka Bulumente ; mveni
u Rulumen­te. Ngayo lecawa gomhla wama 21 ku June, ize nizi ni­kele
intonga zenu. ,Nina Zinkosana lulamani nifake imiteto kubantu aba pantsi
kwenu. Kekaloku, Bakwena, nditi zinikeleni intonga zenu nigcine uxolo.”

LOVEDALE INSTITUTION.

KUYA ziswa ukuba Isikola siya kuvulwa ngolwe sine evekini ngomhla wama
29 ka July 1880 e Lovedale, abazali baya celwa ukuba bati abaya kuviwa
bafike ngayo lomini kanye, nge brakfesi, mhlayimbi ngosuku olunga
pambili.

UMBONISO WE ZILIMO.

PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

(Kaffrarian Watchman.)

LOMBONISO we minyaka yonke wauko ngomvulo ngo suku lwama 26 ku May, e
Ngqamakwe, yenziwa lomini i holide kuso sonke eso sitili. Taba
yintlanganiso enkulu kudibene nezi pata mandla zakwa Rulumente ezi
pesheya kwe Nciba, aba fundisi, naba nini venkile.

Impahla eya ziswa ngaba ntsundu yayi lungile kanye. Kwa miswa i flag
pezu kwendlu yama tyala, kwa betwa intambula, sati nje ngokuba sihamba
hamba samana siqubisana ne ntlbo ntlobo zamabala, sada sangati siku
mboniso wase Glasgow.

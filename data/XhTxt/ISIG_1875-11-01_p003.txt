  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1875.

ICULO LOMTSHATO.

WORDS BY REV. J. A. CHALMERS.

MUSIC BY JNO. KNOX BOKWE.

Hlabela ntlabeli iculo

Leyetw’ imihlali, Hlabela elona limnandi

Elona lomtshato,

Libambeni, nina zintombi,

Niyek’ ukukwekwa.

Nani madodana, yitshoni,

Ningakwekwi nani.

(Chorus) Yimini &c.

3. Kunamhla yendile intombi, Yasekaya apa,

  Namhla ke iyemka kowayo, Iy’ emzini wayo;

Mvuyiseni nonke zintombi Owen’ umlingane,

Iz’ ingay’ libali lemini, Yokwen’ ukugcoba. (Chorus) Yimini &c.

4. Iyemka kuyise, nonina,
Nakuba nakwayo,
lyapuma ebuntwaneni,
Nasebuntombini;

  Iyakuba ngumnikazi mzi, Apo iya kona;

  Isinga kwelasemzini, Esingalaziyo.

(Chorus) Yimini &c.

5. Yivuyiseneni zintombi,

Nani madodana,

Yivuyiseneni bafazi,

Nani bantwanana,

Yivuyiseneni bonina,

Nani bango yise,

Mawuvuye namhl’ umz’ upela

Imke yonwabile.

Chorus. Yimini &c

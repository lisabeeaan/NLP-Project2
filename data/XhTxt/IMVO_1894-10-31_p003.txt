NKOSI MHLELI,—Kwi “Mvo” ye 25 July 1894 ukankanye ukuti kungati
kufumaneke abalesi be Sisutu abamayela namakulu amahlanu singakangelwa
isicelo somhlati we Sisutu. Ko bendicela kuwe ukuba uke undifakole
loyoncwadi ndiyibhekisa kumadoda onke ase Lusutu nase benza pakati
kwabo, ndiqale ngabafundisi bohlanga abakwangabatandi balo o Revs,
Monyatsi no Motiyane, (Basuto­land Msikinya no Magaba, (Diamond-
fields), Nqana (Colesberg) Msikinya (Aliwal North), namanene ohlanga
akwangabatandi balo anjengo Messrs. Kalaka (Morija), Dichaba qo
Mzozoiyana (Mafet-

ing), Sebeta (Matelile), Matlanyane no Keletsi (Wepner), nabanye ke
abafundisi namanene ohlanga endingawa kankanyanga ngokoyikela ukugqiba
indawo epepeni. Nditi make sishukume siblanganiso amagama abalesi be
Sisutu ; ukuma nokuwa kwelipepa kusezandleni zetu. Ukuwa kwalo yi
Disarmament lonto, kukuhlutwa k wezixhobo, niyayazi imfazo yokuhlutwa
kwemipu, kwapalala igazi; Isigidimi safa kwangokuyekelelwa siti.
Inyaniso yona yokulunga kwe “Mvo” ayinamntu ungayikanyelayo,
seyinezigigaba ezenzileyo ezingena kukanyelwa mntu, izikalazo zetu ngaku
Rulu­meni izipakamisela pezulu, akukataleleki nokuba ngu Mbo, Mxosa,
Msutu onesikalazo—ilwela lonke uhlanga olumnyama kuba sibanye ;
lingayekwa na ke ipepa lohlanga ngabatandi bohlanga liwe. Singaba
asibatandi bohlanga xa ngaba singaliyekelela liwe; ukuhlutwa kwezi-
xhobo kuteta ukupalala kwegazi madoda, lento indoda ayifane ihlutwe
intonga ingalwanga. Kuba kubi kwakutiwa ipe­pa lohlanga limi ngarmenze
mnye ngetixa yokuswela abaxhasi. Ningayideli lento manene ohlanga,
inkulu : masizame lamakulu mahlanu nokuba agqitile kukona kungalungayo.
Wena ke Mhleli uz’ uman’ ukuwahlokomisa kwi “Mvo” amagama angenileyo,
nalapo avela kona, —Owako,

W. N. SOMNGESI.

MAKULIWE!—NGOMTETO.

MHLELI OBEKEKILEYO,—Lomteto wase Glen Grey uzokucita umzi kanye, okanye
udala umoya ornbi ebantwini ngaku Rulumente; nditsho ngokuba kwezi
ntlanganiso zombini zazise Ngqamakwe, kwabako ukungavisisani kwabantu
nezibonda, kwanezibonda zodwa, kwasuka kwapitizelwa. Kubonakala ukuba u
Rulumente ufuna ituba lokupanga imihlaba yetu eyaqiniselwa
ngokubalulekileyo. Sibona tina ukuba u Rulumente unga abantu bangalwa
naye ukuze ke afumane ituba lokufumana imihlaba yetu, lomteto kupela
usisibata sokucapukisa. Nditi ke mzi wakowetu ize ningamvuyisi u
Rulumente, lendawo mayiliwe ngokwase mitetweni yombuso, kubekwe pantsi
intonga, nangani yena kubonakala ukuba ucapukisa abantu ukuba bavukele
umbuso, ize ningayingeni lonto mzi wakowetu ; kodwa makuliwe ngomlomo
nangosiba nepepa. Umzi wonke mawuyijonge lendawo, kufunwe indlela ide i
Palamente ilungise lento, okanye ingena kuyilungisa kutunywe amadoda
awele ayekuzibika indawo eziwuhlungisayo umzi Pesheya. Lento yona
yokuvuma yonke into: niyakukwelwa ngomso, Njengokoba namhla nenziwa
amakoboka ngalomteto: yonke into ekufaneleyo ukusebenza mayirole i 10s.,
kwabanjani e Jeputa, yonke into eyinkwenkwe mayiposwe emlanjeni. Vukani
mzi kuliwe ngemilomo, vukani madoda yemka imi­hlaba yoyihlo.—Ndim,
ongazanga wakukataza.

JOHN MAVUSO,

Isigidimi samaxosa

  ICEBO KUMZI ONTSUNDU.

IXESHA lokunyula abameli nbaya kusimela e-Palamente
elipezu kwetu, kanti ke noko sisazolile, asika shukumi
ngoku yixoxa lendawo, sibe zwi nye ngokumisa esiya ku-
votela bona, nokuba sihlangane ngendawo esifuna ukuba
basivelele ngazo.

Nyakenye bekuvakala ukuba nto nye ngexesha elikulu
pakati kwetu. Ngamacala onke beku menyezwa ngo
Tungumlomo; kwati kwindawo ngendawo kwa hlangani-
swana, kusitiwa makubenelwe Pesheya, kwada kwabutwa
nemali ekwatiwa isemakulwini amatatu, yokuxasa amadoda
abemisiwe ukuba awele. Yabanga ke londawo, ngokuba
i-Ruluneli yabeka isandla kulommiso ekutiwa ngowoku
bala abavoti, esiti tina ukuwubiza ngu Tungumlomo.
Kute kunjalo abaqondileyo abamhlope abaxase tina bantsu-
ndu, bati akuseko nto singafeza yona ngokubenela Pesheya.
Yaye ke noko inxenye yetu isadlokova ngamandla, idloko-
vela ukubenela Pesheya. Kuye kwati kwalapo kwavela
ntwana ebale epepeni lama Ngesi eliyi Watchman, yati aku-
konakali; ngokuba kulo mandla wase Qonce, no wakwa
Komani, no wase Dikeni bebaninzi abanga tungekanga.
Yongeza yati lentwana, e-Nqushwa kutungekile; abakona
bazitunge ngokwabp, ngokuba itaitile bazalayo; kukwa
kokwabo ukuyiqaqulula imitungo ngokutabata itaitile.
bangene eku voteni, waqoshelisa ke umbali lowo, ngokuti
nkuba i-Felkoneti ayimfaki encwadini zabavoti osukuba
emi umhlaba omiselwe iponti ezi £25 libandla lomahlulo
(Divisional Council), makati lowo abenele ezi Jajini,
ngokuba zona azikugqita kwinani elimiswe libandla lo-
mahlulo.

Kuzakuti ke lakufika ixesha lokungeniswa abavoti, kuti
kwezidolopu zonke zinemantyi, kubonakale ngobuninzi
babo, ukuba abantsundu bayafuna inene ukuba bangeniswe
emalungelweni okunyula amalungu e-Palamente. Kute
e-Dikeni kwagecwa iqela lamadoda anetayitile, kusitiwa
imihlaba yawo ayifikile exabisweni la £25, kanti lomihlaba
ibe imiselwe elo nani libandla lomahlulo, ekute naxa itengi-
sayo yazuza namashumi amatatu namane. Kwati kwaoko
Pesheya kwe Nciba sabona kugecwa ama-Rulumente ama-
dala, abekulele pantsi kombuso wo-Mntan’ Omhle, emkonze
ngamandla, emlwela imikosi yake, izidwangube ezinje
ngabo BIKITSHA nabanye, ngokutiwa bangama vezandlebe
ngokuba bengazalelwanga pantsi kombuso we Koloni.

Kute sakubona ezo ndawo, sati— Yehl’ into ! namhla kuza
kudlokova iqegu, liza kuyiwisa imvaba liyiqwenge ; kanti
hayi. Lidlokove langquleka ngo Tungumlomo. Kwati
kwakufika ixesha lokudlokova, kwafunyanwa izinto esezi-
tambe nje ngomnweba wembila, Besiba kuyakuke kuta-
tyatwe kule mali ebekutiwa ib' ihlanganiselwe ukubenela
Pesheya kolwandle, zike zisiwe pambi kwe Jaji ezindawo
zenziwa e-Dikeni nezi zenziwa Pesheya kwe Nciba, zipe-
ndule okokuba zingomteto na nokuba azingawo, ukuze
kuti nangona zingasayi kuba saconjululwa indawo ezenze-
kileyo, kuze zicaciselwe indawo eziya kwenzeka ngomso.

Ukwenza kwetu akuqali ukuba nendawo ezisilelayo, pofu
zizindawo ebezifanele ukufezwa. Into siyayiqala, iti yaku-
ba nexeshana lipele igugu layo, siye kuqala nto yimbi,
kanti nayo ayikufezeka. Kuti mhlayimbi kufunwe ukumi-

“INKOSI ZIKULULWE EBUBANJWENI.”

  LUTSHILO ucingo oluqukuqele luvela e-Botwe lweza kupu-
  ma kule Ofisi yase Lovedale nezinye indawo. Kungo-
  vuyokazi olukulu ngoko esiwupakela ezo ndaba umzi
  wakowetu.

  Site ukutshona kwelanga lomhla ebesiza kushicilela
  ngalo esi Sigidimi sale nyanga ingasoze ilibaleke kuti nge
  Jubili zayo nesi senzo sanamhla, sabona umpatiswa luci-
  ngo walapa esizisela ucingo olute sakulunqika lwafike
  lwenjenje ukuteta kwala;—“ U-MAYASE ukululwe ebuba-

  njweni izolo [2 ku September, 1888] kunye ne Nkosi
  zama-Xosa; nobonana naye emva kwentsukwana ezimba-
  lwa!”

  Kuvakele ukuba u-Sir GORDON SPRIGG ngalomini yaku-
  sasa kwe Cawa uye kuvelela amabantinti e-Kapa wati
  makubizwe inkosi ezingo EDMUND, TINI, MATANZIMA,
  GUNGUBELE, no GUMNA, wongezelela negama lika MAYASE
  MOMO umntu omnyama ob’ese siqitini se Robben Island.
  Ubekise lamazwi akuba ekankanye izenzo zemfazwe eza-
  bazisa apa, wati—“Namhla ndinizele noxolo; ningama-
  “doda akululekileyo angase ngawo ababanjwa kususela
  “ kweli xesha. Anisayi kuba sabuyela etolongweni; nantsi-
  “ ya indlu seniyilungiselwe emaniye kukululela kuyo ezi-
  “ ngubo zamakonxwa ninxibe ezintsha zenkululeko esenizi-
  “ tengelwe. Ngomso niya kunduluka nigoduselwe koma-
  wenu. Ize emakaya nizipate kakuhle, nipembelele uxolo
  “ nolungelelwano, nokutotyelwa komteto ka Rulumente.”

  Lo MAYASE kutetwa ngaye, nakuba enge nkosi, yenye
  yumadoda amatandatu awapuma apa e-Dikeni aya ema-

  hlatini ngo Ncayecibi. Ute kuhle kancinane pambi
  kokuba lixole waza kuzinikela komkulu. Kweso situba,
  engekapendulwa ngendawo yokuzinikela kwake, watiwa
  ntca ngetyala lokuba wadubula ipolisa edabini lase
  Nxuba—pofu ekanyela yeua kude kube namhlanje ukuba
  waka walubeka unyawo kwelo dabi. Kodwa lakutetwa
  ityala wugwetyelwa ukuxonywa. Ngesicelo esenziwa ku Sir
  BARTLE FRERE nge 1878, sagungxulwa isigwebo se jaji,
  waza u-Mhlekazi wasisunduzela ekubeni makasiwe esiqi-
  tini ahlale ebubanjweni bonke ubom bake. Asiyekanga
  noko ukumcela. Kute apa e-Dikeni nge Jubili ye NKOSA-
  ZANA, sakumbuza ngale ndoda ingu MAYASE, safaka
  nomda wokucela nenkosi zakowetu ezise bubanjweni.
  Ayisigibanga impendulo ka Sir GORDON SPRIGG esangena
  ngaye isicelo setu. Silindile unyaka lo. Namhla ke
  ukusipendula wenze nje ngoku sesixelile.

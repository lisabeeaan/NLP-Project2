NKOSI,—Ndibe nosizi kakulu ukubona incwadi yombhaleli wako, u Rev. Simon
P. Sihlali, nakubeni ndiba ubani makagafumane amangaliswe nayintonina
kwinto zombuso. Kami ke noko bendilindele izinto ezibhetele kumntu
onjengaye. Weza e Cala entlanganisweni kunye no Figilan, igosa
elihlaulwayo, ngoknva kwatn ke, lika Colonel Griffith, bobabini bafike
benza lanto, u Figilan engxelweni yake kuwe, anelisiweyo kukuyicingela
ukuba “ kukupazamisa ngemibuzo.” Ndabanyamezela bona ngapezu kwenxenye
yezibonda ezidala ezabonakala ngati zanelisiwe—kodwa ndazimisela
ukupendula yonke into ngokuzeleyo, njengoko ndenjenjalo. Kwingxelo ka
Mr. Figilan yentlanganiso, etatyatwe ngexesha ebesazamana nengxoxo
ezintsha, kubeko iziposo eziniuzi; kodwa ndicinga ukuba mandipaule
zibini. Ndazilungisa 26 July; ke ukuba u Rev, Sihlali wayenento yokuteta
ngalondawo, kulusizi ukuba ayiyeke ixesha elide kangaka. Noko ke,
anditandabuzi ukuba izibonda ezinjengo Mtengwane no Solo­mon Kalipa,
nezinye kwezininzi ezaziko ngalomhla, zinenkumbulo ezilunge njengeyake,
bondixhasa xa nditi incwadi yake u Rev. Sihlali ayincomeki. Baya
kuvumelana nam okokuba ngomcimbi we Taitile yama Mfengu ndiqale ngokuti
ndivana kakulu nokunikwa kwetaitile kwabo bayisebenzileyo imihlaba ya
bo, kodwa ndati ndibona ingxaki eyakubako knba seyiko itaitile yobuzwe
bania Mfengu. “ Ndayichaza ke into enditeta yona, ndaqoshelisa ngokuti
ndinqwenela ukuba ndivisiswe kakuble okokuba le ngxaki inokoyiswa,
ndingavuya kakulu ukubona itaitile zinikwa abo bamizamo namalinga
afanelwe kukuba bazifumane. U Rev. Sihlali ucinga ukuba ndingumntwana na
ukuba ndingaboni ngapava kweqingana lake ekubekeni lombuzo. Nangona
ndandibazi abanevoti, kwanokokuba sendineminyaka emininzi ndibonisa
ukulunga kwe taitile zeziqu, ndateta ngokute nqo, no Rev. Sihlali
uyayazi louto. Kwangalondlela ndati abelungu bevenkile bayakuba
nemfanelo eziuganeno kwelase Mamfengwini ezitaitileni kune Netevu
nganye, kodwa ndalate ukuba njengokuba abelungu beve­nkile bengenakuze
bagxotwe emihlabeni abayibiyeleyo nabakileyo kuyo, kalokunje
ayisenaxabiso lingakanani kwi Netevu nokuba abelungu bevenkile
bazifumene nokuba abazifumananga itaitile. Andizange nditi ndiya
kukuchasa ukunikwa kwetaitile kubelungu bevenkile; ndati ndichase ukuba
zifunyanwe ngabo tanci kune Netevu abona bantu bafanele ukuzifumana.

Ngomcimbi wemfundo u Rev. Simon Sihlali walinga ukiindiqotisa kwindawo
kakuba bonke abantwana babantsundu— amakwenkwe nokuba ngamantombazana,
kuqale kwabancinane kunyuse, nabafundela ubufundisi, mabanyanzelwe ukuba
bafunde imisebenzi yezandla ezikuleni nasezi Simnareni. Ndiyibonisile
intlanganiso ukuba anditeti lonto; kupela ke mandimbhekise u Rev.
Sihlali kwezi zibonda sendizikankanyile, ngendawo yokukoliswa
nokungakoliswa kwabo yiloncazelo. Eyona ndawo imbi ekundikatseni kuka
Rev. Sihlali, yile ati ndite kwabantsundu ukuba ndinyulwe ndiya kungena
njengelungu elizimele lodwa, ndaxelela abamhlope e Cala ukuba ndi­vana
nalo Rulumeni upeteyo endingati kongoko ndingene kweloqela.
Ololahlekiso, nokuba ingaba ziziqhamo zokungapulapuli, endingacingi
njalo, alunakuxoleleka kanye. U Rev. Sihlali ubefanele ukwazi kakuhle
ukuba abantsundu andibaxelelanga ukuba ndingozimele ndodwa kupela, kodwa
ndalate nokuba abangefumani kulunga abanyuli ukuba ndifumane ndihlomele
kwiqela elichasileyo, nokokuba ndaye ndingazibopelela ukuba ndilandele
amanqanam endiwaxelileyo kubo. Entlanganisweni yabamhlope, apo ndimbone
kona u Rev. Sihlali, ndite ndinganika inxasoepangeleleyoku Rulumeni kuba
kungebancedi nto abanyuli ukufumane ndingene kwiqela elichase nje kodwa,
kodwa ndizibopelele kwindawo endizichazileyo entlanganisweni. Ngazwinye,
indawo ayitatileyo u Faku e Bandla, kupela nditemba, ukuba ndinyuliwe,
ukuba ndiyomeleze ngapezulwana. Into endikangele kuyo mna, kukwenziwa
kweqela lamadoda amvo zilingeneyo entlanga zombini ezise Bandla; liqela
elinjalo endingahlomela kulo ukuba ndinyulwe. Kodwa u Rev. Sihlali
elandela ubutshaba kwezombuso obubonakalayo kuyo yonke incwadi yake
ufaka amazwi emlonyeni warn endingazanga ndawateta. Loncwadi ayimfanele
kanye.

  VICTOR SAMPSON.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1875.

U-MR. K. MSIMANG wase Edendale E-Natal uti bake banentlanganiso
yamakolwa emnandi kunene, ebiteta ngento ezinje ngokulobola nezitembu.
Uti kanjalo kuya kubako intlanganiso enkulu yama Wesile ngomhla 8 ka
August. Into enkulu eza kuhlanganela yona kukubonwana kutetwe ngokuhamba
kwelizwi lika Tixo pakati kwa- bantsundu. Indleko zayo ziya kuba pezu
kwa- malungu ayo, kuba woti lowo nalowo azibonele ngokwake ngecala lento
edliwayo.

U-MATANZIMA unyana ka Sandile, nama pakati ake wafika E-Uitenbage
kwinyanga engu June, ehamba ebona abantu. Bahlala kwa Mr. William
Citashe intsuku ezi 9, bangena nase tyalike ninge Cawa. Kanjako benzelwa
i-meeting zambini kwabako nezidlo. Lenkosi masiyitandazele, kuba iliqaba
nje noko ubunqola abunzulu kuyo. Inomnqweno wokunga ingaba nendlu
exwebileyo, empahla zingahlanganiyo nembola. Ibonakala ngati iyalitanda
ilizwi lika Tixo.

ABANTSUNDU E QUEENSTOWN—Izipatamandla zomzi wase Queenstown zimise
umteto oti makati wonke oyindoda ontsundu oya kufuna umsebenzi kona
anyanzelwe ukuba ake aye kuzibika kwisandla sakona, lize igama lake
libalwe encwadini, anikwe itikiti ayakuba kuseloko eyinxibe apo
inokubonwa. Ongayanga kuzibika kweso sandla uya kugxotwa. Kuya kufuneka
ukuba obalwayo arole 2s. 6d. ngalotikiti yake. Kumiswe kanjalo ukuba
ontsundu osebenza kona abatalwe 3d. ngesiqingata seyure; 6d. ngeyure,
nokuba zimbini; xa zintatu ibe 1s. ngemini yonke afumane 2s. ati osukuba
engayiumi lemali sele- gxotwa. Oke wabonwa engasebenzi wati akubizelwa
umsebenzi ngomlilope, wala, uya kugxotwa.

Abanye bake bateta nangokuti ngelisiti nenkwe- nkwana yakwala ukusebenza
igxotwe. Lemiteto imiswe ngalomadoda kusafuneka ukuba ifumane imvumelo
ye Government pambi kokuba ime. Sisa- kangele ke ukuba iya kutini na
yona.

LOVEDALE INSTITUTION—Isikolo salapa sivu- Iwe ngomhla 30 ku July. Kute
ngenxa yokusuke kubeko imvula ababi baninzi abafikileyo. Kwase kugqitywa
kwelokuba abafikayo baviwe ngomhla 30, kwamkelwa kubo 24.

IYEZA LEHLABA elenziwe ngemifuno. Linceda kwa oko kubantu aba-

kwalo—Kumntu omkulu lilinganiselwa ngegxebeka encinane, lixutywe
nentwana yamanzi. Kolusana kugalelwa amatontsi ambalwa, kulinga-
niselwe ubudala.

IYEZA LOKOHLOKOHLO elenziwe ngencidi yemifuno. Linoncedo olu-
kulu nakongenwe yinqele, notshe ilizwi. Ukuselwa kwalo—Lilinganiswa
ngegxebekana encinane, lixutywe nentwana yamanzi, liselwe katatu noku-
ba kukane, ngemini; kubantwana, nakwintsana kulinganiselwa ubudala.

IYEZA LOKUXAXAZA elenziwe ngemifuno. Linoncedo olukulu kwi
cesine, nokuqunjelwa, nokungatandi kudla, nezinye izifo zesisu. Ukuselwa
kwalo—Kuzaliswa igxebeka elula, lize lixutywe nentwana yamanzi liselwe
xa kuza kutyiwa. Abantwana nentsana balinganiselwa ubudala babo.

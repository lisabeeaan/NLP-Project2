sayilanda kakulu ngoko imbangi yelilinga lo Manyano, watsho wati,

(shares), senze amashishini ayakufuma- nisa umsebenzi nababantu bakowetu
bafundileyo, bawafunde kuba abawazi, kupela umntu wanela ukufunda
lamaca- pazana amnyamana abhalwa kudala ngumntu angamaziyo, kube kute “
ti ” nge business, (kwahlekwa). Ute umzi ontsundu ungokangelelwe pantsi
kakulu lumelwane olumhlope, yabe nembeko ungenakuyifumana ngalo lonke
ixesha usekulemeko yobu “ boy,” ute ingafu- nyanwa ngokuti kume
amashishini anje ke, laye nexesha lokuba umzi uwaqale lamashishini

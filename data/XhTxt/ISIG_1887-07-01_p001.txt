LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, JULA 1, 1887.

Isigidimi Sama-Xosa

NGOLWESIHLANU, JULY 1, 1887.

UBOMI NO LAULO LUKA VITOLIYA
UKUMKANIKAZI.

INTSHAYELELO.

BOKUMBULA abalesi be Sigidimi ukuba kwi pepa lenyanga edluleyo senze
ingxelo yobuntwana buka VITOLIYA, sada saya kupela ekumiselweni kwake
ekubeni ngu Kumkanikazi wase England. Ke namhla siza kuzeka amabala apa
napaya sitabatela apo slpele kona kwesigqitileyo. Eyona ndawo
ib’ingamandla kweso ib’iyinyameko kanina ukumfu- ndisa ilizwi lika Tixo,
nesimilo esifanele umntwana wom- londekaya, nowabazali abangama Krestu.
Ke namhla singena kwindawo-ndawo esizipauleyo ekubeni sel’engu
Kumkanikazi.

Ukuqala masiti waqolwa ngomhla wa 28 ka June 1838 waza kuwo onke
amatiletile alomhla, waquba kakuhle engoyiki nto. Kute ke emva
kokumiselwa ko Kumkanikazi, zati intliziyo zabantu zaman’ukubadula
malunga nendawo yokwenda kwake, waye yena ngokwake engekazidubi ngento
ezinjalo. Umzi waba nexaladube elikulu ngalondawo, kuba ukuba njalo
bekufuneka indoda eyakuba luncedo Iwake emicimbini yase lulaulweni. Kute
kwakuvela bantu bambi kwenkundla ekutiwa yi Souse of Commons kwatiwa
makahlukane namanenekazi atile abevana nawo, pambi kokungena kwake
ekulauleni. Lonto wayimangala mhlope, laza neqela eliyi Opposition
alavuma ukungena ezihlalweni engeka hlukani neloqela abevana nalo. Kwake
kwe’makata ngalo lonke ixesha abengekayiqondi londawo (u Kumkanikazi)
yokuba umele yonk’ into engumntu nengobanina.

Malunga nendawo yokuzinyulela isoka, kwaku sekuko oyena abe yibeke kuye
intliziyo yake, umntu abesazi ukuba wovunywa sisizwe sakowabo sikolwe
nguye. Kute kokwesibini ukutyelela kuka PRINCE ERNEST no PRINCE ALBERT,
(kuba babeke beza ngapambili) kwaye sekuvakele ukuba u PRINCE ALBERT
uyateta ne Nkosazana. Kute ke noko sebe minyaka mibini beyisompa le
ndaba, akaze ade ayi- pume gqi u PRINCE ALBERT indawo yokuba ufuna ukuba
abe ngumkake ngenxa yokuba amawonga abo eb’engali- ngani, londawo
wayilinda ku Mntan’omhle.

Kute ke nge 14 ka October Inkosazana yawutyila umxelo wayo ku LORD
MELBOURNE, awati naye akuyiva lonto, watakazela kunene, wakolwa kakulu
nangulomntu wab’eteta ngaye, wongeza ngeliti, wonke umzi uyakukoliseka
yilonto.

Umtshato wenzeka ngomnyaka olandelayo. U PRINCE ALBERT wapinda ukuya e
England nge 6 ku February 1840, wahlangatyezwa ngobubele obukulu e
Dover. Watshata ngosuku Iwe 10 ku February kwityalike ye St. James’s
Palace, kusukwa kwi Buckingham Palace, kwagcotywa kunene ngayo lomini e
England.

Kute besenyanga zimbalwa betshatile, bati besa wakile benyuka kwi
Constitution Hill, wadutyulwa izitonga ezibini eposwa u Kumkanikazi
edutyulwa yindodana apa egama kutiwa ngu OXFORD, yayimbulala kanye
ngelayo, yayingeyiyo ingozi. Ngekute ukuba u Mntwan’omhle lo'
wayelipaku-paku, engemntu untliziyo iqinileyo inesibindi, ngeyiba lonto
yamenzakalisa emxelweni. Koko akubanga njalo. U PRINCE ALBERT yena
okwake, wade wanexesha elikulu enexala nesitukutezi ngaye ngenxa yalonto
yalomini.

Kute kwakuba ntsukwana baya kuhlala kwindawo- ekutiwa yi Windsor, eyona
ndawo ebekolwa yiyo u VITOLIYA kunase London.

Kute ngomnye umhla besand’ukubuyela kwase London,, wasinda macebetshu u
PRINCE ALBERT, wapants’ukutshona emanzini.

UKUZALWA KWEZIBULO.

Izibulo le Nkosazana lazalwa ngosuku Iwa 21 Iuka November, intombi u
Princess VICTORIA, lo uzekwe litshawe lase Germany.

Ngo November olandelayo (1842) kuzelwe unyana omkulu u ALBERT, Prince of
Wales. Wazalelwa e ingham Palace. Saqala sapela isinqala esasiko
kubazali esokuba izibulo lingabanga nkwenkwe.

Bate bakukula aba bantwana, saqala sangena isitukutezi kubazali,
ngohlobo abaya kubafundisa ngalo aba bantwana babo.

U Kumkanikazi upange ku LORD MELBOURNE kwa oko, ukuba eke eve elake
ngale ndawo. Kude kwabonakala kunyulwa u LADY LYTTELTON oya kuqeqesha
aba bantwana, kwaye noko sekunjalo u Kumkanikazi eyona nkolo yake
isekubeni mayibe ngunina womntwana ngokwake omqeqe- shayo umntwana wake
imihla ngemihla ezintweni zase nkonzweni zokukonza u Tixo.

Nantsi imigca awayibalela abafundisikazi bentombi yake enkulu uti.—“
Kucacile mhlope kum ukuba ufanele uku- fundiswa ukuhlonela u Tixo, ne
Teko lake, aze ke abe noluvo lokuzinikela ku Bawo osema Zulwini, amtande
kanjalo ngo- loyiko olunkwantyayo, kunge ngolweramnco ; kube ngolo alu-
kutazayo, aye kusapo lwake oluse mhlabeni; ize ziti ingcama- ngo
ezingokufa nepakade elisezayo zingaveliswa ngohlobo olununusayo
olubangela izoyiko kuye."

Ute akukula u PRINCE OF WALES, wanikelwa ezandleni zika Mr. BIRCH ukuba
abe ngomnye wabamfundisayo.

U Kumkanikazi ube nomsebenzi omkulu kaloku woku kangela amapepa-pepa
avela kuzo zonke indawo ezipantsi kolaulo Iwake, kungasatetwa nto kona
ngencwadi ezivela kwinkundla ngenkundla kwazeso sitili sake.

Inkosi ebezi ngapambi kwake bezihlala zino Nobala balo micimbi.
Kubonakele nakuye ukuba kufuneka kwa into enjalo, kwabonakala ingumyeni
wake yedwa ongalungela ukungena kulo msebenzi mkulu kunene ofanele umntu
otembekileyo. Lonke ixesha eli ubusenziwa ngu LORD MELBOURNE ekute emva
komtshato, waqutywa ngu PRINCE ALBERT, wauquba ngohlobo
olungakalazisanga nabani olute gingci olungenantshiyela yanto.

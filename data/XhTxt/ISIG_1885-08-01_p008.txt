ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1885.

ICEBO NGE NCWADI ZESI XOSA.

  UKUBUBA KUKA N. SIKWEYIYA.

NKOSI YAM.—Kwipepa lako, lale nyanga ndibone kumhlati wa
“Mabal’Engwe” indawo yokuba iverses apa ozininzi zo Ngxoxo ngo
Mfundo ukuba ziza kwenziwa incwadi. Ndayivuyola kakulu lonto
kuba mna bendizitanda noko bendinamazwi amaninzi kuzo obe ndinga-
waqondi into ayitetiyo, enditi ngako oko ndilahlekwo bubumnandi
bento endiyifundayo.

Unyanisile umbali wa “Mabal’ Engwe ” ukuba ziyalambata incwadi
ngenteto yetu. Ingamkelwa luluntu ngovuyo nolo ike yako. Kodwa ko
kwaba njengam ingamkeleka ngovuyo notakazolo olunga pezulu ukuba
ibiyakupuma inencazelo yawo onko Iamazwi angaqelekileyo entetweni,

enditembayo ukuba aqeleko esazeka ku Mbali wale “ Ngxoxo” yedwa;
mhlaumbi nabanye aba mbalwa. Kaundixelele wena mhleli u " bedidla-
ba” u " Ncakasana ” namanye amazwi kukutinina?

Baninzi abanga yinoneleyo le " Ngxoxo Ngemfundo ” ngenxa
yokuba bengona kuyilandela, iyinto aba kangela bayishiye.
Kubantu abayaziyo inteto yesi-Xosa nje ngombali wale “Ngxoxo’’
onokusebenzisa amazwi anje, ngokuzitemba, ngobe bala i Dictionary yesi
Xosa nokuba ilulana ekuqaleni ukuze abantu bafumane kuyo incazelo
ngezi terms. Anditandabuzi ingafunwa kakulu kunokuba abantu bema-
na befunisela bequbeka kulamazwi abonakalayo e Sigidimini nakwezinye

izishicilelo ezitsha nje ngo Guqulo olutsha lwezi Balo ezingcwele. Kuko
amazwi angenakwaziwa luluntu oluninzi ngapandle kwabahletyelweyo
ngaba bali bala mazwi. Xa kunjenje nje kubonakala ukuba ubani na-
bani uyakuyila mhlaumbi atete amazwi angaziwayo ngabanye sibe si
Xosa sake eso. Kuyafuneka ukuba sibe nayo into entetweni esinokuya
kuyo sonke ngoku qiniseka.

Maxa ndilapo kunganjanina Mhleli ukuba ipepa elimnandi kunene
like Mr. W. W. Gqoba nge mb'ali yaba Ntsundu elikutshwayo ngoku
ngesi Ngesi kwi Christian Express likutshwe nase Sigidimini zesive
imvo zabo banako ne Bali letu ba Ntsundu ekufuneka libe sesi Shicile-
lweni esinga gugiyo.

Kanti abamhlope (ama Ngesi &c.) kusitiwa ngabantu nje nabo ba-
puma kule Nkosi ingu “ Ntu ” sapuma kuyo nati tina bamnyama?

Ndingayuya undifakele lemida Mhleli kwelopepa lako. Undixolele
ngobude bale.

  Ndim

  Vuso MEMANI.

July 12th 1885.

Lovedale, July 23,1885.

  MHLELI WE Sigidimi,—Ndikubikela ukuba ngo July 5, ngexesha
  lesitoba nge Cawa ngokuhlwa kubube e Xesi u Ntoyapi Sikweyiya.
  Yemka indoda leyo itembele ku Msindisi wayo, yamoyisa u Satana
  kunye nemikozi yake. Andikolwa ukuba ndingane mvume, bendifuna
  ukuba ibaliswe e Sigidimini ingxelo yale ndoda nomsebenzi eb’iwusebe-
  nzele i Nkosi yayo, mhlayimbi nawe unganendawo ongazifakayo ngobom
  bayo, njongoko waziyo ukuba ibingu mshumayeli wendaba ezilungileyo.

WILLIAM N. SIKWEYIYA.

  [Ukububa kuka Ntoyapi Sikweyiya, umfo wasema Tunzini. Lomntu
  utiwo ngcumbu yindawo yokumane elitsho kunene ngasentla ibende,
  kanti ke lonto iyamgqiba. Ube ngomnye wamadikoni alo tyalike ka
  Rev. F. G. G. Kayser wase Knapp’s Hope. Izihlobo mazamkele
  lombiko.—Ed. Isigidimi]

  ISIGIDIMI SAMA-XOSA.

  Andifuni Mhleli ukunga ndingateta into eyakukataza bard pakati
  kohlanga oluntsundu, nokuba lolumhlope. Xa ndilapo nkosi, ndinqa-
  kula into enqakulwe ngumfo wase Dayimani malunga nokungavisisani
  kwake ne Sigidimi lonto uyifake kwipepa ekutiwa Zimvo Zabantsundu,
  lomhla wesitatu ku June lo sinaye. Iyadanisa mna kum into etetwa

  lelonene malunga nobunca be Sigidimi. Tina bantu bamnyama
  azikasifaneli into ezinjalo ukuba sizigcakamele. Eyona nto ndicinga
  yona yeyokuba, ngumnqweno wake wonke ontsundu ukunga anganda
  amapepa esi Xosa kuzo zonke indawo, mhlaumbi xa andileyo zobako
  indawo ezinandipisayo malunga necala lawo banzi. Ingqondo zetu
  mfo ka bawo zisencinane; kodwa ndiyatemba ukuba amapepa ays
  kuzandisa exesheni. Eyona nto ndicinga ukuba ikufanele, gxeka inteto
  epuma kwi Sigidimi, usiyeke sizihlalele sons. Ngati mfo ka bawo

  lomkondo siya kweyela kuwo sonke tina bafo bafunda amapepa.
  Sendite gxeka inteto yodwa, nditsho ngesizatu sokoyikela ukuba tina
  bantu bazidenge siya kumisa ibakana malunga nelopepa lingumakulu
  ezintetweni zalo. Sibe singabulandanga ubuko nengcambu zalo.
  Nditi bamkeli bamapepa ohlanga inteto ezinjengale mazingamkelwa,
  ziyakuvelisa isenzakalo esikulu kwizidenge ezisiti. Eyona ndawo
  iyiyo masinqulelo amapepa ohlanga ukuba ande. Ongalifuniyo ipepa
  elitile makaliguzule elo lingavisisani nomoya wake, okanye makaliti
  criticise. Ngati Mhleli lento iyakuba yingozi kubafundi bamapepa
  esi Xosa abazizidenge. Yona indawo engavisisani nabani nokuba
  kukulipina ipepa lohlanga makayitetise, liyekwe ipepa licume, lande.
  Siya kuya ngapina mzi wakowetu xa apelileyo amapepa? Ngati kuya
  kuba nzima ukuba indoda izakutala isiko elidala lokubeka umnqayi
  emagxeni, igqibe amakulu-kulu emigama. isiya kubika intwana engepi
  kwelinye ilizwe. Ndiyatemba, ndikolwa kanjalo ukuba ininzi impi
  eti, “ Anga angacuma amapepa ohlanga.” Ndinento eninzi Mhleli
  endingayitetayo malunga nalenteto, ndoyeka, kodwa ndingayenza
  indawo leyo xa lento isentetweni. Mzi wakowetu batalani Isigidimi

  ne Mvo Zabantsundu zizalwa siso nje ziya kufuza unina.

  Barkly East, 12 June, 1885. ZWELINJANI.

  ISAZISO SAKWA RULUMENTE.

  No. 572, 1885.

  19th June, 1885.

  IMALI YE ZINDLU, KA 1885.

WONKE ubani uyalatiswa ku Mteto, Act 20, we 1878, ekute ngawo bati bonke
abantu aba nezindlu abalala kuzo, bamelwa kukubatala lomali ibizwa ngulo
mteto. Ke, ngalo Mteto, lomali yamelwa kukurolwa kwango suku lokuqala
luka July, 1885, ize kuti ukuba ubani ugqitisile ngentsuku ezimashumi
asitoba kolosuku, amelwe kukudliwa isheleni ezimbini ngapezulu (2s.)
esel’ eyakuti ke yombini lomali irolwe ngalanga nye.

Kanjalo kucelwa wonk'ubani ukuba ajonge kwi siqendu sesitatu kwa salo
Mteto (Section 3). Esiti, maze avele mhlope wonke umntu oti yena
akamelwe kukurola lomali, kuba lute lufika olosuka lokuqala luka July,
1885, yaye indlu yake engekayaki, okunye engekalali kuyo kanjalo,
kwisituba sentsuku ezimashumi asitoba pambi kolo suku, kuwo lonyaka;
mhlaumbi ibingeko, okunye kube kungekalalwa kuyo. Abangatshoyo ke
boyingenisa londawo kwi Mantyi zabo kwintsuku ezimashumi matandatu
kusukela kusuku lokuqala luka July, 1885.

Bokangela nakwisiqendu seshumi linesibozo (Section 18) esiti, bonke
abangahlaulanga bayakuba netyala.

  J. GORDON SPRIGG,

  Umgcini-Ndyebo wale Koloni.

  Elipepa lishicilelwa E-Lovedale Institution ekuqalekeni kwe nyanga.

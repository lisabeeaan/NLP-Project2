ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1871.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

UMFUNI.

Edendale, E-Natal, March 10th, 1871.

Nditumela lencwadi kuwe Mhleli we Ndaba.
Ndiyakucela Mnumzetu, uze undisize, uze uti emva
kokuyishicilela kwako lencwadi, uyitumele kubafu-
ndisi bonke bezikolo zama Mfengu, ukuze abe lowo
ayihlakaze pambi kwabantu bake.

Ndivela E-Natal nditunywe ngu Nocasa ka
Noshiyo, wakwa Mafunze, ute mandize kumfunela
abakuluwe bake kwelilizwe, nanku ndipambi kwe-
nu—ndiyabuzake kuni nonke ngokuti—Aningevi
mntu kutiwa ngu Qwadis ka Zalana, no Silwana
ka Jeke, no Tuto ka Jeke, no Zunguzane, no Ma-
gazana ? Naboke abazalwana baka Nocasa ate
mandize kumfunela bona kwelilizwe. Unditume
ngokundazi ukuba ndilula ekuhambeni, nemali
endivuzwa ngayo incinane, nomkombe andiwo-
yiki; nokuba ndiliqawe elingoyiki nto.

Omnye umntu endimfunayo, yintombi ka Mpu-
mela, wakwa Dlamini, inkosikazi enkulu, umka
Marawule ka Nonyanda, inkosi ya Mafunze.
Yemka nomntwana oyinkwenkwe, oyena woku-
hlala esihlalweni sobukosi bukayise. Ndibuza
ngokuti, Aningemvina, nokuba useko akaseko
kusinina ?

Ukuba niyandibuza ngezase Natal indaba ndi-
ngati sisahleli kakuhle pantsi kwepiko le Nkosi-
kazi, andingepike nokuzola kwabantu enkonzweni
yabo. Nditsho ngalendawo yokuba abantu aba-
mnyama bekaliswa yilemiteto ikutshwa iminyaka
yonke komkulu, abati ngobudenge babo bakaliswe
nayimiteto yokuba lungiselela. Kodwa okutshwe
ngalonyaka udluleyo umteto awubonakalisi kaku-
hle ukuba uyabalungiselel’ abantu abamnyama.
Ilizwi elipume endlunkulu yama Ngesi, lite—
“ Lowo onentombi kuni nonke nina bamnyama,
makati ukulobolisa ngentombi yake abize inkomo
ezi 10, aze umyeni akupe iponti ezi £5, azise
Ekantolo, aye kubika ngazo indlu yake komkuln.”
Manditike kuwe wena uteta nelipepa, lomteto
unzima pezu kwabamnyama, bati uyabaqumbisa.
Bati kunganina ukuba Abelungu bati masahlule-
lane nabo ngekazi labantwana betu. Bati balelwa
ngubanina bona Abelungu ukuba bangalobolisi
ngentombi zabo, sibonanje ukuba bayalubulala olu
luloboliso Iwetu ngabantwana betu. Buteke
ubulumko bama Ngesi ngayo lento yentombi,
emakosini onke amnyama—lobolisani nina ngoku-
tanda kwenu ngentombi zenu. Elilizwi liqonde
kwamakulu amakosi—elokuti lobolisani ngokuta-
nda kwenu ngentombi zenu. Mandi pele. Uze
undisize mshicileli, unditumele wakuyigqiba len-
cwadi—Uyitumele kum.

Ndingu,

JOHN MUIR, ongu VIMBE.

Nakuba abafundisi benawo bencedisa ngama-
yeza, kodwa abanawo onke anje ngawe Gqira
elafunda lowo msebenzi. Ngako oko igqira
lifuneka kakulu apa pesheya kwe Nciba xa besi-
nokuncedwa yi Rulumente. Andibandezeli kanye
ukuti abantu abantsundu akungecelwe mciza
kubo, ngokuba kuko ezinye izifo amagqira
asemlungwini angaziqondiyo. Nje ngesifo se
hashe, naxa umntu adlisiweyo, nanje ngesifo
sedila,nezinye izifo ezinjalo.

Njengomnye umfundisi owafa yidila eqangqu-
lula ihashe elalinedila, bafuna ukumnceda abantu
wati ufuna elasemlungwini, kanti kuko kwaban-
tsundu abaziyo umciza wezi zifo, bengengawo
amagqira okuvumisa nawemihlahlo, bengabantu
nje kodwa abazi umciza lowo onganceda esosifo.

Kekaloku nangu umbuzo,—kwabo singacela
umciza, nokuba asingeceli kusinina ? Ndibuzela
abakoliweyo. Ndingavuya ukuva impendulo kuba
leseshi be Sigidimi otanda ukupendula ngalenda-
wo kwipepa lenyanga ezayo, ngakumbi nina
eninam, apa pesheya kwe Nciba, ngapandle kwe
Gqira lasemlungwini. Kanjako Isigidimi sikoleka
kangakanana okokuba bendingavuyayo nokuba
sipuma kabini ngenyanga, nokuba sixika icawa
ezi, nje ngamapepa ama Ngesi esiwamkela icawa
zonke.

Ngokuba silukanyo kwizizwe zakowetu, ebezi-
ngazi ukuhambiseka kwezinto emhlabeni, kwabo
ababengenako ukufunda amapepa ama Ngesi.
Nditemba ukuba maninzi amanene namanenekazi
antsundu acita ngapezu kweshumi lesheleni ngo-
mnyaka ecubeni into engeyanto, nakwizi munyu-
munyu ezinje nge pepperment, ekuko mhlaumbi
namadodana acita ngapezu kweponti ukutenga
utywala, nezinye izinto ezinjalo.

Kanye kupa leyo mali yako, utabate Isigidimi
esikuzela nezinto ezihambiseka nezenzeka emhla-
beni.

P. K. MASIZA.

St. Michaels Mission, March 11th, 1871.

Kwi Sigidimi Samaxosa.

INYE into endihlala ndiyicingela soloko ndafikanje apa pesheya kwe
Nciba, enga ingacelwa kwi Rulumente, njengokuba imantyi nabafundisi
bacelwa ukuwela kwezizwe. Inye into esayilibalayo, kukucela igqira
lasemlungwini kwi Rulumente, ngokuba yinto elusizi xa sifayo ukuba
senjenjanina, ngokuba kude ukusuka apa umntu esifa ukuya E-Qonce,
mhlaumbi kwa-Komani, kanjako asinayo nemali yokutabata igqira umgama
ongako. Ngenxa yokuba kungeko Gqira lasemlungwini, mawatike namakolwa
apa kwa Gcaleka ngokunyanzeleka, ngokuba umntu etanda ubomi kunokufa, xa
afayo aye kucela iyeza kumagqira avumisayo nakwawo- mhlahlo, napete
omabubula. Umabubula lihlala igqira eliva ngalo ukuba isifo sinjanina,
libeke libonga izitixo zakowalo. Kodwake ndivuya kakulu ukuba U-Capt.
Blyth ecasene namagqira anjalo.

  E-Bayi, March 15th, 1871.

E-TINARA ngolwe Sitatu ngomhla we 15 kulenyanga siva kwi pepa lesingesi
ukuba U-Justice Dwyer imantyi yakona, ihlaulise umfo ekutiwa ngu Andris
Doldas, ngenxa yokutengisa umqomboti ngapandle kwemvumelo. Imbize ishumi
leponti (£10). Sonkeke apa E-Bayi singavuya ukuba umqomboti ungapati
ubukosi kangaka, kanti awunayo nemvumelo kwa Rulumente, ngo­kuba
uyilahlekisa iremente kuyo indlela yosindiso. Ke singavuya kakulu nite
nina magwangqa amhlope nayikangela lonto yomqomboti. Nani balimi
bamazimba msani ukuwatengisa kangaka ku Mangesi ngokuba Amaxosa na
Mamfengu asezidolopini engawatengeli kuwadla koko ewa- tengela ukwenza
umqomboti ukuze awutengise azuze imali engatyebisi nabani. Maninzike
ama- kolwa awuselayo umqomboti into enxilisayo.

E-Bayi apa asikuko nokuba uswelekile umsebenzi ngenxa kakuba into eninzi
ya Mangesi iye E-Diamond. Sekumzuzwana singenayo nemvula.

Asikuko nokuba singavuya tina base Bayi ukuba site Isigidimi sasixelela
apo singazifumana kona ezincwadi kutiwa ngesingesi zi Dictionary, ezi-
guqula isi Ngesi nesi Xosa. Uzeke ubuzise eziko- lweni za Maxosa na
Mamfengu.

  Ndingu

KLAAS BOZA.

UKUTETWA KWAMATYALA E-QONCE.

U-Adonis wabekwa ityala lokuba wati ngolwe 4 ku October, weba igusha
ezima 28 zika Banks umfuyi. Wati yena akanatyala.

Inqina lokuqala elabizwayo laba ngu Mokabe isibonda salomzi lalihlala
kuwo isela elo. Site sona sazibona ezigusha zisebuhlanti, sazake sabuza
ukuba zivela pina kuba besingazazi. Lati lona isela elo, zezalo livela
kuzitabata E-Ncera. Satike sakubuza isibonda, ukuba yinina lento
lingazange like litete nokuteta ukuti Hnegusha ezihlala kwenye iudawo,
alabi nakunika mpendulo yaneleyo. Kwakuba njalo sasesiyibamba londoda
siyisa ku Mr. Chalmers, zaza ezogusha zatatyatwa nga mapolisa. Amanye
amanq'ina anqinela ukuba ezogusha zazibangwa ngu Mr. Banks. Wati yena
U-Mr. Banks, igusha zake ezalahlekayo zazi mayela ekulwini zaza ezima
28, wazifumana kwa Mr. Chal­mers. Umgwebi wati liyabonakala kaku­hle
ityala; wazake lomfo wanikwa isigwebo seminyaka emibini etolongweni
esebenza kanzima. Kwafumaneka kanjalo ukuba lomfo waka wase tolongweni
ngenxa yokuba uboya.

U-Keepie umqubi weposi wanikwa ityala lokumhlaba ngesitshetshe U-Bele
enxamele- ukumenzakalisa. U-Bele usisibonda sakwa Siwani. Ute yena
akanatyala.

Yati indoda leyo yayihlatyiwe—Umbanjwa lo ubenetyala lam lemali, ishumi
elinesixenxe lesheleni. Kuteke ngo Janu­ary lo udluleyo ndaya kuyibiza
kuba bendisiva kutiwa uza kufuduka. Ndite ndakuyibiza wandipendula
ngamazwi a- rwexayo, ndati mna ndiyakumsa emantyini; wazake waselerola
isitshetshe endihlaba pantsi komkono malunga ngaseza- ntsi kwentliziyo.
Inxeba elo lalingelikulu. Wati U-Piet, umntu omnyama wanqinela U-Bele.
Wati ebengamcapukisanga. Waxela U-Mabombo, ukuba isitshetshe sihlutwe
nguye akuba ebesele- hlatyiwe U-Bele, nokuba U-Bele ebenga- mcapukisanga
U-Keepie. Umbanjwa umbizile U-Albert Chuiner isicaka sika Mr. Smith wase
Mdizeni, wati yena, ngolwe 5 ku January, kwakuselwa utywala babe Lungu
ngu Bele, nombanjwa namanye ama Xosa lawo, buselelwa endlwini ka Keepie.
Wati yena akuya endlwini apo wafika lendoda yenzakeleyo selimkwele
ngapezulu U-Keespie. Wazake yenawawakupa ama Xosa endlwini apo, wati
makangezi kuselela kona. Emveni koko wambona umbanjwa selerwiqilizwa ngu
Bele emte gatya intambo emqaleni. Ama Xosa lawo ebemtiyile kakade lomfo

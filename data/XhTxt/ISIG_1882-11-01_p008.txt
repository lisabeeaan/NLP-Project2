ihlaulwe kwakona imali. Xa ubani afuna ukugcina imali yake kuyo, anga

ehlaulwa xa ate umniniyo wanika isaziso ; mhlaimbi ekutini abube,
ihlaulwa kwindla-mafa yake ngokwase mtetweni.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1875.

IMASHINI YOKUCEBA IGUSHA.—Umfo abati ngu Mr. Douglass Mactavish usand’
ukuyila uhlobo olutile Iwemashini yokuceba igusha. Lomashini isebenza
ngompunga. Ikauleza ngapezu kokomntu ukuceba, ize kanjalo idlulise
ngokucokisa. Umntu oyiyililiyo usahambele kule koloni.

U-MR. N. FALATI uyazisa ukuba uwufumene umkondo we Dictionary ka Davis
ayecela nge Sigidimi igama lomntu oyitnmele kuye. Uti ubone kufika
incwadi ivela E-Natal esiti umbali wayo, loncwadi ifunwayo yashiywa
nguye endlwini apo yena eyifumene E-Rini kuma Seminare akona.

UMNTANA OTSHE NGUMLILO— E-Kapa ute u- mfazi otile ozipilela ngomsebenzi
wezake izandla, wapuma ukuya kusebenza eshiya abantwana ababini
endlwini, inkwenkwe nentombazana. Ite intombazana yabona imatshisi
yamana idlala ngazo, kanti kuzakusuke kutshe ingubo zayo, nayo itshe
ngokokude ife.

UMSINDO—Kute E-Philipstown Ilawo ekutiwa ngu Kleinboy laba kusoloko
limelwe lifundululwa liman’ ukulandelwa licatshukiswa lelinye abati ngu
Adonisi esenziwa. butywala, engenanto kodwa yena U-Kleinboy. Ude omnye
wacapuka wamwisa pantsi U-Adonise wakwela pezu kwake iminiti ezintlanu,
kanti uzakuti esuka abe selefile. Usetolongweni ngoku U-Kleinboy.

INTLANGANISO yamdoda akangela umahlulo wase Colesberg avumelene ngokuba
atumele isicekwi government sokuba kumiswe umteto wokuba knngabiko
namnye ontsundu oya kuvunyelwa ukuba atengise uboya nezikumba, nozigusha
nozibokwe nokudla, ngapandle kokuba uke wacela imzume yokutengisa ngazo
ezonto, kwafunwa umlungu omaziyo lomntu ukuba aze kungqina ukuba
okwenene azibiwe.

IQINGA ELIHLE—Kwezinye indawo ze United States zase America kuko umteto
wokuba utywala bungafumane butengiswe kumntu wonke. Kuti ukuba umntu
wasemzini ubeye kona wabutenga afakwe etolongweni ngalo lonke ixesha
asanxilileyo, aze akuqabuka kubuzwe apo ebebutenge kona kuyiwe kubanjwa
lomntu ubutengiseleyo abe nguye owohlwaywayo. Kuba njalo naxa umntu
ebememele abanye esidlweni, kuti ukuba kuko isipitipiti lonke ityala
laso limele yena.

ABABETWE NGUMBANE—U-Mr. A Tselanyane wase Graaff Reinet uti, Kuko indoda
ebetwe ngumbane kunye nomfazi wayo nabantwana abane. Lusizi olukulu
kuti. Londoda ibihlala E-diamond isebenza kumlungu kona. Lento ke
ibehlele ngomhla 30 ka January, wababeta belele ebusuku. Kwabetwa
netitshare yafa. Bati abantu bomnye umzi babona kusitsha indlu baya
kukangela babona ititshare idle, abababona abanye, kanti abanye bapantsi
kwamalahle sebengamatambo, basebe ncwaba olotutu.

LEVEKI IZAYO imiselwe ukuba ibe yeyokubala zonke izinto ezikoyo kule
Koloni, abantu nemfuyo yabo yonke.

U-SIR BENJAMIN PINE, I-Governor yase Natal kuvakala ukuba uya kunduluka
ekupeleni kwale nyanga ukugoduka.

UKUBUYELA kuka Langalibalele E-Natal, baninzi abati ngako akuyi kuvelisa
ziqamo zilungileyo napakati kwabantsundu.

E-BURGHERSDORP.—Ilau elitile lifakwe eto- longweni karanelwa ukuba
umfazi walo limxabele entloko ngomhlakulo.

E-FORT JACKSON, pakati kwe Qonci we Monti, kuko Umxosa obanjwe wafakwa
etolongweni ku- tandatyuzelwa ukuba udlise umfazi wake.

ISICAKA esibe sisiwe ematyaleni E-Quenstown yinkosi yaso ngokusuka
siyituke siyibete, sinikwe inyanga etolongweni sisebenza nzima, size ngo
Mvulo, nolwe Sitatu, nolwe Sihlanu sipile ngamanzi ereise.

ABAFUNA UMSEBENZI—Siva ukuba kwela Ma- tebele kuko amadoda amawaka
matatu aza kufuna umsebenzi kule Koloni, avume ukuza kuhlala iminyaka
emibini nemitatu. Izizwe ezilapa ziza- kubona zishiywa zezipakati, ezona
kube kusitiwa zisemva.

INKOMO ENENQONDO.—Kute Pesheya E-Scot­land umfo otile ebeta umfazi wake
endle, kuko umhlabi wenkomo obukufupi, yambona imazi etile ebitandwa
kakulu ngulo mfazi, yaseliyi kumbula londoda imkusela umfazi imlwela,
yada indoda yazisindisa ngokubaleka.

PAKATI E-AFRICA—Abaleseshi betu baya kuvuya ukuba isikolo ebekufunwa
ukuba sibe pakati E-Africa sibe sisaziseleli sika Dr. Living­stone
sekuvunyelwene ukuba sime. Baya xakazela kunene ukulungisa. Siya temba
ukuba kwesizayo Isigidimi soba nokuyibalisa imbali yaso kangangoko
kusaqubeke ngako.

U-NOBENGUNI inkosi yamatebele, unyana ka Mzilikazi uti uyakuvuyela
ukutunyelwa kwaba- fundisi ezweni lake. Ubanike imvume ukuze
bashumayele, kodwa ke bangamangaliswa xa bangaguqukiyo kuba lento umntu
ontsundu asinto ivumayo ukupulapula xa kutiwa mayilahle amasiko amadala
akowayo itabate amatsha, ayifani nomhlope. Uti kanjalo abelungu
abasukuba behamba elizweni lake uya kuba kusela abancede xa anako, ukuba
abayapuli imiteto yake ngexesha abakona.

ISIKOLO SAPESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

ABA PESHEYA kwe Nciba, nabanye abakangele ukwa kiwakwesi sikolo, bovuya
ukuva ukuba U-Dr. Stewart ulindelwe kule nyanga sikuyo. Bovuya kanjako
ukuva ukuba uzuze E-Scotland imali ekwangangaleyo yabutwa pesheya kwe
Nciba le ise ise bakini E-Q.once. Uletyaziswe ixesha elide ngapezu koko
ebelindele ngenxa yomsebenzi omkulu awufumanayo ngokwakiwa kwesinye
isikolo esikulu entla E- Zambesi. Oku kulityaziswa kwake kube yinzuzo
kwesi sase Nciba isikolo, kuba endaweni yo- kuba kuqalwe ngomzi
omncinane owa kiwe ngeponti ezi £1500, kosuselwa ngonobom weponti ezinga
£3000.

Akanelanga ukufumana imali yodwa, usele fumene umkandi wentsimbi,
nomenzi we nqwelo osele lapa, abazakufundisa lomashishini. Kuko nabaki
abafunyenweyo bokusaka esi sikolo. Soqalwa ukwakiwa kwa kamsinya’akufika
U-Dr. Stewart.

UMZILIKAZI.

UMZILIKAZI waye ngunyana wenkosana yama Zulu abati ngu Matshobana. Imini
zobuncinane bake wazicitela E-Natal, nakwindawo ezimelene nayo. Kwati
ukuqala kokuza kuvelela kuka Mzilikazi woyiswa ngu Tshaka eyona nkosi
yabinkulu kuma Zulu. Wenziwa ke yena inkosana yomkosi otile, kwaza
ngenxa yokukalipa kwake waduma kakulu, lamtanda kunene eloqela
wayelipete. Ngamhla utile watunywa ukuba aye kulwa nesizwe esasigama
kwindawo akuyo U-Tshaka. Akusoyisa umnene, endaweni yokugoduka wasinga
ngasentla weza kungenela esisizwe sama Betshwana kutiwa ngama Bakhatla,
eloxa, sikule ndawo kutiwa ngoku yi Transvaal. Abemi bakona babaleka
balishiya naye ilizwe labo nenkomo zabo, kwacitakala nemisetyenzana yabo
yokukanda intsimbi nobedu, neyo kubaza imiti. Ngexesha ayemi apo ake
lamacala etyalike yase England neyase America alinga ukutumela
abafundisi kona, kodwa akanceda nto. Kanjalo kulo ndawo U-Mr. Moffat
wamtyelelela kwade kwakabini. Mayela kumnyaka 1836 wakupa U-Mzilikazi
amadoda amabini ukuba aye kubona isikolo sase Kuruman, amanye akwamabini
aye E-Kapa. Kwaqondakala ukuba utandene nabelungu nentlalo yabo, nokoke
akuqubayo ukutshabalalisa ezinye izizwe njengokungati alikange litetwe
kuye Ilizwi Loxolo.

U-Berend Berend inkosi yama Griqua yake yazilinga ngo Mzilikazi,
ikumbula ukuba ibisand’ ukoyisa Amantati. Wawangenela ama Tebele watimba
iqela elikulu lenkomo zawo. Wati xa agodukayo, afika ebusuku Amatebele
abuya azitabata ezonkomo awugxota lomkosi.

Wati kanti U-Tshaka akayilibele lento yokubaleka kuka Mzilikazi. No
Dingana owalandela U-Tshaka wahlala esayikumbula. Ezinkosi zimbini
zamane ukutumela imikosi iya kulwa naye, noko ayenza nto. Abona bantu
bade bamshenxisa kwelozwe ngama Bulu amfikela ngomnyaka 1836. Ukunduluka
kwake E-Transvaal wasingisa ngase Zambesi, efuna ukuze ayi- wele.
Akabanga nakuyifeza londawo ngokusuke avukelwe ngunyana wake, owayefuna
ukulinganisa uyise. Lonyana wahlaula ngobom bake nobabo bebengecala
lake. Enye into eyamtintelayo yi tsetse, isibane esitile selozwe
esibulala inkomo. Wasele sima xe pakati kwama Makshona nama Makala,
wazicita ezo zizwe njengama Betshwana. Akuba enexeshana kona waqonda
ukuba abafana bomkosi wake bafuna ukuzitanda intombi za Makala,
waselenika umteto wokuba mazibulawe.

Ate ama Bulu amlandela epetwe ngu Hendrike Potgieter. Efikile atimbe
iqela lenkomo, kanti azakuti xa agodukayo, afikelwe ngumkosi otile wa
Matebele opetwe ngu Sokindaba ubuye uzitabate inkomo uyibulale inxenye
yawo. Ababeko kulo mkosi bazuza imbeko enkulu pakati kwam akowabo.
Akabuyange amkataze u-Mzilikazi Amabulu emva koko.

Ngomnyaka 1855 U-Mr. Moffat wabuya waha.

Inxalenye yendaba yikangele kwezihlo- nyelweyo.

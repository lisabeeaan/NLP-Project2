4 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 2, 1882.

Heald Town, July 19, 1882.

U CETYWAYO.

Umbaleli ose London we Cape Argus wenjenje ukuteta ngo Cetywayo oko
ebeke watyelela e Parlamente ya Pesheya: Ubonakele engati ukolisiwe
bubuhle bendlu ye Parlamente, kodwa, nakuba u Mr. Dunn ubezama uku
monwabisa ngoku mxelela izinto ezitetwayo, akanikanga so kakulu kwada
kwangena u Mr. Gladstone wandula ke unkabi ukwayama pezu kwe sihlalo esi
pambi kwake; bakutana mandla nga mehlo no Mr. Gladstone wabulisa umnene
ngoku nqwala intloko, waza u Mr. Gladstone naye wancuma, wandula ukwa
latisa u Sir William Harcourt, elinye lamatshawe alaula kunye nawo u Mr.
Gladstone, ote wasele Iwasu waya ndaweni itile apo awoti ambone
kakuhle.”

INKULULEKO YAKE.

Ngo 17 August inkosi yama Zulu isiwe ku Mntan’ Omhle u Victoria kwi
botwe lase Osborne. Izinto ezehlayo xa waye ngapakati kwezo zakiwo
zaziwa zingcungcu zodwa, kodwa kutiwa wati epuma waye cwayite kakulu.
Ngomhla olandelayo usiwe ku Lord Kimberley, kwi botwe ekuhanjiswa kulo
imicimbi epatelele kuzo zonke i Koloni. Elo tshawe limtyele ukuba
woyekwa aye kwakwe lake pantsi kwendawo ezitile ekuya kulindelwa
ezigqalile. Ute akubuyela kwindlu afikele kuyo waxantsa kunye nahamba
nabo ngovuyo olukulu. Kwa ngalomhla kwabikwa kuzo zombini izindlu ze
Parlamente ukuba ukululwe u Cetywayo. Ziko kodwa indawo eziya kugqalwa
pambi kokuba agoduke. Kucingwa ngokwaba umhlaba wakwa Zulu ukuze
inkosana ezamiswa ekupeleni kwemfazwe yo 1879, ezinga nqwenele kuba
pantsi kwake zifumane indawo zazo zodwa. Kugqitywe ekubeni kubeko owoba
ngamehlo e Government pakati kwakwa Zulu. Indawo tina esibona ukuba
ingaba nengozi kukuti kunikwe umhlaba kwezi nkosi kucingelwa ukuba
azisayi kumvuma u Cetywayo. Enye into le yonke yebesi cingela kwamzuzu
ukuba yoba lusindiso lomzi wakwa Zulu. Ngamana asadaniswa. Ufikile
kweli. Amabaso abuya nawo ayanconywa, kutiwa yimitwalo yenqwelo ezine.

Kwanele. ukuba sisishicilele isi (Gidimi senyanga edlulileyo, nesabika
kuso ingcamango zomhlekazi u Sir Hercules Robinson, yokuke atyelele
kweli, yabika ngegunya i Cape Argus ukuba, lohambo yoqalwa ngo 10
October, ngokuti induluke i Governor e Kapa igaleleke ngo 12 e Bayi.
Emva kokuhlala ituba eli mayela evekini enye e Bayi, induluke ngo 18,
isinge e Rini. Uku guka apo yohamba ngolu hlobo:—Isuke e Rini ngo 23,
ifike e Cawa kwa ngalo mini. Ngemini elandelayo iye kwase Rini, ze
induluke ngelandelayo imini isinge e Qonce, apo yoba ntsuku mbini isinge
e Monti, induluke apo ngo 30, isinge e Queenstown, ze ngo November 1,
ihlele e Balfour, nge mini elandelayo ibe se Fort Beaufort. Nge
landelayo isinge e Nyara, ze ngo 4, isuke iye kuwa e Tinara kwa ngalo
mini. Isuke ngo 7 apo isinge e Graaff-Reinet, ize ngo 9 iqengqeleke
kwakona isinge e Bayi. Isuke e Bayi ngo 10 ifike e Kapa ngo 12 November.
Kule ndlela umhlekazi uhamba no Hon. T. C. .Scanlen no Mrs. Scanlen, no
Miss Robinson intombi yake.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

ULURE.—Kutiwa ekungenisweni kwake kwa Zulu u Cetywayo wopahlwa ngu mkosi
wama joni.

Kwi BAYI elinga sentla kwe Natal abati yi Delagoa Bay ingqakaqa seli
hambisa umsebenzi wayo omdaka.

INDABA EZIMBI.—Ngo 13 August u Sikukuni ubadwe kunye naba landeli bake
abebe lishumi eline sitatu, ngu Mamporo.

ONYULELWE ekubeni yinkulu yomzi wakwa Komani ngu Mr. G. Stillwell.
Ngapambili indawo leyo ibino Mr. D. S. Barrable.

Izi “BANXA.”—Nge Cawa, 22 August, kwalwa, e Tinara abantu ekutiwa ngama
Mfengu nama Xosa, esiti tina zizi b'anxa zedolopu.

NGEXA lonke u Judge Twenty man Jones ese Pesheya, indawo yake e
Kimberley iyaku patwa ngu Mr. Advocate Cole wase Cape Town.

U MR. INNES imantyi yase Bathurst unyuselwe komnye umsebenzi e Bayi,
indawo yake izaliswe ngu Mr. Bayne, obe yimantyi e Colesberg.

INGQAKAQA E BAYI! !—Nge ncwadi efike seliza kupuma ipepa letu siva ukuba
sekuko ohliweyo e Bayi. Oko kukuti ke selingene nakweli letu.

EKUPELENI kuka August u Rev. Dr. Stephenson omakabe uyaziwa ngoku ngaba
fundi besi Gidimi, usinge e Australia, ngo mkombe oyi Essex.

IMPILO ku Bishop Jones, wase Kapa opesheya ngoku ikwi simo esibi.
Amagqira elozwe acebise ukuba make asinge e Switzerland apumle kanye.

INDABA ezimbi zezo zivela e Diamond zokuba inxenye kwaba mhlope etenga i
Diamond ngapandle komteto, isuka imbulale umntu omnyama ukuze kungabiko
ngqina.

INDODA eseli nkulu, nebiyi fama izidubule, ngo 26 August, kufupi nendawo
yempambano ka Loliwe, eyi Redhouse, e Uitenhage. Isizekabani yimitwalo
yenyama.

KUTIWA yimiqodi amadodana ama Ngesi asuka e Natal (amanye asuke e Bayi
pakati ko September) esinga e Transvaal, kwindawo abati yi Kaap
ekusanduku kov’uku funyanwa igolide kuyo.

AMAPEPA ekuqaleni ko September abebika ukulindelwa e Bayi kwe nqanawa
eza ne 100 lama China aza kusebenza kweli lizwe. Aba bantu zezi nkumbi
besimana siteta ngazo ngati kodwa siya hlabela.

INGQAKAQA.—Kute xa sekusitiwa esi sifo sibi kunene sesibu damba e
Victoria "West kwabuya kwafika ezokuba sibuye sazi bonakalisa. Apo
siqwenga kona kulo mandla kuse minqubeni yaba mnyama.

U REV. T. D. PHILIP siqonda ukuba ulindelwe ukuba abuye eluhambeni Iwake
kwakwi ntsuku zoku qala zale nyanga u October. Ukufezeka komsebenzi
awabe wele ngenxa yawo sokulinda ngentliziyo ebawayo.

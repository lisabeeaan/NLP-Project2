UMANYANO NGE MFUNDO

INTLANGANISO E PEULENI NGO 8 JANUARY, 1884.

Njengoko kwabikwayo intlangano yama lungu elibandla yaba se Perksdale,
King William’s Town, yaqala ngolwe-Sibini 8 January 1884 yaugqiba
umsebenzi ngolwe-Sitatu ngomhla olandelayo. Kubehle kwabonakala
ngokufika kwamalungu ukuba le yenye yentlanganiso ezinkulu ezaka
zabanjwa nga- malungu. Siquka neqela elikulu lododana olwalungaba kuti-
wa ngaba semzini, amanani ababeko afikelele kumashumi omatandatu.
Isibonakalo somhlambi omtsha nomlisela wakowetu ufanelekile ngazo zonke
indlela sawakolisa amehlo ake wonke onqwenela ukuhambela pambili
kwabamnyama.

Indawana esingena kunceda ukuba singayigxeki ngokuba izingile kwezi
ntlanganiso, yileyo ipatelele ekungawagciniyo kakuhle amaxesha. Kuko
ilahleko enkulu, nenkatazo ngokuti amaxesha amiselweyo angagcinwa,
ngenx’enesisipako intla- nganiso iqalwe ngentsimbi yeshumi elinambini,
abehle afika noko amanye amapakati. Wanyulwa u Mr. Paul Xiniwe uku- ba
abambe indawo yokubala imicimbi esakuzaliswa ngu Mr. J. W. Gawler,
emvenikweni kokuba Umongameli ongapantsi u Mr. J. Tengo-Jabavu
esitabatile isihlalo.

Uqalile umsebenzi—waqala ngamatile-tile angawentlangani- so ukuba
iyintlanganiso. Owona msebenzi ungaba unencasa kuninzi ngoqale ngengxelo
zamagqugula awayemiselwe uku- cedulula imicimbi etile. Igqugula elingo
Messrs Bokwe no Jabavu, elalimiselwe ukubalelana no Dr. Dale, ingxelo
yalo ixelwe ngu

MR. TENGO-JABAVU ote ababalelananga no Dr. Dale kuba emveni- kweni
kokunyulwa kwabo i Palamente ifumene ituba lokuyixoxa yonke imicimbi
epatelele emfundweni. Zite ke zonke inyaniso abebeya ku- cela zona ku
Dr. Dale zasezibekwa pambi kwelo qumru. Ubonisile ugqira lowo ukuba
imfundo kwelilizwe ayinto ihlehleke njengoko ama- nene anjengo Mr.
Donald Ross namanye anga angabonisa ukuba injalo.

UMONGAMELI wenze inteto amiselwe ukuba ayenze ngawo onke amaxesha
intlanganiso idibene. Ngesicelo sentlanganiso sipaula intlo- ko zayo.
Zizo ezi:—Ute bonke bayazi ukuba imeko yemfundo pakati kwabo
yengaqondakaliyo. U Dr. Dale utumele incwadi kuzo zonke izipata-mandla
zezikolo zabamnyama ukuba zinqumame ukuvula izi- kolo ezitsha, zizame
ukuncipisa ezikoyo. Ukuncitshiswa kwazo koba ngokuti zonke izikolo
ezingena ntsapo yaneleyo zipeliswe. Yinto eyaziwayo ukuba ngamaxesha
atile enyakeni ezikolweni zetu kuti intsapo ingenile ezikolweni.
Kucacile ke ugokwale ncwadi ka Dr. Dale ukuba zoti izikolo zilunyenwe
zikwesi simo sobuhlwempu ngenxa yokulima, yokuhlakula, nokuvuna, zibe
sezivalwa ize kuti-ti ke

pakati kweta. Oku ke kukufa kwemfundo pakati kwetu. Yabe ke imfundo le
ingamandla azo zonke intlanga. Lento u Rulumeni aha- mbisa ngoluhlobo
kungokuba esiti upelelelwe yimali. Into enguma- ngaliso yeyokuba
epelelwe yimali nje akayekanga ukwandisa izikolo pakati kwamafama
amhlope; izikolo zama-Ngesi azivaliwe zihamba njengokwangapambili,
ezinukiweyo zezi zetu. Imb'ali yesi simo yinto- nina ? Yiminyaka emitatu
ngoku aqalayo ama Bulu ukuyigxeka nokuyi tshutshisa imfundo yaba ntsundu
ezintlanganisweni zawo, nase mapepeni endaba, ade anyula amalungu awo
awatumela e Palamente ukuba aye kungenisa lomoya kwelo Botwe. Ite nakuba
lento ingano- nelelwanga bani, yada yamkelwa luninzi emzuzwini. Amadoda
anje ngo Rev. George Brown, no Mr. Bowker, ngezizatu ezimhlope, benze
ingxolo enkulu kule Palamente ipelileyo bapumelela ekumminxeleni u Dr.
Dale kwesi simo akuso. , Eyona nto nati iya kusikulula, eya kubanga
ukuba imfanelo zetu eziloloblobo sizifumane kukunyula amadoda aya kumela
icala letu e Palamente apo. Le yindawo ekupu- mle kuyo yonke inkululeko
nokonwaba kwetu. Into emayenziwe kukuba amalungu alentlanganiso
azizamazamise ukuba ancedise kweli tuba. Indlela angancedisa ngayo
amalungu entlanganiso yeyokuba kuti mhla angeniswayo amagama zonke
ititshala zingenise amagama abantu abafanele ukuba sencwadini yabavoti.
Umongameli uzingene nzulu ezindawo kwanezinye wayigqiba inteto yake
eyatabata amashu- mi amane e miniti.

Emva kwale nteto kubeko ingxoxo pezu kwayo ebeyeyoku. bulela icebo
lomongameli, nokuvisisisa indlela yokuhambisa umsebenzi. Amalungu agqibe
ekutini ayakuzama ukuba eli cebo alizalise ngexesha elizayo lokungenisa
amagama. Eli lituba elihle esitembayo ukuba liya kwamkelwa kumzi wonke.

(Ingxelo eseleyo soyingenisa liwelizayo.)

IMFAZWE YASE SOUDAN.

Abafundi betu bangaba bayazi ukuba ngase Sude kwe Yiputa kuko ilizwe
elibanzi kunene ekunqabileyo ukulixela imida yalo; ilizwe ekuhleli
izizwe ngezizwe, abat i ukulibiza yi Soudan, umhlaba waba mnyama. Elozwe
lidla ngokubizwa njalo lihlanganisa i Nubia, ne Darfur, oma Kordofan
nezinye izizwana ezise Sude kwalapo ukolisa ukupela kona umda we Nubia.
Inkosana zakona ngabantu abatanda kakulu ukuzipata. Intlalo yabo
ngapambili ibiyileyo izilaulela abantu bayo kungeko bani wase zizweni
usingisa ni kuyo. Umsebenzi omku-

lu obulisbishini ibikutengisa ngamakoboka nangempondo zendlovu. Kwati ke
oko u Mehemet Ali aye yinkosi yase Yiputa wakupa imikosi yokuya kulwa
nezo zizwe, wazoyisa ke okunene zaba ngabantu be Yiputa. Ilizwe labo
lihamba liba nentlango ezibanzi, ekungeyiyo nto ukuhamba intsuku
ezimbini ningafumani manzi, nicanda kumatafa azele yintlabati eti
ehlotyeni itshise kunene. Abahambi kona sebekolisa ukukwela ezinkameleni
xa bahamba imigama emide, kuba inkame- la iyinto emela inxano, ize
ingenzakaliswa bushushu bentlabati. A- bantu bakona ke zindlandlu apa
ezingazanga zavuma ukutamba, ezibe kusoloko zaro'riswayo kukupatwa ngama
Yiputa; ezite kona kwakuci- twa umsebenzi wokutengisa amakoboka zabatiya
okunye abalauli bazo. Ezo zizwe zake zangati ziti gogololo ngemini
ekwakutunyelwe u General Gordon, (lo ubeke wapesheya kwe Nciba) ukuba
aye kuzipa- ta; besati ukumbiza ngu Gordon Pasha. Wati akumka zabuya
zavu- ka, zase zikohlwa ngumntu eziza kuhlangana zibe pantsi kwake xa
zilwa nenkosi zazo.

Kekaloku kute nge 1881 kwavela indoda abati ingu Mahomed Achmet, oti
yena usitunywa esinikwe ilizwi pezulu, lokuba size kuku- lula abantu
bakowabo elulaulweni Iwama Yiputa. Uti utunyelwe ukuba aze kuvusa
inkonzo yobusilamse, ayihambise kuwo wonke um- hlaba; aze amise umteto
omnye pezu kwabantu bonke, enze into zonke ziti ndinde. Lonteto yake
ayandulange iqondwe, ikatalelwe nazizilauli. Kute ekupeleni kwe 1881, i
Ruluneli eyayikutshwe nga- ma Yiputa, yatumela umkosi wokuya kumcita
lomtunywa; abati bona ukumbiza yi Mahdi. Isuke i Mahdi yawucita awabi
nto lomkosi we- Ruluneli. Emva koko babe baninzi ababona ukuba lomntu yi
Ml.hdi okunene, bangena kuyo. Nge 1882 i Ruluneli ikupe omnye umkosi
ongapezulu, yafika i Mahdi yawucita nawo.

Kekaloku kulonyaka upelileyo i Mahdi ikupe umkosi wokuya kura- ula umzi
abati yi Obeid. yaza i Obeid yazinikela. Inkosi yase Yiput,a ibone ukuba
kuyonakala rnayikupe umkosi oqinileyo uye kumcumza lomfo. Uhlanganise
okunene umkosi wapatiswa ikalipa elitile lase Mangesini abati ngu Hicks.
Kute kuba izikulu zase Yiputa zise nziwa i Pasha—naye wanikwa elogama
lokupakanyiswa. U-Hicks Pasha wanduluka e Yiputa ekuqaleni kwalo nyaka
udluleyo. Bahamba beca- nda kwezo ntlango zimatafa antlabati itshisayo ;
bepumela kwingxo- ndora zentaba ezingenayo nemiti; behamba pakati
kwezizwe ezinga- bafuniyo, abangaziyo nokuba na azizi kubaraula nazo ;
bengazi nokuba bona ababantu bati bababonisa indlela abangebi baya
balahlekisa, babe baye beyelisela sinina. Bahambile noko bada baya
kufika e Khartoum. Babuye badlula kona bakumbula e Obeid apo bukona
ubukulu bomkosi we Mahdi.

Bange bangati nyi apo kwavakala ukuba iyeza impi. Kute kusasa mayela
nexa lokomula, xa umkosi ka Hicks Pasha uza kudlula indula- na engase
Assalia, indulana enamatyolwana neng'ca ende yabonakala ivela eye Mahdi,
ipetwe zinkosana zine zikwele emahasheni zipakamise iglazi. Ite tu
yafunza pakati; kwaba kaloku kungeko bani uteta ni kubani, kuse kubonga
inkanunu, kukonkota umpu wodwa. Alike aka Hicks Pasha amabandla aka
isangqa, azifaka pakati kwabo inkamela ezitwele ukudla noziruluwa ;
atumela ngazo kwakupela, kwafa umntu kwanuka iruluwa. Afunze anga
acimile awe Mahdi, yati inkosana ekudutyulwe ihashe layo yalishiya
yafunza ngenyawo. Bate ukufa kwaba bafunzayo kwanga kuwa amaboni, wada
umntu wanyatela pezu komntu ; waba kodwa lo ufunzayo umkosi ungenamipu
kekuhle wona upete izikali ikakulu. Kude emzuzwini waqonda owe Mahdi
ukuba sapela, wanga ungati qingqi; avakala edanduluka ngemigcobo aka
Hicks Pasha ; banga bangatandabuza abanye—Eh ! lwadilika udonga, kwasala
apa engumntu yedwa; zasala kona zonke inkosana namadoda azintloko alo
mkosi walo mini; asala kona amancama bubomi angali- tyalwa nangumkosi ka
Hicks Pasha.

Ukusuka apo u Hicks Pasha ufunzele kwase Obeid. Kuvakala uku- ba ute
pambi kokufika kona waqubisana nomnye umkosi we Mahdi, walwa nawo
wawugxota. Emva koko kute kanti ababantu bambonisa indlela
bayamlahlekisa, baya bamfaka engxingweni abayaziyo bona, wati akufika
babona uvela Iwamkosi bebesiti baugxotile. Basakile kwa isangqa sabo
semihla kwaliwa lomini latshona. Kuse kusasa be- vukela kwakulo msebenzi
laya latshona elo ilanga. Ngolwe-Sitatu abuye atandana, akumbulana
ngento embi waza ke wacitwa umkosi ka Hicks Pasha, wabulawa ngesase
Sandlwana. Basati kambe kulo mkosi wonke kusinde amashumi amahlanu
kwakupela, nawo abanjiwe; nalo uze waxela usinde ngokusuke afumane
ingubo zabafundisi belozwe azenze omnye wabo. Azikavakali kakuhle ke
noko indaba zeli loku- gqibela idabi. Zakubuya ziqondakale kakuhle
sobuya sibacubele aba- fundi betu.

Kekaloku ngoku kusangati i Mahdi ifumene udumo oludluliseleyo, ngoku
iteta ngokufunza e Yiputa ; izizwana ebezisatandabuza, zingma kuyo.
Kuvakala ukuba nakwabase Yiputa abantu kuko abazamaza- mayo, abase
becopele ukuya kungena kwicala le Mahdi.

Into eyenziwa ngama Yiputa ngoku atumele omnye umkosi awupa- tisa u
Baker Pasha, umfo owaka walihamba elozwe ngapambili, oya kuti ngoko
mhlaumbi akulumkele ukweyeliselwa.

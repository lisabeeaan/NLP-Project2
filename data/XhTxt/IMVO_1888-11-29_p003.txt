Amanqaku.

KUMNANDI ukubona isizwe sama Ngesi pesheya sishukuma lolu nyikizo Iwe-
nziwa ngama Arabi empakatini we Afrika wentengiso yama Koboka. U
Commander Cameron, R.N., ulese ingxoxo pesheya “ ngobukoboka e South
Afrika.” Ute pakati kwengxoxe, ngomnyaka kubulawa izi gidi ezibini zama
Afrika atengiselwa ubukoboka; ute kohla into kwezi mbini—ukuba
lentengiso yamakoboka ayityunyuzwa ngesandla esomeleleyo, i Afrika
ayikuba nabantu. Lenteto iyinene ngokuvakala kwa mabali obu gebenga bama
Arabi azingela abantu abenza amakoboka, kunye nezitunywa zawo
izidlabantu za Mazim elozwe. Leyo ntlanganiso kwakuleswa kuyo le ngxoxo
igqibe kwelokuba mayenze umanyano olugama luyi Gordon-Living­ston
Society, lubute imali yokutumela umkosi nokuba yimikosi yokungqumza
ubukoboka empakatini we Afrika. Kuvakala ukuba izizwe ezikulu zase
Yurope zihlangene ngokulungiselela ukutyumza ubukoboka kwezo nxweme
buqutywa kuzo, okokuba lonto yenziwe ngokugadalala iya kuyilimaza kanye
leyo hambara incholileyo.

ABELUNGU ababhedesha isitixwana esi ngu Sir G. Sprigg badane ngokungum-
mangaliso ngenxa yokungapumeleli kuka Mr. Ayliff elunyulweni lwase
Bhofolo, kupumelele u Mr. Laing no Mr. Hutton. U Mr. Ayliff kudala tina
samncamayo kuba akanaluvo lulolwake, ubengenza nantonina yokukolisa u
Sprigg, kwabonakala ke ukuba ubumeli bake buyinto eputileyo. U Mr. Laing
no Mr. Hutton ngamadoda angasokutengisa ngalo uluvo lwawo, akuqala
ngokukangela amalungelo abantu abameleyo pambi kokuba akangele amacala
obu Palamente. Kute mhla kwakuxelwa abapumeleleyo yi mantyi yase Bhofolo
balubonakalisa ngokungafihlisiyo udano lwabo abanyuli abavotele u Mr.
Ayliff, abaya e ofisini, kwaya batatu qa kulondimbane yase Bhofolo.

UMHLOBO wetu u Mr. Douglass uza kutabata indawo yomfi u Mr. Luke obhubhe
ngolwesi-Tatu lweveki egqiti- leyo, ukumela i Rini e Palamente.
Ukungapumeleli kwake elugqatsweni kwakubange ukuba imizimba yetu iti
teze kakubi, kubangumfo ongenaq’oq’oyi ekuteteni akubona kusenziwa
ubumenemene e Palamente zintshaba zamalungelo abantsundu. Xa sikangela
amalungu asele nyulwe kubonakala ukuba le Pala­mente izayo iya kuba
neqela elinobom labe cala labahlobo.

IPEPA lase Komani i Free Press liswele amanzi abilayo engelibapalazela
ngawo abantu abantsundu ngenxa yoburara elinabo ngakubo, kuba besuke
bamyeka u Folosi bagqiba kwelokuvotela u Mr. Rogers. Pakati kwenteto
yalo liti “ U Mr. Jabavu ubelapa evela e Qonce, akuko matandabuzo
ubahlabelele umhlahlo, kodwa u Mr. Jabavu akanguye usiyazi, engesiso
nesiteti sasembusweni esingabakokela kakuhle, inqala yakulaula esifubeni
somntu ihamba iye kupela nokuba kupina ngamanye amaxa xa ingxamele into.
Ngenxa yokuba u Mr. Jabavu kwanabanye abafana naye benesihluku
ngakulaulo luka Sprigg, sebehleli bezilungiselele ukuchasa nabanina
kanjako wonke ubani ongaxhasa lowo Rulumente. Siyabacebisa abantsundu
ukuba bakangele pambi kokuba batsibe, okanye bobona sekusemva ukuba
ivoti yabo bayinike ‘inchuka eyambete ufele lwe gusha.’ ” — Masiluxelele
umelwane lwetu lwase Komani ukuba leyo mbeko yokuhlabela umhlahlo
ayilunge naye u Mr. Jabavu. Yilanto yokuba u Folosi soloko waba yintlaka
nexolo e Palamente kweliya qela elazenza imbumba yokuhambisa “ Impato
egadalala yabantsundu,” elingayiqubayo nanamhlanje leyo mpato xa
lomelele ngokwaneleyo—eyahlabela umhlahlo. Siti yilonto ehlabele
umhlahlo ka Folosi.

KUKO abantu esinosizi lwabo ngoku- ngasiqondi isimo esikuso kwinto zase
mbusweni. Abanye benza ngabom—abo asinazwi kubo ngapandle kwelokuba-

yalezela ezimfefeni zokungatandi kuva. Tina u Sir Gordon Sprigg
asimchase ngakuba igama ingu Sprigg, njengokuba sihlala sisitsho kade,
sichase izenzo zake. U Sprigg wati ukuqala ukuvakala pakati komzi
ontsundu wavakala ngobubi na- ugobugadavu bempato yake. Ukusasela
kweloxesha wamana ehambisa ngokuhambisa ukuncwinisa abantsundu, alwaze
lutembeke ke kuti ulaulo lwake. Ute akunikwa iwonga lokuba ngu “Sir” yi
Nkosazana sanetemba lokuti ngoku uzakuba nebuyambo, wada walikupa naye
ngomlomo wake wati mazilityalwe izenzo ezidala masikangelisane pambili.
Sati ngxatshoke yenza sibone. Sabhala intapane yemitandazo ngeziroro
nembandezelo esipantsi kwazo, akwabiko namnye awusabelayo. Wati
nomtandazo wama- Gqunukwebe wokuvalwa kwe nkanti ese-Xhesi sewude weviwa
yi Ruluneli, suka jibilili ulaulo luka Sprigg lwati lonkanti ipakati
komhlabi we Bhalakisi ayikona koka Kama. Ukutsho asichase Rulu­mente
sichase izenzo, nabanina ovumelana nezo zenzo zempato egadavu
siyakumchasa kude kube segazini.

U REV. CANON TAYLOR, umfundisi wase London, usand’ ukulishukumisa lonke
pesheya pakati kwabafundisi ngoku vakalisa olwake uluvo lokuba ubu
Mahomede (inkolo yama Slamsi) buqutywa ngamandla kunobu Kristu pakati
kwezizwe ezisebunyameni zase Afrika. Lomfundi- si uti isizatu soku,
kukumata kokuswela umlilo kwabafundisi bobu Kristu, ekusebenzeni
ukuwatimba ama Afrika bewatimbela Inkosi yabo ngokukauleza ngako konke
abanako. Abapostile bobu Mahomede abalali butongo buhlayo ukutimba i
Afrika. Kuti kwakubanjalo zakuvela ngoluhlobo ezinyaniso u Canon Taylor
enze icebo loburoti lokuti onke amahlelo ase Mangesini makabaroxise
abafundisi bawo e Afrika iyekelwe ama Slamsi. Elocebo limtobele
isapompolo u Rev. Canon, ote noko sekunjalo kwa kona abhengeza
ukungapumeleli kwaba­fundisi kwelinye lamapepa. Uti kucitwa izigidi
ezibini zeponti — imali ebomvu yama Ngesi yokugcina imizi yabafundisi.
Kuti kwakukangelwa abagqobokileyo baze bangaze babengapezu kwamawaka
amashumi matandatu, ekubeni ukwanda kwabahedeni buzizigidi ezilishumi
linanye zemipefumlo ngomnyaka. Xa kuqutywa ngoluhlobo abasokuze bagqo-
boke abahedeni bude ubushushu be langa bupele nomhlaba ungabinto. Lo
mfundisi uti ke ngoko ezo zigidi zibini zeponti ngomnyaka ziya moshwa.
Uti ngapandle kwabafundisi abambalwa, lento yobufundisi yindawo nje
yokupumla emisebenzini yapandle asuke umntu angene ebufundisini ofuna
ukupumla. Ukugqita apo lomfundisi uti :— “ Ukuba umpostile u Paulos
wayete pambi kokuba anduluke ukuya kwenye ye hambo zake zoku shumayela
wati ku mpostile u Jacob nase gquguleni elalise Jerusalem makamiselwe
amakulu amatatu eponti ngomnyaka irolwe ngekwata, waza wazenzela
ixobongwana elinomtunzi, nelapu lokungenisa impepo endlwi- ni,
nekalityana yehashe, nomfazi, ukuba waye kangele ezonto ngengazange wali
guqula ibali lelizwe.”

LA madlala alatwa ebu Krestwini ngasentla apa aya kuba nceda abatanda
impumelelo yobu Krestu; ze kuti ukuba kubonakele kuko izinto eziyinene
kuwo zishenxiswe. Umfundisi u Taylor naku­ba ebalula la madlala
akabucasile ubu Krestu, siyatemba ke ukuba inteto yake iya kuba vuselela
abafundisi benze imizamo epindiweyo yokwandisa impembelelo yomsebenzi
ose zintliziyweni zabo.

ELIKULU LAMA BHULU.

U Nkanunu Tshambuluka uti ukulucaza kwake unyulo lwake Qonce luloyiso
lwesininzi sabelungu esoyise amadoda ambalwa apete ivoti yaba ntsundu.
Kuhle kwalonto nakwezinye iziqingata nakuba kute kwezinye lonto yapendu-
leka njengase Alvani nase Bhofolo. U Mr. Ayliff akanyulwanga e Bhofolo
kuba kusitiwa wavotela u Tungumlomo. Ukuba angenziwa u Tungumlomo bekuya
kubako Iqela lase Mideni, ingelilo kodwa eli lixelwa ngu Sir Gordon
Sprigg, beliya kuba lela madoda anjengo Messrs. Sauer, Innes, no Hutton.

ABENZI NTO BANGAYAZIYO.

Abantu abantsundu bayayazi into abafuna yona, baya kunyula amadoda aya
kutetelela imfanelozawo. Ezo mfanelo ke zicasene nezama koloni amhlope,
kuba wona umnqweno wawo ngowokuba aba­ntsundu benziwe iqela lokusebenza
ku­pela kweli lizwe, lonto ingenziwa nange miteto yocalulo, yenziwe
ngokuncedisa imfanelo yendalo abadalelwe kuyo. Abantsundu bafuna ukuba
bayekwe bazihlalele bodwa, nokuba basebenze imisetyenzana eya kubenza
ukuba bahlale intlalo ekapukapu, neyobuvila. Inxenye yabantsundu
abafundileyo bane temba lokuba abazalwana babo bofumana indawo pakati
kwaba mhlope beli lizwe, kodwa inkoliso yaba pambili kubo ngokukodwa
abanezitunzi zokwaziwa kakulu bacinga ukuba obona bulumko kukuba
ligcinwe isiko lohlanga njengo hlanga kwizinto ezinga casene nenqubelo
pambili kwanobu Krestu. Umntu ongendelisiyo ukuqonda akangeboni ngozi
kulonto, angacinga ukuba lamasiko obuya atshone ngokwanda kwe nqubelo
pambili nemfundo. Amava asifundisa isifundo esahlukileyo, ati: O Nigala,
Amatshayina, nama Arabi ati akumelana nabe Lungu apumelele, ke abe Lungu
bazicingela ezintlanga ukuba ziyi ngozi.

IMIHLABA YABANTSUNDU.

Enye into, eli siko lokumiwa ngobuzwe umhlaba liya batintela abelungu
ukuba ube ngowawo umhlaba, into ebingenza ukuba babe zinkosi nangezinye
indlela. Eli siko lidala lifun’ ukufana nele Socialists zase Europe nase
America lixaswa yimpi yezi ngxoxo zintsha zale mihla sikuyo. Woti ke
ukuba u Mr. Tengo-Jabavu uyicasile Imbumba yama Bhulu kwanalo Rulumente
ovumelana nayo, waza abantu bakowabo wabafunela uncedo kwinto zo Sauer
nezo Innes, nezo Fuller, woba ngelake icala uyalu- ngisa. Imbumba le
yeya ma Bhulu, lento ili Bhulu ke yinto eti xa ifumene umhlaba wangowayo
seyi fumene yonke into. Ngabona belungu balungileyo kuba banesipo
sokuwufuya umhlaba ube ngowabo, ngoko ke babe zinkosi. Ukuba Amabhulu
afuna ukusamela maka-

cite ukumiwa komhlaba ngobuzwe, kuba yilonto ewavaleleyo ukuba
bangawutabati umhlaba ube ngowawo ; ukuba lonto ayinakwenzivva kwangoku
ngezi- quluba, mayenziwe ngempato engenanto yimbi ejonge kuyo ngapandle
kwalonto. UTUNG’-UMLOMO ONGQONGQO.

Kubonakala ukuba ixesha liya sondela lokuguqulwa koluhlobo kuvotwa ngalo
ngoku, ekuya kuhlutwa bonke abantsundu abemi ngobuzwe bangabi navoti,
ngapandle kwaba nemfanelo yeziqu zabo eyaneleyo. Loti lakufika eloxa
baqale Abelungu ukuyilungisa intlalo yase South Afrika ngokubona kwabo;
siyatemba ukuba le Palamente intsha iya kwenza umteto ongqongqo, ofanele
Abelungu kanye ongapezu kowe Palamente egqitileyo eyenza u Tung’-
umlomo.

EZABABHALELI.

UMZI OBULEWEYO.

NKOSI MHLELI wendaba zohlanga,— Andimntu uhlal’ ependula zinto,
wondixolela. Umbuzo ngulo mawetu: eyona nto yayingu Tung’-umlomo
yayiyintonina? Lento ndibona epepeni levoti sekuko abakwa Sprigg
nakwabantsundu. Elilishwa liyakuvunyiselwa pina linqabilenje? Ukuba
lelokutengwa zininzi ke izinto zokutenga kulomzi wase Mangesi­ni: iko
nemaii nezinye intwana-ntwana ezimuncwana akona namadinga amabawa
angawutengisa umzi. Manditi ngazwinye. Bendingazi ukuba pakati kwesi
sipitipiti sika Tung’-umlomo kungabako abasatengisa ngohlanga kwa
Sprigg. Ide ne nkosi u Mr. Mhalla ati ukuzitetelela : yena wayengenele
ubukumsha, wati xa seyelikumsha wabona ukuba ufanele ukuvotelwa u
Tshambuluka, yinteto yake leyo kwipepa eligqitileyo. Tina siti
kutengiselwa nina ngomzi? Lwaka lwatengiswana uhlanga ngabaninilo? Lento
lilishwa liwambete umzi ontsundu! Unyanisile u Mr. Makiwane ukuti yena
akanakuzingxashela umpu wokumdubula, neyeza lokumbulala; latsho ikalipa
into ka Makiwane iroti lohlanga. Nditi ubuhlanga buyakuba ninina, sisaya
sitengwa ngentwana ezimuncwananje ? Ipina intlanganiso ye Mbumba
Yamanyama pezu kwelihlazo lingakanana lihlileyo ? Nditi mawetu, lamanene
ebesi- tetelela yonke leminyaka akuyibona lento awasayi kwapuka luvo na
ukusite- telela kwa ndawonye nawayayo ngoku ? Ndikumbula ipepa lase Kapa
elati, ingaba ngummangaliso ukuba kungako umntu ontsundu ongade avotele
u Tshambuluka, lati ne ngilosi zingamangaliswa. Tina ke siti no Satana
angamangaliswa ukuba uke wayiva lento ukuba kuko abantsu­ndu abavotele u
Tshambuluka. Manditi ngelifupi, funani igqira linyange umzi ontsundu
ungenelwe libulawo madoda lento ungavaniyo umzi!—Ndim owohlanga

THOS. J. MQANDA. Sauerville, Fort Peddie, Nov. 19,1888.

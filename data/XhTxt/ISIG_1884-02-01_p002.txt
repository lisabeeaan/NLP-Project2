  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, FEBRUARY 1, 1884.

Kuvakala ukuba u Mr Tamplin ucande e Klipspruit Nek e Aliwal waba
nentlanganiso yabanyuli apo. Ute akucukumisa abantsundu entetweni yake,
wati uyela e Palamente ukuze aba­ntu abantsundu banyanzelwe barole irafu
enzima ngapezulu kwale bayirolayo—incinane kanye irafu abayirolayo
ngokuko- dwa yakutelekiswa nerolwa ngabamhlope. Uti abantsundu in- dleko
zabo zincinane kengoko mabarafe nzima. Makati umntu ngokuba enombalo
kubeko irafu yombalo. Unga umhlambi ontsundu onganeno kwe Gqili
ungawaqonda amazwi elinene, into elizitetayo lakufika kwabakowalo—ukuba
liyela mhlope ukucinezela ontsundu. Okukuba elinene litande ukongeza
irafu kubantu abantsundu kusikumbuza amazwi awatetwa ngu Rehobome, um
Yuda, kwindlu ka Sirayeli. “ Ubawo wenza nzima iyokwe yenu, kodwa mna
ndiya kongeza kuyo iyokwe yenu; ubawo wanohlwaya ngeziniya, kodwa mna
ndiya kuno- hlwaya ngonomadudwane.” Wena ke bavoti base Aliwal! Imizamo
yenu ibifanele ukuba yeyokuba nimbandezele onje angake avelise nempumlo
le kulo nkundla ye Palamente.

Abafundisi bamabandla ngamabandla e Nkosi bebenentla- nganiso zabo
kwindawo ngendawo kuyo lenyanga ifileyo. Imbuto enkulu yabo ibe se
Qonce, apo kute ngaveki nye kwa- hlangana abase Free Church, abe United
Presbyterian Church nabase Wesile. Ingxoxo zezi ntlanganiso
asizezivakaliswa emapepeni endaba. Kolu Pawana sozanelisa ngokuvakalisa
oko sikuvileyo. I Free Church yamkelele kwiqela labafundisi bayo
indodana enexesha ifunda apa e Lovedale, u Mr. Edward Tsewu, akakabekwa
izandla kodwa, oko kungenziwa xa kuko iremente em’funa engumfundisi
wayo. Okwangoku usaye kubamba Etoleni kwindawo ka Rev. Richard Ross.—Ama
Wesile awuqube iveki zombini umsebenzi wawo. Inguqulo ekumeni
kwabafundisi azibikwanga ngokomyalelo we Confer­ence e Kapa, zovakaliswa
ngo April. Amadoda anyulelwe ukuya kwi Conference eyakuba se Rini
nonyaka ngo Revds. W. Tyson, (East London), James Thompson, B. A.,
(Queens Town), E. J. Barrett, (Kamastone), no Charles Pamla, (oya
kumelwa ngu Rev. J. Mahonga ukuba akafikanga): no Messrs. Morum, J. B.
Uellier, no Zech. Tantsi wase Macibini.—Ebise Rini isenjenje ukubagubula
abafundisi (nakuba okona kugu- qulwa kuya kwenziwa yi Conference ngo
April)—(Abamhlope) Annshaw, Rev. J. Wilson ; Fort Beaufort, Rev. J.
Longden ; Somerset East Rev. W. Sergeant; Grahamstown, kutunyelwe u Rev.
T. H. Wainman endaweni ka Mr. Dunstan owangena kwibandla le Baptist.
(Abamnyama) Engqushwa nase Mgwa- lana, Rev. James B. Sakuba; Epeuleni,
Rev. Peter Mpinda; Ebayi, Rev. Sami. Ntsiko ; kwa Matole, Rev. Sami.
Gudula; Enxukwebe, Messrs. Sami. Mvambo no Jon. Mkosi. Abanyu- lelwe
ukuba baye kwi Conference ngo April ngo Revds. W. Holford, (Healdtown),
E. Gedye, (Fort Peddie), J. W. Wood, (Salem), no Ben. S. Dlepu,
(Cradock), no Messrs J. Slater, B.A., Jos. Whiteside, J. T. Jabavu.
Ingxelo zawo onke ama- hlelo ziyakutaza, ngokukodwa zakutelekiswa
nelanga elikulu kangaka.

Isono setu sisifumene. Umzalwana owasaba ukuvota e Cradock usityela
ukuba inteto yokuba u Mr. Hutton ngumhlo- bo wayifunda e Sigidim/ini
ngapambi kokuba kuvotwe, kodwa waxakwa ligama, Iona akabanga nakulazi
kwa- sekufundeni apo, kwada kwasemva koko, kwangakumbi mhla kwavotwa.
Imbangi yokwasaba kwake yiyo leyo. Uti ke umhlobo wetu ukuba sasimbize
ngesi Xosa sati mhlaimbi “ Hatini,” saza elesi Ngesi u Hon. C. W. Hutton
salibeka kwakufupi nelesi Xosa ngewaye qondile mhla­imbi. Kuba ke
okunene singatanga Hatini into eninzi yevoti yalilahleka elo nene : yiyo
lonto lipandle nje namhla ! Sifunda isifundo ke kule nteto yeli tshawe,
esitembayo nababaleli betu bositabata. Sesokuba inteto ihlanjululwe :
ingati nje utyiika lonke selungeniswa esi Xoseni ngapandle kokuguqulwa.
Le­nto sisono esaneleyo sababalelani. Umbali we Mpawana noko yena
utabata ngobungcatu. Impawana ezi zingalahlekwa sisongo sazo xa seziya
kuba zizihlunu zodwa. Akuko nyama iwaswela kuti tu amatambo. Abanye bade
bati inyama emna- ndi yesondele etanjeni. Inteto esizicapula kwizi
Latini, kwizi French nakwizi Ngesi ngamatanjana kodwa, nalomatambo
aluncedo, abanga ukuba lowo uti ufundile amane ekumbula ukuba kuseko
izinto amelwe kuzifunda ze angancomi ke.

Ngomhla we 17 January safumana ukuba kubomvu-ezantsi ngase
Ngqushwa—ukukanya kuyasondela. Kwabako ngalo- mhla intlanganiso enkulu
eyolileyo yonotenga base Bira, nase Cwaru napezu kwe Xesi kwindlu
yesikolo yase Tyityaba. Lengqungqutela yayiyeyokuxoxa no Mr. J.
Tengo-Jabavu owaye kwelozwe ngemicimbi epatelele ezintweni zamasiko
esihleli pantsi kwawo kuzezi mini. Isihlalo satatyatwa ngu Mr. John N.
Mzamo, isinonopu sase Bira, owayongamela ngo- kufaneleke kwapela. U Mr.
Jabavu uxelwe konotenga ngu Rev. B. S. Dlepu wase Cradock owaye hambele
kulo macala wati yindodana ezinikele kakulu ekucedululeni izinto ezinge-
mpato, enomnqweno wokuncedisa abantu bakowayo ngendlela zonke. Nakuba
ngati mncinane yena akatandabuzi ukuba ingwevu zakowabo ziya
kwaneliseka. lyiqalile ke lendodana ukuyingena indawo eyayicelwe ukuba
iyibalise yapela ngokuti eyona nto iyakwenza kapukapu umsebenzi kukuba
amadoda

abuze konke anga angakubuza ngokumayela nolaulo Iwama Ngesi. Abateti ibe
ngo Mr. James Ntutu, Mr. James Ma- tshobangwana otakazele ukuba
intlanganiso iyeyabo bodwa ekubeni ukuba bekuko ibala elimhlope ngebebe
noloyiko ababanako ukuzityila zonke izimvo zabo, Mr. John Gongxeka no
Mr. John Ntshumayelo Mzamo. Ite yaku kov’ ukugqitywa incoko emnandi
nefundisayo yenziwa imibulelo yentlanganiso ku Mr. Jabavu ngo Messrs.
James Mageda, Nongubo, Gongxeka no Mzamo, babesiti namhla bafumene
ukukanya okukulu inga angedinwe nangomso oka Jabavu ngokumane eba-
hambela, no Rev. D. Ntlabati upose amazwi okutakazela. Amadoda awayeko
aye kufupi kumashumi amane—inani eli- kulu elo kumzi wefama.

Inteto eyenziwa ngu Rev. D. Ntlabati kwintlanganiso ya- ma wabo esenze
amanqaku ngayo kwakwezi Mpawana ngo­mhla we 17 kule nyanga ifileyo
ifanele ukwetyiswa. Lente- to elinene laliyibekisa kwifama zase Iwandle
e Ngqushwa, xa lalibulela ukunceda kuka Mr. Jabavu ngokulihambela elo
lizwe. Weleke wati uyabulela ukubona inani elikulu kangangelo lalipambi
kwake endlwini lilupapamele umemo olunjengolo. Kwasekudaleni amadoda
ebelinonelele kakulu isiko ahleli pantsi kwalo—yinto ebigqalwe kunene
ngamadoda imiteto yelizwe. Bekunganele ukulungiselela intsapo yawo
izinto zokudla nokwambata, kodwa omawokulu babo bebehlala beli- kangele
apo isiko lisinga kona ukuze bazi ukuba lempahla bayiqwebayo baya
kuyidla ngalupina Iona uhlobo. Kuba ka- loku ihashe elifileyo lidliwa
yinja enamandla. Inkomo zendo- da zibe zingadliwayo yinkosi eburalarume
nenombuso ombi. Uvuya ngezo zizatu ke namhla ukubona intlanganiso engako
ngemicimbi eyeyolo hlobo. Siyatemba ukuba baninzi abaya kuyivisisa
lenteto njengokuba tina siyivisisa, kuba ayinakuba ingavusi umoya
wopapamo ezintliziyweni esezibuncama. Inga intlanganiso ezinjengezi
zinga cuma kuba kukuzo apo ubulu- mko bunokufunyanwa. Intlangano
ngamandla. Silindele. ukubona amandla eko e Ngqushwa. Ezi zinto
zezokukanya makungoyikwa.

UMSEBENZI.

Umzalwana wetu otile usitumele incwadana ebalwe ngu Rev. W. C. Holden
wase Rini kuwolo mnyaka udluleyo, eteta ngomsebenzi wabantu abantsundu.
Abanokuzifundela isi Ngesi singayaleza ukuze bazifunele loncwadana
etengwa ngepeni ezintandatu, bake bayicinge inteto yake. Amazwi ake u
Mr. Holden uwasingisa kwabamhlope, kodwa indawo ateta ngayo kufuneka
abantsundu abatanda amakowabo beyicingile bababonisa ngayo abantu
bakowabo. Asikuko nokuba u Mr. Holden siyambulela ngamazwi ake, amazwi
okunceda tina ukuba siyavuma ukuncedeka.

Abanye abalesi betu bangaba mhlaumbi abazi ukuba abe Lungu bayakalaza
kakulu ngokusweleka kwabantu bokwenza izinto abazifunayo. Bati tina
bantu bantsundu asivumi ukusebenza noko zibaninzi. Baza- ma ngoko
ukuzisa ama Kuli—abantu base India nase China—ukuba baze kwenza
imisebenzi ekedamileyo. Kekaloku u Mr. Holden kule ncwadana yake ubonisa
ukuba, abantu abantsundu baninzi abasebenza- yo, abazizicaka ezindlwini
zabe Lungu, ezitoreni, ezinqweleni, nako loliwe, nezinye izinto : nokuba
inani labo liya lisanda ngkwanda. Kunjalo nje kule Koloni akuko msebenzi
umiyo, uya kuhlala uko ungade uputunyelwe abantu belinye ilizwe. Uquba
angene kwindawo yokuba ama Kuli aya kuti akufika abete baswele umsebenzi
abantsundu beli lizwe, kuba bona ngabantu abasebenzela imalana engeze
yampilisa omnye umntu. Baya kuvalela nabamhlope abapila ngemisebenzi
yezandla, bacite abona bantu bangabemi beli lizwe. Kwakuba njalo
abamhlope bayakukohlwa ukuba baza kubasa pina abantsundu balapa. Xa
abantsundu bangasena ndlela yakupila, baya kuba kusoloko beyi ngozi
kwabamhlope, kuze kuvele umtwalo ongapezu koncedo olunoku- fumaneka kuma
Kuli. Nala ma Kuli abake bawalinga baxela ukuba amanye bade bawagxota
ngenxa yokunqena, nokunxila, nokuncola kwawo. Avela e China abuye
aruqule ngokuti apo ake afika kona kube kusoloko enze intambo yanye
ukuza kwawo, asuke alizalise ilizwe. Abamanye amazwe ababewaxinele
namhla benza indlela zonke zoku- wagxota. Enye into kuwo yi opium, into
abadla ngokuyitshaya enxi- lisa ngokugqitileyo. Apo bafike kona ise
ingenwa nangabe Lungu. Umntu uqala ngokuti uza kuva ukuba injanina kanti
kukweyela kwake oko. Abanxamele ama Kuli bezisa longozi ye opium kweli
lizwe, mabalumke iselixesha.

U Mr. Holden ungena kweliti, kulilwa kangaka nje ngokuti abasebe- nzi
abantsundu baninzi ababanjezelwe zinkanti, abangasebenzayo ukuba
zivaliwe. Uti wena ufuna umsebenzi omhle oya kuhlala uko, “ vala
inkanti.” Wena ufuna ukuncipisa ukubiwa kwebokwe negusha nenkomo, “ vala
inkanti! ” Wena ufuna ukuncipisa izipitipiti, ukuba- da nokubulala “
vala inkanti! ” Wena unga i Mantyi zingakululeka emsebenzini wazo
oncblileyo “vala inkanti!” Wena unga itolongo zingaze “ vala inkanti! ”
Wena unga ingoma imitombo yosizi upaza- mise ukufa “ vala inkanti! ”

Anga lamazwi ako mfo ka Holden angavakala kubantu bakowenu. Wanga wonke
umntu angahlokomisa eso sikalo sako zide okunene zivaleke. Xa ukankanya
inkanti ude wasicana isisinga esikulu esibo- pelele abantu, impembenkulu
eginya okulungileyo kweli lizwe.

Indawo enkulu asingisela kuyo u Mr. Holden ngezi ndawo nangezi- nye
esingazikankanyanga yeyokuba makwenziwe amalinga okusebenza ngabantu
beli lizwe, kungaputunywa abantu abakufika bandise usizi bakufika. Siya
qokela ukumbulela nokuyaleza ukuba abafundi Singesi bazifundele amazwi
ake bacinge into engenziwa siti bantsundu. Mhla- umbi sobuya senze
amazwi okuhlomela kule nteto yake ngenye imini.

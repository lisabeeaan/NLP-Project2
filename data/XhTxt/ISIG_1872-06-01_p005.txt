Vol. II. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, JUNE 1, 1872. No. 21.

Isigidimi Samaxosa

INDIA.

  SINGATI ukuba sitabate umkombe, sabeka ngasentla kulile
  kwempuma-langa, singaya kufika E-India, emveni kokuba siseleshe
  imayile ezi 2,000. Kwi Sigidimi sokugqibela, sanibonisa ikomkulu lelo
  lizwe. I-India lilizwe elikulu, elihle, elisemazantsi E-Asia. Umda
  walo ngasentla zintaba ze Himalayah. Lilizwe elimhlaba ucuma kunene,
  elimatafa abanzi, elizinta- ba zinde, eliziblambo zihle. Ematafeni
  asikuko nokuba bukulu ubushushu, kanti ezintabeni kubanda kangangokuba
  ingqele ayize yanyibilika nase hlotyeni, esekusitiwa ngoko ukubizwa
  kwayo, yingqele engunapakade. Kwanini izizwe zamanye amazwe soloko
  zanxakamela ukuya ku- rweba kona, zime ukuba zinokufumana indawo,
  ngenxa yokucuma komhlaba wa- kona, nokuba zininzi kweziqamo zawo.
  Abantu abakona ngoku bazizigidi ezimakulu mabini (200,000,000); inteto
  ngenteto ezitetwayo kulo, zingapezu kwe 18. Ningaba nokubuqonda
  mblaumbi ubuninzi babantu bakona, xa sinixelela ukuba le koloni
  sikuyo, iqukwe ne Natal, ne Lamazulu, nazo zonke izizwe ezide zaya
  kumisa nge Zambesi, abantu abakuzo abangapezu kwezigidi ezitatu ; oko
  kuku- ti basisiqingata samashumi amatandatu sabase India. Inkoliso
  yalo elolizwe ngoku ipetwe nga Mangesi, ikomkulu yi Calcutta. Ziko
  nezinye izixeko ezikulu, eziti sisinye, abantu babe ngapezu kwa
  200,000.

Indawo ezakokelela ekubeni Amangesi abe se India zaye zizezi — Kuminyaka
eku 250 edluleyo, kwabako Iqela lamadoda ahlangana ukuba aye kurweba
E-India. Kwamiswa ixesba elitile lokuba arwebe odwa kwelozwe, Lauquba ke
umsebenzi walo eloqela, liti ngamanye amaxesha liqube ukurweba, ngamanye
liqube imfazwe, kwada ngomnyaka we 1850 lano- mkosi omadoda anga,
300,000, neqela

lemikombe yokurweba, laba kanjalo seli- likolisile ukuloyisa elozwe.

Kwati mayela nokupela komnyaka we 1857, ati amasoldati eloqela
alivukela, abulala into eninzi yabapati-mikosi base Mangesini. Ukubizwa
kwalomikosi yabantu belozwe, eyavukela Amangesi kwa kutiwa ngama Sepoy;
inani lalomasoldati awo, awaye kufundisiwe ukulwa, lali kuma 200,000.
Indawo aye nxamele yona, kwa kukuwagxota kube kanye Amangesi kwelo
lizwe. Yati kodwa imikosi ya Mangesi epetwe ngu Havelock, no Law­rence,
no Sir Colin Campbell, incediswa ngama Sikh, isizwe esasite sona sahlala
sikolekile, ahla awoyisa ama Sepoy ngenyanga ezimbalwa. Emva koko, lati
elolizwe laba lilizwe elilaulwa ngukumkanikazi wase England.
Ukumkanikazi lowo uti ukulaula kwake kona, atumele I-Go­vernor-General
(I-Governor eyongamele ezinye) ehlala E-Calcutta. Kummandla wase Madras
nase Bombay kuko ezinye I-Governor, eti leyo ilaule ummandla wa- yo,
noko ibe ingapantsi kwe se Calcutta. U-Sir Philip Wodehouse, lo ubeyi
Gover­nor yalapa pambi kwale ikoyo ngoku, uye kuba yi Governor yase
Bombay, indawo ke leyo epakamileyo ngapezu kokuba yi Governor yase South
Africa.

Abantu bakona bayawaqonda amalu- ngelo olaulo lwa Mangesi. Noko bebani-
nzi kangako njalo abantu, awamaninzi Amangesi abalaulayo. Kanjalo abantu
bakona bayahluma ebutyebini nase kwa zini izinto zemfundo. Kwilizwe labo
ngoku, kuko indlela, nezenqwelo zomli- lo, nemitantato yokuwela
emilanjeni, nengcingo zokutumela indaba. Zonke ke ezizinto
zingabahlumisa ngokukulu aba- ntu bakona ukuba bayavuma ukuzilahla
indlela zabo ezindala.

Enye yendawo ezinkulu kubantu bako- na kukuba bazindidi ngendidi,
ezingenako ukutshatiselana. Ezona zizintloko zine, ngaba bingeleli,
nabalimi, namasoldati, nezicaka. Kuko isahluko esikulu napa- kati
kwezizwe ngezizwe, ezinye zidume ngobuvila, ezinye ngokukalipa.

Ama India anentlobo ngentlobo zenko- nzo. Utixo wawo ngu Brahma. Xa
befuna ukumtomakalalisa, abanye baye bazipose emlanjeni bafele kona;
abanye balale pantsi komlenze wenqwelo enkulu etwele umfanekiso wotixo
wabo, ize ibanyatele bafe; abanye bazixome emtini ngomqolo, banyamezele
nokuba zintlungu ezingakananina; abanye onina babenza amadini abantwana
babo; kuti ukuba kubube indoda, umhlolokazi aye ku- ngcwatywa ehleli,
kunye nesidumba sendoda yake. Kodwa kwindawo ezipe- twe nga Mangesi
aziseko ezizinto, kuba uti oke wazenza abekwe ityala. Singabo- na ke
kodwa ukuba inkonzo yabo yobu- xoki ibibatshabalalisa, kanti noko ayiku-
bazuzisa nto. Baninzi ngoku ababamke- leyo ubukristu; abafundisi babantu
belo lizwe, abamiselweyo ebufundisini banga pezu kwa 81, abashumayeli
bangapezu kwe 1080. Kutiwa abantwana abaya esikolweni bangapezu kwa
69,000. Kwe linani intombi zingapezulu kwe 14,000. Abantu belozwe
banezikolo abazixasa, bazifundise ngokwabo, ezingapezu kwa 6,000.
Yindawo ke leyo efanele ukubonisa abemi base South Africa into abafanele
ukuyenza. Xa sikangela into abeyiyo ama India, nento alinga Amangesi
ukuba abe yiyo, ngokuti ngokukodwa aqube ilizwi, singabona ukuba,
ukubako kwa Mangesi E-India, yinto elitamsanqa kwa bakona.

Grahams Town, March 10, 1875.

Heke. W. Notanyela Somngesi, nditi kuwe idira’ra lako lifikile eliya
ubundipa Iona ngesisa sobuntu obumnyama. Ndandililindele ukuba
liyakufika ngenqwelo yenkabi, kanti hai, liyakufika ngeposi. Nditi ke
asikuko nokuba laubasa ixesha elide lomlilo walo, ufudumele ; kwanotutu
Iwalo Iwaba luncedo emfazini warn. Nditi ke ukuteta into engenyaniso
akulungile kuba wena akuzange undipe nkuni, kupela wena wafumana wenza
into enjengokuba nanamhla usiti kuminyelelwa ama- selwa ngenxa yam,
uteta into engekoyo.

Nditi ke kupina apo ndandiyekona ngapandle ndingenamali?

NTOZINI NTSHONA.

Morley Station, March 12, 1875.

Kuwe Mhleli we Sigidimi Samaxosa, ndiyacela ukuba undifakele amazwana
ambalwa kwelo pepa lako. Kekwako into embi esiyibonileyo apa. Amadodana.
amabini abehamba epikisana ngendlela, yati enye yile indlela, yati enye
hai yile, axela indlela ezimbini. Ade aqumbisana kulonto aya enkosini,
wati omnye ndiyakurola inkomo ndiyakumbulala, waselesuka kwa oko omnye
ebinza kulo ubesiti uza kumbulala, wampangela ukumbinza, watsho ebunzi.
Umkonto wasalela ebunzi utenqi, bade abantu noko baurola bagoduka naye,
akabinantsuku wafa.

Enye into embi, ehle ngo February 22, 1875. umfo otile wasa inkomo zake
emzini otile, kanti kulomzi kuko isikohlakali esihamba nomfazi wake,
samema abanye baya kumlalela endleleni bamfumana xa agodukayo, bambeta;
bati ukumgqibela bamfaka induku ngapantsi yazakupumela ngapezulu, wafa
kwangobobusuku, bamtwala kwangobobusuku, bafika endlwini yake bamlalisa
selefile kwada kwasa, wabonwa kusasa lusapo lwake, lwaxela ebantwini.
Esisikohlakali site sakubuzwa sasesizixela ngokuzimela, siyafunwa
asikafunyanwa nangoku. Ute lomfazi kanti bebe hlangene nelibedengu lake,
ngokuba umfazi ubeyicekisa indoda yake etanda lona. Kwadliwa imizi
emitatu ngesosizatu.

Enye intwana ekwambi umfazi otile wanukwa watshiswa ngamatye wahlala
intsuku ezimbalwa wabuba.

J. Q. MANYA.

Rabula Mission Station, March 23, 1875.

Nkosi yam, Mhleli we Sigidimi zungadinwa kukusa ndivele kwelipepa, kuba
kutiwa lelabomtina ukubizwa kwalo. Ukutsho ke kodwa ndikatazwa
zindatyana ezingepi, zentlanganiso yomnyaka ebihle apa ngo 22 ka March.
Kodwake zona intshumayelo ziqalwe kwange Cawa ngo 21. Kwatetwa kunene
ngendawo zemvuselelo yoku-

hambela pambili emsebenzini wo Sombawo U- Gquma barwaqele, U-Timla
betuke. Abafundisi ebesibacelile bebeba 5, aba, U-Rev. R. Birt, F.
Kayser, J. Lwana, Jas. Mjila, B. Mama, nokoke bangafikelelanga bonke
ngenxa yalemvula yolwesihlanu nezinye imbangi ezite zenzeka;
umasendisitike abebeko ngo Rev. J. Harper, B. Mama no W. Gqoba, kwakuko
abazalwana, Amalawo iremente ka Rev. F. Kayser, base K. K. Hoek, nabase
Qonce baka Rev. J. Harper, kwakusekuyi- nto emnandi kunene, kuse
kungamaqolo aba- ntu, yaye imiti isapuka yinyama, kuba impahla ehambayo
eroliweyo 30 iyonke yaye inqu yemale £7 3s 9d yezipungo yokuxasa abantu
bonke nabasemizini. Masendisitike ukuqupa yangena intlanganiso, yavulwa
ngeculo elavunywa kamnandi, labeta zaxwileka kanye intliziyo zetu,
kwalekelwa ngomtandazo ekwacelwa omnye wabadala. Kugqityiweke oko,
u-Mongameli u-Rev. J. Harper utete amazwana ambalwa esiti wobuye atete
akuyiva i-report yonyaka we 1874. Ndisukileke ndayicaza njengokuyazi
kwam. Emasendisiti ukuyenza kwam mfutshane, kuba asindim ndedwa
obalelayo kwelipepa, sendingena emalini epumileyo kuleremente ngendlela
ngendlela. Nditike imali yalonyaka evele ngetikite £7510s 6d ngeyomnyaka
enikwa ngabantu £24 10s 8d ehlanganiswa ngesabata £5 12s 6d eyokufunda
kosapo £4 1s; eyendlu entsha yetyalike £40. Hai! mandingagunqi
masendisiti iyonke £207 4s. 8d. Ecitiweyo kulomali ngezinto, ngezinto
£142 7s. 6d. eseleyo £64 17s. 2d. Kunjaloke ubume betu apa E-Rabula.
Okukwenjenjeke makowetu sinihlabela umkosi, kuba singabantwana
abasakasayo kuwo lomsebenzi we Nkosi yetu U-Yesu Kristu. Sicelake kuni
izandla, ngoku kodwake kulento ndizakuyikankanya. Kambe yena umntu owake
weza apa E-Rabula uyazi okokuba asikabinayo indlu efanelekileyo apo
singambedeshela kona U-Tixo sisafumbalele kwixotyongwana elingqukuva,
intoke leyo esoti mhla kushushu ibe simfiyoco, ukohlwe nokushumayela
kamnandi kuyo. Sada nge 1874 kwangentlanganiso enje saba pantsi
kwesinquma ngenxa yobuninzi babantu. Sitike sincedeni ziremente
zakowetu, sifuna ukwaka ixotyongwana nati, njengezinye izizwe. Kambeke
nati asiyekile ukubutyabutya ukulinga ukuhlanganisa imali yokwaka indlu
leyo kuba umntu wonke isiko lake yinto efuna ukunceda umntu olinga
ukuzinceda. Ke nantsi eyetu imi- gudwana tina ntwana zangapandle, ziti
ukuze zi- bone iso lemali zibe zise izimba, nombona, noku- ni zinga
sebenzi banini, nalike iqalo letu sodwa apa. Imali eroliweyo ndawonye
nezitembiso £421 10s. 2d. andikagqibike mnumzetu ukuwagquma ndiwazinge
lomaqonqo akowetu, ndobuy endikwazise ngelinye ilixa, xa inani
selilonke, yabona! koko sibancinana kuba lento ukuzalwa wedwa ngumlu
wanyama, unyanisile umxosa ukutsho. Ngendiba nditi kuwe inkomo
ziyatshisana, ziwile inkomo, igusha, ibokwe, imali, zabantwana babantu
befuna ityalike. Uyeva? Nditike makowetu ibambene ngazo E-Rabula,
telelani, ncedani makowetu satshabalala, sizintangatwana ngazinye,
makuvele nelawo lentaba mziwakowetu. Selu- ndixolela nkosi yam andizenzi
bendingafuni ku- ntyuntya, ndigqitwe yimivuyo yokunga ndingakupa
ezindaba ukuze ndaziwe ngamakowetu ingxakeko endipakati kwayo hleze
kusike imfesane konyana nentombi zabantu, ngokukodwa abazelweyo
ngokutsha ngomoya ka Tixo, mhlaumbi bosivelela kulegxakeko sibabikela
yona. Makowetu ukuba Inkosi iyavuma sifuna ukuqala izitena zayo
ngobubusika, ncedani. Ndipela ngombuliso kuni nonke.

INKOSI YASE PERSIA—Inkosi yase Persia esasike sakubika ukuhambela kwayo
E-England, nakwa manye amazwe ase Europe, ite yakufika ekaya yabala
incwadi ebalisa ngokuhamba kwayo. Iti ke ukubalisa nge London:—“
Kunqabile ukuxela ngamazwi ubuninzi babantu nokuhamba kakuhle kwezinto
zalomzi; ukuxela ubukulu bawo, indlela zenqwelo zomlilo apo ezonqwelo
kuseloko zingumtya, zisiya kumacalana onke nokuxela ukuquma-komsi
wamaziko okukanda izinto ngezinto. Amacala omabini endlela ungafika
ezele tu ngamadoda, nabafazi, nabantwana. Yinyakanyaka engati zimbovane.
Kwaba kusoloko ndite ndabulisa ngezandla nangentloko; singapeli
isihlwele esize kundibona. Ubuninzi babantu bakona kutiwa bangapezu
kwezigidi ezitatu. Ukutandeka kwabafazi bakona andikulibalanga.
Ukubekeka kwabo ukuzola kwesimilo sabo, nokupakama kwabo ngendlela zonke
ungakubona kwase mehlweni nasebusweni. Owase mzini ubehle abone ukuba
okwenene sisizwe esisikelelwe ngamandla nokuqonda nobulumko nokukanya,
sizipiwa ezonto yi Nkosi yetu zonke.” Iti ke lonkosi xa iseliteta
ngesizwe sonke sama- ngesi. “ Ukuzipata koluhlanga nazo zonke into zalo
asikuko nokuba kuyabukeka; bahleli ngo- kutandekayo. Bazinkosi kuzo
zonke ezinye intlanga ngobuninzi babantu, nangobutyebi, nokurweba
kwamanye amazwe, nokucokiseka kwemisebenzi yabo.”

Amampondo amashumi mane anukwe ngenxa yokufa kuka Ngayeni, abalekele kwimantyi yase Mzimkulu. Umditshwa, inkosi ya Mampondomisi eyabangazeka iminyaka mine ngoku edlulevo uvunyelwe ukubuyela kubantu bake e-Tsolo.

Umongameli we Transvaal u Paul Kruger ucele ku Sir C. Warren umpati mikosi yen. kosazana, ukuba babe nokuhlangana e-Boshof. U-Sir C. Warren ute bangahlangana e Ligwa (Barkly West).

Iyatontela imikosi e-Ligwa. Amadoda aselekwelo akumakulu asibozo.

E-Paris indoda etile apo isand’ ukunikwa ityala edliwe kunene ngalo, ngokusnka itu- mbuse intwazana etile ngokufuna ukuyifaka amacici. Imkeke yona indlebe yadumba kwada kwexaxe ngentamo ukudumba yaqau- kake. Lakuyikangela igqira lite ngumtunja wenzelwe pezulu kakulu awukona endaweni yawo.

E-Australia amabandla awayehle ukuya kona epuma e-Ingilane ukuya kudlala ibola oyisile kwakona.

E-Ngcobo kuko iqela elinobom lempahla eskiti, eya kutengiswa kona, ukuba ayihla- nga ize ikululwe, ngomhla we 4 ku February 1885.

ISAZISO SOMNINI-PEPA.

ABATABATI belipepa le “Mvo,” abahlaulela inyanga ezintatu ezipela ngo 31 January 1885 kupela, bayacelwa ukuba ba- yihlaule kwangoku imali yenyanga ezilandelayo. Intlaulo mayingabi ngapantsi kweyanyanga ntatu. Ku- kumninipepa ukungawatumeli ama- pepa abantu abangahlaulanga ngo- mmiselo wama Medi nama Perasi wokuba ipepa lihlaulwe kwasekusu- seleni, ati ubani elitunyelwa nje abeselehlaule. Amagosa acelwa ukuba abavuse abo abagoseleyo. Intlaulo itunyelwa nge Cheque na- ngemali yase Posini nange Stamps zehalufa egameni lika JOHN TENGO-JABAVU.

17 January 1885.

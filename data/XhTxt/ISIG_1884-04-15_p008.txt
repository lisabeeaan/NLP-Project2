Sigidimi sokugqibela kube kuko into enje : Ati amaledi antsundu akuti ga
nge piyane abe sele gqibile, kanti i Domestic work awayazi, ekubeni
bekusand’ ukuba ko into yokuba amaledi mawa tumele imifanekiso yawo kwi
nene elingu A. B. C. Esq., i fool kambe zingatumela, kodwa andikolwa
ukuba kuko umntu ongayenzayo lonto. Unantonina lento wena A. B. C.
ungatumeli owako umfanekiso uke ubonwe ngama ledi lawo ufuna imifanekiso
yawo? Ungati kanti ulinene nge collar leyo engu stand up, abe amasiko
obunene tu, asinto wake wayibona. Kanti nesigebenga siyakwazi ukumfaka u
stand up lowo, nomxokelelwane eti kanti i watch ayiko, ube ngabuza
ixesha ati imile, exoka, kanti ayiko ngumxokelelwane lo wodwa, ufike
intamo le seyisuke yaxaseka kanye ngu stand up, sele kohlwe nakuku jika.
Imfundo isalile tina bantu bantsundu, ati amanene la antsundu akuba
nemfundwana ete tyi asele cinga ukuba abetere kuna banye abantu. Ati
kona akuba ne certificate with Honours angabi savuma nokusebenza, ufike
emi emagumbini ezitarate ebonisa wonke umntu ogqitayo. Kanti esi sodumo
lwasemhlabeni asini kukunceda nento le, ukuba akunaso esodumo lwapezulu.
Enye into akuncedi nto ukuba abantu abantsundu batete nge Domestic work
kumaledi abo, ngokuba abawazi amasiko. Umntu ontsundu ukwenzela amasiko
or manners ngelixa asakufunayo. Makakufumane aya kupela onke
lomasikwana, i manners ezo uya kuzigqibela ngalamini natshata, uya
zishiya njalo etyalikeni apo, akubarele kubi. Zinjalo i bari ezintsundu.
Kanti endlwini apo uza kufika kume ingxowa yombona yodwa, azeke alindele
ukuba wenze izi konsi ngombona lowo, uti ke wena kuba akuzange uzibone
zisenziwa ngombona ukohlwe kukuzenza. Aqale ke ukuti ndazikohlisa,

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1874,

Eyesibini indoda, U-Xolilizwe, iyaziwa nayo ngabadala. Yiyo le
yasifunela yasifumanela indawo yokuzinza lakuba lixolile. Ndandingomnye
wabo babeuhambela, xa sikwa Sihota. Saqala Emantyini esabe sipantsi
kwayo siyixelela ukuxakeka kwetu nokuswela kwetu indawo yokuhlala.
Wasixelela yena ukuba sigcine umsebenzi we Government siye kuhlala
kwalapo sibe sikona. Sati lakuba kaloku lisikohlile sesuka nenye indoda
yakowetu saya ku Xolilizwe saya samfumana E-Beaufort. Akuziva inxakeko
zetu wasele tabata usiba erwerwa ebekisa ebotwe, wati iya kuba veki ne
ibuye. Zite zakupela yafika incwadi iselisiti wapeni umhlaba lomadoda,
sauzuza okunene.

Eyesitatu indoda andinasituba sokungena kuyo. Maseke ndipeze.

OBULELAYO.

Burghersdorp, 9th October 1874.

Ku Mhleli wendaba ze Sigidimi Samaxosa.

Nkosi yam faka lamazwana am epepeni lako. Apa tina sifumana uvuyo
olukulu, ngokusuka samkele amazwi awatunyelwa kuti nge Sigidimi,
senyanga ka September, okokuba maze sitandaze. Ngomhla wamashumi 20,
ngokukodwa sahla sawandulela kwangapambili komhla owakankanywayo.
Sauqala umsebenzi we Nkosi kwangolwe 13 ku September saxatisa kunene
sindawonye nezicaka ze Nkosi, sahambisa dakweza kufika lomhla
ukankanyiweyo, sika kwehla okungumangaliso, sawafumana amashumi 90,
abavumayo ukuzinikela Enkosini ababefudula bengateni nayo ngapambili;
kususela kulowo mhla dekuze kulo abaguqukaileyo basekulwini, kunjalonje
ke umsebenzi wona we Nkosi soloko ute roqo usingisa pambili. Lento
iblilenje sibulela Isigidimi, esite sahlaba umkosi okuya tina besisalele
ubutongo, singakumbulelanga. Ngamana Isigidimi singadinwa siti nangomso
ukusihlabela umkosi xa tina sisalele ubutongo.

Assegai Mbule.

Esigidimini akuko zindaba sitanda ukuzishi cilela njengezo zibalisa
ngokuhambiseka komsebenzi we Nkosi kwindawo ngendawo, ezase Africa
ngokukodwa. Singabulela kanye ukuba nabanye ababalelani baya kusazisa
ukuba kunjanina kwawabo amacala—Mhleli.)

Heald Town, November 11, 1874.

Nkosi yam, Mhleli, nceda undifakele elizwana linye. Ndibekisa kuwe N.
Falati, lento uyifumeneyo kum ngati yinto yokuba uke unge uyayicaza
indlela zayo. Lentlanganiso uyimemayo ngubanina umntu oya kumela indieko
zayo apo iya kuba kona? Kanjalo ngubanina umntu oya kuyongamela ukuze
ibe yiyo ngoku fezekileyo? Umema yonke intona, kuba imvaba zininzi
namahlelo; kuko nabamelwe yironoskap nabaka Government, zonkeke ezondawo
kawu zi- combulule uncede mzalwana, ungadeli ungadinwa kanjalo. Sifuna
ukuqonda singapenduli ebunyameni, senze into esiyiqondayo, ukuze sikwazi
ukupendula ngokungayi kukudubula. Xa nditshoyo U-Akanagama ubonakele
okwenene ukuba wenza izinto zobu akanagama kuba yena ilungelo
azibonileyo akazicazi; uyadubula kuba ude ati, seniza kuxelela tina
zidenge. Nangale titshere yekofu, mhlayimbi lonto alinganisa ngayo
ngumntu ekwakutiwe wonikwa ukutya okufanelekileyo. Kakadeke umntu xa
atya imaliyake kufuneka etye into efanelekileyo; akukatalekile nokuba
asinto yake yakowabo. Zindawo zokuba amadoda aziqonde ezo kuba lento
ngumsebenzi akufeketwa kufuneka ecingile aze apendule into ayiqondayo.
Ndoke ndenjenjalo.

A. B. DLEPU.

November 1874.

Ndiyakucela wena wase Rura ukuba ucacise le- ndleko uteta ngayo.
Ngendiba intole ndiya yivisisa ukuba ubungabalanga usontombi yedwa,
akwamshiya usosoka ngendleko yangase mtshatweni. Bendiya kumpendula
kamandi U-J. N. Daniel, ngelizwi lake elibuzayo nelikurwelayo.
Bendiyakuti kulomfo ukuba ubahlanganisile aba bantu bobabini, nditi
unyanisile nokuya ndingabaqukiyo bonke abantsundu, amahlwempu nezityebi,
yinto entle kusontombi nososoka xa ababantu ingabantu abapumeleleyo
ukufika babe nezivato ezingamanewunewu nezicikizekileyo; kusazeka ukuba
woti nokuba uyile emva kwomtshato wabo ufike kukuhle endlwini yabo.
Kweli ati, U-J. N. Daniel kwangena banina entolongweni nditi mna yinto
eninzi engenileyo, nokuya ingayanga kuleyo yakomkulu. Kukungena
entolo­ngweni ukuti umntu ayibukuqele yonke intwana anayo ezingutyeni
nasezintweni esenza udumo emtshatweni wake, kuba enqwenela ukuba siti
watshata umfo kabani; kwodwa kotshiwo ngalo mini yodwa, woti wakuya
endlwini yake ufike kungeko nesitulo sokuhlala kungeko nesitya esi;
elatsolo namhla lambete amaxazi ebulukwe, urnfumane umfazi selesuke
waleli ixegwazana kuba isepa akazange ayalame, kanti akuko nto injengo-
kuba mdaka ukumalupalisa umntu nokuyenza ndala inyama yake. Nditi
mabangati abafutshane balinganise izityebi.

UMTANDA BENGAMTANDI.

Annshaw Middle Drift November 4, 1874.

Mhleli we Sigidimi ndinyamezele ngawo lamazwana nakuba eyimigcana
emidana. Ndivuyisiwe kakulu Sisigidimi sika October, sinendaba ezimmandi
ekufunekayo kakulu ukuba zigcinwe. Zimmandi indaba zelipepa.
Masitakazele sibulele kakulu. Nditsho ukuba ndive oko kubaliweyo ngu Mr
D. Mzamo ngawo umzamo ka Rev. H. Kayser enditembayo ukuba kubavuyisile
bonke abantu abatanda ukuhluma kwe Vangeli nokusikeleleka kohlanga.
Ndiyatemba ukuba kummandi kumadodana aqondayo ase Africa akuva ukuba
sekuko abangapezu kwamakulu amatatu abalingayo ukushiya utshaba olwapula
amanene ase Maxoseni akowetu. Kaulezani ukufuna isando nibete niqekeze
intloko yotshaba. Isandoke eniyakuqekeza ngaso kukutandaza ku Yesu
Kristu—Tandazani kusomandla atumele amandla—Masitakazele ngomdumiso
njengoko sivileyo ukuba itshawa unyana ka Ngqika U-Sandile ufuna
isikola. Nditi anga angati onke amakomkulu akwa Xosa atabate lonkumbulo
kwezawo intliziyo. Sisando ke eso sokubulala utshaba oluyivingceleyo
I-Africa. Ngazo ezimpauke siyakolwa ukuba amandla E-Gospile aya kulaula
kuwo onke amakomkulu abantsundu. Siyakolwa ngokuqinisekileyo ukuba
kuyakuzaliseka okokuba “amadolo kweli lizwe makagobe pambi kwako, zide
ziti zonke ilwimi ziluxele udumo Iwako. ”

  P. KEBE.

  Ezazulwana, October 23, 1874.

Kummandi ukuva indaba zendawo ngendawo, kummandi bafo bo Busakwe, nabo
Pakati nabo Falati noko ndingazange ndinibone, nibala indaba zokuba
indoda izipulapule.

Nati apa besinemini emnandi, besifikelwe ngu- mfundisi wetu, omkulu
U-Rev. Mr. Davis, yati intlanganiso, yangena ngolwe Sitatu emini, isizwe
sika Feltman sihlangene. Waposa amazwana ammandi kunene, umfundisi. Wada
wasikumbuza mhla obawo betu bawela kweli le Nyati, wati yena wayeko beno
Palmer no Ayliff, abafundisi abadala. Yati yakupuma, wati makuze Usapo
Iwesikolo, Iweza, waposa amazwi nakulo.

Into ebembi yile yokuba abanye abantu bevuyele lentlanganiso nje kuko
osongela igusha zika

Hendrick Beme, kute kusisa, za igusha ezingama- shumi amabini anesine
zingaseko, kwafunwa, zabo- nwa ezintandatu, ngapaya komda, ezinye
azikaveli nangoku.

THEO. NDWANDWA.

Empikane, November 12, 1874.

Mhleli, ndifuna ukubikela izihlobo zam usizi endinalo ngokumkelwa kwam
ngu ma. Ubube ngomhla we 7 ka October. Kekaloku kuni nonke bazalwana
E-Nkosini ndicela imitandazo yenu ukuba nindibike kuba ayivumi ukuncama
intliziyo noko yena andixeleleyo ukuba uyagoduka. Nguye owandizama ukuze
ndide ndize kule ndlela ye Nkosi; bekungekuko nokuba bendimtanda.

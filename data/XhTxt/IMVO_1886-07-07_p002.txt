angati umntu omnyama eke wane venkile angatshona kuba kungasuke kuti
bukuqu izihlobo eziza kucela lempahla ze, baze abangezizo bayitabate
yonke lompahla ngamatyala angasoze ahlaulwe. Ke sina- manakani, ngati
amawetu acinga ukuba elipepa yinto enokufunyanwa ngesisa. Yimposiso ke
leyo. Eli­pepa lishicilelwa ngamadoda angenakuze ashukumise nomnwe
esebenzela ityala. Abantu abaxolele ukuba nezikweliti kwi Mvo mabazi
ukuba zintshaba nabatshonisi bayo. Kunjalonje kufuneka imali irolwe
kwasentloko ukuze ibe luncedo. Ewe, siyawabona amatyala erafu zika
Rulumente, kodwa imizamo yamawetu yokuxhasa elipepa ngokurola imali
mayibonakale.

Kufuneka abatati belipepa bali- shumayele balivakalise kwabangeka-
litabati ukuze ukukanyana kwalo kubafikelele bonke, nokuze limiseleke
kuba xa lingenamgqeku lofa. Elipepa limiselwe ukubanekela umzi ukuze
ungagaxazi ukuhamba pantsi kolaulo luka Rulumente. Akuko bani
limtyebisayo, kuba kwanele ukuba imali yalo ilingane nendleko zalo. Xa
ke ama-Afrika esoyiswa kubhatala indleko zalo, saza sacima esisibanana
sokukanyisela umzi igazi lawo liyakubizwa kwa- kuwo. Maninzi amadoda
azikulu anezinto, nafundileyo abefanelwe

kukuxhasa elipepa ngento ezibona- kalayo, koko ke alaulwa ngumoya

omhlope umiwa ngamapepa. Sibhekisa kubatandi nabaveli bohla- nga ukuba
mabavuse pantsi ukwe- nza imigudu yesixhaso sepepa ngo- kubhatala

ngokutumela imali zabo. Si- zakude singati singabantu abatanda imali

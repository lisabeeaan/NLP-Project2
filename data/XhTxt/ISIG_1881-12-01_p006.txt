3. Esisimilo sinje ke simbangela umfana ukuba ade alahle, ahilizele
nezinto, ati mhleni wazilahla, wazihilizela zada zemka kuye kanti
akasoze ahlangane nazo ngakumbi. Ndinga u Esau wakudala angaqondwa
ngamadodana kakuhle. Watengisa ngobu zibulo bake ngesimilo sokupata
eyeka, kanti akasoze abuye ahlangane nabo, wahlukana nabo ngendlelana
encinane, angati ngayo wayefeketa; ekungabonakali ukuba wayiqonda
ubukulu bayo, kwada kwamhla weva ngoyise ukuba namhla itamsanqa
nentsikelelo zonke zamkelwe ngumninawe wake. Waqala kaloku ukulila
ngokukulu ngokurara kakulu, engasancedi nento. Ko ku kupata kuyekwa
kungakanana ukuba kuninzi, kuko izinto ezilahlwayo, intsikelelo zase
mhlabeni nezase zulwini eziya kulahleka ngonapakade. Ubuzibulo bobaptizo
kuba oludodana lubaptiziwe kuhlanga lwobu hedeni olunga zaniyo no Mdali;
ubuzibulo bemfundo kuhlanga olungenamfundo, olungena kukanya; ubuzibulo
bamashishini, amnika umntu indlela elula yokuzuza isonka sokupila,
epantsi komtunzi, kanti abanye batshiswa ngamalanga; zonke ezizinto
kutengiswa ngazo ngenxa yomasutu, yendlalo zobuhedeni pakati kododana
nentombi. Eyona ndawo ilusizi engabonwayo kukuba lomalungelo ayakupuluka
angaze abuye azuzwe.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

UCINGO.—I Volksraad yase Transvaal inike igunya lokuba kumiswe, ucingo
olo suka e Heidelburg luye emdeni we Free State.

UMPATISWA Micimbi yaba Ntsundu, wanduluka e Qonce esinga ema Mpondweni
nge 7 ka November. Siyatemba wohamba kakuhle.

U MR. BATTLE, imantyi yase Dutywa, pesheya kwe Nciba, ubanjiwe ngetuba
lokulahleka kwemali ka Rulumente Ofisini yake.

ABA PICOTI MASIKO.—Eli bandla lisa nqumeme okwa ngoku lobuya lihlangane
emva kokuba ingxelo yabo babuzwayo ishicilelwe, ze lenze ke ingxelo.

I “ NATAL WITNESS ” iti, ngo 31 Dec., 1880, inani lama India kule Koloni
libe li 2 J,536, kweli nani i 11,537 nge botshwe kube lungu, 8,999
ikululekile.

LORD MAYOR OF LONDON.—Upumile u Mr. Ailderman McArthur kwesi sihlalo
sipakamileyo somzi oyi London, endaweni yake kungene ngalo nyaka u Mr.
Aiderman Ellis.

IMICIMBI YABA NTSUNDU.—Siqonda ukuba nase Natal kuza kunyulwa amadoda
oku picota imicimbi yaba Ntsundu balo Koloni; pakati kwawo kuvakala
ukuba kobako u Right Rev. Dr. Colenso, Sir Theophilus Shepstone, elinye
kuma lungu e Rulumente, nabanye kwa we Parlamente, yase Natal.

E LOVEDALE.—Lomzi we sikolo uke wehlelwa bububi obukulu, ngenxa yoku
buba komnye wododana olumhlope, ogama lingu Master Ronald Ross.
Wafunyanwa kukufa nge qubuliso; kwambamba iveki zone; kwati nge 9 ka
November, lamtabata i Polisa eli kulu lamsa kwaba ninzi. Wancwatywa ngo
11.

ILUNGU LASE ALIWAL NORTH.—Amadoda mabini aselete asele ezilungisele
ukuba amele i Aliwal e Parlamenteni endaweni ka Mr. Orpen, onyulelwe
ekubeni sisandla se Rulumente e Lusutu. Impi yedolopu itanda inyule u
Mr. Richard Dowling, eku vakala ukuba ngoweqela lika Mr. Sprigg; ifama
zifuna u Mr. David De Wet, owecala lika Mr. Scanlen. Akakazeki oyakuba
nenani eli kulu.

U MR. HENRY wase Rini unyulelwe ukuba abe sisandla se Mantyi e Bedford
endaweni ka Mr. A. W. Baker onyuselwe kwa kulondawo e Rini.

NGE 17 ka November intwana yo Mlungu yapule ingalo yenye kona apa e
Lovedale ngokuti iyi gweqe ze iti ukuya kuwa enye, iwe nge ngalo.

I GOVERNOR YASE NATAL.—Inkosazana inyule u Mr. H. J. Sendall, obe
lilungu elinci leqela eli kangela ukupatwa kwama dolopu, ukuba abe yi
Governor yase Natal.

PAMBI KWE JAJI.—Amatyala awatetwa e Bayi yi Jaji abe 21. U McDonald no
Paterson ama xigxa abe fakelwe uku panga indlu, nokuba amahashe
agwetyelwa iminyaka esi xenxe esebenza nzima.

U SIR G. C. STRAHAN, owabe bambe indawo ka Sir H. Robinson, ukumka kuka
Sir Bartie Frere, udlule e Cape Town malunga napakati kwe nyanga, esinga
e Tasmania, Australia, apo ayakuba yi Ruluneli.

QUICKSILVER.—Esi sinyiti sinqabileyo kutiwa sifunyenwe e Somer­set East
pezu kwe Bosch River ngu Mr. Palmer inkosana yama polisa. Ukuba ento iyi
nyaniso, asitandabuzi ukuba lomzi woba li Dayimane ngamini nye.

E MZIMKULU.—Pakati ko November lo kubikwe uku b'ub'a kwe nkosana u
Sidoi, no nyana wake omkulu ufa kakulu. Ngu Diki umninawe ka Sidoi
onukiweyo ngama gqira. Wasabele kwa Mantyi sib'ala nje.

ULOLIWE WASE COYI.—I Eastern Star, i koranti yase Rini, iti, amadoda
asebenza lo loliwe aseledlule ku 160, kamsinya nje kulindelwe elinye
ikulu. Isituba esesi setyenzwa zi mayile ezi ntlanu, esezi gqityiwe zi
mbini.

IQANDA LENYOKA.—Intokazi etile emhlope ngapa e Humansdorp eziselwe
iqanda ngama kwenkwana, ete yona lele nkuku. Kute xa selisenza ngalo i
pudini, yali qekeza yafumana inyokana entlana paka­ti. Qek’umlomo.

KUTIWA idayimani enkulu eyaka ya funyanwa e Diamond Fields eyabi bunzima
buzi Karate ezi 461, itengiswe kongena lungelo loku tenga lamatye nge
£50, waza yena wayitengisa nge £11,000. Ontaminani bate batala bayafuna.

amanene avutuzelisayo. Pakati kwawo ngu Mr. Sexton, M.P. ilungu elimele
i Sligo; no Mr. O’Kelly, M.P. omele i Roscommon ; Mr. Dillon, M.P. omele
i Tipperary.

London Times baupinde nge Cawa, Oct. 9, e New­castle-on-Tyne, e England
umsebenzi wabo. Abafundisi kweli bake bati mabake bamenyelwe kweli,
asazi bengafiki nje kwala ntonina.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1871

Apumake amaceba lawo aya kugqugula abuya esiti unetyala lokwenzakalisa.
Wa- gwetyelwa ke inyanga ezintandatu esebe­nza kanzima.

U-Qwesha owayebe igusha zika Mr. Frauenstein ugwetyelwe unyaka onenyanga
ezintandatu esebenza kanzima.

U-Philip Jacob Walter wase Weisbaden wanikwa ityala lokudlwengula.
Lombanjwa wayezuze ituba lokuke apume enamadoda ammeleyo. Lati lakubizwa
igama lake ematyaleni akabiko; wazake umteti wamatyala wafuna ukuba
kuveliswe ipepa elinamagama abo babemmele. Wati U- Mr. Stockenstrom
umteteleli owayengecala lababemmele mabake banikwe ixesha ukuza babe
nokumzisa U-Walter lowo, kwa- kupind’ukuviwa amatyala. Kade belinga
ukumbamba; bangamfumanake mhlaumbi ukuba banikwe ixesha. Kanjalo U-Rulu-
mente akangepulukani nemali yake ngenxa yokuba esuke wabolulela ixesha.

Umgwebi ute yena akaboni ukuba an- gatinina ukuyenza into enjalo.
Wayicasa lonto no Mr. Barry, ayavunywake. Ukuba ke kodwa uzile U-Walter
kwakupind’ ukuviwa amatyala ingabuya ibuyiselwe lomali.

Umgca, no Naiko, no Marafu, no Piyose, no Ntshebe, no Mboya, no Zenzile,
no Richard, no Maclean, no Felana, banikwa ityala lokubulala omnye
umntu, U-Mayeji umfo wakwa Magoloza. Bati bona aba- natyala.

Amanqina abonisa ukuba U-Mayeji lowo ubulewe kube kusiliwa etywaleni.
U-Mr. Stockenstrom Umteteleli, watetelela lomadoda asixenxe siwabize
ngamagama. Acitakala ke amatyala oko Mvulo lowo kungekapunywa ndaweni.

Ngolwe Sibini amaceba apuma kweliti anetyala lomadoda ngapandle kuka
Mboya. Agwetyelwa ukuba arole iponti ezimbini emnye, ati ukuba
akanakuzirola ahlale unyaka etolongweni esebenza kanzima.

U-Carl F. Berger, no Wilhelm A. F. Per­per, banikwa ityala lokubeta
U-Sturman umalusi wase Frankfort, kuba bati bobabini bambeta kunene
ngentonga entloko, nase milenzeni, nase zingalweni, nase mzimbeni, baza
bati bemenjenjalo bamjingisa emqadini wendlu imini yonke nobusuku bonke.
Kwatike kuba inqina elikulu belingeko yabuyiswa ngomva kwixa elisezayo
londawo. Aza lamadoda mabini amelwa kwangokwanga pambili.

U-Jim abati elinye ngu Mpetula wani­kwa ityala lokugqoboza indlu ka Mr.
Stevenson wase Qonce, efuna ukuba impahla. Wati yena akanatyala.

Elona nqina likulu, U-Mr. J. M. Ste­venson, wati akabiko, wazake wadliwa
iponti ezi £10. Angatike kodwa ukuba uvelise isizatu esihle sokungabiko
kwake asinde ekudliweni. Elotyala selotetwa ngexa elizayo.

U-Pazie, umntu omnyama, wafakwa itvala lokuti wayenxamele ukudlwengula
U-Nonkamfe wakwa Totoka. Walikanyela yena elotyala. Kwafumaneka ukuba
akanatyala okwenene.

U-Zenzile wase Tyume wanikwa ityala lokubulala Umagomo umsebenzi. Wati
yena akanatyala.

Umbanjwa lowo watetelelwa ngu Mr. De Wet. Kubonakala ukuba ngalo
Sep­tember udluleyo kwakusilwa amakwenkwe yazake leyo yabulawayo yaya
kulamla; yaza yangxolisana no Zenzile lowo, base besilwa. Umagomo lowo
wabetwa entloko ngenduku wafake. Ugwetyelwe U-Zenzile lowo ukuza ahlale
umnyaka etolongweni esebenza kanzima.

I-DIAMOND.—Kufumaneke ukuba ixa- biso lediamond ezimke nalomkombe kutiwa
yi Celt oye E-England, lingamawaka an- gamashumi asibozo eponti
(£80,000). Kudityaniswake ezisuke E-Natal, nase Bayi, nase Kapa.

EYONA NKOMO INTLE EMHLABENI.— Lenkomo lingesikazi ekutiwa ukubizwa ngu
Lady Fragrant (Unenekazi Ovumba limnandi). Yimazi ka Mr. John Booth wase
Warlaby E-England. Kwimiboniso yemfuyo iselifumene amabaso ekomityana
zegolide angapezu kwa 23. Umniniyo akavumi kuyitengisa nangamawaka
amabini eponti (£2,000) inani ebelike larolelwa enye yenkomo zake
ebekutiwa ukubizwa ngu Lady Grateful (Unomibulelo). Umntu obeyibuka
uvakele selesiti komnye umfo obehambele kona—Hayi ukubantle kwe- ntloko
yayo! Hayi ukufanelana komzimba wayo noko umkulu. Lo Lady Fragrant
ukumhlambi wenkomo esingati siwuxelile inani lawo bakolwe betandabuza
abalese- shi betu.

EZENTENGO (I-FANDESI).

E-QONCE.—Ngo Mgqibelo, ngolwe 15 ku April kotengiswa ngemihlaba,
nezindlu zika Mr. Schermbrucker, kwanenye indlu ka Mr. Gilbert, ekuhlala
kuyo okwakaloku U Mr. Cole.

E-LEG PLAATS.—Ngase Middleburg ngolwe Sitatu ngolwe 12 ku April kuya
kutengiswa ngama 400 enkabi zegusha, ne 10 lemazi zenkomo, ne sibozo
samahashe kwanezinye impahla.

E-BEEG RIVIER.—Ngase Humansdorp ngolwe Sibini ngo 11 ku April U-Mr.
Metelerkamp uyakutengisa igusha, ne- bokwe ezi 300; Imazi zenkomo ezi
20; Inkabi ezi 10, nenqwelana yamahashe.

E-QUEENSTOWN.—Ngolwe Sitatu ngo­lwe 19 ku April kuya kutengiswa ngama-
hashe namahashekazi eskiti ukuba akahla- ng’ akululwe.

E-COLESBEEG.—Ngolwe 3, nangolwe 11, nangolwe 15 ku April kuya kutengiswa
ngamahashe namahashekazi eskiti ukuba akahlang’ akululwe.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

INGQAKAQA.—Kutiwa e Kapa sekuko indoda ehliweyo yingqakaqa.

AMAXWILI.—Siva ukuba ngala macala ase Rini kwenye ifama amaxwili abulele
iqelana lenciniba.

INGQAKAQA NONYULO.—E Kimberley kuvakala ukuba ingqakaqa iya iqubela
pambili, abantu abasebehliwe kutiwa sebe kuma 76, abafayo 51. Kunjenje
noko zona ezonyulo azifile amadoda ekutiwa asenkundleni alishumi.

IZINGWE KWEYEGQIRA.—Kuvakala ngoku ngakuyo lenta- ba zibonwa futi, zade
ngenye imini zangenela umhlambi we Bulu owauye ngempiliso, zapuma
namashumi amane. Ngati sekuko abaye kuzizingela.

UMMANGALISO.—Kutiwa ngase Rini lati liduduma kaku- lu zaye igusha zo
Mlungu otile ziqukene ndawonye kuko inkabi yebokwe pakati kwazo. Lite
izulu kamsinyane laselimisa enkabini yebokwe leyo azabi nanto zona
igusha.

ABABETWE NGUMBANE.—Elase Rafu lixela ukufa kwa- madoda amabini, indoda
abati ngu Mr. Combrink nonyana. Kutiwa kubo bobabini amanxeba abe
semanzini enyawo, noko abeko nasentloko komncinane. Lento yenzeke ngase
Free State.

ONYATELWE NGU LOLIWE.—Kumgama olunge kwa Ko- mani, kuko indodada ate xa
agqitayo uloliwe kusasa wayi- bona ilele ecaleni kwendlela ifile. Kute
kwakukangelwa kwabonakala ukuba mayibe inyatelwe ngomnye wololiwe base
busuku.

ABATSHELE ENDLWINI.—E Tinara siva ukuba abantwa- nana ababini bento
abati ngu Dicks, kute ekutsheni kwe- ndlu yakokwabo, eyatsha ebusuku,
baba lilifa lomlilo. Nakuba ngati abafanga kwaoko kutiwa akubonakali
kuba bopumelela.

AMASELA KWABAMHLOPE.—Kutiwa kuko amafama ama Bulu asentolongweni ngokuba
igusha zabamelwane. Kute okunene kwakufunwa kwifama yenye yalamadoda
kwafu- nyanwa igusha ezilishumi linambini. Kubonakele ukuba lamadoda
ngamamenemene madala.

ODUTYULWE ESIBA.—Liti elase Mtata, e Mampondweni ngobunye ubusuku wati
umtengeli wavenkile itile kwelo, wetuswa kungena kwamntu utile, ute
kanye kuba ebelele umpu ewunxashile wasele yidubulela kwalapo isifa.
Kanti ke inkabi ibiseyinempahlana ebiseliyitabatile.

IBOLA.—Siva kwelinye lamapepa ama Ngesi, ngento embi kunene ekukuti
umntu endaweni yokubola asuke agibisele. Uti ke umbali walo ngxelo
ebalisa ngento ayi- bone ngamehlo ake ati ekugqibeleni, lento abongameli
(umpires) ngebeyinika iliso ukuyigcina ingenzeki.

EZASE LUSUTU.—Kutiwa kwelo kwakutsha nje, lati igqi- ra u Dr. Reed laya
kukangela umntu owayesifa yingqaka- qa ngendawo yokumpa imiciza. Kutiwa
Abesutu indoda leyo bayixele ukuba mayibuye ngomva bangxamela ukuyi-
ndudla ngentonga, yabe yona inkosana yabo ibe selite uku- ba uyapika
mabamdubulele kwalapo.

UKUNUKWA.—Emampondweni kutiwa kuko indoda esa­nd’ ukunukwa, kusitiwa
itakate umfazi. Ngokwesiko lama Xosa wagwetywa ukuba abulawe
njengegqwira. Ivakele lonto e Mtata, imantyi yakona namadoda amabini
baya kukangela lonto, noko bafika indoda leyo seyikululwe, ekanti ke
izisindise ngokuti yakuba ibetwa yavuma ukuba okunene umntu lowo ubulewe
yiyo, yavelisa nezinto eti imbulele ngazo, yatembisa nokumkulula.

EZASE SIKITI.—Ezeleni, eskiti ngomha we 17 ku March kuya kutengiswa
inkunzi ete tshuqu ngomgca omhlope eqolo, ukuba umniniyo akatanga
avele.—Eqanda, apo ngomhla we 13 ku March kuya kutengiswa inkabi efosi
yehashe, emlenzeni wasekunene kuko impawu zokwapuka, owangasekohlo
unenqina elimhlope.—Edonqaba, kwango- kunjalo apo ngomhla we 12 ku March
kuya kutengiswa inkabi emnyama, etiwe qumpu kweya sekunene indlebe.

ABATANDATU ABABUBILEYO.—Elase Russia libika ukuba kwelo kuko intombi
ezintandatu ezibuleweyo. Kutiwa ezi ntombi bezisebenza ndawonye, zite ke
ngamhla utile ekupe- leni kweveki zacinga ukutyelela ekaya mganyana
utile kwi- ndawo le zibe zisebenza kuyo, zenjenjalo ke, zati kodwa

ngokoyika ukupangwa zegucala endleleni, zaza imalana yazo zayifumbatisa
komnye wazo ngokuti mhlaumbi zingayisindisa ekupangweni; ute ke uwazo lo
unemali waziqusheka esitroyini ababelele ngakuso. Kute kunini ezintokazi
zeqwi zalala, kwaza kanye ngeloxa kwafika iqela lamadodana ate akuba
engafumani nto iyimali emizimbeni yazo aselezibulala, etshisa nenca
leyo, wasinda kodwa Iowa yena ubepatiswe imali, ote ke waba luncedo
lokuhlaba umkosi ukuze zibanjwe izihange ezo.

  kungene endlwini yake igosa lopeteyo kulo
  mandla akuwo, nokuba lelogunyazisiweyo
  ngombalo welo gosa, nabani ongxabalaza
  endleleni, otukayo, odubulayo, mhlaumbi
  owenzakalisayo ngenteto elo gosa mhlaumbi
  lomntu, xa ahambisa izimiselo zalo mteto,
  woti, efunyenwe enetyala amelwe sisohlwa-
  yo esinjengokublaula iponti zoshumi, ati
  ukuba akahlauli afakwe entolongweni
  asebenze nzima mhlaumbi, ixesha elinge-
  ngapaya kwenyanga zontatu.

  Isiqendu sa XLV1I. Ngexesha ekoyo ingqakaqa isipata-
  mandla sinokumenza nabani opakati kwe-
  mida yegunya laso ukuba anike ubungqina

  ukuba ubani lowo ute akabi nabo ubungqina
  bokuba waqatshulwa aze kanjako amangale

  ngazo zombini ezindawo sokuba ahlaule,
  ngokwala ngakunye, iponti zombini, ati
  ukuba akanakuhlaula amelwe yintolongo
  ixesha elingengapezulu kwentsuku ezisixenxe

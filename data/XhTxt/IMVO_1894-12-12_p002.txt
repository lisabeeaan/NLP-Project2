iponti kwizibuko le Nxuba eliyi Hunt’s Drift kwindlela evela e
Ngqu­shwa. Kakade umzi ubusand’ ukwenzelwa ibloro kwa Nqobokazi,
uyavuya, waye uyakuyikumbula lento xana kunyulwayo.

Umqondiso Wamaxesha

Umlesi wetu ose Steytlerville uti bekuko intlanganiso ye Bond kwelo
kutsha nje, ekutete amalungu amabini e Palamente amele i Tinara nomzana
lowo wabo ukwangowesiqingata sase Tinara. Into epuhlileyo kukuba u Mr.
Bellingan, M.L C., unikwe ivoti yokutenjwal ogama u Dr. Vane, M.L A.
emke engayifumananga. Sicinga u- kuba olulwalwa iqekekele kulo i Bond
njengakwezinye indawo, ngu Mteto we Bhula.

Ukubanjwa Kwabadlali e Free State

Siyavuya ukufumana ukuba inqaku ebeke salenza ngokubanjwa kwaba­dlali
abantsundu e Pitoli liyapikiswa zizipatamandla zikona. Zaye zinga
um’bhali wepepa lase Free State i Express angaxela apo atabate kona
olodaba. I Express iti, olodaba ilufumene kwipepa lase Nyara i
Enterprise. Siyavuya ukupaula indlela ate ama­pepa ase Free State
abutetisa ngayo ububhovubhovu ebekutiwa benziwe kubadlali abantsundu.
Lonto ixela ukuba izinto ziyatembisa kona.

Impi yase Mbo Ezidolopini.

Impi eina Mfengu ezidolopini ikolisile ukutandana ne “ ranga,” apo
impembelelo zakona zingezizo ezikutaza ubu Krestu nobuntu. Isiqamo soko
ke sesokuba ikolisile lempi ukuti neze ishiye abafazi emakaya,
nebingamagqoboka—okanye ilunge ne- tyalike—ukuti yakufika edolopini
ishweshwe, ingangeni netyalike. Lo­nto ke ininzi. Yaye lempi ingangeni
nakumidlalo enje nge bhola ne tennis. Ngati elinqina alilihle kuni mzi
wase Mbo, kengoko yenzani ubuyo.

Umfundisi Omdala Wabantsundu-

Kusivuyise kanye ukufumana indaba ezivela kumlesi wetu omdala, oga­ma
lisakunjulelwe kunene nanamhla nje ngenxa yomsebenzi wake kubantu
bakowetu—siteta u Rev. Canon Green­stock, owaye ngumfundisi wase Mtwaku
(St. Matthew’s) kwa Qobo­qobo. Kumyoli kuti ukuva ukuba useko. nokuba,
nangona esempelazwe, kude le Empumalanga, usamana ukucinga ngemini
zakudala, waye enononelelo olukulu kwimisebenzi yemizi yabefundisi,
kwaye kumkolisa ukuva indaba ezitile nga- bantu ababepantsi kwake
iminyaka eliqela. Bokumbula abalesi betu nkuoa emva kokumka kwake kwa
Qoboqobo iminyaka ilishumi elinesi- bhozo edluleyo, waka waya e Bhai
okwexesha, wadlulela e Natal; emva koko uve Engilane ate wandulukaapo
waya kubukumkani base Siam. Ngoku ungumfundisi kwizidedebe zomzi ozi-
gidimi zama Ngesi e Bangkok, apo aquba umsebenzi oluugileyo abunilrele
kuwo ubom bake. Sivakalisa uluvo lwabantu bakowetu xa siti, wangamana
wampilo-nde-ntle.

Ubutempile.

Nge Cawa edluleyo ngokuhlwa um- fundisi u Rev. J. Harper ushumayele
intshmnayelo ebukali pezu kwamazwi ati:—Zenzeleni inyawo zenu indlela.
ete-tye, ukuze into eqwalelayo ingaruneki, ukuze kanye ipiliswe. Ute
lento iluselo izibonakalise ngendlela. ezininzi ukuba ingumtshabalalisi
nombhubisi wabantu, yaye kananjalo imininzi imipefumlo ete yakubheka
kulo yawa, yaza yasinga kwantshabalalo. Utywala buzisa ububi, iukatazo
emntwini nakwizindlu, buzibonakalisile ukuba busisitunywa songendawo,
buchasene mpela-mpela kanye nomsebenzi ka Tixo; baye bututela esi-
hogweni imipefumlo emininzi. Uqu- kumbele ngokuti: Maxa ke utywala
bunjalo yimfanelo yake wonke ongum-Krestu, notanda uhlanga ukuba
asebenze ukuba esisitunywa songendawo esikubekisa abaninzi sishenxiswe;
nokuba abo bomeleleyo bazenzele inyawo zabo indlela ete-tye ukuze ke
ngoko ababutataka bangawi xa. balandela indlela zabo ezimagoso zotywala.
Kuhle ukubona ukuba “inkewu” iyaliwa ziramente. Inga nama Tshatshi
angade angene kulo ngoma.

Ububhetyebhetye Bomzi Ontsundu.

Ingqondi enkulu u Dr. Biyde (umdaka oyi President yase Liberia)
kwincwadi yake unenqaku e)iti:— “ Impato yama Ngesi inendawo efike
ibulale ubuntu nokuzicingela kwabo bapantsi kwabo.” Ubunyaniso ba- lento
nabanina ozikatazavo ngentlalo yomzi angabuqonda. Zininzi izinto
ezonakalisa intlalo-ntle yetu njengomzi esinqatyelwe kukuzilwa ngenxa
yobubhetyebbetye, nangona kusemandleni etu ukwenjenjalo. Umzi opesheya
kwe Nciba ungene nzima kwiziselo ezinxilisayo ekubeni umteto ungavumele
ntengiso yatywala kwelo. Nakuba zisaziwa ivenkile ezitengisa. utywala
akuko bani unabuganga bokuzisa emtetweni; nalapa e Koloni intsebenzo
yomqomboti iyembi emzini, yaye itsho lwacima ukanyo lwe zikolo; noko
akuko bani uvelayo alwe nabo. Nemigudwana eyenziwayo ngama Terapile
yeyokushumayela inzilo kupela. Into efunekayo kuku­ba amadoda amakulu
emizi avuke abulwe utywala ngendlela zonke- nokubana abusayi kupeliswa
mpela. yona intengiso yabo nemitayi—eteyenza imizi yetu nezikolo zaba
lihlazcj, bapcla nobuntu—mayipeliswe.

UTSHISO.

SIFUMANA ne mantyi zingakuboni ukulunga okwenziwa lolutshiso lwempahla,
olu ngomnye wemiteto ka Sprigg kwabantsundu ebifanele ukub'uba kunye
naye. U Mr. A. R. Innes i Mantyi yase Bathurst, uti:—

“Esinye sezizatu abanazo abantsundu ngokucasa lomteto wotshiso,
sisahlulo esenziwayo. Enye into evisa ububi obukulu, yeyokuba, kwisitili
esitile, lomteto wotshiso uyanyanzelwa kwabahlala kuso, ze kuti kwaba
ngengabahlala kuso kungafuneki ukuba zibe notshiso ezabo impahla,
njengokuba impahla zenye ilali zinedlelo elinye kunye nezenye, umsebenzi
wotshiso awundawo ke maxa inkomo ezinotshiso zihlangana nenkomo
ezingenalo. Lento ke ibanga ubunzima bokugcina inani lenkomo
ezinotshiso; kodwa eyona nto idibayo ekugcineni inani lenkomo ngotshiso,
kukutengiselana kwabantu, okukoyo, futi, nokugcina impahla yomnye umntu,
iti imana ibuya izakutatyatwa ngabaniniyo abakwezinye izitili. Endaweni
yotshiso, mna ndingavelisa ukuba kulingwe lendawo, kwezinye izitili,
ukuba umkangeli welali zabantsundu, anike lowo na lowo i pass, ekuya
kubalwa kuyo yonke impahla ehambayo yalomntu, ekuya kuti kule pass
kumana ukubalwa ngawo onke amaxesha ukuma kwalompahla. Londlela ke
iyakwenza u Rulumente ukuba ahlale esazi ngentengiso nokuba
kungokwananelana ngempahla pakati kwabantu, kanjalo ahlale esazi inani
lempahla ena bantu abantsundu.

UKUTYOLWA KWABU FUNDISI BASE LUSUTU.

U MR. SPRIGG esihlalweni sake sobu Rulumente, ubezamana nobuqokolo
bokutyola abantu abamsulwa, ngokucasana nemiteto yake akubona ukuba
ayisebenzi kulunga. Inteto yake e Parlamente yayize kukutyola abafundisi
base Lusutu; wada wapume kweliti, omnye waba bafundisi ongu Rev. Theop.
Jousse wanika indoda etile yo Msutu umpu waza wati angawunikeli lompu ku
Rulumente, kanti umtyola nje ngento engazange yenzeka. Yati lonto
yakuvakala ku Rev. Jousse wabala kwa oko emapepeni endaba ukutyila
ukungabi nyaniso kwalonteto ka Mr. Sprigg. Ezincwadi zilandelayo
zazibalwe ngu Rev. Jousse ngalonto, ebalela imantyi zase Lusutu,
ezikanyeleyo ukuba zaka zamxelela into enjalo u Mr. Sprigg :—

Thaba Bosigo, June 13, 1881.

NKOSI ETANDEKAYO,—

Ungaba undincede kakulu ukuba ushicilele epepeni lako ezincwadi
zilandelayo ziya kubonisa ngokusemhlotsheni, ukutyola okumdaka kuka Mr.
Sprigg etyola mna, nabanye abafundisi bakowetu abalapa e Lustu.

Ndingowako

THEOP. JOUSSE, V.D.M.

UKUTSHONA KUKA STEMELE.

E CANADA E AMERICA.

Kwa kwela iqela elikulu labantu ko stemelana aba tatu ngomhla we 24 ku
May besuka e London nase Ontario, besiya kudlulisa umhla eya zalwa ngawo
Inkosazanae Wood­land Cemetry, Wooland Park, Wards Dock, nase Spring
bank. Ekubuyeni kwabo, u Stemelana ogama lingu Vic­toria omnye waba ba
tatu unduluke ngo 6 o’clock e Spring bank, elayishe ama 400 amadoda
nentsapo zawo. Yaye iluhambo lwokuqala aluhambayo lo stemelana nonyaka
nje. Kodwa ngu stemelana omdala ubehlaziywe ngelihlobo lidluleyo.
Walayisha neqelana laba beti begubu isihlwele sabantu sihleli pambili
kuvulwe isituba sokudanisela abatandayo. Ubuye walayishwa e Woodland
Park ama 200 abantu. Ute ekupumeleni kwake pantsi komtantato ka Loliwe,
omayela nesituba semayile enye ngezantsi kwomzi ubukuqela calanye ngenxa
yobu nzima bostemele kwela cala ate lama 600 abantu abambelela kwi cala
elingentla. Ushenxe kancinane watshona kwa oko waqekeka wazintsuntsu.
Mayela nalendawo kwakuko umsinga ona mandla, zemka izidumbu ezininzi
nomsinga. Amadoda enza imigudu ukufuna usapo lwawo, akafeza Into kuba
kwaku pitizela kanga ngokuba banga qondani. Inkoliso yabantyiliselwayo
ngamadoda nabafazi; batshela konina noyise aba ntwana beyela ngoku njalo
bona. Kute kwakuhlwa yangumntu kupela mayela nomlambo, kwamhlope zibane
namabaso. Bagaleleka o stemelana aba 2 ngo 8 o’clock balayisha izi dumbu
zaba funyanwayo. Ixa lapakati kwo busuku lifike kusafunyenwe i 150,
kwakusa funwa i 100 labantu abangeka bonakali. Waye layishe lo stemelana
izindlu ezi mayela ne 1,000. Yayi ngumbono ombi owoyikekayo, izizililo
zodwa e caleni lomlambo kwa kubonwa izi dumbu zimane zinyulwa.
Kubonakala ukuba imbangi yale ngozi yintswelo nkatalo nokumxinanisa
kakulu lo stemelana.—Kaffrarian Watchman.

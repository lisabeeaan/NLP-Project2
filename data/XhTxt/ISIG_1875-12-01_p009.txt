ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1875.

“ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA.”

ABANTU abasatumele amagama abo ukuba basifumane kabini ngenyanga
basengamakulu amabini anamashumi asixenxe anesitandatu. Ke inanana
elincinane kangako akungelunge ukuba kuqalelwe kulo. Sasite kwase
kuqaleni kofuneka inani lide lafika kumakulu amahlanu. Ukuba ke ngoko
ngo December lo amagama atunye- lwayo akalifezi inani elifunekayo kuya
kubonakala ukuba ike iyekwe lendawo kude kupele inyanga ezintatu nokuba
zintandatu.

Enye indawo yeyokuba baya celwa bonke abatabati be Sigidimi ukuba
bayitumele imali yaso yalomnyaka upelayo. Abanye sebeneminyaka ngamibini
nangamitatu bengazange barole nepeni, kodwa besitu- nyelwa inyanga
zonke. Bebefanele ukuzi- bonela ukuba ayifanelekile into enjalo.
Ibingeyiyo neyokude kutetwe ngayo. Kufunwa ke ukuba imali irolelwe lonke
ixesha elidluleyo kude kuze kuba kulo December sinaye namhla.

Sigqibela ngokwenza imibulelo kubo bonke abate basixasa basinceda ngalo
mnyaka upelayo. Sibulela ngokukodwa abate bamana ukusitumela indaba
zamacala abakuwo zokuhambiseka kwe Lizwi nezentlalo yabantu; kwanezixoxa
ngamasiko azizixakaniso. Siyatemba ke ukuba baya kuzingisa kwangalonto
nangozayo umnyaka.

ISAZISO.

KUBO ABAFUNDISI NE TITSHERE NGE MINQOPISO

YOMSEBENZI.

UMONGAMELI Wemicimbi Yemfundo uya bacela abafundisi nabapati bezi kula
zaba- ntsundu ezinganeno nezi pesheya kwe Nciba (nakoma Dutywa) e-St.
John’s nakwezinye indawo ukuba batumele kuyo i- Ofisi yake e-Kapa elinye
ipepa lomnqopiso, lize elinye lisale kumkangeli walo ndawo. Lonto ke
kufuneka ukuba yenziwe kamsinya.

Ukususela kulo usuku lokuqala ku January akuko Titshere iya kwamkelwa xa
sukuba ifunelwa ukufundisa nokuba sisi pina isi kula, ngapandle kokuba
endaweni yokuqala, yenze ipepa lomnqopiso wokuba iya kusebenza kweso
sikula inyanga ezi lishumi elina mbini, ize ke emveni koko, ukuba ifuna
ukupuma, inike inyanga ezinta- tu zokwazisa umfundisi ukuba iza kupuma,
naye umfundisi anike isaziso kwa senyanga ezintatu ukuba ufuna enye
i-Titshere endaweni yaleyo iseliko. Kuyo indawo yesibini ifuneka ukuba
ibe nesiqiniselo esanelisayo nokuzipata kwayo kwindawo esuka kuyo,
nesokuba iwufeze kakuhle na umuqopiso wayo kwindawo ebi fundisa

kuyo, ukuba ibiseliyi Titshere kade ngapa- mbili.

I-Titshere ezingenaziqiniselo (certificates) ukuba zilungile zinezimilo,
kanjalo zinaso isiqendwana esaneleyo semfundo zonikwa inani elinge
ngapezulu kweponti ezimashumi mabini (£20) ngomnyaka zibe ke zisalingwa.
Ezine ziqiniselo zonikwa amashumi amatatu eponti (£30) ngomnyaka, eziya
kuti ukuba umsebenzi wazo ziwufeza kakuhle zinyuselwe.

Kulindelwe kanjako ukuba izibonda nabanye abantu boncedisa ukurola
ishumi leponti ngomnyaka.

  LANGHAM DALE,

Umongameli Wemicimbi Yemfundo

E-Kapa, November 22, 1875.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

ISIFO SAMAHASHE sesiko e-Transvaal.

KWELE DIAMOND kunconywa ukufa kwabantu ezitolongweni.

ukuvcla e-England.

U-MAJOR LANYAN no Colonel Crossman abafo abaza kulaula ele Diamond
sebendulukile ukuya kona.

I-PRINCE OF WALES, unyana ka. Victoria wa- mazibulo usuke e-Suez nge G
ka October ukuya e-India.

INTENGO—Imini emisiweyo ekuya kutengiswa ngayo ifama yase Howe’s Post
pezu kwe Katala ngumhla we 9 ka December ngolwe Sine evekini.

IFAMA zisakala kakulu ngokusweleka kwaba- ntu bokusebenza kulekoloni.
Ezinye ziti yinina ukuba kungeziswa ama Tshayina

U-MR PERKS umfundisi omkulu wama Wesile ohamba ehlola izikolo kwelilizwe
evela pesheya simlindele ukuba alike kulamacala ngeziveki.

UTYWALA.—Umfo otile wase Mgwamanzi uteta ngokuti yinina ukuba abafundisi
bangahlangani nangendawo ezinje ngokwalelwa kotywala.

U-MANKOROANE NO GASIBONE inkosana ezimele lomhlaba kupikiswana ngawo nga
Mabulu na Mangesi kuvakala ukuba kuko enye eziza kulwa nayo.

ISELA ELIMHLOPE.—Kumatyala ase Bayi umfo abati ngu William Morris unikwe
inyanga 13 etolongweni ngokusuka ebe £3 1s. kumntu ontsundu.

IBURSARI ZASE LOVEDALE.—Siya bacela bonke ngokukodwa abase mfundweni
ukuba bakuqondisise okutetwa nge bursari zase Lovedale kwesi Sigidimi
sanamhla.

UBUNXILA—E-Bayi kuko umfo osidenge nesitulu ebeziswe ematyaleni ngenxa
yokunxila. Babekufuna ukumqondisa ityala lake kwabonakala ukuba akuko
nofifi olu, yaselimyeka Imantyi.

U-MARTHINUS PETER, umsebenzi wangase Nqushwa unetyala lokuba ubulele
umfazi wake ogama lingu Rachel ngomhla we 14 ka May. Lo ke ugwetywe
iminyaka emibini esebenza nzima.

ABAGUQULI bezibabo kuvakala ukuba kwi Testa- mente endala sebeye bapela
ngencwadi ye Mibalo. Kuvakala kanjalo ukuba Itestamente entsha ngo-
kukuguqulwa kutsha iya kugqitywa ukushicilelwa kuyo lenyanga sikuyo.

AMAKOBOKA—Ngase Zanzibar kuko umkombe osand’ ukubanjwa utwele abantu
300, amadoda nabafazi nabantwana abebeya kwenziwa amako- boka. Kuvakala
ukuba bafunyenwe bekwisimo esibi kakulu. Bancedwe ngumkombe wa Mangesi.

U-Cornelis Lottering, ogama limbilingu Nelson umpati wamahashe ohlala
e-Tyelera unetyala, lokuba ubulele umfazi wake ogama lingu Jakjie
ngosuku lwe 14 July 1875. Ugwetyelwe ukuba abe likonxwa elisebenza nzima
yonke imihla yokudla kwake ubomi

ISIYALO.-Abatanda ukugibisela ngamtye pakati kwabantu mabafunde lengozi
ihlileyo e-Gqili— Ute umfana otile wenqwelo ogama lingu Bowker egibisela
inkabi ngelitye wayiposa, watsho kum Xosa ngapaya, wamosela. Lowo Mxosa
ubube emva kwentsuku ezi ntatu.

UMFO otile wase Natal uti inani labafazi baka Langalibalele abasadla
ubom 47: amadodana azalwa nguye 79. Ukankanya nokuba kuko abati
u-Langalibalele sekukudala afayo. Abatshoyo bayaposisa. Ngoku usefameni
yake kufupi ne Kapa. Esiqitini selakutshwa.

ABALESESHI betu, ngokukodwa amadodana siba- cela ukuze bayiqondisise
incwadi evela komnye wa- babalelani betu ozibiza ngokuti ungu “
Madodana.” Lendawo yokutyafa nokuba ngumtwalo kwamadodana asikuko nokuba
inkulu. Soke sibuye siyibuyele sakuba netuba.

IMISEBENZI YEPUPA—Kute E-Bayi kumkombe abati yi Fruiterer omnye
womatiloshe wapupa kuko umkombe otshona elwandle nabantu abebekuwo. Bate
abahlanu bakuliva elopupa ababuye bavume ukuhlala kuwo bade baziswa
ematyaleni banikwa iveki ezisitoba emnye besebenza nzima.

OLILELA ITITSHERE.—U-Hendrick Mvimbi wase Herschel uti, “Usizi olulapa
asilulo nalo lokusweleka kwetitshere. Ndiyibika lendawo ngokuti mhlaumbi
kungako oncedayo. Abantwana betu bahamba kozintonjane ngokusweleka
kwendawo abangagcinwa kuyo, kuba into egcina abantwana sisikolo."

KWELAPESIIEYA KWE NCIBA—Umfo otile obe- balele kwelinye lamapepa
amangesi uti, “Kweli lapesheya kwenciba abantsundu asikuko nokuba baquba
kakuhle. Ziyatengwa kakulu ngabo igusha namakuba. Ilizwi lika Tixo
liyahamba. Apelile amare emfazwe kwa Adam Kok. Kubonakala ukuhluma
kumacala onke.”

U-MR. SIYINGA MANTYI wase Mgwali uti kubantu ababalela izihlobo zabo
kulomacala ngebesiti ukuba abakwazi kakuhle bacele omnye umntu ababalele
ngapandle ukuze zingalahleki incwadi zabo. Uti badiniwe zincwadi
ezingenakuqondwa abaninizo. Enye indawo uti makwaziwe ukuba kuko Umgwali
opesheya kwe Nciba e-Batenjini.

U-APRIL FORTUIN ogama limbi lingu Soul Fortuin wase Palmiet River
unetyala lokuba ubulele umfazi wake ogama lingu Griet Syster, ngosuku
lwe 11 ku September, wambeta ngesihlangu sake wada wafa. Lite ke
lakutetwa ityala walivuma umbanjwa esiti okunene oku ndikwenze
ngokuswela usizi. Ugwetyelwe ukuba abe likonxwa ade afe esebenza nzima.

UKUDLALA NGOMPU—Kute kumzi otile wase Free State kwakuhleli abelungukazi

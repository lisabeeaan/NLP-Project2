EZABABHALELI.

AMACEBO NGOHLANGA.

MR. EDITOR, — Kaufake nkosi yam lomigcana, ndiyakubongoza, ndibona into
zintatu ezitsalela emva umzi ontsundu. 1. Ukungazimiseli entweni umntu u
Tixo amenzele ukuze abe yiyo, ngenxa yobuvila abuchasene nomteto wabantu
noka Tixo, wofumana uninzi Iwabantu lutanda ukufumana lube luyilileka
nemizi, ehamba umntu etunga epuma engena, ngokwenjenjalo kungeko nto
yona imngenelayo, kupela into ahamba esilwa nabanye ekubeni
ngesemsebenzini wake, mhlaumbi yindodana enesipiwo sokucinga okunzulu,
mhlaumbi enobunono obutile bezandla, iti yonke ke londyebo yomzi
wakowabo abe nomona ngayo, asuke ke ayimbele entlabatini yobuvila
ayifihle. Xokokuba mhlaumbi ufundisiwe nokuba kunobomi anganyamezeli
emsebenzini ati abantu bayamkataza nokuba ngoyi titshala, ayekufuna enye
indawo, mhlaumbi nako esiya kweminye imisebenzi intliziyo yake ingekoyo
kuyo, kuti kulapo adinwe.

Umfumane ngenye imini selelitshivela elingenandlela. 2. Kukusukela
amasiko obuhedeni ebekufanele nokokuba umntu akalilo ikolwa awayeke
wona, kuba ayintambo eyomeleleyo ukuluruqela ezantsi uhlanga. Simana
sibona futi emapepeni o Mr. Ngcuka bekukankanya ukwaluswa, kwanoku
xolelwa konyana babo ngabafundisi abatile, kodwa ngati bekufanelekile
okokuba ezizinto zingafakwa kulamapepa omzi ontsundu, kodwa bakutazwe
kakulu abawalwayo omasiko obumnyama. Namhla ibhulukwe nelokwe
siyaziqonda okokuba zilungile kuba lupau lokukanya, kunga ke kunganjalo
kuyoyonke into esingisela ekukanyeni. Amanye amadoda anamandla
okuzisebenzela, asike alibale kukubala intombi zawo okokuba soze sityebe
mhla zendayo ezintombi, kutiwe nakonyana msani ukuya nasezikolweni msani
ukuya nasemsebenzini, bazakwenda kakade odade wenu, noti bakwenda ke
nani nizuze abafazi ngenkomo zalomakazi. 3. Kutenina tina lento ngati
asiyinikwanga ingqondo enjengeyama, gwangqa? Kanti noko xa ukangele
ungafumanisa u Sombawo ongenamona nati esinikile intloko ezizele ewe
bubulumko koko sibutshixele etyesini zobuvila, nobudenge. Sise siboniswe
isampuli umhlawumbi umzekelo ngawo lawo pambi kwenu, amadoda o Rev. Tiyo
Soga amsebenzi nanamhla usatetayo neko bona bete cwaka kobandayo. Nazo
into zo Bokwe ekute kwakutiwa namhla zinkewana imizimba ibe mibi, kuba
umzi omhlope nontsundu uyamazi. Nanko umfo ka Jabavu, ote noko
engumntwana kanti noko yindoda ngengqondo, kuba elake iqashiso lidla
ngokuwa enyanisweni, namhla wonke umzi amehlo uwapakamisile ; nakudala
nanamhla abazange bapele abapika inyaniso noko beyibona. Ligqitile
ixesha lobudenge kaloku umzi ufanele ukukangela pambili apo umntana wase
Europe ekangele kona, Lento ke yonke soyizuza ukuba sakele intlalo yetu
yonke ebulungiseni, enyanisweni, ekunyamezeleni,enkutalweni, ekucingeni
okunzulu, emfundisweni, nasekuxhaseni umsebenzi ka Tixo, owona ububomi
babo bonke abakweli pakade. Owako ndinyanisile.

  Xora, November 29, 1889.

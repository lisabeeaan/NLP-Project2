UVIWO LWE TITSHERE.

  KUYA ziswa kubo bonke ukuba uviwo lwe-
  titshere oluba kabini ngomnyaka, lwaba-

  funa ukufundisa kwizikolo ezifumana uncedo
  kwi Government luya kuqalwa ngo 11 ka April
  1876 ngolwe Sibini evekini.

  Oya eluviweni kuyekelwe entandweni yake
  ukuba aluyele nokuba kukuyipina yezi ndawo—
  e-Kapa, e-Rini, e-Qonce, e-Lovedale, e-St.
  Mark’s, nokuba kukwa Captain Blyth e-Nqa-
  makwe pesheya kwe Nciba.

Abezayo baya kuviwa kwezi zinto.

1.  Inteto yamangesi—ukuyi lesesha nokuba-
      la amazwi ne Grammar.

2.  Ukubala—Ezibalweni kofikwa nakwi
      Vulgar ne Decimal Fractions.

3.  Ukuma nokumelana kwamazwe.

4.  Kuya kukangelwa ubuhle besandla ekuba-
      leni.

5.  Ukupata isikolo.

6.  Inteto ya Mabulu kotandayo.

7.  Eya Maxosa kotandayo.

8.  Eya Besutu kotandayo.

  Akuko uya kuvunyelwa ukuba alungene
  uviwo ukuba iminyaka yake ingapantsi kwe-
  shumi elinesitandatu.

  IZINQINISO EZIVUMELANA NOLONA LUPILISO
  ‘‘OLUQINISEKILEYO” LUKA JESSE SHAW, LO-

  KULUNYWA ZINYOKA, NEZINYE IZINTO.

  U-MR. MULLER wangase Fort Beaufort uti: “Iyeza
  lako ⁽Elikupilisa kuqinisekileyo’ lipiliseinja yetu
  ebilunywe liramba ; intloko yayo ibiselidumbe yali-
  qekele—noko yapila ngosuku olunye olunesiqingata.

  E-FORT BEAUFORT kuko ontsundu obelunywe yi-
  nyoka elunyaweni wadumba kunene, kanti noko
  uyakupila kwangalo mini lisagalelwe kwakanye
  eliyeza “Likupilisa Kuqinisekileyo”

  Ngakwa CAPTAIN BLYTH pesheya kwe Nciba kwe-
  sika ZAZELA bekuko intombazana ebilunywe lu-
  hlobo olutile lweramba ilele, amanxeba ada amane
  kwindawo ngendawo zomzimba. Usike umzimba
  wayo wayingqindilili wabanda. Ilitabate kwakabini
  kwakupela eliyeza yapila.

  U- Mr. S. J. RADENMEYER, obala e-Clith Roe
  kufupi nase Rouxville, e-Orange Free State uti
  “ Zinkosi zam, asikuko nokuba kuluvuyo kum ukuba
  nokuninqinela ngoncedo Lweyeza lako ‘ Elikupilisa
  Kuqinisekileyo.’ Sekukafuti ndilisebenzisa mna
  nabamelwane bam. Ibotile selipantse ukupela. Ke
  akuko xesha like alanceda. Likufanele ukubizwa
  ngokuti Lelikupilisa Kuqinisekileyo.

  Abatengisi balo ngu J. Shaw, E-Fort Beaufort,
  nase Nxukwebe, nase Mpofu, nase Mxelo; no R.
  Stocks, no J. B. Temlett, base Dikeni; U-Whitcher
  no Dyer, kwa Qoboqobo nase Qonce; u-Hedding,
  E-Butterworth, pesheya kwa Nciba.

  JESSE SHAW.

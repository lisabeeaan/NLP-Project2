ABATSHATILEYO.

PELEM-DE YOUNG.—E Kimberley, ngo June 11, 1889, kutshatiswe ngu Rev.

SIDZUMO-MPOLOLO. — E Sanerville, (Tyityaba), Peddie, ngo 4 July. 1889, kutshatiswe ngu Rev. J. B. Sakuba, u Petrus P. Sidzumo. unyana omkulu ka Philip Sidzumo wage Zeleni, no Eveline Ellen Mpololo, intombi eukulu ka James Mpololo wase Sauerville.

ABAZELWEYO.

KEBE.—E Katikati, ngomhla we 14 June, 1889, inkosikazi ka Mr. John P. Kebe, ibeleke Unyana.

ABABHUBHILEYO.

MAHLUTSHANA.—U Caleb Mahlutshana unyana omkulu ka Rev. Charles Mahlutshana. ubhubhele kwa Matole, ngo May 27, 1889. Zonke izihlobo mazamkele lowo mbiko.

LANDELLA.—U Eliza Landella, intombazana ka Isaac no Sarah Landella, ibhubhe nge Cawa, 9 .lune, 1889 ; yayizelwe ngo November 10, 1885.

SOMNGESI.—Ngomhla we 17th June, 1889, kubhubhe u Elisina Somngesi e Rini. Ubudala bake, 15 years. Abahlobo mabamkele lowo mbiko.

Izihlobo zomfikazi zibulela umsebenzi owenziwe ngu Mr. J. B. Magwaca ngexesha lobunzima.

GUZANA.—E Debe, ngo June 12, 1889, kubhubhe u Natise Guzana. Izihlobo mazamkele lowo m’panga. Usishiye ngoxolo. Ute “masisale sisilwa idabi lezono, yena ulilwile woyisa ngo Yesu.”

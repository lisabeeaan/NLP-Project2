UKUTOLIKA EMATYALENI.

NKOSI YAM, — Ndifuna ukukubulela ngamanqaku owenzileyo, nangokumana ;
ushukumisa kulendawo ilinxeba kuti bantsundu, yokungalungiselelwa kwetu
ngu Rulumente ukuba sifumane itoliki ezilungileyo.

Nditi lendawo ilinxeba, kuba kuti bantsundu (njengokuba kunjalo kumntu
wonke) kuyinto enzima kakulu ukuba ityala lako litetwe Iide ligwetywe,
kanti akuyivisisanga inteto. Kodwa ke nkosi yam mandibuze ukuba ityala
lelika banina ukuba kube njalo? Lityala le toliki na, nokuba lelika
Rulumente? Mna nditi lelika Rulumente. Kaucokise ukukangela kwi
Estimates of Ependiture, ukangele ukuba ndiyaposisana. Singaba siswele
ukutelekelela ukuba singalindela ukuba amadindala makabe nemfundo
engangaleyo ifunekayo kumntu omakabo yi toliki ematyaleni. Xa ke u
Rulumente angayilungiselelanga indleko yoku tolika kuzo zonke i ofisi
ibe ngumsebenzi omiyo ngokwawo — singalindela into elungileyo na?
Ngokukodwa kweli lipakati, kanti baninzi abantsundu, andikolwa ukuba
ungafumana nokuba yi town enye elungiselelweyo—ngapandle kokubekelwa i
allowance ye £6 or £12 ngomnyaka ngokutolikelwa, eti ngoko ibonakale
ukuba mayinikwe umntu olidindala onokutolikana nokuba kunjanina.
Singalindela i “qualities’’ na ke kumntu onjalo? Mna ndicinga ukuba
singazibulela ezo Constables, ngokuyenza lontwana zinokuyenza
ngalomidana. Kuzo zonke ezi town zeli lipakati ayiko iprovision
eyenzelwe ukutolikelwa kwetu ngapaya koku ndikuxelayo. Andimangaliswa
ngoko, ukuba u Rulumente azenze isitulu kwimpawu ozenzayo amaxesha
ngamaxesha, ngokuba ngoku ngalo system u Rulumente uhlaula £6 or £12
ngonyaka kwi district nganye, endaweni yokuhlaula £90 nangapezulwana
koko kwi district nganye—ngakumbi xa kuya kufuneka (njengokupaula kwako)
amadodana ane qualifications. Ngenyaniso ako, nangoku amadodana anjalo,
kodwa yiyipina enokuwutabata umsebenzi om- kulu kangaka ngemalana
engako—ie., £6 or £12 per annum. Mhleli otandekayo, ungaqonda
kwangalendawo yokuba’ i district ezitelwe provide, okunene zine toliki
ezilungileyo, ezinjengo Port Eliza­beth, East London, Kingwilliamstown,
Kimberley nezinye. Nakwezi town zeli lipakati ziko itoliki ezilungileyo
kanye esezibambelele ngenxa ye salary yobudindala, kuba ngapandle kwayo
azinakupila yilo allowance yoku tolika, ngoko ke (virtually) they are
only constables acting as interpreters. Singatinira ukuqala nge
examinations, kanti ayikenziwa indawo yetoliki kuzo zonke i district
ezinabantsundu at least? Nantso ke indawo ebangela ukuba u Rulumente
angabi nazo itoliki ezilungileyo kwi District Courts ezininzi.
Akungakuba itoliki ezinjalo aziko, nakukuba azinakufumaneka, kungokuba
itoliki elungileyo ayingenqwenele msebenzi wabudindala, kanti
azinakuwunikwa lomsebenzi nga­pandle kokuwubaudakanya nobu Con­stable.
Uxolo nkosi yam, ngokutabata ituba elingaka kungokuba, nawe Mhleli,
usuke watunuka enxebeni elidala, lokuba u Rulumente asilibale kangaka,
kanti nati silusapo lwake.—Owako,

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1881.

LITI ipepa lase Diamond ukulila emitshatweni kupelile ngezi mini. Into
ekoyo ngoku kukuncuma, ati no myeni angaviswa bubi ngu myenikazi
njengokungati ngumpangi ontliziyo ilukuni, ongxamele ukutshabalalisa
ikaya lomntu elimnandi. Ukuba kungaba sekunjalo kwa Xosa apa singaba
siqubele pambili, sibe namhla sinyanisile ukuti ukukanya kufikile kuti;
siwashiyile amasiko amadala akowetu ahamba nesililo mhla kwendiswayo,
esiti sifanele ngamanye amaxesha—kuba uti kanti lomntu uhlolelwe eli
soka; ize ke ukuba litandwe kowabo asiwe kulo ngapandle kwe ntando yake.
Esakaloku isililo yinkohla—asazeka; kuba aba bantu batandene;
behlanganisiwe nje namhla ngumfundisi, kunge mvume yaba zali babo, nabo
ngokwabo. Lendawo yikangeleni makowetu ukuba ilungilena. Kaloku ngumkwa
kuyo yonke into etshatayo, ukuba iti intombi engalilanga uve sekusitiwa,
“Lihlazo, intombi kanantsi niyayazi “ukuba ayililanga na, mhla yemka
kowayo? Lomntana “ubeseletenina kakadeni betu?” Siti ke tina kwezo nteto
zinjalo, Metsho! bazali batshoyo, lento ilungile na kubantu abati base
lukanyeni? Kunga luvuyo kwaba ninzi zite intombi ezi tshatayo
zazigcinela ixesha elifanelekileyo inyembezi zazo.

INTETO ka Mr. Garner Sojini siyifaka ukuze eve amadodana asamelwe kuzeka
iqinga omnye waba ntsundu abanika lona. Asinakuti akakatshati, utshatile
sinina u Gar­ner, kuba ukwazi impendulo yale mbuzo bekungasincedayo
ukwazi uhlobo lokunyamekela inteto yake. Make siti u Garner Sojini
yindoda endala esekumzuzu yatshatayo, yati nokutshata yatshata pambi
kokuba ukukanya kubeko kangaka. Ntonina ke? Siti tina into etetwa ngu
Mr. Sojini ayinakwenzeka. Iqina elinqabileyo leli. Kusakuti intombi
ifunde ide ifumane nezi qiniselo ezipakamileyo, ibe loluhlobo ateta iona
umbaleli wetu. Lonto yonke intle iyatandeka. Ze kuti kanti le ledi
ayizange ibasele nembiza ayizange yafunda noku gcina impahla zendlu;
ixesha layo lapelela ekufundeni i Grammar ne Geography nezi balo,
mhlayimbi nokubeta i piyane, ayabi nalo ixesha lokufunda ukupeka
nokulungiselela impahla zendoda. Zidla ngokuba njalo, sinosizi ukutsho,
intombi zakowetu ezifundileyo. Makangaposisi umf’undi asiti zizo zonke.
Kunjenjenje kwezifundileyo kanti ezingafundanga ziqeshwe kube Lungu,
zifunda ukupeka ozi konsi nozi payi nozi roli-poli nezinye ipacanga
zakwa Mlungu, zifunda kanjako ukupatwa kwendlu, nokulungisa impahla
zendoda, nokukulisa abantwana nezinto ezinjalo afanele ukuzazi umfazi we
verbeterend mensch— njengoko kutshiwoyo ngokwesi Bulu. Xa intombi
itshatileyo umsebenzi wayo kukupeka nokulungisa into zendlu,
imfundo—konayo iyatshonela. Indlu yomntu ofundileyo kufuneka ilungile,
nokudla kwake kufanelekile. Sesizibalile izinto ezaziwa zintombi
ezifundileyo, nezo zaziwa zezingafundanga, kautsho ke Sojini
mabangatshati nezazi umsebenzi na abafundileyo? Lenteto siyibala singena
xala kuba tina sesauwelayo umlambo.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

U SIR BARTLE FRERE, obeyi Ruluneli yale Koloni kuvakala ukuba usaza
kuhlala kweli okwe xeshana.

INDLEKO, ngokufa kwe mpahla ngenxa ye ngqele, kwi siqingata sase

INGQELE.—U Mr. Brown, ifama e Kubusi, ufelwe zinkabi ezi 30 negusha ezi
400 ngenxa yemvula ezinkulu ebeziko ngo August.

SIVA ukuba izinto ema Mpondweni azikati cwaka. U Siyoyo no Valelo bake
batana futu, ezi zoba zindaba ezimnadi kwabo batanda imfazwe.

U MR. JOHN X. MERRIMAN.—Elinene lingu Mpatiswa Mihlaba ne Micimbi ye
Koloni like lahambela kweli lasema Xoseni, ne Diamond loke liyifikelele.

U MR. WILLIAM KOYI nabafana abatatu base Blantyre Empakatini we Africa
babuyele kwe lozwe ngo 18 ka August; ngengxakeko ezaziwa nguye u Mr.
Mapassa Ntintili akavumanga ukubuyela.

IJAJI ZE KOLONI.—U Mr. Justice Jacob upumile ebujajini ngokungapili,
indawo yake inikwe u Mr. E. J. Buchanan obembambele oko ebese Ngilane u
Mr. Jacob. Sele se Rini emsebenzini wake u Mr. Buchanan

ITI i Cape Mercury, ipepa lase Qonce, uti u Rili ute xa yena neqela
labantu bake ebeseza kwi Lokeshoni anikwe yona kwa Gcaleka wabona ihambo
yemikosi ete yametusa, kwati kanjako kuba ute u Capt. Blyth ekeza kuye
waselo buya ngezitende kwango busuku obo.

IFANDESI.— Ngomhla we 8 ka September u Mr. Sheen wase Qonce
uyakutengisa, imazi zenkomo ezisengwayo ezi 80; inkabi ezi 40; izipani
ezi 2. Abafuyi boyiva ngovuyo lonto siyatemba.

IBRANDY KANJAKO.—Kule ndlela ka loliwe iya e Cradock kumandla wase
Somerset East bate ngomnye umhla abasebenzi batabata kakulu i “kanku,”
baza bamtabata omnye bamosa emlilweni.

UBUSELA.—Ama Jelimani amele umandla wase Monti abalele izipata mandla
exela ukubushuliswa kwawo ngama sela. Nento leyo idla ngokuba kufupi
nobusela—ukupanga—ininzi kwisiqingata eso sase Monti.

‘IRONASKAP.”—Intlanganiso yokubuta imali zeremente ebizwa ngeli gama
ibiko e Mkangiso ngo 14 ku August. Intshumayelo zahanjiswa ngu Rev. R.
Lamplough no Rev. E. J. Barrett, iminikelo igqitile kwe yomnyaka
odluleyo.

KWA ZULU.—I Natal Advertiser iti ezivela kwa Zulu ziti izinto zixubayele
kwelo, zisaya zixubayala kanjako, iyenye yezinto ezingenzekayo ukuba
inkatazo ezoyikekayo zibeko kwelozwe. Ama Zulu kutiwa afuna u Cetywayo
ekululwe, akangeke u Sikukuni akululwe yedwa.

OBULELE UMFAZI WAKE.—Into abati ngu Louis Calder ugwetyelwe amashumi
amabini eminyaka entolongweni yi Jaji yase Dayimani ngokusuka ikabe
umfazi wayo abube. Kwa kulo matyala u Jan Pamela ugwetywelwe iminyaka
elishumi ngokubulala u Jan Penjuku. Ezi zigwebo baninzi abati zilula.

INTONGA.—E Jagersfontein imidaka iqekezene intloko. Ngama Tshaka nabe
Sutu abenzi balento. Into ihla ekutini abe Sutu bahlekise ngama Tshaka
ngokuti nina ma Tshaka neyiswa ngama Ngesi, tina siweyisile. Aqumbile
ama Zulu, zadibana, kutiwa abetwa acela ko xongo ukubaleka kwawo ama
Zulu.

U MR. SAUL SOLOMON, M.L.A.—Ngo August elinene linduluke apa e Lovedale
lasinga e Qonce. Like latyelelela e Ncemera, nase Nxaruni, nase Mtwaku,
nase Monti xa belikona. Libuye e Qonce no Mr. Irvine oke wahlala
ixeshana. Unduluke nge 13 esinga e Bedford. Siqonda ukuba impilo ka Mr.
Solomon incedeke kakulu yihambo yake.

U LANGALIBALELE.—Kwi ngxelo yehambiso ze Parlamente ya pesheya sifumana
ukuba u Mr. George Palmer M.P. unike isaziso sokuba uyakubuza ku
Rulumente ya pesheya ukuba seyide yamisa ixeshana awoti akululwe ngalo u
Langalibalele, ngokwe dinga lika Lord Kimberley kwiqela elimele
ukutetelela abamnyama elikwa pesheya.

U CETYWAYO.—Amadoda e Parlamente apesheya amashumi asibozo aye kunye kwi
Rulumente ya pesheya enza isicelo sokuba likululwe elitshawe. U Right
Hon. W. E. Gladstone olipakati elikulu lo Mntan’ Omhle, ute kusatunyelwe
incwadi kwi Ruluneli u Sir H. Robinson ebuzayo ukuba kunokwenzeka na
ukuba akululwe u Cetywayo xa atembisayo ukuba akasokuya kwa Zulu.

I DAYIMAN KUFUPI NE COLESBERG.—Inene lase Kimberly (e Diamond Fields)
abati ngu Mr. Gibbon, liya xola kwi fama ekutiwa yi Andriesfontein
jkufupi ne Hanover esiqingateni sase Colesberg. Akakafumani nto noko
okwangoku, noko umhlaba ubonakala ukuba unganayo idayimani ukuba
uyatanda. Bati ngama kulu amabini anamashumi mahlanu i Idem (claims)
(abase Dayimani bolazi elilizwi) esezitengiwe kufupi nale fama.

IMFUNDO YABA NTSUNDU.—Iti i Cape Argus xa it eta ngengxelo ka Dr. Dale
kwi Parlamente nge micimbi yemfundo, “ Nokuba yintonina. engatetwayo
ngezinto ezabanga lemfazwe ipeleyo, umsebenzi we Rulumente ngakwaba
mnyama usele umi poqo, umhlope; amalinga aselenziwe emfundo, nawe ncwadi
nama shishini, nawokungenisa zonke izinto ezihamba kunye nobu kristu,
mawavuselelwe apinda-pindwe naku Mxosa naku Msutu.”

Kwi BOTWE LAPESHEYA.—Kwi Parlamente ya pesheya u Sir M. Hicks-Beach,
ungenise inteto yokugweba ubabalo lwe Rulumente ya pesheya ngakuma Bulu,
i Rulumente ikuselwe ngenteto enamandla ngumhlobo omkulu wentlanga
ezimdaka u Mr. Joseph Chamberlain ngobuciko obukulu. Amadoda awayeko e
Parlamente ngalomhla aye nga 519 isininzi esavumelana ne Rulumente
sagqita igqiza elaliyi gweba nge 109 lamadoda.

INYOKA YOLWANDLE.—Ipepa lase Kapa i Cape Times libalisa ukubonwa
kwenyoka yolwandle kufupi nonxweme lwalomhlaba. Isiqu sayo silingana
nesomntu onomzimba omkulu ubude zinyawo ezilishumi linesitandatu. Yinto
entloko inkulu. Ibonwe ngaba bese mkombeni obuno General L. Smyth, abate
bakutumele isikitshane kuyo yela emanzini ayabi sabonakala. Omatrose
abadala bati abazange bayi bone into enje ngeso silo ngapambili.

LOVEDALE.—Esi sikula sivulwe nge 1 ka August ukuba siqale umsebenzi we
nyaniso. Abafiki abatsha ngati bagqitile kuma 60, umlisela nomtinjana
ubandakanyiwe. Kuko inguqulo ebeko kubafundisi bododana; ngenxa
yokungapili u Rev. D. Doig Young akabanga nakupata igqiza lake namhla,
oze endaweni yake ngu Mr. C. J. Crawshaw. obese Mgwali wase Mbashe; u
Mr. P. S. Stewart ungene evenkileni yencwadi, kungene u Mr. A. Goodger
obese St. Andrews College, e Rini. Umfundisikazi we shishini kwese
ntombi ngu Miss Alexander ovela e Scotland.

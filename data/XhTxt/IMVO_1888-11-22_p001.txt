QONCE.

NKOSI MHLELI,—Ndibhekisa umbulelo, osuka ezantsi
emxhelweni, kwabo bate ngezitunzi nangevoti bandi-

beka kwisihlalo esibekekileyo sokuba ndibe ngosentloko kulom-
nyadala wevoti.

Elixa ndibulelayo ndilusizi ukuba imigudu eyenziweyo ukuba u Mr. SOLOMON
abe ngongeneyo naye ingapumelelanga ; kuba ngaleminyaka mibini
igqitileyo ute wakoleka wanyanisa ukusiququzelela isitili sase Qonce.

Owenu enyanisweni,

Kubanyuli Abantsundu bomandla wase Qonce, abanyule mna ngolugqatso
beluko:—

ZIHLOBO,—Ndinga, ngepepa le Mvo, ndingasingisa um-
bulelo osuka entliziyweni, ngenxa yohlobo enite, ngemi-

hlali nomoya okululekileyo, nandixhasa ngolunyulo lube luko
lwabameli Kwibandla lombuso.

Ndilusizi kunene ukuba imigudu yenu ingatanga igqi- belele ukupumeleli;
nokoke ngenxa yoncedo lwenu, ndibe noku- lwa kakuhle.

Ndivuya nani kuba nibe nokupumelelisa ilungu elinye lona, umhlobo wam u
Mr. WARREN, ondimazi kakuhle ukuba uyakuba ngumhlobo wenu wenyaniso,
njengokuba ebenjalo kwixesha eligqitileyo.

Nakubeni ndingekoyo kwi nkundla yombuso, ndiyakuhlala ndikuvuyele
ukuhambisela kwenu pambili, ndininceda ngama- tuba onke endinako.

Ndingokolekileyo wenu,

RICHARD SOLOMON.

Lilo eli:—

Kuko Venkile apa e King,

Kekalok’ impahla yayo

Ikup’ ezezinye everything,
Kumbulani Ivenkile leyo.

Umniniyo utiywe ngamakosikazi
Ngapandle komteketiso
Ngu Folokoco,—Manenekazi
Yizani ninqwenise iliso.

Zityal’ ezetu zaba Tshakazi,
Ngokwenyani zihleli zodwa,—
Amanene, namanenekazi
S’thrue, inteng’yetu iyodwa.

Zibhatyi, zihempe, neminqwazi,
Nebhulukwe zezisikiweyo,—
Zonk’ impahla zamaledikazi:
Ngenene zezifanelekileyo.

Siti kwaba batenga kade

Ninga dinwa zihlobo zetu

Kude kube ngu napakade
Ni-tenga kweya kowetu.

U-MATANZIMA NE NKOSAZANA.

KUMHLELI WE-Sigidimi.—Nkosi, ndiyavuya ukubona inteto yako kwi Sigidimi
so 1 ku March, ngendawo yokubuyiselwa komhlaba ongapesheya kwe Nciba
(e-Transkei) kwi Nkosazana. Ndingati ukukwenzela indaba kuko
abasebeyingenile lonto. U Matanzima i Nkosi yaba Tembu aba pesheya kwe
Ndwe, yena seleyibalile incwadi yake, wayitumela ku Mongamele we Micimbi
yaba Ntsundu ecela yona lento, okokuba abuyiselwe kwimpato ye Nkosazana,
kuba kwamhla wawela Indwe wawelela yona. Akwaba nabanye bangalandela
yena. Ndingowako,

MTEMBU.

IMPATO YAMA SATLANI.

Gaga, March 4, 1884.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI wendaba zamanene akowetu adumileyo. Kaundincede
undifakele lombuzo wam, ndiwubekise kwingwevu zakowetu, nakwi Nkosi
zakowetu ezintsundu. Ndingavuya ukuba kuko umntu oyaziyo imini
yentlanganiso yokunikelwa kwetu kuma Satlani, nesiposo setu esenza sona
ukuze siposwe eweni nje ngu Mntan’ Omhle inkosazana? Kwakutenina ukuze
uma asipose eweni nje? Ndifuna ukwazi ngalondawo yodwa, nokuba saliwa,
nokuba sehliswa ngesiposo esitile ku Mntan’ Omhle Inkosazana. Mazeke
niyi kangele kwakule nyanga, kuba kulusizi kulendawo usapo Iwakowetu
lukuyo iyankedamisa, kuba ziyakumbuleka imini zangapambili ze Nkosazana
u Mhlekazi u Queen Victoria, umgcini wenkedama. Mandime apo ngeli liti:
“ Kumbula apo uwe kona, uvuke wenze eyokuqala imisebenzi.”

  PATULENI KAKAZA.

UMHEBEBE ONTSUNDU NE HANGU.

Impukane, February 20, 1884.

NKOSI YAM ETANDEKAYO.—Ndiyatemba ukuba imbuzo zika Mr. J. M. V. um
Hebere zibekiswe kuye wonke ubani onokupendula. Malunga nemibuzo yako
Mr. J. M. V. uze undixolele ngokungawupenduli owokuqala, ndifuna ukuke
ndilinge kowesibini, woti ukuba uqondile ndingene nakulo ukuba amanene
akakuqondisi. Ndingavuya uncede ngalo mbuzo—1. (a) Kanene mhla nanikwa
umteto wokwaluka nina ma Hebere nanikwa nayipi imiteto ? (b) Kwatiwa
zenitini ukwaluka? Ungalibali ukundipendula ngokusencwadini. 2. Malunga
nehangu mna kokwam ukubona nditi yakululwa ngale mini:—Izenzo x. 15—

Kwabuya kweza ilizwi kuye ngokwesibini Haiti: oko akuhlanzileyo u Tixo
ungakwenzi okungengcwele wena.” Lemini ingqinwa zezi ndawo:—Mateyu xv.
11—“ Oko kungenayo emlonyeni akungcolisi mntu, oko kupumayo emlonyeni
kuko okumngcolisayo umntu.” Woti ukuba awukaqondi Mr. J. M. V. utyile
ezi—Amaroma xiv. 14, 17, 20i Amakorinte okuqala x. 25. Eyokuqala ka
Timote iv. 4, 5 naku Titus, i. 15. Ndiyitya ngezindawo ke mna ihangu.
Make ndiyeke nkosi yam ndoda ndiposise.

  JOEL J. MADUBELA.

U MR. A. BELL NOMBALI WE “ MPAWANA.”

  Dulcis Nek, Herschel, 20 March, 1884.

NKOSI MHLELI WE Sigidimi,—Ndivumele ndiyacelwa ukuba ndipendule ngento
endiyenze ngemvo zam nezabanye abantu, kanye ngokungati ndinetyala
ngemvo zam. Bendingazi ukuba kanti kunyulwa nje abantu baba netyala
ngemvo zabo. Ke ndibuzwa izizatu zokushiya inkosi u Sauer zinkosi or
yinkosi endingayaziyo, ndibe ndingavuyayo ukuba amagama ala matshawe
bendingawabonayo epepeni apa, bendiya kukwazi ukuwapendula amanene lawo.
Asifumananga samnyula u Mr. Tamplin kungezizatu ezininzi. Salahlekelwa
kakulu zinkomo zetu, yati inkosi xa ivela e Lusutu yenza intlanganiso
enkulu e Herschel, sabuza ngenkomo zetu ezibiwe ngabe Sutu yati inkosi u
Sauer apo sizifumana kona kube Sutu masize sizitabate, sesuka saya kube
Sutu sazifumana, bala abe Sutu nazo, sabuya seza kubika ema- ntyini, ite
inkosi u Sauer yaya e Lusutu zahamba izibonda zakuti ezino Mr. Platyi
Eland zafika zamxelela u Sir Sauer. Namhla wababuza umbuzo onqabileyo
wati abe Sutu bazitabata niyengapina ? Bati uyazi nkosi ukuba tina
singamarulumente asivunyelwe ukuba sibe. Watembisa ke ukuba siza
kuzifumana ziza kuvela e Cape Town, nanamhla asikazifumani. Abe Sutu
bawabuyisela ke ama Mfengu ase Gqili aba Tembu ababuyiselwanga
kwabonakala ke ukuba abanamteteleli. Abantu abalilayo bangapezulu kude
kumashumi asixenxe la wena mb'ali uteta ngawo. Siti tina Mamfengu
besingalwanga nabe Sutu bekungafanele ukuba silahlekelwe kangaka, ukuba
lemfazwe ibivele ngenxa yetu ngesingateti nento ngokulahlekelwa kwetu.
Ute ke u Mr. Tamplin akufika kuti sambuza sati ufuna ukuba sikunyule uze
ulwenzele ntonina uhlanga oluntsundu ? Utembisile ngezinto ezibete
zaqabula imixelo ke. Sibuzile kwizihlobo zetu ezingabafundisi abamhlope,
bate ke kulungile ukuba simnyule u Mr. Sauer no Mr. Tamplin. Ndibe
ndingenako ke ukulahla icebo labafundisi bam. E Bensonvale esikolweni
mhla ngolwe 18 ku February ndandiko, inteto yam yayiyile ndandisiti
qasha u Mr. Sauer no Mr. Tamplin. Ndingati ke siyivile ngokuyicaza kwake
inteto yerafu yencaw a wayeteta kumanxila asezidolopini alala
ezinkantini. Zininzi izizatu endingaziteta kulenteto ezitetwa siti
Mamfengu. Ngokuke kuti lento ipelile sixolile. Ndingati ndiyapela
zihlobo zam ezintsundu. ndingu

AUGUSTUS BELL.

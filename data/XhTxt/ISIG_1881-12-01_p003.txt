ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1881. 3

NGO NOV., 5, ngumhla ekutiwa ngowoku kumbuza i Guy Fawkes. Kute e Graaff
Reinet xa bekudlalwa wafika u Stoffel i kostauli eli mnyama, wabamba
intwana yomlungu, wayi beta eyabi dlala ngentwana apa ezi dubulayo, zaba
ntwana. Aku bonanga ngani:—likuyekile konke ebeli kwenza ibandla,
laputuma ku Stofile, okwa likumsha e Ofisini yase Graaff Reinet,
lamtantamisa esi bakabakeni lamsa e tolongweni lafunga ukuba kuko
owamkulula lomfumana. Imantyi imdle i £1, u Stofile, ngoku beta lo
mlungwana. Kazi kube njanina kuye ukungena kule ndawo abe mana ejulela
kuyo abanye.

KWE lanamhla ipepa kofunyanwa incwadi ka “Votanumlungu,” eteta ngo
Ntsundu e Parlamente. Kulo ncwadi lombali kubonakala ukuba uzama
ukukanyeza inteto yetu, ati sayenza ngalandawo, sati siyifumene kwi
Eastern Star, eti kuvakala ukuba u Mr. Wauchope uza kungeniswa e
Parlamente yimpi entsundu yase Uitenhage. Woti 11 “Votanumlungu” akuzi
sula intsimbi akangela ngazo xa afundayo, afumane ukuba. ngenxa yenteto
yetu besingamelwe kukude sikululelwe ibatyi, xa sibetwayo, kuba into
esayitetayo ngokwetu kweloxa yabe iyeyo kuncoma ukukanya asebe nako
abantu base Tinara xa ugaba sebe nokubona ukuba ilungelo yabo
inokubavumela ukuba batyumbe nokuba ngubani, bamngenise e Parlamente.
Siya vumelana kanye no “Votanumlungu.” Into kodwa esiyo yikayo yeyokuba
zinteto ezinje ngale yake ezibanga ukuba abantu balale. Tina sikolwa
kwinteto ezibeta abantu bavuke; ityala liwa pezu ko “Votanumlungu” aba,
kuba noko sebe nokukanya nje abazami kubonisa abantu. Umbulelo wombaleli
wetu ngemizamo ye Sigidimi siwamkela ngeratshana elitile.

PAKATI kwaba ntsundu abafundileyo kuko into eninzi ezitanda yodwa, eti
ukuba iyadla iya sela, ingabi sakatala lundunkunku lwaba mnyama
abatshabalala zinto ezininzi. Ihambiso yaba bantu, ayifumanela igama
elifanelekileyo ama Ngesi: ati, ihambiso yabo inje ngeyabo bati, “You
let us alone and we’ll let you alone. Wonke onobu copo obucingayo
entloko wobetwa lusizi ngenxa yoku. Metsho! Metsho! madodana afundileyo
niya yivana lento itetwa lipepa lase Middelburg, Middelburg Gazette?
Liti, kulo dolopu “seku licebetshu ukufumana umntu onge nxila. Isongo se
brandy, nge minyakana embalwa edlulileyo, besiviwa ngama Lawu kakulu.
Ngoku sifunyenwe ngama Xosa, nama Mfengu, na Besutu. Isigwebo sokuba
atshabalale omnyama esihambisekayo ngoku pakati kwa ma India e North
America, sipatelele ngoku e South Africa. “Amanz’ umlilo” amagwangqa
kubonakala ukuba ayaku kufeza oko izifo, izibulawo, nendlala zinga
kufezanga—uku buba komntu omnyama Ukuza kweli shwangusha, kunga ngati
kuya ziziliza kanti kunyanisekile ukuba kuyeza.” Au! Yemka ingcambu ka
Xosa, neyo Mbo, neyo Msutu, bubuti bom Lungu!!!

NGOMNYE umhla saka sabalisa ngolovane olwapants’ ukwenzakalisa abantu.
Into esayi fundayo kwelo bali yeyokuba, oku kuxomekelela, kuguqulwa,
kulahlwa kwama gama, akulungile, kulihlazo kwabo bakwenzayo—ukuba into
elihlazo basayazi. Lento asikukupela kwesikalazo esinga tetwayo ngama
gama abatile babamnyama. Kuko into edanisayo, xa singa funi kuyibiza
ngegama elinzima ngapezulu. Lonto yile yokuba umntu ali cekece igama
loyis’ emkulu lokuvela kwake, ze atande ebizwe ngelase Mlungwini.
Masiti, nanku u George, umzukulwana ka Mtlokotli ngokwa bantsundu,
owanikwa igama lokuba ngu John ngama Ngesi; woyika ukuti ungu George
Mtlokotli, usuke ati kuma Ngesi nakubo bonke ungu George John. Yinto
eninzi yabo benza lonto. Kuti ngati ayi lunganga, ifike umntu omnyama
imenze umntu ongati li basitile, mhlaimbi umntwana weqetsu. Masiyeke
apo, hleze sinyatele amazwane oninzi; into leyo noko ngebe yikangela
abantu bakowetu bayiyeke. Liratshi elikulu ukuba umntu abambelele
eluhlangeni lwakowabo, angabi nantloni ngalo. “Nokuba igama lombali wezi
“Mpawana” bese lingu Gxitolwana Mjakuja Msingomba, napakade ukuba azenze
u R. T. Joseph kuba ebaleka eli lokuqala.

UMSEBENZI wabafundisi e Nyassa uyaqala ukuhloba. Ngo 27 ka March
odluleyo u Dr. Laws wabaptiza u Albert Namalambe. Waba ngumhla omkulu
lowo e Livingstonia. Ezinye ibaptizo emva kolu zezo sazibika kwi
Sigidimi.—Izibalo ezi Ngcwele ziya guqulelwa kwinteto yabantu belozwe
sibala nje ezo cebano olutsha ziya shicilelwa apa e Lovedale, ziguqulwe
ngu Dr. Laws. E Ngilane intliziyo ziya vuta ngalo msebenzi mkulu woku
zikanyeza kwama kolwa. Ipetshana ekutiwa yi Africa, elaqalwa ngulowo
ongasoze alityalwe u Major Malan lenjenje ukubala ngenyanga u October,
nakuba kwe zinye indawo kofunyanwa ukuba lise nkungwmi. “U Rev. Dr.
“Hammington, umfundisi okwa ligqira, no Mrs. Hammington, “sebekwele
emkombeni osinga e Quilamane. U Rev. M. Zimba, [Rev. P. J. Mzimba]
umfundisi wentlanganiso enkulu “ entsundu yase Lovedale, enamakulu
abantu uzinikele ukuba “ aye e Nyassa ; umongameli weshishini loku kanda
e Lovedale u Mr. Blane [Mr. John Mbane,] uza kulandela kamsinya “ ipile
inkosikazi yake; kusuke aba vangeli ababini e Lovedale besinga kwa
kwelozwe.” Tina balapa, siyazi ukuba zinyaniseke kangakananina ezindaba
zinge Lovedale. Kodwa zonke ezizinto zibonisa umoya okoyo pesheya ngaba
sebunyameni pakati kwe Africa.

NGO 9 September lo udlulileyo, kwafika apa e-Lovedale u James Sivewright
Esq. General Manager of Telegraphs, oko kukuti Umongameli wemicimbi yo
cingo apa e South Africa. Ute akufika apa wane ngxoxo no Rev. Dr.
Stewart, ngokuti makamfunele udodana lufike kwi 16, ukuba
lunokufumaneka, ukuba luze kwenziwa abahambisi bencwadi zo cingo kula
madolopu—East London abe mabini (2), e King William’s Town abe mane (4),
e Port Elizabeth abe matandatu (6). Intlaulo yoba ziponti ezine nge
nyanga, nengubo ezinje ngozibatyi, ne bulukwe, nomqwazi nezi hlangu, ne
ntsekelezane, ne mekentoshe. Xa bahambayo, ngo tyelelo, indleko zabo
zohlaulwa. Ezi zi gidimi zo cingo zifunwa zingenele iminyaka emibini,
ekoti yakupela, zisahleli emsebenzini, zifumane £5, kunye nezi zinto.
Abase Monti bafunwa kwangoku. Ezi nyaniso zibekiwe pambi kododana lwako
wetu olu lapa futi-futi, namnye oke wavuma. Kude entlanganisweni eyabiko
ngo 22, Nov., kwenziwa intwaki ku nqulwa umlisela, ukuba wamkele eli
cam; awak’ utsho. Wobona umzi wakowetu amatasamqa alahlwa ludodana.
Lento iba danisile abafundisi, kuba besiti u Rulumente ube qala
ukuwavula amatuba apezulu kumntu ontsundu, nanku ke yena ewakaba—enje
ngehashe eli tyebileyo. Siyatemba umzi wako wetu woyilwa lento.
Litamsanqa eli ngehli futi eli: malumke ke amadodana. O, santyuntya.
Make sime apa.

INTO eke ya vusa ingxoxo kule nyanga u November kuma pepa ama Ngesi,
yeyokuba ama doda e Government “epambene,” kutsho intshaba ze Government
njalo. Ipepa eli bone eli qinga lokwahlula ama doda alaulayo yi Cape
Times yase Cape Town. Isigqibo sayo ifikelela kuso ngolu hlobo. Kuko
intlanganiso ezimbini zama Bulu, ati wona zezo kwalusa imicimbi yawo
kwindawo ezinje ngozi Parlamente. Enye—nendala—yi Afrikander Bond, enye
ati ukuyi biza kwawo yi Boerenbeschermingsvereeniging. Eyo kuqala
izamela ukuqweba uhlanga olutsha kuma Bulu nakuma Ngesi, ekotiwa lolwase
South Africa, ze igama lalo libe zi Afrikanders; eye sibini yeyokuvusa
ifama zama Bulu ukuba zibone ama lungelo azo abekiselele ekunyuleni
amalungu e Parlamente nezinto ezinjalo. Eyo kuqala kwakona yaqalwa e
Free State; eyesi bini iqale kwi ziqingata ze Koloni ezinga pakati, yabe
iciko layo elikulu ingu Hon. J. H. Hofmeyr okwa lilungu le Government. U
Hon. J. X. Merriman ufumene ituba lokuyi gweba eyokuqala e
Graaff-Reinet. Liti ke ipepa lase Cape Town, elibutshaba kwi Government
le intsha, lama bandla oma bini anto nye; xa u Mr. Hofmeyr atetelela
elinye, utetelela omabini; xa u Mr. Merriman agxeka elinye, ugxeka oma
bini lisuka ke eli pepa liti aba lauli bahlukene; makunyulwe enye i
Govern­ment. Kwaba mnyama lento siyazi kotiwa ayinto yanto, siyi balisa
kuba iyinto ebange ingxolo enkulu pakati kwaba zalwana betu aba gwangqa,
inyaniso tina yeyokuba yonke lento itetwa yi Cape Times kuku bibidla. I
Government ka Mr. Scanlen ayikahlukani, akuka biko ngxoxo inkulu
inokubanga ukuba ku guqulwe aba lauli. Ningotuki ke mawetu; sonazisa
ngento ezihlayo.

AKUKO bantu noko ke betu bane pike—bakuhamba kufana nento apa imehlo
aminxeba ingu nonkala—njengaba bantu babinqa ilokwe abamnyama. Akukanye
kusitiwa mabangaqeli ukuhamba ubala bodwa, kuko nezi hange, kube ngati
kuya hlatyelwa. Abangase Fort Beaufort baya wazi ama benubentsu ka
Pembeshiya. U Dume isigebenga esitsha, abalisa ngaso u Mr. Kivit
Swaartbooy wase Gxulu, ngati ugqitile naku Pembeshiya. Ukuba oti afunde
amankazana, la abaliselwa Jama bali, koba kwanele. “Kuko umfana,”
usibalela ngolu hlobo u Mr. Kivit Swaartbooy, “kutiwa ngu Dume’ Edebe
ute, ngamhla utile wahlangana nantombazana itile pakati ko Qoboqobo no
Mtwaku, endleleni yenqwelo walinga ukuti uyayi dlwengula, koko
wahlangana nembambalala yentombazana, waqala kusasa kwada kwase mini,
kwada kwafika abalamli abalamla; wabaleka umnene wati gxwa emlanjeni
wanyubela ngomlambo, wati esabaleka njalo wahlangana nentombi ye Jamane,
wati molo ntombi waposeleka. Yeka ke! kwa lityikityiki apo kaloku
wayimosa, ngangokuba siteta nje kaloku intombi leyo ise gqireni e Qonce,
ayibanga namandla nje ngeyokuqala, yati yabaleka yawuhlaba ekaya, esi
Jamaneni, bati be badula ukufuna emlanjeni, wati radu waba kulomlambo
kutiwa li Tshoxa ngakwa Qoboqobo. Nqwakanqwa nenye intokazi apo, isinga
e Rabula, kwa enye intombi, wahamba encokola nayo ingazelelento,
wayivukela umnene wayijika endleleni. Zwaba kwelimnyama ihlati, lanquma
inqata kwintombazana ayibanga senza migudu leyo intombazana, isuke
yavuma intando yake, iqondile nayo ukuba kukufa kodwa namhlanje. Umfo
omkulu ubuze wati, uyakuxela? Ite yona hayi, ite ibeka yona kowayo waye
yona ebuya, esiza kwakwa Qoboqobo. Kute kuba bekufunwa kakade, kanti
uzisa entanjeni. Ute ematyaleni, ndinetyala Mantyi, ngentombi ye Jamane,
kodwa andinatyala Mantyi ngale ntombi yomntu omnyama yayisiya e Rabula,
ngokuba besivumene. Nentombi ite unyanisile, ngokoyika ndiqondile ukuba
ndiya kufa. Wagcinwa etolongweni ihalafu ye veki wabaleka e poliseni;
usemahlatini kaloku siteta nje. Lumkani! mankazana ukuhamba nodwa,
zininzi izivukavuka ezinje. Bekusand’ukusinde inkazana apa ibihamba
ngoratyazo, yahlangana nabafana ababomvu bebatatu. yasinda ngokusuka ati
omnye wabo, bafondini lityala eli.—Ndim KIVIT SWAART­BOOY.—Egxulu, Oct.
II, 1881.”

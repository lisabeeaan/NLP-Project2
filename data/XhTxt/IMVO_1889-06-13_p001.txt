Bonisani Madoda.

IHASHE eli bomvu lilahleka emntwini e Qonce, emagqubeni. Litiwe cipu indlebe yokohlo, upawana ngase mva; line nxeba pezu komkono wokohlo, nokudumbana pezu kopupu lomkono wokohlo, ne ntsumpa ngapakati komlenze wokohlo. Olifumeneyo eli hashe malise ku Mr. Editor, we Mvo, e Qonce, mhlaumbi ku Nkani, e Toleni, pesheyakwenciba; londoda youfumana umvuzo wayo.

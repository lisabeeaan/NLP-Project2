Vol. VI.

LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, FEBRUARY 1, 1876.

No. 65

Isigidimi samaxosa

U-REV. WILLIAM GOVAN.

ILIZWI lika Tixo lafika intlanga zalendawo zinqula iminyanya yakowazo,
zisondeza amadini kuyo, zitembe amatola namagqira atwase ngendlovu
nengonyama nezingwe. Lafika incwadi ingento yaziwayo, indaba zisenziwa
ngomlomo, imbali zisaziwa ngabahlala namaxego. Lafika ukusebenza iyinto
engekoyo kusanelwa ukuti kwaluswe inkomo kulinywe indinyana ezinyeleni
zamahlati ngamaceba omtati.

Kwafuneka ke ukuba kuti ukuhanjiswa kwelilizwi losindiso kuzekwe ezantsi
ngenxa zonke. Ukuze abantu babe nokuziqonda ezindaba zilungileyo
baziziselweyo, kwafuneka ukuba baziswe into ayiyo u-Tixo,nemisebenzi
yake, nemiteto yake; baziswe into abayiyo bona kuye, nokuzitshabalalisa
kwabo ngesono. Ukuze Izibalo zake ezinezondaba bazazele ngokwabo,
bazixelele abantwana babo, bazihambisele nakwezinye intlanga, kwafuneka
ukuba kungeniswe imfundo. Ukuze bati bekwenza oko babe benezixaso
ezifanelekileyo, kwafuneka ukuba baziswe imisebenzi.

Bangena ke abafundisi kwezondlela zonke. Bati, belishumayela Ilizwi,
eyona nto babeze kwenza yona, baba bemisa izikula, beb'ala incwadi,
beguqula Izibalo, besebenza nangezandla zabo ukuze abantu babonele kubo.

Lati lakubon’ ukuba liyakwenza oko konke elicala labafundisi bama Skote
kutiwa yi Free Church ngoku, laceba iqinga lalendlu ise Dikeni. Lati
masake indlu eyakuvelisa uluhlu olufundileyokwezintlanga, ukuze kupume
kulo abafundisi bolutsha nabashumayeli Belizwi kude kuye kubako nabalusi
bemihlambi ka Kristu nabasi bendaba zake kwamanye amacala. Yakiwe ke
londlu: kwati pesheya kwakutshwa lendoda ingu Govan ukuba ize kuhambisa
lomsebenzi. Yavulwa nge 1841, lomnyaka ingqakaqa yanyatya Amantinde.
Zangena intlanga zonke, wabako Umlungu, no Mxosa, ne Qeya, ne Mfengu no
Msutu. Zafundela ndawonye zonke lwati oluntsundu lwema nolumhlope, lwati
olwenkosi unxulumanano lwemidaka. Yafundisa yanyameka wacokisa lendoda;
yabeta wanyameka wacokisa nawe ufundiswayo. Kusasa kwafundwa into yonke
elunge nelizwi li­

ka Tixo; emva kwesitonga kwafundwa inteto yamangesi neyama Latin,
kwahlakazwa konke ukwazi kwase Mlungwini okubonakalayo ukuba
kungalunceda uhlanga; emva kwe dinari kwangena obe fundiswa Isigrike; xa
limkayo, kwayiwa emasimini kwafundwa ukulima ngezandla, lendoda nayo
ngokwayo iwunqika umhlaba iwuqoba kunene ucoleke ulungelelane kakuhle;
ngokuhlwa yafundisa izicaka, yakangela nokuquba kwamadodana. Ifundisa
nje ke, iyabafundisisa bonke abanokwazana ukuze umsebenzi uhambe. Kodwa
ke mhla yavulwayo londlu laba lincinane iqela elangenayo, kuba oko lento
imfundo ibingaziwa ukuba ihlelinje ingaba yinto eyinto pakati
kwezintlanga zalapa. Panel’ ukuba amashumi abamabini anabantu bane
ngabaza ngemini.

Kwati kwakubon’ukuba kuminyaka mihlanu kufundwa kulendlu, yafika
imfazweyezembe (nge 1846) yawacitacita lamadodana kuba yati into yonke
engumfundisi kwelilizwe yasaba yaya emlungwini. Yawela yaya kwapesheya
kolwandle lendoda ibiyipatisiwe londlu. Yawela nabantwana abatatu
babafundisi noka Soga ongu Tiyo. Liyaxola ngaleyo yesixengxe, sebeko
nabo abafundisi. Bayihlaziya lendlu yabo, bangenisa amadodana. Wabuya
pesheya nalowa wayeyipatisiwe. Kute kungekabiko nomzuzwana lo, yafika
eka Mlanjeni yayicita lemfundo; kuba emkayo amadodana, noko bangasabanga
namhla abafundisi e-Dikeni; kwati kodwa lingekaxoli, yabuya yavulwa
lendlu, wayihambisa u-Govan imfundiso, enomfundisi owafika ngalomfazwe
esiza kwakuyo. Babako baligqiza abafundi, kuba kwati ngalomfazwe
kwenziwa ilali kwizikola ezinjengesase Dikeni nesase Qonce nesase
Bofula, basabela kuzo abezinye izikola, lashunyayelwa Ilizwi lwafunda
usapo. Lakuxola yaqiniselwa ngokunye imfundo, landa igqiza lamadodana
kulondlu.

Kwati, ngalomnyaka zafa kunene inkomo yimofu isandukufika (nge 1855),
kweza kwelizwe u-Rulumente owayekunxamele kakulu ukuba imfundo ihambe
kuzo zonke intlanga azipatisiweyo. Siteta u-Sir George Grey. Wabonakala
egaleleka e-Dikeni, wati kubafundisi, ndifuna ukuba nihlomele
kulemfundiso yenu eyemisebenzi, ndoninceda ngezinto ezifunekayo. Watsho
wakupa imihlaba, wageqa imali. Kwahlonyelwa ke amashishini, okucwela
imiti, okukanda, okwenza inqwelo, nawokwaka izindlu. Yandiswa

kakulu lendlu yase Dikeni. Angena kunene amadodana avela ngenxa zonke.
Kwakubon’ ukuba kunjalo kwafika u-Nongqause kwanqaba ngokuncamisileyo
ukudla, kwati kodwa kwako impumelelo kulondlu, ngokunceda kuka Rulumente
lowo.

Umsebenzi wokufundisa wabapakamela ababafundisi babini kwacelwa omnye
pesheya kolwandle, wafika kanye ngexesha lamatamba (1857). Yashushu
okunye imfundo. Kwakubon’ ukuba ihamba ngolohlobo, kwafika ilizwi eliti
incipile imali yemfundiso ku Rulumente, akasenako ukufunzela into yonke
engenayo kulendlu, unokunceda igqizana elincinane. Kwatiwa, kutiwani?
Kwakuxoxwa, kwabonwa elokuti abamanye amacala mabarole imali ukuze
bangene; bati nabelicala lakulolondlu barole nabo bakugqita kumashumi
amatatu inani labo. Laqekeka ke iqela elikulu lemka alabisabuya
ngokuswela imali; babako kodwa abayirolayo

Kwati ngonyaka we 1861, kwahlonyelwa ishishini lokushicilela; wati
lomfundisi ubefike ngeka Mlanjeni wanyotulwa ekufundiseni wafakwa
kowokubala incwadi nowokuguqula; kwavela omnye pesheya wafakwa endaweni
yalowo. Kwaya kwaya, wapuma obefike ngonongqa use, kwafunwa omnye
kwapesheya. Wati lowa uvele pesheya ngomnyaka woshicilelo (nge 1861),
wagoduka lakupela ixesha abeqeshelwe lona, iminyaka emitatu, kwafakwa
owelilizwe endaweni yake. Yabe kekaloku lendoda ingu Govan iyongameleyo
imisebenzi yonke yalendlu, isiva ukuba amendu ayo kaloku akase
ngawobutsha. Kwafika ke indoda eyakutabata indawo yake; wakululwa yena
kowokufundisa wanikwa owokuhamba ekangela izikula. Ute kodwa ngokuwuqela
lowokufundisa akahlukana nawo kupele. Kwahlonyelwa nendlu yokufundisa
intombi. Kwati kunjalo wabona ukuba kumfanele ukuba agoduke. Wawelake
waya kwelakowabo, kwakubon’ ukuba ikumashumi matatu iminyaka efikile
kwelilizwe.

Sibala nje kufike umbiko ovela pesheya wokuba ibubile londoda. Sisave
umbiko lowo, siyazi kodwa ukuba babeko o-Tshalisi nomfana ka Weir
ekuncwabeni. Eyitabatile nje ke u-Tixo londoda yahlahla indlela yemfundo
pakati kwetu, kusifanele ukuba sibe nendawo esizikangelayo esizicingayo.

Yabiyindoda yamadoda. Ingqondo yabi-

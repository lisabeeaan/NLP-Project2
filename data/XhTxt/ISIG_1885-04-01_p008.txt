ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1885.

INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

IZIGEBENGA ZABA FUNDI.

Clarkbury Institution, March 16, 1885.

MR. EDITOR,—Kawundifakele lemigcana embalwa kweso Sigidimi samanene.
Kute ngomhla. we 12 ngokuhlwa emva ko 8 o’clock, omnye wo titshala xa
agodukayo esiya endlwini yake, kanti mntu ndini selelalelwe ngamatye,
weva sekusitsho isipango samatye pezu kwake ; wanga anganyamezela
kwabonakala ukuba uyafa ngamatye, waguqula naye walwa, wabagxota.
Cebetshu ukumkumla amazinyo ngembokotwe, kodwa ngofefe Iuka Tixo
wasinda. Isitatu soku nasi—lo titshala kutiwa uqinisa imiteto, uyenza
nzima, akabavumeli ukuba baye e steshonini, nase mitshatweni ngokuhlwa.
Lakangelwa ko elotyala, kwabonakala ukuba u William Nkoko, Petros
Siyongwane, John Mlalandle, Charles Tukwayo, Samuel Madubela mabagxotwe
kulomzi.

“AKUKETI KUFA!”

YATSHO imbongi eyacinga amazwi eliculo lama 212. “ Uyaketa

Kufa! ”—Litshilo igqobokakazi elidala lase Bayi, ndakulibikela ukuba
kufike ucingo luvela e Tinara, lubika ukuba u Emily, intombi ka Bangani,
ububile e Tinara ngentsasa yomhla wa 23 ku February. “ Uyaketa
Kufa—uwupumele nganina umzi womfo ka Ntetana.” Ndite nqumama ndacinga.
Ndikumbule umhla otile malunga nenyanga engu December, 1870, ekwangena
esikuleni sam intokazi ka Puru, umka Mxoli. Ndite yini dade upuma pi
usiya pi? Ute, “ Hayi ndikuzisele umtwalo.” Upi lomtwalo? “ Nangu
pandle.” Kanti ngu mnakwabo u Zani Puru uyafa—ngoku kumasikizi.
Situnedzile sasa ekaya. Ekufeni kuka December l879 ubuye wabuyela e
Bayi, wesuswa wabekiswa ngecala lase kaya, wapelela e Rini ngo February
1880, apo incwaba lake likona. Sisalila e Tinara ufikile kanjako u Mrs.
Ntetana. Yini namhla? “ Hayi namhla ndize nomnye umtwalo kuwe Cizama.
yindoda yam, yalala kwasemva kokububa komnakwetu.” Sabutyabutya songa,
yabinqa omfutshane intombi ka John Lukalo ukonga sabona usifa umnyaka we
1880. Kute ngamhla utile ngexa ekukubi seva umntu ofayo esiti,—ndikupeni
pandle ndifuna ukubona ilanga, wakutshwa. Uwapose elangeni amehlo,
wavakala ememeza ngento enkulu ilizwi esiti,—“ Ndakubona langa
bendingazi ukuba ndobuye ndikubone—ndingeniseni.” Wangeniswa, basana
ngamehlo abantu—elapa unyana wake u Makisi, elapa oka Baso u Hendili,
elapa oka Keti u Luke, elapa umfazi wakowetu. Azibanga ntatu intsuku,
wanduluka (1881). Kwamalunga nelo xesha kufike indaba zokububa kwexego u
Puru e Xesi. Lungekapumi ezileni usapo kuhle kwabonakala ukuba u Makisi
unyana omkulu ka Bangani akapilile, usinge naye e Tinara, unina wawa
ngapa nangapa, hayi sahambela pambili isifo. Kuqalile ukuvela inkalo
ezoyikekayo kuba namhla lo ulele pantsi ngu yise wenkedama—wabuba ngo 22
October, 1883.

“ Akunalusizi kufa!

  Akunako ukucengwa, Bubuninzi benyembezi, Akuna kupazanyiswa! ”

Ute akuwati cimi amehlo u Makisi, walila ngombuzo onje unina ku Tixo
wake:—“ Yini Somandla! yinene uyandihluta lentwana yam, ndenzeni?
ndosala nabani? ” Esalila kufike incwadi exela ukuba u Nqishinga,
intombi yake eza mva ko Makisi, ebiseyi nexesha isifa, ibambeke kakulu e
Bayi. Uyitabatile wayisa e Tinara. Ndiyile nge Cawa (5 October 1884),
ndafika intombi ka Bangani isuke yalubelu inzwakazi ebuso bungena xala.
Ite kwa ndisangena—“ Ufikile ompi? umpefumlo wam wonwabile ngoku. Ndinje
ke ompi, bendingazi kodwa ukuba esi sifo siza kumka nam, kodwa uma wati
kum ngenye imini ‘ Nqishinga! uyazi kodwa ukuba uyafa—ufa kakulu ke,
zilungi- sele indlela yako ulincame eli lizwe.’ Ndotuka kakulu ompi.
ndaqonda ukuba uma kuko into ayibonayo. Ndaqala ukuzamana no Msindisi
wam. Uqalile u Satana wandinxamela ngokuti, mawo lixesha eli lokuba
ufune u Tixo, kanti wazalelwu pantsi kwezwi lake ongazange ulamkele?
watsho u Satana ndafun’ ukuncama. Sizamene sada sashiyana ndakulila
ngokuti Yesu weza kufela aboni abanje ngam! Namhla indlela yam imhlope,
ndinoxolo empefumlweni.” Apela apa amandla okuteta. wati kwakugqitywa
umtandazo—“ Amen.” Kusasa ngolwesi-Bini, 7 October, 1884, wapuma
umpefumlo. Kufike kum ucingo oluxela lompanga, kute ukuvalwa kwamatyala
ndaya kubikela usapo Iuka Bangani olulapa e Bayi. ndite ndingena
endlwini ndakauleIwa ngu Henry Baso ngezokuba akulalwa u Emily ubeta
igazi ngo mlomo, (lo Emily ngopambi ko Makisi). Unyukile unina ngosuku
olusemva kokuncwaba. Noko uke wanga uyazomeleza, wamana etabata omayeza
e Hospital. Kusenjalo kuwe umfana ka Keti kwa womzalwana ka Puru.
okwakule ndlu. Ubube ngomhla we 8 ku December, 1884, esongiwa kwa, ngu
Mrs. Bangani no Henry Baso. Ubuye wanyukela kwase Tinara no Emily pambi
kwe Nibidyala, kute cwaka, kwavakala ukuba uyafa u Emily. Ndifumene
ucingo ngo Mvulo, 23 February, 1885, oluti ububile u Emily.
Asisabalileli abo bemkileyo kuba baye ekayeni letu sonke, kodwa sililela
lowo upilileyo utwele zonke ezi ntsizi.

Wauchope.

INTLANGANISO YAMA MFENGU E MACFARLAN.

KUWE MHLELI welo pepa labuntsundu,—Kute mhla nge 10 March, 1885, e
Macfarlan kwamiselwa ukuba izibe yiyona ndawo apo kuya kuhlanganwa kona
zinkosi zonke zama Mfengu. kute noko lonto yayigqitywe kwelo nje, ezinye
azaba nakufikelela, noko ke andingebi salandalanda zinto. Zifikile ke
ezo zafikayo. yangena ngexa lase mini 12 o’clock, saza isihlalo satatwa
ngu Mr. Green Sikundla, umfo ondilekileyo. Kute ke nakubeni kungabangako
ngxelo yangapambili, kuba noko yintlanganiso yamadoda amakulu.
asel’esiti wona ingxoxo azibale entloko, ezafutwayo zona ngumsi,
angagcina into angeze ayigcina lamarashalalana ati tina sifundile. Nto
ihlanganisene ngayo lendimbane ngolusuku lukankanyiweyo nantsi, “
Masenze isicelo sokuba sibuye sipatwe yi Nkosazana, kulaulo Iwetu
oludala, ingelulo olu Iuka.

nqapela ndikule.” Kute noko kuba ingxoxo zasezanele kwintlanganiso
ezaziko ngapambili kwe yayise Burnshill, apo kwakugqitywe kwelokuba
ubani nabani ofuna epetwe apa azatsho, ati ofuna ukutumela isicelo eso
atsho kwa naye. Nto ndayipaulayo kukuteta okuhle kwamadoda amakulu.
Ndihamba nje bendingazange ndicinge ukuba obawo aba kanti banako nabo
ukuyiteta into ipele ngexesha, kuba izinkabi ezike ziyolule into noko
ziyivayo. Ezona nkosi zenene ema Mfengwini, ingeyiyo lento sesaba pakati
kwa Belungu ekuti inkosi kutiwe zizibonda zemiti into egxunyekwa
incotulwe ngotandayo. Ndapaula induna umfo ka Mabandla, i Bele, u
Mbovane, indodana ebuciko bungummangaliso, kuba lomntu upape kangaka
akazange abone necapaza eli lemfundo, noko unako ukufunda engazanga waya
nakusipina isikula, abale nokubala. Wanga lomfo ka Mabandla angaqubela
pambili ekuluvuseni uhlanga Iwakowabo. Eyesibini yayiyinto yasema
Zizini, into ka Wulana, i Zizi lenene; neka Socishe, i Kuze, nezinye ezi
ke ngekutiwa zibonda kufaneleke xa kutshiwoyo kuzo. Andingebali
zintoyinto, kwagqitywa kwelokuba kutunyelwe ke eso sicelo. Lento ndiya
yibulela kuhlanga oluntsundu, kanjalo mna okukokwam ndingati,— “ Pambili
mabandla amnyama! ” Ningabi sayimatela lentlanganiso, yinto entle
wakubona umzi uhlangene, kuba apa e Mlungwini izinto zigqitywa
yintlanganiso, eti apo kuhlangenwe kona kupicotwe izinto ngezinto,
izimeko ezingati zincede uhlanga Iwakowabo nezingonakalisa Iona, yiyo
lento ke olupato lunzima ngakuti sodwa midaka. Uxolo Mhleli ngokwenza
nde inteto yam, andinanto ingapezulu kwaleyo. Manditshonele ngeliti—“
Pambili Mamfengu! ” nashiywa eludakeni.

Ngcwangushe Kenu.

INTLANGANISO YAMA-NGQIKA KWA CENTANE. YENJENJE imbalelwano pakati kuka
Capt. Blyth,. ne Ntlanganiso yama Ngqika yokuqubela pambili Inkutalo, e
Transkei, elandelisa langxelo sayincokolela abafundi betu ngo February
odluleyo :—

Isicelo.

Aloerille, Centane, 6 Jan., 1885.

Nkosi yam,—

Ngembeko ndiyacela ukushumayela ukuba ndinqwenelwe nga- malungu ale
Ntlanganiso (iselixeliwe entloko yale ncwadi), esandukuyilwa kule ndawo
kutsha nje ukuba ndibike kuwe ukuba kokwale Ntlanganiso, nokuba amalungu
ale Ntlanganiso ayayazi imizamo yako yemihla ngemihla ukuxasa inkutalo
kwaba ntsundu obapeteyo e Transkei. Nenkutalo yako engadambiyo ukuba
ubone uhlanga oluntsundu lunyukile Iwakanyiselwa. Okubonisileyo oko
ngeziyalo ohlala uzenza kuzo zonke intlanganiso zabantu obakbyo kuzo.
Ngako ke amalungu ale Ntlanganiso emalingeni awo angeni okuhambisela
pambili Inkutalo, ebona nje ukuba zininzi kwezixakaniso ezingabakoyo
endleleni yayo, ayacela inkuselo noncedo Iwako kuyo.

  Ndim isicaka sako esitobekileyo, ANDREW GONTSHI.

Capt. M. S. BLYTH, C.M.G.,

Impendulo.

  Kwi Ofisi ye Nkulu ye Mantyi, Transkei, January 10, 1884.

Nkosi yam,—

Incwadi yako yosuku Iwe 6 kule nyanga, egameni le Ntlanganiso yama
Ngqika yokuqubela pambili Inkutalo, ifikile kum. Ngoku ke ndifuna ukuba
niqiniseke ukuba nokuba yiyipina imizamo namalinga awenziwayo okunyusa,
nokukanyisela uhlanga oluntsundu, aya kuzuza uxaso Iwam ngokukulu.
Ndingavuya ukuhlangana nawe, namanye amalungu entlanganiso yenu, nditete
nani nge ntlanganiso.

Ndisisicaka sako,

Matt. Blyth,

Inkulu ye Mantyi, Transkei.

Mr. Andrew Gontshi,

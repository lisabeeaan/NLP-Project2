ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1872

AMA MAORIES.

AMA Maories, abemi beziqiti ekutiwa yi New
Zealand, bebengabantu ababudenge bukulu, aba-
zizidla bantu, besenza neminye imisebenzi yobu-
nyama. Basand’ ukupuma kubo obobunyama.
Ukuqala kwabo ukublangana nabafundisi kwaku
nge 1817. Ama Ngesi aqala ukuwalaula nge 1840.
Kute ukuhluma kwawo kwakauleza noko ebemana
ukulwa nama Ngesi. Sekulixesha bete abanye
bawo bavunyelwa ukuba babe ngamalungu E-Par-
lamente, eliqela kutiwa yi Lower House. Kuma-
pepa okugqibela avela kona, siva ukuba bekuxoxwa
ukuba avunyelwe ukuba abe ngamalungu eliqela
kutiwa yi Upper House. Abanye bake bayicasa
kakulu kanye lonto. Kute kodwa ngenxa yokuba
kubonwa ukuba, ngamadoda anokuqonda okukulu,
nakuteleyo emsebenzini, kwapela oko kuwacasa.
Kutiwa ngoku makubeko angamalungu E-Upper
House ye Parlamente.

Siyatemba ukuba ixesha alisekude abayakuti
abantu abantsundu base South Africa, baq'ube
emfundweni nasekuhlumeni, kangangokude babe
ngamalungu entlanganiso zokumisa imiteto, baze
nabo nje ngama Maories bavunyelwe ukuyipata
yonke imisebenzi ebekisele ekulauleni. Mabati
bona bazilungiselele ngokulahla amasiko obuheyi-
deni, babambe zime emfundweni. Singa kanjalo
kungaqondwa ukuba ama Maories asibantu basela
butywala.

IQELA ELITILE lamasodati ama German libu-
lewe ngama French.

UMBOLOMPO.—E-England, kwinxingwa yama-
nzi ekutiwa yi Straight yase Dover kunxanyelwe
ukwakiwa umbolompo oyakuba zimayile ezi 21
ubude, uhamba ngapantsi kwamanzi. Woba
ngomnye wemimangaliso eyenziwe yimfundo lo-
mbolompo xa selugqityiwe ; xa inqwelo zihamba
pantsi kwamanzi ugama omde kangako. Fanu-
kuba uyakudla izigidi ngezigidi zeponti. Seku-
hlanganiswe ama £30,000. Wotabata mhlaumbe
iminyaka emitatu ukwenziwa kwawo.

U-SIR SAMUEL BARER, omanye wabahambi, aba
hamba bekangela ukumila kompakati we Africa
nokuhlala kwezizwe ezikuwo, kwincwadi yokugqi-
bela ayibalileyo, uncoma ubunzima nenxakeko
zokuhamba kona. Kwezinye indawo ubuti urn-
koinbe ahamba ngawo, ukuze ube nokuhamba,
kumbiwe indlela ngemihlakulo ngamadoda ake.
Inxenye yamadoda ake ibulewe ngama Baris,
isizwe esisesemnyameni omkulu. Amanye ama-
gosa ake awawutandi umsebenzi ahambele wona
ngoku—ukutshabalalisa ubukoboka. Uncoma no-
buhle belizwe. Baninzi ababulewe kudinwa nazi-
zifo ezibangwa bushushu. Ubebala ese Ismaila.

EZAPESHEYA.

INGQAKAQA iseko kakulu E-Plymouth, esinye sezixeko ezisemazantsi
E-England.

U-M. THIERS Umongameli wama French uposwe ngompu ngumntu obenxamele

AMAKOSAZANA amahlanu ama Japan ayekufunda E-America. Amanye, awama China
aye kufunda E-England.

AMA NKAZANA atile ase France avumelene ngokwenza amalinga okufumana
imali yokuhlaula ityala lemfazwe. Atengisa nezivato zawo.

ISIXEKO SASE SCHAMACHI kwintaba ze Caucasus sitshatyalaliswe
kukuzamazama komhlaba. Zi- xnbalwa indlu ezisemiyo; baninzi abafileyo
ngenxa yako.

INKOSIKAZI, yase Brazil engumhlolokazi, iwa- kulule amakoboka ali 140
abesemhlabeni wayo, osand’ ukutengiswa. Abayimilwelwe kuwo naba-
Bebengamaxego ibafunele indlela zokubaxasa.

U-MRS. BRETELL, isicaka esidala senkosi zase England, ubube ngomhla wa
27 ka January, ubudala eminyaka ima 90. Ube seleminyaka ima 70 ezikonza
inkosi. Azikonzayo ngu George III, George IV, William IV, no Queen
Victoria.

KUTIWA E-PHILIPPINE ISLANDS abemi bakona abama 300, bayivukele
I.Governmente ye Spain ebebepantsi kwayo. Babulewe bonke ngamasodati.

E-INDIA ama Kukas, esinye sezizwe zelozwe, avukele I-Governmente ya
Mangesi.. Ebengawa 300, kutinjwe ama 70, amanye abulawa, amanye
anxwelerwa.

INCONCONI zase Burmah kutiwa zixalise kanga- ngokuba zide zambanga omnye
umatiloshe ukuba azibulale, ngokusuke atsibele elwandle atshone
emanzini.

IDOLOSI iko kakulu kwindawo ngendawo ze

  abebe ibokwe zika Klass, Iqeya, ate amakulu kuwo-

  abetwa imivumbo 15.

  OBETE INDODA. — Ilaukazi lihlaule esheleni
  zontlanu ngokugibisela indoda yelinye ngelitye..

  UMQOMBOTI.— Umqomboti asikuko nokuba

  nentlombe ngalenyanga.

  OZIXOMILEYO.—Inkwenkwana eminyaka ili 10,
  ebiqashwe ngu Mr. Hugo wase Drakenstein, izi-
  bulele ngokuzixoma ngokwayo esebeni lomti
  Imbangi yokuzibulala ayaziwa.

  URUBULALA.—Umxosa ongu Chalo, ufakwe
  etolongweni E-Rini, ngetyala lokubulala Ilawo
  elingu David. Apokwaqalana kona, kwakungo-
  kusuke lomxosa abete inkwenkwe eyayimtuka,
  lazake elo Lawo laselisilwa, walihlaba ke yena.

  UGQATSO.—Kugqatso lwemayile ezi 10, obelu-
  sand’ ukubako ngase Qonce, pakati kwamadoda
  asibozo, amane ekwele emahasheni, amanye amane
  ebaleka ngenyawo, ashiyiwe ebekwele emahasheni,.
  ibaso ebekubekelwene ngalo lizuzwe ngawenyawo.

  IZICARA.—Ngoku asikuko nokuba ziyafuneka
  kwindawo ngendawo. Kutiwa E-Bayi kufuneka
  abapati babantwana, abapeki, abacweli benqwelo,
  abaki bezindlu; zonke intlobo zezicaka zingaufu-
  mana kakuhle umsebenzi.

  INTLANGANISO yomnyaka yokukunjuzwa koku-
  vulwa kwe tyalike yase Tidmanton iyakuba nge
  Monday ngomhla wa 29 ka April. Ngecawa
  ngomhla wa 28, kuyakushunyayelwa intshumayelo
  ezibekiselele kuko. Zonke izihlobo ezine nkatalo
  ngalo tyalike ziyamenywa.

  E-QOYI kubube inkazana izala, umka Gebenga
  umfo wase Mabeleni. Ishiya usizi olukulu.
  Usana Iwayo ibingekalwanyisi kuba lube luse
  yure nye ubudala. Hayi into imbi ukububa
  komntu pakati kwabaheyideni. Abantu abako-
  lwayo ngebembulela U-Tixo osuse obunyama.
  Lonkazana ibingakolwanga. Ibube ngomhla we
  3 ka March.

  E-NYARA kwenzakele inkwenkwe ka Samuel
  Mabope. Iwe etoleni, ibikweliswe ngamanye
  yapuka ingalo.

  E-QONCE, umfazi. womxosa abati ngu Mityi
  uhlauliswe 7s. 6d. ngenxa yokubeta U-Tolo, Im-
  fengukazi.

  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1876.

INDABA ZALEKOLON1.

ISIFO SAMAHASHE kuvakala ukuba sesiko E-Alexandria (Emnyameni.)

ELINYE lamapepa ase Qonce likankanya ukuba amafama asanduluka ngase
Qunube ukusingisa ngapakati.

AMASOLDATI—Umkombe obusiza namasoldati avela pesheya wapukile ngase
Dassen Island kodwa basinda bonke abebekuwo.

U-MR MOLTENO kuvakala nkuba uza kuhambela kwele diamond ukuya kukangela
intswelo zelolizwe nokulaulwa elilaulwa ngako.

EBANKINI yase Stellenbosch kufumaneke iponti ezi £ 20,000 zingeko, yaza
lonto yakwaziwa wazibulala umongameli webanki.

U-Mr SCOTT oyi Mantyi utunywe kwa Rulumeni ukuba aye kuma emdeni opakati
kuka Rili na Batembu axele ukuba kuko abadlulayo.

U-COLONEL EUSTACE utikutiwe maze amxelele u-Rili ukuba awunakubuya
uguqulwe umda nokuba ukuba akavumi ukushenxa maze alindele iziqamo
zokungeva.

E-BEAUFORT WEST kuko umlungukazi ne Lawo abafakwe etolongweni. Kutiwa
bate ngokutandana bahlangana ukuba bayidlise indoda yalomlungukazi
bayibulale.

U-MR KEYS wangase Beaufort ufunyenwe ezidubule ngokwake, wawapula
umhlati ongase zantsi. Kuvakala ukuba kuko itemba lokuba uyakupila kodwa
akayi kubuya abe nokuhlafuna.

KWA RILI umda womhlaba awukalungiswa. Kutunyelwe U-Colonel Eustace ukuba
aye kuba yimantyi yakona endaweni ka Mr Cumming. Ayikavakali into
eyakwenziwa ngu Rulumeni ngalomda.

U-MR BROWNLEE kuvakala ukuba uza kupuma ekubeni ngu Mpatiswa Wemicimbi
Yabantsundu aze kuba yintloko yemantyi. Kuvakala ukungati uya kuya
kuhlala e-Dutywa. Akakavakali oya kuba sendaweni yake.

NGASE Kapa kuko Ingesi elingu Brinley elibube ngequbuliso ekuqaleni
kweveki kanti umfazi walo nabantwana balo abane baya kufika ngolwe
Sihlanu ukuvela pesheya kolwandle, bafika sekusitiwa akaseko lomntu.

U-MR MOLTENO oyena usentloko ye Goverment yalekoloni uti ababantu bateta
ngemfazwe abanasizatu sokwenjenjalo, yena akakolwa ukuba iya kubako into
enjalo; kodwa ukuba ibeko iyakufika Amangesi selelungile.

UBUDENGE—e-Tyume kwesika Mabandla kute ngomhla 18 ka November izulu
labeta indlwane. Kute ngemini elandelayo kwabizwa igqira lomsutu ukuba
lize kunyanga ukuze lingapindi izulu libete kulondawo. Lizile okunene
eligqira leza kunyanga, labahlaulisa kunene abo bantu.

ABAGUQULI BEZIBALO kuvakala ukuba ngoku kokugqibela abebehlangene baye
bapela ngesahluko se 19 kweyokuqala ka Samuel. Baya kubuya bahlangane
ngo 14 ka February 1877 baze baye kupela ekugqibeleni kweyesibini ka
Samuel.

E-FORT BEAUFORT kuko intombazana ebizakwendiselwa kumntu olixego sekude
kwarolwa amahashe amabini nenkomo ezintandatu. Kute noko sekunjalo
ayavuma intombazana ukuya kulomntu zada zabuyiselwa inkomo namahashe.
Kungekabintsuku zininzi emva koko ifunyenwe lontombazana ngasemlanjeni
ifile.

ELINYE lamapepa ase Qonce likankanya ukuba noko u-Sarili kudunyiswe
ukuba ufuna ukulwa, uyawadla kunene amasela, uyalima, wenza nezinye
izinto ezingezizo ezomntu ofuna imfazwe. Isikalazo anaso sesomhlaba.
Liti kanjalo inkosi zalomacala zifuna ukubuya zicele u-Rulumeni ukuba
awulungise umda. Zite kubantu bazo maze balitobele ilizwi lika Ruluni
nokuba kutenina’

U-SIWANI ute akuva ukuba kuko amafama abalekayo ngase Monti waya kuma
Xosa ami kona. U-Mr Tainton oyimantyi ubale incwadi eya ku Rulumente
etyila isizatu sokuhamba kuka Siwani. Kubonakala ukuba ubehamba eyala
Amaxosa ukuba angaboyikisi Abelungu, ake wamfumana eboyikisa uyakwenza
imizamo ngokwake yokuba ohlwaywe lomntu. Ute yena okukokwake nokuba
kuhle ntonina uyakuba ngecala lamangesi.

U-NEHEMIAH MSHWESHWE ekwakuvakcle ukuba Ubanjiwe ngu Captain Blyth
ngokupembelela abantu

bake ukuba balwe no Rulumeni ube tunyelwe E-Natal ukuba abe eya kugcinwa
kona kude kuvakale into etetwa ngu Rulumeni. Abanye abantu abebebanjwe
kunye naye abalishumi elinesine, bebetunyelwe E- Mzimkulu ukuze
bangapembeleli abanye. U-Rulumente ute makabuyiswe U-Nehemiah aziswe kwa
Adam Koko. Akukavakali ukuba siza kuba yintonina isigwebo sake.

E-QUMRA kuza kutunyelwa iqela lamasoldati aya kuba sendaweni yamapolisa
abekona ngapambili: ukuze ke wona abe nokuya pesheya kwe Nciba ukuba aya
funeka kona. Kulomasoldati aza kuya kona kuko ayakufunelwa amahashe
ukuze abe nokukaulezela into eyonakalayo. U-Rulumeni kubonakala ukuba
ngoku ufuna zipelile ezinteto zininzi kangaka nge­mfazwe kuba zitintela
umsebenzi kumacala onke ziyonakalisa noko kungati akuqondakali.

E-MANZIKANYI kute kuleveki idluleyo kwezakuxelelwa u-Mr Diesel ukuba
kuko amasela amabini ezayo makuvalwe amazibuko. Uxobe kwaoko wapuma
nesicaka. Kungekabi mzuzu ate tu, laza elinye lawo abati ngu Msutu weza
kulendawo akuyo u-Mr Diesel. Ubanjwe ke waya kubotshelelwa esitaleni.
Kwakuba mzuzwana u-Mr Diesel uyekulikangela isela elo, lati kanti
lizikulule, lase liziposa kuye limhlaba ebunzi ngerenqa noko
ayamenzakalisa kakulu. Kuwolene ngengalo laza lamhlaba emhlana. Ute
kwilawu ebeliko malidubule, lasuka laposa. Libalekile isela lafika
kuvaliwe esangweni. U-Mr Diesel urole umpana omcinane (irevolver)
wabokudubula awavuma. Lakubona oko isela libuye labuya lamhlaba amanxeba
engalweni lamshiya elele pantsi, laya kwase sangweni lapuma labaleka,
nangoku alikafunyanwa. Kutiwa lisela elida­la. U-Mr Diesel noko
anamanxeba amakulu, akuko loyikekayo.

AMARE EMFAZWE asaya enyuka ngokunyuka kwamanye amacala, kwamanye
akuvakali nento. Kunqabile ukuyieuba inyaniso apo imi kona. Elinye
lampepa ase Qonce likankanya incwadi eliyi fumene ivela pesheyakwe Nciba
eti kuko abantsundu abebeye egqireni ukuba libanyange ukuze bomelele;
kananjalo aba­nye basela igazi bamane ukuqayisa ngento abaza kuyenza
kumlungu ngezinyanga zizayo. Ikankanya loncwadi ukuba kuyafudukwa kakulu
kulomacala namasara ayabopa. Enye incwadi ekwakwelopepa iti kuko impawn
ezibonisa ukuba U-Mqikela ubencedisana no Nehemiah. Kwela Mabomvana
kuvakala ukuba U-Mr Scott obemiselwe ukuba abe yimantyi kona ugxotiwe
isiza sake sendlu salinywa ngenkabi. Nokoke kunqabile ukubuqonda kakuhle
ubunyaniso balomare kuba iti nentwana encinane yomdlalo iselisenziwa
into enkulu, enye igqwetwe ilungile. Abaya kuloliwe nangoku basaroze
ngakunye into ke leyo ekungabonakali ukuba inge injalo ukuba kube kuza
kuliwa

EZAMAN YE A KWASE AFRICA.

IZICAKA EZINTSUNDU kunqabile ukuba zifumaneke kwele Diamond.

E-TRANSVAAL Ibulu elingu Barnard lidliwe £45 ngokusuka libopelele
isicaka salo emsilene wehashe.

KWELA MAZULU akuko nto ingamandla ivakalayo. U-Cetywayo uneqela lenkomo
alitumele e-Natal ukuza kutenga into zokulwa noko akuqondakali ukuba
kuko nto aseleyilungiselela. Imipu iko kakulu kwelozwe. Neruluwa
iyenziwa kakulu, kutiwa komkulu kodwa kwenziwa iruluwa eziponti
ezimashumi asibozo ngemini. Abantsundu e-Natal bati uyakuti akufika
u-Somtsewu baye kulima elilizwe kade libangwa ngama Bulu.—Standard and
Mail.

E-DAHOMEY akukabiko nto yimbi yenziweyo engapandle kwesasiyibike
ngenyanga edluleyo, kuko ama French atile abanjiweyo yinkosi yase
Dahomey eti iyakuwabulala kwakuliwa ngama Ngesi. Afumane axakwa ke ngoko
Amangesi kuba engatandi kubulalisa ama French. Izipata mandla zase
France zenza imizamo yokukulula abo babanjwa.

E-DIAMOND, umjodi ongu Nathan ubanjwe eseyelisela isicaka esintsundn
ukuba siye kuba Idiamond kwinkosi yaso. Ute akusiwa ematyaleni
wagwetyelwa ukukatswa imivumbo 36 aze ahlale etolongweni umnyaka wonke
esebenza nzima. Ite lonto ukubavuyisa kwabo abembi ababi nakuzibamba
nasematyaleni kuba u-Nathan lowo kade eranelwa. Abembi bamkape wada waya
kungena etolongweni, abanye besiti makaxonywe.

E-NATAL kuko umteto oti xa isicaka site sapula umnqopiso mhlaumbi
asateta ngokuzitoba kwinkosi

yaso siya kubetwa nokuba simhlope nokuba sintsundu. Iruluneli yakona ibe
ifuna ukuba ucinywe umteto onjalo, abake bavume Abamisi-miteto bakona,
bati noko kunganqwenelekiyo ukuba zibetwe izicaka ezimhlope akuko ndlela
yimbi zinokulungiswa ngayo ezintsundu. Kubonakala ke ngoko ukuba mazibe
aziva kanye izicaka zase Natal.

KWELE DIAMOND kuke kwako izipitipiti ezibe zibangelwe kukubanjwa kuka Mr
U. Tucker, omnyo wabafo abebedumile kona. Ityala lake belilelokutenga
idiamond ingapandle kwemvumelo yakwa Rulumeni. Imantyi imgwebele
ukuhlala etolongweni. Ute kuba engumfo otandwa kunene ngabembi kwako
abasongela ukumkulula ngamandla. Amapolisa akona abeselede axoba, kanti
noko ezontsongelo ziya kupelela emoyeni. Zite izihlobo zake zacela
uxolo, akavuma ukulunika umlauli, wati kuko abanye abebekwaneli tyala
lika Tucker abasebenza ezindleleni, akanako ke ukwahlulwa yena.

UKUNQABA KWABASEBENZI E-NATAL Umfo otile wakona obalela ipepa eliyi
Northern Post uti: Noko bangaka abantsundu apa asikuko nokuba kunqabile
ukumfumana owokusebenza. umfiki ubuza into epazamisa umsebenzi wokufama,
ukuba yiyipina. impendulo yeyokuba kukusweleka kwabasebenzi.
Lembandezelo kade kulilwa ngayo kubonakala ukuba kukona, isaya ikula.
Ndiva ukuba ngenxa yokusweleka kwabasebenzi kuko indawo apo imihlambi
yegusha ifayo, ukudla kutshabalalela ezindle, inkomo zidla amasimi
Kwezinye indawo amafama anyanzeleke ukuba aluse ngokwawo, azigaulele
inkuni enze neminye imisebenzi yolohlobo. Isiqamo soko abantwana
bamafama abanakuyi fumana imfundo ngenxa yokwenza imisebenzi engeyipetwe
ngabantsundu.

AMANGWANE.—Abalesi be Sigidimi bokukumbula ukudliwa kwesizwe sa Mangwane
esise Natal kusitiwa sisizela esika Langalibalele siyasinceda ngokufihla
inkomo. Wati u-Lord Carnarvon, Umongameli wekoloni zenkosazana, akuyiva
lonto yokuba sidliwe wati sifumane sadliwa lingabonakali ityala laso,
ngoko masibuyiselelwe. Kwayalezwa ku Sir Garnet Wol­seley oko ebese
Natal ukuze akangele into esifanele ukubuyiselelwayona wati ke yena
masinikwe £12,000. Wati ke u-Lord Carnarvon mayirolwe lomali ngabantu
base Natal, bamane ukurola £3,000 ngomnyaka kude kupele iminyaka emine.
AmaNatal avuma ukuyirola lomali nge 1875, aza kulo mnyaka siwupeteyo ala
ukuyirola yonke akupa iwaka lalinye lanamakulu amahlanu. U-Carnarvon
ubuye watumela incwadi zokuba lomali mayipume yonke akunakwapulwa idinga
elibe selenziwe. Abamisi-miteto base­Natal basa xoxa ngezoncwadi,
akukaqondakali ukuba baya kugqiba pina.

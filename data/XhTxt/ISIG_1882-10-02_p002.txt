Xa siteta ngolu hlobo asicime melilo mayelana nendidinge ndidi zenkomo
ezi Pesheya kwe Nciba. Into tina esicingela ukuba ngezi bonisa yona
yingxowa yendoda. Inkomo le asinto inokutenjwa; imofu nezinye izinto
zintshaba zayo eziti xa seziyi fumene zingabi sema nganeno kokuyi
ncinita. Ifuyiwe nje ngama Ngesi ifuyelwe ukuba ivelise imali, ze imali
leyo iye e Bankeni apo yandayo.

Ngale nteto sicela impi yakowetu ya Pesheya kwe Nciba ukuba ze
iyisebenzise i Banki leyo. Kambe sekuko naba qondi mteto bebala letu
abangati baba qondise abantu nge ntwana-ntwana ezayamene noku qutywa
kwayo. Nati kweli pepa soti senze konke okuse mandleni etu ukuqondisa
abo bafuna beqondisiwe. Eyetu ingcinga eqinisekileyo yeyokuba ukuba
abantu bakowetu bayi sebenzisile i Banki leyo, boyi fumana iluncedo
olukulu. Ukuba umntu abe egcine imali emipakatweni mhlaimbi etyeyeni
usicenge sokupangwa zihange, noku pangwa zihlobo zake ezinga soku peza
ukuza kuye zisiti mazibolekwe, inkomo zibanjiwe, mhlaimbi u Mlungu
undifake ifolokwe ubiza imali, noku pangwa zintswelwana zake eziba
nomntu onemali engxoweni kufupi.

Lenteto asiyi shubanga. Zininzi incazelo esingabuye sizenze Okwanamhla
sibika into entle, neyo tando, neyo kuzi kanyela eyenziwe libandla
elihambisa umsebenzi we Standard Bank.

IMPAWANA.

Yeheni! bafundi besi Gidimi base Natal. Kepa singa sezwanini luto nje
ngani seya tyaya yakuhla ingqakaqa ekenu lapo na? Pela sibuza nje
asisafunyani nancwadi kwini. Soke sibone belo lapo nisinga kona.

“Irabel” u Arabi wase Jeputa ziti ezo cingo lwase manzini uzinikele
kumkosi wama Ngesi. Nokubanjwa kwake kunoku lindelwa ukubekwa pantsi kwe
ntonga. Icala ebeku saziwa ukuba liya koyisa, limele kanjako ukuba
loyise lelama Ngesi. Yonke imbeko ku Sir Garnet!

Umhlobo usibonise ipepa lendaba zaba mnyama base Natal elingu Baqa.
Asipepa likulu noko; isi Gidimi, ukuba besinga lahlula kwizi qingata
ezine, lona belinga lingana nesiqingata esinye; ngenye inteto liyi yesi
Gidimi. Indaba zalo zisingisele esibalweni, kuncinane okunga funyanwayo
okunge zinto zase Natal. Ntwana siyipaulayo yenge minikelo yama Wesile
antsundu, efunyenwe yintlanganiso ebise Edendale ngo August ukuba
ibiseyeka. Impi enkulu irole £60, entsha £10. Atsho ke u Baqa, ati
ubuncinane beminikelo leyo buya mmangalisa. Ixesha lentswelo siqonda
ukuba lingene e Natal ukutenga umhlaba kona abantu abayekile.

Lo Mr. Gladstone udume kunene waqala ukuhlala e Parlamente ya Pesheya
ngonyaka we 1832 kunanamhla. Iqela alikokelayo licinga ngokwenza umgidi
woku kumbuza iminyaka emashumi mahlanu alenkonzo ka Mr. Gladstone ye
Nkosazana nelizwe lakowabo. Uhlobo owotabata lona lomgcobo aluka xelwa.

Qugqwala liqumbile: kutiwe ke undi ngnongnozele, ndati kwaoko ndengqo

Oludaba asingakuba besi ngalwazi, lento singalu ngenisanga kwanga

I Lokeshoni—igama ezaziwa ngalo ingqili ezimiwe ngaba mnyama
ezidolopini—yase Somerset East ibisakuba yindawo ekungcola kungena
mlinganiso; ize kona ngexesha lokwindla kwi tolofiya ibete ixake
abaniniyo. Ukuba ise njengokuba sayi gqibela injalo, singasuke siti
lixesha—ingqakaqa selise mnyango nje o Ntloyiya. beyekile ukuvumisa, no
Dukumbana no Zeep-Pot (mbizu yesepa,) ngazwi nye bonke abemi besi Dikida
baququzela, bawenza wadleka umzi. Lento ingqakaqa zitandana nati
bamnyama kungoku tandanakwetu nokungcola. Oku kupuma ezintliziyweni zetu
ezinzulu.

Kambe sinama nenekazi akowetu amkela isi Gidimi kwezi mini. Sifuna eke
asityebisela into esihlangene nayo ncwadaneni itile ebeke sayi lesa.
Sisingisa kuwo kuba sizindlela ukuba ngawo anoku xela inyaniso kwi
micimbi elolo hlobo. Nantsi into esixakileyo: “Kunganina ukuba intombi
zipuzane, ekubeni amadodana engayenzi lonto?—Zeke impendulo ibe
yeyokuba, amantombazana akananto igqitiseleyo yoku pokolwa.” Singaleka
siti lento ibonisa kanjako abantu abatanda ukupuzwa. Tina noko
sizingqinga kwezi zinto.

Hip ! Hip ! Hip ! Hure ! ! ! Yatyeba into ebesi kade siyiteta. Udaba
oluvela e India luti, e Culcutta u Baboo Romesh Clander Mitter, umntu
omnyama ongum India, unyulelwe ekubeni yintloko ye Jaji zelo zwe
ngexesha angekoyo umnini ndawo leyo. I Governor eyenze oko ngu mnumzetu
u Lord Ripon. Kanti ke noko kwa pakati kwetu bamnyama, kuseko abati
yinto enokwenzeka na ukuba umntu omnyama abambe indawo epezulu! Nanamhla
sisati into eya kubanga ukuba sibe nokuzi lindela ezi ndawo yimfundo
epakamileyo nesimilo esingena sisihla. Apo e India aba mnyama bazi M.A.
abange ngabo abateteleli ematyaleni. Kazi namhla sotinina?

Okwa ngoku kusaqubeka ingxobotshitshi esingena kusi profeta isipelo sayo
pakati kwama fama amelene ne Tunxe. Ama fama afuna i Tunxe litshayelwe
lacokiswa—akwabako mntu mnyama kwelo. I Government iti lomhlaba
waqiniselelwa abantu abamnyama, engena kwenza nto ke malungana nokuba
ndulula abantsundu abakona, nabatanda bemi kwelo. Eyetu imvo ‘ngalento
yeyokuba lehambiso yezi fama isekwe pezu kwentiyo yomntu omnyama
kwapela; sivuya ukupaula ukuba i Government ayizinikele ekunikeni
indlebe kuyo. Bekuke kwatiwa ifama sezifuna undiko, wada no Rulumente
watumela onompilinga. Siya tandabuza tina ukuba kobako nto noko.

Izinto ezi “zinkohla nezanga,” kulomzi wakowetu untsundu singati, xa
siboleka inteto yolutsha, ziya “lala.” Xa sitshoyo asizi kuzicwangcisa
kolu “Pawana,” kuba xa zidweliswe ndawonye zingaboyikisa abatandi
bohlanga bade balahlekwe yimipefumlo yabo. Ngambalwa soti salatise umzi
wonke entweni enye, ze sike tina singa gwebi. Iyabonwa na Hayi:
masingatsho. Masiti: yinto elusizi ukuti kumadodana angama Gqweta
antsundu e Transkei, esizidlayo ngawo, kanti akuko nanye eyamkela
Isigidimi Sama-Xosa. Lendawo asiyibiki ngakuba silambele isheleni zawo

njalo—kofaneleka na xa luzizo zonke ezi zinto, ze yonke into eyeyo

Ngesi umntu uba neratshi ngento yohlanga lwakowabo. Tina lutsha

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 7, 1874.

UKUFA KUKA DE LIVINGSTONE.—Incwadi esand’ ukubalwa ngumfo ose Zanzibar
ebalela ipepa lase Natal iti.—Emva kokukubalela kwam kubuye kwafika
isicaka sika Dr Livingstone, sati kuyinyaniso ukuba isidumba sake sibe
sesise Nyanyembe. Besekufunwe neqela lamadoda aza kusizisa E-Zanzibar
ahamba no Dr Dillon no mpati-mikosi U-Murphy. Kuko nendaba zokuba U-Dr
Dillon ubepambene intloko wazidubula. U-Cameron bati ukupikele ukuwuquba
umsebenzi wake, wokulanda imilambo nokukangela amazwe abeseza
kutyelelelwa ngu Dr Livingstone. Kutiwa kanjalo lomadoda eza nesidumba,
azakuza namapepa ake, nebokisi enencwadi abeyibala imihla ngemihla.

KWELE DIAMOND kuko umfo abati ngu Haskins obengumtengi we diamond, ote
ngezintsuku zidluleyo waboleka kwenye indoda £200. kwenye waboleka £100,
kwaza kweyesitatu wati uyitengisela umhlaba wayo nge £600; kanti
lomhlaba ubeseletembise ukuhlaula ngawo kubantu abenamatyala abo. Ute
kwakuba njalo, watabata ihashe esitalini wabaleka ebusuku. Akuqondwa
ukuba akako kuhlatywe emkondwcni kwaoko, kwatunyelwa nemifanekiso yake
kwezinye indawo, ukuze kube kuvalwa amazibuko. Abantu abebemsukela
bamfumene ehotela ekobudala ubutongo, eqamele ngompu. Ute evuka baba
sebeutabatile wona, engasenakwenza nto. Kufikwe esenayo lomali abebaleka
nayo.

KWELAMA BATLAPIN.—Abafundisi bama French abahamba belibona elozwe,
befuna nendawo apo bangamisa izikolo bati ukubalisa ngalo : “ Ama
Batlapin, akwanje ngezinye izizwe zabantsundu, aqaba imbola. Inwele adla
ngokuziceba emacaleni zibe pezulu entloko kube kupela. Abafazi ungafika
intsimbi zide zapambana emzimbeni nasezingalweni. Amadoda akazikatalele
kakulu izivato. Asibantu bafuye kakulu. Ibokwe zabo luhlobo oluncinane,
angade owase mzini abe ngamatakane. Inkomo ziyekwa zihambe ezintabeni
kuba ilizwe lisapangalele; zimbalwa ongazibona ngase makaya. Lilizwe
eliti ehlotyeni libe shushu kakulu. Into esweleke ngokubi ngamanzi.
Ku­pela yimihlanjana emincinane apa napaya. Akuko nemitombo emikulu.
Akuyiyo nto ukuba umntu agqibe iyure ezimbini ukuya kuka amanzi ngenxa
yokuba kude kwawo. Kwezinye indawo zalo kuko ikoper ne golide; kodwa
inkosi yakona ayibavumeli abamhlope ukuba baye kuyimba. Ngabantu
abasesemva ngendawo ezininzi ngoku- kodwa ngokumalunga nelizwi; asinto
umsebenzi.

OBE UMNTWANA.—Kutiwa pesheya kwe Nciba kuko umfo obenabafazi ababini,
omnye engazali. Ude waqonda ukuba Ionto iyakubanga ukuba angatandwa
yindoda yake. Wenzeke iqinga lokungati unento, waza waya E-Batenjini,
wafikela kwindlu ebinabafazi ababini. Omnye enomntwana omncinane. Ufune
amaqinga okumba lomntwana. Kute pakati kobusuku wayitshisa indlu ; wati
xa baxapazelayo ukupuma, wayakunina walomntwana mncinane, wati
uzakumncedisa ukumkupa xa yena asakupa abanye. Umzuzile ke okunene ;
kanti ute emfumana waselesiti nyawo zam? Zakuba zigqityiwe ukukutshwa
impahla, unina womntwana uqale ukucinga apo makabe ukona. Uye kumbiza
komnye umfazi abebehlala naye kanti akaqondanga ukuba ubemnike owase
mzini; kanjalo bebengambuzisanga nalapo avela kona. Ube kukanyela omnye,
kwalilwa ngaye, kwatiwa unapose emlilweni ngenxa yokummonela
nokumkwelezela. Lomfazi oyena ubebe umntwana, ute xa agodukayo
wahlangana nabanye abebemazi, bambuza ukuba umfumene pina; wati kaloku
niyazi ukuba ndemka ndinento ; bafumana bayeka bengakolwa. Kute ukuze
ude uzokuvela um- kondo kwafika abantu bevela E-Batenjini bamfanisa
lomntwana. Unina wake ute akuyiva lonto weza engasanyateli, wafika
wamtabata engabanga sateta nelimdaka. Akukavakali ke ukuba uyaku-
tiwanina.

INTENGO EZIZA KUBAKO.

Ngo 10 ka April, E-Aberdeen pakati kwe Bay ne Bini kuya kutengiswa
imihlaba emibini, ne- nkabi ezitsalayo ezi 60, ezokuxelwa ezi 24; nemazi
ezi 60, amabashe 5 ; igushakazi ezi 1000; inkabi zegusha ezi 50,
ezebokwe ezi 200; neseyibokwe ezi 600 ; kwanempahla yendlu, nebokuva
ezintandatu, nenqwelwana zamahashe ezintatu. Intengo yobako ngexa
leshumi.

Ngo 14 ka April, E-Queenstown kuya kutengiswa inkabi ezi 300, nezipani
ezili 12; intengo iqale ngexa leshumi elinesinye.

Ngo 18 ka April, E-Q.once, kuyakutengiswa inkomo 80 emva kwe marike.

Ngo 18 ka April, E-Graaff Reinet kuya kute­ngiswa inkabi zenkomo ezi 30,
nebokuva ezimbini Intengo yoqala ngexa leshumi.

Ngo 21 ka April, E-Vaal Krans ngase Rini, kuyakutengiswa imihlaba
emitatu, negushakazi ezingapezu kwamawaka amabini anamakulu ma- tandatu,
inkabi zegusha ezi 1900, nenkunzi zegu­sha ezimashumi matandatu,
negushabokwe ezi 1100, nenkomo ezi 60, inqwelo nesipani sayo, amahashe
okukwelwa nawokutsala inqwelo, nehangu, omakuba, oziere, ozimbiza, nenye
impahla yokulima neyendlu.

Ngo 22 ka April, E-Qumra, kuyakutengiswa igusha ezi 1,200, neqela lemazi
zenkomo, nezinye izinto.

imbotyi 10s. 6d. ; ishumi elinesibini lamatanga 6s.; iflara yenkuni 27s.

itswele 10s., itapile 14s., imbotyi 23s., irasi 14s.

KWEYASE UITENHAGE.—Nge 100 lbs.:—Imbo­tyi 20s., itapile 13s. 3d., umbona
18s., umgubo 22s. 3d., irasi 16s.; intsiba ngeponti 4s. 6d.; isikumba
sebokwe 4s. 6d., esegusha 2s. 1d. ; ikulu lezitungu zehabile 50s. 3d.;
iflara yenkuni 32s.

KWEYASE GBAAFF REINET.—Ngenxowa:— Umbona 63s., umgubo 85s. 6d., irasi
50s., itapile 21s. 3d.; uboya ngeponti 6fd.; iflara yenkuni 10s.

KWEYASE RINI.—Nge 100 lbs.:—Umbona 13s. 6d., umgubo 26s., amazimba 12s.
6d., itapile 15s., ihabile 10s., irasi 13s., imbotyi 16s.; ishumi
eline­sibini lamatanga 8s.; iflara yenkuni 45s.

KUBABALELANI.

Sinosizi ukuba incwadi esizifumene ngale nya- nga singabanga nakuzifaka
ngoku. Sozishicilela kwelizayo ipepa. Siyatemba kanjalo ukuba soba
nokulikupa ngexesha lalo.

ABAZELWEYO.

E-Rini, ngo 7 ka March U-Mrs. England Thom­as uzele inkwenkwe.

E-New England kumlambo oyi Kraai, ngo 19 ka March inkosikazi yakwa John
Mehlomakulu izele unyana.

ABABUBILEYO.

E-Rabula ngo 23 ka March kubube U-Harry J. Boom. Wayezelwe ngo 22 ka
December 1872.

E-Wodehouse Forest (Esikobeni) Pesheya kwe Nciba kubube inkosikazi yakwa
Timothy Makiwane ngomhla 30 ka March, yabubela E-Nkosini. Izihlobo
ezimgama zanga zingase ziwamkela nge Sigidimi lombiko. Umke engagulanga,
kuba wa- lala nge Cawa, wemka ngo Mvulo, ebebeleka.

E-Mkangiso kubube U-Rev. W. Appleyard ngomhla we 4 ka April. Siyatemba
ukuba soba nokunika imbalana yempilo yake kwezayo inyanga.

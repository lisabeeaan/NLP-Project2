  21 November.

AMAGAMA.—Ngati incwadi zakomkulu zirwexu ngokunobom ngamagama emi- daka
yakowetu kuluhlu Iwe Registered Voters. Ayisayi kuba yinto yonyaka
odluleyo mhla kwanyulwa u Belingana (Bele-lengwana) mhla ngento embi,
la- ngena ukungen’ oku i Bhulu i Bhayi lixinene ngo Kaflle, uve kodwa
umntu esiti: “Mha asilifuni i Bhulu,” kanti esitsho nje alaziwa igama
lake kuluhlu Iwaba voti. Kulonyaka sibulela imigudu ka Mr. Edward Ngesi
ekuhlanganiseni amagama.

ISIFO.—Imikuhlane ifun’ ukuti ixake apa noko ingekenzi ziganeko, kakade
elixesha enyakeni apa aliva; ne khaba lisakohliwe kusika onzonza no
beqeza “ For Christmas Festivities ” hleze ati po engazigibiselanga, ise
ngaba ntwenyana abasebe fun’ ukuzixaka i dilesi maka. Hayi izidalwa
zingateni (dondi care).

ZIYANQOZA.—Kwipepa lase kayapa eli- yi Advertiser, omnye webala ubhale
inqaihi encoma inqubelo pambili yohla- nga oluntsundu, njalo-njalo.
Ifike le- nteto ipikiswe ligwangq’apa ese silazi ukutiya kwalo umntu
webala, ize lifike lizibize ngegama elingu “ Colonial Writer.” Ndilusizi
ukuba ndimfutshane esi Ngesine ngeseke ndakunceda “ Native Writer,” kuba
kaloku ezi zetu intloko zagqwagqwa ngo Hoha, ititshala ezindala,
zingekabiko ezi zamaneshoni ze “ Dyu- dyuliyas nozi Kwensikwensi."

IMITSHATO.—Soloko ndaqalayo ukuta- bata usiba ukubhala lama nqabaza,
andi- zange nditete ngamtshato, kuba asinto ungafumane uyalame apa;
kodwa inta- shinge itshile ngoku. Ibe mitatu ngo- kulandelelana; endoti
ndibalule owo- kugqibela (ndokohlwa kubiza amagama since ungeka
hlokonyiswa ngesaziso kwi Mvo) nditi, lo walixaka ikhaba elinge- kabi
nabu civility, pofu linxiba amaqina- ne bhutsi, ngalendlela—ukusinga
etya- likeni i cab zazine coachman ezintatu ezazinxibe ngokufanayo;
yekoko uku- puma etyalikeni ukusinga kwa Ntamo- bomvu.

NGOKUHLWA—ibe yimiqodi ukusinga kona, kuba umntu wawuhlaba wabanzi ukuba
kuya kuvunywa nozi pisi ngo hadi, okwenene siye safika, site sisapula-
pule ingomana ezimbini zivunywa nga manene, sasisagxeleshe ku manenekazi
ingoma ezimnandi, suka kwasu isipo- mpotela apa abati ngu Mbambo Zinomxo
sati, “ Hlalani niti tu! tina apa asize kupulapula ikonsati size
kubhasa,” utsho sekudala ehlabela u “ Mkwekazi siyapela ngamagxagxa,”
sasuka yasisanxwe uku- tata kwentwazana apa ezilumpantsho lusecaleni;
yabake iyapela into engeba ibimnandi.

ngezi nqam itsholoza kamnandi. Umgcini-sihlalo ucele lamanene okupo-
seni amazwi: Rev. B. S. Dlepu ; Messrs. P. Rwexu, W. Vangisa nento ka
Yokwe ; yaza intshebe ka Mdolomba yenza izi- manga kwingoma eti “ Zum,
zum, zum, zitshwjntaka zase Tamara,” esitsho ka­mnandi u Miss S. Matsha

ASITYANGA.—Ivakele kakubi lenteto xa sipumayo. Eyona nto esingaba
siyiku- pela yona isheleni kwi timiti ezinomgqa- iselo onje yini? iko
iti ne banise; kuko ukunika isandla kumsebenzi olungileyo ? Inga iya
kuba luncedo lunina losheleni xa nga silindele ukuba sipunge kube katatu
kuyo, ukuze sigoduke isisu sina- ma “ bhaxa.” Hanewu ! lutsha, suba
kufunwa izandla ezikululekileyo zonce- do, not big stomachs and wide
throats, Wabetu!

AMANANI EMPAHLA E QONCE.—Ngalevek’ igqitileyo kulengiswe—Amahashe
amabin’ nge £16 10/, enye Iponi nge £5 12/6, enye Iponi nge £3 12/6,
Inkabi yenkomo £7 10/, enye Inkabi £7, enye Inkabi £6 15/, enye Inkabi
£8 1/, enye Inkabi £7 7/6. Inkabi ezintlanu nge £7 1/ inye, enye Inkabi
£7 16/, enye Inkabi nge £5 15/.

IMARIKE YASE KOMANI.—Kulomsito ama­nani ayemi ngoluhlobo ngo Mgqibelo
waye leveki idlulileyo :—Inkuni 30/, 50/ ngeflara ; Irasi eluhlaza 10d
ngedasini; Amatanga 6d lilinye ; Ihabile 9/6, 11/ nge 100 lezitungu ;
Ihabile 4/, 4/3 nge 100lbs; Itapile 6/6 ku 11/6 ngengxowa; Ibhotolo 3d
ne 6d ngeponti ; Amaqanda 8d ne 1/1 ngedasini; Inyama yegusha 3d ne 4d
iponti.

IPRODUSI E QONCE.—Ngeveki epelileyo kufike ucingo Iwase London luxela
uku- hla kwamanani nge 1d. to 1d. kwi Grisi, ne 1d. to 1d. kobunganeno
kobo. Ezi- ndaba zibange ukuba ehle kunene ama­nani. baye noboya
obutengiswayo bu- ngepi. Oku kuhla kwamanani kuya kubanga ilahleko
enkulu kuma fama nabarwebi abasaza kubutengisa uboya babo kwimisito
yeli. Emi ngoluhlobo amanani:—Obuhlanjiweyo 6 3/4d to 7d ; Igrisi 4 3/4d
to 4 1/2d; obunye 4d to 4 1/2d ; Obu- rabaxa 2 1/2d to 23d. Imfele
Zegusha 3d ezicetyiweyo, 4 3/4d ezinoboya obude; Eze Seyibhokwe 4 3/4d;
Ezenkomo ezomiswe elangeni 4 1/3d, ezimanzi 2 1/4d, ezisawutiwe- yo 3
3/4d ; Impondo 2d.

nge 100lbs; Ihabile 1/1, 4/ nge 100lbs; Inkuku 8d, 1/3 inye; Amadada 2/5
lilinye; Amatanga 1/6, 3/10 ngedazini ; Utngubo 18/ ngekulu ; I-erityisi

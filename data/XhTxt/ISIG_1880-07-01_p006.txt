IZINTO NGE ZINTO

ALIWAL NORTH.

UKUSINDA OKUBI.—-Kute ngolwesibini ngomhla we 18th ku May, kwasinda
kakubi umfo ontsundu obesebenza ebuloreni yase Aliwal North. Ute
esebenza epezuln suka watyibilika waya kuposeka ezantsi emlanjeni,
ubunzulu obunyawo zi 60, nakuba ebehle wancedwa kulongozi ebe- kuyo
ekubonakala ukuba eyonanto imbulele kakulu kuku hlukuhleka.—Northern
Post.

INGOZI EMBI.—Kuhle ingozi kumfo ontsundu, pakati ko 9 no 10 o’clock
kusasanje. Ababantu pofu bebebabini, be- manukunyusa amatye ukuwasa
kubaki bewanyusa ngeplanga etile ebilungiselwe oko, kutyibilike omnyeke
wati ngoku gxadazela wayakugila omnye lowa, waposeka ezantsi ke lowo,
ukupakama kwendawo awe kuyo zinyawo ezi 10 mhlaumbi 12. Olosizana lute
ukuwa kwalo lwabeteka ngomhlana, kwati kanjalo kwalandela i crowbar
(yilento itwala ilitye nokuba ngamatye) inelityekazi elikulu, yambeta
esifubeni. Usiwe kwaoko kwi Albany Hospital bati asikeva ukuba wenzakele
kangaka nanina.

VICTORIA WEST.

UKUZIBULALA.—Liti ipepa lakona ekutiwa yi Messenger Ite indodana etile,
ebudala bukwiminyaka 17, egama lingu John Duke Jackson, obesele minyaka
ili 18 esitoreni, kuqa­la e Victoria West emva koko e Procesfontein,
pantsi ko- yisekazi u Mr. John. S. Jackson, obengomnye wabagcini bake.
Lonkeke eloxesha ubengumntu ocapula kunene emanukushinta nakuba ebehlala
efunyanwa futi, anikwe izohlwayana ezilula. Wati roqo yena noko wacapula
ede waza kufanje.

Ude uyisekazi wamsongela ngokumtumela elwandle, ayekuba ngumatilose,
okunye amse ebu Tyipukolweni C. M. Rifles), wati noko zonke ezondlela
angazitandiyo, wa- hambisa ukushinta oku. Ubonakala ukuba ubengate qete
kanye, kude kwaba lolwe 4th ku May lo eseba yena, eso- hwlaywa, ekute
kwangawo lomso walomini wasela ityefu wafa, pofu kulingwe kunene
ukunqanda ingozi.—Victoria West Messenger.

EZELENI.

UKUDUTYULWA KOM XOSA.—Kute kusasa ngecawa, ngo suku lwama 23rd ku May,
kwakuko ma Xosa abemane efuna ubusi, kwakuko siqiti sitile emlanjeni
abate ababantu bawelela kuso, makutike kanti kuko Jeliman lingu
Christian Zitski elunxwemeni lomlambo, lisuke ladubula izitonga zada
zazitatu, sati esi sesitatu sabeta komnye wababafo basebeziposa bobatatu
emanzini bawelela ngelinye icala, wati lo udutyulweyo wasilela akuba
ehambe iyardi ezi 200, wawa pantsi; Lomntu usengumfana ubudala bake
yiminyaka ema 20; uteke wasiwa e Qonce e Hospitaleni ngu Mr. A.
Ninnreman, ote wabuye wanika ubungqina eofisini. Ubanjiweke sitetanje u
Zitski lowo ngu Mr. Daly inknai yamadindala, usetolongweni.—Cape
Mercury.

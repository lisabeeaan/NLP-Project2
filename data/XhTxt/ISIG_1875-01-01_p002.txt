Owesibini umfanekiso ubonisa indlu yetyalike ese Roma abati ukuyibiza yi
St. Peters. Yeyona tyalike inkulu kuzo zonke ezilunge nelihlelo bati
yiremente yama Roma. Ukuqalwa kwayo ukwakiwakwaku ngomnyaka 1407; kodwa
kwada kwangowe l612 ingekafezwa kutitu. Ubude bayo ngapambili zinyawo
368, ububanzi 145. Imi lotyalike kwindawo apo bati bona ngokwabo ukuteta
wangcwatyelwa kona umpostile U-Petros, ikufupi apo bati wabulawelwa
kona, noko tina singazikolwayo imbali zokubulawa nokungcwatywa kwake
E-Roma. Ubukulu bayo ngapakati asibubo nabo. Eludongeni kujinga
imifanekiso ekolise ngeyamadoda ekutiwa ngawangcwele ; pantsi kugangatwe
ngeli litye kutiwa libastile, lenziwa amabalabala lalaliswa ngento
entle. Ububanzi kwezinye indawo ipantsi ukufika kumakulu amabini enyawo.
Sikona apo isihlalo sika Popo, apo ahlala kona mhla ngemini ezinkulu
ezinje ngale niyibona emfanekisweni. Kuko kanjalo nendawo apo kuhleli
kuvuta ilampu ezilikulu elinancinye elinesibini, ezingacinywayo emini
nase busuku. Lomfanekiso ubonisa into ekuba yiyo ngeveki ekuhlanganelwe
isidlo ekutiwa yi easter esimiselwe ukukumbuza ukuvuka kuka Kristu.
Sisidlo esikulu kubantu abasigcinayo, abati singekabiko basilungiselele
ngokuzila ukudla. Lwakufika usuku olumisiweyo bahlangana ndawonye beze
pambi kwesihlalo sika Popo auika kuso amatamsanqa. Kuhlangana kulo ndawo
amadoda nabafazi, abakulu nabancinane, abamahashe nabenyawo, indidi
zonke zabantu. Usuku lokubako kwaso eso sidlo lufumape lumiswe nokuba
kukulupina kwezipa-

kati kolwa 21 ku March nolwa 25 ku April. Baye abantu baye kona bevela
kumigama emide, kuba abeyase Roma ityalike batanda ukuya pambi kuka Popo
noko yenziwayo nakwezinye indawo. Eso sidlo sisagcinwa nakweyase England
ityalike.

CAPTAIN BLYTH.

SIYA vuyisana nama Mfengu apesheya kwe Nciba ukuba ibuye yabuya I-
Mantyi yawo U-Captain Blyth noko kwakusekuko abati akuyi kuba njalo.
Nangelixeshana lifutshane aseleke walihlala apa uselebonisile inyameko
anayo ngabo bapantsi kwake. Uzamele ukuzityelelela indawo asebenza kuzo
Amamfengu ezindleleni zenqwelo zomlilo nokuva ukuba anezikalazo kusini
na, nokuba angatini na ukuwalungisela ukuba ziko. Siya temba ukuba ama
Mfengu aya kuhlala embulela umfo ontliziyo injalo, amlandele ngentliziyo
evumayo kwizinto zokukanya aya kuwakokelela kuzo. Uza ewapatele £15,000
zokwaka isikolo sawo. Lomali ihlanganiswe ngu Dr. Stewart. Sigqiba
ngelokuti akwaba imantyi zonke ezipakati kwabantsundu zibe zinje ngaye
ngenyameko nenkutalo, nokuzilungisa, nokuzikanyeza, nokuqonda izinto
ezifunekayo nendlela yokuzilungisa, ezonto zisenzelwa abantu
ezibapeteyo, njengoko anjalo U- Captain Blyth. Asitsho ukuti aziko
ezifana naye, kuba bako abafo bo Griffith, kodwa siti akwaba bezinjalo
zonke. Mhlaumbe sobuye simkankanye ngelinye ixesha.

Isikolo salapa E-Lovedale sivalwe ngomhla 22 ka December. Ngalo mini
babebaninzi abazali babantwana abafikelelayo ukuza kubona nokuza kuva
ngomsebenzi obusenziwa ngabantwana babo. Kubonakele ngendlela ezininzi
ukuba abazali bakunyamekele ukufunda kwentsapo, kuba abanye
bengakwenzanganto ukuzala kwemilambo nobubi bendlela. Site ke ngokubona
oko kunyameka kwabo sacinga ukuba mhlaumbi bako abangabanga nakufikelela
abangatandayo ukwazi ukuba bekusenziwa nina kwesi sikolo. Solinga ukuti
sibekele entloko.

IZAKIWO EZITSHA asikuko nokuba ziyafuneka kuxinenwe kwindawana zonke.

DR. STEWART—Usekwela pesheya apo aye ku- linga ukufumana uncedo lokuba
kwakiwe izindlu. Siyazi ukuba engati ulibele nje asisiso sihle. Usele
hlanganise £2500 yokuncedisa ukwaka. Intlanganiso epata iremente
ezikwamanye amazwe itembise £3000. Ngapandle kwaleyo uhlanganise £1500
eziya kuncedisa kwesa pesheya kwe Nciba isikolo. Ngapandle komsebenzi
wokubuta imali unomsebenzi wokufuna amadoda angeza kusebenzela ilungelo
yabantsundu. Ufike kanjalo wayila eliqinga lokuba kubeko isikolo esiyi
Livingstonia esiya kuba sisikumbuzo sika Dr. Living­stone. Kufuneka ke
ezilungiselele nendlela zaso. Indawo enkulu yaso iyakuba kukungenisa
ubukristu nokukanya pakati kwabantu beli lizwe. Likuze ibe nokuqalwa
kuya kufuneka £10,000. Useleyi fumene indoda eya kuya kona isipate noko
yena esaya kuhlala engumongameli waso noko ikaya lake lilapa E-Lovedale.
U-Dr. Stew­art lowo uyakubuya ekupeleni kuka January.

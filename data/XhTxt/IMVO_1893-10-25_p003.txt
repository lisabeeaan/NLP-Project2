kumsha. Indawo yobukumsha ibizaliswe nguye ngemfanelo. Waye eyindoda
eyenza konke okusemandleni ake, ukusa ku Mantyi intetoyomntu njengoko
injalo, kungeko kete lakucalula ukuqelana, nabuhlobo, nokuvelana notile,

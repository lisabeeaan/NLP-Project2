AMATAMBO EMFUNDO

[ISAAC WAUCHOPE],

1.  Eli ligama lepetshana elibhalwe ngu mhlobo wetu u Mr. ISAAC MOTAUNG
    Umvangeli wase Rafu. Alivakali mnandi igama lalo. Mhlaimbi
    kungalunga umbhali eke wachaza into eliteta yona eligama, amatambo
    emfundo.

2.  Alinaxabiso likulu kwabanokuzifundela i Commentary zesi Ngesi
    ngokwabo, waye nombhali engakangele kwabo ekubhaleni kwake; ukangele
    kubashumayeli nabanye abasebenzeli be Nkosi abangawafumananga
    amalungelo emfundo. Linexabiso elikulu ke kwabo. Bekuya kuba
    lilungelo kumadoda eplan nabanye abaqalayo ukushumayela ukulamkela
    elipepa.

3.  Ekubeni bebaninzi abadla ngokudibanisa u Filipu um-Postile, lo
    urbhalwe ngaye ngoku, nomnye u Filipu, um-Dikoni (Izenzo 6: 3-5)
    owaya kushumayela e Samaria, (8: 5-8), selenga umbhali ngeyifikile
    londawo yokwahlula abo Filipu babini; 1. Unentloni; 2. Ubutuntu
    ingqondo; 3. Usidenge; ngati kum umbhali uzitsolise ngokugqitileyo,
    zaye indawo ezincomekayo noko ate zintatu ebalule mbini—eyokubiza u
    Nataniel neyokumpendula kwake. Okwam mna imibuzo ka Filipu yimibuzo
    eyarola impendulo zoncedo ku Yesu ezintweni ababefana bonke aba
    Disipile ukungazazi. Kwaye kona ukuti akucelwa ngama Grike aye ku
    Andreya kubonisa ubulali buka Filipu. Kodwa ke umbhali naye
    ukululekile ukulandela ineazoayitandayo xa zininzi indlela
    zokuchazwa kwendawo.

Lamanqaku ndiwenza ngokunga umzalwana wam angabona ukuba amalinga ake
akawi pantsi; simkangele simtandazela kuwo. Bonke abam abakokeli baya
kulitabata—ndifuna idazini.

UBOYA OBUHLANJIWEYO.

[INCWADI ETUNYELWE UMHLELI.]

NKOSI,—Sivumele salatise aba fundi bako umonakaio omkulu okule ngakumbi
ngalomcebo wokugqibela, ongulo wokudibanisa uboya obungahlanjwanga
nobuhlanjwe buqingata kuboya obuhlanjiweyo.

Uboya obuhlanjiweyo babantsundu bufumene igama elihle apa nakwimalike za
Pesheya iminyaka emininzi kuba bebuyinto ebekutiwa buyiyo — uboya
obuhlanjwe bhetele pambi kokuchetywa, buchetywe bungekabi natititi
(igrisi). Kodwa igama labo elihle lisemngcipekweni wokuba lipele.
Imichebo etile egqitileyo iziqhuma ezininzi zoboya obhlanjiweyo
zifunyenwe zixutywe noboya obungahlanjiweyo, oko kukuti uboya
obungayanga emlanjeni — umhlaumbi uboya obuhleli ixesha elide emva
kokuba buhlanjiwe bada babuya bayi grisi kwa kona. Kwixesha lokugqibela
into eninzi yoboya obutengiswe njengoboya obuhla­njiweyo apa bufunyenwe
buxutyiwe baza batengiswa ngolohlobo ke. Sicebisa bonke abanegusha,
ngelungelo leziqu zabo, ukuba babuhlambisise uboya babo ukuze babuchebe
babupakishe babahlule kobungahlanjiweyo. okanye bangabi sabuhlamba
kanye, Abatengi beli nabe- laseYurope banokubunika ixabiso ngentembeko
enkulu uboya balamaqela mabini — kodwa xa “ buxutyiweyo ” uboya,
bayapazama ukuze ke balifenyeze ixabiso ngaKumtengisi. Uboya obuxu-
tyiweyo buyabonakalisa uboya obuhlanjiwevo, yabe ke indoda
eyakulahlekelwa eKugqibeleni ingumninigusha. Abarwebi bona bangakohliswa
kanye nakabini kunye nabevenkile ; kodwa bayakuzikusela ekugqibeleni
kulahlekelwe umninigusha. Ukuba bonke abelungu bevenkile bebengati
bangavumi ukubutenga uboya obuxutyiweyo obunjengokungati bobuhlanjiweyo
bebengaba benza ubulumko, kodwa inxenye yabo ayenjenjalo, baye ke
ngokwenjenjalo okwexesha, elitile bekutaza ukungatembeki pakati kwabo
babuxubayo.

ISAZISO SIKA RULUMENTE.—NO. 48, 1691.

Kwi Office yo Mpati-Koloni, e Kapa, 13th January, 1891.

Ngamandla namagunya awatwese nge Siqendu sesixenxe so Mteto wo Bhalo,
1890’ Umhlekazi i Ruluneli, ngecebq le Bhunga Elipeteyo, walatisa ukuba
kuzo zonke izixeko, idolopu, nemizana e Koloni apo kuko Amagqugula
apeteyo, umkangeli wo Bhalo uya kuvelela indawo yokuba kushiywe amapepa
o Bhalo, kwanezalatiso ezingcinva kwalomapepa ngosuku olurgapambili
kolwe 5th April, 1891 kuzo zonke izindlu ezikulondawo awobona zifanele.

Abakangeli bo Bhalo baya gunyaziewa ukuba njengokubona kwabo benze ukuba
amapepa o Bhalo olu ashiywe nokuba kukuzipina izindlu pakati
kweziqingata Zobhalo olo.

Xa ke amapepa lawo ate ashiywa endlwini, kuyimfanelo yomgicindlu, okanye
umgcini wayo, ukulibhala okanye enze ukuba libhalwe ngokazeleyo,
ngokuyinene, nangokulungileyo ngangoko azi ngako, okunye axelelwayo,
okunye akoliweyo, lomapepa abe eshiywe kulondlu, abhale igama lake, aze
awatumele, mhlaumbi awacikele ekubizweni kwawo, egqityiwe
ngendlela-ndlelana zonke, ku Mbhali owabizayo.

Umbhali wofika ngo Mvulo, 6th April, 1891, mhlaumbi kwakamsinya emva
koko, awabize amapepa abeshiywe ezindlwini, awoti ekuwamkeleni kwake
awaqwalasele, azanelise ukuba abhalwe ngendlela na, awoti ukuba ufumana
ukuba akenjiwanganjalo awazalise ngengxelo anokuyifumana.

Apo sukuba umbhali engawashiyanga amapepa o Balo, umbali wowabhala
ngokwake, ileyo indlu eyibhala kwipepa layo elilodwa.

Ngendlu kutetwa isakiwo, umqubi, inqwelo, iuqwelana yamahashe, nayipina
indawo apo kulala umntu, nasipina isikepe, nenqanawa engaba
semazibukweni e Koloni yase Kapa.

Yimfanelo yomnini ndlu nomntu oyipeteyo ukupendulu kamsinyane,
ngokuzeleyo, nangokulungeleleneyo yonke imibuzo engabuzwa ngumbali.

Umnini-ndlu, nomntu ongaba uselo nayo oko wamangala, okanye ota
akakatalela ukuzalisa, umhlaumbi ukubeka igama lake, umhlaumbi ukunikela
amapepa o Bhalo kumbali; nongati okunye asuke amangale, umhlaumbi
angakataleli kupendula, okunye asuke axoke ekupenduleni, okunye axele
ubuxoki okanyo enze ukuba kuxelwe ubuxoki, nongatintela nalipina igosa
lo Bhalo ekwenzeni umsebenzi walo, angadliwa iponti zontlanu zemali,
angati engenazo abe nokufakwa entolongweni esebenza umhlaumbi
engasetanzi nzimn ixesha elingengapezulu kwenyanga, de ale uhlaulolwe
kwakamsinyane.

Kweso soblwayo akaqukwa umntu oyakuti ngenxa yento azoyikolayo asuke
ashiye umhlaumbi amangale ukuxela unqulo lwake, angati ukutyebisa ezo
zoyikalo zaka selebhala kumqolo olungiselelwe oko welizwi eliti
“Object.” Kananjalo eso sohlawayo asiji kubekwa kumntu ongati ngenani
lemfuyo yake engalazi ngokute nqo, asuke enze umlingeniselo nje omalunga
nenyaniso.

Nalipina igosa lo Bhalo elingafika libhengezo into zo Bhalo olo liya
kudliwa iponti ezintlanu (£5), liti lingenazo lifakwe entolongweni
ixesha elingapezu kwenyanga.

J. W. SAUER, Umpati.Koloni.

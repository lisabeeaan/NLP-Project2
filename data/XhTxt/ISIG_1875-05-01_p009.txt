Emngqesha, April 23, 1875.

Nkosi yam engu Mhleli we Sigidimi, ndiyaku- cela ukuba undamkelele esi G-idimini lamazwana alandelayo. Angokububa kuka Francis intonaba- zar.a ka D. Ntsikana ebihlala kuninakulu kwa Nomgwadla (osesipaluka so Mngqesha). Lento- mbazana isishiyileyo ubudala imayela neshumi elinesibmi iminyaka. Ihliwe yicesine, yayibamba ngomqala nokukohlela, yaza yati lomhla ibube ngawo yaxaxaza igazi kakulu. Hele iveke yanye yabuba ngolwe Sine 15th April kweyesibini iveke Incwatywe ngo Mgqibelo selede wafika noyise, kwaposwa amazwana ebantwini yi Titshere yayo. (Ibindikolisile kanye esikolweni ngesimilo nange- mfundo yayo. Ibuba sendite ndiyakuyikupela kwi Klass engayambili. lyalilelwa kakulu ngaba- lingane bayo.)

Kude kwati kona ngolwesihlanu andaba naku- hambisa kakuhle esikolweni ngenxa vosizi elusa- tsheni. Yasuka yasisililo xa kuhanjiswa I-Bible- lessen Into endimangalisileyo ngesimilo sale nto- mbazana isishiyileyo ibikukutanda kwayo indlu ka Tixo ngangohlobo lokuba iti nokuba kutiwa mayisale nendlu xa kuyiwa etyalikeni ingake ivu- me kude kuncanywe. Ibe injalonje iyakuti ilila intsimbi ibe selingasemnyango. Ukutanda kwayo ukutandaza ibi ngaposwa mini ngokuhlwa na- k.usasa kusati ainu, ibide ingxoliswe maxa wambi yile yokuba lentombazana ikwcnza nje kohke oku 1 ibingekazinikeli enkonzweni ye Nkosi. Ite ya- kuguquka yanjalo ngokumbi. Kubonakala ukuba le ntombazana ite yakuguquka yanomnqweno wo- kumka kwelilizwe.

Nditsho kuba kute ngecawa etile knfupi kwa- buba umntwana. Yaza yati lentombazana kwe- nye eyayihamba nayo azi ukuba ngu wupina kuti sobabini oyakulandela lomntwana. Okunye ziti ezinye intombazana ite rgamhla utile kufupi eku- beni ihliwe ngulomkuhlane ; baye bencokola ngomhla womgwebo behleli ngase ndlwini ye Titshere emva kwesikola. “ Andingi lomini ingafika ndisekulomhlaba. Ndinga ingafika ndingaseko apa.” Kuluvuyo bazalwana ukunazisa ukuba umsebenzi wenkosi upilile kanye apa. Ngoku- kodwa elusatsheni Iwesikula. Ite pambi koku- buba kwayo makutandazwe ukuba Inkosi iyenze intando yayo kuyo. Ite kanjalo andisatandiuku- hlala kwelilizwe kwabuzwa ukububa akukubaku- mbulana abazali bako, yati yona ukuba ndifele E-Nkosini andiyikukumbula nanye into yelilizwe Kunjalo ke ukumka kwalontombi isishiya silila sivuya. Mandipele.

P. Tyamzashe.

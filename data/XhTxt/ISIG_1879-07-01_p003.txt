Mandingayi shiyi indawo yokuba kwenye ipatuloni ebipunyiwe kutinjwe
imipu ekuma- shumi amahlanu elutshabeni. Inkoliso ibanjwa ifunyanwa
emiweweni’. Ekuqaleni kuka June ute u-Colonel Brabant, oselepete imikosi
esikundleni sika Griffith, wayila iqinga lokuke kufunzwe entabeni.
Imikosi yake uyahlule yamaqela matatu, yafunza kwindawo ezahlukileyo.
Kuqale inkanunu zazama ukudiliza indonga zabesutu, yati kodwa ngokucana
kwa Baphuti nokunqaba kwendawo abakuyo ayabi nakubambelela. U-Brabant
uti kucingo alutumeleyo. “ Ndite kuba kungati kuko amaroba ndacinga
ukufunza kwindawo ezintatu, andapumelela kuzo zontatu. Amadoda am
akabanga nako ukukangelana nembumbulu za Baphuti ezicana usizi,
kwakuvakala ukuba banayo kunene iruluwa nembumbulu. Enye yenkanunu
yonakele ipelelwe nayi ruluwa.” Abantu abenzakeleyo, babe bahlanu
abasele kona, ingxwe. lera zalishumi elinambini, ekukoyo ukoyika ukuba
abanye abayi kupumelela. Intaba abakuyo Abaphuti asikuko nokuba
inqabile. Ingalungela inkanunu ezinkulu neqela elikulu lomkosi,
ngokukodwa owamasoldati. Indawo eyona ise ibonakala okwangoku ukuba
akufiki mikosi yimbi kukuvingcela kude kusebenze indlala.

Kule veki yokuza kushicilela kwetu ipepa, kuvakala ukuba uya funa umkoei
wama Lawo ase Katala nangase Mpofu ukuya kulwa kwa Morosi. Inani lawo
bati likumakulu amabini namatatu. Owabesutu ekwakutiwa ungamawaka
amabini awukavakali kulamadabi aliwayo ukuba ude wenza nina wona. Siya
temba ke ukuba aya kuvakala amalawo njengokuba evakala amavalanti.

UMHLA WENKOSAZANA PESHEYA
KWE NCIBA.

IMINI yokuzalwa kwe Nkosazana u-Victoria ibe yimini enkulu yemigcobo
kuwo onke ama Ngesi. Sakukangela imisebenzi evelayo kubantu abapantsi
kwayo akummangaliso ukuba mabauvuyele lomhla, bavakalise umnqweno wabo
wokunga ungaman’ ukubako futi. Kananjalo akummangaliso ukuba mabati na-
bezinye izizwe abapantsi kwayo bauvuyele kuba yona isinga bonke
ngokufanayo bangacunyelwa bahlale kakuhle.

Kuyinto entle ke ngoko ukuba maziti izizwe ezintsundu zalapa wakufika
nazo ziwugcobele, zenze imisebenzi yokubonakalisa ukuba
ziyawutakazelela. Ngoku kokugqibela obuko abapesheya kwe Nciba
bakubonise ngendlela ezimbini ukuwubonga kwabo. Eyokuqala ibe ngumboniso
wempahla nezityalo obese Gcuwa Ngomgqibelo, ngalomini kanye yokuzalwa
kwe Nkosazana. Umqali weloqinga lomboniso kuvakele ukuba yayi ngu

wupina umlambo. Ezinye intlobo zezilimo za- zizonke—ezinje ngama
kapetshu nozitapile nolunye uvuvana esingenakululanda nakuba lubonisa
umkondo ohanjwa ngabalimi babo.

Kwimisebenzi yezandla kwabako ozitafile, ozifestile, ozidesika,
ozilokwe, omifanekiso nokunye, esati sakukukangela sakumbula lento ati
oka Soga ku “ Hambo ” ngofaningofani. Asisenasituba sokungena
ekuzicazululeni zonke into ezaziko. Kodwa asinakulishiya elokuti izinto
ezati ngokucikizeka kokwenziwa, ngokufezeka ukuba leyo ngohlobo lwayo
ingabi nasizatu angade umntu akale ngaso zaba mbini; yaba sisando
esasenziwe ngoka Figiland u-John, nentsibi enxitywa emqaleni eyayenziwe
yintombazana yakwa Kentane. Ngezonto zombini baba baninzi abenza inkani
yokuti azenziwanga ngumntu ontsundu—kuba kaloku into eyenziwe nguye
kuhleli kulindelwe ngabatile ukuba iya kuba nesipako! Zona ke zombini
ezonto zalandula, zabatelela abenzi bazo zaludumisa uhlanga oluntsundu.
Okunene zazingezinto zinkulu kodwa ngokucokiswa kokwenziwa zabusa.

Enye into eyenza ukuba lomini ibe mnandi kwababeko yaba zizikolo. Zafika
zamaqela matatu, sako esakwa Feltman, esavuyisa ngegubu, salibeta
ngokutandekayo, kwaba kutsala nokunxiba kwababeti balo, kumbonga
umfundisi wabo nomxasi walo u-Feltman. Sabako esase Gcuwa ekaya
ekwatandeka kuso ubuninzi babantwana nokubonakala kokuba umfundisi wabo
ngeyeba utanda ukubafundisa izinto zokukanya ezinje ngamagubu ukuba ube
nabantu abamxasayo abaqabukileyo njengokuba esiqabukele esake u-Feltman.
Sabako esase Xilinxa eyafika ititsher yaso yasimisa esitubeni, yema
namadodana nentokazi zelozwe bayihlaba ingoma, kwada kwalibubu malunga
naso njengokungati nabo banegubu.

Asisenakungena kumavalanti, nakozika- nunu, nezilima nemidlalo
yamakwenkwe, nakwabati ngokungxamela inzuzo benza ubugqwira bokuza
kubonisa imbuyabatwa, abayixela negama layo, mase sisiti bonke ngalo
mini ababeko bazigcobisa kanye, abatanda ukufunda, babona izinto
ezininzi zesimilo sabantu, waza akuti u-Captain Blyth makudunyelwe
Inkosazana bonke bavuma ngovuyo, esati napesheya kolwandle ayiku-
kufnmana ukuvuyelwa okungapezulu.

Enye indicia ekute kwagcotywa ngayo ukuba Inkosazana ingena komnye
umnyaka ibe yintlanganiso yokuviwa kwezikolo ebise Luxwesweni. Ngalo
mini kubutene zonke ezilunge nalo Mantyi yase Luxwesweni, zaman’
ukutelekiswa kukangelwa ukuba ngubanina wasipina oya kukupa abanye
kwinto etile. Esingesini bakutshwa yinkwenkwana eminyaka ilishumi abati
ngu Alexander Fu-

Mr. King Imantyi yase Gcuwa ekaya. Wa- likumbula kuse kusele intsuku
ezilishumi kukupela ufike lomhla mkulu waza waliquba kwabanye bonke
abalivayo balivuyela kunene. Umntu wase mzini, owayengaxelelwanga ukuba
lento icingwe sekukade kangaka wayengeqondi akukangela enanini labantu
nempahla, nase kucokisweni kwezilimo ezaziko.

Njengoko kudla ngokuba njalo kwimicimbi enyusa abantu abantsundu pesheya
kwe Nciba, babe liqela abamhlope ababeze kubonela, beze kukutaza ngobuko
babo. Ezimantyini ezaziko yaye iyinkulu yazo u-Captain Blyth, neyase
Gcuwa u-Mr. King, neyase Luxwesweni u-Mr. Gladwin. Abanye abamhlope
asingebatini.

Njengoko sesikankanyile babe baninzi ka- kulu abantsundu, amadoda
namankazana nosapo. Ukuyi vuyela kwabo into eyayise nziwa kwabangela
ukuba kungabonakali ukuba lomsebenzi bauve sekumva, baza ubuhle bempahla
zabo nobuninzi bazo, babonisa ukuba abantu balomacala bayenyuka
ngokutyeba nangokutanda izinto ezifanelekileyo. Abayili bomboniso bafike
banyula intili entle kunene ekuya kuhlanganiswana kuyo, eyati lontili
ngalomini yasuka yazala yimfuyo nangabantu, kanti lemfuyo ilapa
akufumananga kuqutywe, kuziswe eziketiweyo. Abaketi bazo
bahlulwa-hlulwa, abanye besiwa ezinkomeni, abanye emahasheni, abanye
kwezinye izinto. Kwinkabi zamahashe kwabako iqela elitandekayo,
elibonisa ukuba kuyazanywa ukupunywa kwingxukuma apa oyibona
ungaxelelwanga ukuba eli lelomntu omnyama. Abamaninzi agxekisa
nawamapolisa, abantu abadume befanele ngokuba nenkabi ezintle
ezomeleleyo. Yati enye indawo etandeke ngokukulu laba linani lamahashe
akwaziyo ukuhamba—abonisa na- ngalondawo ukuba abaniniwo baqele ukuwa-
pata kakuhle. Kwancomeka kakulu nase zinkomeni nakuba singatshongo ukuti
ku- ngangasemahasheni. Ezinye intlobo zemfuyo ezaziko sezobonakala
emabasweni esiwanika kwenye indawo yeli pepa.

Enye indawo esati tina sayincoma ngesi- ngenakukuteta apa yaba zizilimo,
nemisebenzi yezandla. Kokulinyiweyo sabuka ngokukodwa ubuhle bezinto,
nobuninzi bentlobontlobo ezilinywayo. Kwabonakala ngokungati zivela
kubantu abangalungiseleli ukuze badle kube kupela, koko befuna ukuba
into yabo iti imnandi emlonyeni ibe intle elisweni. Ubuninzi
bentlobobasincamisakwase mboneni into esiqele ukuyibona. Abalapa
amagwangqa nogazimtyetyetye, alapa amarwexu, zapelela intlobo ezimhlope,

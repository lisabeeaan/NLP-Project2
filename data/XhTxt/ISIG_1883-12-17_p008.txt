ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 17, 1883.

ISAZISO SAKWA RULUMENI.—Ese 875, 1883.

  Kwi Of si Yompatiswa Mihlaba ne Micimbi,

  EKUBENI enikwe amandla u Rulumeni, sisiqendu sokuqala “ Somteto
  Wemihlaba Engenabaniniyo,” we 3 wo 1879
  ukuba ahambise ngokubona kwake ngokumalungana nemihlaba
  elahliweyo.ngoluhlobo lucaziweyo kwesi siqendu-

  silandelayo, olu:—

  “ Xa zeke irafu emelwe kuhlaulwa ku Rulumeni we Koloni, ihlaulelwa
  indawo mhlayimbi impahla yakomkulu
  yakuba ingahlaulwanga kwada kwayiminyaka elishumi, yaba londawo
  mhlayimbi lompahla selilahliwe mhlayimbi ishiyiwe,
  yashiywa ingenamntu, umniniyo mhlayimbi umqeshi njengokuba kungaba
  njalo mhlayimbi, walondawo mhlayimbi walo-
  mpahla, mhlayimbi ommeleyo ngokomteto engenakufunyanwa, iyakuba
  yimfanelo, ngokomteto, ye Ruluneli ukwazisa
  indawo mhlayimbi impahla esazeke injalo ukuba ayinamniniyo kwi
  Government Gazette (ipepa lakomkulu) nokuba
  kukulipina ipepa angagqiba ekwaziseni kulo, amaxesha angengapantsi
  kwelinye enyanga nganye kwezintatu ezilande-
  lanayo ; kuze kuti ukuba pakati kwezo nyanga zintatu, kususela kusuku
  lokungenisa kwamhlamnene isaziso esisesi-
  balulwe akufiki mninimhlaba mhlayimbi mqeshi, njengokuba kungaba
  njalo, walomhlaba mhlayimbi impahla, akufiki
  nammeli wake ngokomteto, abonise ukuba umhlaba mhlayimbi imfuyo leyo
  yeyake mhlayimbi yeyabo, aze ahlaule
  irafu, ebingasahlaulwa, i Ruluneli iyakuti emva kokupela kwezo nyanga
  zintatu zibalulweyo iwutabate ube ngowayo
  kwakona lomhlaba mhlayimbi lompahla ize yenze ekutandayo ngawo
  ngokomteto ekuqutywa ngawo ngelo xesha apa
  kule Koloni: Pantsi kwezimeko kodwa ezinjengokuba ibe kanti lomhlaba
  mhlayimbi lomfuyo, izakupatwa ngokwezi-
  miselo zalo mteto, imi endaweni yetyala (under mortgage), izimiselo
  zesibozo, nesitoba, neshumi Zomteto we 9 wo
  1844, ezibekiselela entengisweni yomhlaba mhlayimbi yempahla
  elolohlobo, nokusetyenziswa kwemali zawo, ziyakusetye-
  nziswa malungana nayo.”

  Ngenx’enoku ke kuyaziswa ukuba wonke azi ukuba eziziqwenga Zemihlaba
  zisesitilini sase Dikeni (Victoria
  East) ziyakukangelwa njengezingena baninizo, njengoko sekuxeliwe;
  nokuba u Rulumente wozitabatela kwakuye
  ngomhla wokuqala ka March, 1884, idibe ke ngeloxesha kobako babonisayo
  ngobunq'ina ukuba imihlaba leyo
  banelungelo kuyo, bahlaule imali ezingafinyelelanga, ku Mantyi weso
  sitili, njengoko kumiselweyo kwesi siqendu
  sandulelayo somteto.

  JOHN X. MERRIMAN, Umpatiswa.

  IMIHLABA EKWISITILI SASE DIKENI EKUTIWA AYINABANINIYO.

UMTETO ONGOKUCOKISWA KWAMAKAYA.

Womnyaka we 1883.

  ABANTU balatiswa ngesi saziso kwezi ziqendu Zomteto
  ongoku Cokiswa kwamakaya wonyaka we 1883: —

Isiqendu sa XLVI. Nokuba ngubani omangalayo ukuba
kungene endlwini yake igosa lopeteyo kulo
mandla akuwo, nokuba lelogunyazisiweyo
ngombalo welo gosa, nabani ongxabalaza
endleleni, otukayo, odubulayo, mhlaumbi
owenzakalisayo ngenteto elo gosa mhlaumbi
lomntu, xa ahambisa izimiselo zalo mteto,
woti, efunyenwe enetyala amelwe sisohlwa-
yo esinjengokuhlaula iponti zoshumi, ati
ukuba akahlauli afakwe entolongweni
asebenze nzima mhlaumbi, ixesha elinge-
ngapaya kwenyanga zontatu

Isiqendu sa XLVII. Ngexesha ekoyo ingqakaqa isipata-
mandla sinokumenza nabani opakati kwe-
mida yegunya laso ukuba anike ubungqina
bokuba ubani lowo waqapula kakuhle, ze
ukuba ubani lowo ute akabi nabo ubungqina

  bokuba waqatshulwa aze kanjako amangale ukuba aqatshulwe, woba
  sicenge, enetyala ngazo zombini ezindawo sokuba ahlaule, ngokwala
  ngakunye, iponti zombini, ati ukuba akanakuhlaula amelwe yintolongu
  ixesha elingengapezulu kwentsuku ezisixenxe.

Kwi Ofisi ye Mantyi,

Alice, 4 Dec., 1883.

ALEX. STEWART.

Imantyi.

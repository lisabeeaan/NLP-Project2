UKUMKA KUKA SIR BARTLE FRERE.

Port Elizabeth August 17, 1880.

MR. MHLELI.—

Zininzi indawo ezibe zine ntlanganiso ezibekiselela elu bizweni luka Sir
Bartle Frere; zibonakalisa usizi ezinalo ngokubizwa kwake ngu Rulumente
wasekaya komkulu, pesheya ko Lwandle. Indawo esingazi kankanyayo li Kapa
ne Bayi, ne Rini, ne Qonce, nezinye ebekutiwa ukubizwa kwazo zi (Public
Meetings.)

Zifike ezintlanganiso zabonakalisa ngoku mhlope usizi lwazo ngenteto
zamamadoda asukileyo ateta. Abonisa ngoku mhlope ukulunga ko laulo luka
Sir Bartle Erere, nanga malungelo awafumeneyo ngoku bako kwake apa e
South Africa. Nokuba aba mhlope apa e Africa abazange babe ne Rulumeli
elumke, nekwazi uku laula intlanga zonke njengaye. Ke ezintlanganiso
ziteta pezu kwento emhlope kuba bezenzanje seku mhlope ukuba indoda leyo
iyawela, (yanga ingebi sabuya betu) ukuya kupendula ngendawo ezi
kalazekayo ngolaulo lwayo. Sifike sincome inteto ka Captain Salmond,
etetwe ngobuciko. ngengqondo enkulu, ote xa amanye amadoda ebemana
epulula ukuteta kwawo. Wati yena akufika wayibamba emsileni yati uku-
kaba oku yadala izibakala waye u Mr. Salmond emangaliswe kukuba lamadoda
onke amanukusuka, isuke enye iteta into ebitetwa yenye; watsho
wakankanya u Sir George Grey, esiti ukuba kungaba kukangelwa oyena mntu
uyintloko kwi Ruluneli zale Koloni ngu Sir George Grey.

Tina bantsundu singasuke sivuleleke njengamanzi edami, ukuvumela indoda
leyo xa itshoyo, iti, ulaulo Iuka Sir George Grey luya vela koluka Sir
Bartle Erere, siyavumelana naye ngelo. Kuba seloko wafikayo nje, tina
bantsundu sisedyokweni enzima, wati efika waye silwa nati ma Gcaleka,
nama Ngqika; akufeza ezo wangena kweyakwa Zulu, neyase Lusutu.

Zite ezi zingekapeli seva sekusitiwa abamnyama maba - hlutwe izixobo
zabo, benga cetyiswanga; seva kutiwa makutshiswe inkomo, mabahambe nge
pasi, abantsundu ; ezizinto ke zibanga isigquru kwaba mnyama njengam,
zezi- ngavisisekiyo, kuti nakwabanye abahlobo betu abamhlope ; Ezibanga
ke ukuba tina singabi nalusizi nokuba uyabizwa komkulu ; kukona mhlaumbi
woke apendule nange mpa- to yabantsundu.

Singatsho nati njengo mfo omhlope wase Bayi apa ote xa atetayo wati,
“Liyekeni ixego lihambe.”

Ewe amaninzi afanele ukumncoma u Sir Bartle Frere, kuba ezimfazwe afike
wazenza ziwatyebise kakulu, evuyela nokuhlutwa kwabantsundu izixobo.
Asingeteti nto ngo- mpati wemicimbi u Hon. G. Sprigg. Kuba yena asinge
tini nokuba siva kusitiwa ngomso uyapuma. Singavuya nokuva ukuba u Wm.
Ayliff, Umpatiswa Micimbi Yaba­ntsundu uyapuma. Mhlaumbi kobako ituba
lokulunga ngoku shenxa kwake.

Umsebenzi apa e Bayi mkulu ngango kuba abamhlope bade basebenzise nange
Cawa, ukutsho andimemi mntu ndixela ububi bokwapula imi ye Cawa,
ekubonakala ukuba lendawo mayike itetwe ukuze iyekwe.

Abantu yindimbane apa e Bayi. I Eastern Province Standard and Telegraph,
ike yayi kankanya indawo leyo, yati, mayikangelwe indawo leyo kuba
abantu abamnyama, baxina izitilata aba bangasebenziyo. Singavuya bete
aba­ntu bakowetu balumka ukuhamba kwabo, esitilateni e Bayi ngalondawo.
Nditshona ngeliti madodana lilungile ipepa ekutiwa yi Cape Argus,
litabateni.

Ndingu mhlobo wenu,

OBUBILEYO.

Peelton September 23, 1880.

Ndiya zisa kuzo zonke izihlobo, ngoku buba kuka Ross Hartland, wase
Peelton. Ubube ese ngu mfana, akukudala etshatile, ushiye usizi, ku
mhlolokazi ashiye yena namhla. Ubube nge 23, wancwatywa kwangayo. Zonke
izihlobo mazamkele lo mbiko.

OQAYISILEYO NGE NDAWO AYA KUYO.

Moor—Plaats Aug. 20, 1880.

NKOSI MHLELI.—

Ndibikela izihlobo ukuba u Job Q. H. Mekeni undulukile kweli li- miweyo
waya e Komkulu lama Komkulu ngomhla wesi 9 ku Aug. 1880. Ndincede
undifakele inteto zake.

Into yokuqala ute nge Cawa ngokuhlwa wawa, wabuya wavuka. Watyila
Ivangeli ka Mateyu, isiqendu sesi 5, isa hlukwana sama 23,— “Ngoko nxa
sukuba uwunikela umnikelo wako esibingelweni wati ulapo wa kumbula ukuba
umzalwana wako unendawo ngakuwe, wushiye apo umnikelo wako pambi kwe
sibingelo uhambe uye uxolelane no mzalwana wako kuqala.” Watyila iculo
lama 85, “ Kugqityiwe,”— Walihlabela; wati akugqiba washumayela ; wati
akugqiba watandaza : wati akugqiba, Watyila iculo lama 46, “O
Yehova,”—walivuma neli waligqiba. Oku ke kungo mhla wama 25 ku July,
1880. Ute yakupela inkonzo kunina,—“ Ma, usapilile na, nditsho kuba
bendiyi nkatazo kuwe, ndinga wavumi namayeza ako, ngokuba ndandicele
ukumka emhlabeni apa kwamhla ndabulawa li Langa ndivela e Lovedale? ”
Unina ute,—“ Hayi, ezonto ndixolile, into embi kukuba undishiya.”
Upendule wati “Ndiya vuya undixolela ma, sobuya sihlangane.”

Ute ngolwesi 9 ku August, “ I Nkosi sel’ inxamile uku nditabata, andina
xesha lokuteta.” Ute kudade wabo omkulu,—“ Usa tandaza na nje ngasese ”
? Ute yena, hayi. Ute ke qala namhla nje. Ute unina walila. Wati ke yena
wolula isandla watutuzela unina, esiti. “ Musa ukulila Mama, musa
ukulila Mama, i Nkosi iya kukutumela u Monwabisi, hlalani ngoxolo.”

Uvakele sele memelela esiti:—Waboneni Amandla e Nkosi! ” Wa­tsho katatu.
Uvakele esiti:—“ Noko ndinjenje hayi ukuba mnandi indawo endiya kuyo”!
Walala ubutongo ngoxolo olukuln. Ndibikela uyise u Dr. Stewart.

Senjenjalo isandla se Nkosi kwa Mekeni. Oka W. Mekeni ubube nge 8
August, kwa kwaluvuyo nakuye.

QEBEYI H. MEKENI.

ITYALA LOKU TSHISWA KWE NKOMO.

Kutetwe yi Mantyi yase Qonce, ngomhla we 16 ku September itya­la lokwa
pula umteto woku tshiswa kwe Nkomo. Kute mzini utile ka Nkondo, wasuka u
DILIMA wawa pula umteto, ngo kusuke angavumi ukuba zitshiswe, nokuba
zipaulwe inkomo zake ngu Mr. Dor­rington ongu mongameh (inspector)
walomzi. Ulivumile elotyala u Dilima, kodwa nte,—“ Anga sel’ exolela
ukurodonyishwa ngu Rulu­mente kanye kuno kuke inkomo zake zitshiswe
nokuba zipaulwe ngomnye nmntu.” Ubanjiwe ke u Dilima lowo, kuti ke mhla
latetwa ityala wagwetyelwa ukuba arole iponti ezimbini, ati engenazo
ahlale entangeni intsuku ezili shumi eline sine 14. U Dilima ukete
entolo ngweni ngelo tyala.

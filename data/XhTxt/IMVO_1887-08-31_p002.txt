  OTITSHALA.

  U Mr. GONTSHI ungenise ukuba le ntlanganiso mayiwucaciselwe umteto
  wokuba i Titshala zizicaka zika Rulumente, kangangokuba zibe pantsi
  kwe Civil Service Act (umteto olaula izicaka zika Rulumente), eyalayo
  ukuba isicaka sika Rulumente singene kwingxoxo zolaulo. Ute ngokwake
  umteto awucacile mayelana no Titshala. Ititshala zifumana imali ku
  Rulumente, ngelinye icala ziyifumana ebantwini. Inga lusizi olukulu
  ukuba i Titshala ezingaba ngama lungu ale ntlanganisweni zingazifumana
  zise nka-tazweni emveni kokuba zibe ngamalungu ale Ntlanganiso. Ute
  ukutiyile ukuba kwenziwe izinto efukufukwini. Nje ngamadoda ane
  ngqondo ale ntlanganiso anganosizi ukuba i Titshala sizivekele
  engozini ngokunga caci ko mteto ngako ke umisa ukuba lentlanganiso
  mayicele icebo le Gqweta elilumkileyo (Counsel's advice) le Jaji ukuba
  licacise umteto mayelana ne Titshala.

  Emva kwe ngxoxo enkulu inxenye isiti malingafunwa cebo, inxenye ivuma
  intlanganiso yagqiba kwelokuba icebo le Gqweta le Jaji lifunwe eliya
  kucacisa umteto mayelana no Titshala. U Mr. Gontshi wacelwa ukuba
  abhalele kwi Gqweta le Jaji aliqondayo ukufuna icebo
  elo.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

Siyazisa ukuba asilitabateli pezu kwetu ityala ulenteto zababalelani
betu, asitsho nokuti siyammelan azo zonke incwadi zabo esizishicilelayo.

Horton June 26, 1878.

Mhlobo wam otandekayo ndifakele lemigcana ngo mhlobokazi wetu
osishiyileyo u-Sana Sikepe oshiye inkedama ezimbini zingenaye noyise,
ibiyinkazana etanda ilizwi lika Tixo kanye, ingumneedikazi waba fundisi,
epete iqela leremente yentombi, isanda iremente yake, engomnye
wabangqinelwa kakuhle ngesimilo nokukalipa elizwini lenkosi ukuteta
ngalo kwizihlobo zake. Ubungati wakumkangela umbone xa kushunyayelwayo
ukuba ngomitandazo incedisa um- shumayeli pambi ko Tixo. Usishiyile ke
okwake ebezele luvuyo nokonwaba netemba enkosini yake u-Kristu wacela
umbuliso kwi remente wagoduka. Mazazi nezihlobo zake ezipesheya kwe
Nciba yinto- kazi ka Mashumi ebeyendele kwa Hlele e-Rurah.

nako nokuteta ehlala eguqa ngamadolo atandaze, amazwana
endisawakumbulayo omtandazo wake ngala “ Bawo wetu ose zulwini intando
yako maye­nzeke apa emhlabeni njengokuba injalo ezulwini, uze
unditamsanqele nkosi kunye nabazali bam, ubawo no ma no dade wam
omncinane.”

Lamazwi abonisa utando luka Tixo obelusentliziyweni yake, ngumntwana obe
kokelwa ngendlela elungileyo ngabazali. Lomntwana ube yintombi
yamazibulo, ubube xa ayiminyaka emine nenyanga ezisixenxe ubudala bake.

Banga bangati bonke abazali bakubone ukuba ku- yimfanelo yabo bonke
abazali ukuba babafundise ukutandaza abantwana, kwase buncinaneni ukuze
bakule beyazi lenyaniso.

Izihlobo ezikude maze ziwamkele lombiko, zingabi salindela ncwadi.

NATHANIEL DANIEL.

INDABA ZASE BLANTYRE.

Blantyre, March 27,1878.

Njengokuba ndandite kwincwadi yam yokugqibela ka December 8, 1878,
sisapilile, sisenjalo nangoku, nomsebenzi esingawo apa usahambiseka
kakuhle. Njengokuba lendawo yetu, i-Blantyre ilihlati abalekela kulo
amahlwempu, atshutshiswayo iyatandwa kakulu, Sesike sasindisa abantu
abatatu—abafazi ababini, ne- nkwenkwe ngo December odluleyo. Omnye
wababafazi unomntwana, simbalaka, sinemipefumlo emitandatu esiyisindise
kwimivumbo yomqubi makoboka nakwimbandezelo ezingapezu kokufa. Okuke
kulu- ngiselela ukuba basamkele esona sipo esilusindiso ngenceba engaye
u-Yesu Kristu Inkosi yetu.

Ezizinto zindikumbuza ngokunye, nomteto obuma- lunga nesixeko
sokusabela.

Kaucinge ngosizana lomfazi, olutyafileyo, lumemeke umntwana luzimeza
ubom, lwaye lutemba ukuba lute lwanokufika apo ikona iflag yama Ngesi,
luya kusinda kunye nonmtwana walo. Bendinixelele kwi­ncwadi yam ukuba
sikatazwa liqelana elitile loluhlanga lwamasela noko asikabi nakulanda
siye kungenisa ekaya. Kutshanje siqondile ukuba amasela la amane egqiba
impahla yetu asima Ajawa, koko nga­bantu besinye isizwe sesitatu sika
Ram-Ka-Kau.

Ukuba nati besinepepa elifana neli lishicilelwa e- Glasgow ubuya kuti
ulifunda ubone kutetwa ngobu- tutu obungafihlisiyo, ngosukelo
olungummangaliso, nokubanjwa ngobulumko.

Nantsi lombali:—Kute kusasa ngomhla we 13 ka February, bavuswa o-Mapasa
Ntintili no William Koyi yimizamo yento ebahluta ingubo. U-Mapasa
eqondile ukuba kunje, ulinde kwakona ukuba eve ingalo engeniswa
ngefestile ; kodwa kute kuba bekumnyama waxakwa akuyibamba ingalo, kuba
ibibuye yangeniswa. Usuke wawotusa amasela emka nasebe kufumene,
kwenziwe imiqulu emibini. Ute kwaoko u-Mapasa wavusa abanye abebelele
kwakulendlu—ke zisibozo izindlu apa; kusukelwe ke. U-Koyi obe salele
ngokuba ube nxiba ubone omnye umntu esiti gqi ngase zitoreni apo balala
kona o-Walker no Fenuick. Ate amasela akuqonda ukuba selenyatelwe
azilahla impahla abezitwele abaleka. Ute u-Koyi kuba ubepete umpu,
akubona ebaleka wati makeme uya kuwadubula, asuka wona ela eludadeni,
ababonwa. Ubonakele encama u-Koyi; kodwa iroti elingu Mapasa no Mlomba
isibonda ebesivuswe naso yingxokozelo sipange endleleni, kuba besisazi
ukuba ama­sela ayakuti gqi kwasendleleni; kanti siya ku gangana nawo
okunene. Kute kwakona kwakutiwa yima. ni, ela kwase ludadeni, wasele
dubula u-Mapasasite sakuva isitonga saya sonke kulondawo kudutyulwa
kuyo, ndasendibekisa amalalela ngapandle, ndingena mna ndibulisa,
nabanye asafumana nto noko. Elixesha yena u-Mapasa usukela omnye umntu
isi- tuba esikulu (8 miles) kupela enxibe ihempe, engena- zihlangu,
ebaleka kwinca ende kuko nezipunzi. Isela ligqotsela ubomi balo,
u-Mapasa ubalekela udumo lokoyisa, kwanekaya letu. Niyakuti ubefanele
uku­ba oyise, ewe woyisile, weza nalo isela. Singama Ngesi, sizidla ke
ngetamsanqa labo, kodwa ke eligazi, nomoya walomntu (Mapasa) siwugqita
nganina. Sinetemba elimnandi ngalo uhlanga olunokuvelisa aba­ntu abanje.
Lisiweke isela “ e-Ofisini ” laviwa, ngesacana sama Scotch, entetweni
uvumile, nezibo zangapambih wazivuma, wati kodwa ityala walibeka
nasenkosini yake, esiti nayo iyazuza kulo ixoba, yonke lento malunga
nenkosi buxoki, ufuna ukuzikusela kupela. Samgcina inyanga, sati mhla
samkulula

beza abantu, sati kuba ukuba lomntu ke bambona kumhlaba wama Ajawa
nokuba kungelicala lomlambo oyi Kabula, ize abantu bambulale. Ningevi
gwenxa, kutshiwo sibonda ukuba ize abulawe. Emva kwesisiteto utundezwe
lomntu liqela lamdlulisa kumhlaba wama Ajawa. Elinye isela alizange
libonwe, lingazange ligoduke; lingayikugoduka neli belikuti isela kuba
lingabulawa ekaya; fanele ukuba babu Spartara kulondawo, kungakuba ubile
uyaku- bulawanje, koko engabanga nalo iqinga lokuba, ukude abanjwenje.
Inkosi iyakukanyela konke okubekise ebuseleni, ite ngesimdubule lomntu.
U-Mr. Stewart uke wateta nayo ngalento, kokoke tina asifuni kudubula
mntu, sifuna nto yimbi, nditemba ukuba liyeza ixesha oyakuti umteto
wesi- bozo ufike nalapa e-Africa, abantu beve wona kunenkosi.

Bonke abantu esihleli nabo bavuye kakulu sakulibamba isela, bati kum “
bonake asingama Ajawa amasela; sizihlobo tina, ngama Manganja ebayo
ngamasela.” Oku kusibangela ukuba nati siyeke ukubakalazela, kuba oko
bekubange ukuba singa- vani.

U-Mr. Stewart uti kuko indlela ayibonileyo ekapukapu engeyiba ngumendo,
nga kwelika Rama- Ku-Kan, kunale sihamba ngayo.

Ngati amahashe angaluncedo ukuqubela pambili izinto zokukanya apa
e-Africa kanjako angaluncedo ukomeleza abantu emsebenzini. Akuko na
bangancedayo nokuba kunge hashe elinye, kesilinge uku­ba otinina apa
amahashe. Imazi zemkomo esesinazo zihambiseka kakuhle. Uti u-Senhor
Nunes wase Quillimane kwi hashe elilungileyo singarola ukulifumana
e-Bayi i-£50 mhlaumbi £60, lonto kuditywa nokukweliswa kwalo emkombeni.
Anditi yinina amanenana akowetu lomali ayayi cita ngelanga lilinye
emgcobeni.

Ndipants’ ukushiya ukuba iroti elingu Mapas ngowe Livingstonia Mission
kunye no Dr. Laws. Simbasele u-Mapas i-£10. Into ebibaliwe kwipepa
lokubuta lomali yile:—“Ismqino ngo Mr. Mapas Ntintili sibonisa
uzondelelo, noburoti awabubonisa ngokusa kuka February 13, 1878.” Inyawo
zake bezicandekile, zirazukile ngenxa yokubaleka kumhla­ba ombi
onezipunzi zingabonakali ngenxa yobude benca. Ide yazintsuku ejiyile.

Ukuzizisa apa izinto siya zitwala; kodwa ndiyatemba ukuba ixesha alikude
esiyakuti sibe nebokuva eyakusitwalela impakla yetu sisuka nayo
ngasemlanjeni. Ndibuleleleni kwizihlobo ebezite ngo Mr. MacKeith
zatumela izinto eziluncedo apa, nezokutsala abemi belilizwe ukuba beze.
Kanjako ndizazisa ukuba u-Dr. Laws zonke izinto abazitumelayo
uyakuzisebenzisa ngendlela yazo.

Kalokunje naye ulapa, ebala naye ecaleni lam, nditemba kanjalo ukuba
ukusebenza kunye kwalemizi yetu yomibini, kuba ingamasebe kupela unto
enye, kumnandi, kunoncedo.

Ubukristu ebantwini ngebuveliswa buyinto enye, bungabi
zimbaxa—njengokuba ingubo ye Nkosi yetu ibingenamqukumbelo, koko ilukwe
yonke iqala pezu- lu yaya kupuma ezantsi, isiziba esinye.

Siwugqibile umjelo wetu, selebaleka amanzi ecanda ekaya ; senze
imijelwana emininzi esincedayo ukuba sincenceshele inqolowa; namazimba,
ne lisi (rice) nombona, nezitiya zetu. Lento amanzi iluncedo olukulu.
Igusbele zicumile. Sinazo zininzi ngoku, sinembotyi, ne lettuce, ne
beet-root, ne tormato. Siyatemba ukuba ezizinto asikubuye siziswele kuba
sinomjelo.

Enye into sinamashumi omahlanu emiti ye lamuni ne lime—kodwa
isemincinane, kodwa siyatemba ukuba emva kweminyaka emitatu siyakuba
sesiyidla ilamuni. Anditeti ngazo zona i-banana kuba sezitwala ;
ubuninzi bazo zikumakuln omatatu, namane.

  THOS. THORNTON MACKLIN, Medical Missionary.

Kwa Gaga July 11, 1878.

Mhleli make ndikukataze ngokufaka amazwana kwelo pepa lamenene
ngomsebenzi ebesinawo apa ngombla 10 ka July. Sibe sino mbulelo ku Tixo,
wokuba esikulule endlaleni nase mfazweni nase zifeni nakwezinye izinto
ezike zafika ezinje ngo ruxeshe. Yati ukuvunyelwa kwalo ntlanganiso
ngabantu kwaonakala ukuba abazizisi, kuko into ebaqubayo, nababi
bonileyo. Bati nase kunikeni imali bapangelelana ybakolwayo nabahedeni.
Ababekona nditi bangaba aekulwini, imali efumanekileyo ibe ziponti
ezintatu sezineshumi lesheleni elivayo, ngapandle kwegusha.

Entlanganisweni isihlalo sibe sipetwe ngu Ginya, omdala wakwa Gaga.
Kuvunywe amaculo kwaleswa indawo ze Sibalo, kwaposwa amazwi
afanelekileyo, kwenziwa imitandazo. Ndingati ibe yintlanganiso emnandi
kunene. Mandigqibe ngeliti, Hayi imisebenzi ka Tixo okulule isixeko
embandezelweni. Ngaye dunyiswa zizo zonke intlanga.

PATULENI KAKAZA.

OBUBILEYO—UMMANGALISO.

Komgha May 3, 1878.

U-Sarah Ann Q. Mekeni,—ubudala bake iminyaka 9 nenyanga 6 amaxesha
amatatu ute wahliwa sesisifo sambamba inyanga ezimbini kute ekupeleni
kweyesibini wati udanile u-Satana ubendigxota ndisuke nditandaze
ngentliziyo yinto ebe isiti suka ubaleke wati kudade wabo usatunana
ukudlalisa tabata nezam izi­nto zokudlala mna andisayi kuze ndibuye
ndidlale. Ube tandaza ngobusuku ati akugqiba ngalomaxesha asibulise
sibone ukuba ulindele ukubamba esiti ma masibambe ute ngo 2 ka May wati
nje ngomntu odle ubusi mca, ndiyaniqayisela ubumnandi endinabo mna
ngoku.

Wati hina bawo uyazina into owadalwa ngayo mna ndiyazi nje ? Ndati
ngubanina, wati ngu Tixo ndati wazi banina omnye? Wati u-Yesu, ndati
uyintonina kuwe ? Wati ungumsindisi wam.

Wati bawo indlelana yako encinane ibe inenyoka kodwa mna ndiyigqitile
lonyoka. Lonto yandikataza ndati fan’ ukuba kungokuba engasiwanga
elubaptizweni elusana, wati emini ngo 3 ka May wazaba izi­nto zake, wati
kumnakwabo omncinane funda uwazi umtandazo “ U Bawo osezulwini”
wapakamisa isa- ndla sokunene, kanti uza kudlula.

Sendibikela nezihlobo zam.

Q. H. MEKENI.

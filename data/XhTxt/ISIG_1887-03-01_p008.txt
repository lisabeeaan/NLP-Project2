24 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1887.

INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

INGCAMANGO NGE NTLANGANISO YO SAPO.

MHLELI wam otandekayo ndipe indawo kwelopepa Iakowetu ngala-
mazwana.—Bendipakati kwentlanganiso yokuvulwa kwesikolo sase Auckland
ngomhla we 26 ku December. Mandenze mafupi nditi yayinkulu, kwaye
kupelele abantu bendawo ngendawo abazokubonela. Yangena intlanganiso
emva kwetyalike, saye isihlalo sipetwe yi nkosi yakona u Mr. Makalima.
Intlanganiso yavulwa ngomtandazo. Ndancoma ukunxiba kakuhle kwabantwana.
Emveni koko kwenziwe amaculo amnandi kunene. Kuta emva koko kwamiswa
amantombazana, enza amatiletile endingena kuwaxela. Emva koko kumiswe
amanye emahlanu, yati eyesitandatu yabekwa yodwa neyesixenxe neyesibozo
neyeshumi, bonke babe nendawo zabo bodwa. Wavakala u Miss Brown (ongu
mfundisikazi wazo), evakalisa ukuti lamadoda mahlanu ayabangisana,
abanga isitsaba segolide, esatenjiswa ngu kumkani wokufa koyena obubise
abantu abaninzi apa emhlabeni, Mahlanu ke lamapakati, ngu Ndlalo, ngu
Ngozi, ngu Mfazwe, ngu Cesine no Tywala.

Latetwa ke elityala, litetwa yileya engumgwebi, abonisa imisebenzi
emikulu la mane, kwati emva kwawo kwateta le kutiwa ngu Tywala. Kuqala
yazibonga, emva koko yavelisa inyaniso ezabeta zawaginya lomadoda mane.
Yavakala ibonisa ebandleni lonke le ingumgwebi ukuba ngenyaniso u Tywala
isitsaba simfanele. Wasifaka u Tywala kwadunywa.

Ndapawula kulontlanganiso indawo yokoyiswa kwalamanene, yindoda enye u
Tywala. Indoda engafumananga yasifumana isitsaba, koko yonke
intlanganiso yoyisiwe zinene. Ngazwinye indoda leyo indikupile kanye.
Ndaqonda apo ukuba kanti lendoda inebandla nayo kwapakati kwe ramente.
Yinile, ndaqonda kanye ukuba lendoda iya kuzigqiba izizwe, ndamangaliswa
ukuti kanti lendoda iyakwazi ukusebenza ndawonye no Krestu. Uti ngani?
yivake : Kaukangele mhlobo wam kakuhle wofika kuko amaqataqikili kwa
pakati kwale ramente, abantu abaya kumfamekisa ngalendlela wofika ngati
bayayinyelisa, kanti bakude le nalonto uyitetayo Lizalisekile izwi eliti
“ Bayandibeka ngendebe zemilomo, kanti intliziyo zikude le nam.”

Ndinyamezele mfundi ndibangule noko ngati kubuhlungu. BazaIwana nodade
make sikangele kakuhle, singamane amehlo siwagqitisa akuyikusinceda nto.
Yaye enye into anditandi ukumita umntu ize ndizale imfene, uti
nditinina? Nditi ndifuna ukuteta lonto, sendiyicingile, nditi ifana
nantonina lento busuke ubu Krestu, bahlule bona kwabodwa kanti kutiwa
masibe banye. Ekungenxa yesosimo lento ungeva abahedeni besiti “ nina
Magqoboka kupela kwamaxoki.” Bengatsho ngokuba bacase ingqoboko, benikwa
izizatu zokutsho yintsebenzo epambeneyo yama Krestu. Ndiyatemba mfundi
wondiva kakuhle. Batandwa enkosini, kanindiqondise lento, nalamagama,
andiwaqondi bubizwa ngawo utywala ngama gqoboka, kuba andiwa- qondi
kanye, ukuba ngamasi, siyakuti la enkomo yintonina? Ukuba ngamanzi, la
asemlanjeni'' siyakuti yintonina ? Ukuba liyeza lesisu sesiyakuti
unonyongwane yintonina? Ukuba yi tea, sesiyakuti le itengwayo yintonina?
Nanzo indawo enditi kaundiqondise zona malunga nalendoda namhla efumene
isitsaba kuba ndandiba lendoda ayiswele gama.

Mandizokushiya manene nama nenekazi akowetu, ngeliti, inteto yomhlobo
wam um-Dipende ibaluleklle kanye. Make sise ingqondo zetu kuyo, lomfo
uteta inene kuba kulapo intlondi-ntlondi zikona. Oh! Mandiyeke, kodwa
magqoboka yekani imfene nempaka zigoduke musani ukuzivalela pakati
zonihlaza. Owenu umhlobo futi,

GEORGE MJAMBA.

Auckland.

NKOSI mhleli we Sigidimi kaufake lemigcana kwelo pepa lamanene
namanenekazi lendaba ndisiti hayi ilanga elafika labalela pambi kwe
Kilisimesi ngo December 9th, 1886. Elolanga labulala abantu, ama
kwenkwana amabini esalusa impahla, ubudala bawo yiminyaka e 8 ne 9, enye
intwana ifele emonti oyifumana maxa usuka e Humansdorp usiya e Tinara.
Kulomonti enye intwana ifele e Drifontein. Yayi nzima imini leyo. Zafa
ihangu nenciniba, ozi nqolowa nozi habile lafika ezinye ziseluhlaza lazi
raula, kwezo labulala, lafik’ amazimba max’aqumbi adubulayo lawatshisa
afumana uhlala akadubula. Elo langa lenjenjalo ukutshisa izinto, nditsho
nangoku libalele, kodwa elona langa labulala izityalo lelalomini. Akuko
temba, ingafikanga imvula ifumise umhlaba.

2.  INKATAZO E SIGIDIMINI—Ezizaziso singazifumaniyo kuwo Sigidi-

mi ezinje ngokupuma kwe ncwadi ezitwalwa ngabantu nokuba ngono hashe
zipuma ezi Post Office zisiya ezifameni kwimizi yama gosa apo nobani
wesositili uyakuyi fumana kona i letter yake. Bati ke abatwali abo,
mhlaumbi omnye unehashe omnye akanalo ziti ukuba zipumile i letter ezo
abe ke imali ayifunayo ngokutunda kwake kodwa u Mantyi uyakutata onani
lilula kulomanani inkata kuwe Sigidimi kuba akuzazisi kwangapambili
ukuba itener ze post office ziyakupuma kwinyanga etile ngosuku olutile,
sikolisa ukuziva zigqitile, sikude ne office ze mantyi na magosa akude
kuti, leyo ntsebenzo iluncedo nako ntsundu yiyo lonto, nditi Sigidimi
yazisa sizilungisele lomali isilungele nati

3.  Ozi ALMANAKA—Nditi mazibeko epepeni ziyafuneka nje ngepepa lonke.
    Imvo Zabantsundu aziposisi ukuyikupa i Almanaka onke amapepa
    ayenzayo lonto andiboni ukuba ayaposisa. Ukuba kungenxa yemali ukuba
    ncinane, Isigidimi masipume kabini ngenyanga ize umvuzo zibe
    zisheleni ezi sibozo (8s.) ngonyaka mhlaumbi ishumi le sheleni
    (10s), ngati mhlaumbi umsebenzi ne nkululeko ingahambiseka.

4.  UKUHLA KWAMANANI ENTLOBO-NTIOBO ZEZINTO,—Tina ibi ngazange inkomo
    uve kusitiwa nantsiya itengiaa, ungafumane uye ngapandle kwo ponti
    ezisibozo nokuba lishumi leponti, kanti lonkomo imali ingaka, ayiyo
    imaz’enetole, inye mhlaumbi, litokazi elo limali ingaka. Kumnyaka ka
    1886 sibone isimanga, iti imanzi enetole elikulu uyifumane nge ponti
    ezi £3 nezi £4 imazi enetole elikulu amatokazana azekwayo nonkatyana
    ezikwakulontanga yi ponti inye £1 mhlaumbi zimbini £2 kunjnlo ngoku
    apa ukuquba kwe mali, igusha ube ungena kuyifumana ngapantsi
    kweshumi elinambini lesheleni 12s., namhla ihlile igusha, maxa idla
    isheleni ezintlanu idulile 5s. Anditeti ngozinqolowa, maxa inqolowa
    ngoku ingxowa yenqolowa ngesibozo 8s., eyamazimba uyifumane nge
    shumi 10s., eyombona nge sihlanu 5s., itapile nge sine 4s. nesitatu
    3s., inyama yenkomo ipeni inye iponti 11b, akuzange kubeko into
    enjalo soloko ndavelayo andizange ndiyive nakomdala, imalana enje
    ngale yale minyaka.

5.  ITYALIKE ESEYINO MNYAKA EFUMANE YEMA, EYIWESILE.—Apo ipuma kona kuse
    Tshatshi kute ngokuba kuxabene iramente nomfundisi yaziqezula
    iramente ababasaya e Tshatshi yamisa i Wesile kodwa akusoze umbone u
    mfundisi, yaqala ngo January 1886 namhla ngu January 1887 upelile
    umnyaka imi lotyalike ngapandle ko mfundisi yeyama dyakana odwa,
    lomnyaka upelilenje akupehlelelwanga akuzange kubeko nomtendeleko
    abazange baubone, wonke lomnyaka upela- nje kwakiwa indlu yetyalike,
    kwaba lusizi kwababeko mhla yavulwa kungeko mfundisi.

Mandime apo ndifumane ndangena ngenduku emdudweni. Ninga- xali yimali
yepepa iyeza. Igama lam lingacinywa epepeni ndilikolwa lako Sigidimi.
Ndim owenu,

SIMON Y. MTIMKULU.

January 22th, 1887, Wittekleibosch.

NKOSI MHLELI wendaba zamanene uxolo nobuhlobo endingatshoyo ukuti
uyandazi nati kulomhlaba siyapila kakuhle akuko nto ke okwam namhla
ndifumene intliziyo yokuba make ndibale imigcana ibe mbalwa ke nditumele
kuwe nkosi yohlanga Iwakowetu nditsho ndibulele mfo ka Maramane u
Abraham ngesi kalo sake akala ngaso kuma Kristu ukuti. xa umntu oli
kolwa asela utywala akanakubalikolwa kuba utywala ngamalahle ka Satana
yinyaniso leyo yona. Kodwake amazwi endinga waqondanga esi xane andazi
ukuba kukutinina oko nokuti xabo nokuti endakeni nditi andimpenduli
ndibuza lomazwi kupela nkosi, uxolo ndingowako umfokazana kwelama Bulu
Kiluerton kufupi ne Pretoria.

Transvaal January 25 th 1887.

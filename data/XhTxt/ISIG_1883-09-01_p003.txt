ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1883.

  Sicinga ukuba singabokuqala abati ngo Mr. Enoch Booy
  eyona ndawo yake asikuku katsa, kuba ingu Macwili umnini
  walondawo, asibugqira, kuba eko u Ngxito, ingebugcisa
  ekukangeleni inkwenkwezi kanjako. Indawo awayedalelwe
  yona kukuqwalasela indawo ezinjengale ayitetayo kule ncwadi
  yake ilandelayo:—

MHLELI—Ndipe nkosi isituba ndiyacela kumadoda ohlanga i Afri-
kanders ze South African Aborigines Association, ukuba zibunge
malunga nenguqulo yencwadi zama Ngesi esi Xoseni. Zininzi incwadi
ezingekoyo esi-Xoseni zama Ngesi, kodwa zibalulekile. Niyazi ukuba
umzi wakwa-Xosa awuvani nesi Ngesi. Ndim E. BOOT, E-Qonce.

  Ukwazisa into ehlileyo emzini wendoda, esakuba nje-
  ngokuvela komntu omtsha, ukutshata kwamalungu osapo,
  nokubika umpanga, lisiko lase mlungwini: isiko elilunge
  kunene kanjako. Indodana efunde kunene itshate ngalonya-
  ka udlulileyo, ayazisa, yati nenyaniso yokutshata kwayo
  yaviwa kwezi “Mpawana.” Inkosikazi yayo ifumene um-
  ntwana kwakuzo ezinyanga : sasesisiva ngendaba kodwa uku-
  ba kuhle into entle enjalo kulomzi, abahlobo abaninzi bolosopa
  abakayiva into leyo. Silibalisa nje elibali sifuna ukufaka
  umbuzo kwimpi yakowetu efundisiweyo ukuba bengafaki
  zaziso e Sigidimini nje mayelana nezi zinto banokutinina
  abakulileyo ukuyenza into engenziwayo sisizukulwana ekutiwa
  sifundile? Silugqiba olu “ Pawana ” ngokunqwenelela unya-
  na ka Mr. James Gontshi impilo entle nende.

Kuko inxenye yabantu ecinga ukuba kungumsebenzi wayo
ukupendula ntwana nganye itetekayo. U .William Mbenya
ngomnye wabo bantu. Kwincwadi yake ebonakala kwakweli
pepa umnqweno wake ngowokubonisa ukuba umbali wezi
“ Mpawana ” watonyalaliswa kubona incwadi yake enesi Ngesi
esapukileyo awayitumela kwi komishonari zase Somerset.
Le yimposiso enkulu. Saba nosizi, nodano, umpefumlo wetu
wawa sakufumana umntu omvo zifaneleke njengezika William
Mbenya, ehlazisa impi emnyama efundisiweyo. Wonke ofu-
ndisiweyo angasivumela xa siti, mayibe kwatiwa, mhla incwadi
ka My Lord Mbenya yakubona ukukanya kwemini, kaniyibone
ke into eveliswa yimfundo yabamnyama. Isifundo sopawana

esalwenzayo sesokuba ati xa umntu atabata inyatelo elinjengeli

latatyatwa ngu Mbenya, acebisane nabanye.

Ngenye inyanga yaka yaba ngumsebenzi wetu ukutetisa
ityendyana lapesheya kwe Nciba abati ngu Semani ka Ntla-
bati, ngokusuka azenze umfundisana otile wokufundisa abaqu-
bi beli pepa ngezinto ezayamene noku cokelwa kwe ndaba
zalo. Fanukuba olo “ Pawana ” aluhlangananga namehlo ka
“W. Z. S.,” wase Qonce, okwahleli esihlalweni se professor,
esiti, incazo yomcokeli yendawana ezisakuba zingavisiseki
encwadini yombaleli, yintlebendwane. Ngale nteto yake siti
tina u “ W. Z. S.,” usibomela singonanga nto. Mayibe ngu-
mfo onomsindo, nobehle acapuke. Ukuba kunjalo igama lake
u “ Z.” limfanele. Injengelekazi apa yomfazi wom-Grike u
Socrates, ebingadendi kuyifaka umnwe emehlweni indoda
iqumbile, ibanga iziduba-ntini endlwini yayo—igama lale
ntokazi lalingu Zantippe. Asitenqake namhla ukubona u
Mrs. W. Zantippe Socrates enqakula into engepambi kwake.

Inxwaleko ezaveliswa zizi profeto zo-Nxele no-Nongqause,
kumzi wakwa-Xosa, zazimbi ngokungena kubaliswa. Eku-
ngumnqweno wake wonke ovuyiswayo kukubona abantu bako-
wetu benyuka, ukuba ngamana ukulahleka, nokulahlekisa,
nokulahlekiswa okulolohlobo kungeza kwabuya kwapindeka.
Udaba, esiluve kumzi ongemgama mkulu e-Heald Town, luba-
nge ukuba sifumane udano. Iqela elitile lama-Mfengu ase
Nxukwebe lifudukela kwezindawo zintsha zivulekileyo e-Bate-
njini, nakoma pesheya kwe-Nciba, liti isizatu sokufuduka
kwalo kukuba u-Rulumeni ezakwahluta kweli lizwe abantu
abamnyama isiqingata senkomo zabo ; indawo yokupepela ke
ikwezi ndawo bafudukela kuzo. Yabonake lenteto ibuxoki,
ibuxoki obungengapantsi kobo buka-Nongqause. Kanti ke
noko kunjalo into leyo seyifumene amakolwa atile ! Masilu-
nqamle olu-“ Pawana ” ngokubuza lombuzo, “ Hina, bantu
bakowetu, nosuka ninina ematangeni obaleledolweni ? ”

Umbali wolu “Pawana” Iwandulelayo wayesanxiba ipina-
fore, ngexesha apa ama-Mfengu ayepitizela efudukela pesheya
kwe Nciba; wayeyinkwenkwana endletyana zingena mipula,
enyamekele ukuva into esazeke itetwa ngabantu abakulu.
Sikumbula ngentsini namhla imbali zobuxoki, ababekolwa zizo
abaninzi, ngelozwe la Pesheya. Enye yazo yayi yile, esiyaku-
ti sibakumbuze abafundi be “ Mpawana.” Bekusitiwa abafazi
kwelo la pesheya, batyeba ngangokuba inxenye yesiqu sake
sibe mgama kuye yena ehleli apa, ze ube wena mfundi ubeta
inja kanti wenzakalisa u “ my dear ” wako. Yeka ubuhlungu
bomxelo wako xa seumva elila. Elibali lodwa lenza ukuba
ipesheya kwe Nciba kutiwe ngu “ Mpundu.” Ibali elabanga

igama lokuba ngu Pini, soka siliyeke okwanamhla, kodwa nalo
likwahlekisa. Namhla akuko ungaziyo ukuba zonke ezizinto
zabe zitetwa nge pesheya zabe zibubuxoki obusicaba. Lunja-
lo ubaptizo Iwe Transkei. Kantike abemi bayo banyama nina
namhla? Kunanina lento abantu bengena kuvala imilomo yabo
ekuteteni xa bafudukayo?

Ubomi benxila yinto enamacebetshu angenakubalwa. Kulelali imelwe ngu
Cawa, e Kwezana kupantse ukulalelwa ngumbete itshoba lendoda etile
ezibalule ngokutandana nale ntombi kutiwa ngu Layiza omnyama. Ibali
selixelwa yintyewona ebihamba nayo. Kuti xa kupunywayo e dolopini lase
Dikeni kufunyanwe ngonkabi imbodlelwana inamanzana afumaniseke
enevunjana lomti ka lonji. Kute kuba umxelo wamadoda sewa xuzukela
utywala, anga angasela, akasela: kodwa kwati ebusuku buka Badakazi,
lenkewu isinde ekufeni yayiqubula lembodlela yayiposa entloko. Kanti
yityefu ! Kuviwe ngomnene selekaba umoya. Ubehle waputunywa Umlungu
wabakuposa izinto aziposayo akwanceda nto. Wagqotsa ngehashe ukuza ku
Miss Waterston oye nemiciza kwaoko wazama. Siva ngoku ukuba inkabi leyo
inobubetele. Asisakulixela igama lendoda leyo, nakuba abaninzi
bangalaziyo. Esi sesinye isiqamo senxano lamanzi angengawo awadalwa
amiselwa ngu Mdali ukuba kuselwe wona.

Lencwadi siyihlomelayo izitetela ngokwayo:—

NKOSI MHLELI : Ndikulungele ukupendula u A. K. Kodwa kungenga pambi
kokuba apendule imibuzo esencwadini yam, azekanjalo akanyele inene
endizimisileyo ke wonke ongatanda enjenjalo nokuba selede acele namafu
amncedise kum kuya kunga kuvutuza umoya. Qonda nale ncwadi ka A. K.
ndiyibawela njengenyama kuloko andifuni kuhiliziswa entetweni yam ndimke
nobuciko; kuqala makupendulwe ku kanyelwe imibuzo yam ne nene zam.
Owako.—JAMES M. PELEM.

Iqina lokuba kufanelekilena ukuba abantsundu babe ngabatabati nabafundi
be Sigidimi, lelifanelwe kukuxoxwa ngumzi uhlangene. E-Cala,
kwela-Batembu, siva ukuba umzi waka wahlangana ngosuku Iweshumi
linesihlanu Iuka August ukuxoxa lombuzo mnye. Isigqibo asikasiva : kodwa
sitemba sisoyika ukuba lontlanganiso iyakutyatyamba ekubeni kungene
inani elim’mandi ekutabateni Isigidimi. Asilelase-Batenjini kodwa
elilungelwe kuvuselelwa entweni enjengale. Ilali zininzi zabantu
bakowetu ezihleli esitokotokweni—ezinyaweni zobalel’edolweni, engati
sifikile Isigidimi kuzo singenise ukukanya ngezinto ezininzi. Nto
iswelekileyo ngamadodana antliziyo zibanzi, angenabugwala,
okubahlanganisa abantu ukuba baxoxe ngento esakuba zisingisele pambili.
Well done Gala, iso letu soka silisuse e-Hewu silizolise kwelo “ Gala ”
ukulo!

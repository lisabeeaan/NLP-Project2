Okunye ndinicela okokuba niqonde kanzulu ukuba lotinina usapo Iwenu
ukuwadla ilifa amalungelo emfundiso yobu Kristu, ziti ne Sakramente
ezingcwele ze Kerike ka Kristu zime unapakade pakati kwalo, Iwakuba
lukulile, Iwaba ngamadoda nabafazi?

sabiko; akuko temba lokuba komana kufika iqela elitsha laba Fundisi
livela e-England: ngokuba nakona abantu abalingenwe ngabafundisi bama

South Africa lifumene kakulu kumalungelo nokuvelwa ngama Ngesi
ekumisweni kwezikolo zama Kristu, pakati kwabasebumnyameni, kwaba badala
bakoyo bapakati kwenu. Sinetemba lokuba imfazwe zipelile ngenceba ka
Tixo ; into esiyipeteyo ngoku kukubizela abantu ebuhlantini buka Tixo.

Lento yoti ukuze ifezwe, kushumayele aba- ntsundu kwaba ntsundu.
Nanjengokuba u- Tixo selede wazicukumisa intliziyo zabanye ukuba
bazinikele kulomsebenzi, sinetemba lokuba wobuye abize abanye abaninzi,
side sibe nokuti ngamazwi Ombali Wamaculo, “Inkosi yakupa ilizwi,
wamkulu umkosi wabalishumayelayo.”

Kodwa umteto ka Kristu ngulo wokuba, “ Abo bashumayela i-Vangeli
mabapile nge Vangeli nokuba “ Makati ofundiswayo abele ofundisayo kwinto
zonke ezilungileyo.” Kwangokunjalo ke, ukuba ninga i-Vangeli ingamila
yande pakati kwenu, njengomti omasebe zihlala kuwo intaka kwizizukulwana
ngezizukulwana, nifanele ukuyincencesha ngeminikelo yenu yempahla
zasemhlabeni.

Inxenye yenu mayisebenzele imfundo yaba- ntwana bayo, ningasileli
ukuyibatala ilungelo engaka; ukuze inxenye yabo ibe ngabakonzi belizwi
lika Tixo, siti isisalela sabo sibubeke bucala ubudenge bemihla
eyadlulayo; sibe ngabalandeli abanyanisekileyo baka Yesu Kristu.

Lo ngumnqweno wetu ke ngoku, njengoku- ba ama Kristu ase England enipile
imbewu, ukuba nincede nonke ngabanye ukuyihlumisa : njengokuba nani
nincediwe ngabahleli kude nelizwe lenu, abangazange banibone : ngoko
nifanele ukufunda ngakumbi nangakumbi ukuzinceda ngokwenu. Ngakumbi
ekuxaseni abafundisi abapakati kwenu, abangabohlanga Iwenu.

Ngenxa yelituba siza kuqala kwangoku uku- buta Iminikelo iminyaka
ngeminyaka yokuxasa aba Fundisi abantsundu kuzo zonke izikolo zetu.
Sitemba ukuba bonke abantsundu abanenkatalo ngomsebenzi wezikolo
bayakwenza ngako konke abanako ukunceda.

Lomnikelo woba ngowokubatala aba Fundisi abantsundu, naba Shumayeli
kupela, ukuze kuti exesheni lokunqamka koncedo Iwama Ngesi, nibe naba
Fundisi abangabenu, iti i-Kerike yabantsundu ibe njengeyama Ngesi,
nokuba yeyama Bulu, nokuba yeyama French, ibe yi Kerike yohlanga
engemayo nayodwa nakuba ingenaluncedo oluvela kumacala asemzini.

abanye ke, bangoyiki ukuza kunceda ukuziva- kalisa indaba zovuyo ze
Vangeli ka Kristu xa babona ukuba inkabi ebulayo ayivinjwanga ukudla

N. J. GRAHAMSTOWN.

Bishopsbourne, March 2nd, 1876.

BLANTYRE.

Abalesi be Sigidimi bokumbula ukuba ukunyuka kwamadoda aya
e-Livingstonia kwakuko nomfo abati ngu Mr Henderson owayeya kuhlola
indawo engafanela ukuba kumiswe isikolo kuyo lihlelo lakowabo le
Established Church yase Scotland, kuba I-Livingstonia yeye Free Church.
Indawo ke ayiketileyo uyi bize ngokuti yi Blantyre, igama lendawo
azaleIwa kuyo u-Dr Livingstone. Inganeno kwale kutiwa yi Livingstonia.
Kubonakala ukuba banokuhambelana kakuhle. Kwincwadi ka Dr Stewart neka
Mapas ebikwi Sigidimi senyanga edluleyo, kubonakala ukuba abantu bezo
zikolo zozibini bamana ukupana izandla. Oyena mongameli oye kupata
esosikolo sase Blantyre ngu Dr Thornton Macklin. Kwesi Sigidimi ke
sinika inteto yake ngeso sikolo asipeteyo. Uti:—

Indawo esimi kuyo esosikolo sase Blantyre asiku- ko nokuba intle,
ibonakalisa ubulumko buka Mr Henderson oyinyulileyo. Yena wenyuka no Mr
Young ngo 1875 eze kukangela apo kungema isikolo se Established Church.
Akuba elihambahambile elilizwe kwabonakala kuye ukuba eyona ndawo afa-
nele ukukangela kuyo yile imalunga ne Shire kufupi ne Magomer apo
kwakuke kwako isikolo sase Tshatshi. Sati ke ngoko safika nge 1876 kwa
Ramakukan apo kupelwa kona ukunyukwa ngemikombe, u-Mr Henderson
wasishiya wenyukela ezindulini ukuya kumisa isiza sesikolo. Nam ndandiza
kuhamba naye ndatintelwa yicesine, kuba oko yayindibambe kanye. Indawo
ate wamisa yona ibonisa ukuba ababemtembile babengafumane bamtemba.
Kulendawo ayinyulileyo kube kuke kuko umzi, koko tina safika kubalekiwe
kusitiwa umniniwo ubulewe ngu Ramakukan. Wangcwatywa endlwini yake,
yawiswa bemka bonke abanye.

U-Mr Henderson wafika kuseko izindlu ezimiyo waselelungisa zona ukuze
sibe sicopa kuzo side sizakele ezetu.

Lendawo ayinyulileyo inempilo entle. Ipakamile ngokutandekayo—inyawo ezi
3,000. Siyongamele kengoko indawo enesifo. Xa nditshoyo anditeti kut uti
umntu akunyuka angafunyanwa sisifo, kuba siko nasezintabeni xa kuko
icibi kufupi. Apa apo sikona amanzi akanakuma kuba litambeka, nawemvula
akemi. Nobushushu balentaba buyatandeka.

BALAHLEKILE MHLAUMBI BASINDISIWE.

Balahlekile ukuba abasindisiwe. Bonke, ngapandle ko Adam no Heva kwano
Yesu, balahlekile kwakwi yure yokuqala yobom. Buza imbali yobuhedeni
kwaneyobu Kristu; buza kwikulu linye lamawaka abafundisi baka Kristu
kulo lonke ilizwe; buza kwizigidi ezingamashumi omhlanu ama kristu
aselizweni; uze uzuze impendulo enye ecacileyo ; “Bonke abantu
balahlekile bade bafumane Umsindisi.” Oko kuqinisekile, noko
kungabalwanga ngokucacileyo entliziyweni naseluvalweni lwako
mleseshindini, kuba kuse Zibalweni Ezingcwele. Hayi ukucaca nokugweba
kwawo umqondiso wokuba umntu ulalilekile, obalwe ngegazi lika Kristu
endaweni yenkankaue. Ukuba umntu akalahlekile, nokuba unako ukuzisindisa
ngokwake, kunganiua ukuba aze u-Tixo wotando nowobulumko atumele u-Nyana
wake ukuba akuruseshelwe ukusindisa umntu. Akuko mntu uyakuwupendula
lombuzo, ade ahlangaue nayo Inkosi u-Yesu Kristu kwisihlalo somgwebo.
Baleseshindini abatandexayo, bamelwane, nabalingane abangekasindiswa,
nifanelwe kukufuna nimfumane u-Kristu njengo Msindisi wenu, hleze nibe
nilahlekile, nibe nilahleke ngonapakade. Ngeminyaka emalunga namashumi
omabini adluleyo, ndabona umntu ebuba ndevangati usayibambile ingaloyam
ngokubambakwake okoyikeka kunene, nentliziyo yake irazuka ngokukala
ngentshabalalo. Animangaliswa ngoku ukuba intliziyo yam yonke iyabongoza
ililela abamelwane abatandekayo aboni. Senditandazile ukuba Inkosi ize
itumele u-Moya Oyingcwele ngoku eziutliziyweni zenu. Yitini ngamadolo
enu kwanga kunganjalo.

ITYALA LOKUBULALA E-FREE STATE.

E-Free State ngomhla 22 ka May, kube kuziswe pambi kwe Jaji engu
Buchanan umfana we Bulu ongu Isaac Stephanus De Villiers, obudala
buminyaka imashumi mabini anesibini, enikwa ityala lokuba wati
ngokuswela ukutelekelela wadubula ontsundu ongu Witbooy wambulala.

Liqalwe elotyala ngumteteleli wakomkulu, wati ngoku kuyanda kakulu
ukudubula abantu, lixesha lokuba kupeliswe. Ubalise ngokuqaleka
kwelityala wati, amadoda ejuri makaze angatini nento azive pandle,
makaze apulapule kwinto ezakutetwa ngoku.

Ebunqineni kubonakele ukuba kwati ngo Mvulo otile ka February, lo Isaac
washiywa yedwa ekaya nabantsundu ababini, abakwangangaye ngobudala.
Utabate umpu wamana ukudlala ngawo ejolisa ku August. Ute u-August
makangayenzi lento kuba akufanelekile ukudlala ngompu. Uyekile okunene
waya kuwubeka endlwini yokulala. U-Witbooy emva koko uze ku August
bamana besidla ukudla pandle. Ubuye weza u-Isaac waquba kwa okuya
kudlala kwake. Kube mzuzwana wabuza ku Witbooy ukuba udle wada
wagqitisana ukuhluta. Ute omnye Hayi. U-Isaac upendule ngeliti, “Yiza
ngapakati ndize kukupa isonka.” Ulandele u-Witbooy waya kungena;
u-August wasala ngapandle. Kube mzuzwana mncinane u-August weva
kuhlokoma isitonga ngapakati, wati akuya kukangela wafika u-Witbooy
elele ngesisu esopa.; u-Isaac emi engati umangalisiwe esiti ubengazi
ukuba lompu unxashiwe.

Abebemtetelela babonise bona ukuba u-Isaac no Witbooy bebengatiyene
nganto: kanjalo u-Isaac ngumntu kakade otanda ukufeketa: bate lompu
ubuncolile kanjalo ubengazi ukuba unxashiwe; nokuba ulusizi kakulu yinto
ayenzileye.

Ekuzeni kupela kwetyala Ijaji ibonise ukuba elityala lelomntu oselemkulu
enokuqonda. Ute aba­ntu abenza izinto ezinje kuyafuneka ukuba baziswe
ematyaleni, kuba ukudubula abantu kuyanda kakulu. Uqube wati, kubonakala
ukuba kuko abantu abati ubom bomntu yinto yokufeketa ngokukodwa ukuba

“bobontsundu.” Ukuba abantsundu bayona bebefanele ukohlwaywa kakulu;
kodwa ke bohlwaywe ngendlela elungelelene nomteto. Yena akangomnye
wabati ontsundu uyalingana nomhlope ngezinto zokukanya nokuqubela
pambili. Nokoke ekubeni engumgwebi kuyimfanelo yake, ngokukodwa akubona
izinto ezinje zisihla futi, ukuba ayixelele ijuri ngokusekuhleni,
nokucacileyo, ayinyanzele lendawo ngako konke anako, ukuba emtetweni,
oka Tixo nowomntu, akuko sahluko pakati kontsundu nomhlope ngokumalunga
nobom abunikwe ngu Tixo, baza batatyatwa ngumntu. Nangoku sekuko abati
apa e-Free State ontsundu uyinto yokufeketa. Kwelityala zimbini indawo
ekufanele ukuba ijuri izikangele. Eyokuqala yeyokuba umbanjwa lo ubesazi
na ukuba lompu ubunxashiwe. Eyesibini yeyokuba unemizamo ayenzileyo na
yokuqonda ukuba unxashiwe lompu. Isigwebo sejuri kufuneka ukuze sibuye
nezondawo zombini; zonke ezinye izinto maze zikezibekwe bucala, isigwebo
silungelelaniswe nobunqina.

Ipumile ijuri yaya kugqugula ixesha elide yabuya isiti “ akuko tyala,”
waza ke ngoko wakululwa um banjwa lowo.

IMBALI YENKEDAMA.

Imbalana yam, endifuna ukuyibala, ngokufutshane, ukuqala kwayo, koku.
Ndati ndivela kweyakowetu indlu ndingowamazibulo, kwati emva kwam
kwazalwa intombazana; ndati xa ndiqala ukuba yinkwenkwana eqala
ukuqonda, wafa umzalikazi wam. Amazwi ake nakubeni bendingekaqondi
ndisawakumbula kodwa wona, kuba wati xa aza kuqawuka, wateta lamazwi,
“mntanam namhlanje ndiyemka uzakusala upatwe ngabanye abantu wandiyala
wa­ti, “ uzeuzigcine kuba uya kupatwa ngabangakataliyo.” Asahleli ke
lomazwi kum.

Kuteke emva kwentsuku ezingeninzi kwapuma umkosi usiya kwelinye ilizwe,
okwati ekubuyeni kwa­wo, akabonakala ubawo, sahlalake silusapo lodwa,
pofu singeva nendaba ngaye, kungaxelwa nokuba ubulewena; sahlala kungeko
namntu uyindoda ekaya. Kwati ke kwakuba mzuzwana wafika pakati kobusuku,
wati akufika wati masihambe sahamba ke kwasebusuku sawela umlambokazi
omkulu okutiwa ukubizwa kwawo yi Bembe.

Ngumlambo ke lowo onezilwana ezininzi unozimvubu nengwenya ne mfingo.
Sawela ke kulomlambo sapumelela mzaneni utile kwakuxa kuti qipu ukusa
sahlala kulomzana intsukwana ezimbalwa sagqita singazi tina apo siya
kona sifana silandela njengokuba besikokelwa nguye. Sahamba intsuku
ezitile ke sihamba ezindle apo, esahamba inyanga singaboni mzi kupela
lilele nyamakazi zodwa nengonyama. Kwati ekupeleni safumana umzi safika
abcmi balomzi bezizinto ezingayaziyo lento ekukulima ezingawaziyo
amazimba, izinto ezipila ngenyama yenyamakazi kupela. Safika imiti
ibomvu yinyama, ke sahlala apo kulomzana. Esosizwe ukubizwa kwaso kutiwa
nga-Matonga elinye igama labo nga Makewenga abangafuye nankomo
nebokwele. Kwati kwakuba mzuzu sikulondawo kwati ngamini itile seva
kutiwa utshaba lukufupi, kwalungiswa ke ukuba kusatywe. Ndati ke mna
ndakubona ukuba abantu bendlu yakokwetu bayalibala ukupuma endlwini xa
abantu balomzi bexineneyo esangweni ndabona ukuba ubawo kuxa yena
alibeleyo kukulungisa impahla mna ke ngenxa yokoyika ndati mandipume,
ndapuma ndahamba ke nababantu ndayishiya yonke indlu yakokwetu
ndahlukana ke nobawo ukususela kweloxa. Ndahamba ke nababantu
bascbendihleka bati abanye yintwana evela pina le? bati ke abebemqonda
ubawo ngumntwana wento ebesingazi ukuba ivela pina. Sahamba ke. Kuba
lento yehla kwakusasa lati ilanga xa liqalayo ukufudumala kwase
kubuhlungu kaloku kum kuba besendiqala kaloku ukuqonda ukuba namhlanje
kukokunye kuba kulendawo pakati kwababantu bekungeko namnye
obenokubonakalalisa ukundenzela nosizi; kuba ngelixa bendiseyinkwenkwana
encinane, kanye engekabiyiyo neyalusayo. Sati ke kuba lilizwe leziqamo
ezininzi zasendle safika komnye umti otile ogama kutiwa ngumtunduluka
sati xa siqalayo ukudla kulomti, sabona kugaleleka utshaba, kuba belute
ukuvela kwalo, lwavela ngakwelonacala, ebelinendawo ezinqabileyo,
lwasikapela ngakwindawo ezinamatafa; atike amadoda la angecala lakowetu,
akabinako nokuke alinganise nokulinganisa ukulwa ; kubake besendite
ngamadoda angafuye nankomo, ati kwangobunjalo

bunjalo bawo obo aba engenazo nezixobo, namakaka, kuba ikaka livela
enkomeni. Sacitwa kubike ngulomkosi, ndatike kulondawo kuba bekungeko
nehlati eli kusetafeni, kwati xa abantu babulawayo ndafana ndati nca
pantsi ngesisu, ndabona ukuba utshaba seludlulile kulendawo ndandikuyo,
bepelile kunene abantu, ndavuka kulendawo ndandincatame kuyo andabona
namnye umntu, landiya kaloku, ndakohlwa ukuba ndiyakutinina, kuba
bekungeko namzi kulondawo. Ndati xa ndihambahambayo kulendawo abantu
babulewe kuyo, ndabona kusititu inkwenkwana ebiseyilumkile kunam yona,
ndatike kuba nenxano laselindikulele kakulu, ndancedwa ke yilonkwenkwana
kakulu, kuba yona ibipete iselwana lamanzi. Sasesihamba kunye ke
nalonkwenkwana, sakuzimela sihlahleni sitile; kute kungekade kakulu
sabona um­kosi kwaolutshaba lwetu selusiza kudlula kwakufupi nelityolo
sikulo, badlulake batata ixesha elide, kuba besebegoduka; kwati lusati
nqam sapuma kwaoko siba tina selupelile akuseko lumbi; xasesiti kaloku
siyakufuna abazali betu, kweqapu indoda etile, ebikwayeyaselutshabeni,
ebisasalele ngasemva sabona seyifuta kwalapa. Watike umlingane wam lo,
kuba, ebeselengumntu onako ukubaleka, wanduluka, wagalcleka lomfo kum,
wafika wati yimapo, ndema, ukuqondisa ke ukuba bendisemncinane, ndasuka

wasele siquba sobabini, sesilandela kwa umkosi lowa ube usewugqitile
sada sagaleleka kuwo. Ndagqitywa ukupangwa intwana ebendiyibinqile
esinqeni esiti ukuyibiza ngumtika. Sagaleleka kwesisizwe sababantu.
Kwati kwakuba kuhambisekile ixesha, kwavakala ilizwi lokuba intloko
ziyafuneka komkulu, kwaba ngentloko kutetwa ke abantu, abatinjiweyo
emkosini. Watike lowo mfo wasa abanye abantu abe ebatimbile wandishiya
mna. Wabuyiswa kwatiwa makaze kunditabata kuba ebemazi ubawo lowo
mnumzana, bekulapo ubawo ebekona kudala eselisoka. Ndagaleleka kulendawo
ndihamba kunye nalomfo ubenditimbile, sahlala ixeshana. Wade wasuka
ukuba agoduke, ndasuka nam ukumlandela, kodwa ndabuyiswa kwatiwa
mandihlale. Ndasisicaka se nkosazana ebifungwa kwesosizwe, egama lingu
Mfutwamini intombi ka Makalangegazi. Ibifungwa ngenxa yokuba ibi
yenkulu. Umvuzo ebendikonza ngawo, ibikukusinela inkobe, kuba kuti ngazo
zonke imini, kuhlangane zonke intombi zalomzi, abasakwayo ndisine ke
ndide ndidinwe ndandule ukupiwa inkobe, esandleni, nditi ndakugqiba
lomtamo ndi­sine kwakona, ndilamba kakulu, bekuti ke ngokuhlwa entangeni
yamadodana kwindawo ebendilala kuyo ndipatwe ngolunye uhlobo, kuba
bekuti ngokuhlwa nditunywe ukuba mandiye kuba inkuni, zenditi ndakubuya
kutiwe mandiye kuka amanzi ecibini ngopondokazi olukulu olugama lingu
Bandla ubutongo ze nditi ndakubuya kutiwe mandiye kuba umbona
emaqongeni. Yaye iyinto yemihla ngemihla, zonke ezizinto ndizenza
ngokunyanzelwa nangokubetwa xa ndingavumiyo ndipatwe ngempato endingena
kuyixela. Ize nditi ndakubanjwa ngabaninizo ndibetwe kwakona ngokuba
inkuni, bebeziteza kude. Lomsebenzi wema ke owasemini nasebusuku
ndiyinto esesi tubeni sokuti emini ndidudiswe ukuze ndifumane into

esizishicilelayo.

AMAZWI KA REV. C. PAMLA OKUBULISA.

Ate amalungu cremente yase Kamastone nabanye abantu abalunge nakona xa
aza kunduluka u-Rev. Charles Pamla ukuya e-Tina bambulisa
ngokumhlanganisela izinto ezixabiso lifike kumashumi amabini eponti.
Kute kwi Sabata epambi kokuba anduluke wenza lamazwi alandelayo
endikucela ukuba uwafake Esigidimini:—

Ewe zihlobo zam, senivile ukuba ndikutshelwe e- Gatberg (e-Ndenxa),
ndikutshwa yintlanganiso yabafundisi. Kulusizi kum ukuba ndisahlukana
nani. Niyazi ukuba akulula ukushiya abantu obubazi uze uye kwabase
mzini. Anditsho ukuti ndiyakalaza ngokundikupa kwabafundisi, kuba
ndikolwa ukuba bakokelelwe ngu Tixo kulonto.

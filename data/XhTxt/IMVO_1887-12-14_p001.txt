EKUBENI n Rulumente enikwa amandla, yinteto yesiqendu sokuqala so “
Mteto Wemihlaba elahliweyo,” wesi 3 womnyaka we 1879, njengoko
ubuyekeswe ngo Mteto wama 24
we 1887, ukuba makayisebenzise ayabe Imihlaba Elahliweyo ngabaniniyo
ngolublobo luchazwa apa, okokuba :

" Xenikweni kuko Irafu Yomhlaba ebanjwa u Rulumente wale Koloni
ngandawo, mhlaimbi ngempahla efunyenwe ku Ralumente, engabhatalwanga
kwada kwapela iminyaka, emihlanu. londawo mhlaimbi lompahla yayekwa,
yalahlwa, waza yena mniniyo nokuba ngoqeshileyo, xa ngaba kunjalo,
kwanommeli wake ngomteto akafunyanwa kuya kuyalezwaRuluneli ukuba yenze
isaziso ngalondawo mhlaimbi ngalompahla ixela ukuba ilahliwe kwi
Government Gazette, nokuba kukulipina ipepa, eyoqonda ukuba ifanelekile
kube kanye ngenyanga kwinyanga ezintatu ezilandelanayo, kuze kuti ukuba
pakati kwezo nyanga ntatu zesaziso akafikanga umniniwo mhlaimbi
oyiqeshileyo ondawo nokuba yimpahla, kungafiki nommeli wake, ukuza
kumisa ibango lake kuwo, aze alike ayibhatale lorafu ilityala, koti
ekupeleni kwezo nyanga sintatu zikankanyiweyo, i Ruluneli iye kuyitabata
londawo nokuba yimpahla ibe yeyayo, ize iyisebenzise nangawupina umteto
wolohlobo owoba uko apa e Koloni ngeloxesha : kodwa ke amaxesha onke
ukuqutywa kwalomteto ukankanyiweyo mase ulungelelane nezahlukwana ze
sibhozo, nese sitoba nese shumi zom.Miselo we 9 womnyaka we 1844, ukuba
ube ngumhlaba obambisileyo, nokusetyenziswa kwemali zawo zivume- lane
nalowomteto.”

Apa ke kwasiswa bonke abantu ukuba le Mihlaba ibalulwa ngasezantsi apa
ekwisiqingata sase Qonce kutiwa iyelahliweyo, ngabaniniyo njengoko
sekutshiwo; ke u Rulumente uyakuyitabata ibe yeyake ngo 16th May, 1888,
ukuba ayimonywanga kwangapambi kweloxesha, zaza zahlaulwa irafu
ezingamatyala kumantyi wesosiqingata, njengoko kubhalwe ngako
kwesisiqingata salomteto. SCHERMBRUCKER,

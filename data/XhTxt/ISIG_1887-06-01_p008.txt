  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 1, 1887.

INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

KUMHLELI WE SIGIDIMI SAMA XOSA,

NKOSI, ngo ncwadi yam ngomhlahlo wako Msimba kuko impendulo kwi Mvo ye
11th yalenyanga. Sendilenzile elam, u Mhleli we Mvo ulenzile elake,
andisafuni kumbi umzi mawahlule ukuteta kupambi kwawo. Indawana kodwa
endingako ndiyiti tupa yeyokuba u Mhleli esiti—” Ugqibile ukumnakana u
Qitala lakwa Ngqika.” Kwelo ke nditi bendinga zibumbelanga siduli ukuzo
ndincatamo ngaso xa ndixuluba abanye. Igama lokuba Qitala lakwa Ngqika
ndalipiwa ngu Mhleli we Mvo, ndati mna andilicekisile ndiyalivuma,
kwanje ngoko ndali vumayo elokuba—"Napakade.” Ngoko anganakana okunene u
Mhleli we Mvo. Into endixakayo xa ndibalayo yeyokuba ndingazibiza
ngalipina igama, kuba emaninzi amagama endibongwa ngawo yi Mvo
andingezimeli ngagama kuba nangona ndingazi nalimbi, yona Imvo yohlala
inakanile neli ndizibiza ngalo namhla iyalazi elokuba, TOL-IXALANGA.

May, 1887.

ISAZISO SIKA RULUMENTE.—NO. 278, 1887.

Kwi Ofisi yo Mongameli we Mihlaba ya Komkulu,

  EKUBENI u Rulumente enikwe igunya nje ngesigqibo esimalunga ne Siqendu
  so Kuqala so Mteto wemi Hlaba
  Elahliweyo kanye (Derelict Lands) No. 3 we.1879, wokutana qwe,
  nokuyipata yonke imi Hlaba Elahliweyo

  kanye ngoluhlobo lulandelayo, olunje ngolu :—

  “ Makuti xeshikweni kuko irafu emele ukurolelwa u Rulumente wale
  Koloni, ngendawo nokuba ngumhlaba ka Rulumente, esukuba ide
  yayiminyaka elishumi ilityala, ibe londawo, nokuba ngulo mhlaba ite
  yayekwa, yashiywa, yayekwa mpela, kwaza akwabiko nomnini kuyinikwa,
  nokuba ngobeyiqashiselwe, kwaye kunjalonje, kungeko namntu bambekileyo
  esikundleni sabo usendaweni yabo onokufunyanwa, kuyakuba semtetweni
  ukuba i Ruluneli ibale isaziso ngawo kwi pepa lezaziso zika Rulumente
  eliyi Government Gazette, nokuba kukulipina ipepa angatanda Iona,
  umhlaba onjalo, nokuba ngumzi oti ushiywe kanye, sishicilelwe inyanga
  zide zibentatu, izekuti ukuba kwezi nyanga ntatu, akuvelanga bani,
  nokuba ngowaye wunikiwe, nokuba ngoqeshileyo, nokuba ngobekwe ngabo
  kwezo ndawo, oyakubanga ezondawo, aze ahlaule le rafu inexesha ;
  i-Ruluneli iyakuti zakupela ezo nyanga ntatu, iwutabate lomhlaba
  nokuba ngumzi, iwupate kwangesiko lemihlaba enjalo emisiweyo kule
  Koloni, ukuba akutanga (njengoko kwahlala kunjalo) lomhlaba nokuba
  ngumzi, emaupatwe ngalemiteto ube ub’ubambisele ityala, indawo ezikwi
  ziqendu ze sibozo, nesitoba, neseshumi se Simiselo (Ordinance) se 9 se
  1844 ngokumayela nokutengiswa komhlaba nokuba ngumzi onjalo, yaye
  nokusetyenziswa kwalo mali kuyakuhanjiswa ngokwalomteto.”

  Kuyaziswa kumntu wonke okokuba le Mihlaba icwangciswe ngezants’ apa,
  ikwisi Sitili (Division) se Victoria East mayibe ngohlobo ilahliwe
  kanye, nje ngoluhlobo sekuxeliwe ngalo, nokokuba u Rulumente
  uyakuyitabatela kuye ayihlute, ngomhla wokuqala (1st) ku October 1887,
  ngapandle kokuba iyakuba sel’ibangiwe yada yahlaulelwa ezo rafu zayo
  bezigqitelwe ngamaxesha, zahlaulwa kwi Mantyi yeso Sitili, njengoko
  kufuneka kwenjiwe njalo kweso siqendu salo Mteto kucatshulwe kuwo.

  F. SCHERMBRUCKER, Umongameli.

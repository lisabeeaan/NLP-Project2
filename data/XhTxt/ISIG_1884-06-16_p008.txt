IBANKI ZE POST OFFICE ZOKULONDOLOZA MALI.

8 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 16, 1884.

UMANDLA WASE GCUWA.

  EMPAHLA incazo siyinika ngapantsi apa yimpahla
  ehlutwe eseleni, eyoti ke ukuba akuko bani uyazileyo

weza wayitabata, itengiswe kwinkundla ye Office ye Mantyi
enkulu e Ntlambe, ngomhla we 2 ku July 1884:—

Inkabi yehasbe e Mtuqwa, empakamo ukwiminwe eyi
14 1/2 ebudala busesitandatwini iminyaka. Enye yinkabi e
Fosi ete tsbaba ngebala elimhlope ebunzi, seyindala,
inamabala okubola kwezilonda ezidala emhlana.

  R. W. STANFORD,
  Imantyi.

  Resident Magistrate’s Office,
  Butterworth, 15 May, 1884.

Umongameli we Post Office unga angake akukanyisele ngamalungelo
angafunyanwayo kwi Post Office, ezinendawo ngoku yokulondoloza imali
nokuba zimalana ezincinane, awoba neliso kuzo u Rulumente, ayimele
nembuyekezo yazo.

Ukuqala ungafaka nokuba yisheleni enye, ekungati xa sukuba uyifuna,
nakuba ungasekona kulondawo apo wawuyifake kona, uyikupe ngokulula
nokuba kukuyipina indawo eyi Post Office egcina imali apa e Koloni.
Njengoko bese kutshiwo, akukataleki apo ungaba ub’uyifake kona, ongati
mhlaumbi ukuba ub’uyifake e Monti, ngokutanda kwako wongezelele enye,
okanye uyikupe yonke, nokuba ukupa isiqingata sayo, xa ke mhlaumbi
uyifuna ungekona e Monti, apo wauyifake kona, ungati uyikupe nokuba kuse
Qonce, kwa Komani, e Rini, e Kimberley, e Bayi, umhlaumbi e Kapa,
ngazwinye yobatalwa nokuba kupina apo ungaba utanda ukuyifumana kona.

Inzala yoba yi nayipeni (9d) ngonyaka epontini ezeleyo.

Abafazi abatshatieyo, no salpo, ngokufanayo nabanye abantu banelungelo
lokuzifakela imalana zabo, elowo eyake; oko kukuti,—ingabi
nakucukunyiswa bam.

Zonke i Mbumba zamaqela ngamaqela o Buhlobo, ne Ngqwebo, zingazigcinela
imalana zazo ngalendlela yokuyi- nqabisa kwa Rulumente, kuba xa zingaba
ziyayifuna zoyifumana ngokulula, nangokumsinya.

I-Gunya lokuba zibuyekezwe imali obe umana uzifaka, lotunyelwa
kwakamsinya nge posi elandela leyo incwadi yako ebika ukuyifuna kwako
ifunyenwe ngayo.

Ayakufiblwa ngokuzeleyo, ukuze angaziwa ngabanye abantu, amagama abo
bafake imali, kunye namanani emali zabo.

Nokokuba kukuyipina i Post Office unganako ukulifumana ipepa
elishicilelweyo lemimmiso, nemiteto ye Banki ze Post Office, ongati
ukuba ute wanendawo ekusilelayo ukuyiqonda, uti ukuzaneza ubalele
incwadi engenqweneli sitampu ke yona uyisingise ngoluhlobo,—

8 April, 1884.

UMANDLA WASE NGQAMAKWE.

  EMPAHLA, efunyenwe idungudela kwesi sitili, iyaku-
  tengiswa mbla itengiswayo impahla (njengokuba ku-

  senjiwanjalo ekupeleni kwenyanga ezintatu) e Ofisini ye
  Mantyi Enkulu yelama Mfengu ngolwesi-Tatu, 2 July
  1884, ukuba ayibanga namntu uti yeyake kwangapambi
  kolo suku.

  Imazi yehashe e Gwangqa, inebala ebunzi, nesilonda
  emblana: indala.

  Itokazi eli Bomvu, elidumbe umhlati wasekohlo, elibu-
  dala ngati yiminyaka emitatu. Lihlutwe kwisela, kuzi-
  ndlelwa ukuba libiwe.

  Itol’ iduna elimnyama lagqibelela: kunye ne Gusha
  ezimasbumi matatu (30), ne Bokwe ezimashumi matatu
  anesibozo (38).

  THOS. A. KING.

Kwi Ofisi yo Mantyi, Umantyi.

Engqamakwe, 19 May, 1884.

IMIHLABA ESHIYIWEYO KWISIQINGATA

  SASE VICTORIA EAST.

  ZIZIQENDU zomhlaba zikankanyiweyo ngapautsi apa
  ezikwesi Siqingata, yakankanywa ukuba iyeshiyiweyo

  kususela kolokuqala ku March 1884, eyile—

1.  Intsimi ekwi Lot No 15, kunye nesiza esilunge nayo
      esikwi Block No 2, lo ke ukwi Location yama Mfengu e
      Auckland ;

2.  Intsimi kwi Lot No 19, kwanesiza esilunge kuyo kwi
      Lot No 5 Block A, kwakona ;

3.  Intsimi kwi Lot No 30, kwanesiza esiyimeleyo Lot
      No 9, Block D kwi Location ka Mavuso;

4.  Intsimi kwi Lot No 74, kwanesiza esiyimeleyo, No 7
      Block L. kwakweka Mavuso ;

5.  Intsimi, Lots No 28, 66, 67, 55 kwi Location yama
      Mfengu e Roxeni;

6.  Intsimi kwi No 93, kwanesiza esiyimeleyo Lot No 5,
      Block Y kwi Location yama Mfengu ka Gwabini;

7.  Intsimi Lot No 51, kwanesiza esiyimeleyo Lot No 6,
      Block D, kwi Location ka Ngobe kwa Gaga ;

8.  Amasimi akwi Lots Nos. 136 no 144, akwi Location
      yama Mfengu e Mxelo.

  Nayipina ke i Mfengu enga ingazitabatela omnye nokuba
  nguwupina kule mihlaba ikwezi ndawo zikankanyiweyo,
  ingawufumana ngokuti ibatale kule ofisi, imali yomhlaba
  (quit rent) engaba ibe isafunwa ukubatalwa kulo mhlaba,
  kunye nopawu Iwakwa Rulumente oluxela ukuba sukuba
  kubatelwe (tamps) olungaba belute alwabatalelwa.

  Osukuba enendawo engacacileyo kakuhle, angati aneli-
  swe ngokubuzisa ngayo, kulo ugama lingapantsi apa.

ALEX. STEWART,

Kwi Office ye Mantyi, Imantyi.

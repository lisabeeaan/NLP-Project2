ISAZISO.

EKUBENI abantsundu abawumiyo lomma- ndla wase Qonce, benga banini mhlaba
abanye, abanye bengaba qeshi, kungati abakuqondi ukuba bamelwe uku rola
lemali yendlela (Road Rates), njengoko benjenjalo bonke abamhlope.

Kuyaziswa namhla, ukuba bonke, abama- gama akoyo kwincwadi exela ukuma
kwama- xabiso omhlaba wabo, bamelwe ukuba bazi- role ezomali zibizwayo,
kwi Ofisi Yokulu- ngisa Imicimbi Yalommandla, (Divisional Council)
ngapandle kokude kubuye kwenziwe esinye isaziso, kuneso senziwa
ngonyaka, kwipepa lakwa Rulumente (Government Gazette) ngokomiselo we 9
womnyaka we 1858.

Kusaziswa kwinto yonke, ukuba imali yendlela (Road Rates), ifuneka
ibatelwe ngabanini mhlaba, nangaba qeshileyo, kule Ofisi ikankanyiweyo.
Ukuba lomali, kude kwa yinyanga, kususela kwesi saziso sana- mhla, inga
rolwanga, kuya kunyanzelwa nge- gunya lomteto, kunga buyanga kwaziswe.

Ngabezi ndawo ke ekufuneka beyi rolile lemali:—Emngqesha, e-Gxulu,
e-Zeleni, e- Ramnyiba, e-Qibira, kwa Zidenge, e-Ngcobo, e-Rabula, kwa
Nakalo, e-Hlovini, e-Zincuka, kwa Ngwenya (Red Hill), Middle Drift, (kwa
Kama), nakuzo zonke lzikolo Nezixeko, Nemihlaba Yengqesho.

E-Qonce, October 23, 1879.

waubhatala kakuhle lowo mhlaba ukankanyiweyo, wamkela itaitile zawo,

eyeyalowo LEWANI HLANKOMO ukankanyiweyo, ngexesha lokufa kwake efike

HLANKOMO, oyena mkulu umntakwabo osekoyo, nguyena mntu kanye

HADI

Uya kubulelwa nguwo wonke umzi umbhaleli wetu ngamanqaku awenzileyo
ngeyona nto ufukeme kuyo umzi ose Glen Grey kulomteto ka Lozi. Uti : —

NKOSI MHLELI WE “ Mvo ZABANTSU­NDU,”— Kawuncede undifakele kwelo pepa
lako lohlanga lamauqaku ndiwafumene kwi ntlanganiso eyayi kwa
Bangindlala nge 16 October, 1894, isingete umcimbi we Simnara, esaya
kuwupulapula singamadoda amatatu alapa e Lesseyton; esafumana isekelwe
ukukongozela ukwakelwa pantsi kwemigaqo ye Rafu Yomsebenzi. Esati
sakuchasa lonto isekelwa pezu kwayo, sapendulwa ngezwi elasidanisayo ngu
mfundisi wakona, u Debese, wati—sisemva tina sichase lonto, izibonda
sezitengwe ngarnashumi ama­tatu emokolo ukuze zingabi namlomo zona
kulento yocando. Zawamkela ke zaluvuma ucando pantsi kwe Glen Grey Bill.
Esite sisakubuza ngo Mtandazo lo upalazwe sisizwe sonke ukuba
aniwenzanga na nina? Bati basaugenisa amagama, sebeya kuyitumela
yomibini le Mitandazo kunye nalo we Glen Grey Bill nalo ucela Isimnara
esekelwa ukukongo­zela imali ye Labour Tax. Emva kwenteto eshushu
yabachasi, yapunyeleliswa lonteto ngevoti. Abachasi yaba ngamadoda
amane, ango Messrs. David Matsolo, J. J. Tantsi, Z. Rani, no J. W.
Sondlo; abapumelelisi singabalula izibonda ezi- tatu, H. Kalipa, B.
Mbengo, Solomon Teka, neqela ke lomzi wonke owawuko. Saba siyazilahla
intonga siyabuya. Ukutsho ke uhlaba ukangele wena wase Rautini nawe wase
Herschel. Wena wase Rautini uti kungalusizi ukuba kuvuka
langxushungxushu yake yako. Ukufa komzi namhlanje kutengwa nge morgen
ezimashumi matatu, ukubulawa namhlanje ko Mtandazo owenziwa kwindawo
ngendawo ubulawa nge mokolo ezimashumi matatu. Usalilela ukuti wena wase
Herschel i Glen Grey isemva ngo Mtandazo, kanti ayiko umta- ndazo
eseliwukupile kukutuu'droela uku­ba umteto ukawuleze bade bafumane i
Simnara. Ungaba usalila wena, mna sendilile ndancama, sendincamele e
Tixweni mna mntu ungena mogolo. Ke Mhleli andimntu wake wakukataza, uya
kundinyamezela nokuba ndibhale incwadi ende, nditemba ukuba uya
kuyikawulezela ukuyibonakalisa pambi komzi ontsundu. obulawayo.
Manditshonele, Ndim,

ZITANA RANI. Lesseyton, 5 Nov., 1894.

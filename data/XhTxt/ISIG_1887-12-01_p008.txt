ISAZISO SAKWA RULUMENTE.

KUYAZISWA kubo bonke abemiyo, kwiziqwenga (Field
cornetcies) ezitile zesisitili (Division) ukuba

  abantu abamiselwe ukwamkela amagama abavoti, (Register-
  ing Officers) nje ngomteto we Palamente bawuqalile uku-
  wenza lomsebenzi, njengokomteto wabavoti, (Registration
  Act) we 1887. Okokuba siti eso isiqwenga esipetwe ligosa
  (Field Cornet) siqokelele amagama abantu abanemfanelo
  yokuvotela unyulo Iwabaya e Palamente,.

  UKUZE ke kubeko inani elifumanekayo ngokufezekileyo,
  nokuze kungabiko ngozi ingatintela amagama anemfanelo
  mabati bonke abanebango lokuvotela abaya e Palamente
  bangenise izibango zabo kulomagosa ezondawo amiselweyo
  (Registering Officers) bati okunye bacikide indawo zokuba
  azi angaba abalwe kade na awabo amagama, abekwa eluhlwi-
  Iwanemfanelo, kwangosuku olusengapambi kolokuvalwa
  nokuba kungalo kanye,

  KE, abamkeli magama bazo zonke eziqwenga zesisitili
  (Division) ngabo aba bamagama adwele ngasezantsi apa.

I balwe ngolusuku Iwe 5 ku October 1887.

JAMES F. BOYCE.

KWISITILI SASE TSOMO PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

IQELA lempahla eyimidungela kwesisitili, eyakuti ukuba
ayizanga kubangwa ngabaniniyo kwangapambili,

  ibe iyakutengiswa kwezintengiso zibakoyo ikwata zonke
  zempaphla enjalo. Lentengiso iyakuba se Gcuwa ngomhla
  wesine (4th) ku January 1888, ngosuku Iwe Sitatu evekini,
  Yiyo le—

YINKABI emnyama yehashe, enomtshiso onje-—C. F., emalunga neminyaka e 7
ubudala.

YIPONI elubelu, ete fakafaka, etiwe qumpu kwindlebe zombini,
eseminyakeni e 6 ubudala.

YINKABI elubelu, esel’indala, itiwe tshwi kulendlebe yase kunene ine
ntungo ezimnyama, kwane mpau zesilonda.

YIMAZI engwevu kuhle eyenziwe inkonjane kwindlebe yase kohlo, ikwa
nempau zesilonda, iseminyakeni e 4 ubudala.

YIPONI elubelu ebityileyo, inqina lase kunene ngasemva lipants’ ukuba
mhlope, imalunga neminyaka e 3 ubudala.

IMPANGA.

Ngo September ngosuku loku 1, e Batenjini, kubube u SESIWE umfazi ka
Donti umfo ka Kashe wase Auckland.

“ Ngu Yehova onikayo, ngu Yehova otabatayo, malibo- ngwe igama lika
Yehova.”

Kwase Batenjini ngosuku Iwe 10 ku October, kubube u DONTI KASHE, umfo
obe ngumlandeli we Nkosi wenene.

“ Ukolisile sicaka ndini esinyanisekileyo, ubunyaniseke kwizinto
ezimbalwa.”

Kwase Batenjini nge 14 ku October, kubube u NOTOWA KASHE, unina ka
Donti. “ Bebebukeka besapilile, baza abahlukana nasekufeni.” “ Hlalani
niti tu, nazi ukuba ndingu Tixo.”

Nge 15 ku October e Beaconsfield, (Dayimani) kubube u MINA NIGHINGALE no
PERCY NIGHTINGALE Kashe, ukupela kwabantwana baka Meshech Kashe.

“ Lilelabanjalo ikomkulu lezulu.”

Ngo October ngosuku Iwe 13 kubube u TSAMSE into ka Xuba, wasema
Tshaweni. Ngomnye wamakolwa amadala ka Mr. Chalmers omdala.

Ububele e Lusizi apo eb’esel’ ehleli kona, wabuba esate- mbel’ e Nkosini
yake.

Kwaku October kubube iqela labantu abalunge e Pirie. Omnye ngu SARAH
NQUKA owaziwa ngokuba ngu Noketile, intombi ka Hans Nquka (u Duba) omnye
wamagqoboka amadala ase Katala. Ububele e Nkosini yake.

Kwakuyo lonyanga kubube u STEPHEN SIHLABAKA wase Lukalweni. Wemka etete
kashushu ngomqomboti ukunga- lungi kwawo, washiya umyolelo wokuti zekuti
noko yena emkayo nje kungaze kwenziwe tywala endlwini yake.

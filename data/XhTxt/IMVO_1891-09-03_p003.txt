“ Umzokelo ulunge ngapezn kwesimi- selo ” (example is better than
precept) yatsho intloko yetitshala (Mr. Geo. Baker) xa sifundayo
ngamaxesha akudala. Yayibonisa ukuba umzekelo wesimilo se Titshala
ulunge nangapezu kwento eyifundisa abantwana. Oko kukuti, mayiti ukuba
ifuna beiike ngexesha elifaneleki- leyo ingateti kupela ngalonto, kodwa
yona ngokwayo ibe ngumiiki ngexesha— nokuba xa ifuna ukuba bakolwe
mayibe likolwa ngokwayo—nokuba ifuna benozimilo ezihle, mayibe nesimilo
esihle ngokwayo—nokuba ichaza into mayiyilinganise nento ekufupi efana
naleyo ingabonwayo ngeloxesba. Namhla ke lamazwi ndiwafumana ewufanele
umzi wakowetn ontsundu we Koloni yase Kapa enqula ngokwesoko lika Dyan
Wesile. Bantu bakowetu, yibani ne ngxowa efana neyalapa e Natal engu
Nzondelelo.

UMLILO—E-Stellembosch kusand’ ukubako umlilo otshabalalise izindlu

INYOKA—E-Queenstown kuko umfo obulele inyoka waza akuyiti qeke wafika

NGASE GWILIGWILI kutiwa ngezimvula kuko induli esuke yati kepu elunye
icala lemka kunye nehlatana elibe likuyo.

UMKANGELI WEZIKOLO — U-Mr. Charles Clarke umiselwe ukuba abe ngumkangeli
wezikolo ezilunge ne Government kula macala etu.

ABABULELE ABANYE—E-Kapa kute abantsundu ababini bexabene omnye
waselemhlaba omnye ngesitshetshe. Kwenzeke into ekwanjalo nase Free
State.

INTENGO—Ngomhla 17 ka February kuya ku- tengiswa kulomhlaba kutiwa yi
Louws Kraal, E-Adelaide, igusha 600, inkomo 12, amahashe 6, nempahla
zendlu.

U-SIR RICHARD TEMPLE, I-Governor yase India ushicilele ipepa elazisa
ukuba indlala yakona ngoku bayoyisile, ke uyababulela bonke
abancedisileyo.

E-KAPA kuko umlungu ofakwe etolongweni ngenxa yokubeta umfazi wake.
Kulusizi ukuba bengenjiwanjalo nabantsundu abamana ukufeketa ngabantwana
babantu.

U-MR. BRISLEY, isandla sika Adam Koko, usahambela E-Kapa ukuya
kulungiselela U-Adam lowo ngezinto afanele ukuzenza njengokuba nje
luguqulwe ulaula Iwakona.

INTO EFANELE UKUQONDWA—Inenekazi elitile elikwele Diamond liyibatalele
imfundo yabantwana balo ngokutengisa izilimo ezinje ngozikabiji ebezise
sitiyeni esipatwa lilo.

U-NDAMASE NO NGANGELIZWE kuvakala ukuba bazamela ukuhlalelama ngoxolo.
U-Ndamase kutiwa uzihlanganisile inkomo ezibe zitinjwe ngabantu bake
kuba Tembu watumela ilizwi ku Ngangelizwe ukuba zize kutatyatwa.
U-Ngangelizwe utumele ilizwi lokuba ungabungapela ubutshaba pakati kwake
neya Mampondo inkosi.

AMASELA—Kwindlu yocingo E-Port Alfred kusand’ ukungena amasela, afika
kodwa imali ishenxiselwe kwenye indlu ngumpati wocingo kona. Asike
aseletshabalalisa incwadi ebezikona.

U-CAPTAIN BLYTH ukufika kwake pesheya kwe Nciba uhlangatyezwe yinto
eninzi yama Mfengu esese mlanjeni, waya ekaya lake selempahle kumacalana
onke, edlalelwa igubu ngamandla akwa Feltman.

KUBABALELANI—Ozibiza ngokuti ungu “ Nto- mbi ayipatwa” siyi fumene
incwadi yake kodwa asinakuyishicilela engasazisanga igama lake wasi-
cacisela kakuhle apo ahlala kona. Siteta kwalonto nangeka
Mtandabengamtandi.

ONYATELWE YINQWELO—Kute E-Aliwal North inqwelo etile isihla endulini
yegungxu ndaweni, umkokeli obehleli ngapambili wawa, enye yevili
ezipambili yamnyatela emhlatini yaza eyangase mva yamnyatela intloko
wafa kwa oko.

AMAMFENGU—Omnye wababalelani uti anga- maqela esuka pesheya kwe Nciba
ukuza kufuna umsebenzi apa Ekoloni. Kuvakala ukuba kuko iqela eliza kuya
kulungisa Unontongwana, elinye liza kuqeshwa ngabakangeli bendlela
ezilunge ne Qonce, abanye baye kwezenqwelo zomlilo. Avuselelwe kangaka
yilahleko ayifumene ngenxa kanogumbe.

UMKANGELI wemali zakomkulu U-Mr. Van der Reit ufike kwi Ofisi ye Mantyi
yase Qonce oko ebekona ngo December kuko imali ekungaqonda- kaliyo ukuba
itenina ukucitwa kwayo. Inani layo ikwiwaka elinamakulu amahlanu
namatandatu eponti. U-Mr. Judge, Imantyi yase Queenstown utunyelwe
E-Qonce ukuba apengulule ukucitwa kwalo mali.

UBUGQWIRA—Kute kolwe 7 ku January E-Ra- bula kwabiwa inkabi ezinkulu
xebokwe zika Boyi Kwinana. Kufunwe kwafunyanwa sezihlinziwe, kwatatyatwa
izikumba nentwana yesifuba nom- hlehlo, yashiywa yonke inxenye yenyama.
Ezo zikumba kuye kutengiswa ngazo E-Burnshill, kodwa akakafunyanwa
amagqwira lawo. Kanti noko angaqondiyo ngoku “ isono sawo sisaya kuwa-
fumana.”

ONCWATYWE ESAFE ISIDULI—E-Mountreal E-North America kuko intokazi
ebisifa, kwaza emva kwentsuku ezimbini kwagqitywa kwelokuba ifile, yaya
kuncwatywa ngolwesitatu emva koko. Kute xa kusamiwe pezu kwencwaba,
kwafika isihlobo selinenekazi sacela ukuke sibone ubuso balo
okokugqibela. Kuvunyiwe ke okwenene yavulwa ibokisi, kanti kuza kufikwa
liselilele ngecala pofu ubuso libukangelise pezulu, ingubo zokuncwaba
zicitacitekile, ubuso bute nkwa kubonakala ukuba ngumntu obezamela
ukupefumla ode wafa kukufutaniseleka. Kuqondakele ukuba ngapambili ube
safe isiduli kodwa, waza ke wazuza ukuqabuka kuso ngenxa yokushukunyiswa
yile nto imtweleyo, waza waselebulawa kukufutaniseleka.

UNONTONGWANA asikuko nokuba mbi. Osand’ ukuvela kona uti kutinteleke
inqweio 200.

IQINGA LOKUFUMANA IGUSHA—Kuko umfana otile kula macala ase Gxulu ote
wacapukisa ama- kwenkwe amabini amaqaba ada ambeta, wati uyaya ekaya
selenabile, ebomvu ligazi. Ufike waseledlula eyakuti emlungwini ndifile
ngamakwenkwe. Okwenene umlungu akubona induma ugwebe ngeliti ikwenkwe
inye mayirole ishumi elinanye legusha zinikwe lomntu uqekezwe intloko
(kuba kambe emlungwini ukubetwa uduma kutiwa kukuqekezwa intloko).
Azirolile ke njengoko kutetiweyo.

SIYA KOLWA ukuba baninzi abaya kuba lusizi njengati ukuva ukuba U-Rev.
W. Impey, Umo- ngameli we Seminary yase Nxukwebe, uza kushe- nxiselwa
E-Rini. Kwisikolo esikulu nebesifanele ukuya sikula njenge Nxukwebe
leyo, kunika ubu- nzima obutile ubuko bendoda enkulu nebekiweyo
ngamadoda amahlelo onke ayaziyo, njengoko anjalo U-Mr. Impey. Asitsho
ukuti akuko ndoda yimbi ingawuqubayo umsebenzi kona, kodwa siti kulusizi
ukushenxiswa kwendoda enjalo kumzi ofana nalowo.

OBALEKE ETOLONGWENI—Kute E-Fort Beau­fort xa ababaujwa basebenzayo kwati
shwaka umfo abati ngu Tamm. Uiandwe uxnkondo wake waya kwela emlanjeni
noko akabonwa bani. Kute ngobo busuku kwangenwa endlwini ka Dr. Palm­er
kwemkiwa nesali nengubo ezixabiso likwiponti ezisixenxe. Kwenye indlu
kubiwe ihashe kwangobo busuku. Ingubo zika Tamm abenazo etolongweni
zifunyenwe kusasa, kucingelwa ke ngoko ukuba nguye lo wenze oku.
Usisicaka esidala sika Dr. Palmer esiwaziyo amagumbi endlu yake.

UMGCINI TOLONGO—Omnye wababalelani betu uti, “ Emnyameni (Alexandria)
kuko umgcini wetolongo ogama lingu C. Raven, ote watata imali
etolongweni, wayicita ngokuyisela engayi boleka- nga. Ufakweke
etolongweni wanikwa icawa ezi- mbini. Ngoku akukabiko mntu ogcina
itolongo, isabanjiswe itolika yakona engu Mr. W. N. Somgiso. Uti naye
elilizwe lase Mnyameni linengozi, bekusand’ ukubaleka umantyi wakona
ogama lingu John George James Rawstorne kwange mali. Nangoku akakabonwa
apo akona, uzimele ngosuku 7 ku December 1874.”

UBUMENEMENE—Kute kweziveki zisand’ ukudlula umfazi otile ngase Mkubiso,
abati ngu Noanti, yafika indoda selequmbe into engatetekiyo, ingasazi
nesizatu. Kude kwalalwa kwasa nge Sabata ingekazi indoda ukuba konakele
ntoni na. Emini nge Sabata leyo ute shwaka umfazi, kodwa indoda yaqonda
ngokuhlwa ukuba akako. Iputumile ke kowabo kule mizi inganeno kwe Qonce.
lye yamfumana E-Qonce ekaya, yafika yabuza ukuba, Noanti yintonina
elapa. Usuke omnye wasitsho isililo esiti ngumfokazi wapina lo
undilandelayo. Kwakuba njalo ite cwaka indoda, watitu no Noanti. Kwakuba
mzuzwana ibuye yati, Kodwa Noanti yintoni na le uyenzayo—upendule
ngokuti, Yo! nanku umfokazi ndingamazi endilandela, amapolisa alomzi aye
ngapina. Unge uyayeka omnye watula nomfazi. Kube mzuzwana wabuya wabuza
ukuba kodwa yintonina le. Unobanti omdala udanduluke ngokunye ekala
ngamapolisa, yaqonda kaloku indoda ukuba ndafa mandishenxe, yemkake
okwenene yaya kubika kulontombi into eyenziwe ngumntwana wabo. Ufike
Ubanti iseko indoda, efile kukunxila butywala bomlungu, waselesiti
kuyise ketela lomntu inkomo zake, andisayi kuye andisamfuni; akayi xela
ko­dwa into amfake yona. Ite ke nendoda mna sendivuma oko, kuba lomfazi
undenze into embi, okunye bendingazi ukuba uyanxila, yatsho ke okunene
yagoduka ukuza kubiza abantu bakowayo ukuba baputume inkomo ezo—Abake
balubona ukula lalomacala abangemangaliswe kakulu ukuba Unoanti enjenje.

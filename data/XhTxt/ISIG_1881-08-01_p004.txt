ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1881.

SIFUMENE inguqulo yepepa elafundwa ngu Rev. E. Makiwane entlanganisweni
yabafundisi bamahlelo onke, ebise King William’s Town ngalenyanga
ifileyo. Sinosizi ukuti kute ngenxa yendaba ebesezi lungelo
ukushicilelwa asibinakulifaka kwipepa lanamhla. Asitandabuzi bolifumana
abafundi betu kwinyanga ezayo. Kuko nezinye incwadi e-singabanga
nakuzifaka kwangeso sizatu; ezinye kuzo mteto yazo ibingemandla mani
noko.

U SIR ARTHUR KENNEDY, i Ruluneli yase Queensland e Australia, ite,
ngomhla wokuvalwa kwesinye sezikula zakona, ibekisilele kudodana: “Nina
madodana noba nelizwi elulaulweni lwe Koloni, ndiyatemba noti elolungelo
nilisebenzise kakuhle ngapezulu kokuba lisetyenziswe ziqingata zokunyula
amadoda e Parlamente ezitile, ndikolwa anisokuti njengazo nincedise
ukunyula amalungu angenamsebenzi.” Kuti ngati lamazwi ebefanele
ukubekiselela kudonana lwama Xosa naba Mbo akule Koloni. Oyise bawo
benze ingxovu-ngxovu esingenakuyixela ngokunyula amadoda okuwamela e
Parlamenteni, egameni lako konke okufanelekileyo sanga singebuya sive
umntu ongenabuhlobo kwaba mnyama engenisiwe kwangabo.

IZINTO NGEZINTO

U MR. SAUL SOLOMON ne nenekazi lake usahambele kweli lasema Xoseni.

U RILI.—Eli tshawe lipulapule kwi cebo lika Mr. Fynn, umhlobo walo
omdala, lamkele indawo yokuba pantsi ko Rulumente.

LUMKELANI INCINIBA.—E Somerset West, kufupi nje, u Mr. Eksteen, umpati
we Hotele, ukatywe yi Nciniba, wafa kwa oko.

U MR. SPRIGG, obengu mongameli we Bulumente ekutshwe yi Parlamente le
isand’ ukwahlukana, uhambele pesheya ngenxa yokungapili.

EYASE Koloni apa i Rulumente kungati ayifuni kuposwa xesha ekutini iqale
ukwenza aba loliwe inikwe igunya yi Parlamente ukuba ibenze.

IMIKOSI.—Ama Lawu abese Lusutu ngemfazwe ehle ngo loliwe ukusuka kwa
Komani ukuya e Monti. Kumzuzu esukayo ngase Kapa.

U PRESIDENT GARFIELD, umongameli we United States e America, udutyulwe
libedengu ngale nyang’ ifileyo lamngxwelera kakubi. Sele c'aca noko
ngoku.

AMA PEPA ase Rini abika ukububa kuka Mr. Charles Impey, unyana
womfundisi, u Mr. Wm. Impey lo usand’ ukupuma e Wesile use Monti ngoku.

OLOLIWE.—I Rulumente ye Free State izaku hlanganisa amadoda awenza
umteto (Volksraad) yelozwe, ukuba inikwe igunya lokwenza indlela
zenqwelo zomlilo e Free State apo.

IPEPA lama Ngesi lase Kapa eliyi Cape Argus ebeli shicilelwa ngu Saul
Solomon neqela lake, lipumile ezandleni zake ngoku, kuba sele mdala;
lipetwe ngu Mr. Francis J. Dormer.

OZIBULELEYO.—Kwi fama le u Cetywayo ahlala kuyo unamadoda akwa Zulu
ahlala naye. Enye kuwo iqumbise u Langalibalele, ngomnye wabafazi bake,
yati ngokoyika u Cetywayo yazibulala.

UKUBUBA KUKA REV. N. H. SMIT.—Umfundisi we ramente yama Independent
entsundu e Rini, ubube ngequbuliso enikela umtendeleko. Yinto ebang’
usizi le. Siva ubuhlungu kunye nentlanganiso abeyipete.

KUVUNYIWE yi Parlamente, ngenxa ka Mr. Irvine, ukuba kuti ukuba
kufanelekile kwenziwe indlela ka loliwe eyosuka kwindawo ezo kumbiwa
kuzo amalahle e Ndwe, iye kudibana nendlela eza kumbiwa esuka kwa Komani
eya e Aliwal North.

UKUQUTYWA KWE BOKWE.—Ngesaziso se Ruluneli akunakuqutywa mpahla izibokwe
ingena mhlaimbi ipuma kwi siqingata sase Bofolo, sikolwa, nesase Nyara,
ngenxa yesifo esikoyo ezi bokweni zezondawo. Kuvulekile ukuquba
ozinkomo, nozigusha.

UKUHLA KOHLANGA.—Uti umbalelani ne East London Dispatch (ipepa lase
Monti) kupants’ ukungabiko venkile kwelaba Tembu ekungatengiswa kuyo
iziselo ezi nxilisayo, naba ntsundu, kulomandla, baye bebazi rangaranga
ngakumbi nangakumbi.

AMA JUDA.—E Russia, pesheya, oluhlanga lugula pantsi kwe ntshutshiso
ezibu hlungu. Amanene atile e Kapa ahlanganisa imali zokuwancedisa.

UKUPANGWA.—Kulo mandla wase Gqunube kukalazwa kakulu ngenxa yokuba
abantu baya badwa bapangwe. Kutiwa abenzi bezizinto ngaba mnyama.

I RULUNELI.—Intlanganiso ebixoxa ngemicimbi yase Transvaal ipele pakati
kwale nyanga idlulileyo, i Ruluneli u Sir Hercules Robinson wokaye elu
Sutu x’ abuyela e Cape Town.

U LANGALIBALELE.—E Parlamenteni yapesheya kuko ndoda ike yabuza ukuba
wokululwa ninina u Langalibalele. Ute u Mr. Grant Duff ukupendula
kunqabile ukumkulula ngelixesha.

UMNENGA.—Esisilo sibanjwe e Bayi ngu Capt. Robinson, oyintloko yo
stemele ekutiwa yi Warwick Castle, ubude baso babuzinyawo ezipants’
ukuba lishumi elinesibini, ububanzi zintandatu.

U REV. MR. SHUMANN, wase Union Dale wamkele isicelo sokuba
ngumfundisi—sikolwa, we bandla lase Inde­pendent eli Ntsundu—e Cradock.
Ngu Rev. J. F. Philip, B.A., obepete lompi ngoku use Dayimani.

UKUBUBA KWE NKOSI ENDALA.—Enye ye nkosi ebezimele eli kutiwa ngoku lele
Dayimani, u Jantyi Mathibi, kubikwa lipepa lase Kimberley, ukuba
ibubile. Le nkosi yenye yezahlutwa umhlaba lo kumbiwa kuwo amatye
anqabileyo ayabi sabuyekezwa.

NGEMFAZWE le ibiko e Transvaal kwabulawa u Dr Bar­bour ngama Bulu
ngendlela enye engapandle kweyokulwa. Ababulali abazange bafunyanwe,
kodwa kwabanjwa madoda atile aranelwayo. Lomadoda asaza kukamisa pambi
kwe Jaji zase Free State.

NGOKU ibisand’ ukuhamba i Jaji yagweba e Richmond, abe Lungu ababini u
Chase no Cunninghame—ibagwebela ukuxonywa. Inqayi zeso sixeko ziba
camagushele kwi Ru­luneli, ete u Chase, zonke imini zake emhlabeni wozi
gqibela etolongweni; umzalwana wake woba minyaka lishumi kwa kona.

AMA WESILE.—Ngo October ozayo, liti elinye lama pepa apesheya, kobako
intlanganiso enkulu yamadoda awonyulwa kuzo zonke iramente zama Wesile,
eyoxoxa ngezinto zelohlelo. Ngati ngu Dr. Osborn owoti ongamele, nakuba
ke no Rev. Wm. Arthur kutetwa ngaye, ngati yena seloyekela u Dr. Osborn.

INQWELO YOMLILO NABAFUYI.—Liti ipepa lase Monti ezi nqwelo zenze into
enye ukubulala impahla kule ndlela iya kwa Komani. Abalahlekwe yimpahla
ngenxa yayo, pakati kwabanye, ngu Mr. Field no Mr. Manley, owokuqala
ulahlekwe linani elitile le gusha, owokugqibela yimazi yenkomo esengwa
kakulu.

IMFAZWE YASE TRANSVAAL. — I Rulumente yapesheya isand’ ukuxela amanani
ama soldati awabulawayo kwi mfazwe ebise Transvaal. Inkosana ezinkulu
ezabulawayo, ngalo lonke ixesha lemfazwe zimashumi mabini anesitoba,
ingxwelera zimashumi mabini; isajini nama kopulali nama joni, inani
ngama 366 awabulawayo, nama 428 awangxwelerwayo.

U SAUL SOLOMON, ESQ., M.LA,
E LOVEDALE.

Eli nene lidume kunene ngokutetelela abantu abamnyama, ligaleleke e
Lovedale, ngomhla wa 20 ka July, lamkelwa ngovuyo, nangembeko enkulu.
Udodana olufunda amashishini luhlangene lonke kunye nabantsundu bale
lali yase Love­dale pambi kwe ndlu ka Dr. Stewart. Ugaleleke no Dr.
Stewart owabemputumile e Bofolo, waxelwa kwa nguye kwindimbane leyo nga
mazwana ambalwa. Upendule u Mr. Solomon, wati pakati kwenteto yake,
njengokuba evile, wabona, izinto azivileyo nazibonileyo uyakuzama
ngapezulu ukubatetelela abamnyama. Ubulele ngenxa yokumenzela imbeko
kwabo. Njengokuba elapa u Mr. no Mrs. Solo­mon babonakalise utando
olukulu. Ngo 22 ka July u Mr. Solomon wenzele lamadodana imbeko yokuba
adle kunye naye:—Rev. P. J. no Mrs. Mzimba, Mr. no Mrs. Knox Bokwe, Mr.
G. Koboka, Mr. S. Mzimba, Mr. J. Mbane, no Mr. J. Tengo-Jabavu,
Usagqitela e Qonce, xa akona woba kwa Mr. J. J. Irvine. M.L.A. Kube
mandi ukubona umhlobo waba mnyama.

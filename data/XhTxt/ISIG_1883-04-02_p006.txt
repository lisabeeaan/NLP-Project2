ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 2, 1883.

U M. P. NENGXELO KA MR. DONALD ROSS, M.A., F.R.S.E

NKOSI.—Kundixakile ukupendula u “ Ngxam-yom-Wesile,” “ I. W. Wauchope,”
“ Mwesile,” no “ Lamatye.” Kuba ndite ndakuhlabela nhlanga ngamalungelo
alo, ndamisa izizatu ezinq'inwayo ngawo onke amadoda anengqondo,
angatandi kulwa nezazela zawo, ngenkani, namapike, nokanda, nobuciko
bentloko. Ndaye ngokwenjenjalo ndifu- na ukunq'ina ubunyaniso benteto
yam. Kute pezu koko u “ Lamatye,” “ I. W. Wauchope,” no
“Ngxam-yom-Wesile,” bema kwelokuti yonke inteto yam ayinene bubuxoki
ngapandle kwamazwi (arguments) okugxotisa izizatu endizimisileyo. U
Wauchope utete ngokundincazela icuba alibize ngegama elifanele yena,
inteto esiyilindele kumagxagxa endlela (navies) kunokuba ipume emlonyeni
wenene elinje ngo “ Wauchope.” Xa tina singenisa ingxoxo esi Gidimini
sakuba singadlali, kananjalo sifaka into esiyicingileyo, siti kuba
singenjengo Wauchope & Co. sikumbulele ukuba kuko abakanyiselwe ngapezu
kwetu kuyimfanelo ukuba basiluleke. Ibiyimfanelo ka Wauchope &Co. ukuba
bangati xa kuko intaba enkulu emi pakati kwabo nemicimbi yemfundo,
bafune ukutyoboza kule ntaba ngemini enye, ibiyindawo yabo ukuvala
imilomo, kuba kubo bonke abazana nokuma kwemfundo yeli lizwe sicacile
isimo sala madoda ukuhlala ngezimnyama kwawo, enditembayo okokuba aya
kuyaleka namhlanje ukuzigaxela entweni angayiqondiyo. Esinye isipako
esibi esimilweni sale firm ka Wauchope &Co. ngumoya wokungatandi
ukukanyiselwa, ukucingela ukuba bonke ubulumko bumi apo kupele kona
ukwazi kwabo yonke into engapaya kokwabo ukwazi buxoki. Zininzi izizatu
endingazivelisayo ukumela inteto yam nakuba zingenzi nto ku Wauchope
&Co. Sendiya kuvelisa ezimbalwa ezinqinelana nenteto yam yenyanga
ezilishumi elinambini. Wenjenje u Mr. Donald Ross, M.A.,
Inspector-General of Schools and Colleges :—

(a) “Ngenye imini ndangena esikolweni sendoda efundileyo emnyama
    ifundisa i Reduction ngohlobo olusi nyopo-nyopo olungena kuqondakala
    nakubantwana abatatu abebe ngati sebene ngqondo, baye abanye bengena
    ncwadi &c. bedlala ngemva. Ite inkabi ayizuzi nto ebantwini
    iyasishiya isikolo. Okokuba besingafumana iqinga lokusondela
    engqondweni yomntu omnyama besingaba nako ukuyicombulula lengxakeko
    yobupantsi bemfundo yabamnyama.”

(b) “ Kucacile betu ukuba uninzi Iwe titshala zabanyama azifundanga.
    Okokuba umfundisi nesi Bonda abaziqwalaseli ziyawulibala umsebenzi
    wazo.” &c.

(c) Sifunda ntonina kulento, ukuba kanti kuhleliwenje into eninzi yemali
    ecitelwa imfundo yabamnyama ifane ipalazwa.”

(d) “ Ngamakulu amabini anamashumi amabini izikolo zapesheya kwe Nciba
    nase Lusutu ngamakulu amabini, e Koloni sezikolise ukungenwa
    ngabamnyama uninzi Iwezi zikolo zipetwe nga “ Matye,” azincedi nento
    kwinqubelo pambili yeli lizwe, sinobunq'ina bokubonakalisa ukuba abo
    bafumene imfundo ete nyi batabata misebenzi yimbi nokuba imfundo
    yetitshala zapesheya kwe Nciba ipantsi kanye. Okokuba isiqingata
    sezi zikolo besingavalwa ngomso i Koloni ayingelahlekwa nto kulala
    kombete wetshoba lazo. Olwam uluvo ngazo kuba ziyingozi kuba
    zivelisa intsobi ebuxoki yemfundo nokumbeka,” &c.

(e) “Ndikangele kwezi nyaniso, nezinye ezikwanjalo, kweli nani likulu
    lezikolo zabamnyama, nemali ecitwa kuzo, emqulwini we titshala
    ezinga “ Matye.” Ukunxunela kwe titshala ezifundileyo ukushiya
    umsebenzi wazo zingxamele kweminye &c. Ndifike ndenze esi sigqibo
    ukuba elona cala lipantsi nelona kufunyanwa kucitwa imali nje
    ngabamnyama elihlelo lemfundo ye Koloni lifuneka liguqulwe kuba
    uninzi lwezi zikolo lungacukumisi nento entloko nasentliziyweni
    yabantwana bazo abakula bezidenge, nabantu abamnyama abafundileyo
    lib'aqo— kanti lona ugxudululu oluzicingela, nolucingelwa ngabahlobo
    balo ukuba zizimanga emfuudweni luninzi.”

(f) Inye into enyanisekileyo, yeyokuba lengxakeko ayisokuze iconju-
    lulwe ngokuxasa izikolo ezipetwe nga “ Matye,” &c. “ Ezizinto
    azinjalo bubuxoki,” utsho u Wauchope &Co. Bonke ubunqina bamadoda
    afundileyo anjengo Dr. Dale, Mr. Donald Ross &c. bubuxoki atsho
    lamadoda Ngazwi nye okokuba lamadoda akenziwa budenge nakuba ingezwi
    litandekayo alutshaba lohlanga kuba wona anga lungati uhlanga lume
    pantsi kwawo ngokwazi kuba engawafumananga lamalungelo amonela
    uhlanga. E Mlungwini inkalazo ziyaxoxwa emapepeni endaba ngapandle
    kwentlonelo kabani, xa ute cwaka uyakolwa. Yambata ingubo leyo yako
    indala uzigqume ngayo kodwa lumka hleze ube uyambete yado yagqitelwa
    lixesha, kanti selineziroba, tina siya kupendula singayeki. Asihlazo
    ukufela uhlanga.

P. S. Into emnandi eyakuvuyisa bonke abangenguye u “ Wauchope,” “
Ngxam-yom-Wesile,” “ Lamatye.’” Kuba lengxelo ka Mr. Donald Ross iya
kwanekwa pambi kwe Parliamente ezayo kuba i Government izimisele ukwenza
inguqulo kungoko ite i Inspector General yakauleziswa pambi kokuhlangana
kwe Parliamente.

[Ntwana inesikwana esibi ekuxoxeni kwako, M.P., kukuti lowo
ungavumelaniyo naye ungabisamnika imfanelo zake. Lento yokuba u Mr.
Wauchope ungabi sambamba nangesipato, iseibangu Wauchope ngapandle ko
Mr., uyayiqonda ukuba abangakwaziyo ukuba ungumfo olincoko—njengokuba
sikwazi tina—boti uyalwa nalomadoda? U “Hadi ” woba luncedo kuwe
ngalendawo.—EDITOR Sigidimi]

U ENOCH BOOY NENKWENKWEZI.

NKOSI YAM—Ndipe isituba apo. Manditi upantse wayibona into eyenziwa
ndim, gcwizi mhlobo “ He ncence mlanjana, izigodlo zizele.” “ Ncence
mlanjana, izigodlo zizele.” He balela langa, umntan’ako ndimfake
elugageni. Ngubanilo? ngu Yeya : Ngubanilo? ngu Yeya ; Uzanobani ?
noyise. “ He ! cumbelele, cumbelele, pangalala.” “ He! Demazana,
demazana ndivulele ndingene kwelilitye lintunja mbini elivulwa
zinkonjane, zona zimapiko made.”

Mhlobo Mr. Mnga, ndingependuli ungabuzinto kum usenza enye yezo ndawo
zingentla, nezinje.—Wetu, wetu, Weyi! wafa, Yintoni- na? Ngunomauyama.
Upina ? ngase goqweni paya. Yo! Yintohi? yintoni? Tyu! kanti ngu
Hlakanyana lonomanyama.

Unyanisile mb'ali we Mpawana lomhlaba auswele magqudu ne ndu- ku. Upela
apo lo wako usicaka ngegama.

E. BOOY.

King Wms. Town, March 10, 1883.

“ INJ’ EBUYELE EMHLANZWENI.”

E-Hewu, Ox Kraal, February 28, 1883.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI—Kaundifakele lemigcana kwelo pepa lakowetu. Hayi
zihlobo zam, bazalwana nodade bomoya. Ndihlaba umkosi kuni bafundisi,
badala, beremente, zititshala, bakokeli nani makrestu enyaniso. Ukuqala
ndibona isixaki, nxa ndiyenza mfupi lento, nasi eso sixaki—kwiminyaka
emininzi edluleyo sivile sabona nangamehlo izenzo ezikulu ebesingazi
ukuba zinokwenzeka koluhlanga luntsundu esite sakuzibona siziva
ngendlebe sati mayibe inkulu into ebanga oku, sabamba imilomo, ewe,
sincoma kakulu xa sasibona abantsundu besendisa intombi zabo besiti
abafuni kulobolisa, bayapuma ekuloboleni nokulobolisa batsho bayenza
lonto sayibona sayiva nokuyiva, kwavakala kusitiwa ngabanye aba bantu
banokolo ngenyaniso, xa bade bancame inkomo ezingaka, bati abanye hai,
noko selulukolo andingeke ndondle ze ndinganeli nto, batsho balobolisa,
aba bati hi! onantsi basalobolisa kuhleliwenje? He! namhlanje ngezi mini
sebengabanye abangalobolisiyo, bashumayela ulobolo qa apo bakona
ngapezulu kwengqoboko engo Yesu Kristu u Msindisi wetu efele wavukela
tina. Nditi ke kanincede kulo mbuzo wam ngeyenu imibuzo namazwi, nditi
kanindipendule ndifuna ukuva ukuba nanikolisa banina? kwakutshiwo
ngubanina? yayingumoya kabanina owanityilela ukungalobolisi?
kwakulungile na oko? nditi ngoku seniyenza lento nanigxeka abayenzayo
nje, nikolisa bani? nisebenzela bani? kutshiwo ngubani? ngumoya. kabani
lo? kulungilena? Kanene u Yesu lo xa ati abokuqala boba ngabokugqibela,
abokugqibela babe ngabokuqala &c. &c. uteta ntonina bafundisi &c. &c.
Uxolo zinkosi ngalento itetwa ngu Petrosi yokuti inja ibuyela umhlanzo
wayo, utetani yena tyilelani mna sidenge badala beremente &c. &c. Au!
mandingapiki nenteto kuba inendawo ezininzi zokutetwa. Ndim esako
isicaka,

Z. D. KOZA SISHWATUBA.

[Impendulo yale mibuzo ka Koza siyilambele kakubi; kuba ekupendulweni
kwayo kuya kusuka iqina elinqabileyo elibambe into eninzi yododana ese “
Zinja zomoya” kwada kwayile mini.—EDITOR Sigidimi.

UKWANDA KWA MANZ’ABOMVU.

  BONKE abatwali, nabo bonke abalunge kulo mandla bayaziswa ngommiso we
  24 ne 25 wonyaka we 1883,

Bonke abatwali nabo bonke nabo bonke abalunge kulo mandla bayaziswa
ngommiso we 24 ne 25 wonyaka we 1883, ukuba akufuneki ukuba kubeko nto
iyinkomo, iya, nevela ku Mandla wase Monti, wase Cumakala, wase Qumra
nokuba ngowase Qonce, ngazwinye umda ngamanz’e Nesi ngelicala
lingasentla. Wotike ofunyenwe equba, nokuba ngowenqwelo, ngapandle
kwemvumelo yakomkulu, anikwe ityala eliyakuti ukuba liyintlaulo lingabi
ngapantsi kwa mashumi omahlanu eponti (£50) kuti kungenjalo afakwe e
ntangeni inyanga zibe ntatu esebenza nzima, mhlaumbi.

E Ofisini ye Mantyi

Alice, 5 March 1883.

  ALEX. STEWART, Imantji.

INGXELO ZOKUQUBEKA KOMSEBENZI.

  Eka Mr. PHILIP.—Uncome ukukoliseka kwako yinqubo yododana olupantsi
  kwemfundiso yako olukangele endaweni yokuba ngabafundisi. Uxele
  ukundileka nokutozama kwalo kuyo yonke inqubo yemihla ngemihla.

  Eka Mr. SMITH.—Ubonise kalusizi ngamazwi okuyala ulutsha into
  eliyakuba yiyo elilizwe ngenxa yempembelelo yalo yaxa selupumile
  emfundweni. Utsho wazincoma intlanga ezintsundu ngendawo ezifanele
  ukunconywa ngazo, ezingqondweni nezitelekelelo zobuntu, nesonti
  lobuzwe. Utsoliselo ekutini ulutsha lonke olusemfundweni malu-
  zicoselele kakuhle indawo oluzo kufundela zona, nento olukangele ukuya
  kuyenzela amakowalo asesebumnyameni. Ubonise ngobuciko obukulu indawo
  ezizinyaniso zokugci- nwa zenziwe.

  Eka Mr. MOIR.—Ungene kunene kwindawo ezityebisa ubuko nobumbuku
  belungelo, nokusweleka kwesenzakalo emntwini wonke, kwimfundiso
  eyenziweyo e Lovedale. Utsho wati—Bonke abangaba banazo izigxeko
  nezikalazo banalo ituba lokuba ke babonise kambe ezondawo baziketa

  amadlala. Ubonise kunene indawo eyona kujongwe kuyo ngulomzi wase
  Lovedale neminye yolohlobo, ukuba apo ifunzele kona, kukwimfundiso ye
  mipefumlo, kokwexa elisezayo. Londawo uyigocile ngobuciko obukulu
  ebonisa owona mongo womsebenzi wabo bafundisi kweli lizwe.

Eka Mr. CALDER.— Ungene kwindawo ngendawo ezinje ngokwalata inqubo yama
kwenkwe ahlal’e Lovedale Institution kuneza ngemini. Wabonisa inqubo
yentombi kunododana kwinto ngento zemfundo.

Amabaso empahla yase Lovedale Emibonisweni (Exhibi­tion.) Kwimiboniso
eke yako, owase Kapa (Cape Town) we 1884 nowase Bayi (Port Elizabeth) we
1886 i Lovedale ifumene amabaso okupumelela ngobugcisa ekwenzeni izinto
ngezinto. I Lovedale ide yaya kuvela nase London njalo-njalo'. Ifumene
amabaso atile, ekuko abalulekileyo, anje ngelo nkosikazi u Mrs. Stewart,
awayenze umfanekiso omhle kunene womzi wasema Xoseni, nezindlu, ne
ntlanti zawo nje ngoko, nabafazi abavela kuteza njalonjalo. Kwanelinye
lika Mr. Smith alifumene ngencwadi yemiciza yemiti yelilizwe, incwadi
eyakuba licam (boon) kubo bonke abaya kuyisebenzisa bayilandele kakuhle
ukucaza kwayo indlela-ndlela zokwenziwa kwalomayeza.

EKA MR. GEDDES.—Ungene ngokufutshane ekucazeni izinto ezipumileyo
ngokondla usapo, ezinje ngenani lenkomo negusha ezixeliweyo kulomnyaka
we 1886, kwa kukunye nento eninzi yezinye izinto ezipumileyo.

Emva koko ungene eluhlotyeni aqube ngalo umsebenzi kunye nebuto lake,
nokundileka koninzi, nakuba ingazanga ipele imitshakatshezi pakati
kwebuto.

EKA MR. CRAWSHAW.—Ungene yena ekuboniseni ukuba u Rulumente ubemele
ukuncedisa imfundiso zabantsundu ngemali yake endaweni yokuyihluta, kuba
nabo berafa. Ubonise ngobuciko obukulu, wade wangxuta malunga nalondawo
ye grant zika Rulumente.

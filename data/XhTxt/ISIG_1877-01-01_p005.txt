EZASE TRANSVAAL.

INDABA zase Transvaal zale nyanga ifileyo zixela ukuba kwati ngomhla 17
ka November kwapuma amadoda amakulu matatu aya kucita imizi yabanstundu
ebinemayile ezisibozo ukusuka e-Steel Poort. Bati bakufika bakupa

sawo. Kwakuba njalo u-Von Schlickman ufunze kunye nawo wonke umkosi
obupumile, kokokufunzayena nabanye ababini bangxweleriwe. Babonile ukuba
lomkosi wabo uza kutshatyalaliswa base betwala ingxwelera bebuya. U-von
Schlickman ubehle waqauka ingekapeli ne yure emva kokuba edutyulwe.
Bamncoma ukukalipa akubonakalisileyo kwada kwase kupeleni. Ute naxa
selepantsi waba esafunza. Noko yonke lonto iyangcola sakukangela
kwindawo yokuba ebengumbulali wabafazi oko ebesapilile. Pambi kokuba aze
kufa ubesongele ngokuba ngoku uyakubulala bonke abafazi nabantwana
anokubafumana ngokuba abafazi benceda amadoda abo ngokufuna ukudla ; ke
ukuba bayabulawa nabantwana siya kucitakala isizwe.

Umfo otile obala ese Transvaal uti: Elilizwe asi­kuko nokuba lise
kubandezelekeni. Imali iswelekile abemi balo banomoya wokuvukela izipata
mandla. Ndisand’ ukuvela eluhambeni apo ndifumene uninzi lwama Bulu
lufuna ukubopa luye kuwela i-Zambesi. Amanye angxamele ukuba i-Transvaal
ibe pantsi kwa Mangesi.

Ekupeleni kuka November kutiwa kuko iqela lama Bulu nabantsundu abapume
e-Steel Poort bahamba befuna abakwa Sikukuni. Baqubisene neqela elivela
kutimba kwa Magali liquba inkomo ezimashumi asibozo. Ate akuvela amabulu
zashiywa ezonkomo kwabalekelwa ezintabeni. Kuvakele ukuba kuko elinye
iqela elitimbe inkomo 480 negusha 150, asinge kwelo Amabulu, afika
ziqutywa ngamadoda akumawaka amatatu. Abangakwa Sikukuni bate bakulubona
utshaba badubula kwakanye babaleka. Koko kubaleka kwabo kubulewe amakulu

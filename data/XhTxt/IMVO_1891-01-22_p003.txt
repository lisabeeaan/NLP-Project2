NGEZIMINI imicimbi yom’buso ihanjiswa sikangele nge Komityi
(Commis­sion). U Rulumeni waso Natal nmise i Komityi yokupicota isimo
semfundo namacebo okuyilungiselela kwelo. Asilobhunga lokuqala eli
elimiswayo kwelo ukukangela lomcimbi kodwa akubonakali ziqamo
ziveliswayo ngalamalinga. Nakweli lase Kapa ikwa yilonto—Ikomityi azidli
ngakulandelwa zizenzo eziti zilano nokuquba kwazo. Ikomityi yemfundo
eyayingati izakumiswa kweli letu ayikamiswa. Awetu amava ngezi Komityi
abangela ukuba singalindeli nto kuzo.

U FAKU (COL. GRIFFITH, M.L.A.) ubeke wanentlanganiso nabanyuli base
Mtata. Inteto yake ibiyengezinto ezenziweyo yi Palamente ezipatelelo kwi
sitili asimeleyo ngeminyaka emibini egqitileyo; kwanento ayenzileyo
malungana nezonto. Ke azininzi into ezibalulekileyo ezenzelwa eli
pesheya kwe Nciba ngu Rulumeni ngapandle kokwenza amadinga ngemicancato
nangezinye izinto. Yaye nonto ezenziwa ngu Faku e Palamente zingabalele
emzini kuba engumfo ongaxoxiyo ngokuvela. Asiyiyo etyulu ke into exelwe
ngu Colonel Griffith kwintlanganiso yase Mtata.

Ukuba IMVO inentshaba ikwanabahlobo. Zininzi incwadi ezomelezayo
nezikhutazayo esizifumene kubahlobo balomsebenzi abakwindawo ngendawo.

Mvo esisiyalo, besiya kungeniswa, ize into leyo ingakangeleki kakuhle
eziramenteni. Asikabi natyala ko apo. Beka incha mlesi.

Uti ke, esiti besiya kusifaka isiyalo, zo kungabi kuhle mayelana nal.o
ma Tshatshi, kuba “ engazi ukuba amanye amahlelo ati iziyalo nezikalazo

”—uti esenjenjalo ukuteta abuye kwayena, engekaligqibi elo, ateto

U REV. W. PHILIP NE “ MVO.”

MNUMZANA,—Inteto yako engabafundisi base Tshatshi abantsundu,
entlanganisweni yabo e Rini, ayifanele kuyekwa ihambe yodwa
ingenzelwanga zwi ngomnye owayekona ontsundu. Umhlobo wako owayekwenzela
indaba, ute gqwidi ukukubalisela, kuba into ekwakutetwa ngayo, ukuba
ingeniswe EMVENI, asingxelo (report), yincwadi (pastoral letter) epuma
egameni lo Mongameli, esingiswa kwi Bandla lase Tshatshi, kule Diocese.
Ke kubonakele ukuba ipume ngamapepa (circulars), ngati kodwa yeyona
ndlela kwinto enjengaleyo iyeleleyo, andazi ukuba amanye amahlelo ati
iziyalo ne zikalazo anazo ngakwi ramente zawo azipalazele ilizwo lonke,
nakwabangateni nazo. Indawo engokukalazela IMVO ivele mayela nesituba
sezinto ezisakuba zitunyelwa kwelopepa, zeli Bandla; kodwa asibantsundu
bodwa abanezikalazo, nakuba kuugeko namnye ontsundu obo nokuyitetelela,
into ebekoyo kukuvela kwezikalazo kumacala ngamacala kulo mabandla
mayela nezinto ezisakuba zipuma kuwo zisingiswa EMVENI ukungananzwa.
Ukuba kuyatandeka amabala angavoliswa okucacisa into etetwayo. Ingxelo
zezinto ezenzeka “ kwizibhaxana zemilanjana engabhaliweyo nakwi jografl
” azincinane kubantu abakunanzileyo ukupumelela kwalomsebenzi.

Bendingacingi kubhala; ndandizilungiselele ukuhlangana no Mhleli,
ndimbalisele ngomlomo inteto eyabakoyo, ngepepa lake entlanganisweui,
kuba ndacelwa mna yintlanganiso leyo yakowetu, ukuba ndibo ngumlomo
wayo, ukuba ndivakalise into ekoyo. Kwaye kungeko “ kugaqelana ngasese,”
ngabula Myo kwi “Mpawana,” December 31— nokuya ndisati elozwi lifumane
langeniswa lingaqondwanga mfanelo yalo kwesi situba.

Ngati kum kunokuxoxwa ngento yase bandla ngapandle kokucetywa, nakubuka
buso bukabani. Ubuhlobo betu no Mvo bebungasayikubonakalaliswa ngokusuke
sivale imilomo namaxenikweni indlela etenxileyo kwinqubo yayo ete
yazivelisa. Indaba yesi profeto sika Ntsikana ndingabuye ndiyingene
ngenye imini, kuba kusekuhleni ukuba ayiqondwa ukuba yayisingiselwe
entweninina, kobanina kona.—Owako, &c.,

WM. PHILIP.

Z1NTSHABA ZANINA KWI “MVO?”

NKOSI MHLELI, — Ndivumele ndenze abembalwa pantsi kwale ntloko.
Ndicukunyiswe ngamanqakwana endiwabone kumapepa amabini e Mvo,
elokugqibela lexego umfi u 1890, nelokuqala lalonyaka mtsha; amanqaku
ateta ngentshaba ze Mvo. Lento intshaba zabako kwanini nasebulungiseni,
ekungemmangaliso ke ukuba zibeko nakwi Mvo kwabantsundu aba itetelela
bona kuba akuko nto ingena Intshaba, ndingatsho ukuti IMVO le ilunge
kwapela—kodwa ndingatsho mna mntu imngene yada yakufikelela nase’
mongweni. Zintshaba apa ekungeko nto yimbi zipehlwa ngu mona ne kwele,
izinto ke ezo ezendeleyo kulomzi untsundu wakowetu ongasakupumelela
msinya ngazo — ukuba awusoze—ungazilahlanga kwanamhlanje; kuba uti
unantsi akulinga ukwenza lento bam’monele abaya bangayise so lonto,
umhlaumbi iboyisileyo bakubona lowo epumelela kulonto, okunye babe
nekwele ne nzondo ngapaya koko. Zintshaba ezinga i Mvo inga tshona, kuba
abalandeli bayo beyixhumisa kangangokuba bade bati u Mhleli ungu “
Moses,” into ke leyo ehlabe oka Pelem wa Pelema wabona, emoyeni wake
endiwaziyo, kulungilo kuye ukuba ayivise nabelungu lonto, ngokuti oka
Pelem ayibhale nakwi Queenstown Free Press ukuba yivani belungu nanku
oka Jabavu bemenza u Moses. Besendite ezintshaba azina monakicele
kupela, zinentiyo ye siqu ngapaya kuba i Mvo le ayichaswe ngamacebo ayo
nangokwasenibusweni (politically) kodwa, ngati sekuchaswe no Mhleli
ngesiqu (personally) kuba beuga bonke banga ngo Moses—into engunotshe
leyo. Kanti ke lento ilipepa ingumkokeli wabantu, kuba kwa inteto yo
Mhleli kuliwa ukubizwa kwayo yi “leader” or “ leading article,” amacebo
ke alindelwe epepeni kuba lilona lihlalele ukupicota nokupengulula
izinto zonke licebise, laye lizamkela izimvo ezityilwa nangubanina
eziluncedo ebantwini; ezintshaba ke zizania ukutshabhisa lamacebo e Mvo
zizama nokuyiqetula pantsi kwelanga, kuba zisiti iyalahlekisa, zafuna
nokumisa intlanganiso ezingaxeli nto ezingapumelelanga kuba singa
singango Moses sonke. Ewe, nakwabamhlope iko into yokuchasana nzima
kwamadoda. Ingeyiyo lento yetu tina. Kwezase m’busweni e Palame­nte
njengamaqela o Sauer no Sprigg abechasana kude kuye kwashushu kanti boti
bakupuma apo ipele lonto kubuliswane ngezandla. Into engeze ibeko leyo
ukuba ibingabantsundu. Ubungahlal’ usiva kutiwa amadoda e Palamente ate
akungevani engxoxweni yasisipendu ne nduma kwakubi njalo; lonto iboniswa
kwazintlanganiso esisakubanazo, ati amadoda akungeyani engxoxweni
kubonakale ukuba selenamarixi okunga angayigqiba ngeziqula lonto.
Anditsho ukuti amacebo e Mvo alunge konke na makangachoswa ngabo
banendawo abazibonayo bona, kodwa ndala lendawo kubonakala ukuba
sekuchaswe isiqu so Mhleli ngo­

kwake, kuba asingebi zi Politicians kakuhle ngalondlela, okunye apela
amaxesha okuti amapike ezinto agqitywe ngentiywano nentonga kuba
sisekukanyeni kaloku ngoku. Zikulomkondo ke ezintshaba zale ntlantsana
yetu iyi Mvo esifanele ukuyikusela ngako konke esinako ingacimi.
Kuyazeka ukuba abantu aba abazange babe moya mnye kakade, kanti ke tina
zimpatalala zintsundu, tina zinto zicudiswayo zinyatelelwa pantsi
ngomhlope, besifanele ukumanyana ngokuqinileyo, iti intlantsi enjenge
Mvo le siyixhase siwe sivuka nayo endaweni yokuba ibe kwasiti intshaba
zayo.—Au! Ingaba amazwi o Mpololiti u Ntsikana siyawalahla ngenene na?
okuba “ Ukuze nibe yimbumba yamanyama,—lo mona nala makwele nale ntiyano
yeyona mbumba yamanyama na? Napakade, — Qumpase, dade bo Nchwangu! Nezo
ntshaba zifuna ukubhukuqa lo Mvo singazi ukuba zifuna ukumisa lipina
ipepa azisakunceda Into noko, kuba azimanyane nazo ; ngokoko ka Jabavu
livutele ixilongo elo ihambe yona inqwelo ye ZIMVO ingamiswa, basale
bedumbe intliziyo ngamakwele no mona abo banobutshaba kuyo, bode bange
bagxwala emswaneni emzuzwini—yhek ! mayihambo ke!

Uxolo Mr. Mhleli ngokuntyuntya ndiyeka ndingekaneli ngokoyikisela ipepa
lake, kuba lendawo ndiyitabate ngomoya oshushu kakulu.—Ndingowako
otobileyo.

IMPENDULO NGOLWALUKO.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI, — Camagu Mr. Johannes S. Skosana (Oatlands), andazi
nokuba ndingena ngegquduna emdudweni ekupenduleni umbuzo wako oshushu
ngamantombazana asezikolweni atabata amadoda angalukanga azabuye
awatuko. Andazi nokuba wonelana yimpendulo yam kuba sowute lombuzo wako
uwubuza ngobushushu. Elisiko linqabile eyona ndlela ingabangela ukuba
lipele— lokwaluka kwaneli lokungaluki, linqabile eyona ndlela
engenziwayo lokuba kungalukwa ngokumayelana nomteto. Nditsho kuba
Isibhalo sicacisa mhlope ukuti: ukwaluka nokungaluki akunto, into
efunekayo kulamacala emabini nje inye qwaba—lulwaluko Iwentliziyo. Ke
sifumana kungeko sigxeko kumacala omabini. Ke masikangelo apo isigxeko
sivela kona kulomntu walukileyo nongalukanga ekubeni kutiwa akuko sizatu
kwezondawo bahlukene ngazo. Ndifumana isigxeko sivela kowalukileyo
esibhekisa kongalukanga, ndifumane ke ngoko umaluki esapula okanye edela
inteto ye Sibhalo njengoko siti: akuko siposo kwezindawo zombini. Ke uti
umaluki kongalukanga—yaluka. Ke uti ke wena Mr. Skosana—Baya kutinina
ukungaluki xana bahleli kakubi ezindlwini zabo. Nditi ezintombi sukuba
zingengawo ama Krestu, nakuba zibizwa ngelo gama zisekude le nobu Krestu
; kuba zikwazi ukwapula imiteto yobu Krestu, kwano bumnyama busatyikitya
kuzo. Nditi amadodana angalukanga xana afuna abafazi makagcine indawo
ezilicasileyo elisiko lokwaluka, kuba ndikolwa ukuba abazali kwezondawo
abantwana babo bakwimpembelelo zokululekwa ekucaseni ukwaluka. Mhlaimbi
uyakuti yinto yemvela ukwaluka. Ngati mna into eyiyo kukungaluki kuba
umntu uvela engalukanga; yinto yabumini ukwaluka. Ndinqande ukuba
ndiyaposisa Mnumzetu—nditi intombi ezibazali abangalenzanga elisiko
lokwaluka zibafanele abafana abangalukanga. Ndim,

SIBONDA.

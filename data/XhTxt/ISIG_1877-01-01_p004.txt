UKUVULWA KWETYALIKE E-HLOBO.

U-MAZAMISA yenye yamadoda apambili pakati kwe- nkosana zama Mfengu.
Uzikalipele kakulu zonke izinto ezisingisele ekukanyiseleni nase
kufundiseni abantu bake. Kuyo yonke imisebenzi yake unokufana no
Feltman. Ubekiwe ngabo bonke kweli lase Mamfengwini. Uyatandwa kakulu
nangabamhlope abamaziyo ngenxa yokuqonda kwake nokubantle kwentliziyo
yake notando lwake lokutanda abantu. Ute akuba ezakele indlu yezitena
efulelwe ngezink, —into leyo engu mzekelo omhle kubantu bake— waza
wafuna ukwenza indawo efanelekileyo yokubedeshela. Londlu nayo ngoku
iseligqityiwe. Yakiwe ngamatye yafulelwa ngengca. Ivulwe ligqityiwe
ityala layo. Imini yokuvulwa kwayo iyakuhlala ikunjulwa ngabo bonke
ababeko. Kuya kuda kube kudala kubaliswa ngezinto ezinikiweyo nobuninzi
bemali eroliweyo ; bonke bebonisa ukuyibeka kwabo inkosi yabo
nokuyitanda kwabo indlu eyakileyo. U- Ayliff, Imantyi yabo ebekekileyo,
ubekona entlanga- nisweni, sapatwa nguye isihlalo. Imali ehlanganisi-
weyo sikolwa ukuba ifike ewakeni. Bonke banike ngemihlali, bade batintwa
besanikela, baxelelwa ukuba sebeyi fezile imali ebifuneka. Lendlu
yetyalike ebivulwa ilelinye lamapandle ase Gcuwa, isikolo sama Wesile
esipantsi kuka Rev. Mr. Barrett, umfundisi okukutala kukulu.—Kaffrarian
Watchman.

UKULUNGISELELWA KWEMFAZWE
E-ENGLAND.

E-ENGLAND kutiwa kuya lungiselelwa kakulu ukuze iti nokuba iyenzeka into
eyenzekayo ifike kulungiwe. Kuko imikosi etile yamasoldati enyawo
nawamahashe enikwe umteto wokuba mayihlale isililungele ukusuka iye
e-Turkey. Nakwimikombe yokulwa kuko eza kuya kongezelela inani
eselikulwandle lwe Me­diterranean. Elinye lamapepa likankanya ukuba
izipatamandla zisebenza emini nase busuku. Kwenziwa into eninzi yedopisi
kulungiswa nekanunu. Ipepa abati yi Globe, liti kuko iqela lama soldati
esekutiwe malinduluke. Nokoke kulungiselelwa kangako akukabiko nqiniseke
yemfazwe, kubonakala kodwa ukuba i-Russia iyingxamele.

1.  Hai igama lika Yesu, Limnandi pakati kwam ; Lili temba labo bonke
      Beza ku Msindisi warn.

  Chorus— Limnandi igama, Igama lika Yesu.

  Limnandi igama,

  Igama lika Yesu.

2.  Hai igama lika Yesu, Li sisonwabiso sam: Li luvuyo lwentliziyo
      Ezihlupekweni zam.

  Nxa intshaba zompefumlo Zivuka pambi kwam.

4.  Hai igama lika Yesu, Liya ndivuyisa nam. Ndoli shumayela futi Ngayo
      yonk’ imihla yam.

5.  Hai igama lika Yesu, Loba nhl’ ekufeni kwam Ndolivuma ezulwini,
      Pambi ko Msindisi.

EMGWALI.

December, 1, 1876.

Amazwi ka John Dukwana awatetileyo pambi ko- kufa kwake, ebekisa
kumkuluwa wake abemtanda kakulu. Ebekisa kuye, wati. Nkosi Tixo kaloku
ndifuna ukupumla bendifuna ukutandaza ngokwam. Kodwa nina anikuqondi
ngokuba ndikohliwe ukuteta, akuba etshilo wati, kumkuluwa wake, tandaza
uti ukutandaza nkosi yiba nami namhla.

Kute emveni kokuba egqibile umtandazo wati kumkuluwa wake ndifuna
ukupendula namhla umbuzo wako obuhlala uwubuza kum. Umbuzo womkuluwe
wako ubusiti kuye, unjenje ucingantonina ngom mpefumlo wako, wati,
ukupendula, umpefumlo wam unetemba Enkosini. Ndikubizela ukuxelela lonto
wati akuba ebetetile lamazwi wagqiba ngawo akabuyanga atete.

Ute umkuluwe wake akugqiba u-John Dukwana wati lonto undixelele yona,
iluvuyo, olukulu, izihlobo zetu aziko, zikwelozwe, nabazali betu,
nabaninawe betu nodade betu, nabantwana betu, nati, siya kwakona, wapela
apo.

Ke ebekewateta ngezolo nomfazi wake. Bencokola bobabini, wati umfazi
wake, utinina ngomsindisi wako? wapakamisa izandla zake zozibini wati,
ndimbambile ngezandla zozibini, wati umfazi ungabi unosizi ngati, wati
hayi, usizi lwapela kum ndandisiti ndinga ningacula, wati umfazi
ndiyavuya wati naye uyavuya, wa­pela apo. Abafundi balencwadi mabaqonde
akuba ebenepika elikulu kakulu, ubekohliwe, ukuteta lipika. Kunjalo,
ukububa kuka MR. John Dukwana.

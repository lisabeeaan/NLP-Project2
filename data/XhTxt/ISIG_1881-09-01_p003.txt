ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1881.

ENYE kwi fama e Karu ipilise inciniba zada zambini zimiwa ngama tambo.
Enye iliginye ise ncinane, yamiwa lilo emqaleni, wayi hlinza, walikupa,
wabuya wayitunga; yendala enye, wenza kwa lento, zapila noko intaka.

UMNTU xa aya kukwela ku Loliwe maka lumkele ukuba abe nayo imali
encinane ye Tikiti. Aku lindelekile ukuba aba tengise be Tikiti bahlale
bene mali encinane yoku tshintshela bonke abantu abafuna i Tikiti. Bati
ke nokuba banayo i itshintshi bangavumi nayo ngokunga abantu abangeqele
ukuza ngapandle kwe mali encinane.

ISANDLA se Ruluneli elu Sutu:—Kuke kwa celwa u Col. Bowker obeyinkosi
yono ngqayi ukuba azalise lendawo, walile kodwa; kwacelwa ke u Mr.
Joseph M. Orpen, obemele i Aliwal North e Parlamente, wavuma. Sisati
tina ngu Mr. Emile Rolland obeya kuyilungela lendawo. Soka sikangele ke
ukuhamba kwezinto pantsi ko Mr. Orpen, pambi kokuba siti uyifanele
lendawo akayifanele kusinina.

INDODA endala eyatsha ibilele ngase ziko inxilile e Uitenhage kwi nyanga
ezi dlulileyo ukufa kwayo kubange ubu lumko kunyana wayo omkulu obesela
kakulu utywala. Ute akumbona uyise egquma pantsi kwe ntlungu zokutsha
awayetshe ngako, wada wambona ebuba zezo ntlungu wa gqiba ekubeni
akasayi kuze abuye ayipate i glasi. Namhlanje lomambane siva ukuba ngum
Tempile, kanjako ngumfuni. Singa nomninawe wayo angam landela.

NGOHLOBO olungafanelekileyo wahlutwa kuma Mpondo u Mzimvubu ngu Sir B.
Frere wawenza umhlaba ka Rulumente. Akatazekile yilonto ama Mpondo,
akayeka ukumazisa u Rulumente, ote kodwa yena wayinika indlebe ebutulu
imitandazo yawo. Apo kungena kona inqanawa kuma Ngesi apa, kudla
ngokubizwa imali ekutiwa zi Customs ngayo yonke impahla efikayo. Ngale
nyanga u August ama Ngesi atile akona alile ukuyibatala le mali, kwati
kwakutiwa mayibatale, asuka ahlanganisa abantsundu, abaxobisa, azipanga
impahla kuba hlanganisi bemali. I bonile i Rulumente ukuba ayinakubenza
nto aba bantu ngomteto we Koloni yaselisiti, lo mali mayingabi
sahlanganiswa.

U B. MYEDI usicela ukuba sazise izihlobo zake ngokukodwa udade wabo u
Naomi Xaba—uko nokwa Gqumahashe kambe u Naomi—ukuba bada bakululeka
ekuvalelweni ngabe Sutu. Use Herschel ngoku apo alungiselela kona usapo,
nokuba wenziwa inkubele imilenze yomibini wasinda noko ngokuba u Tixo
engakufuni ukufa komoni. Ngo Daniel Msimang, no Jonathana Xaba ati
bangamazi udade wabo lowo. Nama Kumalo o Samuel no Lukas baya celwa
ukuba bazihambise indaba ezo. Ubika nempilo yonina u Mariya. Elinye
igama lika Myedi lo ngu Jacob ongu Suhlawe. Azisakuba naluncedo luni
ezindaba kwisi ninzi sabafundi belipepa kodwa zanele ukubonisa ububi
obuno kwenziwa yimfazwe. Enye into siyifaka lenteto kuba sivelana
nabantu abebe pitizeliswe yimfazwe benga bangonwabisa intliziyo zaba
hlobo abaninzi. Kodwa singake simcele u Mr. Myedi ukuba akangele kupuwu
esilwenze kwisicelo sika Mr. Ntsiko.

ENYE ku madodana afundileyo antsundu ibale incwadi yayifaka epepeni lase
Grahamstown, itetelela uku kululwa ko Edmund Sandile nezinye inkosana
ezi mnyama ezi sebu banjweni e Cape Town. Iti uku xoxa kwayo inkabi
yendodana: u Edmund ufa ngenxa yemfazwe ka Rili um Gcaleka, kanti akuko
nkosi yakwa Gcaleka ibanjiweyo; u Rili uxolelwe, u Sigcau uxolelwe. Xa
kutiwa wa vukela u Rulumente u Edmund nabanye, makahlale ke ngoko ebu
konxweni, uti lomfo u Lerothodi no Masupha no Joel bavukile kanti bona
banikwe nomhlaba wabo, enga sateti nto ngoku banjwa. Ufike lombaleli we
Journal ati kuko umtandazo ozakutunyelwa lu dodana olufundileyo kwi
Ruluneli, wokubeka pambi ko Mhlekazi ezindawo. Lento siyayitakazela;
amandla etu oku tandaza kwi Ruluneli asikazange siwa sebenzise tina
midaka yase Koloni. Into ebesisuke siyenze ibiye posisekileyo kanye,
ebisuke ilucitacite uhlanga lwetu—siteta eyokuvukelana no Rulumente.
Indlela yomtandazo yeyona iyiyo apa kwa Rulumente, asita. ndabuzi
wopendula kamnandi u Sir Hercules Robinson Lomtandazo kufuneka uhanjiswe
kakulu ukuze ube namagama ama ninzi. Wanga lo mtandazo unga ngowabo
bonke aba mnyama ngapandle kokuketwa zintlanga. Ukukululwa kwezi nkosi
ku ngebe nayo nencinane ingozi kokwetu ukubona.

KUKO incwadi ebe mnandi kuti evela ku mbali oti uyi Ntengu ene nkomo.
Asitandabuzi bonke aboyi tabata inkatazo yokuyifunda—singaba cebisa
ukuba bayifunde bonke—bozi pakamisa intloko beve sebesiti, njengoko
senjenjalo, “Amen,” baku gqiba ukuyifunda. I mnandi inteto yayo iteta
izibakala kanjako. Ayifuni mazwi etu; izitetela ngokwayo. Yofunyanwa
kwindawo esiyipe aba Balelani. Enye indawo esikolwa ukuba boyifumana
imnandi aba lesi betu “Nyumbuzo nemiteto elishumi ye Nxila.”

NGO June Isigidimi sabalisa uku baptizwa kuka Kagaso Sazuze omnye
weziqamo zokuqala zabafundisi abaya kushumayela i Vangeli pakati kwe
Africa. Nge 14 ku August u Rev. Dr. Stewart ubaptize abafana bakwa kona
ababini u Kolimbo Nyaleza no Evangeli Sezamantya. Ukubonela olu baptizo
kwavusa ingcingane ezininzi ngabantu abase sebunyameni, bekaya laba
bafana, zongezelelwa yinto yokuba seliyi Cawa yokugqibela beyingena apa
e Lovedale abafana abo. Banduluka ngo 18, besimka nemitandazo yabo bonke
aba maziyo nabamkatalelayo u Tixo kwezi ndawo.

IPEPA lase Grahamstown i Journal lizame ukuguqulela esi Ngesini enye
yezi “Mpawana” lasuka lalahleka. Kwaba ngayaziyo lonto kuba fundi betu
singati sisiqingata esasite kuso ingxoxo ye mipu selindala ngohlobo
lokuba ipele nencasa, nesasitetisa kuso umfana ka Bulube, esi
siguquleIwe kokwama Ngesi. Elipepa lifike liti sivusa ingxoxo yemipu
ngokutsha, kwelixa sasiyibulala tina. Site sakufunda ubudenge be Journal
asabinaku nceda ukuba singahleki. Singavuya lite elipepa ngomso xa
liguqula inteto ye Sigidimi lingayandisi ngapezu kokuba injalo.

LENYANGA ipelayo u August ibe nezigigaba ezitile. Kubeko into
esingazanga sayivanange mbali yokufumana abantu abafanelekileyo bazimele
ibe kungeko nto bayenzileyo yaziwa mntu. U Capt. D’Arcy wabesendlwini ka
Rev. C. Taberer e Mtwaku ehambele, kute ngaxa litile wabonakala elwasu
ehamba, kude kwalixesha engabuyi, bada bazixonxa indlebe endlwini.—Umntu
uye pi? Umntu uye pi? Kodwa mntu waya. Ipumile imikosi ukuya kufuna,
kwada kwapuma no nompilinga. Xoka; lube lunyalo, kunanamhla. Intombi
yomlungu e Bayi yenze kwa lonto. Noko, makubulelwe u Qamata, ibuye
yafunyanwa. Enye intwana kukuzimela kwamakwenkwe amabini esikolo sase
Heald Town, engenzanga nto pofu, ekucingelwa ukuba agodukile. Lento
baninzi abaya kuti lupau lweli xesha. Sisakamise imilomo tina kuku
mangaliswa.

NGOMHLA we 9 ku August kutshatiswe u Rev. Ebenezer Magaba, wase Heald
Town, ku Miss Duma, wase Fort Beaufort, kwa Slinger, umfundisi owaba
bopelelayo aba bantu babini ngu Rev. Geo. Chapman, wakwase Heald Town,
encediswa ngu Rev. John Wilson, wase Fort Beaufort. Babe baninzi
abahlobo be soka ne ntombi ababeko beye kuteta amatamsanqa pezu kwesi
bini eso singena kwinxwaleko zobomi babatshatileyo, sishiya ubunandi
bobu soka nobu ntombi. Isibonakalo sezinto zalomhla womtshato kwaba beko
asika libaleki nanamhla. U Mr. Magaba yindodana encinane efike
yazingenisa emsebenzini wobufundisi kwi ramente yama Wesile. Singa
angahlala ngokonwaba, nangoxolo ebomini bake obutsha nomtshakazi wake,
awufeze ngokufanelekileyo umsebenzi awutatele pezu kwake.

KADE elila amadodana akowetu ngokuti imfundo epakamileyo
akanakuyifumana; izikula ezikulu ezinje ngozi South African College e
Cape Town, nozi Stellenbosch College nozi Gill College ema Somerset East
akana kuzingena. Esi sikalo okunene besiqondeka, kuba bambalwa kwi
midaka abanako ukubatalela abantwana babo ezikolweni imali ingange £100,
£120, etc. ngonyaka, kunye nengubo nokudla. Kobu bunyama boku kohlwa,
kuko namhla intlantsana ebangwe linene elisand’ ukububa e England, u Mr.
Croll. Ib’ isityebi indoda le, yati ke ekufeni kwayo inxenye yemali yayo
yayabela izikolo ezikulu ezitile apa e Koloni. Ezi zikolo yi South
African College; Grey In­stitute, Port Elizabeth; Gill College, Somerset
East. Ngati yi £8000 imali ayinikele isikolo ngasinye kwezi
sizibalileyo. Ute ke yeyokuba imane ibatalela abafundi abatile kwezi
zikolo abafuneka bepumelele uviwo pambi kokuba bafumane ilungelo
lokufunda kwi zikolo ezo ngalo mali. Ucacise mhlope ukuba akusayi
kuketwa bala, ngqoboko, nantoni, no mnyama ke ngoko unoku ngena. Amakaba
anentloko akowetu anetuba namhla, kunjanina akuke azidele angene.

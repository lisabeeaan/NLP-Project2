Kubeko kanjalo neqela elimnandi lababoneli.

Ite incwadi yemicimbi ngokungatunyelwa kwayo ngexesha, kwafundwa inqubo
yentlanganiso yaso Peelton njo ngokubaIwa e Sigidimini, kukunjuzwa
amalungu nje kodwa okwenziwayo kuleyo ingokuko ukuba komiswa ngo ngxolo
yopepa elo ukuba imicimbi leyo ilungelelene njo ngokubalwa kwe Minutes
ze Ntlanganiso.

Kubizwe amagama amalungu—apendula akoyo. Kulandwo izizatu zangekoyo.
Abazitumeleyo babo ngo Messrs. J. Tongo- Jabavu, oto uxakwo ngu msebenzi
wopopa lake. Intlanganiso ibe bungqungungqungu ukusamkela ; Benjamin
Sakuba, umbali obike ukugula komninawo wake, samkelwa; no John S.
Dlakiya umncedisi-mbali oxakwe kukuba kudo e Tinara, nokunqatyelwa
zindlela ezimsinya zokuza kufikelela, samkelwa.

Kukalazelwe indawo yokungafikeleli koninzi Iwamalungu, angatumeli
nezizatu, ekufumaneke ukuba inxenye iselinemi- nyaka ngamibini, namitatu
namine ingasafikeleli. Iyalezwe lendawo e Komitini ukuze ike iyipendle
ize nengxelo kwi ntlanganiso ezayo.

Kungeniswe amagama amalungu amatsha (1) ngu Mr. Attwell Maci, osekelwe
ngu Mr. Solomon Govo, ukuba kwamkelwe u Mr. Kenneth Ncapayi wase Mgwali;
(2) ngu Mr. A. Njokweni, osekelwe ngu Mr. Bolani, ukuba kwamkelwe u Mr.
Henry Ngcayiya; (3) ngu Mr. D. Mbaza, osekelwe ngu Mr. Mafongqo ukuba
kwamkelwe u Mr. Stephen Mtoba wase Rode; (4) ngu Rev. W. W. Gqoba
osekelwe ngu Rev. Boyce Mama ukuba kwankelwe u Mr. John Adams wase
Lovedale.

Omane lamagama amkelwe ebulungwini bo Manyano.

Kute kuba ixa lapambi kwe dinala lalise lipelile kwanikwa isaziso sokuba
eziya kulandela intlanganiso ziya kungena e New Building, ebolekwe u
Manyano ngaba Fundisi base Love­dale. Yapuma impi yaya kutata
izixaso—abafundisi bemenywe ngu Mrs. Mzimba; ikaba liye e Seminareni kwa
Mrs. Geddes, ama ledi eye e Girls’ School kwa Mrs. Muirhead; ababoneli
abangamakaya beye emzini wesikolo sase Dikeni apo -ebelungiselelwe kona.

Ukumka komhla

Kungenwe kanjalo emva kwe ntsimbi yesibini emaxesheni, aze amalungu
abika imicimbi ye ngxoxo aza ezipete.

Kwesi situba u Mr. Bokwe ubike ukuba ufumene incwadi evela ku Mbali wo
Manyano u Mr. B. Sakuba, eze nesigitshimi izisa incwadi ye Micimbi,
nexela isizatu sokungafiki kwake esongeza nendawo ecela ukuba
intlanganiso mayimpe ituba; lokuke apume ekubeni ngu Mbali wayo, ngenxa
yokuswela ixesha ngoku lokuzifeza imfanelo zobo bugosa.

Intlanganiso ivakalise usizi Iwayo ukuyiva lendawo yesi sicelo, yatuma u
Rev. Boyce Mama ukuba aye kuteta no Mbali lowo ukuke asiroxise isicelo
eso okwangoku, kuba kungaxaka ukwahlukana naye okweli xesha.

Kufundwe imicimbi yentlanganiso yase Peelton njengoko ibalwe encwadini
ye Minute, kwahlaziywa indawo eposiweyo, pambi kokuba yamkelwe,
yokuzipendulela kuka Mr. Shadrach B. Mama mayelana noku valwa kwe
nkanti, away’esiti kambe yena uyazihlamba kwibala lokuba kwakuvakala
ukuba “ ngo- mnye waba tumela isicelo e Palamente zokuba zivulwe.”

Zakuba zilungisiwe ezinye indawana ezimbalwa, yamkelwa imicimbi ukuba
ilungile,

Kwangenwa ke kwindawo ezaziyeketisiwe zashiyelwa ikomiti nge komiti
kwintlanganiso egqitileyo:

1.  Abatunywa be Native Educational Association Pesheya kwe
    Nciba.—O-Revs. P. J. Mzimba, no E. Makiwane ababe siya e Transkei,
    no Rev. W. B. Rubusana, no Mr. W. K. Ntsikana ababe siya kwelama
    Ngqika. Kuvakala ukuba ezi zitunywa xanqandwa zingekayi, ngohlobo
    olugxotayo noluswele imbeko. U Manyano nge Mfundo lumangaliswe kanye
    yilo mpato, ekubeni Iona Iwabapata kakuhle kangaka abatunywa bezo
    ntlanganiso e Peelton. Kube kwavakala pakati kwe ngxoxo ukuba kuko
    umoya oti e Transkei akuko bani way’enikwe gunya zintlanganiso
    zakona ukuba abagxote abatunywa abo.

Kunyulwe ikomiti engo Revs. B. Mama, W. W. Gqoba, no Messrs. A.
Njokweni, no J. K. Bokwe ukuke ipendle into enge- nziwayo malunga nalo
mcimbi. Ibuye nengxelo eti—Ingqondo isekubeni singasibuyiseli ngabubi
isenzo sokusa sapesheya kwe

Nciba—masingabi nanto siyenzayo—koko sikangele okuya kupinda kwenzeke.

2.  Incwadi zesi Xosa.—U Mr. Peter Tyamzashe uvakalise ukuba akukabiko
    ’nto yenziwayo, kuba ayizange yadibana i- komiti ngalo mcimbi. Ite
    nakuba ikalaza intlanganiso, yawuyaleza kwakona kwakulo Komiti ko
    Mr. P. Tyamzashe lomcimbi ukuba iwunyamekelele okwentlanganiso
    ezayo.

3.  Utywala.—Intlanganiso yaso Peelton yavumelana ukuba kubalelwe kuba
    Ifundisi nezinye indawo ukuba kwenziwe iqinga lokutshitshisa usolo.
    U Mr, J. K. Bokwe uvakalise ukuba amalinga anjalo enziwa ngaba
    fundisi ngokwabo, abalindelanga ncwadi ibakumbuzayo kubo nabo
    banamalinga abawenzayo ukuncipisa utywala emzini ontsundu.

4.   Ukuspelwa kwesi Xosa.—Ikomiti eyamiselwa ukuba ikangele
    ukupelidishwa kwamagama, yapendulelwa ngu Mr. J. K. Bokwe ngokuti—“
    Londawo yake yatetwa kwase Peelton. Kwa fumaneka ukuba Imvo
    Zabantsundu ayikabi nalo iqonqa eli le 'Aspirate,’ kungengakuba nayo
    ikoliwe kukufaka u ‘ H ’ nje ngoko yenjenjalo.” Yamkelwa londawo
    yintlanga- niso.

5.   Ukubulelwa kuka Mr. Hofmeyr.—Nge “ Relief Bill ” yake. Ikomiti
    ayenzanga nto ngenxa yokuba wapuma sekumva komteto lowo, ekubeni
    ngexesha lokuxoxwa kwawo ubuso bawo bebungaqondakali apo businge
    kona.

6.   Ukukululwa kwe Nkosi ebu Bamjweni.—Amalinga enziwa, akapumelela
    ntweni.

7.   Ukucelwa kwe Ruluneli ukuba ingamamkeli u Tung'umlomo- —Kwenziwa
    akapumelela amalinga lawo.

8.   Imali ze Ntlanganiso eku Malungu.—Ikomiti yapendulelwa ngu Mr. J.
    K. Bokwe ngeliti—Akubonakali inako ukufumane- ka lemali ilityala
    elizi ponti ezingapezu kwe £70. Kubonakala kulingwe iqinga lokucenga
    amalungu ukuba abatalele wona umnyaka omnye ukusa ku June 1888,
    eliya kuti elenjenjalo kwesi situba sipambi kwentlangaiso ka July,
    libe loxolelwa ityala leminyaka ebisalimele, ati ongenzanga njalo
    limhlalele lonke ityala lake okanye aposane namalungelo
    entlanganiso. Yamkelwa londawo yintlanganiso.

9.   Imbali zesi Xosa.—U Mr. W. K. Ntsikana uvakalise ukuba lomsebenzi
    basawenza njengoko zisabonakalayo e Sigidimi Sama-Xosa, nakuba kodwa
    lomsebenzi bewenza pantsi kobunzima obukulu.

Kwesi situba kunikwe umzi wase Dikeni ituba lokuba utete U
Mgcini-Sihlalo (Rev. W. B. Rubusana) wenze intshayelelo ebonisa ukuba
nkulu kwalento iyintlanganiso, ebulela namado- do amakulu omzi wase
Dikeni kuba ete ako.

Kupendule u Mr. Metu Gingxa—umkokeli wakwa Gquma- hashe—no Mr. Matayo
Dungela wase Dikeni, ababulele imiguda eyenziwayo ebonisa ukuba
kuntshula ubulungisa obutile, nakuba angati mhlaumbi omnye adele isiqalo
sayo. U Rev. T. D. Philip, omnye waba fundisi base Lovedale, upose
ilizwana nge- si-Xosa akucelwa, eliti ub’eze kuba ngu mpulapuli nje
kodwa engaze kuteta, nakube evuyiswa kukubona intlangano le.

Zibulelwe inteto ezo ngu Mgcini-Sihlalo, kunyulwe abaya kufunda amapepa
kwezayo intlanganiso o Messrs J. B. Yekele, Robert Nyosi Gaba, D.
Dwashu, no Alexander Njokweni.

Kuvunyelwene ukuba intlanganiso ezayo ibe se Debe kwa Madubela.

Eya ngokuhlwa.

Kungenwe ngo 7.30 p m. kuze kupulapula umvumo ob’ ulungiselelwe u
Manyano. Indlu yazala kakulu, saza isihlalo kwacelwu u Mr Geddes ukuba
sibe naye. Upose amazwi axela usizi Iwake kuba umnini mzi u Dr. Stewart
engeko; nokuba bavuyisiwe kukubona amalungu o Manyano eze kuhlangana e
Lovedale ; nokutemba ukuba imigudu yalo yocuma iziqamo ezihle.

Kuvunywe ingoma yi Choir yase Lovedale, kwaza emva koko kwavunywa
zintsatshana ze Kinder-Garten ezifundiswa ngu Miss Anderson, bancedisa
ngawabo amaculo u Miss Stewart no Miss Muirhead, u Mr David Mzimba, no
Miss Ellie Meyile ititshalakazi yase Rabula.

Igqityiwe ingoma yapalala imibulelo yamanene ukubulela u Mr Bokwe ne
Choir yake, kunye namanenekazi ancedisileyo, ngokukodwa imidlalo
nengonyana zentsapo ze Kinder-Garten. Kwapunywa kancinane emva kwe
ntsimbi yesitoba.

Amalungu aye ngendawo zawo zokulala azabelweyo.

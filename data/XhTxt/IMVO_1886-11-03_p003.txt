sase Wesile ukuya kuteta ngomcimbi wama ngcwaba awake akankanywa kweli

nokumana ekataza ngokupuma futi xa kutetwayo.

ecukumise indawo zokuba umzi wahlu- twa umhlaba omdala wokungcwaba nge-
nxa yomteto we Palamente (Public Heal­th act) oti akuko ngcwaba liya
kuba pa- kati, okanye kufupi nomzi. Ibonisile kanjako i Chairman ukuba

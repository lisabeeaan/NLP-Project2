Nkosi Mhlekazi,

Incwadi yako onditumele yona egameni lika Rulumente ndiyifumene,
ngesandla semantyi ebekekileyo netandekileyo, ndiyibulela kakulu,
ndimbulela u-Rulumente ngokuyiva nangokundenzela isicelo sam. Ndimbulela
konke ukundigcina, nohlobo andipete ngalo u-Rulumente nokundikusela
ezintshabeni ezininzi, ndahlala kakuhle lonke ixesha lam. Kuseloko
ndazinikela ku Rulumente ndimfumene ukuba u-Rulumente umpata kakuhle
onwabe, akululeke osukuba ezibeke pantsi kwake. Ndibulela ukugcinwa kwam
nokwabantwana bam, nokwesizwe sam, indawo yoku- hlala, nokonwaba,
nenkululeko esinayo.

Namhla ke ekubeni i-Nkosi yam u-Rulumente endivumele wandipa ukupumla,
emicimbini yobukosi, diyambulela kakulu ngako, ndimyaleza no nyana wam
endimsalisa esikundleni samnjengoko ate u-Rulumente inkosi yam
wandivumela; ndimcela ukuba ati Rulumente inkosi yam amgcine namhla
unyana wam njengoko ebendigcinile mna yise wake, ampe ukonwaba, noxolo
pantsi kwake. Anditandabuzi ukutemba ukuba yonke indlela yokundipata
kwake wompata naye unyana wam u-William Shaw Kama kwanjengoko
ukwenzileyo kum. Uzeke Nkosi yam undilondolozele unyana wam
nabazukulwana bam, abantwana babantwana bam. Ndimcela u-Rulumente ukuba
ngolawulo lwake kungaze kwapela umntu wenzala yam ukumpatela u-Rulumente
esisizwe sama Gqunukwebe, endimkonze u-Rulumente kunye naso.

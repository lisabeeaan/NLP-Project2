IMPENDULO.

NKOSI,—Besite cwaka lonke ixesha eli kususela kwasekuqaleni kokunduluka
kwa- bavami e Qonce. Izinivo zotu zindululwa namhla kukubona lencwadi ka
Mr. P Xiniwe. Siti akwaba u Xiniwe wayehleli pantsi, wati cwaka
(neutral), akalicita ishi shini lake lokwaba amalungelo abo bonke e
Qonce nangapandle, njengokutsho kwake. Sikutazwa ngakumbi ukutsho,
ngamazwi ka Mr. Stephen kuye, ati, ngokutsho kuka Xiniwe
ngokwake:—“Ekumkeni kwam e England yayingumnqweno awawutyilayo u Mr.
Stephen ukuba ndingake nditete nto ukwenzakalisa Abavumi ndakuba
ndifikile kweli.” Endaweni yokuya kwenza ihlazo elingaka, nokusityila
ingubo ngezantsi kwi- lizwe napambi kwabantu abasasikumbulele- yo ukuba
nati singabantu nesizwe ekunoku- puma izinto nentlalo yobuntu ; aye
kugquka umkondo omhle ka Captain Veldtman, owada wayakufikelela
kwitshawekazi elande kangaka ngofefe, nozuko, u Kumkanikazi,
nakwizinonopu zalo ezinjego Mr. Gladstone. Masiti fanel’ ukuba ulibele
ukuba Abavumi babengayele nzuzo kodwa, babeyelo noku- bonisa
inqubela-pambili ekupucukeni kwe- sizwe mhlaimbi kwabantu abantsundu
Sitsho ngalamazwi ake ati, “ ilinga Labavumi base Afrika yayilurwebo
olunok ilunyukelwa ngapandle kwazo zonke ingxelo zetala lendlalo
(platform).” Kungoko siti ngelete wahlala pantsi akangena kulo. Nokusala
kuka Mrs. Eleanor Xiniwe kulilifu elimnya- ma kuti.

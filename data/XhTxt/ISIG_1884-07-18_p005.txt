Manenendini,

Ndiyamkele incwadi yenu yomhla we 17 June, evakalisa iuxwaleko yabatile
babantsundu ngenxenoku kuqutywa komteto we Pasi kukoyo—okubanga
ukulibalaxa kuhanjwayo, efuna ukwazi kanjako ukuba kungenzeka na ukuba
otitshala nabanye abonakalelwayo emicimbi yabo ngoku kulityaziswa
bakululwe ekuhambeni nge Pasi.

Xa ndipendulayo ndingati, umteto we Pasi, upatelele kupela kubantu
abantsundu abangepantsi kolaulo lwe Nkosazana, pkokukuti abapantsi kwe
nkosi ezimele imihlaba engapaya kwemida yelipetwe ngu Rulumeni: ndingati
kanjako lomicimbi we Pasi waka waxoxwa e Palamente ngonyaka odlulileyo,
waza wanikelwa igqizana lamadoda elobandla ukuba awupicote. Ingxelo
yelogqugula ndiyitumela kunye nale nteto. (A. 15. ’83). U Rulumeni ubeke
wacinga ukuba enze owona mteto we Pasi ukuze uqiniselwe yi Palamente,
kodwa enosizi nje ngesizatu sokuba engasakuba nalo ituba lokuyizalisa
longcamango ngelixesha ihleliyo i Palamente, u Mpatiswa Micimbi u de Wet
uya kuti kwa kamsinyane acele amadoda azipata-mandla ukuba

ake awuqwalasele lomcimbi.

Okwanamhla, kuzakutunyelwa kubahambisi bomteto imiyalelo ekutenjwayo
ukuba iyakukwelelisa ngokunobom, ukuba akusakuba kanye inxwaleko ezo
kulilwa ngazo.

Kulungile ukwe- njenjalo kunokuyichita ngamasola ; kodwa umfana onemali e bankini ululuto kuye ngokwake nakwabanye kune sidenge esihleli emhlana we bashe. Zikwako noko ke indlela zokuyigcina imali. Umntu angazitenga ibokwaua, igushana, nenkomana ezimbalwa. Kubantu abemi ngapandle lento iyalunga. Abasezidolopini nase ntsebenzweni ayibalungeli. Elinye ilungelwana lezi banki umntu onemali yake kuzo uzimele. Ukuba umlazi ufake imali ngegama lake ayina kucuku- nyiswa yindoda yake ecitayo. Nonyana nokuba yintombi efake eyake ayina kurolwa ngumzali nokuba ngumntu omondlayo. Umntu akangezidubi nangokupata imali kuye. Ukuba usuka e Qonce aye e Kimberley imali yake anga- yibiza kwi Post Office yase Kimberley.

Wonke umntu onokusindisa intwana yemali ufanele ukuba ayifake. Imali inomsebenzi omninzi. Omnye umsebenzi wayo umfana angaqala kwase ntloko ukuyigcina imali yake ide ibambe iminyaka ade abe nokuzitengela isikonkwane somhlaba, angati lomhlaba awuqeshise ade awufune ngokwake, uhlale enendawo yake angati ekwalupaleni okanye ehliwe sisifo abuyele kuyo. Ekuwutengeni umhlaba lowo kufuneka eqondisisile ukuba igama lomhlaba lowo liguqulclwe kwelake. Kuyimposiso eqelekileyo, inkulu kanjako, ukuba umntu acinge ukuba xa ate wafumana itayitile yom- hlaba aluwutenga, seyingowake. Akunjalo. Umhlaba ngowa lomntu ugama lise tayitileni, ingenguye lomntu ufumane wane tayitile enegama lomnye umntu. Kuyinto enkulu ke ukuba umntu otenge umhlaba enze ukuba igama lake lifakwe ngu Mantyi kulo tayitile yalomhlaba njengemfanelo.

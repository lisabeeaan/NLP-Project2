AMAXOSA AFUNEKA KWELE DIAMOND.— Kutiwa U-Mr. Campbell, lemantyi isa-
nd’ukumiswa kona, utumele E-Qonce esiti kufuneka amawaka amabini
abamnyama ukuba baye kumbela amaqela qela akona apo.

OFE NGENGOZI.— Umatrose walom- kombe bati yi Zingra uwelwe yi bali
yoboya wafa. Kubonakala ukuba bezili- qela ibali ebezilayishwa
kulomkombe. Ite ke enye yazo yamwela wafa kwa lapo.

UBUSELA BENKWENKWANA E-QONCE.— U-Lamle inkwenkwana yo Mxosa isand’u-
kuziswa ematyaleni ngenxa yokuba inxo- wa yemali. Nje ngohlobo lomteto
wosapo igwetyelwe ukuba ibetwe imivumbo eli- shumi elinesihlanu ngoswazi
olute nqi.

ONYATETAVE YINQWELO.—Kunyatelwe I-Lawo elitile kwelicala lingasentla E-
Bayi, yinqwelo, icancate elunyilingweni kwapela.

U-EMMA inkosazana yeziziqiti kutiwa zi Sandwich ububile. Ube nemfundo
nemikwa enje ngeye nenekazi lengesi. Wabeke watyelela nase London
kuleminyaka idluleyo.

ATI AMAPEPA endaba ase Bombay ngo- nyaka we 1869, kubube abantu abanga-
mawaka alishumi elinesinye anamakulu omane anancinye linesitandatu
(11,416). Bonke ababantu babulewe kulunywa zinyoka.

E-AUSTRALIA kufunyenwe iqekeza le- golide emsulwa, ebunzima buziponti
ezi- mashumi mabani. Ifunyenwe apo beku- mbiwa kona. Ixabiso layo
lingapezu kwe ponti ezingama £9000.

ABAZELWEYO.

E-St. Michael—pesheya kwe Nciba—ngo 22 ku January, umka Mr. Peter Masiza
uzele intombi.

IMITSHATO.

E-Burnshill ngolwe 17 ku December, kwakuko umtshato opakati ko Naniwe
Eigiland wakona, no Timothy Makiwane wase Ncera. Batshatiswa ngu Rev.
Mr. Laing.

ABABUBILEYO.

E-Qonce, ngo 26 ku January kubube, U-Mrs. Brownlee umfazi ka Rev. John
Brownlee. Ebe selenexesha elikulu esifa. Wabuba eselenobudala obukulu.
Amazwi ake okugqibela ebe ngawokuti, “ Zulu, kaya lam! ”

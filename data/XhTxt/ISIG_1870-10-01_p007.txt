ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 1, 1870.

Iwana ka-Mabandla, yati xa iti tshiki yakupa ngenduku enye
yayihlabanisela entloko, bapuma ubucopo.

Imantyi yati makaze atyandwe—-kodwa lati lakuya kona igqira elingu
Palmer akavuma u-Mabandla ukulibonisa incwa- ba. Kwabuya kwapindwa
kwayiwa akavuma. Kute ngokwesitatu kwaya ishumi elinesitandatu
lamapolisa lixobile, akwaba sanqaba ntoke.

UMTETO WELE-DIAMOND. Kumiswe um- teto omkulu ngurulumente welilizwe, oti
akanako ama-Bulu apesheya kwe-Gqili ukulibanga elozwe eline diamond,
kuba ngokwe njenjalo oba apula umnqopiso owenziweyo nabemi belozwe; oba
kanjako enza okucasene namalungelo abo.

Uti lomteto umongameli wase-Eree State akanagunya lokuzimisela imantyi—
kuyakumiswa ezivela kwelilizwe. Kutiwa ke lomongameli wase-Trans Vaal
maze alumkele ukunyatela kwelika Waterboer. Ezindaba zifanele nkuba
mnandi kwabamnyama abalifundayo elipepa kuba zibonisa ukuba urulumente
wase-Koloni ufungele ukuyikusela imidaka ekwelozwe le diamond.
Sekukaninzi bebandezelana abembi ngenxa yokuba abantu bengena mteto
babambe wona.

UKUKUNYUSHELWA KWA MAZWI AMA- NGESI NGOKWAMA XOSA. Ukuze kutyileke
amazwi ama-Ngesi angekoyo ngo kwama Xosa, namagama abantu nawe ndawo
ngendawo, siyakuti ngenyanga ezizayo siwatyile kwipepa lokugqibela
mhlaumbi ezantsi kumapepa onke.

INDABA NGOCINGO.

IMFAZWE YASE-EUROPE.

IDABI elikulu laliwa pantsi kwendonga zomzi wase-Metz, ngecawa ngomhla
we 14 ka-August pakati komkosi we-Tshawe elingu Frederick Charles,
noka-Marshal Bazaine. Lamacala omabini ayalibanga elodabi; elo liti
ndoyisile. Kwabulawa amawaka omane kuma-French. Ati amaPrussia
ekohlisiwe zindaba zobuxoki asondela ezindongeni, aza aba zicenge
zekanunu, aza abulawa kakubi. Bati abanye ngalomini kwafa amadoda
angamawaka alishumi elinesitandatu.

Ngolwesibini kwaliwa idabi elikulu, ekwafa ngamacala omabini kunene
akwakutshwana. Ku- ma Prussia kwawa induna zombini, kwanxwelerwa
zambini. Kuma-French yanye kwanxwelerwa zambini. Kwati lakupela idabi,
ama-Prussia acela ixesha lokuncwaba abafileyo babo.

Ngolwesine ngomhla weshumi elinesibozo enya- ngeni kwaliwa elesitatu
idabi elikwalikulu. Owama German umkosi upetwe yinkosi yama-Prussia
ngenqu. Kwaliwa iyure ezisitoba, kushushu kanye amacala omabini.
Ukumkani wama-Prussia uti wawagxota ama-French wayakuwafaka E-Metz,
wamtintela U-Bazaine ukuba angabuyeli e-Verduu.

Uti yena U-Bazaine akazange agxotwe; kodwa kuyinene, ukuba I-Metz
Ne-Verdun akusahambe- lani. Kuma-Prussia kwatinjwa amadoda anga mawaka
omane, nompati wawo.

Kutiwa ama-French kulamadabi matatu awo- lwecawa nolwesibini nolwesine
alahlekelwa nga- madoda angamawaka angamashumi omahlanu abafileyo
nabazinxwelera nabatinjiweyo. Akwa ngako nakuma Prussia. Inani kuma
Prussia alixelwanga, kodwa ipepa lendaba lawo liti— “Inani lamadoda ama
Prussia a waswelekayo ukuba likulu kwalo lingangoburoti awabubonisa
mhlana avingcela I-Metz.

Kutiwa U-McMahon ubuyela E-Chalons ukuze azuze iqela elitsha lamadoda
aze ahlangabezane ne Tshawe U-Fredreck Charles. Ama German akangelene
kaloku nendonga zomzi wase Stras­bourg onqatyiswe kunene. I-Nancy umzi
ose Lorraine use mandleni awo, asinga ngoku E-Metz,. ekondweni lomkosi
wama French.

Umkosi wama German opetwe ngunyana omkulu wokumkani wase Prussia ongama
waka alikulu elinama shumi omahlanu use St. Dizier.

Kutiwa umkosi wama French osema wakeni angamashumi omahlanu anamatandatu
upetwe ngu Douay no Defailly uyenyuka ukuze ungene ngasemva, mhlaumbi
ecaleni lomkosi opetwe ngunyana omkulu wokumkani we Prussia.

Akwazeki okuhlekileyo kulemikosi yomibini se- loko ngosuku lwama shumi
omabini luka August. Kwintsuku ezilishumi elinesibozo, kusukela kolwo
kuqala de kube lolweshumi olunesibozo kwaliwa amadabi ama tandatu
amakulu kazi, kwaza kwasweleka amadoda anga ma waka alikulu, abuleweyo,
nanxweleriweyo natinjiweyo.

IZICAMNGCO ZAMA-NGESI NEMPIMI-
SELO ZAMA-XOSA.

(Likunyushelwe)

Eligamaliti “Kaffir Express” lisisiguqulo kanye seli liti Isigidimi
Sama-Xosa. Sinyule lona ngokunga singapepa amagama amadala afikelwe
yintshabalalo. Noko akulula ukupimisela ngokwama- Xosa izicingo nenteto
nteto abalwe ngazo amapepa angoku. Elipepa aliyikufika ke ngoko kwesona
Si-Xosa. Inyaniso yile yokuba ubuciko benteto yama Xosa bohlala bukoko,
bungenakuzipimisela ingcingane zangoku nokuxela izinto ekutetwa ngazo
imihla yonke kwezinye indawo zomhlaba, ngapandle kokuzekelela.
Umlinganiselo wale ntswelo mazwi ubonakala kulencwadi kutiwa yeyo “
Hambo Lomhambi,” eguqulwe ngumfundisi umfb ka Soga, ekutiwa ngamafunda
Sixosa sesona siguqulo singenasiposo. Lamazwi ati, castle, helmet,
sling, parchment, telescope, ne mast yomkombe, onke abizwa ngesi Ngesi
azatyilwe ezantsi epepeni. Akuko ndlela yimbi ekungenziwa ngayo. Inteto
yesi-Xosa ikwafana neyezinye intlanga ezingafundanga Ixela isimo abakuso
abantu, isayilungele intlalo yabo yangoku. Maninzi amazwi atyila iutlobo
ntlobo zenca, nawe mi-

konto, nawa mabala enkomo — kodwa akuko mazwi ateta izinto ezinje
ngeziya kesazikankanya. Kwelipepa asiteti ngako ukucuma kwenteto Yama
Xosa okunconywayo. Ababali, nokuba ngabayipina inteto, nokuba
ngabasipina isihlalo, bateta ngokudluladlulanayo ngangokwazi kwabo.
U-Dr. Callaway wase Natal, kwipepa elisandukubalwa, elitetelela inteto
yama Zulu, uti amazwi atetwa ngabalimi bama Ngesi asemakulwmi amatatu
aze kuma Zulu abe ngapezu kwelonani.

Kodwake nokuba kunjalo, kotinina kwakuba kushicilelwa elama-Xosa ipepa
lendaba. Intapane yamazwi angena kutetwa nokuba kungayipina indlela
ngokwama Xosa, ayinakuba ingabiko xa sukuba kuzakutetwa ngezinto
ngezinto.

Masibazise abafundi belipepa, naba nobuciko, ukuba, ngenxa yoku
akanakuguqulwa onke amazwi ama-Ngesi. Amagama abantu, nawe ndawo
ngendewo, nawe nyanga, obalwa njengoko abalwe ngako kulo lonke ilizwe
labafundileyo abanje nga- ma Ngesi. Ukupinyiselwa kwamazwi kobuye
kulunge bakuhambisa abantu emfundweni. Nakuba ama-Ngesi nama-French
bewabala ngokufanayo lamazwi ati Marseilles, Lyons, bawapimisela
ngokwahlukeneyo. Ukuze libonakale kakuhle ipepa, amagama andulela
amagama abantu nawe ndawo, enziwe makulu. Umlinganiso woko nowu- bona
kweli lizwi liti I-Kwezi. Siyatemba ukuba oku akuyikuxakanisa nto;
ekungati ukuba kuxakani- sile kubuye kulungiswe.

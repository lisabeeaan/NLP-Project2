PARTS OP SPEECH. INLTOBO ZAMAZWI.

Amazwi okuteta kwabantu azintlobo ezilitoba, ukubizwa kwazo ezontlobo
kutiwa zi

1.  Article. Oko kukuti ngamazwi amancinane, wona ayimipini ekupatwa
      ngayo amazwi amakulu.

3.  Adjective. Ilizwi elixela uhlobo lwento.

4.  Pronoun. Izwana elilula lokubizwa kwento maxa kungatandeki
      ukupindapinda futi ukuyi biza ngegama layo.

5.  Verb. Ilizwi elixela ukwenza.

6.  Adverb. Ilizwi elixela uhlobo kwenziwe ngalo ukuba kwenjiwa njanina.

7.  Preposition. Isalatiso sendawo.

8.  Conjunction. Ikonco—izwana lokuhlanganisa ilizwi nelinye ilizwi.

9.  Interjection. Izwi loku kuza into ehlileyo.

Ilizwi lokuteta likwa njengezembe, alibinaku pateka kakuhle lingenawo
umpini. I Article ezi yimipini yamazwi, zizo ezi: i. ama. u. o. um. aba.
isi. izi. ili. ulu. in. imi. ubu. izin. uku. Akungeze kwalunga ukuba
kunqunyulwe inteto, kubizwe ngamazwi amakulu odwa, imipini ingafakwanga.
Singaba siya xelisa umntwana ongeka kwazi ukuteta xa siti, Puna mama mpe
masi. Tata beta tana. Kanti ngesisiti: Ndifuna u mama andipe amasi. U
tata wabeta umntwana.

Lemipini ayina kufumana ifakwe kuwo onke amazwi. ilizwi liya lungelwa
ngumpini walo kwanjengezembe. Lendawo yokulungelelana kwamazwi ne
article zawo iyaku cazwa kakuhle sisakufika emagameni, kulawo mazwi azi
noun. Ngoku mandi kendi fake yonke lemipini emazwini alunge nayo, ize
ibonakale kakuhle nxa ifakiweyo.

Ukuba izembe lako ulifakiie umpini ungase ugaula ngalo yonke imini
ungabanga sawususa umpini. Nelizwi loku­teta lenjiwa njalo nalo, kubeko
iganyana ekuteteni oyaku mana ubambelela ngalo knyo leyo article
uyifakileyo. Ukuba ndinento endiyitetayo ngehashi, i article yokufakwa
ehashini ngu i. Kekaloku u i unendawana yokwazana no 1, kuze kuti
kwazeke ngo 1 lowo ukuba ndisateta ngelo hashi, nokuba lizembe nokuba
yenye into egama layo lilunge no i kwohlala kuba njalo futi,
njengoluhlobo ke manditi,

I hashe 7am Zibityile, Zibi namhlanje, Zinjalo nje, ndingavuya
ndimbambile umntu obe Zikwele izolo, ndinga menza aZi hlaule i hashe
Zam. I zembe Zam Zibukali, Zihle kanjalo kunye nompini waZo, ndiya
Zitanda. Kodwa ke noko nditshoyo ngo i no 1 akubi njalo kwamazwi
azekelwe kube Lungu, igusha, isali, imali, u i emazwini anjalo
uyakulandelwa ngu y.

UKUZIMELA KWABA NTSUNDU.

Nqamakwe, December 3,1881.

NKOSI YAM,—Ngoku kuko abaqalayo ukubala ngezinto ezinefa. Ndiyacela
ukuba uke undifakele limigcana epepeni lako elilungileyo. U Mr. N. Ganya
ucukumise ngendawo enkulu. U Mr. Skepe Nzeku upendule ngengqondo wahlaba
entloko, ngokukankaya intlanganiso. Maninzi amadodana antsundu apuma
esikolweni efundisiwe amashishini kakuhle, enezimilo ezilungileyo,
enqwena ukuzimisela ishishini ngokwawo, ati ngentswelo anyanzeleke
ukuziqeshisa, mhlaumbi adungudele ezi dolopini ngokufuna umsebenzi,
iqinga leli,—(Ndiyabacela bouke abafundileyo nabangafundileyo abanemvo
ngo Hlanga). Makwenziwe intlanganiso yabantsundu, kwindawo ngendawo e
South Africa, amalungu ale ntlanganiso arole imali etile ngonyaka.
Kubeko intlanganiso enkulu kanye ngonyaka yawo onke amasebe akwindawo
ngendawo ekuya kubanjiswa umsebenzi wentlanganiso.

Umsebenzi, nento zayo lentlanganiso ibe kuku xoxa, noku bonisana,
nokwenza, ngazo zonke izinto ezimayela no hlanga. Ibe ngumsebenzi wale
ntlanganiso ukupicota, nokukangela izikalo, neziroro zo hlanga—iziqube
ku Rulumente.

Okwesibini, ibe ngumsebenzi wentlanganiso, ukugcina imali yayo e
Bankini, ivume ukuboleka inani elitile kumadodana atile anezimilo,
lomali ifune impahla zokusebenza. Abo bantu abafuna uncedo olunjalo
babalele entlanganisweni, umongameli walontlanganiso azifunde ezoncwadi
entlanganisweni; abobantu bamiselwe yintlanganiso ixesha elitile, emaze
bayibuyise ngalo lomali, kunye nenzalo eyobonwa yintlanganiso.
Baqondiswe abobantu, ukuba bahilizile bawushiya umsebenzi
bengayibuyisanga imali ye ntlanganiso, ivenkile zabo ziya kuvalwa
ngegunya le ntlanganiso, kufakwe abanye.

Akuncedi nto ukwenza izinto ezinde, le ntlanganiso mayi yilwe kwase
kuqaleni kwe 1882.

Kade siteta nge ndywala nokulobolisa (ewe ezonto azilungile). Kodwa
eyona nto efunekayo kuti kukuzimela ngama shishini nemisebenzi yonke
eyenziwe zezinye intlanga yokuqubela pambili. Kuko ukuzidela pakati
kwetu bantsundu, ukuti umntu omnyama akasoze apumelele kwinto etile
netile—akuko kulinga nakuzama.

Akuko mntu umise shishini lake ngenyameko nengqondo ungepumeleliyo
nokuba umhlope umnyama uluhlaza—nokuba ubomvu ngebala! Umntu onomsebenzi
akangebi naxesha lokuya ezi ndywaleni, nelokuteta ngamakazi
amakawafumane mhlaumbi awarole. Ndiyapela ngembeko

ANDREW GONTSHI.

IMICIMBI YE MFUNDO.

EKUHAMBENI kwetu zibe zimbalwa izinto ezisivuyisa nje ngokubona indlu
yokufundela intsapo ; sibe sikutanda kakulu ukuya kulubonela usapo xa
lufundayo, size sibuze kunene kotitshala ngokuhamba komsebenzi wabo,
sive nangabazali ngokuquba kotitshala. Kuti bantu bantsundu akuko nto,
kwezi zase mhlabeni, enobukulu obungangobe mfundo. Kuzo zonke izizwe
yeyona nto ingamandla. eyona nto boxatisela yona abafuna ukuba lube
yinto uhlanga. Saka sabaqana namazwi e-Ruluneli etile eyayiwasingisa
kubantu abamhlope beli lizwe, ibavusa ukuba balumke ; isiti ukuba abayi
xatiseli imfundo bayakubona sebepetwe ngabantsundu, ukuba abantsundu
bate bona bayinyamekela imfundo; isiti isizwe esifundileyo asinakukonza
kwabangafundanga. Nezetu intliziyo zizaliswe kukuba into eya
kusipakamisa kukufunda ; nakuba tina singajonge ekutini iya kusenza
inkosi zabamhlope, singatsho nokuti into enjalo inganqweneleka. Into
enqwenelekayo kukuzi- va ukuba sisisizwe esinye nabamhlope, ukuba
singamakaya, ukuba imicimbi yale Koloni yimicimbi yetu. Asibonike ukuba
siya kufikelela kwindawo yokuyiqonda inkululeko yetu kwane mfanelo zalo
nkululeko ngapandle kokuba sibe sifundile. Kungoko siti sakungena
endlwini yesikolo, sakuyifumana izele ngabantwana ize intliziyo zetu
zixume luvuyo. Kusitiwa nje kuko abantu bokukutazwa, babukwe,
bancediswe, singati tina otitshala babodwa. Umsebenzi wabo mkulu, umhle,
unefa. Singa ngoko singake senze indawo ezitile ngawo : silinge
ukuzipepa ezikade beteta ngazo abanye. Amazwi etu namhla siya kuwamisa
kwizinto esite sakuzibona saziva, saqonda ukuba zikufanele ukubatyafisa
otitshala.

NOVEMBER 16, 1883

Into yokuqala esiya kuyikankanya ngamanani abantwana abangeziyo
esikolweni, kube noko kungeko nto imandla ibabambileyo. Kuwo wonke
lamazwe esibe sikade sihamba kuwo asikumbuli nanye indawo esifike
bekutshwa bonke abantwana abebefanele ukukutshwa. Ikakulu ungafika
behambahamba pakati komzi beyibawisa ititshala elangazelela ukunga
ingabanceda, bebangela ukuba nolunye usapo malufunde lunqena.

Eyesibini into esiyibona kule ncwadana “ yolwimi,” zizindlu
zokufundisela. Kuyi nene kona ukuba zininzi, ukuba kweminye imilambo
zixinene ngokugqitileyo zibangelwa yindawo esiya kuteta ngayo mhla
singena kweye mbedesho imicimbi. Sibone ke ukuba likulu inani lezindlu
ezikufaneleyo ukufundisela. Ezinye zideleke ngobuncinane, ize into
eninzi yazo ufike ingamagxokogxokwana apa angati ngamapempe ; into
ezingati zakiwa ngolunya. Iko imilambo esingayibiza ngamagama, ongafika
kuyo abantu behleli kumanqugwala afanelekileyo, abanye bese zingxandeni,
ize yona eyokufundisela indlu, ufike inamaqubu nase ludongeni ude uye
elupahleni, kanti wakungena ngapakati uya kufika ixaswe ngezibonda
endaweni yentsika, ufike imiqadi izizinti; uti ukuba ufike ngenyanga
elandela ukuba igqitywe, uyibone ibubene.

Wakuba kaloku ungenile ngapakati waza wasingisa iliso lako kwaba
bantwana bona bazayo esikolweni uyakuti nokuba uke wavuya ubuye ube
nendawo ekudanisayo. Into ongakolisa ukuyibona usangena yeyokuba
zintsana lento ifunda apa, lugquqwana olungati lutunyelwa esikolweni
ukuze lungabasindi onina emakaya. Lento yokutumela intsana ikolise ukuba
lapa e Koloni kuma pesheya kwe Nciba nase Batenjini. Kuti kunjalo babe
ababantwana kutiwa bakutshelwe isikolo bengezi imihla yonke ; abanye
besiza kanye ngeveki, babangele ukuba ititshala iman’ukuwupindapinda
umsebenzi wayo, iwupindapinda kubantu abangenako ukuyi gcina kakuhle
into abebeyi xelelwe. Kwezinye indawo bati naba bakoyo bafike umsebenzi
use upakati kakulu mhlaumbi use sipelweni. Apo ke kukoyo ezondawo
utitshala ungafika abantwana ebamise ndaweni nye okunye ebagqadazisa,
engenako ukubacokisisa kanti amandla emfundo ase kucokiseni.

Kuko nokuti abantwana bangahlali xesha lide esikolweni, bapume
bebesafanele ukuba ngabahambi sikolo; bapume elixa baqala ukwenza mnandi
umsebenzi we titshere. Maxa wambi kuba ngesizatu sabazali nabantwana.
Uti umntwana akufika kwi Standard II nokuba sese III akunjulelwe
ngabazali ukuba selegqibile; aze naye akolwe, kuba isibongo yinto engena
msinya emntwini. Ababantu bapume negama lemfundo bengasibonanga isiqu
sayo, ngaba bancolise imfundo yomntu ontsundu, abayenza ukuba ideleke
kumntu wonke. Bakolise ukuba ngabantu abawucekece kakulu umsebenzi,
amanqenera namanqenerazana ongafika betyutya begqoba bepuma pakati komzi
apa, abamkondo unuka kude kudlule inyanga ezitile apo badlula kona.
Intombi yeli qela siteta ngalo ungafika ingakwazi ukuhlakula, ingakwazi
ukutunga nokutshayela kokwayo ; umfana ungafika engawazi amabala enkomo
zakowabo, engaligqibi kakuhle nehashe ake alikwele, kanti akakwazi
nokutolika! Into ebukekayo kubo zingubo zabo esingabanga natuba
lokubuzisa apo zivela kona.

Make siyeke apo namhla sobuya sivele ngomso.

ISELA ELINTSUNDU.—E-Natal umfo ontsundu abati ngu Hlanga, ubemangalelwe
ngokuba ibulukwe ezintatu ne bantyi kwamanye amadoda antsundu amabini.
Akuba evumile ukuba okunene uzibile, unikwe iveki ezimbini, neshumi
elinambini lemivumbo ngapezulu.

E-BATENJINI.—Siyavuya ukuva ukuba ukubalela ebeku- babazwa e-Batenjini
ngoku kudlule, ngoku nakona kuxelwa ukuba lihle kwelizwe, intoke leyo
enika intliziyo ezimnandi kubafuyi ababesebeselusizini.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1887.

UKUFA NGEBAQO—Ati amapepa apesheya kolwandle kuko inkazana efe
ngokusiqupe. Ite kuba indlu yayo ibimelene neyomnye umntu, wati lomntu
wafika ucango Iwendlu yake lupambene isitshixo lungavuleki, waba kutini,
alwavuma, kwaye kweloxa azamana nocango efile yintsini u Clara Smith
(lonkazana) suke yati isahleka njalo, yawa qwiti pantsi yafa kwapela.

ODUTYULWEYO.—Kuvakala ngamapepa ukuba kuko umlungu opantse ukushiya
ukuncunza e Luneberg. Kutiwa ute u Mr. Christian Kohrs esadla i tea
nosapo Iwako ngomnye umhla, kwavakala isitonga, umpu uveliswe
ngefestile, imbumbulu yekata kuye esifubeni yaya kuti gqi egxalabeni.
Kufunwe kunene umntu ongaba wenze lonto akafunyanwa, kwade kwafana
kwabanjwa umfo ontsundu ekutiwa kuranelwa kuye, kuba wake waviwa
emsongela ngokuti uya kumbulala. Ubanjiwe waqanyangelwa ukubotshelelwa,
noko kwasa ekululwe yiminyanya yakowabo, ise yinyashawe inanamhla. Nalo
mfo udutyulweyo uqubela kwase kudleni inca.

ABABULEWE NGUMLILO.—Liti ipepa eliyi Natal Mercury, kutshe indlu kwa
Moodie, ilele amadoda amabini, abasebenza kwi Beehive Company. Enye ife
kwa oko, (kuba umlilo ukauleze kunene) enye kuse isapanyaza. Ite kuba
iqatywe kunene amayeza okutsha, yalaliswa ekoyini njalo. Ipumile ebusuku
yaya kudimbaza isisele semali yayo endulini etile, yafika ngokuqauka
isel’iyipete pofu. [Wafa nelinqwa betu lowo ED. Sigidimi.]

U MR. TENGO-JABAVU.—Kubonakele kwipepa eliyi Port Elizabeth Telegraph
ukuba u Mr. Tengo-Jabavu usinge e Kapa ngo mcimbi wokutungwa imilomo.
Kuvakala kwakona ukuba uyakuti ebuya kona afunde ipepa elingomcimbi waba
ntsundu e Town Hall mhlayimbi.

IKAYA LABANTSUNDU.—Ikaya labantsundu lika Mr. Paul Xiniwe lilo okunene.
Kungati ukuba kuyakuqubeka ngoluhlobo kusabonakala Iona okwangoku
kungaba kwenzeke icam elikulu kumzi ontsundu ngenxa yelo kaya. Wanga
umzi ungalixasa ngomxelo wonke kuba liluncedo olukulu kubo bonke abafuna
ukupumla kakuhle, kwanabafuna izixaso nezamahashe abo xa sukuba beye e
Qonce. Linayo yonke into efunekayo kubantu abandilekileyo naba funa
indawo ezolileyo neyonwabileyo. Qondani mawetu nalo namhla ikaya
labantsundu.

INXWALEKO EHLILEYO.—Kubonakele emapepeni ukuba umhlobo wetu ongu Mr. I.
Wauchope uhlelwe yinxwaleko yokutshelwa yindlu yake ese Tinara, emva
kwemigudu nendleko azenzileyo zokwenzela usapo Iwake ikaya. Asi- kuko
nokuba silusizi ngenxa yalonxwaleko imhleleyo. Hayi ubumpunyumpunyu
bemfuyo yelihlabati! Banyani- sile abati “ There's many a slip 'twixt
the lip and the cup.”

UMDUDO KA MQIKELA.—Kobonakele kumapepa ase Kokstad ukuba
bekusand’ukubako umdudo omkulu e Mampondweni. Kuzeka inkosi enkulu u
Mqikela, izeka udade bo Mhlontlo. Kwalotyolwa amashumi asixenxe (70)
enkomo, kwaba kuyapela.

UMCIMBI WOKUTUNGWA IMILOMO.—Kubonakala emape- peni ukuba lomcimbi
useluvunyiwe nayi Ruluneli ukuba londawo mayime ibe ngumteto ekuya
kuqutywa ngawo, U Mr. Jabavu upele nge Bayi kuba akusayi kunceda luto
ukuya ku Mhlekazi ukunqanda eso senzo ngoku. Lendawo ifanele ukukangelwa
ngeliso elingagaxaziyo kanye. Ngati nendawo ecingwe li Bayi yokuti xa
kunje kutunywe amadoda atile ukuya e England ngawo lomcimbi lifanele
ukuke liyondele kunene eyona nto kunge- nziwa yona ngoku ne Ruluneli
sel’inike isihlahla nje kulomteto, kuba zininzi zona indlela zokwenziwa
kulento, nakuba zingayi kunceda nto zonke.

ILISO LOMZI ONTSUNDU.—E Qonce kuko intlanganiso ezitiye igama lokuba li
“ Liso lo Mzi Ontsundu,” eliti xa likunyushwayo “ Native Vigilance
Association ” Yiyo le ib’- itume u Mr. Jabavu ukuy’ e Kapa ukuya
kutintela u Ruluneli ukuba angawuvumi lo mteto waba Voti. Igxamesi layo
elikulu lise Bayi, zombini sezivumelene ukuba kutunyelwe Pesheya
kolwandle ukuya kuvelela u Mntan’Omhle ngoku tungwa komzi ontsundu
imilomo. “ Iliso ” lizibekele

umsebenzi obanzi. Ukuba likokelwe ngengqondo lingaba lilungelo emzini.
Kodwa ukuba ligaxazile, lazikangela izinto ngokwe gxwem ingelinganiswe
ingozi engehlayo.

INGXOXO.—Ezibekoyo ePalamente ngemali yokuxasa imfu- ndo zibe shushu.
Kodwa oyisiwe amadoda abefuna ukuba zingaxaswa ezaba ntsundu kwindlu
engentla i Legislative Council apo sakutshwa kona isaziso soku
hlanganiswa kweziqamo zentsebenzo zabafundisiweyo. Babe batandatu kupela
ababe ngelo cala, boyisiwe ke. Kwengapantsi ivunyiwe indawo yokuxaswa
kwazo okwangoku.

ICEBO LIKA MR. HOFMEYR.—Ingxoxo ebi nga 27 July ahlulelana amacala. U
Mr. Hofmeyr uti—Ukuba umzi ontsund’ ufanelwe lilungelo lokuvota ngoko
ukwafanelwe kukupatwa nje ngaba mhlope bangabi namiteto ibaqwantisayo,
enje ngeye Lali, ne Pass. Icala lo Mr. Innes liti—Elicebo linendawo eli
sheyi kuba liqala ngoku “ tunga imilomo,” ’ze libe liti makubeko
abakululekayo kwimiteto eqwantisayo, bafane naba mhlope ; kube
kubonakala ukuba intshiyi zalo zitshaulela ekuvulelweni kotywala, kuba
bonke abakululekileyo kwimiteto eqwantisayo bengena kuvingcelwa utywala
bebufuna. Londawo yo Mr. Innes ayipikiswanga yakuka- nkanywa. Icala lika
Mr. Hofmeyr lisoyisile ke kodwa.

IBANDLA LASE DIPENDE.

Kuvakala ukuba kwi ntlanganiso yaba fundisi be Dipende ebise Rini ngo
June odluleyo kwenjiwe nje ukwabiwa kwabashumayeli nabatsha:

1.   U Mr. Wm. W. Gqoba unikwe ngoku igunya elizeleyo (License)
    lokushumayela apo afunwe kona, nemvume yokuba aye kuziqalela
    iremente nokuba kupina apo kuvuleke ucango kona, nokuba angabizwa
    nayiyipina iremente emfunayo.

2.   Kwabebe funda e Lovedale, u Mr. Solomon Matolo fanel’ ukuba
    ukutshelwe e Kimberley ukuba abe ngumVa- ngeli pantsi kuka Mr.
    Bulmer. U Mr. Timothy Makaba uya kuncedisa u Rev. Simon P. Sihlali,
    e Batenjini.

Ixesha lokunduluka kwabo asikalazi. Asikuko nokuba ukumka kwabo bobatatu
kuya kushiya isikewu ekungelula ukusivingca e Lovedale apa ngokukodwa.
Bebe luncedo olukulu emisebenzini yomzi. Umfo ka Gqoba, iciko ekuteteni,
inyange ekuzazini nase kuzibaliseni imbali zakowetu —ukuba umkile e
Lovedale ngati Isigidimi siya kuba bunkedama—kuba nalapo eb’eluncedo
olukulukazi. Ibandla eliya kufumana u Mr. Matolo lozuza umfo obekekileyo
nongqondo izinzileyo. Eliya kufumana u Mr. Makaba lozuza umfo okwanjalo
ontshumayelo ishushu nehlab' emxelweni.

Sibanqwenelela impumelelo apo baya kona, nentsikelelo emsebenzini abaya
kuwuqala ngexesha ete i Nkosi yavuma ukuba baye kwindawo ngendawo zabo.

IMIGCOBO YE JUBILI KWA GATYANA,
WILLOWVALE.

[IVELA KU REV. DR. SOGA.]

Le Jubili yentsapo yenzeka e Willowvale (kwa Gatyana) ngosuku Iwe 28 ku
June 1887. Yayiyingqungqutela yentlanganiso. Inani lentsapo yodwa
yayinga 661, inani lento yonke eyayiko ngalomini ngamawaka omane
(4,000), bevela kwindawo-ndawo ezilunge neso sitili. Lomsebenzi wada
waba ntsuku mbini. Abantu lento bayingena ngemixelo eyicwayitileyo bade
bakupa ozinkomo, gusha ne bokwe. Kwaxelwa ishumi lenkomo (10), negusha
ezimashumi mahlanu (50), sekundawonye nebokwe, ngapandle kwenxowa
zokudla njalo-njalo. Kwada kwanga kusemsitweni. Abantwana bezikula
baviwa kuzo zonke izifundo abebe zilungiselele ngazo, bacula nokucula.
Zat'izikula ezidlule ezinye zavuzwa ngamabaso (prizes) zaze ke zonke
zonelana ngawo. Amaqaba awayeko amangaliswa kunene linani labantwana
abafundiswayo beso sitili, aza aneliswa kunene nazinto ezazisenziwa
ngabantwana abo bafundiswayo. Intlanganiso enje ngale ayanele kuba
iyakuvelisa ukulunga okutile ngobuko bayo, ekutini ixaye uluvo
lokutobela umteto we Nkosazana kodwa, iyakupembelela izishukumise
intliziyo nengqondo malungana nalento iyi- mfundo yosapo. Izikula ezo
zenze inteto ebaliweyo (address) yokucamagusha ku Kumkanikazi kuba epile
wade wafunya- nwa yile Jubili yake, ezite ezoncwadi zatunyelwa nge Nkulu
ye Mantyi u Capt. Blyth wase Ntlambe, owaye celiwe naye wako, ukuze
zamkelwe nguye.

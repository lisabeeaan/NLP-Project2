UMZEKELISO WONYANA OWALAHLA

IKOWABO.

iv.

INDLALA ENZIMA.

Akubon'ukuba udle konke kwabako indlala enzima kwelozwe, waqala ukusive-
la naye, Luka xv. 14.

AMANZI agalelwa esityeni esivuzayo oti exesheni, nokuba uwabeke pi,
apele onke kude kungasali netontsi eli kweso sitya; impabla ehambayo
ukuba ayinamntu uyalusayo, uyiguqulayo, uyikangelayo, uyinqandayo,
uyikataleleyo, uyise iso, yoti exesheni ilahleke, iduke kweminye
imihlambi kuba ingenamniniyo. Umntu odlala ngemfuyo yake, kuse atengise
ngenye, kuse atengise ngenye, koti exe- sheni kungabi sabonakala nanye
ezintla- ntini zake. Yaba yilonto ke kulomfana, umbali sipulapula yona
apa, ite exesheni impahla yake nakuba ibininzi imanele, yesuka yab'anga
yonke, akwasala nokuba ibenye kuba ebepikelene nokuyidla ngo- kuyolisa
inkanuko zentliziyo yake; inkabi zake abezibonga zifike zapela zonke,
akabanga sabonakala engena ebuhlantini bake epete itunga, akabanga
savakala ememelela xa aza kusenga inkomo zake,ezinamasi, imvaba asinto
ibuye yabonwa emzini wake kuba kungaseko nto isengwayo, akuba
ngasavakala kunxakama nto ingumdlezana emzini wake kuba yonke lonto
yapela ; okwakunxila kwake, okwakuqelana kwake namahule, okwa- ku'rexeza
kwake lemihla, okwakuhamba kwake namahilihili, okwakungahlukani kwake
nozinkanti, okwakubuta kwake emitayini, okwakutanda kwake omidudo
nozintlombe, okwaku hlala erintyelwa ngamatyala, kute exesheni kwawenza

umzi wake ukuba ube linxowa. Namhlanje ke sifike ezwini elisixelelayo
uhlobo lokucitakala kwake, kutiwe,—

1—Ufike wayidla yonke impahla yake. Asimfo umbilini, asimfo ukugcina
isisu, ade asuke ati impahla engaka ayigqibe ngokuyidla ; linzulu ke
elolizwi elitshoyo, kuba liti lompahla wayicitela inkanuko zomzimba
wake, akuk'anuka ntoni idliwayo usuke wayingxamela akonwaba wada
wayifumana; akuk'anuka ntoni iselwayo akatintelwanga nto ade ayite
kulomqala wake, kwakuvuka zingcinga zikohlakeleyo akalwanga nazo ade
azeyise, kokukona esuke wazivulela, wawupemba umlilo wesihogo ngapakati
entliziyweni yake, akuk'anuka mihlali akakanga abuze ati ndisiya nje
kulomdudo ndihombe kangaka andisokufika ndizeyelisele na ezonweni? akuva
okokuba kuselwa ekutini akakanga anqumame abuze ati, andisokunxila na
paya ndizihlazise, ndihlazise negama lika bawo? Akacinganga nto
ngompefumlo wake usuke wasukelana nokobubomi; ubeye ezweni
elingenazityalike, elingenabashumayeli belizwi, ekungatandazwayo kona.
Akacinganga ngo Tixo, akaguqanga nga- madolo azitandazele, nakuba
ebengofu- ndisiweyo ezonto kowabo kwa ese ngu- mntwana yimisebenzi leyo
awayilahlayo kuseloko wafudukayo ekaya. Ebenga- sahloneli Tixo,
engasoyiki mntu, kupela usuke waqondela kwizinto zase mhlabeni
ezingenakuwusiza nganto umpefumlo wake. Bekungati akasokuze afe, akaso-
kuze aye pambi komgwebi, ubeselengo- welinani labantu “abasipelo
siyintshaba- lalo, aba Tixo usisisu, abaluzuko lulihlazo labo,
abanyamekela izinto zase mhlabeni.”

Asingulomfana yedwa unyamekela, usukelana, nokobubomi bodwa base mhla-
beni apa. Ihlabati eli lizele ngabantu aba. gcine kupela okwemizimba
yabo benga- teni nokompefumlo wabo, ekusa kuhlwe lemihla bezalisa
behlutisa inkanuko

zabo. Nakuletyalike nditeta nje bakwa- baninzi abanjalo; wena mntu
upulapulayo kendibuze, eyonanto utanda yona wena yintoni, ongati ukuba
uyifumene, uve okokuba ibongo lentliziyo yako ulizuzile? Zivato ezi
zomzimba? Yimpahla le ihambayo? Ngumhlaba lo ulinywayo? Zinkazimlo ezi
zomhlaba? Yimihlali le ilibazisayo? Zindywala ezi ziselwayo? Ngabafazi
aba bangxanyelweyo kangaka ukuba babe linani? Bupakati obu buta- ndwayo?
Bukosi obu bunembeko? Waye ke ufuna ezonto zodwa ungateni no Tixo,
nelizwi lake ulenze ucuku, nemiteto yake ungayilulameli; ukuba kunjalo
ukuhlala kwako emhlabeni wenza lento yenziwa ngulo mfana. Udla ilifa
lako, uzibubisa ngokwako. Owona msebenzi udalelwe wona uko nje
emhlabeni, udalelwe ukuzukisa u-Tixo, udalelwe ukululamela imiteto yake.
Ngumsebenzi owenzayo na ke lowo ungumntu nje? Zonke ezinye izinto
azidalileyo u-Tixo aziyeki kumzukisa kutabatela kwimini ezavela ngayo,
amalanga angaposwayo ngamaxa awo okupuma nokutsho na, okutwasa nokufa
kwenyanga, okuka nya kwe nkwenkwezi ebusuku libete izulu eli libe
liranga’ranga, okuzunguleza kwe minyaka elandelelana ngento entle
amaxesha ayo awokulima nawokuvuna; awokutwasa kocanzibe nawezilimela;
okumka kozinkonjane lakupela ihlobo ; okubuya kwazo kwakuvela
intlokohlaza. Nguwe wedwa kwizidalwa azidalileyo u-Tixo osuke watanda
okwako. Inkomo eludlolo ubehle uyicite, inkabi engatsaliyo ubehle
utengise ngayo, kuba ezonto zingakubuyiseli nto, zingayifezi imisebenzi
yako, akukolwa kufuya mpahla injalo ungumntu nje, ke ku Tixo ongu Mdali
wako, oyi Nkosi yako, ongu Mnini wako utanda ukuzenza umntu
ongenamsebenzi ingenguwo owokuzizekisa ityala lokutshabalalisa umpefumlo
wako. Kaucinge, ubonela nganina ubomi bako? Uzeyelisela nganina eshweni
elingonapakade?

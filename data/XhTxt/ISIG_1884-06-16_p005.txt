ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA JUNE 16, 1884 5

UKOHLWAYWA KWA MASELA.

U MR. JOHN LAING [omele i Bofolo] ungenise e Palamente ngo 6 June indawo
yokuba makumiselwe ukuba isohlwayo samasela empahla sibe sinye—akatswe.
Indawo leyo ibange inteto enobom. Ute intolongo ayincedi luto
ukubupelisa ubusela.

u MB. CROSBIE [omele isitili sase Albany] ute umteto onje ufunwa kakulu
ngamafama ase Maxoseni.

u ME. UPINGTON [Inkulu yolaulo] ute akanako ukuwuxasa lomteto.
Awunabuyambo, kuba umisela ukuba kukatswe nokuba kufanelekile
akufanelekile sinina. Ute ikatsi nangoku iyasetyenziswa ze Mantyi xa
zitandayo.

U ME. MERRIMAN ute uyicase yena ikatsi kuba inganele. Ziko inkibitsholo
zabantu ezingasayivayo nekatsi, eziti zibetwa zibe zibeta umlozi [au,
sakumbula u Adonis Plaatjes apa.—EDITOR.]

u MR. LEONARD ute akwanele ukuba isohlwayo sinqande— kodwa masibe
sesinokwenzeka. Into ayicasileyo kukuba abagwebi bamatyala
babotshelelelwe ekohlwayeni ngandlela nye. Abanye abantu beba ngenxa
yepango, abanye beba nje.

u ME. WALKER [omele i Dodoloro] ute abo bamtumelayo e Palamente
bayawufuna lomteto. Ihlazo lisekubeni aliseku katseni. Isela kwabakowalo
liyanconywa.

O Messrs. Ohlsson, A. H. du Toit, Hockley baxasa u Mr. Laing.

u MR. FULLER ute into enje ayikazanga yangeniswa nakulipina ilizwe. U
Mr. Laing ubemlindele ukuba ingangowokugqibela umntu ongangenisa into
enjengale. Ngolwe Sitatu olu ubulelele amapolisa amahlanu ase Xesi—eyona
nto ingabahlobo befama. Izohlwayo eziburalarume njengezi azipelisi
busela.

u MR. WARREN [omele i Qonce] ute into efunwayo asikukwandisa izohlwayo
kodwa kukumisela amapolisa. Indlela xa isela elidala lifunyenweyo kukuba
livalelwe ixesha elide.

U MR. JONES [omele i Bayi] ute ukubetwa kungalunga xa umntu afunyenwe
ngokwesibini.

U MR. DE VOS [omele i Vusta] uwucasile umteto lowo kuba unganiki lungelo
kubagwebi ukumpata ngokutanda kwabo obanjiweyo.

U MR. DYER [omele i Qonce] ute uwucasile lomteto angakolwayo ukuba
uyakuncipisa ubusela nangayipi indlela. Into eyakude incede kukubujiswa
kwe fam. Yinto yoburau ukuba ijaji ibotshelwe ekatsini kupela.

O Messrs. Barrj, W. Ayliff, Myburg, M. J. Louw, Johnson, no Sprigg
baxasa u Mr. Laing.

U MR. INNES [omele Idike] ute yena ukucasile ukukatsa okunjengoko
kumiselwe kulo mteto ka Mr. Laing. Akazi ukuba isininzi esiteta lula
ngokukatsa saka sambona na umntu ekatswa [u Mr. Myburg uhleke kakulu.]
Bangahleka kodwa yena umangalisiwe. Unencwadi azifumene kwabatile
abantsundu, abaucase lomteto nobunzima bezohlwayo zawo njengokuba yena
ewucasile. Abantu abonileyo bayohlwaywa kakulu napakati kwabamnyama;
abantsundu abohlwayani ngobuq'olo. Kodwa bayazi abamnyama ukuba lomteto
uyawaputuma bona, bati ke akufanelekile ukuba umiselwe de babe banokuba
nezwi nabo ezijurini abekiswa kuzo amatyala abantsundu : [wenze inteto
yokutshabisa kwesi situba u Mr. Myburg.] Ngumteto mdala ukuba ubani
ufanele ukupendula pambi kwabakowabo. Mhlaimbi ilungu elibekekileyo
belingasakuhleka ukuba ubehle walixelela kwasentloko ukuba wonke
onemfanelo yokuvota unemfanelo yokuba ngomnye we juri. Abantsundu banalo
ilungelo lokuvota; banayo imfanelo yokuba ngabe juri ke ngoko. Yiyo
lento ke ebakalisayo abantsundu, uyabuza ke, asifanelekile na isicelo
sabo ? Esi ke asisona sizatu awucase ngaso lomteto yena, kodwa kukuba
engakolwa ukuba kulungile ukuba ijaji nemantyi ingabi sabinako ukumnika
esinye isohlwayo umntu.

U MR. HOFMEYR ute okwangoku kusemandleni omantyi ukumkatsa omnyama,
am’fake etolongweni omhlope; kodwa ngalo mteto bonke ngapandle komketo
bayakukatswa—into leyo ke ebifanelwe kupaulwa ngabanyuli abantsundu
belungu elibekekileyo lase Victoria East (e Dikeni). Yena uyakuxasa u
Mr. Laing. Umntu ngekatswa ngetyala lesibini.

U MR. DE WET [Umpatiswa Micimbi Yabantsundu] ute unyanisekile ukuba
lomteto ka Mr. Laing uyakuba noncedo olukulu. Uyakolwa kanye nguwe.
Abantsundu banalo ilungelo lokuba zijuri, kodwa abavumi ngokwabo.

UMDLALO WE KRIKITI.

Umdlalo webola owawu pakati kwe Kreli Star C. C. yase Port Elizabeth ne
Good Hope O. O. yase Uitenhage wa dlalelwa e Uitenhage. Ngo 24 May,
1884, ikaba lase Bayi lafika e Tinara ngo half past eleven wakusasa.
Into yokuqala eyenzekayo li Lawu elabetwa yibolwa lafa lagalelwa amanzi,
labetwa kunge kadlalwa. Enye into amadoda amane kwaku. ngavunywa ukuba
adlale kuba kusitiwa alunge ne Fear Not C. O., lomadoda ngo R.
Christian, Geo. A. Ross, J. Ciliwe, R. Ciliwe, kanti ke lamadoda
anelungelo lokungena nokuba kukuyipina. Kumanani okuqala awenziwayo li
Bayi kwabako

impikiswano enkulu. Ute urabali wase Tinara yi 34, ute owase Bayi yi 38,
kukangelwe ezincwadini kukangelwa amagama abantu abadlalileyo
kwafunyanwa umbali wase Tinara engazi balanga i runs ezine zika R.
Ciliwe, ute akuxelelwa wakumbula ukuba okunene akazibalanga, hai ke
kwavunyelwana. Omnye umdlali wase Tinara ugwetywe yi umpire ngokusuke
alike mva emitini ibola seliko, walwa watuka umdlali esiti ndingakukaba
e esitsho kwi Umpire. Abase Bayi

babuye bikalaza ngempato abapatwa ngayo li Tinara bati befile bati bona
abafanga bepikisana ne umpires bezituka kananjalo. Kubonakala ukuba
abantu base Tinara babe negugu lokudlala ibola imiteto yayo bengayazi,
kanti into yokuqala ngumteto ukuze kulandele ukudlala. I Tinara
lakutshwa li Bayi nge 36 runs. Nango ke amanani awenziwayo ngabadlali:—

  Kungenwe kananjalo kwimbandezelo abangeniswe kuyo EKUBUTWENI KWE MALI
  YE RAFU ZABANTSUNDU.

Inikelwe kuma Gqweta amdle umntu amti bunyu tu. Atsho ati, uti onetyala
leshumi lesheleni imali elityala kubizwe i 14/ imali ke leyo ekutiwa ye
yendleko zokubutwa kweli shumi lesheleni. Kuti ukuba ayiko lomali atimbe
ezona zigudileyo inkomo. Kubuzwe ukuba kutenina ukuba lemali ingabutwa
ngaba Kangeli belali, kuba ukuba ibibutwa ngabo nge ingeko lembandezelo
ingaka. Batsho bati kutiwa kuko umbuti wale mali ofanele ukuba eb’ezuza
i £10 nge mini. Baye bebonisa imbandezelo abakuye. Ute u Mr. Innes
uyakuyikangela lonto, watsho kodwa esiti izinto ezinjalo ngebezingcinisa
ezimantyini zabo zibakusele kulompato.

Zapela apo intlanganiso ezitabate iyure ezitile zingena zipitipiti. Zaye
zibulela kakulu inyameko ayibonakalisayo umhlobo wazo u Mr. Innes
ngokuwuhambela umzi; zisiti ziyaqonda ukuba izikalazo zomzi zingeniswa
kakuhle nguye e Parlamente.

IMFUNDO NENKULULEKO YABA NTSUNDU.

Yenjenje i Cape Argus ngenteto ka Rev. P. J. Mzimba ayishicilele kwi Mvo
Zabantsundu:—

“I Lecture ka Rev. P. J. Mzimba asandul’ ukuyifunda kwi Lovedale
Literary Society anyatela (u Mr. Mzimba) kumalungelo abanawo abantsundu
engxoxo ezibekiselele kumalungelo ohlanga ib’ifanele ukututuzela
abaxalileyo abanesoyikelo sokuba umntu ontsundu uza kungena ezindlwini
ze Parlamente nge gunya. U Mr. Mzimba uyibeka kwindawo yokuqala imfundo,
amalungelo ohlanga kwindawo yesibini Uke wabalisa ngembali yomfo wase
America, umfo ontsundu, owaye balisa ngesipitipiti esaveliswa
kukuguqulwa kwolaulo lomfo omhlope e America. U Mr. Mzimba uqube wati:—
Indawo ekukankanywa zona ngabantsundu base America, ezondawo zibekisa
nakuti bantsundu base Africa. Maluti uluvo Iwabase Africa abase America,
lube siyalo kuselixa elilungileyo kwaba ntsundu belilizwe bayeke ingxoxo
zemicimbi yolaulo (politics) okwa ngoku. Masanele lulaulo esilaulwa
ngalo ngama Koloni. Masiti ukungena kulomcimbi, singene ngokutazo,
sikutaza umfo omhlope olangazelela ukusinika indawo zokufunda nencwadi.
Singawatintela na Amasatlari xa ayihlutayo lonkululeko kuti bantsundu?
Hayi. Amalungu atile e Parlamente aseleke ayingena indawo yokusihluta
elilungelo lale nkululeko sinayo. Kani- kangele ngala Mteto wa Pesheya
kwe Nciba, ekwakunxanyelwe ukuba lihlutwe iqela elikulu labantu bakowetu
ilungelo lokuvota. Abantsundu ngokwabo bebengena kusitintela eso sicelo
ukuba sibe ngumteto kwela pesheya kwe Nciba. Umntu ontsundu oswele
ukwazi, olihlwempu, nongakolwayo akanakulaula umfo wase Koloni
olumkileyo, okade ebona, otyebileyo, nokuba bambalwa. Abantu abantsundu
beli lizwe kufuneka befundiswe isifundo sokuba bakutale emsebenzini,
ukuze babe nokuma pambi kwe kumkani. Abantu base Africa mabeme
ngemilenze emitatu, eyile: Ukusebenza ngenyameko nenkutalo; bafunde
ngonyamezelo nangokufezekileyo; babe ngabangacitiyo izinto abanazo,
ngokukodwa imali. Ezo nyawo zontatu zifuneka kakulu ukuze sibambele
pambili. Ofundileyo, notyebileyo akanako ukuba uluvo Iwake lunganikwa
mbeko. Ukuze umntu awafumane amatuba okonwaba makatyebe; ingcamango zake
akana kuzihluza engonwabile, akana kuqubela pambili engazisebenzisi
ingcinga.”

EMA MPONDWENI.

Kuvakala ukuba imvumelwano ekwakugqitywe kuyo ngu Mdlangazo nonyana
benkosi betetela ama Mpondo no Mr. Stanford i Nkulu ye Mantyi yase
Griqualand East etetela u Rulumente, yile:—

1.   Ukuba u Rulumente awanike ama Mpondo i £1,000 ngalamhlaba wama
    Xesibe ungeniswe kulaulo Iwale Koloni.

2.   Ukuba u Rulumente arafe amakulu omabini eponti, ngomnyaka (£200)
    nge Cweba eli le St. John’s (Umzimvubu).

3.  Ukuba u Rulumente arole ama £600 ngomhlaba wase Rode ocanda kuwo
    umendo wase Koloni. Ukwenje-njalo ke ayavuma ama Mpondo ukuba
    lisetyenziswe ngu Rulumente i Cweba lo Mzimvubu nokuhamba ngendlela
    ezicanda kwelama Mpondo ancedise ekuzilungiseni. Kuvunyelwene ukuba
    ugcinwe umda, kupazanyiswe ubusela; kuyiwe kumapolisa ukulanda
    imikondo, ziti impahla ezifunyenweyo zibuyiselwe kubaninizo,
    ohlwaywe amasela. Bobu busela obu budale ukuba apambane ama Mpondo
    nama Xesibe nama Baca, ukuba ke budanjisiwe ohlwaywa amasela
    kulamacala mabini zopela mhlaumbi izipitipiti.

Ngeveki yokugqibela kulonyaka udluleyo, u Mr. Stan­ford, Imantyi leyo,
wanduluka e Kokstad ekumbula e Rode apo ebeya kuhlangana kona no
Mdlangazo nge ngxoxo ababeshiyene kuyo kwi ntlanganiso yokugqibela kwi
lali ka Ntola. Wanduluk’ e Mnceba, sisiva u Mdlangazo neqela elikulu
lamapakati bahlangana e Rode apo baxoxa ngemikondo yempahla ezibiweyo.
Ayelangazela kakulu ama Mpondo ukuba ikangelelwe kakuhle londawo, ibe
nendawo ekugqitywa kuyo, ohlwaywe amasela alamacala mabini angaman’
engamaqela akwel’ emahasheni esilwa nezizwe ezisemideni. Kuvakala ukuba
ama Baca nama Xesibe ayakuhlutwa izixobo zawo ukuze ipele lenkatazo
pakati kwawo nama Mpondo. Umda wase Rode, nje ngokunikelwa kwawo ngama
Mpondo, ngumendo wase Koloni. Lendlela iwela kwelama Mpondo yoba lapo,
abaya kuti abarwebi abavela e Koloni besinga e Kokstad nokuba kubuye
kwako izipitipiti nama Mpondo, banganyanzelelwa ekucandeni kwilizwe
lentshaba nje ngokuba bekuya kuba njalo ukuba belifile ilizwe
kulommandla. Emva koko ube nentlanganiso nama Baca u Mr. Stanford,
siyatemba ukuba sobuye sizive indawo zalo ntlanganiso apo igqibe kona.
Okwangoku kutiwa wonke umntu usakoliwe sisigqibo salo mvumelwano.

INTLANGANISO YAMADODANA ESIKO.

Intlanganiso yamadodana Esiko, ingene e Peelton ngosuku Iwa . 23 ku
December, 1886, ngexesha leshumi kusasa. Ivulwe ngomtandazo ngu Rev. W.
B. Rubusana, ote emva komtandazo waposa amazwi entshumayelo atatyatwe
kwi Gal. v: 2. Utete ngenteto yokubonisa indawo etetwa ngalamazwi,
wabonisa ngokufutshane ukuncedwa kukwaluka kowaluke ngentliziyo ku
Krestu Yesu. Kute emva koko wacela u

REV. R. BIRT uyise wosapo ukuba atete (etolikelwa ngu Mr. A. H.
Maci).—Yena utate Amazwi akwi verse yesi 6. Ubonise ukuba ulwaluko ku
MaXosa yintloko yobuhedeni, ubapatizo yintloko yobu Krestu. Watsho wati,
kukohlakele ukwaluka, kodwa kugqitile ukukohlakala komfana oti
ebebapatizwe wangeniswa ngabazali bake esemncinane ebukumkanini buka
Tixo ngobapatizo, asuke ati akuba mkulu anyule ukungena ebukumkanini
buka Satana. Utsho wayala ngokushushu amadodana ake ukuba angaze ayenze
lonto ayicase kakulu into ekukwaluka, abambelele ku Krestu Yesu.

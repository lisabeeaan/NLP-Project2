Umlungu avule zibuqe okomnyaka omnye zize izizwe ezintsundu zilungisane
zodwa. Kuko omnye umfo ontsundu oke wasibuza into enjalo ngazo

UVIWO LWE TITSHERE.

Kumaxesha amabi nalusizi njengala etu, amaxesha esiva ngawo indaba
zemfazwe, kulu- vuyo ukufumana into enje ngoviwo Iwetishere ukuba
sibalisele abalesi betu. Njengoko kwakubikiwe olokugqibela Iwabako
ngomhla 27 ka September kwindawo ngendawo eza- zimiselwe. Siquka lonke
inani labeza eluviweni sifumana ukuba baba 151; kwelinani amadodana 71,
amankazana 80. Kwelinani ke abapumele ngokuncomekayo babe 49 abafumene
iziqiniselo zokuwulingana umsebenzi wokufundisa baba 60,
abangapumelelanga babe 42 noko omnye wabo ete ngenxa yesifo wayeka
lungekapeli uviwo. Abangapumelelanga kuvakala ukuba ababini bakutshwe
kukupela Isingesi, aba 39 bebengaziqondi kakuhle zonke izinto ebe-
zibuzwa. Kuya kulindeleka kubo ukuba bacokise pambi kokuba beze
kolulandelayo. Sibashiya neliti:—ningatyafi, ningadinwa ningadani
ntozakowetu. Umonde nenkutalo ekufundeni nase kufundiseni wonipumeza.

Kuluvuyo olukulu ukubona amanani anamhla ewadlula kangaka awoviwo luka
March. Namhla sifumana ligqitile inani labebeze eluviweni, lingapezulu
nelabapumelele ngokuncomekayo, lidlule nelabafumene iziqiniselo
zokuwulingana umsebenzi.

KUBALESI.

ABATILE kubalesi be Sigidimi bakala ngoku- ti kuteni na lento bangasi
fumananga kwezi nyanga zimbini zidlulileyo. Siva ukuba abanye bacingela
ukuba kungokuba befihlelwa indaba zemfazwe yakwa Sarili. Site sakubuza
ku Mr. Wilson umtumeli wazo wati kungokuba sekulixesha elide
bengahlauli. Kukade ke itetwa lonto yokuba abantu makungade kudlule
iminyaka bengayiroli imali yaso. Nati kufuneka ukuba sitenge amapepa
nomsizi sihlaule abantu abasisebenzayo nezitampu, ke ngoko silindele
abafundi beyitumele imali yaso njengamadoda atembekileyo. Siya temba ke
ukuba abangekahlauli baya kuyi rola kwangale nyanga imali yaso.
Nabamapepa angasafikiyo bebefanele ukuwahlaulela abawafumanayo, kuba
ukuba abenj enjalo boba baweba lawa atunyelwayo kwabe kukangelwe ukuba
boyirola lomali. Kwabanye abasixasileyo situmela imibulelo sisiti banga
bangacunyelwa ngumnyaka ozayo, bafumane imi- gcobo nge Kristmas eyakuba
ngomhla 25 kule nyanga sinayo.

U-MAPASA NO MAKINANA.

KULE veki idluleyo bake abase Qonce nakoma Dike bapitizeliswa kukuva
ukuba u-Tshalisi ubeye ku Mapasa eye kubaxelela ukuba mabarole izixobo
zabo bazinikele ku Rulumente waza akuvuma u-Mapasa lowo, u-Makinana ka
Mhala wabalekela kumninawe wake, ati akuma ngapambili amapolisa wasuka
wadubula. Bate bakuyiva abaninzi lonto bacingela ukuba ezinkosi ziza
kubangela ukuba avuse ucuku nama Ngqika. Kude kwatunyelwa neqela lama
Soldati okuya kukangela ukuba ufun’ ukwenza nto- nina u-Makinana. Ite
into ebuye yabuyisa intliziyo kwavakala ukuba u-Sandile akavumi
ukuzamkela inkomo zika Makinana. Kwixesha ke elinje ngeli, ezipitizela
kangaka indaba kunqabile ukuyi fumana inyaniso. Into kodwa engati
iqinisekile yeyokuba imipu ka Makinana idubule kumapolisa noko
kungenzakalanga mntu, enye ke neyokuba noko eke akavuma u-Mapasa ukuyi
nikela imipu uselede wabuya wenjenjalo, eyesitatu yeyokuba u-Sandile
okukokwake akafuni kulwa nokuba kungaba kuko abayi funayo lonto.

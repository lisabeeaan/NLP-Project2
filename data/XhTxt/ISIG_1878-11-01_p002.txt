Lendawo yokuwezwa kwama Ngqika ngo- luhlobo asikuko nokuba inzima kuwo
lama- doda awezwayo agxotwa emblabeni wawo, ngondilele abangavumanga
ukulwa no Ru- lumente naxa sekupume nenqu ka Sandile. Ukuvuzwa kwawo
ngokutembeka kwawo, asuke apangwe umhlaba aze asiwekwindawo awayete
akatandi kuya kuyo, asuke natnbla angqurulwa ngeliti akufunwa kuba
apendule kuse kugqityiwe. Ukutetwa kwale ndawo kutiwa aya lungiselelwa
kuba esiwa kwilizwe elihle, aya kucandelwa kona, akuyi kubako tywala
bomlungu, akukubako tuba lokuba impahla zabelungu ngokoke abantu baya
ku- sebenza ukuze bapile, kutiwa kuya kubako izikolo. Ezinteto zonke
zizizimfamekiso zo- kufihla ezona zizatu zizizo. Elo lizwe noku­ba lihle
alinakude ligqite elakwa Ngqika nokuba kukangelwa esimeni sempahla
no­kuba kukangelwa ekudleni. Ngendawo yokucanda okunene baya
lungiselelwa, kodwa ke bebengacandelwayo nokuba bebemi kwakulo mhlaba
wabo. Ngendawo yokuba singati namhla kukona bebesenzelwa ituba elihle
lokuba. Kutiwa pakati kwazo zonke ilali kuya kubako ifama zabe Lungu.
Ukuba ke bekusitiwa nganeno kwe Nciba Ama xosa abaxalisile abe Lungu,
linge lingavakali eli lefama eziya kuba pakati kwabo. Makabe ke mhlaumbi
aya kutiyelwa ama Ngqi­ka ukuze abuye agxotwe nalapo awezwe Umbashe
kutiwe ayeba. Ngesiba siti asazi nokuba aya kuhlala ixesha apo aya kona
ukuba bekuyi nyaniso ukuba esuswa ngobusela, kuba agxotwa namhla
kungatshiwo nokuti alwele ntonina, agxotwe kusaziwa uku­ba ebelele. Ke
ngendawo yezikolo nelali nokumiselwa Imantyi siti ezonto zonke bekulula
ukuzenza bemi ezweni labo njengoko kuya kuba njalo kwa Gcaleka.
Eyotywala okunene ingake ingabi ninzi okwalo nyaka, kodwa xa kutiwa kuya
kubako abe Langu peshaya kwe Nciba siyazi ukuba iya kuhamba kunye nabo.
Ukuba inokutintelwa kona noko bakoyo ingaba ibinokuqandwa nangeneno ko
mlambo.

Sarili bese mahlatini ayina- kubanelisa abanye. Kucingelwa ukuba ke

ZIHLOBO zam ezitandekayo, bendingenamnqweno wokuba kubeko imini enje
ngale imiselwe ukukunjulwa, luluntu, kwixesha elide endide ndati ngalo
ndavunyelwa ukuba ndibe nesabelo esifaneleyo kulomsemsebenzi wokuhan
jsiwa kwelizwi—Umnqweno endinawo kupela ngowokumbulela nokumbonga u-Bawo
wetu wasezulwini ngenxa yokulunga kwake ukude andivumele ndibone idinga
lake lizaliseka kangakanana pakati kwabemi belilizwe abahlwelweyo
behleli ebunyameni; kuba noko iminyaka ema 50 ilitutyana elifutshane
lexesha, siti sakuyilinganisa into esasiyiyo kule minyaka imashumi
mahlanu igqitileyo isimo seli lizwe nento esiyiyo ngoku, sive sesitshilo
ukuti, “ U- Yehova wenze izinto ezinkulu, ewe ezingumangaliso, soti ke
ngoko sigcobe sivuye kuye.”

Ndinga ndingati njengokuba ndinelituba nje ndi- vakalise imibulelo
yentliziyo yam kubo bonke abazalwana abakulomsebenzi wokuhanjiswa
kwelizwj, ngenxa yobubele abate ngamaxesha onke bandenzela bona
ngentliziyo ezishushu. Andibalibali abafundisbetu bokuqala o—Mr. Thomson
no Bennie no Ross amadoda ekungafunekiyo ukude abalwe emaewecweni amatye
likunjulwe ixabiso lawo. Osapilileyo kuwo ngu Rev. W. Thomson kupela.
Bati bevuta kulangazelela ukunga bangakanyisela ingqondo yabemi beli
lizwe, babe benako ukutabata isando, nokuba yisara, nokuba lizembe
bazincede ngokwabo xa be kungeko luncedo kufupi. Iyaziwa lento, yabe
imdumisa lomfundisi wase Lovedale, yokokuba wati xa kwakumiswa izakiwo
zokuqala kule Simnara, bakuba bete abancedisi bake abamnyama bonke
babaleka, wasuka wawemba amatye, wawabeka enqweleni, watabata iziniya
wabexesha wasa endlini, ukuze umsebenzi ungatinteleki. Ndaye kananjalo
ndingenako ukubalibala abapati bokuqala be Glasgow Missionary
Society,—inkoliso yabo bagqita mzuzu, baya kwamkela umvuzo wabo, nditeta
o—Henry Knox, Patrick Falconer, Hugh Makay, John Cuthbertson, Hugh
Brown, W. Buchanan, A. Buchanan, J. Robertson, J. Brown, R. Brown, Rev.
G. Struthers, namanye amaninzi, amadoda abekwe kunene ngenxa
yomsebewawo. Abonwabanga bekolisiwe yimisebenzi ababe nokuyenza pakati
kwamahlwempu alahlekileyo ekaya. Lowo wasekaya umsebenzi awubanelisanga,
awubanga mkulu ngohlobo lokwanelisa ezo zicaka zika Tixo. Zazibanzi
intliziyo zabo, bezinikele emsebenzini. Ubushushu bentliziyo zabo
babenza ukuba bakangele ngotando konyana baka Africa abahlwelwe bekude,
ngokunga nabo bangafunwa bezizwe ekuboneni ukuba i-Nkosi u-Yesu Kristu
ngu Msindisi wabo. Babe pakati kwabokuqala abatumela uku- kanya kwe
gospel kweli cala lehlabati.

Ngo 4 ku December 1817 saliwela i-Tyume ngase zantsi kwe Dike eli.
Lendawo iyi Alice ne Lovedale ngoku, yasisinga oko kungeko noko inye
indlu ebonakalayo. Sifunzele kwela nxowa lidala le Lovedale, e-Ncera,
apo Mr. Ross no Mr. Bennie babegxumeko kona. Kute kwakamsinya emva koko
kwenziwa intlanganiso, waza u-Mr. Chalmers nam samiselwa ukuya kungena
ku Mr. Thomson e-Gwali. u-Mr. McDiarmid kwatiwa makahlale no Ross no
Bennie kulo Lovedale indala.

Nakuba u- Mr. Thomson wayete ngobubele obukulu wenza konke abenako
ukusilungiselela, kwafuneka kwakiwe izindlu, sangena ke senza
lomsebenzi. Yaye kananjalo ityalike (elitye lokusekwa lalibekwe ngu Mr.
Thomson ngo 1826) isakiwa. Niboneke ukuba umsebenzi wetu wawu pambi
kwetu. Kwafuneka sisebcnzise abantu belilizwe ukuze uqubeke
umsebenzi—ukuvuzwa bebenikwa amaso, amaqula nexina (intsimbi yezacolo)
imali yakwa mlungu oko ibingento yanto kubemi belilizwe. La maso
bebefike baye kuziwola ngawo inkomo kwelipezulu, ku­ba ngeloxesha
bekungeko mlungu wavenkile, kunge­ko nkatini. Kute kwakwaziwa ekaya
ukuba imazi yenkomo itengwa ngamaqula ambalwa, zati izihlobo zaya
kwizindlu zabatengisi zabuta onke amaqosha amadala ezinokuwafumana,
amanye emahle emakulu. Yazaliswa ibokisi ngezi zinto zokuvatisa
amanenekazi akwa Xosa. Kwafuneka ukuba ihlale igciniwe lendawo yokuba
lama ledi agqale kunene ukumila nebala lequla, etanda ifishini ezintsha
kwanje ngokuba amaledi ase France elumkele ibala nepatroni ye lokwe.
Kungokoke kwafuneka silumkele ukuti sifumbe into eninzi yempahla, kuba
yoti noko ike yafunwa, inyanga nokuba zimbini, bafune luhlobo lumbi
nabala limbi, silahlekwe sidleke ngempahla esukuba siyifumbile. Lento
yasibanga ukuba silivuyele ituba lokuqala lokutenga ngemali yamangesi.

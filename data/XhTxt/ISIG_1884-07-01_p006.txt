6 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA JULY 01, 1884

OBE IMALI YAKWA RULUMENI :—Ngo June 26, kubanjwe kwa Komani inene abati
ngu Coombes ebelingumgcini mali zololiwe ngetyala lokuba imali zakwa
Rulumeni ezikwixabiso le £2,000.

ICUBA.—Ipepa lamafama lase Katikati libika ukwanda kokulinywa kwecuba
kwelo limele u Daliwe. I Jamani elimi kwifama abati yi Klipplaat
lisand’ukufumana £300 ngecuba ebelililimile

AMAXESHA ALUKUNI.—Siqonda ukuba ulaulo Iwase Free State nolwase Natal
lukwakwisimo ekuso le Koloni ngokumayelana nokusilelelwa kuka Rulumeni
ezimalini zokuhambisa umsebenzi wokulaula.

NASE FREE STATE siva ngokwanda kwe ngqakaqa ebikwa e Saltpan, e
Jacobsdal, Beginseldam, Boshof, Cronstadt, Harrismith nakwifama ezitile
ezikwelo. Okwangoku isasebenzana nama Afrika akowetu.

IZIJOLI ZE TRANSVAAL.—Izijoli ze Transvaal ezaziwelele ukuya kuteta ne
Nkosazana ngemicimbi ye Transvaal ne Pesheya, nokufeza eminye imicimbi
kuvakala ukuba zinduluke pesheya ukubuya ngo 20 June.

ISITILI SASE DODOLORO.—Kutiwa imicimbi yokulima iyatembisa nonyaka,
kuyalinywa kakulu. Elinye lamafama apapileyo u Mr. Fred Halse uhlwayele
ingxowa ezi 45. Ingqele ike yawa nzima kweso sitili.

UBU BISHOP BASE NATAL.—Kuke kwako ulure e Natal ukuba kumiselwe pesheya
u Rev. Sir George William Cox, Bart, M.A., endaweni yobu Bishop obungena
mninibo ngenxa yokububa kuka Right Rev. Dr. Colenso.

UMANYANO NGEMFUNDO.—Amalungu elibandla acelwa ukuba apose amehlo
kwisaziso sika Mr. J. W. Gawler esisesitubeni sezaziso kwipepa lanamhla.
Inga amalungu angazikataza ngentlanganiso ati angamalungu ayo.

IMALI YEZINDLU.—Umgcini ndyebo wo Koloni uyazisa ukuba imali yezindlu
yalo nyaka imelwe kuhlaulwa nge 1 July. Ukuba kudlule intsuku ezimashumi
asitoba ingahlaulwanga, iyakumeuywa ngokwasemtetweni seikulile.

IGQIRA LASE NGCOBO.—Indodana engu Mr. J. B. Woolby, M.B., (Lond) ; M. R.
C. S. E. ; L. R. C. P., London ; A. R. C. uzakuba ligqira lesitili sase
Ngcobo; eyakuhlala kwa Ndabeni. Umsebenzi uwuqala ekupeleni kwale
nyanga.

IMPOSISO.—Kwi “ Mpawana ” zelokugqibela sati intlanganiso yamabandla o “
Manyano Ngemicimbi Yemfundo ” yobase Bofolo ngo 16 ku July. Ngesati
ngolwe-Sibini 15 lwalo nyanga. Inga umhlambi ungelahlekiswa yilomposiso
yetu.

I PALAMENTE iyivumele ukuba i Ruluneli ingayeki ukuzixasa izikolo
ngemali yoncedo Iwakomkulu. Kambe bekusoyikwa ukuba elibandla likule
ngama Bulu nje liyakumangala noluncedo ngokukodwa kwizikolo zabamnyama.

ILUNGU LE JURI ELINXILILEYO.—Indoda ebise jurini e Dayimani abati ngu
Clacey ufunyenwe enxilile xa kupulapulwa amatyala ngo 21 June, uvalelwe
bonke ubusuku, ngemini elandelayo wadliwa £7 mhlaumbi iveki
entolongweni.

NGO 19 June ngokuhlwa ama Wesile amhlope ase Rini ayenentlanganiso
yokubulisa u Rev. James Fish no Rev. R. Matterson abafundisi abanyulelwe
kwelo bandla yi Conference yokugqibela. Umhlali ngapambili yaingu Rev.
John Walton, M.A.

UKUDUTYULWA KWABAMNYAMA.—Kusand’ukuhla isihelegu kufupi ne Bloemfontein.
Amadoda amabini amhlope efumene ukuba igqiza elitile labantsundu
linemali, abambe kwaoko abulala mabini owesitatu amngxwelera. Ababulali
abo abakafunyanwa.

Uviwo LO TITSHARA.—U Dr. Dale ufake isaziso kwipepa lakomkulu ukuba
bonke abanga bangangena koluviwo ngo 30 September bazise ngapambi ko 1
August. Isaziso eso masixele (a) ubudala, (b) isimilo, (c) i “Optional
subjects” afuna ukuviwa kuzo lowo ufuna engene.

AMA MFENGU ASE NGQUSHWA:—U Mr. Rose-Innes unike, ngo Mvulo 23 June,
isaziso sokuba uyakundulula ingxoxo pezu kwale nteto :—Elibandla linga
imvo yama Mfengu atile ase Ngqushwa ekungxanyelwe ukuba afudu-kele
pesheya kwe Nciba, ingaviwa kuwo ngu Ruluneni.

UKUGXOTWA KOTYWALA E LUSUTU.—U Colonel Clarke e Maseru ubize isizi se
£20, mhlaumbi inyanga ezintatu entolongweni, ku Msutu otengise ibotile
ezimbini zotywala ngapandle kwesiqiniselo semvumelo (license), utumele
onongqayi ukuya kubamba umlungu we venkile ngokugqita kwalomteto.—Cape
Mercury (June 19).

ABAGUQULI BE ZIBALO.—Intlanganiso yebandla labaguquli be Zibalo ukuzisa
kwinteto ya Maxosa eyeyamashumi omane, iwuyeke umsebenzi wayo ngo 21
June e Qonce. Isapele ngo Danyeli ekuguquleni. Elandelayo iyakuba ngo 26
August, iguqule ime kwisahluko sesitatu sika Mika.

AMA MFENGU ASE NGQUSHWA.—U Mr. Rose Innes (ngo 17 June) ucele e
Palamente, wavunyelwa, ukuba onke amapepa, nencwadi zezipata-mandla zase
Ngqushwa no Rulumente mayelana nokufuduka kwama Mfengu ukusinga pesheya
kwe Nciba abekwe pambi kwe bandla. Into leyo ivunyiwe.

ISELA LEMALI.—Ilau libanjwe e Bobofolo kufupi nje ngokusuka libe £400
zenkosi yalo abati ngu Mr. Vander Merwe owaziwayo kulomacala. Ubasi
wayelele pantsi kwenqwelo imali yona ikuyo. Lifunyenwe linxilile seli
neponti ezimbalwa esiqwini. Oyilandisileyo inkosi leyo wovuzwa nge £20.

O DRINK !—I Free State Express ibalisa ukuba ngenkulu ingqele nemvula
intokazi entsundu yayilele ingenamandla emncenceshweni wesinye sezitrata
zase Bloemfontein. Amanzi ayebaleka; nokuba kwisimo esinjalo kwakwanele
ukubulala nabanina. Kanti ke apo e Free State utywala abutengiswa ko
Kafile.

INGOZI KULOLIWE.—Ngo 23 June kuhle ingozi kuloliwe e Naauwpoort. Itreni
yetroko ikululwe kwi injini yabuya ngomva. Isukele i injini kodwa ayaba
nakuzibamba ngenxa yokukauleza. Itroko ezintlanu zigubeke zonke zitwele
impahla ebisiya e Colesberg. Ezinye zinqandwe ngu Mr. Dalton i Injiniya
zasinda.

EZASE EGYPT.—Imfazwe isaliwa mandla pakati kwe Ngilane nama Arab amele
umandla we Egypt. Umprofite ka Mahomet abati yi Mahdi unomkosi wa 35,000
isinga e Dongolo, apo kuko imikosi yama Ngesi. Ikumkani yase Abyssinia
ivumile ukunika uncedo kwi Nkosazana ngoku- kulula isixeko sase Kassala.

IMPILO YOBU “KARVEYIRE.”—Imambane entsundu yatabata umtwalo oya e Skapu
ngo 17 May e Qonce. Kute xa isendleleni zana imvula kwabanda. Ilahlekwe
zinkabi yatshatyalaliswa yindlala. Incedwe yenye indoda ukuwungenisa e
Tarkastad umtwalo lowo emdle £7. Efikile e Skapu ufumene £8, wasala
naponti nye.

U HON. CHARLES BROWNLEE.—Yenjenje ukuteta ngelinene i Mercury (26 June)
: “ Abahlobo baka Mr. Charles Brownlee abakulo lonke eli bayakuti
kunubembe bakuva ukuba umelwe kusinga pesheya kanjako ngokunga- pili.”
Siva ukuba u Mr. Brownlee usinga pesheya ngale veki ivulelweyo
eyakungena ezinqanaweni ngama zibuko ase Natal.

KWA ZULU.—Usutu lumeyisile u Sibepu. Luncediswe ngama Bulu aku 400,
ekutiwa abulele into eninzi kwicala lotshaba. Kubulewe isitandatu
sabaninawe baka Sibepu ; yena ngokwake wasabela e Tsbowe. Amasodati
abese Natal atunyelwe kwindawo ehlala amehlo o Rulumeni wapesheya. Kuko
namare e Natal okuba indawo leyo izakubandakanywa ne Natal.

ABAPUMELELE uviwo lomteto nonyaka ngo—1. J. A. Foster, no J. A. Neser,
balingene ; 3. J. S. de Villiers; 4. A. A. Celliers ; 5. G. B. Reitz; 6.
T. E. Lawton; 7. E. Jones ; 8. G. M. Findlay ; 9. W. H. Longden ; 10. J.
A. Gibbs ; 11. W. Rose Innes ; 12. H. C. Hull; 13. H. J. Hofmeyr; 14.
Nathan L. Goldschmidt. Aba- bengene babe 16 ngokwe saziso se Registrar

UMPAKATI WE AFRIKA.—Iqela labantsundu abemi e Zanzibar (elunxwemeni
olulunge empuma-langa yase Mpakatini we Afrika) ligaleleke e Kapa ngo 8
June, livela e Kongo kunxweme oluse ntshona-langa Iwelo. Libika ukuba u
Stanley, um’pandi odumileyo, une 2,400 abantu abamnyama apo akona, ukuba
bayevana kanye no De Brazza. Elo lizwe kutiwa lelinye lamazwe amahle
nacuma kunene elizweni. Kuvakala ukuba u Mr. Stanley ubuyela kwase
Yurop: ubeke wahliwa licesine lelo.

AMAKRISTU EZIQITI ZASE FIJI. IZITI zase Fiji, njengoko baziyo abaninzi
zikulwandlekazi ekutiwa yi Pacific, oluse mpumalanga kwase Asia.
Zinomgama ongapezu kwemayile eziliwaka ngase mpumalanga kwase Australia.
Inani lazo zingapezu kwamakulu amabini noko ezimiweyo zikumashumi
asibozo nasitoba.

Pambi kokufika kwabafuudisi kona bekungeko bantu badlula abezo ziqiti
ngokudunyelwa kukukohlakala,—ubusela, ukuxoka, ukubandezelana, nokudla
abantu. Osukuba ebulewe kusiliwa mhlaumbi etinjiwe, mhlaumbi efike kona
engowase mzini ubeba onjalo selelungele ukudliwa. Ibiyinto ekoyo futi
ukuti abantu beziqiti zase Tonga ukuba batyalwe ngamaza olwandle baya
e-Fiji babe abasakubuye babonwe.

Ibiyingozi ke ngoko kowase mzini ukubeka unyawo kona.

Noko kwakunjalo baya abafundisi bama Wesile betembe uncedo luka Tixo.
Ngalonyaka wokuze baye kwabako imvuselelo enkulu e-Tonga, apo babese
benexesba besebenza; azakeamakristu ase Tonga alangazelela ukuba
elilizwi alamkeleyo liye nase Fiji. Alisa ke okwenene epetwe
ngabafundisi ababini abamhlope. Bakufika e-Fiji bamkelwa kakuhle kunoko
kube kulindeleke ngako, laza Ilizwi lenkosi lasebenza imimangaliso kona
kwangeminyaka embalwa, bati abaya bantu bebedume ngokukoblakala baguquka
namhla banobuhlobo ebantwini nabase mzini.

Okwanamhlanje asinxamele kubalisa imbali yokuliamba kwelizwi kona.
Indawo enkulu esinqwenela ukuyibeka pambi kwabaleseshi betu yile yokuba
Ama Fiji ate akulamkela Ilizwi lika Tixo anomnqweno wokulisa kwintsizana
ezise semnyameni. Sinomnqweno wokuba abalapa bayi qondisise londawo kuba
pakati kwabo akubonakali ukuzikanyeza okusondeleyo nokusondela kulento
yase Fiji. Apa akuko zingozi zinkulu ekuqubeni ilizwi kanti ke noko
bambalwa kangaka abazinikela emsebenzini we Nkosi, nabo abakazinikeleli
kuya kwizizwe ezigama—ngapandle kwamadoda ase Lusutu, esake sateta
ngawo, aya ku Banyai. Siti kuma Africa, i-Africa isahleli ikangele
amadodana namankazana aya kuxela la ase Fiji. Liya kuda

libe lide kangakanani na ixesha amakristu alapa esonge izandla besifa
abantu bakowawo? Lomzekelo walamadoda asibozo siyatemba ukuba uya
kucukumisa intliziyo zabaninzi,.bayipulapule inteto yawo eti, hambani
nani niye kwenza kwangokunjalo. Ezondaba siteta ngazo zivela E-Fiji
ziti:—

“Kuluvuyo ukuva ukuba ilishwakazi elisandukuhlela i-Fiji lokubulawa
kwabantu yinqakaqa, alibatintelanga abebefuna ukuya kushumayela Ilizwi
kwiziqiti ezinezidlabantu ezingakwi New Guinea, apo ama Wesile aza
kuqala isikolo esitsba. Ngalo June udluleyo umkombe wokutwala abafundisi
okutiwa ngu John Wesley, unduluke namadoda asibozo ase Fiji aya kwezo
ziqiti zezidlabantu. Ahamba epantsi kuka Rev. George Brown, umfundisi
okade ewupete umsebenzi, obekiweyo ngabo bonke abamaziyo. Kute pambi
kokuba unduluke lomkombe i-Governor yase Fiji yatumela ilizwi lokuba
ifuna ukuke ihlangane nalama Fiji ati aya kuba ngabasbumayeli. uwasile
okwenene u-Mr. Brown. Efikile i-Governor iwaxelele ukuba wona ngoku
angabantu bama Ngesi kengoko iyakubuzeka ngokumka kwawo njengokuba nawo
eya kupendula ngako. Uve kusitiwa abatyilelwanga kakuhle ingozi ezipambi
kwabo, ke ufuna ukuqonda ubunyaniso balonto. Utsho wabaxelela ubukulu
bobunzima abaya kubutwala nengozi abaya kuba kuzo, wazake akugqiba
wabacela ukuba bonke ngabanye abati bakuya noko kunjalo babale amagama
abo pantsi kwamazwi ati, “ Tina bazibale amagama, esizititsharizama
Wesile, siyavakalisa sinyanisile pambi ko Tixo ukuba besazisiwe
ngokuzeleyo ngabaqali kutunyelwa kwelizwi e-New Britain nase New Ireland
nezinye iziqiti, ukwaziswa ngengozi ezingasihlelayo ukuba siyaya,
ziveliswa kukuba abantu bakona bezizidla bantu, nokuba kanjalo lilizwe
elo elinezifo ezininzi ezibukali. Sibe sikuqonda ukubandezeleka esiya
kukufumana ngenxa yokusweleka kokudla esiqele ukukudla, nokungayazi
inteto yabo bantu siya kubo, kube kunjalo nje kuya kude kube zinyanga
sisbiywe sodwa singenamkuseli singenamxa- si: siya zisa ke ukuba site
sikwazi konke oku satyumba ukuya kulishumayela kona ilizwi. Sikwenza oku
ngeyetu intando, kungeko bani osinyanzeleyo, sisenziwa ngumnqweno
wokunga i-Gospel ku Kristu singaya kuyandisa nakwezo ziqiti.” Ate onke
lama-

doda asukela pezulu ukuza kubala amagama awo. Ate aya ezazi ezityilelwe
azicaciselwa ingozi ezipambi kwawo; kekaloku ayazi ukuba abamhlope
balizisa ilizwi e-Tonga nase Samoa nase Fiji kuko ingozi ezininzi; ke
namhla kokwabo ukusa kwabanye amatamsanqa abawafumeneyo. Bayazi ukuba
baya kwezikulu izidla bantu noko ke bagqibe kwelokuhamba, “ Boti ukuba
bayafa bafe, ukuba bayapila bapile.”

Kute ngalemini kuseleshwa ngayo kwake kwahlanganwa kwenziwa umtandazo,
babu liswa zihlobo zabo emva kwawo, wonke umntu esiti yanga intsikelelo
ye Nkosi inganabo —umtandazo esitemba ukuba baya kuwenzelwa kuzo zonke
indawo eyaziwayo kuzo into abayenzileyo.

CAPTAIN BLYTH.

Kwinteto ka Mr. Brownlee ku Mamfengu apesheya kwe Nciba, esiyi shicilela
kwi Sigidimi salenyanga, baya kubona abaleseshi betu ukuba u-Captain
Blyth, uza kushenxiselwa kwa Adam Koko. Sinomnqweno wokunga singake
senze amazwana ngomsebenzi awenzileyo kuleminyaka mitandatu inenyanga
ezine abehleli na Mamfengu.

Abasaziyo isimo sama Mfengu ukuwela kwawo baya kusinqinela xa siti
waungelula umsebebenzi owawubekwe pambi kwale ndoda, naxa siti uwufeze
ngohlobo oluncomekayo kanye. Ngexesha lokuvuleka komhlaba abantu
abesukayo apa babenendawo ezininzi ababezikangele. Abanye babeba namhla
baya kufumana ukuzipata benze abakutandayo, bahambe apo babonayo ;
abanye babebaleka irafu, into eninzi isiba namhla iya kukululeka
emsebenzini kuba bekusitiwa oko umntu uya kuvuna okukulu elime
kancinane, aze aneliswe kukudla esakote ipini lodwa ! lada nelozwe
ukubizwa kwalo kwatiwa kukwa Pini. Kwaye kuko kanjalo abatile abawelela
ukuya kulahla ilizwi lika Tixo nezinto ezisingisele ekukanyeni.

sasihambele kwelozwe nge 1867 ne 1868. Kwindawo ngendawo sahamba
sihlangana namadoda esibe siwazi ukukutalela izinto zokukanya apa,

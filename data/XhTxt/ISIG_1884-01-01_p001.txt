NYAKA OYOLILEYO!

IMICIMBI YE MFUNDO.

KUMAZWI angapambili sise site imicimbi yemfundo sibe siyi buza
nakubazali. Sifumene kubo ukuba ixabiso layo aba- kaliqondi bonke.
Abanye bayi dela baucamise, abanye basafumane bakohlwa, abanye bayi
vumile. Kwabayi fu- nayo abanye baubona umsebenzi kumadodana odwa ;
abazi ukuba eye ntombi ingenisa ntonina; kuba besiti intombi ngumntu
wase mzini.

Kula mazwi alandelayo siza kuketa indawo esibe sizipaula ezitetwa
ngabazah bezikalaza malunga nemfundo, ngoku- kodwa ezimalunga nokusolwa
ko titshala. Eyokuqala kukungahlali ndaweni nye kwazo. Asibantu
bakwaziyo ukunyamezela. “ Ngamatye” aqengqelekayo ; abantu enge- kutiwa
ngompahlana zise ntloko, ukuba bebenempahla. Omfumana apa nambla
wombamba kwelinye ngomso; mhlaumbi akakulala nokulala. Abazali
iyabalilisa lonto, kuba iti yakufika efikayo ibuye ibabuyise ngomva
abantwa- na, ikolise ukuteta kubi ngomsebenzi waleya imkileyo. Enye
indoda yaka yati bangatini na abantwana betu ukufunda xa o titshala
besebenza utrwi yedwa nje, kuko itole elaka lasinga pambili liqwaqwadwa
na ? Ukutsho tina asikalazeli bani, sivakalisa kodwa ukuba kulo lonke
esike salihamba abazali baya kala koku kurauzela kungaka kwenyawo
zotitshala.

Bati abanye benjalo babe bengati abananyameko emsebe- nzini wabo, bange
baquba kodwa ukwenzela ukuze bamkele

ukozo ekupeleni kwe kwata. Bati bakufika apo bakoyo bangakataleli
ukuqelana nabazali, bangakunoneleli ukuba- zama abantu abangekaqondi
ukuba bade nabo batumele abantwana babo; aze umntwana ongezanga
esikolweni bangakucingi ukumlanda, aze ukuba uyafa bavelane naye
nabazali bake. Basuke bange ngabantu abati tina asikatali nokuba
konakele siya kudlulela pambili.

Abanye otitshala basolwa ngokuswela kwabo imfundo, nangokungazi kwabo
ukufundisa. Abantu abadla ngoku- xela imfundo ye titshala ngabantwana;
kekaloku into engummangaliso yeyokuba abadli ngakuposisa. Abazali bona
abakwazi ukuyidumisa ititshala edelwe ngabantwana. Ukungafundisi kwayo
abazali badla ngokukukangela eku- tandeni kwabantwana ukuzifundela
emakaya, nase kugu- quliseni kwayo. Apo kukoyo kona into efundiswayo
bati angafumana abantwana bengaqutywa ukuba batabate incwadi; bengatandi
ukuposwa sisikolo. Enye into abakangela kuyo zincwadi abazifundileyo
abantwana, nexesha abalitabatileyo pambi kokukutshelwa kwenye.

Eyona nto ingapezu kwazo zonke ezi sizikankanyileyo ekalisa abazali sesi
sifo sidla ngokubehla otitshala, abase besiti sisifo setitshala. Beman’
ukwenza intsuka-ndihlala nje, umkondo wabo usala unamavumba amabi
kunene, abete wancola, wapants’ ukuruqula umsebenzi wabo kubantu abazal’
intombi. Kuko abazali esive ukuba intombi ezi bezikupa zingekabi
sexabisweni lokupuma nje kukoyikela eso sifo. Kweminye imilambo kuse
kufunwa ititshala ezi- ngamantombazana kuba kusitiwa awuzihli kangako
lomku- hlane. Singati siyi teta kancinane nje ke lendawo asi- kuko
nokuba inkulu pakati komzi, iwuxakile, asikamva oneqinga layo; mhlaumbi
loda liveliswe kwango titshala.

Enye indawo elilisa abantu imalunga nolutsha ekutiwa lufundile. Bati
alubazi abazali balo naxa lude Iwaqweba into ayigoduki. Ityala
lokutshitsha kwabantu abancinane lifunjwa pezu kwe mfundo ngokukodwa
ngabantu abayi tiyileyo. Nabebeyitanda bakala ngokuti ngoku bakohliwe ;
abanye sebefungele ukuti abayi kuba safundisa mntwana.

Kekaloku kwezi ndawo ndawo zonke asiyi kukupengulula ukuba bungakananina
ubunyaniso. Sizibeka pambi komzi ontsundu ofundayo ukuze ukangele iqinga
elingenziwayo ; kuba nokuba zingaba azinyaniso, kuyi nene kona ukuba
inkulu ingozi eyenziwa kukutetwa ngazo. Ukuba ngoko azinyaniso mayi
tyileke londawo : ukuba ziyi nyaniso mazi- lungiswe ezinokulungiswa.

IMPAWANA.

Unduluke e Kapa ngomvulo 17 December, u Hon. J. W. Sauer esinga e Aliwal
North nase Herschel. Eyona nto ayi- hambeleyo kukuya kubonana nabanyuli,
abamhlope naba- mnyama. balomandla. Indaba eke saziva ngamadoda akowetu
avela e Herschel zezokuba inkita eninzi ike yapants’ ukukohli- swa
lityendyana le Ngesi abati ngu Mr. H. T. Tamplin, oli- gqweta e Rini,
ngokuti mabanganyuli u Mr. Sauer namhla, kodwa yena. Asinguwo ’mangaliso
ukuba u Mr. Travers Tamplin afumane aba landeli kwimpi emhlope, kuba
yena isiqu sake ngum Sprigg wenyaniso ekungeko nkohliso kuye. Kambe
iyaziwa impato ka Sprigg yomntu omnyama; ayina- kunyanyezelwa bani.
Ebudengeni babo inxenye yempi entsu- ndu ite yakumva u Mr. Tamplin
yasel’ imtembisa ukuba iya kumvotela. Siva ukuba ngebemnike umhlana u
Mr. Sauer bonke abamnyama ukuba kungafiki Isigidimi sinodaba kwi- “
Mpawana ” ngayo kanye lembazolwana u Tamplin. Lufu- ndiwe oludaba siva
ukuba wonke omnyama ugqibe kweloku- xasa umhlobo wabantsundu u Mr.
Sauer. Heke, nto zak’we- tu zase Aliwal! Ize kungabiko nanye ivoti eya
kunikwa utshaba. Niwe nivuka nomhlobo wetu sonke ke u Mr. Sauer!

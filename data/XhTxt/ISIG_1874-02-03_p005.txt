ENGO LANGALIBALELE.

Graaff-Beinet, January 14, 1874.

Ndifuna ukupendula umfo wase Natal oti uku- lila nokuhleka kwake, ati
ubona umangaliso, ibo­kwe ilwa nendlovu. Nditi kuye akazina ukuba kuti
xa kuko izinjana zize zimdle umninizo kuba zingamazi? Unjalo ke
U-Langalibalele. Yeka ukuti ibokwe ilwa nendlovu. Into engelisenziwa
kukuba atandazelwe. Lento ihle ngobudenge; ingafaniswa nokuti inja
ingayazi inkosi yayo. Okunene U-Langalibalele usentlango, isiko aka-
lazi. Makowetu tandazani enkosini niti, inja iti ukuba ikwele pezu
kwendlu isikwe indlebe, iyekwe idle ubom.

USIZI.

Natal, December 31, 1873.

Baleseshi be Sigidimi Samaxosa, usizi endilu- bonileyo ngamehlo am
aluluncinane kolo Iwabonwa yi Government yamaNgesi E-Zanzibar. Nditsho
ngokubona usapo Iwa Mahlubi, luqutywa njenge- nkomo, abafazi,
nabantwana, luhamba lulila, aba- fazi belilela amadoda abo, nabantwana
belilela oyise. Ndiyakolwa ukuba U Tixo woluntu lonke, uyakukangela pezu
kwale nyembezi yezidalwa zake, avelise okuyakupelisa lenyembezi okuya
kwenza ukuba kuti endaweni yobubi kuvele ubu- hle. Esisono sika
Langalibalele, sati ngobukulu baso sangena emaxegwini, sakupumela
kubafazi nakubantwana, nasezintsaneni. Ewe bekufanele ukuba kubenje
kwelilizwe lase Natal; ngokuba amakos’ etu ahleli enovalo ngati,
lokungati saku- Iwa nawo. Anovalo Iwama Jebuta, kuma Hebere Nditsho
ngalomteto wase Natal okwatiwa aba- ntsundu mabangayitengi imipu.
Ngokoke U- Langalibalele ufele kulondawo, yokusuka atenge imipu, nesizwe
sake, apule umteto wamakos’ ake. Kwatike kwakuviwa ngamakosi angapezu
kwake, ukuba uyitengile imipu ayibiza waza yena wala nayo. Walizuzake
ityala.

Mandipeleke kulendima yosizi mandibekule yotuli. Ati amaxego elilizwe,
namaxegokazi alo ati elilizwe lase Natal, ezizindlu zalo zendonga zakiwe
ngotuli lokubola kwemizimba yabantu ababubela kulo. Atike ngelanga
lokupela, mininzi imizimba eyakuze ivele kwezindonga, zezindlu zalo,
ngokubulawa kwabantu ngu Tshaka, nayindlala, nazizilo.

Enye into tina E-Natal apa sihleli nabantu base Indiya ekutiwa nga
Makuli. Ababantu bayasihleka tina ma Africa ngokuti kwenza nganina ukuba
nibadle onyoko abananyisayo. Umbuzo uti kubo nitsho nganina ukuti sidla
oma betu? Basipendula ngokuti inkomo ezi ezinanyisayo azingabo onyokona?
enibuya nizidle, ukwahluka kupina pakati konyoko nozinkomo? Kunjalo ke
ukugxekana kwezizwe. Mandipele kuleyo.

OWASE NATAL.

UKUBUBA KUKA MAJAZA.

Pirie, 23 January, 1874.

Mandazise bazalwana ukuba ngomhla we 6 ka January, kubube U-Majaza umfo
ka Mtyanda. Wati beye entlombeni weva ngati kuko nto imlumayo pezu
kwegxalaba. Kwati ke kwakusa yesuka lendawo yati bungu-bungu ngokudumba.
Bati ke abantu bepi babepi ukufuna osiyazi. Basuka osiyazi qipu, abanye
bati yinyaniso ulunywe yinto. Latike elinye, yinyama makwenkwe (kwayeke
oko ikukuti lubende). Kwenyuka ukudumba. Kwaye kutiwa maka ngaseli masi,
wati kuba ebengumfo onenkani, wasuka roqo wasela. Kwada ukudumba
kwamvingca umqala. Wati engasenakuteta wavakala esiti tshiti ukubiza
umninawe wake, esiti uyabona na ukuba namhla ndiyapela? Akapendula
omnye. Kwati emva koko wapuma umpefumlo.

Lendoda ke ebingu mheyideni. Ibiyindoda eti xa abanye baya etyalikeni
ibe yona ihamba apo itanda kona inxila nokunxila nge Cawa. Yati londoda
isishiyileyo yakuba ibi vela emabulwini yauguqula umzi wenye ebingaqabi
yawenza bomvu. Ibisiti nakwabanye akuko nzuzo ekubedesheni U-Tixo. Ke
namhla uyekuzipendulela pamba kwalowo ebemdelile engamazi nokuba
ngubanina oko ebe semhlabeni apa. Lendoda ibe iyivuyele lejubili
izakubako ka Bev J. Boss, iselide yakupa neponti ekwakiweni kwendlu,
yakupa inkabi yebokwe ukwenza izidlo zejubili; kanti noko kunjalo iseza
kunqunyulwa nganeno kwayo. Ewe okwenene sipetwe, nokuba umntu
uyaziqaisa, yona I-Nkesi yenza apo itandayo. Akwaba siyakuti sonke
sikumbule ilizwi eliti “Lindani ke ngoko ngokuba ningayazi iyure nelixa
eza ngalo unyana wo Mntu.”

NTABA HOBO.

Du Toit’s Pan, January 15, 1874.

NKOSI yam kaundipe indawo yalomazwana alan- delayo. Ndingomnye
wabapulapuli be Sigidimi. Nditi hai ukuba mnandi ukuva indaba zamacala
ngamacala. Lento ikukufunda yinto etandekayo. Ibonisa izinto
ezingabonwayo ngabanye abantu. Ukutsho kwam umntu ongafundanga uyitabata
msinya into ; inqumbo yake ihleli ipezulu. Ubehle apendule engekakuboni
apo iyakupumelela kona into etetwayo.

Kuko amazwi atile pakati kwamadoda amane endinga ndingawapendula. Ati
wona kwase kuqaleni aselegweba. Ati amanye asimazi U-J. B. Philips ukuba
wafunda pina. Elomnye liti andikwazi nokuba ungu Mkristu kusinina; aze
omnye ati asazi ukuba wenziwa pina ibishop. Lamazwi ke onke abonisa
ukugweba nokugxeka. Ukuba ndibe ndiya kuwapendula bendingaqala ngelokuti
ubulumko bulunge ngapezu kobudenge; nelokuti ungagwebi ukuze
ungagwetywa. Akuko nto niyakuze niyive ebantwini kuba aniziceli izizatu
zezinto, anizitabati ngokwelizwi lika Tixo. Nisuke nipange ekugwebeni.
Ngenike nilinge ukuzipengulula izinto nizikangele apo ziyakupuma kona
nandule ukupendula ningaputuzeli.

Ku THOMAS KONZA wase Du Toit’s Pan siyazisa ukuba siyifumene incwadi
yake. Indawo ateta ngayo sasiyishicilele ngokuzeleyo JEsigidimini
Samaxosa, ngesingesi nesixosa: saza kanjalo sashi- cilela amapetshana
awodwa esawatumela kwindawo ngendawo. Akusafuneki nganto ukuba ibe
isakankanywa nanamhla.

UMBIKO KWIZIHLOBO ZOBUBILEYO.

Emgwali, December 27, 1873.

Inkosikazi yakwa Nkohla Falati iselikwabanga- sekoyo abaye apo siya kona
sonke. Yati isapilile yaxela ukuba ixesha lokundisebenzela liyapela.
Yandicela ukuba ndibize odadewetu ukuba baze kuyitabata, kuba indlu
esibe sikuyo ibe ingalunge kakuhle empilweni yayo. Bati ke bakufika
bemka naye, kwaza kwema njengenteto yake. Ama­zwi ake kum alilifa kodwa
andinakuwabala. Wandiqinisela wandikutaza kakulu kumsebenzi endikuwo.
Wati ndisebcnzile ndisapilile, mandilale E-Nkosini njcngentando yayo.
Emva kokuba ndadileseshe iculo lika David le 136, wandibuleia waza ke
wapuma umpefumlo.

Wayetshate ngo 16 ka December 1867 waza wancwatywa ngomhla we 16 ka
December 1873. Into enkulu eyambulalayo sasisifuba, ukukohlela, ihlaba,
intlanzo namagxa. Wayeuzalisile umteto wokuba indoda nomfazi boba banye,
bade bashiye noyise nonina. Wemka ebuhlungu ukuba zingabangako izihlobo
zake ake azibone, kodwa ke ndibe ndikadi ndibika. Namhla ke umkile
emhlabeni.

NKOHLA FALATI.

  St. Matthew’s Mission, January 27, 1874.

Nkosi Mhleli, ndihlaba umkosi. Kulahleke ihashekazi Emtwaku. Yimazi
ebuponi, enekolo ebunzi. Inebala enyongeni, oti xa ukude ube litshisiwe
kanti akunjalo. Elobala lingwevu. Ecaleni lasekunene linopau’; ekohlo,
litiwe qipu kabini endlebeni. Elihashe lishiye inkonyana encinane.
Beliye peza kuka Dontsa likwelwe yinkwenkwe, yati ibiliqoboshile yangena
endlwini, yapuma lingaseko. Uyakubatalwa olifumeneyo.

INTENGO EZIZA KUBAKO.

Adelaide, kuya kutengiswa inkabi zegusha ezi 4200, amahashe ali 12,

ezi 1700, iseyibo- kwe ezi 1300, inkomo ezi 7o, amahashe ali 17.

Brakfontein. Unamadam amakulu amatandatu, nezindlu nezitali.

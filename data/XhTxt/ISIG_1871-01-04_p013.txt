ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 4, 1871.

13

  UBUROTI NOKUKALIPA.— U-Mr. Jas.
  MacDonald wase Schietfontein usand’u-
  kulahlekelwa ngamashumi omane egusha.
  Bezibiwe ebuhlanti. Lamasela azibileyo
  sekumzuzu ehambahamba esenza oku-
  ngendawo. Ulandwe umkondo wafakwa
  E-Barbus Pan apo zafika ezigusha zace-
  tywa kona. Zafika ezintandatu zaxelelwa
  apo, wagqitiswa umkomdo wezinye waku-
  fakwa enkantini yase Philipton. Wati
  akufika apo umnini gusha weva ukuba
  amasela lawo aye E-Grasbult umgama
  ozimayile ezilishumi elinesihlanu, weva
  nokuba anemipu nento ezinje ngoziru-
  luwa. Waya noko kunjalo, ehamba ne-
  lawo. Wabona esemgama into zonta-
  ndatu sezirawule umlilo. Waselewawela
  wawapanga, imipu wayisa kwisibonda sase
  Philipton. Umzekelo walendoda ufanele
  ukukangelwa ngabafuyi abamana ukuti
  babelwa igusha zabo. Akabanga nako
  kodwa lomfo ukuwasa etolongweni kuba
  ebengena makamandela.

  IZIHLANGU ZENKABI ZENKOMO.—U-Mr.
  Samuel Wilson wase Graaff-Reinet ufu-
  mene imvumelo yokuba abe ngumfaki
  wezihlangu zenkabi zenkomo. Kutiwa
  ezozihlangu zihlala kakuhle kangango-
  kude zinge zavela nayo inkabi. Azenziwa
  zilingane ngobukulu, ke ngoko angati oye
  nenkabi zake kona, azifumanele izihlangu
  ngokulula, nje ngokungati uzifunela aba-
  ntwana bake.

E-LUSUTU.—Ezimvula zinileyo zibete

  amasimi ayimi gxobozo. Sonke isizwe
  sa Basutu, ngapandle ko Molitzane naba-
  ntu bake, sikoliwe kuku patwa ngu Mr.
  Austin.—Kutiwa abafundisi bama French
  maba nga buye babize mali ivela E-France,
  ngoku nqaba kwemali ngenxa yalemfazwe
  ikoyo.

  UVUMILE U-Waterboer ukuba u-rolu-
  mente amise izindlu zamatyala elizweni
  lake, agwebe intlobo zonke zamatyala.

  IBANKI EZAKUMISWA.—Impahla mpahla
  ezifunekayo ukuyaka lebanki seziko. Ite
  ikasikazi enkulu yentsimbi egcina ama-
  pepa nemali xa iwezwayo yawela ema-
  nzini ayatshona kodwa yadada pezu
  kwamanzi, yada yaya kuwelela nga-
  pesheya. Kwaka kwatiwa imali eyayiko
  ilahlekile. Eyona nyaniso yeyokuba kwa-
  kungeko mali apo. Akuko nanye into
  eyenzakalayo eyayikona. Zonke ezizinto

  siziva ngomntu owayeko ngokwake.
  Umgcini wale banki ngu Mr. G. M. Cole.

  U-MBANE.—Umbane usandukubeta E-
  Colesberg isixenxe segusha ezibuboya
  buhle zika Mr. J. von Maltitz. Umalusi
  wabetwa naye ngumbane akafa kodwa.
  waliva ukuba alilungelelene elinye icala
  lake kwada kwalixesha emveni kokuba
  ubetile umbane.

ndlu, wawucanda kubini umlenze wom-
nye, wada wakupatelela endlwini yokulala.
Wagqulisa kwalapa ku Mr. Meere. U-
Mr. G. Devenish wabetwa wafa kwalapo.
Kuqondakala ukuba wabetwa entloko.
Onke amalinga ayenokwenziwa okumnce-
da akasizanga nto. Isidumbu sake saya
kuncwatywa E-Beaufort West, ngolwa 30

J. G. Devenish imantyi ebisand’ukububa.
Ubudala bayo ibilishumi elinesibozo lemi-

UKUTENGISWA KWE NKOMO.—Ngolwe
13 ku Dee. bekutengiswa inkomo E-
Sinqenqeni. Ibisiti inye inkabi idle iponti
zonhlanu. Imazi ibisidla iponti ezine,
nezintlanu ; itokazi lenkomo libe ziponti
ezine ezinesihlanu sesheleni, mhlaumbe
zibene iponti. Site isipani senkabi esim-
nyama sarolelwa iponti zone inkabi inye.

OBALEKILEYO ETOLONGWENI.—Igqira
lomntu omnyama ebelifakwe etolongweni
yase Dikeni, kuba lalibe amashumi omane
egusha, zika Mr. Long, lite ngokunce-
dwa zihlobo zalo lazuza ituba lokupuma
etolongweni lizimele. Zite izihlobo zalo
zalinika ulugxa Iwentsimbi, zalufaka
ngentunjana ebiyekelwe ukukanya. Li-
luzuzile ke olulugxa lemba ngalo pantsi
komnyango. Elinye lamakamandela lifun-
yenwe kwa kufupi netolongo.

OTSHISWE KAKUBI NGUMLILO.—Kwati
ngolwa 25 ku Nov. inkwenkwana ka Mr.
Jan Hendrick Nienaber wase Kareen-
fontein, idlala nentwana yelawo, ecaleni
lembiza yesepa ibaselwe, zatsha ingubo.
Ute uyise akuyiva ikala, esemasimini,
waselebaleka esiya kuyinceda. Akahla-
nga abe nakuyinceda yada yapants’
ukugqibela ukutsha. Kuya tandabuzeka
nokuba yopila.

ISICOTO. — E - Rickertsfontein bekuko
isicoto esikulu ngolwe 3 ku Dec. Senze
intshabalalo emasimini nase zityalweni
zemiti. U-Mr. J. von Maltitz ube esa-
nd’ukuzoyisa inkumbi, ekubeni ebe sele-
ntsuku zimashumi mane anesine epetene
nazo. Safika esisicbto sawatshabalalisa

amasimi ake nezitiya zake, samshiya eze.

UKUVUZWA KWE MANTYI NGU AUG-
UST.—Isitanda mali U-August weza e-
ofisini ngemali yake ebisandukucolwa
ngabantwana baka Swanepoel. Kwatiwa
xa ayinikwayo makaze ape umntu oyi-
coleyo isheleni zonhlanu, wavuma oku-
nene. Usuke waselerola isheleni zontatu
ezine sikisipeni wayinika imantyi kuba

nayo ibincedisa. Wabokusuka eyala U-
Mr. Van de Riet wati yena makayitate.
Yada yatatwa lemali yafakwa eqageni
lake engaboni. Wemka ke yena evuya
engazi ukuba ikwa kuye.

  IKULU elinamashumi amatatu enqwelo
  zabanjezelwa ngeveki edluleyo kwelinye
  lamazibuko ase Gqili, kuba lalizele ngo-
  hlobo lokude nento zokuweza zingabi
  nako.

  UBUSELA.—U-Jim isicaka sika Mr.
  Stevenson, weziswa pambi kwe Mantyi
  ngomvulo. Kutiwa ube ihashi lenkosi
  yake ngokuhlwa kwomgqibelo ngolwe
  10 ku December, kwafunyanwa okunene
  ukuba unetyala, wagwetyelwa ukuba
  anikwe 25 ernivumbo ngekatsi. Kwa-
  nesika Mr. Lombard isicaka, sifunye-
  nwe sinetyala elinjalo, sohlwaywa kwa
  ngokunjalo.

  E-QONCE.—U-Mr. C. J. Ackerman
  obemi ngase Sheshegu, usand’ukububa.
  Nguye lamlungu besibalise ngaye ukuba
  ubetwe ngalomadoda amnyama agwetye-
  lwe inyanga zontandatu. Liti igqira
  ukufa kwake kubangwe nakoko kubetwa.

  INTO ELUSIZI.—U-Mr. John Trollop
  wase Stormberg, usand’ukubujelwa yin-
  tombazana yake, eminyaka mine. Le-
  ntombazana yayikapa umnakwayo omi-
  nyaka 12 beya kunqanda inkomo zin-
  gekude pofu ekaya. Mayelana nokusuka
  kwabo kwasekubonakala ukuba isipango
  siyasondela. Ite inkwenkwanale yam-
  shiya endulini udade wayo yaqinisa ukuya
  kunqanda inkomo, yati kuye ndiya kubu-
  ya ngawe. Sagaleleka isipango ingeka-
  buyi, ite iyafika yayingaseko intombazana.
  Ayabi nakubona nalapo ibeke ngakona,
  yaseliputuma ekaya. Kufunwe kwafunwa’
  yada yafunyanwa pantsi kweliwa seyifile’
  Ifanele ukuba yayilinga ukugoduka, yeye-
  la kona.

  U-MR. NEL wase Fort Brown obesiya
  kwele Diamond uhlelwe yingozi embi kufu-
  pi ne Aliwal. Kute xa banyukayo eqineni
  yapuka idesilbom yabaleka ngomva inqwe-
  lo, yaya kweyela emjelweni. Kwaye kuko
  abantwana abatatu belele enqweleni apo.
  Omnye watiwa jwi gama xa iwayo inqwelo;
  omnye wapantsa ukufutaniselwa zingubo
  nenxbwa zomgubo; owesitatu, inkwenkwe
  eminyaka ilishumi elinesine, yarolwa
  pantsi kwetyeya, yenzakaliswa kakulu
  entloko. Wenzakala kakulu nomxosa obe-
  bexesha ngokuwa enqweleni.

  UNYANA KA MAQOMA.—Uzwelitye un-
  yana wale nkosi ubeziswe pambi kwe
  Mantyi yase Cradock, enikwe ityala loku-
  nxila nokwenza inxolo emityinweni yako-
  na. Ube namapakati amabini, nabafazi
  bebabini. Uzwelitye uye ehamba nge
  gunya lokuti uyakusinda. Utetiswe yi-
  mantyi ngohlobo angasayikuza alulibale.
  Langati liyadamba iratshi lake kwaku-
  tiwa, woti ukuba ufunyenwe ejikajika

emzini apo ohlwaywe ngokusetyenziswa
kanzima.

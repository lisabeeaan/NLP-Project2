NGOLWESI-NE, MARCH 7, 1889.

IMFUNDISO YE NT0MBI.

UMSEBENZI ohanjiswa sikula sentombi sase Lovedale, awukolisi ngakuvakala ngokuti wonganyelwe ngowesikolo samadodana. Esinye isizatu singaba kukuba ixa eli lonke abantu bebengayazelele nto imfundiso ye- ntombi. Imfundiso yomtinjana ngoku yeyona ngxoxo inkulu kwelama Ngesi, yaye ibangwa kukuba amasoka ase England kubonakala ukuba angxamele intombi zase Amerika ayazinxwema ezase England, ngokudela ingqeqesbo entweni zendlu, nezipatelele ekwenzeni ikaya mnandi,—ukukululeka, ukupapama, uchayito, kumntu ekwazi ukucokisa indlu nokupeka. Njengokuba in gen we nje lengxoxo tina siyakolwa. Indawo yomfazi endlwini iyenkulu kunene. Ayi- gqitwa nayindawo yendoda. Intlalo-mnandi yendlu ijinga pezu komfazi, kwanjengokuba intlalo embi nesitukutezi endlwini ipumle pezu kwake Zaye zininzi izindlu zolutsha Iwakowetu engezihleli ngoyolo ukuba abafazi bebeqeqeshelwe ekwazini into ezenza intlalo yabatshatileyo mnandi; kanti ngenxa yokulambata kwale- ngqeqesbo zininzi izindlu zempi entsha ezingati zitshazile, zishwatyulelwe.

Kwasebunyameni ibiti ikula intombi ibe ifundiswa ngento zentlalo emzini ngunina namankazana akowayo; elixa oyindodana abetyelwa ngamadoda amacebo okuxelenga isonka. Ngokungabiko namhla kwemfundiso yolohlobo impi entsha yonakalelwe yintlalo. Yiyo lonto ungafika ihabilisaka zabanye zazal’umoya, yonda nge Ligwa.

Kungezizizatu esiyibekele ixabiso elikulu imfundiso yomtinjana. Kwingxelo yesikula sase Dikeni sentombi sifumene usizi sakufumana ukuba intombi zihanjiswa kwa- kumgaqo wemfundiso yamadodana —kujongwe kupela ekubeni zipumelele kwiziqibi zemfundo yencwadi nakwi mviwo ze Titshala. Sipaula ngodano ukuba into ezinje ngokulungiswa, ukucokiswa kwempahla yendlu; ukwenza intlalo yendlu myoli; kwanokupeka aziko pakati kwento ezifundiswayo. Kwi 122 yentombi ezibe zifunda kona ngo

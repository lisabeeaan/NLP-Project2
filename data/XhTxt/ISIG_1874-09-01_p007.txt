EBEKISWA KUMAKRISTU ANTSUNDU
NGEMVUSELELO YASE SCOTLAND.

Bazalwana, nditi akwaba nonke nibe ninokule- sesha ngomsebenzi
olungileyo ohambiseka kwelo- zwe. Eziveki zonke bangamakulu abangena
ere- menteni. Abadlayo kwisonka esipiwa ngu Kristu bangapezu kwabo
badliswa izonka ezihlanu. Noko bayahlutiswa. Usaqubeka lomsebenzi
E-Edinburgh nase Glasgow, nakwezinyo indawo. Uya hluma, usanda, usendela
ezintliziyweni zabantu, uxanda uxela umlambo okade uhamba ezingxingweni
ode wafika ecibini apo unokunaba.

Lamanene ase America okuhle ngawo oku ngoku ahambele ngase Node ye
Scotland. Sigqibe ukuva ngawo xa ase Aberdeen. I-Aberdeen leyo sisixeko
esikulu ngokupindwe kabini kune Kapa. Kekaloku silindele amatamsanqa
amakulu kona kuba abantu balapo kade bewalindele lomanene, betandaza.
Kwezinye indawo abake banentlanganiso kuzo, abantu abebeze kupulapula
bebede baba ngapezulu 20,000. Sebengamakulu atile abazinikele ku Yesu,
amadoda, nabafazi, nabantwana.

Makuzeke kungabiko abati, “akwaba ndibe ndise Scotland apo kuko
okurjalo.” U-Tixo akayitandi I-Scotland ngapezu kwe Africa. Nawe
selehleli elungele ukukusikelela. Ukuba ke uyalifuna itamsanga kufuneka
ute wenza into ezimbini Tandaza, ulisebenzele. Nase Node ye Scot­land
liwahlele kunene itamsanqa, amadodana. Ngesingati na nati sicelele awetu
amadodana ukuba asikelelwe? Kunganjanina ukuba siyakuti tina sonke esiya
kulesesha lencwadi sitandaze imihla yonke, kusasa nangokuhlwa,
sitandazele amadodana ase South Africa, siqale ngokusa kwe Sabata
ngomhla 20 ka September? Anga angati axelelane onke amakristu, kuti kuzo
zonke indawo apo abedesha kona angalityalwa emitandazweni, sikangele
ukuba wotinina U-Tixo.

Enye into entle E-Scotland kukuba lemvuselelo ibangele ukuba kusetyenzwe
kunye ngamadoda amacala ngamacala. Nati singenjenjalo. Maku-

ngabiko oti mna ndingum Wesiti, mna ndingu Mrabe, mna ndingoweyase
England ityalike. Sonke singa I-Africa ingafumana ukukanya kwegospel.
Masiti ke sonke sitandazele amadodana ukuze afumane ubidumko bapezulu
nokuze abaninzi bawo babe ngabafundisi belizwi. Kunganina lento kumagama
aberemente, sifumana emaninzi ngapezu kwawododana, awentombi nawabafazi?
Ewe kulungile ukuba abe lapo amankazana, kodwa bekungalunga ngapezulu
kungeko osileleyo, U-Kristu akalungele bodwa, akaketelwe bantwana
nabangati abanakuqonda kukulu; ngowazo nezilumko. Amadodana ase Scotland
akayilibali into emmandi ukuba ku Kristu. Nawase Africa angafika
kunjalo.

OWENANI LENU.

UMPAKATI WE AFRICA.

Njengoko baziyo abaleseshi betu umpakati we Africa usahleli ebumnyameni,
nase budengeni, nase konakaleni okungenako ukufezwa ngamazwi. Abantu
bakona akuko nto zokukanya bazifumeneyo niengoko banjalo abalapa.
Alikafiki kona ilizwi lokukanya. Abantu abanalo nabona balikutaleleyo
ukulihambisa elilizwi abakalazi kakuhle elozwe, akukululeke kakuhle
nokuhamba kulo ngenxa yokuba izizwe ezikona zisilwa futj. Enye indawo
yeyokuba seziqele ukubona abasukuba beze kufuna amakoboka, abamana
ukuzitelekisa bezipanga. Sekute ngenxa yalondawo zangenelwa kukundwebela
omhlope, yazake londawo yakunqabisa ngakumbi ukuhamba kokukanya ukuba
kude kuye kufika kona.

Amadoda asukuba ezamela ukulusa nakona olu- kanyiso afanele
ukungalityalwa siti balapa nokuba wona seledlule emhlabeni. Njengoko
baziyo abaleseshi betu, kuwo onke amadoda aseke azamela ukwazisa
ngompakati we Africa nabantu abakuyo akuko ufike ku Dr. Livingstone.
Yena impilo yake uyi citele kulomsebenzi, wade wasuka wafela kuwo.
Ngenteto zake ngobukoboka obutintele ukunyuka kwabantu kuvele iqinga
lokuba kutunywe nga Mangesi U-Sir Bartie Frere ukuba aye kuwucita
lomsebenzi E-Zanzibar. Ngenxa yomsebenzi wake ubangele ukuba kutunywe
amadoda ngamadoda aya kumfuna, ate ngokwenjenjalo aba nokubona izinto
ezininzi eziya kuba luncedo kwabeli lizwe; Le ubangele ukuba abamanye
amazwe batande ngakumbi ukuwenyusa ama Africa. Okwangoku sifuna
ukukankanya izinto ezimbini ezisingisela kwelocala.

Eyokuqala yeyokuba pesheya kufunwa ukwenziwa into eyakuba sisikumbuzo
somsebenzi walendoda, ukubonakalisa ukuba uhlanga Iwakowabo luyambulela.
Abanye bati makutshatyalaliswe umsebenzi wokutengisa ngamakoboka
kubayileyo eyona nto ebezamela ukuba kufezeke yona. Elinye iqinga
lokumisa isikumbuzo salondoda liveliswe ngu Dr. Stewart walapa
E-Lovedale. Kwinteto etile abeyenza pesheya kwintlanganiso yabafundisi
ute yena indlela eyona ilungileyo elingagcinwa ngayo igama lika Dr.
Livingstone kungaba kukwaka isikolokazi esiya kubamba ukuba ngumzi size
sibizwe ngokuba yi “ Livingstonia.” Uti inga ingaba yindawo apo
kuyakufundiswa kwabantsundu imisebenzi, nento zokukanya, nelizwi lika
Tixo. Indawo enjalo ingenele kugcina igama lika Livingstone kube kupela,
ingaba luncedo olungenakubaliswa kwabantsundu ngokwabo. Kakade ukuqala
kwayo ingake ideleke ize iye ihluma ngokuhluma. Apo ati ngeliba kona
kungakweli cibi kutiwa yi Nyassa kufupi nenxanxasi ye Mur­chison.
Asikuko nokuba kuyasivuyisa ukubona ukuba baninzi pesheya abati eliqinga
lika Dr. Srewart asikuko nokuba lilungile. Kuyakusivuyisa ngakumbi
ukubona lifezeka. Kwamanye amapepa sibona kusitiwa ngekutunywa U-Dr.
Stewart ukuba aye kuwuqala lomsebenzi. Elase Rini ipepa liti ukuteta
kwalonto, “ Kungati kuke

kwatunywa yena ingaba ilahlekelwe kanye I-Love- dale nayo yonke I-South
Africa.” Sitsho nati.

Pambi kokuba siyiyeke lendawo singake sika- nkanye ukuba kuvakala ukuba
usapilile apo akona nomsebenzi abeuhambele uqubeka ngokutandeka- yo

Eyesibini indawo esiti iza kusingisela ekunyu- seni umpakati we South
Africa yeyokuba U-Mr. Henry M. Stanley uza kubuye atunywe pakati E-
Africa. Lomfo ngulo wayetunywe ukuba aye kufuna U-Dr. Livingstone oko
bekungaziwa nokuba usapilile sinina. Ngoku uzakuya kukangela ahambe
ehlola indawo eziquba umsebenzi wokutenga amakoboka, aze kanjalo aqube
umsebenzi ka Dr. Livingstone. Akuyikuba sabaxesha lide pambi kokuba
anduluke. Ukuqala kokuya kwake E-Africa U-Mr. Stanley wabe tunywe ngu
Mr. Bennett umnini wepepa ekutiwa yi New York Herald. Ngoku utunywa
kwangulomfo kunye nomnini wepepa ekutiwa yi Daily Telegraph.

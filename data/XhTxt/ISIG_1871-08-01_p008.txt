NDINGU, K. B.

UBUTO LWASE NCEMERA

(PEELTON).

NGOMHLA we 5 ka July kwakuko intlanganiso E-Ncemera yokuteta ngokwakiwa
kwetyalike en- tsha. Baba baninzi abantu, kuko neqela elikulu lamaqaba;
benzelwe isidlo esikulu, kuxelwe inko- mo zambini. Ekumkeni komhla
abantu baya etyalikeni kwaza kwaqalwa ngezikonzo. Emva kwazo umfundisi
wakona, U-Rev. Mr. Birt, waxela into eyavela kwintlanganiso yabantu
bakona ebisand’ukubako, ekwati kuyo kwarolwa imali nempahla ezingati
ziqukiwe zifike kwi ponti ezi- mashumi asixenxe anesibini (£72).
Kwalandela iziteto nezibuto kuncedisa namaqaba. Abanye batembisa impahla
abanye barola imali. Izibuto zalomini zazimi ngoluhlobo :—Eyarolwa
kwaoko yayizipomi ezimashumi mabini anesibini; eya tenjiswayo iziponti
ezi mashumi matatu anesi- hlanu nesheleni ezimbini ezinesikisipeni
ngapa- ndle. Inkomo zine. Igusha nebokwe zima 83; inxbwa zokudla zili
17; kuko nehangu nenkuku. Ixabiso lempahla lifike kwiponti ezimashumi
mane anesitatu (£43). lyonke into eyabutwayo ifike kwikulu leponti
(£100). Impahla iya kute- ngiswa ngomhla we 11 ka August E-Ncemera apo.

UTYWALA.—Esidakasini E-Somerset East ku- sand’ukulwa amadoda antsundu.
Ayepetwe ngu Mzwelitye umfo ka Maqoma. Abanjwake afakwa etolongweni. Ati
akurola imali, yati Imantyi namhla andinga mali, ngokuba niti nisona
nibe nitembele ekubataleni, ati nongenayo akutshwe zizihlobo zake.
Agwetywake ukuba indoda ihlale inyanga etolongweni. Umqali walempi
kwatiwa yena zoba mbini inyanga. Isiqaleko salento babu butywala.

UMNTU OBULEWE YINGWE.—Kuvakala ukuba ngomhla we 8 ka July ate amaxosa
akumakulu amatatu namane apuma inqina E-Xesi ngase Dubie Drift. Ate xa
asehlatini omnye wawo wagagana nenkunzi yengwe. Ute esayibete kwa kanye
yase- litabata intlonze yentloko yamgquma amehlo ngayo. Ate nakuba ehle
afika namanye amadoda awabi sabanakumceda nto uwabo lowo, nengwe leyo
yabaleka yasinda. Itwaliwe ke londoda ye- nzakeleyo yesiwa kumzi
okufupi, yaqaukela kona emva kweyure ezimbalwa. Iteke inqina yaku- pinda
ukuya yabulala inkunzi yengwe, nemazi, namatole amatatu.

INGQAKAQA.

NAKUBA kudume ukuti E-Bayi kuko ingqakaqa, akukabiko mntu wumbi uke
wahliwa ngapandle kwalowa wase Kapa. Kwimikombe emibini egqi- beleyo

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

UKUBIWA KWE GUSHA.—U-Prince Jacob uku- vumile ukuba ngomhla we 10 ka
January wayiba igusha ka Mr. Horrace Plante umfuyi wase Assegai Bush.
Ugwetywe ukuze ahlale etolongweni um- nyaka onenyanga zontandatu
esebenza kanzima.

UBUSELA.—U-Tili Kobas umfana we Mfengu ukuvumile ukuba ngomhla wa 22 ka
June weba intambo yehashe, inxowa yetapile, izinto zika George Johnson
wase Rini. Ugwetywe ukuba ahlale etolongweni umnyaka onenyanga zontan-
datu esebenza kanzima.

OBULEWE YI MBABALA.—U-Joe ka Matiwana umfo ka Lubambo inkwenkwe yase
Sheshegu yahlatywa yi mbabala ngomhla wa 20 ka July, kuyiwe enqina.
Kwagagana ehlatini yazake yam- kupa iliso. Wabuya wagoduka, wahlala
intsuku zone waqauka ngolwe sihlame.

UKUDLWENGULA.—U-Swartbooy, Umxosa, uku- vumile ukuba ngomhla we 10 ka
March wayenxa- mele ukumdlwengula U-Fingewane intombazana entsundu yase
Southwell. Ugwetywe ukuba abetwe imivumbo ema 25 ekungcneni kwake, aze
ahlale umnyaka onenyanga zontandatu esebenza kanzima; kuze ekupumeni
kwake abuye abetwe imivumbo ekwama 25.

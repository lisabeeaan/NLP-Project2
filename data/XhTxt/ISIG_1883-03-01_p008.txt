IRAFU ENTSHA EBIZWAYO YONYAKA WE 1883.

  NGOKU-KWENJENJE kuyaziswa ngo Mteto we 10, wo 1864 no Mteto we 9 we
  1858,—
  Ukuba, kwintlanganiso yama Cebo omandla wase Qonce, eyabingo February
  9, 1883,

IRAFU YENDLELA yonyaka we 1883, exabiso likwi (3/4) Ifalintyi ezintatu
epontini,
lamiselwa ukuba lihlaulwe pezu kwayo yonke Impahla enokurafelwa.

Le Rafu iyakufeza lamatuba:—Indlela ezinkulu (1/6d.) Ifalintyi enye;
Indlela-ndlela ezipakati kwesi Qingata (1/4d.) Ifalintyi enye; Ukuquba
umsebenzi wesi Qingata (3/4d.) Ifalintyi enye. Lemali ke iqalwa
ukubizwa, kuya kulindelwa ihlaulwe kanjako, kwi Ofisi yama Gqugula esi
Qingata

Ngo 23 ka MARCH, 1883.

  Ngomyalelo,

H. T. FULLER, Umbali-Micimbi.

E-Ofisini ye Gqugula,

E-Qonce, February 13, 1883.

Isiqendu sa 40 so Mteto we 9, wo 1858 siti:—Xa lite i Gqugula lesi
Qingata labika epepeni lakomkulu (Government Gazette) umhla eti imali
emiselwe ngokomteteto ukuba ihlaulwe ngawo, yimfanelo yabo bonke abantu
abamelwe behlaule, ukuba bayiblaule imali leyo kosakuba emiselwe
ukuyamkela, ngomhla, mhlayimbi ngapambili komhla, ekumiselwe ihlaulwe
ngawo, hleze babe zicenge zokunyanzelwa ngomteto ukuba kufunyanwe imali
leyo.

Ukuze ke izimamhlaba ezintsundu zingangeni ezindlekweni ezinokunxwenywa,
malungana nesi saziso sandulelayo,—Kuyaziswa ukuba wonke ubani onempahla
emiyo (enje ngomihlaba nozindlu,) nokuba umntu umi kuyo ngezinye
indlela—imfuyo enjalo ke yemelwe ukuhlaulelwa imali yendlela—ngalo lonke
ixesha lemfuyo imiyo encwadini exela ixabiso lemfuyo, umelwe kuyihlaula
ngokwesaziso sokuhlaula irafu enjalo. Umnini mhlaba, kwa nomqeshi
banokupendula xa zeke imali le ingahlaulwanga njengemfuneko.

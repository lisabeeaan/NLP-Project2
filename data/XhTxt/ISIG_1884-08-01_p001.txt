UDODANA OLUFUNDISIWEYO.

SIYAZI ukuba uyakubulelwa nguwo wonke umzi wakowabo u Eev. ELIJAH
MAKIWANE ngenteto yake entlanganisweni yabafundisi bamabandla
ngamabandla eyayise Qonce ekuqalekeni ko July. Ingxoxo eyayipambi
kwentlanganiso yayiyeyokuba selililona ixesha eli lokuba abantsundu babe
ngamagqira. Ingeniswe ngu Eev. Dr. STEWART ngomoya oncomekayo,
nangohlobo olubonisa wonke ukuba into abeteta ngayo uyivelele macala
onke. Izigqibo alike kuzo asizezinga gxekwa bani; sitandabuza ukuba kuko
maqinga wambi malungana nalomcimbi wokufundiswa kwamayeza ase mlungwini
abamnyama kuna lawo aveliswe ngu GQIRA.

Kodwa kuko ndawana ekuqutyweni kwengxoxo ezimane ukuvakala—ezi azive u
Mr. MAKIWANE emele ukuzipaula. Kubeko kwiqela labafundisi abamhlope
umoyana obonakalisa ukudaniseka kwabo zinto ezibonakala kumhlambi
wamadodana afundisiweyo. Abafundisi balila ngokungabinto kwalomhlambi,
ngokuzaliswa kwawo liratshi nangokuswela kwawo intelekelelo nengqondo
enzulu. Okukuba nje kubange ukuba abafundisi bafike kwisigqibo sokuba
amadodana antsundu afundileyo awakafanelwa zindawo angati kuzo asebenze
ngapandle kweliso lomhlope owongameleyo ukuze into yenziwe ngokukolisa
umxelo. U Mr. MAKIWANE ufumene ke ituba elihle—entlanganisweni
yabafundisi beramente ngeramente—lokuvakalisa eyona nyaniso ngododana
olufundisiweyo. Kwinteto yake siti, Amen, —Amen amaxesha alikulu, uti
umfundisi into ka MAKIWANE (au, ngeba siyabonga ke apa ukuba besinaso
eso sipiwo) kubafundisi abamhlope, “Pambi kokuba ndihlale “pantsi ndinga
ndingenza lamazwana ngemfundo yonke “yabamnyama. Ndikolwa ukuba isipako
emfundweni“ yabantsundu sibekwa kwindawo engeyiyo. E Lovedale “apo
bendikona iminyaka elishumi ndinokuti ababaninzi “abamnyama abahambe
batityi emfundweni, kwabo ke “batetyi ungelifumane eliratshi kutiwa
banalo; yinto “kodwa ebalulekileyo le yokuba bonke aba bayihambisa
“ngokutetyi imfundo basemsebenzini, abanye ungabafumana bengabashumayeli
be Ndaba Ezilungileyo. Angati “ke uninzi Iwabo bafunda ngokunobom
abawucekeci “umsebenzi. Usiya apo basebenza kona uyakum’fumana “omnye
epete umhlakulo omnye ebambe ipuluwa

“ . . . . Ndibona mna isininzi sifumene uncedo kwakwezi “zifundo kutiwa
zisitshabalalisile.”

Asingi singabuya seleke amazwi kula omfundisi u Rev. E. MAKIWANE ngapaya
kokuvakalisa um’bulelo womhlambi wakowabo ngokutyila indawo ababutuntu
kunene abahlobo betu abamhlope ukuyiqonda.

IMPAWANA.

I-Palamente iwuyeke umsebenzi wayo ngolwe-Sine 24 July ekubeni yayiqale
ukuwuhambisa ngo 1 May 1884. Ayigqibanga zinto zibalulekileyo
ngokumayelana namasiko. Umcimbi omkulu obupambi kwayo ibikukuzama iqinga
angati ngalo u Rulumeni afumane imali zokuhambisa umsebenzi wolaulo.
Zifunyenwe ke ngokunyuswa imali emelwe kuhlaulwa ku Rulumeni ekufikeni
kweli lizwe kwempahla yonke evela kwamanye amazwe. Yiyo lonto ke namhla
ibokisi yemicinga yomlilo idla ipeni, inye, nakwenye impahla iyakuba
kwayilonto. Utywala nabo bubizelwe imalana, kodwa ayininzi njengokuba
bekulindelwe.

Umfontsundu ligama lepepalesi-Xosa eliyakuze libonakale kwa Daliwe.
Umhleli walo,nopeteimicimbi yonke yalongu Magna ogama like langu
Ndaba-Mlonyeni kubafundi bezi “Mpawana” kutshanje. Asingi singavakalisa
mxelo wetu ngalo ngendawoyo kuba seke samana ukurwexana no Magna kuzo
ezi “Mpawana” angati ke sizityande igila saza asancoma senziwa
kum’monela. Kambe iyaziwa yonke imbali ka Magna ngabafundi betu.
Elityendyana lizakuba ngu Mhleli namhla leliya beke lingo. David Thomas
lamisa ihotele e Monti esadloba sahamb’apa sakuva ukuba nati sizakuba ne
“Kaya Lendwendwe”—tina balalelwa ngamaqwa bakuya ezidolopeni bekohlwe
yindawo yokubeka intloko. Ngu Host David Thomas lowo ke lonamhla uzakuba
yi Editor. Paula mfundi: Eli lizwi u “host” le lesi Ngesi eliteta
“Umlungiseleli wendwendwe.” Lencazo ke yanele ukuncedisa ukuba nimazi u
Mhleli wendaba zenu omtsha.

Ngo 17 July kwabako inteto e Palamente ngomcimbi wokupatwa kwe Lokeshoni
Inteto leyo yeyayibekiselele ekwakiweni kwelali zabamnyama emihlabeni ya
Belungu; kucikidwa indawo zokumelana kwabantu abemiyo kwimihlaba
enjengale no Rulumeni. Kwade kwafikwa kwindawo yokuba kufanelekile na
ukuba abantu bamiselwe imantyana zoku bapata. Amanye amalungu ate
akufanelekile ngenxa yendleko ebangwa zezi mantyana kutiwa ngabahloli
belali. Ilungu lase Dikeni u Mr. Innes ute “Indleko ibingancipa kakulu
ku Rulumeni ukuba kunyulelwe emisebenzini yobu nobala amadodana
afundisiweyo napapamileyo antsundu ukuba awenze umsebenzi lowo nge £100
ngonyaka kunokuba wenziwe ngabamhlope nge £250. Sebeko abamnyama
abangamakumsha, nababali, namadindala, nababambe eminye imisebenzi
abanika inkoliseko enkulu kuyo njengokuba amalungu abekekileyo esazi.”

E-Bayi u Molweni Mvoko unyana ka Mfengu—unikwe inyanga ezilishumi
elinesibozo (18) ngokuba i watch ka Mr. Gaimes exabiso liyi £45 intwana
encinane ye golide, kunye nenye exabiso liyi £10 kwa yama ledi, kwa Mr.
Symes. Lom’fana yintwana ebudala bukwi 17 intwana endwebe kunene. Enye
yezi watsha yayishiya kwa Mahomet um Arab ukuba imele ityalana lake
lokutya, enye wayitengisa nge 3/ ku Allen Marson umgcini Coffee-shop. Le
yileyo ye £10. Ute akubanjwa wazixela apo zikona. U Marson lo
wayitengayo enye ulinde i Jaji ngetyala lokwamkela impahla eyazi ukuba
ibiwe. U Mahomet ukululwe ngesizatu sokuba ebengayitengi lo watsha. U
Molweni uneqinga lokungena ngomnyango opambili ange utunyiwe, kuti ukuba
kuko umntu obuze into ayifunayo afane abize gama limbi. Ukuba akukomntu
umbonileyo wosele poseka kwenye ye room apume ne watch. Uyazazi apo
zidla ngokubekwa kona ezama ledi, kuba litola lase Bayi. Ngo August 1882
wanikwa inyanga zontatu ngokuba kwa i watch. Siyamvela u Betsy (unina)
umfazi okutele kunene. Wanga u Mfengu (uyise) angashenxa kula Nkantini
yakwa Nkwenkwana.

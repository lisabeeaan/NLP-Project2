ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1881.

KWA KUBA FUNDISI BENKONZO NGE NKONZO.

BAZALWANA BAM :—Ndimangele.

Nditi ndigwebeleni nindifundise indlela yemitshato yetu ba Kristu aba
ntsundu, ukuba ihamba pina kunolu hlobo endalumisa pambi kwenu, endandi
lubuza kuma doda asixenxe.

Ndibe lusizi ukubona impendulo ka Rev. E- J- Warner, neka Mr. M. K.
Mtakati kupela, kwinto ebendiyi qube pambi kwaba fundisi bonke bezindlu
ngezindlu, ndada ndacacisa ndati ngokwe njenje, ndi lungisela yonke
imihlambi yetyalike zika Kristu. Bendilindele ukuba Isigidimi sika
November—ngokwa kuti, sika Zibandlela siyakuza nemfundiso ezalisekileyo
kuma cala ngama cala. Ningaqale niwadele lama doda endandibuza kuwo,
kuba asafuna imfundiso apo isiko lamiswa kona ngaba fundisi abamhlope
okokuba imitshato yaba Kristu aba ntsundu ize ipatwe ngohlobo olutile,
kuba omtina bafundisi abantsundu, naba kokeli, naba shumayeli, sideliwe,
kutiwa ukudelwa kwetu ezizinto ziqalwa siti bafundisi aba ntsundu. Aba
mhlope bona noko bazibonayo sizenza ezizinto zikalazelwayo emitshatweni
abatetinto ngazo.

Kuba kuti, mhlaumbi abanye aba fundisi babe bukali ukuzikalimela izinto
ezisingisela ngasemveleni zobu hedeni, omnye angaqali azibone kuhle,
ngoko aziyekelele, kuze kuti ngoku hambelana kwe remente bafike ezizinto
zicaswe ngabanye aba fundisi, ziyekelelwe ngabanye kwi remente zabo.
Kanti bekungafuneki njalo. Ngakumbi kwezizinto zemitshato; bekufuneka
tina sonke bafundisi simise umteto ubemnye njengokuba sifundisa ilizwi
lika Tixo nga Bible nye, aze nowase Tshatshi, nowase Wesile, nowase
Rabe, nowase Moravian nowase Rhenish, nowase Berlin, nowase Free Church,
ngokwe

ndiselana oko bazi umteto apo uhamba kona.

Kanjalo ndimangele. Kuba bengayi endlwini ka Tixo, nasezi tyalikeni
nasemitandazweni xa kubizwa amagama abo emitshato. Ngako okoke, “
ukuyizondelela kwam indlu ka Tixo kundihlile ” utsho u Da­vid wakudala,
le eyenziwa ngamadodana etu, uninazala kwintombi zetu ibe nguyisezala,
kuba kuti mhla kuqalwa ukubizwa amagama omtshato, onyana nentombi zetu,
kunye naba peleki, nabapelikikazi, bangazibeki inyawo zabo etyalikeni,
bahote, kwenziwe elinye isiko lemvelo, lobuhedeni. Kanene ulutsha luti
bubub'anxa nokungabina ntloni, ukuba isoka, nentombi, baya etyalikeni xa
kubizwa amagama abo? Akwaba bayahlonipa ukwenza imibulo, bakangelwe
ngobub'anxa abenzi besono esibi kangaka! akwaba baya hlonipa ukunxila,
ukuba, nokuxoka, nokwenza obunye ubumenemene.

Ndafika komnye umzi, kwaza kungeniswa amagama omtshato, ndite ndabiza
isoka nentombo leyo, ndite kubo, ukuba anibangako etyalikeni
andiyikuwabiza amagama, ke ngoko ndifuna ukunibona nihleli pambi kwam
etyalikeni. Intombi yangena ifixiza ilila anditabatanga notice yayo
lonto, ndati ndiqala nkushumayela yase yonwabile.

Kanjako ndimangele kuba ziti intombi zetu zingayi nje etyalikeni kanti
zifuna udaka lutile, nomsizi, nemisobo ziziqaba zizenze onomanyamanyama.
Kangelani ezindawo ukuba zibekisela pina. Nditemba zicaka ze Nkosi niya
kuzikangela ngaliso linye zonke ezindawo.

Ikwandim omncinane pakati kwenu. Ndibulela kakulu impendulo ka Rev. E.
J. Warner, no Mr. M. K. Mtakati. Yiva nawe okude ndimangele,
ndimangalele indawo ezikwe lipepa.

St. Marks, 10 November, 1881.

P. MASIZA

INCEDISO ZEMFUNDO E LOVEDALE.

ABA Lauli besikolo sase Lovedale, bayazisa ukuba lemali, ixelwayo kule
nteto, yoku needisa imfundo (Scholarships and Bur­saries), yofunyanwa
ngabaya kuti bapumelele uviwo, oloba sekuqaleni kwe Seshoni ye 1882,
oloti kanjako, lubikwe usuku lwalo kwi Sigidimi esizayo.

I. THE GOVAN SCHOLARSHIPS.

  LIFA lomfi u Reverend William Govan, awati wanikela imali
  engapezulwana kwe £600, ukuba ize kuba luncedo e Lovedale kwi mfundo
  yolutsha olufundayo lwakwa Xosa, nabanye abemi kwaba ntsundu be
  Africa; ekuya kuti iziqingata ezibini

zesitatu zemali ekumiselwe ukuba isetyenziswe ngonyaka, zibe zezoku
kutaza umlisela, ize esesitatu simele abafundi abangu mtinjana, kwa
abangaba ntsundu abemi be Africa. Ezintatu kwezi zonikelwa kwabozi
fumana.

Eyamatanci exabiso li ... ... ... ... ... £12.

  Eyesibini (yomtinjana kupela)... ... _(t) ... ... 10.

Eyesitatu ... ... ... ... ... ... ... 8.

II. THE BROWNLEE BURSARIES.

  MALI eya kutshwa zizihlobo zomfi u Reverend John Brownlee, mhla wafeza
  iminyaka emashumi mahlanu emsebenzini wobufundisi e South Africa,
  inikelwe ukuxasa abafundi abantsundu abanga madodana kwi sikolo sase
  Lovedale. Imali enokufunyanwa nge 1882 izi £25 iya kwahlulwa kubini
  ngolu hlobo :—

Eyamatanci exabiso li ... ... ... ... ... £15.

  Eyesibini ... ... ... ... ... ... ... 10.

III. THE IRVINE SCHOLARSHIP.

IMALI emi okwe xesha, imiswa ngu John J. Irvine, Esq., M.L.A., King
William’s Town, inokufunyanwa ngoku pumelela uviwo, nanga lupi uhlobo
abafundisi aba Laula umzi abangatanda ngalo ukunceda imfundiso yododana.
Okweli xesha inokufunyanwa ngokwi qela eli pambili kwesi sikolo.

Ixabiso layo ngo 1882 ... ... ... ... ... £12.

IV. THE ECKHARDT BURSARY.

IMALI yomfikazi u Catherine Eckhardt, wase Lovedale, awayi shiyela
ukuxasa imfundo yabantsundu abangama Xosa, nama
Mfengu, nama Qeya. Enoku citwa nge 1882.

  Ixabiso li ... ... ... ... ... ... ... £9.

ngezi mviwo. Lungati mhlaimbi lumkelwe njenge baso, ekupeleni konyaka
koyakuti abe ugqibe kakuhle ixesha lemfuudo lemi nyaka emitatu e
Lovedale.

    :—

IV. Zihlaulwa ekupeleni kwe Seshoni nganye.

V.  Aku vumeleki ukuba umntu ade axaswe ngemali ezimbini kwezi, ngaxesha

Elipepa lishicilelwa E-Lovedale Institution mayela
kolokuqala enyangeni.

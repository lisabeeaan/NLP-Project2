ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 1, 1871-

I-KOTON.

INTSHAYELELO.

Ezi mfundiso ndiza kuzikankanya ndilinge ukuzi- xela ngendlela
ecacileyo, nelula. Kangangoko bendinako,ndizipepile zonke indawo
ezingabangela ukuba angaqondi osafundayo. Nakuba intengo yekoton
incinane kanye ngoku kwi marike yase England, ndiqinisekile ukuba kuko
iziqamo ezikulu eziya kuvunwa leli lizwe, ukuba kuya zingiswa ukulima
ikoton, kungati kwakuba zoyisiwe ezona zixakeko zikulu kuyekwe. Akuko
mazwe adlule la etu angase Iwandle ngokufanela ikoton. Into efunekayo
ukuze kubeko inzuzo ngayo, kukuba sibe nenyamezelo, nomonde, nenkutalo,
nembewu elungileyo. Xa kuko oko singamelana nabemi bane America.

UMHLABA OLUNGISELWA IKOTON.

Kwilizwe elinje ngeli ukuba notyani, ekunga- funeki ukuba inkomo
zilinyelwe, eyona ndawo ingamandla,—ekukucokisa ukulima, nokulima nzu-
lu—inokufunyanwa kalula kunakwamanye amazwe. Umhlaba olungiselelwa
ikoton kengoko ufanele ukuba ulinywe kude kube kabini, nokuba ku-
katatu, kuba inzuzo isekubeni amagada atyunyu- zwe kunene ukuze izityalo
zikauleze ukuhluma.

Ukuba kunokwenzeka makufunwe indawo esiti- leyo, apo ilanga linokuti
tya'ra kona ekupumeni, lide liye kutshona; kuba ikoton ngumti otandana
nelanga. Ndingalinganisela ngomhlatyana endi- waziyo, ekwakutyalwe kuwo
ikoton, eloluhlobo kutiwa yi Sea Island. Ngo October we 1870, wazuza
imvulana, napakati kuka November. Ayi- buyanga ine imvula kwada
kwangeveki yokuqala ka April we 1871. Ngezonyanga kwakungeko mvula,
azitshanga noko ezozityalo, zahluma kaku- hle, zatwala kunene, yabantle
kanye ikoton evunwe kulondawo, nentsinga zayo zaba yi-insh enehalafu.
Lomhlaba waulinywe kanzulu kuqala wapindwa- pindwa waza waba
mvungumvungu. Nditi mna imbangi yokuba babe abazuzanga abanye ababe-
yilimile ngomnyaka odluleyo, ibikukuti indawo abalime kuyo ingabi yiyo
efanelekileyo; baze ka- njalo balime ngapezu koko banokukufeza ekuhla-
kuleni, kubeke kusengomnyaka wokuqala, besayi- yavanya ikoton.

Ndanditeke mna kwase kuqaleni, makati umlimi xa asaqalayo alinge i-akile
nokuba zimbini, zinga- dluli esihlanwini, ukuze kuti ukuba ininzi imvula
njengoko bekunjalo nge 1870, angaselencama; ukuze kanjalo ide ifunyanwe
eyona ndlela ilungi- leyo yokupata ikoton, nokwenzela ukuba ihlume
kakuhle; kuze ngomnyaka wesibini kuzuzwe iziqamo kuwo lomti, kufundwe
ngawo. Umti we koton uqama ngakumbi ngomnyaka wesibini, na-
ngelandelayo, ukuba amasebe amadala ayanqa- nyulwa ngexesha
elifanelekileyo.

UKUNYULWA KWE NTLOBO ZEMBEWU.

Ukunyulwa kwe mbewu yindawo engeyilunyu- kelwa kanye. Kwabasaqalayo
ukuyilima, ndingati mabafune imbewu elungileyo, batenge imbewu yohlobo
abasukuba belunxamele, ezuzwe kwikoton ebilinywe kweli lizwe.

Imbewu evela E-England idla ngokuti ibe ngu- mxube wentlobo ngentlobo xa
iselihluma, kubeko

indleko ngalomnyaka, ngokuketa intlobo ezimbi.

Makati umlimi ofuna imbewu entle ahambe eketa eyona ilungileyo pakati
kwe koton yake xa imbumbulu ziqalayo ukubenqeka, azeke leyo ayi-
ketileyo ayenze imbewu. Ngokwenjenjalo woda emzuzwini azuze uhlobo
oluhle; azuze nangembewu kwanjenge koton ngokwayo.

Ngalomnyaka eyam ibewu ndiyikete ngolohlobo. Ikoton injengezinye izinto
ezilinywayo, ngokuti idluladlulane ngobuhle; kotike ngokuketa futi kude
kufunyanwe eyona mbewu elungileyo. Tina kwelilizwe izinto zonke
zingecala letu—umhlaba mlile, nomsebenzi awumelwe yimali eninzi, nama-
cweba akufupi. Ukuze sibe nempumelelo kupela kufuneka ukuba lomsebenzi
siwuqale ngenkutalo, nomonde, nenyamezelo; sizuze noncedwana nokuba
luvela pina ukubanceda abalimi betu abangase- Iwandle, abasebenexesha
becinezekile ngenxa yo- kuba kute kwakumka amasodati, ayabinantengo
ihabile—into eyayilinywa kakulu.

UKUTYALWA KWAYO.

Kwelilizwe apo umhlaba ubanzi kangaka, ndi- ngati mayiti imirozo yekoton
ibe nezituba zenyawo ezisibozo. Kutyalwe pakati emseleni njengoko ku-
tyalwa ngako imbotyi. Ndingati kwi akile inye mayibe ziponti ezi 20
zembewu.

Umsele mauvulwe nge drill-opener encinane, into ebupuluwa itsalwa
lihashe elinyo nokuba yinkabi, ububanzi bawo bube zi insh ezi 3, no-
bunzulu bayo zibe 2.

Ndaka ndabona intsimi isiti ukuhlwayelwa kwayo ngayo inge kwenzelwa
itapile, kodwa yayingenzwanga imisele enzulu, ukuqelelana kwe mi’rozo
kukwinyawo ezisibozo, zisitike inkozo zise- lelwe kuhle ngegaba, yapuma
kakuhle. Zapuma kakuhle ezonkozo. Lendlela ke nditi mna okwam yiyona
iyakubalungela abalimi, kuba angati onesipani esingakataziyo abe
nokulima ngo- kute tse xa zihamba emseleni inkabi, kuba kanjalo
kunganzima ukuhlaba imiqela eqele- lene ngenyawo ezisibozo ngapandle
kokuyitaba- tela kwowokuqala. Ekubeni ukwenza imiqela etetse kuyinto
enkulu ekulimeni ikoton, nditi mna lendlela yiyona iyakubalungela
abalimi ngapezulu. Wonga kanjalo umhlaba ube ulinywe katatu ukuba
ubuseluke walinywa kabini waqwakaniswa.

Ekuhambisekeni kwexesha zoya zikanya indawo ezifunekayo kubalimi, bode
mhlaumbi batabate iqinga lase America lokwenza ukuba imisele iqe- lelane
njengoko kufuneka ngako, ngapandle kokude babuye bayilime intsimi yonke.

Ukuba izulu lilungile imbewu ifanele ukuti ngolwe 10, nolwe 12 ibe
selipumile. Mna uku- qala kwam ukuyilima kwintsimi enkulu, ndayiqala
ngembewu ebiselipuma izitombo ; ngeveki yoku- qala kuOctober we 1867.
Kwati ngosuku Iwesibozo yaseliluhlaza yimiti leyo ntsimi.

Lalilixesha elilungileyo, zazake zakaulezaizityalo ukuhluma kwazo. Ngo
November zangenelwa ngu ruxeshe, zada ekubonakaleni zanga zitshaba-
lele, zati kodwa kuba zazi nengcambu ezendeleyo zakula noko—into eyabako
kwaba kukuba yaba na- masebe angapezulu koko abangako ngomnyaka wo-
kuqala, azake kanjalo akufupi nengcambo. Abawu- qelileyo lomsebenzi
bati, ukutyala imbewu ebiseli-

puma izitombo, kunengozi kwelilizwe ukuba ayi- nakuncenceshelwa kamva.
Into endizazelayO ngo kwam yile yokuba endandiyilimile ngembewu
ebiselipuma izitombo, yakula kakuhle yalunga ngapezu kwayo yonke endaka
ndayilima. Intsi- mi endandiyityale kuyo isalungile; ngelililobo itwele
kakuhle. Andingebuxele ubungakananina bekoton eyakuvunwa kuyo kuba
ayiseyiyo yam; ekubonakaleni ngati icumile kanye.

Enye indawo endiziqondele ngokwam, kukuba ikoton etyalwe emva kwemvula
yaza yapuma kungekabuye kune mvula yimbi, yiyona izitolo zomelelayo,
nemiti iqinayo. Kuye kuti ukuba imvula ine ingekapumi umhlaba ube lukuni
uhla- ngane lakubalela ilanga. Kuba nzima ke ukuba zipume izitombo, ziti
nezite zapuma zihlale zinco- kocekile, ibala libemtubi. Ndingati ke mna
makati umntu kwakuba njalo ayilime kwakona yonke intsimi yake, kuba
kungumsebenzi omkulu uku- yonga imiti enjalo ukutamba; kanti kobuya
kubeko ukuxabangela, into edla ngokubako kwi ntsimi eke yafunyanwa
sisipango ngolohlobo.

Ixesha elilungileyo lokuyilima kupakati kuka September napakati kuka
October, ukuze umntu avune kwangomnyaka wokuqala. Ingalinywa nase
kupeleni kuka August nokuba kusekuqaleni kuka September ukuba kuko
imvula, nokuba lomhlaba awudli ngakulalwa yingqele.

UKUHLAKULWA KWAYO.

Izitole zekoton zifanele ukuhlakulelwa xa ama- gqabi esemane mhlayimbi
ematandatu ; kusetye- nzwe ngepuluwa encinane, le kutiwa ukubizwa kwayo
yi American No. I. erolwa lihashe elinye, nokuba yinkabi. Zonke ezinye
impahla ekuhla- kulwa ngazo zanela ukugwengula pezulu, uzeke umhlaba
opezulu uhle ube lukuni. Malakusondela ikuba ezitoleni ezo,
lingazicukumisi kodwa. Emveni kwepuluwa leyo kulandele igaba lihlakule
ukula olusukuba lupantsi kwemiti. Apo sukuba izitole zixinene
mazipungulwe ngegaba kulinganiselwa ububanzi balo, kuti kwakudlula iveki
ezimbini kubuye kuhlakulwe kwa ngalopuluwa kulandele kwa igaba: kuze
kubuye kupungulwe kwezo zitole bezishiyiwe ekuqaleni kuhanjwe kudo-
nyulwa ezisesitubeni zesibini, ziti ezishiyiweyo zisekwe ngomhlaba.

Kwakudlula iveki ezintatu nokuba yinyanga makubuye kudonculwe ezisengati
zixinene, kwenzi- we ituba elingaba zinyawo ezimbini komnye um- hlaba,
kuwumbi zibe zinyawo ezintatu, ngokwo- hlobo lomhlaba, amagada
atyunyuziswe ngapantsi kweziseleyo, wenziwe ukuba ucoleke.

Njengoko ndizibonele ngako mna, kufuneka isituba esikulu, singabi sikulu
ngokugqitisileyo kodwa. Ukuba ikoton iyakuzuza kakuhle uku- kanya nomoya
akuyikubako tuba lokuba ikoton ibe nebala elibi. Yoba nalo elobala apo
imiti ixineneyo, ingaliva kakuhle ilanga. Kengoko imiti mayipane
izituba. Apo ite yahlangana yangami- bini nokuba ngamitatu, aye amasebe
alukane izeke ikoton igqunywe ngamagqabi ingavutwa kakuhle. Imiti
eteqelele ingaminye yiyona yomelelayo.

ngepulawa. Ukuba ute, wawusebenza wawucokisa kwasentloka

U Mr. J. M. Pelem usitumela lamacapaza kwangalomcimbi:—U Rulumeni
kweliletu unyule u Mr. J. J. Kelly waba yi Registering Officer yecala
lase Queens­town, wamisa u Mr. Nel kwelase Dodrecht. Nomhla we 11 ka
December, 1889, kwabe kuyiwe yinto eninzi yaba Tembu ku Mr. Nel ukuya
kungenisa amabango ekubeni yena ekuhambahambeni kwake ebeshiye into
eninzi yamagama anamalungelo. Ute u Mr. Nel uyawachasa amagama a 304,
watsho wawatumela ku Mantyi wase Dordrecht u Mr. Chalmers ngolwe 12
kwaku De­cember. Ngemini elandelayo saba se Driver’s Drift siye
kungenisa kwa amagama ku Mr. Kelly. Safumana ukuba ekujikelezeni kwake
uwafakile a 400 amagama. Ndabacebisa aba Tembu emveni kwamagama atile
esati sawangenisa, waza wawamkela—ndabacebisa ukuba masingawabheneli
lawo angawangenisanga, usebenze kantle noko masingabi samkataza kakulu
okwa nonyaka; hai bavuma aba Tembu. Zafika izaziso zokuba ababangi maze
babe se DOR DRECHT ngomhla 13 January, 1890. Sasebenza no Mr. Malase
kaloku ukuqondisa abantu; ndada mna ndaba nentlanganiso ezintatu e
Qugqwaru (Buffel Dorns) nezibonda. Sabasebenza kunye nezibonda, hai,
ndiyazincoma kanye, zasincedisa betu. Satabata u Mr. Kelly waba ngu
mteteleli wetu. Lwafika usuku lwe 13 January, 1890. Babako o Messrs.
Malase, Zwedala, Tabata, Nobhoza, Sibeko, Mboko, Kalipa, Matsolo 2,
Mpila, nezinye izibonda. Abakwele emahasheni bodwa baba mayela na 600,
waye u Mhlanga ufune umteteleli, Mr. J. O’Brien. Kwaqala kwateta u
Mhlanga, kwalandela tina; ndaqinwa kanye ngobungqina bokwazi izindlu,
ngokunjalo no Mr. Malase nezibonda; ndakolwa kanye ngu Mr. Malase
nezibonda. Kwaqava uku­teta kwamanene kuba besesi baqeshile kade, suka
gwilikwityi apumelela onke lomakulu matatu etu; e Mhlanga kwangena 30,
enye i 30 yafa ngokungezi, laba lityala labo ke elo. Wateta ngobuciko
obukulu nobubalulekileyo u Mr. Chalmers. Lomfo ka Chalmers ukufanele
ukubulelwa nokunconywa ngesisenzo sake sokupulapula, nokugwebela
ngenyameko engaka. Ndiyamncoma kanye lomfo ka Malase, ndimbona nje
okokuba unje ukulunyamekela uhlanga ,lwakwa Xhosa kangangokuba angoyiki
bani, kuba kulula kona kubantu abaninzi ukuzibiza ngamagama amakulu
"xuba ngo “ Tanduhlanga,” “Sihlobo- Sohlanga,” kanti abanaziganeko baka

Ndiya libulela i bilidokwe lika Zwedala elise linexesha liwu mele lomzi
wakwa Xhosa nento zo Nobhoza, Sibeko, Matsolo, Tabata; ndingayishiya
nganina into ka Mbele u Danti, intw’ emana ukutantamisa kulo Mhlanga
wake. Ngeli futi ke tina Glen Grey yangase Queenstown sikwi 400,
eyangase Dordrecht ikwi 500. Umbuzo mnye — soyisebenzisa ngobulumko na i
vote nge members for the Legislative Council? Akwaba ke betu.

UMANYANO EMGWALI.

[IVELA KUM’BHALELI.]

Olumanyano lwahlangana ngosuku olwavakaliswayo kwangenx’engapambili
epepeni—amalungu awahamba ngololiwe akaulelwa ngenqwelo e Dohne Toll
Station ngo-Mvulo Jan 13. Umgwali ufikelelwe ukumka
komhla—akubonakalanga nto iyegqitileyo yokokuba kuko nto ikoyo. Noko
izisi zaba nendawana ezalata yona, babe liqela abo Manyano abafinyelele
kwase ntloko, kwabonakala ngati iyakusita intlanganiso—Umfo ka Ncapayi
ute wabehle wabazelelela abahambi ngento esingiswa pantsi kwe
mpumlo—kanti wayesiti mabasule imilomo njekodwa eyona nto
yabisezakulandela. Ngomhla olandelayo ingene intlanganiso, engandulanga
iqondakale neyona nto ixoxa yona kwabo bafo base Mgwali, kute kodwa xa
umhla umkayo baqala ukuvuma ukuba bayeva ngoku Asimnqweno wam ukuba
ndingene ngokuchuba chuba izixoxo, leyo indawo woyifumana
kum’bhaleli—Ndawana ite yafuna ukubangela ukuba amalungu angakululeki
kukungaveli komniniwa lomzi, ote ukuqala ukuti nyalulu kwangosuku
olulandelayo. intlanganiso yokuhlwa kosuku lokuqala ite ayabi nabantu
kuba kuvele indudumo, noko umsebenzi wahambiseka, kwachitakalwa ngo
Eleven. — Ngolulandelayo usuku indlu ite yasita kuba umzi ingxoxo ube
solute wakangelana nazo, lwayeke ilusuku lokuleswa kwamapepa.
Elomongameli lihlabe likangele mpela, elibete sati umfo utete inene
eziyinene. Elinye elibhaliweyo ligqweta elitile libambe indawo yokulanda
onke amabala akoyo kotitshala, akabanga nalo nalinye ilizwi elilungileyo
lakutetelela otitshala, kodwa ube yedwa kolo luvo.

Intlanganiso ipele ukumka komhla; xa iyole kanye, ndawana ite yambana
kukunduluka kwe nxalenye yamalungu pambi kokuqukumbela, into efuna ukuti
iqeleke.

inteto ezihanjisiweyo kona siyatemba ukuba zoba nempembelelo eyeleleyo—

Mgwali. Umtinjana wenze into emyoli pantsi kokukokela kuka Mr. Ncapayi

UKWAMKELWA KWE RULUNELI.—U Mr. Mtoba e Rode lase Qonce uti,—Ndibetwa
yinto evakalayo 'Emveni eti i Ruluneli iyakusihambela nati. Ukuba

Monti akaulelwe nokuba wovela ngaso Rini ngumzi ontsundu. Umzi

I BHAYI LIQUBELA EMVA.—Bekuko ipike kwintlanganiso yamadodana alapa,
ngenqubelo pambili ye Bhayi malunga nabamnyama. U Mr. Geo. A. Ross, i
President yayo emele indawo yokuba i Bhayi liqubela pambili. U Mr. S. T.
Ncapai, i Chairman yayo emele indawo yokuba lisinga emva i Bhayi.

nangokucacileyo izizatu ezibonisa okokuba i Bhayi lisinga emva;
wenjenjalo no Mr. Ross kwelake. Woyisa u Mr. Ncapai, kwema indawo yokuba
i Bhayi lisinga Emva.

PEDDIE.—Umzi wase Ngqushwa ubhujelwe namhla yindoda yokugqibela kubadala
abona bebelitemba, nabaveli bohlanga u Mr. James Bartholomew omdala.
Waye ngumteteli wabantu, engumvusi kwaba posisayo kakulu ematyaleni,
ebengumteteli malike kanjalo. Lendoda ubudala bayo iminyaka ibi 74.
Asazi mntu obe kolise uhlanga apa ngapezu kwake, elumela ematyaleni
ngako konke anako, ngamanye amaxesha baye bobabini abantu bemangalelene
abalungise yena ngapandle kwe mantyi, bagoduke bexolile. Umzi wase
Ngqushwa ulablekelwe kakulu, ubhubhe ngo-Mvulo, 13 January. Umngowabo
wamkulu—abantu beku 300. Sikulu isililo esilapa ngenxa yalondoda,
ebezokungcwatywa zinkosi o Mr. John Ngwekazi, 8. Kaulela, Rev. C. Bekwa,
Mr. T, J. Mqanda, Mark Mpahla, kanti ke noko uqutyulwe umzi awaziswanga,
ezinye azivanga kungoko zingabangako. Ezo beziko bezina mapakati azo,
kwakuhle noko kube kulilwa.

IMVUMO YE TINARA NE BHAI, (ngu Mbhaleli).—Tar’ ameva mhlobo “ Ngahlamb
ezantsi Kwake.” Ingxelo yam emfutshane nge mvumo ye Tinara ne Bhayi yayi
nge yiyo yakwele nantelekiso, ndandixela izinto njengoko zinjalo.
Kufun'ukuba kubana ke noko wakundipokela ngoluhlobo lokude uti “ ukuba
andixoki ndiya pupa ukuti kwakuze ikwayile yase Bhayi.” Kaundixelele
wena ke ukuba lento iyikwayile yintonina? Ngobam ubudenge ikwayile
liqela labavumi elinje ngeli lalize kuvuma e Tinara; akukataleleki
nokuba babe likulu nokuba babe bahlanu—obo buqela babubo bayi kwayile.
Inteto yam andiyi roxisi. Ama Wesile use Tinara ayavuma kodwa awavumeli
la kwayile yayize apo. Ndaka ndakuva okweyase Rabe e Bhayi kwamnandana;
ndakuva okweyase Tinara ka Rev. S. Sihunu mhla yenza amahlazo pakati kwa
Belungu e Town Hall. Ndaka ndakuva nokwe kwayile yakwa Rev. J. van
Rooyen nge Sacred Concert eyajisenzelwa ukukunjuzwa kwe nkululo yama
koboka, ndeva i Anthems ezimnandi. Ndakuva okweyase Wesile nge Kresmesi
idibene nosapo lwase Bhayi; ndaqonda ukuba iyavuma ndakukangela
ukuhluzeka kwayo. Ndabuya ndayiva eyakwa Rev. Sihunu e Bhayi yenza
bhetele. Ndeva eyase Tshatshi e Bhayi ipetwe ngu Mr. Nkomo, idibene
kwanosapo lwase Rabe lupetwe ngu Mr. Wauchope omncinane nge 6 January
mhla sapulapula intombazana ka Qoqo neka Adams kupela, sakohlwa ukuba
ama Tsha. tshi ati ngelawo enza ntonina.—Ukutsho ke anditelekisi ndixela
isimo se mvumo yeli lizwe njengoko injalo. Ikwayile endati yaqwela e
Tinara, nanamhla ndisatsho, iseyodwa kwesendizivile.

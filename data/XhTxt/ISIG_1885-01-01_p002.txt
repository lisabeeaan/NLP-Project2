I. EYOMNYAKA.

Usuku Iwe 17 ka December lusuku olwalu mnandi ku-
nene kulutsha Iwase Lovedale kuba yay’iyimini yokuvalwa
kweso sikula, neyokwamkelwa kwamabaso kwabate bagqita
abanye emfundweni. Ngayo ke lemini kudla ngokuti
kubeko intlanganiso eyi ngqibela-qoyi” yokubuya nge-
zitende ukucaza zonke izinto ezenzekileyo kuwo lomnyaka.
Ezikolisileyo, nezingenjalo ; ezivuyisileyo, nezingenjalo ;
ukuqubela pambili okubekoyo kuwo ngendawo ezitile;
imali ezingeneyo ngemfundiso, nezise ngamatyala. Inge-
niso nendleko ezibekoyo kuzo zonke izibaxana ezipehlela
lomzi, sidiba yomibini imizi, isikula se Ntombi neso Dodana.

Kungeniwe ke entlanganisweni kuvunywe iculo, kwata-
ndazwa. Yaye indlukazi leyo inkulu yomzi kuse kuzele
tu ngabazali, nezihlobo, nabaputume abantwana, kwaye
kukona bafikayo abantu, nabancinane. Emva kwako konke
ke oko usukile u Mongameli, u Dr. Stewart, wangena kwi-
ndawo yenqubelo pambili ebekoyo kulonyaka we 1884
kuneminye iminyaka. Sotabata intloko zengxelo ende
kunene ayifundileyo.

INGXELO NGOKWASE MBEDESHWENI.

Ute : Yeyona ndawo iyintloko kuzo zonke, kuba ingama-
ndla ekukokeleni, ekulauleni, ibe iluseko Iwendawo zonke
ezifundiswayo, ekuti ke yonke eyiyimbi imfundiso, nokuba
ivelisa zipina iziqamo, sihlale le yelizwi siyibeke entloko.
Ayina kulinganiselwa nezinye ngokubonisa inqubelo
pambili, kwizinto zase mpefumlweni, nasentweni yonke
emntwini, ibonakaliswe esimilweni somntu ngokuti azuzane
nemikwa yenkutalo, umonde nokuyenza imfanelo ngoku-
yiqonda.

Imfundiso ncwadi nje kodwa ezuzwa ngabafundayo asinto
ib’ingacacisa nto malungana nemigudu eyenziwayo, kwano
zimali abapumayo. Ude umnyaka sawuti gangxa, sine-
nyungu nesitukutezi sokungati awukuba naziqamo kwinto
zase mpefumlweni. Kute kodwa xa selipela le seshoni
yokugqibela kwabonakala mhlope ukuba sifumane sisoyika,
kwati ke malungana nentlanganiso zemvuseleko eke zenzi-
wa ekupeleni konyaka nje inxenye yavuswa; yaninzi ebo-
ndeleyo ubuni bayo base mpefumlweni.

NGOKWASE MFUNDWENI.

Unyaka uyakolisa. Endaweni yokuba ube ngemva kwe-
dluleyo, upambili le. Kuti kwakwananwa imviwo nge-
mviwo kususela kowe 1879 kuze kulo, aye enyuka ngoku-
nyuka amanani apumeleleyo ngokukolisayo. Siteta ezo
’Mvi we Zikula. Kwi ziqibi (standards) ezipakamileyo

zakwa Rulumente kuqukwa nesi sitsha i Standard V.,
amanani apumeleleyo kudodana no mtinjana akutazile
kanye. Asikabi nalungelo lokuwaxela kodwa ade ava-
kaliswe komkulu. Ngoviwo Iwe Titshala Iuka September,
kwanele esakuvakalisayo kwi Sigidimi esidluleyo.

Izibaxa zihlanu zendidi zemfundo kwesododana isikula
kususela kwesipezulu kude kuye ezantsi, abafundisi ba 7.

  Ke kwesamankazana zine, zibe zihlanu ngesisebenzayo.
  Inani labe befunda nonyaka nje kudodana 200. Kwese
  ntombi bane abafundisikazi, inani lentombi libe 127. Nga-
  pandle kwezi, kuko esabantwana bakwapantsi kolaulo Iwa-
  bafundisi,—ititshala zimbini, abebeko nonyaka nje bebe
  101. Ke nguko lilonke inani ngalo nyaka pantsi kwemfu-
  ndiso yabafundisi base Lovedale 428, singababali abama-
  shishini abangama 58.

  Lonyaka ubaluleke ngengxoxo ebekoyo ngehambiso ye
  “ Mfundo-pakati-kwaba-Mnyama,” eyati yavuswa yinteto
  eyapuma kwase Lovedale apa. Ingenise ivuso elingena
  sihlahla kwiqelana labafana abati ngabafundileyo, abahlabe
  imikosana yokuba “ Imfundo iyatotywa! ” Yabe kodwa
  inyaniso ingelapo, abafana abo beyawa yigqweta.

EMACHUBENI kwela Batembu kuya kubako intlanganiso yemicimbi yolaulo ngo
Mvulo, 23 March. Umzi obanyuli ucele abanyulwa bawo be Palamente ukuba
baze babeko ukwenzela ukuba bavane bavisisane ngento ezipatelele
entlalweni yabo pantsi kom’buso. Amanene aceliweyo ukuba azo abeko ngu
Hon. J. L. BRADFIELD, M.L.C., no Mr. J. M. ORPEN, M.L.A., omele isitili
abalunge kuso abantu base Machubeni kwelokuqala Ibandla le Palamente.
Elibakala balitabatayo abantu bakowetu namhla lilelincomeka kunene.
Akufuneki nganto ukuba singene ekuchazeni ukufaneleka kwalento
bayiqalileyo abantu base Machubeni. Ilunge ngendlela zonke. Iyakubanga
ukuba abanyulwa nabanyuli bazane, bavane, babe mxhelo mnye. Lonto iya
kuwuvelisela indyandyabesha zamatamsanqa umzi. Umelo e Palamente
alusakuba nto yanteto kodwa. Luyakuba lulo ngenene.

Kufuti urora umzi wakowetu usiti wanela ukubavota abanyulwa kanti
awusakubuya uve luto ngabo. Ukuba kube kunjalo sisiposo somzi ngokwawo.

abanyulwa xa ngaba abavumi kuziya intlanganiso ezibacelayo. Asindawo
yabo ukumangala. Sibazi bengabantu abatumekelelayo ukuziya intlanganiso
zoluhlobo. Umzi wase Batenjini singati uwupangele owase Qonce kulento,
kuba abapatiswe ukuhlabela intlanganiso bebesebejonge ukuba kuvakale
ukuba izakudibana i Palamente babizelwe entlanganisweni yomzi abanyulwa
bomandla o Messrs. WARREN, SCHERMBRUCKER abendlu yokuqala; no Hon. J. L.
BRADFIELD, Hon. J. M. PEACOCK no Hon. T. BAILEY be Bandla le Ngwevu.

zincinane zayo zipalala kuzo izinto, ngokukodwa ezombuso lo sifike kuwo.

ISIPITIPITI SE BRANDY E MAFETENG.

March 5, 1891.

Nge 25 February.—Kufunyenwe ama­dodana epete utywala ngamapolisa; kanti
atunywe ngunyana wenkosi u Griffith into ka Rotoli. Bawabetile
ama­polisa, noko atimba ihashe, nebotile ezi 6 zoty wala, babaleka
abantu bonke. Emva koko kufike u Griffith ngokwake, selengqakamba
ngumqomboti, (brandy) epete umpu ulayishiwe. Emva kokungxamela amapolisa
ngayo, utatiwe wanikelwa imantyi (Mr. Cannon).

Nge 26th.—Babiziwe ukuba boze bonke komantyi. Balikile behamba nenkosi
leyo yabo. Alitetwanga elompu (lika Griffith) kutetwe elamadodana abeye
kutenga. Afunyenwe enetyala kabini, (a) ngokuzisa utywala Elusutu, amane
anikwe inyanga ezi 6 or £20 emnye; (6) kwawona inyanga ezine, kungabiko
ntlaulo, ngokubeta amapolisa. Omnye unikwe £10 or inyanga ezintatu
entolo­ngweni, esebenza nzima, ngokuma endleleni yaboni—pofu
akatenganga, akalwanga. Emva kwesigwebo ite inkosi yabo (Chief Griffith)
kubo ‘ hambani sihambe sigoduke.’ Nditi kujube amaxakato kwa lapo pambi
komantyi bemka, kwabanjwa batatu, isibini semka kwapela,—amapolisa abuye
azuza induma ezimbi xa abelinga ukubamba; yada inkosi yawo (yomlungu)
yawayalela ukuba ‘ arole,’ xa nayo seyipuma pakati kwama bhunguza, (ete
yasindiswa ngabo ngabom) yabaleka eyamapolisa, lacolwa pantsi elinye
lawo, lagula intsuku ; nangoku alikawenzi umsebenzi walo wokubeta
ixilongo, lisafa. Kutunyelwe ku Sir M. Clarke o Maseru, walika apa nge
27th. Laqala ke ukutetwa ityala xa sekufike no Nkosi Rotoli oputunywe
yikali kamantyi ngo March 2. Lomini botuka kakulu aba­ntu ukungena
kwemikosi yabe Sutu ukuza etyaleni apo. Wena wabona amahashe ‘ Mhloli ’
ngomhla ka Sir H. Loch e Qonce, nditi, ayemancinane lawo alomini kunala
afika apa e Mafeteng nge 2 March. Qonda inkomo ezintatu ezaxhelwa
yimantyi abazivanga nokuziva, baxukuxa imilomo kodwa .Mandinqumle noko
zininzi. Litetwe lagqitywa ityala, ngu Sir M. Clarke, amadodana ali 10
ngo Nkosana Griffith. Isigwebo zinkomo ezi 5 emnye kwabalitoba; ku
Griffith, inya­nga 6, ngompu, inyanga ezikwa 6 ngo­tywala, akwabiko
ntlaulo kuye ibekwayo. Nditi kuwe, ukuba lomcimbi ubunganikelwanga
kwisilumko, u Nkosi Lidziya, onguyise-mkulu ka Griffith: namhla
ngaliyimilambo igazi siteta nje. Koko yena wati kwasentloko akuva lento
wenza amacebo obudoda ; watsho esiti makabanjwe afakwe entolongweni
umfana. Batoba ko ngezwi lake abe Sutu. Sebesitsha. Yonke lento ingenxa
yotywala bomlungu. Woda uqonde nawe wena utumela o Bailey e Palamente,
ukuya kukucelela igilasi yotywala. Kobanzima kubatshisi, nabazitshisa
nge brandy apa ; kanti basakutsha ngumlilo pambili,— Owako, M.M.M.

isonwabo sabo.

sisinyi sokuqala.

NGE NGQUSHWA NO NOBHALA. 

9 December 1890.

Nkosi Mhleli wepepa le Mvo Zabantsundu :—Ndincede undipe indawo epepeni lako, ndifuna ukungqinela inteto eyinyaniso etetwa lipepa le Mvo lomhla  7 November, eliti, o Nobhala abatandwa " ngabantu abantsundu: kunyanisekile oko, akuko ndawo ibafunayo; ngokukodwa Engqushwa. U Mr. Innes ulingqina lam, nguye owayesingete inteto ye nkosi nesizwe endlwini yetyalike yama Wesile e Durban—inteto ebukali yatsolisa ukungafunwa kwabo o Nobhala a Engqushwa. Mna utetayo ndingu & Mngqushwa, lento kute cwaka umtandazo wabo ku Mhlekazi ngo Nobhala benziwa kukuba ingqondo zabo zisesemva,  abaka wazi umbuso ka Rulumente, balinde bona ku Mantyi, ukuba abuze ukuba niyabatandana o Nobhala, bati ke bona hayi asibafuni. Bonke abantu abantsundu abasezidenge embusweni ka Rulumeni bahleli kakubi banjengababanjwa nabatinjwa namakoboka. Uti u Rulumente bauvumile umteto akubona bengateti; inkonzo ka Rulumente ilungele abantu abanjengabomandla wase Q'once nakwezinye indawo abawaziyo umbuso abayakuzamela amalungelo abo; inkosi zase Ngqush va zizidenge ekulweleni amalungelo azo, zoyika omantyi, engati ziyakukutshwa ebukosini bakutetela ainalungelo abo. Konke oko kubangwa budenge babo. Onobhala bona abafuneki, u Rulumente makancede ngamapolisa antsundu namhlope, into eyakuncipisa ubusela.

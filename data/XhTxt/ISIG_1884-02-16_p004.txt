UKUVULWA KWENDLU YAMAFAMA ANTSUNDU.

Kute kwakusa ngomhla we 17 kulenyanga kwabonakala amaqela ngamaqela
abantu benyawo nabamahashe, nenqwe- lo eziseshumini. Ngexesha leshumi
kungeniwe, waqala u Mongameli welibandla u Mr. Levey, emveni kokuba
ivuli- we intlanganiso ngomtandazo ngu Bishop Key, ngokucela u Bishop
Key ukuba ongamele intlanganiso. Esitatile isihlalo u Bishop Key ucele u
Mr. Levey ukuba enze ingxelo ngomsebenzi wokwakiwa kwendlu.—Usukile u
Mr. Levey wati: Imali eyakiwe ngayo lendlu ivulwayo namhla iyi £340,
eseyiroliwe yi £75 8s l1d mhla kwabekwa ilitye, wati uyatemba ke ukuba
namhla elityala liya kupela.

Kusuke u Bishop Key wateta amazwi okukutaza umse- benzi onje ukuba mhle
kwawo, wati abanye abantu bacinga ukuba ukuxoxa ngomsebenzi wokulima
akubancedi luto, kuba besazi yonke into engokulima, kanti ke akunjalo,
boti ekuhambeni kwexesha babone ukuba lendlu iluncedo olu- kulu, kulapo
baya kufunda kona ukwenza intsimi ebivelisa ishumi lengxowa, ivelise
amashumi amabini. Wateta namanye amazwi enkutazo enkulu.

Emva koku esuke ngokusuka amadoda erola inkomo, nemali, negusha,
nebokwe. Kute mayela no 2 Oclock kwapunywa kwaza kulungiselelwa
ukutyiwa. Ibuye ya- ngena intlanganiso ngokuhlwa, kwarolwa kwa imali,
ne- nkomo Kute ukuza kupetwa kwentlanganiso kwafumane- ka imali
esesandleni iyi £92 15 0, ngapandle kwetengisi- weyo. Yapuma
intlanganiso ikoliswe kakulu ngumsebenzi walomini.

Amadoda asemzini abeze kubonela ngunyana omkulu ka Capt. Veldtman, Mr.
P. Mahonga, Mr. K. Tshokotsha, no Mr. Thos. Zwedala, namanye amanene
antsundu.

Nge 29 kule nyanga kutengiswe impahla eyayitenjisiwe ukurolelwa indlu,
imali efunyenweyo ibe yi £223 2 10 1/2. Incwadi zombali zibonisa ukuba
ebiseyiko ngapandle kwa- le yi £183 17 6. Imali eseyiko iyonke yi £407 0
4 1/2. Ngoko ke ityala lendlu lipelile kwako intwana eshiyekayo.

Ndenza ingxelwana emfutshane ngokungabi naxesha, nezimali azikacaci
kakuhle ukuma kwazo, sendiqaulela umzi wakowetu kuba ndisazi ukuba
wokolwa uwutakazele lomsebenzi.

P. MTYOBA.

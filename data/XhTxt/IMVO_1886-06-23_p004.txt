I Dip Efezekileyo.

XA bacela amehlo omzi kwi Dip yabo engena Tyefu ekutiwa
ye “ Fezekileyo,” o-Messrs. DRUMMOND & CO.,

banga bangawa latela lama lungelo angekoyo kwezinye i Dip :—

1.  Ayina Tyefu.

2.  Ayibonakalisi uboya.

3.   Iyalupelisa kanye ukwekwe ezigusheni, nase zinkomeni, namakalane,
    nase ma hasheni, nentwakumba nentwala ezinkomeni nase zinjeni.
    ibulala nezinambuzane ezigqiba imiti.

4.   Inokudityaniswa lula namanzi ashushu nabandayo, ayinankatazo
    zininzi, icokisekile ingafuni nokugwe xwa.

5.   Ixabiso lilula; Igalon inye (inkonxana engangezi zetela) yayo yenza
    igalon ezi 60 zoku hlamba.

  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1887.

KU-MHLELI WE Sigidimi,—Nkosi yam ndiyakubongoza ngala mazwana ndisiti
kuwe ube ufanelwe yimbeko, kuba usendaweni yetu sonke tina, apo sinokuti
kona sinike imbeko nodumo, ndikucela ukuba undifakele lamazwana. Nditi
kuwe asikukuba u Mr. Gqoba lo engapendulwa nje kukuba enyanisile; uyazi
naye ngokwake ukuba akanyanisanga ngale mbali ya Bambo. Ke silindele
tina ukuba u Mr. Gqoba lo asenzele eminye imisebenzi elungileyo, enge
nje ngalento yokutanda ukuxakanisa abantu ezintweni zokukanya,
ababuyisele emva ezintweni zokulunga. U Mr. Gqoba lo unemfundo
engafanele ezindawo amana ukuzifaka kuzo, silindele imisebenzi eya
kumnika sidumo esihle kunesi asifunayo yena. Esi asimncedi, singancedi
mntu kanjalo. Singavuya simve esenza umsebenzi ononcedo kuye nakwabanye.
Uxolo Mhleli, ndilixego lase Mahlubini. Kumhlana ndikucela into mhlobo
wamawetu uze undifakele loncwadi ngobubele bako. Ndim

JAN MILANA, Ihlubi.

Adelaide, 13 June.

[Inteto yako ayivakali eyona nto iteta yona. Ukuba uteta nge mbali yase
Mbo ukuti u Mr. Gqoba akayazi, kusalungele wena kanye, wena maube unayo
eyona inyanisekileyo. Kanjalo, ukuba uti u Mr. Gqoba ufuna isidumo
ngokubala ezimbali zasema Xoseni nezase Mbo, angaba ufuna isidumo
sezontlanga, kuba zonke izizwe ezimbali ibaliweyo, zikona indawo
zezidumo zazo, kwanezokugxekwa, ke ngoko ngati akukayicani kakuhle eyona
nto ufuna ukuteta yona. Okunye lembali yenziwa ngu Mr. Gqoba asikuko
nokufuneka kwayo esizweni nesimhlope, nakuma Hlubi, use ngowokuqala wena
kuma Hlubi nakwezinye izizwe ombiza ngokuti ngumxakanisi wabantu.
Singavuyake usitumele eyona iyiyo, okwangoku soke sibe siquba ngale ka
Mr. Gqoba ayifumana kwa kwamanye ama Hlubi anje ngawe.— ED.

ISAZISO SIKA RULUMENTE.—NO. 278, 1887.

  Kwi Ofisi yo Mongameli we Mihlaba ya Komkulu,

EKUBENI U Rulumente enikwe igunya nje ngesigqibo esimalunga ne Siqendu
so Kuqala so Mteto wemi Hlaba
Elahliweyo kanye (Derelict Lands) No. 3 we 1870, wokutana qwe,
nokuyipata yonke imi Hlaba Elahliweyo

  kanye ngoluhlobp lulandelayo, olunje ngolu :—

  “ Makuti xeshikweni kuko irafu emele ukurolelwa u Rulumente wale
  Koloni, ngendawo nokuba ngumhlaba ka
  Rulumente, esukuba ide yayiminyaka elishumi ilityala, ibe londawo,
  nokuba ngulo mhlaba ite yayekwa, yashiywa, yayekwa mpela, kwaza
  akwabiko nomnini kuyinikwa, nokuba ngobeyiqashiselwe, kwaye
  kunjalonje, kungeko namntu bambekileyo esikundleni sabo usendaweni
  yabo onokufunyanwa, kuyakuba semtetweni ukuba i Ruluneli ibale isaziso
  ngawo kwi pepa lezaziso zika Rulumente eliyi Government Gazette,
  nokuba kukulipina ipepa angatanda Iona, umhlaba onjalo, nokuba ngumzi
  oti ushiywe kanye, sishicilelwe inyanga zide zibentatu, izekuti ukuba
  kwezi nyanga ntatu, akuvelanga bani, nokuba ngowaye wunikiwe, nokuba
  ngoqeshileyo, nokuba ngobekwe ngabo kwezo ndawo, oyakubanga ezondawo,
  aze ahlaule le rafu inexesha ; i-Ruluneli iyakuti zakupela ezo nyanga
  ntatu, iwutabate lomhlaba nokuba ngumzi, iwupate kwangesiko lemihlaba
  enjalo emisiweyo kule Koloni, ukuba akutanga (njengoko kwahlala
  kunjalo) lomhlaba nokuba ngumzi, emaupatwe ngalemiteto ube ub’
  ubambisele ityala, indawo ezikwi ziqendu ze sibozo, nesitoba,
  neseshumi se Simiselo (Ordinance) se 9 se 1844 ngokumayela
  nokutengiswa komhlaba nokuba ngumzi onjalo, yaye nokusetyenziswa kwalo
  mali kuyakuhanjiswa ngokwalomteto.”

  Kuyaziswa kumntu wonke okokuba le Mihlaba icwangciswe ngezants’ apa,
  ikwisi Sitili (Division) se Victoria East mayibe ngohlobo ilahliwe
  kanye, nje ngoluhlobo sekuxeliwe ngalo, nokokuba u Rulumente
  uyakuyitabatela kuyo ayihlute, ngomhla wokuqala (1st) ku October 1887,
  ngapandle kokuba iyakuha sel’ibangiwe yada yahlaulelwa ezerafu zayo
  bezigqitelwe ngamaxesha, zahlaulwa kwi Mantyi yeso Sitili, njengoko
  kufuneka kwenjiwe njalo kweso siqendu salo Mteto kucatshulwe kuwo.

  F. SCHERMBRUCKER, Umongameli.

  Sicelwa ngabahlobo babafi ukuba sivakalise le mibiko:— UMFI U ELIJAH
  NTLONZE.

  Mhlobo wam otandekayo. Kuluvuyo kum ukufumana elituba lokukwenzela
  lemigcana imbalwa. Ke ndifuna ukukwazisa ngento endihleleyo. U Elijah
  Ntlonze akaseko emhlabeni. Umke ngomhla we 19 kulenyanga ingu May.
  Wati xa aza kumka wati lunqweno lwam ukuba ndimke ndisemtsha
  emhlabeni, wati kusoloko ndaguqukayo ndanqwenela ukumka ndisemtsha,
  ngenxa yokungcola kwalomhlaba. Watike “ Tata sendizilungiselele
  ukuhamba ngoku, i Nkosi yam inam noko ndisifanje akukonto. Wati amazwi
  ake okugqibela ngosuku lomvulo ecaweni wati “ Kufike ilizwi kum lati
  imini yangomso akusokuyibona,” wati “ namhlanje izulu likaya lam,”
  wati kwaoko wacula eliculo liti: Nkosi yam ubunditanda ekuhlahlekeni
  kwam, iculo le 112 kweyama Wesile, Ute ngo 9 wangokuhlwa wasibulisa,
  xa iya ku 10 wati “ Namhlanje izulu likaya lam,” wemkake.

  Leyo mini ke mhlobo wam akuzange kubeko enje ngayo ubummandi bayo.
  Bonke abantu ababeko ngalomini kwaba yinqaba ukuba bemke ngobo busuku,
  ngenxa yovuyo olwaluko, kwanga kungasa behleli kuba wati culani nina
  ke zihlobo zam ize nani ningakatazeki, nivuye kuba usilindile ekaya,
  siya kumfumana kona kwixesha elizayo. Intshumayelo engcwabeni
  ibibanjwe ngu Rev. S. Gudula. U Elijah Ntlonze wazalwa nge 13 January
  1865, umke ngo May 19, 1884. Nobanda, Heald Town, May 19, 1884.

UMFI U MRS. JAMES M. DWANE.

Ukububa kwenenekazi u Mrs. James Dwane ngomhla wesibozo (8th of April).
Ufike apa e Mkangiso selesifa kakulu. Yati lontokazi yakubuliswa
yiramente yati: “Ndiyavuya ukuba isicaka sika Tixo sinditumele ukuba
maze ndizokungcwatywa nini,” waye enjalo nje eteta ngelizwi lika Tixo,
yena ute pambi kokuba abube wati: “ Zifikile kum imini zokuba ndifumane
into ebe ndiyisebenzela.” Kute ngamhla utile wacacisa ukuba andisalinde
nto ngoku emhlabeni ndiza kumka, wanikela umbuliso ewutumela ku Mrs.
Mdaba owaye sifa naye ke, wati: “ Zenindibulisele, niti uya kuhamba
ninina, zifikile mna ezam ukuba ndihambe; ” lonto weyiyaleza kumadoda
amabini kum naku Rev. James Dwane, wayaleza nokuba atengelwe ibotile
yobulawu (scent). Ute mhlana wasishiya wati: “ Huntshu weyisiwe u Satana
namabandla ake,” kwakungapambi kwe Bulakufesi ke. Kute ngayo i
Bulakufesi kwagaleleka u Mrs. Dwane omkulu, wabonakala lomntu evuya
kakulu ute ma yiza ndange wezake, ute unina ndize nomkuluwe wako wavuya
kakulu. Wati esahleli kungummangaliso ukuyola kuka Tixo. Kute emva kwe
dinala kwafika umkuluwe wake samxelela wambiza wambulisa ke wati ke
umkuluwe wake ndizokubulisa, wati hlala wogoduka ngomso, wati akugqiba
ukubulisa wati ndiqole ndiza kutshata ngengubo ezimhlope ezimaqaqaula.
Wati ku Mrs. Boges beka isandla ebunzi kum ungasusi wabulisa ngesinye.
Wabiza indoda nonina nabantwana encuma wababulisa, wati kumyeni wake: “
Ungandibambezeli,” wati yena hai, waza watata iqiya yake wayigalela
ubuqolo (scent) wazake wayibeka ebunzi waza wati: “ Isitunzi sokufa
andisoyiki,” Amen kwapela ke. Wati lemini wabuba lo, wabuba Iowa
wayembulisa. Wenza naye imisebenzi emikulu eloke linenekazi leramente u
Mrs. Mdaba naye ubube nge 8 April, 1884. Samngcwaba ke ngomhla wesitoba
ka April, 1884. Yayininzi kakulu iramente eyam’pelekayo enditemba ukuba
yaba kumashumi asibozo ababe mkokele ukuya kungcwaba yayingu William
Mtoba no Mr. S. Magawu, kwakuhle kanye. Wondixolela ke Mhleli we
Sigidimi sohlanga. Ndim,—WILLIAM: MTOBA. Mount Coke, May 30, 1884.

  be-Sigidimi ububile. Ngati kuyimfanelo ukukwazisa ukuze bamazi abanye
  abahlobo bake abamaziyo nangokwa sesi Gidimini oko. Yenye yezinto
  abezinikele ngomqolo wonke ehleli elangazelele ukunga abantu bonke
  bangasiqonda ubunzulu namalungelo aso kosamkelayo. Ub'ubele apa e
  Tsojana nge 26 ku April ngo 12 oclock emini. Isifo esona simndululeyo
  lipika eli-

  nzima elati kona ngentsuku ezimbini lambandezela nga- ngokuba
  angabinako nokusa icala pantsi, kupela kusekelwa ngumntu imini
  nobusuku. Kodwa ke isifo abenaso ngenteto yamagqira ase Qonce o Dr.
  Ross, no Dr. Egan, bamfumaua ukuba une heart-disease, isifo esingena
  kunyangwa ngapandle kokuba azigcine ngokute quite, azinqande
  nakwingcinga ezinkulu, anganxamisi ukutya, angakweli. Lomfoke ite kuye
  lento itetwa ngalamagqira wayibona noko akabi nakuzinceda ukuba
  angafundisi, nokuhamba eshumayela kubahedeni, nokubahlulahlula ubusuku
  etandaza, ndide ndakumbula apo ebekona wakupelelelwa umzimba ngamandla
  okunyuka imimango yokuya ezilalini nasesikuleni. Yasalela into
  yokubahlulahlula ubusuku nemini yaza i Bible kaloku yangakumbi
  ukuyilesa. Esancambaza ukuhamba epumla ukuya emitandazweni yemihla
  ngemihla yetu etyalikeni, lemihla inkwenkwana yam ihamba imlinda
  impatele isitulo sokuhlala ayame, yada yaba zezontsuku zombini zinkulu
  zepika nokukohlela igazi ukwahlukana ne Bible nokuya kutandaza. Into
  ayenzileyo ngobusuku bokuze kuzokusa enduluka watandazela ilizwi ukuba
  lihlale endlwini yoyise, likule kwabapilileyo enkonzweni, livuse
  abatyafileyo, lingene kwintliziyo ezilukuni zabahedeni, nengqola, hba
  qondisise inceba ka Tixo ukuba nkulu kwayo kwabazivumayo nabazilahlayo
  izono zabo kulo ihlabati. Ute emva koko wabulela ku Tixo ukumtumela
  kuyise umoya wake. Ebengunyana womhedeni, waguquka eyedwa kowabo ate
  ngalo nguquko wakolwa umzi kayise’mkulu wonke, waza uyise wanikwa
  ingqondo yokutumela intsapo yake emfundisweni abapilileyo
  nabamanduleleyo ukuya ekaya lopumlo kuyo yonke imizamo yomntu. Ute
  kaloku umfo omkulu wamemeza xa upumayo umpefumlo wati ewela pezu kwam
  : “ Gcinani ukutandaza bubom kumakolwa,” agqibele ngalo emva kwelo
  ukuza kuti cwaka ute kum wandibongoza ngokuti ncama mntakwetu ute
  balulu wacima watsala umpefumlo wapela.

Wangcwatywa ngo 27 ku April nge Cawa ukumka kwemini. Kwaye kulindelwe u
Rev. P. Masiza owayeceliwe ndim, akabinakufika, waselengcwatywa zi

ISEMNARI YASE CUMGCE.

Kuko umnyinyivana wetemba lokuba umcimbi wesikolo sase Cumngce
(Butingville) ema Mpondweni ungade ulunge. Imbali engengxakeko zesi
sikolo iyakufunyanwa kwintetwana eke yako e Palamente ngo 5 June.

U-SPEAKER [ngumgcini sihlalo e Palamente lowo] ute ngapambi kokuba u Mr.
Fuller angenise umcimbi abenike isaziso ngawo angake enze mabini
ngomtandazo ka Rev. Mr. Morris ovela ema Mpondweni. Lenkundla ye
Palamente ayimelwe kupulapula imitandazo evela ngapaya kwemida ye
Koloni, kodwa lomcimbi ngongafaniyo neminye. Awupatelele micimbini
yolaulo Iwasema Mpondweni kodwa unendawana efanelwe ukukangelwa yi
Palamente. U Mr. Morris wayetenjiswe ixabiso elitile lemali waza
ngokutembela kweli dinga waquba imicimbi etile. Ngoku ungundimangele
kule Koloni: kupantsi kwezi zimeko ke upambi kwebandla umtandazo wake.

u MR. FULLER ute makuvunyelwe ukuba umtandazo ka Rev. J. S. Morris
obekwe pambi kwe Palamente ngo 29 May maunikwe igqugula elibucala ukuba
liwucedulule, ze lenze ingxelo ; elo gqugula ukuba livunyiwe libe lelala
manene : Mr. Jonathan Ayliff, Mr. Dyer, Mr. Du Preez, Mr. Douglas, naye
u Mr. Fuller ngokwake. Ute ukusixasa isicelo sake, ngo February 1882,
elinene lingum’fundisi lalinqwenele ukumisa ema Mpondweni, emdeni
welipetwe lulaulo Iwe Koloni, isikolo esinjengezo zase Lovedale, Emtata
nezinye. Lento uyivakalise ku Dr. Dale, Um’patiswa Micimbi ye Mfundo,
wafumana impendulo kwelo nene ukuba kamsinyane zigqityiwe izakiwo
woncediswa ngemali zokuquba umsebenzi lowo njengokuba kusenjiwanjalo
kwezinye izikolo. Ejonge kweli temba ke zamiswa izakiwo ezafikelela
kwiponti ezimawaka mahlanu (£5,000), amawaka amatatu anamakulu amahlanu
(£3,5?0) ale mali avela kuma Mpondo, isenzo esingena mlingamso eso
embalini yezisa kwabamnyama kumsebenzi onjenga lowo. Lati lakufika
ixesha ixabiso ledinga labekwa pambi kwe Palamente, alavunyelwa ngonyaka
odluleyo. Okwesake isiqu akakolwa ukuba zonke inyaniso ngomsebenzi lowo
zazipambi kwebandla mhla ingazanga yavunyelwa into leyo ; ecinga kanjako
ukuba kuko isizatu esikulu emelwe i Palamente ukufeza idinga Iika Dr.
Dale. De ibe idinga elo lifeziwe yonke lomali icitelwe ezo zakiwo inje
ngokuba ila- hliwe.

u MR. STIGANT uyisekele lenteto, wati uyatemba ukuba iyakuvunyelwa.
Ngonyaka odluleyo oko yayixoxwa lento wonke wayenga angancipisa imali,
engakolwa ukuba lomcimbi wakangeliswa.

Esi sicelo savunyelwa.

4 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 16, 1884.

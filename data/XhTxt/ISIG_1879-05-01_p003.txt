ukuba afumane umfazi ofundileyo, aya kuvana naye. Unento ezintsha anga
angafikelela kuzo, kube kunzima ukuzifumana.

Mhlaumbi kungenxa yesi sizatu abate aba- funde kakulu ababi nakuma
ngeyure yesilingo, bada badlulwa nangabebenganeno kubo ngokufunda,
abamfundwanaibingabanelele u- kubakulula ekuzitobeni pantsi komfundisi
wabo, imfundwana ebeyunele ukuba ancotule intloko yake pantsi kwenkosi
ayifake pantsi komfundisi.

Kodwake amadoda afundileyo, ate akulingwa akabi nakuma akamaninzi,
akangawo nangade umntu alinganiselwe ngawo ngokuti aya kuba njalo
amaninzi yakwanda imfundo yamangesi, asingawo nangoku ekungatiwa afana
noninzi.

Abane mfundo ete putu bakolise ukufundi- ewa ezikolweni, umsebenzi
abadla ngokuwuta- tangowokutitsha nowobufundisi. Ukutembeka kwabo ku
Rulumeni aku tandatyuzwa, ize ke imfundo yabo ideleke kwinkoliso
yabamhlope ngeso sizatu. Kodwa andiboni sizatu sakutandabuza ukuba yilo
mfundo yabo ebangela ukuba abaninzi ezikolweni bangalwi no Rulumeni.
Ababaninzi abati befunde ezikolweni bangene kumsebenzi ka Rulumeni.
Kukafuti ke ndisiva abacasana nokufundiswa kakulu komntu ontsundu
bekankanya abambalwa abebebekwe kwindawo ezipakamileyo, abazipate
ngoblobo lokuba kutandabuzeke ukutembeka kwabo. Noko ke akukabonakali
kum ukuba ukufundiswa Isingesi kunokucaswa ngenxa yabo. U-Ed- mund
Sandile, ube ngu nyana we nkosi ebesisandla se Mantyi yase Xesi. Kute
ukuqaleka kwemfazwe kwavakala indaba ezininzi ngemisebenzi yake
engakolekileyo ; kwavakala ukuba ube sezintlanganisweni zamakowabo
ambete umposwa, kwavakala nezinye izinto zokubuyela ebuhedenini ; akayi
xelelanto imantyi yake wapelela ekumkeni, emva koko kubeble kwavakala
ukuba unondiyalwa. Kodwa ke kakade imfundo yake ibingazanga ibe mfundo
ipi, ebengenayo nengqondo encomekayo, ekubonakala ukuba waye ngayi kuza
abe semsebenzini ka Rulumeni ukuba wa­ye ngenguye unyana ka Sandile.
Kwangapambili bekungalindelekayo ukuba alandele uyise nokuba uya kwinto
enjanina ubudenge nokukohlakala. Omnye umntu okankanywa futi ngu
Nehemiya ka Mshweshwe. Yena ke imfundo ayifumana kubafundisi bama French
singatiyayi yeya ma French kunama Ngesi nakuba ndisiva ukuba uyazazi
zombini ezonto, intlalo yake yeyengesi, uyafunda kakulu ngokukodwa
imbalana zama French nencwadi eziteta ngo Napoleon wokuqala, waka
wanikwa ityala lokuba upembelele abantu ukuba bavukelane no Rulumeni
kwati kwakuyiwa ematyaleni yapelela emoyemi lonto. Kakade ke bekuya kuba
ngummanga-

liso kweyam intliziyo, ukuba unyana wenkosi ebizipete njengo Mshweshwe,
unyana obe- selemdala eseko uyise ebete imiteto yama Ngesi wayamkela
ngoxolo yakufika, andiboni sizatu ke kukalazelwe imfundo kona ukuba
ebengamtandi Urulumente. Kungenziwa inteto ekwanjalo ngo Nathaniel Mhala
no Christian Gcaika. Omane lamandoda ake akankanywa kum ngababonisa
ukuba imfundo yontsundu ayi ncedi nto, nabati iyenzakalisa. Lonteto
nokuba ibe ingqinelana ne- nyaniso yezinto, kubonakala ukuba
ibingafanela ukwenziwa ngazo zonke izinto ezilungileyo abangati
abantsundu bazigqwete. Kodwa ke kum kubonakala ukuba lenteto
ayingqinelani nenyaniso yezinto. Xa siteta ngesimilo esingaqondekiyo
sabambalwa abantsundu, sibe sifanele ukuyi kumbulela nempembelelo enkulu
yenkoliso yabafundileyo, abazamela ukuba kugcinwe umteto. Olululo beluko
lwama Xosa nakuba belulukulu lunengcambu ebezinengozi xa
zingalunyukelwanga noko alungenwanga ngentliziyo, njengemfazwe
zangapambili. Nakuba ndingazilibali nezinye impembelelo ezisebenza
okulungileyo pakati kwabantsundu, kuyabonakala ukuba esinye isizatu
esivelisa oku kukwanda nokunyuka kwemfundo yabantsundu nokuba
abafundileyo, ngapandle kwabamba- balwa abangeyiyo nto, bebengecala lika
Ru- lumente, nomteto. Kodwa ke kuncinane ukusetyenziswa kwa bafundileyo
ngapandle kokutitsha, nokutengiswa. Bambalwa kanye abangene kwa
Rulumente, akukolisanga nokuba banyulwe ngobulumko.

Akuko sebe nomsebenzi ka Rulumente, elingenwa ngabantsundu njengokuba
kunjalo e-India. Ziya funeka kakulu izandla netoliki, kodwa akuko
untsundu ndimaziyo ubekwe kwindawo enembuzeko ngapezu kweye toliki,
Inkosana zinikwa amandla amakulu kule koloni nangapandle kwayo, kodwa
lomandla aziwafumani ngokuba zizizicaka zika Rulumente, ziwafumana
ngokuzalwa. Akuko namnye ontsundu endifumana epatiswe umsebenzi ofuna
imfundo ete putu, onje ngowe Mantyi nowokubuta imali, awupatiswe
lomsebenzi ngokuba ewulingene, awupate ngegunya lika Rulumente, awuqube
ngohlobo lwabamhlope, kungabi ngohlobo lwabantsundu. Kuti kwakukankanywa
umsebenzi onjalo zindwebe nezihlobo zabantsundu, ziti awunakwenziwa
akungelungi, nakuba kum kubonakala ukuba umsebenzi onjalo ungacita
okukwahlukana kwezizwe, kanjalo andiboni sizatu sokuba kutiwe
ayinakwenzeka lonto. Kwindawo eziwucasayo umsebenzi onje andiboni
inamandla. Kunqabile ukuba kutiwa abafundileyo xa baketwe ngobulumko
abangewulingani umsebenzi onje ngalo nditeta ngawo, kanti siyabavumela
abangafundanga bengaqeqeshwanga, be-

nganyulwanga nokunyulwa ukuba bagwebe balaule amawaka amakowabo
ngesizatu sokuba bengabegazi.

Elinye lamapepa ase Qonce eliteta ngale- ncwadi Yomhlekazi, liti kuko
nezabafundisi ezihamba nayo, abati bona akungelunge ukuba kubeko

abantsundu base nomsebenzi omkulu olungele bona bodwa owokutitsha. Ke
nakuwo base mbalwa kakulu, ngokoke akungelunge ukuba bancitshiselwe
komnye.

KWA MOROSI.

U-Morosi kule nyanga idluleyo ute waba- lekela kwintaba yake edume
kakulu ngoku- nqaba. Amatuba okuhlangana kwake nemikosi yelakuti icala
abe mabini. Elokuqala kube kutinjwa inkomo ngabantubaka Litsea,
bapumelela kakuhle ngapandle kwengozi ezingade zikankanywe. Okwesibini
kube ngomhla we 8 ka April, mini imikosi yamapolisa neyama Setlani
ibilungiselele ukuze iyingenele ngamandla lontaba ise imcita ngalomini
u-Morosi. Kuqale ukuliwa kwakusasa, kwavulwa indlela ngenkanunu ezimbini
ebezinyuselwe lomini. Umpati mikosi yalo mini uti kwincwadi ayi tumele
kwi Nkulu yakwa Rulumenti. “Ndiyingenelekwakusasa inqaba ka Morosi
ndafunzela ,ngayo yonke imikosi yekoloni ebendinayo, ndadubula
ngenkanunu iyure ezimbini. Ndinosizi ukutiside sancama emva kolulo
olusitabate iyure ezitile, kwasala u- Lieutenant Read namanye amadoda
amabini, ingxwelera zamashumi mabini ngapandle kuka Captain Surmon.
Inqaba zabe Sutu sifike zinamandla nabe Sutubesilwa kabukali, kubonakala
ukuba baya kupela abeletu icala ukuba sipikele ukuzitabata. Ezinkanunu
sinazo apa zincinane kakulu, azenzi nto kwezinqaba zamatye. Into
eseyibonakala kum kukuba sibaraule abe Sutu bade bapelelwe kukudla.
Ndinga ngoko ndingatunyelwa amanye amadoda amhlope. Bendinga ungayalela
u-Mr Brownlee ukuba ati ku Captain O’ Connor makakauleze ukuza kum nawo
onke amadoda anokuza nawo. Andiva nto ku Col. Brabant, oselene ntsuku
ezisibozo ahlukene nam kwi kamp ebise Kraai, ukuze sihlangane kule
ndawo.” Lencwadi yabalwa kwangalo mini kwaliwa ngayo. Ezinye incwadi
zixela ukuba kwababuleweyo kuko nonyana ka Mshweshwe, nakuba zingalixeli
igama lake. Kuse kubonakala noko ukuba akuyi kubako msebenzi mkulu kwelo
cala lika Morosi.

IMFAZWE YASE TURKEY.

IMFAZWE ekoyo pakati kwe Turkey ne Servia isahambiseka mangoku, ilizwana
elitelele i-Servia yi Montenegro. Ngokumalunga hamadabi aseleke aliwa
kunqabile ukwahlula elona cala loyisayo ukuba lilipina. Ezivela e-Turkey
indaba ziti imikosi yakona yoyisile kuwo onke amadabi aliweyo, ababi
baninzi abafayo kwelawo icala kwaza kwelama Servia kwabulawa amawaka
atile. Ama Servia wona ati ababaninzi abafe kwicala lawo, kanjalo
imikosi yase Turkey ngoku iselizamela ukubuya ngomva. Nokoke elona cala
ngati linenyaniso lelase Turkey.

Into ebisabuleleka ukusuka kweyokugqibela imikombe kukuba kungekangeni
zizwe zimbi. Kuke kwako ukoyikela, ukuti iya kungena i-Russia kwicala
lase Servia, ize ke i-England ingene kwele Turkey. Ke- kaloku ngoku
kubonakala ukungati iya kuhlala ne Russia kuze ngoko kungafuneki nganto
okwe England ukuzifaka.

inqwelo zimakulu matatu, inkanunu zisixenxe, nabatwali bempahla

U-JAMES PEARCE

WASE Mgwamanzi pesheya kwe Nciba uyazisa ukuba usand’ ukufumana into
eninzi yempahla ezongezelela kwintlobo ntlobo ezibe seziko
kwangapambili; uyazisa ke ukuba uyazitengisa kwizihlobo zake
ezingamamfengu ngamanani afanelekileyo.

Kwezimpahla zisand'ukufika kuko intlobontlobo zengubo ezise zitungiwe,
iminqwazi yamadoda neyamakwenkwe neyentombi. Iminqwazi yamanenekazi
nebonnet, intlobontlobo zeprint ezizipeni ezintandatu ngeyard
nezingapezulu—into zokwenza izivato, nezintlobo zemicako kutiwa yilama.

Izivato zomtshato ezihle kanye, neminqwazi nezinye izinto ezifanelo
lomini—izihlamju, isali— ikomityi ozibekile nozimbiza—ipuluwa, No. 75,
nezikali zazo—incawa nama felani— ihempe zekoton nengubo zokwandlala
ezibedini.

Ngokufutshane kulovenkile yake ungayifumana yonke into oyifunayo
ngamanani afanelekileyo.

ISAZISO SAKOMKULU.

NGELIXESHA langoku asikuko nokuba bayafuneka abantu bokuza kusebenza
kwezindlela zenqwelo zomlilo ezenziwayo kwindawo ngendawo zekoloni.
U-Rulumente ke ufuna ukubakutaza abo bancedisayo ukufuna amadoda okuza
kusebenza. Uya kuti ke ngoko kosukuba etumele amadoda anokusebenza
angengapantsi kwamashumi amabini anesihlanu, avumayo ukuze asebenze
ixesha elingengapantsi kwenyanga ezintandatu efumana umvuzo omiyo kakade
—otumele amadoda anjalo uya kuvuzwa ngoluhlobo:—

Ukuba aya kweyase Worcester ngase Kapa wonikwa £3 ngendoda inye
ayitumeleyo.

Ukuba kukweyase Uitenhage ibe £1 ngendoda.

Kweyase Bushmans River Poort ibe (e-Qora) 10s. ngendoda.

Amaqela amadoda afuna ukuya kweyase Worcester aya kwamkelwa emkombeni e-
Monti. Umntu oya kuya kubanikela ezandleni zezipata-mandla apo e-Monti
wofumana iponti ngoinntu emnye ongeneyo emkombeni.

Imali erolwayo ngemini kwabasebenzayo 2s. 6d. kunye nokudla ukuba
bakwindlela yase Worcester. Ukuba bakweyase Bushmans River Poort ibe 2s.
6d. kube kupela.

Abaye kweya ngase Kapa boti ukuba bahlale umnyaka kona mhla babuyayo
bangabizwa mali ngokungeniswa emkombeni.

  JOHN X. MERRIMAN. Umpatiswa Mihlaba

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1887.

Into engumangaliso yile yokuba u Sir G-. SPRIGG, inteto yake ibe
nesihlahla esivela kwabamtumele ingxelo eziti— “ Ubunxila kokona
bukulileyo ngoku kunoko bekunjalo zingekavalwa ezindawo zavalwayo.”
Isimanga ke eso. Abafundisi abahamba pakati kwabantu babo ngeveki, bati
bona eyabo ingxelo—“ Ngoku abantu banengqondo eziqabukileyo zokuba
namhla bengasanxili.” Bakwa nqina ababambi ukuba abasaboni beve zinto
zilumezisa amazinyo ngezindlela zinenkanti ezivaliweyo, njengoko fudula
kunjalo ngapambili. Kanti noko kutiwa—“ Bumandundu ngakumbi ubunxila
ngoku.” Ukuba ke bungaba buko, buhleli ndaweni iyintoni na lento
bungabonakaliyo? Ingxelo ezeleyo ebonisa ukuba ukuvalwa kwendawo
ezivaliweyo kube luncedo ingafunyanwa kwi pepa le Secretary yengqu-
ngqutela yabafundisi, kanjalo ikwako kumapepa anjenge Argus ne Cape
Times. Kodwa ke eyona nto iyintloko asiluhlobo emiswa ngalo lento
zingxelo ezipambeneyo nazo ngokwazo zabaxelela u Rulumente malunga
nokuvalwa kwe nkanti, nento ekuvele yona kulonto pakati kwalo mabalana.
Eyona nto yokukangelwa yile yokuvulela ukuba kurwetywe ngotywala pakati
kwabantu abazidenge nabasuke imvuma nje kodwa, ngento esekuko iziganeko
zamava ayo. U Rulumente osukuba enqwenela ukunga abantu abapantsi kwake
bangonwaba, balungelwe, yimfanelo yake ukubakangelela abo bantu kwinto
ezinjalo, etabatela kwizizatu zamapoma enyaniso ezimiswayo ngento
esukuba inje ngale yotywala, abe ngokwenjenjalo ekangelela abo bantu
bake.

UKUCOLACOLA.

AMADUNTSUDUNTSU.—Liti ipepa eliyi Utengage Times ite indoda engu John
Walsh igoduka isiya edolopini yase Tinara ivela kuzingela, yetu kumadoda
amabini antsundu, ate akuyibona ayivulela ituba ukuba icande pakati
kwawo, aye nawo ebekisa kwangase Tinara. Ite yakulunga pakati kwawo,
irakele enye isiti—“ Beta!” akuba, encaze necuba, ete yalilandula. Kute
emva koko, yasel’itshotshobela enye, yati yona kulowo—“ She- nxela paya,
ndokudubula mna! ” Ute lowo kwakusitshiwo kuye wasel’enxamela ukubeta,
wasel’elumeka ngompu umlungu kwa oko, emdubula emilenzeni, watsho walala
pantsi endleleni eyinxwelera, wavakala evulela ekala. Omnye wabonakala
ekamuluka, erole induku, watsho “ mba ” kunene emagxeni kumlungu, waza
ke umxosa lowo wabetwa ngesikanda sompu, yapuka nentonga leyo, yaba-
leka londoda yela ehlatini, yaza inxwelera leyo yasala ilele endleleni
apo. Lento kwasa umlungu lowo eya kuyibika komkulu. Omnye lowa wela kwa
ntsiza akudange kuva- kale nto ngaye.

ABENZAKELEYO EMINXUNYENI.—Ati amapepa kuko amadoda amahlanu antsundu
aququnjelwe zimbumbulu zokududumisa umhlaba. Kute kuba
bekungatelekelelwe nto ngababantu kuba zazifakwe ngumlungu otile akazi-
dudumisa, suke kwatsha kwakuba kusetyenzwa, kwenza- kala abobantu
bobahlanu. Omnye ngati uyakupulukana nengalo nemilenze yonke lonto.
Abane benzakele kunene kwindawo ngendawo. Bonke basiwe e Hospitaleni.

OZIBULELE NGOKUNCAMA.—Ati amapepa kuko ndoda itile kwele golide
Witwaterstrand ifunyenwe isisimpukumpa esingenantloko, emva kokuba
kwakuvakele isitonga sompu, kanti izidubule nge danamayiti. Kutiwa ite
isapilile yamane izililela ngenteto eziti—Yati mhla yeza e South Africa
ivela e Australia yecita i £120 kanti akukonto iya kuyibuyela kulonto;
ke namhla ite nokuza kunka kweli yayisel’iyinto eyeza ibadula ngenyawo,
ehamba ixwaye incawa emagxeni, ingumpa wezala, ukuvela kwayo e Delagoa
Bay, apo ibisebenza kwaloliwe kwelozwe.

INGOZI YOMPU.—Kutiwa ute u Mr. no Mrs. Law base Cambridge besalungisa
izitulo zabo endlwini, kanti kuko umpu omi egumbini, unxashiwe suke wawa
pantsi, le kata ilahle ku Mrs. ngasemva, nokoke selebuy’epila kwakona.

INDUDUMO NOMBANE.—Ati amapepa kuke kwako indudumo enkulu eke yako ngase
Rini, ote umbane watsho kwakubi kumfo obe hamba nenqwelo u Mr. Arthur
Lee, ekwabetwa inkabi ezi 15 esipanini sake, kwafa za ntlanu kwa oko,
waza umbexeshi wafunyanwa elaliswe ngomhlana efe isiduli, kodwa wabuya
wavuka. Kwabetwa nomncunuba osesitiyeni sika Mr. Jolly, engekute ukuba
awubehlanga ucinywe lomlilo, ngesitshile isitali sika Mr. Jones.

IMISEBENZI YOTYWALA BAMA XOSA.—Amapepa axokozela kakulu ngomsebenzi
oveliswe butywala bama Xosa obebuselwa e Bulu ngase Iwandle kule nyanga
ifileyo. Ati kwakuko iqela labantsundu elaliyawahlanganisene kona ukuba
lisele utywala, kwaza kwangeniswa ibekile endlwini ka Keosh, kwaye kuko
umfo ekutiwa ngu Diniso, owasuka wayiqetula wayipalaza lobekile. Kute
kwa oko wasel’ewelwa ngenteto zintombi ezimbini, ate kwakuba njalo,
waqumba, wasel’erola imela, egwaza kuyo yonke into eyayi kulondlu
wabahlaba kakubi abafazi ababini nomntu omnye oyindoda. Lowo uyindoda
kwatiwa kata pezu kwebele ngasekohlo, nasetangeni, wenziwa inxwelera
embi kanye, kodwa ke ugqityelwe equbela ekupileni.

UKUBUNGCA KWABABULALI.—Ati amapepa kuko amadoda amabini awaye gwetyelwe
ukufa kwango April odluleyo, ke lamadoda kwasa engaseko, abaleka
ngobusuku be 19 ka August. Kuvakele isingqi sokumka kwawo kwi sentile le
polisa lase luwuhlaba kwamanye, apuma kwedabalala ukusukela,
kwakusekubonakala mtinzi mnye, ate abekisa kuwo ukudubula, nawo
awuboniswa kukuti menye kombane. Emka omabini kwapela ngobobusuku. Ite
enye yanxaxela kwinkosi etile yom Mpondo ite apo yapo- swa ngemicinga
yenxanxosi kwasa isiwa kwase Mtata. Enye yayi ngekabonakali.

UTYWALA BAMA XOSA E QONCE.—Ati amapepa kuke kwako ityala lomqomboti
kwakona pambi kwemantyi enkulu yase Qonce. Elityala namhla bekubanjwe u
Joseph Mekeni, ngokutengisa utywala bama Xosa e Nyutawuni (Newtown)
engena license. U Joseph lowo ufike wamelwa ngu Mr. Innes, nakuba
kungancedanga nento le. Ite imantyi pambi kokunika isigwebo yabonisa
isizatu sokusinda kwabanye ababe kwamangalelwe ngokutengisa kwabo
umqomboti, ke namhla le into ayifani nezo kuba ubunqina bufezekile
kanye. U Joseph udliwe i £15— engenazo ahlale inyanga ezintatu
etolongweni. [Nango kambe amasi enu benzi nabaseli bomqomboti. Fan’ukuba
limnyama namhlanje kulomfo ka Mekeni. Namhla uti, ukuwabiza—“ Ngamasi

ABAPULUKWE LICAM.—Ati amapepa apesheya kolwandle kute kwabuba maxego
atile abe zizityebi e Nottingham ango Mr. no Mrs. C.—Ate akububa
babuduzela kunene abantwana bawo ukuza kubuta amafa abo e Nottingham
apo. Bafike bayimisa ngokuyimisa indlu leyo yakokwabo, ekute koko
kupililinga, u Mrs. C. omncinane, wafumana kwigumbi elitile umngxubungu
ate akawunqika wafumana umqulu weziteyizi (stays) ezidala, namapepa
asongeneyo ancolileyo, kwanenwele, izinto awati ukuzicapukela oku
akafana nanto, wasel’esiti mazikutshelwe pandle zitshiswe. Zatshiswa ke
okunene, ekangele naye, sel’emane ezilungisa nangokwake ukuba zide
zitshe kamsinya. Kute ngelinye ixa kwe petu elinye kula mapepa avutayo,
“ yo! lipepa lemali uvakele esitsho." Kanti lelamashumi £50-—Basebe-
pangisa ukuwopula. bafumana £800 kupela, ekubeni yonke lonto
ib’ingamawaka amashumi matandatu eponti £60,000, indleko esimanga.

UQWITELA OLUNAMANDLA.— Kutiwa lipepa eliyi pendent kuke kwako uqwitela
olunamandla kunene e Mount Frere, lunotuli, olubete Iwaxobula izinki
ebezifulele izitali ezitsha, zasasazwa ezweni lonke zaqekezwa, zaya
kulahlwa kumgama okude. Inxenye yopahla iye kuxinzelelwa kolwendlu ka
Watson, Iwatsho lonakalisa kulondawo lubete kuyo, kodwa akwabiko mntu
wenzakalayo.

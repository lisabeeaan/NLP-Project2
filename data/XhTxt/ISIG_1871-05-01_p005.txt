Isigidimi Samaxosa

IMFAZWE YASE EUROPE.

UXOLO!

NGOLWA 27 ku February ingcingo zase Europe nezase America zazixinile
kanye, zihlaba umkosi wokuba uxolo lude lwafika. Ezindaba zatula umtwalo
obuseluhleli kanzima pezu kwezigidi zabantu. Abo ba- hlala emazantsi
E-Africa, ngokukodwa abamnyama, abanakuza babuqonde kanye ubungakanani
balemfazwekazi. Ibe nyanga zisixenxe kanye. Ngezinyanga ke kuliwe
amadabi ama 23, ngapandle kwamadatyana ama 49 abengengakanani. Imizi
eyatatyatwa ngokusuka abemi bayo baraulwe, ima 20. Owokugqibela ibe yi
Paris ngenqu, eyona nqaba inkulu ehlabatini lonke. Abantu abakuyo yodwa
bakwizigidi ezibini. Abantu balapa E- Koloni, kususela E-Kapa de kuye
kuma nge Nciba, bangamawaka angamakulu amatandatu (600,000). Abantu
balomzi wodwa kengoko babagqita abalapa ngo- kupindwe katatu.

Kwelinye lalamadabi, elase Gravelotte, abantu ababesilwa babe ngamawaka
anga­makulu omane anamanci osibozo (480,000) ama German engamawaka
angamakulu mabini anamanci osixenxe (270,000), ama French engamawaka
angamakulu amabini ananci nye (210,000). Elo lalandelwa lidabi lase
Sedan, ekwakuko ama German angamawaka angamakulu mabini ananci nye
(210,000), efunza kuma French anga­mawaka akulu nye linamanci mahlanu
(150,000). Ngase Metz, ngentsuku ezi- mbini inkosana zemikosi yama
German ezabulawayo, nezanxwelerwayo zaba ma- kulu matandatu (600),
ngapandle kwa madodanje amawaka ashuminye linesixenxe (17,000).

Apo alwelwa kona amadabi, nokulande- lana kwawo—kwakuse Weissemburg, E-
Woerth, E-Spichern, E-Pange, E-Mar La

Tour, E-Gravelotte E-Beaumont, E-Sedan, E-Naisseville, elangase Metz,
E-Orleans amatatu, E-Amiens, E-Champigny, E- Brie, elangase Paris,
E-Beaugency, E- Bapaume, E-Vendome, E-Le Mans, E- Belfort, E-Saint
Quentin, E-Saint Cloud nelase Valerien. La okugqibela omabini alwelwa
pantsi kwendonga zase Paris.

Kunqabile kanye ukulixela inani labantu ababuleweyo ngalemfazwekazi
inkulu. Kodwa ngoku ama French awangamabanjwa E-Germany angapezu
kwamawaka angamakulu matatu anamanci mahlanu (350,000).

Emva kwenyanga zosixenxe zobubi obu- ngaka akummangaliso ukuba babe
abantu bagcoba kunene kwa kukankanywa uxolo. Kodwake kwafuneka ukuba ama
French alutenge kanzima. Akupa isiqendu esikulu somhlaba wawo esi kutiwa
yi Alsace ne Lorraine. Lomhlaba ukweze lomlambo kutiwa yi Rhine. Lilizwe
elityebileyo elinemizi emikulu, nabantu abaninzi, ne- nqabaezomeleleyo.
Ayakuhlaula iponti ezi- zizigidi ezimakulu mabini(£200,000,000),
ezihlaulela indleko ezihlele ama German ngenxa yemfazwe. Kwelinani
kufuneka ukuba iponti ezizizigidi ezingamashumi omane (£40,000,000)
zirolwe ngalonya- ka; zize izigidi ezingamashumi osibozo (£80,000000)
zirolwe nge 1872 ; ize ese- leyo, ezizigidi ezikwamashumi osibozo
(£80,000,000), irolwe nge 1873.

Ati ama German akumisa ezindawo zinje ukuba nzima kwazo, lati elinye
iqela lama French mayiqutywe imfazwe. Kodwa I-Rulumente yase France
yanikwa intsuka ezintatu kwakupela zokuba inike impendulo yayo. Kwati
ngelixa kusafunwa impendulo, iqela lama German, elimadoda angamawaka
amashumi matatu (30,000) langena E-Paris, ukuze umzi lowo ube uba
semandleni alo kude kuvunyelwane ngo-xolo. Lahlala eloqela intsuku
zombini, kwati kwakuvunyelwana ngoxolo lapuma. Inkoliso ngoku
selisendleleni yokugoduka, ukusinga E-Germany, emveni kokulwa into
ezinkulu zamadabi ezima 23, kunga- : zanga ke kubeko dabi kungatiwa kulo

oyiswa kuyapi, ngalo lonke eloxesha.

U-Napoleon, obeyinkosi yase France, uhlutiwe obobukosi, kwatiwa akakwazi
ukulaula. Kumiswe luhlobo lumbi lokulaula ekutiwa ukubizwa yi republik.
Koluhlobo akuze kwabiko nkosi, nokuba mhlaumbi yinkosikazi, koko kuya
kumiswe amadoda atile, ngabantu ngokwabo, bawamisele iminyaka etile.

Kwanini, I-France ibililizwe elisoloko latanda ukulwa, kodwa lemfazwe
igqitisele ngamashwa, nangokuyitoba, kuzo zonke eyaka yazilwa. Kode kuke
kuqengqeke isizukulwana, iziqamo zalemfazwe zisa bandezele kanzima pezu
kwelozwe. Iziqamo zayo ngokumalunga nemali zezokuba kuya kongezeleleka
kwityala lelohlanga iponti ezikwizigidi ezingamakulu omatatu anamanci
mahlanu (£350,000,000), mhla­umbi izigidi zibe ngamakulu omane
(£400,000,000)—singasateti nto ngemizi eyonakalisiweyo, nendawo ezitile
zelozwe ezibujisiweyo.

Inxenye yomkosi wama Prussia iyakusala kwindawo ngendawo zase France ide
ibatalwe yonke lomali.

Lude lwafika uxolo ekupeleni, nabantu bayabulela ku Lowo unokwenza ukuba
“ zipele imfazwe kude kube sencamini yomhlaba.”

UKUHLUMA.

INKOLISO yesizukulwana ebe sesingamadoda ama- kulu ngale ka Mlanjeni
imfazwe, idlule. Lama Xosa akulayo kaloku akula engqongwe yimfundo.
Impikiswano edla ngokubako pakati kobudenge nobulumko ibonakala ngati
iyadamba. Endaweni yokuba ngakumbi isike ingati zifuna ukuba zizih- lobo
ezizinto zombini. Into ebedla ngokulila ngayo Umxosa “ngumhlaba,” noko
ubanzikangaka nje ongamiweyo. Kodwa mhlaumbi ingati kanti eyona mbangi
yokuba ama Xosa atyalele ngase Ntshonalanga, yayi kukoyika izizwe
ezazingase Mpumalanga kuwo. Kodwa ke nokuba imbangi yayiyiyipina
akazanga ayeka ukufuna umhlaba, nalapo ebengena kuwufumana.

Sisand’ukubaliselwa ngendawana esiti ibonisa ukuhluma kwabantsundu.

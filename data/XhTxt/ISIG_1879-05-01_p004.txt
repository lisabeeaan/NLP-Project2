E-TRANSVAAL.

EKUEENI kuka March kude kube ngu April ama Bulu ase Transvaal ebene
ntlanganiso enkulu kakulu yokuxoxa ngokupatwa kwawo ngamangesi. Into
yokuqala ibe kukubika inteto ebipakati kuka Mr. Joubert no Mhlekazi
u-Sir Bartle Frere. U-Mr. Joubert lowo waye tunywe e-Natal ngama Bulu
ukuba aye kuwacelela ukuzipata. Ubike ke Umhlekazi uti ayisenakutiwani
londawo, apetwe apetwe. Bekuko nenye incwadi evela Kumhlekazi ecela
ukuba ama Bulu akupe umkosi oya kwa Zulu.

Pambi kokuba zixoxwe ezondawo, amadoda amakulu ate makugxotwe bonke
abeze kutengisa utywala, avakalisa nokuba wonke umntu makaze avunyelwe
ukuba atete njengokubona kwake. Indawo efike yabekwa pambi
kwentlanganiso ibe yeyokuba makutiwenina njengokuba nje Amangesi esiti
i- Transvaal ayi nakubuya izipate. Abanye bavele nelokuti
makusekusenzeka okwenzekayo. Uninzi lute masiyi lumkele noko londawo,
make sicele sitete, kuba ukulwa nabe Lungu akuyi kusinceda nto, mhlaumbi
sozi- citela netenjana esinalo lokubuya silifumane ilizwe letu.
Abatshoyo bavelise iqinga lo- kuhlangana nenkosi yase Transvaal kuqala
u-Major Lanyon, beve ukuba akangebina na ngoku unokuwatembisa ukuba aya
kubuya ayekwe azipate. Lilandelwe ke elo qinga, kwatunyelwa incwadi
yokumema ukuba abe sentlanganisweni. Efikile ute yena akana lizwi lokuba
angazipata ama Bulu, kodwa ke utembisa ukuba uya kuwenzela
uloliwe,alungise indlela zenqwelo, kumiswe ucingo neflag endala ye
Transvaal idibane nale yama Ngesi bati yi Union Jack. Ate ama Bulu
ukupendula wona afuna ukuba azipate kupela, zonke ezinye ezi izinto
akateti ngazo.

Emva koko kuvunyelwene ngokuba kuli- ndwe ukuiika kuka Sir Bartle Frere
kuviwe ukuba uya kutini na yena xa alapa. Ngencwadi ecela umkosi wokuya
kwa Tshaka ama Bulu atumele impendulo eti wona akananto yokwenza nalo
mfazwe.

Ide i-Governor, u-Sir Bartle Frere, wafika ngomhla we 10 ku April. Ama
Bulu am- kauleleyo amamkele kakuhle, amvuyela kakulu, amnika nencwadi
ebonisa ukuvuya kwawo ukuba ezile namhla. Ute naye uvuyisiwe kukuba ebe
nokuza, noko emangalisiwe kukuti abanye akutembisa ukuba uyeza
bangakolwa. Ute ke uzele ukuba bonke abantu bayigcine imiteto ekoyo kuze
kukutshwe amadoda alumkileyo ancedisane naye ngendlela elifanel’
ukulaulwa ngayo elilizwe. Akanikanga temba kwase ntloko lokuba ama Bulu
angabuya azipate. Kodwa umise usuku angabuya ahlangane nawo ngalo.

E-NTLOMBANE.

Elase Ntlombane. Omnye olibalisayo uti, kwati ngomhla 27 ku March,
wapuma umkosi wamahashe, we Nkosazana, udibene nowama setlani noka Raaf
noka Weatherley noka Schermbrucker neqelana labantsundu. Usingise
kwintaba ye Ntlombane, apo bekuvakele ukuba kuko amawaka amabini ase
mpini. Ngemini elandelayo ifunze kwakusasa eyetu impi, ibona inkomo
zisidla entabeni. Bate abebenazo balwa buyekelela, wada owetu umkosi
waba woyisile, wazitabata inkomo, waya kukulula pezulu entabeni.
Kuhlaliwe kulondawo iyure ezine. Ngequbuliso kuvakele izitonga ngase
kohlo, kwabonakala kwaoko imikosi emitatu yama Zulu, yaraulwa eyetu
impi. Indlela yokubaleka ibe inye nayo ise ivaliwe. Kweletu icala ube
ngulowo wazibalekela okwake, babulawa kakubi. Abasindileyo ngabafumane
babona sebepumele emabubini ama Zulu. Oka Weatherley upantsi ukubujiswa
kuti tu, kwasinda amadoda asixenxe kwakupela. Kuko amare okuba no
Cetywayo ebebonela xa kuliwayo. Inkomo ezo besesite sezitinjiwe zibuye
zasala nabaninizo.

E-KAMBULA.

Kute ngemini elandelayo ama Zulu aza kungenela u-Colonel Wood e-Kambula.
Uti kwincwadi ayibale ngomhla 30 ka March:—

Kuse ndaweni yam ukukubikela ukuba izolo sibe singenelwe ngumkosi wama
Zulu obupetwe ngu Mnyamana no Tyangwayo. Kuvakele ukuba umkosi lowo
unduluke komkulu ngomhla 24 wawutunywe ukuze uze kundenza into eyenziwa
e-Sandlana. Imikosi ka Cetywayo ibe yenziwe maqela matatu, elinye lasala
komkulu elinye laya e-Kowa, elinye leza kum. Obuza kundiraula ndiwuve
kwangemini engapambili ngomnye wabantu baka Oham, ote ute akuwubona
lomkosi walitula ilapi lokubonisa ukuba ungowakwa Rulumente. Bate
abebemazi bamxelela ukuba ngomso siya kungenela u-Wood. Uhambe nayo ke
impi leyo wada walala nayo. Kute ekuseni wazimela waya kuxelela u-Raaf
wasibonisa nalapo utshaba luya kuti tu kona. Kute ukuba ibete imini,
abonakala eti tu ama Zulu. Abiziwe ama­doda abeye kuzingela,
zahlanganiselwa eka- mpini inkomo, ngapandle kwezingamakulu amabini
ezisuke zakude, ngokuti kanti abalusi bazimele.

KWA ZULU.

KWEZI ntsuku zokuza kubala kwetu zisate nqum indaba zakwa Zulu, esoti
ngoko ke siwabalise ngokungati kuzele namadabi esake sawakankanya
ngapambilini. Elokuqala lelase Ntombi esolibalisa ngamazwi omfo obalela
i-Transvaal Argus. Uti, “Asikuko nokuba ndinosizi ukukubikela ngenye
inxwaleko esihlele ngomhla 12 ka March, e-Ntombi, apo kufe iqela labantu
bakuti. Ngangoko ndinokuqonda kukutshwe umkosi opetwe ngu Captain
Anderson ukuba ukape inqwelo zeruluwa nokudla, ezisuka e-Lydenburg.
Uzikapile ke okunene zada zeza e-Ntombi. Ngomhla 5 u-Captain Anderson
uzekubizwa ekampini, wafumana wazishiya inqwelo ezo. Ngowe sixenxe umhla
u-Captain Moriarty undululwe namadoda alikulu elinamatatu ukuba
aziputume ezonqwelo. Ufike umlambo uzele yaka yambambezela lonto, noko
wada wawela ngemiti emidala. Apa ezibukweni kushiywe amadoda amashumi
matatu anamahlanu ukuba asale elilungisa epekela nabawelileyo. Wona ke
asele no Lindop, waza u-Moriarty no Johnson bagqitela ezinqweleni. Bade
baya kufika kuzo, zaza ke zeza kumiswa elunxwemeni lomlambo ngomhla we
11 ka March. Icala elivulekileyo laba leli lingase mlanjeni. Kulelwe ke
umlambo uzalisa kodwa kute cwaka, kungabonakali base lutshabeni. Ekuseni
ngemini elandelayo kuhlokome isitonga kwako oti utshaba luyeza. Onke
amadoda alunge kwaoko. Ibe ke inkungu ite vingci. Ite ukuqala kokuti
qwenge kwayo ama Zulu agalela isicoto sembumbulu ekampini, kanti
selerulube akwalapa. Adubule ezo zitonga zodwa ayilahla imipu afunza
pakati ngezikali, asuka kaloku adibana namasoldati yangumvubo ombi, um
Zulu wagaulela pantsi ngezembe nesirele. Abasaseleyo bakubon’ ukuba
konakele bazipose emlanjeni. Alandele ama Zulu abahlabela emanzini
abanye, kwasinda balishumi elinababini kwakupela kwabebese kampini, nabo
bancedwa leliya qela belishiywe nganeno komlambo ukuba lisale lilungisa
izibuko. Lite eloqela aba kusoloko lenze isitonga esinye ukudubula kuma
Zulu, ngokukodwa asukela ababalekayo. Kude kwako ama Zulu awelela
nganeno, kwabonakala ke ukuba mabazincede nabo.

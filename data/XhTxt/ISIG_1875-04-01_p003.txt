Kule yetu ikoloni bati abanye kunokuba kuyiwe koma India makuzanywe kwa
ezi zizwe zalapa. Singesiwabulela amadoda ase ntloko yekoloni ukuba
ezamela ukunga kungade kushukume abalapa ngapezu kokuba kuyiwe kumazwe

luncedo apa nakwabo bawafunayo. Indawo ke kodwa esifuna ukukangelisa
kuyo yile yokuba bangati befikile ibe lonto yenye yeziya kutintela
ukunyuka kwabantsundu. Kungokubona londawo lento siti, sibe sifanele
ukubabulela abenza imizamo yokushukumisa kwalapa, abangekavumi ukuncama.

Kwezi zikwakufupi izizwe sifumana ukuba U-C. Levey Esq. umiselwe
ukuhamba hamba pakati kwazo efuna abangavumayo ukuya kusebenza. Kuko
kanjalo omnye umfo omiselwe ukuya kubafuna abantu kwizizwe ezingase
Diamond nangapaya kwayo kude kuse ngase Zambesi,. Londoda isand’
ukutumela ilizwi lokuba ifumene amadoda amawaka matandatu(6,000)
avumileyo ukuza kusebenza iminyaka emibini nemitatu ; angati mhlaumbi
ahlale nangapezulu. Azela ukuza kutabata nokuba nguwupina umsebenzi.
Indleko yokuwazisa kweli lizwe ziponti ezilishumi elinesibini ngendoda;
oko kukuti ewonke aya kurolelwa £72,000. Imali ke leyo eya kupuma kwi
Government.

I-Government ikupe ilizwi lokuba uya kuti umntu, nokuba liqela labantu
abaya kubatala £6 kulemali yokuhliswa kwabo bafumane inani abalitandayo
ukuba lize kusebenza kubo ; baze ke kodwa ukuwavuza bawarolele iponti
kunye nokudla ngenyanga, . Siya temba ukuba iyakunceda londawo kubo
bonke.

Amatamsanqa onke afikele pezu kwetu, kodwa kubonakala ukuba siti abaza
kuba semva. Sesiza kudlulwa nangabavela E-Zambesi. Kungaba na kuzaliseka
ukuti, “ abasemva baya kuba pambili?”

Ngokumalunga nabo bezileyo siyatemba ukuba baya kuncedakala nangapezu
kwaba baze kubasebenzela. Kweli lase mlungwini bazakufika bafunde ukuba
indlela yokunyuka ngumsebenzi, yinkutalo; babone ukuba ngapandle kwawo
akako amatemba; baya kubona ukuba namanene, into zonantsi ezingaswele
nto komawazo, bonke bate bukuqu emsebenzini. Ukuba ngabantu abakangeleyo
ngamehlo abo baza kubona apa intlobontlobo zokukaulezisa nokucokisa
umsebenzi, intlalo yabelungu ukulunga kwayo ngapezu kweyabantsundu.
Bebefanele kanjalo ukumka sebengabantu abaqelene nomsebenzi ; baze
bakubuyela emakaya babonise amakowabo ukuba afanele ukutinina. Silindele
kanjalo ukuba kobako abakubonayo ukulunga kwemfundo nangapezu koko
senjenjalo tina balapa. Ngapezu kwento zonke sitemba ukuba kuko abaya
kugoduka sebefunde ukwazi U-Kristu ekuruseshiwe bamtabatela ukuba abe
ngumsindisi wabo, abaya kufika bamazise kwabanye. Siya kumbula incwadi
ka Rev. G. Tyamzashe awati kuyo xa axela izinto ezilungileyo eziveliswe
kukuza kwabantu kwele Diamond, wakankanya ukuti kuko abade bangabi
sagoduka sebetanda ukuhlala apo kuko ilizwi. Siyatemba ukuba kuya kuba
njalo nakwaba beze apa.

Siyawuvuyela ke ngoko umsebenzi owe- nziwa yi Government. Siyavuya
kanjalo nokubona amapepa atile ale Koloni eyitetelela londawo. Ukuba
izinto ziqubeke njengoko bekufanele ukuba njalo lento iyakuba luncedo
kumacala omabini—kwabamhlo- pe nabantsundu.

obusand’ ukutatyatwa ngu Mpondo kumntu ontsundu opantsi kwa Mangesi,
U-Ndamas wabuya wawubuyisela wamhlaulisa lomntu. Inkosi engu Mhlontlo

UKUBALWA KWBANTU E-GRAAFF-REINET— Umfo obeye kubala E-Gaaff-Reinet,
esikolwa ukuba ubengumlungu, ubuya engalibali ukuba nzima kwalo msebenzi
pakati kwabantsundu. Uti ukuxela ubudala bebesiti, omnye
ndandingumntwana ngexa lokulwa kuka Mzilikazinama Bulu, umfazi warn
wayeyintombazana oko bekuseko amakoboka. Omnye, mna ndandingaka (atsho
alinganise nge- sandla) oko bekuseko amatyipkolo apa E-Graaff Reinet,
omnye mna ndazalwa ngexa lokulwa kuka Mshweshwe na Batembu, umfazi
wazalwa ngomgea. Kupela okoke, sekufuneka ukuba azifumanele ngo kwake
umbali.

OBULELE UMFAZI WAZIBULALA NAYE.

KWEZIVEKI zidluleyo, kwihotela abati yi Hell-Poort, epakati kwe Rini ne
Cradock kuhle into embi kunene. Lehotela ibiyeka Mrs Thornton ondoda
yabubela E-Carlisle Bridge. Kute ngomhla 9 ka February, U-Mrs Thornton
lowo watshata nomfo abati ngu Henry Lawrence Tivey. Bahlalisene impilo
embi kunene, ekuhlala kwa ngxoliswana. Ite kanjalo indoda yaya intywila
ebunxileni. Umfazi uyitetise kakulu ngalento, wamana eyitetela amazwi
amabi esongela ukuti ukuba ayikuyeki ukusela uza kubaleka ayishiye. Ude
waqumba U-Tivey wanxasha umpu wati uza kumdubula umfazi, eloxa ke
besekitshini bobabini. Imotuse kunene lento umfazi, wapuma wabalekela
kumfo abati ngu Bell obemi ngapandle wacela ukuba ayinqande indoda
kulento. Ite yona kuye uyinto efileyo uke wasondela ; utsho waselebamba
emfazini, imbumbulu yangena emagxeni, wenza udwamba kwalapo. Iye
kwasendlwini lendoda yabuya yaunxasha umpu, yapuma iselisongela
ukudubula U-Bell no Hopkins ; kodwa yafika sebengaseko, sebebalekile.
Utabateke isidumba somfazi wasisa endlwini esihlube ingubo ; wanxiba
yena ezake zecawa njengokungati ngumntu obelinga ukubaleka. Kubonakala
kodwa ukuba uqondile ukuti akasenakubaleka. Usike waselezidubula,
imbumbulu yangena ecaleni. Kubonakala ukuba akahlange aqauke. Uyigqibe
yonke indlu eyihlisa eyinyusa ehamba ngezandla namadolo. Ude wakungena
pantsi kwebedi apo kufikwe efele kona. Umfazi uze kuncwatyelwa
kumancwaba ase Rini, umbulali yena wafumana wapandelwa umnxuma endle
wasongelwa eblanketini, wafakwa kona engenzelwanga netyeya.

INTLANGANISO YOMNYAKA YABAFU-
NDISI ABANTSUNDU BAMAWESILE,
ABALUNGE NE RINI.

(Esiyitunyelweyo.)

INTLANGANISO yomnyaka yabafundisi abantsundu bama Wesile nonyaka nje ite
yase Nqushwa, yako ngomhla wa 24 nowa 26 ka February. Ngapandle
kwabafundisi abantsundu bekuko kwabamhlope aba.—Rev. W. Impey, Umpati
wesihlalo, Revds W. Holden, E. Gedye, T. Cresswell, no H. Barton;
kwanabasafunda E-Nxukwebe, befundela ubufundisi. Ababini kubafundisi
abantsundu ababangako, U-Abram Mabula no Joel Ndungane. Lontlanganiso
asikuko nokuba ibimmandi inoncedo. Zite inkonzo zako kweyabamhlope
neyabantsundu ityalike. Kute kanjalo bacelwa bonke abazalwana abebeko,
kunye neqela elikulu lamalungu eremente ukuba batabate ndawonye isidlo
se Nkosi. Omnye umsebenzi oke wenziwa kukuviwa kwabafundisi abase
semlingweni, no- kwabasafundela ukungeniswa kulo msebenzi, abebevela
E-Nxukwebe. Omnye wabafundisi abantsundu ute unomnqweno wokunga angaya
kusebenza pakati kwabapesheya kwe Nciba; kwaza kwavunyelwana ukuba
alunge nowase Queenstown umjikelo. Kuvunyelwene kanjalo ukuze kutunyelwe
umfundisi ontsundu ukuba

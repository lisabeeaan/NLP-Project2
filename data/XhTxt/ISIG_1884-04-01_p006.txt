U NTLABATI NO GUAITLE.

E Bayi ngomhla we 19 Feb. 1884 kwafika ematyaleni umfana owazibiza
ngegama elingu Moses Lucas, abati abanye ngu Andrew Lucas, into ka
Ntlabati, weza kucela i warrant (ipepa lokubamba umntu) yokuba kubanjwe
u John Gautile owanduluka lomhla nge train yase Rini. Isimangalo sika
Lucas sesokuba wayeteta no Gautile nge watch ngosuku lom-Vulo (18 Feb.)
u Gautile efuna uku yanana nge pistoli (umpana wenxbwa). wati u Lucas
kulungile woza nompu lowo endlwini yam ngokuhlwa. Uti ke waya nemipu
emitatu kwavumelana ke ngomnye. Wati ke u Lucas maze u Gautile eze
nepepa lompu kusasa kunye nompu, wemka ke. Uvuke kusasa u Lucas waya
kusebenza, wenyuka ngedinala weza kutya ekaya. Uti ute xa aza kuhla
ukuya emsebenzini watula i waist coat yake efuna ukukangela ukuba kuxa
lipina—wafika ingaseko i watch yake. Ubuze ku William ukuba i watch yake
ipina, wati imke no Gautile. Uhlangene nomfana abati ngu Abednego
omxelele ukuba u Gautile ugodukile nge train yakusasa. Kubonakala ukuba
pambi kokuza kwake ematyaleni u Moses Lucas (?) uqale waya kwa Messrs.
Geard, Son, & Co., apo ebesebenza kona u Gautile, waya kuti— “ Idas,
your boy gone to Kaffirland this morning with plenty stolen guns." Ngati
bate bakukangela impahla baxakwa kukuya kwenza i affidavit (isimangalo
esifungelweyo) ngenxa yokungayiqondi imipana engaba imkile. Ngati bate
kwi Informer (Ntamnani) hamba wena uye kufungela ukuba umke ne watch
yako, ukuze anqandwe e Rini kubonwe ukuba kofunyanwa imipu kuye na.
Okwenene ute esihla e trenini u Gautile wasele banjwa. Amanene awaye
naye yayi ngo Messrs. Skepe Kali, Simon Joninga no Henry F. Miya abasuka
bakohlwa ukuba batinina. Ngomhla olandelayo wafika e Bayi u Gautile
kunye nekostaule lo Mlungu. Ute akuziswa pambi kwe Mantyi kwafunwa
ezimpahleni zake kwafunyanwa—1 Pistol, 1 Dagger, ] Cork screw, 2 doz.
Spoons (Silver mounted), 7 Forks (Silver mounted), 7 Pocket knives, 3
Glaziers’ Diamonds, (into zokusika iglasi), 1 watch. Kute kwakubuzwa
wati u Gautile zonke ezompahla wazitenga ku Symes u Mlungwana osebenza
kwa Geard, i watch yona wayitenga ku Andrew Lucas ngom-Gqibelo 16 Feb.
nge 10s, watsho warola i Receipt esayinishwe “ Andrew Lucas.” Ute
akubizwa u Lucas wayikanyela indawo yokutengiselana nge watch,
walikanyela igama lokuba ngu Andrew Lucas, wati yena igama lake ngu “
Moses Lucas." Wati i watch le itatwa ngolwesi-Bini kusasa (19 Feb.) kuko
umfana ofayo

endlwini ogama lingu William. Uvakele u Gautile esiti— “ Yini kangaka
mtaka Ntlabati, lento ude ukanyele negama lako, ubundizonda ngani
kangaka ? ” Ute akubizwa u William akazi nto ngokuza kutatwa kwale watch
ngu Gautile kwabonakala ukuba u Lucas uxoke wazibopa. Iti i Mantyi—“
William u Lucas ute kuti wena uhlala naye kunye nabanye abafana,
ubusekaya yonke leveki usifa, kwafika kusasa ngolwesi-Bini, 19 Feb., u
Gautile esiti utunywe ngu Lucas ukuba aze kutabata i watch, wayitabata
ke wemka nayo ; yinyaniso na leyo itetwa ngu Lucas ? Ute u William “Hayi
nkosi yonke lonto buxoki, no Mlungu wam uyazi ukuba bendisebenza yonke
leveki. I watch le yona ndiyayazi, yeka Stephen Boy—yalahleka etyesini
yake kunye ne 3s. Saranela ku Lucas ngokuba unezitshixo ezininzi.”
Kubonakele xa kulapo ukuba isikuni sibuya nomkwezeli. Ufikile u Stephen
Boy, kunye namanqina amabini u Twini Qoqo no Piet Magazi, abafunga bonke
ukuba le watch yeka Stephen.

Kute kulapo kwavakala amare okuba lomfana u Lucas lento alifihlayo igama
lake waka wabaleka etolongweni apa e Bayi. Kwalandwa ezincwadini
kwafunyanwa ukuba wabeta u Mlungukazi ngapandle kwe Bayi nge 28 Feb.
1880 wagwetywa nge 1 March, 1880 wanikwa inyanga. Kulandwe kwezase
tolongweni ezabonisa ukuba wabaleka kwabanye ababanjwa nge 12 March
1880. Kwasekungenwa kweli—wanikwa inyanga eyoqala kwakupela leya
wabaleka engekayigqibi. Usamelwe lele watch u Lucas ngenxa yobu ntamnani
bake. Uti u Gautile, “ Ndinamanqina amabini ngale watch kutiwa ndiyibe
ngolwesi-Bini. Elinye liyakunq'ina ukuba labona ukumbatala kwam nge 16
Feb. kwa Barnet apo asebenza kona u Lucas. Elinye yi Watchmaker endayisa
kuyo i watch ngom-Vulo 18 Feb., ndarola 10s. Abizwa lomanq'ina afika
okwenene anq'ina ngokunjalo. Kubonakele apa ukuba elokuba i watch ityala
liyakuwela pantsi. Enye impahla le yakwa Geard u Gautile uyimisa
ngokuyimisa amanani ayo, abonise ukuba warola £7 9s ngayo yonke
kumaxesha ngamaxesha. Kugqitywe ekubeni alinde i Jaji. I Mantyi ngati
iyakolwa ukuba u Gautile lemipu uyitengiselwe ngulo Mlungu kuba ude wati
ku Gautile makati ukuba kuko omnye umntu owaka watengiselwa ngu Symes
ambize abe linq'ina pambi kwe Jaji. Noko akangepumeleli kuba uyazi ukuba
u Symes lowo asiyiyo i Sales man (umtengisi) ngum pakishi kodwa, igxagxa
nje elimtengisele ngokum’bela, abe ke ngoko ityala angaba nalo leli bati
yi “ Receiving stolen goods knowing them to be stolen.” (Ukwamkela
impahla ayaziyo ukuba ibiwe).

Ngo Andrew Lucas, alias Andrew Ntlabati, alias Moses Lucas, alias Henry
Lucas, alias Henry Ntlabati Lucas Mseleni, asazi ukuba singacinga
ntonina ngaye. Lento ukudiza omnye umntu intle xa umdizi lowo akwenza
oko ngokuqutywa sisazelo sobulungisa. Asiko ke esi sazelo ku Lucas kuba

endala ekade itshonisa abantwana babantu, ife ngumnqweno yakubona u

EVELA KU RULUMENTE ISIYA
KWI MANTYI YASEDIKENI.

NKOSI,—Ndiyifumene ingxelo yako yoku- gqibela, kekaloku ukuqubela
pambili noku- hluma okuxelayo wena kuyavuyisa kakulu ; ndikucela ukuba
uyibikele inkosi u-Sikunyana ukuba u-Rulumente ukolisiwe kukubona ukuba
yena uwamkele wawenza amazwi okuyala endandimnike wona; u-Rulumente
utemba ukuba lomsebenzi ulungileyo uncomekayo awuqubileyo ngoku e-Lalini
yake awusokuba yinto yentsukwana ezimbalwa ayakubuye adinwe yiyo ;
okokuba woti azivuselele, ati njengokuba kukade engasemva koninzi
lwezinye inkosi, azamele kaloku ukulingana neyona igqite ezinye
ezintweni zonke ezisingisele ekubanyuseni nase kubahlumiseni abantu
bake.

Amamfengu anawo onke amalungelo aba- ntwana baka Rulumente, kodwa uninzi
lwawo aluwatabati lomatuba abekwe pambi kwawo ngu Rulumente na Bafundisi
ngokuti azinyusele kwisimo nobulumko bokuwasebenzisa nokuwaxamla
lomalungelo bawanikwe ngesisa esingaka. Lento ivelisa usizi ku Rulumente
nakwabo bangabona bahlobo bawo (Amamfengu). Kuyavuyisa ke ngoko
ukufumana amadoda anje ngo Sikunyana nabanye bevuseleleka ukuba bawabone
bawaqonde awona malungelo abo.

Ukwakiwa kwentango zamatye yinto elungileyo eyomelezayo, intango
ezihlalayo zobanga ukuba abantu babe nako ukulima ebusika kwanje
ngasehlotyeni, babe nako ukulima eyeziqamo neminye imiti, liti ilizwe
ngeminyaka embalwa libuye lizale yimiti, njengoko lalinjalo ekuqaleni
kokulima kwa Mamfengu.

Inkoliso ya Mamfengu iwusebenza umhlaba njengokungati asinguwo wabo,
abananto bayenzayo yokuwulungisa umhlaba ngati baza kubuye bawuhlutwe,
mhlaumbi bemke kuwo—u-Rulumente ukubapa kwake lomhlaba neziqiniselo
zawo, kwaye kungetemba nenkolo yokuba baya kuwusebenza bawulungise,
ukuze abantu bawushiye lom­hlaba ulilifa labantwana babo, uzuze ixabiso
ngenxa yokusetyenzwa nokulungiswa okwenziwe ngabo kuwo. Nditemba ukuba
lento

iya kugqalwa yenziwe. Ukugqiba ndingati nditete egameni lika Rulumente
ilizwi lokukutaza kwabo bate barola empahleni yabo ukuze kwakiwe indlu
yomfundisi wabo. Nditemba ukuba baya kuzingisa kulo mizamo yabo ; bate
kaloku bawamkele lomsebenzi ulungileyo kukade wamiswayo pakati kwabo.
Nakubo koti, kungekabi kade, zibe ninzi iremente ezixasa abafundisi,
abavele kwapakati kwabo.

Nditemba ukuba ingxelo yako ezayo yofika ibe yekolisayo kwanjengale
yokugqibela.

Ndingu Mpatiswa we Micimbi Yabantsundu.

C. BROWNLEE.

INCWADI ZASE LIVINGSTONIA.

KUYA kuba luvuyo kubo bonke abalangazelela ukuva ngokuhamba komsebenzi
we Nkosi pakati e-Africa ukuba amadoda asishiye kulomnyaka udluleyo
ukuya e-Nyassa aselede aya kufika kungeko nto inkulu imbi iwahleleyo.
Sibe sifanele ukumbulela Usonto zonke ngokuwalondoloza ngokokude abe
namhla sekuqalwe umsebenzi wokukanya kwelozwe lobumnyama, kube
kushunyayelwa indaba zoxolo ezweni lezipitipiti, nemfazwe,
nembandezelwano.

Kwezincwadi esizinikayo namhlanje na- kwinteto eyenziwe ngu Mr. Young
ukudlula kwake e-Kapa baya kubona ukuba u-Dr. Stewart uye kufika use
uqaliwe umsebenzi, kuba esiti ufike sekwakiwe ishumi elinesitatu
lezindlu, kuko neluxande, kushunyayelwa Ilizwi le Nkosi nge Sabata. Enye
indawo engamandla noko ingati idelekile yeyokuba u-Mr. Young ngexesha
ebisapetwe nguye i-Livingstonia wenze ukuba abantsundu abammeleyo
bamtembe bamtande, beze kuma kufupi naye, bamqonde ukuba okwenene lo uze
ngobukaya. Kwizizwe ezinje ngezipakati e-Africa londawo inamandla kuba
gabulizigcawo abanje ngo Young lowo.

Kuzo zonke izinto ezibaliswe kwezincwa­di zanamhla eyona kuti iyintloko
yeyokuba sivuliwe isikolo sokuqala e-Nyassa savulwa ngabantwana
abalishumi elinabane—amakwenkwe alishumi elinamabini nentombi ezimbini.
Noko ingati incinane londawo ekubonakaleni yalatisa kwinto ezinkulu
eziya kuyilandela. Umnqweno wabaninzi bakuyiva londawo uya kuba
ngowokuti akwaba ndibe ndiko nam ndibonela ukwenziwa kwa-

lonto. Kongezelela eluvuyweni lwetu ukuva ukuba ititshere yokuqala
eyokubamba isi­kolo ngumfana ontsundu wabasuke e-Love­dale ongu Shadrach
Mgunana. Anga angamaninzi amadodana ayakufana naye. Sanga isikolo soka
Mgunana singanda ngamanani sihlume ekwazini kwelilizwe nokwelizayo, anga
lomakwenkwe nentombi angaba ngaya kunyamezela avuyise intliziyo
yetitsher, yawo ngokuti ngemini ezizayo aye kuba zizikanyiso kubumnyama
obuwangqongileyo.

Indawo eyakuba lusizi kubo bonke kukuva ukuba kulamadodana matandatu
ayenyukile isiqingata sawo sibuyile. Kwamane aye pume E-Lovedale kuza
kubuya nye u-Isaac Williams, abe ayesuka e-Bayi ehamba neqela lika Dr.
Macklin ebuya omabini. Kuya kuba lusizi ukuba ubushushu belozwe buya
koyisa ama Africa nangapezu kwabamhlope, Nokoke kulusizi ngokubuya
kwawo, ukuba intliziyo yawo isesemsebenzini we Nkosi aya kunceda noko
alapa ngokubalisa ngento azibonileyo, intsizi nobubi, ubudenge
nobunyama, nesikalo sabati yizani kusinceda. Kanjalo kukona kuza
kuqondakala ukuba anako na amadodana ase Africa ukuti ngenxa yotando ku
Kristu, aye apo kutiwa kuko ingozi, nokuba yeyesifo nokuba yeyenye into.
Anakona ukubuncama ubom ngenxa yowancama obake. Liza kubuya libeko ituba
lokubamabaye abanye. Kuya kubanjanina ke ngalomini ? Banga bangahlala
sebecinga abanentliziyo yokupilela Inkosi, ukuze bangayi kulola izikali
xa sekuhlatywa umkosi.

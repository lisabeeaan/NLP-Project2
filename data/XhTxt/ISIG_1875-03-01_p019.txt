EZIHLONYELWE KWI “ SIGIDIMI ” SIKA MARCH 1875.

UMKANGELI WEZIKOLO—Abaleseshi betu baya kubalusizi ukuva ukuba U-Mr.
Hogarth ucele ukuba akutshwe emsebenzini wokuva izikolo zala macala etu,
esiva ukuba ubangelwe kukuvukwa yingalo. Njengoko sesike satsho,
endaweni yake kumiswe U-Mr. Clarke, umfo owahluke ngendawo ezininzi ku
Mr. Hogarth. Ubeko apa esiva izikolo ezikufupi nase Lovedale ekuqaleni
kuka February. Kwesinye isikolo ufike ititshara iye kufuna ihashe layo,
washiya ilizwi lokuba iya kubuye ikuve akutetayo ngalonto. Ukuhamba
kwake ufuna ukulandela indlela yonikangeli wemali zakomkulu yena utanda
ukufika apo kungakumbulele bani.

KUMLIMI.—Umfo otile obala E-Natal uti: Kuko intsimi yam ebinokula
obeselude lwapantse ukundikupa. Belusiti luyapuma seludubula, nditi
ndihlakula, ndiqesha abantsundu kube kubi ize noko ndingancedi nento,
kube kukona ndingati ndiyalubanga. Kute xa senditeta ngokuba ndiyi shiye
youke lontsimi njengento encanyiweyo ndacinka ukuba ndike ndilinge
elikutyana litsalwa ngamaliashe amabini. Ngexeshana elifutshane liyenze
lontsimi ibiselingati yintlango ukuba ikulungele ukudla. Namhla ungafika
ungateti kukuba kuhle kokudla kwayo nokuluswela kwayo ukula. ukuba
abantu baziqelisa ukusebenza ngemashini abangeyilibale into enoncedo,
engeni- sa ngapezu kokusebenza ngezandla.

EMTATA—Omnye wababalelani betu obala ese Mtata uti, “ Indaba zalapa ziza
kuba zizo. Into eninzi yabantu iva ukuba kusitiwa kuse Mampondweni nakwa
Hala, ayazi ukuba kuhleli izizwe. Amampondo nama Hala ebexalise
ngokupumela- na imikosi kutinjwe impahla kufe nabantu. Kodwa ngoku
inkosi. U-Ndamase no Ngangelizwe bayaketelana. Kungayinto entle ukuba
ezi zweziya kuvana zihlalelane ngoxolo. Into ebonakalayoyile yokuba
inkosi zabantu abantsundi azinakuvana xa kungalamli emhlope. Ngoku kuko
Imantyi nje imfaswe ziya damba, inqondoyandile. Inkosi yama Mpondomise
Umditshwa, uyakankanyeka ngokubulawa kwabantu ababini kusitiwa baya
takata.”

LANGALIBALELE—Kuko abati isizatu sokuba E-Natal kude kube lixesha
elingaka kungeko mvula ibibanjwe ngu Langalibalele, efuna ukohlwaya
abelungu ngokusuka bamtumele esiqitini. Bati kanjalo ikwanguye lo
utumele unogumbe otshabalalise impahla eninzi kangaka yabantu. Imantyi
etile yase Natal ivele abantu abantsundu ebebevela ematyaleni behamba
bencokola omnye esiti: U-Sobantu (kuba kambe bamtsho ukumbiza U-Bishop
Colenso) uyakufika no Langalibalele. Ate amayeza ka Langalibalele amenza
ukuba U-Sobantu lowo angake apumle ingekakululeki inkosi. Ukubonakalisa
ukuba lenkosi ligqira le- nene yenze “ ngomti” wayo ukuba ne Nkosazana
isuke izale yinceba imxolele noko one kangaka. Akuko nto angeyi feziyo
ngomiti yake.

IMPATO YABANTSUNDU E-NATAL—Kwincwadi ebalwe ngu Lord Carnavon ngomhla we
3 ka December siyavuya ukubona ukuba ufuna ukuba ngoku nabantsundu
bapatwe ngamasiko abamhlope kangangoko kunokwenzeka, aze abebe- nawo
angabi sakutazwa ngangokwapambili. Uti ukukutazwa kwalo masiko
kubatintela ukuba ba- ngenyuki ekukanyeni kungabi lula nokulamkela
Ilizwi lika Tixo. Lonto iya kuba luncedo oluku- lu kubo esingavuyayo
ukubona kubandezelwa ku- nene kuyo nakule yetu ikoloni, atetiswe kube
ka- nye amasiko anje ngawokwaluka nokutengisa nge- ntombi nokuzeka
izitembu. Akuko sizatu sokuba abantsundu bavunyelwe ukuba benze izinto
engati ezenzile omhlope asiwe ematyaleni, kanti bati bakuba bengapatwa
njengabamhlope kwizinto ezitile bakalaze.

INTENGO EZIZA KUBAKO.

Ngomhla 11 ka March kumhlaba okutiwa yi Kutfontein kufupi nase Richmond
kuya kutengiswa igusha 2296, inkabi ezinkulu zebokwe 550, ezisencinane
600, nezinye impahla ezinje ngozitafile nezitya, nozitulo, nozibedi,
nomihlakulo, amakuba, irenqa, izikele zokuceba igusha nezinye
intlobontlobo zezinto.

Ngo 25 ka March ngolwe Sine kuya kutengiswa umhlaba omkulu kunene kufupi
nase Graaff Reinet. Ubukulu uzimorgen ezi 1,255. Ngumhlaba osewenzelwe
namadam, ongenzelwayo no- mncencesho. Nokuba umntu unamawaka alishumi
egusha zingaba nendawo eyaneleyo yokudla. Uyakufandezwa ngalomini
ikankanyiweyo ngexa leshumi. Ukusuka E-Graaff Reinet ungumgama- oyihambo
yeyure ezine.

Ngo 6 ka March, E-Somerset kuya kutengiswa inkomo 10, amahashe atsalayo
8, iseyibokwe 100.

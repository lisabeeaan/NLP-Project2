ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MAY 1, 1871.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

IXABISO lento ezonakaliswe ngulom- singa ubusand’ukubako E-Victoria West
lifike kwiponti ezi £20,500.

U-DR. BREDA wase Beaufort uhlabe umfazi wake ngesitshetshe, esisona sama
gqira. Ubube ngolwesitatu usuku emva koko. Ngoku U-Dr. Breda ufakwe
etolongweni ukuza alingwe ngenxa yokubulala.

IQWARAKAZI elihle kunene, lisand’uku- tengiswa E-Bayi, larolelwa iponti
ezi £55. Lenyamakazi yayiseyimbuna ityebile nokutyeba.

UBUSELA.—Ngase Beaufort kusand’ u- kubiwa ibokwe ezi 50 ebuhlanti buka
Mr. Nel wase Suierfontein. Ulandwe umkondo wada waya kufakwa E-Appies
Drie, apo zingati ziwuwele kona umlambo.

ISOMELEZO KEBALIMI BEKOTON.—II- Mr. Cawood wase Bini usandukufumana
imbali yokutengiswa kwe Koton eyayi wele nalomkombe kutiwa yi Briton.
Yayikolise ngoluhlobo kutiwa yi New Orleans. Ukutengwa kwayo enye ibide
irolelwe isheleni enepeni zombini ngeponti.

OWENZAKALISWE YINQWELO.—Kwezi veki zidluleyo ute umfana otile esaquba
inqwelo kulendlela yase Cathcart, wati engaqondanga wabona selepakati
kwemilenze yenqwelo. Iteke emibini yamnyatela esiswini, akwapuka tambo
kodwa. Ke ngoku selesekupileni. Ukwehleka kwalo ngozi inqwelo
yayilayishe iponti zobunzima ezi 3,000lb.

OBULEWE E-BAYI.—Kute E-Bayi aku- bon’ ukuba usadla um Zulu kwezi zindlu
zokudlela, abati ngu Thomas, wabizelwa ukuba aye kunqina ukuba imali
ebi’rolwe ngum Xosa, abati ngu Tshaka yimali yenenena. Uteke yena
yinkohliso bazake baxabana, bada babetana. Isiwe ke lemali kwivenkile
enkulu ebikufupi, kwatiwa nakona asiyiyo mali yenene. Kwahlukweneke,
U-Thomas waya kwa sendlwini yake, wabuya walandela no Tshaka. Uteke
U-Thomas epokozela ikofu ekomityini yake xa atobayo, wavela nga- semva
U-Tshaka wambeta ngenduku en- tanyeni. Bate abantu ebebepandle bakuva
isandi sokuwa kwake babaleka ukuya kukangela, bafika U-Tshaka selemi
pezu komnye lowo, selengati yena ufile. Kute kwakubuzwa ukuba nguyena lo
wenze oku wavuma, wati kodwa ubengafuni kumbulala. Kuye kwabizwa U-Prins
idindala, waza wafakwa etolongweni kwa oko U-Tshaka lowo. Kute ngemini
elandelayo ngexalesitoba waqauka U-Thomas. Ube ngumfo obekekileyo,
weremente yakwa Wesile. Umbanjwa lowo seleke waviwa yimantyi yakona,
kodwa bekusatiwe kusake kufunwe ubunqina obungapezulu, kengoko akakuqale
agwetywe.

EZENTENGO (I-FANDESI).

E-QUEENSTOWN—Ngolwe Sine, ngolwe 4 ku May, kotengiswa impahla ka Mr.
Charles French, igusha nebokwe ezi 268, isipan senkabi, imazi namatokazi
enkomo ezi 14, namatole, amahashe ama 3, nehangu ezi 38.

E-QONCE—Ngom Gqibelo, ngolwe 6 ku May, kotengiswa inqwelo nesipan
senkabi, ezili 16.

E-BEDFORD—(Enyara) ngolwe Sine, ngolwe 11 ku May, kotengiswa inkabi
zegusha nezebokwe ezi 1,200, imazi zebo- kwe ezi 300, nenkunzi zenkomo
ezi 2.

E-RONDAVEL—Kufupi ne Tarkastad, ngolwe Sihlanu, ngolwe 12 ku May
kote­ngiswa impahla ebiyeka Mr. Carl Marais, kuko umhlambi omkulu
wegusha nebokwe, inkomo ezi 65, nenkabi zenkomo ezi 14, amahashe ali 12
nebokuva yenqwelo, kwa- nezinto zokulima, nezasendlwini.

E-ADELAIDE (Ekobonqaba) kuko impa- hla eskiti ehlutwe emaseleni,
eyakute- ngiswa kon’apa, ngolwe 6 ku May, ukuba abaniniyo abadanga
bavele. Impahla yile:—Inkomo ezi 23, amahashekazi ama bini, isali
nomkala.

E-MIDDELBURG—Eskiti ngom Gqibelo ngolwe 6 ku May, kotengiswa inkabi
ze­nkomo ezi 13, nemazi ezi 2, namahashe amabini.

E-CRADOCK—Eskiti, kotengiswa inko- mo, namahashe, ngolwe 4, ngolwe 6,
nangolwe 16 ku May.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

  St. Matthew’ Mission, Keiskama Hoek April 20th, 1871.

Kwi Sigidimi Samaxosa.

Nkosi—Amamfengu atile apa azizalamane zala- wo ayefunwa kwi Sigidimi
Samaxosa esipambi kwesi, andicele ukuba nditumele amagama awo, ukuze abe
nokufikelela kumbaleli wako.

Amagama awo ngawola, U-Lutungata no Mnco- ko no Mkambuli. Ati ayawazi
apo ukona umzi ka Mpumeln, kodwa akazanga evento ngalomfazi nenkwenke
ababefunwa.

Ndingu, &c.,

CHARLES TABERER.

[Abaleseshi betu abantsundu sibacela ukuba batyile kwipepa lesine apo
bozibona kona izalamane zika Lutungata, no Mncoko, no Mkambuli.
—UMLUNGISI WEZE SIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA.]

PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

KWELI lapesheya izicoto zisamana ukufika ngamaxa atile. Umbane usand’
ukubeta ama 32 egusha ebuhlanti.

Baninzi abantu ababubileyo apa ngenxa yoko- hlokohlo. Belunamandla kanye
ngapezu kokwa ngapambili.

Intombi etile ibulewe ngumoya wesisele.

Ama Mfengu akupe ikulu leponti elinamanci mahlanu (150Z.) ukuncedisa
kulendlela iza kwe- nziwa iya E-Qonce.

Ukudla kucumile kanye nonyaka, pesheya kwe Nciba.

U-Ngangelizwe wayeyiqumbele kanye I-Gover­nor ngokusuka iti makahlaule
inkomo ezi 40 ngenxa yokuba ebeyipete ka kubi intombi ka Rili. Ube funa
ukwazi isizatu sokuba I-Governor ingabavumeli balwe.

E-Dutywa kwavakala ukuzamazama komhlaba ngolwe 15 ku April.

Ngelihlobo buninzi uboya obuya kuvela pesheya kwe Nciba apa. Obunye
buhle kanye. Bonke abarwebi babunxamele kanye, ngokukodwa kona ngoku
beve ukuba inani labo linyukilenje.

UKUTETWA KWA MATYALA
E-BEAUFORT.

[PAMBI KUKA MR. JUSTICE SMITH.]

UMGWEBI abe etetwa pambi kwake awase Beau­fort, nawase Bedford, nawase
Dikeni, amatyala etetelwa E-Beaufort, ngu Mr. Smith. Inani lababanjwa
belingamashumi amabini anesine, amatyala esitoba. Inkoliso yamatyala
ibiyeyobusela, Iwaye uninzi Iwamasela ebe izinto ezinjengozinkomo
lulolwase Gqugesi. Izohlwayo zawo sitemba ukuba ziya kubonisa ukuba
amasela akezikufeketiswa.

UBUGEBENGA.

U-Paulus Johannes, wase Bedford, wanikwa ityala lokuba waenxamela
ukwenzakalisa ngabom U-Lena Isaac. Walivuma elotyala, kwatiwake makarole
iponti zombini, ati xa angenakuzirola abe veki ntandatu etolongweni
esebenza kanzima.

OQEKEZE ITOLONGO.

U-Welem ulivumile ityala lokuba bekusitiwa uqekeze itolongo yase
Beaufort, wazake wanikwa iminyaka emibini etolongweni esebenza kanzima.

UKUBIWA KWE BOKWE.

U-Jan Bruintjes wabenikwe amatyala okuti kwa D. Erasmus weba amashumi
amatatu ebokwe; kwa J. J. Triegardt weba impahla mpablana zokunxiba; kwa
Thomas Trollip weba ozibulukwe. Alivumileyo lelo lebokwe lodwa.
Wagwetywake ukuba ahlale iminyaka emibini etolongweni esebe­nza kanzima.

U-Jacob Makalo ulikanyele ityala lokuba bekutiwa weba ibokwe ezimbini
zika L. A. Nel E-Water Kloof, negusha ka W. Andrews. Ate amanqina
amatatu abonisa ukuba waziba ibokwe ezo ; aza amanye amabini abonisa
ukuba wayiba negusha. Kutiwe makahlale iminyaka emibini etolongweni
esebenza kanzima-

UKUDLWENGULA.

U-Jan Snel, I-Lawo, ulikanyele ityala lokuba bekusitiwa wayenxamele
ukudlwengula U-Janatyi. Bute ubunqina buka Janatyi abanelisa nto, waza
waselenikwa iveki zontandatu etolongweni ngenxa yokuba embetile.

OBULELE OMNYE (IGQIBA ELIMNYAMA).

U-Funzana, igqira lemfengu, akalivumanga itya­la lokuba bekusitiwa
wadlisa U-Marie wase Mxelo. Ugwetywe ke ukuba ahlaule amashumi omabini
eponti (£20) ati xa angenakuwarola ahla­le unyaka etolongweni, esebenza
kanzima.

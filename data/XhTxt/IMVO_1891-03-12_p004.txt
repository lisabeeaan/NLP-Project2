ISAZISO SIKA RULUMENTE.—NO. 48, 1891.

Kwi Office yo Mpati Koloni, e Kapa, 13th January, 1891.

Ngamandla namagunya awatweswe ngo Siqenda sesixenxe so Mteto wo Bhalo,
1890 Umhlekazi i Ruluneli, ngecobo le Bhunga Elipeteyo, walatisa ukuba
kuzo sonke izixeko idolopu, nemizana e Koloni apo kuko Amagqugula,
apeteyo, umkangeli wo Bhalo uya kuvelela indawo yokuba kushiywe amapepa
o Bhalo, kwanezalatiso ezingemva kwalomapepa ngosuku olungapambili kolwe
5th April, 1891 kuzo zonke izindlu ezikulondawo awobona zifanele.

Abakangeli bo Bhalo baya gunyaziswa ukuba njengokubona kwabo benze ukuba
amapepa o Bhalo olu ashiywe nokuba kukuzipina izindlu pakati
kweziqingata Zobhalo olo.

Xa ke amapepa lawo ate ashiywa endlwini, kuyimfanelo yomninindlu, okanyo
umgcini wayo, ukulibhala okanye enze ukuba libhalwe ngokuzeleyo,
ngokuyinene, nangokulungileyo ngangoko azi ngako, okunyo axelelwayo,
okunyo akoliweyo, lomapepa abo eshiywe kulondlu, abhale igama lake, azo
awatumele, mhlaumbi awanikelo ekubizweni kwawo, egqityiwo
ngendlela-ndlelana zonke, ku Mbhali owabizayo.

Umbhali wofika ngo Mvulo, 6th April, 1891, mhlaumbi kwakamsinya emva
koko, awabize amapepa abeshiywo ezindlwini, awoti ekuwamkeleni kwake
awaqwalasole, azanelise ukuba abhalwe ngendlela na, awoti ukuba ufumana
ukuba akenjiwanganjalo awazalise ngengxelo anokuyifumana.

Apo sukuba umbhali engawashiyanga amapepa o Balo, umbali wowabhala
ngokwake, ileyo indlu eyibbala kwipepa layo elilodwa.

Ngendlu kutetwa isakiwo, umuquba, inqwelo, inqwelana yamahashe, nayipina
indawo apo kulala umntu, nasipina isikepe, nenqanawa engaba
semazibukweni e Koloni yase Kapa.

Yimfanelo yomnini-ndlu nomntu oyipeteyo ukupendula kamsinyane,
ngokuzeleyo, nangokulungeleloneyo yonke imibuzo engabuzwa ngumbali.

Umnini-ndlu, nomntu ongaba uselo nayo oko wamangala, okanye ote
akakatalela ukuzalisa, umhlaumbi ukuboka igama lake, umhlaumbi ukunikela
amapepa o Bhalo kumbali; nongati okanye usuke amangale, umhlaumbi
angakataleli kapendula, okunye asuke axoke ekupenduleni, okunye axele
ubuxoki okanye enze ukuba kuxelwe ubuxoki, nongatintela nalipina igosa
lo Bhalo ekwenzeni umsebenzi walo, angadliwa iponti zontlanu zemali,
angati engenazo abo nokufakwa entolongweni esebenza umhlaumbi
engasebenzi nzima ixesha elingengapezulu kwenyanga, de abe uhlaulelwe
kwakamsinyane.

Kweso sohlwayo akaqukwa umntu oyakuti ngenxa yento azoyikelayo asuko
ashiye umhlaumbi amangale ukuxela unqulo lwake, angati ukotyobisa ezo
zoyikelo zake selebbala kumqolo olungiselelwe oko welizwi eliti "
Object.” Kananjalo eso sohlwayo asiyi kubekwa kumntu ongati ngenani
lemfuyo yake engalazi ngokute nqo, asuke enze umlinganiselo nje omalunga
nenyaniso.

Nalipina igosa lo Bhalo elingafika libhengeze into zo Bhalo olo liya
kudliwa iponti ezintlanu (£5), liti lingenazo lifakwe entolongweni
ixesha elingapezu kwenyanga.

J. W. SAUER, Umpati-Koloni.

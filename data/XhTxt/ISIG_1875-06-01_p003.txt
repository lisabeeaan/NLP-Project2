INTLANGANISO YABAZILI
BOTYWALA E-RINI.

IQELA elitile lamadoda namankazana e-Rini lite ngo March walomnyaka
lavumelana ukwenza intlanganiso yokuzila utywala nokuncedana. leihlobo
esitile sike sasibonisa imiteto emiselwe amalungu alontlanganiso, esoti
ngokufutshane siyibeke pambi kwabaleseshi betu. Lentlanganiso ayikete
luhlanga, ifuna bonke ukuba bahlangane ngalo msebenzi mnye.

Ukuqala kuvunyelwene ukuba igama layo kutiwe “Yintlanganiso yokuzila
okunxilisayo neyokuncedana.” (Total Absti­nence Benefit Society.)
Abapati bayo, ngabongameli ababini, noko omnye engapantsi, Abagcini
bempahla yeloqela ababini, Umgcini mali, Isekritari, abakangeli bokuba
iyonke na imali, ikomiti yabantu abalishumi elinesibini (amadoda asibozo
namankazana amane).

Pambi kokuze umntu abe nokuteta entla- nganisweni mhlaumbi abe lilungu
lekomiti kufuneka ukuke abe nenyanga zone engasaseli nto inxilisayo.
Angaba lilungu nokuba ngubanina otembisa ukuzila okunxilisayo. Akuketwe
ndoda akuketwe nkazana. Ngobudala kufuneka efikile kwishumi elinesixenxe
leminyaka. Kuya kulindelwa ukuba makangakukutazi ukuselwa kotywala
nakwabanye. Akutetwa nto ngokuti aselele iyeza mhlaumbi umtendeleko.

Ofuna ukuba lilungu kufuneka ukuba ati ukungena kwake arole isikisipeni,
aze kanjalo amane erola ipeni ezimbini ngeveki. Abate bada bagqitisa
kwinyanga ezimbini bengabatali boba sesicengeni sokukutshwa emalungwini.
Kanjalo ote wanetyala lokude afakwe etolongweni angati akutshwe.

Ngexesha epakati intlanganiso uya kudliwa imali engengapantsi kwehalafu
groni, (2s. 6d.) engengapezulu kweshumi lesheleni, (10s.) ote watuka,
mhlaumbi wenza ucuku nomnye. mhlaumbi wangena kwindlela zokubekelana.
Osukuba eteta, nokuba uyindoda nokuba uyinkazana (kuba nawo avunyelwe
ukuba atete) kufuneka ukuba akwenze oko ngokubekekileyo. Ukuba ude
watetiswa ngu Mongameli waza noko akapulapula uyakukutshwa endlwini.

Imali efunyenweyo iyakucitelwa emisebe- nzini yentlanganiso. Owapuleyo
umnqopiso wokusela uya kuba uposene nokuba lilungu lentlanganiso ade
abuye angeniswe ngakumbi. Abasukuba befuduka kuya kutandeka ukuba
bakwazise oko, baze xa banento abafuna ukuncedwa ngayo batumele ubunqina
bokuba abakawapuli umnqopiso. Ukuba ite imali ecitwayo yangapezu
kwengenayo kuya kufuneka ukuba onke amalungu arole intwana yokufeza
ityala elikoyo, ati ongavumiyo asiwe emtetweni.

Intlanganiso zibako ngolwe Sitatu ngo- kuhlwa.

Amalungu aya kuncedwa ngemali ezixa- kekweni zawo, ngati ekungeneni
kwawo arole isheleni ezimbini ezinepeni ezintandatu endaweni yokurola
ipeni ezintandatu kube kupela ; aze kanjalo iveki zonke arole ipeni
ezintandatu endaweni yezimbini. Kushiyelwe ekutandeni komntu ukuti
ayirole lemali eyiyona ininzi aze ke abe ngoncedwayo naye, nokuba arole
isitandatu sepeni mhla wangena aze ngeveki arole mbini, angabinguye ke
otenjiswa luncedo ngemali. Into enkulu yeyokuba babe ngabazili bento
ezinxilisayo.

Uncedo aya kulufumana orole imali ezeleyo lolu : Akuhliwa sisifo
esingangeniswanga zindlela ezikohlakeleyo zake, esite samtintela
ekuqubeni umsebenzi wake uya kufumana ishumi lesheleni nge veki kude
kupele inyanga ezintandatu, ati ukuba akakapili nangeloxa afumane
isihlanu ngeveki ngalo lonke ixesha asenaso. Kuko ikomiti emiselwe
ukumana ityelelela abafayo. Ote wahliwa sisifo makaye azise Isekritari.
Ohlelwe yingozi kwada kwafuneka ukuba mhlaumbi kubeko indawo
enqanyulwayo ligqira, indleko zalomsebenzi ziya kumela intlanganiso.
Yoti ukuze ingaroli nto abe unqa- nyulwe ngenxa yokubanjwa sisifo
kungekuko ukuhlelwa yingozi ngebaqo. Pambi kokuba alindele oloncedo
umntu kuya kufuneka eke wanenyanga ezintlanu elilungu. Kwakuba kuko
obubileyo kuya kurolwa iponti ezintandatu zirolelwe indleko zokuncwatywa
kwake. Oke wasapula isifungo sake woba uzicitile zonke ezindawo zoku-

ncediswa ade abe uselenenyanga ebuye wabuya.

ABANTWANA.

ABANTWANA abaya kwamkelwa, ngobudala bosusela kwiminyaka esinxenxe kude
kube yelishumi elinesixenxe. Amalungu eyaba- ntwana intlanganiso ayakuba
ngabatembisa ukungatuki, ukungatshayi, ukungaseli. Imali borola
isitandatu sepeni mhla bangena, baze ngazo zonke inyanga bamane ukurola
kwalomali. Baya kuba nelungelo yoku- sebenzisa incwadi zentlanganiso
baye nalapo kufundelwa kona. Oselenenyanga ezintlanu elilungu woti
afumane isheleni ezimbini ezinesitandatu sepeni ngeveki ukuba uyafa,
kude kupele inyanga ezintandatu ; ati ukuba akakapili ibuye ibonwe into
angayenzelwayo. Ukuba ububile umntwana olilungu konikwa kwizihlobo zake
iponti yokubatala indleko zokuncwaba, ukuba lomntwana akangapezulu
kweminyaka elishumi elinesibini ngobudala, ati ukuba ungapezulu kurolwe
iponti eneshumi. Ongenazihlobo kobonwa yintlanganiso ukuncwatywa kwake.
Abantwana abangamalungu bomana ukuhlangana nge Mivulo ngokuhlwa.

Kunjalo ke ukuma kwalo ntlanganiso. Sisinike isimo sayo ngokuzeleyo
sinetemba lokuba kobako nakwezinye indawo abanga- kutandayo ukulunga
kwayo, bazenzele eyabo, nokuba mhlaumbi bate abayi landela yonke imiteto
yale ise Rini.

ABAYA KUSEBENZA.

KULENYANGA ifileyo sasinike amazwi ka Mhlontlo xa anikela abantu bake ku
Mr Levey ukuba baye kusebenza. Sinovuyo olukulu ukubona namhla kungena
into enjalo pakati kwezizwe zalomacala. Yenye yezinto ezikutazayo,
ezinika amatemba okuba kanti kungade kubeko nto ivelayo pakati kohlanga.
Ukuba lomadoda awagcinile amazwi okuyalwa kwawo aya kubona ukuba
atunyelwa yinkosi ewatandayo. Ukuba ezinye inkosi ziya kulubonela
oluhlobo lokuyala zoba zilungiselela abantu bazo.

Kuya kusivuyisa ngokukulu ukuba zite ezinye zalomacala zabonela
zandulula awazo amadoda. Xa asemsebenzini aya kuba nokufunda into
ezininzi apa. Sake sawabona lamadoda ka Mhlontlo ukudlula kwawo apa,
safika kubafo abomeleleyo, abaqabukileyo, abakolise ukuba ziziqingqi,
abanwele zinde noko babesiti bazinqumle kuba bebezilile. Safika kumadoda
ati akungena ekuncokoleni ngemfazwe uqonde ukuba iselilibele nalapo
ikona; ati akupata ezinye izinto ubone ukuba kuncinane okukokunye aku-
qondayo. Adlula apa emangalisiwe naku- kubetwa kwamagubu, into yonke
intsha..

Kodwa ke makangayilibali amakwenkwe into yokuba kwelixhishini kuko nento
yoku­ba nenyoka zite-bhatala nazo zifuna izindlu zentaka. Intsizi zihle
e Hebron (e Ligwa) ngengapaya. Amakwenkwana amane ama Bhulu ate endaweni
yokuya etyalikeni nase sikuleni se Cawa asuka aya kufunisela. Ayi-
fumene indlu yentaka amatatu aselesisa iza­ndla ukuva ukuba “ imanina ”
kanti pakati kuko inyoka, ezilume zozitatu ezozandla. Enye intwana ife
kwaoko, amabini emva komzuzwana. Eyesine ikwele ehasheni—ekoko ukuya
kuxela ekaya, suka yayakutsho ngopote pantsi yenzakala: Usizi lwalondlu
ngezi ziganeko lube lolungatetekiyo.

Lite elinye ityendyana ngelisiko likoyo kuti bantu bamnyama lokuba isoka
lirole imali yokutunga, lafumana isikade esikulu. Siteta ngo Mr. John
Pabu, ote selezirolile iponti ezintlanu (£5) zengubo yomtshakazi yamala
intombi. Watsala e ofisini, wafika wagwetywa kwatiwa umteto awuvumeli
ukuba intombi ibizwe indleko yokwala isoka. Nokuba beselidlekile akuko
nto, zibuyiselekile ezondleko loluya tendo belitendwa yintombi ngalo;
yindoda into emangalelwayo yakwapula umnqopiso wofilisho, kuba yona
inomlomo wokuzifilishela ezintombini, ekubeni inkazana ingenawo lowo
mlomo. Aba­ntu bayakupela kudleka ngenxa yelisiko lemali yokutunga kuba
intombi zaleminyaka zikapukapu kangaka ukutshintshisa ngesoka layo, kuba
nokuwabiza ngoku ziti “ zizikolobho ”—into ebonakala intle kukungenziwa
zindleko kungekatshatwa. Umfo ka Pabu yena selefungele ukuti akasoze
apinde, uxo- lele ukutshata nentombi nokuba seyibinqe isikaka namaso,
ayitengele seyikuye.

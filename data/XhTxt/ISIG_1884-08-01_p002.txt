2 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA AUGUST 1,1884

Indaba zehambo yetu kutsha nje kwimizi yama Tshawe o Kama, o Matomela, o
Njokweni no Zulu (omelwe ngu Tshefu ngoku)—amatshawe emizi
ya-Magqunukwebe na-Mazizi na Bambo azinakufumana ndawo kwesi Sigidimi
sanamhla. Sesingakangela kwesilandelayo. Lehambo singafumana sibahlebele
abafundi betu siti isivule amehlo ngento ezininzi. “ Indaba yetyela
ayikoli ” itsho impi evela e Lutukela. Lenteto sino kuyingqinela emva
kwehambo yetu. Okwanamhla kwanele ukuba sibe nokubako kwintlanganiso
emnandi yebandla lase Rura elonganyelwe ngu Rev. Boyce Mama ngom-Vulo 28
July. Ibiyeyokubuta imali yokuxasa ilizwi. Asinantloni ukuti
besingalindele nto ngesikuva ngendlala ekwelo lase Ngqushwa ebangwe
kubalela iminyaka elandeleleneyo, kodwa bati bakuposa abantu saqonda
ukuba okwenene Inkosi ayibashiyile abantu bayo. Usuku lwentlanganiso
lube lolubandayo ngangokuba amanene angabafundisi bezinye indawo
abelindelwe —o Rev. E. Gedye (wase Durban), Rev. J. B. Sakuba (wase
Tyityaba), no Rev. D. Ntlabati (wase Mgwalana)—angafiki. Kwenziwe ke
amavukela-ntloko. Isihlalo sitatyatwe ngumfo ka Lusiba itshawe lase
Kunene u Mr. Joseph Mpahla. Abateti ababesemgangatweni yabingo Rev.
Boyce Mama no Mr. John Tengo-Jabavu (wase Lovedale). Inteto
zingabonakala kwindaba ezo singati sizixele, kodwa inteto yexego u
Mpahla izomeleze kunene intliziyo ze ramente, kwaneyomfo ka Mgudlandlu u
Yisaka. Iminikelo mayibe ibengapaya kwe £20 siquka nempahla. Ngamana
lasikeleleka i Rura.

Umlungu ogama lingu Thomas Holand obefudula engumntu opakamileyo
uzinqumle umqala e Bayi kule nyanga ifileyo. Incwadi awayibalela umfazi
wake, yaza yafunyanwa ilele pezu kwe tafile, yiyo le:—“ My darling
wife,—My life is quite a burden to me, I cannot bear to see you and the
children starve. Forgive me, I know that kind friends will see you well
cared for. I have tried my best. God bless you all.”—Kule ncwadi ke
kubonakala ukuba lomfo uzibulele ngenxa yentswelo. Ushiya umfazana
omncinane obantwana batatu. Lomhla wazibulala ngawo, wati kumkake
makalalise abantwana aye e tyalikeni. Okwenene wenjenjalo wapuma waya
etyalikeni umfazi. Uti akubuya akayibona indoda emagumbini apambili.
Ubone kuvuta isibane ekitshini waya kukangela. Tu nge ndoda yake ilele
ngomhlana kuko isitya sizele ligazi etafileni. Wapuma waya wabiza abantu
abafike babona ukuba izisike ngemela emqaleni—nemela leyo (table knife)
isese sandleni sake senene. Kubonakala ukuba utabate isitya wasibeka
pezu kwetafile waza wazisika etobe pezu kwaso, wopela kuso sada
saqingatisa—wada wapela amandla wawa ngomhlana. Ngomhla ongapambili uke
wati encokola nabanye, wati akakolwa ukuba umntu angohlwaywa ngu Tixo
umntu ozibulele ngenxa yembandezelo zobuhlwempu angena kuzinceda. Oku ke
asikwazi tina—YEHA.

DURBAN-ENGQUSHWA.

I-ANNIVEBSARY YESIKULA SOMSEBENZI WE NTOMBI.

Lengxelo siyitunyelwa ngomnye wotitshala owayeko mhla lomgcobo wako.
Simbulela:

Yaqala ngo 22 June kwada kwa ngo 24. Nge Cawa kusasa 11 A.M.
kwashunyayelwa ngu Rev. Ed. Gedye. Emva kwedinala 2 P.M. kwangenwa
yazala ityalike akwabiko nesituba. Lwahanjiswe uviwo lwentsapo ngu
Mongameli u Rev. Ed. Gedye, lwaquba kuhal usapo, kwaye kuvunywa yi Choir
ezingoma : “ Come unto me,” “ I will extol thee,” “ Cry out and shout
thou inhabitant of Zion,” “ The Chorus swell,” nezinye zesi Xosa,
singati waba mnandi umculo ngokugqitiseleyo. Ngo 4.30.P.M. kwapunywa.
Ngo 7 yangena inkonzo kwashumayela u Rev. Ed. Gedye.

Kusasa ngom-Vulo ngo 10 zavulwa ingcango waqalwa umsebenzi ngo 10.30
kwanikelwa izifundo ebantwaneni ngabaqeqeshelwayo ubutitshara, baquba
ngokuncomekayo. Emva koko u Mongameli ukutaze abazali ngokubatyela
ngomsebenzi wentsapo yabo. No Rev. Chas. Mahlutshana wenza inteto
emnandi kunene esitemba ukuba abazali bayigcina. I Choir yavuma ezingoma
ipetwe ngu Mr. Caleb Mahlutshana: “Kaubuye ubuye sitandwa, Sipapa
ngobusuku pakati kwemikosi yemimoya. Sipumile apo kwati ngo 2 kwangenwa
endlwini eyayinempahla ezenziwe zintombi, samangaliswa kuba
sasingacingeli tina ukuba kunokubako intombi ezinokwenza izinto ezinje
ukaba ntle kwazo kuba sihlala sisiva kusitiwa Ngqushwa emnyama, siti
kuni yizarii nize kuzibonele, siyaqonda okokokuba nizu. ke niqutywa
budenge ukuti yi Ngqushwa emnyama. Yahambiseka kuhle intengo.
Ngolwesi-Biui ibe kwakukutengisa kwempahla ezo. Kute ngo 7 saya kungena
kwindlu yezimuncumuncu ezenziwa zintombi ezi, kwaye kungenwa nge 1s.,
yapuma ke ngo 7.30. Kwangenwa ngo 7.45 yaba yi Concert. Saye silindele u
Fred J. Hodges, Esq., C.c. & R.M. (Peddie) ukuba atate isihlalo,
sanosizi ukuva okokuba akanako ukufikelela, sasesitatwa kwangu Rev. Ed.
Gedye. Kwavunywa, “Alpine Wooer,” “ Gentle Annie,” I Solo ngu Jane Kawa,
i Chorus yi Choir, “ Abenben Adhen,” yi Choir, “We are merry young

musicians,” “To the bright crimson,” “Come home Father,” i Solo, Miss
Mtotywa, Contralto Mr. Maqina senr., Bass Messrs. Mjokozeli, and
Sidzumo, Chorus yi Choir. “They say there’s an echo here.” Vuma ntombi
yase Ngqushwa, nzwakazi yase Ngqushwa. Ezingoma zazihanjiswa ngobugcisa
ngabavumi. Savuya nokubona iqela labamhlope lize kubonela into leyo
ekutazayo. U Mgcini-Sihlalo ulese ingxelo yesikula kususela ku July 1883
kuse ku June 1884. Wati kwaqalwa ngamantombazana ayi 26 kodwa kute
ngenxa yokubalela nesifo apuma amatandatu. Wangena nakwimali ebatelweyo,
neyokwaka indlu, nesaseleyo, necitiweyo. Ngokwangoku kovunyelwa 30
intombi. £8 intlaulo ngonyaka.

lamazwana ambalwa, ndikolwa ukuba liyazi lonke ipakati lase Afrika ukuba
umfo ondim asimfo ukataza ngokubalabala futi-futi. Namhla ndibalanje
ndifuna ukuba umzi wonke wazi apo namhla ingwevu yenzala yabase Lutukela
ikonya kona. Ndikwa Table kwa Mpolo komkulu nditeta kuba ndikwelo cala
lakomkulu. Ndiwele Umzimvubu ngomhla wokuqala ku Ncwaba. Ndipantsi
kwentaba Yentsizo, kufupi nezibuko elona likulu elibeka kwa Adam, naku
sikolo sa Mampondo, kodwa isizwe ngama Hlubi, kuloko esemhlabeni
wetshawe u Mqikela Tuma. Nyana warn Izigidi- mi zam ungazilibali, ngati
ndivalelwe entolongweni ngetuba lokuba indaba zamacala-cala ndahlukene
nazo, andazi ukuba utinina umntu ukupila ngapandle kwelipepa lendaba,
ngummangaliso kum xa ndiva umntu esiti yena akalitati. Kambe abanye bati
buxoki bodwa obubalwayo, mna andikuze nditsho singumxelo wam nje

Njengoko i Bhai lisazeka ukuba yi
England yabantsundu abalesi be “ Mvo”
bangati nqa ukuba z.ivela pina into ezi-
manyumnyezi ko No. 1. Abalesi- bo
“Mvo” mabazi ukuba apo kuko impucu-

ufuna ukubona amanene wona—izigcau,

apa; ukuba ufuna ukubona amanenekazi,

uti waka wabona ntonina? [Ne Qonce
ke wena selipefumlela kufupi.—ED.]

8 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1883.

  UMBIKO.

  E-MACFARLAN ngomhla 21 ka July 1883 kubube umfundisikazi umka Rev. E.
  Makiwane. Izihlobo

  ziyacelwa ukuba lombiko ziwamkele nge Sigidimi.

ELIJAH MAKIWANE.

Macfarlan,

July 23, 1883.

  UMBULELO.

  UMFIKAZI inkosikazi yakwam pambi kokumka kwayo
  iyaleze ukuze ibulelelwe kakulu kubo bonke abantu

  abebevelana nayo beyivelela xa iselukukweni. lyaleza
  elozwi kwindlu yakwa Dr. Stewart, neyakwa Commander
  Hughes, neyakwa Mr. Bennie, neyakwa Mrs. Low,
  ku Mrs. Muirhead, no Miss Waterston ngokubalulekileyo:
  nakwabanye abantu abate bebengabasemzini bazenza ama-
  kaya kuye. Kubantu abantsundu abebemvelela ute aka-
  namibulelo kuni bantu bakowabo. Ushiye umbulelo naku
  bafundisikazi bake bonke. Kuni nonke ke ndaleka owam
  umbulelo. Nam andinazwi ndilisingisa kuni bakowetu

  abantu.

ELIJAH MAKIWANE.

Macfarlan,

July 23, 1883.

ISAZISO—ISITILI SASE DIKENI.

IRAFU YEZINDLU YOMNYAKA WE 1883.

  KUYAZISWA ukuba lixesha ngoku lokuhlaula Irafu
  Yezindlu, yalo mnyaka we 1883 kule nkundla, ema-

  yihlaulwe ke ngomhla wamashumi mabini anesitoba (29)
  ka September lo uzayo, nangapambili kwelo xesba : emva
  kwalomhla bonke abangayihlaulanga baya kudliwa isheleni
  zombini ngapezulu kwentlaulo abamele ukuyirola.

  ALEX. STEWART,
  U Mantyi.

Kwinkundla yo Mantyi,

E Dikeni, July 8, 1883.

IZIKOTSH'-KARI EZITATU.

OBENIKWE ITYALA LOKUBULALA.—Inkosana entsundu abati ngu Mamagabi, yama
Pequina ibipambi kwe Jaji e Transvaal, ngetyala lokubulala umntu,
kufumaneke ukuba akanatyala, into ke leyo ekutiwa ivuyelwe ngabo bonke,
abantsundu nabamhlope.

OFELE EMANZINI.—Indoda abati ngu Birmingham isand’ ukufela emanzini, ite
ibe ihamba namanye, besiya kuhlamba, yafika yaselingena intywila, kanti
ke koko kuntywila akasayi kubuya avuke. Kutelekelelwa ukuba makabe
ekuntywileni ubeteke ngentloko etyeni.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

UMBULALI.—Enye yalamadoda ayebulele into ka Nofeliti ngati ifunyenwe,
nakuba noko singekabi nabunqina buzeleyo.

AMAFAMA.—Lamabandla akala kumacalana onke ukuba impabla zawo zigqibele
kukufa nakukulahleka kwezimvula bezisina.

U REV. MR. MOUNTAIN.—Lomfundisi ngokunje kuvakala ukuba ukulamacala ase
Bayi, ngasempilweni akakabi nabu- njani.

AMADODA antsundu amabini kwase Monti adliwe isheleni ezintlanu emnye
ngokusuka kanti banezixobo abanazo ngapandle kwemvumelo.

ABABALEKE ENTOLONGWENI.—Ngase Ngqushwa kuko amadoda amabini abese
ntolongweni, eza kusiwa e Rini. Kwezintsuku zakutsha nje lomadoda
abalekile, akukaviwa nomkondo ngawo.

OBE ICUBA.—Ontsundu abati ngu Samuel obesand’ ukupuma entolongweni e
Monti, ubuye wamangalelwa ngokuba isinqumka secuba etafileni
yokutengela, evenkileni ka Mr. Ellis ; isigwebo unikwe inyanga ezintatu,
nemivumbo engamashumi omabini anesihlanu esebenza nzima.

OBE INJA.—E Bofolo umfo omhlope abati ngu Jackson, ubemangalelwe ngokuba
inja, ute noko ebeke wakanyela ukuba inja leyo itengiwe nguye komnye u
Mlungu nge 10s, ekugqibeleni wati hayi ubengayibanga isuke yamlandela,
isigwebo sibe yinyanga entolongweni esebenza nzima.

UKUTYIWA KWE GUSHA.—Kwangase Bofolo kusand’ ukubanjwa amakwenkwe
antsundu amabini, abanjwe xa apemba umlilo elungiselela ukuba atye
igusha abeyibile ayihlinza. Ityala alikatetwa kuba umniniyo (igusha
leyo) akakaveli.

ABAPANTSE UKUGQIBELA YITYEFU.—Ngase Rini usapo Iwendoda etile lupantse
ukuti kakalala yityefu, kute kanti ngemposiso kudityaniswe ityefu
ebigcinelwe ukutiyela ama- ramncwa, nomgubo wezonka, into ke leyo
epantse ukuba mbi: Noko ke akubangako ngozi.

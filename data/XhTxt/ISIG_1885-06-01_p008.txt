NYATELELANI PANTSI UTYWALA.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI WE-Sigidimi,—Kaundifakele lam nzwana ambalwa kwelo pepa
lako. Ngenye imini nge Cawa, ngexesha lesibini, ndalandela. abantu
abaninzi, ndangena kwindlu yecawa e Auckland. Ukungena kwam ndafika
indlu izele ngabantu, amadoda, abafazi, abafana, intombi nabantwana
bonko babekona kulontlanganiso. Ukungena kwam ndandingayiqondi
lentlanganiso ukuba yeyantonina, kodwa emva kokuhlala kancinane ndaqonda
ukuba lentlanganiso yeyabazili botywala. Kuqala, abantu abatsha bavuma
ingoma emnandi ka Mr. Mountain. Emveni koko kwabizwa amagama amalungu.
Emveni koko umgcini sihlalo wanika ilungelo kwabatanda ukuteta. Ewasuka
inkosikazi etile, yateta amazwi amukulu, iwabekisa kulutsha, yati: “
Bantwana bam, bulelani u Tixo oninike uncedo olungaka, ngokutumela u Mr.
Mountain pakati kwenu, nelungelo elikulu kangaka le Blue Ribbon.
Mandinibalisele kancinane ngexesha endandilingana nani. Besihlala no
makulu u Jwarha no Nobala, bebesila futi utywala, kodwa bebengavumi
ukuba sisele tina, besiti asifuni ukuba nifunde. Sibuze, siti,
bunantonina? Buti ukus’pendula ‘ Sifuna ukuba zeningaseli emizini yenu
nakwenda.’ Ndite ndakwenda bendigazanga ndasela, ndaqala ukububona
ubunzima botywala; mna ndiyakwazi ukuteta ngabo ngenxa yokuba
ndibufundile. Bendino nyana omkulu igama lingu George, wafunda ukusela,
ubesiti xa avela etywaleni enxilile alike abete kakulu emnyango, nditi
mna uzungalwapuli ucango Iwam, ati yena ndiya kulwapula. Ndakukumbula lo
maxesha, ewe ndiyazazi iziqamo zabo, kodwa andizange ndibusele,
andibufundanga kuma no bawo. Ngoku nina bantu batsha nifunda nakubazali
benu ukunxila. Namhla ndiyavuya ndakubona abantu abatsha benoncedo
olungaka. Ndiyambulela u Tixo ngentliziyo yam yonke ngalomsebenzi
wenziwe ngu Mr. Mountain. Ndiswele imilomo eliwaka yokubulela. Akukonto
yimbi bendizakuyiteta, kodwa bendizakuti bulelani u Tixo bantwana
onitumele u Mr. Mountain. Ize nimtandazele. Yimfanelo yetu leyo. Mna
nditi nokuba upina u Mr. Mountain, u Tixo makamsikelele, amnike amandla,
nenkutalo engapezulu, ukuze abe noku- nceda abanye abantu njengati.
Besisezintsizini, weza wasibonisa indlela yokupuma kwezontsizi. Ke
bantwana ndiyapela apo, kodwa manditi kuni maze nitandaze, nitembeke,
ningafumane nitwale igama le Ribbon yodwa, ukuze nime nqi kulomsebenzi.”

Yahlala lentokazi itete inene zodwa. Kwesuka nabanye bateta. Emva koko
kwesuka u Mgcini-sihlalo, wamema bonke abangaba bafuna ukungena
kulomhlambi ukuba beze ngapambili, ndibone kusuka igqiza lisiya
ngapambili linxiba imityana eluhlaza. Ndi- bone omnye umzali esuka
ngobunxamo eputuma umntwana wake, banxitywe ke bobabini. Ke mna
ndingumhlobo wenu nonke nina nifunda Isigidimi Samaxosa. Anikalazi ibizo
lam, kodwa noko ndingumhlobo wenu omkulu, enye into ndiyanitanda. Nditi
ke bantu bakowetu yenzani njalo. Lemisebenzi yotywala ihambisela
pambili. Ndobuye ndinixeleie ngenye imini izinto endizibonileyo,
andinaxesha namhla. Ukuba nifuna ukusela. selani i Tea, ne Kofu, Namasi.
Yini- na ukuba nibe ngamakoboka ka Satana? Utywala lityatanga lika
Satana abopa ngalo amagqoboka, ukuze akohlwe kukupakama. Nditi nam kunye
nodade lowo wase Hala, “Yelisani iziqamo, nyatelelani utywala pantsi
kwenyawo zenu. Uhlanga lwakowetu luyatshabalala butywala, utywala
ngumkokeli ka Satana, noko anicingi njalo nina.” Molweni nonke bantwana
basemhlabeni. Igama lam ngu,

LANGA.

  UMTSHATO KA REV. M. M. MOKONE.

  Pretoria, Transvaal, January 24, 1884.
  TSHAWE elihle lendaba zumanene, ndiyacela mnumzana nditi kaundi-fakele
  lomazwana ambalwa kwelo pepa lamunene autsundu akowetu. Into
  ngumtshato apa obuko, ka Rev. Magena Maake Mokone, umfo owazekayo
  kakulu kwelase Natala, Ndiyakolwa ukuba bonke ubanga- bamkeli be
  Sigidimi Sama-Xosa bayamazi ngegama. Ke umfo lowo ubesele nexesha
  elikulu kanye ebusokeni kwuse Natala apo ; apa ku uneminyaka ekufupi
  esitatwini. Manditi ke umfo lowo siyakolwa ukuba namhla makabe utshata
  ekufupi kwiminyaka e 31 nokuba ima 32. Ke utshate no Miss Mamapola
  Dorothea August, okupela ko mntwana ka Mr. David S. August, ubudala
  yiminyaka eyi 16 nenyanga ezilishumi. Kute mini laqalwa ukumenyezwa
  igama apa etyalikeni, yati iramente yake yutsho esilusizi isikalo,
  ngenxa yokuba umsebenzi uyifikele ngexesha elibi kakulu apa e
  Transvaal. Sendingati ke noko abakwenzileyo besingakulindele kanye
  tina, kuba elilizwe lisemva kakulu, kusesemnyameni kunye, akukabiko
  buramente. Kupela kusayilwa uburamente ngamagama; kusalindeleke
  kumaxesha ezayo uburamente benene. Situke kakulu malunga ne Cawa
  yokupeli egameni, ukubona abanye bepete iponti nganye, abanye isheleni
  ngantlanu, abanye inxowa zenqolowa. abanye inkabi zegusha, abanye
  amakwange ehangu, nenkuku, kwamana kuba njalo. Kute sukuyihlanganisa
  imali yaba leli nani £5 16s. 3d., ngapandle kwento ezihambayo. Lonto
  ke yabanga ukuba sibe nokuti nqa kakulu tina beli lizwe, kuba into
  enjalo besingakumbuli ukuba ingabu nokwenzeka kwaba bantu balapa
  ngenxa yobulukuni babo: kuba ngabantu aboniwayo ngama

Bulu kwasebuncinaneni, batshoniswa kakulu etywaleni nase midanisweni. Ke
u Rev. Mr. Mokone ndiyakolwa ukuba iminyaka mibini eyizama tempi
ngelizwi le Nkosi; ezama amanxila, ehamba ebakupa ezinkantini,
komidaniso. Ke okwenene ito i Nkosi yamnika amandla okuba kubeko
abafumanayo ukuba bazame ukuzinikela emsebenzini we Nkosi; kuba
sinokutembela kumazwi ati, ‘ Amatye la, i Nkosi inako ukuwaguqula abe
lusapo ku Abraham ’ ngenxa yezitembiso ezifun’ukubonakala kule
Transvaal. Ndingati ke umtshato ube ngomhla we 6 ku January, 1885.
Kwatshatisa u Rev. Frederick Thos. Nicolson walapa e Pretoria, ngokuti u
Rev. Owen Watkins (i Chairman) angabiko, ke wati umtshato waya
kwityalike yama Ngesi, bati ko abantu bafane boyinto nje, abamhlope
nabantsundu, kutshatiswa ngexesha leshumi elinanye, kute ukuba kuqitywe
kwayiwa endlwini ukuba batye idinala. Kwaye kuko u Mr. William Geel,
ititshala yase Jamani, no Mr. Joseph Kanyanne, um-Vangeli wase Tshatshi.
Kute ke kwakugqitywa ukudliwa, wesuka umfo wase Jamani lowo wanika
isiya- lo kumyeni nomtshakazi, kute ukuq'iba zabopa inqwelo ukuya kulo
ntombi, ngo 3 ukumka komhla, zaya kufika kona ekuseni sekukade
zilindiwe. Kwasusela kwaoko ukuvuma kwada kwasa. Kute ukupuma kwelanga
kwabonakala kuyimiqodi kwimimangwana yonke ukuza emtshatweni, kuba
kaloku bekutshata intombi yesidwangube. Yati lomini yaba yimigcobo
kunene. Yati into eyabantle, wati umsebenzi lowo wada waya ekupeleni
kungubangako zipitipiti. Kwati ke ngemini elandelayo kwabotshwa ukuyiwa
emakaya, safika umfundisi u Rev. Owen Watkins, no Rev. Geo. Wearind
sebefikile, ke bate noko banokukalaza kuba umtshato ungabanga
nakubalindela, kwabonakaliswa kubo ukuba bekungenxa yomzalikazi, kuba
engapile kakuhle, unqwe- nele ukuba abone umtshato womntwana wake. Bate
nabafundisi bavuya, babesebeyitengile yonke impahla yendlu, yayimpahla
efanelekileyo kanye yonke. Mandipeze mnumzetu ndiliyilo; okunye kumhla-
na ndilinga ukutabata usiba njengabanye abantu. Ndingowenu

  VUL’-INDLELA.

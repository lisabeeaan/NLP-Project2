KWITITSHERE ZAPESHEYA KWE

NCIBA.

  ITITSHERE nabongameli-zikolo ezincedwa
  kwa Rulumeni bayacelwa ukuba bagqale

lemiteto eshicilelwayo ukuze bayazi bahambe
ngayo

Owokuqala.—Akuko nanye Ititshere evu-
nyelweyo ukuba ivale isikolo mhlaumbi isishi-
ye nomnye umntu ngapandle kokuba ibe ifu-
mene imvumelo kumongameli wemicimbi ye-
mfundo, itumele ngomkangeli-zikolo, mhlau-
mbi ngomfundisi epantsi kwake.

Owesibini.—Akuko Titshere iya kuluguqula
usuku Iwe Holide olumisiweyo zi Almanak
zakomkulu ngapandle kwemvumelo evela ku-
mongameli wemicimbi yemfundo. Lomvumelo
iyakufunyanwa njengoko kumiselweyo kumteto
wokuqala.

Owesitatu.— Ititshere kufuneka ukuba isikolo
zisingenise ngexa lesitoba zisikupe ngelokuqa-
la lokumka komhla. Mayela neleshumi eline-
sinye zingake zibakupe abantwana iminiti ezi-
lishumi elinesihlanu, bake bapumle. Iklasi
zokutungisa mazibeko ukumka komhla.

Owesine.—Ititshere maziti ukubafundisa
abantwana zibalungelelanise nemiteto emisiwe-
yo yokumiswa kwamaqela.

Owesihlanu.—Incwadi yokuza kosapo (Regis-
ter) mayilungiswe iveki zonke.

Owesitandatu.—Ukuba kuko into eswelekile-
yo esikolweni (enje ngencwadi nezileiti no
pensile &c.,) kufuneka ukuba ititshere yazise
umfundisi epantsi kwake.

Owesixenxe.—Ukuba kuko Ititshere eseline-
minyaka emihlanu (nengapezulu) iwupete lo-
msebenzi icelwa ukuze iyibike londawo kum-
kangeli-zikolo xa aze kukangela isikolo sayo.

Owesibozo.—Kuyi mfanelo yamadoda azintlo-
ko apo kumi izikolo ukuba akukangelele uku-
rolwa kwemali etenjiswe Ititshere ngabantu.
Ukuba Ititshere ayiko esikolweni ngapandle
kwemvumelo mhlaumbi isikolo isikupa pambi
kwexesha nokuba kuko enye indlela engawu-
fezi ngayo umsebenzi wayo, abantu abakulu
balondawo kufuneka ukuba bazise Umkangeli-
zikolo nokuba ngumfundisi.

Owesitoba.—Ititshere kufuneka ukuba zibe
nopepa lale miteto zilibetele kunye ne Almanak
Yezikolo ezikolweni zazo kwindawo esekuhleni.

  CHARLES CLARK, Umkangeli-zikolo zapesheya kwe Nciba.

Fort Beaufort.

ISINQINISO ESITSHA.

  Fort White, February 17, 1877.

Ku Mr. J. Shaw, Fort Beaufort,—

Nkosi etandekayo.—Umntu otile wandiyale-
la ukuze ndike ndilinge iyeza lako loxaxazo
mhla ndandipetwe kakubi sisisu. Landinceda
kwakamsinyane kakulu ndaza ndagqiba kwe-
lokuba ndiya kuka ndililinge kwinkwenkwana
yam ebingazange ipile soloko yazalwayo.
Noko amagqira ndibe ndihamba kuwo ubesuke
umntwana ancedeke intsuku ezimbini nokuba
zintatu. Ndilinge elako iyeza. Ndite ndaku-
mnika okwesibini ndabon’ ukuba uyancedeka.
Ite ipela ibotile waba selepilile kwakanye
umntwana. Ngoku sekupele inyanga ezilishu-
mi elinesibini noko asikazanga simbuyele isifo.
Ndingakankanya ukuba nabanye abamelwana
bam endite ndabayalela eliyeza lako balilinga
kwababo abantwana, nabo bancedekile. Akuko
namnye ongati elazi umsebenzi walo ahlale
ngapandle kwalo endlwini yake. Mna andike
ndihlale ndingenalo.

  Ndingowako ongu

J. THOMAS.

KA

JESSE SHAW.

ENZIWE NGEMICIZA YASENDLE YALOMHLABA WASE
SOUTH AFRICA.

LAMA Yeza atenjiwe ngokukulu ngumniniwo nanga-
bo bawalingileyo ngokuwaselela izifo enzelwe uku-
nyanga zona. Azipilisa ezozifo ngokufezekileyo noku
msinya; asebenza ngoku yolileyo embilini womntu
ofayo, kuba exutywe nento ezingenzakalisiyo, nezi-
ngena ntshabalaliso ngapakati, ezifunyanwa kupela
kwi miciza ncmifuno yalo mhlaba, eyona yazekayo
usebenzo Iwayo ebuyezeni bayo; labe kanjako
inani elibizwayo ekutengweni kwawo lili ncinane.

The “ SURE CURE”—Elikupilisa kuqinisekileyo— Iyeza elikupilisa
kuqinisekileyo ekulunyweni zinyoka, nazo zonke izi nambuzane ezino
buhlungu.

“ SPECIFIC ”—Elona yeza—Iyeza elikupilisa ku- qinisekileyo ekuxaxazeni
igazi, nenkoliso yentlobo zezifo zezisu.

“AFRICANUM”— Elamazinyo—Iyeza elikupilisa kuqinisekileyo kwi ntutumbo
yamazinyo.

“ HERBAL TINCTURE ”—Incindi yemifuno—Iyeza elikupilisa kuqinisekileyo
kwi ntutumbo yamatambo obuso, nendlebe, nentloko.

“ EYE LOTION ” — Ukamelo Lwamehlo — Iyeza elikupilisa kuqinisekileyo
ekukameleni amehlo abuhlungu.

“ HERBAL ALKALINE APERIENT.”—Incindi yoxaxa- ziso—Iyeza elikupilisa
kuqinisekileyo ekuqunjelweni kwesisu, ukuswel’ ilindle, nentswela
kukanuka ukudla, icesini, nezinye izifo zolohlobo.

“ EMBROCATION ” — Elokuloba — Lingamafuta emifuno yasendle okutanjiswa
kwindawo eziqaqamba amatambo, nokuti nqi kwamalungu, ukudumba nezinye
izenzakalo.

“ SOOTHER ” — Umdambisi — Lingamafuta emifuno yasendle okutanjiswa
kwindawo ezitshe ngumlilo namadyungudyungu abangwe kutsha ngamanzi
abilayo.

“ CONFECTION OF RHUBARB.”—Elentsana — Iyeza lokupilisa izifo zonke
zentsana.

“ANTISPASMODIC” — Ele Hlaba— Iyeza lokupilisa ukubodla isab'ongo,
nelehlaba.

“ INDIAN TONIC ” — Elokomeleza — Iyeza lokunika amandla kotyafileyo,
nokulungisa umbilini.

“MAGIC HEALER” — Elikupilisa kubu bugp’i — Amafuta apilisa ngoku msinya
amanxeba, nezi londa.

“EMOLIENTUM” — Elokutambisa'—Amafuta apilisa amaqabanga, nawo onke
amaqakuva akoyo emzimbeni.

“POWDER”—Eligutyiweyo— Lixutywa ne “Lona Yeza,” kosukuba exaxaza igazi,
.olunywa sisisu.

“ SYRUP OF HERBS ” — Incindi yemifuno — Iyeza elipilisa ukukohlela.

“ WINTER SOAP ” — Isepa yofaduko — Iyeza elipilisa intlobo zonke
zokujaduka komkulu no mncinane.

Zonke ibotile agalelwe kuzo lama yeza, kubalwe incwadi ezalata
ngokucacileyo indlela zokuwasebenzisa, ayakuti ukuba lowo uwaselayo
wenze kanye njengoko kuxeliweyo kwezo zalatiso, akuzuze ngoku fezekileyo
ukupila azonda kona. Bekungafanele ukubako indlu, na mhambi uhleli
ngapandle kokuzigcinela kufupi kuye lama yeza adume kangaka; abe ngokwe
njenjalo, elumkele amaxesha angawaziyo, okuqutyulwa zezizifo zino
kunyangwa ngawo.

Elipepa lishicilelwa E-Lovedale Institution mayela kolokuqala enyangeni.

UKUCOLACOLA.

MR. MOYENI KOBANI.

Lomfana, wati emva koku buba kwabazali bake weza e Lovedale apa ngo
January 1876, yati iminyaka emitatu wahlaulelwa ngu Rev. P. J. Mzimba
abe msebenzela. Ngo 1879 uzixase ngokufundisa kwisikula sentsapo salapa
yada yangu lo ka 1880 efunda, ngenyameko, nenkutalo. Ute ngawo
watunyelwa ngu Dr. Stewart neqela lokuqala e Kimberley elayakuba
ngabatunywa elucingweni. Inqubo yake apo ivakele ukuba ibe yentle kunene
nencomekayo ngazo zonke indlela.

Ngexesha ebeselapa e Seminareni ubezinikele emsebe- nzini wokufundisa
kwisikula se Sabata kwa Gqumahashe ehamba, pakati kwabanye abazekayo, no
John Yekele aba, no Edward Tsewu lo ungumfundisi ngoku pesheya kwe
Nciba, ekugqibeleni no John Adams. Ebeti akubuya evela kwa Gqumahashe
nge Cawa, agqite aye kushumayela kwa Nkankashe kufupi ne Gxwetera, apo
siteta nje sekuko inani labantu abaguqukileyo. Isiqelo sokuvisa indaba
ezilungileyo akasiyekanga akuyishiya i Lovedale kuba e Kimberley paya
ube ngumncedi omkulu ngelocala ngangokude amiswe ebu Dikonini libandla
lase Rabe kona.

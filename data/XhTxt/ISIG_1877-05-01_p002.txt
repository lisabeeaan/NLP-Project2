abafundisi xa bafun’ ukudlula baye ku Banyai baza bafakwa etolongweni
ngokungati ngamasela, baza babuya bapindiswa indlela beziswa kwase
Lusutu. Kukuyo le Transvaal abebesiti nabakuyo abafundisi, abaquba
umsebenzi pakati kwentlanga ezintsundu kumane ukwenziwa intwanantwana
zokubacapukisa nokubapazamisa emsebenzini abanga bangawuhambisa. Kukuyo
kanye apo beziman’ ukugqunywa izinto ezigxekekileyo zokupatwa
nokubandezelwa kwabantsundu, ati oke wati nkente ahlale selejongwe
ngelibomvu. Pantsi kwe Nkosazana baya kuvulelwa aba­fundisi bamacala
onke ukuba balazise lipela iqinga lika Tixo losindiso kwintlanga
ezi­ntsundu. Endaweni yokupazanyiswa baya kuncediswa ngu Rulumeni. Xa
sitshoyo asiteti kuti opantsi kwa Mangesi akabi sabi nandawo yokukalaza.
Siyazi ukuba nawo ngokwawo abakusoloko lemihla ekala ngo Rulumeni. Into
esiyitetayo yeyokuba izinto azifani.

UBULUMKO NOKUZITOBA.

Queen’s Town, April 15, 1877.

Ku Mhleli.—Ndicela ukubeka amazwana awatile malunga nemvo yam. Kuko
indawana ezimbini, ubulumko, ubulungisa.

Manditi kuqala kuko izwana endiqele ukuliva kuhlanga lwam, eli ati umntu
mna ukuba ndilumkile nje ngo nantsi aba andingeze ndapatwa ngalempato
bapatwa ngayo ngabe lungu. Kungekuko oko kodwa nangenye imilo yezinto
ngezinto kude kube nase mivuzweni. Ukutsho andipiki ukuti lento ubulumko
asinto ikoyo nokuti ayincedi luto. Ewe buko, kanjalo buyanceda ngendlela
zabo. Kanene yintonina bona ubu­lumko obu? Ndingati buko ubulumko
obuncedayo. Bulululamo ukwenza yonke into ekutobekeni kwentliziyo
ekungazibonelini bunto bakuba mna ndim, nokunga abantu bangazi
isilinganiso sabunto bako. Nditi intliziyo enjalo ayina ngxolo pakati
kwayo, ayinamfundekelo, ekungako iyakwenza zonke izinto ekuzoleni zize
zibe buncasa kubo bonke abantu.

Kodwa isilumko ukuba asinalo ululamo makubeko enye into kuba kaloku apo
lungekoyo ululamo kuko iratshi. Eliratshi, liyadala ingxolo empefumlweni
welilumko kuze kupupume inkatazo engenakutwalwa bani, kupume ozinkani,
imisindo, ulunya, nentswela lunyamezelo ezintweni.

Umzali emntwaneni wake into enkulu ayilindayo sisimilo solulamo,
nembeko, noku- nyaniseka kuko konke. Umntwana onjalo unembeko enkulu
kunene kubo bonke aba- maziyo. Kodwa umntwana ongendawo uti

akukula abe nezwi eliti ngoku mna ndine- ngqondo andikuvuma ndimane
ukupatwa ngoluyahlobo, ndilumkile ngoku mna. Elixa ati ulumkile lelixa
ayibanga ukuba intliziyo yomzali iya imdela ngokumdela bade bacitane
nomntwana onjalo. Nditi akanasabelwa elifeni lomzali.

Ndisenje nje ngezindawo ndisondeza kwi- mvo zamadodana awafundileyo
nawafundi- swayo ukuba ayiqonde lento yolulamo ukuba ikupela kwento
eyenza imfundo yabo ibe nodumo lokubanyusela kwezipezulu izihlalo, kodwa
ubulumkwana bokutwala intloko nokudela inkosi zetu abamhlope, obunjalo
ndiyoyika ngati buya kusibeka kwepantsi intlalo. Isicaka esifuna
ukutimba umzi wenkosi yaso siwutimba ngembeko ngotobeko kude ngokunjalo
siyoyise intliziyo yenkosi, ide isipakamisele kwezipezulu indawo.
Yitimbeni inkonzo ngobulungisa nokuzitoba. Amangesi awazizo izidenge
ukuba aninyuse ngezihlalo nemivuzo nxa esabona ukuba oko koxwila
intliziyo ezingena lululamo ze zonakale ngokukulu.

Nantso ingcambo yobulumko apo ndiyi- fumana kona ; ubulumko
obungatobekanga obunganyamezeliyo bubanga umntu lowo ekugqibeleni abizwe
nge gezana intwana edelekileyo engenakuzuza nomsebenzi kwa- bamhlope.

Singabantwana kaloku kulamadoda awa- mhlope ayasifundisa esifundisa
ayajonga ka- kulu ukuba imfundo yetu ipumelela kwesi- njanina isimo.
Nditi mna ukuba imfundo yetu isikokelela kwisimilo sobulungisa akuko
msebenzi ayakusivimba wona amangesi, songena nakwepakamileyo kakulu.
Isimilo esilungileyo sotobeko siyinzuzo nobutyebi konaso, negama lake
alisayikuze lilibaleke kwabamaziyo, kanjalo,loba sisitsaba kwinkosi
ezimfundisileyo zide ziye engcwabeni zigcoba ngaye.

  P. MADIKANE.

Isigwebo site makahlaule ishumi leponti mhlaumbi abe nesitoba senyanga
etolongweni.

ngenxa yokusweleka kobunqina.

U-Mankeli ontsundu oweba igusha e-Xonoxa unikwe iminyaka emibini
nenyanga ezintandatu.

U-Salazi ontsundu owayetengise umpu komnye ngapandle kwemvumelo udliwe
£5 kuba ete ubengawazi umteto.

U-Tyanda ontsundu obebe inkabi unikwe iminya­ka emibini nenyanga
ezintandatu esebenza nzima.

U- Government inkwenkwe entsundu no Jan May abebenikwe ityala lokuba
imali ngomhla 25 ka February, kutiwe u-May makahlale umnyaka
etolo­ngweni esebenza nzima, u-Government ahlale inyanga ezintatu kuba
noko engayibanga lompahla wayamkela esazi ukuba ibiwe.

U-Isaac Arends ontsundu owazibalela incwadi yo- kufumana imali wati
ibalwe ngu Mr Hemming uni­kwe inyanga ezisitoba esebenza nzima.

U-William Johannes Hatting, Ibulu, likuvumile ukuba weba inkabi
ezilishumi e-Tarkastad. Ute Umgwebi kumatyala anje ngala akanakwenza
sahluko pakati kontsundu nomhlope, wamnika iminyaka emibini nenyanga
ezintandatu esebenza nzima.

U-Alfred Walton ipolisa lomlungu elabeta u-Tlakova umfo ontsundu,
lamposa katatu ngembumbulu, udliwe iponti ezimashumi mabini ayakuti
ukuba akaziroli abe nenyanga ezine etolongweni.

U-Martinus no George abantsundu bebenikwe ityala lokuba igusha bazisa ku
Bama waza yena wazamkela ezazi ukuba zibiwe. Kubonakala ukuba u- George
ngokutunywa ngu Martinus waya wazitabata kwa Dick baza ke baziqubela ku
Bama. U-Bama ute yena akazazi ukufika kwazo ukuba zatinina. I- nkwenkwe
yake iti zafika no George wati mayizigcine zezake. U-Bama kutiwe elake
ityala alibonakali u-Martinus wanikwa umnyaka onenyanga ezintanda­tu,
u-George wanikwa iminyaka emibini.

U-Jini umfo ontsundu udliwe £12 ngokubeta um- fazi wake.

U-Charles William Boys owayebulele umntu on­tsundu e-Bolotwa ukululwe
yijuri ngesizatu sokuba esiti yena waya kulomntu ebaleka nehashe lake
wada waya kufika kuye engaliyeki. Uti wati akufika babambana ngompu
kwaza ke ngoko kubambana wadubula umpu. U-Boys lowo waya kuxelela
umfundisi kwaoko ukuba wenzakalise umntu.

E-TRANSVAAL.

KUVAKALA ukuba u-Sikukuni uti yena akazange atsho ukuti upantsi kwama
Bulu. Kubonakala ke ngoko ukuba indaba zokuxolelana kwake nama Bulu zibe
zingxengelelwe. Enye into ebonisa oko yeyoku­ba kute ngomhla 13 ka
March, kwapuma amadoda amashumi mane anamatatu ecala lama Bulu ahamba
ehlola ilizwe lake. Ate xa asondelayo komkulu kwa­puma abantu baka
Sikukuni bawagxota. Ate xa agodukayo bavela kumacala onke abantu abati
makeme. Bakaulezile bona benyukela endulini. Kude kwako abawadubulayo.
Umfo obewapete ute maze angapindi wona adubule. Abakwa Sikukuni bati
ukuvuma kwake ukuba uyakurola inkomo ezimakulu mabini ukuba kuxolelwene
ubefun’ ukuba kusuke lamadoda ami emhlabeni wake, engatsho ukuti uzifaka
pantsi kwama Bulu.

Amadoda atunyelwe ngama Bulu nangu Somtseu ukuya kuva ukuba u-Sikukuni
utini na ngoxolo akakavakali ukuya kufika kona.

U-President Burgers, Umongameli wama Bulu

EZASE MATYALENI E-QUEENSTOWN.

KUMATYALA abese Queenstown pambi kwesikulu saba Gwebi u-Justice De
Villiers ngomhla 9 ka April kuqalwe ngelo kuba igusha Endwe.

U-Nozwe obenikwa ityala lokuba igusha ezisixenxe zomnye umntu ontsundu
ulivumile elotyala wanikwa iminyaka emitatu etolongweni esebenza nzima.

U- Charles Dabson ipolisa elimhlope ebelinikwe ityala lokugqoboza indlu
abe omacuba nekasana zentlanzi nezinye izinto ute wayenza lonto
enxilile. Umgwebi ute asikuko nokuba kulusizi ukubona umfana ongaka
ezihlazise ngoluhlobo, ngokukodwa xa angowamapolisa abantu ekulindelwe
ukuba balungise okonakalayo. Lendawo yokuba wayenxilile ayinakukangelwa.
Ngenxa yokuba ebeselenenyanga ezine etolongweni useleya kunikwa zibe
sitoba esebenza nzima.

U- Carel Spann i-German ebelinikwa ityala loku- tengisa ngompu kum Tembu
ngapandle kwemvumelo ute ukwenze oko ngenxa yokungawazi umteto.

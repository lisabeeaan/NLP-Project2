UMTETO.

WOKULUNGISELELA UKUKANGELWA NOKUPATWA KWELALI ZABANTSUNBU.

Otunyelwe Kwindlu Yeparlamente ngu
Mhlekazi I-Governor.

EKUBENI kufuneka ukuba kubeko uhlobo olugqitiseleyo lokukangelwa kwe
Lali Zabantsundu, nokuba zibe nako ukupatwa ngokugqitiseleyo ukulunga:
Makumiselwe yi Governor ye Cape of Good Hope ngalo iqinga nemvume Yendlu
Zeparlamente yayo ukuba:—

I.  Kuya kuba semandleni e-Governor ukuba iti amaxesha ngamaxesha inyule
    indoda efanelekileyo eyakubizwa ngokuti ngu Mkangeli, eyakukangela
    ipate zonke Ilali Zabantsundu njengoko ziza kubuya zixelwe ngako
    into eziyiyo: Kuze ke kodwa kungabiko Mkangeli uya kumiselwa pezu
    kwezindlu ezingapantsi kwekulu ngapandle kokuba zibe ezozindlu
    zikumhlaba womntu (ongenguwo owakwa Rulumente); nokuba kanjalo
    lomntu unokumiselwa abe ngu Mkangeli welali ezingapezu kwesinye.

II. Wonke Umkangeli uya kubatalwa kwimali yakwa Rulumente, anikwe inani
    eliya kumiselwa yi Governor amaxesha ngamaxesha, kodwa ke abanini
    zindlu, mhlaumbi abantu abableli kuzo nokuba asizizo zabo, abak.vezo
    lali amiselwe kuzo Umkangeli lowo, baya kurola isiqingata semali
    efunyanwa ngu Mkangeli, bonke bayirole ngokufanayo, kungabiko urola
    ngapezu komnye: Kodwa akayi kuti umnini ndlu mhlaumbi ohlala kuyo
    arole ngapezu kwe. shumi lesheleni ngomnyaka; kanjalo kofuneka ukuba
    kuti apo ilali isemhlabeni womntu, umnini mhlaba arole imali
    engangeya kupuma kubantu abanezindlu mhlaumbi abahlala kuzo, abakulo
    lali, abe eyirolela ukuncedisa ukuhlaula Umkangeli.

III. Eso siqendu semali esiya kuvela kubanini zindlu mhlaumbi kubantu
     abahlala kuzo, neso siya kupuma kumnini mhlaba njengoko kuse
     kukankanyiwe kuya kufuneka zirolwe amaxa onke kwakudlula inyanga
     ezintatu inikelwe ku Mkangeli nokuba kukomnye umntu ongati anikwe
     igunya lokuyamkela elinikwa ngumbalo ovela kwi Sekritari Yekoloni,
     oya kuyamkelela eofisini

VIII. Onke amahashe, inkomo, igusha
        nebokwe eziya kufunyanwa nokuba kukuyipina ilali, ezingabalwanga
        ngoluhlobo
        lumiselweyo, nokuba zezingabikwanga ku
        Mkangeli zakufika, nezo ekungaboniswanga
        ngokwanelisa Umkangeli ukuba zezikanantsi,
        mhlaumbe unelungelo kuzo, zingati zitinjwe
        zitatyatwe nguye umkangeli azifake kwisikiti anokufikelela lula
        kuso, zize ke apo
        zipatwe njengesiko lezinye izinto ezisakuba
        zifakwe kweso sikiti: Kuze kodwa kungabiko
        mpahla enje ngale ikankanyiweyo eyakunikwa koyiputumayo
        ngumgcini-sikiti, ngapandle kokuba kube kuziwe nencwadi
        ebalwe igama lo Mkangeli, eti ingakutshwa.

IX. Osukuba ecinga ukuti woniwe ngokutinjwa kwempahla ngu Mkangeli,
      mhlaumbi ngokungayi nikwa incwadi yokuya kuzikupa, uya kuba
      negunya lokuya kuzibika kwi Mantyi yalo Mahlulo, eyakuti yena
      iyipengulule lonto igwebe ngokubona kwayo
      ngokumalunga neso sikalazo, imise ukuba
      kwenzeke njengoko iqonda ngako.

X.  Nokuba ngubanina oya kupazamisa
      Umkangeli woluhlobo kutetwe ngalo uya
      kuti, yakubonakaliswa lonto, adliwe imali
      engengapezulu kweponti ezintlanu, aze ukuba
      akayiroli lomali afakwe etolongweni, awunikwe mhlaumbi angawunikwa
      umsebenzi
      onzima, awanikwe mhlaumbi angawanikwa
      amanzi ereyisi, ixesha elingengapezulu
      kwenyaga ezintatu, aze mhlaumbi afakwe
      etolongweni engalinikwanga ituba lokuba
      arole imali.

XI. Ukuba kuko ummi Welali Yabantsundu ololuhlobo luxeliweyo oyakunikwa
      ityala lokuba ube ihashe nokuba yinkomo nokuba yigusha nokuba
      yibokwe, mhlaumbi ekuya kutiwa uzamkele ezazi ukuba zibiwe,
      londawo yokubaimpahla anikwa ityala ngayo ifunyanwa inaye ibe
      ingabalwanga ngohlobo oselukankanyiwe (abe kodwa ebenalo ixesha
      elaneleyo lokuba aye kuyibika ibalwe)

  iyakuti londawo itatyatelwe ukuba ingqina
  ukuti okwenene unetyala, kuze kuse kumela
  yena ukuzihlangula.

XII. Umntu osukuba engenalungelo
       nagunya lokuba kwi Lali Yabantsundu

angaxelelwa ukuba makemke kuyo axelelwe
ngepepa elibekwe igama le Mantyi yalo
Mahlulo ekuyo lo lali, abe umntu onjalo

yo Mkangeli nokuba kukwenye indawo
engati iyimisele lomcimbi i-Governor ize
iyibike kwipepa lakwa Rulumente, aze ke
lowo Mkangeli nokuba ngomnye umntu
oyamkelayo aye kuyinikela kwaoko kwi
Mantyi Enkulu yalo Mahlulo zikuyo

ezozindlu kubatalwa ngenxa yazo, ekuya
kuti ukuba kuko umnini mhlaba, mhlaumbi

umninindlu nokuba ngumntu ohlala kuyo
ongayi kuyirola lomali ati kwakudlula inya-
nga ibifanele ukurolwa lomali, abe negunya
Umkangeli nokuba ngumntu oyibutayo
lokuya kuyibiza ngohlobo ekumiselwe ngalo
ngokumalunga nerafu ku Mteto 2 we 1869.

IV. Wonke Umkangeli oya kumiselwa
      ngenxa yalomteto uya kuti kulo lali amiswe
      pezu kwayo, nokuba zilali ezingapezu
      kwesinye, abe namandla negunya le Komsasi
      (Field-cornet) nokuba Lidindala.

V.  Kuya kuba yimfanelo yo Mkangeli
      wonke ololuhlobo lukankanyiweyo ukuba
      aligcine ngokunyanisekileyo encwadini inani
      lezindlu ezikulolali angu Mkangeli wayo,
      kunye namagama nemisebenzi yabahlala
      kuzo, nenani, nempau, namabala amahashe,

  nenkomo, negusha, nebokwe ezilunge nohlala
  kulondlu, ize Incwadi abala kuyo ezonto
  ihlale inokubonwa nokuba kunini na Yimantyi ehlala kulo Mahlulo nokuba
  ngomnye
  umntu onikwe igunya yiyo yambalela ipepa
  lokuba angaya kuyibona loncwadi.

VI. Ukuze Umkangeli abe nako ukuyi
    gcina Incwadi enje ngale ikankanyiweyo
    kuya kuba yimfanelo yabo bonke ngabanye
    abemi balo lali ukuba bamxelele yonke into
    angafuna ukuyazi eyifunela lomcimbi, umntu
    walo lali ote ebuziwe ngu Mkangeli, wakuhilizela mhlaumbi akavuma
    ukuxela woba
    sisulu sokudliwa imali engengapezulu kweponti ezimbini aze ukuba
    akayi roli imali
    ayidliweyo afakwe etolongweni, awunikwe
    mhlaumbi angawunikwa umsebenzi onzima,
    awanikwe mhlaumbi angawanikwa amanzi
    ereyisi (spare diet) ixesha elingengapezulu
    kwenyanga.

VII. Kanjalo kuya kuba yimfanelo
     yomntu wonke omiyo kulolali, ukuba ati
     engabuzwanga ambikele Umkangeli ngayo

indlu entsha eyakiweyo nguye kulolali,

nokuba yibokwe, ayakuti abe nayo nokuba
kuninina nokuba uyitenina ukuyi fumana,
ambikele nangokububa komntu ohlala
kulondlu yake, uyakuti ke umntu okuhilizeleyo

BATANDE ABAKOWENU.

1.  Zutande uyihlo okutandayo,

Okuxasayo nanamhla.

  Akuko lutando lunje ngo Iwake, Yen’ akazanga akone.

Chorus—Zutande uyihlo uyalupala, Usele nazo nezimvi;

  Akasena mitsi leyo yobutsha, Uyihlo uyakushiya.

2.  Zutande unyoko okondlileyo,

Etwala intsizi ngawe.

  Uzumsebenzele, umtutuzele, Ekwalupaleni kwake.

Chorus—Zumbeke unyoko umkumbulele, Kuba uyakukungela.

  Mgcine ke ngoko umlulamele, Ade atshon’ encwabeni.

3.  Mtand’ umnakwenu yimfaneleko,

Yaba ngabazalanayo.

  Ungamcapukisi ungamqumbisi, Hlalani nixolelene.

Chorus—Tand’umnakwenu ninga pambani Niligcine ikowenu. Bonakalisani
ngokutandana Ukuba niyazalana.

4.  Tand’ udade wenu otandekayo,

Uz’ ube likaka lake;

  Mpate kakuhle nangobubele, Kuba nguw’ umncedi wake.

Chorus—Zumtande uyihlo okutandayo; Tanda unyoko umondli. Utand’umnakwenu
ningaxabani, Utande nodade wenu.

PESHEYA KWE VAAL.

INDABA zokugqibela ezivela pesheya komlambo oyi Vaal zezokuba ama Bulu
selete azakulwa no Waterboer no Jantyi. Kungaba mhlaumbi oku kuyinyaniso
mhlaumbi kube akunyaniso. Ukuba kuyinyaniso asikuko nokuba anobudenge
ama Bulu apesheya kwe Vaal ukude ati aza kulwa nabantu abapantsi kolaulo
Iwama Ngesi. Kutiwa abantu abantsundu bayayitenga kunene imipu ne’ruluwa
E-Klip Drift. Kuko abavele kulomacala abayitenge kakulu nase Qonce.

  UKUBUBA KUKA REV. J. BROWNLEE.

SIYABIKA ngosizi olukulu ukububa kuka Rev.
John Brownlee wase Qonce, nge Cawa, ngemini
ya 24 ka December, 1871. Ubusel’upantse wangu
mnyaka egula. Ubudala bake bebuyi minyaka
epantse ukuba ngama 81. Igama lake alizanga
lahlukaniswa nokumiselwa kobu Kristu kwesisi-
gama se South Africa. Siyatemba ukuba kwipepa
elizayo, soba nokunibalisela ngokufutshane nge-
mpilo yale ndoda ilungileyo.

  UKUBUBA KUKA XEGO.

  KWA Nomgwadla, Emnqesha, ngosuku lwa 20
  ku November, 1871, kubube U-Xego, umfo ka

Matyahuli, wakwa Ntinde, wase Makabaneni. Ube seleyindoda enkulu,
eminyaka ifanel’ukuba ibi 80. Ubeko kweya malindi; ngulomntu wa-
landelwa ngamankonyana ngalomini. Ubemkulu kowabo, kodwa oko wakuncama
ngenxa ka Kristu. Utete lamazwi ekububeni kwake kusapo Iwake
nakwizihlobo, wati:—Andishiyi lifa lamhlaba; ilifa endilishiyayo kuni
lilizwi lika Tixo. Eli lilifa elikulu; ize niligcine ningalilahli,
hlalani pantsi kwalo. Kupela kweyonanto inkulu pezu kwazo zonke
izinto—ilizwi lika Tixo. Olidelayo akadeli lona uzidela yena. Nigcine
kakulu usapo, nilufundise Izibalo Ezingcwele. Watsho eyaleza konyana
bake. Ubekise kwizihlobo, wati—ndi- yemka, ndiyatshikitshela pakati
kwenu, ndiya kulandawo baya kuyo abangcwele. Ndayikonza ndisemtsha
Inkosi. Abanye bateta ngamahashe abo okulwa; abanye bateta ngenqwelo
zabo zoku- Iwa; kodwa mna nditeta ngegama lika Yehova. Watandaza
wati—Nkosi sindulule isicaka sako ngoxolo. Waviwa etandaza ebusuku,
ukuba ilizwi lika Tixo lihambe, balamkele okumkani bonke.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

E-RINI kuko imashini yokuqandusela amaqanda enciniba. Isand’ukufika
kona. Kwamanye ama- zwe ziyasetyenziswa kakulu noko ziqandusela
nawenkuku.

UKUBANJWA KWA MASELA.—Iqela labantsundu libe liquba igusha ezi 199, laya
kudlula nazo E- Windvogelberg, lisiya Emaxoseni. Ute U-Mr. Thomson
Imantyi ayakoliseka ukuba zezawo, yaselibiza amapolisa. Ahle amfumana
umninizo, azibuyisela kuye, engekazi yena nokuba zibiwe.

E-LIKHATLONG.—Kute ngomhla wa 21 ka No­vember kwatsha enye yendlu
zabantu aban­tsundu. Kute kuba umoya ubumkulu wahla wakula umlilo,
kwatsha izindlu zabantsundu zantandatu, kunye neyomfundisi wakona U-Rev.
Mr- Ashton. Nempahla yake itshe kakulu, kuba yena ubengeko nasekaya,
ubehambe ngendawo zokushumayela.

OBEFUNYENWE YINGWE.—E-Mta kuko indoda entsundu esand’ukufunyanwa yingwe,
yamluma edolweni, inzipo yazifaka embonjeni, yamrapula kunene emzimbeni.
Lite iqela labantsundu laya kuyizingela ngezinja. Enye inja iyishunqule
in- tloko, yazake yabaleka yazimela ehlatini, unangoku ayikange ibuye
ibonwe ngakumbi.

IRAEU.—Ngeveki ezimbini zokuqala zika Dece­mber imali yerafu erolwe
kwilali yaba Tembu E-Queenstown ibe ziponti ezi £1,500.

IKATSI.—Umntu ontsundu obe sand’ ukuba igusha ye German, E-Zeleni,
ugwetywe ukuba akatswe imivumbo ema 25.

UKUBANJWA KWESELA.—U-James Mahali obesand’ukubaleka etolongweni yase
Fort Beaufort ubanjwe E-Nxuba kufupi ne Somerset. Ute ke walinga
ukubaleka azimele ezitolofiyeni. Elinye lamapolisa lidubule, imbumbulu
yapumela engubeni yake, wati ngokoyika ukuti lingabuye lidubule
waselesima. Ngoku use tolongweni yase Beaufort.

INCA ENXILISAYO.—Kuko uhlobo lwenca eku- tiwa yinca enxilisayo, kuba
inkomo zakuyidla zike zinge ziyanxila pambi kokuba zife. Kuko intwana
yayo E-Bayi kwindlu ekutiwa yi chamber of Commerce (indlu yokurweba).

OBULEWE KUKUKATYWA.—Umfo ontsundu E- Queenstown ukatywe lihashe ebunzi,
wafa emva kwemini ezintatu.

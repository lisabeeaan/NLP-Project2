AMA MFENGU NE NKOSAZANA.

MNUMZETU,—Kaundincede ngemigcana ndibulele umfo endingamaziyo ote
kwindawo yokuqala e Koloni kutetwa ngendawo ezinjengoma pesheya kwe
Nciba, ama Mfengu naba Tembu. Utsho ati ama Mfengu naba Tembu yinteto
ngabo ukuba babuyiselwe ekayeni letu sonke bangaponoshono kolwandle
silandeliswe abe Sutu. Utsho ati umnu. mzana odumileyo u Saul Solomon
wayebehle wayibona lendawo kwangapambili, labe ibandla lika Mr. Scanlen
libona njalo nalo. Manditi nditumela kuwe nkosi ye Sigidimi nangani
ndib'ala apa ndingahlanganisene nabani ndiyazi ukuba ndib'ala umbilini
wama Mfengu. Ukuba kungaba njalo ndikolwa kanye ukuba singaba siyabuya
siyakululeka soloko sapuma ebuntwini ngokuya kwafika imfazwe ye 1877
sazinyamakazi, sapangwa imipu, sapangwa imali yevenkile namatyala etu
watabata waginya umntu omkulu. Wati ukutyila ukuba uyasibulala sabulawa
kwa Potsha singena zintonga, ati amadoda awaye tsalela pambili wati
umbuso wo Sprigg watoba amagunya awo, labohla igunya lenkosi ebitsalela
imfundo yesizwe sayo, kwati kwamhla kwafakwa umfundisi wakowetu e Qumra
etolongweni akahleleka pantsi amawetu asatande ubunyama, ati he yena u
Mlungu akafuni mntu mnyama ufundileyo siyakolwa. Ati mhla sapangwa imipu
ati ayapangwa kaloku namagosa ka Capt. Blyth, ati akutsho amaqaba oyisa
kanye Mhlayimbi sibuyela kwa kumkanikazi singonwaba ndingazi. Isibonda
kwakuhla ukulwa esiqingateni saso siyadliwa, kanti mhlayimbi sesingazuzi
ne 1/4 kwa Rulumente, esinye sidliwe £10. Yabona ke silixoba tina
Mamfengu, angabi nantloni u Rulumente ukudla umntu owalusa umhlambi wake
engamvuzi. Singabulela sonke esakuti ukuba kungab a njalo, singati
sikangelwe litongo samkele ubuntu kesive ingcingane zobuntu. Le
Responsible Governnment iti isakuya kuhlangana bati abavana na-Belungu
bahlale bete nxwe ngokwazi ukuba kuyiwe kutetwa ngati kanenene ukuba
sicinezelwe singonanga nto. Ngamana kube njalo kesipume pantsi kobu
bugqwira, ayitetwa tina lento. Ndiyavuya ndingumamkeli we Sigidimi
ndafumana oku kubonakala, nawe uyifake e Sigidimini akwaziwa ukuba kuko
into yale ngxoxo engaziwa siti, ngati ukuba kube njalo abanye
boswelekelwa ngumsebenzi kuma. gwangqa. Ndingavuya kube njalo, ngamana
kube njalo, ke size kuzuza u Mlungu oya kunceda amadoda atanda
ukusingisela pambili silwe olutshaba, otiya ukanyo Iwasema Mfengwini.
Akaseko umfo obesipete pambi kwe 1870 into yasema Blythein u Mahola
ngabakwa Mlanjeni bangama Tamba, abakwa Nbngqause u jikeleza ilizwe
njengombane ikamnco elimzimba unoboya njengepupu oti olayamayo emke
esopisa. Ngu Capt Blyth lowo.

JOHN MAZAMISA.

Transkei, 22 Feb., 1884.

U-J. MALGAS NEBANDLA LEBOLA LASE QONCE.

MHLELI OTANDEKAYO—HLAL’UHLELI !—Ukuba ibingenguwo umdlalo bendingayi
kuyinonelela inteto ka J. Malgas yomhla we 11 ku January Koko ke
sisekeleze umhlaba wonke noluntu lonke ngalo mdlalo. Mandike ndiyityile
ke lendawo yesi sono sibange ukuba sinyeleke kangaka kumhlambi wase
zibukweni ezantsi apa, ukuze eminye imihlambi ingafumane isidele
ngomlomo ka Malgas yedwa. Impi yase Rini yayimeme tina yase Qonce ukuba
ize kusidlalela kuti apa, savuma ke tina salindela ukufika kwayo
isidlale ngomhla we Nyibidala. Sibone emva koko isaziso e Sigidimini
Sama-Xosa sib'alwe ngu Jose Maigas okokuba i Monti iyakudlala ne Rini
kwangalomhla simiselene wona kwa ne Rini. Sati nqa, sati tu. Kute ngo
Mgqibelo ongapambi kwe Nyibidala zafika injuze zeyase Rini, zipetwe
yinto ka John Lwana, nantsiya yake yaifundisa intsapo e Debe kwa Marela,
zati i Rini liyeza lofika nge Cawa, sati tina liza kobanina ? Zati kuni
apa, sake asakolwa, noko asayipuma lendawo yase Monti kubo. Lafika i
Rini okunene nge Cawa latukululela pakati, lalundwendwe Iwetu, lati liza
kudlala nati, asayikankanya i Monti ngokuba ibingasiyalezanga mcimbi
wanto kwi Rini.

Isicelo somhlambi wase Monti besingafanele ukuvunywa siti ngamatuba
amane :—1. Lemihlambi imitatu nje idlalayo ayinanto inye ihlangene
ngayo. 2. Li Rini elimeme lemizi yasema Xoseni yomibini, kucishwe lilo
elingadlala nawo matanci. 3. Besingena kukupela undwendwe Iwetu
olumngcongo kangaka, luvela kude kangaka, besesilupete intsuku ezingaka,
luzokusenza abantu kangaka, belusimeme matanci. 4. Noko isasipekuzileyo
i Monti ibingede icinge okokuba sovumela ukuba maike isiqobele i Rini
ukuze tina silifumane selidiniwe ze kubuye kutiwe ne Rini silitamsanqele
ngokusuke sidlalelwe yi Monti. I Qonce ne Monti zikupelana ezandleni
ukusebenza, lemizi ayidlulani ngemihla yokupumla (holidays), kunjalo nje
tina sizizifologu sonke, nguwupina lomhla wokupumla ungowetu tina Qonce
sodwa ? Hai bo! lempi yase Monti andingetsho ukuti nguwona mhlambi
ungena lukanana. Ndim oyakuhlal’ ingowako enyanisweni,

N. C. UMHAILA.

King Wm’s. Town, 11 Feb., 1884.

kumanene akowetu ehlelo lase Wesile anyulelwe kwi ‘ Conference ’ ye 1884
kuko indawo ezimbini afanele ukuzikangela ngengqondo ezinzulu.

1.  Ukufuneka kwe Native High School (isikolo semfundo epakamileyo
    sabantsundu.)

2.  Apo masibe kona.

1.  Indawo yokuba i Native High School iyafuneka ndingati ndikohliwe
    yindlela yokuyixoxa kweli pepa, ndingasuke nditi njengo Mculi,

‘ Ndiswele imilomo ayaba iliwaka.’

Iziqamo zemfundo kule minyaka zibonisa ukuba sekukufupi ukudlula
kwesizukulwana esaqunyiselwa ngamahlamvu mhlana sabelekwa kungene eso
Baptizo esike esifuna imfundo. Nditi ke amanene akowetu aze azamele
ukuba ibeko le Institution, yinto enkulu ejongwe ngumzi ontsundu ukuba
ibeko and nothing short kuba kufuneka oma Gqira sidiniwe ngo Siyazi
bengazi luto.

2.  Ndinga amanene akowetu angayigqala indawo emayibe kuyo; inteto iteta
    ngezi udawo zimbini i Cape Town ne Transkei, mna ndiza kuvelisa
    indawo yesitatu i Bayi (Port Elizabeth). Manditi i Cape Town kokwam
    ukubona ikude indleko zokuya zingaba nzima pezu kwabazali ngakumbi
    xa amakwenkwe emabini kanti nemali yemfundo iyakuba ninzi ngokwayo.
    Okunye, ingxakeko kumzali xa kuko ofayo into ke leyo eyakubangela
    abazali babe bunqumnqesi ukutumela abantwana babo kulo Institution.

Enye into xa ise Cape Town ifihlakele emehlweni omhlambi wako­wetu.
Kubantu abantsundu kufuneka into abanokuyibona ngamehlo ukuze ibe
nombiza kubo, isiteto setu siti ‘ ikuba litengwa ngokubonwa. Wati umfo
otile kwakutiwa mabarole imali pambi kokuba umhlaba ucandwe wabuza
lombuzo ukuba ‘ ihangu yaka yatengwa na isenxoni ? ’ Ngoko i Institution
kufuneka isemehlweni abantu bakowetu. Ngokuba se Batenjini lendawo
andiyicasile kakulu noko ndingake ndibekise ezindawana.

1.  Kufuneka ete lamadodana aya kufundiswa lemfundo ipakamileyo ayamana
    nama Ngesi ngamacala ukuze abe nokufunda amasiko awo eziqwini zawo,
    aqelane nokuteta nawo ukuze aqine izibindi, umntu angaba nayo
    imfundo epakamileyo ize kanti akakwazi ukuzipata njengofundileyo.

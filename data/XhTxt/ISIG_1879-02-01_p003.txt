Maze kungabiko madoda amenyelwa ukuba apume Umkosi, kungatanga kuqala
kuvunywe yi Rulumente yamangesi, kwanebandla elikulu lakwa Zulu,
kungabiko namabuto abutwayo ingabangako imvumelo leyo.

Umongameli omkulu ngokumela kwake i-Rulumenti yamangesi, nangecala la
leyo Rulumenti, uyakumisa inkosi eyakuba ngum- pateli wayo kwa Zulu,
nokuba kukufupi no Zulu, kumiswe nompatisi waleyo nkosi nokuba mnye
nokuba balinani jengokungafunekayo ize iti i-Rulumenti ingenise
kulonkosi okusukuba kubekiswa kumakosi akwa Zulu, nakwi bandla elikulu
lesizwe.

U Mpande, leyo nkosi ingasekoyo, kwa no Cetywayo ebutsbeni bake,
bebevumele abafundisi abatile bapesheya kolwandle ukuba beze kuma kwa
Zulu.

Ngaleminyaka mibini igqitileyo abo bafu- ndisi, nangona bengonanga kwa
Zulu apo, bate ukuxaliswa kwabo, bade babaleka ezikolweni zabo; kekaloku
ke bonke aba bafundisi mabavunyelwe ukuba baguquke babuye bazime izikolo
zabo ababebalek e kuzo, bavunyelwe ukuba bafundise kwa nje ngokwexesha
lika Mpande, kungabiko owakwa Zulu uyakupazanyiswa ngubani ngenxa yokuba
epulapula imfundiso yabafundisi.

Yonke imicimbi yembambano ngako oku, nangezinye izizatu ezilunge
nowasemlungwini, zindawo zokuviwa yinkosi yakwa Zulu, eko ummeli we
Rulumente,—kungabiko sigwebo, nokuba sesokugxotwa kwazulu, nokuba
sesani, esiyakufezwa singashunyayelwanga yinkosi yakwa Zulu kummeli we
Rulumente, naye engazivumanga,—kuze kuti kwakuvela inda. wo exakileyo,
iti yonaibekiswekuMongameli omkulu.

Okuseleyo ke, kokokuba ati u Cetywayo kwangoku ayikolise i Rulumente
yamangesi ngokuwona kwake kutsha nje, umhlaba wa- semangesini.

Ngokokuqala, ngokokuba ebambe ngamandla watimba abafazi ababini—ezweni
lase- mlungwini—wati ke abo bafazi wababulala ngokubi,—ekuseku gqite
inyanga ezine kwe- nzekile oko.

Okwesibini, kukupata kakubi abelungu aba ngu Smith no Dighton, ngecala
lase Natal elu Tukela, emhlabeni wasemlungwini oko ke kusekunyanga ntatu
kwenzekile.

Okwesitatu, kukungena kuka Mbilini e Swazini ngobumpi, ngokwenyanga
ezimbini zigqitile.

Ngaso ke eso sono sibi sokuqala, i-Rulu- neli yase Natal selite
mababizwe kuyo oMehlokazulu no MKumbikazulu onyana baka Sirayo,
abebepete leyompi ibiwonile umhlaba wasemlungwini. Lawo madoda
makanikelwe kwinkosi ze Ruluneli yase Natal, ngentsuku ezimashumi mabini
emva kokungeniswa kweli lizwi ukuze, bapatwe ngokomteto wsemlungwini.

Beku sekutshiwo kabini yi Ruluneli yase Natal ukuti, lawo madoda
makakutshwe, elo lizwi ke alikenziwa. Oko ke kukongezelela okukulu
ekoneni obe koniwe ngako kwi Rulumenti Yamangesi, ngoko ke, kumiswe
ukuba lendawo ihlaulelwe ngamakulu omahlanu enkomo zize ziziswe ngu
Cetywayo, kwi Rumenti yase Natal ngentsuku ezi mashumimabini, emva
kokuba zibiziwe.

Ngesizatu sika Smith no Dighton, inqu yona yesono kungatiwa ilula,
ekubeni kunga bangako konakala okungakanani kulomadoda abesoniwa, kodwa
kelempatoimbiyeyangabomi, kubantu basemangesini, besemhlabeni
wasemlungwini, eyenziwe ngabakwa Zulu, abebepumele oko, kusisono
esingenakupatwa lula, ngoko ke kumiswe ukuba abo bebesona, bahlaule
inkomo ezilikulu. Nako oku kuhlawula kuziswe yinkosi yakwa Zulu
ngentsuku ezi mashumi mabini emveni kokuba lendawo ingenisiwe.

Kukade u-Mbilini enqatyisiwe yinkosi yakwa Zulu, wati amaxesha ngama
xesha, wenza ubuhange obu nokubulala elizweni lakwa Swazi, nasezweni
elisentla elu Pongolo, elalinikwe abase Trans Vaal ngabo base Swazi,
okokugqibela kukupuma kuka Mbilini lowo kwaba nokububa kwabantu,
nokutinjwa kwenkomo, nokutimbela ekubotshweni kobukoboka into eninzi
yabafazi nabantwana, oko konke ke akukabi naveki zininzi kugqitile.
Umbilini makakutshwe ukuze ukwenza kwake kuyokutetwa ematyaleni ase
Trans Vaal, baze bati abo bebetinjiwe ibuyiswe nayo, iti intlanganisela
yabobapangi baka Mbilini icitwe ipele.

Ukuze ke ezindawo zikanyiswe ngamazwi amafutshane, kungatiwa ekuqaleni,
kubizwe ku Cetywayo okokuba amise iziqiniso ezikolekileyo kwi Rulumenti
yamangesi nakwibandla elikulu lakwa Zulu, okokuba ngexesha elizayo
uyakuwabamba amadinga oku pata ngokulungileyo, abewenzile mhlana
watweswa ubukosi, njengoko kusekucacisiwe kweli pepa.

Okwesibini ukuba u-Cetywayo lowo apelise impato yobubuto; ati endaweni
yayo amise indawo ezingakolekayo ebandleni elikulu lakwa Zulu, nakwi
Rulumente yamangesi; Okwesitatu, ukuba kuvunyelwe abafundisi nama kolwa
ukuba babuyele kwa Zulu, nokumisa iziqiniso zokuba bangabi sapazanyiswa
ngamaxesha azayo.

Okokugqibela, ukuba zilungiswe indaw obuhange nobubi ebezisona umhlaba
wase^(:) mangesini, kuze kukutshwe u-Mehlokazulu, no mKumbikazulu ukuze
bapatwe ngokwesenzo sabo kuhlawulwe inkomo ezimakulu mahlanu ngenxa
yokulibala ekubakupeni, oMkumbikazulu no Mehlokazulu abo, bakubizwa
e-Natal apo. Kananjalo kuhlawulwe ikulu libelinyelenkomo ngenxa yempato
embi abebepetwe ngayo o-Dighton no Smith; Nokuba kurolwe u-Mbilini
nabebesona kunye naye e-Swazi, ukuze bapatwe ngokwezono zabo.

Ke kalokuke kumiswe okokuba ezindawo zipeliswe ngu Cetywayo ngemihla
emashumi mabini zakuba zingenisiwe, kwoti ukuba ke azenzekanga,
i-Rulumete yamangesi iyakwenza ngezizatu ezibonakala zilungele ukuzifeza
ezondawo zibizwayo kwinkosi yakwaZulu.

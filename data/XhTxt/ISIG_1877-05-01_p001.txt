UVIWO LWE TITSHERE.

KULUVUYO kuti ukuba netuba namhla lokuteta ngoviwo lwetitshere. Ilizwe
lakowetu lintywile ebumnyameni obuya kugxotwa zinto ezimbini kube
kupela, Ilizwi lika Tixo nemfundo; esingati ngenye indlela buya kugxotwa
ngabafundisi ne Titshere. Bonke ke abayibonayo londawo balila ngokuba
bapina abafundisi zipina ititshere, ekuti ngoko xa kuko ititshere nokuba
zimbalwa sifumane isizatu sokusivuyisa. Asisayi kudinwa ke ngoko kukuyi
beka pambi kwabafundi betu lendawo imihla ngemihla. Silila ngokuti
akwaba sibe sinokwenjenjalo futi kunoku sinokwenza ngako abe kanjalo
amanani esiteta ngawo ebengapezulu. Nokoke silila ngelo asifuni
ukusidela isiqalelo sezinto noko sisincinane. Siyazi ukuba umti omkulu
uke ube yimbewu, eti igcinwe itandwe noko isenjalo.

Abebeseluviweni ngoku kokugqibela siba- fumana bengangababeko ngo April
odluleyo. Ngeloxesha kwakuko 91, nanamhla abebeko bebekwamashumi asitoba
anamnye. Kodwa abapumelelayo ngo April bangapezu kwaba- pumelele
namhlanje. Oko abapumela ngo- kuncomekayo babe 30, namhla bamashumi
mabini anesitandatu. Ababe fumene iziqi­niselo zokuwulingana umsebenzi
babe 41, namhla bamashumi matatu anesibini. Kusuke namhla kwakula inani
labafumene izi­qiniselo zokuti bangake babambe ekufundiseni iminyaka
emibini. Abanjalo namhla balishumi elinabatatu kanti ngapambili
wayemnye. Abangapumelelanga kwakanye namhla ba 22 kanti ngapambili babe
19. Kubonakala ukuba inkoliso yeliqela langoku ibifumane yaya
ingacokisanga. Isi- kolo esidluleyo zonke ezibe zitumele, ezaba- mhlope
nezabantsundu sesase Nxukwebe.

UKUTATYATWA KWE TRANSVAAL.

ABALESI betu bobona kwindaba zase Transvaal esizinika kwakweli pepa
ukuba u-Sir Theo- phelus Shepstone (esiti tina ngu Somtseu) owayetunywe
yi Nkosazana ukuya kuhlola izinto zelozwe, ulitabatile ukuba seliba
pantsi kwama Ngesi kuba kanye. Indawo yokuba eso senzo sake sibe
sifanelekile na nokuba ubenalo na igunya asiyi kuyipata, soyishiyela
ukuba ayifeze yena ne Nkosaza­na nezinye izizwe Ezimhlope. Eyetu inda­wo
esingakangela kuyo yeyokuba ngokulita- bata kuka Somtseu elolizwe kuza
kukululeka into eninzi yamakowetu ekade ipetwe ngendlela yobukoboka noko
ibe inegama lokuba ikululekile. Pantsi kwe Nkosazana u-Victoria kuza
kubako imizamo yokuba zifumane ukukanya nokupatwa njengabantu ezo
ntsizana zalomacala—zinikwe imfundo nezinye izinto zokuhluma. Kuza
kuvuleka ukuba nontsundu osukuba enako azitengele umhlaba ayakufumana
isiqiniselo sawo njengokungati umhlope—izinto ezo ezibe zingaziwa xa
basapetwe ngama Bulu—nawo engawase Transvaal.

Enye indawo nezizwe eziyimeleyo i-Trans- vaal ngoku ziza kuzola, ziti
endaweni yokucinga ngozimfazwe zicinge ngokufuya inkomo nokulima
imihlaba; kuba Amangesi wona akayi kuke ayivumele imfeketo enje ngale
kade siyiva pakati kuka Sikukuni nama Bulu.

Xa sikangela ke ngoko kwintlalo abaya kuhlala ngayo abantsundu pantsi
kolaulo lwe Nkosazana, siyitelekisa nento abebeyiyo pantsi kwa Mabulu,
asinakuba singavuyiswa kukuba izinto ziqubele ekubeni malide litatyatwe
elolizwe njengoko sive ukuba kwenjiwe njalo. Kuya kuba lusizi kanye
ukuba liya kuza libuyele kwase sandleni sama Bulu.

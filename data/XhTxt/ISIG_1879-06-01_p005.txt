Grahams Town. May 12th 1879.

NKOSI,—Kwi Sigidimi sika May kubonakele inteto zamadoda amabini,
ezipatelele emfundisweni pakati kwaba ntsundu. Eyokuqala, ukuba
andiposisi yeyo Mhlekazi i-Ruluneli; Eyesibini yeka Dr Dale, Umpatiswa
Wemicimbi Yemfundo.

konjengaye ukubekeka : nokuba umntu uteta ntoni epambeneyo nalonteto
yake, kuya bonakala ngokunga tandabuzekiyo ukuba bukulu ubulungisa
obusetyenzwa yimfundo pakati kwabantsundu ngenxa zonke.

uteta ngemisebenzi yakwa Rulumente, ayibonayo ukuba inokufezwa
ngabantsundu abafundisiweyo, nangani beko abatshoyo ukuti “
akungelungi”—Singati ukuba siyakupika noko, singaba siyakuposana
namalungelo ohlanga.

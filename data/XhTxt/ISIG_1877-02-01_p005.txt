OSBORN, KWELAMA BACA.

December 26, 1876.

NDIYA ndiqonda ngokuqonda ukuba into ebambezela amadoda kwezintlanga
zintsundu ukuba angalamkeli Ilizwi, kukuba enezitembu. Ayoyiswa kanye
kukuba abe nomfazi amgxotayo. Yinto ekoyo futi ukuba iti indoda
yakoyiswa zinyaniso zelizwi, kuti qata abafazi bayo, ize ke ilututuzele
unkwintshana lwayo ngokuti make ndilinde ixesha elifanelekileyo. Kungayi
ntsikelelo enkulu kwelilizwe ukuba inkosana ezingati zipakamile
ziyalamkela ilizwi. Inkosi enkulu esate yalamkela ilizwi ngu Kama.
Unyana wake, oyinkosi yama Gqunukwebe ngoku, uhamba emanyateleni oyise.
Kuyinto entle ukubona inkosi entsundu ikutaza Ilizwi nento zokukanya.

Ngolwe Sine ngo 21 ka December kube kuko uviwo lwe Sikolo sase Osborn.
Lude lwapumelela kuhle kanye ololuviwo. Inkosi u-Makaula ibincedisa
nayo, ekwabeni amabaso. Kumvuyise kakulu kanye ukuba acelelwe ukuncedisa
emsebenzini onjalo. Kute emva kokwabiwa kwa mabaso, inkosi leyo yenza
imibulelo ku George Pamla otitsha abantwana ngenxa yemigudu ayenzileyo,
yatembisa ukumnika inkomo. Londawo yokurola kwayo inkomo ibonise ukuba
ikolisiwe ikutanda kanye okwenziweyo. Abamhlope abakweli lama Baca
babonise umoya otandekayo ngokuba lapo eluviweni. U-Mr. John Usher
utembise ukuba uyakumnika iponti ezintlanu (£5) umntana weso sikolo sase
Osborn oya kuguqula u-Hora- tius amse Esixoseni, aze ambize engamboni.

Kute ngomhla 22 ka December u-Mrs. White we- nzela ititshere zakwa Baca
itea.

Ibeko nenkosi u-Makaula ne Mantyi yalapa ne- nkosikazi yayo. Kute emva
kokuba kubulelwe kwa- dliwa ozikeki, kwenziwa inteto yinkoliso yabebeko,
baxela ukuvuyiseka kwabo yimisebenzi abayenzelwe li Lizwi lika Tixo:
nokuzinikezela kuka Mr. no Mrs White emsebenzini wabo wokuhambisela
pambili ubukumkani buka Kristu. Inkosi, u-Makaula, ye- nze inteto
emnandi kakulu. Uqale ngokuteta ngo- mfundisi wokuqala oweza kuma Baca
u-Rev. W. H. Gardner umfundisi obetandwa kakulu. Utete nango Mr. Hulley,
obeke okwexeshana wangumfundisi wake ute uyayitanda imfundo
nokushunyayelwa kwelizwi. Uyabulela kanye ukuba isikolo sase Osborn
sihambi- seleke pambili kanti izipazamiso zaso zibe zingaka. Uyabulela
kanjalo ukuba Urulumente emtumele Ima- ntyi. Onke Amabaca ayikangele
lonto ukuba isise- nzo sika Tixo, kuba le anayo yimantyi yokuqala ibe
ingunyana womfundisi wawo wokuqala. Emva koko utete ngo Mr. no Mrs.
White, wati u-Mr. White ungumhlobo nomfundisi wake, kupela komntu
obemnika amaqinga ngapambili. Ute umnqweno wake ngowokunga lomfundisi
nenkosikazi yakwake bangahlala bade babubele apa, aze abe lapa
amangcwaba abo.

Emva kwenteto kuvunywe amaculo kwagqitywa ngomtandazo kwapunywa. Kutiwa
sekukabini uMakaula epantsa ukoyiseka ukuba ade akolwe etintelwa
ngabafazi.

INTLANGANISO YABAFUNDISI
ABANTSUNDU

BASE WESILE E-RINI.

INTLANGANISO yesixenxe yomnyaka yabafundisi aba­ntsundu base Wesile
abalunge nommandla wase Rini ibiko kuwo lomzi, ipetwe ngumongameli
u-Rev. William Impey.

Ngommiselo we ntlanganiso ebako emva kwemi- nyaka emitatu ngoku
eyabantsundu iza kubako pa-

mbi kweyamangesi. Intlanganiso leyo iqalwe ngolwe Sibini kwada kwalolwe
Sihlanu. Bebeko nabanye abafundisi abamhlope abeze kuncedisana nompati
sihlalo. Inkoliso yabo ibengabongameli babafundisi abantsundu. Bekuko
nabapati sikolo sase Nxukwebe.

Inani labafundisi abantsundu kunye nabase kulingweni nabasafunda
e-Nxukwebe 17. Lentlanganiso ukuqala kwayo ibingati idelekile, kanti iya
kuya ikula ngokukula. Ukuqina kwayo nokutembisa kwabafundisi abantsundu
kude kwadlulisela ukubonakala ngale ntlanganiso. Kute kwakubuzwa
ngesimilo ne­nto ezisingisele elukolweni, nokukwazi ukuquba ili­zwi,
nempilo yomzimba, kwabonakala ngakumbi uku­ba lukulu koncedo lokusebenza
ngabantsundu.

Lukolise kakulu uviwo lwaba sese kulingweni na- basa fundayo. Bate bonke
abafundisi nabafundi (abantsundu) banikela intshumayelo ezibaliweyo.
Kwezo ntshumayelo bebezibalile kuqondakele ukuba lamadoda akabutataka.
Kute kwingxelo zokuhambiseka komsebenzi ngalomnyaka udluleyo kwindawo
ngendawo akuzo lamadoda kwabonisa ukuba ayazi qonda zonke indawo
zokupatwa kwe remente ngokwesiko lakuti. Akuko matanda ngokuba lamadoda
ate abizwa yi Nkosi aza afundiselwa ukuquba ilizwi ayakuba luncedo
olukulu ekuyeni kwazisa Igospel kwezinye intlanga zase Africa.

Kute kwalungiselelwa ukumiselwa ebufundiseni amadoda amane antsundu ate
alizalisa ngokukolekileyo ixesha lawo lokulingwa. Ate ke nge Cawa abekwa
izandla amkelelwa ukuba ngabafundisi abazeleyo base Wesile. (Amagama awo
ngo James Mjila, John Sikwebu, Samuel Mtimkulu no James Sakuba.)

Amadodana amabini abefunda e-Nxukwebe, aza kupuma ngalomnyaka aye
kushumayela ngapandle, abe sekulingweni. Kuko amanye akwamabini,
ama­dodana atembisayo, ekute emva kokuviwa kwawo kwabonakala
kufanelekile ukuba aye kufunda E-Nxukwebe. Indawo zabaza kushenxiswa
ziya kufezwa kweyabamhlope intlanganiso.—Journal.

IMFAZWE YASE TRANSVAAL.

KWEZI veki zidluleyo ute u-Rev. Mr. Merensky aku- ba ehlangene
nompati-mkosi ongu Ferreira no Mr. Schulz umpatiswa wemicimbi
yabantsundu abase Lydenburg, wakupa amadoda amabini aya kumyala
u-Sikukuni ukuba angabi sakuzingisa ukucasana nama Bulu ase Transvaal,
makatumele kwizipata mandla abuze ukuba zingati makutekenina ukuza
kuxolwe. Lomadoda abetunyiwe abuye esiti u-Siku­kuni uyasibulela isiyalo
sika Mr. Merensky, uti makaze kuyiteta lonto ngesake isiqu. Undulukile
ke waya u-Merensky ehamba no Schulz.

Befikile kwa Sikukuni bamkelwe ngu Sikunti no Sebase nonyana ka Sikukuni
u-Koluko, kwabonakala ukuba abantu bonke sebengxamele uxolo, kungengenxa
yokubulawa kwabo ngama Bulu, kungenxa yokoyika indlala kuba amasimi
atshabalele lilanga.

Kute ngomhla 11 ka December u-Sikukuni wacela u-Merensky ukuba ahambe
namadoda amabini aya e-Pretoria ukuya kuva kwizipata mandla ukuba
zingafuna kutiwe nina ukuze kuxolwe. Ute u-Mr. Merensky wova ku Ferreira
nokuba izitunywa ezo zinokuya kufika na e-Pretoria. U-Mr. Merensky ucele
ukuba izitunywa ezo masezihamba no Schulz abe nguye oya kubuya abuye
nazo.

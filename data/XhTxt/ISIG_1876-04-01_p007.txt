Etunxe, March 17, 1876.

Kumcokeli wendaba Zesigidimi Samaxosa. Ndiyalezwe ngu Feni umfo ka Tyali
ukuba ndishumayele kubafundisi, namakolwa akowabo kwindawo ngendawo,
ukububa komfazi wake omkulu, Umtembukazi, intombi ka Jumba inkosi yase
Batenjini.

Lonkosikazi ibube ukutwasa kwalenyanga ; yayiye kowayo. Ifikile kona
ibelekile, yati ngosuku lwesi-hlanu ibibelekile yabika intloko, yakula
lonto, kuyasa ngosuku lwesixenxe iyabuba. Nololusana akwazeki ukuba
losinda.

Lunjalo mabandla akowetu usizi oluyihleleyo lonkosi.

Yanga Inkosi yenkosizonke ingalikamela elonxeba ngomfisi welizwi layo,
yenze ukuba lompanga ubete umfo ka Tyali ati kunubembe, ube yintsikelelo
eya kumenza ukuba ayame kulowo uselezixelile kade ukuba “Ungumfo
wosizi.” Lelam elo.

  JOHN A. CHALMERS.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

KWELE DIAMOND kusakulile ukubiwa kwe diamond.

U-A. J. MACDONALD ESQ., umlungu wase Bayi urole £100 ukuba kwakelwe
unifundisi indlu.

U-OBADIAH PALMER esasite kwi Sigidimi sika February udubule intombazana
yontsundu e-Qonce ugwetywe ukuba abe setolongweni iminyaka emitatu.

ABAZIBHLELEYO.—E-Bayi umlungu abati ngu Miles uzidubule entloko wafa
kwaoko. Kube njalo nase Uitenhage kumlungu obe ngumtcteleli.

IMALI erolelwa amazwi amashumi amabini asuka apa e-Lovedale esiya kwele
Diamond ngocingo zisheleni ezisibozo ezinepeni zontandatu.

ABASEBENZI.—Elinye lamapepa akona liti, “ Ifama zikohliwe ukufumana noko
sisinye isicaka. Abantsundu abavumi nento ukusebenza nokuba sebenikwa
imali engakananina.”

UCINGO.—Kulamacala asinga ngase Nciba selude lwaya kuma nge Qumra. Imali
erolwayo ngamazwi amashumi amabini xa kutunyelwa e-Lovedale zisholeni
ezimbini ezinepeni ezintandatu.

USIZI.—Kuvakala ukuba kute ngezimvula ngase Tsomo kubeko induli
edilikayo ; kwaye pantsi kwayo kuko indlu yontsundu. Amatye adilikele
kulo ndlu ambulala kwangokoko umnikazindlu nabantwana abatatu, kwasinda
umninindlu nabautwana ababini.

AMANZI ABOMVU.—E-Aliwal North kuko umfo ote eflka evela e-Free State
waba efelwa zinkabi ezimbini, zibulawa ngamanzi abomyu. Udlule weza
kulamacala angeneno kubonakala ukuba kuko ezinye esezibambekile.
Siyatemba ukuba izipata mandla ziya kuzama ukusitintela singekandi.

IMANTYI YASE MPOFU.—Ngomhla 18 ka February Imantyi yase Mpofu
u-Alexander Bisset Esq, ube bizelwe pambi ko A.II. Smit J.P. ukuba
enikwa ityala lokuba ngomhla 2 ka January wabeta isicakakazi sake
u-Catherine Pepher. Elotyala lisagqity we eku- beni u-Mr Bisset alinde
ijaji.

OBETE UMFAZI.—E-Cradock kuko umfo omhlope abati ngu Haggins obesiwe
ematyaleni kuba ebete umfazi wake, obesand’ ukutshata naye. Ugwetyelwe
ukuba ahlaule £10. Ute xa apumayo ematyaleni kwako abamlandelayo, wati
xa adlula umjelo bambamba bamfaka emanzini. Abo bantu babemfaka emanzini
badliwe £5 emnye.

INGOZI YOKUFEKETA NGOMPU.—Kute ngase Queenstown umfo ontsundu abati ngu
Daniel waya kufuna amahashe abelahlekile epete umpana omncinane wenkosi
yake. Ute akufika kwiqela labanye abantsundu, wamana ebabonisa ukukwazi
kwake ukudubula kanti uza kusuke azenzakalise emlenzeni. Utatyatwe
wasiwa emagqireni wahlala ixeshana kona waqauka.

E-BLOEMFONTEIN kute kwizitena ezibe zizakutshiswa isicaka esintsundu
esibe sisebenza kona sabona kute tu ipepa. Sibize inkosi yaso
kwafumaneka ukuba kuko umntu obeze kufaka iruluwa kona efuna ukuze
kwenzakale umntu oya kuza kubasela izitena, nazo ngokwazo zonakale.
Litatyatiwe elopepa nenxowa ebineruluwa zati zakukangeliswa ezonto
zombini kwaqondakala apo zivele kona waya kubaujwa lomntu kanti ngu Vos,
umfo obesand’ ukupuma etolongweni.

ODUTYULWE NGENGOZI.—Kute kwiqela elitile lamapolisa ebelisiya ngase
Aliwal North, amana amapolisa (apo ebepumle kona) edubula itekeni.

Lite elinye abati ngu Grant laya kuguqa ukuba lidubule. Kuse njalo kute
tu elinye lati, yinina ukuba ungadubuli umile ? Okunene omnye usukile,
kanti uza kuti xa amayo atyibilike usuke udubule umpu ubulale u-George
H. Double, elinye ipolisa.

ABEMKE NOMLAMBO.—Ipepa langase Aliwal North liti: Apa kusand’ ukudlula
amadoda amane antsundu abevela ngapakati ehamba nabafazi nabantwana.
Alike emlanjeni unamanzi kwaza kwako eti iseza kuweva. Itshone kwaoko
ayabi sabonwa. Ate amatatu aseleyo amana ukulandela ngamanye nawo emka
kwapela onke. Basale abafazi nabantwana besenza isijwili esilusizi.

ABANINZI kubaleseshi be Sigidimi baya kuba lusizi ukuva ngokububa kuka
Mr. Hogarth obe ngumkangeli wezikolo zala macala sikuwo. Ubube
kulenyanga idluleyo. Ngexesha lokukangela kwake izikolo ubete
ngokubonakalisa umnqweno wokunga lungafunda usapo, wayivuselela imfundo
nakwabebe ngayikatalele kakulu ngapambili. Kuzo zonke indawo apo ake
wafika kona ubeshiya izihlobo ngase mva. Siya lila kakulu kanye kuba
lomfo usishiyileyo ube sisihlobo senyaniso kumntu wonke nokuba unjanina
ibala lake.

EZASE NATAL.

INTLANGANISO ZABANTSUNDU.—I-Mantyi zalapa zinentlanganiso nabantsundu
kwindawo ngendawo zokuzityilela ukuba ukusuka ku April ozayo aba­ntsundu
baya kurafa nge 14s. endaweni ye 7s ; ize ke ingabizwa lamali izi £5
yokutshata, abesiti arole yona wonke ozekayo. Abanye bate bona baxolele
ukuba kube kwanjengokwapambili, abanye bati kulungile ukuba ipele imali
yomtshato. Kubeko abalila ngokuswela imali nokuti abayazi into eya
kulandela ukuba le bayi vumile. Kubonakala ukuba abantu bakona
abakaqondi ukuba la ngamanye amaxesha kufuneka izinto ziqubele pambili,
zema ngaluhlobo lumbi. Ukutanda kwabo ukubamba okudala ababi sakangela
kakuhle nokuba iya kuba lilungelo na esukuba ingeniswa. Kute ke noko
kuba ibingasazi kuxoxwa londawo, besekubikwa into egqityiweyo, baziswa
ukuba iparlamente yase Natal imise ukuba kube njalo.

UKDLOBOLA.—Omnye wababalelani bamapepa ase Natal uti: Ndiya likumbula
ixesha endati ndihamba enjikelweni yamatyala ndafika e-Ladysmith. Samana
sincokola no Dr. Kelly Imantyi yakona, yandixelela ngomntu obeze
kumangala kuye ngengxaba.no eveliswe kulobola. Umfo otile waete walobola
inkazana etile waza akuyifumana wayi qeshisa komnye umtu
ebengenakulirola inani lenkomo cbelibizwa nguyise wentombi. Yahlala kona
lenkazana kulomntu iqeshiswe kuyebada banabantwana,pakati kwabo kwako
intombi ezimbini. Zite zakufika exabisweni lokuba zende weza kuzitabata
lamfo wayclobole esiti kaloku naye ufuna ukuzuza. Walile nazo uyisewazo
weza kumangala ku Dr. Kelly, waza vena wagweba ukuba zihlale kuyise.
Lwamfo waelobole ubenele ku Mpatiswa we Micimbi yabantsundu wase Natal
u-Somtsewu waza yena akuliva elotyala wati ezontombi zezalomfo
wayelobole, zaza ke ngoko zaya kuye.

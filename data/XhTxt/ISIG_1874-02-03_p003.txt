LANGALIBALELE.

U-LANGALIBALELE nonyana bake, neqela elikulu lamapakati ake, baya kufika
etongweni yase Maritzburg ngo 31 ka December. Uti kuko ezinye inkosana
zabantsundu ezibe zitembise ukuba ziya kumnceda, ukuba zibona kuko ituba
lokupumelela. No Molappo, lowambambayo umfaka kwakwelo qela. Yena
waselede watabata nenkomo ezi 8000 zika Langalibalele, ukuya
kuzizimelisa. Nangoku ezonkomo zisekuye kunye neqela lemipu. U-Molappo
lowo usand’ ukufumana 2000 enkomo ezingumvuzo wokuba ancedisa ama Ngesi.
Ukuba kodwa kufumaneke ziyinyanise intelo zika Langa­libalele, ziya kuya
kutatyatwa ezo nkomo.

Enye inkosana ese tolongweni ngu Putili ozalana no Langalibalele. Ityala
lake lelokuba naye ebencedisa noko ebesenjenjalo ngolilobo olusiteleyo
Nesake isizwe sicitiwe sahlutwa ilizwe. U-Captain Allison usand’
ukubamba amakulu amatatu aso. Kodwa nako asisenakwenza nto kuba
iselibanjiwe inkosi yaso. Siza kubehle sibone naso ukuba ukulwa nama
Ngesi kukuzibulalisa nokuzisa ubunzima elusatsheni.

Ezokugqibela indaba zase Natal zisixelela uku­ba liyatetwa ityala lika
Langalibalele. Liya kugwetywa ngamasiko abantsundu. Kungabi ngemiteto
yabamhlope. Kubizwe nenkosi ezine zabantsundu ukuba zize kuncedisa U-Sir
Benja- mine Pine.

Ngemini yokuqala yokutetwa yati I-Governor yakona—Namhla sihlangene
ngetyala elikulu elinokwenziwa ngumntu kwabanye, ukuvukela inkosi yake.
Likulu elityala ngokuba lidibanisa nokubulala, nokupanga,
nokubandezelana. Ute kulowo ungumbanjwa wamkumbuza ukuba ngemfanelo
yomteto wabantsundu ngelingasatetwa nokutetwa ityala lake ;
ngeleselebulewe. Kodwa noko wonikwa ituba lokuke azipendulele. Ubize
U-Mr. Shepstone (U-Somtseu) ukuba aleseshe ityala like.

U-Mr. Shepstone usuke wati—U-Langalibalele yinkosi eyayibaleka ku Mazulu
saza sayikusela apa ngomnyaka we 1819. Ute kodwa ngohloho olukohlakeleyo
nolokuvukela inkosi yake wahla- ngana nabantu bake ukuba azishiye
imfanelo aye-

tembise ukuzenza; ute ukuyenza kwake lonto wapumela intaba ze Drakenberg
enomkosi oxobi- leyo oquba inkomo, esazi ukuba ngokwenjenjalo wapula
umteto. Kanjalo ute wapakamisa izikali ngakuyo inkosi yake, waza ke
ngoko waba wenzakalise ukumkanikazi U-Victoria. Enye indawo ute
wavumelana nabantu bake ukuba batenge imipu baze bayigcina ngapandle kwe
mvumelo, besazi ukuba oko kucasene nomteto; baza kanjalo izitunywa
zakomkulu bazikululisa ingubo, bazipa- ta kakubi.

Ute yena uxakekile ukupendula kuba kuko izinto angaziqondiyo, kekaloku
akako amanqina ake, watshoke wabala isihlanu samadoda abaleke ashiya
amapolisa aya ezintabeni. Ute ngemipu ayitengwanga ngomteto wake,
kanjalo ubengenguye ohlagene na Besutu ukuze bancedise. Ngokubaleke ute,
ubesiva kusitiwa use ngozini, kanjalo izicaka zakomkulu wazikululisa
ingubo kuba wayeba zigushe izixobo ezinobuhlungu. Ityala lazo zonke
ezizinto ulinge ukulibeka lonke pezu kuka Mahbulile, enye yenkosana
ezipantsi kwake, napezu kwabantu bake.

U-Sir B. Pine upendule ngeliti ukuba ubenge- nako ukusilaula isizwe sake
ngelesuke yena wazi- nikela kwi Government.

U-Emene, inkosana yabantsundu, usuke wati. Ndiyivile yonke into etetwe
ngumbanjwa. Ababantu alibeka pezu kwabo ityala bebengenakwenza nto
ngapandle kwake. Inteto yake ayifezi nto kuba ibingeyiyo mfanelo yake
ukuti akubizwa komkulu angayi. Ukwenjenjalo kukuvukela inkosi.

U-Teleku, enye inkosana, usuke wati, ngoku akuko mntu ungade asitele
ngobudenge, kuba bonke baya wazi umteto. Kuyaziwa yinto yonke ukuba
akuko unokuhlala nompu ngapandle kwe- mvumelo. Nokuba U-Langalibalele
wayengani- kanga mteto wokuba itengwe, wayengenamfanelo yokuhlala nayo,
esazi ukuba ayinapepa lakomkulu. Kute kwakubona ukuba nguye owonileyo,
kwabu- ya kwaqala yena ukupakamisa isandla. Namhla ukuba bekusalaula
abantsundu ngekungasatelwa, ngeleselebulewe kwanini. Ngelebulela
ukumpata kuhle kwabelungu.

U-Mavuno, usuke wati kwelakowabo umntu owenze into enje ubeya kupatiswa
kwenja elume inkosi yayo. Lomntu seleze kuzipakamisa apa nje, ubengeyiyo
nenkosi kwa Zulu. Ngento aye- nzileyo uhlazise yonke into entsundu,
ukala ngerafu, ungati ingumtwalo kanti ayinjalo.

U-Zatshuke ute, bekumfanele U-Langalibalele ukuba ayihlanganise imipu
yakubizwa, nokuba ubengatshongo ukuti maitengwe. Izinto azenzi- leyo
zicasene nezinto azitetayo. Imposiso yake yokuqala ibe ikukungayi
akubizwa yinkosi. Indlela eyiyo ngoku kukulivuma lonke ityala lake,
angenzi iziqingata. Uteta ngokuti akako amanqina aze ke abale abantu
abakwangababanjwa, ababunqina bungamele nto. Kuyabonakala ukuba
ubekohlisekile. Ubeba yena ama Mfengu onke aya- kusuke amvumele,
amlwise, ukuze ke abe yinkosi yawo onke. Ngoku selekuqonda ukuma
kwezinto ngelesuke ati ewe ndonile; angaxamli abantu ngokuteta.

I-Governor emva koko ite zonke inkosana zime- Iwe kukupendula ngezenzo
zabantu bazo. Xa inkosi ingapulwapulwa, indlela yayo kukuba iye kubika
kwi Government epantsi kwayo, baze bohlwaywe abangevayo.

Kubenjalo ke ukutetwa kwelo tyala ngemini yokuqala. Ngeyesibini, ngomhla
we 19 ka Janu­ary, kubizwe abantu abanqina ukuposisa kwake,
nabamtetelelayo. Asikazi ukuba siyakuba yinto- nma isigwebo sake.

ISENZO ESILIHLAZO.

KWAKUTE ngo 31 ka October kwako inqwelo elaisha E-Bayi kwa Savage no
Hill. Kulom- twalo wayo kwakuko ityeyana yemali ineponti ezingapezu
kwamakulu amatatu. Yahamba lonqweloyada yakufika kwindawo yokulalisa.
Kute kusasa U-Mr. Stabbert, umnini nqwelo, wafika iseliqekeziwe,
ingaseko imali. Waya kuyibika lento kwase Bayi, kwaza kwaranelwa kuye
nelinye Ingesi. Isela elona laliyibile lisand’ ukubonakala ngoku
ngeziveki zidluleyo. Ngumfana abati ngu Poni Cuba obesebenza apo kwa
Savage no Hill. Wati Kanti uyibonile wayilandela lo- ngwelo. Ube
ngumfana otenjiweyo ; osimilo sin- comekayo ebantwini, onesihlalo
esipakamile yo nase rementini. Ubetenjwe yi nkosi yake ngan- gokuba ide
imboleke ne £30. Kute ke emzu- zwini wacela ixesha lokuka agoduke,
walinikwa okunene. Kute ukuhamba kwake kwafana no- kwe nkosana, wakwela
kwinqwelana zamahashe, wahlala ezihoteleni, wahomba into engenkuxelwa,
ehamba nelinye itsolo elinje ngaye ngokuhomba. Ubesiti akufika
evenkileni atenge kube lusizi, ati ukupa nge mali ingeyinto engenawo
nomsebenzi. Wada weza kugaleleka nakoma Beaufort. Eku- hambeni kwexesha
ude waranelwa. Wabona selin- gena limbamba idindalaxa ase Bayi
selegodukile. Ebokisini zake, ezibe zintatu kufikwe impahla le iyinto.
Noyifumana ikankanywe pakati kwezinto nge zinto. Waqutywa ke wasiwa
etolongweni. Selevumile ukuba yayibiwe nguye lomali ikwiponti ezi £355,
nokuba seleyicitile yonke. Ngoku usetolongweni ulinde ukude litetwe
elotyala. Yindodana ye Mfengu yapeshaya kwe Nciba. Ubu- dala isenga 25
eminyaka. Into esinga abantsu­ndu bangayiqonda yeyokuba lomfo ubengumntu
opakanyisiweye zinkosi zake, enendawo etile nase rementini. Kekaloku
siti zizinto ezinje ngale ezihlisa oluhlanga luntsundu emehlweni ezinye;
ezenza ukuba kunqabe ukutenjwa kwalo. Kuko abati umntu ontsundu uyamona
ngokumpakamisa nokumnyusa. Bayakutinina bakuva inabali enje ngale ?
Ukubake kunje linabanina ityala loko ?

UKUZA KWEMALI YE SIGIDIMI.

KUTE ematyaleni ase Bayi ngo 19 ka January U-Wellem, Imfengu, wanikwa
ityala lokuba ishe- leni ezine zika Emma Jonas Umxosakazi. Lite lakutewa
kwabonakala ukuba lomali kwakufuneka ukuba ize kuhlaula Isigidimi
Samaxosa waza yena U-Wellem wenza imicimbi yake ngayo. Ema­tyaleni
kutiwe loti ukuzi limjikele elotyala alin- genise ngaluhloho Iambi
kunolu belingeniswe ngako. Kute xa kusalungiselelwa oko, wairola kwalapo
lomali waselesinda.

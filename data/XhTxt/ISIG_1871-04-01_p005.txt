  IMFAZWE YASE EUROPE.

  NO. IV.

  EYONA ndawo ingamandla kuyo imfazwe
  kususela kolwa 27 ku December ibe iku-
  kudilizelwa pantsi kwe Paris ngembum-
  bulu. lye isongezeleleka ngakumbi nanga-
  kumbi imikosi yama Prussia isiza eminqu-
  beni epambi kwe Paris, yada yakumadoda
  amawaka amakulu matatu (300,000).

  Ngolwe 10 nolwe 11 ku January, kwa
  liwa idabi elikulu kunene ngase Le Mans.
  Imikosi emibini yama Prussia, omnye
  upetwe litshawe lawo elikulu U-Prince
  Frederick William, yafunza kowawupetwe
  ngu General Chanzy. Koyisa ama Prus-
  sia, bambalwa nabafayo ngakuwo, atimba
  kuma French, amawaka alishumi elinesi-
  hlanu amadoda (15,000) nento eninzi
  yokudla.

  Ngolwe 10 olo kanjalo I-Peronne yazi-
  nikela kwinjengel’engu Von Goeben, kunye
  nomkosi wakona, omadoda amawaka ma-
  tatu (3,000).

  Ngolwe 17 ku January umkosi we Loire
  omadoda amawaka alikulu elinamashumi
  mahlanu, upetwe ngu Bourbaki, woyiswa
  ngoka Von Werder.

  Emveni koko U-Bourbaki waya E-Swit-
  zerland, enamadoda amawaka amashumi
  asibozo, waza wazinikela kuma Swiss.

  Ngolwe 18 ku January umkosi obupetwe
  ngu Faidherbe woyiswa ngoka Von Goe-
  ben E-St. Quentin, kwatinjwa amadoda
  amawaka mane (4,000), kunye nekanunu
  ezimbini.

  Lonke elixesha umzi wase Paris ubusa-
  dutyulwa. Ngolwa 23 ku January U-
  Jules Favre watumela incwadi ku Bismark
  emcela imvumelo yokuke ahlangane naye
  E-Versailles. Wavunyelwake okwenene.
  Kwatunyelwa inqwelana yamahashe eku-
  kwelwa kuyo ngu Bismark ukuba iye kum-
  kaulela U-Favre imse E-Versailles, bazake
  U-Bismark, no Favre, bahlangana ngobo-

ukudla. Xa ihlanganiswayo imali be-
kufumaneka amawaka amahlanu eponti
ngemini (£5,000). Kwelinani ke kwase-

  kuc'itwe amawaka angamashumi omane
  anamakulu matatu anamashumi mane
  (£40,340) ecitelwa ekudleni.

  Kutiwa U-Bourbaki, no Ducrot, no
  Trochu, abapati mkosi base France, bate
  ngokuncama base bezibulala.

  Kubonakala ukuba imfazwe ayikazi
  kupela. Nama Prussia angati asalindele
  ukubuy’alwe.

  Yinto ekukuteka nqa le yalemfazwe,
  yokuba izitonga zokuqala zayo zadutyu-
  Iwa ngolwa 28 ku July; yaza I-Strasbourg
  yazinikela ngolwa 28 ku September; I-
  Metz yazinikela ngolwa 28 ku October;
  yaza ne Paris yazinikela ngolwa 28 ku
  January.

  UKUBIWA KWEMFUYO NGABAMNYAMA
  “ MASINGAZIKOHLISI ngokuti kuko abantu
  abangake bapume ebumeni bobudenge,
  nokuba babe zityebi, bande, babe ngaba-
  fundileyo, ngapandle kokuba ibe inoku-
  gcinakala impahla yabo. Ukukuselwa kwe
  mfuyo yiyonanto yomeleza inkutalo, no-
  wona mongo womsebenzi. Xa kungekoyo
  oku akungelindeleki butyebi namfundo.”

  Kuko idumzelo elingaka nje kwinto
  eninzi yabafuyi abamhlope kungenxa ya-
  londawo. Xa ke sitandabuzela ekutini—
  ezizikalazo zabo, nalamanani bawaxelayo
  ento zabo ezilahlekileyo alungile, kuya-
  bonakala ngokusemhlotsheni ukuba ubu-
  sela obungangobu buya kutintela ukuhlu-
  ma kwe Koloni, ngokugqitiseleyo nakuma
  xabiso empahla ezibiwe ngalo madungu-
  dwane amasela anqenayo ukuzisebenzela,
  aze azuze umvuzo womsebenzi. Ilizwe
  lingabuya lihlume kuko ukonakaliswa
  yimfazwe, nendlala, nezifo ezibubisayo,
  kodwa ukungagcinakali kwempahla nga-
  pandle kokuba maibe ifakiwe endlwini,
  kungumvalo kuko ukwanda. Yindawo
  enjaloke le indumzelisa iqela elikulu ekuya
  kuti ukuhluma kwelilizwe kube sekutini
  lipumelele iqinga lalo.

  ndu bayiqiqe imvo ekoyo, namashwa aya-
  kuyilandela ukuba lenkohlakalo iyazingisa.

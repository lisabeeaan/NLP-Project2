INENE lase England, u Sir Don Carrie, M.P., kumjikelezo awenzileyo,
oqale e Kapa waya kwele Golide, ngendlela yase Dayi- mani, nase Natal,
udlule apa ngolwesi-Ne, ebefike ngolwesi-Tatu, esinga e Rini, eyaku- ke
ati gxada nase Lovedale.

I KARA ye Posi ehamba pakati kwe Rini ne Qonce yapuke xa ihla lommgo we
Xera ngokuti yeyele kulemisele isecaleni kwe- ndlela ijube isituba. U
Miss Bell wase Komani imte ukutya pezu komti, elinye inene lafumana
ingozi ebusweni, elixa elinye liruneke ingqwiniba. Abanye abebekwali-
qela basindile.

SIBUHLUNGU kunene yindawo yokuba ku ngekavakali ntoyimbi iginyisa amate
empi- Iwenika Rev. J. A. Chalmers wase Rini.

SINGAVUYISWA kunene ukuba abahlobo betu, abasixhasa ngokutabata “ Imvo,”
ba- ngenza imigudu abanako, ukuba intlaulo yepepa iqosheliswe ku 31
March 1888.

U SIR G. SPRIGG unduluke ngolwesi-Hla- nu e Monti ngomkombe oyi Anglian,
ukusi- nga e Bhayi, seleyindlela yokuya e Kapa.

U HON. F. SCHERMBRUCKER, M.L.C., uza- kuke abambele kwelilizwe ngazo
eziveki zieayo.

NGOLWESI-BINI ngokuhlwa u Sir G. Sprigg utete e Monti ngemicimbi
yolaulo, ngoku- kodwa ngemisebenzi ati ayinakugqitwa nayeyawa pina
amadoda e Palamente, la ale yanyulwa ngo 1884. Uncome ukuba nakuba
eyincipisile imali yemfundo yaba- ntsundu, eyemfundo yentsapo yamafama
amhlope inyusiwe, namanani entsapo aya- nda.

INTLANGANISO yabafundisi abantsundu base Wesile yomandla owonganyelwe
ngu Rev. Vulindlela iyakuba kwa Ndlovukazi ngo 13 January, nentsuku
ezilandelayo. Amagosa eziqingata ayakungena ngom- Gqibelo.

I FIVA iyanconywa e Delagoa Bay (Putu- kezi). Ingenele amanene
abalulekileyo koua apete lololiwe uqaliweyo anjengo Sir Thomas Tancred
no Mr. Philip Knee.

Bafikelwa zimpahla ezintsha ngo stemele bonke abafikayo. Ezi mpahla niya
kuzi fiimana Zizezona

ELINYE lamapepa ase Natal lixela ukuhla- peka okukoyo kwabamnyama base
Na al ngendlela engalungeleleneyo opetwe ngayo urnteto zimantyi. Umfo
wase Mzimkulu umangalelwe ngomnye wafumana wakatswa kuba kuranelwa ukuba
uyaxoka. Ulinge- nisile emagqweteni eliqala le Koloni yase Kapa, ebize
indleko ezide Zafunyanwa zahla. ulwa ngu Rulumeni wase Natal kowase Kapa
ze £25. Kutsha nje umfo ontsu- ndu obekekileyo wase sikolweni ubanjwe
ehamba engenzanganto waselezibalala e to- longweni. Batukutele abantu
yilonto.

KWISIKOLO sase St. Luke’s, Emzimku- Iwana isulu libete esitalini apo
bekuse. nga amakwenkwana ama atu, yafa enye, asinda amanye, labulala
inkomo ezimbini netole.

ISIVUNO sengqolowa nombona siyakuba sikulu nonyaka e Lasuta.

NGOM-VULO ongapaya intwana zama Ngesi ezibe ziye kudlala, nokudla izidlo
emazantsi e Ncemera (Yellowwoods), zibulele iqela lenyoka. Kubonwe yanye
iya kungena pantsi kwelitye, bakulinqika, bctuka uku- fumana ingumzi
wenyoka namaqanda azo. Unina wazo ubulewe, nezinye ezilishumi
elinantatu, batyumza amaqanda alishumi linesihlanu. Inkunzi ifunyenwe
ilele mga- nyana pezu kwelitye. Etnva kokutimbisa okutile, ayisindanga
kwisipango esikulu samatye nentonga. Zonke zixhonywe em- ngeni sezifile.

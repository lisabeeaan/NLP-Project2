UNYULO EBA TENJINI.

Ayakuvuya amawetu akweli, ashushu ngomcimbi wolunyulo, ukuva ukuba uluvo
lomzi waseba Tenjini luqalile ukuyiqonda eyona nto inkulu yolunyulo—
ukumisa iqela elibuhlobo kwabantsundu. Incwadi esizifumeneyo ngo January
2 zivela e Cala, apo abavoti abantsundu bakona, zixela ukuba impunilo
yomzi kona seyijonge kwicala elinye—icala letemba kuti sonke bantsundu.

akaziwa ngo Mr Innes abasondeze u Mr. J. C. Molteno. Intlanganiso
eyakudibanisa inteto entweni enye iyakuba ngo 16 January e Tsomo.

Malunga nemigca ebonakala emapepeni yokubulawa kwe C.M.R. e Luxweso,
Mount Ayliff, lento yabikwa kuti tina ma Mpondo kufunwa, kutiwa
makufuniswane, ngokuba lento yenzeke emdeni wama Mpondo no Rulumeni, Ke
u Kumkani u Sigcau utumele induna ezintatu (3) ukuya kufuna lomagwira
enza lonto ; ke besiba tina lento iyakuke ihambe pantsi kude kuqondakale
into ebuya nezo nduna kuba wona amabhada lawo ayakunikelwa u Rulumente,
azibonele into augayenzayo ngawo; amapepa makake axhalabe ngocuku. U
Rulumente wayesatumele indoda eyateta nati kakuhle u W. P. Leary, Esq.,
R.M. Mount Ayliff, asikasolani naye tina, sisakolwa, nova ngati,
Kanixhalabe ukupemba umlilo.

olungatandabuzekiyo. Umntu owaziyo ama Mpondo akangeze wakolwa, kuba

amanye abegoduka. Akuko nkosi ingade imeme abantu abangabanye emizini
yabo ; ndingati abantu ababeko ngama Bbastile, angemlungu, ange Mpondo,
abazifunela inkonzo enkosini kuba balablwa ngoyise. Buza kubelungu
bevenkile, uyeke intwana ezixabanisayo.

Malunga nokuti ama Mpondo ayala ukupuma impi, ezonto zona lucuku
lokuteta kuba kungeko mntu ungade adele umteto we nkosi yake. Into

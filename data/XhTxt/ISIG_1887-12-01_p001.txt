Isigidimi Sama-Xosa.

NGOLWE-SITATU, DECEMBER 1, 1887.

INTLANGANISO YABAFUNDISI BE FREE

IZIGQIBO ZAYO NGOTYWALA.

KUYO le nyanga ingu October idluleyo, ke kwako intlanganiso yehlelo le
Free Church e Qonce

Kufundwe i Report ye Komiti emiselwe ukukangela ixabiso loselo.

Lo ngxelo ifike yaquba ngenteto enamandla ubume aba- totyelwe kubo a
Bantsundu, ngenxa yokutengiswa kotywala bom Lungu. Kanjalo ifike yateta
nangemigudu eyenziwe kade yokutintela ukungandi kwalontengiso yotywala,
yaze yateta ngenteto ekwanamandla xa ixela usizi Iwayo kwinto eyenziwe
ngulo Rulumente ukuti abuye awavule lamabala ayeke avalwa ngomteto,
eb’efud’etengisa ngotywala.— Lo mcimbi wotywala, ufikelele endaweni
yokuba ati wonke ubani alikatalele ilungelo labantsundu, ayondele
ngenyameko enkulu lo ndawo.

Zonke indawo ezenza ukuba ukufunyanwa kotywala kube licekwa, zimandundu
ngakumbi kona ngoku kuyo le Koloni.

Kufikwe kwasuswa irafu ebirolwa ngabenzi botywala (excise duty) kwaza
kwavulwa nendwawo ebezike zavalwa ukuba zingatengisi tywala kwa
bantsundu.

Abantu abantsundu basela umqomboti kulo lonke eli lizwe.

Ezidolopini bafike bancediswe kukutenga umgubo nemi- tombo, bati noko
bangena License, babutengise kakulu kuyo yonke into eye kusebenza
ezidolopini apo.

Kwake kohlwaywa wamnye umntu ngenxa yokutengisa utywala.

Zininzi zona izicelo ngezicelo eziye ku Rulumente zipuma kwintlanganiso
ya Bafundisi neze Temperance, nakuninzi Iwa bantsundu, incwadi ezinteto
ibuhlungu, uninzi Iwazo lupuma kubafazi bamanxila, kwaza kananjalo enza
intsholo amapepa eli lizwe kwangawo lo mcimbi, Kuyo yonke lonto u
Rulumente usuke werutyu ngo nondyola,

Isenzo salo Rulumente wase Kapa sipambene kanye nesowapesheya kwa manzi.
Side asafana kwa nowase Free State, kuba e Free State, napo ulaulo
Iwapesheya lukona, nje ngase Natal, nela Bahlambeli apo intengiso
yotywala kwa bantsundu isalelwayo nanamhla.

Ke malunga nento abayakupelela ukuba yiyo abantu abantsundu,
sel’ibonakala, kuba ngabantu abayimiginwa, abangekabi nako
nokuzikangelela inxwaleko abayakuba naze kwixa elizayo ngenxa yobutywala
basemzini kubo.

Zikwako impau ezininzi zokuba abantu abamhlope aba- kolisiwe bobu bume
bezinto.

Ike yati i Bishop yase Kapa iteta nengqungqutela yaba fundisi behlelo
lase Tshatshi abapantsi kwayo yenjenje— Eyona nto itintele umsebenzi
olungileyo ukuba ungahambi

nje ngulo mfuziselo unje ukuba mbi kwawo uvelise olunxilo luninzi
kangaka.

Lento yeyokungenwa, icingelwe zizo zonke iremente. Ayifanele kuyekelwa
kwi ntlanganiso ya Bafundisi nakubantu ngabanye abantsundu na Bazili.
Zonke imvaba namahlelo ase South Africa ngezihlangana, ngokuvana
ziyipate ngohlobo lokuba ngayo lento abantu bapakati kwe nyama nozipo.

Le ntlanganiso ye Presbytery ingeyigqiba ekubeni yona iyakuzivakalisa
malunga nawo lomcimbi kwezayo izitili, ize imeme namanye amahlelo, anje
nge Dutch Reformed (Rafomeli) yona isahleli inokuvana kokuzalana, yaye
okunye iyiyona ngobume ekubo ingaba nesoya kweyayo imigudu kune zinye.

Zonke ityalike, nazo zonke izindlu, kwa nomntu wonke ngamnye makatandaze
angayeki kulowo ungu Mvi wemi- tandazo, alisuse eliqalekiso lokunxila ku
kweli lizwe. Kutintelwe ukuba lingabuseli na kwindawo ezisesebunmyameni
zeli lizwe.

Abantu mabayiqonde lendawo yokuba mabame nge nyawo nangokwabo,
balutyafise uselo ngako konke abanako.

Siyayivuyela indawo yokuba esel’eko amawaka amabini abazili kweli lizwe,
abate nangenxa yamadinga abo, abanela kuzila tywala ngokwabo, kodwa
bazamela nabanye ukuba bazile.

Kodwa ke umzi wama Kristu ub’ungafanele kuwuyekelela pezu kwabazili
bodwa umsebenzi onjalo. Wonke um-Kristu, umelwe ’kuyizukisa in-Kosi yake
ngokwenza okulungileyo kubantu bonke. Ke, le ndawo kweli lizwe
ihlatyelwa umkosi wokuhlangula abantu ekuseleni utywala. Baninzi abantu
abangama Kristu ababa bona kungaba kugqibelele ukulunga xa ate umntu
wazigcina okwe nqu yake, aze xa kunjalo ati ukuteta—utywala bama-Xosa
bulungile abunangozi yanto.

I Presbytery namanye amahlelo angeyiqononondisa le ndawo, nokuba
kusezintlanganisweni zamagosa etyalike nomfundisi wabo, ne zabafundisi,
ne zabakokeli, no titshala, nange circulars, nangabantu abaman’ukutunywa
kwimizi yangapandle ngamaxa atile, nakwesukuba iyepakati, kwi ndidi
zonke zabantu, ukubonisa inxwaleko zotywala bonke nokufuneka ko buncatu.

Ngazwi nye. I Presbytery inge iyikangela le ndawo pakati kwabo bapantsi
kokupata kwayo ukuba bawutelele umsebenzi wobuncatu, umsebenzi okuseleyo
nomiseleke ka ntle inganeli kufumano icase ukwanda koselo nje kodwa

ISIGQIBO.

I Presbytery ite yakuba iyivile ingxelo ye Komiti yozilo yagqiba
kwelokuba, nayo iyakuquba kwangohlobo olunjalo ize ingenisele
intlanganiso zabadala namadikoni ayo indawo ebona ukuba ngekusenziwa
zona, zibekwe pambi kwe mihlambi yayo.

Ibuye yayinyula kwakona i Komiti, enikwe amandla okupakelana nezinye
iremente, ibe ijonge ekubeni u Rulumente abalelwe kwakona, nokuzamela
umzi omninzi ukuba mautukutezelwe yiyo le nto, no Mhlope no Ntsundu.

  bakuwutelekisa no Mmiselo we 1853, bafumana ukuba awucasene nommiselo
  lowo wo Nkozana. Kanjaqo uyanqinelana nencazo yo Mmiselo owenziwa ngu
  Mr. Porter, oko waye ngumcazi-Mteto wakomkulu we Koloni—incazo
  eyashicilelwa ngo July, 1853.

  “ Umzi ontsundu ubumi kwisiganga sokuba uqetulwa nganina Ummiselo we
  Nkosazana kwi Nkundla yase Kapa. Xa kusitiwa ngabacazi-Mteto be
  Nkosazana awuqetulwanga, ipelile inkani.”

AMAGAMA EVOTINI.

Mzi wakowetu ningayinyabeli indawo yokuya kuzingenisa encwadini yonyulo,
ngo November lo. Ukuba ixabiso lomhlaba owumiyo, ngo candelo, nokuba
kunge ngqesho, mhlaumbi udibene nendlu yawo, okanye iyindlu yoxande
exabiso liziponti ezi £25, ufanelwe kukungena. Ukuba ungosebenzayo, wabe
umvuzo awufumanayo ulikulu leponti, £100, ngonyaka; mhlaumbi ama £50
kunye nezixaso, ufanelwe yivoti. Xa ke ungu mvoti, namhla uya-
kukululeka ezi Pasini, neminye imiteto ebicapukisa umzi ontsundu.
Wangeniseni ke amagama enu ningasileli nina banazo ezo mpau zimiswe
ngulo mteto mtsha.

UKUCOLACOLA.

OHLATYWE WAFA.—Ipepa lase Kapa, (Cape Times) libalisa imbali elusizi
kunene yokuhlabana kwama kwenkwana amabini u St. Paul no Marnie
awayesalusa igusha. Lama kwenkwana ngama Lawo. Afumane axabana ngentwana
engaqondekiyo, asukelana, apotulana ngefisa, adilingana nangentloko wade
u St. Paul warola imela, wamdovuda ngayo u Marnie watsho wamosela. Ute u
St. Paul aku- bona lonto wanaba ukubaleka ukusinga ehlatini. Ite intwana
eyab’ibonela ikwali Lawo yakubona esiwa pantsi u Marnie yasel’iwuhlaba
umkosi, yasukela, kwasuke kwenatya ukusinga ehlatini kokubini,, koko
yashiywa yancanyiswa yinkewu yegqwira. U Hazlett (igama lalontwana) uye
kuwuhlaba emapoliseni. Kuyiwe kukangelwa ngu Chief Constable Gould, baya
bamfumana esalele kwakulandawo u Marnie esopa kakubi, enenxeba elipantsi
kwebele. Watwalelwa e Hospitaleni eyi New Somerset, apo waqaukela kona.
No St. Paul umbulali, wade wabanjwa lowo.

UKUBULALA OKUBI.—Liti ipepa eliyi Cape Argus kuko umfazi obulewe kakubi
yindoda yake, nto kutiwa ngu Char­les Heynes. Lomfo esenza lonto nje,
waye sand’ukuya kumangalela umkake kwi inspector yamapolisa, esiti
lomfazi akahambi kakuhle, waye ke (lomfo) ebonakala ukuba uke weva
utywala. Upendulwe ngokutiwa makagoduke akapilile. Kute kwasemva kokuba
ete gwiqi waya endlwini yake, abevanga ngani. Kugaleleke isipekepeke
esima- pikana, nto bati ngu Benjamin Miller, yafika yawisa eliti —“Nanku
umfazi ehlatywe wafa kulandlu ahlala kuyo u Benjamin lowo. Ate dabalala
kwaoko amapolisa, afika abona umbono ombi kunene. Alike lomfazi
ehlanganiswe ingalo zombini, wabotshelelwa emlenzeni wekoyi, Iwaza
unyawo Iwasekohlo Iwaboshelelwa entloko. Ate akumbenca, afumana
inkenkema yenxeba esiswini, kute nyeke imihlehlo, kwaye sekuko iziqwenga
zayo ezite sa pantsi mganyana emntwini.

UKUBANJWA KOMBULALI LOWO.—Liti ke elopepa, amapolisa lawo afike indlu
leyo izele zizikundla zegazi, kuko nemikondo yenyawo ezinyatele
zinegazi. Kutiwa ke umfo wati akukova ukuyenza into leyo, wanxiba
izihlangu zake, wahlamba nezandla wabekisa kwi Mountain View- Hotel apo
wabanjelwa kona. Esiwe pambi kwamatyala, ufika wakuvuma kalula
akwenzileyo, usalinde isigwebo se Jaji ngoku.

U BOKOLO NABANTSUNDU.—Kubonakele kwi Graaf-Reinet Advertiser ye 3
October ukuba ke kwabonakala inteto ka Bokolo ngabantu abantsundu kwi
-East London Advertiser eti—Yena ubehleli elukelengile kade ukulukangela
uninzi Iwabantu abantsundu malunga nokunyuka nokuhla kwabo.

INTO ABEBEFUNDA BEYIYO.—Uti ama Xosa la abefuda ezizityebi zenkomo,
ambete imigqweto, neminweba yenyamakazi, etyebile, egudile, eqaba
imbola, imizimba yawo ifumile, itanjiswa amafuta. Okoke kwakunge 1828.
Namhla aqaba ucumse nje, kwimizimba exwebileyo, ambete imiraji
yezambunjela, nokuba ngamanye amadlavu, angancedi nto nasengqeleni
nasebumanzini. Ebefuda enezindlu ezilunge pezu kwezi anazo namhla, kwaye
nokudla kwawo kungasenjengoko ebekudla kudala. Uti wati yena ukuqala
kwake ukuwabona, wawafumana engabantu abanzima, abanesitozela,
ababekekileyo.

NAMHLA AYINTONINA?—Namhla asisizwe seziyakala zamanxila, amadlavu ento
ezingento, ezingasafanelwe nakukangelelwa ntweni, nakutenjwa nganto,
kanti kwimini ezipambili eb’etembekile. Nantso ke into evelileyo kuyo
leminyaka imashumi matandatu idluleyo.

INZUZO IYAGEZISA.—Ipepa lase Dayimani liti—Kusa- ndukufumaneka indyebo
kwifama ekufupi e Potchefs­troom. Kutiwa ute mfo utile umele iqela
elitile lase Kimberley watabata i Klem (claims) ezisekulwini ukutabatela
iqela alunge kulo, wahlaula imali engamashumi ama hlanu, £50, wabe
esapungula okokwenjenjalo. Kutiwa lite i Bulu lakuyifumana lomali
esandleni, laqala laqungqisa amehlo, latweza amapumlo, umlomo waya
usakama ngokwa- kama kwada kwavela nelesizo izinyo, kwabonakala ukuba
kufuneka igqira lamazinyo kwesosituba, kuba kubonakele ukuba lizakuhliwa
kuminxana imihlati. Ligeziswa kukuba namhla selityebe lingawoti. Kutiwa
lahamba litwela kwezi- tilati zase Potchefstroom, liyipete ngezandla
lomali ingama- pepa. Kutiwa lagqityelwa lihamb’apa ukugeza. Ubuninzi
befama yalo busentengisweni, nge xabiso elingamawaka angamashumi omane
£40,000 eponti. [Liya kubanjwa kude xa kunjalo.—ED. Sigidimi.]

INDLODLO NE NDLA-MHLABA—Kumapepa apesheya kubo- nakala indaba ezilusizi
kunene zabantu ababuba yindlala.

Kutiwa kwade ngenye imini kwabonwa isihlwele esikulu kunene sabantu,
abazimfaqele zendlala, amadlakadlaka, ento esezikangele ngotuzulu,
zinenzululwane yepango zise Paris, ibotwe lase France. Ababantu,
babelinde ngapandle komzi wamapolisa, belinde ukwamkeliswa ukutya.
Kubonakele kwesosiqu kusiwa ndodana itile, qwiti siduli sendlala,
kwasekupangiswa ngentwana yowuca neveyine, kanti lomntu yinkazana
ebifuda ifundisa, yab’ize kufuna umsebenzi e Paris apo.

EZASE MA MPONDWENI.—Ite i Watchman ye 7 ku Octo­ber 1887 kuko enye
ivenkile esand’ukupangwa kwaNgqungqushe. Le venkile yeyo Messrs Anderton
Bros, yivenkilana eligxamesi lenkulu. Le venkile itengela ndodana apa
bati ngu Overton. Kutiwa kute ngam Gqibelo utile, kwafika u Ndibaniso
eyona nkosi konyana baka Mqikela, waye way’ehamba nompakati omnye.
Lenkosi ifike yacela zintwana-ntwana kulevenkile, yafike yalandulelwa
konke. Kunge kungalobolelana ngamazwi kancinane, wavakala lompakati
esel’esiti—“ Uyitukelani inkosi le?” wakanyela kwa oko u Overton.
Ivakele inkosi isiti—“ Unditukile kakade, hlaula kwangoku, ukuba akuvumi
ndiyakuyipanga le yenkile yako.” Kute xa kukulawo, u Overton wasel'
equbula izembe, esiti—Yipange ! Akuyipangina!! Ite nqumama inkosi
yati—Kaundipe ibokisi yematshisi. Ute xa ati kwitshi ukuyakuyitabata,
uve esitiwa xamfu ngasemva, lati elazembe lake wajanyelwa ngalo.
Usombulule wepululu, waya wela edotyeni. Bamsukele ixesha elitile,
wabancamisa u Mlungu. Babuyele evenkileni, bafika bemka nempahlana
zepontana ezimbalwa bemka. Kute ekutshoneni kwelanga u Ndibaniso wafika
namashumi a 20, eze kwakupanga impahla, wafika eko u Overton eputume
ibatyi yake. Afike ambamba ange ebulewe ngezembe kodwa wayekwa.
Kusenjalo ke kwelozwe.

UMTETO WOTYWALA E TRANSVAAL.—Ati amapepa kwi- miteto yotywala yase
Transvaal kuko umgca oti—Akuko Bastile, namnye umntu ontsundu welilizwe
oyakutengiseIwa utyalwa, ngapandle kokuba abe nepepa alibalelwa
ngum-Lungu wake. Umtengeli tywala oke wapambanisa kulomteto, akakubuye
anikwe License ngakumbi, okunye, nonayo xa enze lonto, uyakuyihlutwa,
aze adliwe iponti ezintlanu—ngawo onke amaxesha ayenzayo lonto.

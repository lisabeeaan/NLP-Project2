4 ISIGIDIMI SAMAX0SA, MARCH 1, 1876.

ICAWA ETUNXE.

(Rev. J. A. Chalmers.)

UMZEKELISO WONYANA OWALAHLA IKOWABO.

ISIDLO AWENZELWA SONA.

Nizise itole eliya lityetyisiweyo, nixele; sidle sibe nemihlali. Ngokuba
lonyana wam ubefile, wabuya wapila; ubelahlekile, wafunyanwa. Baqala
ukuba nemihlali. Luka xv. 23, 24.

Undwendwekwa Xosa olux'elelwayo lolubukwayo,lolunikwa imbeko. Iba
zindaba akugoduka umntu ebehambele izihlobo zake waza ke wamana exelelwa
apo abelele kona; ufike ati ndagaleleka kwa nantsi bakohlwa yinto abenza
yona kona ukundibuka kwabo, ndaxelelwa into etile etyebileyo ; ndegqita
ndasinga enantsi ndalala kwa nantsi ndaxelelwa nakona. Bati ke
nabazipulapulayo ezondaba baqonde okokuba lomntu okunene ufike wapatwa
kakuhle kwelozwe abebanibele kulo. Ke, lomfana mhlenikweni wagodukayo
ufike wenzelwa ngokwesiko esilaziyo tina bakwa Xosa kuba ute uyise
akugaleleka kowabo watsho kwizicaka ziye kuguqula ezindle paya itol’
iduna lenkomo eliya lona lityetyisiweyo zilixele. Ziyazile izicaka
lenkomo kutetwa yona kungati ibiselixeliwe kade ngumniniyo wati, lenkomo
ndifuna ukuba ityebe, ukuze ndixele yona nyakana unyana wam ate
wagoduka, kungati ibigcinelwe lomsebenzi iza kwenziwa wona namhla. Esiti
mayixelwe nje ke lomzali lenkomo ubonakalalisa ububele bake kumntwana
wake ambonayo namhla, ubuka yena ngokukwenjenje kwake, ugodusa yena,
umbuyisela kwasendaweni yake abeyilalilile yena ngokwake, uyamxolela
lento imbi ayenzileyo, uguqula elazwi litetwa ngumntwana wake lokuti,
andisafanele kubizwa ngonyana wako. Ukuba ebesamqumbele nge
engamxelelanga, ukuba ebengamenzi nyana wake ela tole lityetyisiweyo
ngelingayanga liguqulwe.

I.  —Ukuze nditi ke elokuqala, lenkomo ixelwayo ibonakalalisa ukuvuya
    kukayise; kumnandi kuye unyana wake egodukile nje, kuluxolo olukulu,
    logama wonke awayengeko unyana wake ebengonwabile uyise ngenxa
    yomntwana wake, ubeselusizini, kube kubuhlungu kuye ukucinga ukuba
    umntwana wake ukude kuye, uhleli pakati kwabantu basemzini,
    uyazitshabalalisa kona, uyazeyelisela ezinkoblakalweni kona,
    uyabulabla ubuntu bake kona. Ngoku embona egodukile ututuzelekile.
    Ke, kukwanjalo naku Tixo, ukuzitshabalalisa kwaboni akumniki luvuyo,
    akatomakalele yena kubona abantu abazizidalwa zake beyapula imiteto
    yake, belinyatelela pantsi ilizwi lake. Uti encwadini yake
    “Akanamihlali ekutshabalaleni kwaboni, unga bonke bangabuyela kuye
    bazuze ubomi.” U-Yesu oko wayesemhlabeni apa wati akuyibona
    inkoblakalo yomzi wase Yerusalem akuqonda ukuba awulivumi ilizwi
    lake, awuvumi kuyamkela inyaniso yake walila inyembezi wati, “
    Yerusalem, Yerusalem, wena ubabulalayo abaprofeti, ubagibisela
    ngamatye abo batunywe kuwe, kufuti kangakananina nditande
    ukubabutela ndawonye abantwana bako, .ngohlobo eti inkukukazi
    iwabutele ndawonye amatole ayo ngapantsi kwamapiko anavuma.” Ngoko
    kwenjenjalo kuka Yesu wayesibonisa usizi oluviwa nguyise akubona
    tina bonyana bake zintombi zake singavumi kulwamkela usindiso afuna
    ukusizuzisa lona, akuko mzali emblabeni apa otanda, ukulahlwa
    ngumntwana wake amzeleyo, no Tixo ke akumkolisi akusibona sipumile
    kuye singasavumi ukuba asilaule. Wena mntu usekude no Tixo,
    usasukelana nenkanuko zentliziyo yako, noko wonwabileyo kwezozono
    zako kulomikwa yako ikohlakeleyo, noko unconywayo ngabanye abantu
    yazi okokuba.

uko ontliziyo ingonwabileyo ngawe, iselusizini ngawe, ilila inyembezi
ngawe, yeyomzali wako ongu Tixo. Unga yena ungade uguquke uze kulililela
ityala lako, mblenikweni ke kuguquka umoni nonjani, nopi, node watini
ukuwona kwake umpefumlo wake kuba yimivuyo kuye, kuba seletshilo unyana
wake ukuti, “Nditi kuni, kubako uvuyo ezulwini ngomoni emnye oguqukayo
kunamashumi asitoba anesitoba amalungisa wona angaswele nguquko.”

II. —Ndifike kwilizwi lesibini nditi, uti apa uyise makudliwe ibe
    yimihlali. Kwenjiwa njalo na mhlenikweni wafudukayo? Kwabako nto
    ixelwayo na lomini? Zabezihlanganisiwe yini izicaka lomini, kwatiwa,
    vuyisanani nam ngokuba nanku ndilalilwa ngumntwana wam? Nanku unyana
    wam efuduka esiya ezweni elikude ukuyakuzipata kona? Hayi,
    kwakulusizi olungatetekiyo lomini, wanga ngati umzi ubujelwe,
    bekungeko nto yakuvuyisa bani lomini nayise nazihlobo nazicaka, kuba
    lomfana lomini wayezibulala. Lemini yanamhla yona yeyodwa, lanyana
    wemkayo ubuyile, uzitobile, uyazililela, uyalibona ityala lake,
    makudliwe ke kugcotywe, umzi wonke kayise uvuye ube nemihlali,
    ahlute lonyana wake ulambe kangaka, onwabe naye eve naye namhla
    ukuba ukowabo uxolelwe, ayibone nalemihlali ukuba ingaye. Ke nomoni
    oguqukayo wenzelwa izidlo ngu Tixo, wenzelwa imihlali nguye,
    akuxelwa nkomo kodwa, inyama netyebileyo akuyidla umntu ayonwabisi
    mpefumlo, ayixoleli zono, ukudla okubekwa pambi komoni oguqukayo
    lilizwi lika Tixo, lona lisixaso sompefumlo lona likukutya
    kompefumlo, lona lihlutisa intliziyo. Lomfa- na ukuze ayixelelwe
    lenkomo ugodukile, kwelazwe likude ebeqauka yindlala, ute ukuze
    ahlute wabuyela kowabo, ibiyindlala yodwa abeyibona paya, bekungeko
    bani umxelelayo, umzuzisa nto idliwayo, ukuze akubone ukudla
    ukumbule uyise waya wagaleleka kowabo. Ke nelilizwi lika Tixo
    alaziwa msebenzi walo ngabasekude nalo ngabangekakolwa ku Yesu
    Kristu, ngabangekatsho ukuti, “Bawo ndonile kulo izulu nasemehlweni
    ako.” Ukuze umntu aliqonde alive ukuba lilona lixasa umpefumlo
    kufuneka ukuba apume ezonweni zake abekise ku Tixo ilizwi eliti,
    “Manditinina ukuze ndisindiswe?” Ote watsho ngentliziyo yake ipela
    nguyena uyakuva. ukuba ufumene ukupila, ukuhluta, ubomi. Ukudla
    kompefumlo ngu Yesu Kristu umsindisi, nguyena ufike ayanelise
    intliziyo ihlute yonwabe; elizwini lake uti “Inene, inene, nditi
    kuni ukuba anitanga niyidle inyama yonyana womntu nilisele igazi
    lake aninabo ubomi kuni, Oyidlayo eyam inyama alisele elam igazi
    unobomi obungunapakade ndaye mna ndiya kumvusa ngomhla wokugqibela.
    Kuba inyama yam ikukudla inyaniso, negazi lam liluselo inyaniso.
    Oyidlayo eyam inyama alisele elam igazi uhleli kum nam ndihleli
    kuye.” Ewe u-Yesu Kristu uba nto zonke kumoni obuyela kuyise u-Tixo,
    uba yingubo yokwambata, uba kukudla kompefumlo, uba ngumhlauleli
    wamatyala, uba ngumteteleli ku Tixo, uba bubomi, uba ngamandla abe
    luncedo abe yinqaba. Omfumeneyo u- Yesu wamazi wamamkela wakolwa
    kuye ufumana kwalento yafunyanwayo ngulomfana akugoduka, yena ufike
    wamkelwa nguyise wazuziswa ingubo, nomsesane nembadada waxelelwa
    itole elityebileyo wadla wahluta waba nemihlali.

III. —Imbangi yokuba uyise enze imisebenzi engaka ukuba mikulu kwayo
     yintoni? Wambatiselwa ntoni lomfana lengubo unikelwa ntoni
     lomsesane nezimbadada? Lenkomo ityebileyo uyixelelwa ntoni?
     Unantoni angasuke apiwe kwintwana yokudla engaba ingumbeko, lento
     enzelwa izinto ezingaka kuteni? Ebengamlahlanga yini uyise?
     Ebengamke ngaratshi yini? Ebengamonanga uyise yini, pofu ke
     yeyentoni lemisebenzi

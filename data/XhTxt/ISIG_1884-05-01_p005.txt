IZINTO NGEZINTO.

KUBAMKELI BELI PEPA.—Singavuya kakulu iqondiwe indawo vokuba
abanamatyala amadala, oko kukuti abantlaulelo yokuxasa ipepa eli
bangekayifinceleli, mabalinge ukuyingenisa msinya, ukuze ke ipepa labo
lime. Amagosa kwindawo ngendawo oyikangelela lendawo. Singavuya kanjako
iqondiwe indawo yokuba wonke osukuba enga anganqumka ekubeni ngumamkeli
wepepa eli, ati xa sukuba etumela isaziso esinjalo, atumele nenani
alibambayo ukuzisa kwelo xesha anqumka ngalo.

U NTUNJA, ogama limbi lingu James Magobe ukululwe yi juri e Tinara
etyaleni lokubulala intombi ngomhlanziso.

IMFAZWE E SOUDAN.—Siva ukuba isayeke yenza isitonga sasinye,
kuyaqondakala sakuva ingxelo, ukuba ama Arab kanye alwa ngoburoti
obukulu.

OZIDUBULEYO.—E Monti indoda abati ngu Thomas Brown isand’ ukuzibulala.
Ukuzibulala kwayo izidubule ngompu. Izikweliti: Au ! lumkani makowetu.

INKUMBI.—Siva ukuba kuko amangqandende ngamanye enkumbi ake abonwa ngase
Nyara, emayibe zintlola. Noko akubonakali ukuba zofika kwelixa linje
ukubalela.

LAMAXESHA NGAMANYE.—Liti ipepa lase Bofolo Fort Beaufort Advocate kanye
ngoku kunzima ukufumana imali yezindlu kwabantsundu, kuba bati imali
inqabile, la amaxesha ngamanye ubunzima.

E KAPA.—Kutiwa kuko iqokobe leqanda lenkuku elati lakuqekezwa
kwafunyanwa elinye iqanda ngapakati lipilile lingenayo nento. Ubukulu
beqanda elo lafunyanwa pakati kwelinye kutiwa bungangeqanda lehobe.

UMZIMBA KA CETYWAYO.—Kuvakala ukuba iziblobo zake, nenduna, ziye
zawutata e Tshowe, kute nakuba bekungavunywa nawo zawuba ngobusuku.
Kutelekelelwa ukuba ziye kuwuncwaba emancwabeni enkosi e Mvolosi.

E MZIMVUBU—Kutiwa indodana emhlope yabinzwa entloko, yenye yamadodana
antsundu eyayizingela nawo. Omnye uti ube yena yimfene wabinza ke.
Indodana leyo ayihlalanga xesha lide emva kwengozi leyo, ihle yabuba.

IMFAZWE E TRANSVAAL.—Kusabikwa imfazwe emideni ye Transvaal, ngoku kulwa
u Mzila no Mzilikazi inkosi ezimbini zelo. Kwelinye idabi u Mzila
woyiswa walahlekelwa li 1000 lamadoda inxenye yafakwa emlanjeni.

EZOCINGO, zixela ngenene elitile elati linxilile emkombeni ngase Natal,
ladubula inkosana yomkombe, elite noko layiposa. Ibanjwe kwalapo indoda
leyo, ityalal otetetelwa komnye wemizi eselunxwemeni apa e Koloni xa ite
yafika.

KWELABA BATLAPINI.— Siva ukuba apo amapolisa asand’ ukubulala abatatu
babantu baka Mankoroane, isizatu sona asiko, kubanjwe indoda eb’inamanye
yadutyulwa, ate amanye akubona oko abaleka, amabini awo adutyulwe
ebaleka njalo.

IMITSHATO YANGOKU.—Elase America likankanya into eliti ingummangaliso,
ekukutshata kwabantu abancinane ngapambi kokufika exabisweni lokuba
ngabangatshatayo. Liti ke Iona okwalo, kubonakala ukuba kuyekelelwa
kwabantwana ezintandweni zabo.

OBULELE UNINA.—E England, kumzana ekutiwa yi Brixton, kuko umfana
oranelwa ukuba nguye onqumle unina umqala, nakuba into leyo
ingekafumaneki ukuba injalo kanye, kuko inkolo yokokuba umfana lowo
angayenza into enjalo.

UKUHOMBA KWAMANYE.—Kutiwa e New Guinea yinto ekangelwa kakuhle kakulu
kona, ukubona isifuba sendoda sisincinane. Ati ke amadoda ukwenza oko,
azimanyange ngentambo ezilukelwe lomsebenzi. Xa kunjalo nje ke,
ezabafazi izifuba kubukwa zakuba banzi.

EZOZILO.—U Mr. Eraser wase Nyara, siva ukuba uke wenza inteto emnandi
kunene kubantwana kona malungana notywala, ecapula ezibalweni indawo
ezibonisa ukungafaneleki kwe wine, eyikanyeza kanye indawo yokuba kutiwe
ingumomelezi kwabatyafileyo.

KUBATENGISI-MAQANDA.—Kubi kuti ukuva ukuba kuko isikalo sabamhlope,
esiti amaqanda atengiswa ngabantsundu makalunyukelwe kuba adla ngokuba
ngamaqanda abolileyo. Bade bati abanye ngokwazi ukuba ukuviwa kwawo e
Mlungwini aye ahlukuhlwe, baqale bawapeke ukuze angagungquzi.

U JOEL NO JONATHAN.—Noko bekusand’ ukubako intla- nganiso kwa Letsea,
apo wayebizelwe kona u Jonathan, akubonakali ukuba baya kuhlalisana
kakuhle ngalo lonke ixesha akoyo u Joel. Siva ukuba kwakutsha nje
kusand’ ukubako elinye idabi pakati kwabantu baka Joel nabaka Jonathan.

UKUBALELA.—Kwifama ezingase Nxuba siva ukuba libalele kangaka,
ngangokude elinye ifama kona liswelekeIwe nangamanzi awanele ukuselwa
ngabantu balo, siquka nentsapo, elowo abe nomlinganiselo awutatayo
ngemini. Kutiwa elinye nakuba linefama ezimbini alinalo idlelo elanele
inkonyana ezimashumi mahlanu kupela.

SAYAWAHLETYWA.—Sifumana kwelase Monti kanjako, ndawana ifike ikankanye u
Sarili, yabe ke isiti ama Ngqika ayawate akuva ngo Bili enza
ingxumbungxumbu, liti ke, sitsho ngohlobo eba ngalo, nokuzipata kwawo,
nokukatazela kwawo ukuwela, kwaye pakati kwamadoda awelayo kuko amadoda
abe ngamapakati ka Sarili.

U SOLDANI, um Xosa wangase Monti ubulewe ngamanye litsho elase Monti.
Ite indoda le ib’ihamba nga Cawa itile ngokuhlwa, yaqubisana namadoda
amatatu. Ite enye sipe umnqwazi lowo, ite yakuba ingavumi nawo indoda
le, yasel’ ikupa enye ngembokotwe zinzi, yatsho entloko waya wafumba,
ute noko awileyo aqokela ngenduku amanye, amshiya eludwamba. Ayihlalanga
ntsuku ngapi indoda leyo ihle yaqauka. Abakaziwa ababulali abo.

NGEVEKI yokuqala (15—21 April) kungene intlanganiso iquka abafundisi
bodwa. Ingxoxo zale ntlanganiso siva ukuba zibe ninzi, kodwa njengokuba
zihanjiswa ingcango zivaliwe akusemandleni etu ukuzivakalisa. Indawo
yokuba kufanelene ukuba intlanganiso zeziqingata (District Committees)
zabamhlope nabamnyama zihanjiswe kunye ibuyiselwe kwakwezo
ntlanganiswana eziyakunika ingxelo yazo kwi Conference eyakuba se Natal
Etekwini (Durban).

UMPAKATI WE AFRIKA.—Indaba ezivela kwicala lase Ntshonalanga lompakati
we Afrika zibika ukuba u Mr. Stanley usand’ukufumana indawo ezintsha
ebezingaziwa. Ufumene, pakati kwezinye izinto, enye imbaxa yomlambo
abati yi Kongo.—Inkumanda yama Arabu isand’ ukutimba kwelo intsapo e
1,800 ukuya kuyenza amakoboka.—Abantu abatile abamnyama abamele elo
bapitizelise abatile abamhlope abamele i Kongo, babulala inxenye yabo.

OQENGQELEKE EWENI.—Ngase Bloemfontein litsho elase Bini (Grocott's Penny
Mail) kusand’ ukuhla ingozi embi kakulu. Ute umfo otile egaula inkuni
kwisiwana ebesingekude kuyapi endlwini yake, ehamba nenxenye yabantwana
bake. Ute esagaula, abantwana bemana bedlaladlala kwakufupi naye, enye
yentombi zake eminyaka ikwi 15 ubudala yatyibilika, ite ekuweni yabeteka
kwelinye ilitye elite kanti libucopa lakwela pezu kwayo. Kute noko
kupangwe egqireni msinya alenza luncedo Iwanto, kuba ingozi ib’inkulu.
Ihleli ixeshana yaqauka.

I DISSELBOOM KWAKONA.—Lendawo ingozi ezininzi zidla ngokuhla malungana
nayo, ibuye yahlisa enye ingozi. E Talika (Tarkastad) kusand’ ukuhla
ingozi embi kakulu, ute umfana otile enxamele ukukwela nge disselboom,
suka tyibilili waya wawa pambi kwevili, ikwele ebusweni yatsho
yavutulula amazinyo amatandatu kwawangapambili, yegqwizi emhlatini, yaza
kwapula olu lusentla kolwempetu ubambo yenjenjalo. Noko kuko amatemba
okuqubela empilweni kwendoda leyo. Mayibe ibe yomelele kanye inkewu le !

ISELA LENKUKU.—E Monti itii Fast London Dispatch, wati omnye u Mlungu
abati ngu Mr. Daniel evela kwindawo ezingapandle komzi, equba inqwelo
yake waqubisana no Mxosa ngapandle komzi emokamokana nenxowa ate akuba
kufupi waqonda ukuba izele zinkuku, kwaye kumaxa indoda le irole iboso
ilungiselela ukuzinqamla intloko. Ute u Mr. Daniel ngokutelekelela ukuba
zibiwe, wasingisa kwinkewu leyo ukuba izizise kuye, kodwa ite endaweni
yokumnika yasel’ iqaula iboso layo isonda ngaye ; koko ute u Mlungu
wayipazamisa ngokuyibeta ngesabokwe entloko. Kute kwakuba kokona izayo
inkewu waselekala ngompu u Mlungu kwinkwenkwe eyayimkokelele. Yeka!
yasel’icela koxongo inkewu izishiya inkuku.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 3, 1871.

UBUGQI.—Siva ukuba E-Tarkastad ku- bube I-Lawo abati ngu Moses de Wai
obeselenexesha elitile esifa. Ute pambi kokuba aqauke wakohlela kunene
kwaza kwapuma kuye inyoka ebude buyihalafu yonyawo, necilitshe, nesinye
isilwana. Lento ibange ukuba kuxalwe kunene E-Tarkastad apo. Izihlobo
zalomntu ububileyo zinuke elinye I-Lawo ziti utakatwe lilo.

NAKUZILUMKELA INCINIBA EZI.—Sino- sizi ukunibikela ukuba U-Mr. Kinnear
ngamhla utile ukatywe kakubi yenye ye nciniba zake. Isizekabani kukusuke
aye kungena ebuhlanti kwabe kuhlangene inkunzi nemazi; iyezake kuye
esiqwini inkunzi leyo, uyakuti U-Mr. Kinnear akufun’ukuyibamba esifubeni
ukuti ma- kayityale ingezi kuye, suke imkabe kunene esifubeni ayokufumba
evabeni lameva. Ude ke kodwa wabonwa sekwahlukene nalentaka imsindo
mkulu; kunamhlanje upetwe zizandla zegqira. Kutiwa lomsindo ungaka kule
ntaka uhlokohlwa yibatyi emnyama ebinxitywe ngu Mr. Kinnear, ete ke
ngoko intaka leyo ayamazi noko ayinkosi yayo endala ekuhlala kubonana
yonke lemihla.

I-KATSI E-QONCE.—Kute ngolwe 14 ku February, ama Xosa amatatu akuvuma
ukuba anetyala, kuba evumile ukuzamkela ibokwe ezimbini, esazi kodwa
ukuba zibiwe agwetyelwa ke ukuze akatswe imivumbo emashumi mabini
anesihlanu. Omnye umfo wafunyanwa ukuba ube igusha, nayeke wagwetyelwa
ukuba abetwe.

INDABA EZIMNANDI .—E-Canada, kwe- lase America, kuhanjiswa umsebenzi
osand’ kuqalwa kona, loke, wokwenziwa kwengu- bo zoboya begusha,
sekufuneka uboya obuninzi. Londolozani imihlambi yenu, nina bafuyi,
nifune uhlobo Iwegusha ezine vol entle, kuba iti entle imelwe yimali
engapezu kwe yenye, ngepeni. Ngebesiti abantsundu abanamafelana egusha,
bahla- ngane ngokutenga inkunzi enoboya obuhle, engaba nokufunyanwa nge
ponti ezintlanu nezintandatu.

AMAXWILI.—Asikuko nokuba maninzi kwawo amaxwili kalokunje kweli lase
Uitenhage, nakwa Glen Somer efameni ka Mr. J. S. Kirkwood, kungekukoke
nokuba ayakataza. Ngayo leveki idluleyo igqiza lawo libulele itokazi
lenkomo. E-Balmoral, ayiqukule inkabi ka Mr. Bean yenkomo, awufumana
kodwa wona umvuzo wokukalipa kwawo. Sifunyenwe isidumba senkabi leyo
sadyojwa ityefu, kwati kwakusa ngomhla olandelayo kwafunyanwa amaxwili
anga 20 ebulewe yityefu. Asand’ ukuliqala amaxwili elisiko lokubulala
nenkomo ezinkulu, kaloku awisa nento ezindala zenkabi. E-Alexandria
zininzi inkabi ezindala kwanemazi zenkomo ezi- bulawa kwa ngamaxwili,
aselesuke kaloku ayindinisa kubafuyi.

UMFO wase Sinqenqeni ute ese Klipdrift, ekwele diamond wabalela umhlobo
wake, ngokuti “ Uze umnqande wonke omtandayo oti uya kwele Diamond.”
Asikuko nokuba ebengazuzanga ke lomntu esitshonje.

KUFUNYENWE UM XOSA selefile enza- kaliswe kunene, kumzi okutiwa yi
Klein- meestersklip E-Frasersburg. Akuko matanda ukuba kuko into
embuleleyo.

ELINYE iqela legusha libiwe kwa Mr. John Diesel E-Winterhoek. Ezogusha
zilishumi elinesibozo. Apina ama Polisa ?

EZENTENGO (IFANDESI).

E-Linton, ngase Adelaide nge Wednes­day, ngolwe 15 ku March, emzini ka
Mr. W. Scrooby, kuyakutengiswa ngu Mr. Lee, Imazi zegusha ezi 1,800,
Inkabi zegusha ezi 300, Inkabi zenkomo ezi 18, Amahashekazi anga 20,
Inxowa zenqolowa 400, Impahla zendlu kwa nezokulima.

E-Queenstown nge Tuesday ngolwe 11 ku April, kuya kutengiswa ngu Mr. E.
B. Brown, Harm ezi 12, imihlaba ka Mr. A. N. Ella.

E-Qonce, nge Wednesday, ngolwe 15 ku March, kuyakutengiswa ngu Mr.
Ellis, Impahla ka Mr. J. S. Lombard, kuko mihlaba itile, engase Qonce,
nenkabi zenkomo ezi 18, imazi enetole, ibokuva enedyokwe zayo, kwa
nezezindlu nezokulima impahla.

E-Uitenhage nge Wednesday ngolwe 15 ku March, kuyakutengiswa nge 150
lenkabi zenkomo.

E-Wolvekop ngase Middelburg, nge Wednesday, ngolwe 15 ku March,
kuya­kutengiswa ngu Mr.Haupt, 2,000 emazi zegusha, 2,000 enkabi zegusha,
600 eseyi- bokwe, 36 enkabi zenkomo, 56 emazi zenkomo, 40 amahashe
namahashekazi.

E-Rini, nge Wednesday, ngolwe 8 ku March, impahla ka Mr. C. Penny,
obubi- leyo, obengowase Salem, iyakutengiswa ngu Mr. Thomas Brooks,—kuko
izindlu kona E-Eini apo, nempahla yendlu,enjengo zitafile, nezitulo,
kuko nemazi 4 zenkomo, namatokazi 2, nenkabi 4, nehashe elinye.

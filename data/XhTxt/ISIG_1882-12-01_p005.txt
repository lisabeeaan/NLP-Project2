INGCULO YABAMNYAMA.

Ngosuku lwa 24 lo November, u Mr. Baker umfundisi wentsapo, wase Heald
Town, waba nobubele bokuba acwayitise ibandla lengxogxo, i Lovedale
Literary Society, ngokuti eze nebandla lake labaculi, acule apa. Eyona
nkahlukazi awabezele yona. yabi kukwenza inteto pezu kwe mvumo yaba
ntsundu {Lecture on Kafir Psalmody.) Abaculi aba bezelauku bonisa
intlobontlobo zokuvuma ezikoyo. Oku kwenziwa ngendlela efaneleke
ngangokuba ingabi naku cazwa ngenteto. Abavumi, kwakona, babonisa eyona
ndlela kufanele ukuculwa ngayo. Ubuhlwempu benteto yomntu bukwasixaka
ukuba sixele ukulunga eyenziwa ngako nale nxenye yomsebenzi wabo.
Intloko yesibini neyokugqibela ibe kukuvuma amaculo atile esi Ngesi,
abumandi babeta indlu enkulu kunene—i Lecture Hall, abayaziyo abaka base
Lovedale, ekungena kuyo intlanganiso zengxoxo zododana—lendlu ke, izele
mome, ngamahlelo omabini oluntu amanene namanenekazi—abamhlope
nabamnyama, banxila yimihlali. Njengoko sesitshilo, amazwi siwaswele
okucaza, nangokuka ncinane, incasa yoko sakuvayo ngendlebe zetu
ngobusuku bo November, bo 24. Wonke omhlope wayivuma italente yetu
bamnyama yoku cula, xa ite yarolwa kweso sikaka isongelwe kuso, sobu
tyakala. Wonke ondlebe zagquteka kwakudala makabe selaka weva ngendumela
yokuvuma kwe ntsapo yase Heald Town. Tina apa e Lovedale, sati sakuva
ukuba bayeza salinda ngoku kwenja ebawayo, ongafika amehlo esukela
igxebeka. Imibulelo esbushu, neyovuyo, ku Mr. Baker naba vumi,
yentlanganiso, nabemi be Lovedale yenziwe, egameni labo bonke, ngendlela
efanelekilyo neyamkelekileyo, ngenteto, ngu Andrew Smith, Esq., M.A.,
wasekelwa kwa ngokufanelekileyo ngu Mr. Samuel Mzimba, waxaswa ngu Mr.
J. Knox Bokwe no Mr. Tengo-Jabavu. U Mr. Baker uyamkele imibulelo, wati
yena uyinika aba Vumi, kuba ngebe ’yintonina ngapandle kwabo. Emva koku
vunywa ko “God Save the Queen,” inkita “iye ngonina,’’ izincoma
itamsanqa lokuba ide yeva eyakuvayo.

Amagama aba Vumi siyawa hlomela Umpati wabo— Rev. E. Magaba. Abavumi be
Air:—Misses E. Sakuba, N. Duma, L. Ngcayiya, E. Nxawe, S. A. Ngcwai, B.
Vundla, M. A. Kubevana, A. Ndwandwa. Abavumi be Alto:—Misses M. Sakuba,
H. Gama, M. Mfecane, F. Ndlazi; Masters, C. Kunene, Harry Sitela, Simon
Nqana. Abavumi be Tenor:—Messrs Bobert Solomon, Hebert E J. Booi, Thos.
Tunyiswa. Abavumi be Bass :—Messrs Saul Msane, Samson Malimba, John
Zini, J. Matshoba, Petros Sidzumo, Wm. Mtongana.

ABALAULI.—Ngo 22 Nov., u Hon. T. C. Scanlen, no Hon. J. W. Sauer bafike
e Bayi, basinga e Aberdeen Boad, apo bakwela ekalityini, eyabasa e
Beaufort West. Kambe u Mr. Sauer uvela e Lusutu, u Sir. Scanlen ubeye
kuhlangana naye e Aliwal North ngemicimbi yase Lusutu. Xa bebel’apo
bahlangana ne nkosana za Besutu, kwangemicimbi yelo. Apo bagqibe kona
akuko bani waziyo. Kodwa u Sir. Scanlen entetweni yake e Cradock uti
imfazwe yinto yokugqibela engenziwayo yi Rulumente ayintloko yayo.

IMIZAMO YOZINGISO—Kute nto bati ngu Charles Fuller iseluhambeni ivela
ngakulamacala ase Victoria West, isiya kwindawo apa abati yi,
Matjesfontein camp, ya qubisana ne nkunzi endala yenciniba. Yekake, ite
kuba indoda ingenatnaqinga okulwa nayo intaka le yaselidal’ityobo nge
mbokotwe; yeka wanga kaloku uyibiz’okunye. Uqalile wabaleka, yaba seliko
yayeka ngokukaba, yamkahlela, kute kusenjalo waqubisana na ntongana
wavitikisela; inge ingejaju yabuya, kwasekutandana ngomzamo, kwesana
kunye pantsi, kute ngalomigungqo, kanti uyite vite intamo kwapela kona.

“KADE NDIHAMBA, KADE NDISIVA.”

Abafundi betu sesabatyilela umxelo wetu ngotyelelo apa e Lovedale, lwaba
Fundisi naba Dala bebandla lama Bulu, ngomhla we 18 wenyanga ka October.
Ukuba besi ngabantu abatanda uku cula indumiso zabo, ngeba namhla senza
umtyangampo; sivakalisa isipiwo ekubonakala ukuba sinaso—soku profeta.
Abafundi bomhlati apa kutiwa ngowe Myawana bokumbula ukuba sati, “Ukuba
sinoku gweba ngesaku fundayo ebusweni bawo, singati bakoliswa koko
bakubonayo.” Lencwadi siyi hlomelayo, ibibalelwe umhlobo wetu apa, ngu
wabo, owabeko entlanganisweni awateta kuyo u Rev. Professor Marais, e
Stellenbosch. Alazifundele umfundi, singampazamisi ngezetu inteto. Yiva
ke :—

“Inteto yam iya kuba mfutshane kakulu. Ufikile u Professor Marais. Nge
Cawa yoku qala efikile ucelwe ngu Sir. Neethling ukuba apose amazwi kwi
nkonzo yamva ngehambo yake, ngokukodwa pakati kwaba mnyama. Uyihambisile
lonkonzo nge nteto yakowabo. Ute, ixesha lifutshane uya kuposa amazwana
ambalwa ngendawo engena kufezwa kuxelwa ngamlomo, into engade kambe
yanelise liliso, nendlebe. Ute, ndifuna ukuteta nge Lovedale yodwa,
nomsebenzi omkulu, omhle kunene ohanjiswa kulo mzi unto zonke zime
njengoko bezifanele ukuba njalo. Uxele ngoku menywa kwabo ngu Dr.
Stewart nobubele abenzele bona. Uncoma nendlela, nenca embala yase
Lovedale. Uncome izakiwo ukuba zihle ngoku kodwa isikula esitsha esakiwe
ngamatye amahle kunene. Uqale ngoku ncoma umsebenzi omhle awubone
usenziwa kwese ntombi, wacukusa namanani entombi ezifunda apo, nengubo
ezihlanjwa, zitelekishwe kona, nemali engeniswa kuku sebenza kwakona,
nokunxiba kakuhle kwe ntombi, nokuswela iratshi kwazo. Utsho wati
maziyekwe i zendelings (mission­aries) ukuculwa ngabantu, mkulu
umsebenzi wazo, mhle pakati kwaba mnyama. Ute, sipumile apo sabekiswa
kwi sakiwo esihle kunene sododana, apo ndibone into entle intlanga nge
ntlanga zifunda, omhlope nomnyama bebekwe ngento entle, begqatsile
emfundweni. Uncome imfundo ukupakama. Uncome i Library, ate ayiko enjalo
e Stellenbosch. Apo inteto yake ayipelelisele kona kusema shishinini
afundwa e Lovedale. Ute umsebenzi owenziwa apo ema shishinini
angawutelekisa nokuba nguwupi e South Africa. Uncome into ayibone
isenziwa ngu mfana omnyama eteta nocingo! Ute, enye into
e’mangalisileyo, ngu mfana omfutshane, oncipileyo, okule nge ntloko,
opape, olumke kunene, olitemba lika Dr. Stewart, umfana ogcina i Account
Books nento yonke eyelele kwezo nto; kanjako lomfana une talente
yokwenza ingoma ezimnandi. Igama lalo mfana ngu no. Knox Bokwe. Utete
nge Young Men’s Christian Association, nokupuma kwama dodana ukuya
kushumayela ngapandle nge Cawa. Uncome umsebenzi omhle owenziwa ngu Mr.
Mzimba umfu­ndisi ofunde e Lovedale, encediswa yinkosikazi yake efunde
Pesheya.

Inde inteto ka Professor Afarais, mandi sendisiti ugqibe ngeliti: Kade
ndihamba, kade ndisiva nge ndlebe, kodwa lehambo yam yanamhla idibene ne
Lovedale iyintloko. Hambani niye kubona into eyenziwa e Lovedale nize
nandule ukuteta.

Ndibone kwa isapuma ityalike ukuba lenteto yake igqute indlebe zento
eninzi yama Bulu kuba libotwe lawo i Stellen­bosch. Ilizwi elivela e
Stellenbosch aliwi pantsi kuma Bulu.

Lehambelo yaba Fundisi bama Bulu e Lovedale, iselisema pepeni ama Bulu.
Obalileyo uyincome yonke into yase Lovedale, wati naye “Yiya ubone, ize
ugwebe.”

Stellenbosch, November 9, 1882. D. G.

KWELAMA ZULU. —Kwelo kuvakala ukuba kungoku, uluvo lwe nkosana zelo,
ngoku lolu lungileyo, lubangelwa ke ngakumbi kukuva ngokubuyiswa kuka
Cetywayo e Kapa, yinto leyo eseyi sazeka, kubonakala nongu Mpatiswa ka
Rulumente okona, uyalelwe ukuba atete amazwi obuhlobo, ngendlela ndlela
zoku buviswa kuka Cetywayo kwezo nkosana zibonakala ukuba zivuyisiwe
yilo ndawo. Kuvakala nokokuba ite enye yenkosana zake, Usibebu akuva
ukuba uyeza wabalekela kwelama Tebele; wati yena u Oham obonakala ukuba
ungodanileyo kanye, wacela ukuba aposwe enqweleni asingiswe e Kapa, aye
enze uxolo no Cetywayo.

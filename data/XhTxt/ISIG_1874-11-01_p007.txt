ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1874.

INGAZI.—Kute E-Kapa ngokuhlwa kwecawa omatiloshe abatandatu
ebebeselunxwemeni bakwela ebotini ukuya emkombeni. Kute kuba ulwandle
lube lungenisa nalomadoda enxilile, yapenuka iboti. Ababini babe
nokudada baya kupumela elunxwemeni, kodwa abane akukavakali nto ngabo,
sekubonakala ukuba bafile.

U-LANGALIBALELE NEMVULA—Elinye lamapepa ase Natalliti, pambikokubaimvula
ine abantsundu bebesiti ibanjwe ngu Langalibalele uqumbele ama Ngesi.
Ngoku inileyo bati, ubuye wabetwa lusizi Iwaba fazi bake nosapo Iwake,
wayinisa noko abaqumbeleyo abamhlope.

IMFENGU EYENZAKELE NGUMPU. —Kute ngase Rini Imfengu etile ifuna inkomo
yafika kwindawo etile apo bekutiywe ngompu, umlungu abati ngu Mr
Cockcroft efuna ukudubula inyamakazi ezibe zimkataza. Ite kanti inyatele
encukumisweni, wayidubula etangeni yafa kukopa kakulu igazi.

UBUGQI—U-Mr. H. Brown wangase Qonce uti enye yegusha zake ibizele ixwane
elinmanqina asibozo, nemizimba emibini, kodwa intloko inye. Iinilenze
ngasemva ibe imine, imikono imibini, eminye isemhlana. Alipilanga xesha
elotakane. Omnye umfo abati ngu Mr A. F. Preez wakula macala angapakati
ubenenciniba enyawo zine.

ONYANA ABABINI baka Langalibalele ababe gwetyelwe ukuze babe nyanga
ntandatu etolo- ngweni, sebeligqibile ixesha labo. Omnye oyena
bekulindelwe ukuba uyakuba yinkosi endaweni kayise, use Edendale. Omnye
uhlala no Latshuke. Isizwe sika Putili sesibuye sabuyela emanxiweni aso,
kodwa inkosi yaso ise setolongweni.

OZIBULELE KAKUBI.—E-France ute umfo otile wazisika inxeba elikulu
emqaleni ngesitshetshe efuna ukuzibulala. Ubonwe kusasa engekaqauki
wafunelwa igqira lamnyanga kwada kwabonakala ukuba uyapila. Kute
kwakudlula intsuku ezisibozo emva koko watabata isikonkwane wasibetelela
ngesando entloko saya kuma ngobucopo. Zite zimbona izihlobo zake kwaba
sekuse mva.

UKUVIWA KWE TITSHAEE.—Njengokokwakwa- ziswe ngako ngale nyanga ifileyo
bekuko ukuviwa kwe titshere. Abebeye kuviwa kwindawo ngendawo 54.
Kwelinani abapumeleleyo babe 37, amankazana kubo 27, amadoda elishumi
kukupela. Apa E-Lovedale bekutunyelwe intombi ezimbini U-Lousa Pezisa no
Susan Macgillivray; bapumelele bobabini. Kwesase Rini isikolo
sabantsundu kupumelele batatu, omnye U-William Philips wakwelipambili
iqela.

U-PRESIDENT BURGEES, Umongameli wama Bulu ase Transvaal akavumi
ukuyihlaula imali ekube kusitiwa makayirole yi Governor yale koloni
ngenxa kakuba umfo otile apatwa kakubi kulomhlaba ubangwayo, ati ama
Ngesi ngowawo. U-Sir H. Barkly ubete lomntu wayebete omnye kakubi
makanikelwe ezandleni zake aze kumsa ematyaleni. Kodwa akavumi naye
U-President Burgers. Sisakangele ukuba kode kutini na.

IMVULA.—Siyavuya ukuva ukuba lemvula isand’ ukubako ifikelele indawo
ezininzi zekoloni. Asikuko nokuba kumnandi ukuti xa uhambayo uve amasele
ekuxelela olwawo uvuyo, inca nemit nentyantyambo zikubonisa olwazo,
izipani zenka bi emasimini zikuxelela ukuba ayifikanga ngapa mbi
kwaxesha layo, ukuhluta kwenkomo namaha she xa zivela endle kubonisa
ukuba akuko nto ingancedekanga.

ISELA LOMLUNGU.—Umfo abati ngu Johannes Beukes ubanjwe ngase Colesberg
equba inkomo 70 namahashe amabini, nompu nezinye izinto azibileyo.
Inkomo ezo ebeye waziba E-Gqili. Uti ubesuke E-Cradock ehambela
lomsebenzi kukupela, noko ebefuna ukuba ezabantsundu zodwa. Ute kanti
seletabata nezabamhlope baza ke bamjandela baza kumfumana selekule
koloni. Ubuye

wapindiswa wasiwa kwase Free State apo zizeza- kona ezonkomo. Inani
labezibile litandatyuzelwa kumakulu amabini.

INGOZI YOMPU—Kute ngase Graaff Reinet umxosa otile abati ngu Jan Ehart
waya kuzingela epete umbolombini. Ubuye edubule ngomlomo wamnye, wafika
kuko ekaya izihlobo zake ezingamankazana, wamana ukuzoyikisa. Ude
wacukumisa encukumisweni yalomlomo ungekadutyulwa esiba yena
ngodutyulweyo. Ite imbumbulu yangena ecaleni lentombazani abati ngu
Stempi, yaqauka kwangobo busuku. Pambi kokuba ife ite inosizi ukuba kube
kungeko bantu bamhlope ababonayo ukuze baziqondele ukuba ayibulawanga
ngabom.

OBULELE UMNTU NGASE COLESBERG.—Umxosa abati ngu Rixdollar usetolongweni
ngetyala loku- bulala mkwenkwana eminyaka isibozo. Kubonakala ukuba
lonkwenkwana ibe itunywe nguyise ukuba iye kunqanda amahashe. Ute uyise
akuyibona ingabuyi waya kuyikangela wayifumana ihleli pantsi inenxeba
esiswini, amatumbu iseliwabambe ngesandla. Ite lenkwenkwana yakubuzwa
yaxela ukuba iqubisene naye umbanjwa lowo wati mayize kumgugula intloko.
Ite yakuza wabuza ukuba ayiboni ndawo isikiweyona, kanjalo ingetande na
yona ukusikwa. Akutsho isuke yabaleka, wayisukela wayihlaba. Ipile usuku
Iwa- lunye kwasa isifa. Uti yena uyenzakalisile ebedlala.

UKUBULISANA KWABAZALWANA.—Pesheya E- Scotland kuko abazalwane ababini
abati ngu Robert no Edward. U-Robert ubeza kuya E-America, waza ke omnye
umzalwane nezihlobo zabo bamkapa. Pambi kokuba angene emko­mbeni batenge
intwana yotywala. Bute xa buza kubatalwa ababazalwane bapikisana ngenani
engelirolwa ngomnye, bade basuka balwa U-Edward wamluma kunene
esidleleni omnye. Bate bakwahlulwa U-Robert kanti upete intsinjana,
wakulula ngayo entloko watsho into enkulu yoduma, wamshiya elele pantsi
elungiswa ngabanye kuba beselilixesha lokunduluka. U-Edward akwazeki
ukuba wapila na, no Robert lowo ute akufika E-Liverpool apo ebeya
kusukela kona ukuya E- America, wabanjwa wafakwa etolongweni.

UMFO OTILE obala ekwelapesheya kwe Nciba uti, “ Kusand’ ukunduluka
amaqela amabini ama Baca eya kufuna umsebenzi ekoloni. Ama Baca
abonakala ukungati ayawadlula ama Mpondomisi ngokukutala nokutanda
umsebenzi.—Abatembu nama Mpondo basamana ukutimbelana inkomo. Aba Tembu
bebetimbe amahashe 20, aza ama Mpondo atimba iqela lenkomo. Kupela ke
kupikwe ngalonto. Enye into ebisand’ ukwenzeka kukulwa kweqela lama Hala
nelama Mpondomjsi. Isisusa bekute kupambene abatile, wati Umpondomisi ku
Mhala nina ningamagwala njengenkosi yenu, kwase kusiliwa ke—Kuvakala
kanjalo ukuba kuko amadoda amabini amhlope enza iruluwa Emampondweni.
Ute omnye umfazi ontsundu eyisila elityeni wafaka ilahle enqaweni
watshaya, noko ebeyaliwe ukuze angakwenzi oko. Lisuke ilahle lawela
eruluweni yadubula, watsha kunene lonke elinye icala lake.”

E-FIJI.—I-Fiji zezinye zeziqitana ezikolulwa- ndlekazi kutiwa yi
Pacific. Inkosi yakona ibisand’ ukuvukelwa sisizwe esitile esibe
sipantsi kwayo, esihlala ezintabeni njengabatwa. Sasite sabona iqela
lamakristu ehamba sawabulala, yaza inkosi yakukuva oko yakupa umkosi
wokuba uye kusohlwaya. Wapuma ekuqaleni kuka May, wasoyisa esosizwe
wasicitacita, waselufuna ukusitshabalalisa ukuze singabuye sikataze,
kuba sibe singaqali ukwenza ucuku. Nenkosana yaso ite yabulawa, umzana
wayo watshatyalaliswa. Kwafunyanwa kuko namadoda abevela kwisizwe
esingalwiyo, aza agwetyelwa ukuba abulawe, Ngoku selubuyile lomkosi

wakomkulu. Enye into esand’ ukubako kona yintlanganiso yomnyaka
yokuvuyela ukufika kwabafundisi kwezo ziqiti zabo. Kuye kuhla- ngane
nabangakolwanga bapose kunene imali yokuncedisa ukuhamba kwelizwi, ibe
yimini yemigcobo leyo kubo.

U-RILI NO NGANGELIZUE—U-Ngangelizwe ubetumele U-Fonise namanye amadoda
ukuba aputume umfazi wake (intombi ka Rili) owaye mkiswe zintonga. Kute
kwakutiwa ngu Rili ku Tonise, “ Utunywe ngubanina ? ” Wati yena, “ nga
Mahala.” Kutiwe “ bapina abanye abafazi baka Ngangelizwe? ” Wati
“Bagxotiwe.” Ade ati Amagcaleka, “ Tina into esiyaziyo, yeyokuba U-Rili
watumela umntwana wake ku Mahala. Kwamana ukuvakala ukuba upetwe kakubi,
bati abanye aba no Feltman, kuya xokwa. Kwada kwaqondakala ukuba
bekunyanisiwe, sabona efika apa amatambo entombi ka Rili. Sayisa Emin-
ngwini lonto kwatiwa ningawenza njengokubona kwenu. Sinako na ke ukuti
lamatambo siwatumele kumntu owenza oku, obagxotileyo nabanye abafazi
bake nenduna zake, ozipete kakubi nezonkedama zisele ngase mva niti nina
ninosizi ngazo? Goduka ke wena Tonise uti akaziwa apa lomntu ufunwayo.
Samtumela kwa Hala tina asikazange sixelelwe ukuba umnkile kona.” Ute ke
U-Tonise wabulela elozwi wagoduka.

INTENGO EZIZA KUBAKO.

Ngomhla 11 ka November E-Murraysburg kuya kutengiswa ngomhlaba omkulu
kunene.

Ngo 4 ka November E-Aberdeen kulamacala angase Graaff-Reinet kuya
kutengiswa inkomo 100, izipani zonondlebende 8, igusha 1000, amahashe 8,
nezinye izinto.

Ngo 25 ka November E-Stomfontein ngase Burghersdorp kuya kutengiswa
igusha 1,900, ibokwe 100, inkabi 15, imazi 80, amahashekazi 30, isali
10, ibokuva nenqwelana zamahashe ezintatu.

ABAZELWEYO.

E-Hlobo ngomhla 2 ka October 1874, U-Mrs. James Mazamisa uzele intombi.

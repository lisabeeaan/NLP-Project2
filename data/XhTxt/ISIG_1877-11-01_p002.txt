ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1877

KUMATYALA ASE DIKENI.

KUMATYALA omhla 29 ka September kube kuko nelika Jan Nongcula no Jan
Tshisa no Pita Nkomo no Ngcobo ama Mfengu akwa Gaga abebete u-Sulani
Jakob Umxosa wakwa Nangu e-Ncera. Ebungqineni kubonakele ukuba u-Sulani
ebevela kutabata udade wabo kwa Mankazana. Ute xa adlula kwa Gaga
wablangana nala madoda omane evela ngase Dikeni equba amahashe amabini.
Ate kuye nqanda lomabashe, wenjenjalo okwenene. Ate lamadoda xa adlulayo
kuye enye yawo u-Pita Nkomo yaselikulula ngenqindi, wazisindela isiti
mayiti ngombo esifubeni. Unge angati nyi waselefika oka Nongcula embeta
ngesabokwe sokukwela, wehla katatu saqauka eso sibe- pana. Emva keko
ubete ngentonga yesibepu yada nayo yapuka. Kude kweza kwahlula u-Ngcobo.
U-Jan Tshisa ubehleli ekangele epete umpu. Ute akubuza u-Sulani ukuba
kuteni na lento enjiwa nje bati bonke (ngapandle kuka Ngcobo) “kungokuba
kuko imfazwe pakati kwama Mfengu nama Xosa.” U-Jan Nongcula ute “Siku-
betela ukuba ungu Mxosa, omnye simbete wada wawa, nawe siya kukwe
njenjalo.”

Ababanjwa abo akubangako nto bayitetayo. Kubonakele ukuba bebesela
e-Dikeni ke lento bayenze benxila. Imantyi ukunika kwayo isigwebo ite,
“Elityala likulu. Abonileyo kufuneka ukuba bohlwaywe kakulu,
kungengakuba kodwa bafanele ukohlwaywa, kwenzelwe ukuze lento ibe
sisiyalo nakwa- banye abenza izipitipiti. Makuqondakale mhlope ukuba
u-Rulumeni akawungeni lo- mahlulo we Mfengu no Mxosa. Ukuba abantu
batobele umteto kunye kuye nokuba bamhlope nokuba bantsundu. Kuko
inkatazo ngoku kwa Gcaleka, inkatazo abazenzele bona ngokwabo abalapo
ngokungatobeli mteto. Kodwa lonkatazo ayiko apa, ikude nati, asinanto
yokwenza nayo. Abantsundu abanganeno kwe Nciba sinobuhlobo nabo. Tina
apa sisenoluya xolo soloko luko Iweminyaka 25 edluleyo. Ukuba ke kuko
olupazamisayo siya kuba sikulu isoblwayo sake. Niyazi ke nani ukuba
andimntu ufu- mana asongele into angayi kuyenza. Maze ama Mfengu nama
Xosa alapa namhlanje aye kuxelela izihlobo zawo ezise makaya ukuba
ongenise isipitipiti pakati kwezi zizwe zibini uya kohlwaywa nzima.
Ndiya nika ke igunya kubo bonke abantu abanemizi nakumadindala,
nakwabanye abantu ukuze bambambe kwaoko umntu opitizelisa abanye nokuba
uyi Mfengu nokuba ungu Mxosa. Kulusizi kum ukuba into enje yenziwe ngama
Mfengu alunge nase Dikeni. Ukunxila akuteti nto, kwenza ukuba
bugqitisele ububi betyala. Wena Jan Nongcula

ndikunika inyanga ezintatu etolongweni usebenza nzima. Wena Pita Nkomo
nditi hlaula iponti ezintlanu (£5), ukuba akuziroli ube nenyanga
etolongweni usebenza nzima. Wena Jan Tshisa ngokuncedisa nokupembelela
kwako nditi rola iponti ezimbini (£2) mhlaumbi ube veki mbini
etolongweni usebenza nzima. Wena Ngcobo akunatyala.”

Ate ke amadoda adliwe imali ahlaula kwaoko, sati isiqingata sayo sanikwa
ku Sulani.

KUBATWALI BENDABA.

NGO Mgqibelo ngo 6 ka October bekutetwa e-Qonce ityala lika Ramsanje,
lokututa inda- ezibubuxoki ezingabangela ukuba abantsundu benze
izipitipiti. Lomfo ubesisicaka senkosazana yama polisa. Ubete ngenxa
yesifo wavunyelwa ukuba eze kwa gqira e-Qonce. Ute ke xa akona wabalisa
kwinkazana abati ngu Sara indaba zapesheya kwe Nciba, wati abelungu
bagxotiwe, bate bakugxotwa babamba abafazi nabantwana babatyanda baza ke
babaposa emlilweni. Ute u-Sara lowo noko akakolwa kuba engazange eve
kusitiwa umntu omhlope wenze into enjalo. Nokoke ube nokoyika wada
wakuxelela indoda yake. Ite yona ibuxoki yonke lonto. Ide yaza kuvakala
naku Dr. Fitzgerald wasele mbamba u-Ramsanje emzizisa pambi kwe Mantyi.
Ite ke yona makaze abe nenyanga ezilishumi elinesibini esebenza nzima.

Singatanda ukwazi ukuba i-Jaji iyakutini na sesisigwebo sika Mr. Innes.
Kuyi mfanelo kanye kona ukuba abatwali bendaba ezibubuxoki bafumane
isohlwayo, ngokuko- dwa kumaxesha anje ngala, engati intwana encinane
ivelise okukulu. Kekaloku singa- tanda ukuva ukuba kutiwa nina ngomntu
otwala indaba ezinjalo azise kwabamblope kutiwa nina ngaba babala
incwadi emapepeni bati naba abantsundu befun’ ukulwa no Mlungu. Nalonto
sikolwa ukuba ibubuxoki obungavelisa izipitipiti, ebekufanel’ ukuba
kuqondakale ukuba akulilo yini na ityala nako. Isohlwayo sika Ramsanje
noko asifaneleyo sikolwa ukuba sidlule ebukulwini betyala nokoke sitemba
ukuba siya kuba sisotuso kubantu abahamba bexela ubuxoki bebazi ukuba
bubuxoki.

IMBOLA E-LUSUTU.

EKUBENI kwazisiwe kwi Sandla sika Rulu- meni ukuba apa (e-Lusutu) kuko
abarwebi abatengisa ngembola kwaba ntsundu, nase kubeni imbola leyo iya
kuba sisitintelo eku- hlumeni kwabantu; kuba kuya kuti xa abantu
bakulayo ekufuneni imbola bancipe

ekufuneni ingubo zase Mlungwini, ngokoke bancipise intengo, nina ke
ngoko ngalo igunya endilinikelweyo ndiya kwalela ukutengiswa kwembola
apa e-Lusutu.

Ndiya zisa kanjalo ukuba kuko umrwebi oya kuwapula lomteto uyakuzenza
isisulu sokungabuyi ayi nikwe imvumelo yokurweba njengoko kumiselwe
kwisiqendu sesine somteto womhla 29 ka March 1877.

CHARLES GRIFFITH.

“ MAYENZEKE INTANDO YAKO.”

NGEZI nyanga zalo nyaka sikuwo kuseloko kute qo ngokuvela kumacalana
onke imibiko yemipefumlo egqitele kwa napakade. Nakwesi Sigidimi sibika
omnye. Ngumnqweno, nomtandazo wetu ke ngoko, ukunga lamazwi ayole
kangaka eliculo, kwakunye nale ngoma siyivelisayo namhla, zingancedisa
ekututuzeleni intliziyo ezitutumbayo pantsi kwesibeto se Nkosi “eti lowo
imtandayo imqeqeshe, ibatyakatye bonke onyana ebamkelayo;” no Bawo onga
singafund’ ukuti “Mayenzeke intando yako.”

U-SHADRACH MNGUNANA.

Abalesi beta sikolwa ukuba baya kotuka babe lusizi njengokuba kunjalo
kuti ekuwubaleni lombiko bakuva ukuba u-Shadrach Mngunana, enye yamadoda
asishiye nyakenye aya e-Livingstonia akaseko,ubube ngo 27 ka June.
Kwincwadi ka Br Stewart esiyi nika kwakwesi Sigidimi sanamhla baya
kubon’ ukuba uqauke umtambo, waza wabuba kwakamsinya emva koko.

Amadoda anokufaniswa naye pakati kwabantsundu akamaninzi. Yona noko
igama lake libe lugekaziwa kakulu siti bantsundu ngukububa kwake
kufanel’ ukuba kulilwe luhlanga lonke, angalilelwa zihlobo zake zodwa,
kuba nalapo amkele kona ubengenje ngaba bafa beye kumba idiamond
nololiwe, ubeye kusebenza umsebenzi ongamngeniseli nto, obumxolise ngesi
sizatu sokuba ungowe Nkosi nokuba ungonceda abantsundu.

Ukumazi kwetu kungexesha lokuza kufunda kwake e-Lovedale. Ngemini
zokubako apa ubengumfana okululama kungangokude abanye bati umatile.
Ubebonakala njengokungati lento iliratshi uyazi ngokuyiva nokuyi bona
kwabanye kube kupela, akazanga wayiva kweyake intliziyo. Ekukutaleni
emsebenzini awupatisiweyo ubengadlulwa bani kuyo yonke lenkita yase
Lovedale. Nase kufundeni noko singenakutsho ukuti ibikalipile intloko
yake, inyameko yake ibinkulu kanye, ekuvela ngayo yonke intliziyo yake
ukungasazi isifundo sake nokuba akangxoliswanga yititshere. Kwati ke
ukunyameka kwake kwamenza. ukuba aman’ukuhambela kwi klasi ezipambili
noko bebungakalipe kakulu ubucopo bake.

ZIPINA INKABI?

Umrwebi otile, womlungu, obehamba nenqwelo esuka E-Colesberg esiya kwele
Diamondi, ute wamana ukuhamba ebusuku, njengoko badla ngokwenjenjalo
nabanye abaqubi benqwelo belilizwe. Yena ubenanqwelo mbini. Kekaloku
ubeselenentsuku engalali, ngokoyikela ukuti kuko nto ingonakalayo. Uteke
ngabusuku butile, esahleli kwi nqwelo epambili, weva ukuba ubutongo
bumpete, wati ke kuba esazi ukuba izicaka zake azinakuhamba ngapandle
kwake, wati make nditi nqwa kancinane, enqweleni apa. Akazi ukuba ulale
ixesha elingakananina, ute kodwa akuqabuka wa- bona inqwelo imi pakati
endleleni; yammangalisa ngokukulu ke lonto. Kute kuba bekumnyama nokuba
mnyama, wakangela ukuba zimi njanina inkabi, wafumana isipani singeko
sonke. Ute kuba engamboni nomkokeli wamemeza esiti “ Zipina inkabi?”
Upapamile umkokeli, kanti ubekwalele enqweleni apa, wavuka engazi naye
ukuba ziye zatshona pina. Ihlileke inkosi leyo enqweleni, bati
bakukangela bafumana ingeko idisselboom, zimke nayo inkabi, zifike
zayapula kwi bolt eseku- gqibeleni. Baye kukangela ukuba kunjanina kwese
mva inqwelo bafika inkabi zimi edyokweni, umbe- xeshi nomkokeli belele
ecaleni kwazo; kanti bate bakubona imile epambili inqwelo nabo bati— “
wo, ha—nou ” batike bakubona imi umzuzu nabo bati make siti nqwa.
Ihambileke lonkosi nomkokeli wayo ukuya kufuna inkabi. Balande-
ngendlela, bada bati xa sebediniwe bazifumana zisabambe indlela, zirola
idisselboom, zingaqondi zona ukuba konakele ngasemva.

IMFAZWE KWELA MATEBELE.—Njengoko aba- nindzi bazi ngako Amatebele
sisizwe esaqepuka ku Mazulu, sipetwe ngu Moselekatse. Wati ke U-
Moselekatse lowo wayinyula iselusana inkosi eya- kuba sesikundleni sake
akufa; kodwa yati shwa- ka nonina isencinane njalo. Wati akububa U-
Moselekatse nge 1868, walandelwa ngunyana om- kulu U-Lobengule, esiti
kodwa usabambele eyona nkosi. Emzuzwini kwavakala ukuba lomtwana wa-
yenyulelwe ubukosi ugama lalingu Kuruman, use Natal. Kwakutshwa amadoda
atile ukuba aye kumkangela, ati akufika agqiba ngeliti asinguye, ngumntu
nje ozeza yena. Emva koku Amatebele amenza inkosi yawo U-Lobengule,
ukuba kaloku abe yiyo ngokutenqi.

Kodwa ekupeleni kwalonyaka odluleyo U-Ku- ruman wati usaza kuputuma
ubukosi bake. Wati U-Lobengule akuva ukuba uyeza U-Kuruman,
wawahlanganisa Amatebele wati, afuna ukulandela U-Kuruman uyakuwayeka
enjejalo. Ati kodwa wona ebeselenyule U-Lobengule ukuba abe yinkosi
yawo; akolisekile nguye, ke ngoko awanakuya kumntu angamaziyo nokumazi.
Nokoke abako ambalwa amlandelayo U-Kuruman.

emamkele kakuhle singalindela ukuba ngoku kuza kulwa Amatebele nama

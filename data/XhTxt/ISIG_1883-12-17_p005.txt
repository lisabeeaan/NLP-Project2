ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 17, 1883.

AMAGQUNUKWEBE NO RULUMENTE.

Siboniswe ngumhlobo wetu incwadi yakwa Rulumente enengxelo yabacedululi
bepike elipakati ko Rulumente nomzi wakwa Kama. Okwanamhla sizanelisa
ngokuqiwula indawo abate abacedululi emva kokuva elama Gqunukwebe nele
Rulumente, malupeliswe ngazo olu dushe. Ama Gqunukwebe akaneliswa noko
sisigqibo sabapicoti ate u Rulumente wakolwa siso yena. Abaqondisi-mteto
abamele umzi wakwa Cungwa ke bacebise ukuba ubenele ezijajini. Eyona nto
ifunwayo kukuba indawo nganye ikangelwe yodwa kungenziwa into
enjengaleyo yenziwe liqumru—lo Messrs S. Melville, B. H. Holland, no
Alex. Stewart—eligqibe ngoluhlobo.—

1.  Ukuba kwati nge 1854 u Sir. George Cathcart watembisa u Kama nabantu
    bake umhlaba otile, ozincam zawoke angazixelanga oko, elodingake
    laqiniswa ngokungapezulu nge 1858 ngu Sir George Grey.

2.  Ngapandle kwalomhlabake kwakuko umhlaba we Balakisi ngase Xesi
    (Middle Drift) owawumalunga nemayile ezimbini uwutat’ububanzi,
    kwanomnye ke owawumalunga nezine, wona mhlaumbi nezintandatu xa
    uwutat’ububanzi, ke wona lo ungase Qanda, i Balakisi leyo yayimiwe
    yimikosi eloxa, ezi Balakisi ke zona zazingapandle kanye kulomhlaba
    wawutenjiswe u Kama.

3.  Uteke u Kama akuba ezibanga ezindawo esiti zisemhlabeni wake,
    waboniswa ukuba zona okwazo zazinganikelwanga kuye ngu Sir George
    Grey nge 1858.

4.  Kwatike nge 7 February, 1861, u Sir George Grey, ngesicelo sika
    Kama, wenza isiqiniso esibonisa ukuba okwenene umhlaba lowo ngowake,
    nosapo Iwake kwanabantu bake, eteke ngoku yakuba lenkosi isivelisile
    okwenene sanqina ubunyaniso bayo lento.

5.  Nakubake bezite zaveliswa ezizingqiniso azibonakalalisanga ukuba
    abantu baka Kama banento yokwenza nalomhlaba unezi Balakisi.
    Ngokungapezulu wada wati u Sir George Grey xa amnikelayo ezizi-
    ngqiniso, wamalatisa kumabala awayeqatywe ngokukodwa, ingalake anezi
    Balakisi abonisake ukuba umhlaba lowo wona zimi kuwo ngoka
    Rulumente.

6.  Nezwi kananjalo elavela kwa Rulumente lokuba zonke indawo ezizeze
    Bakana, nendlela zimiwe lonto nayo ixela ukuba lamabala ayegcinelwe
    nto yimbi.

7.  Kananjalo zonke izenzo ze Ruluneli yalapa neya pesheya, emva koku
    qiniswa esekukankanyiwe, zibonisa nazo ukuba lomhlaba we Balakisi
    wona wawungabandakanyiwe kumhlaba owawupiwe u Kama nabantu bake,
    kengoko u Rulumente angenza akutandayo ngawo.

(a) Ukucandwa kwamabala ke wona amele le Balakisi yase Qanda,
    kwakungabambisene nanto nomhlaba ka Kama, ekwatike ngoko kwamiswa
    kubo aboke babekwayo, ngentando ka Rulumente.

(b) Kwanokutengiswa kwendawo ezati zatengiswa kuwo, nengqeshi- so,
    ngentando ka Rulumente, ekungeko ntoke yatetwayo ecasene noko ngumfi
    uKama ezondawo nazo zibubungqina.

(c) Kwandawonye nokutshiswa kwezindlu zabantu baka Kama ezazikona
    ngomyalelo we Mantyi, ekwazini kuka Colonel Maclean, emva kokuba u
    Mr. Miller wayete wazimisa ngokuzimisa indawo zalomhlaba ezi Bondeni
    wazityela ukuba umhlaba we Balakisi ngoka Rulumente wona.

(d) Ukwalatwa kwe Bakana ese Dowie ngu Mr. Chalmers, exela ukuba
    yibakana eyahlula umhlaba we Balakisi, eyibonisake ngomyalelo
    owawunikwe yi Mantyi Enkulu, ndawonye nesigqibo esenziwayo sokuba u
    Simpson unegunya lokubiza inxwaleko zonke emhlabeni wake kubantu
    baka Kama, ngokusuka benze ngokutanda emhlabeni wake.

(e) Intlaulo eyanikwa u Mr. J. C. Perks, kusenzelwa ukuba u Rulumente
    wayecandise indlela entsimini yake eyayiqeshiswe kuye, kwelinye
    lalamabala e Balakisi, nako kuyangqina.

(f) Ukunikwa kwesi qiniso (title) ku Mr. R. Cumming, wase Xesi (Middle
    Drift) kwakuba kufumaneke okokuba indlu yake imi kulamabala
    akankanyiweyo ; noko kuyangqinelana nezizinto sizikankanyileyo.

8.  Kananjalo landlela indala yayalatwa ngu Whitefield no James,
    yatyilwa ngokungapezulu ngu Simpson no Gosa, yayi yindlela endala
    eya e Fort Wiltshire eyayiko nge 1861 malungana nexesha lokupiwa
    kuka Kama umhlaba, eyayiyindlela ke kanye eyayisahlula umhlaba we
    Balakisi ngase mpumalanga, ekwayilandlelake yayalatwe ngabantu baka
    Kama, besalatisa u A. E. Murray ngo February 1882.

9.  No Kama ngokwake, xa wayekalaza ku James ngokuti u Simpson umi
    emhlabeni wake, ngapaya kwe Balakisi, watsho naye ukuba akananto
    yokwenza nomhlaba we Balakisi.

10. Kananjalo no Sir George Grey ngokwake wabesazi ekuwunikele- ni
    umhlaba lo ku Kama, ukuba o Simpson, Blackbeard, James no Whitefield
    bemi kulomhlaba we Balakisi akabasusa, wabeke ngoko ebonisa ukungabi
    nanto yokwenza kuka Kama nalomhlaba.

11. Ukuba oko kutetwa ngamangqina ka Kama kuyinyaniso ukuba xa kwati
    kwakucandwa elinye lalamabala ngase Debe, u Kama waya kumangala ku
    Colonel Maclean, kwati noko kwacandwa, lonto ngokwayo ibonisa ukuba
    u Kama wafunyanwa engenandawo yokumisa inyawo, ukuba umhlaba lowo
    ngowake.

12. Kananjalo ukuba igusha zase Lovedale azizange zisiwe e Debe ngapambi
    kokuba u Mr. Govan atenge elinye lalomabala, ngapandle kokwenza zwi
    ku Kama, intoke naleyo evelise ubugqina.

13. Kananjalo nenkosi u William Shaw Kama uqale ukuwubanga lomhlaba nge
    1881, kanti ngeloxa uyise ubeseleneminyaka elishumi efile.

14. Kananjalo nokunikelwa kwalemihlaba inezibalakisi sezikankanyi- we,
    inikelwa kwi Ruluneli yeli yeya pesheya, nge 1881, nalonto

ngakwayo ingqina ukuba u Kama uyaposisa ukuti lemihlaba yanikelwa ku
yise nge 1861.

15. Nakuba singabanga nako ukufumana incwadi eziyalela ngoku- candwa
    kweli ibala lise Debe emazantsi endlela yase Fort Hare, xa kona
    sikangele endaweni yokuba umhlaba lowo watengiswa ngapambi ko
    October we 1861, kusazekake ukuba eloxesha lonke kwakungekonto
    yayinokwenziwa ingaziwa ngu Colonel Maclean, ekubonakala ke ukuba xa
    nje wayiyeka yatengiswa, yayingabandakanyiwe nesipo sika Kama.

19. Sifumana kananjalo okokuba ifama ezazinikwe u Kama nonyana bake
    bobatatu azibangako engxelweni apa, nokoke bekungengakuba bayahlutwa
    amalungelo abo kuzo, kuba iziqiniselo zona zimi zibatetelela.

20. Sifumana ukuba kananjalo ngemposiso esiqiniselweni sika Kama
    ekungeko nto itetwayo ngalomabala e Balakisi, lontoke noko ayiteti
    nto xa nje ayede alatwa ngokubalulekileyo kwamhla mnene ngu Sir
    George Grey.

22. Ngokumalunga nokucasana kuka Kama ngendawo yokuba lomhla- ba
bebelima bedlisa kuwo abantu bakwa Gqadushe ucandwe, esiti wenziwa
kukuba kungacetyiswananga naye ngapambili. Siqonda tina ukuba abantu
beso sikolo banokwenza nalomhlaba njengabantu bakwa Kama, e Xesi, nase
Peuleni, mhlaumbi ngapezulu kuba abantu bakona bona bema kona ngapambi
kwabaka Kama, kengoko banelungelo ngapezu kwabo.

Kunjalonje esiqinisweni apo indawo ekankanya ukuba u Rulumente angenza
ngokutanda malungana nendawo ezinjengokunika imimiso emhlabeni lowo ka
Kama nabantu bake, londawo ibonisa ukuba lomhlaba siwukankanyileyo u
Rulumente ubenokuwucanda ngapandle kokutini kuka Kama. Kuba ukuba pambi
kokuba u Rulumente enze into emhlabeni lowo, ukuba bekunqweneleka ukuba
avise u Kama kuqala, bekungafuneka evise Amagqunukwebe onke kanye. Into
ke leyo engenziwanga ekucandweni kwe Peula ne Xesi, (Middle Drift)
ngokoke kubonakala ukuba u Rulumente akabonanga mfuneko yokwe- njenjalo.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

INGQAKAQA isahamba apa e Dayimani. Sekufe 72 kwi 99 yabantu ebahlileyo.
Ingene ngoku nakuma gwangqa.

INGQAKAQA ELUSUTU.—Esi sifo singene nakwela Besutu. Ulaulo Iwe Free
State selumise umteto wokuba kunga pakelwana zi-bembe nga-Besutu nama
Bulu.

INXENYE yemali apa waka wasenkatazweni ngayo u Mr. Pattle obesakuba
yimantyi Edutywa ifunyenwe entsimini kufupi nendlu. Kusabuziswa
ngokubako kwayo apo.

AMA-BULU esitili sase Bofolo agqiba kwintlanganiso aba nayo
ngolwesi-Tatu ukuba azamele ukungenisa o Messrs Ayliff no Laing
e-Palamente. Impi emnyama iteta ngo Sir H. Stockenstrom kupela yona.

KUNYULO oluquka iziqingata ezinjengezoma Qonce noma Komani kufumaneka
amanani abavotelweyo emi ngoluhlobo okwanamhla, Schermbrucker, 1368 ;
Bertram, 1104; Brown, 1102 ; Gately, 1038 ; Van Heerden, 983; Hart, 591.

UNYULO E DIKENI.—Ama-Bulu agqibe kwelokuba aza- mele ukungenisa eyawo
odwa indoda e Palamente. Kwi- ntlanganiso abenayo kufupi nje ezibukweni
le-Xesi ngakwe Lentonga agqibe kwelokunyula u Mr. J. H. Bezuidenhout.

UMZI UVUKILE!—Siva ukuba into zakowetu ezikwele Dayimani zizama indawo
yokutumela owazo umntu e Pa- lamente. Intlanganiso enkulu yazo ibingo 12
December. Inteto ebalulekileyo kuyo yenziwe ngu Mr. Joseph S. Moss.

AMALUNGU ENDLU ENKULU.—Elinye lamapepa ase Nyara i Guardian, liwamisa
ngoluhlobo amadoda ekungati apume- melele ngolu nyulo lutsha saka
salubika :—Meurant, unevoti ezi 1584 ; Hutton, 1537 ; Van den Heever,
1350. Abanye bangapantsi kulamanani.

INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

UMLIBO KA GCILISHE.

NKOSI MHLELI.—Kwakona imbewu ka Gcilishe. Kaundincede kuko intombi
yakowetu endingaziyo ukuba isekona nokuba yalupele yafa. Igama lentombi
ngu Sikoliwe oka Timini wase Madladleni, e Mangweni kwa Zwana, kwa
Mbambo, kwa Madlala, kwa Zulu, kwa Manyaza, kwa Cobemini, kwa Binita.
Yayendele ku Mndawo i Reledwane Yazala u Ndongeni, no Zweni, nomka
Hlakanyane ka Cebetsha. Uku- ba ngaba nguye ndingavuya undazise kakuhle
ukuba kuza kutiwanina ukubalelwa kwabo. Ngati omnye wavusela u Gcilishe
imbewu. U Gcilishe ngumnina? U Mndawo ngumnina? Intombi yakowetu
Sikoliwe Timini.

JOHN MAZAMISA.

SINOSIZI ukuba babe baninzi abasiposisileyo ukusiva nge Sigidimi, baba
ne Samangesi siza kuba kabini. Esona kutetwa ngaso ekufunwa sibe kabini
sesa Maxosa. Kekaloku naso asinakuqalwa ukwenjiwa njalo lide inani
labasifunayo libe kumakulu amatandatu nasixenxe. Amagama asafikileyo
asekwikulu elinye kupela. Enye indawo esingayikankanyayo kwabange
kawatumeli yeyokuba ote wapindisa ipetshana esasilite kata kwesika
August loza kufika nokuba ubeke isitampu sepeni endaweni yesepeni ezine,
ukuba ngapakati ubale igama lake lodwa, ukuba kanjalo akalifakanga
envelopeni.

SIR ARTHUR CUNNYNGHAME.

NGOMHLA 20 ka August apa e-Lovedale sibe si- tyelelelwe ngu Sir Aurthur
Cunnynghame. Umo- ngameli wemikosi ye Nkosazana, ne Lieut. Govern­or
yale koloni. Kuvakele kungaseko xesha ukuba uyeza nase Lovedale, wafika
ke ngoko engalungiselelwanga nakakuhle ukuze amkelwe ngokohlobo
olufanele umntu ose simeni sake. Ugaleleke mayela kwixesha lesine eha-
mba ngenyawo namadoda amabini. Ate kuba amakwenkwe ebesemisebenzini
ahlanganisana esapete ezompahla zokusebenza, abanye benamakuba, ilapa
imihlakulo, elapa amazembe, amalapi elapa okusula ifestile—elowo nalowo
epete into abesebenza ngayo—kuko kanjalo negubu. Ute xa aza kufika apo
ami kona amakwenkwe, igubu lamamkela ngokumvumela ele Nkosazana.
Akugqiba ukuwabona, uye kwizindlu zokusebenzela, kubashicileli, abakandi
nabacweli bemiti, waya kubona nezikolo nendlu zokulala kwanezokudlela,
neyocingo. Ukwahlukana kwake namakwenkwe ngexa lesitandatu, bate bonke
bake badumela Inkosazana katatu.

Kute mayela kwixa lesitoba ngokuhlwa weza entlanganisweni yamadodana
yokuxoxa izinto ngezinto. Ute kuyo wateta, ebulela ukwamkelwa kwake
ngobubele, nezinto azibonileyo. Ute, “ Ngale ndawo yase Lovedale kade
ndinomnqweno wokuke ndize kuyibona, kodwa ke bekuko izinto ezininzi
ezite zandixaka. Asikuko nokuba kundivuyisile ukubona intlobontlobo
zemisebenzi equtywayo apa ukubona ukuba abafundi baqeliswa ukusebenza,
ukulungelelana kwento zonke. Ndite ndakuya nakwezokupekela izindlu
ndafika yonke into ise ndaweni yayo. Ndibafumene abantsundu befunda
imisebenzi enje ngeyozinqwelo, into ke leyo eya kubavula amehlo babone
intlalo yokupila kwezizwe ezinokukanya. Ndite ndaku ya nase lucingweni
ndafika lukwapetwe ngontsundu, ote ukutumela kwake kwabonakala ukuba
usebenzana nento ayaziyo ayiqondayo. Kundivuyisile kanjalo ukubona apa
kuhlanganisene abavela kumacala ngamacala, bede banduluka nakwele
Diamond, nase Lusutu kuko nonyana benkosana, beze kufuna lamalungelo
angaka endifike ndawabona. Nditemba ukuba baya kuti bonke babonakalise
iziqamo zabakufumeneyo,

babonise ukulunga komsebenzi abawenzelwa nga Mangesi, ukuzisa ukukanya
nenkululeko kuzo zonke indawo. Sendulusizi ukuba ndingasi bonanga
esentombi isikolo, ngenxa yokuswela ixesha, kuba ekubambeni kokukanya
esizweni kuhlala kuluzizi xa amankazana ashiywa ngase mva. Kuluvuyo ke
ukwazi ukuba nawo apa alungiselelwe indawo. Kundikolisile kanjalo
ukubona ukuba pakati kwezinto ezifundiswayo akulityelwe uku- vuma,
nokuba ele Nkosazana iculo lingashiywa ngase mva.” Utsho ke wabuya
wabulela ukwam- kelwa kwake kakuhle nokuvuya ukuba efumene elituba
lokuke aze kubona nalomzi.

Ute akuhlala pantsi kwesuka u-Dr. Stewart wabulela ukuba ede u-Sir
Arthur watabata inxamleko yokuza kubona i-Lovedale waxela nosizi Iwake
ukuba ete waqubulisa ukufika waza ke ngoko akalungiselelwa, noko
mhlaumbi oko kukukona kulungileyo, kuba uwubone umzi unje ngoko uhlala
unjalo imihla yonke.

UKUBEKWA KWELITYE LE SEMINARI
YASE MGWALI.

UMHLA wa 21 ka July uya kuhlala ukunjulwa ngovuyo e-Mgwaliwase
Batenjini, kuba ngawo abakona bebone umsebenzi omkulu pakati kwentlanga
ezintsundu, wokubekwa kwelitye lesiseko se Seminari entsha.

Umsebenzi walomini uqalwe ngenkonzo ete ya- patwa ngu Rev. E. J. Barrett
washumayela kulamzwi ati “ U-Tixo ungu Yehova osikanyiseleyo, bopelelani
umnikelo womtendeleko ngentambo, kude kube sezimpondweni zesibingelo.”
Ityalike ibizele ngabamhlope nabantsundu. Kute kwakupunywa apo kwarozwa
ukuyiwa esizeni se Seminari, abantwana besikolo bepete iflag. Kufikiwe
kona u-Mr. Hargreaves uleseshe ipepa elidla ngokuleseshwa ngemini
ezinjalo, ate akuligqiba walifaka ebotileni kunye ne Sigidimi Sama- xosa
namapepa endaba amabini amangesi. Ekwe- nzile oko ushiyele ku Mrs.
Garner, ukuba abeke ilitye lesiseko. Emva koko kuleseshwe ipepa elicela
ukuti langa itamsanqa le Nkosi lingahlala linaso eso sakiwo siza kumiswa
kulondawo, licela nokuti langa lingakauleza ukufika ixesha eziya kuti
indawana zonke ezimelene nabantsundu zizaliswe zi Seminari ezinje
ngaleyo.

Kugqityiwe oko kuhlanganiswe imali, kwabutwa kulondawo yodwa £312.
Bacitakele abantu baya kupunga apo bebelungiselelwe kona, ngu Mr.
Hargreaves, kungenwa ngesheleni.

KISIKULA SENTOMBI SASE NCEMERA.

ULIXESHA kwavakalayo ukuba umsebenzi apa omhle kunene wabafundisi,
isikula sentombi esise Ncemera, uyonakaliswa kanye. Abahlobo bomsebenzi
wa­bafundisi sekumzuzu bekangele ngosizi olukulu amalinga apilileyo,
ekutiwa ngawe Bandla lase Ncemera, okubhubhisa isitiya esibukeka kunene
pakati kwetu bantsundu kweli lizwe. Asingebi siyayenza imfanelo yetu
njengeliso nendlebe yomzi wakowetu, ukuyibetisa ngomoya into enje ngale
kube kubhuqiswa, kusonakaliswa elona nqata lomsebenzi wabafundisi.
Yindawo yake wonke owunqwenelela impumelelo umsebenzi ka KRISTU
kulomhlaba ukuba enze konke anako ukuwucima lomlilo utshisa into entle
kunene. Ngalamncapaza esiwenzayo sizama ukufeza eyetu indawo ukunqanda
umonakalo omkulu.

abantu abalungileyo base Mangesini, ukudibanisa intombi zabantsundu

intombi, kwinto zentlalo ehlambululekileyo bahlaba bekangele; bafanele
kubulelwa luhlanga oluntsundu.

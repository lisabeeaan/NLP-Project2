ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 17, 1883

AMAXAMBA E-PALAMENTE.

UKUFUDUKA KUKA MR. MZAMO E BAYI.

Sekuyiminyaka eyaneleyo namhla aqalayo amagwangqa ase Bayi ukuti
abamnyama mabafuduke kumzi apa bati bema kuwo kwakudala. Isizatu
besesokuba abemi be Bayi abamhlope besanda, kubonakala ukuba makwakiwe
kude kupatelele Emaxambeni apo. Abemi base-Maxambeni, kunye nabablobo
babo batimbise ixesha elide, bebonisa ngezizatu ezivisisekayo ukuba
asimfanelo yama-Ngesi ukuba abasuse ekubeni besebenze izinto ezintle
kangaka ngokumayela nokuba nezindlu ezintle. Kute xa lamapike
ahambisekayo wabe usanda wona umzi wamagwangqa. Namhla ke ungawafumana
Amaxamba esesazulwini sedolopu. Ama-Ngesi alike namhla kwisigqibo sokuba
awuqinise ngomteto wase Palamente umnqweno wawo wokuba umzi wase
maxambeni ufuduselwe kwenye indawo. Lomcimbi ke ungeniswe ngu Mr.
Pearson, umnyulwa wase Bayi. Lengxoxo ilandelayo ibeko ngo 29 August
pezu kwalomteto.

U-MR. MACKAY [Umnyulwa wase Tinara] ute makuvunyelwane ngelokuba lomteto
ungamkelwa. Wati lomhlaba kuxoxwa ngawo wanikelwa kubantu abawumiyo
ngomnyaka we 1855 ngu Sir George Grey, namhla yiminyaka emashumi mabini
ananye bekuwo. Ngokubekiselele kwindawo leyo bafuduselwa kuyo ute ikude
nomzi kubantu abasebenzayo. Kutiwa lomzi mdala uxinene, ungcolile
kanjako. Ngokwemiteto yequmru elilaula imicimbi yesixeko sase Bayi
ingxinano leyo ibinokunxwenywa ; inanamhla isesemandleni alo ukuba
liyicwete yonke into engcolileyo. Xakona sekutetwa ngokungcola kwendawo
leyo anganyula ukuhlala kuyo kunokuba ahlale nokuba kupina pakati kwe
Roeland Street ne Balekisi, e-Kapa. Emaxambeni izindlu zicokisi- we
zifanelekile. Akuko zinyaniso zokumisa inteto yokuba abantu base
Maxambeni basisoyikiso kubamelwane babo. Kuko kule lokeshoni inani
elikwi 1,500 labantu abangamalungu amabandla ka Tixo. Ulo- yiko
lungabangwa zizihiba ezingena mfanelo yokuba zibe kulo ndawo— nazo xa
zivela ezinkati. Abantu sebede balelwa nokuba bake mhlaimbi baxole
izindlu zabo ngumteto wama komishonari. Abantu abantsundu banamalungelo
atile abamelwe kukuba bawafumane. Mhla bawunikwa ngu Sir. G. Grey abantu
abamnyama lomhlaba wawumgama nedolopu, zizindlu zabamhlope ezinyuke zaya
kubo. Kodwa namhla kutiwa ngenxenokuba omhlope ake wada wafika komnyama
maseleshenxa kengoko. Abamnyama benenkolo yokuba abasoza basuswe bade
baka izindlu zetyalike ezintle ; yinto elungileyo na ukuba emva kwayo
yonke lento basuswe. Lento yokugxotwa kwabo iyaku- zala uburara
nokungakoliseki; bayakuti ngenxa yokuba besuke banebala elimnyama
benziwa izixatula zomntu omhlope. Ngaleminyaka mibini mitatu
ibiselisisiqelo ukuba ati ofuna ukungena kwibandla labapete umzi wase
Bayi iyamkolisa indawo yokokuba Amaxamba afuduswe ibe kukungena kwake.
Ukuba kutiwa mabafuduke abantu abamnyama Emaxambeni kuba bexinene, into
enjalo inganxwenywa ngokuti abafiki bafunelwe enye indawo. Iko imiteto
yomzi wase Bayi engasetyenziswayo xa abantu baxineneyo, abanemvume
yokuyihambisa abamnyama ngokwabo. Into enje ibingeze yenziwa
kwabamhlope. Akunqwenelekile ukuba abantu bagxotwe imayile ezitile
bafuduselwe kufupi nenye i Lokeshoni.

U-MR. PEARSON [Umnyulwa wase Bayi] ute licam labamnyama ukuba basiwe
kwindawo enempilo njengaleyo bafuduselwa kuyo. Ukuba yinyaniso ukuba
yinto enengozi ukubafudusa ababantu, anga ngowokugqibela kwabati
mabafuduswe njengokuba ilungu elibekekileyo (u Mr. Mackay) lisazi.
Uyatemba ukuba i Palamente ayisakulamkela icebo lika Mr. Mackay.

U-DR. ATHERSTONE [Umnyulwa wase Rini] ute akavumelani nelungu
elibekekileyo (Mr. Mackay) kwindawo eliti kuyo akuko zipitipiti
Emaxambeni. Uzame ukubonisa ukuba lilungelo labantu base Maxambeni
ngokwabo ukuba bafuduswe.

U-MR. LEWIS [Umnyulwa welama Qwelane] ute akuko bani uke wati
izidubantini aziko Emaxambeni, kodwa into etetwa nguye nabavumelana no
Mr. Mackay yeyokuba ezizaqunge zidalwa ngabantu abangena makaya apo.

U-MR. IRVINE [Umnyulwa wase Qonce] walate kubungqina buka Rev. Smith
Spencer ngokumayela nokuzola kwabantu basema-Xambe- ni. Ucinga yena
ukuba abantu abo bayafuduswa mabahlaulwe nange tyalike nezikula zabo.
Ibifanele ukungalityalwa indawo yokuba aba bantu ngabo abalenze i Bayi
into eliyiyo namhlanje. Unga angazi ukuba bekungasakubako impitimpitina
e Kapa ukuba bekusitiwa ihkulelwane zakona maziye kwaka kulamikutuka
yamatafa angapandle. Ukuba ukutembeka (Oh, Oh, kwahlekwa) yinto esekoyo
apo e Palamente, uyababongoza ukuba kebenze unqumamo ngapambi
kokudonyula abantu abafanelekileyo.

Kugqitywe kwelokuba lomcimbi ucedululwe. Indawo change ingxoxwana
yeyokuba abantu babuyiselwe indleko yabo ngendawo yokuba beza kuzishiya
ityalike zabo. Lendawo kuqononondiswe ngayo kakulu ngo Messrs. W.
Ayliff, Mackay, Irvine, no Fuller, abate makuhlaulwe isiqingata semali
ibe ngo Messrs. Pearson, Brown, Sprigg. Isate ga apoke ingxelo epambi
kwetu.

U REV. D. MALGAS.—Umbaleli wetu. ose Komani uti— Elinene linduluke apa
ngomhla wokuqala ku September; lisinga ke e Bayi apo linyulelwe ukuya
kona. Usizikazi olukulu ke olo kwi Remente ebiyeyake, kunamhla batwele
izandla entloko. Naye uyishiye ngokunjalo. Owetu ke umtandazo ngowokuti:
Tanga i Nkosi ingamgcina, kude kube seziminini zake zokupela.

Ngobusuku bo 30 August 1883 kwakuko intlanganiso enkulu yokubulisa u
Rev. D. Mzamo, ote, ngenxa yoku ngapili kakuhle kwenkosikazi yake,
wanyanzeleka ukuba ake ashenxe e Bayi, asinge ngase Natal. Kule
ntlangano kwakuko abantu bamahlelo ngamahlelo, nakuba yayenziwe yi
Remente yake. Ihlelo lase Wesile lamelwa ngu Mr. Philip Job ngokwenza
amazwi, elase Rabe ngu Mr. John Adams. Intlanganiso Yoncedwano
(Ethiopian Benefit Society) ngu Mr. P. Rwexu ; eyo Manyano ngu Mr. I. W.
Wauchope; eye True Templars ngu Mr. Jonas Bassi. Amalungu ezi
ntlanganiso ayekolisile ukubako ngangokuba kude kufuneke ukuba inteto
zenzelwe etyalikeni sakuba isikula sizele. U Mr. Adams owayecelwe yi
Remente ka Mr. Mzamo ukuba aze abe ngu Mongameli, eso sihlalo
wasishiyela ku Rev. S. Brook, owati nakuba wayengalindelwe ngenxa
yokuxakeka ukuba wobako, wafika kunye nenkosikazi yake. Kute
kwakugqitywa ukupungwa imihluzi esikuleni kwapunywa kwayiwa etyalikeni.
Kwaqalwa ngokuculwa ingoma emnandi kunene, ihlatyelwa ngu Mr. Mzamo.
Emva koku kusuke

u REV. SAMUEL BROOK wati—namhla sihlangene ukuza kubulisa umhlobo wetu u
Mr. Mzamo, isicaka se Nkosi ebesisebenza pakati kwenu. Ndiyatemba ukuba
baninzi pakati kwenu abati mhlana baliva ilizwi lika Tixo baliva lipuma
emlonyeni wake. Baninzi abakade bemva eshumayela i Gospile baza
bayamkela, baba namhla ngabavuna iziqamo ezimnandi zobu Krestu. Baninzi
abakade besamkela esandleni sake indebe yocebano nesikumbuzo sokufa kuka
Krestu. Ndiva ubuhlungu obukulu ngokumka komfundisi wenu; yonke imizamo
yokumtintela ayincedanga nto. Kambe ukumka kwake angaba uqutywa yintando
ye Nkosi, esifanele ngoko ukuba sihlale emtandazweni wokuba yanga i
Nkosi ingawusikelela umsebenzi aya kuwo.

u MR. JOHN S. ALAMS, umlomo wama Rabe ute, namhla kumka ubawo
wetu—sisala enxoweni. Wafika apa u Mr. Mzamo wazenzela izihlobo
ezininzi, kumahlelo onke—akatshongo ukuti ndingumtile, ngoko ndiya
kwayama kumatile odwa—ndingumfundisi—ngoko andiyi kuzikatalela ezinye
izinto zokunyusa uhlanga. Hayi, waba luncedo kumacala onke, wazixasa
zonke intlanganiso nemihlambi yokuzama ukunyukela pambili. Namhla
besifanele ukumtandazela, ukuba isimilo sake angene ngaso wada wemka
esenaso kulomzi wase Bayi angahlukani naso nalapo aya kona. Sovuya
sakuva ukuba kuhambisa kakuhle naye, kwanjengokuba naye eyakuvuya akuva
ukuquba kakuhle kwetu ngasemva.

u MR. NKOBE, umncedi wase Tshatshi usuke wati—kuwe Chairman, namabandla
amahlelo ngamahlelo, tina mhlambi wase Tshatsh siyanibulela ngokuza
kwenu. Ziti izi Balo masilile nabo balilayo Ngokulila kwenu kunye nati
nipungula olwetu usizi. Azi singabantu abaya kuba yintonina namhla emka
nje ubawo no ma abebe sikokela ngenyameko engaka. Sishiywa ngu Bawo
osivelayo kumatuba omzimba, nange Lizwi lika Tixo, umhlobo wenkedama
nabahlolokazi, umveleli wabafayo. Umfo ongacekisanga nenxila—umalatisi
wezidenge, nomkutazi wamavila, oyala ngo Tixo, ayale iqaba kwa ne orlam.
Kade simzama ukuba angemki ngoku soyisekile, sesisiti yanga i Nkosi
ingaba naye nalapo aya kona.

u MR. PHILIP JOB, umlomo we Wesile: Kuminyaka mininzi ndimazi u Mr.
Mzamo. Namhla silahlekwa yindoda yamadoda. Akafikanga abe ngumfundisi we
pulpit yodwa, aze ati akuhla kuyo abe ugqibile. Wawanonelela onke
amalungelo ohlanga. Kufuneka benje ke abafundisi bonke. Zixaswe nguye
nezama Tempile, neze Benefit, nale yetu ye Mbumba, koko ute emnye
wazenza baninzi. Hamba mhlobo warn Mr. Mzamo—beka u Tixo wako pambi
kwako kolunga konke pambi kwako, kolunga konke apo uya kona.

u MR. WAUCHOPE ufunde ipepa elibalwe ngumhlambi we “ Mbumba.”—“ Ku Rev.
D. no Mrs. Mzamo,—Mzalwana, sibe nosizi olukulu kwamhla seva ukuba
uzakumka pakati kwetu. Siyalila kubi namhla, kuba kumka indoda yamadoda.
Akunanto— hamba, kuba isele nati imisebeazi yako, ongenakumka nayo,
eyakuhlala iyinkumbuzo eyakubanga ukuba singaze sikulibale. Hamba roti
ndini wena ungazanga uzifake enxoweni izandla zako, kodwa wohlala
usipete isirweqe sako ulwela uhlanga. Wati wakubona inyembezi
zabafelwayo nabafelweyo beswele izondlo nezongo wapembelela oluncedo lwe
Benefit. Wakubona intshabalalo engeniswa luselo lwe Brandy wapembelela
uncedo lwe Tempile. Wati wakubona imfazwe zigqiba intlauga ngenxa
yokumana kuliwa nge mfakadolo, endaweni yokuba kuliwe ngosiba,
wazingenisa nawe kule Mbumba ukuze ukutaze ikaba ekulweni imfazwe
yosiba. Hamba, kuba wasixobisa kade ngezwi owati, ‘ Walweleni amalungelo
enu, akayi kuhla ngokwe mana kuse selefana nombete, ningazami.’ Au !
kambe silusapo Iwako, sikumbule nalapo uya kona. Hamba kakuhle mfundisi
wohlanga Iwetu. Singabako,— I. W. WAUCHOPE, Chairman ; J. NGABA,
Secretary ; JOHN GEORGE QABA ; PHILIP JOB ; DAVID MQIKELA ; FENI NGONYA.

U MR. MZAMO upendule wati:—

KWIMBUMBA,

Bazalwana bam be Mbumba yohlanga nindivusela usizi ndinalo kade kuba
ndisahlukana nani. Yiyipina into endiyenzileyo ukuba ndingade nditetelwe
lamazwi afanele ukutetelwa owenze into eluncedo kwi Mbumba? Mna
bendizimanye kuni ngokuzinqabisa kwimisebenzi enje nge ncuku eziti
zenziwe ngabo bangayifuniyo le Mbumba, kuba befuna ukuxamla ezonto
zentiyano, nezahlulo zentswela kuvelana yohlanga. Ndim obulela
ukundigcina kwenu, namhla siza kwahlukana

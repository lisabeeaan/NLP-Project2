Isifundo Afrika ase Amerika

[Ngu Dorotil’ ongwevu ngezihlati.]

Nkosi yam Mr. Editor.—Kaundivumele ke nditete ngezi “Jubilee Singers ” osowuke waziva. Ndite ndakuva ibali lomfo wase Kimberley ndanga ndingalila ezimatontsi inyembezi, ndakubona ukuba esona simbuku sento akasiqondi umfo wase Kimberley, usuke wafuza ama Afrika amanye. Kupela izolo nemihla tina ma Afrika ungafika siwungula uboya sibusa ekaya ize intaka siyishiye endle isale ityiwa zizizwe. Ezona zinto ziukulu zigqite nokucula kulamadoda ase Amerika ebezifanele namhla ukuba zizifundo ezikulu kumakowetu zezi.

Ababantu ugabantwana bama Ko- boka—kanti kwiminyaka ema 20 mhlaumbi ngapezulwana bekululekile sebefunde njengama Ngesi. Tina kwiminyaka eseyisondele ekulwini siugazange sibe sebukobokeni sisezi “Barbarians” ngapandle kwabambalwa.

Ababantu bepumile nje behamba kwezindawo abafuni mali yenxhowa zabo, bafuna imali yokwaka izindlu zemfundo zokufundisela abantu abantsundu bakowabo base Amerika kwanabeli lizwe abanokufikelela kwelo.

Ulangazelelo abanalo ukunyusana; xa tina neliswana elinye esibona ngalo Imvo Zabantsundu sizama ukulityapaza ngenxa yomona, namakwele, ngokungayiroli imirumo, nokungazami ukuba lande, kuba soyikela ukuti hleze umninilo atyebe angawoti.

Yintonina imbangi yokunyuka kwabo kamsinya kangaka? Yinto yokubuzwa kubo leyo, kodwa inxalenye yalombuzo ndolinga nam ukuyipendula.

Kukuba bazipatele imfundo: oko kukuti bayazifundisa — ngenteto elula bayafundisana—abanyiswa mntu kofileyo umbele njengokuba tina sivuma ukwanyiswa kofileyo ngabelungu belilizwe. Andikwekwi, nditsho. Tina imfundo esiyinikwayo yile ekutiwa iyingozi, esuka umntu imdalele iratshi, ufike umntu ehamba ezincoma ngokwake, ati izidenge zakowabo azikangelele pantsi; ati, ote wambona ukuba imfundwana yake ite tyi kweyake, asuke amtiye enze zonke indlela zokumtoba. Akuko mntu ontsundu angade atsho kumlungu ukuti sifuna imfundo epakamileyo, yinqaba. Ungafika esiti umlungu alikafiki ixesha lokuba nifundiswe into zama Ngesi—inteto eseyingenwe nangabantsundu abafuna ukuba kuzokutiwa bafundile. Into esisimanga kukuba lijikela ngapina ixesha lemfundo ukuya kufikelela ku ma Amerika angamakoboka ? Manditete ngemizi yemfundo embalwa. I Lovedale, umzi wokuqala wernfundo, mandiwushiye okwanamhla. I Ngqushwa, ikaya lama Mfengu, i Fort Peddie yamzuzu, apo ubudenge buvalelwe njenge preserved fruit, emnyameni wapakade— impi yakona seloko yakumatela kofileyo umbele kwakwimini zo Elefu nezo Debese kude kube namhlanje. I Healdtown (i Nxukwebe), inkulisa bantwana kwambaimbai, apo kulityelwe kukwe- nziwa lomdlalo wabantwana kutiwa ngumtyibilizi, kupela umntwana ukuya pezulu, aze ati tyibilili ayokuti kahlahla ezantsi, aze abuye enyuke ehleka, uyakwenza lomdlalo ade adinwe. Lomtyibilizi wase Heald Town waqalela kwa kwimini zika Rev. John Ayliff, ise nguwo nanamhla. Into e worse kunentsuka-ndihlale yamakwenkwe. Wakala wakala no Rodolo kwi Sigidimi wada wapelelwa, suka kwahlaliwa kulo mtyibilizi.

Imbangi kukauleza kwabo ; bona umntu ofundileyo bambeka pambili bati kuye, SikokeLe, siya kukulandela, sicebise, amacebo ako sowabamba. Xa kuko intlanganiso babeka yena esihlalweni. Tina akunjalo, kusuka kunxame okalipenise (amaxegokazi egusha). Ziti inkau, xa enye iblinzwe isikumba yayekwa, zibaleka zizapule ezinye; inkuku, ukuba ixwitwe intsiba yayekwa, ziya kuyixhola ezinye ide ife; inkomo xa inegazi, iyagxwalwa zezinye. Sinjalo ke tina ma Afrika ase Afrika. Umntu akutunda uyagxwalwa, axholwe, abalekwe, angancedwa, angancedi bani. Nangoke amashwa etu. Abanjalo abafundileyo. Nokuba li Jamana ebelipantsi, mhlaumbi beke lane famana ngase Bhalini, mhlaumbi belitula inkuni ezitrateni zase Qonce, mhlaumbi lada latetela ne Marike, ukuba lifundile ama Ngesi wona awakatali kukulisa e Palamenteni ati Sikokele. Anjalo ke ama Amerika. Yinxalenye le ke yokunyuka kwawo kamsinya kangaka.

Pambi kokuba ndize kugqitela ekuculeni kwawo manditi kuza kwenziwa ntonina ngumhlambi wakowetu ? Akuko nantshukumo, akuko nasifundo esiya kufunyanwa kula madoda? Manditi, Mr. Editor, kuko ifa kulamadoda endikolwa ukuba alaziwa ngamakowetu endinga ndingalityila. Ati lamadoda imfundo yonke epakamileyo singayizuza yonke e Amerika. Ati into enyusa umntu asiyiyo imfundoyencwadi kupela; uhlanga olufuna ukunyuka malufande namashishini. Ati kwenye ye Institution zabo amashishini afundwa kona ama 20. Ati, ukuba sinokuzibonela indlela yokuya kona, singamkelwa ngemivuyo. Ati indlela imhlope, se- kuko ama Afrika ake afunde kona abuye atunyelwe kweli, ngati matandatu afundayo ngoku. Umnqopiso wawo ngulo  wokuba umntu makatembise ukuba uyakubuya aze kufundisa umhlaumbi aze  kusebenzela abantu bakowabo.

BATININA ABANTU?—E KIMBERLEY.

Bati u Rev. A. Macaulay uziqale ngo Mvulo (6th inst.) weveki egqitileyo inkonzo zake zemvuselelo kwabantsundu e Wesile e Malay Camp, baye beziya kakuhle abantu. Zibe nesiqhamo esihle. Lomfundisi ushumayela kwabamhlope kuleveki.

Bati tyole yancamisa i Farewell Concert enikwe ngu Mr. Titus Mbongwe nge 30th ult e Beaconsfield pambi kokunduluka kwake. Kuvunywe nenxenye yengoma apa ezimnandi zama Amerika o “ Turn back Pharoah’s Army,” “ A meeting here to-night,” nezinye njalo-njalo. U Mr. Mbongwe ufunde ne essay ngesilungu (English) “ Ngento emayinikwe Abantsundu,” yapulapuleka. Siyatemba ukuba asiyo yokugqibela lo concert eseko nje u Mr. John Mbongwe nomzalwane wase Malay Camp.

Bati lempi intsundu yase Post Office iyayikalazela kanye inqubo yale Postmaster intsha ngakuyo, akuko kuvana kuba ligwangqa apa elingati alilindele nto kontsundu. Akuko kufa kuhle madoda.

Bati ikaba lebhola (P.T.C.C.) lenze i timiti ebalulekileyo e Malay Camp nge 13th inst., ukuqoshelisa indleko zokuya e Mnyhadaleni. Laye linga praktisi ikaba lisapula uluti. Bati u Mr. Nano naye ubhale isi Kimbili e Mveni ngebhola, inteto efuze enye yezamandulo.—Pambili Cawood.

Bati u Charles Madevu (ibhoyana) ube pambi kwe mantyi u Tulutulu (J. L. Truter, Esq., R.M.) ebanjelwe ukudlwengula u Emily Ngece (umfazi otshatileyo) endlwini yake ntambama ngo Mvulo 13th inst. Limiselwe u Mgqibelo wale veki. Amanz’ amlilo akaswelekile kolu bhici.

Bati kubanjwe isipani (16) so Gweva abantsundu bodwa e Toitspan ngalo Mvulo (13th inst.). Kambe o Gweva ngabantu abatenga imbokotwe ngobusela (Illicit Diamond Buyers). Baya kuxambula kwi Special Court yakuhlala. Kakade bachope pezu kobubi nentsizi ezinde zentolongo abasixelenga ngale ndlela esisonka.

Bati lisabalele ngakunye kweli, kupela zintuli nengqele, kube kunjalo nje selipantse ukulibaleka nexesha eyagqibela ngalo ukuna.—Hard times—ndibhala nxa eqala ukuwa amatontsana angepi.

Bati pezu kokuba amaxesha enje ukuba nzima kwawo, unxilo lona lunesivuno esikulu kuba arnatyala okunxila ahlale ezele unomgcana onesiqhuma lemihla kule gantolwana ka Makena yobuvuvana bamatyala epasi namanye njalo-njalo.

Bati zingade zibe ziziqwala itoliki zonke zase matyaleni, kodwa ziyekele le  yakwa Lenye. Kunzima ukuqonda ukuba ubulungisa bunokuqhutywa kakuhle ngetoliki enje. Sesoyeka ukuteta kuba kuchope “ umona-kwele ” sa- kwalata nto, kodwa omnye u Moss uyafuneka kula ofisi.

Bati besiya kugxumeka olunye usiba kwi cap ” ka Mr.nxa abenokuti avakalise beve nabapulapuli nxa akumsha ematyaleni e Mantyi, ingabiyivo lento isabugqugula. Siyazi tina ukuba wenza ngabom kuba u “Baas” John khe simve nxa atolika kwezinye indawo —Deze waare.—Ngu Dayimani. Kimberley, October 17th, 1890.

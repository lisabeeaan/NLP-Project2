ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1875. 7

ISILUMKISO—Isicaka esitile e-Dikeni esibe sisebenza ku MacBrien, ute
akusizisa ematyaleni ngokuauke singabiko emsebenzini singenamvumelo
yokumka, sadliwa iponti, kwatiwa isicaka esisebenza ngenyanga
aainakuyiabiya inkosi yaso nokuba kuse mini nokuba kuse busuku ngapandle
kwemvume.

UNYANA KA KOFU, inkosi yama Ashantee usa- nd’ukufika E-England. Omnye
weminqopiso ukuze lixole oko bekuliwa nama Ngesi wawungo wokuba unyana
lowo uyakuya kufundiswa E-England. Namhla ke kuzaliswa elodinga.
Kuvakala ukuba yinkwenkwana eqabukileyo ekukangelekeni. Ubudala uminyaka
ilishumi elinesine.

APA E-LOVEDALE kusand’ukunduluka amado- dana amabini u-Mr. H. Price no
Mr. W. Soga unyana omkulu ka Rev. T. Soga. Omabini lamadodana aye
kufunda ubugqira pesheya. Kusand’ ukunduluka kanjalo no Miss S.
McGillivray no Miss Levey ukuya Elusutu ukuya kuquba umsebenzi
wokufundisa kona. Ngabo bonke umnqweno wetu ngowokuba yanga i-Nkosi
ingaba nabo.

E-SPAIN uti umfundisi otile wakona usahambiseka umsebenzi welizwi.
Zininzi kona izinto zokukutaza nezidanisayo. Alibangalininzi inani
labangene erementeni, into enkulu kukuba abangeneyo bazipete njengoko
bafanele ukwenjenjalo abakristu. Kuko itemba ukuba kuya kubako
ukuhambiseleka pambili okukulu ngalonyaka umiyo. Anga amakristu endawo
zonke angabatandazela.

UKUTENGISWA KWE ZIBALO E-ITALY—Njengoko bazi ngako abanye bekuke kungeko
mvumelo yokuba kutengiswe i-Zibalo e-Italy oko ibisepantsi kwamandla ka
Popo. Enye yendawo ezinkulu ezinika itemba lokuhamba kwelizwi kona
kukutengwa kwencwadi yelizwi ngabantu. Ngomnyaka odluleyo ekwaziwayo
ukuba zitengiwe zibe 6,558, i-Testamente ezintsha zaba 16,377,
izicatshulwa zezibalo zaba 15,827.

OBULEWEYO—Ngase Bayi kuko ilawana elingu Dingan elibuleweyo ngomnye
umntu. Libe lite lalibala kwihotela etile kwada kwasebusuku. Kubonakala
ukuba lite lipuma kona waba lomntu selelilalele walibeta entloko lati
lakuwa isiduli walitabata waya kuligqibelela mgama. Lifunyenwe intloko
le izele zinduma. Ute kanti umbulali uyishiye kona intonga yake kunye
nesitshetshe waza ke waziwa ngezozinto wabanjwa. Ufunyenwe ingubo ezi
zizele ligazi kwada kwabomvu nekausi.

E-YEDDO, ikomkulu lase Japan e-Asia, kuko ihospital enkulu exaswa yi
government yakona. Umfo obeyipete ubebatiyile aba Kristu engavumi ukuba
kubeko igqira elikolwayo kona. Kute kwezinyanga zidluleyo abantu
bafumana indlela zokuba agxotwe, lomfo, bati bona bafuna ukuba
ihos­pital ibe sesandleni sokolwayo, oya kulungiselela imipefumlo yabo
kwanje ngemizimba. Ngoku bate ababegxotwe kona ngapambili babuye babizwa
Nakwezinye indawo abantu balila ngokuti sifuna abafundisi bokusazisa
u-Yesu. Liya lisanda inani labafuni. Omnye umfundisi wakona uti asikuko
nokuba linxanelwe ilizwi.

ILIZWI LE NKOSI E-CHINA—Umfundisi otile obala ngokuhambiseka kwelizwi
lika Tixo e-Foochow, esinye sezixeko zase China uti—Nakuba umsebenzi
wetu e-Foochow ekaya ungabanga nampumelelo intle kakulu, ute
watamsanqeleka kanye kwindawo eziyi ngqongileyo : baliqela abaguqukayo
bazingise enkolweni noko inkulu intshutshiso. Kulo mnyaka udluleyo
kubaptizwe abantu 184. Bangapezulu kakulu kwabo abasafunayo,
abangekamkelelwa erementeni. Komnye umzana umfundisi wakona uti kuguquke
abantu abalishumi. Amakolwa ayazama kanye ukulazisa ilizwi kwabanye.
E-Ku-cheng kukutshwe amadoda 10 ukuba abe ngabashumayeli.

INTLANGA EZINTSUNDU—Umfo abati ngu Sir Samuel Baker, omnye wa ahambi
abakulu apa E-Africa uti kwenye indawo ukubala ngentlanga ezintsundu :
“Akwaba ababantu base England aba mana ukusizela ama Africa
bebenokuwabona njengoko ndiwabona ngako ; usizana lwabo beluya kupeja.
Ama Africa ndingawalinganisa nezilo, kube nakuzo kuko eziwadlulileyo
ngobuhle besimilo. Asibantu bakwaziyo ukubulela, abanalusizi,
abanalutando, abakwazi kuzikanyeza, abayazi imfanelo, abanankonzo, zinto
ezunqweno, ezizilungisela okwazo, ezinolunya, ababandezeli. Bonke
bangamasela, amanqenera, into ezimona, esezihleli zilungele ukwenza
amakowazo amakoboka, zipange ababutataka.”

UKUNYUKA KWABANTSUNDU—U-Nqawana Lo- sina uti ngokunyuka
kwabantsundu—“Sitotywe kangaka nje zezinye intlanga esinye sezizatu
kukuti tina kude kutshaye nabantwana namankazana. Nditi ke soba
nobuhlobo ninina noluhlanga lungu Ngesi? Niti losivumana xa kusatshaya
namanenekazi akuti? Makwahlukwane namasiko anjalo. Yinto entle ukunyuka
kohlanga. Siyanyuka, musani ukutsalela ezantsi. Anibonina ukuba ucingo
luqutywa ngabetu, into ebesingazi nofifi ngayo ; intambula ibetwa lusapo
lwetu? Yintoni engasiqondisiyo ukuba kungade kubeko into nakuti xa
sizamayo? Kodwa ke kufuneka matanci uloyiko luka Tixo noncedo Iwake,
kuba zonke izipo zivela kuye.”

ISIKOLO SENTOMBI—E-Shawbury, umhla 27 ka May ubungumhla omkulu. Ngawo
kube kube- kwa ilitye lesiseko sendlu ezakuba sisikolo esihla- la
intombi. Bebebaninzi abantu abebezile; kumiswe ukuze libekwe ngu Mrs
White, umfazi ka Rev. C. White wama Wesile ose Osborne. Bekuko neqelana
labamhlope. Kubonakele ukuba abantu bayakuvuyela kanye ukuba kumiswe
isikolo esinjalo pakati kwabo, kuba besiti bayanqwena kakulu ukuba ifund
swe intsapo yabo. Kute xa U-Rev. W. S. Davis atyila umsebenzi waso
kwaqondakala ukuba abantu bapulapula ngentliziyo ezivuyayo. Imali
eselite- njisiwe £90. Eyakufuneka ngokwakiwa kwaso iyakuba £150.
Kwenzelwa ukuze kwamkelwe intombazana ezimashumi mabini. Umpatikazi wazo
uyakuba ngu Mrs Davis.

E-TSOMO NASE SIKOBENI—Izikolo zapesheya kwe Nciba zingaba ziyalungisa
ukuba zite zabonela kwesase Tsomo ngokwakiwa kwetyalike. Indlu yakona
akayi tandabuzi umhambi esekude; akuke kubeko nokufuna ukuti ingaba
yindlu kabanina leya. Uti esiya kube kuvakala kanye ukuba uya
esikolweni; ivakale londawo noko idelekileyo inqu yomzi. Iti okukona
asondelayo umntu iye itandeka ngokutandeka, kanti iseza kumncamisa
akufika ecaleni kwayo, isule umlomo akungena ngapakati. Ngendlela zonke
yindlu efanele umniniyo. Bangati ukuba abantu abayimeleyo baya kufana
nayo ngokomelela nokutandeka nokuqina elukolweni, ukuba nabo akuyi
kubakondlela yokuba angatandabuza ukuba bangaba aba ngabapina, akuko
sikolo singafaniswa nesabo; kuba nalapo sikoyo simi kwindawo etandekayo.
Kuvakala ukuba nabase Sikobeni abantu bafuna ukwaka indlu efanelekileyo.
Sebehlanganisa kunene imali. Bawabale aba liqela amadoda aselerole
iponti ezikumashumi amabini eva ngesihlanu indoda inye, into ke leyo
engasaziwayo kweli lase koloni; kuba apa iselisiti indoda mhla irole
iponti ezintlanu bakuze bonke. Kuvakala ukuba abelocala lase Sikobeni
bawuvuyele kanye lomsebenzi wetyalike, into ke leyo eluvuyo olukulu
nakomgama umntu. Bafuna indlu eya kumela Isikoba no Cofimvaba. Enye
indawo angafike ayivuyele owase mzini kukwanda kwenani lengxande nezinye
izinto zendlu ezibonisa ukuhambela pambili kwabantu, ezinje ngozitafile
nozitulo, nozincwadi.

AMA NEW ZEALAND—Elinye lamapepa apeshe- ya liti.—“Inani lama Maori
(abemi base New Zealand) lifike 37,000 kwisiqiti esingasentla;
kwesingezantsi 3,000. Ama Maori noko ebeke anda, kubonakala ukuba ngoku
ayehla; angati agqitywa ngumteto wemvelo ongavumiyo ukuba acume umntu
omnyama kwakufika omhlope. Noko eyona ndawo inkulu iwonakalisayo
yinkohlakalo yawo. Kuyalingwa ngako konke ukuba afundiswe into zokukanya
: kekaloku imfundo asike azonakalise ngayo akuyifumana ; Ilizwi lika
Tixo ngati akavumi ukulamkela ngangoko kuzanyelwa ukuba kubenjalo.
Mhlaumbi noko kunje angaba aseza kuma, abuye azibone indawo zokuposisa
kwawo kwangapambili. Abantu abakaulezayo ukuhluma ngamabastile, abantu
ekubonakala ukuba baza kuba zintloko.”

E-MOMBAS—I-Mombas ngumzana omi elu- nxwemeni lwase Africa ngasentla
kuhle kwe Zanzibar. Abafundisi abakulu abapete londawo ngabalunge ne
Tshatshi. Okoyo ngoku ngu Rev. W. S. Price. Uti ke ukuteta ngentlalo
yabo kona : Umsebenzi wokuqala isikolo esitsha kwelizwe elisebunyameni
asintwana yokufeketa. Kulondawo ngokukodwa kusweleke nokudla
okupilileyo, ilanga liya bandezela, izifo zizonke, ekungummangaliso
nokuba babe basapilile. Inkoliso yabo iselivuka elukukweni. Uti ke noko
kunjalo akukabiko kubo oncamayo ; bangavuma ukutwala ubunzima ngenxa
yowabafelayo. Ba- namatemba amakulu ukuba uyakupumelela um- sebenzi
wabo. Izikolo azipeteyo zilundiswa ilizwi nemisebenzi yezandla. Ufuna
ukumisa esinye E-Mombas, esinye sibe se Kisulidini.

SINOSIZI ukuva ngokububa kuka Rev. Charles New, wase Wesile, obekweli
lase Africa, kulamacala ase Zanzibar. Weza apa e-Africa kwange 1862.
Iminyaka esitoba wayicitela e-Ribe, efunda nenteto yabantu bakona,
ebafundisa ilizwi lika Tixo. Ubenamaxesha kanjalo ati ahambele abapakati
efuna ukukangela apo kungamiswa kona izikolo. Ngomnyaka 1872 wacelwa
ukuba ahambe nabantu ababeya kufuna u-Dr. Livingstone. Yati yakuba
ingapumelelanga londlela wahambela e- England. Ngo March, kulonyaka
udluleyo, ubuye weza kwase Africa ukuza kuquba umsebenzi wokufundisa.
Esisifo simgqitisileyo simfumene ehambele ku Tshaga inkosi enkulu emele
lentaba kutiwa yi Kilimanjaro, eye kukangela ukuba ba- ngamkelwa na
abafundisi. Ufike wamkelwa kakubi ngu Tshaga, wapangwa nempahla zake.
Kwaleke xa abuyayo kulohambo, akabi seza kufika nase kaya.

KWINCWADI ka Lord Carnarvon asand’ukuyi- tumela ku Sir Henry Barkly uti
kunganjanina ukuba kubeko intlanganiso yamadoda ale koloni neyase Natal
nawase Orange Free State nase Transvaal, axoxe ngokuba kuhlanganwe
kuncedwane kwizinto ezisukuba zisihla. Indawo enkulu ateta ngayo uti,
akungeveliswe luhlobo lunyena lokupata abantsundu, endaweni yokuba
bapatwe ngemiteto ngemiteto. Uti umntu ofanele ukongamela
kulontlanganiso ngu Sir H. Barkly, ati ukuba akafuni kongamele U-Sir
Arthur Cunnynghame. Le yetu ikoloni uti mayimelwe ngamadoda amabini,
u-Molteno no Paterson, omnye amele abangase Kapa, omnye amele lamacala
etu. E-Natal ukankanya u-Shepstone; aze kwele Diamond abale u-Southey
nokuba ngu Barry. I-England uyikupele u-Froude ukuba aze kuyimela.
Ezinye indawo uti zingazibonela zona amadoda emaziwakupe. U-Molteno ute
yena akayingeni lonto itetwa ngu Lord Carnarvon; esingaziyo ke ukuba
angabuye atinina. Indawo kulentlanganiso ekuqutyelwa kuyo kufunwa ukuze
kuti ukuba kuko isizwe esintsundu esifuna ukuvukela abamhlope zincedane
zonke ezindawo zikankanywayo.

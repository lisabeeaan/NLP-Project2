SIR GARNET WOLSELY obeyi Governor yase Natal utunyelwe e-Jipete ngenxa
yezipitipiti ezikona.

E-KATBERG kuko inqwelo ete inyuka yancotuka ideselbom yayakweyela
eliweni yati vitiviti yonke ukwapuka.

LANGALIBALELE—Imali erolwa yi Natal ukuxasa u-Langalibalele ngamakulu
amabani anamashuiui mahlanu eponti yonke iminyaka.

E-NATAL umfo abati ngu Mr Underhill udliwe 50s ngokusuke apate kakubi
ihashe elikwele. Ubokuti libe lidlokova ayapulapulwa londawo.

OBEBETA UMFAZI—E-Dikeni kumatyala ake ako bekuko nelika Mbulawa wase
Sheshegu obebete umfazi wake omkulu ngentonga. Isigwebo sibe sokuba
ahlale intsuku ezisixenxe etolongweni esebenza nzima.

KWELE DIAMOND.—U-Thomas Iloya wase Diamond Fields ubala ngokuti bahleli
ngokunzima kwelozwe, kuba ontsundu ufumane abanjwe kungeko nto
ayenzileyo. Uti ukuba uke wabonwa evatile kuse kutiwa ezongubo uzitenge
ngemali ebiweyo.

ABAPATI MZI base Somerset bafuna ukumisa umteto wokuze umntu ontsundu
kona ahambe enxibe into yokumpaula ukuba akanguye oqeshiweyo, ukuba
iminyaka yake yobudala ifike kwishumi elinesihlanu. Kutiwa baya kuti
abanolo lupau xa bafunwa ngumlungu banyanzelweukuba baye kumsebenzela.
Ote wala uyakugxo twa kona.

OBETE UMFAZI—u-Sibindi, Imfengu yase Mxelo ubeziswe ematyaleni e-Dikeni
ngokubeta umfazi wa ke, embetela ukuba enike utywala komnye umntu. Ute
umfazi iti lendoda indibeta' ibe isiti, Ndibulala inja yam, endayirolela
inkomo zam. Ite indoda yakubuzwa lamazwi yakanyela yati isuke
yamgibisela ngompa kwakupela. Imantyi inike isiyalo kulomfo ukuba
angadlali ngomfazi wake, yamdla £5.

E-PERAK, ilizwe elipantsi kwa Mangesi elise Malay Peninsula emazantsi
e-Asia, abantu bakona bayibulele inkosana Yomlungu ebibapete. Kute
kwakuvakala oko kwatunyelwa amasoldati ayakuncedisa. Ati akufika
abobantu balwa nawo kwako abulawayo. Kuko ukoyikela ukuba ziza kuba
liqela izizwe eziza kuhlangana zivukele Amangesi kwelocala. Kutunyelwe
ke ngoko e-Hong-Kong ukuba makubeko kwakamsinya iqela lamasodati eliza
kuyipelisa lemfazwe.

UMBUZO—Umfo wase Bayi abati ngu Sampson uti kulomacala abo kuko umfana
obefake umsesane entombini esiti uyayifilisha. Kute kupi wayala wati
utando lupelile, yamsa ematyaleni wadliwa kunene. U-Sampson ke ubuza
ukuba yintonina ukuba kube njalo kanti nje nase zintombini kufuti ukuti
lupelile utando, xa kufike elinye isoka ? Impendulo ke ingaba yeyokuba
nomfana makayise ematyaleni intombi efekete ngaye. Asikuko nokuba
Amangesi ayalungisa ukuzindla ezintwana zimana ukufeketa ngabantwana
babantu. Mhlaumbi ngezingadliwa nokudliwakubandezelwa oyise kuba
ngezifakwa entologweni.

PROFESSOR NOBLE.—Pakati kwamadoda asishiyileyo kulenyanga ifileyo kuko
no Professor Noble, umfo okufa kulusizi Kanye kuti. Ubengomnye wamadoda
ate nakuba emhlope engengabo nabafundisi, waba esenza imizamo emikulu
yokulunyusa uhlanga oluntsundu ngako konke anako. Kuye ibala lomntu libe
lingesiso sixakaniso. Ubengenantloni ukuzibiza ngokuti usihlobo
sontsundu, ukuba ngumntu ozindilileyo. Ngokufa kwake siti silahlekelwe
ngomnye wabancedi nabahlobo abakulu, abamisebenzi ibiya kwenza ukuba
siye simtanda ngakumbi nangakumbi okukonasiya siyi qonda. Lomfo
simlilelayo usishiye ngomhla 20 ka December 1875, xa ngobudala eminyaka
46.

EBATENJINI—Elinye lamapepa ase Qonce liti. Kuvakala ukuba ngoku
u-Dalasile uselesihlobo esikulu sama ngesi, into leyo esisiqamo
semisebenzi ka Rev. J. Gordon, umblobo omkulu wa Batembu. U-Ngangelizwe
kuvakala ngaye ukuba kuko umfazi asand’ ukumbeta kakulu, noko
bungacacile ubunyaniso balonto. Ngomhla 17 ka December ubesate ngokoyika
ukuba uza kubanjwa wabaleka waya kuzimela ehlatini, walushiya usapo
lwake lungena mkuseli. U-Mr. Bowker ulunikele kwizihlobo zake. Enye
indawo evakalayo yeyokuba inkosana ezingabanga nokufikelela
entlanganisweni ebiko zitintelwe yimvula zibuye zeza ku Mr. Bowker ukuza
kunika imibulelo yokuba zamkelelwe pantsi kwepiko la Mangesi.

F-Queenstown kuko ipolisa elingu Conybeere elite lahlangana nabantu
abantsundu behamba indlela bebabini. Libabuze ipasi, bate bakulinika
layirazula lati mabarole ishumi lesheleni emnye. Kute kunjalo kwafika
elinye ipolisa laya kubuxela obubumenemene. U-Conybeere lowongoku
usetolongweni.

KWELAMABACA—Umfo otile obala ckulomacala uti, “Ndiva ukuba kuko
isipitipiti pakati ko Makaula inkosi ya Mabaca no Mhlontlo eya
Mampondomisi ngomhlaba. Kuko Amampondomisi ate aya kwaka kumhlaba
ekupikiswana ngawo. Uti ke u-Makaula makafuduke, ukuba akenjinjalo uya
kuwatshisela izindlu. Sekuko iqela lamapolisa aye kukangela ngalondawo.”

KUMATYALA ASE RINI.—Kumatyala abetetwa e-Rini pambi kwaba gwebi abango
Dwyer no Smith bekuko nelika Ntanjana ebambene no Pohl. Isiqaleko salo
sibe singoluhlobo. U-Ntanjana waye hamba nenqwelo yake eyakutengisa
ngokudla e-Cradock; kuko nenqwelo ka Linda neka Zwelinjani. Bate
bakufika e-Kobonqaba yapuka eka Ntanjana inqwelo yatunyelwa kuba kandi.
Kute kusenjalo kwafika u-Pohl wati ukuba baye nazo e-Somerset uya
kubanika iponti ngenxowa. Zisiwe ke okwenene yakugqitywa ukukandwa
inqwelo. Kufikiwe uzitabatile lo Pohlwaya kuzitengisa wafumana imali
engepantsi kwaleyo abatembise ukubanika. Ubuye esiti uza kubanika 15s
ngenxowa, bala abanye. Babone seletengisa impahla zake ebopa esiti uya
e- Transvaal, baza ke bamsa ematyaleni pambi kwabagwebi. Isigwebo sibe
sesokuba lo Pohl ayirole iponti ebekunqotshiswenc ngayo aze ahlaule
nendleko zokuya ematyaleni.

ISAZISO SAKOMKULU.

Kwi ofisi Yompatiswa Wemihlaba Yakwa
Rulumente. December 2, 1875.
NGELIXESHA langoku asikuko nokuba bayafuneka abantu bokuza kusebenza
kwezindlela zenqwelo zomlilo ezenziwayo kwindawo ngendawo zekoloni.
U-Rulumente ke ufuna ukubakutaza abo bancedisayo ukufuna amadoda okuza
kusebenza. Uya kuti ke ngoko kosukuba etumele amadoda anokusebenza
angengapantsi kwamashumi amabini anesihlanu, avumayo ukuze asebenze
ixesha clingenga pantsi kwenyanga ezintandatu efumana umvuzo omiyo
kakade —otumele amadoda anjalo
uya kuvuzwa ngoluhlobo:—

Ukuba aya kweyase Worcester ngase Kapa
wonikwa £3 ngendoda inye ayitumeleyo.

Ukuba kukweyase Uitinhage ibe £1 ndoda.

Kweyase Bushman River Poort ibe 10s
ngendoda.
Amaqela amadoda afuna ukuya kweyase Worcester aya kwamkelwa emkhombeni e
Monti. Umntu oya kuya kbanikela ezandleni zezipata-mandla apo e-Monti
wofumana iponti ngomntu emnye ongeneyo emkombeni.

Imali erolwayo ngemini kwabasebenzayo
2s. 6d. kunye nokudla ukuba bakwindlela
yase Worcester. Ukuba bakweyase Bushmans River Poort ibe 2s. 6d. kube
kupela.

Abaye kweya ngase Kapa boti ukuba
bahlale umnyaka kona mhla babuya bangabizwa mali ngokungeniswa
emkombeni.

  JOHN X. MERRIMAN.
  Umpatiswa Mihlaba

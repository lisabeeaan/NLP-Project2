INDABA ZOCINGO EZIHLOMELA

KWI

Isigidimi Sama-Xosa.

LOVEDALE, FRIDAY, 4 p.m. 14th Oct., 1870.

6 o’Clock kwada kwangokuhlwa ngexa lika 5 o’Clock. Ukumkani Wama-French
wayesilwa ehamba pakati komkosi wake ebushushwini bedabi, wada wasuka
wagqityelwa ukoyiswa umkosi Wama-French.

Ute kwa kamsinya emveni koko ukum- kani Wama-French watumela incwadi
kukumkani Wama-Prussia esiti “ Ekubeni ndingafanga ndipete umkosi wam
ndisi beka ezinyaweni zako isirele sam.”

Zakuba zifikile ezindaba E-Paris kwa- miswa ulaulo olungenankosi, nokuba
kungabisabako kupambana pakati kwabo, ngalo uhlobo lolaulo.

Lemizi kutiwa Yi-Metz, Ne-Strasburg, Ne-Phalsburg, Ne-Toul, Ne-Montmedy,
ayikoyiseki. Ama-Prussia kaloku sele- kufupi Ne-Paris, ekuqondekayo ke
ukuba kuza kunqumka ukuhambelana nesosi- xeko.

Ngo Sept, ngosuko Iweshumi elina- lunye ukumkani Wase-France wamkelwa
ngobubele yinkosi Yase-Prussia, zaza injengele Zase-France zakululwa
kuso isifungo sawo, sokupata ubukosi aza ama- soldati asiwa E-Germany.

Inkosikazi Yase-France U-Eugenie, yada yakwazi ukuzipata kakuhle nase-
bunzimeni. Yati yona itanda ukwenzela usizi, kunokupata ubukosi, nokuba
ayiku- c'asi okutetwa luninzi. Indulukile E- France yaya kucanda
E-Belgium isinga E-England nonyana wayo.

Ama-Italian alungiselela ukuya E- Rome, nemikosi yawo seyikufupi kweso-
sixeko.

Umkombe otile umke nomsinga waku- jikela E-Cape Finisterre unamakulu
omahlanu abantu, eko Ko-Captain Coles umenzi wawo.

Ama-Russia anxamele ngokunye uku- timba ilizwe Lama-Turk,
sekulungiselelwa imfazwe nakona.

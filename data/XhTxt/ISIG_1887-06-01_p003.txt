UKUCOLACOLA.

UKUBUYISWA KWEZIXOBO.—Kuvakala ukuba lamipu yayibolekwe ama Baca nama
Xesibe ngu Rulumente ib’iyi mikoko ukuyigodusa, kususela kolwe 14 ku
April. Isibonakalo samaqela sinje, inxenye, ayekwel’ emahashen’ inxenye
ihamba ngenyawo, ingamaqela anga 50 njalo-njalo, ehamba evuma umhobe
lonto isuke ikumbuze ezamini zodushe lwezo mpi nama Mpondo.

LUMKANI MASOKA.—Ati amapepa ite ndodana itile yaya kwintombi eb’iteta
nayo, iye kupulapula ingonyana ezimnandi kunene. Kute kuse njalo,
wagaleleka uyise wenkazana, wayikaba lendodana yaya yawa, eteta izwi
eliti—“ Ndiyazi mna ukuba ezingonyana zezokuruta umxe- lo womntwan’am.”

IWEDINIKEKI ENEXABISO.—Kuvakala ukuba nje ngokuba intombi ye Ruluneli u
Miss Robinson yendile nje i wedding-cake yodwa ipunyelwe zi £140.

UKUFA OKUSIQUPE KOMXOMI.—Liti ipepa lase Rini—Ite indoda eb’ingumxomi
wabanye iminyaka emininzi yahliwa sisifo ngebaqo yafa. Igama layo ngu
Von Witt. Apo ibihlala kona kuse Kimberley, De Beer’s Road, inendlu
ekudliwa kuyo.

OXONYIWEYO.—Kuko umfo oxonyiweyo e Malmesbury, ogama lingu David Manuel.
Lomntu waye bulele intombazana engu Martha Titus ngo April 1880.

UKUZIBULALA KWE NKUNZI YE NKOMO.—Kutiwa kuko inkunzi ye nkomo ezibulele
ngokwayo ngase Malindi, ngo- kusuke ingene emanzini anzulu, isuke
iyifake intloko kuwo, ide isuke iqauke. Kutiwa lento iyinyaniso.
Lenkunzi sel’indala kakulu. Kutshiwo lipepa lase Monti eliyi E. L.
Despatch.

ODLIWE IKULU LEPONTI.—Kutiwa kuko indoda edliwe i £100 ngokutengisa
amanz’ ashushu ngapandle kwe Lisence. Lomntu kutiwa akaqali, ubeke
abanjwe ngapambili, atetiswe angayeki noko. Namhla ude wasilelwa isidudu
semfe. [Tshotsho! ubeba koba njanina ? ED. Sigidimi.]

ABAQIWULE UCINGO.—Kuvukala ukuba kuko amasela aqiwule ucingo kwi fama ka
Mr. Wright wangase Rini, aze eba inkomo ezilishumi emka nazo.

IPALAMENTE.—Ipalamente ihlangene nge 27 ku May.

Akukabiko nto zibalulekileyo zenziweyo. Kodwa kuyoyikeka ukuba ingxoxo
ziyakuba shushu.

IMISEBENZI YOTYWALA.—Ati amapepa kuko umfazi oze- yeliseleyo eminxunyeni
yase Dayimani enxilile, wafunyanwa sel’engumqulwana ezantsi kwakusa.
Lonto yenzeke e Du Toits Pan kuyo lenyanga ifileyo ingu May.

INJAKAZI NE DOLL.—Liti ipepa lase Monti kuko injakazi eb’izole yaze
yalahlelwa ezonjana. Kute kwakuba njalo yabona intombazana idlalisa nge
doll, yasel’itsala ukuya kuyo, yafike yamane ikwina, ilila, yade
yayinikwa le doll yayisa esikundleni ebesinenjana zayo. Kodwa
bekubonakala ukuba yanele, ngentsuku ezitile. Kute kwakuba ntsuku
yatyalusela ukunganeli, yaman’ukukwina, ibaleka ifuna zinto, ize kanti
noko ayikuxola akutatyatwa lo doll.

IYEZA ELISIMANGA LE NGQAKAQA.—Liti ipepa lase Kokstad, paya ngase
Mzimkulu, kuti ukunyangwa kwaba- ntu abahliwe yi ngqakaqa, kukiwe
izitshitshi zeminyani yamazimba, ize kutiwe makakamise ke umntu ofayo,
aze ke akuhlwe kunene lomaqakuva, ade ope, kusingiswe nase ncarancareni
paya kukuhlwa, kukolwe ke ukuba opile nje ngoyena ulungileyo. Kutiwa
abantu abafileyo ba 11 kumakulu amabini anamashumi mahlanu (250). lyeza
elo libangela intlungu enkulu kunene, kwanokukupa igazi. Liti ke elo
pepa, into engumangaliso yile yokuba kungabi yeyona ininzi into ebulawa
liyeza elo kunokufa sisifo ngo- kwaso. Enye ke into liti kwalona, ngati
elinani lifileyo kwanelisindileyo, yinto ebiyakwenzeka kade leyo, nokuba
bekuqatshulwe ngoku kwase Mlungwini, baze banyangwa kakuhle ligqira
bakuba behliwe yi ngqakaqa.

ISIQOLOLWANE SENDODANA.—Ati ama pepa, ko Samson balamaxesha kuseko u
Willie Carr wase Blyth, umfo woxolo ongaze afane adlele mntu ndlala
engabalekelwanga, noko anamandla angaka nje, engadanga acatshukiswe
ngumntu. Kutiwa kute ngomnye umhla ngokuhlwa ese hotele kunye nento
eninzi yamanye amadodana, wamane egweleqwa yenye indodana, imane imdla
bukwabasa ngokumqola, waba sicululwane sokuhlekwa, wake wanyamezela noko
seku njalo. Kute kwakuba kudala kunjalo we kwapu, watabata lentsimbi
kutiwa kitchen poker, suke wayiti jike- jike ngokulula njengokungati
utandela ucingo emqaleni walandoda eteta izwi eliti—'‘..Make ndikubeke
kobobume, ude uke udambe, kuba akuko bani unako ukukukulula lontsimbi
ngapandle kwam.”

ISIQARALA SENTSINI.—Suke kwe qara intsini kulendlu, bakubona umntu
ehamba nesidanga sentsimbi kaloku, esel’ ekohliwe yinto yokwenza.
Uvakele sel’ezibika ngelicela itaru ku Willie, ate naye ukulukulula olo
xaxazo, wange ukulula umsontwana wocingo olutambileyo waba uyakululeka
umquli lowo.

OMIWE YI ORANGE WADE WAFA—Liti ipepa lase Tinara, kuko umfo ote esidla i
orange engowase Monti, suke kinxi enye imvaba emqaleni, puhlu amehlo
umntu, wabonakala selesiti qiti pantsi eyalula amehlo, kanti kuyapela
njalo.

AMASELA ABELUNGU—Ipepa lase Kokstad libalisa nga- masela amabini
angamagcisa kulomsebenzi. Kutiwa afika e Cedarville Drift esitoreni sika
Mr. Uys, ehamba ngenyawo, Afika acela indawo yokulala ayipiwa kindlwana
ebunxam, nakuba u Mr. Uys wayewaranela ukungati ngababantu bake beba
amahase elugqatsweni olutile. Ute ke ngeso sirano wanika umteto
oqinileyo ezicakeni zake wokuba maze zibagcine kunene, igcinwe nempahla
yomzi. Kute ngokuhlwa omnye wezicaka, waya kulubopelela ngapandle ucango
Iwalandlu alele kuyo amasela, kodwa kwasa elugqobozile epume emka.

UKUNAKANWA KWAWO.—Kulandiwe sesinye sezicaka, siwabonile esingisa ngase
ndulini esemva komzi, abonakala sel’ekwele emahasheni amabini awabileyo.
Kute xaka kwa oko ukusukela, kudwalazelene kwadwalazelana, lade
lafunyanwa elinye. Kute xa u Mr. Uya asemke neqela elisukela leya
inkewu, suke pululu naleya ib’ibanjiwe, emka omabini kwapela.

ELUSUTU.

Ekupeleni kwalonyaka udluleyo bekuko intlanganiso yomnyaka yabo bonke
Abesutu. Kwinkosi ezinkulu ebe nokufikelela ngu Masopa yedwa: u- Letsie
no Molapo bate abana kufikelela ngenxa yesifo.

Ute u-Mr- Griffith akuba ezibulisile inkosana wati makuleseshwe imiteto
ukuze kungabiko uti bendingayazi, akunikwa ityala. nkuba kuko awapuleyo.
Utete ke ngokuti i-Government yase Koloni ifuna amadoda okuya kusebenza
ezindleleni zenqwelo zomlilo. amadoda ayileyo aya kufumana 1s. ngemini
ngenyanga zokuqala zontandatu, kuze ngezilandela ezo afumane 1s. 6d.
nokudla. Inkosana bezifanele ukubakutaza abantu bazo ukuba baye kona
bangayi kwele Diamond, apo bafunda ubunxila. Eyesibini into ingokuvuna,
ukuba bonke bakugqibe ngo July, inkomo zibuqe ngosuku lokuqala ku
August. Lento uyiteta ngokuba kuko abati bangavuni ngabom ukuze bamane
ukubamba impahla zabanye. Eyesitatu, isikolo sakwa Rulumente sesiza
kugqitywa ukwakiwa. Mabaze ke bati abangafuniyo ukubatumela abantwana
babo kwizikolo zabafundisi kuba beya kufundiswa nelizwi lika Tixo kona,
babatumele kwesi sakwa Rulumente.

Ute akugqiba zesuka inkosana zabulela amatamsanqa eziwafumana ngokuba
pantsi kwamangesi, uxolo, ukunqaba, ukonwaba, ukutyeba kwabo
nokuhambisela pambili, nezinye izinto. Ngokuvuna zite ixesha elinikiweyo
lifutshane, malolulelwe ekupeleni kuka August.

Kute kusatetwa intlanganiso yacitwa yimvula waselesiti u-Mr. Griffith
yobuya itetwe londawo yexesha lokuvuna.

Imfundo kwelozwe kubonakala ukuba ibihamba ngokukutazayo noko
ingakaulezi ngangoko bangatanda ngako abaqubi bayo. Kulomnyaka kuko
amadodana amatatu apumele uviwo lakwa Rulumente lwabafuna ukufundisa
intsapo. Isikolo sentombi esise Thaba Bosigo sinabafundi 35;
esamakwenkwe esise Morija sinabafundi aba 74,—

Ngokuhamba kwelizwi kuko izinto ezininzi zoku- kukutaza. Kuko iqela la
Besutu abangabashumayeli, nabafumli a bentsapo abakolise ukuti babatalwe
kwangabantu. Nokoke ziko nezinto ezizixakaniso. Esinye esibe siku
kulonyaka upelileyo kubeko abafazi nentombi abati bayatwasa, izitixwana
zibe ziteta nabo zibanike ilizwi elitunyelwa ku Besutu, eliti, Amangesi
ayaxoka ukuti kuko u-Tixo, kengoko mayilahlwe yonke into azo nayo
ezigusha, ibokwe, amahashe, amakuba, imbiza, inqawa, ingcawa namafelani
nazo zonke ezinye intlobo zengubo ezize no Mlungu. Kubeko abantu
abavumayo ukuba bazilahle inqawa bati ngezinye izinto abasakupumelela
ngapandle kwazo. Kute kumacala anabo bantu kwanqaba ukuyiwa
kushunyayclwa nge Sabata. Batumele ku Makristu ilizwi lokuba. ukuba
akalilahli ilizwi azakutsha. Kubeko alahlayo ngenxa yelozwi! Kodwa ke
impembe-lelo yabo bantu ngoku incipile kakulu. Kubonakala ukuba
bebetunywa zinkosana ukuba batete ezizinto bahambe behlunguzelisa
intloko.

Esinye isixakaniso esikulu kukubambelela kwamakolwa kumasiko amadala,
ngokukodwa ulwaluko.

UKWAMKELELWA KWA MAHALA
PANTSI KWA MANGESI.

Enye yendawo zokubulelwa ezenzeke kwizizwe ezipesheya kwe Nciba
kukwamkelwa kwesizwe sika Ngangelizwe ukuba sibe pantsi kolaulo lwa
Mangesi. Kwasekuke kwakankanywa ukuba kwintlanganiso eyayiko ngomhla 17
ka December u-Ngangelizwe wakutshwa ebukosini ngu Bowker umpati
wamapolisa. Kwakukankanywe nokuba i-Government ikupe u-Mr Probart ukuba
aye kuba yiyona Mantvi inkulu yezo zizwe, eya kuzityilela ukupatwa eziza
kupatwa ngako.

Wati ke efika kona kwakubizwa intlanganiso yesizwe sika Ngangelizwe.
Tabako ngomhla 24 ka December, bababininzi abantu kuyo, bafika kumawaka
amabini abase mahasbeni bodwa, kanti izulu lalilibi. Zati zonke inkosana
ezingabanga nakufikelela ngokwazo zatumela abantu bokuya kuzimela.

emva kokuba u-Mr Probart kuxeliwe into ayivo ngu Mr Wright no Bowker,
usuke wati :—Zinkosi namapakati ase Batenjini ndicele ukuba size
kuhlangana namblanje ukuze ndinazise ilizwi elitunvelwe ngam ngu
Rulumente. Asikuko nokuba kundivuvisile ukunibona ningaka; enditembake
ukuba niya kuhlala nilipulapula ilizwi elivela kwa Rulumente

naba ababeke pezu kwenu. Ndiya nazisa ke ukuba u-Rulumente uyivumele
into eyenziwe ngu Mr Bowker no Wright, yokuba nipatwe ngamangesi. Mna ke
ndize kuquba lomsebenzi sebeuqalile. Lento iveliswe ngumnqweno wenu
wokunga ningapatwa ngokulungeleleneyo ningabi sezandleni zombandezeli
owenza apo atanda kona. Ute lomnqweno wadibana nokungatandi ukuhlala
exaleni lokuba niza kungenelwa zizizwe ezimelene nani. U-Ngangelizwe
kubonakala ukuba ubalekele ehlatini. Ufumane wabaleka kuba amapolisa
abeye kona, ebeya kukangela ubunyaniso benteto ebczivakala,
ekubonakalayo ngoku ukuba bezingenene. Kodwa ke noko u- Rulumente uti
indlu ka Ngangelizwe nabantu bake bafanele ukukuselwe egunyeni lomfo
onje ngaye ukubandezela nangokufeketa ngabafazi abangonanga nto. Kobuya
kubonwe ekuzipateni kwake nokuba abe negunya lokulaula alinikwayo. Ndiya
temba ukuba ngoku niya kuhlala nizinze kungabiko zipitipiti, niqinise
emsebenzini, kuba kaloku niza kupatwa ngohlobo aya kulungiselelwa
ngokufanayo oyinkosi nolihlwempu elidelekileyo. Akuko bani eniya kuhlala
ningcangcazela ngaye, nezizwe ezinirauleyo kuya kuyeka ukunikataza.
Ndiyanazisa kanjalo ukuba upalele umsebenzi ekoloni ezindleleni zomlilo.
Nanamhlanje angati enduluka amadoda awufumane, azuze nomvuzo
ofanelekileyo. Ndiya kulindela ke ukuba ngoku nibe ngabuntu
abaubinqeleyo umsebenzi, niyikonze ngokufanelekileyo i- Government ezo
kuninceda mhla nibe nixakekile.

REV. GEORGE T. PERKS. M. A.

Kulenyanga ifileyo omnye wamadoda amakulu ake asityelelela ngu Rev. G.
T. Perks. Lomfo ngomnye wabafundisi abakulu beeala lase Wesileni. Ubeke
engumongameli wentlanganiso enkulu yabafundisi belocala pesheya. Ngoku
ungomnye wezandla zalontlanganiso. Apa uze kukangela ukuhamba komsebenzi
welizwi lika Tixo, nokubana iremente zelilizwe zinganakona ukubaxasa
abafundisi bazo. Uhamba ke lomfo ngomcimbi ongako ukuba mkulu nokuba
mhle. Sivatemba ukuba ukusityelelela kwake kuya kuvelisa iziqamo
ezikulu, kuvuselele ukuhamba komsebenzi we Nkosi pakati kwabantsundu
nabamhlope. Kungaba luncedo kumacala onke ukuba aya kuba maninzi amadoda
anjengaye aya kumana ukusityelelela apa, aze kubona intswelo zetu aze
aye kuzibika apo ebevela kona. Baninzi kumazwe apesheya abakumbulela
ukuti apa e-Africa awumkulu kakulu umsebenzi ofuneka ukwenziwa. Sikolwa
ke ukuba ukuza kwabafo abanje ngoRev. Mr. Perks kuya kunelisa ingcinga
ezinjalo. Asinamatanda ngokuti ukuba lapa kwake kuya kuvuyelwa ngawo
onke amakolwa nokuba ngawalipina ihlelo, amhlope nantsundu.

Asinakuzingena zonke indaba zokuhamba kwake' Wamkelwe ngovuyo kuzo zonke
indawo ate wafika kuzo ngabamhlope nabantsundu. Apa e-Lovedale ubeko
ngomhla 7 ka December, wabuya wadlula wasingisa ngase Qonce. Ngomhla 9
uye wafika e- Mkangiso wamkelwa kwangentliziyo evuyileyo kwanjenga
kwezinye indawo. Ute akuba eke wakangela isikolo sakona waleseshelwa
ipepa elixela ukuhamba komsebenzi kaTixo e-Mkangiso, nokuba eso sisikolo
esidala esabako ngokusebenza kuka Rev. W. Shaw, nokuba omnye wabafundisi
baso bokuqala ngu Rev. W. Impey: okunye kulapo kumiswe kona
isishicilelo, apo sekuko nemashini ewukaulezisayo umsebenzi, ede
yenjenjalo ngokukodwa kwi Testamente Entsha. (Batsbo bampa enve yazo ezo
Testamente). Usuke u-Mr. Perks wabulela indawo ezitetiweyo zibekiswa
kuye, wabalisa nangokuhamba kwelizwi pesheya kolwandle, wabulela kuba
efumana uhamba ngokunje ukutandeka umsebenzi.

Asinakuzitini indaba zokuhamba kwalomfundisi nenkosikazi yakwake.
Sesofumana sigqibe ngeliti anga amazwi ahambe cwateta kwindawo ngendawo
angaba nje ngembewu ewele emhlabeni olungileyo eya kuvelisa
izixakato—yanga Inkosi ingaba naye kuzo zonke indawo, imgcine eluhambeni
lwake, imnike impumelelo emsebenz.ini wake awutunyiweyo ide isike
imgoduse; kuze ngehambo ayibambaya kuncedakale intsizana zelilizwe
lakowetu, kudumiseke igama lalowo ate ukuteta ngaye xa ase Mkangiso
wayipeta inteto yake ngamaz.wi ati “Kulowo uhleli pezulu kwayo iterone,
nakuyo imvana, makubeko umbongo, nembeko, namandla, kudo kube
ngunapakade.” Isitvilelo. v. 13.

UKUVULWA KWE TYALIKE
KWELAMAMFENGU.

Ngomhla wa 25, ka November savuka apa Egcuwa, saya e-Mpukane ekuvulweni
kwendlu ye Tyalike yakona, esizweni sika Skerew sahlangana nenkosi u-
Blyth, isiza ngenye indlela, sasesi galeleka kunye Safika intle indlu
leyo ilungisiwe kakuhle, ipakamile kanjalo, ine veranda ngamacala onke.
Enye into eyabamandi yeyokubayenziwe sisandla semfengu yonke, kanti
umntu akangetsho eyibonile.

Into eyafuna ukusidanisa yabanye asabona bantu abangangokwezinye izindlu
esike sazivula kweli lizwe noko bepelele abakwa Skerew u-Ntandatu, no
Bulube, nenye inkosi endikohlileyo igama layo laliqelana elivela kwa
Feltman nasesi kolweni nakwa Gcume.

Kanti lisiko lento into mhla iza kulunga, ike ikataze ukuqala. Ndati nam
ngokuba benditandazile kwasekaya andatandabuza ngoncedo lwe Nkosi
ndatemba kakulu.

Into yokuqala sangena senza iculo, satandaza kwafundwa indawo encwadini
ngu Rev. J. Mahonga ndaza ndaxela ukuba indlu ka Tixo siyivulile iyindlu
yeremente yama Wesile, ndaxela ukuba pezu kwemisebenzi ebisenziwa
zizandla zabantu bakwa Skerew bagaule basika inca, batuta amatye, benza
zonke izinto ezibezifuneka bezenza. Kekaloku pezu kwaleyo imizamo kuseko
ityala le £40 Os. 6d. imali yezinto ebezitengiwe, neyomaki neyokutenga
imiti nezi konkwane nento zonke ebezifuneka ; ndacela u-Captain Blyth
ukuba abe ngumpati wentlanganiso.

Yangena ke inkosi leyo yateta kakulu, yenza amazwi amahle, endati
ndakucinga ndabona ukuba u-Tixo uwatandile amamfengu lento awamiseleyo
i-Mantyi enje ukulunga ekwaziyo ukuyala abantu ngokungati ngabantwana
bayo.

Kwateta u-Rev. Mahonga no Skerew no Samuel Mzamo umvangeli wase
Zazulwana, nabanye, kwabe kuposwa imali kwati ke kwakupela yona kwa
ngenwa ezigusheni kwarolwa zona, kwati noko pakati sanga sifuna
ukunqnmama kwako amandla amana ukupuma pantsi, wati nobenikele wapinda
ngokwesibini nangokwesitatu, kwada kwabonakala ityala lipelile.
Kwabekuxelwe kakulu kulungisiwe, satya sonke nenkosi ezo yaba yimivuyo
kakulu ngenxa yendawo yokuba indlu ka Tixo siyivulile ingenatyala.

Ndikohliwe kukuteta okuninzi ngokuba ndingayikubalisa ngamazwi ayetetwa
ngayo lomini, nditemba ukuba noko amadoda ayeko, nalawo aye engekakolwa,
ayakuti kuyo aqale ukutabata ukucinga ngawo umsebenzi we Nkosi kude
kubeko abayakugqoboka.

Mandipele apo ndawabulela kakulu amandla ka Tixo.

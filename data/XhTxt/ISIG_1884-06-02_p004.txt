IZINTO NGEZINTO.

Intlanganiso yama Rabe ase South Africa, eyamashumi omabini anantlanu
ibihlangene e Bayi ngalo nyaka. Abafundisi bodwa ababeko baba ngapezulu
kwamashumi omatatu, abangabapuma kwityalike ngetyalike zase Rabe.

Isihlalo sagcinwa ngu Rev. B. E. Anderson wase Oudtshoorn, owayitabata
ngentliziyo evumayo indawo leyo yashiywa ngum’fi u Rev. J. Hoyle wase
Kapa, owayenyuIwe nyakenye ukuba abe ngumgcini sihlalo.

Kute ke emva kokuba esitatile isihlalo eso kwanyulwa u Rev. R. Birt wase
Ncemera ukuba ngalo nyaka nozayo abe nguye oya kugcina isihlalo, no Rev.
H. Kayser wase Hankey ukuba abe ngolandela umgcini sihlalo, atate indawo
yake ukupela konyaka ozayo. I Rafu lanyulwa ukuba libe yindawo
yentlangano ezayo, no Rev. R. J Jarvis ukuba abe nguye oya kushumayela
ngalo ntlangano. Kwangenwa ekukangelweni kwamapepa oviwo lwamadodana
amabini antsundu, yavakaliswa ke ingxelo engawo. Enye ayifikelelanga
kanye kwindawo ebekukangelwe kuyo, kwaza ke kwagqitywa kwelokuba ike ibe
ihambisa ngezifundo zayo isenza nomsebenzi olunge nokushumayela. Enye
yawo u Mr. Simon P. Sihlali, ongowokuqala kumadodana antsundu
owapumelela oluviwo kutiwa yi Matriculation, ubequba izifundo zake
ngokwake e Rafu eshumayela nakwi ramente ya Besutu apo, wamkelelwe ke
yena ebufundisini, ubekwe izandla kule veki idlulileyo.

Kwakuba lapa kuvuswe ingxoxo eyaka yenziwa nyake­nye : Engokwakiwa
kwesikolo esoba sesokuquba abafundela ubufundisi nokuba bubuvangeli,
sabamhlope kunye naba- mnyama. Nyakenye kwakuyekelwe ekutini koka
kulingwe i Lovedale mayelana nalondawo, ukuze i Free Church ne U nited
Presbyterian ke ziqube kunye, kunyulwe namadoda awoyikangelela londawo.
U Rev. T. Durant Philip, B.A., wase Rafu usuke wabika ukuba indawo leyo
isafumaneka iqubeka kakuhle, nentlanganiso zabafundisi ezisemva pesheya
zalamahlelo zivumelene kwelokuba ibe lelo hlelo libe nendoda efundisayo
apo e Lovedale, ze abafundi bangene kwi Klasi zabo bobatatu. Ndawana
ingekacaci iseyeyokuba abantsundu abafundela ubufundisi bafundiswe na
ezinteto zasemzini ezinjengozi Latini ? Kuba nakuba amanje la wona
amahlelo engakoliswa yindawo yokuba ziyekwe, yona, i Union ke, ayiboni
ngozi kulonto. Ngelaxa u Rev. T. D. Philip wayeye pesheya wahlanganisa
amakulu amane eponti (£400) wawafeza ukuwahlanganisa e England, kanti ke
noko kunganqweneleka amawaka amabini pambi kokuba kubeko into
enokwenziwa. Noko ke ngenxa yobunje bamaxesha, akukabiko nto yona ade
amabandla akweli lizwe enza yona. Ite nemalana ebingene ngezinye
yaditywa kwakule nayo. Amadoda lawo ke ayenyulelwe ukuyikangelela
lendawo asacinga ezinye indlela zokungenisa imali, ukuze inge
iyakauleziswa lendawo.

Wati emva kokuba u Rev. R. Birt owayenyulelwe ukugcina isihlalo
esitatile, u Rev. T. D. Philip wakankanya imvuselelo ekoyo kwabantsundu
e Rafu kususela kwixa awayekona u Rev. J. Mountain. Uqube ngokuncoma
wenjenjalo wada wakankanya nendawo yokuba amaqwela kona asele kwi 1,400,
into ke leyo ayincome ukulunga.

Kuveliswe kananjalo nendawo yokwenziwa isicelo ku Rulumente sokuba
kuvakaliswe njengase Natal ukuba kungabiko nto ibutywala itengiswayo
kwabantsundu. Isi­celo eso ke ebebesebete basilungiselela.

Rabe.

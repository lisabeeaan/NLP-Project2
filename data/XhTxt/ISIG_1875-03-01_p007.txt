ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1875.

Ubube ngomhla wesine ka January, yinto- kazi ete ngomhla wesibini
wenyanga, igula, yabuza ukuba upina unyana wam omkulu? Kwatiwa akako.
Yati hambani niye kumbiza, baza bafika esifa naye unyana, waselesusa
unyana wake naye ukuya kubona unina kulu lowo, usukile ke u-George
Masoka lowo ungumzukulwana, efikile ubuye wabuza ukuba akakafikina
unyana wam omkulu? Kwatiwa hai kufike u- mzukulwana wako, ngokuba uyafa
yena. Wati- ke kulungileke uze uqubele uyihlo amazwi am ngawola.—

“ Ndiyagodukake namhlanje ndiwugqibile um- sebenzi wam emhlabeni apa.
Kwakutiwe kum, ezulwini kusekaya lam ndalipulapula elozwi, ndalamkela,
ke ndinovuyo olukulu lompefumlo warn, ndibendingumoni kodwa ndenziwa
ngcwe- le. Kulusizi ukuba ndingena xesha lokuteta.— Ilizwi endinishiya
nalo apa nditi ize nitandane nigcinane bantwana bam, indawo eyakubanga
ukuba nitandane kukuba nikonze U-Tixo, nikolwe ku Yesu Kristu,
naniyakuhlala ngomanyano, ukuba umoya_(;)wenu uyaninqinela.” Zihlobo,
hai amazwi amandi atetwa ngongafundileyo, ngowa se Lutukela.

Nkosi sendivingcele abanye. Hlala kuhle Mhleli nabantwana bako bonke
abatabata elipepa.

JAMES ZWAARTBOOY.

Mission Station, Herschel February 18,1875.

Kuwe Mnumzetu Mhleli Wesigidimi.

Ndinga vuya nkosi yam, undi fakele lama- zwana ambalwa. Into endiya
kuteta ngayo yile. Sibesipiwe imvula. Yona asiyiyo nayo. Kute ngomhla 17
ka February, kwa fika into ye nkumbi; zati ukufika kwazo zenza into
endingazange ndiyibone; kukuqala ndizibona ezingako. Ndati mna ukusuka
kwam, ukuba ndibe ndise sikolweni sabantwana, nda kuti ukupumela pandle
ndabona umzi wakuti use ugutyungelwe zinkumbi; ndaqala ukubona ukuba
kunamhla nje sitshabalele; ndakuqala ukususa abantwana ukuti mabaye
kuncedisa abanye, ndixela abazali babo. Ndesuka nam ndakugqita
evenkileni ka Mr Jones, ndabona ukuba nalapo ndiyayo, Emasimini andisayi
kuwu wela umlanjana ekutiwa ngu Nongongomana uku fikela emasimini
okuqala; ndafumana ukuba umbona ka Sanna Lethlaka use upelile kanti
waulikaba elihle; ndakufika kwe yam, hai; ndabona kungekabi njalo,
zikona kodwa, kusa lungile. Hai sancedwa sazikupa emasimini etu.
Lakutshona ilanga, seva enye imbali ifika no David Phori, esiti kwawakwa
Sitoli, enye yawo akuseko nto. Ndixela nje U-Sanna, umka Lethlaka,
akuseko nto no Andries Leanka no Andries Tsufu, nam kwenye yamasimi am,
idliwe njalo zabuqa. Ke azikapeli noko, siva ukuba kungona zizayo nga
Mandla; ke zihlobo zam tina ngenxa yako oko sesibekise enkosini; ukuba
ingumsikeleli kwezinjalo intshaba. Azisa yoyiki ne- mvula kuba kungona
zipapayo. Andipele ngeliti noko kunjalo Inkosi mayidunyiswe, ngokuba
yona ayi livimba, njengati sivimbayo, kuba tina bantu singamavimba.

  BENJAMIN MOYEDI.

UMANYANO LWEREMENTE

YABANTSUNDU.

  Uitenhage, January 20, 1875.

Mnumzetu ungadinwa kukuhlala ukatazwa siti, noko mna ndiqalayo ukubala
kuba ndise sehlatini andikapumi. Ndikatazwa zizilo zam, ziti make
nditete.

I-Bayi ne Uitenhage, zihlangene ngemiteto ukuze zibe nye zitsale kunye
ngantambo nye, lube lunye nolaulo Iwazo. Ati amaremente ase Uiten­hage
Amarabe nama Wesile, ahlangana axoxa

ngesimo abakuso bona bantu bantsundu, ukunga- hlangani kwabo, baeinga
ngokuti kufanele ukuba bona bantu bantsundu babe banye. Batsho besiti
pakati kwentlanga ezintsundu kuseko ucuku nokuketana nokungatandani. Ke
koba kade kangakanani na sizintsali. Ngokukodwa kuti bantu bakolwayo,
ngumzekelo mnina esiya kuwunikela elizweni," kanti kutiwa abahedeni boba
lilifa letu bakolwayo. Bayasibona ke nabo ukuba sahluke- ne. Kuko Umrabe
nom Wesile nom Tshatshi. Bayasihleka, besiti nokuti bangapina O-Tixo?
Kubangwa yinina oko? Kukwahlukana kwetu. Sisilibele isiteto so Msindisi
wetu esiti, bayakuna- zi ngokutandana kwenu ukuba ningabadisipile bam.

Pulapula ke mleseshi. Kute ngomhla wokuqala ka January iremente yase
Uitenhage yahlangana neyase Bayi. Ite eyase Uitenhage yakupa amadoda
amabini ukuba aye kweyase Bayi, ahlangane nayo kuxoxwe indawo yokuba
mababe banye. Iremente yase Uitenhage yaqala ngekoleki, yayinika lomali
kulomadoda atunyiweyo, yati, ntozakowetu hambani nisidla namahashe enu.
Ahamba ke lomadoda aya agaleleka E-Bayi. Hai nawase Bayi kwafikwa
selengati ayipupile. Safi- ka kumatsha ntliziyo apetwe zinto zo Hani
nento zo Vandala nezo Mavundla. Sasifake kunene ingwevu zakowetu zase
Wesile nezase Tshatshi, apo kuhleli O-Ranuga nento zo Mcuce nezo Mdingi
no Simane no Tywetywe. Ayingenisa ke amadoda ase Uitenhage into eze
ngayo. Zabona- kala sezibeke indlebe into zo Ndungane nezo Mza- mo, xa
abantwana babo behlangene ngokufuna ukuba banye. Avakala amadoda ase
Bayi eyibulela lento. Akubona ukuba okunene sahlukene tina ziremente
zabantsundu, kufuneka intlanganiso yalento. Atike niti mayitiwe nina
lento. Ati siti masihlangane sonke endaweni etile, emva apo sipuma kona.
Kukuyilwa kwentlanganiso yabantsundu ke oko.

Wena, Mhleli, ndiyakwazi ukuba ungumfo otanda udaba, mhlaumbi ungatanda
ukuwazi namadoda abetunywe li Uitenhage. Bekususwe umfo ka Mahlasela
noka Hoha.

Into enkulu luhlanga, Iwakowetu olutshabalalayo. Akuko nto
ingasibulaliyo. Sibulawa nayi Kofu, nangamazimba, nazintombi zetu; kanti
kunjenje kungokungahlangani. Make sihlangane sonke sikangele into
esingavumelana ngayo ibe yeyo hlanga, mhlaumbi ibe yeyeremente zonke.
Ngokuhlangana kwetu kobako mhlaumbi amadoda anomoya ka Tixo ayakuvelisa
izinto eziluncedo.

Injalo ke into esiyi zamayo apa. Kuwe Mhleli ndingakucela ukuze
ungandideli ngokupitizela kwam; xa nditeta ngalento ndisike ndikohlwe
nakukumisa inyawo.

JONAS HOHA.

IZINTO.

INKUMBI zisa xalisile ngase Queenstown.

UKULA kuko apo luya kuda luncanyelwe nonya- ka nje.

IPARLAMENTE yale koloni iya kuhlangana ngomhla 14 ka April.

E-RINI ngomhla ka 18 March kuya kutengiswa imihlaba yeziza emibini.

UMBONISO—E-Queenstown ngomhla 21 ka March kuya kubako umboniso wezilimo
nempahla ehambayo.

E-TRANSVAAL ngelixesha U-President Burgers asahambele pesheya ubukosi
buyakuba bapatwa ngu Mr. Joubert.

OZIBULELEYO—Umfo abati ngu William Moo­ham ute esemkombeni watsibela
emanzini ngabom watshona kwapela.

UKUFIKA kuka Sobantu E-Natal kubeko aba- mzileleyo ukubonisa ukuba
bebengafuni ukuba abuye abuyele kona.

INDLELA YENQWELO ZOMILLO ihanjiswa ngoku- tandekayo kanye pakati kwe
Qonce ne Monti. Baya ncoma bonke abake baya kona.

KWELE DIAMOND umgcini wetolongo unikwe iminyaka emihlanu etolongweni
esebenza nzima ngokusuka ebe imali yomntu obesetongweni.

IMBANDEZELO—Ipepa lase Diamond Fields liti E-Transvaal uti ukuba umntu
ontsundu uke walala pantsi komti xa ahlwelwe endleleni adliwe iponti.

ILITYE LOKUSILA—Siya vuya ukuva ukuba indawo ezazonakaliswe yimvula
elityeni lokusila lase Beaufort lika Mr Ainslie sezilungisiwe ngoku
selisila.

UKUKABA—Ngase Kapa kuko umfo okabe omnye waya kubeteka emtini ngentloko
wafa kwaoko. Umkabi lowo use tolongweni ngoku ulinde U-Mgwebi.

E-NATAL ubukosi buya kuba bupatwa ngu Colonel Montgomery ngelixesha
ingekafiki I- Governor entsha ezakupata lokoloni ibe sendaweni ka Sir
Benjamin Pine.

KWELE GOLIDE kuvakala ukuba kuko abayi- fumanayo kakulu, kodwa inxenye
ibuya ingama- hlwempu angapezulu kwamhla yaya. Kulungele abantu abaya
sebenemali kakade.

UNONTONGWANE kunconywa ukuba asikuko nokuba mbi ezinqwelweni. Imantyi
yase Beau­fort itumele kwi government ukuba londlela ngelike ivalwe
okwenyanga ezimbini ide isetyenzwe.

ISIKOLO SASE LOVEDALE sivulwe ngomhla 2 ku February. Abantwana
abangeneyo bakuma- shumi amatandatu xa sesiquka nabamhlope. Sivuya
kakulu kubona abazali abantsundu bekuqabukela ukufundisa nentombi zabo.

ABASEBENZI abebeye kufunwa kwele Diamond ukuza kusebenza indlela
zenqwelo zomlilo, esasite ngalenyanga idluleyo bahambe bekataza ukuhla
kwabo, nanamhlanje abakakuyeki ukwenza izipitipiti nalapo basebenza
kona.

UMGCINI TOLONGO wase Mnyameni (Alex­andria) ongu D. Carnavon ufike kona
ngosuku 26 ku February, wangena emsebenzini wake ngomhla 27. U-Mr. W. N.
Somngesi ubuyele kwase mse- benzini wake wokuba ngumkumsheli.

OBETWE NGUMBANE —Umbalelani wetu ose Falconer (Engcwazi) uti, “ Indaba
ezikoyo apa kute ngomhla we Nkosi, February 14, umfazi omkulu ka Gwali
Njamela, ehleli endlwini yake nabanye abafazi wabetwa ngumbane wafa kwa
oko.

ISIFO SAMAHASHE siva ukuba kuko esesike sa- wabulala kulo Mahlulo
nangase Qonce. Kuvakala kanjalo ukuba iko kakulu Iglanders. Om­nye umfo
ute esand’ ukulitenga ihashe ngeponti ezimashumi mabini ananye kwafuneka
ukuba makalidubule.

UMBUZO—Yiyipi na into engabanceda ngapezulu abantsundu kuko ukuzinikela
ekulimeni, kuko ukuzinikela ekufuyeni? Angapendula onako, ati ukuze
impendulo yake ibe noncedo kwabanye azivelise izizatu ezimbange ukuba
abe kwicala alitetelelayo.

ABENQENA IMFUNDO—Ititshari etile yase Queenstown ite isahambele endaweni
yazalelwa ngumlambo. Endaweni yokuba zibe lusizi intsapo zisale zivuma
ingoma eti—Yizalele ititshari mlambo ungabohli, ungake uvume ukuba
sihlangane nayo, zala mlambo, sincede usive.

SIVA UKUBA U-MR. LEVEY obesisandla nekumsha lika Captain Blyth pesheya
kwe Nciba ukutshelwe kwizizwe ezingasentla kwa Mamfengu apo aya kuzuza
£500 ngomnyaka. Siya vuya ukukuva ukunyuswa kwake kuba engumfo osebenze
imisebenzi emihle wayi quba ngohlobo olutandekayo apo ebekona. Umnqweno
wetu ngowokunga angati oya kulandela abe sendaweni yake abe nalontliziyo
yobepambi kwake.

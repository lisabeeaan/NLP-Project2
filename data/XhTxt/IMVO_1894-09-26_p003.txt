LUHLOBO LUNINA AMABAQUBE NGALO?

Yintonina amabajonge yona? Uyavumelana ne Nkulu kweliti into amabajonge
yona kukuba bangavuli tuba ukuba upalale umzi kulemihlaba, bangavumeli
abamhlope ababuhlwempu ukuba bange­ne pakati kwabantsundu, kodwa lemizi
igeinwe njengamadam abayakumana ukufumana kuwo abasebenzi abayakuba
luncedo lokuhluma kwe Koloni. Uyakumbula kakuhle ukuba ngo 1892 wenyuka
waya apo e Glen Grey, waza e Queenstown, kwinteto awayenza edinaleni
ebalulekileyo kona, wateta amazwi ati, lomcimbi banokuwuvelela ngenkalo
ezimbini, enye yeyokuba uqutywe ngendle­la yokubacitana kanye abantu
kwezondawo nokuba yeyokubagcinela zona. Okwesake isiqu wati, uvumelana
nokuba lemihlaba igcinelwe abantu. Wagxwagxushwa kunene ngokusuke atete
ngolo­hlobo oko. I Cape Argus ne Ons Land zeza nzima pezu kwake, kodwa
namhla uyavuya ukuboua ukuba lonqubo uyakolwayiyo umzi, yaye Inkulu
ibambelele kunene kuyo. Kuko indawo abona ingeko Emtetweni
engeyingenisiwe, ye­yokuba umhlaba wedlelo upatiswe i Bhodi ze Lokeshoni
ukuba ziwugcinele abantu. Abo ke banento nabamnyama abanakuba
bangabuboni ubukulu belo nqaku. Lonto ayingonakalise nto enqubeni
yomteto, ngalo lonke ixesha kugqitywe indawo yento oyakwenziwa yona
umhlaba ongaliny wayo, kumiselwe uku­ba kungenziwa nto ngawo ngapandle
kokuba kubeko oliliso lika Rulumeni. Umhlaba ongapandle kowedlelo
bayalungisa ukuba bawabe ube ngamasimi kodwa ngendlela engenandleko
inkulu abanokuyifumana. Akasakungena elucukwini ngalondawo, kuba Inkulu
seyite ivisisene nabo bayakuwuquba Umteto lo abate ke bayaleza amaqoudo
abekwe pambi kwabo. Kodwa, nangapandle kokuba ade aqayise ngokwake
ukuqonda pezu kokwabo, ngati ngekubeko indlela elula, engenazindleko
yokugeinwa kwamagama ababelwe imihlaba kwi ofisi ze mantyi kunale
yalatwe ngu Mteto. Kuba inabakumbule ukuba indleko yczitaitile iyakuba
yenkulu kunene. Iponti ezintlanu ngendleko yo Nocanda igqite le lonto
kwindleko yemfanelo enokutwala yimihlatyana engako, yaye nemali
eyakurafwa ngonyaka inzima kunene : ati akongezelela neyokutshintshwa
kwamagama kuyakubonakala ukuba ababantu babahlaulisa nzima kunene
ngemihlatyana engepi. Ucinga ukuba xa bayilungisileyo lonto kuyakufuneka
benze amacebo okuvalelwa ukupulukwa kwabantu ngumhlaba, kuba asele kulo
Mteto awanele, Bekungafuneki ukutengiswa nje nakubanina, kanjalo
mauugahlutelwa izikweliti umhlaba. Abayituni into yokuba umboleki
ngemali awuhlute umhlaba abemi kuwo ababantu. Bengayivelelanga londawo,
bayakubanyauzela ebuhlwempini bokugqibela abantu, yaye yonke into
abebezaina yona ingasakupumelela. Mabafake inqaku Emtetweni eliyakwenza
ukuba lonto inqabe ngokupindapindiweyo. Abangefumane, bengamadoda ateta
into yengqondo bavakalise uloyiko loku­ba bayakuba benza umteto
wocalulo, kuba wonke lo Mteto into oyiyo ungowocalulo, yaye irafu
yomsebenzi iyinto yocalulo ebuhlungu kunene, ke ngoko akunakuyekwa

INKULULEKO KA CETYWAYO.

Waya kwase Oude Molen ekubuyeni kwake Pesheya u Cetywayo, kude kufezwe
zonke izinto ekwaku nqweneleka zifeziwe pambi kokuba abuyele kwelake.
Ngo 12 December, ucingo lusibikele ukuba ezindawo zifinyeziwe, nokuba
into leyo yazisiwe kwelo tshawe. Izimeko abuyela pantsi kwazo kwa Zulu
siva ukuba zanekiwe pambi kwake wazamkela ngemvume kwi Botwe lase Kapa.
Ingaba sishici- lela nje usendleleni yokusinga e Port Dunford, eli
bayana lise Iwancibeni Iwakwa Zulu. Apo wohlangatyezwa ngongamehlo
enkundla ya Pesheya kwa Zulu u Mr. Osborne, kunye neqela lama scldati
awoti ampeleke ukumsa Ondini, ibotwe lakwa Zulu.

Amalinga okukululwa kwalo mnumzana, mabe bayazi abafundi betu, ukuba
ngaka Bishop Colenso, lomhlobo mkulu wabamnyama ungadinwayo kukusimela.
Litamsanqa letu ukuba besinelungelo lokuwalandela amaqinga ka Dr.
Colenso pakati kwalo lonke ixesha abezama ukufumanela eli tshawe
inkululeko. Lencwadi ivela kweso sicaka so Sombawo, ibalelwe indodana
elapa elibalelana lidala no Bishop, yofundwa ngemihlali ngumzi omnyama.

Bishopstowe, Nov. 14, 1882.

NKOSI YAM ETANDEKAYO,

Utyelelo, kufupi nje, lomhlobo wetu u Rev. E. Solomon, lundikumbuze
ukuba kanene andikayi penduli incwadi yako eyab'alwa kudala ngo Oct. 16,
1880, endite, kodwa, ndaseloko ndiyi londoloze emqulwini wencwadi
endimelwe kukuzipendula kwakufumaueka ituba. Kususela kwelo xesha, into
eninzi yemicimbi, yasekaya, nebalulekileyo, ngokukodwa imicimbi
yekumkani yakwa Zulu, ibete yalidla lonke ixesha lam, ngangokude
ndingabi nakuyi pendula incwadi yako.

Kodwa ngoku ndikubulela ngentliziyo enzulu, ngamazwi owabalileyo egameni
lako, nase gameni labanye bohlanga Iwakowenu, awandifumana ngexesha
kanye endandiwafuna ngalo, ndipakati kwemizamo ngo Cetshwayo, edubule
ngoku—sitemba—ekubuyiselweni kwilizwe lake.

Nokuba luhlobo lunina lokucaswa, nokubanjezelwa, nokudaniseka, engati
sidibane nalo kumalinga etu okufumanela imfanelo zabo abo bangabantu
kunye nati, siyazi ukuba sino Tile kwicala letu, Olumke kwanjengokuba
elungile, oya kusebenza ezake izigqibo ngeyake mdlela,—

  U Tixo we Nyaniso, cngenayo inkohlakalo, Ufanelekile, Ulungile Yena.

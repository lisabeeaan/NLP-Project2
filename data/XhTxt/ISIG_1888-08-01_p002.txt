INTLANGANISO EYI NGQUNGQUTELA YABA FUNDISI.

NGOWAMASHUMI omabini anesine namhla umnyaka imana dbako e-Qonce
lentlanganiso iyingqungqutela yaba Fundisi. Nje ngesiko layo, ihlangene
ngentsaaa yolwe-Sine, ngomhla we 5 ku July, beliqola elimnandi
abafundisi abayizileyo bamahlelo ngamahlelo. Umongameli wayo ngu Rev.
RICHARD BIRT, ingwevu eseliminyaka imashumi mahlanu yafikayo kweli lizwe
pakati kwe Midange, eaele namhla ilalise e-Ncemera. Yakuba ivulwe
ngomtandazo, umonga- meli ucele ingxelo-micimbi, efundwe ke ngu Rev.
JOHN D. DON, u-Nobala wayo.

Ingxoxo ebe namandla yepatelele ku Selo lo Tywala, ekufumaneke ukuba obu
babantsundu ngumdungudelisi omkulu otintela ukuhambela pambili kwe
Lizwi. Kule ngxo- xo bate bavelela ubunqina obufezekileyo bokuba utywala
babantsundu bukokelela ngqokobo-Mlungu i-Brandi, noku- ba kanjako
bugqitisele ngoku ukuselwa kunakoko bebu- njalo kumaxesha angapambili,
yabe se ilisiko elandileyo ngoku ukubuxuba obu bemitombo ne brandi.
Kumiswe i-Komiti yaba Fundisi abantsundu ukuba bab'ale incwadi
eyakutunyelwa kwi Remente zakowabo, itete ngeziganeko ezibi zo Tywala
bama-Xosa. Kuvunyelwene kanjako ukuba ingayekwa imigudu eyenziwayo,
yokulwa nokwanda kwe ntengiso ye Brandi; ukubongoza i-Palamente ukuba
ivale inkanti, nokuncipisa ngerafu ezipakamileyo amalungelo akoyo ngoku
okuzuzwa ngokusisulu kotywala base Mlu- ngwini.

Kuleswe amapepa ngamapepa avelisa amacebo okuqu- tywa komsebenzi
kwizipaluka zawo zonke. Pakati kwalo mapepa elibalulekileyo libe
lelifundwe ngu Miss STURROCK, umfundisikazi wase Ncemera, elixoxe ngo “
Msebenzi opa- kati kwa Bafazi ne Ntombi.” Kuvunyelwene yintlangani- so
yaba Fundisi ekubeni makubeko u Manyano lwama Kristukazi, oluyakuti lube
nentlanganiso yalo ; nokwenzi- •wa kwama “ Kaya ” amankazana ezidolopini
ayakugcina ubuhle besimilo. Kumiswe i-Komiti yaba Fundisikazi abango
Miss STURROCK, e-Ncemera, Mrs. MUIRHEAD, e- Lovedale, Miss BLAIR,
e-Mgqakwebe, Miss BOND, e-Nxaruni. Mrs. HARPER no Mrs. ROPER, e-Qonce,
ukuba avane namanye amanenekazi kwezinye indawo ngalomsebenzi.

Amanye amapepa afundiweyo atete nge “ Ntlalo yaba- Ntsundu ezi Dolopini”
nge “ Zikula ze Sabata,” nge “ Cebo Lokuyolisa Izikungo,” ne “ Ndlela
Yokuncedisa kwama Kristukazi e-Lizwini ” elifundwe ngu Rev. P. J. MZIMBA
—nesilishieilela intloko zalo namhlanje.

Owona mcimbi ubalulekileyo, we Ngqungqutela ube ngowo kudunyiswa kwe
Jubili yo Mongameli wayo u Rev. RICHARD BIRT esibala ngayo komnye
umhlati wesi Sigidi- mi.

Akulityalwanga kanjako ukukuzwa kompanga welungu layo, nombali
weyangapambili iminyaka, u-Rev. JOHN A. CHALMERS.

Kunyulwe u-Hon. CHARLES BROWNLEE ukaba aze onga- mele eyonyaka ozayo
i-Ngqungqutela, kwenziwa nezinye indawa-ndawana, kwaqukunjelwa.

INDLELA ANGATI AMAKRISTUKAZI ANCEDI-

SE NGAZO UMSEBENZI WELIZWI.

YINDAWO enkulu kakulu le, ebesifanele sonke ukuyitanda, kuba umsebenzi
we Lizwi wohambiseleka pambili ikakulu ngokuti sisebenzise kuwo izandla
u-Tixo azibeke kuti.

Ilizwi eli yinto ebukekayo, libukeka ikakulu xana liku- mntu oyinkazana.
Amankazana ngabantu abanoluvo olu- namandla, abalamkela ilizwi
ngentliziyo.

1.  Abafazi belilizwe bafanele kakulu ukulibulela ilizwi lika Tixo.
    Libakulula becinezelwe pantsi nzima yintlalo

yobuhedeni. Ubuhedeni kuwo onke amazwe nakuzo zonke intlanga
buyamcinezela umfazi, kodwa ubuhedeni bentlanga zasema-Xoseni umfazi
bumtwalisa intsizi nenkatazo ezipa- ntse ukugqita nakwezo buhedeni base
India. Intombi ibinyanzelwa ngegunya ukuba yendele kwi xeketi lomfo
engamtandiyo, mhlaumbi kwisilima, mhlaumbi kwinkatavu yexego, nokuba
sel’ileli nemibimbi, lizikewu, lizincwe, name- hlo sel’esoloko elila
ngati anecapoti; ib’inyanzelwa ngo- mnquma inzwakazana nguyise,
nabanakwayo, kuba eloxego linenkomo. Namhla intombi zendela kwinzwana
ezitandwe zizo. Omnquma wenduku wapuliwe kukukanya kwe Lizwi. Abafazi
bebenkulwa ngendukwazana izolo nemihla ; kude kupume
nemipefumlo—kwabanye ngezizatwana ezideleki- leyo kakulu. Ilizwi
libakulule kakulu.

2.  Abantu abati badle ngokulamkela msinya ilizwi lika Tixo
    ngamankazana. Kwa kwimini zaba Postile, xa babe- lishumayela
    ezintlangeni zabahedeni bekudla ngokuti lamkelwe ngabafazi kuqala,
    amadoda alandele. Nanje- ngalamini o-PAULOS, e-Filipi, babemana
    beshumayela kuba- fazi ngase mlanjeni waza U-LYDIA walamkela ilizwi.
    Ngoko ke ama-Kristukazi afanele ukuncedisa emsebenzini welizwi kuba
    kudla ngokukolwa abafazi kuqala.

3.  Abafazi bafanele ukuvunyelwa basebenze emsebenzini welizwi ngokuba
    nyabona baninzi ezirementeni. Kanikangele ezityalikeni, elona qela
    lininzi lelabafazi, kanikangele kumalungu eremente elona qela
    lininzi lelabafazi; kanika- ngele kumabuzwa nakusapo lwezikula
    ze-Sabata elona qela lininzi lelentombi, kanide nikangeie nasezi
    kuleni zemini, kwimizi ngemizi, ukuba nakona elona qela lininzi
    asililo na elamantombazana ?

Kufanelekile na, kulungile na, ukuba umhlambikazi owona ’mninzi U-KRISTU
YESU, i-Nkosi yetu, ewubize ebu- nyameni wawungenisa ebomini
ungasetyenziswa ? Umlilo we-Gospile ka YESU KRISTU owapenjwa nguye,
umelwe kukulitshisa lonke ilizwe lobuhedeni. Bavele e-Yurope abafundisi,
nase Amerika, bepete umlilo we-Gospile. Ama- hlatikazi amnyama ’
obudenge buka Mahomed ayavuta namhla, oda abe lututu; izikunikazi
ezomileyo ezidala zo- tixo wobu-Hindu zingama gqengengqenge kukutshiswa
yi- Gospile,—zoda zipele; amadobokazi amakulu azalise intaba zobudenge
base Afrika—yatsha,—yimililo kwizizwe ngezizwe ehamba ipenjwa
ngabafundisi bale Gospile: kuyafuneka ke kakulu ukuba ati wonke umntu
omamkeleyo U-KRISTU, ancedise ukukaulezisa ivangeli Yake ukuba ifikele
bonke abantu.

    ukongamela intla- nganiso zemitandazo pakati kwe rementekazi yodwa.

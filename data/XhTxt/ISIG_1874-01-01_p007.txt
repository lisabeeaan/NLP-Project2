ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 1, 1874.

  IZINTO NGEZINTO.

E-TRANSVAAL kuvakala ukuba A-Makorane afuna ukwenza inkatazo.

INGQAKAQA.—Umnquba wabantu abebenayo ngase Kapa uselucitiwe, abobantu
bapilile bonke.

UMBANE.—Kwindawo ngendawo zekoloni zinizi izinto nabantu ekuvakala ukuba
benzakaliswe nguwo.

UCINGO ngoku selude Iwaya kufika nase Cradock. Indaba zokuqala ezihambe
ngalo zitunyelwe ngomhla we 3 ka December.

ODLISIWEYO.—kutiwa E-Qonce, kwisikolo sika Brownlee kuko ontsundu osand’
ukudliswa. Izipata mandla zisayi pengulula lonto.

UKUHAMBA KWENQWELO ezituta impahla kuya- nconywa pakati kwe Rini ne
Bayi. Omnye mnfo ubale 280 endleni.

AMAPOLISA.—E-Cradock kufunwa inani elitile lamapolisa amahashe
nawenyawo. Awamahashe arolelwa 6s ngemini, awenyawo zibe ntatu.

UKUPANGWA.—E-Lesseyton ngapaya kwase Queenstown ute umfo ontsundu
esahamba wafikelwa ngabanye abantsundu bebabini bampanga £20, bambeta,
bamshiya besiba ufile.

KWELE GOLIDE.—Inani labantu abasebenza kona abamhlope baku 300,
abantsundu kuma 500. Ukudla akusanqabe kakulu. Igolide isafunyanwa
kunene. Kwele Diamond iseyimirozo abafudukayo.

ABEZE KUSEBENZA.—Ngelixesha U-Mr. Fuller ate waya E-England ukuya kufuna
abantu bokuza kusebenza, abafikileyo sebengapezu kwamakulu amatatu.
Omnye umkombe ozakufika uza nabembi bendlela aba 150.

REV. K. EDWARDS.—Asikuko nokuba silusizi ukuva ukuba Rev. R. Edwards
wase Bayi ude wati ngokungapili, nokuziva ukuba akanakuwufeza umsebenzi
wokukangela iremente yake, wazisa ukuba uyapuma kulomsebenzi.

UKUPANGWA.—Umqubi wenqwelo abati ngu Nel ute xa angase Queenstown
wafikelwa ngama Xosa amabini, ambeta ngesabokwe senkabi, ampanga £75.
Kuko amabini ase tolongweni ngokuranelwa. Kufikwe ene £47 angenakuzixela
kakuhle apo azizuze kona.

ISITYEBI so MSILAMSI.—U-Samodien, isityebi esikulu so Msilamsi,
kuvumelene ne Government ye Free State ukuba ake I-Ofisi zayo ezise
Bloem­fontein. Uyakuzaka nge £17,000, azigqibe ngeminyaka emibini.
Abantu bokuzisebenza, abanje ngabaki, nabacweli, nabenzi bezitena,
uyakubaqesha E-Kapa.

UBUMENEMENE.—Kute E-Richmond, kwezi- ntsuku zisand’ ukudlula kwafika
umfo ontsundu ku Mlungu, wati usand’ ukupuma etolongweni, uswele intwana
zonke, kekaloku ubambekile nasisifo. Ute wacela indawo, watembisa ukuba
angati encediwe kobubunzima akubo abe sisicaka esikolekileyo kanye.
Uvumile ke lo Mlungu, wamnika indawo yokulala. Kute kusisa wabe engaseko
lomntu, ebaleke nesiqingata senxowa yomgubo nezinye izinto.

NGANGELIZWE.—Kuko amare okuba U-Ngange- lizwe usand’ ukubeta omnye
wabafazi bake, wampata ngohlobo awapata ngalo intombi ka Rili. Isizatu,
kutiwa usuke lomfazi watabata isitya abesela kuso U-Ngangelizwe, waya
kuka amanzi. Usuke wasele qumba U-Ngangelizwe, wamlandela umfazi,
wambeta wawa, waduda pezu kwake, wati nokuze angaselemgqibela kwanceda
U-Rev. Mr. Hargreaves. Bati abanye isizwe sake siteta ngokuti sidiniwo
yimikwa yake.

I-KOFU.—E-Nqushwa umfo abati ngu Mr. Roger Foxcroft unemiti yekofu
ehluma emhlabeni wake. Inani layo ngamashumi amabini, iselixakata.
Selevune iponti ezintandatu.

E-BATENJINI, kutiwa bekuko isityebi esitile so Mtembu, kufupi nalendawo
ino Rev. C. Pamla. Ute lomfo kweziveki zisand’ ukudlula wafikelwa
yinkosana yake, yambeta entloko wafa isitulu, yaselimgqibela ngomkonto.
Nonyana omkulu walondoda ubulewe. Izidumba zabo ziposwe eweni ukuze
zidliwe ngamaxalanga. Abafazi nabantwana bayo bagxotelwe endle bcngenako
nokudla. Impahla yayo, inkomo ezise 100, negusha nebokwe eziku 200,
yatinjwa. Kutiwa isizatu sokuba enjiwe njalo kungokuba umana etakata
inkomo zenkosi, ezingenisela idolosi, zibe ezake zingenanto.

ABESUTU ABAZE KUSEBENZA.—Iqela la Besutu elikwi 150 lisand’ ukudlula
E-Rini ukuya kusebenza kwindlela zenqwelo zomlilo ezingase Bayi.
Elonaqela likulu, elikumakulu amane, kulindeleke ukuba lifike kweyase
Monti indlela. Beza bepetwe zinkosana zabo. Kwabaya E-Monti kuko
no­nyana ka Mshweshwe. Asikuko nokuba baya kuba luncedo olukulu, nabo
ngokwenjenjalo basendleleni yokunyuka. Umtombo wobutyebi ngumsebenzi.
Isizwe esinqena umsebenzi kupela ukuba siyakusalela emva. Amaxosa
afanele ukuyipapamela lendawo yomsebenzi. Ngoku aseleza kupangelwa
ngabantu abavela E-Lusutu napesheya kolwandle, abeze kunyuka ngomsebenzi
opambi kwendlu zawo.

INTENGO EZIZA KUBAKO.

Ngo we 12 ka January E-Kenkel Bush kulendlela iya E-Bayi, kuyakutengiswa
inkomo ezi 180, inkabi ezi 150, amahashe ama 64, ibokuva ezintsha ezi 4,
amakuba nezinye impahla zokulima.

Ngo 20 ka January, E-Somerset, kufupi nomhlaba ekutiwa yi Deelkraal,
kuyakutengiswa igusha ezi 2,200, iseyibokwe ezi 600, inkomo ezi 100,
inkabi esezitsala ezi 60, amahashekazi amane, nezi­nye intwanantwana.

E-Alexandria, kufupi nomhlaba ka Mr. Fourie ngomhla wa 20 ka January,
kuya kutengiswa igu- shakazi ezi 300, inqwelo, ne kuba, nempahla zendlu.

Ngo 4 ka February kwindawo ekutiwa yi Schans Kraal kufupi nase Richmond,
kuya kutengiswa igusha ezingapezu kwamawaka amabini, ibokwe ezi 750,
izipani zenkabi ezine, amahashekazi asibozo, nezinye izinto.

Ngo 19 ka February, kumhlaba ekutiwa yi Brak- fontein E-Somerset kuya
kutengiswa lowomhlaba nezindlu ezikuwo, nenkabi zegusha ezi 300,
negushakazi ezi 500, nebokwe ezi 300, nezinye izinto.

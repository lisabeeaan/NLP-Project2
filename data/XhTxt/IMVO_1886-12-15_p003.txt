icebo lokuba nalike ituba lokuwatyumza Amampondo. I Journal yona iti

linantlanu njengokungati kutyunyuzwa oqongqotwane abamnyamana abambalwa.

Brownlee eyabonakala kwi Mercury Mr. Brownlee selena- mava kakulu kwinto
zabantsundu enolwazi olukulu ngazo, ngangokuba abo bangaqele kakulu
impato yabantsundu bati bakuva uluvo luka Mr. Brownlee sebesiti
kugqityiwe ; lowo amtamsanqelayo maka tanyusanqelwe, nalowo amqalekisayo
maka qalekiswe.—Ngelifupi sebesuke bati utinina u Bhuluneli kulento !
aze ati akuteta kube sekupele yonke inteto oko kutete yena. Kodwa bona
abayazi nqo impato yabantsundu, bayazi mhlope ukuba kwimbambano nolaulo
lwa Maxhosa akuko qitala lifana no Mr. Brownlee. Elinene laka
lasesihlalweni esipezulu layi mantyi yama Ngqika ixesha elide lifu- mana
imali eninzi. Asazi nto waka wawanceda kuyo ama Ngqika, nawaka
wawakokela kakuhle kuyo, into ekoyo esiyaziyo yona yile yokuba isi alam
esingu SANDILE sabulawa kulawa mahlati ka Matole kunye nento eninzi
yamakalipa ake, enye into isizwe sama Ngqika silusali pesheya kwe Nciba
nalapa e Koloni. Ngeloxesha u Mr. Brownlee wanyuswa wenziwa Umpatiswa
Wemicimbi Yaba­ntsundu, waza nayo wayipata kakubi ngangokuba kwenziwe
ubulumko bokuba selesenziwa Imantyi Enkulu yase Kokstad. Ukugqibela u
Mr. Brownlee wanikwa ikupa lemali eninzi ayamkela engasasebenzi; saba
netemba lokuba asikubuye simve kwimpato yabantsundu. Kanti, yeha! useko.

kwanjengo Mr. Brownlee imicimbi yaba­ntsundu ubhale ngengqondo enkulu
incwadi asivusa ngayo ukuba siwachase amanyatelo alukuni ngoluhlobo.

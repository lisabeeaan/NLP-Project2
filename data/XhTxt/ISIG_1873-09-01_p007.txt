IXABISO LEDIAMOND lihlile; ezinokutengiswa kalula ngoku kupela zezilunge

IBALUNI.—Kuza kwenziwa amalinga okuwela olulwandle lukulu kutiwa yi
Atlantic ngebaluni. Kuzakusukwa E-America kuyiwe E-England.

E-ENGLAND kusand’ ukubako intlanganiso eluhlobo olulodwa. Abantu abebeko
kakulu kuyo ngabenzi bengubo. Enye yendawo ekuvunyelwene ngazo
kukuhlanganisa iponti ezi£60,000, ukuze kutengwe inqwelo zomlilo zipiwe
inkosi yase China. Lento yenzelwa ukuze ama China abe nokuwaqonda
amatamsanqa anga wafumanayo ukuba ziko ezweni lawo inqwelo ezinjalo.
Nobona ke nani ukuba kurolwa inani elikulu ukuyifundisa inkosi yase
China. Kodwa noko ayingelilelwe lonto kuba lonkosi ingumpati wesahlulo
sesitatu sabantu bomhlaba wonke; ingati ke ukuba inokwazi yona okwayo,
ibe nokunceda abantu abaninzi.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

IPARLAMENT ebiko ngapambili icitiwe, kuza kunyulwa enye kwaka msinya.

INKUMBI.—Kutiwa inkumbi ziyimihlambi ku- mahlulo wase Bini nendawo
eziwungqongileyo.

KWELE GOLIDE.—Indaba zakona ziyakutaza kunezenyanga ezidluleyo, noko
nangoku kungeka qondakali kakuhle into ekuya kuba yiyo.

UKWANDISWA KOCINGO.—U-Sir. Henry Barkly ubalelana no Mongameli we Eree
State ngokwa- ndiswa kocingo oluya kwele Diamond. Kufunwa ukuba lucande
E-Free State lunyatele E-Philipolis nase Fauresmith luye E-Dutoitspan
nase New Bush.

KWELA MATEBELE.—Kusand’ ukubako imfazwe pakati kuka Lobengule inkosi ya
Matebele no Mzila inkosi yase Gaza. Isizatu sayo ubete U- Lobengule
watandana nentombi ka Mzila, wazake yenaU-Mzila akavuma ukumendisela
intombi yake kuba U-Lobengule engekaqiniseki ebukosini bake. Kute
kwakuba njalo U-Lobengule wabubonisa ubukosi bake ngokungenela umhlaba
ka Mzila, nangokumgxota ade aye kumbandezela ngolwandle. Mhlaumbi lento
ingaba iya kumlungisa U-Mzila, kuba kutiwa ngumbandezeli omkulu.

PAKATI E-AERICA. — Kwelinye lamapepa enyanga ezidluleyo sasite kutiwa
U-Sir Samuel Baker ubulewe. Sinovuyo ukunixelela ukuba lomare ayenge
nyaniso. Indaba ezivela kuye ziti umsebenzi abehambele wona ube ne-
mpumelelo ; uti upelisiwe umsebenzi wokutengisa amakoboka, ngoku
’indlela eya F-Jipete isuka E-Zanzibar ivulekile.

ABAYA KWELE DIAMOND.—Ipepa elitile lase Free State liti:—Abasutu, na
Mabasalong, na Mantatee, nama Awuwa, nezinye intlanga zabantsundu,
badlula bengamaqela imihla yonke ukuya kwele Diamond. Kuti ukuba kuko
oti mabake bahlale apa basebenze asuke ange uyancokola nokuba selesiti
wobanika imali eninzi. Abenzi bezitena apa indoda bangayi nika nokuba zi
2s. 6d., ngemini, kuba abasebenzi baswelekile.

KWELAMAZULU.—Ixesha lokumiswa kuka Ce- tywayo ukuba abe yinkosi beliya
kuba pakati kuka August. U-Mr. Shepstone ube menyiwe nga Mazulu ukuze
inkosi yawo ibekwe nguye esihla- Iweni. Ubeselesukile E-Natal ehamba
namadoda alikulu. U-Mr. Shepstone ukwindawo eyodwa ngokumalunga na
Mazulu. Ake amnyula ukuba amele inkosi yawo U-Tshaka. Kungeso sizatu
lento namhla amcela ukuba aze kumisa inkosi yawo U-Cetywayo. Kutiwa
izinto eziya kwenziwa ekumisweni kwake aziyi kuba njengato ike yabonwa
kuleminyaka idluleyo.

UMKONJANA OMCINANE otile ongu stemele
ngoku usebeuza elunxwemeni Iwase Natal, utwala
iswekile ukusuka apo ilinywa kona uyise E-Dur-
ban. Uyakuba luncedo olukulu, kuba ngoku
zinqabile inqwelo zenkabi.

EMZIMVUBU.—Incwadini ebalwe ngumfo otile
ngo 20 ka July kutiwa:—Ilizwe lase Mzimvubu
libonakala kakuhle. Imvula ibisand’ ukuna.
Kodwa ebusika inca ike yasweleka, inkomo zafa
kunene kukubutisa. Kwezinye indawo ibiyindla-
la nasebantwini. Abanye bebesebepila zingcambu
zemiti abanye bepila kukuxela inkomo. Umko-
njana obutunyelwe kona unyuka ngomlambo
utwele ukudla, upantse ukupenulwa zimvubu.
Zike zausukela ugama otile. Kanjalo Amaxosa
ebesand’ ukubulala ingwekazi.

UMKOMBE OBUNAMAKOBOKA.—Umkombe wa
Mangesi wokulwa ekutiwa yi Briton usand’ uku-
timba owama Arab obutwele amakoboka. Inani
lawo ebe 116, amadoda ema 26, abafazi bema 22,
amakwenkwe ema 44, intombi zima 24. Afunye-
nwe efumane axinaniswa kwindawana encinane,
kungasatandeki ukusondela kuwo ngenxa yevu-
mba. Asand’ ukuziswa E-Natal, apo akutshelwe
kona kwezi veki zisand’ ukudlula. Akutandeki
ukuwakangela ngenxa yokunqina. Lomkombe
ubunawo uqekezwe amaceba ngeruluwa, abanye
abantu ba wo babanjwa.

ndisa abantwana emini, afundise abantu abakulu

lyakubanela abantu abanga 300 xa iseligqityiwe.

UKUQUTYWA KWELIZWI KWABANTSUNDU.—E-
Port Elizabeth kusand’ ukubako intlanganiso
enxulumene netyalike yabantsundu yama Wesile.
Lotyalike ibizele ngabantu. Kuvulwe ngomta-
ndazo ngumvangeli webandla lety alike yase
England. Emva kwawo kucelwe U-Mr, John
Theophilus ukuba atabate isihlalo. Inteto yake
ibe yebekisela ekutini mauhanjiselwe pambili
umsebenzi welizwi, kwandiswe ukwaziwa kwenda-
ba ze Gospel. Ute yena lomsebenzi usautanda
njengoko, noko seleminyaka mine ewupete. U-
U-Bev. B. Impey utete ngomsebenzi wabafundisi
E-South Africa neziqamo zawo. Babeliqela
abantsundu abatetileyo, kwabonakala ukuba
baunyamekele lomsebenzi. Ngalomnyaka abantsu-
ndu balapa barole £50 18s. 9d., zokuquba ilizwi.
Kweyanyakenye idlule nge £17. Iteke intlanga-
niso yambulela obesesihlalweni, nomshumayeli,
nabahlanganisi bemali. Asikuko nokuba kuyata-
ndeka ukubona kuko izitembiso ezingaka zoku-
hluma komsebenzi wabafundisi. Kuyaqondakala
ukuba nabamhlope baya bebanyamekela ngakumbi
abantsundu ngecala lelizwi:—Lembalana siyita-
bata kwipepa lendaba lase Bayi.

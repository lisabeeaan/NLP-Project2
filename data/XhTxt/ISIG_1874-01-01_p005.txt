Ukuqaleka kwayo kute umkombe womntu otile wase United States, wapikela
ukuya E-Cuba utwele abapati mikosi base Cuba nabase America; nezinye
izinto ezingavunyelwayo. Uteke wabanjwa ngowase Spain kwaza kwabulawa
abantu aba 53 kwangoko. Abanye bekuvakala ukuba kusatetwa ityala labo
ukuze nabo babulawe. Ite ke I-Government yase United States yatumela
imikombe yokulwa ukuya kutintela oko kupalazwa kwegazi, nokubuzela abo
bantu bayo babuleweyo. I-Government yase Madrid iti oko akwenzekanga
ngemvume yayo. Isipelo asikazeki into esoba yiyo.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

UMFANA OLAHLEKILEYO.

  Du Toits Pan, kwele Diamond, December 5, 1873.

Kuko umntwana wakowetu ogama lingu Frans Mosiman, mhlaumbi Olifan,
ofunwa nguyise ekaya. Ngunyana we nkosi yama Tebele kwelipakati
E-Africa. Igama loyise ngu Sibideyela, inkosi yakwa Mledhane.
Wagqityelwa kutiwa uyekufunda esikolweni. Asazi ukuba kukusipi na. Wake
wase Somerset nase Uitenhage. Omaziyo apo akona ndombulela endazisile
ngokunditumela incwadi, ize ngegama lika John Zuma. Asikuko nokuba
uyafuneka ekaya lomntu.

SAMUEL POLIFAN.

EBUZELA U-PHILIPS.

Du Toits Pan, December 13, 1873.

NKOSI MHLELI—Nditi kuwe uze unga qumbi kukuba ndicelela indawo, lemigca
imbalwa. Ndi- pendula umfo ka Gosa. Nditi mzalwana, kuhlena ukwenjenje
ukuteta ngelinye inene. Uzuti kuba wena wake wafunda kumapepa ama Ngesi,
undazise ukuba kuko imfanelo na kulonto. Andikolwa ukuba oku ukutete
ukucingile. Ukuba elolizwi ulitete ulicingile, wena wake wakwisikola
esikulu, ungati uhlazise igama lomnye umntu, kanti akulibambanga naye
ezindaweni. Ndake ndambona efunda ngokuhlwa nase Rini. Wena usuke
selungena ngokugweba. Wena D. K. Vandala kwinteto yako, uti
ukuzipakamisa kwako utobe elinye inene ngokuligweba. Ude uti
mayingamkelwa incwadi yake ngati ubatalelwa nguwe. Buza kumabi wamapepa,
ukuba kukona inyanga engekabatalelwa. Kukutinina okukuti umntu akubuza
into kusuke kutiwe yi bishop, ngu mongameli; kuba kambe ngamazwi enu
lawo, kuyakutiwanina ukuviwana xa kunjalo?

CAESAR KUTSI.

E-Bura, December 16, 1873.

Kumhleli we Sigidimi Samaxosa, ndibulela indaba zesidluleyo kuba kuzo
kuko indawo ezinkulu noko ezinye zimbi. Oko kutsho nditeta ezase Natal
zika Langalibalele, ukusuka avukelane nenkosi zake. Lento kude kubenje
kukusweleka kwemfundo. Abantu bakowetu abavumi ukulutumela ezikolweni
usapo. Makutetwe nenkosi zabantsundu ukuba zifundise abantwana zize
kuqabuka, kuze kupela nemfazwe, kuba ukungafundi yenye yezinto
eziyibangayo. Xa abantu bakonzileyo bafanele ukulandela amasiko esizwe
esibapeteyo. Kodwa tina bantsundu kuma Ngesi apa sitabata utywala
kupela, sibunabele ngapezu kwemitete ye Nkosazana.

Mandiziyeke apa ndize kuwe mfo wase Bini. Ndibulela amazwana owabalileyo
ngabantu banga- pandle kwedolopu abaza kutengisa. Ndikoliwe yilondawo.
Nanga ke ningabagxota bonke abezayo kuni, ningaketi. Ngokukodwa
amantombazana, kuba asuke amane ukubaleka kuti boyise, etembe ukufumana
indawo kuni bantu bemizi emikulu, kanti amanye seleya kusuke adungudele,
angabi savuma ukugoduka. Asivumi ukuba aze kuqeshwa sincedeni ningawapi
indawo, ngapandle kokuba anokuqondisa ukuba imvumelo ayizuze koyise.

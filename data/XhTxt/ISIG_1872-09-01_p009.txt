Ezihlomela Kwi Sigidimi Sama-Xosa

LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1872.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

U-RARABE ufuna ukwazi ukuba U-W. N. Gaba uti ukulobola kulungile,
akulungile sinina? Maka- yi cacise into ayitetayo.

U-JOSIAH MATODLANA, wase Alexandria, uti isifo sokohlokohlo asikuko
nokuba sibe sandile ebantwaneni ngobu busika. Unomnqweno wokunga
singaxika Isigidimi ukupuma kwaso.

Ku SNEYMESI VAZIDLULE.—Siyakubulela nge- mbali yako ebonisa iziqamo
zobunxila. Okwa- ngoku inde kakulu kweli pepa le Sigidimi, kodwa
soyigcina njengoko uyiguqule ngako, size silinge ukuyishicilela
siyifinyezile.

U-XOBA KALIWA wase Natal, upendula U-W. Klass Mhulushe, uti umzali ubiza
into emfaneleyo xa afuna ikazi. Uti yena akaboni ukuba ukulobola
kuyalelwa Ezibalweni; uti inkatazo zomana ukubako pakati kwendoda
nomfazi nokuba kulotyolwe nokuba akulotyolwanga, kengoko akunakutiwa
zibangwa likazi.

U-JEREMIAH MASINGATA ubala ngo Hlebihashe osand’ ukububa E-Ncwazi.
Ubengakoliwe noko umfazi wake ebe ngu Mkristu. Kute ngoku ebe- gula
kwako Amakristu ayekumfundisa ngendlela eya ezulwini, koko usuke
wawagxota. Ute kodwa akubona ukuba itemba lokupila aliko, wawabiza.
Ambuzile ukuba unatemba lantona, wati yena unje ngomntu ofuna into
emnyameni. Kuhlala kuyin- gozi ukuzidukisa indaba ezilungileyo, kude
kufike iyure yokugqibela.

U-P. J. PAKATI, wase Cradock, uvumela U-P. Kalipa ukuti utywala
makutetwe ngabo, ngokuba bonakalisa umsebenzi ka Tixo. Uti uke ababone
nabafundisi bentsapo besela xa bahambele intsapo zabo. Yena ngokwake
akabuseli. Uti ukupen- dula U-Mackoy, utywala obu asibubo bodwa obubanga
ukulwa, nokutuka, nombulo. Eyona mbangi yezizinto yintliziyo yomntu xa
ingeka guqulwa lufefe luka Tixo. Ku John Mahaley uti—“Funda Isigidimi
sika April nempendulo yam kwesika May pambi kokuba uti andiyiqondi into
oyitetayo, napambi kokuba uti impendulo yam ayibekisele entweni.”

U A. T. PEPPETTA, wase Graaff Reinet upen­dula umfo oti into eyaziwa
ngabashumayeli abantsundu kukulilisa abafazi ngapezu kokufundisa abantu.
Impendulo yake ibekisele ekutini lenteto ilucuku, kanti ucuku yinto
ewonakalisayo umse­benzi ka Tixo ukuba ayilunyukelwanga. Uti kanjako
Amakristu makalumkele ukwenza izinto angaba nokuhlekwa ngazo ngamaqaba
afundileyo. Kanjalo ubalisa ngokuba kube kuko iholide E- Graaff Reinet
ngo 6 ka August; nokuba ngalo mini Ilaukazi latshela endlwini nomntwana
walo. Indlu yalo ibe ifulelwe ngamaxamba amadala eswekile. Libe
linxilile ukuze oku kubeko, lisele utywala babe Lungu.

U-MNININDLU ubala ngokuti amadodana afu- nd levo akufanelekile ukuba
atshaye icuba. Uti baninzi abati bakuhambela ezindlwini zabahlobo babo
sebefika betshaya bengacelanga nemvumelo yokwenjenjalo. Icuba eli
libanga ukuba abantu bapulukwe yimali yabo nalixesha. Kanjalo uti
akufanelekile kanye ukutshaya kwentombi. Iqi- nga alinikayo lelokuba
baze bonke abangekazeki bafune abafazi abangatshayiyo. Siyakolwa nati
ukuba eliqinga lilungile.

INTLANGANISO YEREMENTE E-TSOMO.

Intlanganiso yomnyaka yeremente, yama Wesile isand’ ukubako E-Tsomo Post
etyalikeni yakona. Abantu bebebaninzi kangangokude abanye ba swele
indawo beme pandle, noko lotyalike inokwanela abantu abakuma 600.
Intlanganiso leyo ivulwe ngomtandazo ; wazake emva kwawo U-Rev. Charles
Pamla wabika ukuba esihlalweni uza kuba ngu Captain Blyth.

Kuleseshwe ke ipepa elixela isimo seremente kona; labonisa ukuba inani
labantu beremente ngama 405, abasalingwayo 197, bebonke ngama 602;
indawo zokushumayela zima 33; izikolo zemini zi 10 zinabantwana abakuma
40 kwisikolo sinye.

Imali eyamkelwe ngalo mnyaka imi ngolu- hlobo :—

Erolelwe ukuquba ilizwi £50 4 0

Eyetikiti neyeklasi 60 0 0

Erolelwe izikolo 50 0 0

Eyokuxasa izikolo ivela kwi Government 80 0 0

£210 4 0

Kute emva koko kwateta U-Captain Blyth no Rev. James Morris, no Mr.
McGlashan, no Mr. Levey, nenkosi engu Janjasie nengu Feltman, nabanye.
Imali ehlanganisiweyo ekupeleni kwe- ntlanganiso ibe £67 12s. 6d.,
yayidlula eyanya- kenye nge £17 8s. 6d. Abanye barole imali abanye
barola ukudla nempahla ehambayo.

U-NKOHLA FALATI, wase Roxeni, upendula U-Gaba ngokulobola. Uti—

1.   Kuyinyaniso ukuba yinto ekoyo futi ukuti xa kuko abafana ababini
    abafuna ukufilisha intombi, aze yena uyise wayo akangele kwelo
    linomzi ongade walamane nomntu. Maxa wambi liti nexego elidala
    lizeke intombi njengo kungati lise ngu mfana; kuti ukulobola oku
    ngamanye amaxa kubangele ukuba uyise wentombi ayipate kanzima,
    ayibete ngentonga nezibokwe.

2.   Ubuza ukuba U-W. K. Gaba xa eteta ngo­kulobola ubekiselela kwinto
    ekube kuyiyo Ema- xoseni na, nokuba uteta into ekuyiyo ngoku pakati
    kwama Kristu. Amakristu wona akasateni namasiko obuhedeni, namaxesha
    ebesenziwa ngawo lomasiko, kuba ebe ngengawo amaxesha alungileyo.

3.   Ukulobola asikuko ukunikana nokwamkela kubeke kufunwa ubuhlobo ;
    kukutengisa nokute- ngelana, abe omnye efuna inzuzo. Ubunyaniso
    balendawo abungeguqulwe ngamazwi odwa.

4.   Kuti ukuba kulotyolwe ize indoda izive ikululekile ukuba impate
    kanzima, imbandezele umfazi wayo. Ewe okwenene angazimela kodwa abe
    seleke wacinezelwa.

5 Ngokwenza ubuhlobo obo uteta ngabo, okwe- nene ingake inge iko londawo
mhla kwatshatwa. Kodwa uyise wentombi ubefanele ukuba ubuhlo- bo bake
angabubonisi ngokubandezela intombi yake kuba ebandezela umntu
oyizekayo. Pakati kwa Makristu antsundu zibaninzi kanye indleko
ezingeniswa ngumtshato. Kuwe W. K. Gaba nditi uze ungandipenduli
ungakange uyici- nge lento upantsi kwengubo yako.

UKUBUBA KUKA NOYI OMNYE WABA- NTSUNDU ABAGUQUKA KWASE NTLOKO.

U-Noyi, abebesebemti ukumbiza ngu R. Balfour ngoku ebesele ngu Mkristu,
ubube Elujilo ngo 30 ka July. Ube selefikile kuma 80 eminyaka ngobudala.
Ube ngumfo odumileyo, obekiweyo ngabo bonke abamaziyo, ngomnye
wabokuqala abayamkelayo Igospile. Wati efika kweli lizwe U-Rev. John
Brownlee waba sele ngumshumayeli kakade.

Wati kuminyaka ema 60 edluleyo ehamba indlela yake waqubisana nomfundisi
ongu Wil­liams, waza ke wamva eshumayela. Kwaye kum- hla aze eve ngo
Nyana ka Tixo, nangokubafela kwake aboni. Wati eyishiya londawo laba
seli- mgene ilizwi, wahlaziywa kuye U-Kristu kwa ngalo mini. Wazinikela
Enkosini kwa ngalo mini; kodwa kude kwafika U-Rev. Mr. Chalmers enge-
kabaptizeshwa.

Kute kwiminyaka emitandatu kwa bonakala ukuba buyamngenela ubutataka
bobuxego ; ubumfama buhle bamfikela. Uteke noko angasaziqondiyo izinto
ezimrauleyo akakuyeka kona ukutandaza wakuquba kwada kwase kupeleni.

Ute umhlobo otile obemazi kwa kudala waya kumbona kweziveki zokuba aze
kububa; wati ngokunxamela ukunga londoda indala angayi kumbuza into
ezindala wateta ngabafundisi abango Bennie no Brownlee no Thompson no
Chalmers. Kute bakukankanywa wanga ubuyela kwase kuziqondeni kwake
izinto ; zapalala inyembezi waza wati “ ngoku ndiyaqala ukubona.” La
ngamazwi agqibele ngawo ukuteta apa elizweni.

Ngoku yena uye kupumla, imisebenzi yake iyamlandela. Ubengomnye wama
Kristu alungi- leyo pakati kwama Kristu antsundu.

Izinto zahlukile kanye ngoku koko bezikuko kwiminyaka ema 60 edluleyo,
kuba oko umfundisi ubemnye kulentlango ingaka obehamba esiti “Lu-
ngisani umendo ka Yahova.” Ngoku siti ukuteta izikolo zetu, nendlu
zetyalike, nabafundisi bentsapo, ezonto zingamakulu ; siti abakoliweyo
bangamawaka. Imbewu iwele kumhlaba olungileyo, esinako ke ukuti kuko
umsebenzi omkulu owenze- kileyo. U-Noyi yena okwake umkile kwodwa yona
impembelelo yake yokulungileyo ayinakuci- nywa bani.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

E-RINI kuko umfo ontsundu ofunyenwe efile, ebulewe butywala.

E-FORT BEAUFORT.—Inxenye yamapolisa abe- kona kutiwe makaye kwele
Diamondi.

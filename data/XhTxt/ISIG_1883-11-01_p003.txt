ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1883.

Hayi, mfo ka Gcilishe uyindoda: sikuvumile. Into oyenzileyo kwibala
lezaziso siyayitakazela tina. Yinto enqweneleka kakulu ngezimini
zokulahlekana kwezihlobo ukuba abantu balandwe iminombo. Akwanele kuba
njalo kwapela, sinamagama atile kwapakati kwetu bala limnyama
alahlekisayo— ongati wena utile ngu Mlungu kanti akanguye, selenguye
ngenxa yegama loyise. Imvo zetu ngezi ndawo zaziwa nguye wonke.
Okwanamhla sivakalisa umxelo wetu oti ukuba kuko abamagama oniwayo,
alahlwayo, nawalahlekiswayo, nantso indlela yokuwalungisa ivuliwe
ngowembewu ka Manxele. Huk ! Makalungiswe ke amagama size kwazana !

Kube yimihlali emikulu kuti ukuva ukuba inteto esayenzayo ngemicimbi
yonyulo Iwamalungu e Palamente, neyayandulela yonke inteto yepepa
lokugqibela, yabanga ukuba umhlambi wase Nxukwebe uzipicote izintso,
uhambe ugxaleka ubuza kuye wonke ukuba masenze ntonina ukuze sisinde.
Okwezetu iziqu tina sasingalindele ukuba lenteto iyakufikelela
ezindlebeni zama Mfengu ase Nxukwebe, kuba uninzi Iwawo alusinanzile
Isigidimi. Inye, zimbini ingwevu ezisamkelayo; uninzi olu lungaba
desepile baka John Balel’edolweni, Esq., onqulwa emacaleni entlanti
kugcakanyelwe ilanga. Imizamo yetu yokuba abantu bakowetu bafunde
amapepa isawa pantsi ngoku- mayela nezi ngwevu. Siyatemba namhla
siwazisile nje ngemfuneko yokuba apapamele izinto zonyulo aya
kusibulela. Siva ukuba amagama akankanyelwa ukumela Isitili sase Bofolo
e Palamente ngango—Mr. John Laing, Mr. William Aylif E, Mr. E. J.
Painter, Mr. L. H. Meurant, (owayengu Mantyi walo dorp mzuzu) no Mr.
Richard Solomon, M.A., ongunyana ku Mr. Saul Solomon umhlobo odumileyo
wase Kapa. Inya- niso yona yeyokuba ukuba abamnyama bazibokoxele ku Mr.
R. Solomon boba bade bayenza eyona nto ilungileyo soko bati bahlala
pantsi kolaulo lo Mntan’ Omhle.

U Mr. Richard Solomon sinokumazi okuqingatisileyo. Uyise ngumfundisi apa
olungileyo wase Bedford (Enyara), u Rev. Edward Solomon, ongumninawa
walongwevu idume kunene, yaziwayo kumzi wonke omnyama, u Mr. Saul
Solomon. Kwimini zamzuzu wabefunda apa e Lovedale kunye nomninawa wake u
Mr. W. H. Solomon ongumteteleli omkulu e Jajini e Dayimani ngoku.
Uyiqube ngokwanelisayo imfundo yake kwakweli lizwe, ngokuba apumelele
uviwo olulingana ne Matriculation yezi mini ngo 1868; waza ngo 1869
wapumelela olulingana nolwe B.A.; ngo 1871 olulingana nolwe M.A. Ngalo
maxesha lamagama emfundo epakamileyo ayengekani- kwa kweli lizwe, uye
pesheya ke apo ate ekuzeni kubuyela kwake kweli lizwe ngo 1878
wapumelela olwe M.A. (e Cam­bridge) kwanolokuba ngumteteleli e Jajini.
Efikile kulo mhlaba ubehle wazipaula ngobukali bake ezintweni zemfundo,
nasemicimbini yomteto. Utshate ngo 1882, ukuba asiposisi, nentombi ka
Rev. John Walton, M.A., Inkulu yabafundisi base Wesile beli lizwe.
Ingati abantu bakowetu besiqingata sase Bofolo bemvotele apa ibe
bangenise inene eliyakuba sihombo epalamente ngazo zonke indlela,
eliyakunyatela emkondweni omdala wakwa Solomon, zelibonise ukuvuleka
kwentloko zabo balivoteleyo. Nanko ke kwezenu izandla mhlambi ontsundu
wase Fort Beaufort!

Iveki yesitatu kule nyanga ifileyo ibiyebalulekileyo kubemi be Lovedale.
Isizekabani ibikukumka kwamadodana amatatu abenexesha efunda kulomzi
esiya e Livingstonia, empakatini we Africa. Ngu Mr. George Williams, no
Kagaso Sazuze, no Joseph Bismark amadodana lawo. U Mr. George Williams
yindodana yase Bedford, eyeza kulomzi emva kokuba ipumelele uviwo lo
Titshala ngokuncomekayo, isiza kuqeqeshelwa umse- benzi wobufundisi
kwibandla lase Independent. Ibise imi- nyaka mine ilapa ifunda i Tioloji
(Theology) ingenelwa ngulo moya wokuhambisa indaba ezilungileyo kwimfama
ezikude nje. Xa se isityela ihambiso zengcamango zayo ekuzeni kufika
kwesi sigqibo sifumana ukuba yashukunyiswa ikakulu ngumfuziselo ka Dr.
Somerville, wokuti, emdala ngangoko angako azenze idini elipilileyo
ngokuhambisa Ilizwi kulo lonke ilizwe. IT Kagaso Sazuze yindodana eyeza
no Dr. Stewart ngexesha lokugqibela awaye kwelo. Ixesha eli lonke
ibifunda apa ukuze ibuye iye kwelo lakowayo. IT Joseph Bismark uminyaka
mibini wafikayo, naye kwa ngemfundo.—Ukumka kwabo ke ibe lixesha
elifanelekileyo ukukumbuza izizwe ezimnyama esezinako ukukanya
ngemfanelo zazo malungana nabo bemi base Africa abasebumnyameni.
Intlanganiso ye Cawa ngokuhlwa (14 Oct.) yaba yeyokuteta ngemicimbi
yelozwe, aza amazwi aposwa ngu Mr. George Williams nge Singesi.—Ngo-
Iwesi-Bini ngokuhlwa benzelwe isidlo sokubabulisa eskuleni sentombi
esiva ukuba sahambiseka kakuhle imbali.—Ngolwesi- Tatu ngokuhlwa kubeko
umtandazo ngenxa yabo no Mr. William Koyi osekwelo, aza amazwi kuwo
enziwa ngu Mr. George Williams, no Kagaso, no Bismark.—Ngolwesi-Ne
ngokuhlwa Isidlo sokubulisa senziwe kwindlu yokudlela yomlisela. Isidlo
esasifaneleke kunene sincomeka. Kuso emazwi amnandi aposwa ngu Mr.
Geddes, Mr. Smith, Mr. J. Tengo-Jabavu no Mr. J. K. Bokwe. Umtandazo
wabo bamaziyo u Tixo, nabanobuncoko Naye, uyacel wa ngawo lamadodana,
ngabasebekwelo, nangomsebenzi wokuzikanyela ahamba ngawo.

Nge Cawa yo 21 Oct., sabonela into entle eyenziwa yintsapo Yeskolo se
Cawa sase Heald Town. Umhla lowo wawungo- wokubuta imali yokuncedisa
ukuhanjiswa kwelizwi. Ute akubiza umongameli weskolo ukuba zize ipeni
zentsapo Iwaquma utuli. Wasuka ngokusuka umtinjana, kwabonakala ukuba
akuko xesha lokupefumla; kwakunjalo nje nempi egxulushileyo ingayekile
yenze into enye igalela kwityeyana yayo. Sisakuva yinto emi imi le
yokuba amadodana akutshwe zintombi ngokubeka kule mali. Ute umgcini
sikolo akuyivakalisa indawo yokuba ityeya yendyebo yomlisela iseze.
Yeka! aqala pantsi ukuya kugalela abeta yazala, ada amanye amakaba
ayipakami- sela pezulu ayinyanzela pakati imali. Abonisa ngoku ukuba
yinto yangabom le yokuba eyawo ityeya ihlale ilamba lemihla. Esi
sibonakalo sasikumbuza tina ukuba kanene imvula inile, yiyo lento
izandla zabantu zikululekileyo. Ngamana amaxesha amahle!

Ngapandle kokolula inteto engasakuba luncedo luni nakubani masiti
asiyazi eyona nto anga singenza yona umbaleli wetu esampehlelela ngegama
lokuba ngu Zantippe. Inqubo yetu yileyo kuhanjiswa ngayo ngabo bonke
abanembeko yokucokela indaba zamapepa. Ukuba unga singayinika umhlana
lehambiso indala, ibekekileyo, unjengo mntwana olilela inyanga pofu
enaso isonka sake esinebotolo. Nguwo lo ke umgulo wokugqibela wale
mambane :—

MHLELI—Ndipendula inteto eku Pawana olukwi Sigidimi sokuqala so
September. Amazwi am ayakukunjulwa luninzi Iwabalesi Sigidimi. Umb'ali
wo Pawana lowo uti u Mcokeli sukuba evisisisa inteto esukuba
zingavisiseki kakuhle. Nankoke kwa u Mcokeli ebala kwakwelo pepa kwipepa
le 7 ngapantsi kweka Mr. Mbenya inteto. Apo akuko luto aluvisisayo;
unq'inisa amazwi am. Gcina apo. Elinye inq'ina lale nteto yam kwakweli
pepa lale mini leli:—U Mcokeli ute akuyifumana incwadi ka W. Z. S.
waseleyipendula, njengoko aq'ele ukwenza ngako. Yilonto ke
ndiyitetayo—leyempendulo ikwipepa le 3, ibe inteto ikwe le 7. Namhla
umlesi uyakuva ukugxekwa kwenteto enge- kayifundi nokuyifunda. Kananjazo
nokuba i Sigidimi esi sisezandleni zobanina, abanjanina, asiyikuze
sifike kwimfezeko esingasena kuhambela pambili. Mandincatame apa. Ndim
owenu osicaka esitobekileyo W. Z. S.—King William’s Town, 26 October,
1883.

Ingozi ehlungisa kunene yehle e Kapa ngo 9 Oct. Kwenzakele u Rev. James
Hoyle, wase Independent, ngokuti akuqonda ukuba ikalityi awabekwele kuyo
izakuwa atsibele pantsi ati ukuwa kwake abeteke ngentloko emqadini
wesinyiti owawukufupi nalondawo, afe isitulu kwalapo. Egodusiwe kubehle
kwabonakala ukuba ingozi leyo inkulu, kwaza ngolwesi-Ne (11 Oct.)
wadlula kweli. Asite nqa nakancinane tina ukuva ukuba usizi ngenxa
yokudlula kwake alubanga nakuteteka koweramente nongenguye weramente
yake. Ngapezulu kokuva futi ngo Rev. Mr. Hoyle emapepeni encedisa kuwo
wonke umsebenzi oyelele ekwandiseni ubukumkani bo Pezu-Konke, yaba
yintsikelelo yetu enkulu ukumbona nokumva eteta kwi- ntlanganiso
esasikuyo apo e Kapa. Samfumana eyindoda enobushusbu, nozondelelo,
nentobeko, nenkutalo, nayo yonke into afanele ukuba nayo olisoldati lo
Somandla kweli lizwe. Onke amazwi ake ibingabekiselelwe entliziyweni
yompulapuli, ubuciko ibingento azamela yona, kodwa ubenabo kuba ubeteta
into esuka entliziyweni yake ezantsi. Elo bandla lake lilahlekelwe
ngumfundisi ekungelula ukuyizalisa indawo yake. Ngamana u Sombawo wabopa
intliziyo zentsapo nebandla lake. Umsebenzi wokuncwaba nolandelayo
onjenge ntshumayelo waqutywa ngu Rev. Wm. Thompson, no Rev. Professor
Foot, no Rev. J. Jarvis.

Indlela ahambisa ngayo kuti u Mr. Simon Ntlabati isnonopu esintsundu
sase Tsomo, isikumbuza inteto esiyive ngenye imini isingiswa entsatsheni
ngumfundisi ontsundu. Lomfundisi uvakalise ukukoliswa kwake yinto
ayibone kweso sikolo— ubuninzi bentombi ngapezu kododana; wati into leyo
ibonisa ukunyameka komtinjana ngapezu komlisela. Asaneliswanga kukutsho
esi sicaka so Sombawo. Site naseziramenteni kukwanjalo. Ngamadoda
aqwalela emva. Besinokubonisa ukuba into leyo ayinjalo kanye : kodwa
kute kuba umfundisi asinto ipendulwayo sasesiyeka, sazitembisa ngeliti
sesiyaku- wupalaza umpefumlo wetu kwi Mpawana. Abo bacinga kunye nalo
mfundisi singa bangakumbula lento inye, yokuba ukuwa kwabo kukwanje
ngokungena kwabo. Nqwa nama Qeya amabinqa la. Kuti wayi mhla agqobokayo,
kanti nokuwa kwawo kukwanjalo. Indoda xa ite igqobokile: igqobokile;
kwanjengokuba xa ite ayingeni entweni etile: ayingeni. Unjalo ke umhlobo
wetu u Semani. Ngenye imini kwakwezi Mpawana, savakalisa indawo yokuba
seyiliqela ngoku eliquba umsebenzi wevenkile e Transkei, sabuza ke ukuba
kutenina lento eliqela lingafaki izaziso e Sigidimini njengokuba bese-
njenjalo abamhlope. Akudlulanga ntsuku safika isaziso somhlobo wetu u
Mr. Simon Ntlabati. Inqaba yasemalini yokusihlaula I Hayi yona
ayibangako. Asimhla wakuqala isenzeka lento kumzalwana wetu ose Tsomo.
Sinamagama awatabata Isigidimi e Tsomo, kwatiwa nantsi imali isiza ;
nanamhla sisabambe elodinga—yona imali ayiko. Lomhlaba i Tsomo
ungumangaliso, sitsho sikumbala ibali lowayeseluhambeni wabandezelwa
ngumfazi ngenkabi kwakwelo.

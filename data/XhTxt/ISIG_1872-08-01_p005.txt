INTLANGANISO YABAFUNDISI,
(MISSIONARY CONFERENCE.)

EYESHUMI intlanganiso yabafundisi, ebako ngomnyaka, ibiko E-Qonce
ngomhla we 10 ka July.

Bekuko iqela elikulu labafundisi bama- cala asesitandatwini nase
sixenxeni, na- manye amanene awunyamekeleyo umse- benzi abangawo
abafundisi, neqelana lamadodana antsundu. Intlanganiso leyo ibe
yonganyelwe ngu Charles Brownlee Esq., Imantyi yase Qonce ; Isekritari
ingu Rev. Mr Chalmers. Umsebenzi upantse ukugqiba intsuku ezimbini.

U-Rev. Dr Stewart uleseshe ipepa “ Eli ngentsebenzo yentlanganiso, into
enceda yona, nendawo equbela kuyo, nokuba kungatiwa nina ukuze iwufeze
kalula lomsebenzi.” Kute lakugqitywa ukuleseshwa, kwako ingxoxo enkulu,
ekute kuyo kwaboniswa ukuba inoncedo olu kulu lendawo yokuhlangana
kwabafundisi bamacala ngamacala, banikane amaqinga okulwa nolutshaba
balwa nalo bonke. Kuvunyelwene ngokuti maliti elo-nelo icala linyule
indoda ekuya kutiwa ukubizwa kwayo “ Ngumbaleli.” Umsebenzi wayo
ngowokuti, indawo ebezixoxwa kulo ntlanganiso izise kwi ntlanganiso
zabafu- ndisi becala layo, ive ukuba zitinina zona ngalo ndawo, izeke
ize kukuxela ezikute- tayo, xa abafundisi bamacala ngamacala babuye
bahlangana Kutiwe kanjalo loti elo icala linyule umntu ongumfundisi
wakulo.

Kute ukumka komhla U-Rev, Mr Birt walesesha ipepa eli “ Ngokufundiswa
kwa bantu abantsundu abangaba heyideni. Ute ngokufutshane wabonisa isimo
abakuso ngoku bapantsi komtetowama Ngesi; wati ke intlanganiso ngeyibala
isicelo, ??? I-Governmente ukuba yake izikolo pakati kwa maqaba
izixase.”

Kute akuligqiba, yaka yaxoxwa londa- wo kwagqitywa
kweliti:--Lentlanganiso iyayivuma indawo etetelelwa lelo pepa, yokuba
I-Governmente yenze amalinga angapezulu okufundisa abantu abantsu- ndu ;
nokuba kanjalo amadoda “ angaba- baleli ” aze lento ayingenise kwi
ntlanga- niso zamacala akowawo, ize into etetwe ngawo lomacala ababaleli
bayizise kwi ntlanganiso yomnyaka ozayo yabafundisi bamacala ngamacala.

Yakugqitywa leyo kuleseshwe incwadi ebezivela kwi ntlanganiso zokuzila
utywa- la zase Qonce, nase Dikeni, nase Fort Beaufort, zibonisa ukuba
ubunxila buya- nda pakati kwabantsundu ; zicela ukuba kuhlanganwe
kucelwe ukuba imiteto yokunika imvumelo kubagcini nkantini ike ibuye
ikangelwe, lincitshiswe inani lazo.

Kubeko inxoxo ende kakulu ngalonto ; abafundisi bayililela indawo yokuba
kunje babonisa ukuba uhlanga oluntsundu luya kubehle lutshabalale ukuba
luqubela pambili koku. Iqela labafundisi libonise ukuba ubunxila buyanda
kanye, lateta ngento elizaziyo. Kwanyulwa amadoda amane ukuba abe
yikomiti yokubala isicelo esiya kutunyelwa kwi Governore, aze asibonise
entlanganisweni yakubuye ihlangane ngengomso. Upele apoke um- sebenzi
okwalo mini.

Kusile ngengomso ibuye yahlangana intlanganiso yabafundisi, kwaleseshwa
isicelo ebekutiwe ’zesibalwe. Indawo ezingamandla kuso
bezizezi;—kuyabonakala lemihla ingakanje, ukuba buyakula kanye ubunxila
pakati kwabantsundu ; utywala ngoku babufaka pakati kwama- siko abo,
kuti ezidlweni nasemidudweni yabo bube sendaweni yamasi; ngoku akunxili
madoda nabafazi kwapela, koko kungene namakwenkwe namantombazana; apo
kuko inkantini, kwenzeka izinto ezingenako nokukankanywa, nokukangelwa
ngaba mhlope nabantsundu abanokuqonda; ukuba lento iyaquba, uhlanga
oluntsundu luza kubehle lutshabalale ; uza kuti umntu ongumhambi abe
sengo- zini yokupangwa nokubulawa, nempahla

yabantu ihlale isesicengeni sokubiwa; amadoda apakamileyo antsundu, anga
makristu, nangengawo, afuna uxolo,noko- nwabelana, ayayibona lento,
atike, maya- ziswe kwi Governmente ; eyona nto ibangela oku zezinkantini
zingaka, zikufupi kwabantsundu. Kulommandla wase Qo­nce wodwa,
azingapantsi kwa 45, zibe ke ezinye zixaswa ngabantsundu bodwa. Indawoke
eceliweyo kwi Governore, ibe yeyokuba kungabiko wumbi umgcini nkantini
oyakunikwa imvumelo yokuba amise inkantini kulommandla, kuze kanjalo
elinani seliko kakade, libehle lincitshiswe.

Bazake abafundisi baxela ukulila kwa- bo ngenxa yokumkelwa ngabazalwana
babo O-Rev. James Laing, Rev. John Brownlee, no Rev. Tiyo Soga; babala
ezincwadini zalo ntlaganiso amazwana axela ukubalilela kwabo.

Kuxoxwe ngezinto ngezinto. Site nge- nxa yokuswela indawo,
sasesikankanya ezo ite intlanganiso yane ndawo epuma kuyo ngazo.

Ite ke intlanganiso yaqukunjelwa ngo- mtandazo ngu Rev Dr. Stewart.

UKUVULWA KWE LOVEDALE MIS-
SIONARY INSTITUTION.

LE Institution ibuye yavulwa ngomhla wa 25 ka July. Umsebenzi olunge
nokuviwa kwabangenayo, upantse ukugqiba imini ezimbini. Inani labebeze
kucela ukungeniswa libe 78 bebonke, kuko abamhlope, nabantsundu nama
Lawo. Abasibozo ababanga nakwamkelwa ngamatuba atile. Kwelinani intombi
bezi 16, amakwenkwe enga 62. Awamhlope ebe 6. Ngapandle kwala bekuko
amane awanga angeza ngemini kodwa, abe- ehlala komawawo. Abebeze kufuna
amashi- shini bebe 20 xa sesiquka nentombi ezize emsebe- nzini. Ezinye
izikolo ezibe zike zingatumeli kakulu E-Lovedale namhla zitumele iqela.

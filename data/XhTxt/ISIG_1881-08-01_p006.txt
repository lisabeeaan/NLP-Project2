ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1881.

ABANTSUNDU NO GQIRASHE.

INXENYE yengxelo ka Dr. Fitzgerald wase Qonce kwi Par­liament

U Dr. Fitzgerald utumele kwi Parlamente le isand’ ukwahlukana ingxelo
yempato ye Hospital ayipeteyo e King Wm’s Town. Emveni koku caza imo
yezakiwo azongameleyo uhambisa atete ngendawo, esingenayo nencinane
intandabuzo ukuba ayakuvuya ukuyiva amawetu. Uti bo u Gqirashe kwi
Parlamente:—

“Ndisakangele pambili kwixesha ekuya kuti kufundiswe amadodana atile
akwa Xosa” [ngama Xosa kutetwa yonke impi entsundu, ama Ngesi enene wona
awenz’ izahlulo-hlulo zingenamsebenzi ezinjengokuba sizenza tina
bamnyama pakati kwabe bala letu.—MHLELI WE Sigidimi.'] Atsho ke ati
lomadodana abe “ngama gqira, amiswe kwi zingata ngezi qingata enyanga
pakati kwa bantu bako wawo; lendlela,” ut’ u Gqirashe “yoba yeyoku
sondeza uncedo lokunyangwa kufupi nentlanga zeli lizwe. Kufuti
ndimangaliswa yimi gama ehanjwa ngaba mnyama befuna uncedo lwe gqira;
abanye besuka koma pesheya kwe Nciba kwelama Mfengu, koma Gqushwa, koma
Bofolo noma Xesi nezinye indawo ezimgama, baze bafike bandimele ndide
ndiwugqibe umsebenzi we Hospital.

“Icebo endandilibeke pambi kwe Rulumente epumileyo kwa ngayo lendawo
lamkelwa yiyo, ngelali hanjisiwe ukuba kwakungabangako mfazwe, kuba
nemali, kwaku sekulindelwe i Parlamente ukuba inike imvumelo yayo ngayo;
ndinyanisekile ukuba, xa uxolo lubuye lwabako le Rulumente, nokuba
yiyipina eya kulandela yoli kangela kakuhle iqinga lokusondezela
abantsundu amayeza. Esisenzo asisayikuba sesokuvela usizi abantu kodwa,
soba sesokubulala o “siyavuma mhloko,” abafike badlulele ngapaya
kokusebenza ngama yeza, bahambe bebulala kodwa, besahlukanisa abantu,
into aba ngebeyi nyanga.”

U REV. MR. SMIT, okufa sikubike kwi “Zinto ngezinto,” ngogama elifanelwe
ukubizwa ngembeko ngabo bonke abase Koloni. Kusentetweni ye Cape Mercury
esikupa inxenye yoko sikutetayo. Wazalelwa e Koloni apa, wazinikela
emsebenzini wobufundisi eseyindodana, wabambelela kulo msebenzi pakati
kwazo zonke inxwaleko zawo kwada kwase kufeni eli soMati elilungileyo
lika Kristu. Kwakumnyaka we 1849 kufumaneka ukuba wabe selingu mfundisi
e Rini. Le ramente yeyokuqala kwezi ntsundu eyazi xasa ngokwayo.
Sekutiwe abantu abamnyama batanda kakulu imali, kodwa le ramente
ibonisile ukuba itanda kakulu umfundisi wayo; imenzele amabaso amaninzi
ngexa ebesapile. Ekubeni ibe le ramente ishukeke kangaka kungatiwa
kungemizamo ka Mr. Smit. Ufanele imbulelo ke ngoko. Ukumtanda kwabo
abantu bake bekungenasipelo, nelahleko yabo ngoku buba kwake yoba
buhlungu kanye. Bonke abebemazi u Mr. Smit bebemtanda. Ubenobulumko
benyoka nokutobeka kwe hobe. Enyanisekile kwinto ayitetileyo,
ubengumhlobo otandekayo, into abegqibe kuyo ibingadli ngakuba yimposiso,
necebo lake beli lelidla ngokufaneleka. Esimeni sake sobu fundisi ubenga
bekwe bantu bake bodwa, kodwa nangabezinye iramente. Ubevelana naba
manye amabandla; zininzi izinto azenzileyo ukuncedisa amanye amabandla
akwase Rini. Ube ngumshumayeli onamadla. Ufe u Mr. Smit ebonakalisa
olotando aseloko enalo. Intlanganiso ye bandla labapebleleli beli
nentlanganiso e Rini nge veki engapaya kwedlulileyo, kwati ngolwe Sibini
kwako umtendeleko ekwati (noko engu mfundisi wase Independent) kongamela
yena. Wabenika isiyalo kwisiqendu esiti “Singa singabon’ u Yesu” wateta
kakuhle nangamandla wada wati “Abo bapilel’ u Yesu bohlala

belungele ukumkaulela”, yawa kweso situba incwadi awayeyipete, uvakele
eteta into, kodwa engavakalanga, kodwa waye ngati ufuna amanzi, wapuma
umpefumlo, kwa lapo, ewela ezandleni zika Mr. Nutall, owabesele sukile
ukuze amncede. Ukupela okuncomekayo kom Kristu bekungena kufikelelwa.
Sibe njalo isipelo somnye wababezinikele kumsebenzi wokunyusa abamnyama.

UKUZINIKELA KUKA RILI

Lenteto ingo Rili siyiketa kwebibekwe pambi kwabafundi bayo lipepa lase
King William’s Town eligam liyi Cape Mercury. Lipepa eliteta inteto
zengqondo:—I Rulumente epumileyo yabetusa abantu ngo April odlulileyo
ngokubika ukuba iteta no-Rili, ute watenjiswa indawo yokuhlala kwindawo
engekude kakulu no Mkangiso. Ezinteto azidubulanga nini, ute kodwa u
Mpati Micimbi yaba Ntsundu, lo mtsha wacinga ukuba makabuye atete no
Rili; ute ke wacela u Mr. Wm. Fynn ukuba alityelelele eloshawe, eve into
elingati likoliswe yiyo. Singati u Mr. Fynn lo ubesaziwa ngu Rili kwa
sebuntwaneni, wabe kanjalo eke wahlala kwa Rili engamehlo ku Rulumente.
Edinwe yinteto eninzi abete u Rulumente wanayo kule minyaka idlulileyo
upume u Mr. Fynn esandleni sika Rulumente wazihlalela efama. Utabate
kunye naye, mnye babini kwabamnyama, yekoko ukuya kwa Bomvana, watumela
abantu ku Rili ukuba baye kuti uko. Ngomso olandelayo amaqelaqela
abamnyama ehla kuyo yonke imimango esiza ku Feni. U Rili nonyana bake
ababini u Sigcau no Sitshaka nabanye bagaleleka namagora akumakulu
amabini. Emva koku bulisa, nengxelo zemigcobo ekumboneni u Feni kanjako,
yaqala ingxoxo ende ekupeleni, ngentando yake betu, u Rili uzinikele
ezandleni zika Feni. Walile u Feni, esiti akayiyo imantyi; kodwa wati
oku wokuxela ku Capt. Blyth, zekuti, kuba u Rili selebutataka,
bahlangabezane. Bavumelene ngalondawo, wahlangana u Capt. Blyth nenkos’
endala. Kwalapo u Rili wazinikela isiqu sake nobukosi bake ku Rulumente,
sikolwa ukuba kutiwe makeme kufupi nomzi wake omdala. Kweziveki
zidluleyo abantu bake bebenikwa umblaba kwisiqingata esipantsi kwe
mantyi engu Mr. Streatfield. Yonke lento yehla ngenxa yesenzo sale
Ru­lumente isand’ ukungena; kodwa siya temba ukuba u Mr. Fynn
akasokulityalwa kuba into ayenzileyo, uyenze kakuhle. Ngexesha lemfazwe
umntu owangabe bulele u Rili ubeya kunikwa amagama amakulu, u Mr. Fynn
ngokufumana ngoxolo u Rili ubefanelwe ngamagama amakulu ngapezulu. Siya
temba i Rulumente yobonisa ngesenzo sayo ukuba “uxolo lune zoyiso zalo
nalo njenge mfazwe.”

UMBIKO.

Ely Station, July 16, 1881.

Sibika isilileko esibi nesilusizi kunene, sibikela abahlobo abakwindawo
ngendawo ukub'uba kwenkosi yetu umfo ka Mabele u Ndiko ; emva kokugula
okude. Lomfo ubebekekile ehlonelwe kakulu ngabo bonke abengumongameli
kubo. Wab'uba ngomhla we 11 kule nyanga, wangcwatywa nge 12, ngumfundisi
u Rev. Mr. P. J. Mzimba, wase Dikeni, owateta amazwi amakulu kunene
nalusizi, eyala, etutuzela abalilayo. Ebonisa ingwevu zakowabo ukuze
ziwulungise umzi lowo ushiywe wodwa ngapandle kwezipitipiti nempambano.
Yaba yinto enkulu kunene ukuba inkosi ize kumbelwa ngumfundisi.

  Olilayo

W. SKEPE NZEKU.

kutetwa ngokumfaka kwindawo le ipetwe ngu Colonel Griffith ngoku.

U-DR. STEWART ute ke: Captain Blyth, Manenekazi, zi Mantyi, ba Eundisi,
zi Nkosi zama Mfengu, nani bantu nonke,—Ndicinga ukuti akuko namnye
okubonileyo okwenzeke kulentlanganiso, ungake akutandabuze ukuhambela
pambili kwabantu aba Ntsundu kweli lama Mfengu. Umsebenzi wanamhla
ungatyilwa ngendlela ezininzi. Uyakuteta kumazwe amaninzi, kubantu
eningazanga nababona nabangazange banibone. Asikafiki kwinani ekujongwe
kulo, kodwa ke ndiya ziqonda kakuble izinto ezitinteleye. Lentlanganiso
nomsebenzi wanamhla, ubonisa okungenzekayo kubantu belilizwe, xa bepetwe
ngobulumko nangokulungileyo. Kanjako lupawu olubalulekileyo, olubonisa
ukuhambiseka komsebenzi waba Fundisi nowe Mfundo; ngendawo yokugqibela
yimpendulo kwabo bantu bati ukuteta ngabantu belilizwe, abanakuze
bahlume. Ndifuna ke ukuposa amazwana ngezo ndawo. Esi sakiwo, nayo yonke
imizamo elunge naso, sixela ulaulo Iweli lizwe lama Mfengu. Andikutsho
oku ngakuba u-Captain Blyth usesihlalweni, nangakuba kuko imantyi apa,
nangakuba kurolwe imali; kungokuba lonto iyinene, neyona ndlela
ilungileyo yokuwukangela lomsebenzi.

Kudlule iminyaka elishumi linamitandatu ndacandayo kuqala kwelilizwe,
ndino Mr. R. Ross. Lalite cwaka oko elilizwe. Lalingena bantu lalingena
mizi, lingena mihlambi, emehlweni am ngoko ubuhle bababuncinane. Nani
ngokwenu niyayazi imbali yelilizwe lama Mfengu. Kungene abantu laye
lizaliswa kuhle, kwavela imizi, ati amatambeka abonakala ezaliswe
yimihlambi. Emva kwexesha elitile kwafika u-Captain Blyth, laqala ke ixa
lokuhambela pambili. Andifuni kwenza utelekiso olungafanelekileyo, kodwa
ndiya likumbula ixa lakudala, ndiya libona elitsha. Kwaqala kwenziwa
indlela epuma e-Nciba, kwalandela Imiboniso yempahla nezilimo, ngoku ke
sesi Sikolo se Mfundo. Konke oku nokuba bekungati kungenziwanga,
belungati noko ulaulo Iwelilizwe luhambiseke ngokubonakala kusanelisa.
Ndiya kwaleka eli lodwa—Akwaba bekungati ku Tixo, ziti zonke izilauli
zikangele kumalungelo abantu belilizwe; zilaule ngobulumko nangenyameko,
njengokuba kunjalo apa. Asingabantu bonke abangavumelana nam, ngale
ntsingiselo, mhlaumbi abanye bandicasile. Ndilapa nje namhla ndize
kuteta ingcinga zam ngomsebenzi oselu sekugqityweni. Kum ulitemba
elikulu njengomqondiso nembewu yento ezayo, ndikutsho nje oku ndipinda
endikutete kwezinye indawo ezimgama uzimayile 10,000.

Indawo elandelayo, yeyenteto eyenziwa futi, yokuba abantu belilizwe
abanakuhluma. Akufuneki nokude nditi ningakolwa yilonto, ngokuba nani
nazi kakuhle, nati sisebenza nani sazi okuhle kunoko, okukona abantu
basebenza ndawonye nabantu belilizwe, kokukona babona ngakumbi ukuba
uhlumo lunokwenzeka. Kuko nakubo ukuhambi-

seleka pambili okungenasipelo, kwanjengakubantu, nokuba ngabalipina
ibala, abadaliweyo ngu Tixo. Ndide ndibe nentloni ndakuva abantu besiti,
abantu belilizwe abasokuhluma; lonkolo ifuneka itshabalele msinya ukuze
kulunge. Bendingati kwabo bantu, yiyani pesheya kwe Nciba niye kufunda
into ; hambani niye kwingqondo zenu niye kufunda ngapezulu. Tabatani
inani labantu belizwe; nize ke nikangele esisakiwo. Anditsho ukuti
sesona sakiwo singumangaliso ehlabatini, kodwa singummangaliso emb'alini
yaso. Besingayi zuzayo lemali ifunekayo, ipindwe kabini kwelinye ilizwe;
ndazuza e-Scotland imali epantse ukupindwa kane kule, yeminye
imisebenzi. Kodwa indawo eyenziwe ngabantu belilizwe, ekwakiweni kwayo,
kubangela ukuba ilunge ngapezulu, yahluke kuzo zonke izakiwo zohlobo
Iwayo. Ngokunga tandabuzekiyo inexabiso ngamatye nekalika yayo, kodwa
ixabiso lokulungisa isimilo alinakubalwa. Izindlu zetyalike zaba Ntsundu
kwelilizwe, nesi sakiwo ziyimpendulo eyaneleyo, kuwo onke amapupa ateta
ngokungabi nakuhluma kwabantu belilizwe. Kanjalo lomsebenzi ubalulekele
ngokukodwa, ngokubonakalisa ukuhambela pambili kwentshumayelo yelizwi,
nemfundo. Abanye abantu bati lomizamo ayipumelelanga; ningakolwa, kuba
abazikolwa nabo ngokwabo. Ngenye imini ndilese incwadi ebalwe yibishop,
iti ishumayele iminyaka emashumi mahlanu kwelilizwe, kodwa lomsebenzi
awupumelelanga, awunasiqamo. Andinakukolwa yilonto. Andinako ukuti
engcingeni yam ihlabati limshiye kangaka u-Tixo, kangangokude izimiselo
zika Tixo zoyiswe. Ihlabati ulinikele u-Kristu, asingise kwesi sipelo ke
onke amanyatela okuhambela pambili oluntu.

Ndifuna ngoku ukubekisela kuncedo oluvela kubafundisi bamanye amahlelo.
Kuyatshiwo futi ukuti abafundisi bamahlelo ngamahlelo basebenza
ngokucaseneyo. Kungaba kuko ukbzwana Iwenyaniso kulonto; ukuba ke
kunjalo, akuko hlelo libalulekileyo, lingatabata ilitye ligibisele
kwamanye kuba lona limsulwa; mhlaumbi liti, nina nihlala niposisa, ke
mna ndimsulwa. Esisakiwo singumqondiso wokuba angasebenza, ayasebenza
ndawonye entweni eba- lungeleyo bonke. Ke ndifuna nokuvakalisa izibulelo
oku- kokwesiqu sam, ko Messrs. Barnett, Warner, Hays, Davidson,
nakwabanye, kwanje ngakwi mantyi zelilizwe, ngoncedo ezilunikileyo
nenkatazo ezide zangena kuzo kulomsebenzi. Nditemba ukuba boyikumbula
lemizamo yabo nangamaxa azayo ngovuyo, nakuba boya besanela ngakumbi
kamva kunokwa ngoku; ndibekisela kwixa esiya kulishiya elilizwe,
sizibuze lombuzo ngokwetu “ Yintonina esele kuti kuwo wonke umsebenzi
wezandla zetu? ” Kusala kupela oko sikwenzele ilungelo yabanye,
kungengakuba besinqwenele mvuzo wamntu. Kaloku ndifuna ukuteta
ngomsebenzi ozayo walendawo, nange Komiti zaba Mhlope naba Ntsundu.
Linye ke namhla ibanga elifeziweyo, ebite inxenye yabamhlope abalunge
kwi Komiti, yakangela kulo:—kukuba namhla bakululekile kulomsebenzi.
Ndiyazi kuko imantyi, nomfundisi, abate bakuvakalisa oko. Pambi ke
kokuba bona, nabanye abebelunge kulo Komiti bapume kuyo, masinga libali
ukuwubulela umsebenzi wabo ongaka, bewunikela ngemvume. I-Komiti yabantu
belilizwe yona yayimiselwe ukuze ihlale iko, ino Captain Blyth umgcini
sihlalo wembeko. Akuko matanda, ubuko bayo buyakuba luncedo olukulu
kubafundisi ne titshara, ezipete esisikolo. Imisebenzi yalo Komiti yoba
kukuha- mbela isikolo, ivakalise indawo zokunceda umsebenzi, inike
iqinga, incedise ekuxoxeni amatyala abagqiti miteto ekufundweni; ize ke
izamele ukuba isikolo sibe sentliziyweni yabantu belilizwe; incedise ke
nangayipina indlela ekwiqinga elamisa mhla mnene labalelwa ukuba ibe
yimimiso yalendawo. Lekomiti yaba Ntsundu ibangele amaxala amakulu,
nokotuka kwabanye. Anditsho ukuti bako abanjalo apa. Abanye abantu
babucitela ubom babo, ekotukeni izoyikiso ezicamangcwe ngabo. U-Captain

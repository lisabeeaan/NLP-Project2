ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, FEBRUARY 1, 1883

UMTETO WEZICAKA.

  UMZI WASEMA GQUNUKWEBENI.

Amatyala atetwe yi Mantyi yase Bayi kulo mnyaka ufileyo afikelele
kumawaka amabini anekulu lineshumi elinesibozo (2118). Inkoliso
ngawokunxila, nokulwa, nokutukana, nokuba. Inkoliso yamasela,. nabatuki,
nabalwi ikala ngomti wo Talaso, iti, “ Nkosi ukuba bendinganxilile
ngendingayenzanga lento.” Kuko elinye iqela elikulu labantu bakowetu
abangena ebunzimeni ngenxa yobudenge ngase kwazini umteto, ngakumbi
lomteto we “Nkosi nezicaka,” abati ngokukumsha yi Masters and Servants’
Law Amendment Act No 18 of 1873.

Sokankanya indawana zibe mbalwa, ukuze umzi wakowetu uzazele ngokwawo
apo utsho ngakona umteto. Isahluko VII. Umteto 18, we 1873. Isiqendwana
1.—Ukuba Isicaka sapule umnqopiso we nkosi yaso ngokungawenzi umsebenzi
ebekuvunyelwene ngawo,

2.  —Ukuba sishiye umsebenzi waso ngokuhiliza ngapandle kokucela kwi
    nkosi yaso,

3.  —Ukuba sinxilile emsebenzini saza asabinako ukusebenza kakuble,

4.  —Ukuba umsebenzi waso asiwenzanga ebesifanele ukuwe- nza,

5.  —Ukuba sikwele ihashe lenkosi yaso, sasebenzisa nokuba yinqwelo yayo
    ngapandle kwemvume yayo,

6.  —Ukuba asilulameli umteto wenkosi yaso,

7.  —Ukuba siti ngokunxila mhlayimbi ngokunga nyameki sonakalise impahla
    yenkosi yaso,

8.  —Ukuba salile sakutunywa, nokuba kuko impahla ebeku- funeka siyise
    kwindawo zayo, saza oko asakwenza,

9.  —Ukuba situke inkosi, nokuba yinkosikazi yaso, nokuba ngomiswe
    yinkosi pezu kwaso,

Siya kudliwa inani elingengapezu kwe £2, sihlale etolongweni ixesha
elingengapezu kwe nyanga.—W.

Ngeminyaka emibini epele ngenyanga engu December, 1882 zenjenje ukuxela
izipata-mandla zale ndawo yemfundo ya Pesheya kwelama Mfengu: Sibulela
ngovuyo itamsanqa elibumoya elilandele ezitendeni zemizamo yetu
yokufundisa.

Kwambla saqalwa esi sikula abafundisi seloko bema ngokuti babe babini
abamhlope, babe babini abamnyama, libe linye inenekazi elimnyama eliyi
titshala yokutunga. Ukuguquguleka kwe titshala akubangi bambiselo
pambili emsebenzini. Kodwa nakuba oku bekuko umsebenzi wona uhambisele
pambili, namanani andile. Intsapo ese Simnareni ngamashumi matandatu
anesitatu ; eza ngesikplo 9 ; abafunda ishishini loku cwela 15; abafunda
kwi klasi yabalungiselela ukungena e Simnareni 17; abangena emtungweni
bengabalwa abo bangena neklasi abakula manani andulelayo 7. Inani
lilonke 111. Kuko nesikula sangokublwa sabasebenzi.

Indawo ekujongwe kuyo kukupumelelisa intsapo kugqatso lo Titsbala Iwakwa
Rulumente. Iko intlanganiso yododana ezama ukuxoxa ngesi Ngesi.
Ukusweleka kwezityudeni ezi pambili ukuncedisa ekuteteni kakuble kuyinto
elilelwayo.

Umsebenzi owenziwayo ngaba cweli pantsi ko Mr. Thomas Brown mkulu.
Abapati bomzi bacinga ngokumisa amashisbi- ni okwenza inqwelo nokukanda.
Intsapo inexesha esebenza ngalo emasimini emva kwesikolo.

Umsebenzi wofefe uhamba ngokunqwenelekayo pakati kodo- dana olufundayo.
Nge Cawa kuko amaqela atyelela kumaqa- ba ngapandle. Emva kokumelwa
kakulu abuye afumana inkululeko.

Imali exasa umzi yevela kwa Rulumente nehlaulwa ngaba fundi, ngonyaka we
1881 lemali yayi £268 12 10, ngo 1882 inyukele kwi £385 5 4. Amabaso
apiwe isikula zinkomo ezintatu ezilixabiso le £150 linene lase Qonce.
Umhlobo ontsundu ubasele kwa isikolo eso nge £20, yokuba kutengwe
intsimbi.

Yinto evuyisayo le yokuba akukabiko kufa kubi.

Kwakumhla kwagqityelwa, selihambile ilanga, mhla saba- pos’isali “
ogxwal’intloko,” sigqibe kwelokuke siyekuzibonela ngokwetu lento uquba
yona lomzikute cwaka zoletu nje: eba- kugxoga nangamatye umblobo wetu we
“ Mpawana,” ebakuvusa ngendlela ezininzi owase Sigidimini kungatsitsi
nto.

Site masiligabangxe ukuze silibone kakuble. Okwenene, sabamba indlela
yenqwelo, sayakungena kumzi wakomkulu, sakumisa nge Pewula—umandla esiva
ukuba kwati ngokucula kwentsapo yawo kwimini zamzuzu, akaba nantloni u
Rev. George Thomas Perks, M.A., isijoli sebandla lase Wesile, ukuba umzi
lowo waziwe ngegama lake elalinge pantsi. Nambla ke umzi lowo yi
Perksdale, oko kukuti yintlambo ka Perks.

Ibe litamsanqa letu ukuba siwuhambe liko igala kude kube sekupeleni
kwendlela yetu. Ubuhle bezilimo—omazimba nombona—basibiza kwakwa
Ngwenya, kude kwaya e-Xesi esko- Iweni iluhlaza oluhle, olupete
bumnyama. Sicande pakati kwesikolo eso sase Annshaw. Izindlu zibonise
kuti ukuba abantu baso abakaugenwa ngumoya wokunyuka Izindlu
ezizingxande singabalwa kwiminwe yesisandla sibala ngaso kanti noko
azingeyifeze. Esisikolo sine xesha simi, bekunqweneleka kuko noko
izindlu ezibushiyayo ubungqukuva, zibe namasimi pambi kwazo. Asimzamo
mkulu ufunekayo ukulungisa ezizinto, esitembayo ngexesha esoti sibuye
siwatye- lelele ama Gqunukwebe soba nokuteta ngokudluliseleyo ngeso-
sikolo sidala sidumileyo, ebesimele ukwaziwa sikangeleke sihle
ngenxenokuba igama letshawe elikulu u Kama, elilikolwa lo- kuqala
lenyaniso kubanumzana bakwa Xosa, libandakanyiwe nawo.

I-Perksdale sifike sakoliswa yiyo. Imfumba ngemfumba esiti ingaba
zingqolowa mhlaimbi zihabile zixele imbali yazo ngokupapama kwabantu
ukulima ezonto, ekungexesha lide lidlulileyo bezingeko pakati kwetu.
Asinakuba singabancomi abemi balomandla ngokunonelela kwabo ukulima.
India yetyalike abangena kuyo kodwa asiyefanele abantu abajengabo ukuba
nempahla. Kwasema Jodini, yinto ebisakuti ukwenziwa kwayo indlu ka Tixo
igqite ezabantu ngobuhle, ze ingabinaxesha ingacokiswa.

Eyona ngxoxo inkulu, avele ngonwele kuyo ama Gqunukwebe yeyomhlaba wase
Debe, owaunikwe amnumzetu u Kama kwanini, wangenwa nga be-Lungu
ngezamini belingekaqondwa siti bamnyama ixabiso lomhlaba. Aba be-Lungu
ke sebezimbaza kulo mhlaba. Pofu abarafi ku Rulumente, bengarafi naku
Nkosi u Kama. Ngabanyantsuli nje. Into ke ezanywavo kukuba kuqondwe
eyona nto kuyiyo. Sisiva tina into eninzi yenyaniso ingakuma Gqunukwebe.
Ngxakwana siyibonayo yeyokuba kungeko mntu uyiqondayo ihambiso yama
Ngesi—yolaulo Iwawo, nokuquba kwawo izinto ezisakuba zinjengale—pakati
komzi. Yimvo esafika kuyo kudala le yokuba, into yokuba inkosi icetyiswe
ngamapakati amadala kupela ayinafa. Amapakati amadala awakwazi ukulesa ;
awazanga abanatuba lokuqondisisa ihambiso nohlobo lokuteta Iwasema
Ngesini; awakwazi kubala izinto zakomkulu azisingise ku Rulumente,
zekuti ngokuvisisana kubeko umoya woxolo—zonke ezizinto aziko kumapakati
amadala. Kanti ke siti tina ayengamacebo amabungu ezi- ngwevu awacita
umzi wakwa Gcaleka; awacita umzi wakwa Ngqika. Into ebesingatanda ike
yalingwa yeyokuba kwindawo ezimizi isemiyo, njengalo wasema Gqunukwebeni
ke, kuti apa emabungeni omzi kubeko amadodana amatsha, anezimilo,
aziwayo ukuba ekucebiseni kwawo angewu kokelele eweni umzi. Inteto
ngalomhlaba wase Debe ikubonisa mhlope ukusweleka kwamapakati anjengala
siwabalulayo. Iko inkomityi eyamiswayo ngu Rulumente ukuba icedulule
indawo ezitile ngalomhlaba. Asazi ukuba soba yintonina isigqibo sayo.
Kukwazana kwetu ngati umpatiswa mihlaba wakwa Rulumente, u Hon. J. X.
Merriman akana mvelano inkulu no Kama: oku sakubona entetweni yake e
Parlamente ngalonyaka ufileyo. Ekubeni inteto zo Mr. Sauer no Mr. Irvine
zabonisa ukuba bazinxanelwe inyaniso ezinokubanga ukuba bafike kwizi
gqibo zabo ngokufanekileyo.

Ekuteteni kwetu ngomzi wama Gqunukwebe solumkela ukuba singati
sesiziqongqota. Lento ingenza ukuba inteto yetu ibe lutyuka olungaziwa
bani sipelo. Ntwana siyililelayo

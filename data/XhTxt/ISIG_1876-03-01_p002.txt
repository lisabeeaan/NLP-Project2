U-ADAM KOK.

Kwi Sigidimi senyanga edluleyo sasikankanye ukuba ugomhla 30 ka December
u-Adam Koko uwe enqweleni waza wafa kwakamsinya emva koko. Namhla soke
senze amazwi ambalwa ngempilo yake.

Lo Adam Koko ngumfo owaqubisana
nezixakaniso eziqale ukuncipa kule minyaka idluleyo. Kufuneke ukuba onke
amabanga awatabatayo awahambe nzima, kususela kwase kuzeni kuba yinkosi
kwake. Sitsho kuba kwati akububa uyise sahlulelana pakati isizwe, elinye
iqela lafuna u-Abraham elinye lafuna lo Adam siteta ngaye. Lada elopike
lahlulwa kukupalazwa kwegazi, u- Adam wabufumana ubukosi ngokoyisa.
Akumoyisa u-Abraham lowo bamvuma bonke ukuba nguye oyinkosi yama Griqua
nezizwana abemelene nazo zamvuma, wada wavunywa nanga Mangesi.

Akubufumana ubukosi waqonda ukuba mkulu unzima umsebenzi opambi kwake.
Abantu babe sezantsi ngendlela ezininzi. Umsebenzi babe ngazinikele
kuwo, bengateni nokulima, incinane nemfuyo yabo. Kanjalo babe ngenaxesha
lide befikile kulo ndawo e-Philippolis pesheya kwe Gqili. Kwezonto
kwakongezeleleke ukuba abantu bebesand’ ukucitana bodwa xa kubangwa
ubukosi; yaza ezidlule zonke kwakukwanda kobunxila.

Ezindawo zonke zambandezela kakulu
kuba ababantu bake ubebakangelele ukuba
balusapo lwake, eyivela yonke into ebagxekayo nebonakalisayo. Kwafuneka
ukuba afune amaqinga okubanceda. Enye yezokuqala izinto azenzayo
kukuzamela ukutshabalalisa ubunxila. Wamisa umteto wokuba makungabiko
uza kutengisa ngotywala kufupi nomzi wake; wati akubona ukuba kuko
ababuyela nakude wamisa ukuba bungasondeli kanye emhlabeni wake. Yati
noko lonto imnika inxamleko nenkatazo walwa nayo. Endaweni yokulinganisa
ezi nkosi zikokela amapakati ukuya ezinkantini,
ubesiti apo ake wabona ifatyi ase eyiqekeza
bupalala bonke nobotywala bayo.

Enye indawo aqonda ukuba iya kumnceda kakulu, ngokukodwa ekukatazweni
zezinye izizwe, kukuba ahlale enobuhlobo nama
Ngesi. Wati ke obobuhlobo wabufumana,
amnceda kwaoko ebese yinkosi ezipeteyo,
amnika £300 ngomnyaka, amana emnika
namaqinga akuba exakekile.

Abafundisi abeza kuye wabakutaza ngazo zonke indlela anokwenjenjalo
ngazo, wakubona ukuba akuko sizwe sinokunyuka sicasene nabo. Ati ke onke
amaqinga awenzayo okunyusa uhlanga alupeteyo akauleziswa ngokungapezu
koko ebengenza ngako ukuba abafundisi bebengeko, kuba nabo bangena
bamncedisa.

UKUHAMBELA KUKA HONOURABLE

CHARLES BROWNLEE KWA KAMA.

U-Mr. Brownlee, Umpatiswa Wemicimbi Yabantsundu ufike apa kwa Kama ngo
Mvulo ngomlila 14 ka February. Apo abesingisa kona kuse Qonce na pesheya
kwe Nciba.

Kute nakuba bebebaninzi abantu abebezele ukuhlangana naye, batinteleka
ukuza kufika kwindawo abekuyo ngenxa yokuzala kwe Xesi. Ngolwe Sibini
ku. tunyelwe kwimizi engelicala linganeno komlambo ukuba uko u-Mr.
Brownlee, ufuna ukuhlangana nabantu. Bafikile bakumakulu amabini
namatatu, beza kungena etyalikeni.

Ute u-Mr. Brownlee kubo, “Zihlobo zam, ndinovu. yo ukuba ndibe
nokuhlangana nani namhlanje. Niya bona ke nani ukuba namhlanje
sihlangene etyalikeni asiko apo sidla ngokuhlanganela kona—kwa Mantyi.
Isizatu soko, kukuba kwesituba sokuba ndagqibelayo ukubonana nani,
nipangwe obeyi nkos; enguyihlo wenu. Kekaloku namhla ndinibizele ukuze
ndike nditete ngaye, ndivakalise ukunivela kwam ngalondawo inihleleyo.
Sihlangene kwi Tyalike abengena abedeshele kuyo; sikufupi nengcwaba
lake. Asizi kuxoxa ngazinto zokuma komhlaba, koko size kulilelana
’sililisane sisizelane. Amazwi am ayakuba mafutshane. Eyona nto
eyakulunga mhlaumbi kukuba ndinileseshele incwadi endayi balela inkosi
u- Kama kwinyanga epambi kokuba asishiye. Kuloncwadi niya kukufumana
konke endinga ndingakubekisa kuni namhlanje.” Yiyo le ke loncwadi:—

E-Kapa Kwi Ofisi Yompatiswa Wemicimbi Yabantsundu.

August 14, 1875.

Ku Kama, Inkosi Yamagqunukwebe. Ngombuliso!

Ekubeni wena ucela ukuba, uti, ngenxa yokuhla kwamandla etotywa
bubuninzi beminyaka, ukululwe ezinkatazweni zobukosi, ukuze ufumane
ukupumla nokuzola okunqwenelekayo kubantu abangangawe okukufanele
ngokukodwa wena ngenxa yemisebenzi yako ngemini ezidluleyo, kuyi mfanelo
kum ukuba ndike, egameni lika Rulumente, ndenze amazwana aya kuba lilifa
lonyana wako nabantu bako, abe nesifundo kumakowenu ezinye izizwe.

Indawo endibala ngayo namhlanje asikuko nokuba inkulu. Yimbali yesizwe
iminyaka emashumi mahlanu. Ibonisa ukuba kwakutenina ukuze abantwana
begazi abaratshi, baka Palo, bazitobe kwabendlu ebideliwe ka Kwane.
Nokoke aya kuba mafutshane amazwi am ngokoyikela ukuti oti akuba made
ingavakali into endiyitetayo.

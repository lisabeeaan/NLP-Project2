ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1877. 7

ngakoleki komntu ngakuyo.—Siyaqonda ke ngalendawo ukuba u-Liwani
akamiswanga ngetuba lokuba engunyana womfazi wokuqala, kodwa ngokukoleka
kwake ngapezulu ko Janjala, njenge siko lika Rulumeni ukuba linjalo kade
kubantu base koloni.

Kanjako kwakuyo lencwadi, ngetuba lempahla, eyona nto ililifa e-Koloni,
sifumana u-Mr. Brownlee esalatisa u-Xokelelwana umteto we 18 womnyaka we
1864. Lomteto ke siyazi ukuba umisa ukuba ilifa labantu abantsundu
e-Koloni malihambe ngendlela yesiko labo. Isiko ke leziziwe ezintsundu
siyalazi ukuba lelokuba eyona ndlalifa ngunyana womfazi omkulu. Ngako ke
lomteto ekupumeleni kwawo uyamisa ukuba oyena mfazi ngomkulu nokuba
ngowokugqibela ekuzekweni kwake, ngokuba unyana wake yindlalifa.

Kanjako lomteto ndiwufumana umisa isitembu, sonke, ngokuba onyana
bezizindlu nokuba zingapi inani lazo, ngulowo unokudla ilifa lendlu
yakowabo njenge siko. Ngalomteto ke kusekuhleni ukuba lendlela yabafazi
kubantu abantsundu base koloni isemi nge siko labo nanxa indoda
inesitembu, ngokuba ilifa lise nokuhamba ngedlela yalo ngokudala, into
ke —nditeta ukudliwa kwelifa—ebonakalisayo ubunyana kwa nobufazi bomntu.

Nditi ke elilizwe lika Mr Brownlee lokuba owokuqala umfazi nguyena
uyakubalelwa ukuba ngomkulu, nokuba nguyena anguye ngokomteto
alinakunceda nto pezu kwa lomteto we Parlamente omise indlela yamafa,
ngokuba ukwabiwa kwelifa kuya kwelatisa futi oyena uyindlalifa, noyena
mfazi ungunina we ndlalifa.

Nditi ke lomfazi wokuqala ati u-Mr Brownlee nguyena inguye ngokomteto
buyakubonakala pina ubufazi bake, unyana wake uyakudla ntonina? ngokuba
ubukosi, into angati u-Rulumeni ekutandeni kwake ayabele unyana walowo
umfazi, asiyiyo’ nto yabantu bonke, kanti ilifa lona, into yabantu
bonke, limele ukuba lidliwa ngunyana womfazi omkulu nje ngokwe siko.

Manxa nditshoyo ke ndifuna ukubonisa ukuba lomteto we parlamente
utintele lendawo ilungileyo ebefuna ukumisa yona u-Mr Brownlee
kulencwadi yake. Kanjako nditi lomteto we parlamente ulihlazo nesono kwi
Rulumeni ezibiza ngobu Kristu, ngokuba ekusebenzeni kwawo uyamisa
isitembu e- koloni kwizi zukulwana ngezi zukulwana, ngokuba manxa
lomteto umisayo okokuba onyana balamankazana, nokuba akwishumi kwinye
indoda, base nokudla amafa nje nge siko labo, kusekuhleni ukuba lomteto
umisa ukuba bangabafazi bonke, ngokuba ngunyana womfazi yedwa onokudla
ilifa. Lomteto ke osebenza okunje bekumele ukuba tina ma kristu sizame
ukuba ubuyiswe ngetuba lokonakala kwawo.

Manxa nditshoyo anditi umntu ontsundu, ilifa malingahambi ngendlela
yesiko labo ukuba batanda njalo. Ilungile leyonto. Kodwa nditi umteto
omise lendawo maumise kunye naye umfazi omnye, ungamisi isitembu sonke
ukuba sibe ngabafazi nonyana balamankazana onke indlilifa zalendoda nje
ngoko lomteto usebenza njalo.

Okwam ukuqondo nditi elilizwi lika Mr Brownlee alinakunceda nto pezu kwa
lomteto we parlamente omisa indlela yamafa. Ukuba ndilahlekile ukuqonda
kwam ndingavuya undibonise. Ndisisicaka sako.

ILIFA.

Paterson, June 5,1877.

Nkosi yam Mhleli we Sigidimi Samaxosa. Andimntu ukukataza futi,
ndinyamezele kancinane ngale migcana; sishiywe yindoda enkulu apa ngo
May 27 1877 egama lingu Sibatywa Gapi; lendoda iyaziwa ngabaninzi
ekoloni nalapa kuba ibiligqira lamayeza. Mandifutshanise bazalwana,
lendoda yalala elukukweni ngo September, ebesiza futi etyalikeni,
wahlukana nayo kwakukula ukufa wamana ehanjelwa ngabakokeli,
bemtandazela nomolokazana wake, ebesiti akubuzwa ngompefumlo wake ati
andikanikwa ukunazisa oko, lindani nina nova ngam, waqala walahla
utywala, esangcambaza, walahla icuba, wafuna amayeza omfundisi odwa.
Lenjenjalo elixego lihumashumi osibozo ubudala, lati ngenye imini
kumkokeli, bendingazanga ndenze bubi, ityala lam ku Tixo, kukuba
ndingamkonzanga ndada ndangaka, kanti kade endigcina ezingozini, lonto
ke yonke indixolele Inkosi nditenjisiwe ngelifa elingunapakade ezulwini.
Wati kunyana omkulu nalo ilifa endikushiya nalo, sasi-

lindele into tina ngelifa lamayeza, njengoko kudla ngokubanjalo
emagqireni, kanti namhla lo ulahlekiwe ngamayeza kwema Imvana esusa
izono zelizwe pambi kwake, wati maxa iselifikile iyure wati kum­kokeli
mkape kaloku umntu wako, agoduke naba abeze kumtabata, watandaza ke
umkokeli, wati ekutandazeni kwake, ngamana nkosi abe lo utembele kuwe,
walandela ofayo wati ewe Nkosi ndikufumbete ngokupelelisileyo wahlala
isiqingata seyure, emva koko kwatandaza elinye ixego eliku mashumi
asixenxe ubudala wati xa ati amen, kwapela. Yivani maxego awasalibele
yimi sholongu, shiyani onyani benu nelifa elingu Yesu ningabashiyi
nemfene namayeza nem- paka nenkomo negusha.

JOHN MAHALEY.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

UKUCANDWA KOMHLABA.—Siva ukuba u-Mantyi inkosi yesika Mqalo ivumile
ukuba umhlaba wayo ucadwe. Ngokwenjenjalo ide yasenzela into elungileyo
eso sizwe, kuba akunakunyukwa apo umhlaba ungacandwanga.

U-LORD CARNARVON utumele incwadi yokuba uyavumelana nesenzo sika Somtseu
sokuyi tabatela pantsi kolaulo Iwamangesi i-Transvaal. U-Somtseu
umiselwe ukuba abe yi Governor yakona afumane £ 3,000 ngomnyaka.

E-TRANSVAAL oko kube kusalaula Amabulu ebesiti xa ontsundu aya kucela
incwadi yokuya kufuna umsebenzi kufunwe ukuba ayi hlaule. Ngoku kulaula
Amangesi kutiwa nokuba ontsundu unqwenela ukuya kuwufuna pina umsebenzi
makayi fumane incwadi yake ngokukululekileyo.

UNYANA omkulu we Nkosazaua uza kuhambela e- Australia kunye nenkosikazi
yakwake. Kuse kulu- ngiselelwa izinto eziya kufuneka. Kufunwa ukuze
isuke ngo November walonyaka mhlaumbi ngo March 1878. Uya kuqala aye
e-Australia aze ke abuye nge new Zealand, kutiwa uyise wayenomnqweno
wokuba kuyiwe kwezo ndawo, ke Inkosazana iya vuya ukuba abantwana bake
beza kuzalisa lomn- qweno woyise.

OBETWE NGABANTSUNDU.—Umfo otile ontsundu abati ngu Klass Bartman obesuka
e-Somerset esiyaa e-Baviaans River eputuma umlungukazi ute xa
asendleleni wafikelwa ngama Xosa amatatu. Amabini abambe amahashe omnye
wakwela enqweleni ukuza kubeta u-Klass. Unge uza kuhla, bati ababini
bakuyibona lonto bayiyeka imikala beza kunceda omnye, wafumana ituba
lokuquba amahashe bengekabi nakumbamba. Uqube wada waya kuqabela
endulini kanti uza kuti xa ahlayo kwicala elingapaya iwc- lonqwelana.
Kuncedakele ukuba loma Xosa ebe- ngasa sukeli.

OBULEWEYO.—Ipepa lase Cradock liti: siva ku Dr Fehrsen ukuba kuko umntu
osand’ukubulawa ngohlobo olubi kakulu. Umbulali no buleweyo ngama Lawo
bobabini. Kubonakala ukuba omnye lo ususwe ngumfazi walo ubuleweyo ukuba
makaye kubulala indoda yake. Elilawo libulele elinye lite ukuze
lilifumane elinye lalilibizela endle langa liya kulibonisa inyosi.
Bakufika kulondawo lite naziya paya, wati akutoba omnye ekangela weva
induku iselimi entloko yamapula itambo emva kwendlebe. Emva koko
litabate ilitye layi cumza intloko, laposa amatye pezu kwesidumba
lagoduka ukuya kuxelela umfazi ukuba liyi bulele indoda yake. Kungekabi
mzuzu umlungu obeliqashile elo Lawo uzekulifuna, kanti ke oko kukuza
kuvakala komkondo. Umbulali lowo kuvakala ukuba uselelivumile ityala
lake.

UMSEBENZI WABANTSUNDU.—Umfo abati ngu Mr Napier ute kwinteto ayenze
e-Rini ngomsebenzi wa- bantsundu: kwezinyanga zisibozo zidluleyo abantu
abantsundu abebekolisa ukuba pentsi kwam bebebako 800 maxa wambi bake
babe 1000. Inkoliso yabo ibinga Maxosa na Mamfengu na Besutu. Ndibe
netuba elihle ke ngoko lokuqonda ukusebenza kwabo. Ke nditi abasenzi
abantsundu abanga pantsi kwabamanye amazwe endike ndibabone. Into
abayisweleyo kukuqeqeshwa. Nditsho ndibazi abasebenzi bama Ngesi nama
Irish noma Scotch nama French, nama Sardinia nama Italian nama Belgians
nama Turk. Abantsundu abatati xesha ukuqonda into efanele ukwenziwa.
Andiboni sizatu sokuba xa ba- fundisiweyo bangabi ngabaki nabenzi
benqwelo. Ndiva abanye besiti abantsundu besiza nje apa emsebenzini
kukuba befuna ukutenga abafazi nemipu. Andiyikolwa mna lonto. Baninzi
endibaziyo aba-

pindapindileyo ukuza kusebenza baman’ukufika nabatsha. Abantsundu abeza
kusebenza apa baya kufunda bafundise nabanye ukuba umsebenzi ngumtombo
wobutyebi.

UKULAULWA kwezinto e-Free State akungeze uku- feze. Ematyaleni akona ute
umfo ontsundu obebile wanikwa iminyaka emibini etolongweni esebenza
nzima nemivumbo emashumi mahlanu. Ute omhlope obebambe umntu ontsundu
wambeta weba nemali yake waqekeza nesitora waba imali ekumakulu amatatu,
waqekeza nodonga Iwetilongo wanikwa inyanga ezintandatu etolongweni
esebenza nzima.--Cape Times

KWABOMVANA.—Umbalelani otile wepepa lase Rini uti “ kuma Bomvana kuko
into ebifanele ukubanga umsindo kubo bonke abayivayo. Kwinyanga ezimbini
ezidlulileyo inkosi yama Bomvana abati ngu Siziba yapikisana ngecuba no
Mrs Philip umfazi womgcini venkile. Ute akuteta ngamandla yasuka
lonkosana yaxelela amapakati ayo ukuba ambete. Ambetile okwenene
ngezabokwe. Ute u-Siziba ukubonisa ubukosi bake wangena evenkileni
watabata impahla nemali. Imantyi yama Bomvana ayinakwenza nto kuba
u-Moni uti akapantsi kwake u-Siziba. U-Siziba uti uyinkosi engapezulu
naku Moni. Ke nokuba u-Mrs Philip ubenze ntonina ngelengavunyelwa u-
Siziba ukuba enze into enje. Nabanye abantsundu sebesongela ukubeta
abamhlope kuba bengaboni nto ayenziweyo u-Siziba.” [U-Rulumeni ute
akuwabona amazwi alombali watumela ukuba ipengululwe into ateta ngayo.]

UMBONO.

E-Gwali kumhlaba ka Mr. Joseph Amos kuko inko- mo ete ngomhla 31 ka July
yazala amankonyana amane—amaduna amabini namatokazi amabini. Ngamabala
amaduna ebefana omabini, amatokazi efana. Azelwe epilile onke noko
ebemancinane kunoko adla ngokuba ngako amankonyana enkomo. Lento
ibamangalisile bonke abezizigama. Akukabiko namnye oti wake wayiva nase
zimbalini zakudala.

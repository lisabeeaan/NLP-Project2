Somerset Hast, 18 June, 1883.

My Lords,

WILLIAM MBENYA.

Lencwadi siyitabata kwi koranti yase Somerset East. Izi- meko
ngendlelana emfutshane zezi. Kwakufuneka umntu wokongamela ilokeshoni
yase Somerset East. Lendawo ke yaziswa epepeni. U William Mbenya ngomnye
owabalela kwizipata mzi wase Somerset, efuna ukunyulelwa kulo ndawo.
Umoya owamqubayo ukuba ati emnyama abe ngoti ufuna indawo leyo
siyawuncoma. Nokuba wenze ngabom na, lencwadi yabalwa ngohlobo
olwaneleyo ukuhlekisa sonke isizwe esisoma isi Ngesi. My Lords! Bravo!
Mbenya ! Bravissimo! Ngapambi koku besiba u My Lord usetyenziswa xa
kusingiswa kwiqela lamatshawe ekutiwa zi Earl Pesheya. Apa e South
Africa akuko mambane ingabizwa ngolu hlobo. By wanting— singakanyiselwa
kwangu mbali malungana nokuhlanganiswa kwala mazwi mabini. I am one of
the tenders—f possibly can. Yours truly Obedient Servant. Koku
kumiswa—ngesisiti kukwapulwa kwenteto ye Nkosazana, esiti tina kubangele
ukuba incwadi yomzalwana ivakaliswe kwimbombo zone zesi- bakabaka.
Lencwadi ngeyisifundisa sonke umoya wokululama, nokuzidela. Ukuba u
William Mbenya ubeke wayinika tile ungumhlobo wake ukuba ake ayikangele
ngesingahlaziswanga isi Ngesi sake. Siya qokela siti: Two heads are
better than one even if the other be a sheep’s head ; xa sihlaziyayo :
intloko ezimbmi zigqitile kwenye nokuba enye yeyegusha. Siva ukuba lo
mhlobo wetu sisityudeni sama Latini. Wosivisisa ke xa siti, ne sutor
ultra crepidam.

Akufuti ukuvakala kwale ndawo kwelipepa lohlanga, kangangoko bekufanele.
Andikukanyeli ukuba kwezinyanga zigqitileyo ivakele xa bekuxoxwa
langxoxo ka Mr. M.P. Ziko izinto zona ezizezinye engavakaliyo
ngokwaneleyo ngazo. Le yokuvalwa kwesikolo yenye.

Manditi sivalwe esisikolo ngolwesine ngombla 14 June.

Site sakuba singene isikolo luzitabatile usapo indawo zalo, namadodana,
nentombi ezi zilungiselela ubutitshala, bavunyelwa nabahlobo ukuba
bangene. Kute mayelana nexesha leshumi elinanye sabona engena umpati
wesimnari, (Governor of Institution) u Rev. Wm. Holford. Akungena
zapitizela i Titshala kanti kufunwa amapepa. Sibone umpati sikolo efunda
kwelinye lamapepa ngenqubo ye class ezipambili entsatsheni, egqibileke
upose amazwi u Mr. Holford, walandelisa u Mr. Lightfoot umpati wesikolo.

Enye ke into eyenzekayo ndoyiyeka, ndigqitele ekutini: Kwapunywa ke apo
kwayiwa kwizindlu zase simnareni ngumlisela nomtinjane lo ufundayo,
kuyakuxelwa okwabo ukuquba, yalandela nenxenye yezihlobo. Kungenwe kulo
ndlukazi inkulu abati yi- “ Lecture Hall,” baqala ukuxelelwa abafundi
ukuquba kwabo eluviweni.—Zintatu ke i class. Kweyokuqala intloko ibe
ngu: Wm. Mtembu, wase Natal. Kweyesibini ibe ngu Cleopas Kunene, kwawase
Natal. Kweyesitatu, ibe ngu Miss Caroline Ntsiko wase Qugqwala. Ke
ningabi nina bafundi le class yeyentombi zodwa, ixubile, eyona nto
ininzi ngamadodana. Siyicacisa lendawo ukuze abafundi bangaposisi, kuzo
zonke ezi class ziko intombi.

Kwaba mnandi ukubonela ukunyuka kwabenyukayo, abangezanga kukaba utuli
lwase Heald Town bona. Enye impi yona sayibona ukuba ingafanela ukucazwa
ngendlela esaka nati oko sasisese skolweni sayalwa yindoda endala ngayo.
Yati londoda: “ Maboyisi ningabbali zizinkwa niyeke ukufunda.” Injalo ke
enye impi ilibele “ zizinkwa.”

Kubuye kwakona u Mr. Holford, no Mr. Lightfoot baposa amazwi okuyala
besingisa kumlisela nomtinjana. Kwapunywa, yekake ukusinga emahasheni.

Into esayipaula yambi tina basemzini, kwakuko aba Fundisi abantsundu
bebabini, akuzange kusingiswe nakumnye ukuba apose nelimdaka. Le ke
yintlanganiso yabantu abantsundu, noko sasiba tina kungalunga kugqibele
nokuba kucelwe wamnye kubo: Siteta uluvo Iwabo bonke abantsundu ababeko.
Semkake yona lento isiranisa. Kwabanjalo esakubonayo, akufuneki sitete
ngapezulu. Abatanda ukwazi sovulwa kwakona ngolwe-Sine, ngomhla wa 25 ka
July.

Lomsebenzi siwenjenje sifuna ukuba opi napi abone umsebenzi wokufundisa
intsapo ukuba mhle kwawo. Izinto esazibonayo ngobo busuku ezenziwa
zizandla zezintombi ngokwazo zasimangalisa tina babeko. Izinto kanjalo
ababeviwa kuzo sazibona, nemibuzi yayi qatana. Umntu ofuna ukuza
kuzingqinela ngokwake siya mmema ngo July 23, siza kuvula izako ezitsha,
eziya beseke sazikankanya kwi Sigidimi esidluleyo. Nabafuna ukutumela
abantwana babo kusa vulekile, kodwa ubudala babo mabu ngabi ngapezulu
kwe 16 or 18 years. Siya kuvulwa isikula ngalomini. Hai ubuhle bako
zwendini lase Ngqushwa.

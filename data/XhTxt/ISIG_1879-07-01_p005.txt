izitintelo ezivale indlela bebede bati, ngenxa yokuyinqwenela lonto,
bange eebeyibona ilaulwa ingu Napoleon IV. Yenziwa ke yonke imigudu
yokumlungiselela ukuze limfanele igunya lobukumkani ebekulindeleke ukuba
uya kulizuza ngenxa yegazi nangenxa yobu Napoleon. Kwatike kuba i-France
ililizwe eliyintloko ngokulwa, laye ibongo lakwa Napoleon e-France,
ilibongo elimi ngodumo lokulwa, i-Prince Imperial yaqeqeshelwa kubo
bonke ubugcisa bemfazwe. Amehlo ehlabati ebeseleqala ukukangela ngexala
ukuba ingaba izakuba yintonina lendodana. Kodwa nokuba ubeya kupelela
ekubeni yintonina, i-France ibingekambizi ukuba aze kuzalisa isihlalo
sobukosi sama Napoleon, kananjalo akavunyelwanga ukuba abengummi mhlaba
wase France. Wayelangazelela ukuzifumanela udu-mo ngempilo yomlwi, yaza
noko i-France, kuba ilaulwa ngabapati abamiswe ngabantu, ayaba nako
ukumfumanela indawokulemikosiyayo mininzi ngoko-yikela indawo yokuti
angafuna ukuvusa ubukumkani.

LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, MARCH 1, 1873.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

IPARLAMENT iyakuhlangana ngomhla wa 24 ka April.

AMAXOSA alishumi elinesitandatu ebesilwa ngo- Mgqibelo E-Q.once. Enye
indoda ibetwe yapuma iliso. Baninzi abanenduma.

U-CHARLES BROWNLEE Isekritari yento zaba- ntsundu unyulwe ukuba
Eparlamente amele I-Albert.

IMIPU.—Imipu efike kule Koloni ngalonyaka udluleyo ivela kwaroanye
amazwe ebe 33,162. Inkoliso yayo itengiswe kwabantsundu abakwele Dimond.

U-MR. BROWNLEE kuvakala ukuba ude wakufeza ukulungiselela uxolo, pakati
kuka Ngangelizwe no Rili. Siyavuya ukuva ukuba kunjalo, nokuba ngoku
ipelile imfazwe.

UMLILO.—Kumzi wase Wellington kusand’ ukutsha izindlu eziliqela. Ayaziwa
kakuhle imbangi yawo, kwodwa kutandatyuzelwa ukuba ngabantu abenze nje
ngolunya.

KUKO IDIAMOD ezi karat zili 160, efunyenwe ngumntu ontsundu, obenomhlaba
wake wokumba. Ibala layo alilihle kakulu, kodwa ubukulu bayo buya kwenza
ukuba azuze ngayo.

U-MR. WHITE usand’ ukubetwa nga Maxosa amabinq abefuna imali E-Cradock.
Ute akuyilandula aselemwela ngenduku, wati akuwa azifunela ezinxoweni
zake. Nangoku akakabanjwa.

UKURUMAN, lo sasite unxamele ubukosi bama Tebele kutiwa usesemzini ka
Mangata. Ufuna ituba lokuhlangana nomongameli wase Transvaal, aze acele
kona indawo yokuhlala. Abantu aba- naye ngoku balikulu.

OWABULAWA E-CLANWILLIAM.—I-Lau elingu Gallant elalibanjelwe ukubulala
intombi ka Lannie, njengoko sanixelela ngako kwi Sigidimi esi-
ngapambili, ngoku uyalivuma ityala lake. Uti yena wayembambile xa
U-Telemach anqumla um- qala.

UKUSINDA OKUNGUMMANGALISO.—Kute E-Ba- yiumntwanakaMr. Boshoff
obudalabungumnyaka, ndlala endlwini, kwangena inyoka, waza waseleyi-
bamba. Kwati kanti kuko ikati eyibonayo, yase- liyibamba iyihlafuna
intloko ingekamenzi nto umntwana.

INKUMBI NOMLILO.—Kutiwa kute kwaflka iqela elikulu lenkumbi emhlabeni ka
Mr. Pelser E-Bur- ghers Dorp waba yena engeko eloxa. Bate aba- fana
ababini abebegcine umzi batshisa isikota esidala, befuna ukugxota
inkumbi, kuteke kuba ubungalindwe mntu umlilo, wada waya kufika
emasimini ehabile, kwatsha izitungu ezi 2,450, ebesezibekelelwe,
akwasala nokuba sibe sinye kwihabile yake yalonyaka.

OBULEWE E-PANMURE.—Umfazi ongumlungu abati ngu Johanna Statze usand’
ukubulawa E-Panmure. ufunyenwe lipolisa selefile endlwini yake, lati
lakuxela kwizipata mandla, kwaranelwa kumfo obesand’ ukupuma
entolongweni abati ngu Antyi. Ute efika kona waselebanjwa, kwafunya- nwa
ezinye impahla zalomfazi ubuleweyo zinaye, zaba ezake lomfo zibe
zifunyenwe ngase ndlwini yalomfazi. Ngoku usentolongweni kona, noko
lingekatetwa ityala lake. Indoda yalomfazi yile sasite isand’ ukuzixoma
E-Monti.

INXOWA ZOKUDLA ezitengiswe nga Besutu nga­lonyaka udluleyo azingapantsi
kwa 40, 000.

UBUSELA.—E-Bayi kuko amasela asand’ ukupa- nga indlu ka Rev. Mr. Eish,
akuba ebeze entla- nganisweni yabafundisi E-Rini.

OBULEWE E-QONCE.—Kute ngalo Mgqibelo udluleyo, E-New Town E-Qonce,
kwavakala uku­ba kuko umntu obuleweyo, ngokuhlwa, ngasenkantini ka
Bryce. Kuvakala ukuba kute ngexa lesitandatu Umxosa otile waya kufuna
utywala selenxilile kade. Ute umnini walo nkantini akumbona eselenjalo
akavuma ukumtengela, wati makapume, kuba ebelwazi ucuku Iwake.
Akavumanga kodwa yena Umxosa, kwada kwatu- nyelwa ukuba kuye kubizwa
ipolisa. Kute xa lisaye kubizwa, iqela elitile lamanye Amaxosa lalinga
ukumtsala ukuba emke, pakati kwabo kuko abati ngu Jacob. Lomxosa
unxilileyo usuke waselerola isitshetshe emhlaba entliziyweni U- Jacob,
wafa kwa oko. Inxila elo libanjwe lafakwa etolongweni. Alikatetwa ityala
lalo. Asikuko nokuba ubunxila nokulwa kuyanda pakati kwabantsudu.
Lemihla ingakanje zimpambano zodwa.

AMABULU NABESUTU. — Kweziveki zisand’ ukudlula lite iqela elitile labe
Sutu, amadoda amashumi asixenxe, layakudlula E-Free State lipete imipu,
livela kwele Diamond. Kute xa base Boshof babonwa ngu Inspector
Reyneveld Inkosana yamapolisa, yabayalela ukuba bayini- kele imipu yabo,
kuba umteto welozwe ngowokuba kungabiko mntu udlula nompu engenamvumelo.
Abavumanga kodwa bona Abesutu ukuyinikela. Kwakuba njalo kusuke
kwasekuliwa, kwabulawa Abesutu ababini, bakwababini nabanxwelerwayo.
Ihle lonto sekute ratyn kakulu, kwati kwakuba mnyama abesutu bazimela
babuyela kwasemva. Ngoku baman’ ukutyolana ngokuti ngonantsi aba- qale
ukudubula.

EZAMATYALA E-DIKENI.—Kuko ityala elisand’ ukuziswa kwimantyi yase
Dikeni. Bekuze ama­doda amabini, iyileyo ibanga intombazana ebuda­la
buyiminyaka elishumi elinesitandatu. Bekuse- kulixesha ihlala kwenye
yalomadoda, kodwa ibisand’ ukubalekela kwenye isiti ifuna uncedo. Kute
xa kubuzwa izizatu zokuba kubenje, kwabo- nakala ukuba inesihlobo
esitile ebifanele ukugci- nwa siso, kuba abazali bayo bengaseko. Iteke
Imantyi, lontombazana ingazinyulela umntu efuna ukuhlala kuye zide
zibizwe izihlobo zayo, kuviwe ukuka zitinina. Ate lamadoda ebeyibanga
akakoliswa sesisigwebo, enye yawo yayitwala yayigodusa. Ite Imantyi
yatumela ukuze lomfo abeko ematyaleni, eze nayo lontombazana. Ute kodwa
lomfo weza engenayo. Kwakuba njalo Imantyi imfake etolongweni, yati
akayi kupuma ingafika- nga intombazana. Ngemini elandelayo izile
lontombazana ihamba nezihlobo zalomfo usetolo- ngweni. Ite yakufika
yamnika ityala lokuba uyibetile. wayinyazela ukuba ihlale emzini wake
ingavumi. Elotyala ulivumile, wazake yena nomntu obemceda banikwa iveki
etolongweni besebenza kanzima. Bati noko sebesiti bozakurola imali
eninzi yokuze bakululeke ayavuma ukuyitabata Imantyi, yati ikonje yona
ize kunceda abangenamandla okuzikusela ngokwabo. Isizatu sokuba ababantu
bayibange kangaka lentombazana kukunxamela ukuze bafumane ikazi layo.
Boba- bini bati bazizihlobo kuyo, noko bezezigama.

  Enyulula, Pesheya kwe Nciba, Kufupi ne Tsomo Post.

JACOB MVINJELWA.

Somerset East, February 18, 1873.

Ndinibikela into eyehlileyo apa. Umfazi ogama lingu Leah August Makure
uzimele nenye indoda, washiya eyake. Ubaleke no Jonas K.wari Umsu- tu.
Bekusekumzuzu beranelwa ukuba bayazana, kodwa bati bakubuzwa ngapambili
bakanyela. Sekuminyaka mitandatu lomfazi atshatayo. Ushiye umtwana wake
wamazibulo; into ilusizike leyo. Ndicela kuzo zonke izipata mandhla
ukuba apo banokufunyanwa kona babanjwe kona nje ngo- mteto wale Koloni.
Impau zabo bantu zezi—U-Leah yintwanazana engenamzimba, ebuso bude,
kwanesilevu eside. U-Jonas ngumdaka omnyama olizwi lincinane xa Ɓtetayo,
ekupeleni kw^(r)amehlo omabini uqatshulwe intlanga zambini elisweni
lilinye, ubude zinyawo ezintlanu.

Bovi SOBIKWA.

Makowetu ndiyapinda ngokunjalo ukubuza umbuzo kubafazi abamadoda akwele
Diamondi. Ngulo, incwadi amadoda ayazitumela E-Koloni kanti abafazi
abazipenduli, basuke bahlale ngo- kungati abakatali. Lisiko
lasekoloninake elo ? lisiko elibi elo lokwenjenjalo. Amehlo ama­doda
akwele Diamondi apa eselebomvu kuku- kangela incwadi ezitunyelwa
ngabafazi bawo. Kebafazi bakowetu kukungena kobulumko na oko? Ndaka
ndeva izwi elatetwa ngumfundisi wase Dikeni pakati kosapo, E-Roxeni
Iwesikolo, esiti tina Mangesi sixolele ukulahlekwa yimali eninzi ngenxa
yamapepa endaba ngendawo nge- ndawo. Nitike ukuba kunjalo zifuneka
kangaka- nanina indaba ezivela kumfazi? Nditike bafazi bakowetu abase
Koloni apa sisebenza ekufeni' Kuko amanye amadoda amatandatu apa E-New
Rush afileyo ngo November. I-Bulu elawa emxu- nyeni lawela pezukomntu
warazukake amatumbu wafa kwangoko lapila I-Bulu lawa isitulu labuya
lavuka. Kude kubengalemini yanamhlanjenje nowabalelaninina amadoda ? xa
selesencwabe- nina ? Hai ukuzeka intombi zamazimba yintlalo
yamaqabambola. Pendulani incwadi ezibalwa ngamadoda enu ningamane
nilibele ukupikisana ngolobolo into yobuheideni; gcinani imini epuma
ngayo iposi. He makowetu ndipenduleni nanya- kenye ndiyakuteta lento
ukuba ndisapilile. Maxa uyitumela incwadi E-Koloni ingapendulwa yinto
embi. Maxosa vukani, ebudengeni bokungabalelani, pendulani amanye
amadoda Maxosa ase Koloni ningati nakuyifumana incwadi nisuke nidole
nihlale nayo E-Koloni apo. Nawe mfazi yenza ngokunjalo wakwamkela
incwadi apo E-Koloni.

Sadlula e-Harrismith, saqabela intaba ze Kwantlamba saya kungena
e-Natal. Kwati xa inqwelo zisemdeni we Natal kwafika ontsundu ohambisa
iposi. Ndamxelala lomfo ukuba ndifun’ ukuziqeshisa komnye umntu,
nalenkosi yam ndingayi shiya kwangoku ukuba ndiyazi apo ndingaya kona.
Wati lo Mzulu ndingaba ndiya kuhlala apo akona elixa ndisafuna inkosi.
Kwati ke kwakuhlwa ndazimela. Inkosi yam elandelayo yati mandiye kuteza.
Yatsho kungeko hlati iyimitana emincinane yodwa. Azibanga ninzi inkuni
endize nazo ndaza ndangxoliswa Yandikalazisa kakulu lonto. Ndingekabi
nazintsuku zininzi kulenkosi kwafika obesicaka sayo ngapambili,
wandixelela ukuba andiyi kuza ndaufumana umvuzo wam kuyo; ukuba yena
bebeke basana naku Mantyi nayo yatembisa ukuba iya kuhlaula kanti
iyatshiza. Yayinditembise ukuba iyakundinika isheleni ezintandatu
ngenyanga kunye nokudla kwam (umgubo wombona). Yati intliziyo maze
ndizimele. Kwati kwakuhlwa ndaya kuzikelela umgubo ndabopa. Be­nditi apo
ndifike kona sendiboleka imbiza yodwa ndizenzele umqa; nditi ukuba
andenjanganjalo ndite- nge izikwebu zombona omtsha kuba kwakusekwindla.

Kwati xa ndiza kuwela Utukela ndahlangana nendodana eyati mandiyincede
ngokudla ilahlekene nowayo obetwele ingxowa. Sabasa umlilo ecaleni
lomlambo soja umbona endandiwupete. Kungekabi kade kwafika inqwelo kanti
eliqabane lam liyazana nababexeshi bazo. Ndalindela ukuba
uyakundikumbula kuba ndibe ndisand’ ukumnceda. Kwati zakukulula
ezinqwelo kwapekwa inkobe zombona Xa kudliwayo yanga landoda ayindazi,
ndase ndincedwa bubele bomfo esibe singazani kanye naye. Kwakudliwa
bahlala bade bahamba ubusuku besenzelana amabali. Ndati mna kuba
ndandidiniwe ndalala kwaoko. Baman’ ukutabata amalahle bewabeka
kwezingubo ndiqamele ngazo. Zade zatsha ndase ndivuswa ngumlilo.
Ndakubuza ukuba ngubanina lo wenze oku, bandiwa ngentsini bonke. Kwakusa
ndazishiya ezonqwelo. Ndaqubisana nendodana ebibuyela kwinkosi yayo
kupele ixesha ebilicelele ukugoduka. Ndahamba naye ndaya kucela
umsebenzi enkosini yake. Yandibuza igama lam nokuba ndingowasipina
isizwe yandixelela ukuba ayibaqashi Abesutu. Omnye umfo wati
uyakundinika isheleni ezintlanu ngenyanga andavuma ndati ndifuna
isitandatu. Ndadlula apo ndaya kulala kubafo abantsundu abahambisa iposi
bandenzela ububele obukulu. Bandixelela ukuze ndinduluke kwakusasa ukuze
u-baas angandiboni.

Ndesuka apo kwakusasa ndaya kwindlu ebihlala abantu abasebenza indlela.
Ndacela ukuba bandivumele ndote bati make ndihlaule. Ndakuyilandula
imali beza kufuna ezingxoweni zam bati bakuba bengafumani nto
bangayipangayo base bendihluta enye yengubo zam, bati ke mandote.
Bandipa nomqa wetanga nomgubo wombona. Ndahamba yonke lomini kwaza
ngokuhlwa ndaya kufika kumlambo omkulu onomtantato abati ngu Mpofana.
Kwakuko indlu enkulu eyayimi kona endaya kucela kuyo indawo yokulala,
abake bavume abantu bayo kuba ndandingenamali. Ndase ndilala pandle
kwakufupi nendlu. Ekuseni yancamisa ingqele, kwanzima kum nokuba
ndipakame. Ukusuka apo ndafika kwiqela elalisimba indlela. Ndati
ndakucela umsebenzi kumongameli wandinika incwadana yokuba ndiye nayo
edolopini. Lomini ndalala kumzi wefama elalikufupi no Mgeni. Kulondawo
zandenzela ububele obukulu izicaka. E-Pieter Maritzburg ndaya kufika nge

INTLANGANISO YABAFUNDISI E-FREE
STATE.

INTLANGANISO yabafundisi bamacala ngamacala ebako kanye ngomnyaka e-Eree
State, ibiko okwesitatu ngomhla 27 ka October no mini ezilandelayo.
Abafundisi abebeko ibingo Rev. A. Mabille wase Morija e-Lusutu, Rev.
James Scott wase Bloemfontein, Rev. Mr. Wynne wase Harrismith, Rev. Mr.
Harris wase Thaba Nchu, Rev. G. A. Maeder wase Witzieshoek, Rev. C.
Fraser wase Philippolis, Rev. C. Theron wase Bethlehem, Rev. G. Dadloff
wase Bloemfontein no Rev. Mr. Smith wase Aliwal North. Bekuko neqela
labangengabo abafundisi.

Esihlalweni kucelwe ukuba kuhlale u-Rev. James Scott, ancediswe ngu Rev.
A. Mabille, aze u-Rev. Mr. Maeder abe ngumbaleli. Ekuqaleni
kwentlanganiso ubeko no President Brand Umongameli we Free State. Emva
komtandazo kutetwe ngokuhambiseka komsebenzi kwindawo ngendawo
ezibafundisi bebekona.

U-Mr. Mabille we French Mission ute kwelabo icala e-Lusutu banezikolo
ezikulu ezi 13 namapandle 60. Kulomnyaka udluleyo kwamkelelwe erementeni
abantu abamakulu mane; abasengamabuzwa bamaku lu matandatu. Izikolo
zokufundisa amadodana nentombi e-Morija nase Thaba Bosigo zisahambiseka
kakuhle. Umsebenzi wokuhamba kwelizwi e-Lusutu uke wapazanyiswa ngaba
profetikazi bobuxoki. Ukububa kuka Mr. Catchet obengumzekelo omhle
kunene ngendlela, zonke kushiye isikewu esilusizi. Asafuneka kakulu
amadoda okushumayela. Inani labantu e-Lusutu lifike 130,000; pakati
kwabo abafundisi abakoyo balishumi elinesixenxe abamhlope, bangamashumi
amatandatu abashumayeli abantsundu. Ku Besutu abase Nomansland
abafundisi be French Mission bamise izikolo ezibini ezikulu
ezinamapandle asixenxe nasibozo.

Kwisixoxo esilandelcyo kuvakale ukuba kulo Mandla ka Mr. Mabille yedwa
kuko imizi 400, abantsundu abamncedisayo ba 22 kupela. Inani labantu
abeza etyalikeni nge Sabata likolise ukuba 20,000. Zonke inkosi
ngapandle kwezimbini ziwucasile umsebenzi wabafundisi, inxenye yazo
iqelene nencwadi zika Bishop Colenso.

U-Rev. James Scott ubalise ngokuhamba komsebenzi wabafundisi, beremente
yama Wesile kummandla wase Bloemfontein. Inani lamalungu liku 3,200
inkoliso yawo ngabantu abantsundu. Abasekulingweni 900. Abadla ngokuba
setyalikeni ngomhla we Sabata 10,000. E-Cronstadt nase Colesberg kuko
umsebenzi omkulu ohambisekayo pakati kwabantsundu, kodwa e-Pretoria
akuko nto inkulu ihambisekayo ngenxa yezixakaniso ezikoyo. Iremente yama
Wesile yauqala umsebenzi kulondawo kwange 1843 umnyaka ekwamiswa ngawo
isikolo sase Thaba Nchu. Iziqamo zomsebenzi walomnyaka udluleyo
azifikanga apo bekunqweneleka, nokoke kuko izizatu zokuba kubulelwe.
U-Mr. Wynne unesikolo sabantsundu e-Harrismith esiquba kakuhle kanye.
Ulusizi kakulu kukwanda kobunxila kulo Mahlulo.

U-Mr. Smith ute usand’ ukuwuqala umsebenzi e- Aliwal North. Iqelana
asenalo nga Besutu abatandatu abavela ku Mr. Mabille. Unesikolo
sangokuhlwa nesakusasa esifundiswa nguye nenkosikazi yakwake. Inani
labantwana lidla ngokuba 40. Uzamela ukumisa isikolo sokufundisa
imisebenzi. Usandukuqala iqelana labantwana elifundiswa ilizwi elinika
amatemba amakulu.

U-Mr. Maeder ubalise ngokuqaleka komsebenzi welizwi kuma Bulu
e-Witziehoek. Waqala ngamalungu 15 ayelunge nabafundisi be French
Mission, ngoku anyuke anga 70. Inkoliso yabantu abammeleyo iyeza
etyalikeni nge Sabata. Usand’ ukuqala izikolo ezibini ezihanjwa
ngabantwana abakumashumi amane namahlanu sisinye. Iruluneli yase Free
State iyancedisa ekuzixaseni. Umsebenzi uyanda. Kwabantsundu ngokukodwa
uselude waya kupatelela e-Harrismith. Amafama akona akakucasile
ukuhanjiswa kwelizwi pakati kwabantsundu ukuba aya boniswa kakuhle.

Kutetwe nangezinye izinto ezinje ngokufundiswa kwemisebenzi, ne mfundo
nokuqutywa kwelizwi ngabantsundu, ngobunxilanokuba angatiwa nina amasiko
abantsundu acasene nelizwi. Kute xa iza kucitakala intlanganiso kwenziwa
isidlo.

IMBALI YENKEDAMA.
No. 4.

KULEYO inkosi ndanduluka ebusuku. Ndaseke ndayixelela kwangapambili
ukuba ndifun’ ukumka ayavuma. Ukuya kufuna kwam inkonzo ngelixesha
ndandingxamele ukufumana intwana yokudla ize ke ndidlule. Ndabuyela
kwase Magaliesberg ukuya kucela incwadi. Eloxa Amabulu ayesilwa na
Magwabana, isizwe Sabesutu, engavumi ukuba umntu ontsundu ahambe
ngapandle kwepas. Ukusuka apo ndaya kulomlambo bati yi Moyi. Kulondawo
ndaqeshwa li Bulukazi elalingenasicakakazi ndaqeshelwa ukupeka neminye
imisebenzi yendlu. Ngomhla wokuqala kwatiwa mandihlambe izitya. Ndati
kuba ndingayiqelile lonto ndapula zazine kwangalomini. Ndabona
inkosikazi yalendlu iwatata amaceba iwagcina, ndati ngentliziyo fanukuba
iwagcinela ukuze ibonise wona ematyaleni mhlaumbi ingandiniki yonke
imali yam. Ndasendibaleka ndiya kuziqeshisa kumqubi wenqwelo owatembisa
ukundinika isheleni zontlanu ngenyanga, nokoke akaze wayirola nokukanye
kanti ndade ndanyanga ntatu kuye. Walaisha izikumba ezisa e-Durban
e-Natal, kwatiwa mandikokele, umsebenzi ke lowo ebendingazanga ndiwenze
ngapambili. Yamane ukuxinga futi inqwelo xa ndiyikokeleyo. Benditi xa
kufikwe kwindawo enegumbi indlela ndihambe mna njengokungati akuko
sipani emva kwam, izeke inqwelo ixinge. Noko umbexeshi ibili Lawu, ibiti
yakuxinga inqwelo ize kunceda nenkosi yam. Ngenye imini yati endaweni y
okubeta inkabi yezakubeta mna ngesiwepu nesabokwe. Yakupuma kulondawo
inqwelo ndaya kutabata impahla zam ndifuna ukumka. Lakundibona Ilawu
lakwaza enkosini lati “Baas! baas! u-Jonas uyemka” Ite inkosi “upi?”

nezandla nentamo, ndanekwa emhlabeni. Yandibeta inkosi yam ngesiwepu
isipindile, kuba yai yisikulule entongeni. Ekuqaleni yake yalibala inani
lemivumbo yabuya yadinwa. Ibiti yakuba nepika ike ipefumle ize ibuye
iqale kwakona. Kwada kwatiwa “Hambuye kubamba intambo sifede ndini
somxosa!’ Ndabuza ukuba ndingake nditete izwana na, kwatiwa woyiteta
lonto kwakukululwa. Sakufika kwindawo yokukululwa kwenkabi ndati

UMANYANO LWASE MAMFENGWINI.

  ELIBANDLA kutiwa ukubizwa kwalo yi Fingoland District Society, layilwa
  ngo February womnyaka we 1882. Ati ke Amamfengu a Pesheya kwe Nciba
  avumelana pakati kwawo ukuba ngo mnyaka ileyo indoda irole 2/6. Le
  mali iti yakuba ihlanganisiwe icitelwe ekulungisweni indlela, neminye
  imisebenzi yolohlobo engaba lilungelo kulo lonke lipela elo lase
  Mamfengwini. U Rulumente naye unoncedo alwenzayo, ngokurola ongezelele
  imali engangaleyo ihlanganisiweyo, iponti pezu kweponti.

  Imali eseyi roliwe ukuzisa ku November lo we 1884, izi ponti ezi
  £3,191 1s 0d, kubandakanywa nepume ku Rulumente; kuyo ke eseyicitiwe
  izi ponti ezi £2,602 19 4.

  Liti ke iqela lezibonda zendawana ezilunge kwimimandla emitatu, eyile:
  i-Tsomo, i-Nqamakwe ne Gcuwa, ezo ziye kuhlangana kwa Mantyi yazo
  kanye nge nyanga; zabeke ukwenjenjalo zisiya ukuba zihlangane ndawonye
  nayo, kuvunyelwane ngenani emalikutshwe ukuba kuhlaziywe indlela;
  inani ke noko elingenakuba ngapezulu kwe ponti ezi £50 ngenyanga;
  ekungati ukuze kudluliswe koko ibe londawo yoka iye kukangelwa yi
  Komiti Enkulu, yona ke ehlangana kanye nganyanga ntatu kumzi we Mantyi
  Enkulu, engu Mongameli no Mgcini Mali, yo Manyano olo. Amanye amalungu
  e Komiti leyo zi Mantyi zonke zama Mfengu, ize ibe ke zizibonda eziti
  kulemimandla njengokuba imitatu nje kupume zibe lishumikulowo.

  Kuti ke yakuba imiswe ngokumiswa imali leyo, ekoyo
  nec'itiweyo, yakuba ixeliwe nendlela ec'itwe ngayo, kwaza
  kwaxoxwa ngendawo ngendawo,—yonke ingxelo itunyelwe
  ku Rulumente onke amaxeslia emva kwanyanga ntatu.

  Ingxelo ye Komiti eyenziwe ngoku ukuhlangana kwayo
  ngo 4 ku November, 1884, yokuma kwe mali ze Fingoland
  District Society yofunyanwa njengoko injalo kumhlati we
  zaziso kweli pepa.

UMNTU apa e Alice obe kankanywa ukuba une ngqakaqa siyavuya ukuva ukuba
uqubela ekupileni kanye. Siyatemba ukuba kananjalo uyakuba ngowokuqala
nowokugqibela oya kukankanyelwa esisifo apa kwesi sitili. Unjalo tina
owetu umnqweno apa. Kutiwa kuko ababini abanayo e Ncwazi. Lumkelani
indwendwe zelocala.

Ngati ukuba asilumki zonke ezinye intlanga ziya kusishiya. Kuko isiteto
esiti—“ Azipali ngakupala kunye,” ekubonakala kunjalo sakutelekisa
ukupala kwezinye intlanga zakufika kweli letu. Ama Jelimani namhla ale,
kanti afika sibutya, sidyiba, siziny'itilili kade ngolu Iwetu upalo.
Namhla kufika ama Indiya nama Tshayina, ayadlula nawo, kuba apala
ngolunye uhlobo. Yinene le yokuba ama Tshayina nama Indiya aya dlula.
Sotabata imizi ibe mibini i Bayi ne Tinara ukubonisa ukuba loma “
raqasi,” (sasingaba sitsho ukuwatuka ukufika kwawo) ayemka.

Bokumbula abalesi be Sigidimi ukuba kuko umhlobo wetu ob’eke ese Tinara,
owaka wasibalisela kweli pepa ngokutengiswa komhlaba we Municipality
ongasentla kwe Naba, kwindawo eyaziwa ngabakona ngokuba yi Boschpad.
Wati lomhlaba kuqala watengiswa ngezikonkwane ezilingene isitiya nesiza
sendlu enye, baza beb'ukuqu nabamnyama ukutenga kanti basakanukiswa.
Zaditywa zonke ezo zikonkwane zazi kumashumi amabini, zenziwa maqela
mane, lenyuka inani, zaza zema nabe Lungu bodwa. Abanye kwaba Belungu
bafuna ukuba baqeshise ngazo kwa kwimidaka; ayake itsho. Afikile ama
Tshayina, abona ukuba lilifa lonto. Namhla olo dada lwe Boschpad luhlaz’
uduna, apo kumigushe, makapetshu, matswele, zityukambile, zibatata,
zitapile; ongati ungene kona usuke uqwi siduli. Wanqika umfo wase China
walahla emvikweni ezo zipunzi, wabeta ngento entle intsimi. Namhla
imalike yase Tinara ungafika eyona nto ininzi izikiliva zama Tshayina,

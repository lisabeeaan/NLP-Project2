  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1877.

ISICELO KU MADODA OHLANGA.

MZALWANA,—

Ungaba mhlaumbi usel’ usazi ukuba pakati kwetu BANTSUNDU uya unyuka
ngoku nyuka UMNQWENO wokunga singa fumana imfundo ne lizwi lika Tixo.
Akulindelekile ukuba side kuwe sitete ngokubuleleka kokubako ko mnqweno
onjalo, nama tamsanqa esiya kuwa fumana tina bantsundu ukuba ute wona
awabuya ngamva, koko wahambela pambili.

EYONA NDAWO esifun’ ukuku kangelisa kuyo yile :—Abafundisi base Lovedale
ngoku nxamela ukunga tinteli lomnqweno sitete ngawo,—ngokuti balandule
indawo kuninzi Iwaba celela ukwamkelwa kwi mfundo ye Institution
ekona,—BAKA INDLU, esikolwa ukuba iyakubadla ngapezu kwe ponti ezima
waka mahlanu, (£5,000). Kekaloku indlu enjalo yeyetu, ebesifanel’ukuti
ukuyi vuyela, nokubulela kwetu, sibonakalise ngoku NCEDISA ekwa kiweni
kwayo.

Asitandabuzi ukuba nawe, Mzalwana, uya kuyibona lonto siyitetayo
ngapandle kokuba siku cancisele ngamazwi amaninzi. Apo inteto yetu
isingisa kona fanel’ukuba seluyi qonda, sitemba ukuba yoti impendulo
yako, ize ilupete oluncedo lufuneka ngoku kulu kangaka, nokuba luyi
mali, nokuba yi nkomo, mhlaumbi ihashe. Asiyi kankanyi igusha, nakuba
nayo iya kwamkelwa ngovuyo. Kodwa bebenga bangati abo banako bancedise
ngentwana ezite putu, ukuze lube nendawana olufeza yona uncedo Iwetu
kulo msebenzi ungaka. Ukuze kuqiniseke ukuba oku sikwenzayo
asiku’rwebeli okwezetw’ iziqu, oti amagama abo bavumayo ukurola
ashicilelwe kwi Sigidimi Samaxosa, ize kuxelwe noko bakurolileyo.

Ngomnyaka we 1873, saka savakaliswa esi sicelo sivela kodwa kuba
fundisi. Sinosizi ukuba kuma kowetu baba babini, naba tatu kupela, aba
pendula nge ponti ngantlanu (£5). Kwatinina ukuze babe mbalwa kangako
nje? Kuti sonke tina bantsundu isicelo esinje, besifanele ukusamkela
ngovuyo, luti noncedo Iwetu silwenze ngentliziyo ezicacileyo, nezingena
mona; saye ukwenjenjalo sibonakalalisa ukubulela kwetu ngama lungelo
emfundo esiwa fumeneyo, nasaza kufunyanwa LUHLANGA. Nokwenzeka kwento
enje ngale, kuya kukutaza ngakumbi nangakumbi, abo bacande ilwandle besi
zisela lentsikelelo.

  PAMBANI J. MZIMBA, Chairman.

LOVEDALE,

November, 1877.

  JOHN KNOX BOKWE, Sec. and Treasurer.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1887.

I-INSTITUTION YASE BLYTHSWOOD.

Nyakenye le Institution yase Blythswood ite nayo kwanje ngezinye i
Institution yahlutwa i grant ngu Rulumente ezincedisa imfundo. Ite
londawo yabonisa ukuba kuyakuqubeka nzima malunga ngecala lemali, kodwa
ko bate abo bongamele lo Institution abatanda kwenza nto ingati
ilincipise inani labo bebesebefundiswa kade. Kubekiswe isicelo kuma
Mfengu sabekiswa nge Committee, isesiti makubeko into ayenzayo malunga
nesi sikewu. Ama Mfengu, ayivumile londawo, ayonza ngesisa, arola
amashumi amahlanu eponti £50 kweso siqingata salomnyaka. Londawo yongeza
kwamanye amashumi amahlanu awaye- rolelwe ukuncedisa ukulungiswa
kwendawo ezonakeleyo ezakiweni zomzi, ngonyaka ongapambili.

Lendawo yalenyameko ingaka yama Mfengu, ukunyamekela le Institution
ayirolele imali eninzi kangaka ekwakiweni kwayo, ibonakaliswe zezi mali
amane ezirola, kwandawonye nokuyixasa ayixasa ngako ngokutumela
abantwana bawo kuyo, abamanani aya enyuka ngokunyuka. Londawo asikuko
nokuba iyabakutaza abo bapete umsebenzi kona.

INTLANGANISO EB’ISE NAZARETE
(SHESHEGU.)

Kubeko intlanganiso emnandi kunene e Sheshegu, ngosuku Iwe 26 ku
October. Lentlanganiso ibi yenye yezimnandi kunene, abantu banikela
ngamagqasi, nokucwayita, okukulu. Kwati ngokuhlwa kwabako ne
tea-meeting, kwavunywa zi Choir ezimbini zavuma kamnandi, zayivuyisa
okunye intlanganiso. Imali eroliweyo emhlope ziponti ezimashumi mabini
anesixenxe, £27 nento ngapezulu. Zalapa inkomo, gusha, bokwe, hangu,
nkuku, nxowa zamazimba, mbona njalo-njalo. Xa yonke lonto iqokele- Iweyo
ngamanani apantsi imalunga namashumi amane £40—Azi kuyakutini na e-Mxelo
nase Roxeni mhla ifike kwezondawo.

INTLANGANISO ZE MVUSELELO
E LOVEDALE.

Kuke kwako intlanganiso ze mvuselelo e Lovedale, kususela kusuku Iwe 16
ku October, kwade kwalolwa 23. Yayizintlanganiso ezazijonge kulondawo
kupela. Zamana ukwenziwa ngokuhlwa kuzo zonke ezontsuku, iyimitandazo,
nentshumayelo ezimfutshane ezinge Ndaba Ezilungileyo,
kushunyayezwa yonke into engabafundi kulo mzi, emhlope kwa nentsundu.
Abafundisi abangapandle kwaba lo mzi abeza kuncedisa ngo Revs. P. J.
Mzimba wase Lovedale, A. Hanesworth wase Bofolo, W. Stuart wase
Burnshill, noE. Makiwane wase Macfarlan.

Babe mbalwa namhla abazinikeleyo, akubangako ntshukumo inkulu ivelileyo
ngenxa yezi ntlanganiso, into enjengaleyo ivuyise abafundisi base
America, abase Natal. Kodwa kona ukushunyayelwa kwe gospeli akunakuswela
ziqamo, nangani singaboniyo apo iye yawela kona imbewu leyo, nalapo
sel’iqala ukuntshula kona. Lona ixa lokuhlwayela lahlala lileletu ;
kanti Iona elokuvuna, lifika kunyenentando ka Tixo.

INTLANGANISO YA PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

Intlanganiso yo Manyano nge Mvo Zabantsundu yahlangana kwa Nomaheya,
kumandla wase Nqamakwe kusasa mhla ngolwe Sihlanu evekini nge 30th ku
September 1887. U Mr. J. Mazamisa wase Hlobo ub’esesihlaIweni. Amanene
azizibonda ayengapezulu kweshumi,zaye intsika zentlanganiso ziko zonke.
O Messrs J.Mazamisa, A. Gontshi, J. Sikwebu, L. Mgidi, K. Dingiswayo, no
K. Masiko, namanene azikataleleyo anje ngo
Messrs P. Lusaseni ayeko. Intlanganiso yabankulu, yazala indlu akwabiko
nendawo yokuhlala.

Imicimbi ebekwe entlanganisweni mitatu. 1 I Trankei
ifanelwe na kuhlomela kwisibeno ngo Mteto we Voti esingu Tungumlomo wase
Koloni.? 2 Umlungu omakanyulwe abe ngum Meli e Palamente ingangubani na.
3 Ukufundwa kwe ncwadi engecebo legqweta le Jaji mayelana nokuba
izibonda ne Titshala zizicaka zika Rulumente na, kangangokuba zibe
pnatsi kwe Civil Service Act umteto ongavumeli izicaka zika Rulumente
zixoxe indaba zolaulo?. U Mr. Gontshi e sukile ute: Uzakutabata indawo
yokuqala, acinga ukuba inkulu. Ute ufuna ukuba intlanganiso

ike iqonde into esisiao isibeno sase Koloni pambi kokuba iwise izwi
layo. Ucaze u Mteto we Voti nje ngoko umiswe yile Palamente
isand’ukungena ungene kwinto esiyiyo; Isibeno ekutiwa ngu Tungumlomo
ucaze umteto ka Mr. Hofmeyr. Enjenjalo—ute
yimfanelo ye Transkei kuzo zonke indawo ezinkulu ezipatelele banzi
eluhlangeni oluntsundu ukuba ivelane,idibano ne Koloni, ekukuseleni
amalungelo ohlanga oluntsundu e South Africa apa. Ute ngayo lendawo
yesibeno unosizi olukulu ukuti singati sipumelele, i Transkei
ingalahlekelwa. Ute u Tungumlomo ufuna ukuba ivoti mayiyekwe ime
njengoko ibinjalo. Ngokwake ukubona
Kunqabile ukubonisa ngobunqina obucacileyo ukuba, u Rulumente ngokwenza
u Mketo wo Xande Iwe £25 0s. 0d. ukuze umntu avote, inga ngokwenjenjalo
wenze iqinga lokukupa abantu evotini, oku umteto, kungenakuboniswa ukuba
ubekise kubantu abamnyama bodwa. U Rulumente unalo ilungelo
lokuyihlambulula ivoti ngalo lonke ixesha, abonayo ukuba kuko kuyo
abangayifaneleyo. Nokuba umketo lo woxande liqinga, nokuba asililo,
imfanelo u Rulumente unayo yokuhlambulula, kwaye kunzima ukubonisa ukuba
umketo ubekise kwabamnyama bodwa. Xa sikangela inqubelo pambili yohlanga
oluntsundu e South Africa, ngokukodwa e Transkei uyakolwa ngumketo
woxande evotini, xa uxakwe ngu Mteto ka Mr.
Hofmeyr. Ute akakolwa ukufumane sibambe ukuvota sonke, namaqaba
nabahedeni, singenamisebenzi yanqubelo pambili yohlanga ebonisayo ukuba
siwafanele amalungelo okuvota. Ukuba ke kwako umteto oyakuba nelungelo
ngecala lohlanga, ngulo mteto womketo wo Xande Iwa £25 0s. 0d. oxaswa
ngu Mteto ka Mr. Hof-
meyr. Into efunekayo eluhlangeni asikuko ukuba kuzale ivoti siti.
Yimisebenzi yenkutalo eyalata ukukanya, eyakuba sihlahla naku Mlungu
ukuba uhlanga oluntsundu oku Iwenza imisebenzi etile, masilungenise
kumalungelo atile. U Rulumente uyekelele kuye wonke ubani, kwi nqenera
ebelivota lingenayo indawo yokuhlala, ukuba lenze umsebenzi otile
oyakuba sihlahla, somoya omhle wenqubelo pambili yohlanga ukuze ungene
evotini. Wonke ubani

  ongelilo ivila elisezantsi kusemandleni ake, ukuba alisabenzele
  ilungelo le voti, ngokukodwa eyakutsalwa ngamalungelo apantsi ko mteto
  ka Mr. Hofmeyr. Indlela yokulwela amalungelo etu, ngobuninzi be voti,
  ayinamalungelo akaulezayo nasimbuku ukunyusa uhlanga. Kanti unenkolo
  enkulu xa abantsundu bayakuguqa ngamadolo balwele amalungelo
  ngemisebenzi yokuzalisa umketo wengxande evotini, yindlela ekaulezayo
  neyakuba neziqamo eluhlangeni exesheni. U Mr. Gontshi utabate imeko
  yezinto e Koloni, wayilinganisa nemeko yezinto e Transkei.
  Abantu abaninzi ngamaqaba naba heyideni, bambalwa abantu abanokukanya.
  Ukuba isibeno sase Koloni, sipumelele umteto e votini wema nje ngoko
  ubunjalo singagqunyelwa ngamaqaba naba heyideni—Mhlaumbi utshaba
  olufuna ukungenisa utywala pesheya kwe Nciba, ngokutemba ivoti

  ngenxa yobuninzi be voti
  yamaqaba naba heyideni, ngapandle ko mteto ono mketo evotini, akukonto

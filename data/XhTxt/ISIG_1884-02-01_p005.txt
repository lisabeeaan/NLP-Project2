sikolo ze ingabi nakulifumana ibaso layo ke i Titshala xa abantwana
bengapumelelanga pambili. Nto ngade incede kwesi simo kukuba u Rulumente
acelwe ngencwadi ukuba awususe pezu kwamagxa abantu umtwalo wokuhlaula
imali zezikolo ; kodwa kubeko endaweni yayo irafu etile eyakuba
sendaweni yayo. Amaqegu abo kambe ako, incwadi leyo ke ingab'alwa ngo
Messrs Bokwe, Jabavu, no Xiniwe.

IDLAKA LIKA SOGA UMFO KA JOTELO.

Sibe sihambile sada saya kufikelela kweso sikolo sise Mgwali kulo mhlaba
ube ungoka Sandile ube umiwe nga Mangqika. Sive indaba ngokucacileyo
ngomsebenzi woku- ncwaba amatambo ka mfi u Soga omdala yena. Kuvakala
ukuba lengwevu yasema Jwareni ibite ngale mfazwe ka Ncayecibi ayapuma
kwaoko kondiyalwa. Nakuba inkoliso yonyana bayo bebese sikolweni e
Mgwali, bembiza uyise lowo ukuba aye kubo, akayibonanga londlela. Ude
omnye unvana obenaye uyise lowo, wazama ukumfudusa ngokuse- lesuka
equbula inkomo eziquba. Ixego litume amadoda ukuba aye kuzisonga
azibuyise; enjenjalo ke. Lite xa ilizwe selife kwada kwako abafayo
kulomblaba ka Sandile wati ukusuka kuka Soga emzini wake akaya e Mgwali
esikolweni wakumbula ku Anta, kwaye kungaseko xesha, sebezintsali
abantu. Wahamba namntu mnye. Kute kumgama otile gqi umkosi wama Mfengu
angaku Rulume- nte, wonda ngabo ; kuye kufikelela ndoda itile kuqala ku
Soga ngokubashiya ngamendu abanye, ingu Mpondomse ngesizwe. Yakufika ute
guququ msinya umfo ka Jotelo, wenqumama, wateta amazwi ayelele kula, “
Yintoni ondisukela ngayo ? mna ndingu Soga.” Ute lomfo ungena nceba kuba
ebeselete qiwu umkonto kade akapulapula, waselebinza eyihlaba longwevu
yawela apo. Ute wakufika umkosi wamazi umfo ka Jotelo, wamtetisa lomfo
wenze oko ulusizi kukupalala kwelo gazi.

Lomfo ka Jotelo ngesizwe ngum Jwara. Ama Jwara akwizizwe zasema Xoseni
nezase Mbo. Afunyanwa kuma Bele kwezase Mbo, abe engamapakati
anobukosana obuhlonelwe babekwa kakulu ema Beleni. Lomfo ka Jotelo
ibiyindoda engalicasileyo ilizwi kwanemfundiso yase sikolweni.
Ibimkutaza umfazi nokuba ngumntwana akunone- lela ilizwi nokuba
yimfundo. Umfundisi u Mr. Chalmers kwimbali yonyana ka Soga, umfundisi
ongumfi u Tiyo, uti u Soga ib’ingumfo oliso libazileyo, emde umntu. Kwa
Rulumente kwaziwa yena ukuba ngum Xosa wokuqala owenza umjelo, nowalima
nge puluwa itsalwa zinkabi. Ib’ingumfo onesi Tembu kakulu, abanye bati
abafazi bake bebesibozo, abantwana abazeleyo abangapantsi kwamashumi
amane.

Isidumbu somzimba womfo ka Jotelo salala apo, kungeko bani
ungamncwabayo. Kute ke ngoku sekudlule iminyaka emihlanu, onyana bake
abase Mgwali ngokumane beteta nomfundisi wabo u Mr. Cumming ebomeleza
kulonto baza-

ma indlela yokuba baye kubuta lomatambo baze bawancwabe. Bate kuba
beyivile kakuhle indawo awawela kuyo baya becele incwadi nemvumelo ezi
Mantyini. Bawafumana lomatambo elapo onke, ngapandle kwezandla, nenyawo,
nomhlati ongapantsi. Babuyela e Mgwali nawo, asongelwa kakuhle eziqiyeni
ezimhlope, alungelelaniswa kakuhle ngendawo zawo, alaliswa emkombeni
wokuncwaba, Ngo- mhla we 12 ku December 1883 kwayiwa emncwabeni. Baya
abantu kakulu kwelo ncwaba. Langa nezulu liyaba- vela ; kuba kwati xa
kusekundulukweni legubugubu izulu ngamafu, lafefeza ngemvulana
etambileyo kwada kwagqi- tywa wonke lomsebenzi langa izulu liyalila
nalo. Linjalo idlaka lika Soga umfo ka Jotelo. Sincoma ukalipo oluba-
ngwa lilizwi le Nkosi ukude abanyana banyikanyikane nento zomfi
ezingapatwayo kwa Xosa.

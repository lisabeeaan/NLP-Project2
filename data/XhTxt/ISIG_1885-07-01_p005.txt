ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1885.

AMATEMPILE.

Intlanganiso yomnyaka yama Tempile ibise Bayi nonyaka. Kulo nyaka
ufileyo yayise Graaff-Reinet, ekwati nakuba kwakumiswe indawo ezimbini
eyakuba kuzo eyalo mnyaka, i Tinara ne Bayi, kwabuye kwabonaka1a ukuba
ama Tempile ase Tinara akwisimo sengxakoko, nezipitipiti ezitatele kwase
Ramenteni— kwase kugqitywa ukuba yoba se Bayi. Nakona ko zibo ziko
izixaki, ekute kodwa ngomonde nenkutalo yabalungiseleli bentlanganiso,
zoyiswa, yaza yako ke intlanganiso. Isixaki esikulu zintawelo ebantwini,
ngenxa yelanga ukubalela, nokunqaba kwemisebenzi. Esona sixaki sikulu
noko malunga nale yanonyaka intlangano, kukuba um Bali-micimbi omkulu u
Mr, Burness ebe sezingxingongweni malunga nemali zamanye amabandla,
ezibange ukuba angazikupi ngexesha izaziso, kwaza ke kwako ukupitizela
nokungavisisani. Kubonakele kwa kwinyanga zokuqala zomnyaka ukuba akuko
nto ilungileyo ngecala lombali, yaza ngoko intlanganiso yabuyiswa
ngomva, yeza ku May endaweni ka April. Indawo kambe emandla, kwabo
sukuba i Tempile enkulu iya kuba semzini wabo, kukulungiselela ukwamkela
izitunywa eziza kuvela itempile zazo indaba. Kuti ke kwiminyaka emibi
kungalungi nto. Ate ke namhla ama Tempile ase Bayi, akubona ukuba aya
kuxakwa, acela izihlobo ezitile ukuba zezamkele abaze e Tempileni enkulu
e Bayi, endlwini zazo, endaweni yokuqeshelwa indlu njengapambili.
Okunene ke bagaleleke kwa ngom- Gqibelo kwada kwa ngom-Vulo abavela
kwindawo ngendawo, baza batiwa sa pakati komzi, wati omnye, mhlaumbi
ababini baba komnye umzalwana, njalo-njalo. Ngawo la amagama e “

Ngom-Vulo 25 May, 1885, abutene onke ama Tempile endlu ezintlanu zase
Bayi, etyalikeni yabo Sutu e Russell Road, apo aputunywe kona ligubu
lebandla elintsundu le St Cyprian Benefit Society. Apume apo ama Tempile
sezi baba emoyeni izivato,—kukokele eziluhlaza, kwalandela ezibomvu,
nango ebeka pandle kwe Bayi, lingasabetwa nje igubu livusa umnyele. Aye
atinta ngo ntolongo, atelelwa ngama Tempile e “ Vrede Temple ” apo,
abuya ngo Princes Street, akweza i Queen Street, aza kunyuka ngo Russel
Road, aya angena e Edward’s Memorial Church. Yazala vingci tu lotyalike
inkulu kakulu, kuba yawomeleka yonke lonto yayilandela igubu. Kute
kwakubona ukuba kunge- ne, kwada kwangena nento oti wakuzikangela ubone
ukuba lendlu iyityalike—kumhla zapehlelelwa zizintsana,—ukuba zaka
zayingena, wavala umntu emnyango. Yeka ke! Kwahlokoma limbi kaloku
ixilongo.

Kusuke ingwevu ka Gqadushe u Rev. Henry Kayser, wawati qo qo ambalwa,
wafunda isahluko Ezibalweni

Ezingcwele, waza wavakalisa ukuba kuza kushumayela u Rev. J. P. Philip,
B.A., unyana ka Rev. T. Durant Philip, waae Rafu. Unyule amazwi kweye
Mizekeliso xxin, 31—32. “ Ungakangeli i wayine xa ibomvu, xa ibengezela-
yo ebekilcni, xa ihlayo kakuhle; ukupela kwayo iluma njenge nyoka,
isuzele ngoko nomadudwane.” Ute umfo ka Philip—“ Namhla andinanto
nenxila, andinanto nabazili- Umntu endinento naye apa namhla ngulowa
usela ngobunchtu—(Matig) ngomlinganiso.” Bate kuba bebaninzi abapuza
amatamo abo, esingaba baleli 'manxileni, sababona beboka-boka, befunana
ngamehlo. Ubonisile pakati u Mr. Philip ukuba lento umntu ukwakiwa kwayo
ifana ne Injini le ka Loliwe, kuko mbiza itile efike izaliswe ngamanzi,
kuze kubaswe, ati akubila lomanzi kudaleke lento siti tina
ngumpunga—bati bona si steam. Esi steam singati sivulelwe sonke onakale
umashini, kunjalo nje singati sike sagqita emgceni igqabuke imbiza.
Umzimba womntu ke une steam esinjalo ekufuneka songiwe, salu- nyukelwa
ukuba singenzi ngozi kuye. Lomntu ke umana epuza itamo, eti ufudumeza
umzimba, upelisa amandla e steam somzimba wake, kuba oko kufudumala ati
uva kona akupuza kukupupuma kwe steam okubangwa ngulo mlilo amana
ukuwubasa, kuze kuti kamva eve etyafile, kufuneke ebuye wakwezela kwange
tamo—qoko! itshe imbiza— kukufa kwake oko. Watsho wati okunye lomfo
upuza itamo ufana nombexeshi oliqobola okuseloko emi ngesabokwe
emahasheni, aze ke ngoko adinwe, noko ingamahashe alukuni, ingekapeli
ihambo yawo. Umfo ongaseliyo yena uyazonga amandla ake kuba ihambo yake
inde, ufuna nombexeshi olumkileyo, omana ukuwatinta kuhle ngomkala
amahashe ake, aze afike apo aya kona engati eb’engahambi ’ndlela. Asinto
ikoyo lento ibubu ncatu etywaleni, kuba lilipina inxila elavela
lalinxila kwamhla laqak. ukusela? Abaqali ngokutabata itamo
elingxengiweyo yini, kanti oba mabini ngomso—ngomso ’mnye ibe yi glasi
erwada, engangxengwanga? Ugqibe ngokulinganisela nge Bulu elalinesicaka
esiselayo. Lona noko lalinge mzili laliyi ncatu, eti mhla lowo ipuze
itamo. Site sakuhlala sinxila isicaka esi, sabizwa ngomnye umhla yinkosi
yaso, yati, “ Wa Dyonashe! lento ungena kusela matig,” (eh lizwi lipuma
kwi maat, measure—umlingo) “ Lento unga- seli ngomlinganiselo uxele mna
yini Dyonashe? ” Ute u Dyonashe, | Kambe nkosi, kaundipe umlinganiso—i
maat —kuba igxebeka yi maat, ne glasi yi maat, neli t'unga ndisengela
kulo ikwa yi maat 11 Yabona apo ukuba u Dyonashe mayimyeke aziketele
eyake i maat. Ubonise ngalo mlinganiselo u Mr. Philip ukuba asinto ikoyo
lento ubuncatu, kuba enye yanela yi glasana encinane kanye ngemini,
yimbi kabini, yimbi kahlanu, Enye ngenxa mhlaimbi yokomelela komzimba ne
botile ipela ayimnxilisi; Kodwa zonke zipelela ebunxileni.

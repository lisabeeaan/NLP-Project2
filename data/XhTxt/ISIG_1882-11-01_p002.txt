October, 1882, e Somerset East. Baba baninzi abevayo emixelweni ukuba
indoda enkulu elungileyo idlule. Ezelwe ngomnyaka we 1797, pakati
kwexesha elokunjulwa futi ebalini lase Europe, kuba kungalo u BONAPARTE
wabe pitizelisa ukuzola kwelozwe, inkwenkwana u LENNOX kubonakala ukuba

mabini wagaleleka kweli ngo 3 November 1818, kunye nebuto lamashumi
omatatu anesi bozo (38th Regiment), awabe yinkosana (Captain) kulo.
Kakade, wayeze apa e South Africa nje, ubehle wazika ezimfazweni; iyinto
emnandi ukukumbula ukuba pakati kwazo zonke inguqu-nguqulo ezehla enezwi
kuzo, wayifeza eyake indawo ngohlobo olumnika ukunconywa, olunika imvo
awabe siti ulwela zona imbeko; noluvuyisa izihlobo zake. Ubugora bake,
ukubekeka noku ngcwengeka kwe zenzo zake, inimba abe nayo nge micimbi
eluncedo kubo bonke abemi beli lizwe, ngapandle koku tyumba. bala— zezi
zinto, esiti tina, zobanga igama lika MR. STRETCH ukuba libalwe ngama
gama egolide ezinkumbulweni zaba ninzi. Ngomnye wama doda, pakati
kwawake anento nemicimbi yolaulo, awali sebenzela eli lizwe ngapandle
koku katalela eyawo inzuzo, azinikela enga sekeleze nto.

Singati kwaku ngomntu ontsundu ikakulu ati amalinga ka MR. STRETCH
enziwa. Igama lokuba ngu XOL’ILIZWE, awabe saziwa ngalo ngoku banzi
ngabe nteto yetu, liteta into eninzi malungana nokux'unyiswa kwake
ngabamnyama pakati kwaba tandi bento engumntu. Oko wabe kwenza emzini

awabe seloko elungele, enemvume ukulunika abo balufunayo—eli cala
lempilo yake lelona mhlaimbi, lite qampu, nakuba mhlaimbi lingaziwa
kakulu. Kambe ngoluhlobo lwezenzo umenzi onyanisekileyo wovuzwa ngoku
zeleyo ngulowo wati, “Ekubeni nenjenjalo nakum“nye waba bazalwana bam

nakwi xesha abe sele twele imitwalo yokwalupala ulwimi lwake aluzange
lupumle, nosiba lokubala akazange walubeka pantsi—konke oku ekwenzela
abantu bakowetu. Wabe njalo u XOL’ILI­ZWE okufa kuyi lahleko kwaba sese
bunyameni naba bandezelweyo.

IMPAWANA.

Asimangaliswa ngumzalwana wetu u Mr. John Knox Bokwe ukuba ati namhla
ukuvuka kwake, nokuba kuse kupumleni, nokuba kuse butongweni akade
esitele kubo, ahlabele iculo “Lokuzukisa”! Abafundi betu bozibonela
isizekabani komnye we mihlati yezaziso zale nyanga, apo kubikwa
okuhlileyo ngomhla we 15 ka October. Okokwetu, nase gameni laba fundi
betu siya vuyisana nomzalwana wetu ngoko kumhleleyo, ngokukodwa kuba
kumkumbuze enye yemfanelo zake esi Gidimini, ebeku bonakala ukuba ufuna
ukuyi libala.

Incwadi ka “Rodolo,” umbaleli ose Healdtown, yenze ukulunga okukulu
ibete abenzi bama benu-bentsu awabalisa ngawo umbaleli wetu, babonakale
bebancinane kanye. Bofunda ngomso ukuba izinto abazenza besiti aba bonwa
zobengezwa ezincocoyini ze tempile, nokuba mhlayimbi itempile leyo
asiyiyo i “Jubilee.” Siva ukuba kuko abantu abaranelwa ngokuba ngabo
ababengezi balo mahlazo, ekute ke bakangelwa ngentlaka zamehlo, kwabo
benzi balento singati mabanqumame ekwenjeni njalo, sobabiza ngama gama
abo apa esi Gidimi­ni, baziwe nguye wonke.

Intlanganiso yebandla lama Bulu ale Ring ekukwako ntetwana ngayo kwenye
indawo kwakwezi “Mpawana” yokunjulwa ngendawo yokuba yati nge Cawa
ngokuhlwa yasi zisela u Rev. Professor Marais, wase Stellenbosch
Theological College (i Semnari yoku qeqesha aba fundisi). Eli nene
lingum fundisi lashumayela nge Cawa, 15 October ngokuhlwa, kwabo bahlala
kulomzi, intshumayelo ebushushu, ezele zimfundiso ezipatelele ebomini
bomntu—ukukatalelwa kwabo ukuze buhlumele entsikelelweni—eyaka yaba
ngumgcobo wetu ukuyi pulapula. Lengxoxo yabi sekwe pezu kobom balo ndoda
yabi lungile u Danyeli wakudala, abaziyo abafundi bezi Balo ukuba yabiyi
ndoda yomtandazo. Intloko zobom bayo zacazwa zamhlope njengoku kanya kwe
langa. Nanamhla sisawa nandipa amazwi e Professor.

ihle e Bedford, ibaliswa lipepa lakona i Advertiser. Ute xa u Rev. Sydon
ashumayelayo yesuka inja esazulwini setyalike, yekokoko ukusinga
esikwelweni ihamba ijoja, itimla, ngangokuba umfundisi angabi nako ukuyi
yekelela into leyo. Enye ngomhla we Cawa apa e Lovedale ayi cebanga

ukuba odade abahlala besiza nayo ukuba ize kudla isonka sosapo abayi
lumkeli. Sisayi kumbulu nge ntsini into eyehla kwindlu yetyalike
engekude kakulu kwi ntaba ye Boschberg, mhla omnye wabakokeli,
etyalikeni, xa kushunyayelwayo wayitabatayo inja wayi pakamisa ngo msila

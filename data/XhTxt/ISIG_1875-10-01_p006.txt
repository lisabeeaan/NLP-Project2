ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 1, 1875.

INTLANGANISO EBINGENDLU
YETYALIKE.

St Matthews, September 17, 1875.

Ngosuku lwe 1'5 ku September 1875 besi ne- ntlanganiso emnandi kunene
apa kulomzi wakwa St Matthews, ebiyeyokunikela imali yokunceda ukwaka
ityalike entsha kona. Kute ngolosuku kwabanda kunene ngexesha lakusasa,
sada soyikela ukuti isininzi sezihlobo zetu asiyi kufika, kute noko
ngenxa yempembelelo ka Mr. Fielding Imantyi yase Xesi ate wanceda ngayo
ngokuhlangana nenkosi zeta azimeme, babaninzi abantu ngapezu koko
besitemba kona. Siyambulela kunene u-Mr. Fielding ngempembelelo yake
nangokuba ko kwake kulontlanganiso. He! kute ngolosuku mayelana nexesha
lesitoba kusasa kwangenwa emtandazweni omfutshane kwati wakupuma kwake
kwahlalwa umzuzwana omayelana ne yure enye kwa buya kwangenwa, yavulwa
intlanganiso ngu Rev. C. Taberer umfundisi wakona ngomtandazo omfutshane
wokncela intsikelelo yo Somandla pezu komsebenzi lowo ebesihlanganisene
ukwenza wona. Kwati kwakugqitywa u-Mr. Fielding obesesihlalweni wesuka
waposa amazwana exela into esihlangene ngayo’nokulunga kwayo wati
ebetemba ukubona isininzi ngalemini kodwa uyakolwa ukuba ezinye izihlobo
zetu zitintelwe lizulu, kodwa noko uyatemba. ukuba zobuye zifike, wanika
amazwi okukutaza kanye wahlala pantsi. Kusuke u- Rev. C. Taberer
umfundisi walomzi waxela isizatu sokuba kufuneke ityalike entsha enkulu
kunaleyo besinayo kakade esisesi sokuba, le sinayo ayiyilingani
intlanganiso, mhla sukuba abantu bebaninzi,kude kupunyelwe pandle.
Wabonisa nohlobo ekufanele ukuncediswa ngalo ngabafunayo ukuwunceda
lomsebenzi nokulunga kwawo nokubukwa kwawo ngu Tixo xa abantu bewenza
ngotandoluka Tixo : wahlala pantsi. Ute osesihlalweni wacela inkosi engu
William Kama ukuba ipose amazwi isukile ke inkosileyo. (Yayinto entle
kanye ukubona inkosi zohlanga Iwetu zihleli ziwunyamekele umsebenzi onje
ukulunga kwawo.) Ate amehlo abo bonke abasendlwini anamatela
kuye,inxenye ikumhlana imbonayo inxenye imazi kakade. Ute waposa
amazwana amnandi okuxela ukulunga nokufaneleka nokufuneka kokuba abantu
babe ne ndlu yetyalike nokuba yinto eyenziwa ngabantu ngemali. Waxela
nokuwuvuyela kwake lomsebenzi, watsho warola £6, wahlala pantsi. Kucelwe
inkosi u-Edmund Sandile wesuka wati—Mnandi ngu Mgqunukwebe, ndize kubona
into eniyenzayo nina mpi yase Mtwaku. Kambe into seyipambi kwenu akuseko
kutini kuba seyiko. lyinto elungileyo kunene indlu yetyalike, sekuxeliwe
ukuba bake bade abantu bahlale pandle xa babaninzi; yilweni, watsho wati
akuseko kutini into seyiko seyimelwe kukwenziwa, kulungile kanye ukuba
yenziwe, watsho wati ndikupa inkabi enkulu yenkomo ne £1 ngapezulu.
Kwacelwa inkosi unyana ka Ntontela waposa amazwana okubonisa ukuyi- vuma
kwake ukulunga kwalento watsho naye wabeka imali. Bababaninzi abasukayo
babeke, bebeka ngokucayita kanye. Ndite ndakubona ukucayita kwabo
ndakumbula ilizwi lomfo ka Zuma lokuti “musa ukuputuzela.”
Ndingasendisiti bebebeka into ete putu, abanye bebeka ngenkomo, abanye
nge £15 nange £10 nanga pezulu. Into ebukeke kunene
kukunikelakwabantubakowetu abangamaqaba; hai ke kona ekunikeleni asinto
ubungake uti eli lelamaqaba icala. Kwabonakala ukuba abeka ngokucayita
kanye, into endite ndakuyibona ndati ukuba intlanga zakowetu
ziyakuzingisa ukwenjenje alise kude ixesha ayakuti osapilileyo abone
izinto ezinkulu pakati kwalo. Enye into eyabukeka kunene kukurola
kwamadodana amatsha, nangakumbi azi titsher zalapa nabancedisi, zide
zati zona ititsher zangati zixelelene ukuti zezinga roli ngapantsi kwe
£10. (Lento ke ibonisa ukuba kuko imibulelo yokufundiswa

kwabo bafuna ukubonisa uhlanga ukuba lento abantu bancedwa maxa
bazincedayo nabo ngokwabo.)

Kute kusenjalo besanxamile abantu ukunikela kwafika ixesha lokuba bake
baye kuzixasa ngento zokutya ; kute kwakubuywa kwangenwa ya kwa ngu
washiywa ngase kunikeleni! Kwakaloku sekufika nezihlobo zetu zamanye
amacala ezite nazo zabonakalisa ukuwuvuyela kwazo umsebenzi zarola ngoku
cayita okukulu. Pakati kwezihlobo zetu zamanye amacala ndiya kubalula
abe mnye, kungengakuba ezinye andina ndawo ndingazidumisa ngayo;
kungokuba lo usenze sahleka kakulu. Ndiyatemba ukuba wondixolela ngoku
kankanya igama lake. Ite lendoda yangena yahlala, ite ngomzuzwana yesuka
kwabonakala ukuba fanele ukuba inxamile yaposa amazwana, yaxela ukuba
ingumhambi ibona ukuba kuko into eyenziwayo apa kungoko icinge ngokuti
mayike ize kuvela. Ke ifika impi ibambene ngazo ngokoke kuba ikwa yeya
kowayo ngenye indlela, ifanele ukuba ike nayo ibinze, yatsho yati
ndifuna ukuze ndipatele nomfundisi wam indaba, into endiyibone e-St
Matthews yatsho yambiza ngegama. Ite apo isihlekise kona yati tapu yati
ke ekubeni ndibona ne ngwevu ezindala apa nendizaziyo ukuba kudala
zaguqukayo, ndisuka ndingaboni nto yenziwayo nazizo, sike ndimane ukuva
oziponti nga £5 nanga £4 sendiyakusuke ndibeke ipeni le ibomvu ndingabi
senza nto. Watsho wayirola wahlala pantsi. Ute kanti upangelelene
nexesha lokuzakubeka kwama dodana ate abeka £10 inye, ite lendoda
yasemzini yasuka yati ekubeni ndisiva lonto ndirola igusha yarola
nemali. Yatike lento yenziwayo ngulomfo yamnandi kanye, nana- mhla
siyamkumbula wabavusa ngakumbi abantu ukuba bazidele babone ukuti yini
umhambi ukusidela. Lomntu nditeta ngaye ngumfo ka Nqadini kwa Zidenge.
Andinakuzigqiba izinto zalomini andikolwa okokwam ukuba ndake
ndasentlangenisweni emnandi njengale ekwa yeyoluhlobo. Ngoku mandinazise
ngenani elarolwayo mawetu lilo eli, £342 6s. 0 1/2d. Elinani ke
lelabantsundu kuba kaloku kwakutiwe mabazame bodwa. Kwa kungeko mangesi
kulontlanganiso ngapandle kuka Mr. Fielding, ngokuba umfundisi yena
ukwasiti andingembali ke ngoko xa apakati kwetu. Iya buleleka lendawo
yokuba elinani leletu sedwa eli ndilibalileyo enditembayo ukuba amangesi
abantu abatanda kunene ukunceda abanye bangati abayi- bonileyo lento
bancede kanye, nditi banceda na- bangazincediyo ngokwabo, akungebiko
matanda- buzo okuba xa sizincedayo banga telela. Izihlobo zetu ezinye
ezidala zalomzi sezi pesheya kwenciba, enditembayo ukuba beziselapa
ngebezinikele into, kuletyalike siyakayo. Ndinetemba ukuba nangoku
zimgamanje zisakumbula ngalorazi. Singavuya kakulu ukuba zingati zincede
ngento ezinganceda ngazo ; kunge kuzo zodwa nakwezinye izihlobo
ezingatandayo ukubona lento ihambiseka. Siyatemba ukuba baninzi
abawutandayo umsebenzi onje nokuba upi, nje ngalo ka Nqadini ; uncedo
nokuba luvela kubanina lungamkelwa ngovuyo ngumfundisi wakwa St Matthew.
Luhlanga Iwako wetu lento yeyetu. Mandiyeke, ndibale incwade ende kodwa
Umhleli makandixolele soloko ndaba ngumamkeli we Sigidimi kukuqala
ukubala, faneli ukuba baya kuqala bonke ababalelani ukubona igama lam.

JACOB BOOM.

INTLANGANISO EBIXOXA NGOLOBOLO. E-Bencoti, September 11, 1875.

Besinentlanganiso enkulu, ebukulu bungafani nanto kuba ibiyimini
yokuqekezwa kwe Banki za- kowetu. Abafundisi abebeko ngu Rev. W. Davis
no Rev. C. White. Lentlanganiso ibise Osborne (e-Tshungwana) ngomhla we
9. Into enkulu ebitetwa kukuqekezwa kwe Banki elulobolo ; kuba kute
kwakutiwa namhla makupele, ati amanye

amadoda apefumlela pezulu, esite, tina bakokeli xa besi kulo ntlanganiso
nezozicaka ze Nkosi ezi- kolekileyo kuti sacela ituba tina bakokeli
lokuba kesihlangane sodwa, basivumela ke bati sobuya si- hlangane
ngomso. Sihlangene tina bakokeli ndi- ngezitukululi izinto ebezitetwa,
imibuzo, impiki- swano, inteto, imibuzo eti iyacitwana lemizi, zona
ntonina zona inkomo? Sakupila nganina kanjalo. Inteto ezinje ngokuti
namhlake nisiti nje masiyeke ukulobolisa, ngumhla esiya kuba pantsi
kwama Lawo, kuba lento ukulobola besiwanqabele ama Lawo. Zazinteto
ezinjaloke kuti sodwa bakokeli, sada saqekezelana kubini. Lide loyiswa
elinye icala savumelana kweliti namhla asukuba salobolisa, sati kanjalo
makupele nocelo ati umntu akuba use intombazana yake endaweni aselesiti
mabandipe inkomo, sati umntu makangayikucela engenalishwa lanto
limhleleyo.

Nani zihlobo zam ezikumacala ngamacala le- ndawo kaniyikangele yokuti
indoda ingapezulu ngempahla kulowo utabate intombazana yayo nanko isuka
iya kucela inkomo. Lento ke isekwalulobolo, kuba umntu olobolisayo
akakatali yena nokuba owake umhlambi mkulu owomyeni mncikane usuka aye
kulobolisa noko. Mandishiye apa ke, nditi ke kusile ngomso sihlangene
kwa nezicaka ze Nkosi. Sazixelela imvume yetu, noko ke bekuko imibuzwana
etile ebesimana siyibekisa kuzo. Sacela indawo enkulu ukuba makumiswe
umteto wokuba osukuba ecela inkomo kwantombi yake makakutshwe
erementeni, njengokuba iyi- mvume yetu tina bakokeli. Ifezwe apo ke.

P. K. TUTA.

Kwa Zidenge, September 17, 1875.

Nditi bonisani bazalwana sicela amehlo. Sila- hlekelwe ngamahashe nge
Cawa ebusuku ngomhla 12 ka September. Matatu, elinye limfusa yinkabi,
iminyaka yalo yobudala mitandatu, linopawu kulendlebe yenene
ihalafumatyi. Elinye yiponana ebomvana emfutshanana, eyomeleleyo, engati
ukuba umntu uyibopelele iman’ukurona; inekolwana encinane kunene pantsi
kwesingci, angeyiqondiyo umntu ongalaziyo ; iminyaka yayo ikowe
sitandatu. Elinye yimazi ebomvu emanqina makulu umsila unqunyulwe;
iminyaka yalo mihlanu libuhantom wakulikangela kufupi.

JOHN NTLOKO.

E-Nxukwebe, September 23, 1875.

Ndicela amehlo kuni zihlobo ; ndilahlekelwe yinkabi yehashe ebudala
buyiminyaka emitatu. Lihashe elibomvu noko lingebomvu kanye, libu-
gwangqa ibala lalo, line bala elimhlotshana ebunzi : liequbu emhlana
kulendawo ihlala isali : eloqutyana angati nokuba umntu akalibonanga
alive akupata emhlana. Inyanga zisembini ndinalo elohashe, lilahleka ngo
August kuwo lo mnyaka. Angati umntu olifumeneyo ndimvuze ngangenkatazo
azifumeneyo.

ANDRIES MBAMBISA.

Durban, September 17, 1875.

Nkosi yam ndifakele umbulelo wam ndiyabulela ku Mr. J. Shaw naku Mr. J.
M. V. abapengululi bogqoboko: kodwa u-Mr. J. Shaw ndimbulela ngo-
kungapezulu. Yebo wena ubuzayo, ukugqoboka kukuhlaba kwelizwi lika Tixo
ngokuba alizi kamnandi alitutuzeli liya gqoboza intliziyo elilitye kube
buhlungu alile abelusizi adane obenenyembezi yobuhlungu oburara kanjalo.
Sifunda ukuba ilizwi lika Tixo lihlaba kude kube kukwa hlulwa kompefumlo
nokomoya nokwmalungu nokwomongo. Elilizwi lokugqoboka liteta ukuhlatywa
kwentliziyo ngumkonto wevangeli.

PHILIP XULU.

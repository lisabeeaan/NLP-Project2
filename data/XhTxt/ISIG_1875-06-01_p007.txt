ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 1, 1875.

UMBUZO.

  Ezase Natal, April 7, 1875.

Uyabuza umfo wase Natal kumzalwana wake obizo lingu Jonas Hobo, uti,
kawumqondise ngezindawo zontatu ezinibulalayo, ikofu namazimba, nentombi
zenu ezite ngokubanje zenziwa kukuba ningahlangani, utike, umbuzo
womzalwana wako ose Natal, cacisa ezizinto zontatu uzibalileyo. Uti wena
intlanganiso, mayize itinina ngazona, ebuzanje, lomzalwana wako, ubuzela
endaweni yokuba usiti ziyanibulala ezizinto zontatu. Uti ziyanibulala
ngokutini na? Incwadi yako iteta ngomanyano lwabantsundu, isifake pakati
sonke tina makolwa antsundu, ndiyayibulela mna lencwadi yako,
ndingavuyayo ute wapendula.

Mandingene kule. E-Natala apa sibona izimanga, kute ngalenyanga ifileyo,
ukulandelana kwemibiko, yababuleleneyo yasuka yamangalisa. Nombiko
uziswa ziziqu zababulali. Kude kwakatatu bebulalana ngokulwa, belwa
ngenduku ezizikanda zingange selwa lomntwana. Kute ukulwa kwezinye
intsizana, zamlambomnye, zasuka zahlulelana, ezinye zangase kunene,
ezinye zangase kohlo, kute ekuhlanganeni kwazo, yakupa eyase kunene
Kweyase kohlo, yegqobo elukakayini yafumba pantsi, kwakupela, yakupa
eyase kohlo kwenye yase kunene, yegqobo kwase kanda yabeka pantsi. Ite
le ibetiweyo yase kunene, yakuvuka, ibonile ukuba uselefile lo ubetwe
ngumkuluwa wayo, yasuka noluduma Iwayo, ingalihlambanga neligazi layo,
mayifike ke emakosini ibomvu. Ekufikeni kwayo ifike ngo- kuti, makosi
nindibonanje, ndivuka pantsi, be- ndifile, ndibetwe ngubani, besisilwa,
undibete oluduma ngenduku ndawa pantsi, ndite ndakuvuka ndambona
esingale, ndamgibisela ngenduku entloko, wafa. Sendizizise ukuba
ndixonywe ngamakosi. Yenze iqinga lokusindisa umkuluwa, wayo, alikatetwa
elotyala. Eyesibini ingozi, kulwe amakwenkwe emabini ite eqalileyo
yenqile entloko yafumba pantsi, ite ivuka leyo yegqobo entloko yafumba
pantsi, kuleyo ibete ukuqala, yafa kwakupela. Futi leyo inkwenkwe yaya
kuzibika emakosini, ingalihlambanga negazi lo- duma Iwayo. Mayifike kwa
nokuti, makosi nibonanje ndivuka pantsi, besisilwa nobani, wandibeta
oluduma ndawa pantsi, ndivuke ndayibeka eyam induku kweyake intloko,
damfumba pantsi, ufe kwakupela, ndizizise ezinkosini ukuba ndixonywe.
Alikatetwa futi elo. Ingozi yesitatu, eyenziwe ngumfana ogama lingu
Mfundisi. Ute u-Mfundisi ehamba nabakowabo, beya kusela utywala komnye
umzi, bafika bangena endlwini, ibandla lipakati, lalomzi. Makuti
akungena abizwe ngabalingane bake, besentla, enyukeke; ma- yimbuke
kakuhle intanga yake, kuti kusenjalo kungene enye intsizwa, imbone
u-Mfundisi pakati kwentsizwa zakowayo, iti ubekwe yinina apa Mfundisi?
Ute omnye ibiyini na? Ati omnye uyazi, ndati kuwe mhla sihlanganayo nda-
kukutshaya, ute omnye pakamisa ke live lonke elibandla lisendlwini apa,
uzuti ukwenza oko libe lisazi; ati omnye seliya kuva ngawe, ati esitsho
abeyifaka induku, ku Mfundisi, atiqole entloko abeke pantsi, makati
epantsi u-Mfundisi, kuti ukuvuka kwake, abeselecole induku esandleni ati
gqobo u-Mfundisi kulentsizwa abeke pantsi. Makapume ngokwe kati
u-Mfundisi kulendlu, malisale ibandla linyikanyikana nesidumbu
esifileyo. Makabe sityambela futi u-Mfundisi ukuya kuzibika emakosini,
makati efika emakosini noluduma Iwake abe siwangazo, esiti, Makosi
nindibonanje ndivuka pantsi nditshaywe ngubani, wandifumba pantsi,
ngenduku ndite ndivukapantsi ndandisihla ngeyam induku, ndegqobo ekanda
ndabeka pantsi, wafa kwakupela, sendizilenje emakosini ukuba ndixonywe.
Manditike alikatetwa futi nelo. Nantsoke imihlola yelilizwe lase Natal.

Ndingati ukubalisa sihleli kakuhle pantsi kwe- piko lentombazana,
lomntanomhle, u-Victoria osipete kakuhle.

Ukuba ndibalisa ngesimilo sabantu belilizwe lase Natal ndingati ngabantu
abazitobe ngokuku- lu kumntu omhlope.

PESHEYA KWENCIBA.

Wodehouse Forests, April 23, 1875.

Ku Mhleli we Sigidimi Samaxosa, Nkosi yam ndicela ukuba uke undifakele
lamazwana amba- Iwa epepeni lako, nakuba ndingenguye umbaleli walo. Kuni
makowetu nonke, ndinga ndingake ndibekise lamazwana, nditi ukubona kwam
uhlanga luye nyuka, ngendawo ezininzi, endima- na ndizibona ngamaxesha
ngamaxesha; ekuha- mbeni kwam nasekuhlaleni kwam. Ukutsho makowetu,
kubonakala ukuba abantsundu nabo baya beqonda, ukuba bafanel’ ukuba bati
into elunge kubo, bangene ngentliziyo zabo zonke kulo msebenzi; nokuba
bawenza ngenxa ka Tixo ; nokwasebantwini. Ukutsho ate amamfengu la
apesheya kwe Nciba, enza umsebeuzi omkulu, emba indlela. Kute kusenjalo
kwa bekiswa kuwo ngesikolo sokufundisa usapo lwawo ; asele wisa iponti
£1,500 ukuba kwakiwe esosikolo. Enye into ngokusuka kuka Rev. C. Pamla
aselesuka efaka ezinxoweni zawo ukumfudusa, evuma ukuba umsebenzi ka
Tixo mawuhambe. Enye into ete yona namhla yabangela ukuba ndibale, kunge
tyalike yase Tsomo, ngokuvulwa kwayo nge 14 ka April 1875 ekute
ekuvulweni kwayo, kwa shumayela U-Rev. Davis, kwati emva koko kwa-
punywa ixeshana kwabuya kwangenwa. Umfundisi wase Tsomo, waxela imali
eyakileyo, neseyibatelwe, nelityala, lati ityala la £482.
Mandisendisiti, ate amabandla akowetu atsala ku- nye, ngantliziyo nye,
kwakuko no Captain Blyth no Fenny Esqr. (wasebatenjini). Lati ukupela
kwe lotyala kwabonakala kum ukuba okwenene, kuyafika ukuqonda, ukuba
sifanele nati bantsundu ukuba sinyamekele izinto ezimalunga nati;
sizifeze. Enye into nesizwe saba Tembu siyasebenza naso ukuquba
umsebenzi wokukanya kwanezinye izizwe. Apa kwesisikolo nge 8 ka March,
babonakalisa nabo ukuba sebeqonda ukuba abafundisi abantsundu ngababo.
Ukutsho U-Rev. J. Mahonga bamfudusile ngokufaka izandla zabo ezibatyini
bakupeimaliezitilezokumbulisa bevuma ukuba lihambo ilizwi le Nkosi,
bamtwala ngenqwelo zabo bamsa apo atunyelwe kona namhla, Kwisikolo sase
Wod`ehouse Forests, umfundisi waso ucele ukuba angabi sawateta amatyala
omzi, ukuze aselequba lomsebenzi welizwi kupela, kwavunywa, bati abantu
bazinyulela abaya kubapata kwanyulwa amadoda amatatu ukuba apate kude
kubuye kufike omnye u-April. Ecaweni yesitatu yake kubuye kunyulwe
amanye.

OWASEBATENJINI.

Mount Coke, Emkangiso, April 1875.

Nkosi yam mhleli ndiya kucela ukuba undifake- le lamazwana am ambalwa
kwesositya sakomkulu :—Ngomhla we 4 no we 5 ku April, apa Emka- ngiso
sibe sine “ Anniversary” yezikula, zase “Wesile,” yaqaleka nge Cawa
kwada kwango Mvulo. Kwakuko into eninzi yabantu, nabavela kumapandle;
kwakuhlanganisene izikula ezine esase Mkangiso nesase Qugqwala, nesase
Buffalo Ford, nesase Tamara; ezizi kula ke zazine Ti- tshere zazo.
Babengeko kodwa abafundisi abamhlope abavela kwezinye indawo, kupela
waye ngu Rev. W. Holford owale ndawo kakade. Kwabantsundu abafundisi
kwakuko U-S.P. Gasa, no J. Ndungane, no A. Mabala no W. Mtoba.

Kwati nge Cawa kwangena inkonzo ngexesha le 11 kwashumayela umfundisi
U-J. Ndungane, yazala tu ityalike ngabantu akwabiko nendawo, abanye
bahlala pandle, yayinkonzo emnandikunene, kute kwakupunywa kwayiwa
edinaleni. Kwa-

buywa kwangenwa ngexesha lesitatu ukumka komhla, kwaviwa abantwana
ukufunda kwabo, bamana kanjalo bevuma ingoma ezixela ngowapezulu. Kwati
emveni koviwo kwapunywa; kwabuya kwangena inkonzo yangokuhlwa ngexesha
lesi 7, kwashumayela umfundisi ongu S. P. Gasa. Kwati ngomvulo yayenye
imini enkulu kunene. Inkonzo yangena ngexesha le 11; ezati intsapo
zayenye into kaloku zahomba kunene, zenza into entle ukuya kungena kwazo
etyalikeni zicula amaculo azo. Kute emveni kokuba kutanda- ziwe kwaviwa
izifundo, Iwati olwanamhla uviwo Iwaqiniswa kwizinto ezifundiswa
kwizikula zemini ezi kukulesesha isingesi, nokubala nokwenza amanani;
zabako nembali eziyolileyo ezati zakufundwa zabahlekisa abantu. Manditi
kuba andisayikuzixela ngazinye izikolo ukuquba kwazo ndingafuni ukugqiba
indawo yabanye; ke noko azifananga zonke kwabako ezenza into ekukuti nqa
ekufundeni kwazo. Into eyenziwayo ngalomini ekukucula nokufunda
kwabantwana kwabonakala ukuba silusapo lokukanya ilanga lobulungisa,
“Igospel,” lisikanyisele.

Kute kwakupunywa etyalikeni zatamba ngento entle intsapo ukuyakwamkela
izonkana ezimna- ndana, isikula esitile sine “Flag” yaso. Kwati emva
koko kwayiwa emdlalweni kwati ngokuhlwa kwako “ Itea-meeting” enkulu
wati umfundisi wetu u-J. Ndungane wamana esibalisela indaba zase Bayi
apo avela kona, kwakonabanye abatile abaposa amazwi onwabisayo kwati
emva koko kwacitakalwa. Nazo indaba esisand’ukuba nazo Mhleli.

S. MTIMKULU.

Durban, April 7, 1875.

Mhleli nkosi yam ungandimangali. Ndifakele lamazwana ambalwa.
Ndiyawavuma amazwi omzalwana endinge nakulixela igama lake, kodwa amazwi
ake endiwavileyo ndingawaxela Esigimini sika March odlulileyo oteta
ngalo ulobolo. Ewe okunene mabangatsho ukuti akutengiswa ngentombi. Kuya
tengiswa, zininzi indawo ezibonakalisa kakuhle ukutengiswa kwentombi
ngoku ba abanakumnika nxa inkomo zimbini njengoko umlungu
engenakundinika ibatyi elixabiso liyi ponti ukuba ndinasheleni mbini;
kungokunjalo ukutengiswa kwentombi. Futi uti akugqiba ukukupa inkomo
bandule ukumbiza ibodwe elikulukazi mhlaimbi ixabiso 15s., babize
nemikonto nomgugulandevu. Futi babize ingubo ka sonto- mbi, igama laleyo
ngubo lingupupa-ndimnika. Ewe bazalwana kuti ngomhla abamtshatisayo bati
abanewabo ntombi bapate amakakana abo ubeve bewabeta ngenyawo besiti “
Nangumntwana mfokazana,” besitsho ngokuba sebekwenze ihlwempu
ngokutabata yonke imfuyo yako izivato zomfazi wako sezizakuba
ngamanebunebu zibe mdaka nawe uvate amanebunebu ubemdaka,—uti wakuzala
umntwana ubisi alueelelwe emzini, ubulongo bokusinda indlu bucelwe
emzini. Manenekazi akwaxosa nawasembo kangelani nazo inkedama eziswele
oyise nonina nezihlobo kange lani nibuzisane omnye nomnye nxa nibabiza
inkomo beswele into zonke. Intombi zenu bayakuzigcina ngantonina? Omnye
uzakukangela unyana “emanebunebu emdaka asuke atabate amehlo awabeke
pezu kwendoda akohlwe kukuwabeka pezu kweyake intloko. Luninzi ugxeko
olunigxekayo nina nikanyisiweyo yivangeli endingelubalisiyo. Mandipeze
kodwa ndilusizi ngokuba amanenekazi akwaxosa engayi kundiva ekwteteni.

PHILLIP QAWE.

OBULEEE OMNYE.—Kufupi nase Uitenhage, umfo otile ongu Mxosa ulwe nomfazi
wake ute xa ambetayo, kwegqi Ilawo lamtinta, waselelitya ndyata ngenduku
u-Mxosa lafa. Kanti nomfazi ude wasiwa kwindlu yamagqira.

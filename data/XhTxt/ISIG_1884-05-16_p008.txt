IBANKI ZE POST OFFICE ZOKULONDOLOZA IMALI.

Umongameli we Post Office unga angake akukanyisele ngamalungelo
angafunyanwayo kwi Post Office, ezinendawo ngoku yokulondoloza imali
nokuba zimalana ezincinane, awoba neliso kuzo u Rulumente, ayimele
nembuyekezo yazo.

Ukuqala ungafaka nokuba yisheleni enye, ekungati xa sukuba uyifuna,
nakuba ungasekona kulondawo apo wawuyifake kona, uyikupe ngokulula
nokuba kukuyipina indawo eyi Post Office egcina imali apa e Koloni.
Njengoko bese kutshiwo, akukataleki apo ungaba ub’uyifake kona, ongati
mhlaumbi ukuba ub’uyifake e Monti, ngokutanda kwako wongezelele enye,
okanye uyikupe yonke, nokuba ukupa isiqingata sayo, xa ke mhlaumbi
uyifuna ungekona e Monti, apo wauyifake kona, ungati uyikupe nokuba kuse
Qonce, kwa Komani, e Rini, e Kimberley, e Bayi, umhlaumbi e Kapa,
ngazwinye yob'atalwa nokuba kupina apo ungaba utanda ukuyifumana kona.

Inzala yoba yi nayipeni (9d) ngonyaka epontini ezeleyo.

Abafazi abatshatieyo, no salpo, ngokufanayo nabanye abantu banelungelo
lokuzifakela imalana zabo, elowo eyake; oko kukuti,—ingabi
nakucukunyiswa bani.

Zonke i Mbumba zamaqela ngamaqela o Buhlobo, ne Ngqwebo, zingazigcinela
imalana zazo ngalendlela yokuyi- nqabisa kwa Rulumente, kuba xa zingaba
ziyayifuna zoyifumana ngokulula, nangokumsinya.

I-Gunya lokuba zibuyekezwe imali obe umana uzifaka, lotunyelwa
kwakamsinya nge posi elandela leyo incwadi yako ebika ukuyifuna kwako
ifunyenwe ngayo.

Ayakufihlwa ngokuzeleyo, ukuze angaziwa ngabanye abantu, amagama abo
bafake imali, kunye namanani emali zabo.

Nokokuba kukuyipina i Post Office unganako ukulifumana ipepa
elishicilelweyo lemimmiso, nemiteto ye Banki ze Post Office, ongati
ukuba ute wanendawo ekusilelayo ukuyiqonda, uti ukuzaneza ubalele
incwadi engenqweneli sitampu ke yona uyisingise ngoluhlobo,—

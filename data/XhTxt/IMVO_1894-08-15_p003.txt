MABAZILUNGISELELE INGUQULO.

Kwixesha elizayo kuyakuba nzima oko­kuba wonke ontsundu abene akile
ezi­ntatu zomhlaba uemazi yenkomo. Kamsinyane abantu bayakuba baninzi
ngokupindiweyo kwinani abalilo ngoku. Mabaqondiswe okokuba abantu
kufuneka benenguqulo entsha, njengokuba nabamhlope bebenayo. Kwiminyaka
eliwaka egqitileyo e England bonke abantu bebepile ngokulima.
Kwabonakala ukuba umhlaba awunakubatwala abantu ngenxa yokwanda kwabo,
ngoku isitoba sabantu eshumini sisemsebenzini ezidolopini. Ikwayinto
enjalo kuma Xhosa. Maba­qondiswe okokuba kwixesha elizayo isitoba
sabantu eshumini, kufuneka si pile ngomsebenzi wezandla, lonto ke ngati
ingabalungela. Ingaba kuko ilungu elingavumelana nendawo yekokuba
makunikwe umntu ngamnye ontsundu i akile ezintatu zomhlaba nemazi
yenkomo na? Imantyi zapesheya kwe Nciba zikala ngokuti maninzi amadodana
angenakufumana masimi, laye nedlelo kubonakala okokuba lincinane nalo.
Abantu abantsundu mabanyanzelwe baye emse­benzini. Bayakude bafane
nezinye intlanga kwixa elizayo bazinikele ekusebenzeni ngezandla. Akuko
nto intsha ku lento, esisimo masikaugelwe njengo­kuba besanda nje abantu
abantsundu. Siyintonina isimo samadodana antsundu ngoku? Asel’ indywala,
ahlale ezimbutweni zokunqena. Sisimo esimnandi ke eso kubo ngoku, kuba
basenomhlaba omkulu. Imantyi zapesheya kwe Nciba ziman’ ukumxelela
okokuba zona aziyazi into ekuzakuba yiyo. Lomcimbi ke mauxoxwe njengoko
yena seletshilo. Kupela kwento eyakwahlula lento. Yiyo ke imbangi ebange
okokuba afake lerafu kutiwa yirafu yobukoboka kulomcimbi. Yena
akayiqondi njalo. Akuba alate ubukapukapu berafu ute, kwibhulasi
zabelungu uva okokuba barola iponti ku Rulumeni, wave ke engayazi into
abayirolayo kumninifama, kanti yeyona nto bakolwa yiyo abantu
abantsundu. Yena okokuba ibiyakupu- melela lendawo ajonge kuyo, abonayo
okokuba iyakuke itate ixesha ebeyakuzipelisa ezibulasi zisezifameni
zabamhlope. Endaweni yazo ebeyakwenza izixeko zabantsundu ezipautsi
komteto ofanelekileyo, kwanendawo zomsebenzi. Apo ingozi ikona inkoliso
yabo baba kwindawo apo impahla ebiweyo isatyiselwa kona, (Kwahlekwa.)

DR. LIVINGSTONE.

  U-Mr. Stanley umhambi owayesuka E-America ukuya kufuna U-Dr.
  Living­stone ngoku ubuyile. Ubeselenyanga zili 18 wayayo. Londlela
  yake idle iponti ezipakati kwamawaka asibozo nasitoba. Umfumene ese
  Ujiji ngakwicibi ekutiwa yi Tanganyika; bahlala ndawonye inyanga
  za-ne. Bavuya bobabini bakubonana, esingati nati sibenokusiqonda
  isizatu so- kuba avuye U-Dr. Livingstone, kuba ebeseleminyaka ingaka
  engaboni mntu uvela kwelakowabo. Ubesele kunxanelwe ke ngoko ukuziva
  indaba zawa Pesheya amazwe.

  U-Dr. Livingstone utumele incwadi pesheya ate kuzo waxela inxenye
  yezinto azibonileyo, ezikolise ukuba ngokumelana kwamazwe akona. Uke
  waya nakulizwe ekutiwa yi Manyuema, elibantu bakuka- ngeleka kuhle,
  bebagqita ngento zonke abanye ababa ngqongileyo. Ilizwe labo linconywa
  ukucuma, nenyamakazi asizizo nazo. Noko kunjalo bazizidlabantu
  esingenakuxela ke ukuba babangwa yinina ukuze babe njalo, kantinje
  banconywa nokukutala.

Kuseko kakulu ukwenza abantu ama koboka. Lomsebenzi usaxaswe ngokuko-
dwa ngama Arab nama Banian. Badla ngokuti bafune indlela yokuzitelekisa
inkosana ukuze bati abantu benkosi esukuba igxotiwe babenze amakoboka.
Ububi obuveliswa ngulomsebenzi abuteteki; uba- ngela ukuba kungabiko
kuhluma nokuqu- bela pambili kwezontlanga. Enye indawo enkulu enxanyelwe
ngu Dr. Livingstone kukutshabalalisa lomsebenzi. Bati abo bawuqubayo
balinge indlela zonke zokuba angabi nakupumelela. Ukuba bekungengenxa
yabo uti ngeleseleugqibile nomsebenzi wake. Indawo asafuna ukuyifeza
pambi kokuba agoduke kukufumana apo ipuma kona I-Nile. Wotabata ixesha
eli- ngapezu komnyaka uk-uwenza kwake.

NGALO ULWALUKO.

BAZALWANA bam nonke nina eniyakufunda lencwadi, ndinamazwana ambalwa
ngalendawana yolwaluko, ngati kaloku luyasoyisa impela. Ndifuna ukuteta
kuqala ngalendawana yokugodusa in- kwenkwe, yakugqiba ukwaluka, izaluse
ibisalelwa nguyise, aze kwayena ayeku- yitabata ayise ekaya endlwini
yake aze kubalikankata layo. Ngati mna lonto inzima, ibifanele ukuba
sisibeto somntu oyaluse yena ngokwake inkwenkwe. Londawo nakuba ingati
ibonakala kwabanye ukungati iluncedo lokukwelelisa inkwenkwe kumasiko
atile akohlakeleyo ayilupelisi ulwaluko, iyaluvumela, inika igunya
kumntwana lokwapula izwi loyise, ngelinye icala iyabulala, ngokuba,
lonto uyise ubeyala iyenzile. Kutiwa yinkosi hlonipa uyihlo nonyoko
ukuze imini zako zibeninzi ozipiwayo ngu Yehova U-Tixo wako. Uyise izwi
lake lapulwe, akahlonelwe nxa kwalukwayo yinkwenkwe. Makangatike uyise
nonina, nabantwana bake bayekutwala lomsebenzi ngokuyipekela
nangokuyibopa. Abantwana babantu abantsundu bakohlakaliswa yilonto
kangaka, kukuti umntwana apule izwi loyise kungabiko nto enziwa yona.
Londawo itabata amandla kuyise, umntwana im- nika igunya.

IDIAMONDI E-CALIFORNIA.

KWILIZWE ekutiwa yi California kusand’ ukafunyanwa idiamondi. Kutiwa
ziya zuzwa kakulu. Lento ke iyakuti mhlaumbi ibangeie ukuba zishiyiselwe
ezalapa E-South Africa, kuba bayakuti abanye abebe ngebeze apa baye
kona. Kanjalo ixabiso lazo lizakuba lincinane ngoku. I-California
lilizwe elise North America ngasentshona-langa kwi United States.
Lilizwe ebelidume ngobuninzi begolide yalo.

Sotike sonke sivumelane ukuba ulwa- luko lusisono kuti tina basenkolweni
ka Kristu Yesu ; nomntwana wekolwa owalukayo ulahla ubapitizesho
ayekwenza into engancedinto. Sitike abantu abalukayo benza isono. Nxa
uyise engenakumalusa ngokwake umntwana wake, kungakumbi ukuba azaluse,
ukubake uyadela imiteto ka Tixo neyo yise neyo nina, makangancedwa
ngabo, bazingenise esonweni sake. Lento yokugodusa inkwenkwe yakuzalusa,
ku- pela kwento embi; igqita nokuba ayaluse ngokwake uyise,
ngalendawo,—kuqala uyayalela uti lento sisono yaluke noko ; imdele
uyise, asuke uyise yakwaluka abelikankata abengumnini Iwaluko, indlu
yake ibe lisutu; avunyiswe ukwenza into ebengayifuni ngumtwana wake.
Yikangeleni kakulu lendawo, madoda akuti. Amangesi akalwazi ulwaluko
olu, kudaniswa abazali kuvuyiswa abantwana ngalento.

6 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 16, 1884.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

E NATAL.—Beke kwako ulure lokuba u Sir H. Bulwer

i Ruluneli uzikupile ebu Rulunelini.

ISIKOLO SASE NXUKWEBE, siva ukuba naso sovalwa ngomhla weshumi
elinesibozo enyangeni, (June 18.)

OBUBILEYO.—Sifumana um’biko wokububa kuka Sir Bartie Frere, obeyi
Ruluneli kweli ngexa lokulaula ko Sprigg.

U MR. FROST, ilungu le Palamente lakwa Komani sifumana ukuba
selisingenisile isicelo sefama zama Bulu ase Glen Grey.

ISIFO SOLWIMI EMAHASHENI.—Esi sifo sandile kwindawo ezinjengoma
Somerset, Nyara, Kobonqaba, Bofolo nama Qonce.

ABALAHLEKELWEYO.—Lomhlambi singa ungake uqwalasele kwizaziso ze Mantyi
yase Gcuwa neyase Ngqamakwe ezibonakala kwakweli lanamhla.

KWELE GOLIDE.—Siva ukuba kwelo kusand’ ukufunyanwa umnxuma otyebe kunene
yi golide, ekutiwa umniniwo ufumene isiqumakazi esinobom.

UKUNGENISWA KWAMA GAMA ABA VOTI.—Kususela ngoku kude kuse kumhla wa 31
ku July, amagama abo basakuba benqwenela ukunga bangaba ngaba voti oti
anikelwe.

O DRINK!—E Kimberley (Dayiman) kusand’ ukufunyanwa ngamini’nye izidumbu
zabantu abahlanu abamnyama mganyana nomzi—iziqamo zokulala pandle
nokusela.

UMTANTATO E TSOMO.—Njengoko sasivakalisile ngapa- mbili, ilitye lesiseko
salo mtantato, libekwe ngomhla we 22 ku May yinkosikazi ka Mr. Levey i
Mantyi yase Cala.

OBULELE UMFAZI.—Ngase Qonce siva ukuba kuko indoda entsundu esand’
ukubanjwa ngokubulala umfazi ngenduku. Ngati aba bantu babulalana
bebenxilile bobabini. O Drink!

IZIKOLO ZAKWA RULUMENI.—Ezi sikolo ziyakuqala uku- fumana ixesha
lokupumla (Holidays) lasebusika ngolwesiHlanu, 27 June. Umsebenzi
uyakuqalwa kwakona ngo 22 July 1884.

ABAXONYIWEYO.—Kusand’ ukuxonywa amadoda amabini antsundu kwa Komani
kufupi nje ngetyala lokubulala ikuli e Bushman’s Hoek. Axonyelwe
entolongweni elivuma ityala lawo.

IMVULA.—Siqonda ukuba imvula iwe kulo lonke, xa nje siqonda ukuba ide
yawa nase Ngqushwa. Kwezinye indawo, ngokukodwa ukusingisa kwelipezulu,
iwa ukuba iwe njengesicoto.

ISICELO E PALAMENTE.—Siva ukuba ifama zama Bulu zenza isicelo zisingisa
kwindlu zombini e Palamente, ukuba aba Tembu abemi e Xonxa (Glen Grey)
bafuduswe uze lomhlaba utengiswe kwifama ezimhlope.

OZOSILEYO.—I Qeya ngase Kapa lite belikangela izitena ebelizitshisa,
ngokuva ingqele lakwela ngapezulu ukuba live ilita. Lite qwiti butongo,
kanti ke kupela oko seliya kufunyanwa libakekile kusile.

UKUHANJISWA KWE LIZWI.—Kweziveki zidluleyo apa sisand’ ukufikelwa ngu
Bev. Dr. Hole, indoda epumele ukubuta imali yokuba kwandiswe inani le
Baibile kuba zifuneka kwindawana zonke.

UMDLALO WE BOLA.—Ezitatyatwa kumapepa apesheya zisixelela ngomdlalo
webola obukona, apo igcisa lasema Ngesini abati ngu Dr. Grace lite
ngamaxesha amabini elingene ngawo lahlanganisa amakulu amabini.

EZAKWA ZULU.—Nakuba sesisoyika ukubamba into ngokumalungana nento zakwa
Zulu, soka sibavise ngendawo nati esiyifumana izalisile kwawase
Mangesini abafundi betu, ekukuba endaweni ka Cetywayo kumiswe u Dinizulu
ngama Bulu.

IMFUDUKA.—Ama Ranuga apuma kweli pakati siva ukuba asabeke ayeka. Inkomo
zawo zinconywa ubuhle nobuninzi. Isizatu sale miqodi kutiwa ama Ranuga
ayagxotwa zinkosi zawo ngokumonela ukubona ukwanda kwempahla yawo.

UNYIKIMO LOMHLABA.'—E England siqonda ukuba kusand’ ukubako unyikimo
lomhlaba e Essex nakuba lungabanga lukulu ngokoyikisayo ; noko ke lube
nako kona ukuziwisa ikostini, nokuzicanda indonga zezindlu kwindawo
olubeko kuzo.

EZAKWA ZULU.—Ngom-Vulo, 2 June, isijoli sama Bulu sahlangana no Sir H.
Bulwer, i Ruluneli yase Natal, esasingenisa indawo yokuba imvumelwano
yawo no Dinizulu, ukumkani ? mayinonelelwe, nokuba ama Zulu makayekwe
azilaule pakati kwemida yawo, onganyelwe lulaulo Iwase Natal, nolwase
Free State, nolwase Transvaal.

I JAMANIKAZI NESI XOSA.—Kwi Cape Mercury sifumana indawo apa eteta
ngobunqina obabuza kunikwa yintombazana ye Jamani, eyati kwakuba kutetwa
si Jamani, si Ngesi, si Bulu na yi Mantyi kwe ti, kwada kwatetwa isi
Xosa ukuze yenze impendulo, kwangaso nayo.

OBE UMHLAMBI UPELA.—Liti elakwa Komani kusand’ ukuhlutwa eseleni lase
Sidutyini ngabantu baka Mr. Levey wase Cala kutsha nje amashumi omabini
anantlanu enkomo. Kute kwakubuzwa incwadi yokuquba wati yena umfo omkulu
ungumalusi, uyalusa ukwenjenje akaqubi. Kanti ke noko iqinga lenkabi
aliyi kuyisindisa.

UKUNYUSWA KWE RAFU.—Ulaulo olutsha olumiyo ngoku, lusavakalisa uhlobo
olucinga ukuba kwenziwe Iona ukuze kuzaliswe inx'owa yakomkulu. Oluhlobo
ke makowetu lolokuba kuti kuyo yonke into engenayo apa e Koloni enjengo
zingubo, zihlangu, njalo-njalo u Rulumente abize inani elitile, into ke
leyo eya kuwanyusa ibete iwaxome amanani ezinto. lyawa kuba yenye
yembandezelo le kubantu abantsundu.

INTLANGANTSO YOKUZAMA UKUNYUSA UHLANGA.—Sicela ukuke usifakele isaziso
sentlanganiso. Intlanganiso yoku­zama ukunyusa uhlanga oluntsundu
yesitili sase Victoria East iyakuhlangana kuyo lenyanga ngolusuku 27 e
Roxeni. Ingxoxo—Kulungile na ukuba ipesheya kwe Nciba linikelwe kwi
Nkosazana ? Abavuli bengxoxo ngo Messrs. J. Tengo-Jabavu no G. C. Sicina
njengokuba sayesitshilo kwesangapambili isaziso.—Secretary.

EZASEMA-MPONDWENI.—Kufupi nje ibuto lonongqayi lihamba no Mr. Leary, u
Mantyi wase Mt. Ayliff, lifunzele kwi nkosana abati ngu Matyana, lacita
izindlu nemizi ka Nqunqu osand’ ukugqita imiteto engomda opakati kwama
Mpondo nama Xesibe. Kuvakala ukuba kwabako impitimpitana, kodwa
abacaswanga mntu. U Rulumeni usand’ ukubikela u Mqikela. ukuba ufuna
engene kwelasema Mpondweni walungisa indlela ecanda kwelo. Ingati
ubunqumnqesi ukuvuma u Mqikela ngendawo yokuba uyakungati u Rulumeni
unemfanelo kwelo lasema Mpondweni.

NGO 4 June e Palamente kwabako ingxoxo pezu komteto awayesiti u Mr.
Wolf, olilungu lase Dayiman, maumiselwe kweli lizwe wokuba indoda efelwe
ngumfazi ibe nelungelo lokutshata nomsakwabo, nomfazi ofelwe yindoda abe
nelungelo lokutshata nomtakwabo ’ndoda. Lomteto ucaswe kunene licala
lama Bulu nangu Mr. Merriman, ote abantu beli lizwe abakayifuni into
leyo, ngebesebeuvakalisile umxelo wabo ukuba bebeyifuna. U Mr. Wolf ute
woka ayekelele okwanonyaka ngonyaka ozayo nguye okoyo. Yaba iyapela apo
ingxoxo okwanamhla.

ISIFO SOLWIMI.—Njengokuba kwipepa elipambi kweli sasivakalise ukuba
igqira lakomkulu u Mr. Hutcheson usapumile ukuke akangele into esiyiyo
esisifo emahasheni. Kwingxelo yake uti, esisifo siqala ngokwenza
izilonda emlonyeni, ize ke loncindi ivuzele elulwimini yenze uqweqwe
kona olubangela lentlungu ingaka ke. Izinto zokulingwa azikankanyileyo
asisayi kuzinika zonke kodwa sonika ibenye eyiyeyokuqala
ayikankanyileyo. Uti makudityaniswe igxebeka ye sulphur ndawonye ne
teaspoon yalomgubo kutiwa yi saltpetre, uze ke lomgubo umvumvuzelwe eha-
bileni enyeliweyo, yanqunqwa kakuhle, lonto ke ibe yinto yemihla.

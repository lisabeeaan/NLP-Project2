INDLU EHLELI KUHLE.

[NGUM’BHALELI.]

Umntu onendlu ukufanele ukuzamela okukona kuhle ukuhlalisana nosapo
lwake, ukuze ati nokuba uyasilelelwa ube uko wona umzamo. Endlwini
ehleli kuhle aliko elilizwi lokulaula. Umtshato asibubo bukoboka:
yimvumelwano, kukuvana. Indawo yokulaula neyokutobela ayinakubako.
Abavumelani baya ' lingana bobabini; ke bobabini kuko izinto ekufuneka
benzelene zona.

Indoda ibingafanele ukuti oko ingeniswa yiyo imali yokuxasa usapo ngoko
yiyo yodwa efanel’ ukulaula indlela yokuchitwa kwayo. Okuya yati, “
Ndiya . kunika yonke impilo yam yomhlaba,” yatsho ngesifungo, yayiteta
ukuba ukususela kwelo lixa baya kuba negunya elilingeneyo kuyo yonke
lompanla, ukuba ke ngoko indoda mayingalenzi yodwa igunya lokuchita.
Kananjalo umfazi kwelake icala ubefauele ukuba abe uyasebenza, abe
uhambisa imfanelo ezixhasa indlu ngohlobo olukwalana nolwe ndoda, nakuba
owake umsebenzi ungahlaulwa ngemali. Ngokoke bayafana.

Eyona ndlela ebifanel’ ukulaula ngayo indoda enalomnqweno upantsi
wokulaula, kukuba ibonakalise ukudlulisela kwayo ngengqondo ngohlobo
lokuba umfazi wayo ahlale eyivuma indawo yokuba umyeni wake ungapambili
kuye, ayilandele ngovuyo into esukuba ifunwa nguye. Kodwa kona ukuti
umfazi makabeke pantsi lonke olwake uluvo nayo yonke eyake iminqweno
kukumenza ikoboka, into engakulingeneyo kanye ukuba ngu nina wabantwana,
engakufaneleyo ukuba ngumalusi wezimilo zabo nempilo yabo; kukumenza
ikoboka ngohlobo oluya kuyenzakalisa indoda, lubulale abantwana,
lumenzakalise umfazi.

Indoda ke ukumpata kwayo umfazi yagcina okuya kumbeka yayimbeke ngako
ngemini zokufilisha, yaza yahlala imvisa ukuba ungumntu naye apa
endlwini, londoda iyakufumana umncedikazi wenene; umfazi ocingayo,
onengqondo; isihlebo esinokunceda ekuxakekeni, esiyakuhlala sibukeka;
uyaku fumana inkazana enesincoko esifundisayo, engafani naba asuke ange
usisipili sokuba indoda ibone kuyo ingcingane zayo. Ikoboka yinto
edinwayo nokuba yonwabile icwayitile. Okukona umfazi apatwe ngobuntu
kokukona azipakamisayo nendoda kokukona iyakuva ukuba ndilikaka,
umkuseli wesidalwa esibukekayo, kuze ngoko kuyifanele ukuba izive
ubuntu.

XAKALASHE NAMATEMPILE.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI,—Nceda undifakele lamazwana ambalwa, endipendula ngawo
u “ Xakalashe,” kwinteto yake ebonakele kwi Mvo ya 28. Ngamazwi
amafutshane—ndoyahlula hlula ngoluhlobo:—a. Ndibulela inkutazo okutaze
ngayo amatempile Xakalashe, sendinga ngewumnye nati, kodwa ungemi
endulini ubonisele, es bulif toro. b. Ati uku­ze amatempile ancome,
umhlaumbi avakalise amalinga awenzawo ukuncipisa uselo, abe ahambele
kwezinye indawo njenge nenekazi elo lase America, eyati longxelo
yawakutaza amatempile, yati ko Xakalashe Bros., yanjenge ntsomi ka
Dyakalashe no Volufu, ukuhlekisa ingahlekisi (by right) yona. c.
Ngokumalunga nentlanganiso asukuba amate­mpile emenywe kuzo, ayazitumela
izi culujeje zawo, ezikupa ngokuzazi ukuba zingumhlambi wamatempile
onke, kuba kaloku amadoda kuyo yonke imisebenzi yokunceda uhlanga
akafani, afana nge ndevu zodwa. Kwintlanganiso yokugqibela, awakupa
amadoda amatempile kuba babeko o Revs. Dlepu J. Pritchard, no Messrs. T.
Mkoti, B. G. Sinuka, ngamatempile ke lawo, ufuna anjanina Xakalashe
?—Muni-somakini. Owako.

IGAMA NESAMBATO SOBUFUNDISI.

MNUMZETU, — Ndivumele ke ndenze linye, mabini kumzi wakowetu ngentlekisa
ekubonakala ukuba iya ikhula ngokukhula. Eligama lobu Fundisi liieta
ntonina kowetu apa kanene? Bendiba ligama angabizwa ngalo osukuba
efuudisa, nokuba ngu Titshala; kodwa ke noko liqukayo njalo, kubantu
bakowetu lazelelelwa omiselweyo (ordained) umfundisi, alazelelelwe yona
lento yokuba bati abashumayeli, nabayali, njalo-njalo, babizwe ngalo.
Yintlekisa ke leyo engaqondwayo ngabantu abavumayo ukwenjiwa njalo
ukubizwa, bengenalungelo kodwa. Makamangale umshumayeli kwakutshiwo
kuye.

Masingene kwisambato sobufundisi (clerical dress). Kutenina lento
abashu­mayeli betu (Catechists or Laypreachers) bantsundu batandana
nokunxibisa okwaba fundisi ? Akunjalo kwabamhlope, lontlekisa ayaziwa;
kodwa kuti wofika ede waya kufaka neqhina elimhlope (clerical collar).
Obu budenge endaweni yokumfumanisela imbeko asukela yona umninikubenza,
usuke angazelelelwa luto abe yintlekisa.

Abashumayeli behlelo lase Rabe (noko ndingenguye mna), mandibanike
imbeko yokungazifaki koku kugabadela, andazi nokuba benziwa kukuba
kwababo aba Fundisi kukolisile ukubako kwaba ngazi nanziyo izambato
zobufundisi. Kodwa amaqela amabini endingomnye wawo mna, ama Tshatshi
nama Wesile, bubutataka obendeleyo kubo obu. Noko ndinenkolo yokuba
elidlala belifuna ukwalatwa kodwa, ukuze lize kukweleliswa. Ndingowako,

UKUQWELA KWE BHAYI. —Umbhaleli oti ungu “Ngahlamb’ezantsi kwake” uyarora
ngecapapaza elancoma imvumo yase Bhai uti :—Kwipepa lako le 5 December,
ndifunde izipefumlo zomoya we kwele ezisekwe kumculo owenziwe yi Kwayile
yase Wesile idibene neyase Bhayi. Uti umbhaleli wako : “ Umbhali owati i
Tinara likupe yonke imilambo fanele ukuba uqele ukuva ukurasa
kwabantwana." Makazi umhlobo wetu ukuba kwakunga funzelwene kukutshwana
li Bhayi ne Tinara. Kwakungaze kufezwa nkani epakati kwalombhali nowaye
bhale ngapambili umculo. Ngako oko ukuti kwake la “ qwela ” i Bhayi
ufaka umoya wekwele wokucita imvisiswano ye ramente sase Wesile.
Asizange tina sizincome, singasange sibe nayo nencinga yokuya kuvuma e
Town Hall (Kamakama ngahlamba ezantsi kwako); kuba asikwazi ukuvuma. Ke
yayindawo yom’bhaleli wako ukuba abe wayempikise kwayoko lombhaleli
owancoma i Wesile — ibe kulomculo bekuzekufezwa inkani yabo—ebeya kutsho
ke ukuti “ laqwela i Bhayi.” Umculo lowo wona wokuqala waumhle
kwanjengalo usand’ ukudlula kangangokuba ne " Uitenhage Chronicle ”
yancoma yati yapulapula i “ High Class Music." Of course asiyivumi
indawo yokuba siyazikupa zonke indawo, into esingayifuniyo kukuteta into
ngendlela yamakwele—-lomhlobo wayemelwe kukupikisa kwayoko lowo
wabhalayo. Ukuba akaxoki uyapupa ukuti kwakuze ikwayile yase Bhayi.
Yayingama nene amatandatu awayeye kuvuma i pieces nge Organ. Fan’uba
wati umzalwana weva oluhadi wotuka kakula. Tina siyavuya nokuba
kunconywa i Bhayi kuba sibanye— kodwa wona umculo wabamhle kumacala
omabini. Nabase Bhayi batsho. Imbangi yokuba lenkunkuma ndiyipendule
kukuba sizimisele ukuncedana ne Bhayi, zaye incwadana ezinje ziyakufaka
umoya ombi— endikolwayo ukuba ayisokwamkelwa ngamacala omabini lenteto.

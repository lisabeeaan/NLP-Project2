ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1887

Emva kwezinteto ingxoxo ibe shushu ehlakulelwe indleIa ngo Messrs C.
Bokwe, W. W. Gqoba, yatelelwa kakuhle ngabanye ababeko belo Bandla. Yawa
iminikelo ngoko hlobo olwagqityelwa kudala apa e Dikeni, ukuba Iwake
Iwako, esoyiquka ekupeleni kwale ngxelo. Yapuma eyase mini intlanganiao
kunga akungepunywa.

ITIMITI.

Kuta kanti amakosikazi esikolo esise mfonjeni e Lovedale alungiselele
intlanganiso yangokuhlwa eyi "Timiti,” ebe nkulu, yayola isimanga.
Izaziso zazibike ukuba bafa sisitukutezi salondlu yomzi ixhawe
ngezibonda! Ingenwe i Timiti leyo ngemali. Okwenene ke izele cike tu
indlu leyo, kanti koko kusaza kuwa iminikelo. Kwaba zinkomo, zigusha,
zibokwe kwanezinye impahlana; kwazinxowa zokutya, kwazimali. Zaye zonke
ezizinto zikutshwa ngentliziyo ezivumayo, nezicwayitileyo.

ISIQAMO.

Ngokufutshane isiqamo sidlule le kwinto eb’ilindelwe nangubani. Imali
ebekwe etafileni ngokungena kwentlanganiso zombini iqukiwe ibe ziponti
ezilishumi elinesine (£14) ngapandle kwezidingiweyo, inkomo zintandatu
(6), igusha nebokwe ngamashumi mabini anesine (24) ; inxowa zokutya
mayela kumashutni amatandatu (60). Sikolwa ukuba iqukiwe yonke into
ngexabiso lemali, iminikelo yalo mkumbuzo walonyaka we Jubili yoba
pakati kwama £50 nama £60.

IMIBULELO.

Intlanganiso ipume mayela ku 12 wasebusuku, baye abantu bona
besarozisile ukurola bengatandi ukuyeka. Banqanyulwa ngendawo
yokutenjiswa enye emayibeko, kuba uninzi Iwabanye engelurolile nalo
lungezanga ngezixakeko. Siyambulela u Tixo ngeso senzo, saye siqukumbela
ngeliti — Pambili manenekazi, nani zidwangube zeli Bandla, kambe yiyani
kulityisa elo lizwi lite—‘ Masinga- pumli apa kuba asikabi lilo ikaya
eli.’ Ziseninzi izinto ekufuneka nizingene, ezinje ngeze mfazwe
emayiliwe ye- ntlalo yobuzwe obukanyiselweyo.

Amanenekazi ab’epambili kwimisebenzi yolungiselelo iwale
ntlanganiso—efake pakati umfundisikazi wawo— ngo Mrs. Govan Koboka
(isibondakazi sawo), Mrs. Gqoba, umgcini-ndyebo ; Mrs. J. K. Bokwe,
umkwezeli wayo; o-Mrs. Maloni Toto, Gaula Gazo, Fiyoco Voyi, Ebenezer
Mtule, namanye ekungazala ipepa siwabale onke. Imibulelo ye Bandla
elintsundu lase Dikeni ifanelwe uk ungalityalwa ukubekiswa kuwo kunye ne
choir yosapo Iwawo etsholoze ngento emnandi kunene.

IPULO LE MIDUSHANE.

KUMHLELI WE “ SIGIDIMI.”

NKOSI,—Tina sonke esiwunyamekeleyo umzi ontsundu siyayitakazela,
siyibulela into eyenziwe yi Midushane ngo mhla wesitatu kule veki
igqitileyo. Kwabekuko ingqungqutela yentlanganiso yelohlelo, e Qonce apa
yalomzi ka Ndlambe ipetwe ngama Tshawe onyana baka Siwani, benoyisekazi
into zika Mdushane, o Ndileka no Simiti, kunye nezidwangube zakwa
Mdushane, o Mabofu no Mema no David Mali.

Into ke eyanqinisisayo ukuba imi Dushane ayifeketi kukuba kwafunquka
namanenekazi akona, ezakulilisela ngabantwana namadoda awo, epume ipulo,
etimbisela usapo kweli ramncwa liwuzungulezayo umzi, Ezi ntokazi ke
zipetwe ngu no Popodi umka Belebele, u no Yamile umka Simiti into ka
Mdushane, u no Yesi umka Mbaso, u no Jemsi umka Ziwani, Ateta ke
lomanenekazi, avelisa amazwi obuciko, kuba namhla ebetwa bububele,
ngenxa yento ake ayibona ebicita usapo, namhla engati noko
ikeyakweleliswa, ibuye yasondela yaxalisa. Abalisa ngentsizi ebezipezu
kwo- mzi oko inkanti ibise Tamara. Akuti indoda igodukele ukunkala
umfazi ngenduku, uti umntwana angabi nantloni ukubeta abazali bake,
bati, oko ke sekupelile, namhla konwabekile, bafuna ukuba kuhlale
kunjalo, bati utywala mabungabi sasondela kubo. “ Kwakona ke zintokazi
zakwo Tshiwo ningadinwa nangomso.” Hlabani umkosi ningayeki, Uyihlo u
Siwani wazamana nalo intakati iminyaka emitatu, wati ngowesine wamoyisa
wamgxota. Unyana ka Mdushane wemka elusebenzele usapo Iwake, walushiya
lunokukonwaba kunconywayo namhla ngalomanenekazi. Lilila elo,
bambelelani kulo. Ndanga ndigawazi amagama oyise bezi ntokazi, ukuze
ndiwatate, kuze kuti xase sitshone pantsi tina, baze abazukulwana beta
bati „Intokazi ka Nantsi nentokazi ka Nantsi baduma ipulo,
bayakuhlangula usapo Iwakowabo kumtshabalalisi.”

Nina matshawe nani zidwangube zakwa Mdushane, zintlahlela zalomkosi,
bahlanguli bosapo Iwakwa Xosa, ningadinwa. Nango umfuziselo wokuzama kwe
nkosi yenu, ninawo, eyati noko yoyisiweyo ayancama, yavela ngokuvela
iminyaka emine, inene yema yasala nayo. Amazwi enu abaliwe, angamanqina
enu, okokuba ningabahlanguli noko noyiswayo, ityala aninalo, losala nabo
bangatelelanga kulomsebenzi mkulu niwuzamayo. Mna okwam ndiyanibulela,
nditandaza ngokuti,—Wanga u Somandla angawusikelela umsebenzi wenu
anenzele inceba.

Ukuqoshelisa make ndibuye ngomva nditi, lentlanganiso esemakulwini omane
ngenani, yayize kwi Mantyi eyintloko u Mr. Chalmers, yafika ehambile,
yase isamkeIwa yi Mantyi engu Mr. Dick, yakukupela kayo ukuteta isiti
makubekiswe ku Rulumente. U Mr. Dick ubapendule ngamazwi ofefe,
ebakutaza kulomsebenzi bawupeteyo esiti amazwi e Midushane uya kuwasa
komkula, etemba ukuba akayi kuputa,

Batete onyana baka Siwani, kwanga kulaula amadoda akade ebona,
ebekungati nomfi lowo ebavile abantwana bake ange ete,—Namhla
ndingasitela, umzi ndiwondlele amadoda.

Ateta amatshawe onyana baka Mdushane, kwabonakala ukuba nangona u Somzi
engaseko, usapo aluzizo inkedama lusele nabondli.

Zateta izidwangube ngamazwi abazileyo ahlabayo, amazwi ezicaka ezigcine
usapo olushiywe nazo, zavelisa ubupakati benyaniso.

Masivakale isitonga sezwi lenu mhlambi wakwa Siwani, sitsho singadambi,
nimsulwa aninatyala, qinisani ningatyafi.

Nango ke umsebenzi luhlanga. Imidushane iseli yihlahlile intsunguzi
yenza umendo ngenani ngawo, ukuba niluyekile usapo Iuka Siwani noba
ningalulahla uhlanga lonke. Kutiwa sebeko abohlanga oluntsundu abo sebe
bekise komkulu besiti mabuvulelwe utywala, nanko ke ukupamba kuka
Mnyaluza kulumkeleni.

Xego Daka.

UKUQALWA KOMSEBENZI.

Umsebenzi nge uqalwe pakati ko 9 no 10 kusasa, kwati kuba intsapo
zondawo ngendawo zingapelelanga ngexesha zonke wasel’uqalwa ngo 10.

Kuvulwe ngeculo nomtandazo ngu Mr. Makiwane. KuqaleIwe ezantsi kwi
ntsapo ezi Below the Standards; kwati kwalapo kwaqauka kuhlangana, kuba
namhla kuqubisene amajila ngamajila emilambo ngemilambo. Kate ngenxa
yokuyola komsebenzi kwabonakala ukuba ixa liyadleka makuyiwe kwaba ku
Standard I. nangani kungapelanga konke ebekusafuneka kwaba ngapapantsi
kwabo. Abaviwe kwezi ziqibi zozibini ngama 91.

Kungnewe kwabo besiqibi sesibini, kuqondakele ukuba uyajiya umkupa
bawucokisile kakuhle umsebenzi wabo kwabonakala ebusweni betitshala zabo
ukuba zonwabile, azixalile nento ngabo, kuba zibafundisile kakuhle.
Asisateti ke kona ku Standard II. kusuke kwe rhe, kwanga kukumka
kwamaqwara. Kambe zona imbengu ezihamba zisala kwezondidi ngendidi
asisateti nto ngazo, kuba ifaniselana iba yimfe iba nembengu noko, buti
nobusi bube namakambi. Kuhambe kusala amakambi kuzo zonke ezizikolo,
bepuma abapumayo beshiya abanye Abaviwe kwesi siqibi ngama 45.

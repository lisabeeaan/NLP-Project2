ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 1, 1887.

AMABAL’ ENGWE.

UMBULELO.

Sivuye kunene sakubona emapepeni ukuba ipelile imfazwe yasema Mpondweni
kwanohlobo ekugqitwenwe ngalo, kuba bekazakuhla izinto ezimbi, pezu
kobunzima obusekoyo nanamhla obupezu kwento yonke engumntu wale Koloni
obubangwe lixelegu elikulu, imfazwe ka Ncayec'ibi. Inanamhla izitonga
zayo zisavakala, kusatamba ixalanga nanamhla pezu kwelilizwo lakowetu.

Kulo lonke ebelisiliwa, licitakele, linjalonje lisaqubela pambili
ekubeni njalo. Namhlanje akungemahluli obosehlatini nobengekulo ngento
yonke. Impato, icitakalo, ukutinjwa ngenxa yento-ngento, kude kutshulwe
nezisele ngokwe mfazwe kanye, zigede ezifana nqwa kwane zemfazwe. Namhla
yonk’into iyafana, umtinjwa nomtimbi, umc’iti nomcitwa, kulele ngangubo
nye.

Kodwake kuyo yonke lento, kuko into engumangaliso ekoyo, ngo kukodwa
kuwo lomnyaka. Kute okukona umzi ugqibeleyo ukuci- takala, ubuxeke
wancama, nango mbendeni nezitwayi, kwakukona amasi emiyo kulemizana
yonke. Kokukona ilizwe lisuke layi Kanana, limpompoza ubusi, namasi.

Kuko ipiko logcino elisite wambu, Iasi gubungela lasikusela pezu kwazo
zonke ezizinto. Kanjalo lencitakalo, ayiketi mabala nazizwe, kuba ipetwe
sisandla esinenceba. Yinto eninzi ezibadubadu nezimhlope
ezikwac'itakeleyo nazo, zisafunyenwe yimfakadolo yamashwa emfazwe ka
Ncayec'ibi. Yinto eninzi ehamba ifuna inkonzo naku bantu abantsundu,
ihamba nosapo Iwayo, IMFENGUZA, kuba icitakale kuba ute obezuzile ngo
Ncayecibi, wandisa inyango nentlanti zake kwinto yonke, wafika
umbendeni, nesitwayi, isidiya, uqonqa, imofu, ubende, inyongo, ibula,
umdoko, amatyala entlobo-ntlobo zonke namhla simfaninye, umntwan’ufana
nonina, sipotula ngobencuka kunye.

Kuko konke oku, sifanelwe kubulela, kuba lowo usidalileyo akasi-
libalanga noko, kwade kwanamhla. Usasinqaka, usasilamlele kwe- zinto
zonke. Nguye lowo upakati kwezo nqwitela nezo ndudumo nemibane
yalomashwa nezo ncitakalo. Nguye omane esikamela amanxeba, ngezwi lake,
kuba yiyo into asinike yona akalinganisela wayefuna ukuba siqonde ukuba
uyasitanda noko singamtandiyo tina ngenxa yezono zetu. Kute apo sukuba
zifuna ukuqwebeka kona imfazwe, wahamba elamla. Kozindlala usihlutisile,
wasipumputa amapango etu nawe ntsapo zetu.

Ude wancamisa kona kulo nyaka we 1836. Ilizwe lityebe lavusa okudala,
kungati pofu akubangako zimvula zininzi. Kusike imvaba nomazana linye
esele ku mbendeni. Asingawo masi, ngamatonto kulo lonke ilizwe. Kute
kunjalo, bati ubusi ukuba buninzi yayinkohla Namhla bunqikwa nazipuluwa
emasimini, kuhluta nenja yehlwempu, kududa no Mtwa wentaba.

Sifanelwe kubonga nokubulela ngazwinye senze isihomo somme melo ubonga
lowo upezu konke, usebenza ngendlela ezifihlakeleyo kuti, ezingati
inxenye ziza kanzima, akuba eteta ngezwi lendudumo nemibane, kanti
kwakuwo lomafu antshinyela amnyama aqume inceba nemfesane, aqume ifa
malunga nati, kanti kokona asisondeza ebumeni bokuzicingela, sinqonde
ukuba umhlaba uyadlula kunye nezinto zawo

  Siyavuyisana kanye nama Mpondo, ekubeni epulukile engozini
  inamhla, kodwa malumke, ayaleke kwinto ezitile ezinje ngokuba
  ubusela, nokungalamkeli ilizwi, nempotsha nezinye izinto ezinje
  ngotywala bom Lungu.

  Singati kuma Baca nama Xesibe, senze isiyalo esiti—Mangatalasi
  atwele kakulu ngenxa yesisigqibo kugqitywene kuso, ukumbule ukuba
  oyisile. Aya bonwu kakuhle nangu Rulumente ukuba anenkatazo.
  Ke makalumke angabi yimbangeli yemfazwe emva koku, hleze isikuni
  sipetukele kwakuwo. Lomti ungu Rulumente ngulo mti kutiwa ngu
  mgalagala, wona ungati ukuba ukuwucandanga use luhlaza, ube aku
  sokubuye uwutini. Ngumti olukuni nje ngelityo eli. Kunjalo um-
  cana lo ikwangumti ofana no Rulumente lo. Lumkela uhlobo loku-
  kwezela kwako xa ubase inkuni zomcana. Awunanto xa usand'
  ukuwubasa, kodwa kona xa sel’uqala amalahle, ho! wafu zihtlantsi
  uke wacukumisa eziko. Zoququmba intlantsi kutsho nabantwana,
  ungaketi. ati yena umkwezeli aligabonakali.

  Unjalo ke kanye u Rulumente, usawaquba ama Baca nama Xesibe nje kodwa,
  ukuba akayiyekanga lenkatazo namhlanje, akuko matanda, ukuba, u
  Rulumente wowaququmbela ngohlobo angazange alubone napakade. Siyazi
  ukuba lenteto ye Sigidimi okwanamhla isaya kuba rara kwabo ibayalayo,
  kanti ke yinqobo yeyeza.

Lento inyinga iselwa irara, kanti ke luncendo olukulu koyiginyileyo,
Kukwanjalo ke malunga nalenteto ye Sigidimi, isaya kuba luncedo
ukuba itatyatiwe, ngala macala omabini, nokuba namhlanje iyakuvaka-
la imbi kangakananina.

  Kube mnandi sakubona inani elitunyelwe ngumfo ka Vela ose
  Dayimani elingena kwi Mvo Zabantsundu. Kumhlope kona ukuba
  nama Gqunukwebe la aseza kulitabata elopepa, asabanjwe zimbande.
  zelo ezi sesitete kade ngazo. Kanjalo sitemba kwa okunjalo e Sigidi-
  mini.

Batabati be Sigidimi abange kahlauli, tumelani yonke imali enisene.
tyala ngayo, kuba kumhlope ukuba amagama anjalo azakunqunyulwa
angatunyelwa amapepa awo. Fan’ukuba niyite meqe londawo e Slgidimini
senyanga efileyo. Kunyaka ozayo kuyakuma ngohlobo olutsha kwinto
ezininzi, kwane zase Sigidimini, ke ngoko abasasifunayo mabahlaule
amatyala aso onke. Lihlazo ukupuma u Mhleli eshiya amatyala enu epepa.

Hutshu! Hutshu! Fan’ukuba imbali zama Xosa kunye nentsomi namhla
ziyapela kwelipepa li Sigidimi kuba UMHI.ELI waso omdala obetandana
nezonto kakulu epumile namhla, kuyakonwatywa kanye ngabebezikalazela,
kulilwe ngabebe zifuna zombini ezonto, kuba zinge- ko kuwo onke amanye
amapepa esi Xosa.

Kuke kwako i Timiti (teameeting) yama Tempile alunge kule Tyalike ka
Rev. P. J. Mzimba ngo Mvulo we 13 ka December 1836, Kwakuko iqela
elimnandi. Zamnandi kanye nenteto ezawiswayo zokukutazana komhlambi,
ezivele kumadlula ngamadlula, ekubeko nezivela ku maxego anamava ento
akade ezibona ezenziwe luselo lotywala. Kwaku ndawonye nokubulela ugcino
Iuka Tixo, kwanoku- ziyaleza ngokutsha, kumabona-ndenzile onyaka ozayo.
Yacitakala inge ayingepumi, ngenxa yobumnandi bayo.

Kuke kwako nenye kwa i Timiti ekwamnandi kunene, yokunduluka kwebuto eli
lifundayo lase Lovedale, yayiyole yancama. Sati isidima sayo sabangelwa
nalundwendwe olwaluko olungu Rev. Dr. Cameron wase Kapa, wehlelo lase
Depente. Ngumfo owateta ngamazwi amnandi kunene, obuciko, namazwi angena
kwele. Kwaye kanjalo kuko nolunye undwendwe u Mvi-zikula owenza kwa
inteto emnandi kunene.

Kwaye kuko nolunye undwendwe olwalu sel’luntsuku luko e Lovedale, imvumi
enkulu u Mr. Aitken wase Qonce umfo wengoma kwayekwa, [Uvile?] obete
wayihlokulula imvumo yase Lovedale okunye. Kwaye kute mhla wafunda ipepa
lamava ake endawo ngendawo ake wakuzo, walivanga nge ngonyana zakowabo,
ezabeta umzi wakumbula kwelo ninakulu Pesheya kolwandle, ngokukodwa ama
Sikotshi. Ngumfo otandwa kunene yiyo yonk’ into yalapa, ngokukodwa
likaba kuba uneb'asi kakulu umfo, ugquma ade abubule ad’alugqumelele no
hadi (harmonium).

Imvula inile kakulu ngosuku Iwe Cawa ngokuhlwa nango Mvulo nge 19 ne 20,
into ebete yasivuyisa kunene, nangani inxenye ibisizakele kakulu
yakubona lingati liyapuma kwakona, yayikangele kwindawo yokuba lomzi
untsundu uyawakupila ngenkinqa, iti inxowa yombona ibe kwa ziponti
ezimbini £2 ixele kwa nyakenye.

Ize nilime kunene umbona, itapile, imbotyi, i elityisi ngayo lenya- nga
ingu January, nilime kunene nerasi zase busika, into yonke
yokuhlanganisa kozintswelo, rafu, ndlala njalo-njalo, ningake ninyabe
bantu bantsundu nakuyipiwa imvula, yenzani into ngayo.

Siyavuyisana ne Mvo ekupumeleleni kwayo kumagama enyanga zesi Xosa.
Maninzi ke wona engafani, into engenakutiwani ke leyo. Akolise ngokuti
siti eso isizwe siyibize lonyanga ngokulungelelene nezimo zayo, nokuba
zizibonakalo nezi vakalo ezikoyo ngayo. Zide zimbi zibe namagama
nangamabini nangamatatu, kuba abo bayibiza ngento edla ngokubako ngayo.

Musilinganisele siti—U December lo inxenye iti yoyo nkanga. Ngu Nkanga
ke ngoko, generic name, kanti kukwa yinene ukuba ngu Lwezi. U January
abanye bati yeyo mnga, okunye kutiwe lilanga lomnga elo lalo nyanga. Ngu
Mnga ke ngoko. U July ngu Kala, kuba lityatya- mba ngayo ikala,
(intlaba) kodwa elinye nelona ibizwa ngalo ngu Ntu- lukuzi, kuba ino
ntuli kakulu. Kuko no Msintsi, Ndozosela, Pezukom- kono, Gogode, Mkonyo,
njalo-njalo.

La magama alandelayo abekelwe ukubonisa nje kodwa, asingawo kanye
ezibizwa ngawo ngokute dzu. Zezi—Mkonyo, Gogode, Msi- ntsi, Kula,
Pezukomkono, Mnga, Nkanga, Mkonyo, njalo-njalo. Zona ezomsintsi zimbini,
ngu Matupa, no Madikazi. Okoke kukuti—Iyo- dwa inyanga yaxa use matupa
umsintsi, ibayodwa eyaxa udubuleyo qoro xa ke umadikazi. Amagama enyanga
obuye abekwe nanini.

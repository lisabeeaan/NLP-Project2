DIKWENI ngolwesi Bini olu ukuhluza izinto eziya kubhekiswa ku

Umzi unga ungambona nangamehlo lomhlobo wawo mdala

Ruluneli.

SIVA kakubi ukuba kubeko noluncinane urano mayelana nokuba u Rulumente
usayilambele imihlatyana emiwe ngamawetu ale Koloni. Angati umntu eke
waqwalasela kumfanekiso wemihlaba yeli, ahlute yindawo yokuba ubuninzi
bomhlaba bunikwe amafama amhlope ngu Rulumente, aza amawetu agandelwa
kumhlatyana omncinane kunene. Nxa ke izinto zimi ngohlobo olunje amelwe
amawetu ukundwebela ukuginyelwa okukumbi kwale mihlaba ingasepi anayo.
Siwenza nje lamanqaku sibingelwa zincwadi ezivela kwimi mandla emibini
apo kuko imihlaba egcinelwe amawetu. Imimandla ye Glen Grey ne Ngqushwa
Nakulomandla wase Qonce ziko izikalazo zokuginyelwa kwemihlaba yamawetu.
Make noko sizibeke bucala indawo zase Ngqushwa nezase Qonce, sizigcinele
elinye ixesha, kuba ngoku sifuna ukuke sitete ngeyona ndawo
ixinezekileyo le yase Glen Grey. Ke malunga nalomandla, singake sibek’
indawo yokuba amawetu ayevumelene no Rulumente ukuba kwabelwa
iziqiniselo zemihlaba zeziqu izikolo esihlanu. Unocanda sekuzinyanga
ezitile ewuquba lomsebenzi. Kute nxa acandela isikolo sakwa Bangindlala
kwako ukungavisisani okutile pakati kwabantu no Mantyi ngendawo
ezishiywayo, noko kwabuya kwalungiswa kakuhle. Ke ngoku kucandelwa esase
Mgqukwebe ekutiwa yi Agnes. Ukuqaleka kolucando kwaye kunqotshiswene
ngokuba isikolo eso soba nokwenzelwa idlelo etaitileni yaso. Kwakubon’
ukuba abantu basikelwe amasimi banela, umhlaba oshiyekileyo uyaka
wenziwa idlelo. Akuncomeki ku Rulumente ukuba ati umntu ongumlomo wake
kwisitili sase Glen Grey ibe nguye kuqala owapula umnqopiso. Usuke u
Mantyi wayalela u Nocanda ukuba angawubandakanyeli etaitileni yabantu
base Mgqukwebe umhlaba omkulu owawusoloko ulidlelo labo kwanini. Umzi ke
wotuke kunene yilonto, kunjalo nje yonke i Glen Grey yotukile seso
senzo. Lento ke iya kubangela ukuba abantu bakowetu, abebe bupakupaku
kade, bafun’- ukuti bazincame itaitile. Asikolwa kanye ukuba lento
yokuginywa komhlaba wamawetu yenziwa ngesigqibo se Bhunga elingu
Rulumente; esitembayo ukuba amawetu ayakuti angamcingeli kakubi ade u
Rulumente alenze elona lake, Kuba, ukuba asiposisi ukuluqonda uluvo
lwale Koloni, aluko ekuwuhluteni umhlatyana angaba asenawo amawetu
azolileyo, ahleli ngoxolo. Umzi umi ngengxinano nangapandle kokuba ubuye
upangwe igadana obusenalo.

INGQONDO ZOBUDODA.

U MR. WILLIAM ROGERS INGQONDO kwipepa lase Katikati (Chronicle) wenze
incazelo yokuziroxisa kulomnybadala ube uko. Izizatu azicazileyo
zezibonisa ukuba ngumfo otanda izwe lakowabo ngentliziyo yake yonke,
zaye ziya kuluqubela pambili udumo lwake ngengqondo eveueyo yobunumzana
embusweni kuwo wonke lomandla we Mpu­malanga, ngokukodwa kowase Komani.
Umfo ka Rogers wenjenje ukubhala ebhekisa kwababemtembise ivoti zabo:— “
Akufuneki ukuba ndinikumbuze uku­ba ukuzingenisa kwam kulomnyhadala
wonyulo ndandingenela ndigqibe kwelokuba ize ndilande ingcambu zeyona
nkohlakalo zinkulu zale Koloni, yenye yezinkulu inkohlakalo leke Utywala
obutshipu. Kulamadoda asixenxe ebese mnyhadaleni, wokumela lomandla abe
matatu azimisela ukuba ayakuxoxa icala lokuba utywala burafiswe ngo 10s.
Kekwati kuqala anga lamadoda ayakupumelela ekunyulweni omatatu. Kute
kwakuqondakala indawo-ndawo ezitile, kwabuya kwabonakala ukuba
akunakwenzeka kanye oko. Kwafuneka ukuze kupumelele mabini, enye iroxe.
Iqela elikulu labatiye utywala lite lagqiba kwelokuba lixhase mna
ndedwa, libalahle ababini abanye. Ndite andakolwa andayamkela lonto.
Ngokweliqela icala ebendinga lingake linqine beliyakufumana ivoti ibenye
e Bandla, ukuxhasa lomcimbi ugqite eminye endaweni yoku­ba zibe mbini.
Ndite ke ndakuqonda ukuba ukuze kungabiko lishwa ndagqiba kwelokuncama
eyam indawo ebendite ndayifumana, ndiroxe ukuze sekungena madoda mabini
ecala eliyakuxhasa irafu ye 10s. etywaleni. Ukuba indoda eti iyayitanda
intlalo entle yoluntu lonke ayizilungiselele ukuzikanyela kwingxingwa
yomqatango onje, ngenxa yowabo abo, nangenxa yemvo zayo, ayifanele
kutenjwa nganto zihlobo nabaxhasi bayo.” U Mr. Rogers uqukumbela
ngelokuvakalisa itemba lokuba abazizihlobo zake nabaxhasi bake
bozitakazelela inda­wo ezimbangele ukuba azincame aroxe emnyhadaleni
ngenxa yokunga banganelungelo bonke ngabanye. Esisenzo sinje
kulomqatango wonyulo sesineziqamo ezitile; kuba ke sifumana kwipepa
lakwa Komani, nakwase Monti enconywa ngesisenzo,—kutetwa kakuhle ngaye
apo kunene bekungazanga kutetwe mnandi, ekuqondakalayo ukuba ngonyulo
oluzayo xa ate wavuma ukungeniswa, uya kwamkeleka kwabaninzi
abebengazange bamcinge.

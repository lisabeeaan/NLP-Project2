ngwa yase Peiwar. Eyetu imikosi ibipetwe ngu General Roberts. Omnye
wabebekona uti, Sigqibe intsuku ezintatu sikangela ukuba singangena
kulipina icala, saqonda ukuba utshaba lunqabile, umkosi wetu mncinane.
U-General Roberts uqonde ukuba into angake ayilinge kukucotela utshaba
ngobusuku aluvelele ecaleni. Ngobusuku bosuku lokuqala ku December
kunduluke imikosi emitatu, yahamba ku- mhlaba ombi kunene ifunzele ukuze
kuti kusisa ibe ingena iluvelele ngase kohlo utshaba. Okwenene kute
kusiti qeke aba engena awakonibe. Zilile kwa- kubi, lalibam-bam kumacala
omabini. Enye yenko- sana zecala letu abati ngu Kelso iwiswe kwase ntlo-
ko. Kude kwaziyure ezintatu kungatandeki kunga- qondeki into okuya kuba
yiyo, kusiwa umntu. Lide ikohlo lotshaba lazamazama, labonakala lishu-
kuma. Kaloku kungene umkosi omtsha kweletu icala wafunza kwibuto
elipakati lotshaba. Kunge kungabambana yawa enye inkosana yeletu icala
abati ngu Major Anderson. Kude kwapela iyure zibambe- ne, lwashenxa
nalapo utshaba. Kweso situba kufike eminye imikosi emibini yecala letu.
U-General Roberts wenze iqinga lokuzamela ukuluvelela ngase mva.
Eloqinga lipumelele kakuhle, kuba abantu balapa ukoyika kwabo ukujikelwa
ngase mva bangati bazimfene. Ude kaloku wabaleka wonke umkosi wa-
tshaba, safumana ukupefumla ukumka komhla. Kweletu icala kufe inkosana
ezimbini. Kubantu nje abafileyo bedibene nengxwelera ngamashumi asitoba.
Abotshaba asibanga nakubaqonda.

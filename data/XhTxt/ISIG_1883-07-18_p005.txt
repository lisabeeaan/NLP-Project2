INTLANGANISO YOMANYANO.

E Bayi ngomhla we 4th July 1883, bekuko intlanganiso yomnyaka “
Yomanyano.” Pambi kokuba singene kuyo ingxelo yale ntlanganiso, soke
senze incazo yokubonisa into oluyiyo olu “ Manyano.”

Igama lolu Manyano ngokwama Ngesi yi “ South African Aborigines
Association.” Yintlanganiso emiselwe ukuba ibe liziko lokuxoxa zonke
izinto zokunyusa uhlanga. Nokuba zeze Mfundo, nokuba zeze Ngqeqesho,
nokuba zezo Laulo, nokuba zezamatuba empumelelo, “ nokuba zeze mihlaba
(ukubandeza igada lobawo ngokutenga amatuba kulemihla mibi.) Amasiko,
imikwa, nazo zonke izinto zenveli ekubonakalayo ukuba ziyi ntintelo
ekuhlumeni kohlanga ziya kuxoxwa kuyo le Mbumba.

Zopuma kuyo le mbumba izicelo ezibeka e Botwe, nemitandazo yokutandazela
inkululo yenkosana ezemka nemisinga yemfazwe. Koti ukuqutywa kwezi zinto
zimiselwe i Committee zazo, ukuze zibe nokuqutywa ngenyameko. Yobako i
Committee yemfundo, neye miteto yolaulo, neyokwalusa amalungelo e
Locations, neyo kugqala amalungelo aba voti committee to attend to the
Registration of voters.) Yobako i Committee enkulu ye ntlanganiso
yomnyaka eziyakupakela kuyo zonke ezinye imicimbi, ukuze ilungiselwe
intlanganiso yomnyaka. Koti emizini yonke kwa kumiswa le Mbumba, kuyilwe
i Committee zokukangela imicimbi efanele ukungeniswa entlanganiswem
yomnyaka. Le yomnyaka ayikuma ndaweni nye, yoti ngomnye umnyaka ibe se
Bayi, kanti ngomnye yoba se Cradock, e Grahamstown. e Qonce njalo njalo!
Abo iya kungabiko kubo botumela abatunywa babo (Delegates) bokubavela
indaba, nokuba yimilomo yabo. Sizama isiqalo somcimbi onje ke ngayo le
Mbumba. Sisiva ngendlebe ngati ziko intlanganiso eziyelene nale yetu.
Asizidelile, ukuba ziquba lomcimbi wetu sobamkela abatunywa bazo,
kanjako imicimbi etunyelwa zizo kuti soyixoxa. Noko ke asiyiboni into
exakayo ukuba zingangeni zonke pantsi kweli gama le Nzala (aborigines)
yeli lizwe ngokwe mvelo.

Iko kanjako nale ibifudula ibizwa ngokuba yeyo Titshala, namhla kutiwa
yi “Native Educational. Association” Mkulu umsebenzi wayo, unjalo nje
ufana nentlaka yeliso ngobu delicate (au! lindixakile elo esi Xoseni.
Kuko abati ke nayo leyo inokuwuquba lomsebenzi wale yetu siyiva
singayivisisi londawo. Ngani? 1. Lentlanganiso yobangenisa engozini o
Titshala, kuba boxakwa bakungena kweze lizwe. 2. O dade boxakwa
kwakungenwa kwezo Laulo. 3. Umongameli ngumlungu, woxakwa kwakutetwa ngo
Langalibalele, no Gonye, no Matanzima 4. Isingesi sowaxaka amaqaba
ohlanga. 5. U Dr. Dale worazula izivato zake ambate intlakantlaka ahlale
elu tutwini ukuba usapo Iwake lupumile emadlelweni amnandi emicimbi
yemfundo, selu penu-penuka ematambekeni, nase mazeni agqumayo e
Politics. Le yetu yoba likuselo kuzo i Teachers, kuba zoxoxa, xivote,
zingaveliswa amagama azo kupele kwimicimbi yemfundo. Ngokunjalo no
Wauchope abafo be Civil Service bokuselwa kwayiyo.

I President—u Mr. S. P. Sihlali wasitabata isihlalo ngo 8 p.m. Wenza
ingxelo eyabonisa ukuba amalungu e Mbumba e Graaff Reinet akwi 140. Waka
wafumana incwadi ezivela pesheya kwe Nciba zikwa takazelela le Mbumba. E
Graaff Reinet ngumxube kunye naba kumsha isi Bulu, londawo ibanga ukuba
imicimbi ingaqubeki kaku- hle, kufuneke i toliki. Yobuye ifunelwe ituba
londawo.

Kwatunyelwa isicelo kwi Municipality sokuba kunge kutotywa imali yeziza
e Lokeshoni, lomali yi 6s. ngenyanga eyi £3 12s ngonyaka. I Roma
ayakiwanga ngamhlamnye, ngoko msani ukutyafa. Imali yokungena i Rafu yi
6d., umnikelo wenyanga yi 3d. ezi 3s. ngonyaka.—

U Mr. Wauchope i Chairman ye Committee yendlu enkulu, neye sebe lase
Bayi wabulela amazwi e President, wabulela nokubona abantu abavela
kwindawo ezikude beze ngomcimbi we mbumba. E Bayi inani lamalungu
likufupi ekulwini.—Imicimbi eke yaxoxwa emikulu. 1. Ngowo kugxotwa kwe
Location. 2. Ngowemiteto ye Municipality emana isenza imigibe
yokubambisa izidenge zakowetu, ngakumbi umteto wokuzituma (26th
Municipal Regulation under sect 4 of the Municipal Bye Laws) ekusaza
kutunyelwa isicelo ngawo kwi Town Council, nakwi Chamber of Commerce. 3.
Yimizamo namatuba okunceda izipatamandla ekuncipiseni izipitipiti,
nemiqomboti pakati komzi. 4. Ngowo kumiswa kwe nkati, nezicelo
ezabekiswa kwi Licensing Board. Nengqibo (Resolutions) ezitile ezatu

nyelwa kwi Town Council malunga ne ngqakaqa.

Isicelo malunga nomzi lo, sabalwa satunyelwa ngo John Mackay, Esq.,
M.L.A. umhlobo wetu. U Rev. Mr. Mzamo umqubi wemitandazo (Chaplain)
akadinwa, akatyafi ekulweni idabi elipakati kokwazi nobudenge, izwi lake
aliteta futi xa akutaza umhlambi, lokuti—“ Amalungelo ohlanga
ayasetyenzelwa, akawi njenge mana yase ntlango,” aselede amila kuti.

Colesberg akwi 60. Kuseko ubu- denge kuma lunga namasiko okuqutywa

Wati propose u Mr. Momoti, waqokelwa ngu Mr. Wauchope “ Ukuba luluvo
Iwale ntlanganiso ukuba umsebenzi wokunonelela amalungelo ohlanga

Kwavunyelwana emva kwe ngxoxo.

U Mr. Mzozoiana wati propose waqokelwa ngu Mr. Philip Job, “ Ukuba

umngenisi wayo lengxoxo, bona ngapa e Colesberg bayawoyika ama Bulu kuba
ngo Bawo. Kwavunyelwana.

Wati propose u Mr. Wauchope wasekelwa ngu Mr. N. Gwantsa “ Ukuba luluvo
Iwale ntlanganiso ukuba umda waba kufaneleyo ukuvota (Franchise) mawume

U Mr. Mzozoiana wati yinina lento asuke alihlute igama letu amana esiti
singo Kafile, eze ati wona angama Afri­kander, kanti elo kanye ligama
letu. Yile yetu i Afri­kander Bond ! Leyo yabo yi Boeren Bond or yi
Paupers’ Band, kuba bafika selimi kakade eli lase Africa.

January 1884.

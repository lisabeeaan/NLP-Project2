4.  Oku kuvalwa kwe nkanti zodwa akuyi kuba luncedo ezi Rementeni kuba
    sinabafazi baba hedeni, sinentsapo zaba hedeni. Umhedeni uyatanda
    ukuba zivalwe inkanti kuba unabantu bokumsilela namhla um
    ramentekazi uya kusila ade abe sitywetywe etyeni, uya kukutuka
    izandla namadolo kukusila, umntwana akasoze asibone isikolo. Nditi
    ubunzima boluvalo buya kupumla pezu kwe ramente napezu kwaba
    Fundisi. Ndingati ubulungisa nobulumko balento ukuba buko bunje
    ngokubamba inyoka ngomsila.

5.  Manditi kwakona ngokuvalwa kwe nkantini asisayi kungenisa bulungisa,
    kodwa siya kulahlekwa. Uselo luyakuba ngapezulu ngokuba abantsundu
    banokuzenzela utywala ngokwabo obungenamteto, obungena license,
    banokusela batengise nje ngokutanda kwabo, i reyisa yenza utywala
    obubukali ngapezu kwa mazimba, itrofiya yenza utywala, nengqolowa
    yenza utywala, kuya kusila kude kusile neqeya lase zintabeni.

Ubunxila obu abandiswanga yi ‘ Brandy ’ kakulu, ngumqomboti owesa abantu
e brandini ukwanda kokuselwa kwomqomboti kwaqalela malunga no 1856
ngokusuka bungeniswe emitshatweni eloxesha bekusasilelwa emipandeni
yodongwe, kute pambi kwe 1860 kwa ngenwa ezifatyeni, emveni koko aqalile
amadodana ukuza no small one (half bottle, i brandy ke leyo)
emitshatweni, wapembeka umlilo. U nintshi Iwamadodana luya ezidolopini
selungamanxila kade, nditi luya emlungwini selukwazi ukunxila ngapandle
kokunxilii ewa yi brandy, luti ke Iwakufika emlungwini lungakwazi
ukusilw- isilingo se brandy kuba luqelile ukunxila.

    Rulumente amise umteto wokuba kungabiko migidi yatywala bamaxosa,

    kungabiko lunyanzelo Iwendoda emfazini ukuba asile utywala, haze
    babonise ezi zizatu zenziwa butywala ati u ‘ Fikizolo ’ kwafa
    abantwana bababini, ndifuna ukuti intsana, zishicilelwa ngonina
    benxilile butywala bomgidi kwa Nomadolo, mhlaumbi omnye umfazi

    Bayi nase Bini, nangezinye induma zapesheya kwe Nciba, ezi zizenzo

IBANKI ZE POST OFFICE ZOKULONDOLOZA IMALI.

Umongameli we Post Office unga angake akukanyisele ngamalungelo
angafunyanwayo kwi Post Office, ezinendawo ngoku yokulondoloza imali
nokuba zimalana ezincinane, awoba neliso kuzo u Rulumente, ayimele
nembuyekezo yazo.

Ukuqala ungafaka nokuba yisheleni enye, ekungati xa sukuba uyifuna,
nakuba ungasekona kulondawo apo wawuyifake kona, uyikupe ngokulula
nokuba kukuyipina indawo eyi Post Office egcina imali apa e Koloni.
Njengoko bese kutshiwo, akukataleki apo ungaba ub’uyifake kona, ongati
mhlaumbi ukuba ub’uyifake e Monti, ngokutanda kwako wongezelele enye,
okanye uyikupe yonke, nokuba ukupa isiqingata sayo, xa ke mhlaumbi
uyifuna ungekona e Monti, apo wauyifake kona, ungati uyikupe nokuba kuse
Qonce, kwa Komani, e Rini, e Kimberley, e Bayi, umhlaumbi e Kapa,
ngazwinye yobatalwa nokuba kupina apo ungaba utanda ukuyifumana kona.

UKU TSHINTSHWA KO MANTYI:—I Cape Argus ibika uku nyulelwa ko mantyi
kwezinye indawo. Iti u Mr. Simpson, imantyi yakwa Qoboqobo, unyulelwe
ukuba abe yi mantyi yase Walvisch Bay (kwela Maqwelana); u Mr. Shaw,
wakwa Kentame, utabata indawo ka Mr. Girdwood, wase Tsomo (e Mbulu),
otabata yena indawo ka Mr. Shaw. Ayikamxeli oza kutabata indawo ka Mr.
Simpson.

KWELA BASUTU.—Indaba ezivela kweli zisate nqum. Ezi ntsukwini zokuqala
zo July kuke kwa hanjiswa udaba lo cingo lokuti u Masupha ubulele ama
polisa amabini, kwa fumaneka mva kungeko siseko kulo nteto. Ezamva nje
zikolisa umxelo. Bonke onyana baka Mshweshwe bavukele u Masupha ngenxa
yobu ngwanyalala bake.

IBANDLA LASE LOVEDALE.—Uibandla eliquka ama Sheshegu, nomi Xelo, noma
Gaga, noma Roxa pantsi kwalo, libe nentlanganiso yalo, yokukumbuza umhla
wokuvulwa kwetyalike yase Lovedale ngo 26 July. U Rev. Mr. Mzimba ese
sihlalweni. Inexelo yonyaka inikwe ngu Rev. E. Makiwane obeyongamele
okwe xesha. Ibonise ukukoliseka kwemicimbi yemali ze remente; ukudanisa
kwemali zo Titshala; noku nyabela izikula kwa bantu.

kwaziswa ukuba u Hon. Thomas Charles Scanlen umiselwe ekubeni yi
Colonial Secretary (u Mpati Micimbi yeli Lizwe) endaweni ka Hon. John
Charles Molteno ; u Hon. James Weston Leonard abe yi Attorney General
(intloko yaba Teteleli ematyaleni) enda­weni ka Thomas Charles Scanlen.

I GRAAFF REINET COLLEGE.—Iminyaka ibonise ukuba esi sikula asina kuma.
Kute ngenxa yoko kwamenywa intlanganiso ngo 27 June, yoku kangela ukuba
kunga tiwanina, efike yagqiba kwelo kuti mauke uhanjiswe njengo kwanga
pambili umsebenzi. Siqonda ukuba into efunekayo kuku guqulwa kwe
gama—ukuba ingabi yi College. Amadoda afundisayo kuyo okwa namhla ngu
Dr. J. K. Da'll no Professor W. Le Cornu, M.A.

KWINDLU enkulu ye Parlamente ya Pesheya, eyi House of Lords kubeko,
malungana napakati kuka June ingxoxo ngo mteto ebeku sitiwa maubeko,
wokuba indoda itshate no msakwabo mfazi xa afileyo umfazi. Lo mteto
uxaswa kakulu ngo nyana be Nkosazana, ucaswe kwa ngoku njalo zi Bishop.
Kute kwaku votwa waliwa umteto onjalo nga madoda a 132 angawu vumiyo, na
128 awu vumayo.

IZIKOLO NE NGQAKAQA.—U Dr. Langham Dale, umongameli micimbi ye mfundo,
ukupe isaziso kuzo zonke ititshala napa pati bezi kolo ezamkela imali
kwa Rulumente:—(1). Ukuba umntwana ngamnye aqatshulwe ebusaneni pambi
kokuba zinyanga ezi ntandatu ubudala; (2). Ukuba kungabiko mntwana
uvunyelweyo ukuba eze esikolweni de ibe upau loku qatshulwa luya
qondakala; (3). Ukuba abantwana baqatshulwe kwakona ekugqibeni unyaka
weshumi. Ezi zimiselo kucelwa o Titshala ukuba bazazise kuba zali.

ISIFO ESOYIKEKAYO E QUEENSTOWN:—U Rev. Daniel Malgas wenjenje xa
asibalela ngesifo esilusizi ebesike safunda ngaso emapepeni. “Intwana
eludaba apa sesi sifo esibulala aba belekayo, singaziwa nanga magqira;
nditeta nje sesigalele ishumi kwaba mhlope.—Into elusizi, efake ivuso
elibi kunene kwaba sebe mbovu uku beleka.” Sifunda ukuba pakati kwabo
sebemke naso kuko la manenekazi, amakosikazi ezikulu e Queenstown—Mrs.
Wood, Mrs. Cole, Mrs. Waugh, Mrs. Parsonson, Mrs. Townsend, no Mrs.
Edwards.

E TRANSVAAL.—Ingxabano naba mnyama, emideni yase Transvaal kuvakala
ukuba ziya pela. Nge xeshana eli futshane ebeku vunyelwene ngama Bulu no
Mankorane ukuba ke kuyekwe ukuliwa, u Commandant Ferreira ube sisi
gidimi se Government yase Transvaal na Basutu, nge ndawo ezitile ekutiwa
zovelisa uxolo. Kweliuye icala lelo lizwe siva ukuba u Alareshane obe
ngxamele ukulwa no Sikukuni ubanjiwe ngu Mr. Abel Erasmus wase Lydenberg
wasiwa e Pretoria. Zizo ke ezi zimeko ekucingelwa ukuba zoke zipe i
Transvaal ixesha loku pefumla.

Unyana we Nkosazana oza pezu komkulu, no waka wa hambela kweli lizwe—u Prince Alfred, Duke of Edinburgh, u pantse wantywiliseka e bambisa intlanzi, kufupi nelizwe eliyi Spain.

Imali yezindlu.—Umhla wokuhlaula imali yezindlu wabungowo 1 July. Ukuba lomali ayi hlaulwanga ko Mantyi kwada kwangomhla wa 30 September koti kwalekwe isheleni zombini ngapezulu.

Intlanganiso yama Bulu yase Holoholo, ifumene, esi kundleni som fundisi wayo opume ngobu dala, indodana u Rev. Wm. Rousseau, owaziswe kwi ntlanganiso ngu Rev. Mr. Roos no Rev. Mr. Stegman.

U Chief Justice Kotze, obeyi Jaji enkulu yase Transvaal use Cape Town. Aku lindelekile ukuba abuye abuyele kwelo ngenx’ enokuba amkele kwi Government ye Koloni le ubu Jaji e Diamond.

U Mr. Scanlen no Mr. Merriman, bobabini ngamalungu e Government, bake batyelela e Beaufort West kunye namanene apete imicimbi ka Loliwe, besiya kuhlola uku hamba kwe sebenzi apo.

Imicimbi yase Lusutu.—La manene anyulwe yi Government ukuba acedulule imicimbi yase Lusutu—Colonel Griffith, Hon. J. A. Burger M.L.C., Mr. Cecil John Rhodes, M.L.A., no Mr. Abraham Auret, M.L.A.

Izifata mandla zama dolopu onke ziyazi zamazamisa ukucokisa imizi ngoku tshayela inkunkuma, kuba ingqakaqa ingu mza woku ngcola. Naba mnyama kuya nqweneleka ukuba bangayi nyabeli lonto, kunye ne mizimba yabo.

E Ireland.—Imicimbi kweli isemi kwanjengoko. Ukugwinta kusemi'ngokuma. U Cardinal McCabe, inkulu yebandla lase Roma, ide yabala incwadi evuma uku kohlakala kwezinto pambi kwezulu nabantu. Ama Irish akolise ngokuba ngama Roman Catholics.

Imvula.—Kuma cala angase Kapa imvula ezimnandi zike zana. Naku mandla wase Queenstown siva kwa lonto. Kulo wasema Xoseni like laruqula ngoku balela, kute kodwa ngo 22 July savuka lisenza umkungu ongabanga mandla mani nawo.

Kuvakala ukuba u Mongameli wo nyaka walo lonke ibandla lama Wesile, a Pesheya nakwa manye, odla ngoku nyulwa e Busika, woba ngalo nyaka, ngu Rev. Charles Garrett. Nje ngoko sabikayo kwe sika August odlulileyo, utabata indawo ka Dr. G. Osborn u Mr. Garrett.

I Government ya Pesheya.—U Right Honourable W. E. Gladstone upumile, ekubeni yi Chancellor of the Exchequer (Ubupatiswa Micimbi ye Rafu), endaweni yake kungene u Right Honourable G. J. Goschen. Ukungena kuka Mr. Goschen kwi Government, kuya kuyomeleza.

Ukuya kuka Cetywayo e England.—Eli Tshawe linduluke ukuya e England ngo July 12. Pambi koku nduluka kwalo laqatshulwa kunye naba landeli balo. Lixesha lesibini eqatshulwa u Cetywayo; mhla waqala kumhla waqatshulwa no yise u Mpande nge ngqakaqa apa enkulu.

Isibeno sika Bishop Merriman.—Isibeno sika Dr. Merriman kwi Privy Council (kuma tyala amakulu apesheya), malunga nesi gwebo se Jaji zontatu zama tyala ase Kapa, esasi koliseke ku Dean Williams, sipelele ekuvumeleni kwa u Dean. Esi simo sibange ukungo nwabi pakati kwe bandla lase Tshatshi.

E Natal.—U Mr. Saunders, kwi Parlamente yase Natal, uke wati makuvunyelwane ngendawo yokuba, de abantu aba pitizelisa ulaulo lwakwa Zulu, abase Natal bohlwaywe ngumteto, alusoze lubeko uxolo kwelo. Inteto leyo yaliwe sininzi samadoda  yaba ke iyawa. Siqonda ukuba ibe isingiselwa ku Bishop Colenso—ingxamele uku giba yena.

U Sir Bartle Frere.—Ngo 8 ka July, eli tshawe liteta entlanganisweni etile Pesheya, litete ngoku shushu licasa isenzo se Government ya Pesheya kwi nto efuna ukuyenza malungana noku kulula u Cetywayo. Libonise ukuba u Cetywayo wayo nile ukuze libange imfazwe kwa Zulu, lati mhlana abuyelayo kobuya kupalale igazi kwelo.

Kuko ingxakeko enkulu kwi Government yoku fumana inene lokutabata indawo yobu Judge President (ubuntloko be Jaji) base Diamond Fields. Indawo leyo isand’ukwaliwa ngu Mr. Advocate Cole, wase Cape Town. Siva ukuba u Mr. Recorder Buchanan, oyi Jaji yakona uyinyekile, ekoti ukuba akanyulelwa kuyo, asele puma kanye ebu Jajini. Into leyo yobanga enye inkatazo.

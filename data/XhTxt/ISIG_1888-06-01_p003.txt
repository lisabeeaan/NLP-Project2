UMZI wesi Kolo sase Dikeni wahlanganisana ngamini itile umenywa ukuba
unyule isibonda endaweni yomfi u Mr. Govan Koboka. Siva ukuba kunyulwe
kwa umkwe wake u Moli Nqohro, ominyaka ingapezu kwamashumi ’mabini
ebupoliseni, engoku ubuyele ekaya ukuza kupumla sel’evela e-Mtata.
Ngumfo ofanelekileyo. Nakuba engakolwa, uya- zazi indlela zase lizwini
akulele kuzo, sitemba ukuba umzi wowupata ngesiko lase sikolweni. Icebo
esimnika lona lelo- kuba angake azikutaze izinto ezinje nge ntlanganiso
ze- ndywala, nemitayi, nentlanganiso zolutsha ezidulusele kwi- nto
ezinje nge ntonjane. Yoniwa zezonto imizi le.

KULONTLANGANISO yomzi wase-Dikeni siva ukuba ama- dodana angenise
ingxoxo yokunga ingako imvume yokusisa emitshatweni, xa lomitshato
ingenazipitipiti. Amadoda amakulu abebeta kwe tu ukwala, esiti elosiko
liyasonakalisa isimilo solutsha, ngoko into enjalo mayibe lisiko lomzi
elimi- leyo lokuba ingavunywa. Umtshato kufuneka ucitwe kwase mini,
abanini-mzi bamangalelwe ekusisiweyo emizini yabo. Nxatsho ke.

OXONYWE KABINI.—Umbaleli we Watchman ose Win- burg ubalisa ngesenzo
esibi kunene esenzeke apo ekux'o nyweni kwababanjwa abane babe Sutu
abebegwetyelwe ukufa ngetyala lokubulala. Uti kubonakele ukuba umxbmi
unobungxamo bokunga angade awufeze lomsebenzi mbi kunene, ate
ngobutyututyutu abenzileyo ekulifezeni ishi- shini elo lake wati kanti
ugqwakazile kowokugqibela umbanjwa. Ute eba ufile wayinqumla intsontelo,
waka- hleleka pantsi umntu. Zite ezo zidumbu zozine xa kulungi- selelwa
ukuba ziposwe kumngxuma obe usel’ umbiwe kade, suka timbilili lendoda
yokuggibela. Ekoko ke mntu ndini ukusingwa kwasemgangatweni wokuxbma,
bati ababeko babonela umbono ombi kunene ongaqelekileyo—babeta ba-
gqitisa ububi bawo akubambelela emilenzeni yomntu oxb- nyiweyo umxbmi,
efuna ngokwenjenjalo ukuqondisisa ukuba umpefumlo upumile mpela.

AMABE EMFAZWE.— Ezivela pesheya kolwandle zibika ukuba ilizwe limaxongo
kubi. Amakomkulu akona aku- ngebi ’mangaliso ukuva ukuba aqubisene

U-LOLIWE OMTSHA.— Ngosuku lwe 12 ku May bekuko intlanganiso kwidolopana
yase Dikeni (Alice) yokucedulu- la umcimbi wokwenza u-Loliwe

ICAM DOKWENDA.—Kuvakala ukuba ute u-Rulumente wase France elangazelela
ukutumela igqiza elitile ukuba liye kuba ngabemi besiqiti esitile,
wakupa isaziso sokuba kufu- nwa intombi ezilikulu ukuba ziye kwendiselwa
kwabo bemi belozwe litsha. Ute ukuyenza kapukapu lendawo watembi- sa
umvuzo otile kwabate bayamkela londlela nokutunyelwa kwelozwe ngapandle
kokubutala. Elo nani libe linqwene- leka lifumaneke kalula. Ukwenda
kunqabile !

INDLOVU EZIFUYIWEYO.—Ngase Tinara kuvakala ukuba kuseko imihlambi yezi
zilo ekungavunyelwe bani ukuba aye- nzakalise. Bati abagcini bayo
ngamhla utile batanda ukuke baye kuyibona emadlelweni ayo. Bate
besayibuka botuswa kukukonya kwazo, ezatsho langa lonke ihlati
liyashukuma. Lontlokoma yabankwantyisa abo banumza- na.

UKUSTAMPISHA—Umbaleli otile welinye lamapepa ase Dayimani unika icebo
lokuba singabekwa elulwimini isi- tampu xa siza kuncauyatiselwa
esigqubutelweni sencwadi. Ulwimi luya yikota intlaka size eso sitampu
sibe si- cenge sokuwa. Liti elonene into efanele ukwenziwa, kungekunye
kwani, kukubeka incam yolwimi esigqubute- lweni size isitampu sibekwe
kulondawo ifumileyo, sonama- tela ke unyaka nonyaka.

UKUMKANI wama Jelimani, Emperor Fredrick III, one- sifo somqala
esimkatazayo, kuvakala ukuba uyacaca. Lomqala wake kwade kwafuneka
ugqojozwe pantsi kwe nqula apa, kwafakwa intsinjana eholoholo
amakapefumle ngayo. Akanakuteta ngapandle kokuke awuvingce um- nxunyana
lowo ngomnwe eloxeshana atetayo. Ngelinye ixesha uti kodwa uzale
bububovu, izeke kufuneke ihlaziyi- we intsinjana leyo.

IZIGANEKO ZOMOYA.—Emveni kokuba sikove ukubabikela abafundi betu
ngeziganeko zomoya kulo mandla siwumeleyo, sifikelwo zindaba ezibika
ubukulu bezonakalo ozibangileyo kwindawo ngendawo. Pakati kwezinye ucite
izinki yendlu yetyalike yase Mgqwakwebe, kwa Hleke, nakuba ingacita-
kalanga yonke; ikwa yilonto kwindlu yomfundisi wase Mkubiso.
Abasibaliseleyo bati bazibeka bucala izindlu ezinqukuva, isonakalo
esenziwe ngumoya kuzo siyoyikeka ; inxalenye yazo uyibushulisile umoya,
wazishiya izinti zopa- hla zingenaso nesibi sengca ngapezulu sokukusele
abantu bazo ezimvuleni nasemaqweni. Uvelwano lwetu lukulu nezihlobo zetu
ezintsundu ezixwaleke ngolo luhlobo; noko ke lomoya uzishumayeze
intshumayelo ekade ziyiva ipuma kwimi lomo emininzi, yokuba kwahlukwane
nala- manqugwala, kwakiwe ingxande eziqinileyo.

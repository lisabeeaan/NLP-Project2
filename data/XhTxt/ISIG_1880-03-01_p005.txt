ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1880.

Ke kaloku into ezalwa yilenteto ezintliziyweni zabo bonke abahlobo
babantsundu yile.

1.  Nakuba iyimvelo kanjalo kufanelekile ukuba kubeko ibongo elinjalo,
    ixesha lalonto alikafiki—kodwa liyeza.

2.  Imfundo yabantsundu isenehambo ende pambi kwayo andul’ ukuba
    amadodana antsundu kutiwe ayifanele lonto.

3.  Amadodana antsundu alangazelela iwonga elilelo aka- kabi kona
    nasendleleni esa kulo. Ehlabatini apa akuko lizwe ndilaziyo ekuti
    kulomntu ngenxa yemfundo le yodwa sekusitiwa ufanele ukungena
    e-Parlamente. Ongxamele lonto kufuneka ete amaxesha onke wavelisa
    ibango elilimbi eligqite imfundo. Kufuneka ebonise umsebenzi otile
    awe- nzileyo mhlaumbi awulingileyo. Kufuneka ete wabonisa into etile
    elilungelo loluntu ate wayitabata wazinikela kuyo abonise ukuba
    uyavuma ukuba lento apulukwa yiyo ngenxa yelo lungelo loluntu.
    Ndiyabayala bonke abantsundu abafikelelwa ngamazwi am ngoku nokuba
    kungelinye ixesha ukuba bangazikohlisi kulendawo. Asinto yaka yaviwa
    pakati kwabamhlope, napakati kwabamnyama ayisoze yenzeke, lento
    yokuti imfundo le yodwa imenze umntu afanelwe yindawo kweyona
    ntlanganiso ipakamileyo yokudala imiteto kweli lizwe.

Nditeta ndigqite nakule ndawo nditi;—nokuba uluntu olu Iona bese
luvutiwe malunga nalento akuko madoda ahleli seleyilungele. Kanjalo abo
bafuna iwonga elinjalo kufuneka bete kuqala benza into eninzi yomsebenzi
onzima nolukulu pambi kokuba sibe nako ukuti into enjalo ingafaneleka.
Elokugqiba, nditi yinto elusizi kum ukuba nditi, kangangoko
ndinokuqonda, lomsebenzi ubuya kufika uba- lungiselele iwonga elinjalo
aba Ntsundu, awenziwa.

UKUDIBANA KWE NTOMBI NODODANA.

KWENYE inyanga, ndandenze amazwana ngoku fundiswa kwentombi, ndifuna
ukubonisa ngoku kodwa ukuba baninzi abangeka kuqabukeli. Nanamhla ndisa
nxamele ukwayama londawo, ndikangelise kule nyaniso yokuba, xa intombi
zingafundiswayo, kubulawa naba bafana abenzelwa imigudu kangaka
ngabazali.

Kuko intambo abaya kuyi bona aba nokuqonda, ebope amadoda nama nkazana,
ekuti ngenxa yayo, kuhlale kunga vumeleki ukuba kubeko mgama ungakanani
pakati kwala maqela mabini. Kutiwa kambe kuya nyukwa luhlanga. Kule
nduli inyukwayo, nditi, maze niqonde, ukuba xa anyuswa odwa amadodana,
zibe intombi zishiywa ezantsi, kuya kufunyanwa ukuba amadodana atsalwe
zizo, ada abuya eza kwalapo zikoyo ; ize ngoko ibe icitakele yonke
imizamo, ebe yenziwe yokunyusa abafana. Enye yembongi zase Mangesini ike
yavakala imemelela isiti:—

  Indoda ne nkazana

  Babambene ngezandla, Benyuka, beble kunye Kokuhle kwanokubi: Kuse
  bukobokeni Nase nkululekweni, Kuse kukanyeni Nase bunyameni Bahleli
  bedibene.

  Xenikweni inkazana

  Uyi sbiya ngase mva, Uyishiya ebubini Bentlalo nesimilo, Oyindoda
  wotinina Ukunyuka eyedwa?

Lembongi, manene akowetu, isishumayeza inene. Impe- mbelelo yenkazana,
noko ingati ayibonakali, asikuko nokuba inkulu, inamandla. Masikumbule
ukuba umfundisi wokuqala, ofundisa umntwana, yinkazana; yiyo eqala
imxelele okuhle nokubi. Kananjalo, nokuba wena yise uyamfundisa unyana
wako, umnyuse, liyeza ixesha eniya kwahlukana naye, aze aye kuba
sesandleni kwase nkazana—esomfazi

wake. Kuya kuba njanina ke, xa omnye apezulu, omnye ese zantsi?

Umbali wala mazwi, ude ati, ingabi esi sikalo singaka sokuba imfundo
iyaputa kumadodana antsundu, sibangelwa nakukuba intombi zishiywa ngase
mva, kuba apo kunjalo, aya kubuya aguquke namadodana, aye apo zikoyo
intombi. Makowetu, masingahluli izinto ezingahlulekiyo. Oko bendise
semfundweni, sibe silazi icala elintombi zisileyo, ngokukangela
kwisimilo sabafana abavela kwelo cala abeze kufunda. Ebeti ukuba
amadodana lawo azibekile, andilekile, silindele ukuba siya kufika
zinjalo nentombi zalo macala, ibe lontliziyo isenziwa kukumbulela ukuba
nkulu kwempembelelo ye nkazana endodeni, nokuba pakati kwaba bantu
bobabini, kuko umnxeba oba bopileyo, ukuze uhlale umfutshane umgama
wokushiyana kwabo.

Ukutsho ke, lusapo Iwakowetu, nditi akuncedi nto ukuba siqabukele
ukufundisa amadodana odwa, sikucukuceze okwe ntombi; nakuba tina siya
hlula, bobuya baye kuhlangana, abe oyena uya kubuya msinya, ingulo sibe
sisiti tina siyamnyusa. Nditi, ofuna ukupakamisa udodana, makenyuse
nentombi.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

Siyazisa ukuba asilitabateli pezu kwetu ityala lenteto zaba balelani
betu, asitsho nokuti siyavumelana nazo zonke incwadi zabo
esizishicilelayo.

MR. MHLELI.—

Kum kuluvuyo olukulu ukubona ukuba uhlanga Iwakowetu olu Ntsundu
luhambisela pambili, emasikweni okukanya nawe ncubeko. Enye yezinto
ezibonisa ukuhambisela pambili kohlanga, zingxoxo zokupatwa kwelizwe,
endizibona futi epepeni lako. Ndingavuya ezonto zixoxwayo zite zavelisa
okulungileyo, zaluvusa, zalubonisa uhlanga izinto ebezifanelwe
ukwenziwa, ukuze lubenako ukuhambisela pambili.

Into exelwa ngu Mr. Jabavu kwincwadi yake yokugqibela, andiyiqondi
kakuhle. Kokwam ukuva ngati uti, ixesha lifikile lokuba amadodana
aNtsundu abe zijaji, ngama gqweta, ngamalungu e-Parliament, nemantyi.
Kokwam ukuqonda, lomalungelo asiwalelwe mntu, sinako ukuwafikelela ukuba
sinazo ingqondo, nobulumko bokuba zizo ijaji, na magqweta. Kufuneka
singa ngxamanga, satabata ibanga pezu kwebanga. Eyam inkolo yeyokuba
ingqondo, nobulumko bokuba zezo zinto asikabinabo, ngoko ke ixesha
alikafiki, liyakufika kupela xa sinalo ngqondo. Asinakuba zijaji,
ngapambili kokuba sibe ngamagqweta ejaji (advocates), pambi kokuba sibe
zi advocates kufuneka sipumelele i- Examinations ezinqabileyo. Ke tina
luhlanga lu Ntsundu, ukuba andiposisi, akuko namnye kuti, xa ndishiya
u-Mr. Soga ongasekoyo, owake wapumelela nanye i-Examination ngapandle
kweye Teacher, ne Uni­versity School Examination. Ke xa sisapumelele
i-Examination ezipantsi ngoluhlobo, sicinga ukuba sesinokufumana indawo
ezipakame kangaka na? Ewe maninzi amadoda alumkileyo, noko angazanga
apumelele kwezi Examination ndizixelileyo. Indoda enobulumko ne- ngqondo
yayo, mayiyibonise ngemisebenzi yayo. Ipina ke kuti tina siti
sinokukanya, imisebenzi yetu esitetelayo ukuba ozindawo sizifanele?
E-Parlamente akutunyelwa madoda afunde incwadi kupela, kodwa amadoda
anengqondo akwaziyo ukuxoxa. akwaziyo ukunika, nokutyila amaq'inga
alungileyo.

Abateteleli benkolo yokuba ixesha lifikile, bati, “ E-India abemi
belozwe banazo ezondawo zipakamileyo.” Kwabo bati ixesha lifikile nditi,
Ama India anamagqweta, namanye amadoda afunde kakulu apumeleleyo kwi
Examination ezipunyelelwa ngabamhlope, pambi kokuba babe nelungelo
yokuba ngamagqweta. Abantu base India bane ncubeko enkulu, engenako
ukude ilinganiswe neyetu. I-India igqitile kwiminyaka eli 120, ipantsi
kolaulo Iwama Ngesi! Mingapi eyetu iminyaka sipantsi kolo laulo ?
Asinako ukuzilinganisa nama India. Bendingaxela indawo ezininzi
zokubonisa ukuba ixesha letu alikafiki; kodwa ndiyatemba liyasondela.

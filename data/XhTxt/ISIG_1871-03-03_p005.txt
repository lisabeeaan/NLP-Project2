LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, MARCH 3, 1871.

  Isigidimi Samaxosa

  Ngolwa 24 ku November, inqaba yase
  Thionville yadutyulwa, waza lomzi wa-
  tshiswa ngembumbulu kwindawo ngenda-
  wo; kwati ngolwa 25 wazinikela, kwati-

  njwa amadoda amawaka mane (4,000)
  nekanunu ezimakulu mabini (200).

  Ngolwa 26, ama German abuya agxo-
  twa ngama French kumzi wase Amiens.
  Kodwa ngolwa 27 ama Prussia abuya
  atyalela pambili, awutimba kwakona umzi
  wase Amiens, umzi onendyebo, unama-
  waka amashumi matandatu abantu (60,000)

Ngecawa nangomvulo ngolwa 27 nolwa

28. ku November, kwakuko ukulwa oku-
      kulu ngasentla kwe Orleans nangakwezo
      nqaba zingase Paris.

Ngokuhlwa kolwa 27 nakusasa ngolwa

29. ku November, umkosi wama French
      ose Paris walinga kunene ukugqoboza
      kwizintlu zemikosi yama Prussia, waza
      kodwa wabuya watyalelwa kwasemva
      kwabulawa into eninzi yawo.

  Ngolwe 4 ku December, wabuya wati-
  njwa kwakona umzi wase Orleans ngama
  Prussia. Okukutinjwa kwabe sekukokwe-
  sitatu ngenyanga ezimbini. Umpati-mkosi
  wama French, enamadoda amawaka anga-
  makulu mabini (200,000), wahlehlela emva
  etyalwa ngumkosi wama Prussia. Kwaza
  kwalandela intsuku ezine ekwaliwa ngazo
  zone, aza ama French aya etyalelwa emva
  nakuba alwa ngoburoti obukulu. Kwi-
  ndawo ngendawo zase France bekumana
  kubako amadatyana pakati kwemikosana
  emincinane. Kodwa umkosi wase Loire
  owawuza kunceda lowo use Paris awuba-

  nga nako ukwenjenjalo.

  Ukudutyulwa kwe Paris kwaqala ngo-
  lwa 28 ku December, enye inqaba kwezi-
  ngapandle ekutiwa yi Mount Avron yata-
  tyatwa; nenqaba ekutiwa zi Bosneay ne
  Nogent zada zecwaka, oko kukuti ama
  French akabisaba nako ukudubula aku-
  dubula ama Prussia. Ngolwe 4 ku Janu-
  ary, yayisaqekezwa ngenkanunu I-Paris
  leyo.

IMFAZWE YASE EUROPE.

NO. III.

UMZI we Paris usaraulwe, ufikelwe nayi-
ngqele eselide yayinto emhlope pantsi,
kungateteki kona ukubanda. Abemi bayo
basawatyala ngokungamandla ama Prus-
sia, ekubonakala ukuba into engade iboyise
yindlala yodwa. Ukuba bukali kwengqele
bekungangokude elinye isentile lome yiyo
life kupele kwakulondawo belilinda kuyo,
nama soldati ama French aselede atunye-
lwa ehospitaleni ngenxa yengqele leyo,
aliwaka elinamakulu mabini (1200), abe
awama Prussia emawaka matandatu
(6000). Imikosi yama French nama

Prussia ilwa yonke lemihla. Ububi obuse
France abuteteki. Into eninzi yabantu
ebike izityebi ngapambili, inxenye inem-
fuyo elingeneyo, ngoku selingamanqiba.

Soyiqalelake lembali yemfazwe kwalapo
sayishiya kona kwesi Sigidimi sidluleyo.
Bokumbula abaleseshi betu ukuba I-Metz
yazinikela kuma Prussia ngolwa 26 ku
October, nokuba umkosi omkulu omadoda
angamawaka alikulu elinamashumi osixe-
nxe anesitatu (173,000) watinjwa.

Ngolwa 29 ku October, ama German
awufikela umzi ekutiwa yi Dijon osand’
ukwakiwa. Awuxatisela amadoda awo
lada lalixesha lesine, awati ngalo ama
German awudubula ngezinkulu ikanunu;
wazake wazinikela kwakuba njalo.

Ngolwe 6 ku November, alwa ama
French abuya awuhluta kuma Prussia
umzi we Chateaudun owautatyatwe ngolwe
18 ku October.

Ngolwe 9 nolwe 10 ku November, kwa-
iiwa elinye lamadabi amakulu, elahlala

intsuku zombini, waza umzi we Orleans
wabuya wahlutwa kuma Prussia. Kwelo
dabi ama German alahlekelwa ngamawaka
amatatu amadoda kudibene abuleweyo
nangxweleriweyo.

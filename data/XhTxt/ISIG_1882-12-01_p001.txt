Afrikaan ne Volksblad, amapepa esi Bulu ate avula lengoxo. Iqinga ate
alihambisa, loku dwelisa pambi komnyama, i Ngesi ne Bulu, ati emva
kokwalata izipako nembasa ezintle, kubo bobabini ababantu, acela omnyama
ukuba azityumbele oyena ayakumlandela— eli qinga, sitsho tina, lelingena
kugxekwa. Lisagqitile nakoko—liteta izinto ezinkulu ngentlalo yontsundu,
xa kanti ziyakuti ezintlanga zimbini zilaulayo, zizame ukukupana
kwindawo yokuba nguwupina oyakumpata kakuhle omnyama.

I Zuid Afrikaan icela isi Bulu ukuba kesibaneke lenyaniso imdaka,
yokokuba tina babala lintsundu, sityekele ngase Ngesini kuna ngase
Bulwini. Kambe lengxoxo ayiqali kuviwa siti. Kanti kwaba mhlope i Zuid
Afrikaan valame amaqanda eskwenene, esaziyo ukuba abengaziwa, mhlaumbi
ebete anyatyelwa. Esoti ke ngoko sizanelise ngoku posa amazinywana, apa
napaya, kwindawo ezite ezi koranti kwakunye ne yama Ngesi i Cape Times,
engene emdudweni ukuba ikusele elo hlelo. Iti uku tshayelela kwayo i
Zuid Afrikaan,—“Abamnyama ngamaxesha onyulo, baba ngakuma Ngesi, kuba
bekolwa, okanye benziwe “ bakulwa, ukuba i Bulu lijonge ekubenzeni
amakoboka.” Ibehle ingadendi i Volksblad ukuyikanyeza lenteto, kumhlaba
wokuba omnyama akasi ositungula kangangokuba

acinge njalo. Sisesimeni sokuti tina asiloyiko lobukoboka olubete
omnyama waqipukelana ne Bulu. Kodwa lu’rano lokuba, ngapaya kwalamafu

Kanti ke ukuzamana nesijwili nokulilela okudlulileyo, akunasiqamo sani,

longxako isaba leleyo okwa kaloku. Ukuba boti ukususela kolwa namhla,
endaweni yokumhluta ilungelo yokuvota—into entsha ke leyo kumazwe apetwe
ngolu hlobo sipetwe ngalo—bacaze amalungelo okuvota; ukuba endaweni yoku

ukuba beboti endaweni yokumnisela omnyama ngengxokolo yenteto
edlubukileyo yokumtuka, beboti bamyeke ahambe indlela yake—ukuba,
ngendlelana emfutshane, bezoti intlanga ezi zilaulayo, njengoko i
Volksblad itshoyo, zizamele “ ukunyusa omnyama, entlalweni nase
ngqondweni,” belinga sakuba leli lizwe lodwa ebeliya kuvuna izixakato,
kodwa nokuba li Ngesi, Bulu, kusinina, ebeloti lizamele ukuxolelanisa,
limlaule nge sonti, beliya kumfumana etyekele ngakulo.

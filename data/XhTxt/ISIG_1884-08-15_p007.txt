ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA AUGUST 15, 1884

UKWABIWA KWE MANTYI.—Sifunda ukuba zenjiwenje ukwabiwa i Mantyi:—Imantyi
emiselwe e Swellendam ngu F. J. Hodges ; e Peddie, W. B. Piers; e
Murraysburg, W. B. G. Blenkins; e Aliwal North, J. D. Hugo ; e BoBofolo,
O. W. Andrews; e Albert, A. Stewart; e Dikeni, H. Pugh ; e Fraserburg,
P. B. Borcherds ; e Stockenstroom, A. H. Gracia ; ehlonyelwe e
Kimberley, G. B. K. Brad­shaw.

IBEDI ZENTSIMBI.—Kwa elase Nyara lisinika isiyalo sokuba wonke ubani
otenga ibedi yentsimbi, ake kuqala ayikangele ukuba iqatywe kakuhle na.
Lifika ke lenze im’balana yomntwana obetwe ngum’bane ebelele, ekute
kwakubizwa igqira lahlaba ukuba um’bane utsalwe kubengezela kwentsimbi
ekubonakala ukuba ibe ingaqatywe ngokwaneleyo. Ekukukuti ke lumkelani
ibedi ezingaqa- tywanga kakuhle.

INGOZI ZE MIPU.—Elase Tinara libika ingozi ezisand’ ukuhla ngenxa yemipu
kwelo. Enye inkwenkwe yati iwupete wadubula watsho wayidlavula isandla,
ngangokude kufuneke lo wokwalata umnwe ususiwe. Enye ibe yingozi
yenkwenkwana ye Lawu ate uyise ebepete um’pu wasuka wadubula watsho
kunyana lowo yangena malunga egxalabeni yanxula yapants’ ukukwela
entliziyweni. Ingxwelera ezo ngati ziquba kakuhle.

EZE BOLA.—Namhla izinto zimi ngolunye uhlobo. Kumdlalo webola obupakati
kwama Ngesi nama Australia, ngeli ixesha ama Auetralia akutshiwe nge 46
runs. Kuma Australia amadoda awenze amanani apakamileyo abe mabini, enye
yenze i 69 enye i 60. Kuma Ngesi ibe ligcisa lawo u Dr. Grace,
ngokungena kokuqala owenza i 107 wabanjwa. Akufuti ama Australia ke ati
adliwe zezinye indawo, njengoko bokumbula abafundi betu.

E KOKSTAD, siva ukuba ama Mpondo asand’ ukwenza isidlokazi esikulu,
esibe malungana nokutomba kwenye yentombi zika Mqikela; inkomo ekutiwa
zaxelwa lomhla zaba kumashumi omatandatu. Kuvakala ukuba naye u Mqikela
ngokwake uke wahambela umfundisi u Mr. Tonkin e Goso, apo alike wenza
amadinga okuba uyakuzamela ukuwuxasa naye ngokwake umsebenzi
wokufundisa, wati naye kananjalo uza kutumela abatatu kubantwana bake,
watembisa kananjalo ukuba uyakwenza isipo esizinkomo ezimashumi mabini.

EZAMA POLISA.—Amapolisa ahlala e Qonce siwafumana ekalazelwa kanye, iti
i Telegraph, yenjenje enye indoda ukubala ngawo:—“ Lamadoda akawufanele
kanye umsebenzi emzimbeni kunye nase moyeni anawo. Elinye kuwo yinto
eyahlala isifa ukanti ke noko ihanjelwa zisheleni zontlanu ngemini.
Elinye ikopulali yinto engakwazi nokufunda nokub'ala oku, into kanye
engawufaneleyo umsebenzi esand’ ukuba ntsuku ntatu ingeko isiti Iyafa. U
Mongameli yena yinto ongayibona emini kupela ikwele pakati komzi apa,
ukukwela endingetshoyo nokuti kukwela xa umntu afumana emana ekwele
nje.”

OTSHE NGUMLILO.—Ngase Alexandria kuvakala ukuba kusand’ ukuhla ingozi
embi kunene. Kute ngamhla utile intombazana abati ngu Miss Nightingale
ebikangela usapo ndlwini itile yashiywa yodwa nendlu, kuko nomntwanana
owayelalisiwe, yati imi kufupi neziko ilangatyi labambelela ezingutyeni
zayo. Ikubonile oko ibalekele egumbini lokulala ukufuna ingubo
yokuzitandela ukuze iwoyise umlilo, ifikile ayiyifumananga ngapandle
kwengubo awayelalisiwe ngayo umntwana lowo, ngokungatandi kumpazamisa
ipume yafumana yabaleka pandle, abate abaputumileyo bafika seyilele
pantsi igqibele ukutsha.

ISAZISO KUBO BONKE.

ZINKOSI NANI NONKE Mzi WAKOWETU.—Njengokuba lonke ilizwe lenza
isimbonono sokunga kungabako ubuninzi bamapepa endaba ngokwenteto yetu
tina bantsundu. Ndivumeleni okokuba nditi kungumnqweno wam okokuba
kubeko elinjalo apa kwa Daliwe (Cathcart). Liya kushicilelwa kwa Mr. C.
W. Deecker, i Proprieter ye “Farmers'’

Chronicle.” Ndiyawucela wonke umzi ontsundu okokuba uke undihlangabeze
eluveni Iwam, uti ukuba unako uke utumele amagama abo bafuna ukuba
ngabamkeli balo, ukuze afakwe emqulwini wencwadi yabamkeli balo.

Intlaulela yoba yi 18s ngonyaka, kwabalitunyelwa ngeposi; ne 15s
ngonyaka, kwabalifumana ngapandle kwe posi; kofuneka imali itunyeIwe
kwasekuqaleni. Imbanga yoku kunyuka kangaka kwemali kukuba lopuma kanye
ngeveki, kane ngenyanga, yaye i Press idulu ngokwayo ; ke ngobuninzi
babalamkelayo yoba kukuhla, nokuncipa kwento erunyelwa ipepa eligama
lingu “ Mfontsundu.”

“ Umfontsundu” akasayi kubako ngapandle kwe 300 ne 400 yabalitabatayo
ukuze lib'atale indleko zokushicilelwa. Kambe mziwakowetu yazini ukuba
lento ingamapepa ixaswa, yondliwa Zizaziso, yiyo into eyenza ipepa lime.
Nalo ke itole lika “Gidimi” azi umzi ontsundu uyakulitakazela uze
ulondle na? Kokwenu ukulityumza iqanda xa u “Gidimi” asalifukamileyo,
ikwa nini eninokulipa ubushushu lide liqanduselwe. Ngemigudu eniya
kuyenza xa elipepa lilitole lide libe yintaka epapazela iye kufika
ezincamini kwimbombo zone zehlabati, onyusa uhlanga ngokutsho kwake
sombona ngokusivelela kwa tanci. Ndingowenu futi osisicaka
esitobekileye,

DAVID MAGNA THOMAS.

ISAZISO.

  KUCAZWA kwamahashe afunyenwe edungudela kwi Sitili sase Tsomo,
  ayakutengiswa kumsito wempahla

wenyanga ezintatu Entlambe ukuba akakululwanga kwangapambili—

Inkunzana em’fusa yehashe eminyaka mitatu. Ayika qalwa.

Inkunzana ebomvu yehashe eminyaka mitatu. Ayika qalwa.

Intsundu yenkunzana yehashe. Inembasana encinane ebunzi. Inqma lasemva
limhlope. Amanqina angapambili amhlope ngezantsi kwezihlahla. Ngati
liminyaka mitatu. Alika qalwa.

Imazi em’fusa enomcokozo, ngati iminyaka mitandatu ubudala, amanqina
asemva amhlope pantsi kwezihlahla. Linempau zokufakwa intsimbi kumanqina
angapambili, linesilonda emhlana. Libityile.

Inkonyana enembasa. Amanqina angasemva amhlope. Ngati iminyaka mibini.

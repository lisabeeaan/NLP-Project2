NGELOBUSELA.

Umadlonya ngokuba amashumi amabi­ni egusha zika Andrew Develing, ifama.
Ulivumile ityala wauikwa iminyaka emi­tatu.

UKUBETA.

U Joni, wase Rodani e Glen Grey ngo- kubeta Umntuyedwa ngenduku noku-
mgwaza ngesikali. Bahve kwa ntonjane. Ubunqhina buka Sigangalala
umntakwa- bo Mntuyedwa bobokuba utike wamqu- bula pakati kobusuku
ngapandle kwesi- zatu. Imibuzo ye Jaji ivelise uteto yimbi. Umbanjwa
ubize inqhina awayete maze lisanyanishwe yimantyi yase Lady Frere,
kwafumaneka lingaziswanga, ngoko li- ngeko, nemantyi leyo ingeko. I Jaji
ite u Rulumeni seleke waziswa ngento enje- ngeyokungezi kwemantyi: xa
bababa- ndezelayo abantu yimfanelo ukuba babe- ko abo basazeke bete
mawaliudele Ijaji amatyala. Umbanjwa ute umkonto umniniwo ngu
Sigangalala, yingozi ehle besilwa yena enqanda ihlahla. Ufunye­nwe
engenatyala.

I Jaji yalate kwakona kumatyala anga- lungiselelwanga zimantyi
zangapandle anjengawase Tarka, Glen Grey, nelinye lase Cathcart, yati
ayisakuwateta namhla. Kugqitywe kwelokuba kuhanjiswe awase Queenstown.

UBUDLWENGU.

U Petros ngobudlwengu kwi Laukazi elingu Margaret Erasmus. Ubunqhina
balentokazi bobokuba akazange wambona lomio ngapambi kwesi sigigaba,
kanti oku kwenzeka wambiza ngegama. Unina unqhine mayelana nesikalo
kwintombi yake awaya wafumana kunjeke. Igqira linike ubunqhina.
Ufunyenwe engena­tyala.

UKUTWALA.

U Gani, ngokutwala u Constance Bar­rett intwazana eminyaka i 13.
Intomba- zana ite icelwe ngumbanjwa ukuba ba- gcagce, ayavuma, kodwa
umnyanzele bahamba, banduluka ngo 9 ngokuhlwa. Balala endle intsuku
ezintlanu, ubekuti makagoduke akavuma, bade bafunyanwa ngamakwenkwe
amabini antsundu am- godusa. Ubesenza ukutanda umbanjwa eloxesha
bebesendle. Uhambe nengubo zake kwane 17s. U James Barrett ute unguyise
walentwazana akazanga wam- vumela umbanjwa ukuba emke nom- ntvvana wake.
Ufunyenwe enetyala wanikwa iminyaka 3 ne kati 15.

UBUDLWENGU.

Umolos, ingqeshemba yendoda, ugoku- dlwengula u Nodopi, umfazi, ngase
Tunxe. Ubunqhina bobokuba u Nodopi nelinye ingqubakazi babesela enkanti,
wave etunywe yindoda yake ukuba aze kuyitengela. Incokolise intyewu aya-
howya bani. Kude kwahlwa belapo besela. Ukumbule eloxa Unodopi ukuba
akako yindoda yake, wonda ngendlela. Umbanjwa ulandele, nakuba belite
ingqubakazi asiyiyo indlela eya kwelake ikaya leyo. Kute kungekude
lentokazi iza ngemva yeva ngesikalo, kanti kuku­ba ibe ise iquba
ukutanda kwayo inqeberu. Ufunyenwe enetyala wanikwa iminyaka 7 ne kati
36.

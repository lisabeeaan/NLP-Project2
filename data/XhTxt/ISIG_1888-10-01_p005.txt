INTETO YETENGWA.

Isigitshimi siqale ngokuti, ngcxeahana elingapambili besi* ngazanga
sacinga ukuba bosingavula umlomo ukuteta into ehi* ngileyo ngo Mr.
Schermbrucker, kuba ” sibe simbalela ukuba ngomnye wabantu
abakoblakeleyo (entweni zombuso )e-Koloni apa.” Nambla kodwa uzikupile
kwi Mbumba yama Bulu, ngumntu esimilo sitshintahekileyo. Akusakuba
kwenziwa nto ilungileyo ukukumbula izono zendoda eguqukileyo, esinci-
piseleyo izikweliti zerafu, yaza yakulula i Nkosi. Imise inyawo kunene
kwindawo ye Nkosi, kuba ibonisa ukuba u Tshambulu- ka uguqukile,
emazingakankanywa mayela naye izigqito zatna- xesba agqitileyo. Itengwa
lihambise lati ngekunyulwa u Tshambuluka no Solomon, kodwa u Warren
akafanelwe kuxa- swa, kuba kungeko nto asenzele yona. Bambi kokuba
iblale pantsi lendoda ite kwakona ayikanyeli ukuba u*Tsbambuluka ube
yindoda ekoblakeleyo ixa eli lonke, ngoku kodwa ungomnye umntu, esingati
kutabata kolwanamhla, simbalele pakati kwa- bahlobo babantsundu. Ute abo
bati i Nkosi zikululwe kuba kubonwa ivoti Ie abananto bakele kayo;
ngumcingo nje kodwa wabo, akuko mntu unokugwetywa ngokuzindlwa okunjalo.

IMPENDULO YOMZI ONTSUNDU.

Ikovile intlanganiso ukupulapula ite -. (1) Inga intento engo- kuguquka
kuka Mr. Scbermbrucker ingayiva ngaye ngokwake, ngapambi kokuba
izimisele.

(2) Ibuze ukuba kunganina ukuba kungabonakali magama abablobo
    babantsundu kakade pakati kwabo bamcelayo, kanti nje uguqukile.

(3) Ite ke noko ukuba, njengokuba esitsho umtunywa uyalubona ulahleko
    Iwake Iwangenx’engapambili u-Tshambu- luka; abantsundu bangamamkela
    ngovuyo. Kambe izihlalo abanamandla kuzo e Palamente zitatu, zibini
    kwindlu yokuqala; sinye kweyesi Bini. Umnqweno womzi ontsundu ke
    kukuba babe nabahlobo abatatu e Falamente, abangi bangafumana
    babacise omnye. Isihlalo sika Tshambuluka ke sisese sake leminyaka
    yomibini izayo; ekoti ke ukuba yinyaniso ukuba 'Uguqukile simnyule
    kwakona lakupela eloxesha.

(4) Mayelana nendawo yokuba sisiti isigitshimi, ukuba aba- mnyulanga u
      Tshambuluka, kuya kulaula i Mbumba yama Bulu, kanye masikupe e
      Palamente u Solomon nokuba ngu Warren, yinto ke leyo eyakubanga
      ukuba sibe ngabantu abanabahlobo ababini kupela e Palamente,
      endaweni yokuba ngoku bebatatu abahlobo betu.

EZASE TSITSIKAMA.

[s. Y. MTIMKVLU.]

IMIPANGA kwityalike yase Moravia. U-SALIMI MTI- MKULU, idyakanakazi
ubube nge 11 ku'April, 1888. Waye engum-Kristukazi oway’ ewusebenza
umsebenzi we Nkosi yake u-Yesu Kristu, ubube elixesha angene emsebenzini
we Nkosi; ekwiminyaka emashumi omabini, ude wafana wabuba kungekonto
yaks yampazamisa etyalikeni, ebebiza kwabasesemva esiti “ Yizani ku Yesu
owafayo ngenxa yenu.” Akagulanga; saba sisiqupe ukub uba kwake;
akatetanga ne- nto. Sitembile malunga ne Nkosi yake ngenxa yomsebenzi
abewenza.

Kwakona omnye obubileyo SUSANNA HLELA, ubube ubudala bake bukwi minyaka
engamashumi omabini ane- sibozo, yintombazana ekulele esikuleni yafunda
yada yaku- tshwa esikuleni, yamkelwa erementeni yangumntu olilungu
elizeleyo erementeni; itembekile kuba fundisi bayo. Ubube ngebaqo ;
ub’esele umhlanziso wati okukuhlanza ahlanzayo wapuma umpefumlo.
Sitembile ukuba uye ku Kristu Yesu. Ubube nge 15 ku May 1888.

Kwakona kubube ngo 6 ku August, 1888, intokazi emke kakuhle emhlabeni
U-BABEKA intokazi ebingazange iposwe lusuku ezityalikeni zonke ngapandle
kokuba iyafa, into­kazi ebibanika ubumnandi abafundisi maxa beze kuteta
ngezwi le Nkosi, ivuyiseke kakulu lentokazi ibulele kanye. Intokazi
oyati andinalusizi ukuba ndishiya abantwana. U-Tixo wandinika amandla
ndabona bangaka konke, usizi aluko nangona ndibona ukuba ndiyemka apa
kulomhlaba wentsizi. Ikwa yintokazi elandela abapambili kule remente
yase Wittekleibocsh; ukwisbumi elinesibozo iminyaka eli- lungu
erementeni yase Moravia.

isimanga walala ngo May 1886, aka- zange abe nandawo imxalise kakulu
into abenayo ubekupa igazi ngomlomo umke seso sifo. Ibikwa lilungu
elizeleyo.

INDABA ZAPESHEYA.

Ezokugqibela ezivela kona ziti, kube kusahleli-
we kantle. Nepike ebeliko pakati kwe Great
Britain ne United States belingati liza kubehle
lilungiswe ngoxolo.

Ukumkanikazi ubehleli kwinqaba ekutiwa yi
Balmoral, E-Scotland. Ubesapilile kakuhle ; ko-
dwa noko amagqira amkangelayo ati, ngeyeke
apumle ixesha elide, kuba woti akubamba umse-
benzi, mhlaumbi kubuye kuvuke isifo esaka
sambandezela ngobukwindla bugqitileyo. Itshawe
ne Tshawekazi lase Wales bebesebebuyile kuhambo
ebebeluhambile, besapilile.

Kuleminyaka mitandatu, abantu base Paris
bancipe ngabantu aba 400,000.

E-Boston, E-America, bekuza kubako intlanga-
niso yomvumo, eyakuqala ngomhla we 19 ka June
kude kube ngowe 4 ka July. Abavumi baya-
kuba 40,000. Indlu ekuyakuhlanganwa kuyo
inganela abantu aba 100,000. Yakelwe lomsebe-
nzi.

E-Persia bekubonakala ukungati iyakuba yi-
ndyebo nonyaka, kungati landlala sasike sayibika
izakubehle ipele.

Kwintlanganiso ye London Missionary Society,
ebisand' ukubako kuvakele ukuba kubutwe iponti
ezi £5,000 ngamnyakamnye, zirolelwa ukuba
kwakiwe isikolo esikulu kwelama Bechuana soku-
fundisa abafundisi abantsundu. Igama laso kuya-
kutiwa yi “ Moffat College.”

nyaka isitoba esand’ ukulunywa yinyoka, isiya

  abantu abasixenxe ngenxa yokusela amanzi abe-

  INGOZI EMBI.—Kute E-Bayi inkwenkwana ka
  Mr. S. Meyer, ebudala buyiminyaka emine enesi-
  qingata, idlala ngentonga, yabeta ihashe layikaba
  entloko, kwako uduma olukulu, yavela nendlu
  yobucopo. Igqira lihle labizwa, kodwa akusazeki.

  E-BAYI kuko intombazana ekusekulixesha aba-
  zali beyinyanzela ukuba iyekutshata nomntu enga-
  inkataleleyo. Bade abazali bayibopa usitupa,
  bayiqamangela kunene, bati abayikuyikulula ide
  imvume lomntu. Ite Imantyi yakuyiva lonto

  yaselituma ipolisa. Asikazike ukuba ipelele pina
  londawo.

  UTE umqubi wenqwelo otile watunywa uku-
  ba akape umhambi otile ngayo. Ute akuba enga-
  buyanga ngexesha abefanele ukubuya ngalo, kwa-
  yiwa kufunwa, wafunyanwa seleludwamba endle-
  leni, intamo yapukile. Kutandatyuzelwa ukuba
  ute xa abuyayo, inqwelana leyo yawa, yamwisa
  naye.

  UKUBULALA.—Kuko umfo otile ontsundu obe-
  seleminyaka mibini ekwele Diamondi. Kekaloku

  ubeselegoduka, enemalana ayisebenzele kona ezi
  ponti £20. Ute nga Kwanontongwana wapam-
  bukela mzini utile ukuya kupumla. Ute xa
  amkayo wakatshwa ngomnye umfo, ohle wabuya
  kungekabi mzuzu mkulu, esiti akuko nto yonake-
  leyo. Selokoko akukavakali nto ngalomfo ubeveia
  kwele Diamondi; kuranelwa ukuba ubulewe ngu-
  lowo ubemkapile.

  U-REV. R. Ross wapesheya kwe Nciba upantse
  ukumka ne Gqunube. Ubete pambi kokuba
  angene, watuma umntu okwele ehasheni ukuba
  ake aweve amanzi. Ubonakele ngokungati upu-
  mele lula, waza wangena ke no Mr Ross ekwele
  enqweleni yamahashe. Kute selepumela yatatya-
  twa ngamanzi inqwelo kunye namahashe, kwehla
  konke ; wapumela ngokusuke azuzane nesebe lomti
  sekumasikizi. Amahashe on.abini afile, inqwela-
  na yona ibuye yafunyanwa.

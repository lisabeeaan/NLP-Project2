ukuba umzi ontsundu awuwaqulunqi amangcwaba abafileyo bawo, usuke
wafumana wenza amalindi akangeleka kakubi, nangalati kuba amawetu
anoluvo lobuntu ngakwabafileyo—ukuti indawo olupumle kuyo utuli Iwabo
bayicokise. Amacebo abonwe yi Bbdi, adweliswe kwicala lezaziso,
ukupelisa olutyesho, siyatemba ukuba ayakuhlangatyezwa ngomoya
otumekclelayo ngamawetu, enjalonje alula ukufezwa. Banga abalesa
lamanqaku bangawavakalisa banzi lawo macebo.

Editorial Notes.

E Bhayi ngomhla we 18 April 1887, bekuhlangenwe ngomcimbi womtandazo oya
e Palamente ne Jubhili. Isihlalo si- tatwe ngu Silwangangubo oxele
indawo ekuhlangenwe ngazo ezi: Umtandazo otunyelwe yi Komiti ese Qonce,
ecela ukuba sitelele ngokutumela amagama etu. U Rev. Ntsiko : Noko
Mr.Chairman pambi kokuba kubhalwe amagama ndingasingake sixoxwe esi
sicelo, kuko indawo endiyichasayo kuwo, le ye Taitile zobuzwe. (U Mr.
Gawler : Hear hear !)

Mr. P. Y Rwexu—Nditi mna masam- nkelwe, silungile kanye, amadoda ase-
nzileyo acanile kanye. Sifuna ubuzwe tina, asifuni bu Lawo. Uqokelwe ngu
Mr. R. Gaika, ote yena lento ifunwa ngu Mr. Ntsiko ye taitile zeziqu
uvayazi ukuba yinkatazo, kuba woyitabata om- nye kanti omnye akateni
nayo. Into eyakulunga kukuba isizwe eso ngokuma kwaso sibe ne taitile
yobuzwe baso.

Mr. Gawler—Mna Mr. Chiarman ndiyichase ngomxhelo wam wonke indawo ye
taitile zobuzwe, kuko inkatazo enkulu eya kuvela apo, kuba obo buzwe
kutetwa ukuba abantu bapatwe zibonda eziya kuzisikela enqateni, ati
umntu ezimtiyi- leyo zingampi ndawo yokulima. Kanye makabe nelake ibala
eline taitile yalo.

Chairman—Noko lomcimbi bendinga- wuvulele ngxoxo, kuba into zonke zetu

abasicelanga ukuba masisixoxe esi sicelo, indawo yetu ke kukusamkela
nokungasamkeli.

Rev. E, Magaba—I Komiti yase Qonce iwupicotile lomcimbi we taitile
kanye. Uluvo Iwayo lukwa lunge nolu lo Messrs Ntsiko no Gawler, kuba

endlwini.

AMAXOSA NA MAQEYA.

Lo Tournament ibilapa elwandle izo kuvelisa ontamnani okade umzi
ubaranela, abahlwayeli bebhala ngokuti “ kunganina ukuba Amaqoya ashiywe
kule Tournament kanti nawo zi natives.”

Ndiyawucela umzi ontsundu ongapandle kwe Bhayi ukuba undilandele
ngenyameko kulenteto yam, iyakusingiselela ekutini: tina zi cricketers
zalapa ezintsundu asiyingeni imfeketo yokuba Amaqeya abe ngama Xosa
mhlana ama Xosa enza into ebalulekileyo. Ngokungapezulu ndiyawucela umzi
wakowetu, ukuba lento uyinikele ingqalelo enkulu ; kuba lombuzo ube
sisibi kulo Tourna­ment, ube unjengempukane ekutyeni, kananjalo uzale
ubuxoki obuninzi.

1.  Ewe, siyayivuma indawo yokuba Amaqeya zi native, kwanjengati; kodwa
    ebusweni balenyaniso yendalo (imvelo) ati wona Amaqeya akazo
    natives, koko zi coloured people, ongati ute native Eqeyeni, ube
    ulituko ngobu-Xosa, uve selisiti: andinguye Kafile mna.

2.  Asidibani nama Qeya ezimbutweni, kuba wona azicingela ukuba abhetele
    kuno Mxosa; ke tina siyazidla ngobuXosa betu, asinantloni
    ngobuhlanga betu.

3.  Bekusitiwa Amaqeya anendlela ezininzi zokwenza imali; ekubeni
    sapantsa ukungawufumani umhlaba ngenxayawo, ngokubhatala kakubi.
    Tina siyazivuma ukuba singama Xosa. saye ke sifuna ukwenza into zetu
    zokuqubela pambili uhlanga lwakowetu; kananjalo, tina sifuna
    ukuzibona izipako zetu, size ke silingo ukuzilungisa, into ko leyo
    esiya kuyenza ngokungadibani kwetu nama Qeya; kuba uti umntu
    akuzibandakanya nama Qeya asuke amsuzele ngobu-Qeya,‘ ngenene,
    angabisabazi abantu bakowabo, ati bapantsi. Ewe, inyaniso yona
    yokuba ubuninzi bomzi ontsundu usesebunyameni ngobu-Ngesi—
    usesebudengeni ngokanyo, nencubeko njalo-njalo, saye kwa lapo sisazi
    ukuba akusayi kuze kuhle ngelosi ozulwini, izo kushonxisa ubunyama,
    nokuza kuvelisa ukanyo nencubeko kumzi wakowetu oatsunuu. Yindawo
    yetu leyo, ekungoko singenako nangayipina indicia ukuzidibanisa
    nabantu abangasazole nto, abasicukucezayo.

1. Le Tournament asingumdlalo wokuqala obalulekiloyo kumzi ontsundu wase
lwandle. Kukade amadodana antsundu esiva kudlalela imali e Rini iminyaka
ngeminyaka, akazange aba ne Qeya; kuba apa elwandle lemizi mibini
ayidibani. Okunye kwakuko i match ezibalulekileyo e Rini, kuhlangene i
Rini, Qonce, ne Bhayi. Sesuka apa saya e Rini, kwaye kungeko bani
wabanazwi lokuba kutenina ningawanyulanga nje Amaqeya. Kwakona apa e
Bhayi samema i Rini ne Qonce. Amaqeya ayezolilo ngenene, kuba ayayazi
indawo yawo. Sabuye samema i Kimberley, aluzange olucuku lwanamhlanje
lubeko. Kwi Tournament yokuqala kumzi ontsundu eyayiso Qonce, lamenywa
no Bhayi, kwahlangana i Fear Not no Ethiopian, zazake zona zamema i
Buchanan ne Kreli Star nezinye, kodwa wona Amaqeya akazange akankanywe
nangubanina ukuba nawo zi natives makamenywe. Amadoda ayitetayo lento
ayeko ngayo lemihla ndiyikankanyileyo ngentla apa, akazange amangaliswe
ngokungamenywa kwama Qeya, lento namhla iwamangalisayo akuba engaseko
kuti kukutinina, nditeta ukutsho mna, ngenene, nditeta ukubuza mna,
andikwekwi.

5.  Ububi apo bukona kulento ibinqelwe ngabantu betu, oko kukuti
    abantsundu ; ngenyaniso Amaqeya wona akanalo olucuku, kuba akananto
    ayitetayo agcine indawo yawo azibekele kuyo engenamcimbi nati.
    Ngenyaniso, nditeta ukuteta mna into njengoko injalo.

6.  Amaqeya xana apumayo apuma odwa maxa sukuba ememile ahlangana odwa
    apa elwandle, andinanto nezinye indawo. Maka ndipikise umntu,
    andihlebi, andifuni bumenemene, ndihlambulula i Committee yetu
    ekuhanjwa kuqingqwa ngayo kwezikona zonko zase Bhayi, kungcoliswa
    amagama abantu abangenzanga nto bani, abangenzi nto bani nangoku,
    kupela bajonge umsebenzi wabo.

Ekupeleni ndiyavuya ukwazisa umzi ose Maxoseni owalahlekiswayo
sizoyisele zonke inkatazo ebezibekwe endleleni yetu. Sapumelela kakuhle
emdlalweni waza nomzi wakowetu wasixhasa ngokuncomekayo, aye lama Qeya
besifundekolwa ngawo ongeko nokubako, Zidanile intshaba kuba
zazitandazela ukuba zesityiwe, kwada kwako yena ongafihlisanga owamana
ukukwaza njengegeza eli lingenangqondo; kanti ziza zonke ukuba sizidle.
Namhla indwe itwelwe siti, yiva wena ungavanga. Ndim,

HAI-EWE.

NKOSI MHLELI, — Nam, ongomnye kowabo baka Mr. Philip, lingalindeleka
izwi lam kulenkalazelo “ Mvo.” U Mr. Philip ukucacisele udaba olwaba ngo
Mvo ; wena ufelenca kwelo “ Mhlobo- sigitshimi” oweva ngaye. Lipike ke
elo, ekulindeleke nilanyulwe. Amanqaku owenze ngempendulo ka Mr. Philip
asisanga. 1 Akatelekiseki nempendulo ayibhalileyo. 1. “ . . . Unenteto
engavakaliyo . . . wenzela izwi isenzo sake nowabo.” Umsulwa kodwa wena
ekundibazeni, xa ude wadwelisa amanqaku amahlanu (5)? U Mhleli ubengena
kudwaba egalela ngenqindi emoyeni. Mayibo iko into, nesenzo. 2. “
Engekaligqibi elo, &c.” Wagqiba apo yena nge Sivalo, nentshicilelo
yempendulo yake yalata isigqibo. Ube usekeleze ndawonina wena? 3.
“Inteto ka Mr. Philip ibe ngum’filiba, &c.” Lenteto inomdintsi kum
wokungati kudlaliswa ngegama lake ; njengalo uti, “ oka Pelem wa
Po­lonia.” ' 4. “ Ukuba uteta ngokusilelelwa kwezinye . . . yinto ko
loyo ebesingena kuyinceda nati, &c.” Ukuba u Mhleli ubempendule u Mr.
Philip ngalamazwi elinqaku IMVO ZABANTSUNDU ngezanelisiwe. Makubo le
okokuba IMVO kuko owayingcikiva oyinyelisa. Ingcaciso ka Mr. Philip
ikangele ukuba uyinanze. 5, ''Xa ngaba igqugula . . . ubesitola ezimole,
&c.” Kuya kucaca ngokucaca okokuba wahletyelwa. Umhlebi ngumpambanisi
luxolo emhlabeni apa. Okokuba nanitolwa kuzinyelwe, yingcinga yo “
Mhlobo-sigitshimi ” lowo yedwa.

Kwawena Mhleli uvala ngokuti—Saye simlindele nesigitshimi segqugula, &c.
Intlanganiso yayisazana ngo sigitshimi esingu Mr. Philip kupela.

Elokugqiba ndimema nawupina umfundisi owaye kwintlanganiso leyo ukuba
angamangalana ukuba umxolo wenteto eyayitetwa wawungenguwona lo: Kuko
into eninzi kumalungu etu ekalazela ukuba azidli uyakupuma into
abazitumelayo e “ Mveni.” Angakumbulana u “ Mhlobo- Sigitshimi” ukuba
akabanga mnye na- babini kubahletyelwa (ngabula yena) babafundisi

OHLABA UMKHOSI !

Mzi wakowetu ontsundu ukwenjenje “ndihlaba umkhosi” ndisiti vingcani
amazibuko zimkilo. Kufike apa ngezintsuku ucingo oluxela u MR. BRUNO
LEIST igqweta lase Jansenville elaye liteta ityala apa elikulu lamadoda
amabini antsundu awagwetywa ekay’ apa e Rafu, pambi kwe Jaji ngetyala
lokuba kwakusitiwa abo impahla (ibhokwe) zabelungu abatile kumandla wase
Jansenville, ukuba ubanjiwe.

Ipepa lase kay’ apa i Advertiser laliko lalihlafuna kakulu elityala
kwasemva kokugwetywa kwalo. Elinene ke ndite­ta ngalo, nalo labhalola
kumapepa ngeloxesha incwadi ezite zabhengeza ukuba nkulu kwenkohliso
eyenzekayo, ukuqalwa, nokuqutywa kokutetwa kwalo eli­tyala pambi
kwomantyi yase Jansenville. Nantso ke makowetu imbangi yokuba lomnumzana
apatwe ngoluhlobo, kukutetelela omnyama (ngokutsho kwabo) ilizwi
olilungileyo. Nawupina umntu otanda ubulungisa akanakuba angateti
akuyiva imbali yokutshona kwalamadoda. Namhla ndibhala nje lamadoda into
zlka Ntoni zise matyeni apo zitunyelwe kona iminyaka emine (4 years)
ngempahla ekubonakalayo ukuba yeyawo,

Ityala elaba sisimanga napambi kwe Jaji—kuba Umgwebi u Justice Colo wati
akuba eve kwa amanqhina azintloko okuqala, wati ebhekisa kwi Crown
Pro­secutor, “ abubonakali ubunqhina belityala.” Yati i Crown Prosecutor
“ nam elityala sondiliyeka, ubunqhina abuko kwaba
babanjwa,”—ekubonakoleyo uku­ba ababanjwa bakululwe, kute kunjalo i Jury
zalihluta manditsho, elityala kwi Crown Prosecutor, babiza amanqhina.
Kute noko sekurolwe i passes zaba ba­banjwa, nangapezulu koko amanqhina
amhlope, awabonisayo ukuba sihlo kwesimilo sabababanjwa (ujengokuba
bekubonakele kuye wonke ubani owayeko ematyaleni), i Jury zabafumana
benetyala lobusela, babake bayagwetywa njalo,

Nditi ko bahlobo bam, kumbulani nge­tyala lomfundisi u Don, nikangele
ukuba elityala singaliyeka na lidlulo lingaviwanga ilizwi lomzi
ontsundu. Nanko ko omnye ofayo ngokutotelela ubulungisa bontsundu.
Ndobuye ndivele, ndixolelo Mhleli ngokuntyuntya, ziyemka, nentliziyo
iyatsha.

OZILANDULELAYO.

NKOSI MHLELI, — Nceda undivumele ndipendule u “Dayimani,” ngenteto yake
ayincoma ubushushu! Elinene ukuba kungaba lilo, lindityola ngokuba
ndilutshaba LWEMVO ZABANTSUNDU. Into endingazange ndibe yiyo. Kwaye
kunjalo nje ndingazange ndifuno nokuba yiyo, Kanamanjazo akuko nentwana
enganditintelayo xa ndifuna ubutshaba nayo, Ukuba ndiyanyanzelwa ukuba
lutshaba Iwayo ndiya koyiswa ngamandla. Kwaye kunjalo nje mzi wakowetu
lo Dayimani akabungqini ngaziganeko obubutshaba. Kum ufana nomntu ofune
wafuna uhlobo angapokoza ngalo lomoya wake ushushu—uvumba libi— wacinga
ukuba kungati ukuze kulungo ange utetelela IMVO, kona ukuze afumane
uxaso olutile.

Mna into endilwa nayo BUBUDENGE, BUBUDENGE qa, ndingakatali nokuba
benziwa ngubanina. Kananjazo mna njengomntu ozipeteleyo nozipeteyo, xa
IMVO nokuba lilipina ipepa lingavumiyo ukufaka into endicela ukuba
ilifake, yindawo yam ukuba ndicele elinye ipepa —nokuba bangapina e
Dayimani abangasakukolwa yilonto.

Ngendawo yesi profeto sika Ntsikana abakolisa ukufunda ngayo ubuciko
abatile abapetwe ngo Dayimani into zo Gama Lifihlwayo—amagwala amakulu
abinza emva kwengqame! Ingati lenteto ka Ntsikana siyamkele tina bantu
yayalezwa tina, lo Mbumbana yenu ingacitakala, kuba tina siyayazi into
ewayete masibe yimbumba okwenzeni yona. Musani ukuti cwaka oku kwetu.
Owako futi,

JAMES M. PELEM. Lady Frere, 26 Jan., 1891.

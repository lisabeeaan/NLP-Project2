yam ebihlwayelwe ngenqolwa endala. Ngomnyaka olandelayo ndayi hlwayele
kwangenqolowa entsha yabuya ayalunga azomelela nendiza zayo zatamba.
Ngolandelayo umnyaka ndihlwayele inqolowa ebeseliminyaka mitatu
yavunwayo yaza ukulunga kwayo yancama. Indawo yokuqala ke ngoko kumntu
ofun’ ukuncipisa irusi kukuba ahlwayele ngembewu eselineminyaka emitatu
nokuba mibini ivuniwe. Eyesibini kukuba bazitshise indiza pambi kokuba
balime, enye indawo balime kwase kuqaleni kom- nyaka.

ITYALA LIKA NEHEMIYA MSHWESHWE.

KWEZINYANGA zidluleyo sibe sonke sibeke indlebe sipulapulisa ukuba kuya
kuteka nina ngo Nehemiya obekutiwa wayefuna icebo lokulwa no Rulumeni.
Ityala lake ngoku selide latetwa, sakukangela ebunqineni obunikiweyo
simangalisiwe ukuba kube kuteni na ukuze abanjwe abe ngumntu ogqibe
inyanga ezininzi kangaka igama lake lingadityaniswa nemikwa
engalungeleleneyo. Sibe siba woti nokuba ukululekile kube noko
kubonakala ukuba bebefanele ukumranela abade bambamba, kanti akuyi
kubako nokuba libe linye ilizwi eliya kusingisela nokusingi- sela
ekusondezeni ityala kuye. Sifumana kunqabile nokubaxelela abalesi betu
ukuba bekuteni na, kuba ayibonakali into abeyenzile.

Isisusa sesisipitipiti kubonakala ukuba kwakuko impambano ngehashe kwada
kwaliwa. Kwakuba njalo u-Mr. Liefeldt Imantyi yeso sigama watumela ukuba
abebekweso sipitipiti zebeze eofisini abavuma ukuya. Utumele ku Captain
Blyth ebuza ukuba kuza kutiwa nina. Ute yena makususwe amanye amadoda
amabini aye kubabiza abobantu. Esusiwe lomadoda afike kuselwa utywala
aselengena nawo esela. Ate ke kunjalo ayiquba indawe eze ngayo, kwaza
kwako ekutiwa bateta rabaxa. Endaweni yokuya kuyibika lonteto asuke
lamadoda atunyiweyo abeta omnye umntu ngesabokwe. Esukile amadoda- na
awurolela pandle lomsila waza wona ukupuma kwawo wati yimfazwe ngomso
noke nibone. Oku ke kwenzeka ngomhla 20 ka September engeko u- Nehemiah
emke ngomhla 14 waya kwa Thomson umgama ongapezu kwe kulu lemayile.
Kusile nge- mini obusongela ngayo umsila kufike u-Captain Blyth ehamba
namapolisa neqela labantsundu bexo- bile. Bate abanye yazaliseka into
ebitetwa ngumsila, baza batabata eyabo imipu balungiselela ukuhlanga-
nisa. Ngenteto yabo bati bebesoyika kakulu. Ute u-Captain Blyth kubo
bekani pantsi izixobo zenu, bala bona. Ute uze kuteta nabo kuba enguyise
wabo mababeke pantsi intonga. Bate ukupendula yake yabonwa pina into
yokuba uyise wosapo eze oluxobele intonga. Bate ke ngokoyika ukuba uza
kubenza into abavuma ukuyibeka pantsi imipu wada wabuya wanduluka
u-Captain Blyth. Uze kufika pakati kobusuku u-Nehemiah ngalomini kanye
kube kuxotyelwene ngayo. Akuyiva lento usuke wasigcwe- legcwele ukulwa
naba bantu bake esiti bangatini na ukude benze into enjalo, mabaze baye
kuzinikela ngokwabo ku Captain Blyth bacele uxolo lwento abayenzileyo.
Okwenene ngokokulwa kwake bazisile ku Captain Blyth wababamba.

Indawo yokuza kungena kungena kuka Nehemiah etyaleni asiyi boni kuyo
yonke lenteto ; kuba ababantu bayibalisa ngoluhlobo ngamanqina abeze
kunqinela u-Captain Blyth ukuba upakati no Nehemiah. Inteto eyabivakala
ukuba wayetumele abantu bokuti zekutatyatwe imipu ayibanga namntu
uyaziyo. Siti ke kubunqina obunikwe e-Qonce asimboni apo angena kona;
kodwa sambona selenguyena ubanjiweyo. Bamtabata bamsa e-Natal abavuma
ukumamkela abakona; wabuya waziswa kwakwa Adam Koko.

Ekona kutiwe ityala lake malize kutetwa kwela Mamfengu kwabuya kwatiwa
hayi e-Qonce. Kuze kuqutywa amadoda angapezu kwamashumi amane aza
kunqina ukuba esisipitipiti sabangwa nguye. Kulonkita akabanga ngapezu
kwesitatu abiziweyo ukuza kuteta. Ate awona mangqina azintloko ebekutiwa
acasene naye amhlangula kwati tu ngangokuba ade ati Umgwebi,—“ Nehemiah
akubangako nezwana elinye lobungqina elikufaka entweni, ngoku
ukululekile ukuya apo utanda kona.”

Ukukululeka komntu yinto ehlala iluvuyo kubo bonke abebemkangele. Kuya
kuba njalo ngakumbi kulo kuba kuba nzima koyikise kuti sonke kwakuvakala
ngati kwezinkosi zintsundu kuko efuna ukulwa no Rulumeni. Inkosi enjalo
siyikangelela ukuba ingumciti wohlanga. Kuti ke ngoko sakuva ukuba kanti
ibityolwa kubuye imixelo. Ngokukodwa ke xa akululwe yi Jaji into esaziyo
ukuba ayinakufumane igqwete inyaniso nokuba injanina—xa iti akanatyala
isukuba ibona ukuba okwenene aliko ; iti xa ilinikayo ibe ilibona
okwenene. Siti ekubeni ekululwe ngu De Villiers siyazi ukuba bekungeko
nto, kwanje ngokuba sisazi ukuba ebemgwebile ubeya kuba ubonile.

Kuvakala ngoku ukuba u-Nehemiah lowo ufuna ukubiza umvuzo walenxamleko
yake kade eyifumana ebanjezelwe emisebenzini yake, enqangiswa ati ese
Natal abe ekwa Koko ade ezekufikiswa apa. Ukuba iyinyaniso lonto soke

E-LIVINGSTONIA.

Bati abase Killimane sekuzinyanga ezine bengafumani zindaba ngo Dr
Stewart. Kumpati mkombe obeke waya apo zifunyanwa kona iletter zabo
ufike kusitiwa kwezinyanga zine kuti kwakutunyelwa aba­ntu abapete
impahla eziya e-Lizingstonia bangabi sabuya kungavakali nto ngabo.
Kutandatyuzelwe ukuba banqandwa ngama Arab endleleni. Kutiwa ate
ngokwazi ukuba nyakenye uya kucitwa kube kanye umsebenzi wobukoboka
asuka awuxatisela ngokunye. Kucingwa ke ngoko ukuba asuke abantu
abatunyiweyo abapange impahla neletter nabo ababambe. Sisalindele noko
ukuti koqondakala okukuko ngenyanga ezayo. Okwangoku masibabike
abapakati ngemitandazo kulowo unokubanceda.

UBUKOBOKA EMANTLA E-AFRICA.

Isizwe esikade sixatisele ubukoboka emantla e- Africa ngama Yeputa.
Ebengekayaleki noko ake afelwa ngamazibulo awo, eyela kulwandle olubomvu
ngenxa yokwenza usapo luka Tixo amakoboka. Siya vuya ukubona ukuba afun’
ukwenza imizamo eqinileyo yokuwucita lomsebenzi. U-Gordon, Umlungu,
ekade simkankanya nakwesi Sigidimi uti unikwe igunya lokuba apate indawo
ezona bezingmandla kumlomsebenzi, awutshabalalise ngokubona kwake. Naye
ngokwake uti ngoku liya kuba lityala lake ukuba awupelanga.

ILIZWI LOMBULISO.

LAMAZWI alandelayo angumbuliso ka Major Malan nesiyalo abesibekisa
kwiremente zabantsundu pesheya kwe Nciba ngokukodwa kweyase Mbulu
abepatiswe yona. Usicele ukuba siwashicilele, kuba angumbuliso wake kuwo
onke ama Kristu ake wanokuhlangana nawo kuleminyaka mibini idluleyo.
Atetwa kubadala namalungu omhlambi wase Mbulu ngo 30 ka December 1875.
Ngawo la ke lomazwi:—

Bazalwana bam abantsundu eli lituba lokugqibela endiya kuba nalo, lokuke
ndenze amazwana kwintlanganiso yabantsundu kwelilizwe lenu likulu.
Inciba ingumda owahlula Ikoloni kwezizizwe zanganeno ezide zaya kuma
ngolwandle. Umtandazo wam ngowokuti anga amazwi angumbuliso warn ku
Makristu auganeno apa kwe Nciba angancedisa ukwaudisa udumo lwe Nkosi
u-Yesu Kristu ngokunivuselela ukuze nimkonze ngokugqitiseleyo ukukoleka.
Lamazwi ezibalo endinikaugelisa kuwo akweyama Filipi iv.4-9,ati akuba
ete, “ Vuyani enkosini u-Yesu Kristu amaxa onke” azekanjalo asiyalele
ukuba “ sicinye nyezinto ezisukuba ziyinene, zitembekile zicokisekile,
zitandeka, zinokuduma okuhle.” Akufuneki nganto namhlanje ukuba nditete
naui ngokuvuya enkosini. Kukafuti ndisiti kuni tembelani kwinkosi u-Yesu
yedwa, niyikonze, kukafuti ndinixelela ukuba u-Nyana ka Tixo, ungu
Msindisi owaueleyo; okokuba akufuneki mntu umhlope, akufuneki namfundisi
wokuza kunikulula. Uncedo lwabo kupela luse kunixeleleni ngenceba yake
namandla notando.

Namhlanje indawo endifuna ukuteta ngayo kukukonza kwenu i-Nkosi
enganelanga kuba ngu Msindisi wetu, ete kanjalo yangu Kumkani no
Mongameli wetu. Ngemini yezolo sibe sihlaugene ekuvulweni kwetyalike
yakwa Lutuli. Ibiyimini yeminikelo. Into yokuqala ke ngoko endiya kuteta
ngayo kukusebenzisa kwetu izinto ate u-Tixo wasinika zona. Kuya
ndivuyisa ukuba nako ukuti Amakristu alapa kukafuti erola imali eni nzi
kunokuba indlu eyakelwe ukubedeshela u-Tixo ivulwe inetyala. Ute
umnqweno okoyo kwizizwe za maXosa wokunga kunga. hamba ubukumkani buka

Wakuba umpefumlo ukoliwe ukuba unyana ka Tixo walipalaza igazi lake
ngenxa yesono sawo; ukoliwe ukuba yena uyavuma ukusindisa aboni, imfundo
yokuqala neyona inkulu emele lompefumlo kukuhambisa ukwaziwa kuka Kristu
Nosindiso lwake. Ewe kulungile ukuba inkolo siyixase nakwelakowetu
ilizwe, ukuba sizicokise sizilungise izindlu zokukonza i-Nkosi, kodwa
eyona mfanelo iyintloko kukutumela Igospel kwabangekazange bayive.

Imfundo ke elandelayo yeyokuba nifundise abantwana benu. Elilizwi lika
Tixo ndinalatise kulo liti cingani nyezinto ezisukuba zitembekile.
U-Rulumente welilizwe uyancedisa ekufundisweni kosapo lwenu. Indawo
ezininzi zinikwa £20 ngomnyaka ukuba zona zirola £10. Kekaloku kum
kulusizi ukwazi ukuba kuko apo abantu balitembisayo elishumi leponti
kanti abayi kulirola. Yindawo yokutembeka na leyo ku Rulumente?
Yeyokutembeka na kumntu osukuba etitsha? Kuya kuhla ntonina xa kuqutywa
ngalondlela? Woti umfundisi abuzwe kwa Rulumente ukuba bayayirola na
abantu lamali bayitembisayo, ati ke, hayi, aze ke u-Rulumente abalele
Omantyi. Mna ukuba ndibe ndingomnye wo Mantyi benu ibiya kuti indawo
engayirolanga lomali idliwe igusha ezixabiso lifike kwi £10, ngenxa
yokwapulo idinga. Ukuba ningama Kristu kumbulani ukuba nifanelwe kukuba
nyabanyanisekileyo. Ilizwi lenu malibe ngapezulu nakubom benu. Yibani
nyabatembekileyo.

Kuleminyaka mibini idluleyo Inkosi indivumele ukuba ndiyifunde intlalo
yeremente zase South Africa. Ezinye, ngenceba yake, zinomsebenzi omkulu
eziwenzele udumo lwegama lake. Akuko zisemva njengani. Iremente eyona
ipumeleleyo yeyase Lusutu. Yona ayaneli kuxasa zikula netyalike
ezipakati kwayo; iti ngokungapezulu ixase abavangeli 55, abashumayela
Igospel pakati kwesizwe sakowabo. Kulomnyaka (we 1875.)

onomhlaba awutengileyo, oli- lifa labantwana bake uya kuhlala ekwaba-

umntu ontsundu uhleli eceba ukugxota omhlope, lomnqweno wokutenga
imihlaba siwuncoma ngokunikwa kwawo ubuntu kulowo uwute- ngileyo

eyatengiswa kwa Ngqika kwabako nabantsundu abase sihlanwini abatengayo.
Singa ke singababikela bonke abe besenomnqweno ukuba eminye imihlaba iya

lokutengiswa kwayo bolubona esazisweni kwakwesi Sigidimi, Into esingayi
cacisayo apa yeyokuba kuya kutengiswa ifama ezikumashumi asibozo; nokuba
ubukulu bayo ikolise ukuba liwaka le akile, inani lokuqesha likolise

kukuti oyayo apate ama- shumi amatandatu nasixenxe ukulungiselela ukuze
ngalomini yentengo ase erafela iminyaka emibini, ahlaule indleko
zokucanda, no- kulumleka ukuba emarkeni iti into kanti mhlaumbi iya kuti

Ese Monti imihlaba imaqela matatu. Elo- kuqala liya kutengiswa pambi kwe
ofisi ye Mantyi ngexa leshumi elinanye, ngomhla we 13 ka October 1879.
Imihlaba eya kutengisa ngalo mini iya kuba mashumi mabini. Yimihlaba ke
elingene isiza sendlu nesitiya. Imali ekuqashiswa ngayo ikolise ngokuba
kwishumi leponti neshumi elinambini.

Usuku lokutengiswa kweqela lesibmi le- mihlaba ese Monti bolubona
esazisweni aba- fundi betu. Eyalomini imihlaba (yomhla 5 ka November)
mihlanu, ke ilingene amasimi kuba ububanzi bayo iziakile ezilisnumi,
imali yokuyirafela yiponti ngomnyaka. Into eninzi zindleko zokucanda
ezikwiponti ezintandatu, isiqiniselo yiponti enesheleni zombini
ezinetiki (£1 2 3).

Eminye imihlaba esingayi kankanyanga esazisweni iya kutengisa ngomhla 4
ka November kwa pambi kwe ofisi ye Mantyi. Yona yimihlaba elingene
amasimi nedlelo. Ubukulu ikolise ukuba liwaka leakile elinamakulu
amabini namane. Imali yokuqesha idla ngokuba £22. Iko enganeno koko
nengapezulu.

Elesitatu iqela lemihlaba litengiswa ngohlobo lwalo lodwa. Kutiwa ofuna
yona angabalela Imantyi yendawo ekuyo imihlaba, enze isicelo sokuba
uyawufuna umhlaba otile, kuti ukuba akuko mntu wumbi wenze isicelo
esikwanjalo awunikwe lomntu, aqashiselwo okweminyaka elishumi erola
isheleni ngeakile ngomnyaka. Ukuba babe babini mhlaumbi batatu bacela
mhlaba mnye mini nye Imantyi iya kubamisela usuku abaya kuhlangana ngalo
pambi kwayo baze ke bashiyisane kona, unikwe kogalele ngapezu kwabanye.
Kekaloku owufumeneyo kuya kufuneka ukuba irafu ayirole ekungenene
komnyaka, ati ipela iminyaka emibini abe ubuncinane selelimi iakile enye
kuzo zonke ezilishumi mhlaumbi ke abe ute waka indlu. Engenjanganjalo
loba lupelile uqasho lwake aze Urulumente abuye awuqashise ngokunye. Oya
kuqesha amakulu amahlanu eakile, aze abe ebewarolela £25 ngomnyaka woti
kwakupela iminyaka elishumi arafe nge £2 10 kube kupela ; obeqeshe
iakile ezilikulu emva kweshumi leminyaka arafe nge shumi lesheleni. Ke
kwabawufumana ngoluhlobo umhlaba akuko uya kuvunyelwa ukuba aqeshe
ngapezu kwamakulu amahlanu eakile.

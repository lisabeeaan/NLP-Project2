UVIWO.

Iziqiniselo zemfundo (Certificates) ezifunyenwe ngaba- funda e Lovedale
nonyaka nje zingamashumi omatatu anesibozo (38.) Kwi University zi 17.
Kwi Teachers’ Examination zi 21. I University zenziwa kwa ngo April,
kwanje ngesiko lazo kade.

I Lovedale yayingenise amagama a 25, abamhlope beba- tandatu (6)
abantsundu belishumi elinesitoba (19). Kwa- pumelela ishumi
elinesixenxe, amadodana aye lishumi elinesibini, nesihlanu sentombi.
Abamhlope baba bata- ndatu, abantsundu baba lishumi elinanye. Kwi klasi
yokuqala kwapumelela babini abamhlope, haze abane bapumelela
kweyesibini.

I Teachers' Examination.

Olu uviwo lwenzeke ngo September. Kwangena ama, shumi amabini anesihlanu
(25.) Abapumelelayo ba 21- abasinelweyo ba 4. Amagama abo sake
sawashicilela nga- pambili.

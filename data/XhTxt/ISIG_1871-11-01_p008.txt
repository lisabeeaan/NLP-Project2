ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1871.

OFUNYENWE EFILE.—U-Jonas isicaka esintsu- ndu sika Mr. Samuel Hall wase
Gorea Kop, sisa- nd’ ukufunyanwa sifile sixomeke emtini. Iza- ndla zaso
bezibotshelelwe ngapambili, ekubonakala ke ngoko ukuba sibulewe ngomnye
umntu.

E-BAYI inqwelo ezintlanu zisand’ ukulayishela kwele Diamondi, zirolelwa
isheleni ezilishumi eli nesihlanu ngekulu. Imali ayinikwe kwaseku-
sukeni umnini zinqwelo ibe ziponti ezi £500. In- koliso kodwa irolelwa
isheleni ezi 30 nge 100lbs.

CHARLES BROWNLEE ESQ. IN THE CHAIR.

KUTE ngenxa yokusweleka kwendawo asabi nakuzifaka incwadi ebezivela ko
Robert Ntla no Matthew Mgunguza, nabanye. Ezinye singazifaka mhlayimbi
kwelenyanga ezayo.

JAMES LAING, Convener.

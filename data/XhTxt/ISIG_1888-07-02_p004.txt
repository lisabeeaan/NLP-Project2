UKWANDA KOBUNXILA KUMZI ONTSUNDU.

Intlanganiso ye Presbytery yaba-Fundisi be Free Church ite ngokubona
ukwanda kobunxila kumzi ontsundu wase buhedenini, nowase zikolweni,
yadala i-Komiti yaba Fundisi yokuba bahambele kwi Remente ze Free
Church, batete ngesi sonakalo singakanana. Abo bafundisi ngaba : Revs.
B.Ross, Moir, Stuart, Ferguson, McLaren no Mzimba- Ababini baye kwezi
pesheya kwe Nciba, abane baye kwezi ngaponoshono kwe Nciba. Baqale
ngeyase Lovedale epe- twe ngu Mr. Mzimba; zantontelana e Dikeni nendawo
zangapandle. Baza bahlangana namadodana nomtinjana ofundayo kwesi
sikolokazi solutsha e-Lovedale sipetwe ngu Dr. Stewart. Badlulela
kwesase Macfarlan isikolo esipe- twe ngu Mr. Makiwane, yangu Mr. Ross no
Mr. Mzimba kwabe komiti ababa nokufikelela. Emva koko basinga kwesase
Burnshill, bacela u Mr. Makiwane ukuba abapele- ke.

INTETO YABATUNYWA.

Indawo abangayo kuzo zonke ezindawo babika ukuba umzi uyatshabalala
bubunxila, amabandla ka Kristu anace- bo linina lokupelisa obu bunxila ?
Indawo zokutintela ukwanda kwe brandi ebeziko ngapambili u Rulumente
uzi- valile. Ezinjengezi :—

1.  Irafu ebirolwa ngabenzi bebrandi newayin iyekiwe, ayisabizwa.

2.  Amabala abedaliwe okuvala inkanti ezitile acitiwe- zivuliwe ezo
    nkanti. Kube kubekiswa ngencwadi, ecelwa u Rulumente ukuba awandise
    amabala; kwaba kukala nabafazi besizwe se Midushane ukuti inkanti
    mazivalwe, akwanceda nto. U Rulumente amabala uwapelisile.

3.  Kuko inteto yokuncipisa nemali ye license erolwa ngu- mnini nkanti.
    Nemali erolelwa ukutwalwa kwefatyi zo- tywala iyancitshiswa.

Abafundisi ke bati ibrandi ipalaziwe pezu komhlaba. Ubunxila bandile
kakulu. Iremente ukuba zineqinga ma- zivele nalo. Utywala bamazimba nabo
bandile kakulu, umhlaba buwonakalisile. Namhla akuseko ntlanganiso
yenziwayo ngapandle kotywala. Emidudweni kusuke ku- nxilwe;
emiyeyezelweni kusuke kunxilwe; ezintonjaneni kuyanxilwa. Akuhlangana
amadoda embalwa, mhlaumbi ukupendula abayeni, makubeko intwana yotywala.
Aku­seko lulimo lwenkabi, luhlakulo lwelima, naluvuniswano kungeko
tywala. Amadoda akalali emizini yawo xa kuko utywala; nabafazi bashiya
intsapo ezindlwini ngenxa yo- kusukelana notywala kwimizi ngemizi.
Indywala zihanje- lwa ngamadodana nazintombi. Kunxilwa kakubi ngumzi
wakowetu. Kubi, kulusizi kumzi ongakolwayo ngenxa yotywala, basuke
abantu baba ngamanxila. Umntu obo- nwa enxilile akenzelwa zintloni,
ikangelelwe lonto ukuba ilihlazo. Yinto ehlekwayo, ayincokola naye
umnxili nge- ukululeko akuqabuka.

ILIZWI ABALITUNYIWEYO.

Intlanganiso yaba-Fundisi ibanikele abatunywa bayo indawo eyavumelana
ngazo ngo April odluleyo, ezihanjwe zisanekwa kwi Bandla le Free Church
elintsundu. Zezi:—

“ Ikomiti yoselo ihlangene yagqiba kweliti imiteto le ila- ndelayo,
mayinikelwe iremente nentlanganiso ya-Badala.

“ Ibandla lonke, emfihlekweni, emakayeni, emitandazwe- ni, eziremeteni,
nase zintlanganisweni za-Badala malinikele intandazo enyamekileyo ku
Tixo u-Somandla, ukuba ati- ntele umsinga woselo.

“ 2 Intlanganiso ya-Badala mayilumkele ukuba imiteto le ilandelayo
isetyenziswe kwindawo ngendawo.

(a) Akuvumelekile ukuba amalungu eremente adibane, nokuba enze
intlanganiso zoselo ekutiwa zindywala, ne- mitayi, kanjalo mabangabiko
nakwe zinye intlanganiso zoselo.

(b) Abantu abaqashiweyo mabanga hlaulwa ngotywala.

(c) Usapo lwamakolwa malungayi ezintlanganisweni zama- -qaba, nokuba
zezalupina uhlobo.

3 Amalungu entlanganisweni ya-Badala namalungu e- ntlanganiso
yama-Dikoni ngabanye, nabe remente, naba- fundisi bosapo ezikolweni
zetu, mabalinge ngako konke abanako ukunceda i-Session ukusebenzisa
lemiteto ngoku- cokisekileyo.

“ 4 Intlanganiso ya-Badala mayilungiselele intlanganiso ezimiyo
kwiziqendu ngeziqendu zokuxoxa lendawo ngoselo, bati kanjalo bacele
uncedo lwaba Fundisi na Badala kwi- ndawo ezikufupi.

“ 5 Intlanganiso ya-Badala mayilinde lendawo yoselo, ikutaze abantu
beziqendu zabo ukuba babekise lendawo ku Rulumente ne Palamente.

“ 6 Kwagqitywa kanjalo kweliti i-Komiti mayizahlule kubini, ityelele
iremente ne Session ngayo lendawo. O-Mr. Moir, Mzimba, Ferguson, no
Mongameli, botyelela ezo zi- nganeno kwe Nciba; o Messrs. Stuart no
McLaren ezo zinga-Pesheya kwe Nciba.

“ BRYCE ROSS, Mongameli.”

INGXOXO ZE REMENTE.

Kwi remente yase Dikeni ite lenteto yakubekwa, icazwa, amanye amadoda
amaninzi ayibulela; akubona ukonakala komzi butywala. Kwabako indoda eti
yona ayiboni nto ingabulelwayo kule nteto, kuba ngo tixo abatsha, beze
nabafundisi abatsha. Bona bagcine imfundiso endala, ya- bafundisi
'abadala, no Tixo omdala. Yimpendulo na ke leyo injalo, neqinga na
lokucita ubunxila pezu komzi ?

E-Mkubiso uhlanganisene umzi webandla leremente apo, kuvakala ukuba
isibonda seso sikolo sitetela abatile senje- nje: “ Indlela emayenziwe
makuyekwe ikofu le, yiyo le isijacileyo sangamahlwempu sanje.” Liqinga
na elo loku- pelisa utywala nobunxila ?

Ngati kodwa kuyaposiswa ngulowo uti abadala abafundisi bebe ngashumayeli
ngezenzo. Kwabehlelo le Free Church—u-Bene omdala iti imbali way’
engumzili ; u-Lose naye way’ ekwanjalo; u-Lanki iti imbali ub’engumzili.
Wake watyolwa ngokuba uyasila ngomnye waba seli-tywala kuba kwakumana
kusenziwa i- Ginger-beer endlwini yake. Eyivile lonto wavakalisa kwa-
lomini ukuba kususela kwelo xesha uyakuyizila nalonto ingebubo utywala
ngenxa yoko kutetwayo ; u-Gaveni wazi- ngenisa kwiqela labazili
entlanganisweni eyayise Alice, ngokusuka amadodana abefundiswa nguye
aqwele kulo ntlanganiso. Ngoko ke inteto yokuba lentshumayelo intsha,
iyapikiswa yimbali nezenzo zabo bafundisi badala.

Abafundisi amazwi abo bawaqukumbele ngamazwi ka Tixo ati: “ Nyana womntu
ndikwenze umlindi kwindlu ka Israeli, uya kuva izwi ngomlomo Wam,
ubayale. Ndakuti kokohlakeleyo uya kufa ngenyaniso; ukuba akumyali, no-
kuba akuteti umvuse okohlakeleyo aguquke endleleni yake ekohlakeleyo,
ukuze asindise ubom bake; lowo ukohlakeleyo uya kufa etyaleni lake,
negazi lake ndolibiza esandleni sako. Kodwa xa umyala okohlakeleyo aze
angaguquki enkohla- kalweni yake, angayiyeki indlela yake ekohlakeleyo;
uya kufa etyaleni lake, nompefumlo wako uwuhlangule.”

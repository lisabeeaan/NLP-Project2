IPARLAMENTE YALEKONI.

LEPARLAMENTE ibe iko icitwe ngomhla wa 31 ka July. Akuko xesha ekwake
nganyaka mnye kw.enziwa umsebenzi ongade udlule lowenziweyo ngoku.

Eyona ndawo mhlayimbi inkulu yenziweyo kukumisa umteto wokuba kwenziwe
indlela zenqwelo zomlilo kwindawo ngendawo zekoloni, eziyakuti ubude
bazo xa zihlanganisiweyo zibe kumakulu asibozo emayile, imali eziya
kuyidla ibe kwizigidi ezihlanu zeponti. Ziyakusuka kona E-Bayi nase Kapa
nase Monti. Eyakusuka E-Kapa banxamele ukuba ide iyekufika E-Beaufort
West. lyakuba luncedo olukulu, kuba ngoku kunzima ukupumela kwenkabi
eziya kona ngenxa kakuba ilizwe libambe ubuntlango. Noko ke ayiyikucanda
kwindawo ezizintlango kube kupela. Ezakusuka E-Bayi iyakusinga
E-Cradock, kuze kodwa kubeko isebe elipambukela E-Rini nase
Graaff-Reinet. Esuka E-Monti iyakuya E-Queenstown, kuze kubeko isebe
eliya E-Qonce. Kulindeleke ukuba yoti ipela iminyaka emihlanu zibe
sezigqityiwe zonke ezondlela, sibone kuhamba ezomlilo inqwelo endaweni
zezi zenkabi!

Omnye umteto omkulu ngomisa uhlobo ayaku- nyulwa ngalo amalungu alendlu
yeparlamente kutiwa yi “Legislative Council.” Ngapambili ikoloni
ibeyahlulwe kubini, lisiti elinye icala layo linyule abalo lize nelmye
linyule abo; kodwa ngaku izakwahlulwa amacetyana asixenxe, lize lilinye
litumele amadoda amatatu. Kekaloku nabantsundu kungakululeka ukuba baye
kungena ukuba banemfundo nobutyebi obaneleyo. E-New Zealand, apo
kulaulwa kwangohlobo olunje ngolu, eparlamente kuko nabantu
abangabelozwe kakade. Kufumancka kuko kubo amadoda anokuqonda izinto.
Batintelwe yintoni na abalapa ukuba nabo bangayizameli londawo?

Kumiswe kanjalo umteto wokuba kwakiwe imitantato emitatu epumela
I-Gqili. Omnye wayo uya kwenzelwa ukuwela inqwelo ezisukuba zisiya
Elusutu, omnye upumele kwelizibuko kuti­wa yi Bethulie, owokugqibela
upumele E-Hope Town. Abake bahambe nenqwelo siyakolwa ukuba
bayakuyibulela iparlamente ngalo msebenzi.

Kuko nemali ekutshelwe ukuba kuyiwe kufunwa E-Europe, abantu
bokusebenza, neyokuba kuhanjiswe imfundo kubo bonke abakule koloni,
nokuba kwandiswe inani lendawo ezinemantyi, neyokukutaza imikombe etwala
impahla zalapa ezivela pesheya nokuba zeziyakona ; kuko neyokusa ucingo
kwindawo ezitile, neyokwaka izindlu zokubonisa imikombe ingozi yayo
ebusuku, neyokulungisa indlela zenqwelo kwindawo ezitile.

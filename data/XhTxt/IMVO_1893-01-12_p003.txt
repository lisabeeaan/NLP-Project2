Khama kuba engabatandi. Obe kona uti :—Nge 27 December wababutela bonke
ndawonye nabantu bake abambalwa wati, Maxosa yazini into endinibizele
yona yile: Nditi hambani nimke nouke apa kwa Mangwadu, ngokuba abantu
bam abasafumani kuzeka ngenxa yenu kuba nisuke nazeka abaPutini
abantwana begazi abanganifaneleyo nina, kengoko nitshabalalisa imbewu
yaba Putini na- ndisa eyama Xosa engafuneki nganto apa, ko hambani
nonke. Andilindele mpendulo kuni, nditetile, ndigqibile. Ke nditi, ndaza
ndabona inkosi yom-Sutu engafuni bantu ; ndaza ndayiva nenkosi engafuni
kupendulwa ngabantu kodwa iteta, igwebe, igqibe yodwa.

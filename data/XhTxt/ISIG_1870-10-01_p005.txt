Isigidimi Sama-Xosa.

LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, OCTOBER 1, 1870.

Isigidimi Sama-Xosa.

KUBALESESHI.

IXA ekuqalwa ngalo amapepa endaba, lixa elibalulekileyo nokuba
kukwimbali yasi- pina isizwe. Esisitunywana situnyelwe ukuza kukangela
ukuba selifikilena eloxa kubemi belilizwe. Kutiwa elokuqala ipepa
lendaba lase Europe lati tapu E-Venice ngexa ekwakuliwa nama Turks
ngonyaka we 1563. Zazilangazelelwa kunene indaba kwaza kwako umteto
wokuba kuveliswe ipepa ngenyanga zonke. Ngobukulu bendaba ezikoyo,
nakuba zingazo izinto ezahlukeneyo ezi—kukulwa okupesheya, ne diamond
zase Africa—situmela Isigidimi Samaxosa nezo ndaba.

Ukwamkelwa kwalo el ipepa kuya kunceda ukubonisa ukuba lominqweno
nezenzo ezilusizi ezizamazamisa uluntu kwela pesheya, zinokuleseshwa
ngenyamekona ngabo litunyelwa kubo ngokukodwa, abasemizini nase
zikolweni zelilizwe. Ngapandle kwa matanda, kuko iqela lemidaka
elilungelweyo lipepa elinje, nakuba ngenxa yokungaziwa kwenani labantu
belilizwe lingena kuxelwa eloqela. Kodwa sekuminyaka imashumi mane
imfundo imi. Ukuba amalinga abafundisi abe nokupumelela bekufanele ukuba
abe selengamawaka anokulesesha inteto yawo, nakuba bekungekabiko
zincwadi ngexa lokufika kwabafundisi kwelilizwe. Nokuba kufunyenwe
elonani lodwa yoba yinto enenzuzo naleyo ekubeni iyakutyila okuncinane
okufeziweyo. Ukunqwenelwa kwozincwadi ngumlingo oqondisa ingqondo
yabantu nokuba ngabapina.

Ngapandle kwamatanda into engum- fundisi omkulu ngezimini ngama pepa
endaba. Besingati Isigidimi Samaxosa simi sincede ngokupindiweyo eku
fundiseni nase kwandiseni ukwazi, nokuvuselela umnqweno wokufunda. Kodwa
asinakutemba kakulu ukuba oko kuya kwenzeka. Laba libi, lilifutshane
ixesha lama pepa andulele eli. Kwindidi ngendidi zezikolo

kekwa lingwa ukumiswa amapepa emidaka akwabiko mpumelelo noko. Makunikwe
imbeko kwabo bageca indlela yobulumko. Sekuminyaka imashumi matatu
lapelayo ipepa elingu Mshumayeli Wendada, lisa shicelelwe kali shumi
eline sihlanu. Neli Sibuto Samavo lahla lapelelwa ngumsebenzi lisa
shicilelwe kasi xenxe. Neli Li-Kwezi latshona kungekabi mzuzu likanyisa,
lisa shicilelwe kane. Eli Sisitunywa Senyanga, elona lalinamandla lilele
sitatu kula mapepa likutshwe zizihlobo zetu Ama-Wesile lanqanyulwa
ngokulusizi ngokusuka libetwe kwase ntloko yimfazwe Ka-Mlanjeni. Emveni
kwemi nyaka eli shumi elina mibini, kwati tapu ele Ndaba, langa liya
quba. Into elayifezayo kukushiya ingqungqutela yetyala E-Lovedale
leponti ezimayela nekulu. Siya zibongoza ngokukulu izihlobo zetu eziyi
midaka, oko kuba ziyikangele kunene lendawo ziyiqiqe, le siza kuteta
ngayo, ukuba inga buye ihleke.

Sakukumbula ngale mbali yamalinga anga ncedanga nto, nangendleko ete
yabako, ingaba yinto enokutelwa nqana, sakuti sisikupa Isigidimi
sinokoyika. Sikankanya ezizinto ukuqondisa abalifundayo eli pepa,
lenyaniso yokuba, xa bangalixasanga ngomxelo, lofana nawa ngapambili;
kuze kuti emveni kwambalwa akutshiweyo, ati aseleyo abekwe bucala,
ndawonye nentlanganisela yama pepa ama Xosa ekoyo E-Cape Town.

Abafundi belipepa bosixolela ngokumisa ngoku sekuhleni indawo eliya kuti
elipepa lizuzwe ngazo. Indawo elingayo kupela yilungelo yama Xosa.
Sibabongoza ngokukulu ukuba basincede basixase; imali yalo yorolwa
ngapambi kokuzuzwa kwepepa.

Elipepa aliyi kuketa bankonzo itile; lomela indaba yezikolo zonke
nelungelo yabemi belilizwe. Alinto yimbi ngapandle kokuba lipepa
lendaba. Kungati kunqweneleke ukuba kubeko ipetshana eleyamene nelizwi
ngexa elizayo. Kodwa amapepa endaba kakade aba ngapambili kwencwadi
ezivela ngamaxesha atile.

Elipepa lobalelwa abo bakwaziyo uku- funda kwa nabo bazi tandayo indaba
zamanye amazwe. Ingaba yimposiso ukulilungiselela abo bangenayo nentwana
yemfundo nakuba bebengaba nokulifu-ndelwa. Mhlaumbi ukuqala yoke inge
inkoliso yendaba zalo ayibalungele naba kwaziyo ukufunda. Makuqondeke
kodwa ukuba indaba nama Ngesi nezama Xosa azikuhlala ziteta nto nye. Xa
sukuba ipepa liguqulwe lonke liyakuxelwa.

Kweli lanamhla zoke zinge ziswelekile indaba zemidaka. Imbangi yoku
kupela kuku swelesa kwaba balelani. Siyatemba ukuba londawo yobuya
ilungiswe kwaya kulandela.

Bonke abafundisi nabo bonke abanqwe- nela ukuhluma kwa bantu belilizwe
baya- celwa kakulu ukuba bancedise kumalinga nakwimizamo yokwandisa
ukwazi.

IMFAZWE YASE EUROPE.

KWELOKUQALA ipepa le-Sigidimi kukankanywe nto mbini, ekuncokolwa ngazo
kakulu ngelixesha kuzo incwadi zendaba. Ezonto ke, zizi diamond,
nemfazwe yapesheya. Kwelinye ipetshana kutetwe nge diamond, kweli
kwoxelwa ngemfazwe.

Kubambene izizwe ezikulu zapesheya—Esama French, sibambeni nesama
Prussia.

Bezivumelene ezizizwe zozibini, ukuba Ama French ayakunceda Ama-Prussia
ukutimba I-Hanover, isizwe Sama-German. Aze Ama- Prussia ancede
Ama-French ukulizuza elozwe elipakati kwelawo nomlambo oyi Rhine. Ate
kodwa Ama-Prussia, alizuza elase Hanover, ngapandle kwama French, aza
akabisavuma ukuba Ama-French alifumane elase Rhine. Aseleba nomona
Ama-French,—aqala ukulungiselela im- fazwe. Akubona oko, Ama-Prussia,
aselexoba nawo, efunzela kwelama French. Ekuqaleni kwa- gxotwa
Ama-Prussia, kodwa kamva agxotwa kwada kwa kabini Ama-French, ada aya
azivalela esixekwene esinqabileyo.

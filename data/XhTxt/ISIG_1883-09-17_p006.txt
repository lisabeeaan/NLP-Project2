ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 17, 1S83.

LADYSMITH-NATAL.

IZINTO NGEZINT0.

Sibulela u Mr. Joshua Dlamini ngoludaba Iwelozwe likude.

  Drie Fontein, August 18, 1883.

Ndicela indawo kwelo pepa lamanene, andimntu uhlala ekataza,
undinyamezele. Indawo eyimbangi yile—ndibe ndize apa e Drie Fontein
entlanganweni ebizwa nge Nzondelelo—le Nzondelelo kukuzondelela ublanga
Iwakuti oluntsundu ukunga lungayiva i Vangeli emagumbini omane omhlaba.
Lontlangano yaqala ukubako ngonyaka we 1875 ngo August, yonke leminyaka
ibisenziwa kude kube kulo August ka 1883, yaqala ngomhla we 8. Kwaye
kubutelene izihlwele zabakolwayo ezivela kwindawo ngendawo ze Bemente
yakwa Wesile. Inkonzo yavulwa ngu mfundisi wetu u Rev. P. G. Mtembu,
washumayela kwi Vangeli ka Luke 10.27—“ Uyakumtanda u Tixo wako ngayo
yonke intliziyo yako, ngawo wonke umpefumlo, nangamandla ako.” Kute
ngomhla we 9 yalila intsimbi ekuseni. Inkonzo yaqutywa ngu Mr. Philip
Mcunu, washumayela kweyokuqala ka Samuel 7.3—“ Kwelelisani o Tixo
basemzini.” Watsho lomshumayeli wati, U-Tixo akumfuni umtwana (isono)
wenye indoda engu Satana— ukuba niyamtanda konzani yena yedwa. Ngo 11
o’clock kwema ingwevu yakuti engu Mr. Jonathan Xaba washumayela kweka
Mateyu 10.37—“ Otandayo uyise umhlaumbi unina ngapezu kwami
akandifanele, notandayo unyana umhlaumbi intombi ngapezu kwam
akandifanele.” Emva kwake kwema u Mr. J. Zuma ebulela i Remente
ngokuzondelela kwayo ukunga uhlanga Iwetu lungayiva inyaniso. Yapuma
intlanganiso ukuya kwamkela idinala. Kute ngo 5 o’clock ukumka komhla
yalila intsimbi, kwashumayela umlwelwe ongu Mr. E. Nyembe kwi Psalm
65.1—2 “ Indumiso iyakulinda Tixo ! e Ziyone, nakuwe isibambiso siya
kufezwa. Wena uvayo umtandazo! uluntu lonke loza kuwe.” Ngomhla we 10
inkonzo yaqutywa zingwevu zakuti, ezateta ummangaliso. Ekuseni yayingu
Mr. Abraham Twala. Ngo 11 o’clock yaba ngu Mr. Johannes Kumalo, lengwevu
yateta yati—Kaloku amandla am aselepelile, lenkonzo yapezulu ndiyiqale
ebutsheni bam, ngoku sendimdala, sendipele amandla, zite izinto endaye
ndizinqwenela ebutsheni bam sekukukona zifeziswayo xa ndingasenawo
amandla. Ndinga ke angati u Tixo wezulu ongu Mdali andilondoloze. Ukuba
bendinokolo ngenditandaza ngomtandazo wendoda yakudala yakwa Israel engu
Simeon, eyati—“ Kaloku isicaka sako Nkosi masindululwe.” Kuba
inzondelelo yam ngohlanga Iwakuti iyenzeka. Ndinga ke ukuze sibe
sebuntwini sifanele ukupuma emacaleni, niyeke ukutabata izinto zabe
Lungu, ningenamandla umntu makazisebenzele ngawake amandla ukuze
lenzondelelo ikule. “ Lowo uba umi makalumke angawi.” I. Korinte 10.12.
Emva kwengwevu kwema u Mr. Samuel Pakati, washumayela kwi Vangeli ka
Yohane 7.37—“ Kekaloku ngomhla wokupela ongomkulu womtendeleko wema u
Yesu wadanduluka esiti, ukuba uti umntu anxanwe makeze kumi asele.”
Yapuma ke intlanganiso, kwati ngokuhlwa kwabuyelwa. Wema u Mr. Stephanus
Kumalo wati, “ Bebenga hlanjululwanga balishumi na? abalitoba pofu
bapina?”—Luke 17.17. Emva kwema u Mr. Jacob Mnguni wati, “ Tina kodwa
asiwamkelanga umoya welizwe, koko simamkele u Moya ovela ku Tixo.”—I.
Korinte 2.12.

Kute ngomhla we 11 ku August ekuseni wema u Mr. Simon Butelezi esiti, “
Ungaziqumbisi ngenxa yabo bakwenza okungalungileyo, ungabi namona
ngakubenzi bobume- nemene.”—Psalm 37.1—2. Yapuma intlanganiso. Kute ngo
11 o’clock kwatetwa ngemali, emva koko kwapunywa (kuba
yayingum-Gqibelo).

Nge Cawa ngo 11 kwaye kushumayela u Rev. Luke Msimang esiti, “Hamba
wenze ngokunjalo nawe.”—Luke 10.37. Uti makati ovileyo azise omnye,
njengoko wati u Yesu tanda, tina masitandane. Ama Ngesi ati akuliva
ilizwi lapezulu aba nenzondelelo yokunga zingati ezinye izizwe zilive;
acanda ulwandle aza kufika kweletu, sayiva ke inyaniso yelizwi lika
Tixo, makube njalo ke nakuti, ati ovileyo avise omnye—ongekeva. Kute ngo
2 o’clock kwashumayela u Eliam Msimang esiti, “ Ngokuba engalibekanga
pantsi kwazo Ingelose ilizwi eliza kuza elona siteta ngalo.”—Kweyama
Hebrew 2.5. Kute akuba egqibile ukushumayela kwapunywa ngalowo umhla,
kwakulindeleke umtendeleko, akwenzeka ngenx’ enokuba lalisina umfundisi
akafika.

UTYWALA.—E Mampondweni kusand’ ukufa amadoda amatatu bekusiliwa
etywaleni.

UKUBA.—Lomkwa mbi kangaka, ngoku e Kimberley kutiwa uqutywa nasemini.

U MR. SAUL SOLOMON.—Siva kananjalo ngaye okokuba ngati kungoku ubekisela
ekupileni.

UKUTAKATA.—Kwelama Mpondo kuko amadoda amatatu abulewe ngumtshana ka
Nqwiliso, ngenxa yokutakata.

OBULELE USANA LWAKE.—Ngase Piquetberg umfazi ontsundu abati ngu Katyi
Bloois, ufunyenwe ebulele usana lwake.

IDAYIMANI ENKURU.—U Mr. W. Miller wase Jagersfontein ufumene idayimani
abati ukuyixela izi Carat eziyi 582 ubukulu.

IMANTYI ENGUMCEDISI.—U William le Saeur, Esq.; unyulwe okokuba
abengumcedisi e-Murraysburg, no Bobert Charles Ferris, Esq., ukuba abe
ngowase Bofolo.

UBUMENEMENE.—Pesheya kutiwa ukupanga kwande ka- ngaka ngangokokuba
amadoda anxibe ilokwe ezenza abafazi iqingake lokubafumana kasisulu
abantu.

AMATYALA PAMBI KWE JUDGE E QONCE.—Ngelixesha amatyalatyala nje ayi 20
awempambano matandatu. Avela Pesheya kwe Nciba onke okubulala ma hlanu.

INTLANGANISO YABALIMI ABANTSUNDU E CALA E BATE- NJINI.—Ilitye lesiseko
lendlu yalentlanganiso ikankanywe ngasentla, lobekwa ngomhla wamashumi
omabini kuyo le- nyanga.

UTYWALA EZINQWELENI.—Kuko indoda eti, yiti kanye ukuba ufuna ukukwela
ezinqweleni kangela kanye engenatywala bokutengisa, kuba apo kunxila
nentwana encinane yomntwana.

U REV. BRYCE ROSS.—Lomfundisi njengokuba ebengeko ixesha, ngoku ubuyile,
ufike ngeveki epambi kwale idluleyo. Kunye naye ngati kufike intombi
zika Rev. Tiyo Soga zombini.

U BISHOP JONES.—Lemambane kuvakala ukuba selibuye yabeka kwasekaya,
njengokuba ibike yanexesha ihambahamba kwindawandawana ezitile. Nokoke
ngati ayikapili hlambululu kwingozi eyayiyihlele e Rini.

INQANAWA KA NOWA.—Amapepa ama Ngesi azele yingxelo yokuvunjululwa kwe
nqanawa ka Nowa. Kutiwa ibonwe pezu kwentaba zase Ararat, ngokusuka kuye
kuncipa ubungqingqwa bekepu elihleli ligubungele ezo ntaba.

IMVULA.—Apa, e Dikeni, bekuko intlanganiso yokuta- ndazele imvula
ngolwesi-Tatu, (Sept. 13) kute ngengomso, i Nkosi yayipendula imitandazo
yabantu bayo, ngokuti iyitulule imvulana. Akwaba kungaba njalo kulo
lonke.

ISELANA LOMNTWANA.—Intwana ekutiwa ngu Charles Raven e Kapa ibi pambi
kwe Mantyi, ngokusuka ingabi nakuyixela imali eyifumana ngezonka
ebitunyelwe ukuba ihamba izitengisa. Isigwebo sibe sesokuba ipiwe
imivumbo elishumi elinantlanu ngo swazi.

OBE IZEMBE.—Intokazi abati ngu Sophia Smith igwetyelwe intsuku
ezilishumi isebenza nzima, ngenxa yokuba izembe lenene elitile. Ukuliba
kwayo elizimbe lentokazi ibitunga ingubo zelinene, ite inyatikazi
ukupuma kwayo kanti imka nalo ; Ipumile iye kuba inkuni ndaweni itile,
umninizo eyibambile kufumaneke ukuba kanti nezembe asilayo.

UTYELELO LWENDODA ENDALA ENGUMNTU WELILIZWE.— U Rev. B. W. Thompson,
unyana ka Rev. W. Thompson, wase Kapa, emva kweminyaka engamashumi
omabini emkile, uzakutyelela apa. Lendoda ngumbali micimbi ye London
Missionary Society. Ngokuhamba eshumayela ilizwi uyicandacande yonke i
China, namazwe angakona, ngokuke uzakucandacanda eli letu. Lendoda
ngumfo owa funda kwelilizwe, kunye no Sir Henry de Villiers, no Chief
Justice Reitz no Mr. Judge J. Buchanan. Siyakolwa okokuba u Mr.
Thompson, ininzi into eyakumamkela ngemivuyo.

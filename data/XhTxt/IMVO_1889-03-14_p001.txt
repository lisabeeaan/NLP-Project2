NDICELA amehlo ngehashe lam elalahleka ngomhla 25 ka November, 1888. Limfusa, limpemvu, amanqina angasemva amhlope mabini ngapambili linye, lelase kohlo. Emva komkono wase kunene kuko ibala elimhlope elibukudlwana. Linopawu kule ndlebe yase kohlo. Ehlezeni linen, dawo eman’ukudubuleka. Lidlala kakulu xa likwelweyo.

Elesibini limtuqwa linebala elimhlope emva komkono kumacala omabini, angati mhlaumbi angabonakali xa kubikwe isali. Lomabala alungelene nezindawo kufakwa izitibili kuzo. Linentsimbi amanqina omane, isingei asiside kakulu. Ondilandisileyo ndomvuza.

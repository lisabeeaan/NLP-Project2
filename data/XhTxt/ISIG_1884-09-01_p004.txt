IMBUMBA ENTSHA.

Intlanganiso yomanyano yabamnyama kuqukwa zonke intlanga ezintsundu,
isand’ ukumiswa e Katala, ibizwa ngokuba yi “ Stockenstroom Original
Africander Asso­ciation,” eya kulinga ukuqubela pambili izinto zemfundo
nezibekiselele emisebenzini, yaye kananjalo iya kukangela
ekulungiseleleni izinto zonyulo kwanezinto zonke zengxoxo ezilunge
nezinto zolaulo.

U Mongameli no mncedisi wake (President & Vice- President) u Mbali no
mncedisi wake (Secretary & Assis­tant Secretary) sebenyulwe bonke. Koba
kanye ke nge nyanga awoti ahlangane amalungu ale ntlanganiso, awoti ke
xa sukuba ehlangene, kubeko amalungu nokuba ngamhlope na nokuba
ngantsundu awoti afunde amapepa ngezinto zempato yolaulo lwa Mangesi,
kwanezinto zolu ulaulo, kwanezinye izinto ezingati zibe luncedo. Noko ke
asikukuti zoyekelelwa izinto ezimalungana nezinye intlanga —kodwa
ngokukodwa kuya kukaugelwa kwinto zase England neza Mangesi alapa e
Koloni.

Njengohlobo lwe Afrikander Bond kuya kulindelwe ukuba kubeko amasebe ayo
kwindawo ngendawo, apa e Koloni, pesheya kwe Nciba, e Kokstad, e
Kimberley, nase Lusutu. Sekuko amadoda amabini ate akankanywa yile
ntlanganiso aya kumela i Stockenstroom ngexesha elizayo.

Yoti ke xa selide intlanganiso le yanabela nakwezinye indawo, liya kuti
igama liguqulwe, kutiwe yi “ Native South African Association.”

KUBALIMI.

U September lo yinyanga engadli ngakuba namvula zinkulu, eti ibe
zimvulana kodwa, yaye eyona nto inkulu ikolisa ukubako ingumoya
onamandla ovela ngasentla malunga ngase ntsbona-langa, kuti ngamanye
amaxesba kuwe nezicoto ezidla ngokutshabalalisa izinto ezisapumayo.

Eli iselixesha lokuhlwayela ozi nqolowa, ozi ralasi, nozi habile, nakuba
ihabile ehlwayelwa ngoku ike ishwence endaweni yokuvutwa. Intlumaya,
imbotyi, umbona, amazimba namatanga eli lixesha ezingahlwayelwayo zonke
ezizinto.

Ukuba emyezweni kuko imiti onga ungamisela amasebe ayo kweminye, eli
iselixesha, nokuba yimiti efuna ukuxotyulwa amaxolo amadala eli
iselixesha nakuyo. Kodwa kwimiti enjengo zi apile noma pere lonto
ungayenza ekufeni kwenyanga le, xa iqalayo ukuvelisa imidumba, naxa
amaxolo asele ebugxukugxuku, engatshele kakulu emtini.

Into ezinje ngo matanga nozi vatala nezinye into zolo hlobo, zona
ungazihlwayela kumhlaba otyetyisiweyo, kuba zinto eziba butataka
ebuncinaneni, eziqaulwa nazimvula xa sukuba zinkulu, ke zona ukuba unako
ungazikusela nge glass nokuba kungesiziba se calico usixome pezu kwazo.
Eli ikwalixesha lokuhlwayela ozi beetroot, ozi turnips ne minqate,
njalo-njalo.

FEBRUARY 20, 1890.]

  IMVO ZABANTSUNDU (NATIVE OPINION). 3

Sir G. Sprigg: Abantu base zikolweni bayasela na nabo?

Mr. Kalipa: Bebefudula bengaseli, kodwa ngoku ndiyapaula ukuba bayasela.
Bayaqala.

Sir G. Sprigg: Emakubke abafundisi akuko nto bayenzayo ikukulunga
okudluliseleyo?

Mr. Kalipa: Abafundisi bona bayazama ngako konke ukulunqumamisa uselo,
kodwa nditi unxilo luhlalel’ ukuboyisa.

Emva kwale mibuzo u Sir G. Sprigg ubuze ku mantyi ukuba yena
uyakunqhinelana oka kutetwa ngu Kalipa ngonxilo. Wamkanyeza kwabanda
ngokumayelana ne brandy. Amatyala aveliswa butywala bamazimba. Ute
akutsho u Mantyi iqela labantsundu laqwab’ izandla ukuvumela umantyi.

U Sir Gordon ute ndiyayiqonda into eziqwatyelwa yona izandla ngumzi.
Ndivisisa ukuba aba bavumela nomantyi. Ute wakuba ungapenduli umzi
wabuya wati unga angatabat’emhlambini ukuba wona womelana nokutetwa ngu
Mr. Jenne??? nokuba ukwelika Kalipa.

Isibonda esingu Lawu site akuko matyala eza pambi komantyi ngoselo lwe
brandy. Abamnyama banxiliswa bobo Mxhosa. Nanamhla kuko enye indoda eze
kubika umntu ofe ngoselo lomqomboti.

U Mr. Thomas Zwedala ute kuko ngoku isiko lokuti umqomboti udityaniswe
ne brandy, atapuluke kulowo mvubo amatyala amaninzi.

U Sir G. Sprigg eyilihekise ebantwini lonteto

Umlanjeni yena ute abamnyama baya yitenga i brandy kodwa bangayixubi.
Nase zintonjaneni iko i brandy. Bangaba ngabase sikolweni abayenza
umxube.

U Mr. Isaac Juqashe ute, yena uhleli pakati kwa Maqaba, yinto ekoyo
ukuti amadodana ngokukodwa ayitenge i brandy ayidibanise no mqomboti
ezintonjaneni. Akakanyezi nto itetwa ngu Mr. Jenner, kuba u Mr. Jenner
engazi luto ngento ezihambiseka ngapandle.

U Mr. Jenner ubonise ukuba uba namaxesha asijikelayo isiqingata abone xa
kwenziwa umqomboti, ati ke alive ivumba lomqomboti, abengati alive xa
ilelo mxube we brandy nomqomboti. [U Juqashe: lowo mxube usuka ube
livumba elinye nomqomboti. (Kwahlekwa.)]

NGOKUPATELE KWIZIRORO.

U Sir G. Sprigg ubuze ukuba kungaba kuseko enye into abanga bangayi
bhekisa.

U Mr. Thomas Zwedala ungenise indawo yokuba ebone emapepeni ukuba u Sir
Gordon ebeke wanentlangano nezibonda zezinye indawo ngokunyuswa
kwentlaulo yezibonda ngu Rulumeni.

Ubhekise ku Mr. Jenner u Sir Gordon ngenani ukuba zinikwa ntonina, lite
lakuba lipendule elonene ngokuti ezikulu zifumana £12 ngonyaka,
ezincinane £6, wati u Sir Gordon, ewe ike yakankanywa zizibonda ezitile
e Xesi into leyo wati uyakuke ayikangele ayenzele elayo.

Kute kwakuba kungeko yimbi imi buzo u Sir Gordon, wati waneleke ukuba
Abatembu besosiqingata bangabantu abonwabileyo abangenaziroro
nabanelisiweyo.

U Mr. Thomas Zwedala ute: Pambi kwe Palamente egqitileyo bebenazo
iziroro, kodwa baneliswe kakuhle ngokuhlala kokugqibela kwe Palamente.

U Sir Gordon Sprigg: Lonto ibonisa into enifanelwe kuyenza yokuba
nitembe u Rulumeni. U Rulumeni yintloko yenu. (Kwadunywa.) Uqhube wati u
Sir Gordon: Ndivuyiswe kufika pakati kwenu ndifumane naneliseke
ngoluhlobo. Ninelizwe elihle kanye apa, kwanemantyi elungileyo.
Ninomnyaka omhle; kuza isivuno esihle, zonke izinto zezokuba nicaite.
Ukuba niyandipulapula ngomcimbi woselo bendinga cebisa mna ukuba,
“Luyekeni uselo.” Izwi lika Rulumeni lelokuba msani ukusela kanye.
Lomcimbi wokusela ungowenu kwapela. Ukuba kuko inkantini akunyanzelwe
ukuba uye kuyo. Ityala lokusela lelabo. Kakade ukuba bayasela iyakuba
yimbubho yabantu. Lonto ziqondeleni. Kudlulisele ukuba bayikangele
ngokwabo lento babuyeke kanye utywala, banga kangeli ku Rulumeni ne
Palamente ukubanceda entweni eyeyabo kwapela. (Kwadunywa). Yahlukene
intlanganiso.

SIVE SABONA.

Siva ukuba kuko ukungaqondi pakati komhlambi owamkela IMVO ZABANTSUNDU
ngokukodwa olunge e Batenjini, mayelana nokuhlaulelwa kwepepa ixesha
lonyaka. Kubonakala ukuba ukutumela imali nge Kwata kuyapazamisa—
kupazama kumtumeli mali zekude kupazame kuba bhali. Sekufuti sifumana
izicelo kwabatile becela ukuba bavunyelwe ukuwuhlaulela wonke unyaka
ngexesha elinye. Singa ke singati oko kuvumelekile, kodwa intlaulo ifike
ekuqalekeni kwawo unyaka. Lomgcana ke ngopendula izicelo ezininzi.

Mayelana nokulungiselela ukufika kwe Ruluneli e Bhai umzi wakona
unenteto ezimbini. Enye yeye siroro sowomlibo ka Xakalashe ati inteto
eyaleswayo yeye gumgedle lo Messrs. Balla no Wauchope, umzi awazani
nayo. Impendulo ka Mr. Wauchope ifikile, ifika itsho ngabuhlungu
ukububaza umoya wo Xakalashe nabo angaba uteta egameni labo. Ungaba umzi
ubungahlatyelwanga ntlanganiso ngokuti kungabiko xesha lakuyimema. Eyona
not inkulu ngoku kuba kuko nto na ingahambisekanga kakuhle
abayikalazelayo o Xakalashe, nabati ukuba ibiko intlanganiso ngebe
kudlulisele ngayo. Njengaba mgama e Bhai, masiti safumana udano mhla
sayamkela inteto ka Xakalashe eyayipefumla iziroro namangumngum ngayo
yonke into eyenziwe ngabamnyama base Bhai ukwa mkela Iruluneli. kuba
tina yabonakala, yavakala kanamanjazo, kamnandi yonke into eyenziwa
egameni labamnyama. Saziva sizingca ngomzi wokowetu wase Bhai kuba
yinteto yawo eyaroronya inteto yohubele yokuqala ngakuti bamnyama.
Nobeririza, ozolisayo ukucinga, njengokuba wahambiseka kakuhle nje
umsebenzi, ubefanel’ ukuti, “Ewe, ingaba ayenzekanga njengokuba bendinga
ngeyenzeke ngako yonke into yokulungiselela ukwamkelwa kwe Ruluneli
ngumzi ontsundu wase Bhai;

ingaba mhlaimbi andisalifumananga! Ituba lokuba isiqu negama lam
livakale malungana nomsebenzi omhle ozukileyo njengalo. Kodwa into
zakowetu o Wauchope no Bhalla zikufezile konke; umzi ziwutejize
ngobungcwalisa. Umsebenzi uhambisekile kuhle.”

Akuko nto ingapumelelayo ilindiselwe intlanganiso yabantu ukuba iyenze.
Izinto zonke ezinkulu zoncedo ungafumana zihambiseke ngenx’enomntu omnye
ote wanenkolo kulonto ayenzayo, ikomiti nentlanganiso zingenza into xa
zingunge zaxhasa lomntu mnye, xa ziqutywa ngamakwele nobushushu
bentloko—ncama kukufa kwayo nalonto ilungileyo. O Mr. Bhalla, Mr.
Wauchope nabebenabo, ibingamadoda okubulelwa, ataruziswe ngokuwumela
umzi apo ubungasakuba nakuzinceda ukuba kungazikatazi bona. Benze into
entle abangazikatazanga ngayo o Xakalashe, kanti udumo asilwabo lolo
Xakalashe abo. Siti konakele ntonina ke? Amadodana antliziyo zicacele
ukulusebenzela uhlanga atwele nzima kenina ngoku kuhlala kuko
imindwakela
engenamgudu waluto, ehleli ichopele ukuwatuka ngento ezingekoyo zokuba
asebenzela udumo lwawo. Seleza kusuka atinina umntu ukunceda umzi
wakowabo? Mayipele lento khaba: imbi!

Umntu oke wahambela kwelingase Lusutu, waba nokubona izimbo
zentlanganiso, nemibuliso yakona, akangez’ awulibala umtshazo wo Mbuliso
kumntu omkulu wentlanganiso zabamnyama beli. Siwudele kanye umbuliso
wempi yeli ngelixesha
ibijikela i Ruluneli. Lombuliso ka “Hule !” awunanto uyitetayo kwizizwe
ezimnyama; unjalonje mbi. Kungade kube kuhle uguzulwe emibulisweni yetu
Maxhosa Asikolwa ukuba lemihlambi ibisiti “ Hule! ” kwi Ruluneli
iyahlula ku “ Hurrah ! ” osuka. kuye lo Hule wom’buliso, ne “ Hule ”
ihenyukazi. Ngesi sizatu sodwa ngele
guzulwa u “Hule!” ekubuliseni, ubengateti nto kakade. U “ Bayete ! ”
wabase Mbo usadlulisele ku “ Hule ! ’’ Kanti kwezetu indlebe nentliziyo,
umbuliso wa Besutu awukabi nanto iwukhupayo. Ungowokucela itamsanqa ku
Nkulunkulu, waye unento oyintsonkotayo angevayo otetayo ngomxhelo
akwenzayo. E Lusutu kuqala umntu omnye ngokutyandyuluka esiti “ Imvula
mayine! ” Zize izizwe zonke zitelele ekutini: “Mayine!!”—imolula uyi ku
Mayine. Akuko nto imyoli ezindlebeni njengokupulapula lo Mbuliso. Imvula
kutiwa iza namatamsanqa. Xa ke icelelwa ukuba ine pezu komntu omkulu,
ekuhlanganeni nase kwahlukaneni kwentlanganiso, kucelwa ukuba awe
amatamsanqa pezu kwezonto. Sicela into enqabileyo na mabandla e “Hule”
xa siti malembelwe ingcwaba elinzulu i “Hule” kutatyatwe u “ Imvula
mayine ! ” wase Lusutu ?

Kwelipezulu e Batenjini utywala bomlungu babunike igama lokuba yi
“Kwepile.” ’Mbangi yalo kokukuti akubupuza umntu ubone ebute ntshwa
ubuso ngokomntu olume ibhumbulu le kwepile. Ke sati ngoku sibe sinyukile
sabona ngomfo wase Swatyatala (siva ukuba ayitanda kakulu I “Kwepile”
amadoda akona) selesilwa kwakutetwa nge kwepile. “Akuko not yenze yona
Ikwepile,” ivakele isitsho isilwa lemambane. Yaye ibibonakele ihexa
ixela I mast kwanini ehasheni. Samangaliswa tina besingekazi ukuba
yintonina evusa umnyele endodeni. Kanti kenoko kuko amadoda ase
Batenjini apikise u Mr. Hendrick Kalipa no Mr. T. Zwedala pambi ko Sir
Gordon Sprigg e Cacadu bakuti unxilo luya lubheka pambili e Batenjini!
Ngomhla we Ruluneli e Komani sayibona yanela imilomo enco yamadodana,
eyalata, nangapandle kokuba kude kutetwe, ukuba ayitya ayityele “i
kwepile,” kanjako abonakala nelokuhamba nokuzimka ekwele emahasheni,
nokuqononondisa ngento enye ngolwimi olushwabeneyo amadodana, ukuba
aselengamakoboka “ekwepile.” Site sakukhumbula ukuba kanene zixovula
kule ndibongo nje ezinzwana zabafo azisoza zincotuke, sazamla omde wona
umzamlo. Kuba ke madoda ike yangena indodana entsundu kobutywala
ayincotuki naxa selifuna, nokuba seyiposana nesonka—yinqaba u Noqokoto
umka Qantiba.

  THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 20, 1890.

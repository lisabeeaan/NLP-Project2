AMAMFENGU ASE NGQUSHWA.

Abalesi be Sigidimi bayakukumbula okokuba ku mqodi wemfuduko ya Mamfengu
ase Ngqushwa awaye siya pesheya kwe Nciba kwafika ilizwi elivela ku
Rulumente okokuba la Mamfengu angafudukeli pesheya kwe Nciba kodwa
ahlale apo e Ngqushwa. U Mr. Adv. Innes omele e Palamente isitili esine
Ngqushwa uyingene lendawo ngo July 8. Yiyo le ilandelayo ingxelo
yengxoxo :—

U MR. INNES ute makuvunyelwane ngendawo yokuba,—Luluvo lwe qumru elo
ukuba amamfengu atile ase Ngqushwa ashenxiselwe ezindaweni ezi
fanelekileyo ezi pesheya kwe Nciba; okokuba umhlaba ayakuwushiya e
Ngqushwa utengiswe ngoko mteto wemihlaba elinywayo nokuba kungayimbi
indlela elilungelo kuyo ingeniso yakwa Rulumente; nokokuba i Rulumente
iwabuze Amamfengu lawo okokuba ngumnqweno wawo na ukuwushiya lomhlaba
wase Ngqushwa aye kulowo umtsha, uwalungeleyo aya kuwunikwa. U Mr. Innes
ute yindawo eyayigqitywe kwakule Parliament nyakenye ebangele ukuba
makayingenise lendawo; eyayi yeyokuba ukushenxiswa kwaba Ntsundu
ezindaweni zabo makube yimvume ye Parliament. La Mamfengu akumipefumlo e
8,000, atyebile nge nkomo, selexinene kakulu into ebange ukutshabalala
okukulu kwenkomo, ngako oko ubuhlwempu babantu. Imantyi yeso sitili
inika ingxelo yokuba inxenye yabantu kuyakubonakala ukuba mayifumane
ukutya ku Rulumente, ukuba abapungulwa. Kungumnqweno wa Mamfengu
ngokwawo nama fama ukuba maba shenxiswe. Uyazi (u Mr. Innes) ukuba kuko
umhlaba ngase Mbashe ongena bantu, apo Amamfengu angasiwa kona; ngu
mhlaba abanganikwa wona, kungabiko bani onokuti bawupiwe ngesisa, ngenxa
yokuba kuyakuba kukwananiselana. Akungelungi kanye ukubeka amafama
amhlope e Mbashe. Eyona nto ilungileyo kukuba amafama amhlope abe pakati
kwabanye abamhlope kunokuba abe pesheya kwe Nciba. Kuko amadodana
afanelekileyo amhlope ase Ngqushwa angangenayo kulomhlaba wase Ngqushwa
ngokuwutenga nokuba nge ngqesho

u MR. JOHNSON (umnyulwa wase Dikeni) ute uyavuya ukubona umhlobo wake
etetelela aba ntsundu. Amamfengu angaba asiwa elizweni ayakuba
nempumelelo kulo enga pezulu, into ke engabonisa iramente ngeremente
okokuba le Rulumente ayibacasili abantsundu. Amafama akana salelo
ekuweleni kwa Mamfengu.

u MR. J. A. DE WET (Umpatiswa we Micimbi Yabantsundu) ute lomcimbi wa
Mamfengu uqutywe kakuhle ngu Mr. Innes, nento ayitetayo iyaxaswa
zingxelo zezipata mandla zalapo. Kuyinyaniso ukuba ababantu ba bujelwe
yimpahla, kodwa akanakuti kuko intsizi ezimhlope pakati kwabo. Kutiwa
kungabako ingozi ekuhlanganiseni amaqela amakulu ama Mfengu pesheya kwe
Nciba, ne Parliament ya nyakenye yayinoluvo lokuba akulungile
ukushenxisa amaqela amakulu abamnyama. Ama Mfengu ase Ngqushwa anga
17,000 malixele ke i qumra elo ukuba kulungile sinina ukuba asuswe.

u MR. SAUER ufune ukwazi eyona nto itetwa yi Rulumente.

U MR. DE WET ute akanakuxela okokuba luyintonina uluvo lwe Rulumente.

U MR. SAUER ute makaxele kaloku ufanele ukwazi.

U MR. JOUBERT ute, xa kwaku xoxwa ngomda wela ba Tembu kwatiwa akuseko
ndawo pesheya kwe Nciba yokusa aba Ntsundu. Emva koko beva ngokuya kwa
ba Ntsundu pesheya kwe Nciba. Akasiboni isizatu sokuba ama Mfengu
afumane umhlaba ngapandle kwokuwutenga. Nokoke uyavuya ukuba bebonisa
ilizwe lonke okokuba banonelele amalungelo aba Ntsundu ngapezu
kokunonelela awaba mhlope

U MR. UPINGTON ute maze lilumke i qumru linga fumane livumelane no Mr.
Innes. Nguwupina umhlaba welo lungu libekekileyo? Kukuba abobantu
baxinene na? Akuko sizatu sokuba abantu mabanikwe umhlaba ngenxa yokuba
besezintsizini nangenxa yokubalela kwe langa. Ilungu elibekekileyo (u
Mr. Innes) liteta ngamadodana amhlope aya emkosini ngokwenjenjalo lifuna
ukulandelwa sisi qendu selo qumru, kodwa yena u Mr. Upington akasiboni
isizatu sokuba makususwe aba Ntsundu ngenxa yalo madodana. Ngokumalunga
nolaulo uyicasile kanye into yokuhlanganisa abamnyama ndaweni nye.
Ucinga okokuba makuxutywe, ibe ngabamhlope nabamnyama. Ukuba abantu
baxinene kwezondawo maba hambe bayokuzibonela indlela zokupumelela.

u MR. M. J. LOUW ute ukuba ama Mfengu axinene makayokufuna imisebenzi.
Kuyingozi ukuba abantu abamnyama bahlanganiswe ndaweni nye apo
kuyakulaula ubumnyama

u MR. VAN RENSBURG, ute amalungu abekekileyo ateta ngo ku dibanisa
abamhlope nabamnyama awazi ukuba sikulu kwesiqalekiso sezi Locations kwi
fama. Kukudibanisa ezi locations nefama zabamhlope ekuvele obubusela
nobubadubadu obu sisibetu kumafama angase Maxoseni. Kulunge kanye ukuba
abamhlope babe cala nye.

U MR. DOUGLAS ute u Mr. Upington, akayiyo fama okanye ange cencelezi
ukuteta ngokuxutywa kwaba mnyama nabamhlope. Omhlope ohleli pakati kwaba
Ntsundu uti ukuba akabuyeki ubufama, kubonakale epangiwe wasala ze kuyo
yonke intwana yake. Akuko ndawo yokuba kwoyikwe into engavela pesheya
kwe Nciba, inkatazo zabo zikwa lapa e Koloni. I Rulumeni ababenayo
kuxesha elidlulileyo yenza konke enako ngoku ngqwabaza ama Mfengu, kodwa
nokupangwa kwe mimpu akuwadalanga injubaqa.

U MR. VENTER ute aba yimpopoma kuma Mfengu nge be setyenziswa.
Bangabazisa e Kapa (Cape Town) okokuba babuye batabate lamatafa ase
Kapa, lento ingabanika inkululeko (civilization).

U MR. FROST ute ezizizwe sezi sendiselana kangaka ukuba kungabiko loyiko
Iwenkatazo ezingabangwa kukucukucezana kwezintlanga, kodwa ucinga ukuba
yinto elungileyo ukwahlula abantsundu kwabamhlope. Bayenaniselana
imihlaba; akunje ngokuyeka bemke abanye ngenxa yingxinano, ukuze bagcine
lomhlaba ushiyiweyo.

u MR. DYER ute ama Mfengu kusoloko ahlalelana ngoxolo no Rulumente,
sisiqelo sawo ukuba akangele ukuyifumana intlalo yawo ku Rulumente
ngalumbi uhlobo kwaba mhlope.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA JULY 18, 1884

U MR. BRABANT ubuze ukuba olufuduko lwaba Ntsundu luyakupela ninina.
Ipesheya kwe Nciba lidamu eseli zakupupuma, liyaku qekeka. Ukuba ama
Mfengu ayawulima kakuhle umhlaba ungawaxasa. Eyona nto ilungileyo kanye
kukuba ipesheya kwe Nciba limiwe ngaba mhlope baxutywe nabantsundu,
ngolohlobo ke zingade zipele imfazwe.

U MR. MERRIMAN ute uyicasile kanye into yokuxuba, yeyona mbangi
yemfazwe. Nala mafama ase Monti atanda ilizwe lawo, ngawo kanye la
angenise inkatazo ngokugalela abamnyama ezifameni zawo. Onke amafama
ngamanye eye kubekwa pesheya kwe Nciba angafuna amapolisa alishumi
okuwagcina; pezu koko kungebiwa, ukuba ke kubanga imfazwe.
Abanakubalahla abuntsundu, kuyabangena ukuba babafunele umhlaba, kulunge
ke ukuba babase pesheya kwe Nciba. Yinkolo yake ukuba into ebungozi kubo
mayibe nxamnye, uyavumelana no Mr. Innes ngayo yonke intliziyo yake.
Abantsundu mabasiwe pesheya kwe Nciba zekupele yonke imvilikitshane
yokuba mabamelwe e Parliament. Abantsundu mabapatwe ngamandla kodwa
ngobubele. Yinkolo yake ukuba abantsundu mabapatwe njengaba Ntsundu
okwexesha elide elizayo. Unga kungebiko ba Ntsundu e Koloni ngapandle
kwabazicaka zamafama, nabanemihlaba yabo ngokwabo, behleli njengabemi
pantsi kwemiteto yabo. Ukuba aba Ntsundu pesheya kwe Nciba banqumlana
imiqala, abamhlope mabangapuli intliziyo zabo ngenxa yalonto. Akunje
ngokubulawa amhlope amafamama nokutshiswa kwezindlu zawo.

u MR. BARRY ute umhlaba wapesheya kwe Nciba maunikwe amafama amhlope aba
Ntsundu aba basetyenziswe.

u MR. INNES ependula ute, ngapandle kokuba ama Mfengu afuduke onke nako
konke okwawo akayi kuvumelana nokuba kufuduswe inxenye. Uyazincoma
okokuba ebeyimbangi yokuba i Rulumente ivakalise okokuqala ulaulo Iwayo
oluqinisekileyo abaluvileyo ivuliwe nje lentlangano. Yimfeketo into
yokuba inene eliyintloko ye Rulumente limxulube pezu kwendawana enye
njengokungati umfumene ematyaleni. Ingxoxo yake (u Mr. Innes) kulo
ntlangano uyiseke pezu kwenyaniso zalomcimbi. Lilipina ituba
anganempumelelo ngalo amafama amhlope pesheya kwe Nciba? Sisicelo sama
Mfengu ngapezu kwamafama amhlope okokuba ayingenise lendawo. Ukuba aba
bantu abasiwa pesheya kwe Nciba kulamhlaba ungena bantu uya kungenwa
ngabanye aba Ntsundu abavela kwezinye indawo pesheya kwe. Nciba,
bawuzalise ungafunyanwa mntu ke.

INGXOXO ZE PARLAMENTE.

U RILI.

U MR. BLAINE ubuze ukuba kuko ngxoxo ikoyona pakati kwo Rulumente no
Rili, inkosi yama Gcaleka eselibadubadu lelizwe, yokuba afunelwe indawo
epilileyo yentlalo abe neqelana lamapakati ake kulo ndawo, abu
pelelisele kuyo ubomi bake ngoxolo.

UMPATI MICIMBI YABA NTSUNDU U Mr. Ayliff—ute kuyabonakala ku Rulumente
ukuba akana sizinzo ama Gcaleka ngenxa yesi simo ikuso inkosi yawo, esi
sokuba semahlatini. Uyavuma u Rulumente ukumbeka kwindawo etile kwase
Koloni apa mgama kwisizwe sake, uza kwazisa Imantyi ezi pesheya kwe
Nciba ukuba zibe nokuhlangana naye ngenteto.

U MR. BLAINE ute uvuyile ukuba u Rulumente atabate indlela yobulumko.
Ama Gcaleka ngoku azi ntsali ezite sa kwizizwe ezi pesheya kwe Nciba
kude kuye e Natal ezizicenge zokwenza izi piti-piti. Ukuba inkosi yawo
ibinokuxolelana no Rulumente, ebeya kuba njalo namapakati. Akangetandi
ukuba abuyiselwe kwase nxoweni lake, ute akuva inteto ye Mantyi yama
Bomvana eti wenza ngako konke u Rili ukwalela abantu bake ukuba bangalwi
kule mfazwe isand’ ukubako, waye esenjenjalo efuna ukuze azuziswe indawo
yokonwaba emihleni yobudala bake apelelwe yiyo ese konwabeni. Kanjalo u
Capt Blyth no Mr. Streat­field bancoma ukuzipata kakuhle kwama Gcaleka
abepetwe ngu Rili, enga angafumana isikundla sokonwaba angabi zintsali.
Utsho wati uyatemba ukuba uyakufunelwa kwa kamsinya ikaya nendlela
yokuxaswa kwake.

UMPATISWA WE MICIMBI YABA NTSUNDU ute, akuko nkosi inyanyekelwe
ngokulula ngu Rulumente nje ngo Rili nakuba engasayi kumbandezela. Kwase
ntlalweni yawo ama Gcaleka akazanke anabuhlobo nale Koloni, beku ngaba
zibele zika Rulumente ukuba enzelwe uluto, engafanelwe zizo.

I COLONIAL SECRETARY, U Mr. Sprigg, ute, bekuko kule minyaka mibini,
mhlaumbi mitatu indawo eza veliswayo ku Rulumente ngo Rili, zab'anga.
Kwezi nyanga zimbalwa zidluleyo yabonisa Imantyi etile ukulunga okungaba
koyo ete kwabekwa u Rili kwase nxoweni lake, kuloko ayivunywanga
londlela.—Kusandul’ ukuvakala ukuba u Rdi angazi aikela ukuba unikwe
isinyaniso sokuba akasayi kusiwa esiqitini (Robben Island). Uyavuma u
Rulumente ukusinika eso sinyaniso. Utsho wati uvumelana nabo bati nge
efunelwa indawo yentlalo u Rili, angabekwa kwindimbane yabantu bake,
angasondezwa enxoweni lake.

U MR. SCANLEN olilungu lase Cradock ubuze ukuba akuko saziso sokuba uya
kuvuzwana umntu obambe u Rili Ukuba siko besi fanele ukupeliswa.

I COLONIAL SECRETARY ite akazi ukuba saguqulwa sacinywa, uyakuke
abuzise. Ukuba asika guqulwa soguqulwa sicinywe. Ute kanjalo wayete
ukupendula umbuzo ka Mr. Blaine olilungu lase Monti ngoku zinikela kuka
Rili, wati kuko amabanga enziweyo ngu Rulumente mayela nokuzinikela
kwalo nkosi. Kufike incwadi evela kwi Mantyi

enkulu yapesheya kwe Nciba u Capt. Blyth, isiti u Mr. Streatfield,
imantyi yeso sitili yayicelwe ngu Rili ukuba bake bahlangane bobabini.
Behlangene ke ute u Rili uyavuma ukuzinikela ukuba akasayi kutunyelwa e
Kapa mhlayimbi esiqitini (Robben Island) ngu Rulumente. Ipendulwe ke
loncwadi ngu Rulumente kwatiwa u Mr. Streatfield makamxelele ukuba
iyavunywa ngu Rulumente lonto ayitetayo. Ukuba uyazinikela u Rili
uyakubekwa ngu Rulumente kwi nxowa lika Jali. Utsho wati uyatemba ukuba
yoqondwa ngabendlu yamatyala inyaniso yendawo yokuba ukuzinikela kwe
nkosi enkulu ngapezu kwezinye ebine gunya elikulu pezu kwezizwe zaba
Ntsundu ezise South Africa, ingavelisa uxolo nokonwaba kubo bonke
abantsundu abahleli kwezo ndawo zase South Africa.

Kuvakala ukuba ide yafika nempendulo ka Rili. Uti yena ufuna ukubuyela
ezweni lake kwa Gcaleka, ebukosini abe pakati kwabantu bake.

U MR. THEAL.

Kwi PARLAMENTE edluleyo kwabonwa u Mr. Theal ukuwufanela lomsebenzi woku
gcina incwadi zayo, oko kukuti i “Librarian” ye Parlamente. Kongezelelwa
kuwo lo wokuba ngumkangeli nomcedululi wama pepa amabali amadala ale
Koloni.

Kekaloku ngesizatu esingabonakaliyo, intloko ye Rulumente yamkupa kuwo,
yafaka ndoda yimbi egama lingu Rev. H. V. C. Leibbrandt, ebi fudula
ingumfundisi wasema Bulwini, e Graaff Reinet. Ute u Mr. Theal
akuyikalazela londawo, waxoliswa ngeliti lomsebenzi sikunyusela kuwo,
libanga eliya kukukokelela kongapezulu. Ufakwe esikundleni sika Mr. R.
J. Dick, obe yi mantyi yaba mnyama e Qonce.

Ixoxwe kakulu lendawo kule Parlamente, zabizwa zonke incwadi ezibe
ziteta lonto pakati ko Rulumente no Mr. Theal, ekute ngokuvela kwazo,
zayikanyela indawo ebitetwa ngu Mr. Sprigg, yokungati ngu Mr. Theal
okete ubumantyi kunalo msebenzi.

u MR. BRIGHT.

Kubeko kwa ingxoxo enkulu ngokukutshwa kuka Mr. Bright, ekubeni ngu
mncedisi Wompatiswa Wemicimbi yaba Ntsundu (Under Secretary) e Kapa,
ukuze kufakwe u Mr. Innes, lo ubeyi mantyi yase Qonce. Zite izizatu
ezirolwe ngu Mr. Sprigg azawanelisa onke amatyala. Kude kwavakala
nezigxeko zokuti lendoda ikutshiweyo yenzelwe iqinga lokuba
kulungiselelwe umzalwana ekuzekwe ntombi zamntu mnye, kuba nanku u Mr.
Innes enyuselwe kwatiwa wozuza i £900 ngomnyaka, wabe u Mr. Bright yena
yonke leminyaka ebefumana £600. Simbi isizatu ekutiwe sesona sibange
ukuba akutshwe sesokuba umsebenzi ubungapezu kwamandla ake, kuba ngumntu
ongomeleleyo.

UKUNGATENJWA KWE RULUMENTE.

LENTETO ilandelayo yeka Mr. Scanlen ilungu lase Cradock ngoyintloko yale
Rulumente ingapandle. Oko kukuti ingati ipumile le Rulumente ka Sprigg,
ibe nguye oyakuba sendaweni yake kunye namanye amalungu amane, babe
ngamadoda amahlanu bebonke, abaya kuba ngu Rulumente.

U MR. SCANLEN ke ubeke pambi kwe Parlamente ingxelo esisimburu equka
yonke imisebenzi ye Rulumente engengamteto ukuba le Rulumente ka Mr.
Sprigg ayifanele kulaula le Koloni. Eso simburu sengxelo sesi:— “ Ukuba
lendlu ye Parlamente inoluvo lokuba izenzo zale Rulumente yangoku
ngokukodwa malunga nelizwe lase Lusutu nela pesheya kwe Nciba zibe
luhlobo olubange ukuba konakale awona malungelo makulu elilizwe.”

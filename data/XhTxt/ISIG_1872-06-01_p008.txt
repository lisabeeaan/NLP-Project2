IZINTO NGEZINTO.

UBOYA.—Kuko amare okuba E-England ixa- biso loboya lihle ngetiki
epontini.

OWENZAKELEYO. — U-Mr. MacDonald wase Queenstown, uwe nenqwelana yake
yamahashe, wapuka imbambo zantatu.

ABEMKE NOMLAMBO.—Amadoda amabini antsu- ndu amke ne Ncwenxa xa awelayo.

ICESINE libuye lavuka ngokunamandla kwele Diamondi. Baliqela ababulewe
lilo.

IDOLOSI ikulile E-Kareiga. Omnye umfo upu- lukwe zinkabi ezi 50 ngenxa
yayo.

IMVUIA.—Lemvula ibiko ngo May, ayinganga- mvula yaka yana ngenyanga
enye, kuleminyaka mitandatu idluleyo.

KWELE DIAMONDI iziqwenga ezitatu zomhlaba ekumbiwa kuwo, zitengiswe
ngeponti ezi £3050.

KWINTENGO YENKOMO ezi 170 E-Rini, imali ebirolwa ngenkomo, ibisusela kwi
£5 10s., kude kube kwi £9 7s.

OBEHILIZILE.—E-Richmond U-Isaac Hende- reck, ugwetywe ukuba abe veki
mbini etolongwe- ni, ezuza ukudla okuncinane, ngenxa yokusuke ahilize
ngexa lomsebenzi.

INTENGO.—Ngomhla we 12 ka June kuya ku- tengiswa ngase Monti, igusha ezi
200, imazi zenkomo ezi 50, inkabi ezi 24. inkunzi ezi 4, igusha ezi 10,
nehangu ezi 15.

UKUHLUMA PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.—Siva uku- ba abantsundu bomlambo otile
pesheya kwe Nciba batengise ngamabali oboya ali 150, baza batenga
ipuluwa ezi 250. Lendawo ibonisa ukuba bayahluma.

OBULEWE YIMASHINI.—E-Bedford umfana aba- ti ngu Maritz, ute ngengozi
umlenze wake wati gangxa pakati kwevili ezimbini zemashini yoku- bula,
watyumka wada weza kumisa ngetanga, kwasa eqauka.

ISIKOLO SASE BANGWATO esibe sike sise sika Sekomo, sicitwe nga Matebele,
ngezingxabano zimana ukubako pakati kwawo zokubanga ubukosi.

ILISHWA ELIKULU.—E-Monti imikombe emi- hlanu iqekezwe kukulwa kolwandle.
Omnye walomikombe okutiwa yi Qwawza ubu namabali oboya ali 1300; ixabiso
labo lizi £25,000.

UKUCEBA IGUSHA.—Abatengi boboya E-Bayi, batumele ipepa kubafuyi begusha
balommandla, lokuba igusha ngezi ngacetywa kabini ngomnyaka, kuba uboya
buba bufutshane, bungazuzi ntengo intle.

OBELINGA UKUZIBULALA.—U-Charley Timber Umxosa ongu mbanjwa E-Bayi,
uzipose pambi kwomlenze wenqwelana yokututa amatye kwada kwakabini,
efuna ukuzibulala.

UBUSELA—Endlwini ye posi kwele Diamondi, kubiwe inxowa ezibe zine
diamondi nezinye izinto ebe ziza kutunyelwa E-Kapa. Ixabiso lazo beli-
zi £50,000. Kutiwa umntu oyakuxela into enga- banga ukuba afunyanwe
lomasela wonikwa £300.

KWELA MAMPONDO— Amadoda ayetunyelwe ukuba aye kulungisa izinto kwela
Mampondo ngoku abuyile. Azishiye izinto zise ngxekengxe- ke, kungeka
qondakali kakuhle ukuhlalelana kwazo. Akabanga nakuyifeza kakuhle eminye
imida yezizwe zakona. Kodw a noko kubonakala ukuba zoke zizole
okwangoku.

ISITORE sika Mr. Wood’s wase Rini sitshi- swe ngumlilo. Indleko ehle
ngenxa yoko zipo- nti ezi kwi £15,000 ne £20,000.

ABABUBILEYO.

E-Ngqura ngomhla wa 21 ka April, kubube umntwana ka Andrijan.

E-Ngqu’ra ngomhla wokuqala ka May, kubube- umntwana ka Orloro.

E-Bobotyana, ngomhla we 12 ka May, kubube U-September umfo ka Nakani,
Umgqunukwebe.. Umfazi wake umshiye esifa, engenayo nento yoku- mondla.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1883. 7

IMFENE ENDUNA.

  Grahamstown, 12 July, 1883.

KADE ndinyamezele andisena kunceda, abantwana babantu bayapela
abangamankazana awendele kula madoda abo angamanxila. Ungabona indoda
isebenza kwa Mlungu ifumana iponti nge veki, umhlaimbi ino mntwana mnye,
umfazi mhlaimbi uhlamba ingubo zo Mlungu ufumana isheleni enye (for
example) uza kuyitata aye kutengela lomntwana ukutya, liti ke eli xelegu
lingumyeni wale ntokazi lakuyi fumana le ponti liye kusela liyigqibe mu
landule ukugoduka. Lakufika selsilwa liti malipiwa esasonkana somntwana
lilambile. Kambe namapango amgqibile umduna. Akuti umfazi lontwana yeyo
mntwana, hai ndiya botolosha mna andikatali lilo mna elo ntyontywana
lako, atsho selebeta, ati umfazi ngenxa yokoyika intonga selesika. Uza
kumbona umfazi ehleli ecaleni kwendlu esingete umntwana elila ezo
nyembezi. Ke manxila ziza kubuzwa kuni nisukenje nazizinto ezite ti
indlebebe nempumlo nemibilini butywala, abantwana babantu nifeketa ngabo
nje niza kufunyanwa ngu Somandla. Ukuti inxila kukuti imfene enduna.

  STEPHEN LOUW.

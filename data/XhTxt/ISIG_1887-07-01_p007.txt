ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1887. 55

UKUNIKA ICEBO.

Ku MHLELI WE-Sigidimi Samaxosa, Lovedale:-- KUSE kulixesha elitile
ningavumi ukuhlangana ngabuhlobo ne Mvo Zabantsundu ati omnye akusingisa
izwi elibi komnye, omnye apendule ngilibi naye, nditsho ngempendulwano
zenu malungana nesikula. sase Ncemera. Mna emxelweni wam ndilindele
ikakulu exegwini elinguwe ukuba ukolise ukupendula inyaniso yodwa,
ungakolisi ukulandelisa mazwi afana nasingiswe kuwo umane ululeka,
uyala, umfana ngamazwi oxolo angadubuliyo, kuba wena kuhleli kufuneka
ukuba unguyise no mfundisi wetu, ekuya kuti nangokuvala umlomo maxa
wambi nje ngomfo ka Mzimba, kanti lonto iyateta, kuba lonke ilizwe
libeke indlebe. Woti mhla upendula gadalala likuvele umhlaumbi
likubuzele. Gcina indawo yako xego lakowetu Sigidimi ngabulali.

Regu, Middledrift., 25 July, 1887. J. MASINGATA.

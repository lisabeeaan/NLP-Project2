ISIGLDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 16, 1881.

IDABI E DE AAR.

Liti ipepa lendaba lase Kapa:—“ Sibaliselwe ingxelo eyaneleyo
nenyanisekileyo ngo Mgqibelo ukumka kwemini, kwinene elinduluke e De Aar
ngedabi ebeliko kwimini ezimbalwa ezidlulileyo. Kule ngxelo kubonakala
ukuba abantu abantsundu abasebenza indlela ka Loliwe wase Ntshonalanga,
ikakulu ngama Zulu; babe abo besebenza indlela kaloliwe esuka koma Bayi
isinge pezulu koma Naauwpoort, ingabantu abantsundu bale Koloni, ikakulu
ingama Mfengu. Indawo ekuxatyenwe ngayo yile, kuba- ngwa inkanti ka
Friedlander, esentlangano apo yendlela zika loliwe ezimbini, epuma e
Kapa nepuma e Bayi. Bati abanye kwabanye musani ukusela kule nkanti
yeyetu.

Kute ke ngomhla we Krismesi, kuba ingumhla ekunga- setyenzwayo ngawo,
kwadityanwa kule nkanti kwaliwa ngenduku, ngamazembe nezikali ezimbalwa
; abanye bexu- luba ngebotile. Kwabonakala ukuba bebengazilungiselele
dabi—ama Mfengu etna 40, ama Zulu eli 150. Lapela elo dabi bebaninzi
abanenduma.

Kute ngolwe Sine kwakulo veki ye Krismesi xa ama Zulu asebenzayo kwegqi
endulaneni pezu kwawo ama Mfengu ema 250 exobile, aselelahla umsebenzi
ama Zulu eya kuxoba. Ama Mfengu afunzele ekampini yama Zulu,
aseleyifunza eyawo ama Zulu, anga ayabaleka ama Mfengu kanti ayarola
kupela, ate akufika kwindawo etile ati guqu. Kwasentloko kuwe inkosana
kuma Zulu ebipambili, agxotwa ama Zulu kufe amadoda amatatu, kufe mnye
kuma Mfengu. Inxwelera ekungeko temba lokupila kumacala omabini zi 8,
abenzakele kuhle be 20.

Kudlule olwesi Hlanu, kwati ngo Mgqibelo anxama ama Zulu, ngokukodwa
ngenxa yenkosana yawo efileyo, aye efuna ukupindezela, ayegxotwe ngolwe
Sine apangwa nempahla zawo ekampini. Amema kakulu omabini lamacala. Ama
Zulu acele u Mr. Oke inkosi ye Engine ukuba awaboleke i Engine
kuputunywe amawawo akwi zitishi zika loliwe ezikude ukuba baze
kubanceda, walile u Mr. Oke, wati mabayiyeke lento, abavumanga. Idibene
uku- jika kwelanga, aye ama Zulu enga 500, nabanye bekwanga- ko. Ama
Zulu abuye agxotwa. Kutiwa kufe ngale mini ama 70 kuqukwa amacala
omabini. Lento ke ezo kula- nyulwa ngawakwa Nonibe esuka e Kapa. Kute
noko bekuvakala ukungati um Zulu ne Mfengu basameme abayeka, kwakufika
owakwa Rulumente evela e Kapa ene kanunu akubuyanga kubeko dabi limbi.
Ama Mfengu ayepetwe nawo yinkosana ikwele ebasheni xa kuliwayo. Kube
lusizi kuti ukuba kupalale igazi elingapezulu kwe 100 ngelize, kubangwa
inkanti—akubangwa mhlaba, nazi- nkomo kona besingade siti kuko
isizatwana semfazwe.

UKUHLUMA KWAMA MFENGU.

Umntu osuka apa e Koloni esinga kwelasema Xosen
imihla yonke uyakuhamba efumana ubungqina benteto et.
“ I Mfengu ayicolwa.” Kunqabile ukubona ukuba ayikabi¹
mininzi iminyaka ayecitakele ngayo ama Mfengu, lazai
igama lawo laba ligama lokuhlekisa nokutukana. Umhambi
namhla ufika enelizwe elibanzi elihle elibizwa ngawo ;
kanti kunjalo nje akuko mlambo uya kufika ungena Mfe-
ngu. Nangokungapezu koko kwindawo zonke akoyo kuzo
uyakufika ingawona angabantu ngemfuyo ; ufike esiti
nokuba akanazinkomo zininzi, kube lapo akoyo amazimba.
Ungancama ngendlala ezinkulu ezinje ngale ka Ncayecibi,
namhla ilanga lingaba belenze iminyaka emibini libalela.
Lomini kunjalo yile mini uya kufika abanene betengisa
kukubi : kuseko izisele ezingekapatwa! Nokuba uyifu-
mana inye pakati kwesizwe uya kudla ngokufumana kuku-
lapo bapila kona abantu balo mlambo. Kukolise ukuba
njalo nangecala lezikolo. Kweli lapesheya kwe Nciba
asazi ndawo zite kuyo ukuxinana izindlu zezikolo zafana
nelase Mamfengwini ; asazi ndawo bakutshwayo kuyo
abantwana ngapezu kwakona, ngokukodwa kona ukuba
kuko ititshere epilileyo. Siyakumbula ngenye imini
simangaliswa yimirozo yabantwana eyayisinga esikolweni,
bevela kwindawo ezikude, esati sakubuza ku Titshele wa-
xela ukuba benjenjalo yonke imihla.

Kekaloku nangezindlu nakuba esaka inqukuva, wona
akolisile ukupuma konqupantsi ; aye amanqugwala aka
wona ekolise ukubukeka ngapandle, acokiseke ngapakati.
Abantu abangapambi kwawo ngezindlu, ngokukodwa malu-
nga nezikolo, ngaba Tembu baka Tand’uhlanga abati abe

Lungu ngu Mr. Levey. Ngokufutshane umhambi ufike abone ukuba ama Mfengu
afumene ubuntu, akwindawo yokuba angabi nantloni ngegama lawo.

Kekaloku imbangi yoku yintonina ? Ukupendula tina singati ukunyuka
kwabantu asizange sikuve kubangwa nto nye. Noku kwama Mfengu, elibakala
linye alitabatileyo, ayinye into elibangeleyo. Esinye isizatu
esiwabangileyo kukucitakala. Zite imbandezelo akade eziva nesizatu
sokuba efike apa engasenanto azibambe ngobuhlanga, anamatele kuzo nokuba
na zikohlakele—siti kute kuba efike apa engasenalo iratshi lobunantsi,
aba nako ngoko ukuzamkela msinya izinto alike zise Mlungwini, ngokukodwa
ilizwi lika Tixo.

Indawo ke noko ezizona sifuna ukubandezela kuzo esite tina bahambi
sakuzikangela safunela ekutini ancedwe zizo kukusebenza nokugcina.
Ukusebenza kwawo siyakubona nati ukuba kudelekile xa kutelekiswa
nokwabamhlope. Kodwa ukuba ukutelekisa nokwabanye abantu abantsundu
ufika ubone ukuba endlwini apa akuko uhleliyo, kubu Jelimanira.
Ngamaxesha anjengawoku hlakula, ungafika emini ngobushushu obukulu,
umntu wakona eya kupumlela ekubeni, avuke kusasa abuye ngokuhlwa, ufike
engati uyabambezeleka nakukudla. Ngamaxesha anjalo akuze kutandeke
nokuba ubakangelise kakulu abantu basema Mfe- ngwini.

Eyesibini indawo encedisana noku kusebenza kukugcina into eqwetyiweyo.
Okukugcina ngokukodwa kuncomeka kumakcsikazi. Kulapo amandla akoyo, kuba
nokuba umntu uyazuza kakulu akumncedi nto xa angakwaziyo ukugcina, ecita
eman’ukutembela kwinto asaza kuyiqweba. Inkoliso yetitshele ibantu
bangati abananto nje kukunga- kwazi ukugcina into engenisiweyo.

Kuko mbuzo ke emasiti xa siseluhambeni siman’ukuzi- buza futi ngawo,
owokuba elikaba linyukayo liza kusinga pambili liza kubuya ngomva sinina
? Asikabi nako uku- wupendula nanamhla lombuzo. Onako anganceda; tina
sisafumane sanoloyiko kodwa. Indawo yona ecacileyo yeyokuba ukuba ikaba
liyakucekisa ukusebenza ngezandla, ukuba uti umntu ongenayo nebokwe ange
angavata njenga- banumzana, ukuba baya kungenwa liratshi legama kungaba
konakele. Kungaba kugqibelele ukonakala ukuba umti- njana uya kulahlekwa
kukugcina konina. Noko ke lo- mbuzo siwushiyela kwabanye ukuba
bawupendule ; owoku- ba ulutsha lusingisa pina lona ?

UMDLALO WEBOLA.

PAKATI KWABA NTSUNDU BASE BAYI NABASE RIXI.

  Nge 29 Dec., 1883, ngokuhlwa kwaba bupitipiti e Railway
  Station ngamadodana ase Bayi awaye esiya kudlala ibola
  nawase Rini nge 31 Dec., 1883. Ngo 11 a.m. ngosuku
  lokudlala yahlangana i Port Elizabeth “ Fear Not C. C.’
  versus Grahams Town “ Try Again C. C.” kwi City Lords—
  umhlaba omhle kunene wabe Lungu, owawuqeshwe licala lase
  Rini nge ponti enesheleni kunye ne mattings zawo okwalo
  mini. Kwakukangeleka kakuhle, noko lamacala avumelana
  ukuba lungandlalwa olukuko njengokuba lungasuke lupitize-
  lise amehlo amadoda, ze iti ukuza kwebola zinge ziliqela. I
  Bayi latunyelwa pakati, lamelana nebola zika Smith no
  Hall, ezafumana ikatsi ete tsha ngokukodwa ku Klaas no
  Williams. U Klaas wayifumana i 11 yake ngezihlandlu ezi-
  mbalwa awamana ukuyitsho ngapaya kotango. U Williams
  isibozo sake wasifumana nge style esihle kunene. Yapela i
  1st Inning yase Bayi nge 55. Langena i Rini okokuqala seli-
  jongwe kakade zi bowlers zase Bayi o Christian no Makwena.
  Abokuqala u Smith no Alexander bake baxatisa, batembisa
  ukunika inkatazo singahambi kodwa sona i score. Bahlulwa
  yi catch yobugcisa ngu Foley kwi leg, yekake kwanga kupuma
  kwamazimba engxoweni kwabonakala ukuba ibola zo Makwena
  no Christian ziyingozi netyefu engenayeza kwicala lase Rini.
  U Christian wada watabata i wicket zantatu zilandelelana nge
  over enye. Labonakala ne Bayi lidakumba ngulo “ Foyiya-
  foco ” wekaba lase Rini, kwada kwagaleleka u Peters ote
  esafika wazilungiselela ukurazula wati ngexeshana elifutsbane
  wahlanganisa i 14 ' not out ’ efumene enye ye 6, kwaye ku-
  ngasancedi nto kuba amawabo abesese mancwabeni. Lapela
  i Rini nge 29 ngapantsi kwe 1/2 yase Bayi ekufuneke libuye
  langena i Rini, laselicela ukuba i Bayi libuye lingene ukuquba

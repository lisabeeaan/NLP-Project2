IMBAMBANO YASE QUNQU.

Sezimbini inyanga bebambene abantu base Ndwe nama Bhulu ngomhlaba
abagxotwayo kuwo. Kwati ukuvela kwala malahle ase Ndwe o Messrs.
Brad­field, Weir no Byrne u Rulumente wasika umhlaba obanzi ozimokolo
ezi 25,000 ewenzela uloliwe, esiti boti ukuba abanini malahle aba
sibakankanyileyo bembe uloliwe osuka kona oya e Komani bose bewunikwa
lomhlaba ube semandleni abo. Lomhlaba unemizi emininzi emiwe nga Batembu
kuba ngumhlaba wabo kade badalelwa kuwo, bapile nguwo. Elixa abanini
malahle bengekamenzi

uloliwe u Rulumente ute uza kumana ewuqeshisa ngomnyaka lomhlaba.
Okunene ke ngo 1886 walilelwa yintsimbi ye ngqeshiso, kwati kuba
ababantu basebenza lamalahle ngaba bemi kulomhlaba, balima lomhlaba,
njengokuba bebewulima kade basuke o Messrs. Bradfield, Weir no Byrne
basebewuqesha ku Rulumente baza bona babiza imali emalunga ne ponti
ngomzi kwaba bantu. Ngo 1887 babuye bawuqesha kwangolo- hlobo. Kute
ngomhla we 3 October lo ugqitileyo wabuya walilelwa yintsimbi, namhla
abawuqesha o Messrs. Bradfield, Weir no Byrne ngesizatu esingaziwa bani
; waqeshwa ngama Bhulu ango Messrs. J. D. van Rooyen, no J. Labuscagne.
Batuswa abantu kukuva sebexelelwa ngama Bhulu lawo esiti banikwa inotisi
yentsuku ezine ukuba bafuduke kunye nempahla yabo yonke, okokuba zide
zapela ezo ntsuku zine beselapo inkomo, negusha, nebokwe, yonke into
yabo iyakubanjwa isiwe esikiti. Baqala ukuva ukuba kanti lomhlaba umana
ukubekwa efandesini leminyaka yonke. Kwehla isipitipiti esikulu kuba
bebelime bapula uluti kwaye ukutya kwabo kukuhle.

Bapange ku Mantyi, wasuka wati lowo mhlaba nalo ngqeshiso ayi pambi
kwake. Ute u Rulumente akuva eso sipitipiti, wabhalela Amabhulu ewanika
inotisi yokuba emva kwenyanga ezimbini uya kuyibulala ingqeshiso yalowo
mhlaba; lwavuka pantsi okunye utuli lwe mfazwe pakati kwa Mabhulu na
Bantsundu. Nge Cawa yomhla we 11 November, zabanjwa inkomo zabo zaya
kuvalelwa ebuhlanti buka van Rooyen apo kutiwa kuko ilongo. Apuma
amadoda aziputuma, afika umkosi wa Mabhulu sewu balindele. Kute pakati
kwe ngxolo yenteto lase lirola umpu ongxashiweyo elinye i Bhulu,
alibamba amadoda awupanga lowo mpu awusa ku Mantyi, obhalele ku
Rulumente kwa yoko ngalento. U Rulumente utumele Imantvi yase Monti u
Gwebecimile (Mr. W. M. Fleischer) ukuba ayipande lonto atumele ingxelo
yokubona kwake kuye. Ute akuyiqwalasela u Rulumente ingxelo ka
Gwebecimile wagwebela Amabhulu.

Kunzima kwezo zi alam eziza kubha- ngazeka naba fazi, nentwana

ngesizatu esingesiso sabo. Abantu abakwi fama ka Mr. van Rooyen kutiwe

Kungenwe kwimicimbi yezakiwo ze
tyalike yaxoxwa. Emva koku u Mr. K.
Mayekiso wavakalisa ukuba uza kunge-
nisa ukuba acelwe umfundisi wesi siqi-
ngata woyikela ukungati liyakupela
ixesha lendawo isilele kanti ke akuko
monakalo ungalingana nalowo.

Umhlali-pambili umcele ukuba akenze
kuhle u Mr. Mayikiso, ngesizatu seminye
imicimbi ebisatetwa.

Kungenwe kwimicimbi yemfundo,
kwapetwa ngokumiswa kwe komiti yo-
kuxuma imfundo kwesi Siqingata.

Zigqityiwe izinto, kungenwe kweyona
ngxoxo ka Nyanga-ntatu ete yasekwa
kulombuzo, “ Yintonina engenziwayo
ukuze uhambe umsebenzi we Nkosi,
kwesi Siqingata?” Ite lendawo yatsha-
yelelwa ngeculo nentandazo, emva koko
Umhlali-pambili unike ingxelo ngempilo
yase Moyeni eziramenteni, ete yatomala-
lisa, wenza amazwi abhekiselele esimi-
lweni somfi u Solomon Sibelekwana;
wagqiba ngesiyalo sokuba masisebenze
xa kusesemini kuba kusiza ubusuku
ekungasokusebenza mntu ngabo.

Kulandele inteto ezishushu esingaka-
nkanya eka Mr. Mayekiso nabanye. Site
sonke seva ukuba u Tixo uko pakati
kwetu ngokubalulekileyo. Esi sincoko
simnandi kunene sipetwe ngeculo nom-
tandazo.

Siqingata ukuba siya kuba moya mnye.

Wesile. Ndinga ndenza imbambezelo

UMBULELO.

Zikululw’ ebubanjweni

Inkos’ ebezise shweni,

Zisindile ekufeni

Zibuyel’ elusatsheni.

Yaqingqitwa iminyaka

Zililelwa ngamawaka,

Kwajokisw ukutandazwa

Kongu Mdali akwapezwa.

Ocelayo uyapiwa

Enjenjalo amakolwa

Aqubuda akadluwa,

Azingisa ad’ eviwa.

Azuzile isifundo

Sokuviwa komtandazo,

Aboniswe iziqamo

Zomanyano, nemizamo.

Asacela asafuna

Ama Krestu konesisa,

Zingeniswe enkolweni,

Zikululwe ezonweni.

Ikululwe imizimba

Emashweni obukonxwa;

Eyona nto ingamandla

Intliziyo ingumbanjwa.

Aqamele ngelo dinga

Liti “ celani nopiwa,”

Abulela enetemba

Ku Yehova onenceba.

Mayinyuk’ imibulelo

Ibheke kosivileyo;

Zipupume intliziyo

Yimivuyo nendumiso.

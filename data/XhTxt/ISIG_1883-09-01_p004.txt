ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1883.

U BISHOP JONES E LOVEDALE.

  EBATENJINI.

Njengoko sabikayo kwesokugqibela ukuba u Right Rev. W. W. Jones, D.D., u
Bishop wase Cape Town, uyakudlula kweli ukubuya kwake Emtata, ute
okwenyaniso wafika ngo 22 Aug., ehamba no Rev. Canon Lightfoot, no Rev.
R. J. Mullins wase Rini, no Rev. T. Chamberlain, B.A., wase Alice. Kute
kuba apa ngosuku Iwesitatu evekini, siba nomtandazo emini emaqanda,
kwacelwa ngu Rev. Dr. Stewart u Dr. Jones ukuba apose isiyalo kudodana.
Evumile wenze isiyalo kumazwi ati: “ Nkosi, utanda ukuba ndenze ntonina?
”—Izenzo, IX. 6.” Ubonise ngokumhlope ukuba ngamnye ngamnye unesipo
asinikiweyo yi Nkosi afanelwe kusisebenzisa. Wati luse Lovedale apa nje
udodana luziswe ngu Tixo—abazali nabafundisi abasemakaya zinto kodwa
asebenza ngazo u Somandla. Into abafanele ukuyenza kukuquba izinto
ezipambi kwabo ngamandla onke, bekumbula ukuba njengokuba bengumlisela
nomtinjana nje bayahlwayela izinto abaya kuzivuna ngexesha lobom
elizayo. Mabaqabuke ke bayiqonde kakuhle into abayihlwayelayo.
Abafundiselwa ukuba baze babe ngamadoda nabafazi abazonwabeleyo,
nabazonwabisa ngento abazifundisiweyo. Into atanda u Tixo beyekuyenza
kukugoduka baye kushumayela kubantu bakowabo.

Indawo akuyo, nezimeko zayo, nenteto yake ebipauleke ngozondelelo
nobushushu nobuciko, zonke ezo zinto ziyaku- banga ukuba utyelelo Iwake
apa e Lovedale lungaze lulityalwe ngabo baba netamsanqa lokumva,
nokumbona. Abo batanda ukubona, betandazela kanjako ukunga umoya wobu
Krestu ungabandakanya amabandla baya kuyipaula ngovuyo lento
ibalulekileyo ayenzileyo u Bishop wase Cape Town —yokuvula umlomo wake
ayale izimvu zomhlambi we Nkosi nakuba zingezizo ezobuhlanti
abongameleyo. Lento ayenzileyo u Bishop ibalulekile ebalini leramente.

IZIKOLO ZABAMNYAMA E PARLAMENTE-

Yehlile into ebesikade siyixela, ukuba izikolo ezi bezixase imfundo yetu
zise ngozini yokucitwa. Nokuba eso sipelo simdaka siyakuhlana nokuba
asikuhla asikabi sesimeni sokuxela. Izoyiko esinazo zisekwe pezu
kwalengxoxo siyilandelisayo, eyabise Parlamente ngo August 21, esiyifaka
njengoko ibonakalayo kumapepa ase Cape Town :—

U-MR. BROWN [Umnyulwa wase Dikeni ute makuvunyelwane ngale nteto :
—Yimvo ye Parlamente ukuba iziqamo zemfundiso yabamnyama, eyamashishini
neyencwadi, azanelisi; nokuba indimbane yemali yakomkulu ekutshelwa
ukuncedisa indawo zokufundisela abamnyama ingagcinakala icitelwe
kweminye imicimbi. Ukuba ukusuka kolwanamhla makungahlaulwa mali komkulu
yokuncedisa ukuxaswa kwenani elitile elifunda ezikolweni, de ibe
abahlaulelwa abo nabo bahlaula imali ekwalingana naleyo irolwa komkulu.
Mayibe kwa yilonto malungana nokuhlaulwa kwe-Titshala ezifundisa
kwizikolo zabamnyama—ixabiso elilingana nelo lirolwa kwa rulumente
malivele kuyo yonke indawo efumana oloncedo Iwakomkulu, nokuba
lifunyanwa ngemali ezihlaulwa yintsapo na, nokuba lihlanganiswe
ebantwini, mayiti kanjako lemali yoncedo yakwa rulemente ihlaulwe
ngumantyi wesiqingata esikuso isikolo eso kubazali abayirolayo imali
leyo ilingana neyo ncedo Iwakwa rulumeni.

UMONGAMELI [Sir D. Tennant] ute, lengxoxo ibanzi kunene, kuko ib'ungana
elimiselwe nxamnye yi Parlamente ukuba licedulule umcimbi wemfundo enge
ibekwiswa kulo ke.

U-MR. BROWN ute unqwenela ukuba i parlamente ivakalise umxelo wayo pezu
kwale ngxoxo. Ukuba ishishini alinakuzimela ngokwalo alinamsebenzi.
Iziqamo zezi zikolo azipumeleli. Kupuma kwezi zikolo ngonyaka ulutsha
olu kumakulu amabini, ebekufanele namhla, kukangeIwe nobuninzi beminyaka
lehambiso imi, ukuba iziqamo zemfundo ngese zibonakala. Akazi namnye
yena ofundiswe kwezi zikolo ozimeleyo ngenyawo zake. Ukuze kufunyanwe
ukunyaniseka kwabo bafuna ukufunda amashishini yena ucebisa ukuba barole
isiqingata sendleko. Oku makwenziwe nakwizikolo zangapandle. Apo izikolo
zihlaulelwayo abo bahlaulayo bayakuzama ukufumana inzuzo koko
bakuhlauleleyo ; inqubo edlulileyo ibonisa ukuba kudlulisele
ukuyihlaulela imfundo. Ucinga kanjako ukuba kulunge ukuyihlaula imali
komantyi kunokuba ihlaulwe kubafundisi njengokuba kunjalo namhla nto
leyo ivelisa isiko elingakolisiyo

U-MR. REID [Umnyulwa wase Tinara] ute u Mr. Brown uyib'ale lenteto ati
makuvunyelwane ngayo, engazazi inyaniso zalento ateta ngayo. Ukuba
izikolo zabamnyama bazifumana zingeko esimeni abasinqwenelayo
mabakumbule ukuba zizikolo zabantu abasapuma ebu barbarini. Uncedo
Iwakomkulu Iwezi zikolo lukwi ponti ezi £54,000, ekubeni abamnyama
ngokwabo nezihlobo zabo bekupa iponti ezi £45,000. Uyatemba ukuba
akusakubako nto yenziwayo injengokunyelisa imigudu yabo bazamayo
ukufundisa nokunyusa abantu abamnyama.

U-MR. SAUER [Umpatiswa micimbi yabamnyama] usuke wati lengxoxo mayike
iyekwe okwanamhla, ze ibuye ixoxwe ngo 29 Aug.

(Ivela kumbaleli wetu.)

Aug. 7, 1883.

Intlanganiso yezikula zase Batenjini ebihlangene ngo Mvulo 6 Aug. kwada
kwango 7 Aug. 1883, igqibe kwelokuba kutunyelwe amadoda atile ngemicimbi
yomhlaba aye kuhlangana no Rulumente.

Pakati kwamanene yaba ngo Mr. T. Mahonga, (Chair­man), Revd. J. Mahonga,
Messrs. J. Menye, T. Zwedala, Jno. Mpaki, F. Nomtleke, Rue. Cobus, S.
Kalipa, N. Tantsi, J. Vanqa, J. M. Pelem, (Secretary). Kwahlanganiswa
imali yokutwala indleko zabanyulwa kwafunyanwa £103 14s 9d. Kwaza
kwanyulwa lamanene amagama ezayo abengawo ayakuhlangana no Rulumente
ngemicimbi aya kuyinikelwa :—Rev. E. J. Warner, Messrs. T. Mahonga,
(Chairman,) S. Mpila, T. Zwedala, J. M. Pelem, (Secretary.) Aba banyulwa
bayakuhlanganiselana e Mt. Arthur ngo Mvulo ngomhla we 13 Aug., 1883,
zebanduluke kwangalomhla. Kuyatembeka ukuba ngalomhla iyakuba seyminzi
imali kuba isahlanganiswa. Intlanganiso yetitshala yase Batenjini
impatise indawo ezininzi u Mr. J. M. Pelem ukuzisa ku Dr. Dale. Enye.
yezindawo yimali avuyiswa kunene yindawo yokuba indoda enjengaye ibe
nokuya e Kapa.

QUEENSTOWN.

Aug. 22, 1883.

Sibulela u Mr. Bell B. Kota ngoludaba luvela kwesi sixeko :—

“ IMBUMBA YAMANYAMA.”—Ekubeni sifunde epepeni lendaba ngentlanganiso
Yomanyano ekwindawo ngendawo, nohlobo ezama Iona (lontlanganiso).
Amanene alomzi agqibe kwelokuba amise intlanganiso ekwalolo hlobo.
Okwanamhla asinanto singayitetayo ngayo, kupela sinxwala kwindawo ekoyo
kuzo, kuba kakade umfana ubambelela ezwini lendoda enkulu, oko kutsho
siteta ukuba sohlala sixonxe indlebe kwintlanganiso eziloluhlobo, ukuze
nati sifumane amanyatela okungenisa imicimbi yokuxoxwa. Ke sesinyule
nongumbali oyakuhlala exonxe indlebe naye kwabezinye indawo (ababali).
Nguye lo,—Mr. E. N. Majambe.

Kwakona apa e Queenstown ngomhla we 18 kulenyanga alwile amanxila
ebusuku, wati esiza usibanibani watsho pakati kwamehlo ngomva webotile
ku “ Kotso,” yatsho indodana kwapuka yonke intwana elitambo. Endinosizi
ukuti akahlalanga xesha lide emva kwengozi leyo. Kute ngobusuku bomhla
we 21 laqama itshoba. Sinosizi ukuti tina batanda ukupindezela ububi
ngobubi abenzi besi senzo abakafunyanwa.

UMNQAYI WEGWALA.

NKOSI YAM MR. EDITOR ndiyacela ukuba undivumele ndipendule u Mr. Jas.
Pelem ngelopepa lako lakowetu. Ndite ndakubona incwadi ka Mr. Pelem
ndotuka ndaba kuko nto ikoyo, kanti hai ngumnqayi wegwala. Ukutsho
nditsho kuba ndifumene izoyikiso nezituso, sekuko ne libel,
neziqalekiso, izinto ezingazanga zacingwa siti. Kutiwa igwala yinto
exoba ifohlele, kutetwa ukuba yinto eti intonga inazo ezanele ukulwa
ihambe ingena ezindlwini zonke ingati ifuna intonga kanti ifuna
ukulanyulwa. Yinto emnqayi mde mkulu wokoyikisa ukuba niyangxolisana
yinto engxolo inkulu (kuba kaloku inqwelo eze iviwa ngengxolo), yinto
epanga ilwe, yinto eti kuliwa ngenduku ipange ezikalini.

Yindlela yabantu abaswele izimilo ukutanda ukuhlisa abanazo babe
besenzela ukuba baze kufana. Anditsho ukuti u Mr. Pelem akanaso kodwa
nditi inteto yake isuke yafuza olohlobo Iwabantu kwindawo ati u Mr.
Wauchope akangetandi ukuba sibuzwe isizatu sokupuma kwake e Tinara.
Nditi mna mhlope angatanda ngapandle kwentloni ukuba sibuzwe. Nditi ke
sibuze wokuxelela, nokuba usibuza kum ndokutyebisa. Ngokunyulelwa kuka
Mr. Wauchope e Palamenteni ndingati kuse. mhlotsheni ukuba kunokwenzeka
ukuba kunyulwe umntu ontsundu kwicala lase Tinara ne Bayi kunganyulwa
yena, kuba wanyulwa e Tinara ngamadoda anengqondo ukuba abe yi Chief
Templar ngenxa yokufaneleka kwake, nase Bayi wanyulwa ngamadoda
akwanengqondo ukuba abeyi Chairman, nawe uyawazi inxenye yalomadoda.
Into mna endimangalisileyo yeyokuba ati umntu ngenxa yokungavisisani
nama.

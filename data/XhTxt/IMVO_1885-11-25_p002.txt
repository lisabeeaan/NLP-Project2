kwelityala makapume egantolo, waza wati amazwi ekuma. ngelwe ngawo,

njalo. Kamatyala amaninzi oluhlobo i Juri ixakwa yakufika kwinqanam
lokwahlula kumaxa kute- nina ukuba kutiwe ubani utete amazwi asusa
isimilo somnye. Kulomcimbi abasokufumana ngxakeko kuba amatyala
ayakubatvela ukuba ukumbekityala umntu omsulwa kukumhluta isimilo. Yiyo

igama. Lamazwi atetwe ngokwehla ngomhla we 16 January lo ugqitileyo. U

I Jaji ivumele u Mr. Solomon

U Mr. Maasdorp uhambise mfaka u Pelser emkumbini wobungqina ukuze
ayiqube eyake imbali ate u Mr. Maasdorp yeyokuba umfi lowo wayemncinita
ngentonga yentsimbi, wamdubula ke ngokuzinqandela ihlahla yaza imbumbulu
ete yehlahle kwigxalaba lase kohlo yatyutya yaya yema emqolo. Abacase u
Pelser bati udutyulwe ehamba pambi kwehashe, kodwa ubungqina begqira
bonibonisa ukuba ubedutyu. Iwe ngoku njalo imbumbulu nge ingahambanga
ngoloblobo. Inyaniso ezizintloko zatatyatwa yimantyi, kodwa eyifundile
inteto ka Pelser ugqibe (yena Maasdorp) kwelokuba angambeki tyala u
Pelser. Ngeba ke into le yayipeleli apo kodwa kweli lizwe amabango
ngamawonga okulaula abauga abantu banga. katali nokuba babambana
ngantonina.

ISIZATU SEZI ZIBONGO.

Kutiwa kwati kwakuko indlala kakulu, aze ama Hlubi amane epiwa ukudla
komkulu, ayeke emaninzi. Kutiwa kwakusiti xa kusak'angezwa aba
bangapambili kwabanye babe bemelwe ngenzipo ngaba bangasemva, bebarweca
ukuba bazokukangela bacele, zada inzipo zamnyama kurwecana.

UMNOMBO WENKOSI ZAMAHLUBI.

U-Mtimkulu (wanq'angi) uzele u Ncobo inkulu, no Radebe, ekunene.

U Ncobo uzele u Dlomo, ezalelwa ngu Radebe umninawe wake. U Dlomo uzele
u Mashiyi inkulu, no Ngwebu ukunene. U Mashiyi uzele u Ntsele inkulu, no
Jingose, no Dube, abase kunene.

U Dube u uzele u Ndlakuse inkulu, no Dayide umninawe wake, kwindlu
yasekunene, no Ncetezo ixiba.

U Ntsele uzele u Bungane. U Bungane uzele u Mtimkulu inkulu, no
Mpangazita enkunene. U Mtimkulu uzele u Langalibalele inkulu, u Ludidi,
u Luzipo no Mhlambiso. U Ludidi uzele u Mtengwane inkulu, no Zono
ekunene. Bonke ke aba base Griqualand East.

U Luzipo uzele u Mkatshane. Lo u Pesheya kwe Nciba ngoku. U Mhlambiso
uzele u Mtongane inkulu, no Sigonyela (Ebenezer) ekunene nabanye. Aba,
bakwa Matole.

U-Mpangazita ukunene kuka Bungane, uzele u Sidinane, Mehlo-makulu,
Monakali, no Ntaba. U Sidinane uzele u Zibi. U Mehlo-makulu uzele u
Ntsinda-ntsinda no Milani. U Monakali uzele u Zibi, ozele u Ncanywa,
Sifuba, nabanye. Aba base Ncwazi. U Ntaba uzele u Tomose, umele i
Tsitsa.

UMNOMBO WENKOSI ZASEKUNENE.

U Radebe wanqangi uzele u Zulu inkulu, no Mashwabada ekunene.

U Zulu uzele u Dlamini (wanqangi) u Dlamini uzele u Radebe (wesibini.) U
Radebe lowo uzele u Dlamini kwindlu enkulu. Kwiqadi lendlu enkulu kwa
Manala intombi yakwa Nala, kuzelwe u Ngqumango.

Kwindlu yase kunene, kwintombi yakwa Ncwana engu Ma-Ncwana, kuzelwe u
Buhlalu.

U Dlamini lowo wesibini, uzele u Ngwana inkulu, kwaza ekunene kwazalwa u
Mndayi kwintombi yakwa Tubela.

U Ngwana uzele u Mijoli, inkulu, no Malangwana, ozele u Dzangwe, inkulu,
no Pezisa ekunene. U Dzangwe uzele u Mgijima, ozele u Kapoko inkulu,
Zweni, no Kafe, nabanye. U Pezisa uzele u Kala. U Kala uzele u Nkupiso,
Mavuso, nabanye. Ekunene kuzelwe u Maqubela no Mvunga, ozele u Luzipo. U
Mqubela uzele u Msutu, ozele u Zulu inkulu, no Ty'efu ekunene. Unina ka
Maqubela ib’iligorakazi u Gubela igama lake, i Belekazi. Besakuti
ukubongwa kwempi yake—

Gubel’esigubudwini,

  Mabandla ka Gasela, Kwa Gubela, akuvalwa ngamivalo Sekuvalwa ngama
  kund’amadoda, njalonjalo.

IMVUNGUZANE.

Kutiwa kwati ugexa lika Maqubela wati esableli komku- lu, kwafika
isaqwiti esingumvunguzane, samtabata semka naye, kwati kwakubon’ukuba
sekulelwe kwazilwa kwanca- nywa, wagaleleka esapilile, kwati kuba
kwakusekwenziwe zonke izinto zokusebenza eloshologu, wati akufika,
bamoyika, kuba besiti sel’engumkolonjane (ghost) wabalilolo elihamba
ezindle, wada wabuye wabuba.

U Mndavi ngo wentombi yakwa Tubela. Uzele u Mangwane inkulu, ozele u
Mzimba, ozele u Ntibane, ozele P. J. Mzimba, Samuel, David, Joseph Dube,
no James Laing, kwanamantombazana ke. Kwa nama Ntintili, nama Langa,
azalwa ekunene kuka Mangwane.

U Ntintili uzele u Ndlakadla inkulu, Nkewu, no Mpetshu. Unina wabo ngu
Ma-Mkwane. Abendlu yasekunene, ngu Gobonci, no Bonani. U Ndlakadla uzele
u Lubisi, no Lamani kwindlu enkulu. Ekunene kuzelwe u John, no James.

U Nkewu uzele u John, Pilingane, George, Mapasa, Ngebungwane, nabanye.

U Mpetshu uzele u Mashumi, Fani, Jona, nabanye. U Gobonci uzele u
Velelo, John, David, nabanye.

U Bonani uzele u Tomose, James, Mzimba, nabanye. U Lubisi uzele u
Mndayi, Tomose nabanye.

UMNOMBO WAMA BELEDWANE.

U Mashwabada uzele u Makwelo, no Sibenya kwindlu enkulu no Ngob’izembe
ekunene. Unina ka Ngob’izembe ngu Ma-Reledwane.

U Ngobizembe uzele u Mvelase, ozele u Ndaba-zandile, Mhlomi, no Citwa. U
Ndabazandile uzele u Ncwana.

U Mhlomi uzele u Mabele, ozele u Ndiko, ozele u Mshwe shwe. U Citwa,
uzele u Mdeni.

U Sibenya uzele u Lutshaba inkulu, u Matshaya ekunene. Unina ka Matshaya
ngu Ma-Ra-ule.

U Makwelo uzala u Mahlapahlapa inkulu, no Maguqi umninawe wake. U
Mahlapa-hlapa uzele amawele, u Dubo elikulu, no Ntloko-mbi, afela
edulini elikula nabanye. U Maguqi uzala u u Bupaluli inkosi ye Mihlanga.
Iti—Vapi! ukufunga. U Bupaluli use Lusutu. Impi ka Mehlo-ma- kulu iti
ukufunga—Zulu ka Ntini! Ikolise ukuba se Witte- bergen intlalo yayo.

U Lutshaba, ozele u Zimema inkulu, owafa ngemfazwe ka Mlanjeni, no
Matomela ekunene. U Zimema uzele u Zulu ose Hewu ngoku. U Matomela uzele
u Mbane inkulu no Diba oweqadi lakulo Mbane.

ISAZISO SAKWA RULUMENTE.

KUYAZISWA kubo bonke abemiyo, kwiziqwenga (Field
cornetcies) ezitile zesisitili (Division) ukuba

  abantu abamiselwe ukwamkela amagama abavoti, (Register-
  ing Officers) nje ngomteto we Palamente bawuqalile ukuwenza
  lomsebenzi, njengokomteto wabavoti, (RegistrationAct) we 1887. Okokuba
  siti eso isiqwenga esipetwe ligosa (Field Cornet) siqokelele amagama
  abantu abanemfanelo yokuvotela unyulo Iwabaya e Palamente,.

  UKUZE ke kubeko inani elifumanekayo ngokufezekileyo,
  nokuze kungabiko ngozi ingatintela amagama anemfanelo
  mabati bonke abanebango lokuvotela abaya e Palamente,
  bangenise izibango zabo kulomagosa ezondawo amiselweyo
  (Registering Officers) bati okunye bacikide indawo zokuba
  azi angaba abalwe kade na awabo amagama, abekwa eluhlwi-
  Iwanemfanelo, kwangosuku olusengapambi kolokuvalwa
  nokuba kungalo kanye,

  KE, abamkeli magama bazo zonke eziqwenga zesisitili
  (Division) ngabo aba bamagama adwele ngasezants’ apa.

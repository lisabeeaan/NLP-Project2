6 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1876.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI

Lu Toits Pan, January 13, 1876.

Nkosi yam, uze undinyamezele unclipe indawana yala mazwi ambalwa,
ndiwatumela ngokufuna uma ongu nina womfazi wam; ndahlukana naye e-Bayi
nge 1871 ndesuka ndeza apa kwele Dayimoni. Solokoko naye andizange ndive
nento ngaye. Okukwenjenje zihlobo zam baleseshi nditemba nina ukuti nini
ngabanye kwindawo ngendawo ekoti ukuba kuko olazileyo igama eli lika ma
lowo ndimfunayo amfundele lamazwi. Uma lowo yintokazi yase Mampingeni;
umkondo wake bati uwcle Inciba waya kwantombi kwa Thom Mangindana. Nize
nindivele batandwa ukuba akakona umntu onjalo, nindazise Sigidimini

P. NIKIWE.

Egwatyu, January, 1876.

Kawundifakele lemigcana. Baninzi aboke babezingxwelera ngamazwi ka Rev.
E. J. Warner, nam ndingamazwi endoda leyo, umntu ohleli pakati kwaba
heyideni akayikusitelwa yinteto ka E. J. Warner. Ezizinto zikankanywa
nguye ati ngezicaswa, nyakana zigugayo abashumayeli boze babe novuyo
olungatetekiyo. Kanjalo masingatsho ukuti, ezizinto azikupela kunceda
ntonina ukuteta ngazo? ngokuba indlovu ifa yakubinzwa futi. Ukuba
lomnumzana umane evelisa indawo ezinje angake azikatule igusha zomalusi
olungileyo ukuzibuyisela kumninizo noyise, wake wawuquba lomsebenzi sati
isininzi sabatembu esilunge. ne Queenstown wasiguqulela ku Somandla.

Bells Hoek, January 10, 1876.

Kute kwezintauku zidluleyo, lamana apa ukukataza ngokududuma nokubaneka.
Komnye umzi libete inkabi yenkomo yawela esangweni. Bate abantu balo mzi
bancama ukoyika abavuma nokusenga. Nditi ke kunganina ukuba boyike
ababantu? Kungokuba namhla biya kubona ukufa, ukumka ngequbuliso,
bayawubona umsebenzi onokwenziwa ngu Tixo; kungokuba lamadoda akanayo
incwadi yokugoduka. Kuya bonakala ukuba noko abantu bamana ukungati
badelisile nje akuko ungamoyikiyo u-Tixo. Iyeza imini abaya kubonakalisa
abantu bonke ukuba nguye yedwa obfeanele ukupakanyiswa abongwe. Kuya
kupela okuya ubumane usiti upina u-Tixo ube ubambe esonweni. Yiza ke
ngoko xa kuseko inceba.

JOSEPH NQASE.

Gaga, January 26, 1876.

Kuko indawo eyenziwe nkulu ngabanye abantu
kulamacala sikuwo. Siti sakuteta nabo ngokutombisa nangezinye indawo
ezinjalo bati abangcke bakuyeke bona kungatshiwo nge yi Mantyi ngesiqu.

  PATULENI KAKAZA.

  NGOMZALWANA OBUBILEYO.
  Ezazulwana, January 25, 1876.

Mhleli we Sigidimi ndifuna ukuba ufake lamazwana
epepeni. Lomntu ndibala ngaye ngomnye wabebe tabata Isigidimi. Ke
ububile namhla, kugqitise ubuhlungu mhla kwayiwa kuncwatywa, umfo
obekutele engemntu osebenzayo. Bate abantu bakukangela indlu yake enkulu
emhlope, no tango oluyi ngqongileyo, nentsimi enkulu epambi kwendlu
lake, nezinye izinto zokusingela pambili abeselezenzile kwelilizwe
lapesheya kwe Nciba, kwanzima ukusweleka konje umntu. Ngelifupi manditi
kuko into eyolisayo noko. Pakati kwako konke oku, amazwi ake abe mahle
kakulu, ute wateta ngekaya lapezulu, waxela ukuba naye unendawo kona,
ayilungiselelwe ngumfeli wake. Igama lale ndoda nditeta ngayo ngu
Kendrick Beme.

THEODORE NDWANDWA.

Isikolo Sakwa Mateyu.

Ngenyanga edlulileyo u-Bishop wase Rini neqela labafundisi balommandla
wake, baye bekwesi sikolo sakwa Mateyu esise Qoboqobo, beze
entlanganisweni
yomnyaka yabafundisi kwa xoxwa ke into eninzi kodwa eyona yayiyi ntloko
yakukubekwa kwelitye lomseko we Tyalike entsha esandu ukuqalwa kwesi
sikolo.

Oku ke kwaqalwa ngolwe Sine (ecaweni) emini enkulu, kwaye kwabe kuko
intlanganisela eninzi yabemi besisi tili, ne gqiza elimnandi
labantsundu. Kute xa kuyiwayo lwaye usapo lwesikolo nomqikela waba
ntsundu ukokelwe ngu Bishop nabafundi sibaye bevuma berozile besinga
kweso sakiwo sitsha inzwana enkulu yelitye liqingqiwe, lanamagama axolwe
kulo eziza zobe ezibomvu nezimnyama ilengalengiswa pezu kwendawo
elizakuzinza kuyo, lwaye upahla olu lwalo luvatisiwe kunene ngamasebe
emiti eluhlaza nange nqolowa nange tyatyambo, ekupeleni komtandazwana
owa uhanjiswa ngu Bishop nangabafundisi u-Mrs. Taberer, inkosikazi yomfo
onzondelelo inkulu kakulu ngomsebenzi wake notandwa kunene ngabake
wasondela ukuza kulungiselela ilitye elo. Kute kwafakwa esi sekweni
lebotile idla ngokufakwa yaye inentwantwana ezitile ezifakwe kuyo. Uze
u-Mrs. Taberer wamana esinda udaka nge Tolofile eyayi bolekelwe lomcimbi
kuMrs. J. D. Barry. Kugqityiwe oko lihlisiwe ilitye lazinziswa endaweni
yalo apo, waza u-Bishop egameni lom Trinity wazisa okokuba elotye
lilungile, lasekwa ngokwenyaniso kwavunywa iculo kwabutwa umnikelo owaba
ngapezu kwe £10. Emva koku kundulukiwe yati inxenye yamenyelwa
e-Tafileni ngu Mrs no Mr. Taberer. Asinakugqiba lemigcana yetu ngapandle
kwelizwana elinye lokubulela imigudu nemizamo ka Mr. Taberer opete esi
Sikolo. Utyelele kona ungabona uqiqe ukoliseke. Kuko isikolo sentombi
sabantsundu ezihlala kona, ngapandle kwesikolo semini sabangapandle.
Uyahanjiswa kunene umsebenzi wokucwela imiti nowokukanda ibekile.
Umhlaba uyancenceshelwa ucuma. Kuko ngapandle koko eminye imisebenzana
ye Tyalike neyemfundo eyeyokususa ubudenge nobutyakala bom Xosa.

  Akuko mangesi wambi ngapandle ko Mrs. no Mr.
  Taberer kwesi sikolo; abashumayeli ne titshere no Mongamelikazi
  wesikolo sentombi, abacweli nabakandi
  be Bekile ngama Xosa bonke. Kubonakala ekuhleni
  ukuba le yiyona ndlela yokunyusa uhlanga; pezu
  kwako konke oku wanga u-Tixo usomandla angamsikelela u-Mr. Taberer
  emsebenzini wake.

  JOHN W. GAWLA.

  INTLANGANISO YE TITSHERE.
  Lovedale, January 12, 1876.

Nkosi yam, kaundifakele lemigcana, i gentlanga
niso ye teacher ibiko apa ngomhla we 12 ka January
Yaqala ngexa leshumi, abantu ababeko ababanga lilo inani abebelindelwe
ukuba babe lilo.

Umsebenzi walomini waba ngulo, ukukangela
ukuba iyanqwenelekana ukuba ibeko intlanganiso.

Kwati kwakuba kuvunyelwene ukuba ibeko kwamiswa ezindawo.

I. Umsebenzi wayo ube ngowokuvisana ngomsebenzi owenziwayo kwindawo
ngendawo, nokunikana amaqinga ukuze umsebenzi ube nokuhambela pambili
(2) lyakuhlangana kabini ngonyaka kwindawo ezobuya zimiswe yi kommiti
(3) eyesitatu indawo yile ayisayi kuba zi Teacher zodwa eziya kuba
ngamalungu, kodwa ngabo bonke abalangazelela ukuba umsebenzi lo wemfundo
uqine. (4) Umsebenzi woqutywa ngumongameli nombali ne kommiti abantu
abaya kunyulwa kumalungu ayo lentlanganiso, abayakuti bawubambe unyaka
upela—(5) Imali emayirolwe ngonyaka zisheleni ezimbini. Ifunelwa amapepa
nezinye indleko. Wabanjalo umsebenzi wentlanganiso ngalomini.
Intlanganiso eyavakalayo ngobumnandi bayo,ukuba xa ite yaxaswa
yakutalelwa, iyakuba luncedo olukulu kakulu kwabaninzi, ngokukodwa kwabo
bawupetey umsebenzi wokufundisa.

  Bayakuti ke bonke abafuna ukuba ngamalungu
  apa batumele amagama abo kumbali, woti umbali lowo abe nokuwamkela
  kude kube ngu March, kwandule ukuti emvako March, bati abafuna
  ukungena
  balinde intlanganiso eyakubako.

  J. MSIKINYA.

  INTLANGANISO YOMNYAKA EBISE
  GILLTON.

  Lentlanganiso ibiko apa ngo suku lwe shumi elinsibozo ku January
  ibipetwe ngumfundisi u-Rev. F.
  Kayser. Eyasc mini ingene emva kwesitonga yapuma emva kwedinara yati
  eya ngokuhlwa yangena xa
  litshonayo kute kusasa akwabiko kuteta kuninzi at
  namadoda atetileyo akolisa ngokunika iziyalo. Kwa
  kolisa ikakulu nge minikelo eyimala nekukutya kwaba
  sekaya; ati amaqaba akupa izinto ezihambayo. Ipumile eyase mini singe
  singati qu ngokufutshane kwizinto
  ezisiwa pantsi kwempumlo. Sabuya sangena kweya ngokuhlwa.

Kanti emini imazi ibipangwe ingekehlisi, kute kupela kwakolisa
ngokurolwa iminikelo, yati neminikelo yapela yonke kubantu abakulu,
zati'itopu nepikoko aza shukuma. Kusuke mfo utile waposa amazwi kashushu
ebekisa kulutsha ngenxa yokuti luhombe kangaka kanti aluroli nepeni. Ute
kwa akuti ukugqiba lwaselupokozeka ulutsha ngeminikelo ati amadodana awa
ezibantini nako zambuleli omnye warola umsesane weponti enesitandatu (£1
6s.)' endingetetiyo kwabebe ne mali kuba bengabanga satandabuza zati
nenkuku zalapa. Kute kaloku nentombi azabi saceba yati, imisesane yalapa
ibanti zalapa ati amajikazi alapa ite enye indoda ukuvala ucango yarola
iqaga lokugwada elarolelwa isheleni ezimbini. Ihlanganiswa ke yonke into
eyarolwayo yaba £38.

JOHN YEKELE.

UMNTWANA OWEYELE ESIZIBENI.

January 31, 1876.

Ndifuna ukwazisa ukububa komntwana oweyeleyo, intombazana ka Ziwani umfo
ka Rune wesika Melani Vella opulukene nomntwana wake mhlana nge Cawa
ngomhla wamashumi amatatu alenyanga. Ute ukuze apulukane naye wasuka
uyise waya etyalikeni e- Rwarwa kunye nabakulu abantwana., wasala, unina
ekaya naye lomntwana; wasuka wapuma umntwana waya kwaninakulu wati akuba
lapo wabuya wapuma kanti ubeke ngendlela nanko esiya e-Rwarwa engabonwa
mntu. Bade babuya etyalikeni abantu wambuza uyise wati umntwana upina,
wati unina fanele ukuba ungezantsi paya; wayeka akutsho oko. Liyise
wabuya wavunuka wati umntwana upina waqala ukutuka unina wafuna wahamba
ebuza balandula bonke kwati kwaoko yapongoma imixelo yabantu sati ukuva
into yokugqibela ngaye salandiswa lilawo lika Mr. P. J. Campbell,
u-William, safikelwa luratyazo sisafuna. Kute kusasa ngomandara safuna
wafunyanwa esizibeni ekungeneni kwe Rwarwa lingena Etyume. Lomntwana
ubudala bake uyiminyaka emibini umgama awuhambileyo eyedwa ungati
sijungqe semavile. Asinto ukuba lusizi lonto, akuteteki kubazali.

Emgwali, January 29, 1876.

Ndiyawafike ebugaxagaxeni Encemera kumi oginyi’nkabi bebabini bezele
yikofu ne tea. Mna! Hi kunjenjenje kuyintoni! Yimbuliso yomzi
kubafundisikapi bentombi.—U-Miss. Sturrock no Miss. Lizie Henry.
Sapungela kwendala ityalike sabuya sapuma sangena kwentsha. Wema u-Rev.
R. Birt wati, zihlobo zani ningamanqina emizamo yam.

Ake atitu amadoda, kwangesiko let u lokunga akuko mntu uyakuteta ekubeni
kutiwe makatete onento Mna ndandiba akuko uyakuteta lornhla. Ngati libi
elisiko lokutembiselana okungakanana, kanti kuza kuti xa sekutetwa
kungakunjulelwa ukuba kuza kusa msinya kube kokukona angxamayo kaloku
naxa sekusitiwa lipelile ixesha.

Ekusukeni ambulela kakulu u-Miss Sturrock, abadela ababekona ngapambili
baza bemka pofu oko babeko besibabulela. Ukuba kwakuko omnye wabo nge
ezidele wancama. Wesukake u-Miss Sturrock wati uza ne £500. Yokulungisa
indlu yokufundela ntombi. Wati kutiwa ngabelungu imfundo iyasona tina
Maxosa. Wati hayi. Lento ingati iyasona kukuba simana sifunda
iziqingatana. Nokoke akunje ngoko nikumbulela ngako nina bamhlope. Utsha
ndakumbula okokuba u-Rev. B. Ross wateta kwangayo lonkani ekuvulweni
kwetyalike yase Peelton.

Wamisake u-Miss Sturrock lowo u-Nontsi Tyamzashe ukuba aliambc
eshumayela kumaqaba ngeqinga
lapesheya.

NKOHLA FALATI

Mahalis Hoek, February 1, 1876.

Mhleli we Sigidimi, nditi akwaba intliziyo yetu sonke ibiriye ngalento
yokuba sisifumane kabini nge nyanga Isigidimi. Singa nati singade sifane
na Mangesi wona ati akuteta ngendawo etile ayipikele ide ifezeke nokuba
inkulu inzima. Kwandivuyisa mna ukuba kuvakale kusitiwa siza kuba
kabini; ndati kaloku neletu ipepa liza kusondela kwawa Mangesi. Ewe
ndingati wena ungazitandiyo ezindaba zimnandi uya kubona use ushiyiwe.

BENJAMIN MOYEDI

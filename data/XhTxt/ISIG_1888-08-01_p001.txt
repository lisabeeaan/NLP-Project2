ISIGIDIMI SAMA- XOSA

I-JUBILI YASE NCEMERA.

LONYAKA sinawo ukumbuza i-Jubili yomnye waba Fundisi abafika kweli lizwe
sisese bugxwayibeni. Ezindawo kumi izikolo kuzo, kwezinye kumi idolopu,
yaye izindada kugqu- ma iramncwa kupela ; apo bekwakiwe, bekulaula
ubudenge nobunyama. Ugaleleke ngamaxesha anjalo pakati kwe Midange
u-Rev. RICHARD BIRT nge 1838—emva kuhle kwe mfazwe ka HINTSA. laikolo
sake sokuqala sasinga- lamacala angase Mxelo, apo nanatnbla ziseko
ingwevu ne ngwevukazi—ama-Kristu enene—ezisateta ngo “BALITI,” ezaqala
ukushunyayezwa nguye ivangeli, ezaqala zafundiswa nguye o-“ A.” Kute
kusaqubeka kakuble lafa ilizwe, nge Mfazwe ye Zembe, 1846—wabe
ngeloxesha sel’eneqela labantu besikolo—ucumile umsebenzi. Isiqamo
salomi- nyaka isibozo yokusebenza kwake, Sibonakele xa kufike ixesha
lokucitwa kwesikolo sase Mxelo yimfazwe leyo; ngexesha obuti ubu Kristu
bomntu bubekwe emlingweni. Kwafuneka ukuba asabe aye e-Koloni u-Mfundisi
lowo, laye inani eselinamatele kuye, lilikulu elinamipefumlo mine, 104.
Kwelo nani ababe sebe ngama Krista yayinga 22. Esi sisiqamo seminyaka
esibozo. Ababaninzi abafundisi abati kwilizwe elitsha bacunyelwe kangako
besaqala.

Lite lakuxola wafudukela e-Sikobeni, eya kuqala elinye iqalo apo kunye
nabaya bantu. Uqube iminyaka yomine konke kuhambiseka kakuhle, umsebenzi
usanda ngoncedo luka Tixo. Yakiwa ityalike, nesikula, yagqitywa nendlu
yomfundisi, ezizindlu zontatu ikakulu zimiswa yimigudu yabantu ngokwabo.
Ngapezu koko zasezimi ngento entle izindlu zabanini sikolo, ingxandana
ezise zigqityiwe zisitoba, —kuba kambe u BALITI yena wavela walwela
ingxande esi- kolweni sake. Kute kuse njalo, gqi lemfazwe ka Mlanjeni,
1850, suk a kwakona yaliciti, yatshiselwa pantsi yonke lomigudu, kwasala
iziqu zabantu kupela, abasaba baya e- Qonee. Namhla linyukile inani
alitimbileyo kunangeye Zembe. Oko wapuma nama 22, namhla ngama 65 ama
Kristu; inani labantu besikolo nje kodwa ngapambili yayi 104 qa; namhla
yimipefumlo engama kulu 400 aze kuga- leleka nayo e-Qonce, besaba
lomfazwe. Bahleli iminyaka emibini laxola ngowesitatu.

lento ayindivumi, mandic'ile ngoku ndishenxele abanye. Kanti hayi; kuba
esi sizwe Base ma-Ngesini asibantu umonde—okukona baxamlekayo koku- kona
bafunzele pakati; sisimilo sabo eso. Ngalo mfuzo yesi- zwe sakowabo,
exaswa ngokukodwa yimpembelelo yo SOMA- NDLA, omane esiti kwisicaka
Sake,—“ Ungadinwa kukwenza “ okulungileyo; usayakuvuna ukuba
akutyafanga,”—u-Mr. BIRT ubuye waqala elinye iqalo ukuxuma umsebenzi

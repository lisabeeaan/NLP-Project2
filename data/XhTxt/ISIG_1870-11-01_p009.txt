UKUVULWA KWE TYALIKE ENTSHA.

KWISIKOLA sama Wesile esise Nxukwebe kuvulwe ityalike entsha, ngosuku
lwe 19 ku October. Kwabako ngalomini into eninzi yabantu. Kwakungati
inkoliso yeso sikolo ibibutene ngase tyalikeni. Abelungu ababekona babe
ngapezu kwe kulu, bevela esi Nqenqeni nakwezinye indawo. Yazala indlu
leyo, kwada kwa- bako neqela elikulu ngapandle. Inani labo bonke ababe
kona belimayela nama kulu osibozo.

Omnye umfundisi wakona, u-Mr. Lamp- lough uleseshe kwi ncwadi Yokuqala
Yokumkani kumazwi okutandaza noku- yala kuka Solomon, ngemini yokuvulwa
kwe tempile yase Jerusalem. Kwakugqitywa oku, kwavunywa,kwatandazwa,
waza omnye umfundisi wakona, u-Mr. Impey washumayela kuwo lamazwi “
Andabona tempile kona ” (Izityilo xxi. 22.) Pambi kokupunywa, kwatetwa
ngemali yokwa- kiwa kwendlu. lyonke yaba ziponti eziliwaka elina mashumi
omahlanu. Ese- yiroliwe, netenjisiweyo, ngamakulu oma- hlanu anomvo.
Esaseleyo ikwa lelonani. Kwabutwa ngalomini iponti ezisemashu- mini
amabini. Kungatiwa imali yalendlu ininzi, kodwa indlu leyo iyifanele.
Ute umfo obekona osand’ ukuvela pesheya, lomali ibingarolelwa indlu
enjalo nakwe lama Ngesi. Yakiwe yonke ngamatye, ibonakala ukuba iyakuma
ixesha elide.

Kuhlanganisenwe kamva kwityalike endala apo bekulungiswe ukudla, kwakoke

inxenye yentombi nabafana besosikola, abavuma ngento emnandi, becula
ngohlobo ekuculwa ngalo ngaba kufundileyo ukuvuma, bengenzi ngendlela
yokukwekwa, ekwenziwa ngayo emitshatweni, xa kutwa kuya vunywa.

Ibuye yabako intlanganiso ngokuhlwa yokutandaza, epume xa kuza kuba
lixesha lokulala.

INDABA ZEMFAZWE NGOCINGO.

Cape Town, Friday, 28 ku October.

I-Celt umkombe we post ufike apa namhla, emini enkulu.

Imikosi yama Prussia iraula I-Paris.

Umkosi wama French okumawaka an- gamashumi amabini anesihlanu (25,000)
ulwe nowama Prussia, okumawaka elishumi elinesihlanu (15,000) ngapandle
kwomzi agxotwa ama-Prussia kwabulawa into eninzi yawo. Kwaza kwaku
pindwa, kwagxotwa ama-French, kwa banjwa amawaka amabini kuwo.

Kusamana kusiliwa. Amawaka anga makulu omatatu (300,000) ama-Prussia
araula I-Paris. Kuko E-Paris apo ama- French angamawaka angamakulu
omatatu anamashumi mahlanu (350,000) axobileyo.

Akuvunywanga kwakufun’ ukuxoliswa. Ama-Prussia abiza umhlaba, awavumi
ama French. Omnye umpakati wama French U- Jules Favre uyile ku-Bismarck
umpakati wama Prussia, kodwa ngenxa yokubizwa komhlaba, ababi nakugqiba
ndawo.

Ati ama-German kwaku dutyulwa kakulu ngenkanunu nangemipu ezitrateni
zase Paris, ngolwa 21 ku Sept., engazi kodwa ukuba kuliwa ngamanina.

Zisemi nqi inqaba eziraulweyo, ezo ke zingqonge I-Paris.

Ukumkanikazi U-Eugenie uye ku nyana wake E-England.

Ama-Russia asaxoba kunene. Kuko amare ngokungati umnqopiso we 1851,
afuna uku wapula. Ilizwe lase Italy ebeli- fuda lahlukahlukene, ngoku
lihlangene, laba bubukumkani obunye.

Impi yama Italian ingene ngoku E- Rome ibotwe lakowawo. I-Pope, indoda
enkulu ebilaula kona, inikwe indawo yokuhlala, nemali yayo isahanjiswa.

Kwakutshona I-Captain kwatshabalala amakulu omane anamashumi osibozo
abantu anamntu mnye (481), kwasinda ishumi elinesibozo kupela. Ubute
lom- kombe waqutywa lilizakazi elikulu wape- tuka watshona kwaoko.

Ngolwe 17 ku Sept., U-Trochu elinye igosa lase France, lapuma E-Paris
nomkosi ongamawaka angamakulu omahlanu ukuya kulwa nama Prussia
abeselekufupi neso- sixeko.

Ama-Prussia akavumi ukuba kulan- yulwe.

U-MacMahon igosa lama French ubu- be enamanxeba alishumi eline sibozo.

I-Paris ayivum’ ukutoba; ngoku yenza imipu eyakudubula ngo mpunga
wamanzi. lyakuba sezindongeni zomzi, eyakulu gqiba utshaba.

Kutiwa U-Bazaine eliny’igosa lama French, upumile E-Metz kunye nomkosi
wake. Kutiwa uligxotile iqedlana lom- kosi wama Prussia, waza waya
kuncedisa E-Strasbourg.

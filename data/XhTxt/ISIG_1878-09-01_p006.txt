UGUQULO OLUDALA NOLUTSHA.

Ngubeni Edendale, Natal August 1, 1878.

Mandikupendule mfana warn John Mvete, ukufunde pina okukuteta kokumema
abakoweni, abantsundu, ngokuti, manihlangane nilwale oluguqulo ngokuba
lunendawo ezininzi, ezingavisisekiyo. Elam liti kuwe, msa ukumema abantu
bokukunceda wakulwa nendaba ezimandi. Into ebikufanele wena nabanye,
ukuba niyenze, yile yokuba niwahlele amazwi eniti akavisiseki, niwabeke
odwa. Ningadidiyeli ngokuhlanganisa alungileyo nangalunganga. Ezinguqulo
zontatu ziteta inteto yamaxosa, namazwana afakelweyo enteto zasemzini
angamanye, ningasoniseli nina lutsha, ngokupolisa intliziyo zabaguquli
bezibalo, sokala ngani, sakupulukwa leli tamsanqa lingaka, into ekoyo
kulenguqulo intsha kukuteta ngobuciko benteto yamaxosa, ayavakala onke
amazwi kaxosa, asinge piki nokuti sekunga ngekutiwe kulendawo kwabekwa
ehlizwi. Ebuninzini bamazwi ka H. W. amahle ndikwelokugcina ilizwi lake
endilibulela ngapezu kwawo onke, amanye amazwi ake, uti isiko esilenzayo
tina ukuba uya funa ukuva ngati lelo lokukutshw akwento efuna ukutabata
isikudla saleyo ilungileyo kunayo, mandiyeke kuleyo.

Bafana bam, inteto kaxosa ipelele kwezincwadi ezaguqulwa ngu Mr.
Appleyard, nale intsha inguqu­lo ayifikanga namazwi angaziwayo, into
yazo ezingu­qulo kukupambana kwamazwi, ngokuba ilizwi lilinye libizwa
ngendlela ezimbini, nezitatu, akuko sonakalo ngalondawo, nokuba zikona
indawana ngazinye, zokuposiseka. Masinganyelisi izibalo, ngezinteto,
umntu ozilesesha kakuhle, ezincwadi ziguqulwe ngu Mr. Appleyard, nale
iguqulwe kutsha, ufumana kungeko kwahluka ngenteto yamaxosa, zivakalisa
kaku­hle zonke, endikubonayo okwenze ukuba kubeko ukwahlukana pakati
kwabanyo, nabanye, yilento ye- nziwe ngabafundisi bakuti, bama Ngqika,
yokudela inguqulo ka Mr. Appleyard bamdela ngokungati uhambe ecaleni
lenteto yamaxosa, kwaza kwakange- leka ukuba bona, baza kuteta ngenteto
yamaxosa, engapandle kwale itetwa ngu Appleyard, bazenza okungati
lenteto yamaxosa yavela kuma Ngqika, se- kungati ezinye izizwe zayamkela
kuwo. Ekuboneni kwam ndingati, kuti tina badala, lamazwana abumini
ayayincolisa inteto, anje ngelilizwi lika H. W. lokuti, amise Izibakala,
akonake amazwana angama­nye, kulenguqulo intsha, amazwana abumini.
Enguqulweni ka Mr. Appleyard, akako lamazwana abumi­ni.

JOHN M. VIMBE.

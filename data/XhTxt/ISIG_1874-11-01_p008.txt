ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1874.

U-JEREMIAH WOODLAND,

UMHLOBO wabantsundu, uyababikela ukuba bati abake beza apa
bafike bamangaliswe kukubona ubuninzi benkomo abafika ziko,

  kanti mhlaumbi kuko ababete ungafumana mbini nantatu kube kupela.
  Yizani kuzibonela ngokwenu. Nangoku U-J. Woodland kuko inkomo
  anazo. Ngalenyanga izayo ulindele ukuze kufike ikulu. Kuko inkomo,
  namahashekaze nezinye izinto ezilindile ukutengisa. Bati abake beza
  kutenga base bepindapinda. Ngomhla 7 ka November kuya kubako
  intengo enkulu yempahla ngempahla ebuhlanti besikiti sase Cradock.

ISAZIS0.

KUYAZISWA ukuba I-Government ibamisile aba bantu baza kuka-
nkanywa ukuba babe zizibonda. Abemi bendawo abakuzo aba

  bantu nabamelene nazo kufuneka beqondile ukuba :

  U-SHWELI ZOZI umiselwe ukuba sibonda salomzi wa Maxosa use
  Lovedale; U-MDINI abe sesemizi ya Mamfengu ese Mxelo, kweficala
  lingase mpumalanga; aze U-MDIKO MABELE apate Amafengu akwelicala
  lingase ntshonalanga E-Mxelo.

  PERCY NIGHTINGALE,

  Imantyi yase Victoria East.

Alice, September 22, 1874.

UMSEBENZI WOKUQABA OZINQWELO.

KUFUNEKA amakwenkwe amabini antsundu, aqabukileyo napapileyo,
ukuba aze kufundiswa umsebenzi wokuqaba ozinqwelo. Ekuwufun-

  deni aya kubotshwa iminyaka emiblanu. Woti ohambise kakuhle kwada
  kwase kupeleni azuze ibaso le £5 mhla wapuma.

  JAMES MCGILVRAY.

Alice, July 21, 1874.

KANGELANI APA.

U-McGREGOR uyababulisa abahlobo bake abantsundu abakwindawo
ngendawo, kwimilambo ngemilambo, kwa Ngqika, nakuma Mfengu

  apesheya kwe Nciba nanganeno, nakuba Tembu. Ufuna ukubulela kubo
  bonke abebesiza evenkileni yake kule minyaka mibini ase Qonce. Uti
  kubo mabangadinwa. U-MCGREGOR ufuna ukuzixelela izihlobo zake
  ukuba ngalo nyaka uzimisele ukutenga Uboya begusha, Nezikumba,
  Nokutya. Kwizihlobo zake uti yizani! esitoreni sake noyifumana yonke
  impahla elungele bonke abantu, ngamanani alungileyo. Funa isitore sika
  MCGREGOR ngase markeni E-Qonce.

IZAZISO ZIKA JESSE SHAW.

IYEZA LAMEHLO. Lenziwa ngemifuno, linoncedo olukulu, linika
ukonwaba. Ukuselwa kwalo, kugalelwa amatontsi ambalwa elisweni,

mhlaumbi liqatywe ngosiba futi, kumane ukupindwa lide lipile.

IYEZA LENTSANA NABANTWANA. Linoncedo lilungile kuzo xa ziqu-
njelweyo, naxa zinesiteipe, nezinye izifo zolohlobo. Ukuselwa kwalo.—
Elusaneni olunyanga ntandatu kunikwa ihalafu yegxebeka oncinane, lixu-
tywe nentwana yamanzi. Liti kwabanye lilinganiselwe iminyaka yabo.

IYEZA LEHLABA elenziwe ngemifuno. Linceda kwa oko kubantu aba-
kulu lakuba bukali ihlaba, kwanakubantwana abane Siteipe. Ukuselwa
kwalo—Kumntu omkulu lilinganiselwa ngegxebeka encinane, lixutywe
nentwana yamanzi. Kolusana kugalelwa amatontsi ambalwa, kulinga-
niselwe ubudala.

IYEZA LOKOHLOKOHLO elenziwe ngencidi yemifuno. Linoncedo olu-
kulu nakongenwe yinqele, notshe ilizwi. Ukuselwa kwalo—Lilinganiswa
ngegxebekana encinane, lixutywe nentwana yamanzi, liselwe katatu noku-
ba kukane, ngemini; kubantwana, nakwintsana kulinganiselwa ubudala.

IYEZA LOKUXAXAZA elenziwe ngemifuno. Linoncedo olukulu kwi
cesine, nokuqunjelwa, nokungatandi kudla, nezinye izifo zesisu. Ukuselwa
kwalo—Kuzaliswa igxebeka elula, lize lixutywe nentwana yamanzi liselwe
xa kuza kutyiwa. Abantwana nentsana balinganiselwa ubudala babo.

IYEZA LOXAXAZO NESISU, elinceda amaxa onke kwezo zifo. Ukunikwa
kwalo—Igxebeka encinane ibenye, kugalelwe amanzana amancinane ;
linikwe katatu nakane ngemini; kwintsana nase bantwaneni kulinganise-
Iwe ubudala babo.

Abatengisi balo ngu J. Shaw, E-Fort Beaufort, nase Nxukwebe, nase
Mpofu, nase Mxelo; no R. Stocks, no J. B. Temlett, base Dikeni;
U-Whitcher no Dyer, kwa Qoboqobo nase Qonce; U-Hedding, E-Butter-
worth, pesheya kwe Nciba.

U-JAMES PEARCE

UYAZISA ukuba esitoreni sake esise Mgwamanzi pesheya kwe Nciba, azike
zisweleke izintwana ezifunekayo kwabantsundu. Uyalinga ngako konke anako
ukuba afumane ezona mpahla zilungileyo ezinokufumaneka, aze abize
amanani alula azilingeneyo. Usele kulungele ukuzitenga zonke izinto
ezilinywayo nezinje ngoboya, azirolele imali. Uyatemba ukuba uya
kubatomalalisa bonke abatengelana naye ngokuti abize amanani azifaneleyo
impahla, angabadli. Uyabamema bonke abafuna amakuba, amazembe,
imihlakulo, nozitshetshe nazo zonke izinto zolohlobo, kwanozibekile,
nengubo, netea, neswekile, nekofu, nesepa, nozityuwa nezinye intwana
ezibunjalo angazifunayo umntu.

ZESI XOSA, ZIKA DAVIS. Zombini ezincwadi zesi Xosa zoba
nokutengwa E-NXUKWEBE, NASE LOVEDALE INSTITUTION, NASE

INCWADI ZABANTSUNDU.

KUKO incwadi zabantsundu ezisand’ ukushicilelwa, ezinokufunyanwa
apa E-Lovedale ezizezi:

  Incwada yamaculo ka-David nezinye izicatshulwa ze Zibalo Ezingcwele.
  Alungiselelwe ukuze abe nokuvunywa ngokukodwa kwiremente zetyalike
  yase England. Ubukulu bayo inamapepa 152. Ixabiso, 2s 6d, ne 3s, ne
  12s njengokubotshwa kwayo.

  “ Uyiza ku Yesu,” usand’ ukushicilelwa wahluke komdala ngokubotshwa
  kakuhle ngapezulu, nokuba amazwi epelidishwe ngokutsha. Ubukulu
  ngamapepa 88. Ixabiso l0 1/2d.

  Eyesibini Incwadi Yokufunda inokufunyanywa nayo ngoku. Amagama
  ayo apeledishwe ngokutsha, yaza kanjalo yongezelelwa imbali ezitile,
  ilu-
  ngiselelwe ukuze ibe nokufundwa na ngabantu abase bebakulu. Ubukulu
  ngamapepa 144. Ixabiso 1s 9d inye, ne 18s xazilishumi elinesibini.

Elipepa lishicilelwa E-Lovedale Institution mayela kolokuqala enyangeni.

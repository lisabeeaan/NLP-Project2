8 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 2, 1881.

INTLANGANISO YO TITSHALA.

AMA lungu elibandla oba ne ntlanganiso yawo e Mgqakwebe, wakwa Mdingi,
ngolwe Sitatu, 11 January, 1882, apo i Secretary (Umpatiswa Micimbi
yayo) asele lungiselele ama nene.

Inxenye yezinto ekoxoxwa ngazo selise zandleni ze Secretary.

  J. WM. GAWLER,

December, 1881.

KUFUNWA UMVANGELI.

KUFUNWA um Vangeli ontsundu owoti abenoku kum shela isi Ngesi esi
Xoseni. Umvuzo zi ponti ezi ntatu ezine shumi lesheleni nge nyanga (£3
10s per month.) Abayi funayo lendawo banga balela, batumele incwadi
zezimilo nokwaziwa kwabo ku

REV. W. LEITH,

LE Senari iya kuvulwa ngomhla we 18th January, 1882.

Ku kangelekile ukuba yonke intombazana ezayo iya kuba ipete incwadi
yesimilo ebalwe ngumfundisi wayo. Iti kananjalo ize nemali—iponti
ezintatu—esandleni.

W. S. DAVIS.

October 12, 1881.

ISAZISO.

NDIYAZISA kuni nonke ba ntsundu base Transkei, ukuba ndingu Mteteleli
wase Ofisini (Law Agent). Ndommela umntu ondifunileyo nokuba kupambi
kwayipina i Mantyi ngawo wonke umonde. Kanjalo kukululekile ukuba sitete
nokuba kupina apo kukoyo i Mantyi pantsi kwo laulo, lwase Koloni.
Imiqondiso ebaliweyo pakati kwa bantu ndiyayenza nje ngomteto.
Izikweliti ndiyazi hlanganisa ndimnike kwangoko undimangele endi
kuhlanganisileyo. I Ofisi yam ikangelene nenkulu ye Mantyi. ANDREW
GONTSHI.

November 12, 1880. Transkei.

INCEDISO ZEMFUNDO E LOVEDALE.

ABA Lauli besikolo sase Lovedale, bayazisa ukuba lemali, ixelwayo kule
nteto, yoku ncedisa imfundo (Scholarships and Bursaries), yofunyanwa
ngabaya kuti bapumelele uviwo, oloba sekuqaleni kwe Seshoni ye 1882,
oloti kanjako, lubikwe usuku lwalo kwi Sigidimi esizayo.

I.  THE GOVAN SCHOLARSHIPS.

ILIFA lomfi u Reverend William Govan, awati wanikela imali engapezulwana
kwe £600, ukuba ize kuba luncedo e Lovedale kwi mfundo yolutsha
olufundayo lwakwa Xosa, nabanye abemi kwaba ntsundu be Africa; ekuya
kuti iziqingata ezibini zesitatu zemali ekumiselwe ukuba isetyenziswe
ngonyaka, zibe zezoku kutaza umlisela, ize esesitatu simele abafundi
abangu mtinjana, kwa abangaba ntsundu abemi be Africa. Ezintatu kwezi
zonikelwa kwabozi fumana.

  Eyamatanci exabiso li ... ... ... ... ... £12.

  Eyesibini (yomtinjana kupela) ... ... ... ... 10.

  Eyesitatu ... ... ... 8.

II. THE BROWNLEE BURSARIES.

IMALI eya kutshwa zizihlobo zomfi u Reverend John Brownlee, mhla wafeza
iminyaka emashumi mahlanu emsebenzini wobufundisi e South Africa,
inikelwe ukuxasa abafundi abantsundu abanga madodana kwi sikolo sase
Lovedale. Imali enokufunyanwa nge 1882 izi £25 iya kwahlulwa kubini
ngolu hlobo :—

  Eyamatanci exabiso li ... ... ... ... ... £15.

  Eyesibini ... ... ... ... ... ... ... 10.

III. THE IRVINE SCHOLARSHIP.

IMALI emi okwe xesha, imiswa ngu John J. Irvine, Esq., M.L.A., King
William’s Town, inokufunyanwa ngoku pumelela

uviwo, nanga lupi uhlobo abafundisi aba Laula umzi abangatanda ngalo
ukunceda imfundiso yododana. Okweli xesha inoku­

funyanwa ngokwi qela eli pambili kwesi sikolo.

  Ixabiso layo ngo 1882 ... ... ... ... ... £12.

IV. THE ECKHARDT BURSARY.

IMALI yomfikazi u Catherine Eckhardt, wase Lovedale, awayi shiyela
ukuxasa imfundo yabantsundu abangama Xosa, nama Mfengu, nama Qeya. Enoku
citwa nge 1882.

  Ixabiso li A. ... £9.

UNCEDO luka Mr. Govan, olwa miswa zizi hlobo zake, aluna kufunyanwa
ngezi mviwo. Lungati mhlaimbi lunikelwe njenge baso, ekupeleni konyaka
koyakuti abe ugqibe kakuhle ixesha lemfundo lemi nyaka emitatu e
Lovedale.

I.  Izinto abaviwa kuzo abafuna bexaswe ngezi mali, zifana nezo ziviwa
    abangenela uviwo lwe Titshala, noviwo lwe zikolo lwe Cape University
    :—

II. Ezi ncedo zemfundo zinoku funyanwa ngaba Ntsundu kupela.

III. Zezo mnyaka omnye, kodwa zingamxasa umntu neminyaka emitatu, xa
     umntu aya kuquba kakuhle, ze abe nesimilo kanjalo.

IV. Zihlaulwa ekupeleni kwe Seshoni nganye.

V.  Aku vumeleki ukuba umntu ade axaswe ngemali ezimbini kwezi, ngaxesha
    nye.

VI. Ezi ncedo zezabo baku funda apa e Lovedale.

VII. Uncedo lomfi u Mr. Govan Iwesibini lolomtinjana kupela luno
     kwahlulwahlulwa, mhlaimbi kungabi njalo ngokutanda kwaba fundisi
     aba Laula lomzi. Zonke ezinye zezododana.

VIII. Abafuna ukulungena olu viwo, kufuneka bepete incwadi zokwa ziwa
      kwabo, neze zimilo, zivela kubafundisi abapuma kubo.

Elipepa lishicilelwa E-Lovedale Institution mayela kolokuqala enyangeni.

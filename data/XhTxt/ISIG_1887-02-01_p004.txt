ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, FEBRUARY 1, 1887.

UMFI U MR. J. J. IRVINE.

(Cape Argus.)

Umpanga wokububa kuka Mr. J. J. Irvine ofike Eqonce ngo January 3
uyakuvakala kalusizi kakulu kulo lonke elase Koloni napesheya kwe Nciba.
U Mr. Irvine obexinwe zizinto ngezinto ude wawela waya e England nomfazi
wake unduluke kwezi Cawa zimbalwa zidluleyo. Ububele e London ngomhla we
Cawa ngomhla we 2 ku January. Umfo obe ngumlingane ka Mr Irvine uti
ukusibalisela kwake ngaye.—Umfi waqeshwa ngu Mr. Charles Brownlee obe
yimantyi yama Ngqika e Gqolonci ngomnyaka we 1850. Akahlalanga xesha
lide kuyo wangena wangumrwebi kweso sitili. Ute ngenyameko nangomonde
emsebenzini wake wanokwazeka okukulu, wada wayakuma e Qonce wangumrwebi
kona apo agqibelele kona ukutyeba. Uxa- nde kamsinya kumacala onke,
ivenkile zake bezikwela pesheya kwe Nciba, kwa Adam Koko (e Kokstad,)
nase Lusutu apo zapeliswa yimfazwe eyayikona.

Ngo nyaka we 1879 wanyulwa waba lilungu le Parlamente lesitili sase
Qonce kwada kwa ngumnyaka we 1883 ate ngalonyaka akakukatalela ukubuye
abelilungu le Parlamente. Ube ngelilo iciko elinobunxamo entetweni,
kodwa ukulunga kwake kumenze ukuba angalibaleki. Ngumfo obefana no Mr.
Saul Solomon obengatandabuzi ukuya kuzilungisa izihlobo zake akuqonda
ukuba zilahlekile ekucingeni kwazongaba Ntsundu.

Watenga ifama e Kubusi (Waterford) wacita iponti ezingama waka amashumi
asixenxe, £70,000, etenga impahla ebambayo yapesheya kolwandle,
omashini, wenza ne mijelo nezinje iziuto ezilungileyo, ebonisa
ngokwenjenjalo ukuba ukufama kweli lizwe ukuze kumngenisele umntu
makakutale abe nobucopo obulungileyo. Ube buyekelela u Mr. Irvine
emsebenzini wake kwakufika amaxesha amabi, wada wacinga ngokumka kweli
lizwe, babe njalo nabanye abarwebi. Waba nengcinga zokuya e Australia
ukuba ute akufika kona wakolwa ngulomhlaba. Eyile ke akahlalanga ubuye
wabuya nempahla ebambayo’ yoku lungisa imfuyo ayitenge kwe lozwe.

Ukububa kwake kuyinxwaleko elizweni letu xa sikwaziyo sikuqonda
okulungileyo asenzele kona noko abesaza kusenzela.

U Mr. Irvine ub’ezeke intombi ka Mr. Dods Pringle wase Lynedocb kwa
Mankazana, wazala kulomfazi unyana wamnye abehambe naye ukuya kwela
pesheya. Owokuqala umfazi wake wabuba engagulanga xesha lide, wakatazeka
kakulu ekububeni kwake; waza emva kwalowo wazeka omnye, wabuba naye; lo
ke anaye ngowe sitatu. U Mr. Irvine ube ngowe Hlelo lase Tshatshi; umfo
obe nesandla esivuzayo emicimbini yalo tyalike nakwezinye ityalike nezi
kula. Imikwa yake elungileyo, uhlobo abelulo pakati kwabanye abantu,
nakwizinto zalentlalo ibe ifanele ukuba ngumzekelo kuti.

Longeza elinye ipepa liti,—Nakuba ebe ngumntu ohlale ebucala ebenconywa
ngabo bonke ngokuba ebejonge kwindawo enye etembekileyo, ubomi bake
buvumelana nesimilo sake. Ebengohlala esel’elungele, evuma kanjako
ukupimisela ekuhleni ingcamango yake, alipakamisele izwi lake kwicala
loxolo, lobulungisa, nelenyaniso. Incwadi yake awayitumela kwi Argus
ukuba ishicilelwe, umgudu nesenzo sake sokugqibela esisemhlotsheni;
enika icebo elibekiselela kuxolo kwela ma Mpondo, ebala esezintlungwini
etyafile kuba eselukukweni lokufa, ese nqanaweni e Kapa ebekisa kwela
pesheya kwolwandle apo wayesinga kona, zonke ezizinto zimxela umntu
abenguye. Ubecasene kanye nabo bonke ababezamela izahluko pakati
kwentlanga zale Koloni. Ukububa kwake kuyilahlekelo enkulu kwi ntlanga
zaba Ntsundu, kuba bapulukene nompimiseli ongoyikiyo weminqweno noluvo
Iwabo.

IMITETO YE ZIKULA ZABANTSUNDU 1887.

  Kufuneka Izibonda ne Titshala zezi kula zaba ntsundu ziyiqondisise
  lemiteto ilandelayo, eshicilelwe ukuze kuqu- tywe ngayo:—

I.   Akuko titshala nokuba kuteni eyakuvala isikula sayo, mhlaumbi
      ishiye umntu endaweni yayo, ingazuzanga imvumelo ku Mongameli we
      Mfundo, nokuba ngu Mkangeli we Zikula, mhlaumbi ngu Mfundisi
      owongamele eso sikula.

II.  Akuko titshala iyakuguqula ngokwayo imihla esimelwe ukuba sivalwe
      ngayo isikula, umhlaumbi izimisele olwayo usuku olungekuyo i
      Almanak yesikula.

III. Kufuneka itishala zingenise izikolo zazo ngo 9

o’clock ide ibe ngu 1 o’clock. Ixesha lokutungisa abatungayo lisemva kwe
dinala.

IV. Kufuneka ititshala zizilungisele intsapo zazo ukuhlolwa nokuviwa
    ngalondlela imiswe kwisaziso nge Ziqibi zemfundo.

V.  Ekupeleni kwe veki zonke i Register zesikula mazi- qosheliswe
    ukulungiswa, lipaulwe inani labo bangena kweso kutungisa isikula.

VI. Ukuba kuko into engekoyo esikuleni (nje nge ncwadi, izileti, ne
    pencil, njalo-njalo,) kuyimfanelo ye titshala ukumazisa kwa kamsinya
    u Mfundisi owongamele eso sikula nge ntswelo ezo,

VII Ititshala esel’iminyaka mihlanu, nangapezulu, ite qo ifundisa pantsi
kolaulo Iwe Hlelo Lemfundo, iyacelwa ukuba iyikankanye londawo ku Mvi we
Zikula xa ’sukuba ehambele ukuza kuhlola isikula sayo.

VIII. Kuyimfanelo yezibonda zezi kula ukuyikangelela indawo yokuba
      ititshala ziyizuze imali abamele ukuyirola abazali babantwana;
      nokumazisa u Mkangeli wezikula mhlaumbi u Mfundisi ozongameleyo
      indawo ezikalazekayo kwi titshala zezikula kwezo zitili (ezi nje
      ngokungabiko esikuleni ingacelanga, nokusikupa pambi kwexesha laso
      isikula, njalo-njalo.)

IX. Kufuneka ititshala zigcine ipepa lalemiteto ne Almanak zesikula,
    namapepa ateta ngeziqibi exonyiwe endaweni esekuhleni esikuleni
    sayo.

Umkangeli wezikula uyakwaneliswa eluviweni Iwake zezi ncwadi zama Xosa
zokufunda ezi:—

Isiqibi I.—(1) Incwadi Yokuqala (Lovedale) Yaba Fundi; mhlaumbi (2)
Isipeli Sokuqala sishicilelwa nge sishicilelo sase Wesile; mhlaumbi (3)
Isipeli Sokuqala sase Tshatshi, sishicilelwe ngu Rev. A. J. Newton.

Isiqibi II. Eyokuqala Yokufunda yase Lovedale (indawo ezi kapukapu) ;
mhlaumbi incwadi elandela Eyokuqala eshicilelwe e Wesile.

Isiqibi III. Eyesibini Yokufunda (ezona ndawo zinqabileyo) mhlaumbi
imbali ezi kapukapu.

Isiqibi ---: Itestamente End'ala Nentsha.

isiqibi. Uhambo Lomhambi.

Abafunda ezi Simnareni boviwa kwi Siqibi V. Abayi kuviwa kodwa kuso
ukuba abazange bapumelele ese IV. ngapambili.

brandy) ngokurwetywa kangaka ngayo ute ke noko awenzileyo amalinga
amakulu elangazelela ukunga i- Nkantini zinga. mgama nawo zingabi pakati
kwomhlaba wabo, loyiswa icala lake ngobuninzi benani elicasene naye. Ute
u Rulumente watembisa ukuba uyakuyiroronya lendawo, lamnandi elo dinga.

TAITILE ZEMIHLABA.

Ulande amanyatela okuqala enziweyo. Kulandwe lombuzo ate wawufumana
entlanganisweni leyo abenayo, ukuqondisisa ukuba bafuna ete lowo nalowo
wane Taitile yake ngesiqu kusinina, nokuba zi Taitile zesizwe nomhlaba
waso na? Ate amadoda ase Nqushwa afuna iziqiniselo zemihlaba yelali nje
ngokuba kwakuvunyelwene njalo no Rulumente wapesheya. Aye kananjalo
encurunela lendawo yokutengiswa kwemihlaba elunge nawo angazuzi nto
ngayo.

zifama ezimhlope ezimelene nabantsundu. Njengokuba kubiywa ngengcingonje
kuti kanti ubiwe umhlaba, otuke ontsundu umda sewu nganeno kwendawo
yawo. Upendule u Mr. Innes ngeliti—“ Ilusizi londawo, kodwa ukulungiswa
kwayo kusezandleni zaboniweyo, xa kungaba zicacile impawu zokushintwa

NGOLWESI-BINI, AUGUST 1, 1882.

INGQAKAQA INGENE.

MAYIBE into eninzi kuba fundi betu seyike yeva ngoku fika, kweli lizwe,
kwe sifo apa esoyikekayo—ingqakaqa. Akusoku sifanela ukuti cwaka, singe
asikatali, pantsi kwezi zimeko. Ukuba besine xesha nendawo ebanzi apa
kwisi Gidimi, besingake sibalise ezokungena kwaso elizweni, nama vukela
ntloko osiyazi bezo mini. Liko ixesha esaka sangena pakati ko luntu,
saqwenga, sadlavula, kwaku ngeko ukalima nga yeza—kuba aye ngeko. Ngelo
xa akubanga mntwana we gazi—kwa susela ku Mntan’ Omhle kwasa kwi
ququlurana eli mnyama, ungqakaqa erodomsha, exola, ehamba nge ziqula
esoyikisa. Siva ukuba u WILLIAM III., owaqala ukulaula pezu kwe England,
ngo mnyaka we 1689, yamxola. Ngelo xesha yonke iledi, nengabe kusitiwa
imbi, yab’intle ngalo lonke ixesha enga xolwanga. Iminyaka inga pantsi
kwe kulu, lati lafunyanwa iqinga lokuyi tintela ngo hlobo olu
bonakalayo. Akusaku nceda nto uku qwalasela kwi zinto ezabanga ukuba
laziwe. Umfumani womciza lowo, owaziwa ngokuba kuku qapula namhla, ngu
DE. JENNER. Lento ikuku qapula inamandla kanga ngokuba apo, ngo nyaka we
1796, Pesheya beku sifa abantu aba 100, aba 20 bebeba ngabe
ngqakaqa—ingqakaqa eyabi ngo 1870—1872, pakati kwe 15,000 abantu ababe
sezi hospitileni ze ngqakaqa, amagqira afumana ukuba, kwababe qatshulwe
kwahliwa bane, nabo ayabehla kakulu. Ati amagqira umntu owaqatshulwa
elusana, wapindwa xa apakuti kwe minyaka eli shumi lina mbini ne shumi
lina ntlanu, anga hlala esazi ukuba usindile, de mhlayimbi ibe ngu mntu
ebi mele ukumhla.

Pambi koku beka, kuba lesi betu, ezona nto sicinga tina ukuba, zimenza
umntu abe ngo welo qela elimele kukuhliwa, soka siqau-qau ezona nyaniso
zinkulu, malungana nebali lokungena kwale ngqakaqa isifikeleyo namhla.
Nge stemele oyi Drummond Gastie, owasuka e England ngo January 6,
odlulileyo, nabantu aba 380, oweza nolu vunduvundu. Emva koku shiya
isiqiti esiyi Madeira esise ndleleni yokuza kweli, yabonakala ingqakaqa
komnye wenani laba hambi. Wabehla wabekwa yedwa. Ekufikeni e Ka­pa ute u
stemele wagwetyelwa ukuba aye kuma yedwa e Saldanha Bay, nga sentla kwe
Kapa, iveki zontatu. Lento yabiyi mposiso enkulu, kuba yabinga banga
ukuba nababe ngasa kuhliwa, ibahle, kuba behleli emkombeni onayo Danke
kodwa, Sombawo, akuko wahliwayo. Kuko ibali eliti, kwati xa kutulwa
impahla, kwatiwa kwi kuli (coolie), maliye kutshisa impahla etile yalo
mntu ube hliwe. Lite lakufika ingubo zintle lakulelwa ngu mnqweno lowo
mdala watshabalalisa u HAKANI no GEHAZI bakudala—litabatile lisiti liya
kunxiba. Yehla apo ke into. Yati kuba ingqa­kaqa ifika e Cape Town, umzi
odunyelwe kukungcola, yaseli kauleza. Ibe bubu sadu-sadu abantu; abanye
bangena ko loliwe sebene ntloko zayo—into leyo ebange ukuba sive seli
gqamsholo e Aberdeen kufupi ne Graaff Reinet, naku fupi koma Victoria
West, singa sateti nge ndawo eziku fupi ne Cape Town. Izipata mandla
ziya zama ukuyi nqanda ukuba ingahambeli pambili. Siya tandaza, wonke
efanele ukutandaza, ukuba amalinga azo asikeleleke.

Siza ke ngoku kwezo nto zibanga ukuba umntu imtyumbe, noko engo
qapuleyo. Ukungcola kwe mizi kwazo zonke intlobo, nokungcola kwe mizimba
nengubo, akuzisi ngqakaqa nakuba kumenza umntu isicenge sezifana zonke
ezoyikekayo:—kodwa, paula ke, yonke lento iyayi kutaza, iyenzela indlela
egangatiweyo. Ebekungake bangati abantu babehle bazi hlambulule; bazi
kusele. Banga peli ngoku qapula kodwa, kodwa bapapamele imfundiso ze
Vangeli ye sepa na manzi. Asiteni nokuba bezi hanjiswa ngalu pina uhlobo
ngapambili ezi mfundiso: ngoku mazi nyanyekelwe. Enye into emayi vukelwe
kukuba ati ubani onwabe, azixolise pantsi kwezi zimeko, kuba yonke
intwana enjengo valo ivula iroba esirweqeni salowo uzilungiselele ukuzi
kusela ukuze asinde, kwi ntshuntshe zo mbub'isi.

IMPAWANA.

Amagama abo bahlaulele isi Gidimi ngo May, June, July, no August,
obonakala kwese nyanga ezayo, kunye nezinye inteta ezininzi, zetu nezaba
Balelani, ezingena kungena, ngenxa yoku sweleka kwe ndawo.

Siya vuya uku fumana ukuba into eninzi yama pepa nama doda andilekileyo
acinga kunye nati ukuba ukubuyiselwa kuka Cetywayo kwa Zulu—yiyo into
enga buyisela ukuzola noko nwaba kwelo. Into esimangalisayo yeyokuba le
ngcamango ide ingene nakuma pepa abe butshaba kunene ku Cetywayo; siteta
i Cape ^(r)limes. Eyetu i Government ese Cape Town kubonakala ukuba
imele ukugoduswa kwelo Tshawe. Nakuba into leyo ingeka tetwa kulindelwe
ukuba abuyele kowake umhlaba u Cetywayo ekubuyeni kwake Pesheya.

Singa bantu abatanda ukuncoma, ngokwe njenjalo uku kutaza, yonke into
isingisele eku lungeni. Umhlobo ose Cradock wenene elitile eli lapa
wenjenje ukuli ncokolela ngento eyenziwe ngama Wesile antsundu
elo:—“Ibandla letu eli mnyama apa, lake indlu yom Fundisi walo. Idle
£105. Lo mali yonke ihlaulwe. Oku kuya buleleka malungana nale
ntlanganiso, kuba okwa ngoku ise ncinane, amanani ayo ngama shumi
amatandabu amalungu azeleyo, na 74 angaba lingwa.” Made virtutel—ngoku
kumsha, “unga dinwa bandlana ndini ”—sitsho tina.

I Cape Argus yo July 20, ishicilele ingxelo yoviwo apa kutiwa yi
Matriculation. Nje ngokuba zipambi kwetu nje ingxelo ezi sifumana ukuba
izikolo ezininzi zama Ngesi zase Koloni apa nezikula zase Bloemfontein,
zino dodana olupumeleleyo. Indawo epambili ihleli no A. S. Hayton, wase
St. Andrew’s College, Grahamstown. Abapumelele Honours ba 19;
abapumelele kodwa ba 58. Olu viwo ukucacisela abanga lwaziyo, lolu ati
umntu xa alupumeleleyo ibe “akuntsuku zatywala” apumelele oluyi B.A.
Ngangoko saziyo use mnye kuma dodana antsundu osalu pumeleleyo, ngu Mr.
Simon Peter Sihlali, ofundisa e Independent Mission School Graaff
Reinet. Ewe; lunzima. Kodwa umonde nenzondelelo ezi zombini zina mandla.

Nokuba ubuza banina oqondayo wofumana ukuba apo usizo lwaba mnyama,
pantsi ko laulo olunje ngolu sinalo kule Kolo­ni ye Temba eli Lungileyo,
kukuba sibe ngaba nyuli bama doda aya kusitetelela e Parlamente. Nokuba
u tile uyinyamekele, uyidangalele sinina lonyaniso, ayisakuba buxoki
ngenxa yoko. Kuti ke ibe yinto enandipeka kanye ukubona imizamo yabanye
bebala letu, bepenu-penuka, njenge mfama zikokelwa, besinga kwelo cala
kutiwa inkululeko, nayo yonke into ehamba nayo, ikulo. Ute umbali wolu
“Pawu” akufumana itutyana lokuk’ awushiye umsebenzi wake wangena kwesi
Siqingata sase Qonce, ngexesha loku ngeniswa kwama gama abo bafanelwe
kukuba se ncwadini yaba voti, wateta namadoda azinamhlaba kweli, ebonisa
imfuneko yokuba bapapamele amalungelo abo. Kute kuba ixesha beseli
cutekile amadoda enza ama vukela ntloko, agxagxamisa, angenisa inanana
elite putu kulo ncwadi yezima mhlaba. Amadoda ase Rabula nawase Tyume
afanelwe kuku balulwa malungana nale hambiso ibukekayo. Xa ngoku indawo
zamazwi nemityukata yenteto zitatyatwa zizenzo—siya qala ukukolwa.

Mkubiso wati “Nina bantsundu kade nibelekiwe ngoku kutiwa yihlani
nizimele ngezenu inyawo.” Le yase Dikeni iremente yeuye yezokuqala
pakati kwabantsundu, iselinexesha elingapezu kwamashumi amahlanu
eminyaka iko, kanti noko kumhla iza kuqala ukuzimela ngezayo inyawo.
Umnqweno wetu ngayo ngowokuti yanga ingayisikelela Inkosi ekwenjeni
njalo kwayo. Yanga nayo ingawa ngedolo kube kupela kuba amehlo ento
yonke azakukangela kuyo, ekangela ukuba iremente yabantsundu inokwenza
ntonina emva kokuba iselinexesha elingakanana incedwa.

Eyesibini indawo, neyona mhlaumbi ingapezu kwazo zonke nezinye, ibe
yeyo- kuba kubafundisi ebekuza kunyulwa kubo bekuko nabantsundu.
Kwelicala le Free Church kube kumhla ize ibeko into enjalo. Siti ke
ubukulu balondawo abunakubaliswa, kuba akunakuze kuqondakale ukuzinza
kwelizwi ngalo lonke ixesha elise sezandleni zabamhlope bodwa. Koqala
ukucaca xa sebanele abantsundu abapete iremente zabantsundu ; ngokukodwa
xa nazo sezitumela abafundisi kwizizwe ezise semnyameni. Ubufanele ngoko
ukuba ngumtandazo womntu wonke pkolwayo ukuba u-Tixo atumele amawaka
abasebenzi abantsundu. Akatsho yinina elizwini ukuti.—“Okwenene luninzi
lona uvuno, kodwa abavuni bancinane. Tandazani Ice ngoko ukuba umnini
kuvuna atumele abavuni esitiyeni sake.” Ababewutandaza lomtandazo
bayakubulela ukubona inkosi ingati iya bapendula nakuba kuse kancinane.

Eyesitatu indawo ebiko kulontlanganiso, intsha kweziremente ze Free
Church, ibiyeyokuti abantu abangamalungu ere- mente mabazinyulele
ngokwabo umfundisi wokubaluleka abakokele. Ngapambili londawo ibifezwa
ngabafundisi bodwa, ukuti unantsi makaye ekutini. Namhla ke kwiremente
eseziqinile kunikwa amandla okuzinyulela ngokwazo pakati
kwabanokufumaneka.

Umsebenzi walo mini asingebi sawuca-

zulula apa. Sesingafumana siti wapelela ekubeni iremente ibize u-Mr. P.
J. Mzimba ukuba abe ngumfundisi wayo. Bati abafundisi bakuba bexoxile
ngalo ololubizo bavumelana ukuba balubeke ezandleni zika Mr. Mzimba
lowo, kuviwe ukuba uya kuluvumana yena. Lwanikezelwa kuye ngumfo ka Ross
omkulu wati “Asikuko nokuba ndinovuyo ukubeka esandleni sako isibizo
sokuqala esenziwe ngabantu bakowenu kuwe sokuba uze kuba ngumfundisi
wabo. Wena ungowokuqala oke wakulendawo ukuyo namhlanje,” watsho wati
“kufunwa ke ukuviwa ukuba uyavuma akuvumi kusini na?” Ute omnye
ukupendula “Ekubeni ndibizwa yiyo iremente ndiya vuma, ndisiti yanga
Inkosi ingaba nam.”

Waba uyapela ke umsebenzi walomini, imini eya kuhlala ikumbuleka kude
kube nini kwiremente yase Dikeni, ebifanele ukuba yimini enkulu kubo
bonke abantsundu.

Okwangoku sowapeta lamazwana ngo- kubika ukuba abafundisi bamise usuku
Iwesibini ku December ukuze ibe yimini yokubekwa izandla kwalowo ka
Mzimba. Umsebenzi uya kuqala ngexesha leshumi elinesinye. Koti nge
Sabata elandelayo ashumayele intshumayelo yokuqala ukuba ebekwe izandla.

INTLANGANISO yokupela komsebenzi walo mnyaka we 1875 olunge nale
Institution iyakubako ngo December ngomhla 22. Umsebenzi uya kuqala
ngexesha leshumi kusasa, baze abafundi bacitakale kwa- ngalomini. Zonke
izihlobo ze Institution ziyamenywa ukuze zibeko.

Usuku ekuya kupindwa ngalo ukungenwa woba ngumhla wokuqala ku February
1876. Ngolo lusuku kobako uviwo lwabafuna ukungena. Abakufunayo ukungena
baya celwa ukuze babeko ngosuku olumisiweyo. Ukufika mva kungenisa
inkatazo.

ODUBULE UNYANA—Elinye lamapepa ase Queenstown liti, “U-Siqungati,
umninawe ka Ngangelizwe ute edubula unomyayi, wati xa atsala incukumiso

I-BURSARI ZASE LOVEDALE

I-BUBSABI KA ECKHARDT.

Ekuvulweni kwe Lovedale Missionary Institu­tion ngomhla 2 ka February
1876, i-Bursari ka Eckhardt, yemali eyashiywa ngu Cathrine Eck­hardt,
iyakuvulekela ukuba izanyelwe ngamadodana Amaxosa nawa Mamfengu nawa
Malawo.

Ixabiso lale bursari ziponti ezilishumi (£10) ngomnyaka. Opumeleleyo
angayifumana iminyaka emitatu ukuba uzipete ngokufanelekileyo, nasezi-
fundweni uquba ngokutandekayo njengoko kumana ukubonakala eluviweni
lokupela komnyaka.

Izinto abaya kuviwa kuzo abantu zoba kwazezo bayakulingwa kuzo abazamela
eka Govan i-Bursari. Ukuviwa kwabazifunayo kuya kuhlanganiswa kube mini
nye kuzo zombini, imibuzo ibe minye.

Intlanganiso yabafundisi base Lovedale iya bacela abafuna ukuzizamela
ezi bursari ukuze babe neziqiniselo zezimilo zabo.

I-BURSARI KA GOVAN’.

Le bursari iveliswe zizihlobo zika Rev. William Govan obelapa
e-Lovedale, zihlangene nabebefu- ndiswa nguye, ixabiso layo ikwishumi
elinesibini (£12) leponti ngomnyaka. Oyifumeneyo womana efumana lomali
iminyaka ide ibe mitatu. lyakuqala ukunikwa ngomnyaka we 1876.
Kunqweneleka ukuba zihlale zikunjulelwa ezindawo : —

1.   Otanda ukuyizamela kumlungele nokuba ungowalupina uhlanga nebala.

2.   Abafuna ukuze bayizamele mabaze batumele amagama abo kwakunye
    neziqiniselo ezikolekileyo zezimilo zabo, bazitumele ku Mongameli
    wentlanganiso egcina lomali ye bursari ngapambili komhla wokuqala ka
    January 1876, nokuba kungawo kanye.

3.   Oya kuyifumana woba ngopumelele kwimi- buzo eyakubalwa emapepeni.
    Ololuviwo ke lobako apa e-Lovedale ngolwe Sitatu nolwe Sine evekini
    ku February ngemini zesibini neyesitatu kuye nge 1876.

4.   Opumeleleyo kuya kufuneka ukuba imfundo yake ayiqube e-Lovedale
    ngalo lonke ixesha asayi fumanayo lomali ye bursari.

5.   Isimilo esingakolisiyo singenza ukuba apu- lukane nayo umntu nokuba
    kuninina—Abaya kwahlula londawo yoba yintlanganiso yabafundisi base
    Lovedale neya Bakangeli Bemali.

6.   Izinto abaya kuviwa kuzo abafuna ukulinga zoba zezi:—

(1) . Ekubaleni kopelwa nge Compound Pro­

  portion kuqukwa i-Vulgar ne Decimal Fractions.

(2) . I-Grammar yamangesi kunye ne Analysis.

(3) . I-Geography exela ukuma nokumelana

kwamazwe.

(4) . Imbali (a) Kweyase Great Britain kusu-

  selwa kumnyaka we 1603 kuye kowe 1709. (b) Eyale koloni sikuyo.

  (5). Izibalo Ezingcwele—Incwadi zika Samuel zombini neye Zenzo zaba
  Postile.

Isigidimi Sama-Xosa

NGO-MGQIBELO, MARCH 1, 1884.

IPESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

SIBANGWA zincwadi ezivela pesheya kwe Nciba, nazinteto esiziva futi
kumawetu angaponoshono kwalo mlambo ukuba sibuye senze amazwi pezu
kwengxoxo yokubuyiselwa kwalo mhlaba kwi Nkosazana. Kwinteto yetu
yokugqibela sabonisa mhlope ukuba le yeyona ngxoxo inkulu ngezi mini
pakati kwama Ngesi. Ngesati se inexesha ixoxwa emapepeni abamhlope ;
noninzi Iwabo selunendawo olugqibe kuzo malungana nayo. Liko iqela
elingavuya ngemihlali emikulu libona ukuba abamnyama abapesheya kwe
Nciba batatyatelwe pantsi kom’buso we Nkosazana kuba besiti umtwalo
onzima, opumle pezu kwamagxa olaulo Iwale Koloni wokwalusa ne Transkei,
boyisiwe, ngenxa yentswelo nobutataka, ukuba abemi beli lizwe bawutwale.
Liko iqela elingavumiyo ukuba lemihlaba incanywe ngamasatlari ze kungene
Inkosazana eti ukubapata abamnyama imane ibaboba. Kodwa kwinteto yake e
Cradock wenze mhlope u Mr. SCANLEN ukuba u Rulumeni owonganyelwe nguye
uya kuyingenisa imfanelo yokuba lento yenziwe kwangale Palamente ingati
ibeko ngo May.

Ukutsho ke, siti into le seyigqityiwe ngohlobo egqitywe ngalo
ngamagwangqa alapa e Koloni ; bantu ungaziwayo umbilini wabo
ngabamnyama—abakweli lizwe nabanga pesheya kwe Nciba (ngepesheya siquka
aba Tembu, nama Mfengu nama Baca). Lombali uke wahambela indawo ezininzi
ezimiwe ngumzi omnyama kwakule Koloni. Encokweni sifumana ukuba umxelo
womzi wakowetu ongaponoshono kwe Nciba ngongayitakazelayo indawo
enjengaleyo. Imvo zomzi ongapesheya asinamatuba okuzifumana kakuhle.
Asinamatanda ukuba imvo zawo zikwanye nezabahlobo nabazalwana bawo
abasala kweli—nabo bangayivuyela into yokubuya kwempato ye Nkosazana
pesheya. Ide yati yona incwadi ka Mr. JOHN MAZAMISA, osesinye sez’-
nonopu zelama Mfengu, yangenisa ukukanya ngendawo odulusele kuyo umxelo
wabelozwe. Nabo into leyo bangayitakazela. Imvo zomhlambi welaba Tembu
zingafunyanwa kwinteto zamanene awaye kwintlanganiso eyayimenywe ngu Mr.
W. E. STANFORD (U NDABENI), ezati, nakuba intlanganiso leyo
yayingahlanganiselwe kuxoxa lomcimbi zamana zipatelela kuwo. Indaba zalo
ntlanganiso siziva ngencwadana esiyitunyelwe ngumhlobo wetu ose
LukaIweni-Lwenyanga. Siyifaka njengoko injalo—

Ku MHLELI WENDABA.—Nkosi ndicela indawo kwelo pepa lako lendaba, zoba
mbalwa indawo endozikankanya ndizivakalise. Ngomhla we 19 ku February
besinentlanganiso apa e Batenji- ni pambi kwe Mantyi yetu u Ndabeni (Mr.
W. E. Stanford), esibizele ukuba alese incwadi pambi kwetu ebivela kwi
Mantyi enkulu (Chief Magistrate), Emtata, iyincwadi ke ekutiwa ibalwe
siti Batembu, sayitumela ku Rulumente sicela ukuba imantyi zonke
zigxotwe kushiyeke ibenye. Ite ke i Mantyi yakukov’ ukuyilesa incwadi
leyo yahlala pantsi emva kokuba ibixelile ukuba yona ayinanto iyakuteta
yona kuba yona ibicelwe yi Mantyi enkulu leyo ukuba yenjenjalo, yaza ke
yalindela ilizwi lenkosi nezibonda into eninzi eyayiko apo lomhla. Zaza
zapendula zonke izibonda zati, asiyazi loncwadi tina, siyazifuna imantyi
zetu. Kanjalo sinqwenela ukupatwa Pesheya yi Nkosazana. Yapendula
nenkosi yama Hala u Mgudlwa yati ndiyanukwa ngale ncwadi andiyazi,
andinanto ndirora yona ngemantyi, ngazwinye ziyatandwa imantyi kunye
nolaulo Iwa Pesheya. Ndingowako,—OBEKO.

Sinokuti ke lento yokubuyela kwempato kwi Nkosazana

iyavunywa, inqwenelwa nokunqwenelwa nangaba Tembu.

Ukuba kunjalo ke, ziya kuvakaliswa ninina ezimvo zomzi ontsundu ? Sesite
sizindla ukuba i Palamente iya kuhlangana ngenyanga engu May. Ukuba
sinyanisile, ixesha selicutekile. Ukuba ke ama Mfengu apesheya na
ponoshono kwe Nciba ayawunqwenela um’buso we Nkosazana akancokoli :
ukuba aba Tembu nabakwa Baca bakwanjalo —lemizi mayenze intlanganiso
kwangoku izivakalise imvo zayo izitumele e Palamente nase Ngilane
ngencwadi, ze iti ingxoxo isondezwa pambi kwe Palamente ibe imvo yayo
isaziwa. Wangena imvo zetu zingaziwa u Nqapela-Ndikule (Responsible
Government), mazaziwe ke namhla xa kuko ituba lokuba apeliswe kwisituba
esibanzi njenge Pesheya kwe Nciba. Xa tina ngokwetu singazikataziyo
ngawetu amalungelo nangeyetu inkululeko nangawetu amasiko, akuko uya
kuvela encwabeni ukuza kusilungiselela. Amadoda amakulu azibonda
mabinqele pezulu azifune azifumane imvo zabantu ngelituba azitumele
ngencwadi efanelekileyo kumadoda alaula kunye no Mr. SCANDEN nakwipakati
elikulu le Nkosazana u Right Hon. LORD DERBY. Uhlala umzi ulila usiti
awaziswa ngezi nguquleko zinkulu. Nantsi ke enye siyibika namhla!
Mayibekwe bucala inteto kwenziwe!

IMPAWANA.

Indaba ezingentlalo yesizwe sa Besutu asizezikolisa umxelo. Bayazi
abafundi betu ukuba apo e Lusutu kuko izikolo zebandla lase Roma. Ezi
zikolo zisand’ ukutyelelelwa ngumfundisi omkulu ohlala e Natal kutsha
nje. Egodukile uti u Most Rev. Dr. Jolivet ubafumene a Besutu beyinto
angazanga wababona beyiyo ngapambili. Esisimo sibangwe yimfazwe. Inkita
eninzi yabo izinikele ekuseleni indloloti nakwezinye inkazimlo zomhlaba.
Yimfuneko enkulu ukuba ulaulo Iwapesheya lumise imiteto eqinileyo kuba
olo hlanga luya kutsha- balala. Uti u Bishop Jolivetuke walifumana ituba
lokuhlangana no Letsea, no Masupha nezinye inkosana, zatembisa zonke
ukuba ziya kublala ngoxolo pantsi ko Rulumeni we Nkosazana. Lixesha
lodwa eliya kubonisa ubunyaniso bala madinga. Eka Dr. Jolivet inkolo
ifana neyetu, uti yena, imfundiso ngapandle kwengqoboko kukulinganisa
ezona nto zimdaka entlalweni yaba mhlope. Kulula ukusinga ezantsi. Inga
i Ramente zingabakumbula abase Lusutu kwizilingo abeyele kuzo.

Kuko ihlazo elimdaka elibeko e Aliwal North libangwa yimpi entsundu. Apa
e Aliwal sineqela elimnandi labamkeli nabafundi be Sigidimi. Apo
ulonakalo Iwehla kona kuse Herschel. Umbazo esingati siwubuze ekupeleni
kwale nteto siyakuwubekisa kwizihandiba zelo lase Gqili ezinjengo Mr.
August Bell. Ngomhla we 18 February e Gqili bekunyulwa ngabo banelungelo
amadoda amabini okuvakalisa imvo zomandla wase Aliwal e Palamente. Icala
elaziwayo ukuba linobuhlobo ngakwabamnyama, nelitwele ingcikivo ngenxa
yabo pakati kwamagwangqa lalisiwa livuka no Mr. Sauer no Mr. De Wet: elo
linga yonke imitwalo enzima nenemviko ingapezu. kobucopo bomnyama
lalisenza yonke imigudu yalo ukuba u Mr. Tamplin abe ngomnye wamadoda
atunyelwayo. Lo Mr. Tamplin mabe sezisaziwa imvo zake ngabafundi bezi
Mpawana. Kwenye yenteto zake zoburawu ngakubantu abamnyama wati tina
bantsundu asitwele rafu ngokwaneleyo, engene yena e Palamente uyakwenza
ukuba kubeko nerafu yencawa ezi siqutuma pantsi kwazo ! Wonke ke
omaziyo, kwanokuba ungowonqulo lomnumzetu u Sprigg, ubengati wopetshwa
njengokuba abantu bebengenjenjalo ukubetela kude kwindlu ezele inyoka.
Ngodaba esilufumana e Herschel siqonda ukuba kuko amashumi asixenxe
abamnyama awavota ukuba olutshaba Iwemfanelo zetu luye e Palamente, luye
kuquba amaqetseba obuqota-qikili okububisa tina kulo nkundla ! Ukuba ezi
bezisezimini zakudala beliya kuxelwa ibali kwizizukulwana ukuba sati
tina sakuziva ezindaba sarazula izivato zetu sahlala entlakantlakeni
naselututwini. Singa singakanyiselwa ke ngomnye wabase Gqili apo ukuba
imbangi yintonina ukuba lamashumi asixenxe abinze impi yonke emnyama
yase Africa ngokumshiya u Mr. Sauer. Lombuzo siwubekisa ku Mr. August
Bell ikakulu. Asifuni kushwabula ngapambi kokuba sizive izizatu zama
Mfengu ase Gqili.

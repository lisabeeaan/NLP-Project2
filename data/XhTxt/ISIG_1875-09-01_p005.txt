ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1875.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

Kwa Macelesi, July 8, 1875.

Kekaloku Mzalwaua wetu, Mr. E. J. War­ner, ngendiba ndiyakupendula ukuba
bendingumfo oqele ukupata usiba pakati kwenu apa E- Sigidimini, kuloko
andinguye, koko ngenxa yomnqweno endinawo andinakunceda ukuba udingaposi
ilizwana lam noko lilinye. Ukutsho nditi kuwe mzalwaua, ngati wena uteta
ngezivato, uti zilulobolo, nezidlo ukuba zikwalulobolo, kanjalo uti
usitsho ube usiti asintoyanto. Nganina pofu ukuba uti kusahanjiswa
amasiko obuheyideni. Manditi elokugqiba, ukulobola oku, kuyintonina?
Lento ke ukulobola, tina njengoko siluhlanga, yilento kusakuzeke
kutatyatwe usiba kubalwe nxa kutshatiswayo. Kwayekunjalo tina ukulobola
oku.

K. N. M. KWAKWA.

Esitongwane, July 9, 1875.

Kuko ibango, pakati kuka Makawula, no Mlilo- ntlo, uti umda luqolo
Iwetshungwane, kusapikiswe-=ne njalo, zezo nkosi zimbini. Kukwako elinye
ibango, pakati kuka Makawula, no Mqikela. Uti u-Mqikela u-Makawula
makangemki nomhlaba wakowabo, ukungena ku Government makaye
elukwatlambeni; apo wayevela kona uyise u- Ncapai makangemki nomhlaba
wakowabo, kuba u-Ncapai, waye ngene kowabo, emhlabeni kayise u-Faku.
Kuba uti u-Mqikela, kwati kwakufika Amatshaka bamana ukuwapepa ngecweba
ati akuwela kwelinye icala lecweba asinge kwelinye icala lecweba.

Wada wancama u-Tshaka, u-Faku uti kwabanjalo; naku Ncapai wamanaukuti,
akuvela ngelinye icala awele, wada wancama, u-Tshaka aye kwelinye. Uti
ke u-Mqikela, maka ngemki, nomhlaba wakowabo. Bona abazi nto bobabini,
no Makawula ; bebe sengabantwana. Akapenduli, nalinye u-Makawula;
kuleyonteto. Ukangele yena into eyakumqala, kupela. Kuba yena uyazi
ukuba uyise wakupa, ikulu lenkabi; ukuvela kwake elundini, ukungena
kwake kwa Faku. Zinjalo ezelilizwe indaba.

NDINGOWASE TSHUNGWANA.

UMBULELO WOMFOKAZANA.

E-Natal, May 31, 1875.

Ndiyakolwa ukuba kuvumelekile ukuba umfokazana abulele nakwabakulu
njengokuba ku- njalonaku Tixo. Ndinokubabulela u-Sobantu, no Somtsewu,
ngokuyitwala kwabo impikiswano yabo ngendaba ka Langalibalele ukuyisa
kwabo pambi ko Mgwebi u-Lord Carnarvon. Ukuyihlela kwake kakuhle lendaba
ka Langalibalele andazi ukuba ndingakufanisa nantonina. Ukuwalungisele
la kwake lamadoda omabini akubonakali ukuba kuko emke ikala pambi kwake.
Ebuninzini bamazwi alendaba ka Langalibalele ndingati azinkosi mabini?
Lelokuti kuka Lord Carnarvon ku Kumkanikazi ncipisa isohlwayo sika
Langalibalele. Nelokuti lika Kumkanikazi u-Langalibalele nonyana wake
ndiyabakupa esiqitini solwandle. Ulapoke umbulelo womfokazana. Lombuzo
uti ku Mahlubi nifanele ukutinina ukumbulela kwenu u-Sobantu,
ngalomsebenzi mkulu kangaka wokulitwala lonke ilizwe lase Natal
ngentloko yake ngenxayokupikela ukuba kusindiswe inkosi yenu, odele ubom
bake pezu kolwandle, ngenxa yayo, benifanele ukutinina ngo Lord
Carnarvon ngokuyicelela kwake inkosi yenu, kwi Nkosazana ukuba
isincipise isohlwayo esibekwe pezukwayo? Benifanele ukutinina ukumbulela
Umntanomhle u- Victoria ngokuyikupa kwake inkosi esiqitini So- lwandle
apo ibisitya isonka ngenyembezi, iwasela amanzi ngenyembezi, iwota
umlilo nge nyembezi. Apo ibinomkonto oyihlabe entliziyweni, emini na-
sebusuku. Mandishiye apo Mahlubi.

Ngenye indlela kusendaweni yetu sonke tina luhlanga oluntsundu lwase
South Africa ukuba sibulele ukuhlala kwetu ngapantsi kolondolozo Iwa
Mangesi. Bekisisani kakulu luhlanga lwakowetu into enditsho ngayo. Nditi
okukulwa besisilwa na Mangesi ngeminyaka edluleyo ukuba ebetanda
ukusenza amakoboka ngesisekona ebuntwini ? Ndisitsho nje nditsho ngokuba
bekuti kwakulwa abamhlope nabantsundu lusuke lonke usapo Iwetu kunye
nonina nalo lusiya kubelungu luya kucela ukutya, sibe tina madoda
sixinwe lihlati. Ukuba abelungu bebelutabata usapo lwetu balufake
emikombeni baye kutengisa ngalo kwezinye izizwe ngesibuza kubanina ukuti
ulusepina usapo lwetu? Mandivalelise ngelokuti Nkosi kangela
Ukumkanikazi wetu u-Victoria. Kulisa amapiko ake ukuze ayisibekele yonke
I- Africa ukuze ibe ngapantsi komtunzi wawo. Ka­ngela u-Lord Carnarvon
nezinye izikulu akunye nazo. Ubongeze ngobulumko pezu kobo banabo.

E-Natal, July 19, 1875.

Ndivuyile Mr Jesse Shaw, ngokusipendula kwako ngalombuzo wam
kakugqoboka. Mandikuyale nam ndiziyale ukuba kungabiko ukudubulana
pakati kwetu sobabini, ngokupendulana kwetu. Mandibonise eyam intando,
nditanda ukuba abantu bayiqonde igqoboko. Njengokuba inkolo ne nguquko
ziqondeka, Nokuxolelwa.

Mandisibulele isilinganiso sako nge Fatyi. Sihle kakulu endaweni ye
Fatyi. Umbuzo wam uti ukugqoboka komntu kuyintonina ngapandle kwe- lizwi
lomenzi wetu. Njengokuba ukukolwa, noku- guquka, nokuxolelwa, ezozinto
ziko ngapandle kwelizwi, ndinokumbuza umntu ukuba nditeta naye ngokuti,
uya kolwana ngala mazwi am? ati ewe, ndinokuti inkosi yako yakuxolelana
etyaleni? Ati ewe. Ndinokuti, uguquke ninina ekuteni ? Ati ngomhla
otile. Manditinina ukumbuza ngogqoboko? Ndibuzakuwe ke Mr. J. Shaw.
Masiziyek’ ezingapandle komntu izinto.

Empofu, August, 16,1875.

Nkosi yam Mhleli we Sigidimi Samaxosa ndincede undibekele lamazwana
kulemvaba yamapakati. Bekisa kuzo zonke Iremente zako wetu ngakumbi
kuleyo yase Kamastone leyo eteta ngolwaluko iti yona iyalupelisa, buzake
kuyo ukuba iyazina lento ilulwaluko ayiveli apa ivela Eluhlangeni, yinto
esinge nakupanda ngeyetu inteto, asinakuyinqika ngeletu iqinga, kuba
abantwana betu baya kusoyisa pakati kwezo ntlanganiso zetu. Nalo iqinga
lokutshabalalisa ulwaluko liveliswa ngabafundisi kulo ntlanganiso
yomnyaka ebise Qonce ngolwesi 7 nangolwesi 8 ku July 1875. Siti
alusayikupela ulwaluko ngapandle kwokuba kubekwe igama lika govern­ment
kuzo zonke indawo ukuba ulwaluko lungabiko, bangabiko abakweta apa E
koloni, bangavunyelwa bati abafunyenweyo banikwe ityala egameni lake
u-government.

  K. GIDO.

UKUVULWA KWE TYALIKE EBENCUTI.

Ngomhla 6 ka August, 1875, ngolwesihlanu evekini, letyalike ibihlaziywa
isenziwa ukuba ibe- ntle kuba yayenziwe ngokudala. Kute kusasa ngolwe
Sihlanu kwako umsebenzi, intlanganiso yapatwa ngu Rev. C. White
washumayela encwadini ka Yisaya 46 : 10, yati yakupuma intlanganiso
kwangena into enkulu yetimitini, ukuti kwami yinto enkulu kukuba
kwakuzele, belapa o-Mhlontlo no Ntsantsi no Madolo no Mntonintshi
namakosi ase Mampondweni. Endingasendinqamlela kwelokuti kungene amaqela
amabini ukutya esosidlo, kwaye kungenwa ngesheleni umntu ngamnye.
Sagqitywa ke kwapunywa, Kute kwabikwa ukuba kuyakungenwa ngokuhlwa
ukute- twa ngemali yokulungisa lendlu. Okunene lite

lakufika eloxesha yabetwa intsimbi yada yangena intlanganiso, kwakucelwe
ukuba isihlalo sitatwe ngu F. P. Gladwin Esq. Okunene kwabanjalo,
yavulwa ngu Rev. W. S. Davis ngeculo kwatandazwa ngu Mr. P. Tuta. Emva
koku osesi hlalweni utete ilizwi lokuti ndiyavuya ngenxa yalomsebenzi
wenzekileyo wokulungiswa kwalendlu, nangenxa yalentlanganiso inkulu
kangaka. Ndite ndakufika pezolo akuya kundibonisa u-Rev. W. S. Davis
lendlu ndetuka, ndati asiyiyona leya beke ndiyingene, ndakubona izihlalo
zayo ukugangatwa kwayo pantsi, kunye nengcango zayo. Wenjenjalo ke
wabonakalisa ityala layo, wati ityala lalendlu elifuneka lirolwa
ngobubusuku liziponti ezilikulu elinamashumi matatu anesibini (£132),
wati ke lonto yintwana encinane esinokuyifeza ngabo obubusuku, nditi
lomalana yintwana encinane enokurolwa siti ngoku ; watsho waxela elake
inaui alirolayo waxela £7 10s, wahlala pantsi keyena. Yaselisuka inkosi
Umhlontlo yarola £10, kwabanjalo ke wangu washiywa, kwakuko nabamhlope
abancedayo kunene ukunyusa lomnikelo, kwa umfundisi wetu ne nkosikazi
yake, nompati wevenkile, ndibancomake ukunyusa lomnikelo. Kwada
kwanqanyulwa kwatiwa make kuyekwe kubalwe, kwafunyanwa imali eroliweyo,
iziponti ezilikulu elinamashumi asixenxe aneponti ezimbini ngapandle
(£172), yabonakala ukuba igqite ngamashumi amane, wati u-Rev. W. S.
Davis yekani obe sacinga ukunikela makagoduke nayo, ndiyavuya kakulu
okokuba bendisiti asinakuyifeza lemali ifunekayo kulendlu. Ndiyavuya ke
ngokuba siyifezile watsho ke wayigqiba intlanganiso.

P. TUTA.

Cradock, August 16, 1875.

Kute ngemini etile kwabanjwa abaiazi betwele inkuni namalongo kwatiwa
akufuneki ukuba abantu abantsundu bateze inkuni nokuba lilawu kufuneka
betenge e-Markeni. Hai kwesuka wonke umzi wabantsundu kwanama Lawu ukuya
kucela inkuni kwikomsasi zalapa zati ukubapendula asazinto tina kutshiwo
ngumpati wenu ongu Mr. William Allen oti maningatezi nkuni nitenge
njengokuba naye etenga, yati enye engu Mr. Cawood ikomsasi hambani niye
kuzicela ku Tixo inkuni, basuka babuza indlela yokunyuka wati mabanyuke
ngentambo bayicela kuye wasuka wabazimela akuva ukuba bafuna indlela
kuye. Injalo lonto apa kwaliwa nokuba umntu acole amalongo odwa.

Nitinina nina niteta ngokwaluka niti kuyakuza kupelena pakati kohlanga
lwakowetu kuba ndisitsho ndivumela umzalwana owaka wati ukwaluka
akunakupela kuba abantu batukwa kwangama krestukazi. Ukutsho apa kuko
amantombazana atuke umfana ngobukwenkwe kodwa lomfana akabonanga eteta
nento kulomantombazana afana amcunukela, enye yalomantombazana
ingumramentekazi omdala ebuko- Iweni nabazali bayo. Nabo kwada kwalusizi
ukumtuka kwabo maxana ezibuzela ukuba ndenze ntonina kanene. Nditsho
ukuti singateta ngako akusokuze kwapela ukuba kunjalo ukuba bati
abantwana abafundisiweyo batete njalo. Lamantombazana ebehamba esikuleni
kungoku apumileyo kuso.

Ndivuyile ukuva ukuba Inkosi yabatembu u- Ngangelizwe ubeteta ekuvulweni
kwetyalike. Yinkosi enayo nemisebenzi ebulelekayo ngokuba ngexesha lake
zininzi ityalike ezakiwe e-Batenjini kwanokunikela kwabatembu kakuhle
ezityalikeni zabo ngokubonela kuye. Ilizwe livange ngetyalike
ebatenjini, zityalike ngetyalike, oku kukuti iremente ngeremente.

Ibi yinkosi etanda umfundisi wayo ose Mgwali ngokukodwa, ebatanda bonke.

lemisebenzi ikange- iwa ngamagosa; xa epakati kwabantsu- ndu uyenza
ngemali emiselwe yona. Akayenzi ngemali epuma kweyake inxhowa. Kodwa
yena ontsundu uye- nza ngemali yake. Andiboni mona ke mna ekutengiseni
ngeziziuto okokuba azokubuyisa intwana yemali yake.

EZE ZITILI NGEZITILI.

CUMAKALA. — Ibiyinto engabukelwa ngumntu obepulapula inteto ekusela
intshutshiso ka Donga, no Swaartbooy, no Bobotyana ebisenziwa ngu Mr.
Bousfield umteteleli wale ukundla pambi kwemantyi encedisayo u Mr. F. H.
Hewett—ongazanga umntu omnyama oza pambi kwake alindele ukuba angabina-
tyala nokuba selengade eze namangqina alikulu. Uvalelwe pandle namhla
ngu Gxobolekile. Wabakulula ngentliziyo eyopayo. “ Uvile Gadela,
ugityiselwe.”— U Mr. John Stephanus Lokwe walapa ufungiswe namhlanje
pambi kwemantyi yalapa ukuba ungu Msila oncedisayo walenkundla (Deputy
Messenger).

E BURGHERSDORP. — Kungene umfo etyalikeni ofumane watyibilika, wawa pezu
komfundisi. Ubanjwe kwalapo ngoku ukwityalike yabaxibiliki—intolo-
ngo.—Kunconywa ukubalela e Gqili.

HERSCHEL. — Olwesi-Ne, 5 January, ibingumhla omkulu kwizizwe zase Gqili,
kubekungeniswa amagama pambi ko Mantyi. Lamagama ashiywe kuba umhlaba
ungakangelelwe xabisweni ku- sitiwa lidlelo nobuzwe. Abantsun d u
bebemelwe ngu Mr. Horace Halse ote ubangenisa ngo Sec. 8. Constitution
Ordinance kuba bengabantu base Koloni abangena buzwe bahlukileyo, aye
ama- simi abawalimayo engemadlelo, amawa- kangelelwe exabisweni ngeko.
Akugqi- tywanga ntweni ngalomini ibimele ku- hlala kwakona i Felkornet
ngo-Mvulo. Amawetu azimisele ukuyipanda lendawo yobuzwe.

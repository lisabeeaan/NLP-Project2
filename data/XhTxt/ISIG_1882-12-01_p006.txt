UMANYANO E COLESBERG.

Lengxelo siyifumene kumbaleli onexabiso wase Colesberg Bekuko neyomnye
umbali kwa eyale ntlanganiso, kodwa enye yanele

Ndiya kolwa ukuba uya kuvuyakunye naba lesi besi Gidimi ukuva
ngomsebenzi olungileyo owenziwe e Colesberg.

Ekupeleni kwe nyanga engu August u Mr. Mzozoyana wafumana incwadi ivela
ko Messrs S. P. Sihlali, W. P. Momoti, no M. Pelem bebika intlanganiso
yomanyano yabantu abantsundu eya kuba se Bayi. Ute yena kwa oko wazisa
umzi. Ngomhla wa 30 October kubeko intlanganiso, kweza iqela elinobom
lamadoda. U Mr. Edward King ubeke kwi ntlanganiso (proposed) ukuba u Mr.
Mzozoyana utate isihlalo acacise into engayo lentlanganiso, usekelwe ngu
Mr. Swanepoel, waduma umhlambi.

MR. MZOZOYANA (Chairman):—Kuluvuyo kum ukuma apa noku bona eli qela
lingaka. Noko ndinosizi ngento yokuba ndiza kuteta nge nteto yase mzini
kuni, isi Bulu. Isiqalo nesiseko sale ntlanganiso sivela kuma doda
akowetu esiwacingela ukuba anengqondo, nemvisiso yokumeka nokwenziwa
kwezinto ehlabatini. Tina bantu bantsundu asinanto siyaziyo,
siyiqondayo, sizilungiselela yona njengama Ngesi. Nto siyaziyo kusibenza
ukuba sifumane into yokutya nempahlana eyimfuyo— ngapaya asinanto
sizilungiselele yona, asinaqinga silicebayo, asinanto siyenzayo
elilungelo letu ngoku nangexesha elizayo, neya kuba lilungelo kuba
ntwana baba ntwana betu. Le yintlanganiso yoku vula amehlo nengqondo
yohlanga, yeyoku sikupa ebunyameni nasebu dengeni; yeyoku pelisa inteto
eti mna ndiyi Mfengu ndingum Xosa, ndili Bastile, ndingu Msutu, ndingum
Rolong; andizange ndikonze, ke le ntlanganiso iti, masibe banye,
sivelane. Mandipele ke bazalwana ngeliti kukuni ke ukuyi gweba
lentlanganiso. kokwenu ukuyamkela— mna kodwa okwam nditi ilungile,
sendiya mkele kade.

MR. C. Z. NTOZINI Into esingayo ngokuhlwa nje seyi cacisiwe. Ukuba
sipume sivumelene ngokuba nati e Colesberg sibe nentlanganiso elisebe
lale isekwa e Bayi, n okwe nyaniso abantu base Colesberg bakuba benze
into ebinge ibalulekile kwinto zoku kanya, zoku nyusa uhlanga.
Intlanganiso iya kuba sikolo esikulu ngoku bekisele kwinto zolaulo—I
Responsible Government into eyiyo soyi qonda ngoku ngapezulu. Woba
ngumsebenzi wentlanganiso ukumana icela amalungu amele i Colesberg e
Parliament, ukuva uluvo lwawo nge Micimbi yaba Ntsundu ; amalungu e
Ministry elaulayo yohlangana nawo kuko ituba; intlanganiso iya kuzamela
ukuba wonke ontsundu afanelwe kuvota abekwe kwincwadi yabanyuli
bamalungu e Parliament—ukuze sandise e Parliament inani lama Ngesi
elivelana nati. Amalungu entlanganiso yetu obako kwezama Ngesi—uvoto
namhla siya kulwenza ngendlela yalo. Intlanganiso yama Bulu e Cradock
igqibe kweliti i Government mayincipise imali encedisa ngayo izikolo
zabamnyama— Into ke leyo eya kubangela ukuba intsapo yenu ifundiswe zi
Teachers ezizidenge. Intlanganiso iya kuyikangela iyicase into enje
ngale. Masipume sivumelene ngokubako “ kwesebe ” lentlanganiso e
Colesberg

MR. Moss SWANEPOEL:—Intlanganiso enje ngale kumzuzu ndiyi nqwenela—yanga
ingema—sisinge pambili.

MR. ISAAC JOHANNES:—Ixesha eli lonke besisitwe litinzi lobunyama,
lobudenge. intlanganiso iza nokukanya—masamkele itamsanqa.

Atetile namanye amanene anjengo Mr. Martinus no Mr. Arrie Fortuin.

U MR. SMILE CAMPBELL ubeke (proposed) ukuba Intlanganiso Yomanyano lwaba
Ntsundu apa e Colesberg imiswe kususela kweli lesha, ati onke lamanene
abe ngamalungu ayo.— Usekelwe ngu Mr. C. Z. Ntozini—kwavunyelwana.

Umsebenzi wokunyula amagosa wamiselwa umhla we 7 November. U Mr. C. Z.
Ntozini ukusa kulomini eyi Secretary.

Ifikile i 7 November ilangazelelwa ngumntu wonke, amalungu entlanganiso
yomanyano eko onke neqela elitsha elize kungena. Kuvunyelwene ngokuba
ngobo busuku intlanganiso yonganyelwe ngu Mr. Mzozoyana. Wenze amazwi
okukutaza amadoda. Umsebenzi we­ntlanganiso yangapambili uleseshiwe.

U MR. JANUARY JANUARY ute “propose” u Mr. Moss Swanepoel ukuba yi
Chairman ye “sebe” lentlanganiso yomanyano e Colesberg— Usekelwe ngu MR.
JAN CORNELIUS.—Kwavunyelwana.

U MR. JOHN EDWARD ute “propose” u Mr. C. Z. Ntozini abe yi
Secretary.—Usekelwe ngu Mr. January January.—Waduma umhlambi. U Mr.
Ntozini uvelise indawo ezingamxakayo kumsebenzi wolu hlobo—zibonakele
izizatu kwintlanganiso.

U MR. C. Z. NTOZINI ute “propose” u Mr. Mzozoyana ukuba yi
Secretary.—Usekelwe ngu Mr. Edward King.—Kwavunyelwana.

Committee—January January, Edward King, Smile Campbell, Arrie Fortuin,
John Edward, Andries Panise.

N.B.—Amalungu entlanganiso ngoku nga 44.

KUVAKALA ukuba ubukunikani bama Bulu obuse Trans­vaal buke baqekeka,
esingaziyo sibala nje ukuba bude batinina. Umkosi ose mazantsi awuvumi
kunceda ose mantla. Kakade lento iyabavuyisa abamnyama belozwe, abati
abasoku hlaula rafu Bulwini, ngapandle ko Mr. Hendrique Shepstone, obe
ngu Mpatiswa Micimbi yabo oko i Transzaal ibise pantsi kolaulo lwama
Ngesi.

umsebenzi wokuzi kanyela wokuba zi Agent zesi Gidimi:—Mr. John W. Nkomo,
Humansdorp ; Mr. P. Mtyoba, Southeyville; Mr. Anderton Ngesi,
Farmerfield. Sinosizi ukuvakalisa udano lwetu ngendlela awuquba ngayo
amagosa amabini, amatatu apetwe kodwa lelase Bedford. La akabonakalisi
kuzi zamazamisa—into leyo ke edanisayo.

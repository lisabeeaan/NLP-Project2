NGOLWESI-NE, MARCH 26, 1891.

ICEBO LOMMELI.

  PEPA lase Komani ekutiwa yi
  Representative, elingumlomo

ka Mr. BAILEY, livelise icebo lokuba—makasikwe amagama abantsundu
emqulwini wabavoti kuba bengamnanzanga u Mr. BAILEY kulomnyhadala
ugqitileyo, koko besuke banyula u Messrs. PEACOCK no BRADFIELD. Nasi
isikalaso

omangalelwa ngaso umzi wakowetu
ontsundu, liti: “Ngemigudu emikulu yomzi wase Mlungwini umanyene,
kupumelele indoda yanye kupela esitatwini samadoda ebekufuneka eye
kumela umzi kwi Ndlu ye Ngwevu, isibini ke sinyulwe ngabantsundu.”
Elipepa ke xa litshoyo liyilibele indawo yokuba kade likala ngokuti
Zontatu ivoti zenu maziposwe ku BHELi,” belifuna ke oku kakade.
Kukaninzi kwalona elopepa lase Komani, kwanabunye abantu ababe ziduba
intloko ngo BHELI, baye bexelelwa lipepa lase kay’ apa I Cape Mercury,
okokuba kufuneka isitatu samadoda Kwindlu ye Ngwevu. Kodwa yonke lonto
ayikatalelwanga bani, basuka balilisela ngo BHELI kwakupela. Nantso ke
into ebifuneka kweloqela, kuba lona belijonge indoda enye ukuba ibe yiyo
emayinyulwe, elixesha bona abavoti abangqondo zivutiweyo besuke bayibona
kakuhle indawo yokuba makunyulwe amadoda lamabini ango PIKOKO no
BRADFELE, akufaneleyo ukuya kumela eli lase Mpumalanga. Bakwenzile ke
oko bekufuneka. Eliqela libe likala ngo BHELI malibe lifumene itamsanqa
elikulu kuba kaloku limngenisile umntu elibe likade limfuna. Ndawana
ingummangaliso kukuba angabi ukabo le ko Messrs. PEACOCK no BRADFIELD,
malibo eliqela belilisela ngokuba “ Zontatu ku “ BHELI ” belilincinane
mpela kunengxolo ebeliyenza. Sakukangela ukuma kwala madoda matatu o
Messrs. BAILEY, BRADFIELD, no PEACOCK, sifumana eshiyene kancinane
kangangokuba kunqabe ukuchana ukuba yiyipina eyakuba pambili akufika
amanani evela e Kapa. Lonto yodwa ibonisa ukuba akunyaniso okutetwa yi
Representative xa iti u Mr. BAILEY upunyezwe zivoti zabamhlope, ekubeni
o Messrs. PEACOCK no BRADFIELD bepunyeleliswe ngabantsundu kupela. Eyona
nyaniso yile, umzi ontsundu, njenge siqelo sawo, ufike umzi omhlope
ubambene ngendawo yokuba ngo Messrs. PEACOCK no BRADFIELD, okanye ngu
Mr. BAILEY na amabamele esi sitili e Palamente. Qondani ke, lamacala
abambeneyo ngawase mlungwini omabini ; ufikeke umzi wakowetu wayilamla
lento ngokuzinyulela icala ofanelwe lilo. Ute ke emveni kokuqonda izimvo
zalamadoda matatu waselutyumba ngapandle kwamatandabuzo u PIKOKOno
BRADFELE okokuba ngabo abakufaneleyo ukuya kumela eli lase Mpumalanga
kwindlu yo Ngwevu kuno BHELI. Ekubeni ke o Messrs. BAILEY, BRADFIELD, no
PEACOCK benyuliwe, kuyakuba yimfanelo ye Bandla ukugweba ukuba
Abantsundu benza imposisona ekunyuleni lamadoda mabini, ekubeni
abatshabisi bubo benga wonke umntu angakolwa
ukuba kunjalo. Besingake lendawo ingalanyulwa msinyane pakati kwetu
nelipepa lase Komani, ngokuti i Bandla lise libekela entloko malunga
nelipiko. Kodwa kona ukuteta ngokukupa abantu ekubeni ngabavoti kuba
besuke benza into emiswe ngumteto, ukuba banyule lowo bakolwa nguye,
kuti ngati yeyona ncakam yobudenge. Elipepa lase Komani kwandawonye nabo
libameleyo, mhlaimbi abazi ukuba tina elilungelo lokuvota salinikwa yi
Nkosazana ingavumi yena i Palamente yalo Koloni. Akuko nanye i Palamente
abangayibulelayo ngelilungelo; kuba okokuba bekuya ngokoninzi lwayo le
Palamente ngesekudala sasikwayo emqulwini wabavotayo. Ekunikweni kwale
Koloni ilungelo lokuzipata, yaba yenye yendawo ezipambili le yokuba
namawetu maze abe nezwi entweni zombuso, kuba leyo iyeyona ndlela
abangaba nako ngayo ukukuselu amalungelo abo. Nxako ngaba kukangelwa
indawo yokuba elilungelo balisebenzise ngemfane lona, siyatemba okokuba
I Ruluneli yosivumela kwindawo yokuba akuko qela liyisebenzise kakuhle,
ngomanyano oluhle, nangokulipakamisa elilungelo liyi voti, njegommiselo
wale Koloni, kunati Bantsundu.

  Imposiso Zomqulu Wabavoti.

INCWADI yomqulu wabavoti
base Qonce ebekuvotwa ngayo ngolunyulo lugqitileyo ityilo ubumenemene
obungummangaliso. Amagama abantu abaninzi akabhalwanga, kanti abaniniwo
baye bezifake ngokuqinisekileyo ngobhalo
lo 1889-90. Akafakwanga encwadini ngohlobo olungaqondekiyo. Nokokubana
zizipatamandla zesi siqingata, okanye ngabashicileli baka Rulumente e
Kapa abanetyala, kufuneka kucikidwe kakuhle; kanamanjazo siya kubhinqela
pezulu ukuba lendawo mayifezeke iqutywe ngu Rulumeni ide iqonda kale
mhlope indlela yayo, ukuba ngaba kuyakufuneka ukuba u Rulumente
ashukunyiswe ukuze enze imfanelo yake emhlope malunga nokukuqataniselwa
kunje.

Apo lendawo isimangalisileyo kukuti ingabi kupela ibe kushiyeke abantu
abaye beye kuzifaka kwi Felkornet, kodwa bade nabantu abaye beze pambi
kwe Mantyi u Mr. CHALMERS ngomhla abebemenyelwe ukuza kubhenela
ukungafakwa zi felkornet kwamagama abo encwadini yabavoti, nokumisa
amabango emfaneleko zabo ukuba bangeniswe, bazake bapumelela kwabonakala
befakwa, kodwa ngendlela engaqondakaliyo namhla abako emqulwini
wabavotayo kuba amagama abo engashicilelwanga. Asikuko ke nokuba
lentsebenzo ihambe ishunquka ekufezeni imfanelo yababhali-magama
iyacapukisa. Siyakolwa ukuba uninzi lwamakowetu lwenzakele yilendawo ;
nalowo ube lisango lamaqinga okusika amagama amakowetu emqulwini
wabavoti ubefanelwe sisohlwayo esifanele onke amatshivela anje ngaye.
Sinobunqhina obucacileyo esiqamele ngabo nxa senjenjeke ukuteta. Soka
sitabate isitili sibe sinye; kubonakele ukuba i Felkornet zawabhala onke
amagama aba­ntu abafanelekileyo ukuba ngaba­voti, zaza zawazisa pambi ko
Mantyi epelele. Ke ityala lokungabonakali kwawo emqulwini lipakati
kwababhali base Ofisini bo Mantyi nako Messrs. W. A. RICHARDS & SONS,
bona bangabashicileli bakwa Rulumente e Kapa, nempengululo emayenziwe
nokuba kutenina, ize ichane kakuhle abona bantu amabatwale lengxowa
yelityala. Kuba ke lento sitshoyo akuko bantu bokudlala. ukanti
bebulaleka nje babulalekela ilize.

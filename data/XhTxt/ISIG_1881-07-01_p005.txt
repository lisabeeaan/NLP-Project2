Ukufuneka kwokangelo lwezi kolo futi.

Ndiyakumbula kwi minyaka emitatu egqitileyo uhambo lwam kunye nomfundisi
wesikolo endandiya kusikangela sikwapantsi kwogcino lwake. Abantwana
ndandi beva kwi Geography, kodwa akwabiko nento abanoku ndixelela yona
aba bantwana, wavakala umfundisi esiti, O! fan’ ukuba usapo luya koyika,
kaunike mna ndiyakolwa kungabako into abayixelayo, ndamnika ke. Waqala
ngokuti, “He ndixeleleni igama lesi siqiti,” qutu akwavakala nezwi e
klasini, "kukuti nqa, niya sazi, bendinixelele apa kulecawa igqitileyo,”
cwaka, “ndanixelela ntoni nge Sardines?” Yavakala isiti yonke i klasi
“Sardinia.” Wababuza enye indawo, kwafuneka ukuba apinde angene kweza
zikumbuzo, ekute emveni kokuba zenziwe zonke ezo zikumbuzo yandula
ukufika impendulo. Ndambulela umhlobo wam ndamxelela kodwa ukuba nam
ngalondlela ndingade ndibe nempendulo endiyifumanayo. Konke oku ke
kubonakalisa imfanelo enkulu yokuba izikolo zikangelwe futi.

Ukunga pumi kwe mail ye Sikolo.

Imali ye sikolo yenye into ekataza abafundisi abanga bongameli bazo.
Ndiyaxelelwa ukuba abantu aba Ntsundu banikela emisebenzini ye tyalike
ngoku kululekileyo, kodwa bano moya omkulu wokuba yimfanelo ka Rulumente
nabafundisi ukufundisa abantwana babo, ndicinga ukuba obubunzima nge
mali buyakuhle bupele ukuba kwezinye izikolo kutatyatwe iqinga eli
lungileyo elenziwa zizikolo zase Mgqakwebe, e Mkubiso nase Mtwaku,
lokuba kupa esikolweni bonke abantwana abanga semva ngemali.

Kunzima pezu kwe Teacher, ukuba zingayifumani imali esisixaso sazo,
ngenxa yokuba abazali aba Ntsundu bengavumi ukuhlaulela imfundo
yabantwana babo. Enye i Teacher yandixelela ukuba imali ayibanjwa
ngabazali ye sikolo nga £30, abanye ababini bati babanjwa i £19 emnye.
Njengokuba kungatandeki ukuba i Teacher mazi yifumane imali yazo ngokuya
ematyaleni, kufanelwe ukwenziwa indawo yokuba zincipe ezindleko
zingakanana kwi Teacher.

Imfundo yama ntombozana.

Ndiyavuya ukubona ukuba imfundo yama ntombazana ifumana inkatalelo
ngoku.

Izindlu ze zikolo nempahla yazo yangapakati.

Izakiwo nempahla zanga pakati ze seminary zaba Ntsundu zilungeile. E
Newlands kwakiwe indlu entsha yesikolo, e Lovedale kwakiwe izakiwo
ezikulu, nase Mtwaku, nase Rini kuko indawo ezilungisiweyo zandiswa.
Kodwa izindlu zezikolo zanga pandle zakiwe nge zinti no daka zine
mpahlana encinane yangapakati, zimbalwa kwezo zikolo ezine ndawo
zokubalela (desks) ezi ngapezu kwesibini kodwa izihlalo zibako kuba
ezizindlu kwenzelwa kuzo inkonzo nge Cawa. Inkoliso yabantwana ihlala
ngapandle kwe sleti ne kopi.

Esendikutetile ngobunzima bokufumana imali yesikolo, kuyabonakala ke
ukuba aba Ntsundu aba nalu nikelo ekwakiweni nasempahleni ye zikolo
zabo.

Ngako oko mandipinde nditi makungabiko luncedo lutsha luka Rulumente
lunikelwayo, ngapandle kokuba ati lowo olufunayo abonakalise ukuba indlu
leyo okuza kwenziwa kuyo isikolo iwufanele lomsebenzi.

IMINI YE NKOSAZANA.

E ZOLO, PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.

BEKUKO Ezolo umdlalo wabantwana besikula, obusenziwa yi teacher kunye
nosapo lwe sikula. Lomdlalo ubehle wabonakala ngezidlo okokuqa mkulu,
kuba lute usapo lwarola inkabi zegusha zantandatu, yati iswekile ne kofu
nebotolo kunye nezinye izidlwana ezimnandi ezibe zenziwe zintombi
namanye amakosikazi yayinto eninzi. Lomdlalo i Teacher ibilucele usapo
ngokuti masigcine usuku lokuzalwa kwe Nkasazana u Queen Victoria. Ite
yahlanganisana yonke into engumntu wase Zolo apa kwada kwako nabanye
abazalwane bezinye indawo. Kude ke okwenene kwafika ixesha lokuba
kutyiwe. Lwayeke usapo lumana luvuma lengoma “God save the Queen” kude
kwase kubeni kugqitywe ukutyiwa ezonyama, nomingqusho.

Kusuke inkosikazi etile u Mrs. Kotyana Gidigidi weza kumfundisi wentsapo
wati nantsi intwana yemali uze utenge into oyitandayo. Bateke abantu
bakubona lonto bawenza nabo amabaso beteta kwalonto. Emzuzwini usukile u
Teacher wosapo, wati itini tu nantsi into: tina apa sinetyalike

entsha eyakiwayo, ngako kufanele ukuba sincede kulomsebenzi ngenkozo
zemali ukuze kufezwe indawo ezingaba ziya funeka.

Kusuke omdala wasesi Gubudweni u Maliwa Ntlati, wati, lento ndiyibonayo
nendiyivayo inkulu, kuba abantwana base Zolo, bafana negama lomlambo
wabo ukuzola kwabo watsho wati ndirola 2s. 6d., mawuhambe lomsebenzi.

Kusuke u Lambata Mgidi, inkosi yase Zolo, nomdala weremente wati, lento
siyayi bulela xa usapo lwetu lunentlanganiso enje ukulunga kwayo.

Kusuke u Siko Dudumashe, wateta ukuti, lusapo, qinisani imfundo, kwaye
kuya kunilungela.

Kusuke u John Ntintili, wati, kuyinto emnandi ukubona usapo lute cwaka
lupulapula amazwi atetwa ngabazali.

Kusuke i Teacher yal’ usapo u Fumba Sidziya, wati, yabonani bantwana,
fisani amasiko amadala oyihlo benu, ukuze nikule ekwazini okuyimfanelo,
hayi into imbi ukuti kanti nihleli nje umntwana akatembeki kubazali
bake, ngenxa yokuba amasiko amadala ehleli pakati kwenu. Bemana beteta
abantu bendawo ngendawo beyala usapo, lwaye ke usapo lute roqo ngokurola
imali negusha nebokwe, lwada lwagqiba imali eyaba lishumi leponti £10 0
0. Kwatiwa ke lemali yeyoncediso kwinto efunekayo etyalikeni, yahamba
lontlanganiso kwada kwapuma ilanga kumana kushunyayelwa kutandazwa,
wangumdlalo oyole kunene.

Siya temba ukuba olusawo lwenze into efanelekileyo, ukwenza kwalo indawo
yokuziyolisa isuke yazalisa intliziyo zabazali ngovuyo; lwaye ke usapo
lunxibe kakuhle kanye. Sigqiba ngelokuti lwanga olusapo lungahambisela
pambili.

LAMBATA MGIDI.

UMFO OSIMILO SIBURALARUME.

KUKO pakati kwama Bulu amabulu anobubele enobuhlobo kwa basemzini,
inxenye izidla kunene ivisa intlungu. Nantsi inteto etetwa ngalama Bulu
azidla kunene avana nokuvisa intlungu. Sibalisa sinosizi nge Bulu
elalivela e Humansdorp, elanduluka ngolwesitatu e Somerset lisiya
kurweba. Lite kuba lalingalazi elozwe kwanendlela ezi pambukayo laqasha
aba Ntsundu ababini, omnye abe ngumkokeli omnye ancedise elu hambeni. Lo
ke ungumncedisi waye kwele enqweleni ehleli ngapambili, lite i Bulu
yakufika inqwelo kwindawo ehlayo makatsibe pantsi ablekishe ibleki. Ute
akubon’ukuba uyehla utyibilikile wawa pambi kwomkono yatyunyuzwa
imilenze yake yinqwelo. Yabi busuku obubanda kakulu, lamshiya apo i Bulu
laqonda ukuquba inqwelo yalo, livuma ngom lozi ingoma zakwa lizwi.
Wafunyanwa kusasa lomfo emayela ekuqaukeni, watwalwa wasiwa kwi fama ka
Mr. Watson, bafika apo kuko abantu abakolwayo.

EZIVELA KUBA BALELANI.

Incwadi ezi sukuba zibalelwa ukufakwa e Sigidimini, kufuneka u Mhleli
yena elazi igama lo mtumeli ncwadi, nakuba lingayi ku shicilelwa ukuba
umninilo akalifuni ukuba laziwe luninzi.

Siyazisa kanjako ukuba asilitabateli pezu kwetu ityala lenteto zaba
balelani betu, asitsho nokuti siyavumelana nazo zonke incwadi zabo
esizishicilelayo.

Tsomo, June 24th, 1881.

NKOSI MHLELI :—

Ndiyakucela ukuba undifakele lamazwi. Ndiyaba ncoma abantu abati
umfundisi ukuze abe ngozeleyo makafundiswe, ndibekisela ngokukodwa
kwityalike yase Rabe, nditsho ngokuba ndinga zange ndimbone kuyo
umfundisi onga fundiswanga, andizange ndive nokuba ike yenziwe lonto.
Lizwi lika Tixo lifuna ukupatwa ngobulumko bemfundo. Manditi
ngokufutshane abantu abafundileyo bano kupatwa sisi tyakala na? Nditi
akuncedi nto ukufundiswa ngaba fundisi naba kokeli abangafundiswanga,
kulungele ukuba bashumayele ezidengeni, andifuni ukubiza abantu
ngamagama, ukuteta kwabo kunje ngoka ngati aba moyiki u Tixo. Nditi
abafundisi naba kokeli aba ntsundu maba fundiswe.

Ndim,

JOHN MTENGANA.

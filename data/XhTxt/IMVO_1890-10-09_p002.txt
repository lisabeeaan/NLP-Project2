NGOLWESI-NE, OCT. 9, 1890.

UTYELELO LOMPATISWA-BANTSUNDU. 

UKUBA ubungabananiswe ngentlanganiao—eyona nenge-yona—umzi olunge kulemimandla ye Qonce, eli ibilixesha lokuba udibane ukulungiselela ukubuya ko Mpatiswa-Bantsundu kwelipesheya kwe Nciba. Ngetuba le- mbambano usarwaqele umoya omhle kwabona bekukangelwe kubo ukunceda umzi, ekungeko luncedo lungaveliswa kuhlangana pantsi kwezimeko; ekubonakalayo ke ukuba kuyakude kudambe imbudede ebangwa ligazi elishusbu lefiva ngapambi kokuba zibe nokukangelwa ngumzi otobeleneyo no- vanayo into zokuwunceda. Ixesha lonyaka lokuzola linokuvelisela umzi uxolo, litobe negazi, into ezingenakudalwa ligazi elivutayo.

Njengoko bobona abalesi umzi opesheya kwe Nciba ubenamatuba amahle okupalaza uluvo lwawo pambi kuka Mr. Faure ; zaye impendulo zelinene zipolile, izezengqondo, zipuma kwintliziyo ebonakala ukuba iyakunqwenela ukvana nomzi. Impendulo zo Mpatiswa sizifumana zingenazidubuli, nandawo zakubonisa amagunya, njengokuba bezisakuba njalo ezika Mr. (ngoku ongu Sir Jawbus) de Wet. Ngati anokuzityanda igila ke kumfo onjalo amawetu. . Nokuba kungaba akuko zinto zininzi zi- hlininikisayo okwangoku, sihleli sijonge ku Rulumeni ukuba avelise, ancedise, amacebo okublanjululwa kwentlalo yomzi esinguwo ngendlela zonke anako. Ibizinto zokubhekiswa ezo ku Mpatiswa, ekuko itemba lokuba woba enowabo ebu Rulumeneni, umhlobo odumileyo wetu u Mr. Rose-Innes kweli letu.

Ekubeni lingeko ituba Jokuhla- ngana, bekukwalungile ukuba ube mzi lowo useusenza intlanganiso ya- makaya, ufute izinto-obona ufanelwe kuzibhekisa. Ingati imizi ngemizi enento yokubhekisa yazise Umhleli welipepa kwangapambili, azise nge- nx’engapambili ngelona xesha aya- kufika ngalo amapakati asebu Rulumenini namatuba angati amiselwe ukupulapula inteto ezingasingiswayo. Huku ! Mayibe libhungane emakaya ke mzi Sonazisa ngexesha.

Impawana.

U Mr. Richard Solomon, M.A., ose Kimberley ngoku, unikwe 'inibasa’ yewonga ngum’buso ngobugcisa kumsebenzi awupeteyo wokuqondisa-umteto. Imbasa yelowonga ngamagama angu Q.C., angomnye ke wabanokucebisa Inkosazaua entweni zomteto. Uvakuvuya nomhlobo wawo umzi ngokwanda kwake emsebenzini wake.

Kwinteto yokuncoma ete yawa ngokwemvula pezu kwabavuma abangama Afrika ase Amerika, sitabata inqaku lepepa lase Rini eliyi Penny Mail eti “nyanyani imvumo emnandi oluhlobo akubonakali ukuba yoza ipinde iviwe e Rini, ngapandle kokuba abe u Mr. McAdoo uyakucelwa ukuba abuye alihambele i Rini.” Ukusuka e Rini ababavumi basinge e Bhofolo, buyaku gqita e Dikeni ukuza e Qonce.

Uya kuvuya umzi ukuva ukuba ulure ebeke sati luko ngokubulawa komfo ongu Tukuta wase Twecu nomfazi wake e Xerha, lufumaneka lubuxoki. lye yafika e Bhai indoda leyo ; ipilile ayinanto—itsho nkubhalela izihlobo. Kuya- bonakala ukuba Imvo zisebenzisa uku- qonda ukungawupakeli umzi indaba ezinjengezi zingeka ngqineki inyaniso. Sibe sisitsho kakade sisiti ixakile ukuba ingavakali emapepeni endaba into engaka, esite ke ngelotuba asatumekelela ukuyihlokomisa ngokwento eyinyaniso.

Rogers ucelwa ngamanene atile abalulekileyo ase Qonce nase Monti ukuba avume abekwe pambi komzi ngolunyulo luzakubako Iwe Bandla Lengwevu, waye ngoko si- vayo ongatt uyakusamkela isicelo eso.

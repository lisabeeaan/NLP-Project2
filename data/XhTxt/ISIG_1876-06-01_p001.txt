Isigidimi samaxosa.

UMBONISO WAKWA COFIMVABA.

KWA Cofimvaba ngomhla 3 ka May kube kuko umboniso omkulu kunene wezinto
ngezinto. Bonke ebebekuwo, bamke bengalibali ubuninzi nobuhle bempabla
yabantsundu, nokukuqabukela kwabo ukuza kwizinto ezinje ngemiboniso.
Ngemini epambi komboniso lalibonakala ngokungati libi izulu, kwade kwako
ukoyikela ukuti liyakuwonela umsebenzi ngelandelayo imini. Noko
lalinjalo izulu abafikayo baqala kwangolwe Sibini, lati litshona ilanga
kwaba ngakwa Mr. Levey, Imantyi yabantsundu bakwa Cofimvaba, sekufumane
kwazinqwelo zenkabi nezamahashe, kwazinkomo, kwantoni.

Ngemini yomboniso bavuke benamaxala abantu okuti kazi iza kuba yimini
enjanina. Omnye wabebekona uti ukuteta ngayo: Ndati ndakuvuka kusasa
ndavuya ukubona ukuba iza kuba yimini entle. Ndite ndakuposa ameblo
ebantwini ndafika benyakazela, zaba inkomo, amahashe, igusha, ibokwe,
nezinye imfuyo zabantsundu iselifumane yayinto nje. Ndite ndikuza
ubuninzi bezifikileyo, ndabona ukuba kukona zisavelayo kumacala
ngamacala. Ndisabuka ezo, kuvakale kuhlokoma ingoma kwelicala langase
Queenstown, babonakala abaninzi bejika bewabekisa ngakona amehlo kanti
kukufika kwababeti bentambula abantsundu base Queenstown. Balitsho iculo
kwalusizi ndabona ukuba liyablaba kwabantsundu.

Kute mayela nexesha lesitonga kwamiswa amadoda aya kuba ngabagwebi
kwizinto ngezinto ezizisiweyo, kwaza kwayiwa ngo Mr. Charles Levey
namanye amanene kwindawo ebebekuyo abantsundu. Ufike kona umfoka Levey
wabaxelela into emayenziwe ngabeze kubonisa, apo bafanele ukuya kuma
kona balinde ukude kufike abagwebi. Basukile ke abagwebi baya kuqala
kwieala lezityalo, ukusuka apo baya ezinjeni bafika zingamakulu.
Bagqitele emahasheni, endiwancomileyo kanye ukuba mahle kwawo. Kulandele
igusha nezipani zenkomo nozibokwe. U-Feldtman, inkosi yamamfengu,
ebikona, ude wavakala esiti kungainqaba kwelakowabo ukuba afumane
impahla engamelana nale ebeyibona, into ke leyo ebancomayo kanye
Abatembu. Imitungo ibikutshelwe

amanenekazi atile, abuye engakulibali ukuba mihle kwayo. Kute ngelixesha
kukangelwa impahla ngabagwebi baba ababeti ntambula bemana ukuyihlaba
ingoma bawenza mnandi kanye umsebenzi walomini.

Emva kokuba bewugqibile umsebenzi abakangeli, U-Mr. Levey ubekise
kwabantsundu amazwi okubulela ukuba lomsebenzi bewutabatele pezulu
kangaka, bawenza ukuba ube nompumelelo etandekayo. Ubike ukuba amagama
ezinto ebeziugenisiwe ebe 1,243, nokuba ebeya kugqita kwelonani ukuba
bekunqwenelekile. Akugqiba kubizwe amagama abampahla idlula eyabanye
banikwa amabaso abo, kwaqondakala ukuba bemka bevuyile kanye ukuya
emakaya. Ndite ndakuposa amehlo ebantwini ndati fanukuba. bakumawaka
amane. Inqwelo— ezenkabi nezamahashe—bezingapezu kwamashumi amahlanu.
Kwinkosana zabantsundu endiziqondayo ngo Stokwe, Gecele, Ndarala,
Siqungati, no Feldtman; zonke zibe zinamapakati azo.

Ate amabaso akwabiwa kwayiwa kwenziwa ifandesi yezinto ezitile, yati
nayo yongezelela ebumandini balomini. Ite ipela baba abantsundu
sebeyimirozo ukusingisa emakaya, bonke bekolisekile ngumsebenzi
ebebewuhambele, ngapandle mhlaumbi kwabadaniswe kukungafumani mabaso apo
bebewalindele, into ke leyo engenakutiwani bani.

Masingayi shiyi indawo yokuba kute emva kwefandesi kwayiwa edinaleni.
Kumanene abeko kuyo ndingakankanya u-Archdeacon Waters, Bev. C. Crawley,
Bev. C. T. Patten, Bev. A. J. Newton, Messrs T. W. Edkins, Lumsden, no
Usher. Njengoko linjalo isiko labamhlope ngemini ezinjalo kute
kwakugqitywa ukudliwa ake amadoda enza inteto. Pakati kwabatetileyo
kubeko no Bev. Jonas Ntsiko, owenze amazwi amahle kunene ngokuhlalelana
ngoxolo kwabamhlope nabantsundu, ebonisa ukuba abantsundu bayayi bulela
imizamo enje ngale yokubanyusa esimeni sokonakala abakuso. Ute abantu
baya beqonda ngokuqonda ukuba u-Rulumente akabatabateli ukuba abacumze,
koko efuna ukuba pakamisa. Kwabantsundu uti akuba sityebi umntu adliwe,
axelwe igusha ebifuyiwe, kanti emlungwini osityebi ompahla zintle uba
nguyena uya kunikwa amabaso, into ke leyo ebingazanga yaviwa kwelilizwe.

Kubo bonke abalangazelela ukuhluma kwabantsundu kuya kuba luvuyo olukulu

ukuba lomboniso upumelele kakuhle kangaka. Siya temba ukuba usisiqalelo
sezinye izinto eziseza kulandela. Siya temba kanjalo ukuba uya kwenza
ukuba abantu babe nenyameko kuyo yonke imisebenzi abayi peteyo, bafunde
ukucokisa, baze ezimpahleni bafune ezona zidluliseleyo. Kwizinto ezite
zadeleka abebeko bakankanya noboya, babe ke becukumisa indawo evakala
futi, yokuba kweli lizwe basuke abantu banxamele ubuninzi bento,
bakulibale ukufaneleka nokucokiseka kwayo. Imiboniso le iluncedo
ngokwenza abantu ukuba bangaxoliswa zizinto ezidelekileyo.

Emabasweni kuko amabini esingatandayo kanye ukuva ukuba aya kufunyanwa
ngobanina. Sikolwa ukuba omabini aya kunikwa ekupeleni komnyaka. Elinye
lawo lelinikwe ngu Mr. Edkins eliya kufunyanwa ngumfo otyale wakulisa
imiti emakulu matandatu, elesibini lelendlu eyakiwe ngalomnyaka edlule
ezinye. Amadoda afumene lomabaso ebefanele ukwaziwa ngumzi wonke.

Elokugqibela lelokuvuyisana no Mr. Levey ngokupumelela kakuhle
komsebenzi awuqalileyo, nokumbulela ngemizamo yokunyusa abantsundu
ayenza imihla kamihla nokuba upina apo akona. Ukuba Abatembu baya kuvuma
ukumlandela siqinisekile ukubo kwakamsinya uya kwenza ukuba abe sisizwe
esimana ukwalata kuso xa siti ngekusenziwa okufanelekileyo. Nangoku
ngelixesha afikileyo kona azizininzi izizwe ezisigqitayo ngokutumela
abasebenzi ezindleleni, ke namhla pakati kwaso sekuko umboniso ekungeko
uwudlulayo kweyake yako kulomacala.

UVIWO LWETITSHERE.

KUMAPEPA ateta ngoviwo Iwetitshere olube luko kwindawo ngendawo
zekoloni, kususela e-Kapa kude kuye kuba pesheya kwe Nciba kubonakala
ukuba ababe nikele amagama abo babe 123, baza abafikelelayo ngomhla
woviwo, owe 11 ku April, baba likulu elinamnye. Kwelonani abapumeleleyo
babe ngamashumi amane anababini, baza abamashumi mahlanu anesitoba
basilelelwa. Abapumeleleyo bahlulwe bamaqela matatu. Elokuqala
lelabapumelele ngokuncomekayo. Inani labanjalo lishumi elinesitatu.
Abeqela lesibini, abalishumi elinesitandatu ngabafu-mene iziqiniselo
zokuba baulingene umsebenzi wokutitsha.

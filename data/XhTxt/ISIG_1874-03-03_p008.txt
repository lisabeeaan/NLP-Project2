INGOZI.—E-Plettenberg Bay, kulamacala angase Kapa kute ngombla we 13 ka
February kwahlangana abantu abatile befuna ukuke ba­ngene elwandle
okweyure ezitile. Ulwandle lube luzolile ukungena kwabo, kodwa kute
kungekabi mzuzu wakula umoya lwalwa ulwandle; abuye kwangoko kwakuba
njalo, bati kodwa xa baza kufika elunxwemeni yabukuqeka iboti, abantu
abasitoba babeteka ngamandla kwindawo ekupunywa kuyo, isine sabo safa.

Kwi PARLAMENTE yakwele Diamond ngomhla wa 28 ka January kube kuxoxwa
ngokuba kwamkelwe ama Batlapin nama Baralong ukuba abe pantsi kolaulo
Iwamangesi. Ezizizwe sekumzuzu ziyicela lonto, zisiti zifuna ukuba
zizuze ukuhlala ngoxolo. Ama Bulu ase Transvaal azinxamele kanye, noko
abezipete kakubi oko zibe zingena-

ndawo yokupepela. Indaba ezazivakele zokuba ama Batlapin azinikele kuma
Bulu, kutiwa zazifumane zaqalwa, ngokunxamela ukumfamekisa abase gameni.
Iparlamente ivumelene kwelokuba kuyimfanelo, kunyanzelekile ukuba ezo
zizwe zitatyatelwe pantsi kokuselo Iwa Mangesi; kungengakuba anxamele
umhlaba, koko kungokuba efuna ukuba kuhlalelwane ngoxolo, nokonwaba;
nokuze abemi belozwe babe netuba lokunyuka.

kwakulo ndawo ibe iyitabate kuyo yati “ Nantsoke itshintshi yako yiz a

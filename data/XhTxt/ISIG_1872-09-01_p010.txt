EZIHLOMELA KWI SIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1872.

UBOYA ngoku bubuye benyuka ngenani E- England.

E-BAYI knko umfazi osand’ ukuhlaba indoda. Ngoku usetolongweni, indoda
yona akuko temba lokuba iyakupila.

KUVAKALA ukuba Umhlontlo no Mditshwa, ezinkosi zibe zisilwa, ngoku.
zizakuba pantsi ko- laulo lwale Koloni.

INKOLO EGWENXA—Kutiwa abantu abantsundu base Mampondweni, bakolwa ukuba
ezimvula zingaka zenziwa kukufa kuka Faku. Bati kuti kwakufa inkosi line
izulu nokuba kuninina.

UMHLANGAZI, inkosi yama Mpondo usand’ uku- dlula apa E-Lovedale, esiya
E-Nxukwebe. Kulo- mhlaba uze kufuna mafundo.

UKULWA KOLWANDLE—E-Natal kube knko nkulwa okukulu kolwandle, ekute
ngenxa yako kwaqekeka imikombe emine; kodwa akuko mntu ufileyo.

OTSHILEYO—Ute umntwana womntu ontsundu -Natal wasondela kakulu emhlweni,
zaza ingubo zake zalumeka kuwo, watsha wafa kwakamsinya emva koko.

KWA NONTONGWANE kutiwa kusand’ ukubako umoya omkulu. Ubunamandla
kangangokude iti enye inqwelo ebitwele uboya, uyibukuqe—yati imilenze
yabeka pezulu.

E-BAYI, U-Erm st Graves ugwetywe ukuba abe ntsuku zisixenxe etolongweni,
ngenxa yokusuke angabiko emsebenzmi wenkosi yake.

INTSIMBI YAMAXESHA.— E-Rini kuzanyelwa ukufunwa intsimbi yamaxesha.
Imali eseyitenji- swe ukurolelwa yona zi £530.

I-GOVERNORE izakuhambela kwele Diamondi. Abantu bakona sebelungiselela
kunene, ukuze ba- yamkele ngokufanelekileyo.

UBUSELA E-KALFRARIA.—Kuvakala ukuba aba- fuyi bangase Monti asikuko
nokuba bayalila ku- kubiwa kwempahla yabo, ibiwa ngabantsundu. Abanye
bati ngeveki ezimbini babelwe igusha ezi- kum 20 nakwa 50.

ABAGUQULI BEZIBALO, kokukuhlangana koku- gqibela bebehlangene ngako,
bagwiqule incwadi zika Paulusi kuma Galati, nakuma Efese, nakuma Filipi,
nakuma Kolose. Bavumelene ngokuze babuye bahlangane ngo November
bahambise bade baye kupela kwincwadi ka Paulusi kuye U-Filemone.

INGOZI EMBI.—Kuko ingozi elusizi esand’ ukuhla E-Rini. Kute abantwana
abatatu baka Mr. A. E. Nelson, bekwele inqwelaneni yamahashe belinde
umqubi wayo, otuka amahashe, wati umntu obewabambile wawayeka. Abantwana
abo bahle bawa, benzakala kakulu, omnye kwasa eqauka.

ISENZAKALO.—E-Beaufort West ite inkwenkwana eminyaka mitatu ubudala
yayakwalusa igushakazi ezinamatakane endle inomkuluwa wayo. Bate babasa
umlilo; wazake omkudlwana waka wemka ixeshana elifutshane, wafika umni-
nawe wake etshiswe ngumlilo selefile.

UKUBIWA KWEZIKUMBA.—Amadoda antsundu amatatu agwetywe ukuba abe
nyangantatu ento- longweni, ngenxa yokuba izikumba zegusha ezitatu
E-Qonce. Kubonakala ukuba bebezibela ukuze bafumane imali yokutenga
utywala.

U-SANDILE ufike E-Rini ngomhla wa 21 ka August, wamkelwa ngobubele
obukulu yi Mantvi yakona. Uhamba nabafazi buke ababini. Kuva- kala
ukungati wode aye kufika nase Bayi. Ukudlu- la kwake waka walala apa
E-Lovedale.

UBUGEBENGA.—Kuko Amaxosa amatandatu asand' ukuziswa ematyaleni E-Qonce
ngenxa yokubeta ama Mfengu amabini E-Nxarunu. Afunyenwe enetyala lawo
Maxosa, wati obe yintloko yawo wadliwa ishumi leponti, bati abanye
barola iponti ngantlanu.

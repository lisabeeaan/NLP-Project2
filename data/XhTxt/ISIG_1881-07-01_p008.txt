ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JULY 1, 1881.

ILIFA ELISHIYWE NGU REV. WM. GOVAN.

ABAFUNDISI base Lovedale bayazisa ukuba imali eli
lifa elashiywa ngu Rev. William Govan elishiyela imfundo yale
Institution yase Lovedale isandul’ ukufunyanwa. Lomali igqite kancinane
ku £600. Kuyaziswa ke ukuba ilungelo lokufundiswa kwomfana emnye nge £10
ne ntombazana enye nge £5 okwe zinyanga zintandatu, liya kubako
ngokwenziwa uviwo.

Uviwo luya kubako e Lovedale ngolwe Sihlanu no Mgqibelo usuku Iwe 5
nolwe 6 ku August. Izifundo abaya kuviwa kuzo zezi zibalwe ngapantsi,
zikwafana neze Government Examination for the Elementary Teachers or
third class certificate, ngapandle kwe School Manayement, German and
French.

Oluviwo luvulekile kwaba Ntsundu nokuba ngaba lupina
uhlanga lwase Africa abafunda apa e Lovedale ama £20
ale mali ngonyaka aya kupuma ukuba akutaze abafundi
abanga madodana, ze i £10 ngonyaka libe lelo kukutaza

umtinjana, abolu hlanga lwase Africa. Abafuna ukungena kwolu viwo baya
celwa ukuba batumele amagama abo ku Secretary of the Education Board
ngosuku nokuba ngapambili kwolo 1 ku August, 1881, kunye nencwadi
zesimilo ezivela kubafundisi babo nokuba ngabaqeshi babo.

Indawo zoviwo ke zezi:—

LOVEDALE,

30 JUNE, 1881.

INTENGISO YEMPAHLA YAKWA RULUMENTE.

  GOMHLA wa 20 ku July, kuya kutengiswa e Mjanyana kwela Batembu impahla
  ehambayo yakwa Rulumente, yile:—

Inkomo 50

Amahashe 3

Igusha 30

Ibokwe 7

Kuya kufuneka ukuba ibatalwe kwalapo, impahla etengiweyo.

Enye impahla eyakutengiswa kwangelo xesha ukuba ayi
funyanwa ngabaniniyo, yile:—

Ihashekazi eligwangqa, eline, mbasa umsila unqunyulwe
wamfutshane. Inkabi ye hashe egwangqa, itiwe quzu kwi
ndlebe yekohlo, likwi minyaka esitoba.

Imazi yenkomo emnyama, ejiye ngomlenze wase kohlo

  (Signed) JAMES BOYES

  Imantyi yase Mjanyana,
  TEMBULAND,

8 JUNE, 1881.

IMIHLABA KWA QOBOQOBO.

  UKO imihlaba yakwa Rulumente ese Keiskama Hoek (Qoboqobo) nase
  Frankfort (Donqaba) eya kutengiswa pambi kwendlu yamatyala yase Qonce
  ngentsimbi ye 11 a.m. ngosuku Iwe 17 ku August, 1881.

  ISAZISO,

  DIYAZISA kuni nonke ba Ntsundu base Transkei ukuba ndingu Mteteleli
  wase Ofisini (Law Agent).

  Ndiya kukokela ndiqube ityala lo ndifunileyo, ngawo wonke umonde, na
  nje ngoko mteto, pambi kwe Mantyi, nokuba ye pina e Transkei. Ndiya
  kuquba ne zibeno zabab'enayo pambi kwe Mantyi Enkulu e Nklambe. Ikaya
  lam lingase ofisini e Ngqamakwe.

  ANDREW GONTSHI General Agent.

Ngqamakwe, November 12, 1880.

KUFUNWA E KIMBERLEY-DIAMOND FIELDS.

1.  I TEACHER yesi kula sabantwana abateta isi Bulu eya kubafundisa.
    Kuya kubako nesa ngokuhlwa. Umvuzo kuqala uya kuba £100 ngomnyaka.

2.  1 TEACHER yesi kula saba Ntsundu e Newton esase mini nesa ngokuhlwa.
    Umvuzo kuqala uya kuba li £120 ngomnyaka.

3.  I TEACHER yesi kula saba Ntsundu esise Bultfontein esase mini
    nangokuhlwa. Umvuzo kuqala uya kuba li £120 ngomnyaka.

Kungakolwa ngokukodwa zi Teacher ezi nabafazi, nezi nokwazi, nokuwuqela
kanye lomsebenzi; zinamapepa axela izimilo zazo; zinga Malungu e
Remente.

Abawufunayo lomsebenzi botumela izicelo zabo kunye ne ncwadi zezimilo ku

  Wesleyan Parsonage Kimberley.

OFUNAYO.

ZIHLOBO bonisani ndifuna aba zali bam ababemi e Xilinxa kwesika Mzola
baza bafudukela eNdenxa pambi kwalemfazwe yama Mpondo, andibeva nento
andazi nokuba sebafa na. Igama ngu Ntshona. Zanga zingati izihlobo ezazi
i gama elinjalo zibalele ku.

NTOZINI NTSHONA.

Grahamstown.

ISAZISO.

UMR. ANDREW GONTSHI, uyazisa kuzo zonke izihlobo zake, ezise Transkei,
zaba Ntsundu; ukuba ngapandle kwoku tabata akokele amatyala pambi kwe
Mantyi, unako ukwenza iminqopiso ebaliweyo pakati kwa bantu nje ngokwase
Mtetweni. Iminqopiso enje nge ngqesho zozi nqwelo, inkomo, igusha,
amahashe, nokuba yeye ntengiso, nemboleko. Xa abantu benza iminqopiso
ebaliweyo pambi kwe Mantyi ngomteto, ukumangalelana kunga ncipa kakulu.

ANDREW GONTSHI,

Ngqamakwe,

May 20, 1881.

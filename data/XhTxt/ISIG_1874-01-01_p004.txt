ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 1, 1874.

IMITWALO YENQWELO.

EKUBENI ngexesha langoku bebaninzi abantsundu abahamba nenqwelo,
kungatandeka ukuba bake bonke babe nendawo ezitile abazikangelayo
ngemitwalo yenqwelo. Kubantu abatwalelwa impahla kuko izikalazo ezikulu
zokuba inqwelo azi wulingene umsebenzi wazo. Kungoko lento kukalwa
kangaka ngezomlilo inqwelo. Kanjalo sidla ngokubon azisiti inkabi ezike
zahamba indlela enje ngokusuka E-Bayi ziye E-Gqili zibuye
zingasenamsebenzi, ezinye zifele ezindleleni; nezide zafika ekaya zike
zihlale inyanga ezitile pambi kokuba zibuye zihambe. Ngeli xesha ke
abatumeli bempahla bakohliwe, nomnini nkabi akangenisi nto, mhlaumbi
uyadleka kukuziqeshela inca. Umbuzo abengebezibuza wona abantsundu,
kwanje ngoko benjenjalo nabamhlope, ngowokuba yintonina imbangi yokuba
kubenje, nokuba akuko ndlela yokuncipisa obububina. Omnye umfo omhlope
obala ngalendawo uti, Endaweni yenqwelo ezinzima ngangezi zangoku
bekufanele ezilula, kuze ka­njalo umtwalo wenqwelo ube ziponti ezi 6,000
endaweni yezi 8,000 nezi 10,000; size isipani sibe zinkabi ezi 12. Ngalo
ndlela inqw'elo zingaba nokukauleza, zihambe lula; inkabi zingabuya
zingadlekanga kakulu. kuze kungafuneki nganto ukude zihlale inyanga
ezitile pambi kokuba zihambe; kanjalo kungako nezokupumzana inkabi,
kuzeke ngoko umnini nqwelo afumane inzuzo engapezulu nomnini mpahla
ancedeke, ikoloni ibe nokuqubela pambili.

UKUVULWA KWETYALIKE YABANTSUNDU E-BELFONTEIN, KWELE DIAMOND.

Letyalike yabantsundu ya Mawesile ivulwe apa E-Belfontein kwele Diamond
ngo 30 ka November 1873. Ivulwe ngu Rev. Gwayi Tyamzashe umfundisi
ontsundu. Kwati kwakusasa babonakala abantu sebevela kumacala onke,
wafika yena selizele indlu. Bada abanye bahlala ngapandle. Inkonzo
yaqalwa ngomtandazo, kwazake kwavunywa iculo ngokwa Besutu. Kwati kuba
kwaku Malau, kmnaBastile, kumaXosa ku Besutu kuma Mfengu, kuma Zulu,
yayinto emnandi ukuva intlanga ezingaka zidumisa U-Mdali wento zonke.
Washumayela encwadini ka Yisaya lii. 9. washumayela ngamakumsha amabini,
elinye lisisa Esisutwini, elinye Esibulwini. Eyokumka komhla yapatwa ngu
Mr. James Poote, wateta kwincwadi ka Hagai ii. 9.

Lendlu yakiwe nge £83 11s. 3d. Lemali yonke selibatelwe. Sinovuyo
olungazenzisiyo ukuti ayi- natyala. Mlila yavulwa kwahlanganiswa £7.
Ngemini elandelayo yaba yi tea-meeting. Yekake abantu ukuba baninzi,
yazala indlu. Wati nmongameli akuba eyixelile into engayo intlanganiso,
wabiza U-Mr. J. Poote ukuba atabate isihlalo, bamamkela ngovuyo olukulu
abantu. Kwakuko nabamhlope namakosikazi abo. Itikiti zokungena zazisidla
2s 6d.

Iziteto zabamnandi kanye. Kwateta U-Rev. G. Tyamzashe no Yapi Nginza no
Nkosi Brandine no Africa Ncindi. Umntu owateta into ezihlekisayo waba
ngu Africa Ncindi. Xa abekisa kumanxila wati, wona afana ne nkubabulongo
yona ifumane iqenqe imbuba yayo ingayikangelanga indlela, ingazi nalapo
iya kona, ize iti yakufika eweni yeyele kunye nayo, ivuke ingasayazi
nokuba iye ngapina imbumba yayo. Utike amanxila akayihloli indlela yawo,
nawo ayakuwa eweni alahlekane nabo utywala kungazani, kungancedani
aqabuke umntu seleze noko bekungati ekuqaleni kumnandi. Yabayolisa
abantu intlanganiso leyo yada yapela. Bavuma ngokutandekayo kanye abantu
abatsha.

wababalelani betu.]

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1883.

Abafundi betu mababe sebeke beva ngokuswela kuka Rulumeni imali yokuquba
imicimbi yolaulo. Lenyaniso ke selite yabanga inteto eninzi kwindawo
ezinjengoma Palamente. Ikwayilento ebange ukuba lentlanganiso
yokugqibela ye Palamente yenze ingxolo engaka ekukupeni lemali kuhlaulwa
kuyo otitshala, nezinye imali ezincedisa ukuqutywa komsebebenzi
wezikolo. Kuvakala ke ukuba ngati u Rulumeni uza kubapata gadalala bonke
abantu abanezikweliti zake ezinje ngeze mali yokurafa (Quit Rent,)
emabati bonke bajuce ukuba babehle bawazi amaxabiso abawakwelita u
Rulumeni kwakamsinyane. Siva ukuba akasokudenda ukuyikupa indoda
emhlabeni wayo xa kuko imali engaba iyayikwelita. Ngokwenjenje ke
sisebezela amakowetu, siti, Iwu ! Nahilwa !

Xa izihlobo zibujelweyo, zifuna ukuvakalisa umpanga kwe- zinye lisiko
ukuba zifake isaziso epepeni lendaba. Isaziso eso siba nendleko
kwanjengokuba inendleko eminye imicimbi epatelele ekuncwabeni. Yiyo
lonto ke esizama ukungabaniki nkutazo abahlobo betu ababujelweyo ukuba
babehle babalekele epepeni lomzi wonke nendaba ezibanga usizi kulomzi
wabo, babe bengazimisele ukuhlaula. Bako abantu ababalulekileyo okuti
ukufa kwabo nembalana emfutshane yabo inge ingaziwa luninzi, beko
nabantu abamagama angaziwayo ekuti ke kwakubonakala imida ngabo
kungabiko bani unabele ukuyifunda. Ukutsho ke sigqwatulela lemibiko
siyilandelisayo esingasakuteta nto ngayo kuba sibuqonda ubunyaniso
balonteto yasemzini iti de mortuis nil nisi bonum “ makungatetwa nto
imbi ngabo bafileyo.” Sebozinyulela ke abafundi:—

Izihlobo ezimgama ziyabikelwa ukuba ngomhla wesine ka October kubube
inkosikazi ka Klaas Bangani, wase Ncera, yemka ngokonwaba okukulu itemba
layo lise Nkosini ebikade iyikonza. Abantwana bayo abebekade beyonga
ibatutuzele kunene ngetemba ebe ibambelele kulo, yasuka endaweni
yokulila ukuba iza kufa, yacula kuba ivuyele ukugo- duka.—KLAAS BANGANI,
Ncera, October 23, 1883.

Nguwo lo omnye umbiko :—

Ngokububa kuka Turumane Tshiswa. Wasishiya namazwi amnandi kakulu kanye
umfo ka Tshiswa. Wati, abantu abeza kundikuza bona babuye bemke, kodwa
yena u Yesu wati akuza kundikuza ndamti xipu ngazo zozibini izandla,
ndabambelela andabi savuma ukuba sahlukane naye—wena wabona ixolo
nentlaka, kutetwe nguye oko ke. Kwati ke ngolwesi-Hlanu ukujika kwelanga
wati ndiyagoduka namhlanje, yapendula inkosikazi yake yati ugodukela
pina ikaya ileli nje, wapendula wati amakaya mabini na, ikaya linye
lizulu lamakolwa. Wati ke umfazi wake ukumpendula hamba sitandwa sam
ngoxolo. Udade wabo ubuze ukuba unetemba na? Ute ewe, kutiwe kum ngena
eluyolweni Iwe Nkosi yako. Ute ke emva koko mandi leseshe ngoku watsho
kumkokeli wahamba ngoxolo ewundlalela umhlaba. Nalo ululo Iwempumelelo
lokugqibela lomfo ka Tshiswa um Krestu wenene.— PATULENI KAKAZA, Gaga,
November 2, 1883.

Sicelwa ukuba sazise ukuba Indlu Yentlanganiso yama Fama aseba-Tenjini
iyakuvulwa ngomhla we 17 January 1884. Amapepa amema amanene anqwenelwa
eko seletunyelwa ; no-Mcokeli Sigidimi selelifumene elake. U Mr. Levey,
imantyi ebukwa kunene yelozwe, unga noko kungavakaliswa ukuba nabo
bangasakufumana mapepa okubamema bolindelwa. Seyiko indawo elungiselelwe
abantu basemzini nokuba balilipina inani. Nalo ke umemo mzi ndini wase
Koloni nowakwa Blayi! Imisebenzi enje ibifanele ukunikwa inkutazo
ngowaseba Tenjini nongenguye owaseba Tenjini, kuba xa kucume abalimi
wonke umsebenzi uyakucuma. Bebefanele ukunikwa inkutazo ke abo
basebenzana nomhlaba. Siyatemba ukuba u Mr. Levey ne Komiti Yebandla
laba Limi, akasokufumana mhlaba wokungatomalali ngohlobo ongati ubukwe
ngalo lom- sebenzi wabo mhle. Sifuna i Mantyi ezinjengo Mr. Levey aba!

Xa sibe sisateta ngemisebenzi emihle ka Mr. O. J. Levey, R.M., sikumbule
incwadi esiyifumene pakati kule nyanga ifileyo, kumnumzana oti ungu
Daniel Zondiwe. Ibingumhla wokuqala tina ukuba sive ukuba u Mr. Levey
hleliwenje unabatile kwaseba Tenjini abangatanda emkile, kodwa site
sakufumana ukuba igama lombali wesi sililo ngu Zondiwe kwapela
ukumangaliswa. Into eninzi kubafundi betu iyakusivumela xa siti amagama
amaninzi etu bamnyama anikwa ngenxa yento etile, eti mhlayimbi ihle xa
lowo unelo gama azalwayo ; mhlayimbi ngento ebonakele kuye. Sifumane ke
kuko amagama anje n sawo “ Langa,” no “ Nomalanga ” abazalwa ngamalanga
o Qilo; o “ Mpitimpiti ” jabazalwa xa lipitizelayo ilizwe; njalonjalo. U
“ Zondiwe ” lo mayibe ngumntu oncanyelweyo, oti naxa ngaba wenzelwa into
entle abe yena esiti uyabulawa. Inteto yake ke enyelisa umfo ka Libi
ayisakufumana magqoboka maninzi kwabo bazaziyo inyaniso zezinto
zelaba-Tembu. Yiyo le ke lonteto :—

Mcokeli kaundincede undifakele lamazwana ambalwa ndikatazwa yinteto ekwi
Sigidimi sika October, 1883, eti siti tina Batembu siyakoliseka kanye
ngu Mr. Levey ngangokuba angade ati nokuba uyakutshwa sale naye. Akuko
nto injalo—ikulowo uteta lonto yedwa, makangasuke aquke, makati yena
ukolisa yena yedwa. Lonto imbi kuti kanye. Nawe Mcokeli yosukuzibala
into ezinjalo, asiyazi lonto tina apa.— DANIEL ZONDIWE, Pesheya kwe
Ndwe.

U Mr. Andrew Lang, imambane yapesheya, ipimisela ingcamango yokuba akuko
luhlobo kwintlobo zombini zoluntu, oluyitiye incwadi njenga mankazana.
Ixesha eli lonke besiba siti sodwa abebeyibona lento—masiti abayibone
bayivakalisa. Abanye abantu abatandi kuzipaula izinto eziyimilenga ngase
mankazaneni kuba besoyika ukuhlaziswa kwe ledi. Yinto kodwa emi yodwa le
yokuba amaledi, nafundisiweyo, ngakumbi awakowetu eyitiyile incwadi.
Kuwo kunje ngokulengalengisa ilapu elibomvu pambi kwenkunzi yenkomo
ukuyifunda. Isixakato sale ntiyo yencwadi ke kukuba iti yonke into
ebifundiswa eskuleni ilityalwe. Ufike ngoku umntu engati akazanga
wafundiswa.

Eteta ngolu hlobo nje u Mr. Andrew Lang ngemfundo yamanenekazi, kukuba
pesheya amadoda awaka adibana nencwadi ungafika eyipete xa kungeko
msebenzi wumbi awenzayo. Kodwa sifunga u Tebe ubeya kutabata elinye
inyatelo entetweni yake ukuba ubeke wawabona amadodana afundisiweyo eli
lizwe. Tina lomhlambi siwubopa ngantambo nye kunye namaledi awo.
Uyayitiya incwadi wakupuma ezikolweni. Yiyo lonto ke ungafika amadodana
awapumelela imviwo ezinje ngezo titshala mzuzu sele ngama atalala angati
akazanga afutwa xa kutetwa ngento zemfundo namhla. Lento ibangwa kukuba
incwadi ivalwa kwamhla kupunywayo eskolweni, ze ke umntu abuyele
kubutyakala awayenabo ngapambili. Madodana bambelelani etshobeni
lencwadi ukuze inganilahleki lonto nizingca ngokuba niyifumene
ezikolweni.

Ngomhla wa 22 Nov., u Honourable C. W. Hutton, olilungu le Rulumeni,
uhlangene nentlanganiso enkulu yama Mfengu ase Heald Town. Uwaxelele
ukuba ungevoti zawo kulomzi. Ubonise ukuba yena nabo alaula kunye nabo,
bazamela uxolo nokonwaba kwabamnyama. Ute ngenxa yokuba bengavumi
ukumbandezela omnyama ngemiteto erara sebetiyiwe sisininzi sabamhlope.
Ute imizamo yabo ukufumanela omnyama imfanelo zake mininzi, ayipumeleli
kuba kusweleke ibandla elibaxumayo. Ute into efunekayo libandla ukuba
abanyuli batumele e Palamente amadoda aya kubancedisa. Emva kokuvakalisa
izinto aliliswa zizo, nokubuza imibuzo ate wayipendula ngokukolisayo u
Mr. Hutton wahlukene nomhlambi uxoliswe kunene. Ibe ngummangaliso kuti
ukuva ukuba ama Ngesama atile abeselezama ukuwugqweta lomsebenzi mhle
ngokutumela ngocingo kulo lonke esiti ama Mfengu akawafuni lamadoda
alaulayo ngoku. Ezindlebeni zolwaziyo ulaulo oku kakade kukuti ama
Mfengu ase Heald Town adiniwe ngu Mr. Sauer nempato yake yoxolo—afuna
ngoku u Mr. Sprigg no Mr. Ayliff nawo onke amabenubentsu exesha labo!
Nguwupina omnyama onga angayigcakamela lontlalo ?

Ixesha lokusondela kwe kresmesi lixesha elisakuzala zindaba ezimnandi
nezimbi. Lixesha ongafika imibilini yonyana baba- ntu izele yimihlali
ngenxa yamatemba ezinto zelo xesha lezidlo neholide : ilixesha
elinganyali ukuba nemipanga epelisa amandla. Sibala nje sipahlwe yinto
eninzi yencwadi ezibika ukububa okuhle kwabantu kumacala omane omhlaba.
Pakati kwezi ncwadi sifumana ebika ukububa kuka Mr. Charles Hlawe
ngeveki yokupela kuka October e Cradock. U Charles Hlawe yindodana
ebudala bungaba bebukwiminyaka e 27. Ikaya layo li Xesi lakwa Kama.
Ifunde kwiskolo salo lali ; yaya kulungiselelwa ukufundisa intsapo .e
Heald Town apo ipume ngo 1879 inesiqiniselo sokuqala (First Class
Certificate, Heald Town,) kwa nesokuwufanela umsebenzi sakwa Rulumente
(Elementary Certificate of Competency.) Ikutshelwe ukuya kufundisa e
Rini, emva kweminyaka emibini waya kuzalisa kwalondawo e Somerset East
ngo 1881. Kuzo zombini ezindawo ibizenzele abahlobo ngesimilo
nokuzindila kwayo. Ebutitshaleni ubelolohlobo luginyelwa amate
ngabafundisi; encedisa nasemsebenzini we Ramente. Masili apa asinankolo
inkulu ekuqineni kwesimilo setitshala enentloni ngegama lika Krestu.
Intshumayelo zake ubungafika zishushu, zondele ekucukumiseni intliziyo.
Entlalweni yake ibingumzamo wake ukubonisa kwintanga zake inkululeko,
imfundo, nobujentimani benyaniso. Ubelilungu, elipilileyo Iona,
lentlanganiso yokuxuma ibambiso yemfundo pakati kohlanga. Amazwi ake
okugqibela siwatyelwa ngumfundisi owaye naye ekutshoneleni kwake
kwitunzi lomfula wokufa, wati—“ Andinakuteta ngenxa yobutataka, kodwa
inyango selilungiselwe mna ngapesheya kwe Yordane.” Wenjenjalo oka Hlawe
ukubazisa abakoyo “ okwe kolwa ukububa.”

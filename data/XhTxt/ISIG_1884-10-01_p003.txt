Abahlobo betu abase Bayi basibikela ezokuba kuzakubehle kubeko um’Vumo
oyakuhanjiswa ngabantsundu onjengalowo usandukubako e Dayimani. Ukuba
asiposisi lo wase Bayi ulungiselelwa ngu Mr. Paul Xiniwe. Inteto
enjengeka Mr. Joseph Moss, esand’nkupitizelisa Amangesi atile,
iyakwenziwa ngu Mr. Isaac W. Wauchope, ngobuhle benteto ye Sixosa.
Sibanqwenelela impumelelo abahlobo betu.

Ucingo lubika ukuba u Mr. de Wet uhamba eba nentlanganiso ezinkulu
kwelipesheya ko M’bashe. Intlanganiso abe nayo no Ngangelizwe inendawo
ebalulekileyo. Kuyo kubonakele emhlotsheni omoya aba simana ukuva ukuba
bako pakati kwa Batembu. U Nehemiah Tile ulesele u Mr. de Wet incwadana
etyila izinto ezifunwa nga Batembu angumlomo wabo yena, eti i Mantyi
mazishenxiswe, mabapatwe yinkosazana bona, amandla amakulu abe nenkosi
ya Batembu. Incwadi yoka Tile ilahlekwe sidima sayo ngokusuka kubeko
inkosana ezitile zemvelo eziyikanyezayo. Wasele ngena ngelo nxeba ke u
Mr. de Wet ukutetisa u Tile nabalandeli bake, eyala u Ngangelizwe ukuba
angeyoliselwa ngamacebo abo. U Mr. Tile waziwa kunene ngum’bali wala
manqakwana; ungumhlobo wake omkulu. Siti ke kuye ngesele eyiyekelela
lento ayingxameleyo kuba akubonakali ukuba inokupumelela. Seuxola,
Mtembu omhle.

Kwa Mpundu kutshe izindlu ezimbini nge Cawa pantsi kwezi zimeko. Kuko
amadoda ase Rabe amana eya kwenza inkonzo kumaqaba akwa Mpundu. Ayake
ngale Cawa azama ukuhlanganisa abantu, lada lahlangana iqela pambi
kwenye indlu. Ite xa ipakati inkonzo u Nyaniso ka Mcuce wabona
inkwenkwana encinane—lentangana imela amatole—isiti jike emva kwenye

ukupazamisa inkonzo, kuba ebengekaqondi noko ukuba kubsha yona. Ute
kwakona—“O yatsha indlu ; yatsh’ indlu !! ” Yapela kwa oko ityalike
wapalazeka umhlambi. {Fire ! fire ! Preacher and congregation to the
rescue!) Yati kuba ngu ngqu-pantsi yabehla yati bam— babona sekusitsha
esecaleni. U Mr. John Delman umshumayeli walomini uzame konke anako
kunye neqela lake abanceda nto. Yatsha yapela naleyo Uvakele esiti
lixesha kaloku— abantu nababehlanganisiwe ngumlilo wase mhlabeni, masike
senze enye inkonzo sibakumbuze “ Ngomlilo ongacimekiyo.” Bate abanye
hayi masesi goduka. oti amaqaba sitshisa indlu zawo sibe sifuna ubuciko
bokuteta. Kwagodukwa u Mr. Tunzana esarana. Lento isikumbuza izwi
elatetwa liqaba e Ncera kubafana base Dikeni apa ababehlala beya
kushumayela kona. Lati eloqaba.—‘‘ Nimana nisiti kwati-kwati u Satana
nje, nini aba kanye o Satana. Wafika apa omnye u Satana (Umshumayeli)
wase Xesi wati kanti esilibazisilinje inkomo ziyabanjwa es’apa zingene
emasimini omlungu. Wagqibela ubusatana obu, kuba salila ngaye xa sihamba
siboleka imali yokuzikupa. Latsho elinene lati, ngomnye umhla soze sire
nge mbumbulu sezi gquba pakati kwetu site qeke imilomo sipulapula
oludwekesho Iwenu.—Botani ndisaya kusonga inkomo.” Latsho lavutulula
isibunu lanyantsula lemka triumph­antly too, loyise o Satana ngokwalo.

EZASE SOMERSET.

Ngendaba zalomzi sibulela um’baleli u “Mdengezito” oti ngo 23 Aug., 1884
:—

Akuseva nto nge Somerset pakati kwamagumbi omane elopepa lidume kunene
lako (Isigidimi) okwexesha elide. Xa nditshoyo nditeta ukufumana udaba
kum’balelani wako lo undim. Kaundinyamezele ke mnumzetu kule migeana
ilandelayo. Masiqale ngokuzincoma. Nditi pakati kwezitili
ezingazincomayo ngamadodo eziwatumele e Palamente tina Somerset ne Nyara
(Bedford) singakwintloko epambili: Ngani? Omabini la manene
esawatumelayo atate indawo ezinexabiso kwelo qumru ; u Mr. de Wet yi
Sec. for Native Affairs, u Mr. Hockley yi Chairman of Committees.

Ngomhla wa 22 Aug. ngokuhlwa lamanene ahlangene nabanyuli bawo e
Somerset East Hotel (Jones) kwi Dinalikazi enkulu, anetuba ke ngoko
lokuhlangana nabanyuli bawo, lokwaneka imvo zawo kubo, nabo ezabo kuwo
ngokumaluuga nolaulo lwale Rulumente intsha. Kanjalo nokuyaleza indlel’
emhlope ku Mr. de Wet owayeza kuhambela abantsundu kwelo lenu lasema
Xoseni. Andikungena ke kwingxoxo ezenziwayo edinaleni apo, ngabanyulwa
kwanangabanyuli, kuba ndingadl’ indawo yako epepeni apo.

Pambi kokuba angene engxoxweni yake u Mr. Mahila, owaye ngu mshumayeli
nge Cawa, 10 Aug., ngokuhlwa, eyayisekwe Mat. xiv. 2. etyalikeni yama
Rabe apantsi ko Rev. W. Leith, ucele ukuba yonke into eyindoda enkulu,
nom’fana, (kusokuba kodwa akamangapi ama­kulu kule Ramente) maze ke
isale yakukov’ ukuba intlanganiso ipumile, okunene emva kwentshumayelo
yakuba inkonzo ipelile asala onke amadoda. Ute u Mr. Mahila—“ Madoda
akowetu ndifumene incwadi ku Mr. Jabavu, olumaziyo kakulu uninzi Iwenu
apa, obenexesha elide esebenza pakaa kwenu. Undicele ukuba maze ndihlabe
ube banzi urakosi kuni ngomsebenzi aza kuya kuwenza e Qonce, wokupata
ipepa kona; kufuneka ke nimxasile nalamkela. Ndinicela ke ukuba maze
sibe nentlanganiso ngokuhlwa kwangomso (um-Vulo) etyalikeni yabantsundu
yase Wesile.”

U Mr. D. K. Vandala ume wati ndalekela nam kwinteto ka Mr. Mahi­la, nam
ndifumene incwadi ku Mr. Jabavu ekwa ngepepa aza kulikupa e Qonce.
Elipepa igama lalo Zimvo Zabantsundu. Elipepa linelungelo lokuba nokuba
ngubanina ongumamkeli walo xa enento anga angabala ngayo, abale
azityand’igila,, kuba lelokuba uhlanga luzixele kulo imvo zalo, watsho
wayaleza ukuba amadodana zalixase.

Kusuke u Mr. Jno. Zikode, otete ngokuvuyiseka kwake ngento eyenziwe ngu
Mr. Jabavu, wati oku kubonisa ukuba kade ezama oka Jabavu ukulwela
uhlanga lwakowabo kwa eselapa pakati kwetu.

Kume u Mr. Boyce Skota otete amazwana am’balwa. Ute u Mr. Jabavu wamazi
ngapambi kokuba bonke abapulapuli bake bamazi. Ibiyindodana ekuteleyo
kwase sikulenl, ebuncinaneni bayo. Uyavuyiswa linyatelo alitatileyo,
makakutazwe ke ngokulamkela nokulibatalela.

Onke amadoda ngantliziyo nye avuma ukuba oyifikelela intlanganiso leyo
eyakuba ngom-Vulo.

undofa nawe. Lilifa lompefumlo. Imali sisicaka abati obawo mhla

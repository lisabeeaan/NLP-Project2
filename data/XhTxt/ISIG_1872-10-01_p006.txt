INDABA ZAPESHEYA.

AMAKALA ayafuneka kakulu E-England, nemali yawo inyukile. lyafuneka
kakulu ne koton. Ibu- ye yehla imali ye diamondi.

UBUSHUSHU base New York bebubukulu kanga- ngokude kufe abantu abamashumi
asitoba mini ’nye bebulawa bubo.

ISIXEKO sase Oxford, esibemi banga 35,000, sinomntu omnye kupela
ongumbanjwa, into enga- kolisi kubako ; kute ukubonakaliswa kokuba
ivuyelwe kwaxonywa iflag emhlope pezu kwendlu yetolongo.

INYAMA NAMALAHLE zinqabile kanye ngoku E-England. Ngenxa yokunyuka
kwemali yamalable, iyenyuka neyentsimbi. Intsimbi ebekuza kwakiwa ngazo
indlu yokulumkisa imikombe ebusuku, kwakona apa E-South Africa,
ayikamkelwa, ngenxa yokuba umntu owayeyitembisile uti akanakuyitumela
ngemali ekwakuvunyelwene ngayo.

I-GOVERNMENTE YAMA FRENCH iyifumene yonke imali yokubatala ama German
ngokuyiboleka. Ibide yangapezulu nakoko ibifuneka ngako.

U-MR. BABE, owayefumene kunene idiamondi oko ebelapa E-South Africa,
upangiwe ezodiamondi E-New York. Ixabiso lazo beliziponti ezi £2,500,
ezifake enxoweni epezulu yebatyi. Lite elosela lizibileyo layisika
inxowa ngobugcisa balo, wati eqala ukuqabuka U-Mr. Babe laba lingaseko.
Liyibe xa akwele kwenye yenqwelana ezitwala abantu esitilateni sase New
York.

I-GOVERNMENTE YASE EGYPT ikupe umkosi omadoda anga 2,000 wokuya kulwa ne
Abyssinia. Kute ngomhla wokuqala ka July lowomkosi wata- bata indawo
ezitile zomhlaba wama Abyssinia. Ubusalindele ukongezelelwa ngamanye
amadoda ama 3,000, ukuze ati akufika kuyiwe kuvingcelwa I-Magdala umzi
olikomkulu. Umkosi opetwe ngukumkani wase Abyssinia ungamadoda anga
10,000 ; kodwa ke noko akubonakali ukuba womelana nama Egypt kuba wona,
anazo -kakulu izixobo zokulwa kanjalo kuko nabelungu abangene kubo.
Abantu base Abyssinia kumzuzu besilwa bodwa, kade kanjalo kuxalise
abapangi, abalalela abahambi. Kungati ke mhlaumbi ukoyiswa kwabo sesinye
isizwe kubangele ukuba balungise nesimo sabo.

UMFO OTILE ONTSUNDU usand’ ukufa kakubi ngenxa yokusuka ziti indawo
ebenzakele kuzo kungabiko mntu uzinyamekelayo, zide ke ngoko zibole,
yena selebulawa kukubola.

ABEMBI BAVUMELENE ukuba bayekwe bazimbele nabantsundu abebenemihlaba
yokumba E-Bulfontein nase Dustfontein. Abangavunye- Iwayo ukuba bobuye
bazimbele babini kupela. Bayakolwa ngoku ukuba abanye abo, abangapandle
kwabo babini, banezimilo ezilungileyo.

ABEMKE EKUMBENI.—Kute ngomhla we 4 ka September kwako into epantse
ukoyikisa. Amadoda antsundu anduloke ngalo mini ukuya emakaya awo,
pakati E-Africa, ebe makulu matandatu. Esukele E-Klip Drift, esiti
ukuhamba kwawo afale ngamane, onke epete imipu. Abasukuba bevela
ngapakati E-Africa abadli ngokuhlala ka- kade ; usuke umntu enzele ukuba
afumane umpu.

KUKO UMFO osand’ ukuya kwele Diamondi, esi- yakukangela ukuba
akangefumani qela likulu na labantu abangavuma ukuza kusebenza,
lemisebenzi kufunwa ukuba iqalwe apa kule Koloni. Woti ukuba
akanakubafumana kona abasebenzi, bayekufunwa kwamanye amazwe, kuba kweli
lizwe alifumaneki inani elaneleyo.

IDIAMONDI EZAZIBIWE enxoweni eyayitwelwe yinqwelana yeposi zifunyenwe
ngobuqinga obukulu. Ute U-Berry, umntu oberanelwa, akafakwa etolongweni
enabanye ababanjwa, wafakwa endlwini yomnye wabalaleli. Uteke lomlaleli
ku Berry, woti ukuba nam undicapulele kwezo diamondi ndikuvumele
ubaleke. Ute yena U-Berry kuba engazi ukuba utiyelwe wavuma ukuti
ndokucapu- lela. Umlaleli lowo uxelele amapolisa ukuba abe esiya kuvala
amazibuko, ngelixesha U-Berry esa- yakuzixela apo zikona. Ute esazikupa
apo ebe- zimbele kona aselefika amapolisa, embamba.

  IZINTO NGEZINTO.

  IMASHINI yokumba indlela, esebenza ngompu-
  nga, isand' ukufika kulekoloni, ivela E-England.
  Izakubehle ilingwe, ize iti ukuba ihambisa kaku-
  hle, kubuye kuziswe ezinye.

  UMXOSA otile usand’ ukwapula umlenze wake
  ngengozi E-Uitenhage; akavumanga ukuya en-
  dlwini yamayeza, nokuba longozi yake ikangelwe
  ligqira lakona. Kute kwakuba njalo iqabane lake
  lamsa egqireni Lomxosa ; ute engekabi mzuzu ko-
  na wafa, kukusuke aminxane imihlati.

  UMLILO—E-Port Elizabeth kufe abantu ababi-
  ni ngale nyanga idluleyo, bebulawa kukutshiswa
  ngumlilo. Kute ngomhla we 3 ka September
  kwatsha ivenkile ka Mr.Bates, waza umniniyo
  wafunyanwa ngemini elandelayo selelilahle. Kute
  ngokuhlwa okulandela oko kwatsha indlu yokuba-
  ka, waza umfo abati ngu William Holins obelala
  kuyo, wafunyanwa selefile kukutsha. Umzimba
  wake ucolwe ungasenayo imilenze nezandla.

  UBUSELA—Abafuyi abakumatafa ase Bontebok
  bayabelwa kakulu. Ngoku bangenwe kukuqumba
  okukulu, esingemangaliswe kusitiwa bamise imi-
  teto enzima pezu kwamasela. Omnye umfo obala

  ezindabeni uti mawutshatyalaliswe kuti-tu umzi
  osukuba ungeniswe kuwo umkondo. Baninzi ke
  abantu abati abuyi kupela ubusela kungenjiwa-
  nga njalo. Sinosizi ukuva ukuba nase Dordrecht
  buyakula ubusela.

  UKUTYALWA KWEMITI E-ORANGE FREE
  STATE.—I-Governmente yase Orange Free State
  ihlanganise imbewu eninzi yentlobo ngetlobo
  zemiti. Lombewu inikwa ngesisa kubo bonke
  abalimi, kube kusenzelwa ukuze ibe nokulinywa
  kakulu imiti. Lonto iyanconywa ukulunga ngabe
  bonke abantu abanokuqonda, ngokuba iswelekile
  kakulu imiti kona.

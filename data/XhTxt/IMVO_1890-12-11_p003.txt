THURSDAY, DECEMBER 11. 1890.

  Government House, Capetown, 3rd December, 1890.

  G. SEYMOUR FORT, P.S. Tengo Jabavu, Esq.

  Sepuru, Paul Sinamela, Wellington Majiza, Chas. Mzozoiyana no Geo.
  Polisa,— Umtinjana: Misses Charlotte no Kate Manye, Byble, M. Keyi,
  Nobhengula no Annie Sanders. Nakuba u Mr. Walklett efuna ibuto
  lokuwela ukuya e Ngilane aba bamagama angentla abasakuwacanda bonke
  lamanzi abanzi etyuwa. Ke, ukuposa igada kulento yenziwa ngulomnumzana
  manditi, ibiy’into engaba nesihomo ngokunye (ukuba ibingenziwa
  ngolungxamo obungaka) ukuba u Mr. Walklett ebecele kuwe Mhleli
  wamazisa imvumi ezaziwayo ezikoyo kumzi ontsu­ndu kwindawo ngendawo,
  angabuti bantu bandawonye—ndingateti nto nge saziso esise pepeni—kuba
  into le ibalulekile, yabe iyeyokuqala kumz’ ontsundu apa e Africa,
  okanye iya kujongwa ngamehlo abukali ngabase Ngilane apo kufunzelwe
  kona, xa kunjalo ke ibinga ngayinto efezekileyo ukuze umzi unga
  gxekeki ngapaya kwalatnanzi made. Kanamanjazo lento indikumbuza ixesha
  apa ekwakuko inteto yokuba makuwezwe amadodana antsundu ayokudlala
  ibhola e Ngilane, yaba nkulu ingxoxo engokufuneka kobu civility, i
  manners, nencubeko, ngazwinye ubulungu, kwabanokuya kona.
  Kukwangokunjalo nakweliqela liyakuya kona, ziyafuneka ezizinto ukuze
  bangaxakani nezihlangani abodibana nazo kwelozwe, njengama Amerika
  apulukileyo. Koko kusizeleka umz’ontsundu usarolwa ngengalo ngabelungu
  nakwinto obungayenzayo nawo ngokwawo, unawo ; nje amadodana
  abengayibumba ilunge into enje ipetwe yindodana entsundu— ngabula nto
  ka McAddoo nempi yayo— koko esonge Land la elele obude ubutonngo ko “
  foyiyafoco.” Itsibe msinya ke, yona indodana emhlope izenzele udumo.
  Okunye u Mr. Walklett kambe ute e Town Hall “ utabata amanyatelo ama
  Amerika ngokwenjenje, yabe netyhabongo (imali) eyakubutwa
  ngalomsebenzi iyakuncedisa i Mission Work ne zikolo zabantsundu,”—oko
  knkuti ke kulungile ukuba elibuto lipehlelelwe ngeligama lokuba zi
  “African Jubilee Singers,” kuba kaloku lizakunceda ama Africa,
  emayibeke lento iyakukangelwa ngumzi. Mandiyeke apo kuba sekunjeke
  Mnumzetu, Dorotile Ongwevu Ngezihlati, kaubuye uvele sive ngale yeli
  into. Camagu, Mr. Mhleli, kukuhlala kweyilo ukungayi futshanisi
  inteto; senditshonela ke.

  Ndingowako otobekileyo, DAYIMANI.

Kimberley 3rd December, 1890.

Abatembu ne Komkulu Labo.

[NGUMBHALELI WETU.]

  IHAMBELO KOMKULU.

  Ngo November Oth ibingumhla aba Tembu base Roda nase Cala ne Ngcobo,
  bazimisela ukutyelelela i Kumkani u Dalindyebo Mtirara. Roda ne Cala
  langena e Ngcobo ngomhla we 6 no we 7 November, apo bafika
  sebehlangatyezwa lizwi lakomkulu, libalatisa ukuba ze bangene ngo 12
  November komkulu. Lindulukeke elibandlu selinama Qwato ngo 11th
  November, lakungena komkulu ngo 12 November emini.

UKUNGENA

  ahambe ngamabini amadoda esezinkabini zawo ili Roda pambili, Amaqwato
  ngemva. Inani lalikumashumi amahlanu anesitatu, wamhle umjikelo
  ekungeneni komkulu; ate esati nqumama aseleblangatyezwa zizipakati
  ngobuvuzayo ububele—yaye ingu Rev. N. Tile no Ngeanga abeza pambi
  kwezikulu ezo. A enjenje u Mr. Tile:—Silusizi kakulu zipakati
  ukunazisa ukungabiko kwe Kumkani yenu, nakuba siyilindele, tina
  singabantwana, sinicela ukuba nisinyanezele kucebo letu lobusapo,
  kunye ne Nkosikazi: sihlahle imizi emitalu sonikupela abantu bokunisa
  kwezondawo, nobuya naziswe ke akufika Ukumkani.

UKUMKANI

  ugaleleke ngo 18 November, waziswa wadibana nomzi kunye nezo zikulu
  ngolwesi-Ne, 20 November. Utikile umhla lowo avele macalana onke
  amahashe abangezantsi aba Tembu, lada lapakama ilanga kufikwa.

INTLANGANO

  Ingene emini emaqanda, eya komkulu mpi yaselenze ingqumru enkulu pambi
  kwe varanda, Ikumkani ne Nkosana namapakati zipantsi kwayo.

OWAMA NONI.

  [Ibakula lesiqalo lifana nelo le Xonxa ne Cala.]

  Sigcoba ngombuliso omkulu tina Manoni, sinje ngamhla u Ngubengenka
  wata bata izirele Embolompo, wakutala ukunyanzela aba Tembu ukuba
  mabacite utshaba olwaluza kutimba umhlaba wase ba Tenjini. Baza
  bagcoba aba Tembu pambi ko Ngubengcuka, akulucita. Sijije ngamhla u
  Ngubengcuka watata izihlobo wakucita utshaba olwaluse Gqotyini luza
  ukucita umhlaba waba Tembu.

UMANYANO.

Ibeshushu ingxoxo ngalo, wati u Mr. Thomas Poswayo wateta ngobugcisa
obukulu kuba engomnye wabalangazeleli ubunzulu balo. Yaza lengxoxo
yapelela kwezi zigqibo:— Kuvunyelwene ukuba kubeko Ingqungqutela yo
Manyano’lwaba Tembu. II. Kuvunyelwene ukuba intlanganiso yokuqala
yokwenza izimiselo zalo, ibe Komkulu mhla kuvulwa ityalike yakona.

ITYALIKE.

Saziswe ukuba igama layo yityalike ye remente zelizw’e laba Tembu,
nobeko lwelitye lwamiselwa ngolohlobo. Kuvulelekile kuletyalike
ukushumayela onke amahlelo obubedesho, nakuba yona imbedesho yakona
ihamba ngemimiso yobu Wesile; kunjalo akuseko ngxoxo ngawo lomcimbi kuba
selunamaxesha uxoxwa ulike ugqitywe ngolohlobo.

IMFUNDO,

Ngumnqweno wo Kumkani kunye naba Tembu ukuba kubeko imfundo Komkulu.

Emveni kokuvulwa kwendlu kuya kufunwa i teacher epapileyo ekolwayo,
ukuqala umsebenzi wemfundu Komkulu. Inkosana zonke ziyakutumela
abantwana bazo kunye namapakati azo; yaye Ikumkani izama onke amacebo
asingisele ekusixaseni eso sikolo.

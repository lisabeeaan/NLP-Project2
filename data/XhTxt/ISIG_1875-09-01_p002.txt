  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1875.

Putuma liseko ixesha, liseko itemba, liseko ituba lokuxolelwa, imihla
yako iyapela kusondela imini yomgwebo oyakubuzwa ngayo ityala lokuwona
umpefumlo wako.

11—Kuhle ntonina akuba lomfana eku- dlile konke abenako? Qata indlala,
inge- yakudelwa nalondlala kuba kutiwa apa yayindlala enzima; elalizwe
abeye kulo lomfana libelisiti kakade libe namaxesha endlala eshushu
etshutshisayo. Ipangelelene lendlala ke nokupela kwempahla yake, kuhle
izinto ezimbi ngaxesha nye, zabonakala nga- mini nye izinto abengazanga
azibone. Amashwa kakade yinto eyenza isipango ukufika kwayo; kupele
impahla yake, kwapela ukudla nasebantwini abesazana nabo. Nakuba ibiko
kakade lendlala kwelilizwe ebengekayiqondi yena, ibimpahlile ngenxa
zonke engayiboni yena kuba ebese nenkomo. Babeqauka abantu ngamacala
onke engenalufifi yena lokuba babulawa yindlala, ebese nezinto yena
ezigxotayo kuye inxwaleko enjalo, ebesanele yilemfuyo yake, isamquba.
Elibele nje yena yilemihlali yake akaboni ukuba ucotelwa lilishwa,
Engazanga nje ayibone indlala ebengacingi ukuba iselikufupi naye. Ke
nomoni emhlabeni apa angake azilibazise ngezinto zake azenzayo, ngezinto
zake azitandayo, angake ati lemihla adlobayo, Hai ukuba mnandi
kwelilizwe, kufumane kutetwa xa kutiwa u-Tixo uko, nokufa kuyasizuma,
nesihogo yinto ikoyo, buxoki babafundisi obo, buhilihili bamagqoboka
oko, angake ati okwenene umutu ongamkonziyo u-Tixo sifumane sinunuselwa
xa kutiwa guqukani natshabalala, buyani neyela emngcipekweni
ongonapakade. Ngobeteta ezonto lomfana kumakolwane ake okuya ebesahluta,
esanxila, esarexeza, namhla yakupela impahla akuba engasakuboni ukudla,
uqale wabona ukuba indlala yinto ikoyo na emhlabeni.

Lendlala kutetwa yona apa iwugqibile umhlaba, yinto abantu abapela
kulamba lemihla ngamacala onke, yindlala yodwa apo kuko moni
ungalamkeliyo ilizwi lika Tixo ungakolwayo kunyana wake; bonke abantu
abangekaguquki basendlaleni. Izinto zase mhlabeni azinakuwanela
umpefumlo, azinakuwuhlutisa. Unyana ka Tixo uselezixelile kade wati,
usisonka

sobomi, watsho wati “ ukuba ubani ute wadla kuso esi sonka wodla ubomi
kude kube ngunapakade.” Ukukolwa ke ku Yesu wayame kuye uzinikele kuye
kukudla isixaso sompefumlo, ukungakolwa kukuba sendlaleni. Inye into
emhlabeni apa efike iwulungele umpefumlo iwuxase iwomeleze, ngu nyana ka
Tixo, lilizwi lake. Umntu ofa yindlala ufike abadule efuna indawo
ezinokudla. Lemigcobo yase mini nase busuku kubadula kwetu oko sifuna
into eyofika isiyolise incamise umxelo, kunjalo nje ayifumaneki kwe
zonto. Inkohlakalo ezi ziti andinafa, andinabomi. Kupela ngumntu ote
wakolwa ku Yesu Kristu yedwa onako ukuti, nantsi ke namhla into endibe
ndikade ndiyifuna ndingayifumani, nabu ubomi nantsi indyebo, nantsi
ilungelo, nali itamsanqa. Nditeta nje zininzi izityebi ngokwase
mhlabeni, ezinenkomo, nezisele, namasimi nantoni, zingamahlwempu amabi
ngokwase mipefumlweni kuba zingateni nalento itetwayo yile ncwadi.
Baninzi abantu abaduda imidudo bengati bonwabile kanti kunjalo nje
bayaqauka yindlala empefumlweni kuba besiti asisokubulahla ubuntu betu
ngokuzinikela entweni esingayiboniyo. Lemiyeyezelo zimpau zalendlala
ndiyitetayo, ezingongobala ziyaxela ukuba abantu bayapela, ezindywala
zezivakalisayo ukuba umzi wonakele, lemikwa yokupimisa yokurexeza
yebonisayo ukuba kungene indlala enzima. Okukwenjenje komzi kusisaziso
okokuba abantu bakohliwe yinto abayenzayo befuna ubomi, oku kukusa
kusukelwana neminye imihlali, kuhlwe kubekelwe kweminye, uti omnye
umnyaka udumele elinye isiko, uti omnye udumele elinye zimpawu ezo
zokuba ayiko kwezizinto eyonanto imanelayo umntu iwukolisayo umpefumlo
wake. Indlala ingenile iyawubuqa umzi kuba abantu benga- vumi kuguquka
bengavumi kukolwa, bengavumi kuzinikela enkonzweni ka Tixo, Wena mntu
wasekutini, oti wonwabile nantso indlala selikungena, selizakukububisa,
ke ungakolwanga leli lizwi lika Tixo ndikuxelele usazakufumanana nento
obungayikumbulele, ukufa okungunapakade.

III—Ndifike kwilizwi lesitatu nditi k tiwa apa, ite landlala inzima
yakulingena kaloku elolizwe likude, wagala naye lom-

fana ukuswela. Okuya ebemana esiva kusitiwa kuye esenempahla, namhla ke
ufike ezweni elinendlala ebehleka alale ayibonge ayigxeke, ngati yinto
yena angasokuze ayive. Ide yamngenela wavana nento embi, kuba ekaya
ebekufumana ukudla kwake kwemihla ngemihla, ehluta, engaswele nto.
Ukulamba asinto ake wayiva kowabo, asinto ake wayibona namhla yakupela
impahla amakolwane ake alike amzimela, bate nabantu ababem- xamla
abamazi, wakohlwa yinto ayenzayo, wakohlwa yinto atenga ngayo, wakohlwa
apo enza isikwelite kona kuba esaziwa ukuba ulambata, akasenanto,
wakohlwa ngumntu azibika kuye kuba edumele ubuhilihili nobunxila,
kungaseko mntu umtembayo. Akukumbula kunantsi ebe- suke ati kuye,
uzibulele ngabom ngokwako uvuna iziqamo zemikwa yako yobugeza. Lendlala
imngeneleyo lomfana ibi yeyokumvuselela, ibi yeyokumkumbuza ikowabo, ibi
yeyokumbuyisa agoduke, asinge ku Yise. Mfana usenobuntu ebengayikuke
alale nalusuku lunye endleleni apo, ebeyakusuke akumbule ekaya apo
angapila kona. Lendlala ibiyeyokumgxota kwelozwe, apume kulo,
angabisalihlala, ibi yeyokumotusa abambe indlela esinga ekaya.
Akagodukanga noko, ibi ngekancami intliziyo, ubesatanda ukuzila- ula
nokuzipata. Libe lingekapeli iratshi entliziyweni, ebesatembe amaq inga
ake ; ubesati akuko nto iyakumenza nto. Aka- yalekanga nakuba ebona
ukuba ubuntu bupelile, nempahla ayiseko, namaqabane ake amlahlile.
Fanel’ ukuba bekuko ufifi lokuba wenze into embi ekumlahleni kwake uyise
akagodukanga noko, akacinganganoko usuke wayenza lendlala into engento
kanti komnye umntu nge iba imkumbuza ikowabo, omnye umntu ange ebekele
ekaya kwa oko.

Isapindwa nanamhla lento yenziwa ngulomfana ngoku kungagoduki kwake
akuyalwa yindlala. Wena mntu ungekakolwa, usalimele mgama ilizwi lika
Tixo, akuqali namhla kubizwa ukuba ubuyele kuyihlo ongu Mdali wako.

babekelwe ukufa kube kanye kuti ke emveni koko ibe ngumgwebo.” Kunjalo

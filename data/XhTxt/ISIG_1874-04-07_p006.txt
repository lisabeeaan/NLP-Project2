ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 7, 1874.

OBULELE OMNYE.—Kute ngase Queenstown kwaxabana ama Xosa amabini ada
alwa. Ate xa alwayo omnye warola isitshetshe wasiti kata ecaleni komnye,
wambulala kwa oko. Ngoku lowo ubulele omnye usetolongweni kunye neso
sitshetshe sake.

U-GWADISO kutiwa ufuna ukungenisa inkatazo kwakona kuma Mpondo.
Uselekupuma kutatu ukuya kungenela aba Tembu, afike anqandwe kukuba
U-Mbashe uzele. Kutiwa kanjalo uke walwa nenxenye yabantu baka Ndamasi
kwako ababulalayo.

ENYE INGOZI YOMPU.—Kute E-Seymour, E- Mpofu, inkwenkwana yo Mxosa imana
idlala ngompu, yawujongisa kwintombazana etile yacukumisa ixayi,
wadubula umpu, imbumbulu yangena pantsi kweliso lasekunene, yafa kwa oko
lontombazana.

OZIBULELEYO.—Ute umfo abati ngu Mr. Early, kwele Diamond, wazibulala
ngokuzidubula. Wayete wafelwa ngumfazi wake kulonyaka udluleyo; kekaloku
seloko oko ubeba namaxesha asuke apa- mbane ubucopo. Kutandatyuzelwa
ukuba uzidubule xa anjalo.

UMBANJWA OLUMKILEYO.—Kute E-Transvaal, umfo otile obesetolongweni,
akubona umgcini tolongo ekufupi wambiza ukuba ake eze kuye. Ute omnye
akusondela umbanjwa watsiba emnyango watshixa ngapandle wemka, wasala
umgcini tolongo enguyena uvalelweyo.

PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.—Omnye umfo obala kwelozwe uti’ “ Asikuko nokuba
umcancato uya- nqweneleka ezibukweni. Ezinye inqwelo ezisand’ ukuwela
besezinentsuku ezilishumi zilinde ukubohla komlambo. Kanjalo isikolo
sama Mfengu sesiza kuqalwa. Amatye aselembiwa.”

E-TRANSVAAL kutiwa ngezimvula zidluleyo ibe nkulu indleko engeniswe
yimilambo, nokubulawa kwempahla yimvula. Omnye umfo ulahlekelwe zinkomo
ezi 500; komnye igusha ezitshabaleleyo zaba 1300, ngapandle kwendlu
yenqwelo nezinye izinto. Kwabanye kumke izindlu abebehlala kuzo.

E-MONTI isoldati ebeliye kuquba namanye amabini, lite kanti selingene
kakulu emlanjeni, la- tshona kuba belingakwazi ukudada. Kute kwa-
kubamzuzwanalabuya lati tu, labiza abanye ukuba beze kuneeda. Bate
befika laba selibuye latshona, kanti sekukupela oko; isidumba salo
sasesicolwa ngemini elandelayo.

IMITI YAKOMKULU.—Umpatiswa wemicimbi elunge nemihlaba ye Government,
U-Mr. Charles Smith, utumele kwi Mantyi, umteto wokuba zilinge konke
ezinako ukutintela ukutshatyalaliswa kwemiti esemihlabeni ye Government.
Ote wabanjwa ewona umti nokuba kungokuwuxobula amaxolo, uyakusiwa
ematyaleni.

ITOMBI KA MR. ORPEN ipantse ukubulawa ngumsi wesibane. Ibe ite xa
ilalayo yasilibala ukusicima, savuta sadaseza kufika esikondweni, kwati
ngokutsha kwamapepa asazeka asixase ukuba singanqungi, igumbi ebikulo
lazaliswa ngumsi, wapantse ukuyibulala. Icolwe ngabantu kusasa ingaseva.
Kodwa kutiwa ibuye yaqabuka.

INKOLO ZABANTSUNDU.—Kute kulamacala angase Qonce iqela lama Xosa laya
kuquba em­lanjeni. Kwakuba mzuzu omnye uvakele esiti “ngoku ndidiniwe,”
watsho watshona. Bate ebebequba naye endaweni yokuya kumnceda, babaleka
baputuma inkomo ekaya beza nazo emla­njeni, besiti ubiziwe. Zifike
inkomo zamiswa pezu kwesiziba kwatiwa yoti emtandayo itsibele pakati
ukuze ke apume yena. Kodwa akubangako ikwenzayo oko. Babe kummemeza
lomntu akenyuka emanzini, bada bagqiba kwelokuba baye kumenzela
intlombe; belindele ukuba uyakupuma’seletwa- sile, elelikulu igqira.
Noko zenziweyo zonke ezonto akakadange avele.

KUMBONISO wase Queenstown owawubikwe kwangapambili ukuba uyakuba ngomhla
we 18 ka March kunconywa ubuhle bempahla ezibe ziko. Kwabantsundu
abafumene amabaso ngu John Nakin ngenqolowa, yaba yiponti; U-Daniel
Nakin ngomgubo, iponti; U-John Nakin ngomgubo om- hlope, iponti;
U-Daniel Nakin ngepesika, ishumi lesheleni.

E-MAMPONDWENI kutiwa Umpatiswa Wemici­mbi Yabantsundu ubemisa umda
pakati kwomhlaba we government oza kupatwa ngu Mr. Orpen noka Ndamas.
Abaninzi bebelindele ukuba kuyakubako inxabano ngawo, kodwa ama Mpondo,
akatetanga nento. U-Ndamas ubetumele inkabi ezimbini azipa U-Mr.
Brownlee, noko angazivumanga omnye.

AMAZULU.—Elinye lamapepa ase Transvaal liti—U-Cetywayo utumele ilizwi
kwinkosana za Mazulu ezisemhlabeni wetu, ezibe zikade ziwubanga, ukuba
zize zishenxe zakugqiba ukuvuna. U-Cetyayo azimtandi inkosana za Mazulu,
ngo- kuba engazivumeli ukuba zizenze zonke izinto ngokutanda kwazo.
Kubonakala ukuba uyakuzuza inkatazo enkulu.

KWELE GOLIDE.—Kutiwa into enkulu enqabileyo kwabafikayo kukufumana
indawo elungileyo yokumba. Umntu ude ake agqibe nokuba yinyanga,
kwabanye zide zibe ntatu, esafuna. Kubanzima ke ngoko kwabanyuke
bengenazindlela zokuba bezigcina xa bengekabi namsebenzi. Abafika kuqala
bayayifumana kakulu. lyakuba nembuyiselo enkulu kwabaninzi.

E-GRAAFF REINET kusand’ ukubanjwa umfo obebaleke kwele Diamond,
ebezinikelwa kuye idiamond ezibiweyo. Ufunyenwe umnene selehamba
ngokulibeleyo ezitilateni, engumnumzana opeleleyo, njengomntu
ongasakatazwa naluvalo lwake; kanti noko izono zake zisamlandela, ziza
kumfumana. Ubuye wasiwa kwakwele Diamond, apo liyakutetwa kona ityala
lake.

KUMLAMBO OYI VAAL ute umfo ontsundu efuna ukuwela ngokuhlwa, weba iboti
ebibotshelelwe elunxwemenilomlambo, wangena ngayo emanzini, umlambo
uzele. Kute kwakuba pakati apuluka amapini omabini, yaza iboti yehlisa
umlambo. Kubeko abamvileyo ekala, waba noko engasenakuncedwa, wemka
kwapela. Iboti ifunyenwe selibambeke ematyeni ebifike yati ganxa pakati
kwawo.

U-REV. J. SCLATER wapesheya kwe Nciba undulukile ukuhambela pesheya
kolwandle. Liti ipepa lase Qonce ukuteta ngaye, “Imisebenzi yalondoda
pesheya kwe Nciba asikuko nokuba ibimikulu; impumelelo yayo ibonisa
uhlobo lokuzinikela kwake kuwo. Neremente yake iyakumlilela ngelixa
angekoyo. Singake angati yena nodade wabo, bahambe indlela entle
kokokutyelela kwabo.”

ABASEZINTSIZINI.—Umfo otile obala E-Mpongo uti ukubala ngentlalo yalapo,
“ Imvula ziyazona- kalisa indlela. Akuko apo zingaxingiyo inqwelo.
Igusha zifa ngemihlambi. Isihlava simngenele umbona. Abantu bangenelwe
sisifo samatambo. Abalimi nabafuyi bakala ngokusweleka kwezicaka, noko
bebaninzi abantu abantsundu ababarauleyo. Abavumi ukusebenza.” Xa
kunjalo mayibe imbi kanye intlalo yabo bantu.

KUBAQUBI BENQWELO.—Kumatyala ase Rini kube kuko nelomfo owayezise
impahla E-Rini zisuka E-Bayi, waza ngenxa yokulibala kakulu endleleni
wadliwa iponti ezintlanu. Lonto ke wayisa ematyaleni ngo January, kodwa
elotyala litetwe ngale nyanga ifileyo. Isigwebo sibe sesokuba udliwe
efanele, kuba abatwali bempahla bafanele ukubakumbulela abantu
ababatwalelayo, bazi ukuba kubo kuyindleko ukulibala. Abaninzi basuke
bakangele imali abaya kuyifumana kube kupela.

ELUSUTU.—Inkosana zakona kutiwa zihlanganisa imali yokukulula U-Sekate,
enye inkosana. Ubesand’ ukugwetywa ukuze abe setolongweni iminyaka
emibini esebenza nzima, ngenxa yokubulalisa umfo otile. Kekaloku
inkosana ezo zibe zitumele isicelo ku Sir H. Barkly, ukuba ziyikulule.
Inani ekutiwa liyafuneka nga £75. Abanye abantu bamangalisiwe kanye,
ukude sivunyelwe isicelo esinjalo. Bati umteto ubufanele ukuqina
ngokufanayo kwabazinkosi, naba ngama pakati, kwizityebi namahlwempu.

IKOTON.—Kwintlanganiso esand’ ukubako E- Rini yabalimi bekoton nabalinga
ukuyikutaza, ite ikomiti yabo “ iyaqonda ukuba ikoton iseza kuba
ngumsebenzi omkulu kweli lizwe, oya kuba nenzuzo enkulu. Kanjalo ikomiti
ngalo nyaka ungeneyo iyakwenza amalinga okupa imbewu yekoton
kwabantsundu abafuna ukuyilinga. lyakutunyelwa kwi Mantyi zendawo
ngendawo ukuze ifunyanwe kuzo. Kulungiselelwa nokuze kumboniso oyakubako
E-Rini ngalo nyaka uzayo, abantsundu abaze kubonisa eyabo, babe
nokufumana amabaso.”

UKUTENGISWA KOTYWALA.—Kwele Diamond bate ebagcini Nkantini bahlangana,
babala ipepa lesicelo, balisa kumatyala akona, ukuba avumele ukuze uty
wala babelungu butengiswe nakwabantsu- ndu ngokulula kwanje
ngakwabamhlope. Bate abembi, namalungu entlanganiso zokuzila utywala,
nekomiti yabembi, bakukuva oko, babala esabo isicelo sokuze amatyala
angayipulapuli indawo yabagcini nkantini. Lomaqela mabini asakangelene
karara. Asazi ukuba lilipina eliya kupulapulwa. Singa tina bangoyiswa
abatshabalalisi bohlanga.

KWELE GOLIDE.—Umfo abati ngu Mr. Parkin osand’ ukuvela kona uti, abantu
asebesimba baku- makulu asixenxe nasibozo. Bonke baquba ngo-
kutandekayo. Kuko indawo entsha efunyenweyo egolide ininzi. Kubafiki
akuqale kuqondakale, kuba akulula ukuzuza umhlaba wokumba; nomsebenzi
unzima. Enye into embi kukufa kwenkomo ; zingenelwa sisifo esifana
nedolosi. Nondlebe-nde bayafa. Impahla sezidla ngokutwalwa ngabantsundu
ukusuka E-Delagoa Bay, kulinganiselwe ukuba umntu azuze 10s. nge l00lbs.
Noko ku­njalo abanenyameko baya kuzuza kanye.

INKANTINI KWELE DIAMOND.—Imiteto ekuza- nyelwa ukuba imiswe kwele
Diamond ngokumalu- nga nokutengiswa kotywala yile inxenye yayo.— Akuyi
kubako namnye oya kuzuza imvumelo yo- kubutengisa ngapandle kokuba
ungumntu onqine- Iwayo sisitatu sabamelwane bake, ukuba angaba
nokuyipata kakuhle, nokuba umnyango wendlu aza kuyigcina kuyo
ungapambili kwapela, akuko ndawo abantu bangafikela ngasemva, ibe
kanjalo kwizitilata ezisekuhleni apo ziya kubonwa into ezihambisekayo.
Kufuneka ukuba kungabiko nka­ntini ipete nomnye umsebenzi. Zonke ziza
kuvalwa ngexa lesibozo ebusika, libe lelesitoba ehlotyeni. Ofunyenwe
ebuxubile uyakohlwaywa kanzima; obanjwe enomnye awapuleyo adliwe £50,
ngokokuqala, kuze ngokwesibini adliwe £100.

KWA RILI.—Umfo otile obala E-Batenjini uti, “Kuvakala ukuba U-Rili
ulungiselela ukubuya abangenele aba Tembu. Nokuba iyinyaniso na lonto
andingetsho, kodwa ixala laba Tembu, asi­kuko nokuba likulu. Bafuna
ukwazi ukuba iya kubanceda na I-Government. Oko baboyiswe ngu Rili
umhlaba wabo bake bafuna ukuwunika kwi government, kodwa babuya
bagwiiika. Ndikolwa ukuba I-Government iyakuke ibayeke bafunyanwe ngu
Rili. Bayoyika ukulikusela ilizwe labo, kanti noko abavumi kulinikela
kuma Ngesi. Zingalunga ngapezulu izinto, ike I-Govemment yautabata
lomhlaba. Enye into evakalayo kukuba inkosi ezinkulu zama Mpondo,
U-Mqikela no Ndamas baza kubanga ubukosi. Ngulowo ufuna ukuba abe
nguyena mkulu, eseloti ke mhlaumbi elopike labo lahlulwe ngezikali.”

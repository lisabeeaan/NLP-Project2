ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 16, 1884. 7

INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

IMPENDULO KA SICINA.

IZWANA elinye Nkosi yam Mhleli, kendabona igama lam, andifuni kupendula.
Ke inteto le inzulu, ndakubonisa izwi eli lodwa, Ubukosi. Ubukosi
makowetu ne Nkosi azihlangene. Inkosi le lukozo olunqabileyo
olufihliweyo. Ubukosi uti nawe abuseko lilizwe : Pofu! Ubuza ntoni ? Oba
Mamfengu bacitwa ngumfi u Ayliff Emgwanqa pantsi komti mhla zabiwa
Inkosi, zacitwa ngamabaso azo Emgwanqa. Qonda e Victoria East apa wena
uti kuseka Inkosi, watsho ke yena umfi ukuti maze kukonzwe u Rulumente
no Tixo—Elo lizwi lako likonza abana? Ukuba kunjalo litamsanqa, ukuba
akunjalo lilishwa. Aba babini bayevana, amasiko abo—oko kukuti (ubukosi
babo) yahlula ubukosi nenkosi. Uyakundiva ndipendnla ixego ngelizwi,
omtsha yena makondle amapepa ase Palamente, acedulule lombuzo kwi BLUE
BOOKS. Ngohlanga ndingumtandi walo ilizwe ndiquka wonke umzi opantsi kwe
Koloni. Pulapula isililo sakwa Zulu mleseshi wepepa sipantso
ukundikumbuza umfi u Matomela. Ndingowenu

G. C. SICINA.

UKUDUTYULWA KWABANTSUNDU E QONCE.

1.  NGUNYANA ka Dyafuta omi Ekwelerana pantsi kwamahlati ngakwa Ross.
    Wadutyulwa ngunyana ka Matole, u Mlungu obemi e Qonce ngakwesi
    sikolo sika Buluneli ngomhla we Sabata. Asizange sive nto ngelo
    tyala. Wati umfana ka Captain Dyafuta koka Matole msukundijolisa
    ngompu wondidubula ngokuba yi Cawa; wati oka Matole Ukuba uyintoni
    uyintaka nje: watsho kwatsha, wekata ezantsi kwenkaba, bamtwalela
    kwa Ggirasbe kwaye kungasabonakali ukuba no Gqira unokunceda nto,
    wabuba kwangalomini. Asivanga nto ayenziweyo umbulali lowo, idindala
    lake yayiyi Mantyi yase Qonce eyayihamba nombulali lowo ekwela
    amahashe akowabo umbulali lowo. Asizanga seva tyala lombulali lowo,
    asizanga simbone edlula nasemnyango wetolongo.

2.  Kwadutyulwa intombazana e Newtown ngu Mlungu e Qonce apa, ayafa
    kodwa yona, wanikwa iminyaka emitatu.

3.  Kwadutyulwa inkwenkwe yakwa Mdushane li Jeriman Equzini ayafa
    yapants’ ukufa kodwa. Akuko nto yenzekayo saka sayiva ngelo Jeriman.

4.  Kwadutyulwa inkosana yapesheya kwe Nciba li Jeriman Enyatela. Asazi
    nto ngalonto. Sapela ngama Jerimane.

5.  Kudutyulwe umfana ka John Dungane nge hagile : uselapa yena kwa
    Gqirashe. Abangalaziyo elo igama lokuba ngu John ngu Tunyiswa.

6.  Umfana ekutiwa ngu Edward, umfana omhle kanye ngapandle kwekwele,
    udutyulwe kwa li Jeriman ngakwa Henisi Etyusha pantsi kwamahlati.
    Ndiyoyika ukuba ijuri zase Qonce ziyawa kuti alinatyala elo Jeriman.
    Sada safa betu ngama Jeriman ; hayi ukuswela imfundo safa zezi juri
    zeli lizwe, abantu abangayaziyo lendawo iti: “ Ungaketi ubuso
    ekugwebeni.” Nale iti: “ Ungagwebi ngokukohlakeleyo.” Azi ke pambi
    ko Mgwebi Omkulu koba nje na ? Ndisicaka sako

N. G. NONTSHINGA NELLA.

UMSEBENSI WE MBUMBA.

NKOSI MHLELI WENDABA.—Ndixolele ngemigcana embalwa endifuna ukuba
undifakele kwelo pepa lako lidumileyo. Lemigcana nditi ndincede ngayo
ingesikalazo endinaso—nasi ke :

Kuqala ndifuna ukubekisa kwi “Mbumba Yamanyama” ngokuba eligama likulu,
ke sifanele ukulifuna ngeziroro zetu. Ndifuna ukuva kuyo ukuba ibona
nina ngohlanga olu enzima. Kambe ukuba asikangeli asisokubona nto. Ke
nditeta malunga nempato, nemiteto ekoyo kuti bantsundu. Mandixele
enditetela kuzo mna okwam. Kuqala kumatyala atetwa zi ‘ Jaji.’ Niti
kulungile na, kuyimfanelo na, ababambi masela, ukuba ibe kwangabo
abamiselwa ukuketa amazwi, nokupengulula amatyala ala masela ebesiwe
kwangabo ematyaleni. Kanti ke noko nilindele ukuba babone imfanelo
zamazwi amasela lawo. Mna andiboni ukuba banako, andilindele mfanelo
kwabanjalo. I Farmers zonke azifuni ukuba zibone umntu ontsundu engene
ematyaleni ngobusela aze abuye akululeke nokuba inteto zake sezimhlope
ngokunjani. Ke ndibuza “ Embumbeni ” ukuba iyikangela njani na lonto ?
Ukuba inamalungelo okubekisa komkulu iziroro nezililo zohlanga
Iwakowayo, kutenina ukuba ingayibekisi lonto ? Amazwi o Mprofeti u
Jeremiah kwi Zililo zake isahlako 3. verses 33—36 anjengokungati waye
watetela esi simo uhlanga oluntsundu lukuso. Manene akowetu vukani!
Bekani imipu nemikonto—nilwe ngemilomo yodwa, nincedise abateteleli
abambalwa bohlanga oluntsundu. Uxolo Nkosi yam ngokutabata ixesha lako
kangaka andisokubuye ndenjenjalo. Ndim owako

F. D. KETIBALA.

Queenstown, June 6,1884.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI wendaba zamanene akowetu adumileyo kakulu kanye,
kawundipe isituba kwelo pepa lakowetu ndifuna ukupendula u Mr. John
Harper wase King William’s Town. Uti ucela iramente zonke ukuba
zihlangane zitumele isicelo e Palamente ukuze i Palamente ihlangane ne
Buluneli ukuze i Ruluneli itumele umteto wegunya pezu kohlanga Iwakowetu
oluntsundu ngesizatu sokuselwa kotywala, kuba kwaka kwabako uncedo
ngomteto wegnnya lobusela. Nditi mna umteto wona ulungile ukumondla
umntu owuhambisayo abile, kodwa ke noko awukwazi ukusindisa umntu. Nditi
kwala elixa amapolisa ezalise lonke ngomteto, kube kokona impahla
ipelayo kubiwa. Nditi mna kuwe nangu umteto onamandla: “ Memela iramente
ku Tixo zitandazele uselo olunamandla ukuba lupele.” Kwakuya kutike xa
kutandazelwa ukuba lupele, kopela noluncinane uselo, obuti wena
wakukangela uti ukuzitetelela kwako, ukuba benjenje ngekungekonto.
Ukutsho ke mna nditi memela iramente ku Tixo kwakuya kuti ukuza komoya
ka Tixo pakati koluntu lonke kulunge into zonke. Nantso indlela eyoti
ukuba ipumelele bati onyana betu nentombi zetu xa batandazayo, “ Tixo
wobawo betu sikululele intshaba zetu njengabo.” Kunokuba bati Palamente
sibandezelele intshaba zetu njengobawo. Mandime apo. Ndim,

PATULENI KAKAZA..

Gaga, June 10, 1884.

INTSHUTSHISO E DAYIMAN.

MNUMZETU, ndiyaqonda ukuba ilizwi lako libanzi, ukwaza livakale kude ;
kauncede ukwaze ngale ndawo umhlaumbi bangade beve no M.L.A. haze
'bafake isandla. Ezantsi apo ndiyatemba ukuba nakuba iko lento kutiwa
ngumteto we pasi, andikolwa ukuba uyafana nalo walapa; kuba apo ipasi
isetyenziswa nxa umntu ahambayo esiya kwezinye indawo, njengokusuka e
Koloni aye pesheya kwe Nciba. Apa ke tina akunjalo, ipasi iyibatyi
yomntu, ukuba uke wati ngenxa yokuba umntu osebenzayo kuqelekile
ukuyikulula, wayishiya nxa utunywa kwenye indawo, woti ukuze upile ube
akuqubisananga nazo inja zakomkulu, kodwa ukuba zikubonile uyakukutshwa
ngu Mlungu wako sewuvalelwe kwamnyama amagumbi. Kude kubeko nengqina
ezenziwa kanye mhlaumbi kabini ngenyanga. Kwezi ngqina kuye kuti ngenxa
yokuba umzi ubanzi, abantu abamnyama abandawonye ukwaka kwabo, bate sa;
iti ke ingqina yakupuma ibekise cala nye, iye kubangenela ezindlwini
ibabambe bonke abafunyenwe bengena pasi kwelo cala ; ize ke ngenye imini
yakupuma ibekise kwelinye icala iye kwenjenjalo nakona.

Kodwa ke kuleyo indawo yokubangenela ezindlwini ngebeba bayalungisa,
kuba bapelisa ubuvila, kuba iko lempi ifane ihlale nakuba ingaswele
msebenzi. Kekaloku into embi yile yokuba bengaketi, kuba ungafana unayo
ipasi, mhlaumbi ubuye edinaleni, nxa ungenayo abati ukuyibiza yi special
pass exela ukuba lapo kwako ufumene imvume enkosini yako, uya kubanjwa
nawe kunye nelo qela lingenazo, ati ukuba u Mlungu wako akaputumanga
ugwetywe nawe njengabanye. Into ke leyo ebonisa ukuba akusenziwa
ngumteto, kwenziwa kodwa kuba simnyama, singenabani unokusibuzela, kuba
nokuba bayakwazi kakuhle ukuba ungumntu osebenzayo, bemazi no Mlungu
wako, unga- yivelisanga ipasi kupela uyakulala kwemnyama. Nokuba
ungumntu ozenzela ishishinana usaziwa kakuhle kofuneka noko unayo; leyo
ke bati yi protection pass. Kambe kuko nabanxiba nezakwa Rulumente
(uniform) kanti noko nxa ungena petshana libonisa ukuba ungumsebenzi
wakwa Rulumente uya kubanjwa nezo ngubo zako zobu Rulumente.

Ndiyibala nje lento mnumzetu ndenziwa buhlungn bentliziyo, kuba nditi
ndakubona abantu bakowetu bequtywa bekonxiwe ngati ugamasela, ndive
kukubi kum ; ndive ndisiti akwaba bendinokufumana oluka Moses, bendiya
kumka e Dayiman apa nxa ndide ndabonwa kwangomnye wama Hebere. O!
ndixolele mnumzetu, ndingumfiki nokuba ngumfiki, ndiyawa nxamele
ukuposisa ndisafika njalo. Mandipeze ndikukataze ngokwaneleyo, ndisesako
isicaka,

LILAZINGASULWA.

Kimberley, 25 April, 1884.

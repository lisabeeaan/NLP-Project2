ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 1, 1887.

AYE PINA AMADODA UMZI UYONAKALA.

KU MHLELI WE SIGIDIMI.

NKOSI—Uzungandisoli uti ndikutululele umncono ndati ndaqalela
ngokwongulela Imvo ucambu namhlanje. Noko nike nirwixane, malupele ucuku
nibambisane ngalompanga ungangezwe, upezu kwetu; nditsho ke ngendawo
yotywala. Nditi; wuhlabeni umkosi niwudabalalise. Mandinga nqwanqwi
mandi sendingena emongweni wento.

Ummeli wetu esisand’ukumnyula wayesiti o Qonce apa mhla ngengqungqutela
yabanyuli ababeze kupulapula imvo zake, wati akaluboni uncedo oluvela
ekuvingcweni kwentengo yotywala kwezinqila zingasatengisiyo ; esiti—
way’etingaza esitubeni okunye kukuvula ezindawo ziva- liweyo, okunye
kukuba yaliwe intengo emhlabeni wonke kwabantsundu. Nantso ke indawo
engati ifumanekile, ibe yeyokubulelwa luluntu lonke. Andinatemba noko
ukuba soyizuza—Nangani ummeli wetu eyakuzamela londawo ibanzi, sixasa
tina kuyo ngamandla onke, masifele kule sesiyizuzile. Masingake sivume
ukuba siyihlutwe, kuba tina sikangeleyo siyazi ukuba iyanceda.

Ummeli wetu usatingaza uyatandabuza. Masizisuse ezi zitingatinga zake,
masiwapelise amatandabuzo, simkanyisele, ukukangele oko kuti. Ukuba ute
wabuduza ebumnyameni ityala lingasala nati. Makavele obe kwezinqila
zivingciweyo, nobe melene nazo, amxelele abekubona kungeka vingcwa nako
oku akubonayo namhla kuvingciwe nje. Ummeli wetu akalilo igqweta
lokugqweta inyaniso; ukuba simbeke emkondweni sau- kanyisela, akayi
kusidanisa, woxatisa anyanise ukushenxisa esi sidlabantu, lo Molokwe
ugqiba abantwana betu. Mazipume incwadi kuti sonke ngabanye, zimnele nje
ngesipango sehlobo—Makati elele zimgubungele, makati akubeka ngapa abone
ngazo,—ati akuguquka abone zona zodwa,—kute kupi napi, angaboni nto
yimbi,—ade agqoboke, apele amatandabuzo agqoboze nabanye, ize inene
isale nati.

Malivele ixegwazana elibone umxasi walo etshona encwabeni lotywala,
liwuhlabe umkosi! Mayivele inkasankasa ye xego, ebone onyana,
nabazukulwana belandelelana bekukuliswa ngulo msinga wotywala, iwuhlabe
umkosi!! Mayivele inzwakazi eyayizekwe linene lamanene, esel’ ihleli
ezaleni, imajacu, ingasenalo neselwa lomntwana, ngenxa yokuba umzi wayo
waya kutyebisa inkatini, mayiwuhlabe umkosi!! Mabavele abazali ababulela
onyana ku Tixo babaxasa ngesimo sobulungisa, babafundisa batemba, bati
ngombulelo, namhla igama letu liyakuduma ngomsebenzi wabantwana betu,
indlu yetu iyakuqina ; lati kunjalo itemba labo laya kutshona kwisiziba
sotywala balila isililo solilela ukububa kowamazibulo, mabauhlabe
umkosi!! Iyepina indoda ebone isitandanekazi sayo siye kweyela
kulentyonkobila yotywala, isinyula singanyuleki, mayiwuhlabe umkosi!!
Upina umntwana ohlala etwala oyise nonina ebagodusela ekaya
bengenangqondo, abati bakuqabuka babuye babuyele kulomgxobozo undibongo
bahlala beseyela kuwo, mabauhlabe umkosi!! Bapina abazintsizana ababona
abatandwa babo beabdula pezu komhlaba, kanti akuseko buntu, sekuhamba
amadlaka angenampefumlo, mabauhlabe umkosi!! Upina u Mhleli we Sigidimi,
umhlahla-ndlela, ogecela uhlanga; umondli wabo Ntintili nabo Mngunana
nabo Koyi abebe hlaba umkosi, apo kungazange kunkenteze Mxosa,
abamancwaba angabekwanga litye ngowazana nabo? Yinina ukuba abe
ngusikulumi ongenamlomo, yinina ukuba angauhlabi umkosi. Malipakame
ilizwi kwimimango ngemimango, liti—“ Aye pina amadoda, umzi uyonakala!”

Ingxoxo inkulu pakati kwetu ngamalungelo etu okunyula amalungu okusimela
e Palamente—Siyakolisa ngaleyo ndawo. Ke ngamalungelo ayakusinceda
ntonina lawo, xa unogumbe wotywala engene kakulu pezu komhlaba wonke, xa
seluntywilisiwe lonke uluntu Iwakukuliswa? Zise zintlambi ezinamandla
zodwa ezisavele ngentloko. Andivelisi nomanyama-nyamana wokununusa u
sapo. Kade udibona. Ininzi into ema Xoseni nase Mamfengwini efe yatshona
pantsi isashukuma. Ndingabalisa nangezinye izizwe ezitshone kwapela,
kulo mbulali, kulo mtshabalalisi, kulo Satana.

Masixase oka Solomon ummeli wetu. Sendite kwangoku- njalo, wosizamela
ngomxelo wonke woti ukuba ufezile, woba ngumhlanguli ngenene, amongamele
ngentloko namanamagxa uyisekazi u Saul—

  Usiduma banzi Ukokel’ amabandla Umnye. akayikupambuka.

Kumkosi onjalo nam ndingatwasa ndibe liciko—ndinga vuleka umlomo
ndibonge—Andikapeli. Ndisatshayelela. Ndisawisa zwi nje, ndikangele
ukusatyelwa kwalo, ndobuye ndivele. Namhla ndisa hlaba umkosi ndisati.
Aye pina amadoda umzi uyonakala! Utsho u

XEGO DALA.

UKULESWA KWAMA PEPA.

Kwaleswa ipepa eliyole kunene ngu Mr. R. Ndungane, elalingo hambo lwake
Iwe 1879, esitilini sase Ndenxa (Gat- berg.)

INGXOXO.

Kubeko izixoxo malunga nendawo ezitile zelopepa, ngokukodwa malunga
nendawo ate kuyo—“ Kwelozwe lase Ndenxa, wabona isizwe esibala limnyama
kunene sinenwele ezinde, ekutiwa ngama Aprentyisi ukubizwa kwaso, igama
awati akabanga nakuliqonda, kuba abangawo ama Slams, kuba bakonza u Tixo
opilileyo bona. Utsho wati okwake yena uyatemba ukuba bayinzala ka Ham,
ekute esasiqalekiso sidala sapumelela kwabanye.”

Omnye wamalungu woyiseka mpela namhla wada wanga kungaba kuyinene kanye
oko, nangani ebengakolwa ncam. Omnye wayiqinisela kanye indawo yokuba,
abantu base South Africa bayinzala ka Ishmael, abayiyo eka Ham.

Kuvele indawo eti—i Arabia ayizange iluvelise uhlanga olungangolu Iwa
Bantu (Bantu Race) ama Africa enene, inzala ka Ham, abaneqalekiso
enanamhla lisaya lisuswa ngu mnqamlezo (Cross) we Nkosi. Lendawo ate
umbali wepepa akayiqonda kweli gama liti Aprentyisi, iteta ukuba babe
ngama Apprentice kuma Bulu. Liteta lonto elogama belixake umbali
welopepa.

Waye qubisene nesizwe esinendawo efana nesa kowabo, “ nakuba sona
singenalo eligama libi kunene lobu Mfengu.” inteto leyo ete yakutetwa
yasika, kwinxenye eyayiko entlanganisweni apo, yade yatutuzelwa yinteto
eyati—Esisizwe singama Mfengu, sizakuba luhlanga olutsha, sifunzele
pambili, siyanda ebu Krestwini, nasentweni zokukanya.

OMNYE WE MITETO YENTLANGANISO OYI RULE VIII.

Lomteto upete indawo yokuba, wonke ubani olilungu lale ntlanganiso ete
akabiko entlanganisweni, engenasizatu sivakalayo e Komitini, wodliwa i
2s. 6d., ngapandle kwenxaso le imele ilungu ngalinye kade. Kodwa ke
lomteto wacitwa nge saziso sanyakenye, ekungoko sekumi inani lenxaso
lodwa eliyi 5s. ngomnyaka.

Ke kwako abati makubeko abancedisi be Treasurer ne Secretary, koko
londawo ayipumelelanga.

Bekuko nenye indawo ebiteta ngokuba maziguqulwe i School Holiday. Nayo
leyo ayipumelelanga.

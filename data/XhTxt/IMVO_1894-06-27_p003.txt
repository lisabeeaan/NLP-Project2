IMVO ZABANTSUNDU (NATIVE OPINION) JUNE 27, 1894.

I QONCE KE MFUNDO,

Iroba lokuvalwa kwe Zikolo ngolwesi Tatu lwegqitileyo 20 June, afike
amatshantliziyo ngemicimbi yemfundo yohlauga alenza into enkuiu — enze
imvuselelo enkulu yomoya wokuba i Qonce liyenze eyalo indawo kumsebenzi
omkulu kunene wokuba abamnyama balapa babe ngabantu abafundileyo. Oku
kwenziwe ngendlela epumelele kamnandi kunene yokucela ukuba intsapo ye
Sikolo ease Wesile esipetwe ngu Mr. P. Sidzumo ivume amaculwana
alungiselelweyo; encediswe kakuble kulonkonzo yenkulu ikwaya
yalotyalike. Kwababeko ukukutaza into ekungayo ibingo Mr. Tengo- Jabavu
obecelwe yi Komiti ye Skolo ukuba aze atate isihlalo: awunike isihomo
esingapezulu umsebenzi ngokunxiba isivato esimiselwe Libaudla Lemfundo
epakainileyo sabapunyeleliswe lilo (sobu Under-graduate be Cape of Good
Hope University)—isidabane nomnqwazi; kubeko o Rev. J. Mahonga, Messrs.
T. Putu, J, G. Hako, D. Gudula, K. Lotsha, J. Mbasa, G. Damane, P.
Xiniwe, R. B. Mlilwana, C. P. Matyolo nabanye. lotlanganiso ibe yenobom
yaye ibonakele ukuba izilahlele ekuzonwabiseni ngento zonke ezite
zaqutywa; abangayilibaliyo ukuzola bonke ababeko.

IMVUKO NEZIPITSHI.

Umgcini-sihlalo isitulo, wachola into ekungayo, ukutabatelakweyake
imbonakalo de kube yeyonisebenzi, uyaleze bukali ukuba intlauganiso
iwugcine ngentliziyo ezishushu umsebenzi kuba usayilwa, yaye indlu
eyilwayo ingandule ibentle; kodwa banetemba ukuba ngo- nyamezelo eso
sakiwo (semfundo) basibeka isiseko, soda sibe sesikulu abaya kusibuka
bonke.

Utsho wacela intsapo ka Mr. Sidzumo evume u “Heavenly Father”; yatsho
kwabhetele.

Kuposwe amazwi ngu Mr. John Mbasa, umvan.eli, ote, opambi kwabo ke
ngumsebenzi otamsanqelekileyo wobu Krestu, ekufuneka wonke umntu efake
igxalaba anganeliswa kukuba mgumpula-puli. Uvakalise usizi lobuncinaue
baba- utwana angaziyo ke uokuba bapelele na apo, bekufuneka kubanjwe
amaxhwanana amaqaba la azalise idolopu atiwa gwa ngesikolo. (Kwadunywa.)

Yakugqiba u Mr. Tengo-Jabavu ucele u Mr. Lotsha ukuba apose amazwi,
wamazisa ngokuti ungum Rabe ngobu Hlelo, kodwa imfundo ingapandle
kwamahlelo —iyeyokubafunqula ngobuhlanga, avuyayo ke ukuba amcele u Mr.
Lotsha.

U MR. LOTSHA uvuyiswe ku ku bona abafundi nofundisayo pezu kwalonto
ibunjwayo kuba iyakude ibe yinto exesheni. Ute lenkonzo iyilwayo
imkumbuza endala, kuba uyakumbula eselula ukuba ityalike yase Wesile
ibikwisitrata ebekusakutiwa sesabafazi kudala, esiya sinetyalike endala.
Njengokuba setshilo Umgcinisihlalo izinto ziquty wa kancina no ziyo
zikulu ngokukulu, yanmn’ ukufudu-fuduswa ke Ityalike ukude izokuba
kulendawo ikuyo ngokunje. Okwenene into iqalwa ngabantu ababini
nabatatu, ku­yo kungena abanye bade banole, njengo­kuba selete Umgcini
sihlalo, yayinguye no Mr. Mpondo kwengapambili. Ukumbula enye into kwa
Bidli, apo kudala kwadibana amadoda ambalwa etandazela ukuhlako Moya;
bamane ukubona kukanya isibane etyalikeni yonke imihla ngoku- hlwa.
Ubede abuze ukubayintonina cye- nziwayokona—kanti ngamadod’ ambalwa
azama imvuselelo yo Moya wada naye waya walunguza ngefestile; utsho wati
ke naleyo into yati kanti itshayelela inguquko engazange yapinda kona.
Naie into yode ibe yinto. Ute njengo kuba imfundo ipakati komzi nje
yiudawo yabalazi namaxegokazi ukuba eze azokuvuyisana nabantwana
nokubayala ukuba bakutalele umsebenzi wemfundo, abazali mabangagwagwisi
ngokuti barola imali zabo bona ngekwata—kuba noinalusi uye alandeiwe
ngumninimpahla, ngokunjalo netitshala kufuneka ilandelwe ; kufuneka
ibuzwe into ebifunda yona intsapo yakubuya eskolweni ngabazali. UvuyTswe
ngumse­benzi ukubamhle, watsho wati, iminyaka ayifani, nyakenye bobona
ngapezu koko, kungabiko nokukohlela oku (hoor, hoor) (bebekohlela abantu
ngetuba lomkuhlane okoyo). Bona madoda abanakufundisa abantwana
babondle, lowo ngumsebenzi wonina. (Kwadunywa.) Bangade bona madoda
basebenze, barole imali zokufunda, kodwa ngonina amababakutalele.
(Kwadunywa.)

UMONGAMELI walate ubukulu balamazwi obudoda ka Mr. Lotsha ate ubengamazi
ukuba usiteti kangaka. Abasakuba sapulukana naye ngamaxesha azayo. Wenze
amanqaku okubonisa ukuba yeyonina ikakulu ingqeqesho eshicilele- kayo
engqondweni yomntwana, wabonisa ngamadoda abafunda ngemisebenzi yawo.
Bangake bangaba bayeva bangati betwele amabhaku nje babe abeva
(kwahlekwa) bayayipika lonto, Uyatemba ukuba ama­zwi ake akasokuxakanisa
mntu kuba aba­nye baye bajiwule iqiya elixa bancedwayo. Walate ubukulu
bembnto ekungayo wati isinala ayinto yimbi kukwanda komse benzi wesikolo
endaweni. Akuko nto inganqanda ukuba bangafundi bapume lele imviwo
belapo e Qonce bengadange baye koma Lovedale. Ecela u Mr. Putu ukuba
atete ute, mabanyamezele bakutobele ukuyalwa ngeziudlela. Umzi obomvu
uba namaxesha oti wenze inteto zokululeka nokuyala ulutsha;
kwanabamhlope banalo. Bamzi uyakupumelela ngokutinina ke bona base
.zikolweni xa kungayalwayo? xa into enkuiu iyakuba kukudela nokuti nxhim
kwakuba kuyalwa besinga kungenziwa ezinye izinto. (Kwahlekwa), Uminyaka
ilishumi ekule dolopu akakumbuli kusenziwa zinteto zolohlobo; umzi
maunyamezele ukuyalwa ungadeli—njengo­kuba lisitsho elinye iculo labo
ukuti “Yala Nkosi singadeli.” Imbuto zabo zahlala izezingancedisi kwaka
nto nje- ngezimitshato; kulungile ukuba nabo ke basingate into
ezincedayo. Utsho wacela

U MR. PUTU ote, uti akukangela umntwana ave enokumtanda enobubele kuye
nokuba ngowomnye umntu. Ute imfundo yinto enkuiu. Isikalo sabo kukuba
imfundo yabo iyahilizisa ayincedi luto kwabanayo;ke ngoku into
amabayikangele kukuba bebefundise njanina lento isuke yajika
yasiqalekiso, balinge ukuteta nqina limbi. Yena ngemfundiso engumbopi
zincwadi nje uxolile. Unga wonke ofundiswayo anganolwazi lomsebenzi
wezandla. Ute imfundo ayinamsebenzi kakulu xa umntu engenayo ingqondo
yake yokuvela, imfundo yona xa isekelwo yingqondo yokuvela, iyamzinzisa
umntu, abe nesonka ngayo, abe luncedo emzini wakowabo, akwazi ukuhlonela
abantu kwanokubapata, Isiposo kukungakutazwa kwentsapo, ka­nti ingozi
yaboyimfundo encinane edala iratshi kuba umntu ongazi nto eba uyazi
yena: isuke ke imdlokovele, ambatshe ke. Xa umzali emfundise kakuhle
umntwana wake umtsibisa amaza namafukufuku oiuhlobo alukankanyileyo,
Lengozi ke ikonina. Ute elilizwe lishwabene kuba atnadodanaaswele
ukuqonda; beselifanelwe kukuba nco ngoku—kube zimesilane, bacweli
bazimeleyo, basiki, njalo njalo. (Hopr, hoor). Ute abasoze babe luhlanga
ngapandle kwalomisebenzi kufuneka beyifundisilwe ukuze bapile babe
ngabantu. Uyaleze onozala ukuba ba’sebenzise Intsapo yakugoduka iteze
iukuni (hear, hear), ik’ amanzi, iqukeze kungatiwa kwati kwati iya
kutinina ifundile nje. Njengokuba engumbopi-zincwadi nje ngekulungile
ukuba inkosikazi yake ibingumsiki babe ngamaxakaxaka bance disane
(hear). Uyaleze ukuba imfundo inganyatyelwa ukuze kubeko intsindiso
ngayo. (Cheers).

eziva engxashekile ngomcimbi wokufundiswa kwabo bonke abantsundu, kodwa

ebonisa ngokubako kwayo ukuba iyaxhasa ukuba makufundiswe. Umsebenzi
wemfundo ngomkulu kunene obufanelwe kukusingatwa ngenyameko ngumzi
wonke; kuba ubuqaba abusoze buncedise kumashishini abalangazelela ukuba
amiswe ngabohlanga; ayakubako ngalemfundo kupela. Njengokuba ke u Mr.
Putu ebesiti iyahlininikisa imfundo yabanye mabalinge ke ukuba kupele
ngabo, ingabuye itetwe ngabo lonto. Ilungile imfundo nakwabakulu.

ngebeteta amafene pipipi. Ngobuko babo baka umzi— ezinteto zobabuta babe
ngumzi, ngebeyitandazela ke bayikutalele. U Satana udume ngokupa
abangasebenziyo umsebenzi nokumana ebafakela imicinga yokuciuga, ide
indoda ibonakale ivnka ebhedeni yayo isiya kwezonto ayicingise zona,
(Kwadunywa ) Ingaluncedo kubo endaweni yokuciuga ezonto zongendawo bete

“ Mr.” no “ Miss.” Bazama ke ukuba baze babe ngo Mr no Miss abafundilevo
abanezimilo bonke. Hai into ibiyakuba ntle ukuba libe elikaba beliyakuba
leli- funde lonke, U Rulumeni ubalungisele mababize abantwana bese

ngumfundisi akufika endaweni kukuzama isikolo nokuba akukabiko makolwa,
asingenise pantsi kointi nokuba ngumqolomba, afundise intsapo. Obona
butsolo emzini kukuba nesikolo nokuba bapele ngo e “b a” kulungile, kuba
yinto enjengamanyatela omntu obeka nnyawopambi kolunye kanti uyakude.
Ubonise ubukulu balento ukude umntu ongafumane ayirole imali, u
Rulumeni, ayirole ngemfundo elixa bona benza kancinane. Imfundo inkulu
ke ngoko kuba kupela kwento abafumana imali ngayo ku Rulumeni. Ute
intsapo sisipo sika Tixo kubazali, yindawo yabo ko ukubafundisela
intsapo yake—ute namhlanje abozeli nokozela (busebuhambile ubusuku) kuba
bayaqonda ukuba inkulu into esiyenzela bona. Nangona ingabalele kakuhle
inkonzo esiyenzela bona ngokwangoku, xa benganyatnekelwanga, noko
befumene amabhaso nje ahangezi esikolweni. Uya­leze abazali ukuba
bafundise intsapo ukuze bati bakufa ingabaqalekisi emangcwabeni abo, iti
silele apa isikelemu esingumzali warn, esingandifundisanga ndinjenjenje.

bafundisiweyo eziya kuba luncedo lomzi, Utsho wati intsapo ibaninzi

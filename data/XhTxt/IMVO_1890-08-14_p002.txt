Ixabiso elirolwayo e Dordrecht ngombona  omhle olinywe ngabantsundu abamele izipaluka zangakona libamba kwi 5/6 ne 6/ ngenxhowa.

E-Kimberley kuko umfo ontsundu obepambi kwamatyala ngokushunqula indlebe  yenkazana i gamazinyo, onikwe umnyaka ebunzimeni.

E Leydknburg kwelama Bbulu kubhubhe Ibhulukazi elingu Mrs. Fourie, suke kuti sekukutshwe nezaziso zexesha lokungcwaba, kwakubon’ ukuba kudlule iyure ezilishumi kubonwe ngomntu eshukuma ebuyelwa bubom. Wayeseledleke kakulu ukuba abuye abenexesha kweli, ate ke wabhubba impela ngemini elandelayo.

E Bloemfontein sekulungiselelwa imigcobo yokuvulwa kololiwe, okuyakuba ngawo lonyaka.

E Kimberley ngenye imini kuxabene amadodana amnyama abedlala i cards, suke  enye yaqubula isitsbetshe yegqupu kwenye  entliziyweni, yaposa enzaleni.

E Bloemfontein kuxelwa ezengozi efu. nyenwe ngu Right Rev. Bishop Knight Bruce, owe ebekwele ehasheni wakutuka ubuso wapuka um’bombo.

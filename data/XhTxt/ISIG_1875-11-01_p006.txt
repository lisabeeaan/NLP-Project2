ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1875.

UKUBUBA KUKA KAMA.

KUBO bonke abanento abayaziyo ngale nkosi kuya kuba lusizi ukuva
njengokuba kulusizi, ukubala, ngokuti namhla seyikwinani labangase koyo.
Kwinyanga ezayo sitemba ukuba imbali yake soyingena ngokuzeleyo.
Namhlanje indawo enkulu sifuna ukubikela bonke abazizihlobo zake okokuba
useleye elupumlweni lwake. Njengoko baziyo abaninzi ubesele neminyaka
emibini elele pantsi. Ngalo lonke eloxesha ubonakalise kubo bonke
abakufupi kuye ukuba kuye ukufa akusenalo ulwavila, itemba lake liku
Kristu abemkonza ngemini zokupila kwake. Ilizwi lokugqibela atete loua
lelokuti “Namhla nakuba ndibe ndinabo ubutyebi nemihlaba nezinye izinto
zalo mhlaba, zonke ndizibekele bucala ndasala no Kristu yedwa, oyena
ulilifa lam.” Enye indawo abonise ngayo ukuba ungomnye wabanokuti “Kufa
lupina ulwavila lwako?” ute xa aza kuqauka wabonakala encuma ngokungati
selebonana no Msindisi wake.

Ubube ke lomfo ngokuhlwa ko Mvulo, ngo 25 ka October waza wancwatywa
ngolwe Sitatu ukumka komlila. Abebeko encwabeni baya kuhlala beyikumbula
lomini kude kube nini. Kuqale kwakusasa abantu befika lati liza kufika
ixesha elimiselwe ukumcwaba waba umzi useluzele ngabanxibileyo namaqaba.
Emva kuhle kwexesha lesitatu kuhlanganisenwe ngase ndlwini ka Kama, saza
ke isidumba sake sakutshwa sabekwa pambi kwendlu. Bate abantu abakoyo
babeta bazalisa. Kwabamhlope bekuko Imantyi yakona u-Mr. Fieldin? neyase
Dikeni u-Mr. Nightingale no Mr. Cumming nabanye. Kwabantsundu singase
sisiti bebede basuka nase Nxukwebe nako ma Dike; kwinkosana singase
sikankanya oka Sandile noka Maqoma.

Ngenye indlela singati ukuxela into ebiko bezilapo izityebi namahlwempu,
amagqoboka namaqaba, omhlope nontsundu, umxosa nemfengu— kwaqondakala
ukuba lenkosi isishiyileyo ibibekwe zindidi zonke.

Siqube ke ngeyokuya encwabeni. Site sakuba isidumba sibekwe ebaleni
kwavunywa iculo— kweyase Wesile incwadi eliqala ngokuti.

“Tina bantu siyapela
Ukububa kusondele.”

Emva kwalo kwenziwe umtandazo ngu Mr. P. Mzimba wase Lovedale. Ugqityiwe
kusingiswe etyalikeni kufike kwakokela intsapo zesikula, zilandelwa
zezimantyi sezike zakankanywa kunye no Rev. Benjamin Impey umfundisi
wakona namanye amangesi. Emva kwabo kulandele abatwele ityesi, yaza
yangamakosikazi azizihlobo zobubileyo, yabake luninzi lwabanye. abantu
Bebonke site boba kumakulu asixenxe nasibozo. Benze ke urozokazi olude
kunene kanti bebehamba ngabatandatu, beko nabade bafika esibozweni nase
sitobeni. Kufikiwe etyalikeni umfundisi uleseshe kweyokuqala ku
Makorinte xv. wenza iculo wabuya walesesha umbedesho kwaza ke kwapunywa
ukuyiwa encwabeni. Ngeqinga elibe liveliswe ngu Mr. Impey wase Rini,
lifike incwaba lambiwa ngase tyalikeni, kubonakaliswa ukuba

yena ibiyinkosi etanda ukuhlala ngase masa- ngweni endlu ka Tixo. Kupuny
we ke etyalikeni kwase kuyiwa encwabeni ngasentla kuhle kwendlu
yetyalike. Akuba efakiwe (ngexesha lesine) kubuye kwenziwa izilesesho
ngumfundisi kwaculwa amaculo amabini, elamaxosa nelamangesi. Emva koko
u-Mr. Tele upose amazwana osizi okuba namhla kulilwa, wakankanya
nentlalo ka Kama, ngokulinyamekela nokulikutalela kwake ilizwi le Nkosi,
ngokulungiselela kwake into yonke engu- mntu nokuba yeyalupina uhlanga,
ngokunyusa kwake isizwe sakowabo ngokusuka abambelele ku Kristu nokumka
kwake ngoxolo xa ashiya lomhlaba, kuba umke encuma. Ubonise kanjalo
ukuba umzekelo walomfo ka Cungwa ubufanele ukwenza into yonke ibambelele
kulo Kristu abebambelele kuye u-Kama.

Zibe njalo ke ezalo mini yesitatu. Site sazi- nqamlela ukuzixela kuba
sitemba ukubuye sizi- kakanye. Nokukude sitete kangaka namhlanje
sesisenza ukubeka ilitye kumfo ote ngemikwa yake wazifumanela imbeko
nokutandwa yinto yonke, umfo esite xa simkapayo seva sikumbula amazwi
aku II. Samuel III. 38. esiya kugqibela ngawo ukunibikela ukuba namhla
u-Kama akaseko, la ati—“Anazi na, ukuba namhlanje iwile inkosi enkulu
kwa Sirayeli? ”

INTLANGANISO YAMAKRISTU ASE

SOUTH AFRICA.

INTLANGANISO ya Makristu ase South Africa, eye yokuqala eyaka yako
kwelilizwe efana nayo, ibise Qonce ngo 14 nowe 15 ku October. Kube kuko
kona abafundisi bamahlelo ngamahlelo, bede banduluka nase Lusutu, kuko
abantu abamhlope nabantsundu. Indawo eyona inkulu ekube kuhlanganelwe
yona ibikukutandaza e-Nkosini ukuba igalele u-Moya wayo Oyingcwele pezu
kwa Makristu elilizwe, ishukumise intliziyo zabangakolwayo, izityile
ngakumbi kubantu bayo.

Umsebenzi uqalwe kwangomhla 13 enyangeni kwahlanganwa etyalikeni yama
Wesile, kucelwa ukuze i-Nkosi isikelele umsebenzi wentsuku ezi- miselwe
intlanganiso. Kute kwalapo babaninzi kunene abantu.

Ngeyokuqala imini ebimisiwe, ngolwe Sitatu evekini kuhlangenwe kulendlu
inkulu yase Qonce kutiwa “ Yindlu Yomzi.” Intlanganiso apo ipatwe ngu
Rev. W. Impey. Isiqendwana selizwi le Nkosi ekube kuvunyelwene ukuze
kutetwe ngaso ngalo mini sesi kuma Kolose ii. 9, “ Ngokuba kuye
(U-Kristu) kuhleli bonke ubuzali Bobutixo buse mzimbeni.” Kuxoxwe ke
kulamazwi ngabafundisi bamahlelo ngamahlelo, kuvumeleke ukuba esuke
atete ozivayo nokuba ulilungu leremente nje kodwa akamfundisi:

Ukumka komhla into enkulu ibe kukubalisa ngokuhamba kwelizwi. Abanye
abafundisi bate bona baya kutazeka apo bakoyo, abanye bateta ngezinto
ezityafisa intliziyo. Omnye wabase Lusutu ubalise ngokuba bebesandu
ukutumela Ilizwi kwinkosana ekutiwa ngu Sikukuni yaza ayalivuma

ngoku sebelitumela ku Banyayi isizwe esikwi Li- mpopo. Utete nangokuti
nawase Lusutu Amakristu ayakutaza ngentlalo yawo.

U-Rev. Mr. Chalmers utete kakulu ngezixakaniso ezitintele ilizwi apo
akona. Ute uke wahlangana namaqela amatatu ama Ngqika, elinye lise
kulwini lamadoda nabafazi nabantwana, bonke benxilile ngemini ye Sabata.
Ute akuzibalisa ezombali wabuza wati, Lingatini na ukuhamba ilizwi le
Nkosi kubantu abazinikele kangaka etywaleni.

Babeko ke nabanye abafundisi abatete ngoku- hamba kwelizwi. Kute
ngokuhlwa kwabuya kwahlanganwa, umsebebenzi wafana kwanowaku- sasa,
isihlalo sikwapetwe ngu Rev. W. Impey.

Ngemini elandelayo kubuye kwahlanganwa kwaxoxwa ngalamazwi ati, “
Nizalisekile kuye; oyintloko yabo bonke ubupati negunya.” Kolose ii’ 10.
Nangalomini ke intlanganiso ibivuleke kumntu wonke ozivayo. Eyakusasa
ibeyonganyelwe ngu Rev. Dr Stewart wase Lovedale.

Bonke abebekuyo babuya bengayilibali intla- nganiso ebumandi bunjalo,
apo ate amakristu akuhlangana kwabonakala ukuba okunene ali- libandla
lamntu mnye, okukuti kuko amanantsi namanantsi lucukwana olungeyiyonto.
Siyatemba ke ukuba ziya kuba ninzi intlanganiso ezinjalo. Pambi kokuba
sigqibe singakankanya ukuba umsebenzi wale ibise Qonce uza kushicilelwa
ngokuzeleyo E-Lovedale ngenteto yesi Ngesi, wenziwe incwadwana.

UKUVULWA KWENDLU YETYALIKE
E-NCEMERA.

Njengoko kwakubikiwe lendlu ivulwe nge Sabata ngomhla we 10. Kute
kwakusasa ngalomini kwa- bonakala ukuba abantu abakoyo baliqela elikulu.
Ngexa leshumi kuhlanganiswene ngakwityalike endala kwayiwa kwentsha
kufaliwe, kukokelwe lilungu elidala leremente yakona. Kwakufikwa
endlwini—inzwana entle kunene, ekufaneleka ndingekufeze ngamazwi.—U-Mrs
Birt wayivula, bangena ubantu bevuma culo linye ngenteto ezintatu.

Intshumayelo yenziwe ngu Rev. W. Impey, wateta kwincwadi ye Sityilelo
xxi. 22. Ndite ndakukangela inani labantu ndati lingaba lifikile
nakwasixenxe amakulu. Ekupeleni kwenkonzo kubutwe £21. Ezokumka komhla
inkonzo zipatwe ngu Rev. J. Read.

Kute ngolwe Sibini yabuya yazala ityalike, kwabe kuko neqela labafundisi
lamacala ngamacala. Isihlalo sibe sino Mr. Innes, Imantyi yase Qonce.
Emva kokuba kuvunyiwe kwenziwa umtandazo, umpati wesihlalo ute
kuyamvuyisa kanye ukuba abe sesihlalweni kwintlanganiso enje ngale. Kuko
abati banceda ntonina abafundisi kweli lizwe? Abatshoyo bebefanele ukuza
kubona letyalike kunye naleremente. Utsho wabiza u-Mr. Birt ukuba axele
ngomsebenzi wokwakiwa kwayo.

Ute u-Mr. Birt indlu le irolelwe £1,730 : ke kusaza kurolwa £100 yokwaka
utango, ukuze indleko zayo zizonke zibe £1,830. Imali eselityala £440.
Uqube wati abantu abayi bonayo londu baya kumangaliswa ukuva ukuba
irolelwe imalana encinane kangaka. Esinye sezizatu ezibangele oko
kukuvuma ukuncedisa abate abantsundu bancedisa ngako. Bekuti ukuba kuko
into abafuneka ngayo besukele pezulu, inkabi zabo bazikupe ngapandle
kwemali.

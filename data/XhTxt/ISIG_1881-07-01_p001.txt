UKUBUBA KUKA MAJOR MALAN.

KWESI Sigidimi sale nyanga sisendaweni elusizi kakulu yokubika ngokububa
kwomhlobo omkulu, wenene waba ntsundu base South Africa, ongu Major
Malan. Zisandu kufika ezindaba ezazise ukulahlekwa kwetu ngumhlobo
okufupi kangaka kuti. Ziyaku shukumisa abaninzi, kuba nakubahlobo bake
bambalwa abaziyo ukuba ube sifa.

Bonke ababe lazi kanye elinene lingu Mkrestu wenene, babe linike yonke
imbeko eno kunikwa umntu. Aba ninzi bebe mazi kancinane, abaninzi bebe
mazi nge gama kodwa, kwabo ke ebesaziwa njengomntu olutando lukulu,
kwizinto ezilungileyo zobungcwele. Kodwa kwizihlobo ezikufupi zake
nakwabo bebe siqonda isimilo, nemisebenzi yake, ube yenye into.
Ingqoboko esulunge kileyo, ye nyaniso, ukuzinikela ngenyameko yonke
engcwele emsebenzini we Nkosi yake, zizinto ebezi kanya kwelinene
ekuhambeni nasemi sebenzini yake yemihla ngemihla, kwizindlu abezazi
ebezimbeke nje ngomhlobo oxabiso likulu, ushiye kuzo imfuziselo yobu
krestu esulungekileyo. Elinene laka lalwa ezimfazweni ezininzi eza
zekayo, kwenye yezapesheya imfazwe lasinda ngoku ngumangaliso maxa
sifumana ukuba wapuma enamanxeba amaninzi embumbulu ezingubeni awayezi
nxibile. Uyazeka nalapa e South Africa, e Qonce kakulu, Lusutu, e Natal,
nakwela Mabomvane, apo waqala izikolo zazibini ngendleko zake.

Elinene lisishiyileyo namhla la yishiya indawo yalo yokupata imikosi,
ukuba lenze imisebenzi engama lungelo abantsundu, noko sikolwayo ukuba
abaninzi betu bebengamazi nange gama. Mininzi imisebenzi emikulu
nelungileyo awayenzayo enga zange ivakale.

UMFANA WASE LAKE NYASSA.

Ubaptizo e Lovedale.

Wamkelwe e Ramenteni ka Krestu ngo baptize u Kagaso Sazuze, umfana wase
Zambesi, ngalecawa idluleyo June 12th 1881, ngexesha eli lentlanganiso
yangokuhlwa. Angakangelelwa ukuba usiqamo sokuqala sesi kolo esise
sazulwini se Africa, esizuzwe esosiqamo e Lovedale. Aku kabiko
babaptizwayo e Livingstonia, nakuba kuvakala ukuba ba tatu ababaptizweyo
e Blantyre kutsha nje. Luzekelele u baptize Iuka Kagaso Sazuze, wave lu
langazele kwamzuzu. Ababini abacele kunye naye ukuba babaptizwe kutiwe
mabake benze kuhle okwe nyangana ezimbalwa kuba ixesha livelisa ubu
ngqina obuse mhlotsheni nge ngqoboko. Olu baptizo lwabonelwa ngenyameko
ngabo bonke ababeko. U Dr. Stewart ekuyaleni kwake u Kagaso, ungene
kwixa lamhlamnene aba qubisana ngalo no Kagaso kwi nduli zase Shire nge
1877, ngokumcela kwake kude kube kabini ukuba ahambe naye engavumi yena;

nangokuhamba naye emva kwoko. Ekumyaleni kwake ute, “yiba ngu Mapostile
kubantu bezwe lakowenu. Wanga u Tixo angaku tamsanqela, akulondoloze,
akukutaze ukuze ulufikise kubo olukanyo, kuba besahleli ebumnyameni
obukulu, aze akuvuze ngemipefumlo’emininzi; watsho wati uyatemba ukuba
woza alike kuye engumshumayeli we Gospile, pakati kwamawabo kulontlambo
ye Zambesi, kwezo nduli ze Shire.

U Kagaso ubekwe ehlonelwe kakulu e Lovedale, ngenxa yesimilo,
nokutembeka kwake kuyo yonke imisebenzi yake kususela kumnyaka awafika
ngawo apa 1878. Ubomi bake obusezayo sibushiyela ezandleni zika Tixo,
obabizayo aba tume abasebenzi bake.

James Green Esq., wase Balfour, ese sihlalweni. Umgcini mali no Mpatiswa

Independent njengokuvela kwayo kwityalike ye London Missionary

zezona yayi sekwe pezu kwazo.—“Njengokuba umanyano lwama kristu amahlelo
ngamahlelo lufuneka ngokukodwa ukuhambisa lomsebenzi mkulu kunene, ukuze
lutintele ukuba lunako ukungavisisani okungaba kuyaziswa ukuba sisiseko

Society), ukuba ukuyilwa kwayo akubanga kwoko kuba kutunyelwe ityalike

Church), nokuba yimiteto namasiko ayipina ityalike, ekungati ngawo
kubeko ukungavisisani pakati kwabantu abazintloko ; kodwa ukuba

ukuba ngawona avumelanayo nezwi lika Tiko.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

ISIKOLO sase Heald Town ngati sipumla ngolwe 19 ku December.—Isikolo
sentombi sase Ncemera sipumla ngolwe 10 December.

PESHEYA KOLWANDLE kuko iqela elimiswe kona ukuba likangele,
ngokucokisekileyo impatano yama Bulu naba- ntsundu beli.

OLAHLEKELWEYO.—Ifama elise Rafu ngawo wona lonyaka lilahlekelwe
ngamakulu omatatu amatakane eseyibokwe, amlahleke ngokutyiwa zimfene.

SIVA ukuba u Mr. Charles Brownlee wase Kokstad usinge e Tsolo, ngendawo
yokuya kukangela isikalo sama Mfengu, (la asuka e Zeleni) esiti umhlaba
awunikiweyo awuko.

OFUDUKAYO.—U Rev. W. B Philip, B.A., wase Inde­pendent obengu mfundisi e
Komani, ngati uhlalele ukuba asingise e Kapa, apo ke aya kutata
umsebenzi okwanjalo.

INKOSANA YAMA BECHUANA.—Inkosana u Mankoroane siva ukuba usinge e Kapa,
apo kuvakala kona ukuba ubeye kupalaza ku Rulumente umxelo wayo owopayo,
yimpatwano yake nabamhlope abakona.

EZONYULO.—Umhla wompeto kwezaselunyulweni lwama- lungu e Palamente Endlu
Epezulu, kumiswe umhla wolwe Sitatu nge 5 ku December, ukuba ube nguwo
okoqondakala ngawo abayayo nabangayiyo.

INGQAKAQA E KIMBERLEY.—Kubonakala kuya kunyaniseka ngokunyaniseka ukuba
okunene yinqu yengqakaqa le ise Kimberley. Mva nje siva ukuba no Dr.
Sauer ute akukangela wati yinqu yengqakaqa.

UKUFA KUKA MAMPOER.—Umhla we 22 November lo ubaluleke ngokuxonywa kuka
Mampoer, owaye gwetyelwe ukufa ngokusuka alwe nama Bulu. Indoda leyo
bati yaya ngokonwaba endaweni yesipelo sayo.

OGWETYELWE UKUFA.—Ngase Batise indoda abati ngu Stuurman, ifunyenwe
ibulele u Mlungu, esingasaziswanga ke isizatu, kodwa ke indoda leyo ite
yakulingwa ematyaleni kwafunyanwa inetyala elilingene ukufa.

INGQAKAQA E LUSUTU.—Abantu ababini ebekuvakele ukuba babanjwe
yingqakaqa, ngokwengxelo yegqira lakona, bati noko bakukangelwa ngu Dr.
Reed wase Maseru, kwabonakala ukuba asiyiyo, ngumninawe wengqakaqa.

UBIZO E LUSUTU.—Elase Kapa lixela ukuba umongameli we Koloni osendaweni
ka Sir H. Robinson i Ruluneli, ufumene izwi lokuba abize intlanganiso
yabe Sutu ; kuvakala ukuba u Capt. Blyth uselemiselwe ukuba ayenze.

ISIKOLO SE NTOMBI E MGWALI.—Bekusekulixesha elitile sisiva ukuba lomzi
mtsha wentombi awakiwa, ngezizatuza- twana ezitile esingayi
kuzikankanya. Kunamhla sivuye kanye sakuva ukuba kubuye kwabuyelwa
ukwakiwa kwaso.

UMPANGA KA MANZI.—U Archdeacon Waters wase Sidutyini ububele e Monti
ngale veki idlulileyo. Igama lalo mfundisi laziwa kangaka ngabamnyama
ngangokuba kofuneka senze amazwana atile ngaye kwipepa letu elizayo.
Okwanamhla asinaxesha.

ISIKOLO SASE MGWALI (Clarkebury).—Elase Qonce lika- nkanya ukuba u Rev.
R. Lamplough umfundisi wama Wesile e Qonce ufumene incwadi ku Mr.
Irvine, ote akufumana ukuba imali eyayingati iya kukutshelwa ukwakiwa
kweso sikolo kwa Rulumente ayisapumi warola £50 erolela eso sikolo.

OMNYE OKWAGWETYELWE UKUFA.—U Pascoe, indoda eyayinetyala lokubulala
abantu ababini, umkayo neshweshwe lake, inikwe ityala lokuba ixonywe.
Ngamafupi lendoda kwakusekwahlukene nalo mfazi, mayiti ke ive ukuba
umfazi lo unendoda emshweshwileyo, aselesuka ebacotela ebanqumla
ngezembe.

OKULULWE EKUFENI.—Elase Bayi lisibikela ezendoda ekululwe ekufeni. Liti
kute xa amanene amabini, amagama singazi kuwanika, ebehlamba elwandle,
xa elinye beliselinxiba, beva ngesikalo emanzini sendoda
eyayisekutshoneni; kwa oko lomadoda omabini azigibisele esingisa pakati;
ngoncedo Iwapezulu ahle afika ampakamisela pezulu, sati isikitshana
ebesisiza kusiza safika sebempete.

AMA STEYN EMATYALENI.—Lamadoda ityala lawo loku- bulala ama Lawu,
litetwe e Kapa. Kutiwa umgwebi ubonise kanye umoya wokuwatetelela,
ngangokude ati kubagqibi tyala (jury) mabangatati nto kubunqina buka
Plaatje, no Colesberg, no Hans, (amanqina ke lawo ati okunene aba bantu
badutyulwe ngamadoda la). Ite ke yakubuya ukuba ibiye kugqugula i jury,
yapuma neliti abanatyala.

U NYULO E ALIWAL NORTH.—Siva ukuba u Mr. Advo­cate Tamplin naye usand’
ukwenzelwa isicelo e Aliwal North, ate ke wasamkela, esokuba naye abe
pakati kwamadoda aya kumela eso siqingata e Palamente. Sisiva, imvo zake
zimelene kanye nezi zolulaulo besipantsi kwalo elixesha. Le namhla
igalela ko Hon. Mr. Sauer, o Mr. H. S. Caldecott no Mr. De Wet amadoda
ke la abeselese nku- ndleni.

OZIBULELEYO.—Inene elingum Afghan ngokuzalwa, lisand’ ukufunyanwa lifile
pesheya. Lendoda ibiligqira ngokomsebenzi, ekubonakala ukuba ute akuba
engayiqondi kanye impumelelo yake, waselezidlisa. Lendoda ishiye
nencwadi, efunyenwe izele yinteto etetwe yindoda ebonakele ukuba
yonwabile, nakuba singayi kuzingena, inteto ezinje ngexela into omaze
wenziwe yona umzimba wayo, nezinye ezinjalo.

OBALEKE E QUMRA.—Abafundi betu bobona ukuba kwa- kuwo lomqolo wezinto,
sikankanye indoda elipolisa ebaleke ngase Qumra, kunye nombanjwa ongu
Golding ebimgcinile. Kekaloku siva ukuba lendoda seyibuye yabanjwa, kwa
Komani ke apo ibanjelwe kona. Ite pambi kwe Mantyi e Qonce yenza kanye
amazwi ekuvakele ukuba yayiwenze mhla yabanjwa; angala ke okuba ukuze
ibaleke nje kunye nombanjwa, kungesitembiso sokuba uya kuyinika i £100
nehashe. Ityala ke alikatetwa lona noko okwalo.

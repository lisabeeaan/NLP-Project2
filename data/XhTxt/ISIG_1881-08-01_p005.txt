ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1881.

INTLANGANISO YO TITSHALA E BURNSHILL.

JULY 13TH.

IVULWE intlanganiso ngo 7 ngokuhlwa ngomtandazo ngu Rev. Wm, Philip, we
Kaffir Institution Graham’s Town. Engafikanga nje u Mr. J. Shaw, i
President, i Vice-President u Mr. J. Tengo-Jabavu wabe sesihlalweni.

I.  IYURE ZOKUBANJWA KWE ZIKOLO.

U MR. GAWLER, i Secretary, ute kuko into emxakileyo, u Mkangeli Zikolo
uti isikolo masingene ngo 9, sipume intlanzane ngo 11, ngemiso, ze
sipindele ngo 2 sipume 4, ukujika kwe langa, kuba esiti ngolu hlobo
lokusingenisa ngo 9 sipume ngo 1, kubanga ukuba angabi nakuzi kangela
kakuhle nakamsinya izikolo. Into ke ekatazayo kulento itetwa ngu
Mkangeli Zikolo yeyokuba abantwana, kwi zikolo zaba mnyama abangeze beza
kwesamva isikolo. Ukuba i Rulumente ibingati imane ibalungisela idinara
abantwana kwakufupi nesi kolo into enjalo ibingake ilingwe.

U MR. S. MAKAPELA, ute lomteto we yure waka watiwa bandlakanya nakuye,
kodwa wati umongameli ongumfundisi make enze kuhle woke abuye eve ku Dr.
Dale, selokoko akazange abuye eve nto ngendawo leyo.

U MR. TENGO-JABAVU, i Vice-President, ute kwi ndawo abekuyo e Someaset
East, wafika isikolo besingeniswa nge yure ezi ati u Mr. Howe-Ely, u
Mkangeli Zikolo makubanjwe zona, kodwa yena, eqele ukuba isikolo
sibanjwe kanye ngo 9 ukuya ku 1, akavumanga, kude noko kwemangaye. Ngalo
lonke ixesha zizalisiwe iyure ze Rulumente ezine, akaboni yena ukuba
kuko imposiso.

Emva kwengxoxo etile kuvunyelwene ukuba azinge lunge iyure zo Mkangeli
Zikolo.

U MR. TENGO-JABAVU, ute lemvo yentlanganiso mayitunyelwe ku Mongameli
Mfundo, u Dr. Dale, nge ncwadi etobekileyo, nokuba incwadi leyo maze
ibalwe ngu Mr. J. W. Gawler no Mr. C. Hlawe.— Kuvunyelwene.

II. IMPATO YO TITSHALA NGABA FUNDISI.

Omnye kumalungu entlanganiso uxele into eyamhlelayo ngokuti apatwe nkosi
mbini, Ute kuko izinto ezinje ngozi slates nozi pencils ezazifuneka
esikuleni sake, wabikela umfundisi, walile ukumkolisa ngesi. celo sake.
Kamsinya ufikile u Mkangeli Zikolo, wagqoshonqa wahamb’ apa ngenxa
yokungabiko kwazo zona ezizinto. Ixelile i Titshala leyo imbangi,
wabuzwa umfundisi, sinosizi ukuti, ukanyele wabeta koma. Ilungu
eliyingenisileyo lenteto lite bekungenziwa ntonina ukuze ezizinto
zinqandwe.

U REV. MR. PHILIP, ubonisile ukuba kunqabile ukuba intlanganiso le
ingenza nto kwindawo enje, kuba Umteto we Parlamente umise ukuba ezinye
i Titshala zibe pantsi kwaba fundisi; enye into asibonke abafundisi
abazipete ngolu hlobo i Titshala ezipantsi kwabo.

U MR. TENGO-JABAVU, uvumelene no Rev. Mr. Philip kuyo yonke inteto yake,
walatisa wati ingxoxo yonke yemfundo isaza kukangelwa yi Rulumente. Kuko
inteto yamadoda awaye nyulelwe ukupicota isimo semfundo, noko kubonakala
ukuba eyaba mnyana ayinikwanga so kakulu. Intlanganiso yabafundisi
bamahlelo onke ebise King Wm’s Town icele ku Rulumente ukuba ike ibuye
ikangelwe indawo yaba ntsundu, watsho wati bekunganjanina ukuba le
ntlanganiso yenze isicelo esikwanjalo ze kubeko amadodana nawa ntsundu
aviwayo kule ndawo yemfundo yonke yaba mnyama.

Ivumelene intlanganiso ukuba zeke kulindwe impendulo ye Rulumente kwisi
celo sentlanganiso yabafundisi ebesesiti ibise Qonce.

III. APO INTLANGANISO ZOBA KONA.

Ingxoxo enkulu ibeko pezu kwe ntandazo ka Mr. Gawler etandazela u Mr. J.
Shaw ngokungabiko kwake ngenxa yokungapili nenkatazo ezimhleleyo. U
Mongameli Ntlanganiso ucela ukuba intlanganiso ziye e Fort Beaufort
ukuze abeko. Isininzi sentlanganiso asiyi vumanga lonto, kude kwati
ekugqibeleni kwavunyelwana ngokuti maze ibese Mgqakwebe wakwa Sambula ze
isondezelwe o Titshala abangakulo mandla. Pakati kwesituba sale
ntlanganiso nezayo eyoba ngo January u Mr. Gawler i Secretary woba
ebalelana no Mr. Shaw ngendawo yokungabi nakufikelela kwake nento
emayenziwe xa izinto zimi njalo.

IV. IMALI ZE NTSAPO.

U MR. MAKAPELA, ubonise ukungapumi kwe mali ye Titshala kubantu abane
ntsapo ehamba izikolo. Kwindawo ezinje imali ye Ti tshala ebanjwe
ngabantu ide yafikelela kwi £9, nakwi £10, kwaku gqitywe kwe dlulileyo
intlanganiso, kweliti i Titshala mazimangale kwi mantyi, ze isigqibo
sixelwe ku Dr. Dale.

U MR. NEBUCHAN MACWELA, ute ukumangala ematyaleni akunge ncede luto,
kungacita izikolo. Ngokwake uti bekunga njanina imali yabantwana
yamkelwe ngaba fundisi.

U MR. MAKAPELA, ute yaka yenziwa lonto kwesabo isitili, kwatiwa imali
mayibatalwe kumfundisi, akazange abambe ne dobilityi. Ingaselisamkelwa
ngo Titshala kunaba fnndisi.

U REV. MR. PHILIP, ute bekunga njanina zite zanikwa amakwenkwana ate nqi
ezikuleni ukuba ahambe engena kwindlu nganye, eyamkela.

U MR. TENGO-JABAVU, utakazele, wati, njengokuba umgwebi okohlakeleyo
wabe engenakuvumela ukuqekezwa intloko ngu mhlolokazi, koba njalo
nakubantu bakowetu abangavumiyo ukubatala oko bakubamba i Titshala.

Ipumile intlanganiso ngo 12 ebusuku ngenxa yobude bengxoxo.

JULY 14.

Ihlangene ngo 11 emini intlanganiso yavulwa ngomtandazo ngu REV. WM.
PHILIP, U MR. TENGO-JABAVU ese sihlalweni.

V.  UKUNGENISWA KWABA NGEZIZO I TITSHALA.

U MR. MAKAPELA, ute ngokwake ukwazi bekungazange kubeko salelo sokuba
bangangeni abangezizo i Titshala.

U MR. NEBUCHAN MACWELA, ute kuko inteto ekwaku vunyelwene ngayo ukuba
bangangeniswa abangezizo i Titshala.

U MR. GAWLER, ucazile ukuba kwavunyelwana ngenye inteto eyati bangangena
kodwa bangabi nawo amalungelo atile.

U MR. XINIWE, (obengeniswe kade) ute makuvunyelwane ngale nteto “Bonke
abantsundu abacubekileyo bavulelwe ituba lokungena kulentlanganiso xa
bavunyelwe yi Committee (babini batatu abanyulwe yintlanganiso ukuba
bakangele, ukuba, abantu abanjalo banezimilo ezingabanga ukuba bafanele
ukungena na.

Ibiko nenteto abete no MR. GAWLER makuvunyelwane ngayo ebikwafana nale
ka MR. XINIWE, noko ibingeko kuyo indawo yokuba lamalungu afuna ukungena
abekwe pambi kwe Committee. Kuvunyelwene ngeka Mr. Xiniwe inteto.

Emva kwe ngxoxo etile ngemali ebatalwa ngama lungu, nasemva kwe nteto
etile ezivumelene ngayo i Titshala yokuba zibakutaze abantu bakowazo
ukuba batandane nokufunda, ivalwe ngomtandazo ngu Rev. Mr. Philip
intlanganiso. Zemka ukumka komhla i Titshala.

EZASE MAMPONDWENI

KUNINZI ezikuvayo indlebe zetu ngokwehlayo kulo mandla we pesheya kwe
Nciba; noko konke kubekiselele ezi mfazweni. Sidiniwe zindaba ezivela
ema Mpondweni nabati bavela kona, sikatazeke kanye saku beka emapepeni
sifumane kusitiwa “Luxolo ! Luxolo!” kanti ngokwetu bamnyama kulapo
kangeko luxolo. Datyana luvela kulatna cala silufumana encwadini evela e
Mtata. Solubeka pambi kwe mpumlo zamawetu, ukuze eve oko siti
kuyinyaniso okuvela kwelase ma Mpondweni kubalwa linene, umbalelani we
Representative ipepa lakwa Komani:—

Eko onke amare emfazwe nezituso, kuqala kusitiwa ngama Mpondo, ze ngoku
kutiwe ngaba Tembu, ndicinga,” utsbo lombali, “ukuba ndixela inyaniso xa
nditi, uxolo, kulo mandla, yinto ekoyo ; ndicinga kanjako ukuba bonke
abalapa badibene ekuvuyeleni ukufika koxolo, kuba sesidinwe mu yimfazwe.
Ndiyazi ukuba nakwaba mnyama kukwanjalo; ama Mpondo acwayite kakulu
ukuva ngoxolo, nakuba iyinto eninzi ebitetwa kusitiwa ayapitizela, kodwa
noko bekubangwa luloyiko lokoyika u Rulumente kunge ngakuba ebefuna
lifile. Uyakuba seluke weva, ndingatandabuzi, ukuba kuke kwatiwa ama
Mpondo angxamele ukubulala zonke i Mantyi, ezobako ekubekweni kwe litye
le gumbi le “Kilner Institution.”—-(I Kilner Institution sikolo esakelwa
amadodana e New Bunting—sikolwa—MHLELI Sigidimi)—ukuba zobako. Kwada
kwatiwa, sekusaziwa ukuba yotinina ukuhla yonke lento. Kuko konke oku
andibanga nakukolwa ukuba lonto yinyaniso; ndiyile ke ekubekweni
kwelitye ndiyavuya ukuti lomsebenzi wahambiseka kakuhle; kwakuko ama
Ngesi amayela kuma shumi amabini, nanye i Mantye eyake yavelisa ne
mpumlo. I Mantyi enkulu yetu, ebekekileyo u Major Elliott, kwakulindelwe
ukaba nguye oya kupata indawo epambili kumsebenzi lowo, yayingeyiyo leyo
noko intando yake. Esi sikolo sakiwa ngexabiso elikuma waka matatu
namane, eloxabiso umfundisi u Mr. Morris uqinisele ukuba ama Mpondo
olikupa ngapandle kwenkatazo—selide yati inkosi kumzi ngamnye maze
kupume inkomo ene wonga. Andina ntandabuzo, ukuba oku ikuteteyo
kwenziwe, yobako lo mali.

Aze umbali lo ahambise nge ndatyana zempilo nezokwakiwa kwe tyalike
ngama Wesile ase Mtata nentwana ezi ncinane ezinjalo esiti noko
azinamsebenzi kuba fundi betu.

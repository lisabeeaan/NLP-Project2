ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA JULY 01, 1887

UMANYANO NGE MFUNDO.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

  Siyavuya ukufumana ikaba laseba Tenjini likubona ukufaneleka
  kokuzidibanisa ngomcimbi wokufaka isandla ekuncediseni ukuqutywa
  kokukanya kwezi zizwe zakowetu. Eliqela litsha silinqwenelela
  impumelelo kulo micimbi linga lingayifeza. Singalikumbuza ukuba
  lingazimiseli ukuba incasa ibe sekuteteni, ibe izenzo zilambata.
  Izenzo kambe kukubalela amagunya nokusebenzisa amahashe nomzimba
  nokuzikataza kwimicimbi esazeke injengokunceda umzi ngemihla
  enjengonyulo, nokungeniswa kwamagama ezincwadini zakomkulu, nezinye
  intlanganiso zomzi. Lencwadi ivakalisa ukuyilwa kwesebe lembumba yo
  Manyano Ngemfundo ijakomeleza amabandla alontlanganiso, kuba
  iyakubonisa ukuba imita yelo bandla iyafikelela naseba Tenjini, nakuba
  ingenzi ngxolo inkulu ngamaxilongo avakalisa ukulunga kwayo kwezinye
  imbuto. Intlanganiso engenazenzo injengentsimi engahlakulwanga. Inga
  ingalunyukelwa ke lento lolu manyano lutsha. Lencwadi itunyelwe kwi
  Vice-President Yomanyano nge Mfundo :— Mount Arthur, Lady Frere, 21
  May, 1884.

Sihlobo, intlanganiso yeli lizwe ebizwa ngokuba yi South African Native
Association isicela ukuba sixele uluvo Iwayo olulolokuba lentlanganiso
ivane, icebisane, itiwe incorporate kwi Native Educa­tional Association.

Lentlanganiso eyi South African Native Association iyawubulela kakulu
umsebenzi owenziwe yi Native Educational Association ngokukodwa
ngamalungelo ohlanga oluntsundu. Le ke intlanganiso iyafuna ukuxela
uluvo Iwayo ngengxoxo ezingemfundo yabantsundu ezite zatetwa zaxaswa yi
N. E. A. Iti ke lentlanganiso iyavumelana ne N. E. A. kwingxoxo
zemfundo, ibulela nokubulela okwenziwe yileyo ntlanganiso ngale ngxoxo
nangezinye.

Lentlanganiso icinga ukuba ingaba yenzelwe itamsanqa nentsikelelo enkulu
ite i Native Educational Association yavuma ukuba iyiti incorporate.
Kambe madodana akowetu tina sibeke intonga kuni, soti ke ngoko ekubeni
tina siyintlanganiswana encinane silindele ukuva kwakuni into
eniyicingayo kwanengxoxo eniziqondayo ukuba zingawunceda umzi wakowetu
ontsundu. Sitemba ukuva eyenu impendulo msinyane. Siyapela sizizicaka
zenu ezinyanisekileyo,

  DAVID THOMAS MALASI, President. RICHD. KAWA, Vice-President.

MR. JNO. T. JABAVU,

Vice-President N. E. A.,

Lovedale.

ISIKOLO SASE HEALDTOWN.—Umsebenzi wesi sikolo uyakuqala ngo 31 July.

E BLOEMFONTEIN uviwo oluyi Matriculation lungenwe zintombi zodwa. Inani
sisitatu.

I MISSION SCHOOLS.—Izikolo zangapandle ziyakuvulwa ukuhambisa umsebenzi
ngolwe-Sibini 22 July.

INGQAKAQA isahambisa imbujiso enkulu ngasema Dayimani, sekufe inkita
ngase Ligwa (Barkly West.)

IMALI E DAYIMANI.—Bati abake bayilinganisa, imali ehlaulela abasebenzi
nge Migqibelo ikwi £40,000.

AMAKONXWA E DAYIMANI.—Kutiwa inani lamakonxwa e Dayimani alingagantsi
kwamakulu amatandatu anamanci amane.

KUNOKWENZEKA ngoku ukuba umntu asuke e Qonce aye e Rini abuye abuye
ahlaule ishumi lendaliso kupela (itsho i Mercury.)

I TOLL ENTSHA.—Igqugula lomandla wase Dikeni ligqibe kwelokumisa i Toll
kulendlela iqabela e Lungcwini isinga ko Komani.

“ HARD TIMES.”—Kunxweme lwesokunene lomlambo wase Monti kutiwa izindlu
ezingenabantu zikumashumi asixenxe anesitoba.

IYEZA LEZINYO.—Indodana egama lingu Du Toit isand’ ukububa e Kapa. Ifa
ikov’ ukufaka umcizana otile ezinyweni elibulalayo.

IMIKOSI.—Ibandla lama sodati abati yi 58th Regiment, elikumakulu amatatu
lingene kumkombe oyi Asiatic ngo 17 June lisinga e Natal.

KUBAFUNDI BETU.'—Ngendawo yokuba abashicileli bezakuke bafumane ixesha
lokupumla ipepa elizayo lingashicilelwa mayelana no 18 July.

E DAYIMANI U Charles Eales, igwangqa lase Somerset elingumtransport
lilindele ijaji ngokubulala umntu omnyama ongu Charles e
Alexandersfontein.

UBUMANTYI OBUKULU BASE KOKSTADT.—Indawo ka Mr. Brownlee kuvakala ukuba
iyakubanjwa ngu Mr. W. B. Chalmers obesakuba yimantyi yase Qonce.

UMFUNDISI OMDALA.—Kutiwa u Maneli u John Edwards wase Wesile ungene
kumnyaka wobom bake wamashumi asibozo. Kanti ke noko usenempilo entle.

IMIHLABA ETENGISAYO.—Kuko imihlaba eliqela yakwa Rulumeni eyakutengiswa
pambi kwe Ofisi yemantyi yase Cumakala ngentsimbi ezilishumi ngo 17
July.

INTLANGANISO YABAFUNDISI.—Intlanganiso yabafundisi bamahlelo ngamahlelo
ekutiwa yi “ United Missionary Conference,” iyakuba se Qonce ngo 3 no 4
July.

ISIXEKO SASE CALA.—Intengiso yeziqwenga zomhlaba wale dolopu intsha
iyakubase Komani ngo 16 September ngexesha leshumi. Lemihlaba iziziza
namasimi.

ABATSHE BADA BAFA.—Enkonkobe kufupi nje indoda nomfazi bashiya indlu
yabo bati xa babuyayo bafika selisitsha nabantwana abane abancinane
bojeke bada bafa.

UKUBUBA KUKA CAPTAIN HUNT.—Elinene beliyimantyi yase Aliwal North libube
ngo Mgqibelo 21 June ngesiqupe. U Captain Hunt wabeke eyimantyi yase
Ngqushwa ixesha elide.

I KOBONQABA (idolopu) lizimisele ukunqanda amanzi ngempompo into
eyakubanga ukuba kubeko ngokunye i erf eziyakungenisa imali enobom. Eli
linyatelo elisinga pambili.

UKAFILE ODUTYULWEYO.—Ngo 16 June kudutyulwe u Mxosa ezibukweni lase
Monti. Bati ukuyibalisa kwabo lomfo udutyulwe kuzindlelwa ukuba yihangu
kuba waye kasa entsimini.

ISIKUMBUZO.—Abanga banganezwi ezintweni zamasiko eli lizwe banga
abangelibali ukuwangenisa ko Mantyi bendawo abakuzo amagama abo (ukuba
akakangeni) ngapambi ko 31 July.

UMLILO E KAPA.—Ngo 5 Dec. zonke izindlu atengisela kuzo u Mr. P. J.
Stigant, M.L.A., wase Kapa, zitshile, kunye nempahla zazo ezinqabileyo.
Ilahleko ekutiwa u Mr. Stigant akasakuba nambuyiselo ngayo ifikelele kwi
ponti ezi £20,000.

Unyulo lwabaya kupata umsebenzi we ntlanganiso yododana apa,
kwisiqingatasokuqala sonyaka ozayo, lube ngo 8 December. Ludubule
ekubeni umongameli (Chairman) ibe ngu-Mr. J. K. Bokwe; umbali micimbi
(Secretary)—Mr. Jeremiah S. Makoloi; umgcini mali (Treasurer)—-Mr.
George E. Williams; abaqubi msebenzi (Committee)—Messrs John B. Kayser,
William Dewey, Samuel Mzimba, Edw. Tsewu, Samuel Sihunu, Augus­tus
Welsh. Imibulelo yentlanganiso yenziwe ko Messrs Walter Rubusana,
Percival R. Frames, ngohlobo abaqube ngalo umsebenzi, owokuqala
wobu-Chairman owesibini 'wobu-Secretary. Lentlanganiso yeluncede lunge
luncinane kudodana nakwabanye ekufundiseni ngezinto zentlalo yencubeko.

U-Enoch Booy pakati kwe nkwenkwezi! Uhlanga ngenyaniso lusinga pezulu.
Amadodana afunwa yi South Africa nganjengo Mr. Enoch Booy aba—amadodana
angasakuti ixesha lawo alicitele ebuvileni. Akubonina ukunonelela
ukukatsa amaqabambola ? Akubonina ukuxasa Isigidimi ? Namhla use
zinkwenkwezini! Nokuba abanye basikwe umlomo, tina siyamncoma umhlobo
wetu. Kodwa xa simva eteta ngo “ Venus-Star!! ” no “ Juniper!!! ”
Esebenzisa inteto ezinjengo—“ I am kindly informs you!!!!”—xa ateta
ngoluhlobo, asinakunceda ukuba singamyali, njengabamtandayo, ukuba ati,
pambi kokondela ezinkwenkwezini, akangele pambi konyawo ukuba umhlaba
ahamba kuwo ngozisweleyo sinina izipunzi, nentshengeca, nezihogo. Akuko
nto imbi njengokuwa ukangelwe yindimbane.

Imizamo yetu yokulenza elipepa ukuba libe lelinoku fundeka lifundise,
lonwabise, siyaqonda ukuba iya ibonwa. Xa sifumana amagama nawabakude
njengase Pretoria, Transvaal, sivuya kunene. Ntwana inqwenelekayo
yeyokuba ngamana lemita yesi Gidimi, ikanya nase Transvaal namhla
ingabonwa ngokucacileyo nangabemi beli sikulo kunokuba kunjalo ngoku.
Yenjenje imambane emnyama xa itumela imali yayo ekwi £1 8s. (Yayo yodwa
!) :—

ku December 1877 to December 1883 next year. Sendizohlwaya ngokwam

singeva okanye singaboni izinto ezitetekayo ezihambisekayo. Pakati
kododana olufundisiweyo nolungafundiswanga indaba enkulu encokweni,
nasekusulungekeni, yengento enokuyenza intombi kwisebe elikulu lokupeka,
nokutunga, nokutelekisha mgubo. Ukwazi kwetu ngentlalo yaba tshatileyo
kukulu esingateta kokwetu ukuqela xasiti apo impambano zendoda
nenkosikazi yayo ziqala kona, kusekutini abe kanti u Noyesi, mhlaumbi u
Nofatyi, mhlaumbi u Sarah Ann, mhlaumbi u Lizzie, mhlaumbi u
Mary,—akukatalelekile nokuba ngubanina igama—upeke idikidiki le kofu,
mhlaumbi udongwe Iwe sonka; akalitungelanga iqosha le kala ngasemva,
uyenze into emdaka i hoshempe abeyi telekisha—njalo njalo, ufike indoda
seyitsale ubuso, yekuhlu iqeba, isilwa, kanti emzuzwini yoda isebenzise
isixatula, nefisa, nomnqayi. Ibe ngovuyo olukuluke ukubona aba Miss
besondezwa ukuba baye kwamkela amabaso—o Alice Sopete (Rabula) no Annie
Babile (Bira) ngokutunga ; u Eliza Dikweni (Mgqakwebe) no Jessie
Mampunya (Rabula) ngokutelekisha. Hleliwenje ne Rabula eli linento?

intsapo e-Heald Town. Abazali abafundisa intsapo kwesi sikolo
bobanobuhlungu bentliziyo okutile, bakuva ukuba u Mr. George Baker
omkulu, no Mr. George Baker omnci, bauyekile umsebenzi abebe kade
beubambile wokufundisa, ngezizatu-zatu ezibonakalayo. Ngexesha elide
kunene abe fundisa e-Heald Town u Mr. Baker ubenike inkoliseko enkulu
kubazali, angati olandelayo ayifikelele ibe yinqaba kodwa ukuyi dlula.
Asingqivazeli ukuti umka namatamsanqa abo bonke abamaziyo. Indawo
ayishiyayo izakupatwa linene elivela Pesheya, u Mr. Lightfoot. Eyonyana
siqonda ukuba izaliswe ngo Rev. Gana Kakaza obese Cradock ozakuti
ubizelo Iwake esitiyeni se-Nkosi alusebenzise emfundisweni yentsapo.
Lendawo ibingenakuzaliswa ngokufaneleke ngapezulu. Ekubeni ngumfundi,
ebu-Titshaleni, ebu-Fundisini sinetamsanqa lokumazi u Mr. Kakaza.
Sisitsho ngabo bonke ubuganga ukuba kuzo zonke ezindawo akazanga waba
sezinyaweni zankwenkwe wabe funda nayo, za Titshala wabe fundisa
ngaxesha nye nayo, zamfundisi wabe kunye naye. Ukuba imvo zetu
zokumnqwenelela intsikelelo uteni zizo, siyamnika.

IMPAWANA.

“ SIBANIZASHE, u JAMES? Yinto exakana nomfazi ukuya “ etyalikeni.” Uti
omnye ukupendula: “ Mawo! Yinto “ ezele. libongo. Lomfana unje ukusa ?
Uba betu woba “ ngu Mlungu ? ” Atsho abambe umlomo, ati ukuba ngo
tanyusanqelwe ngokuba nendevu asuke azisonte. Iti ke lento ibe ngu “
ndaba mlonyeni,” ide ifike nasezindlebeni zika yise ka JAMES. Avakale
ngomnye umhla esiti : “ JIM! Ndifuna ukuhlangana nawe.” Hayi, eze u
JAMES. Aqale ngokuti: “ Ke, mntwana wam lento yokuba semlo- “ nyeni
wabantu andiyitandi. Lento yokuba uzinikele “ kangaka emfazini, ube
yinto ehlala ihamba nomfazi ayilungile. Myeke ahambe nabanye; uhambe
namanye “ amadoda wena. Wosuka kunje umfazi akwenze isihlangu. “
Yintokazi yase mzini yona kunje.” O, u JIM abonakale ete tozololo.
Uyabona nge Cawa lomfazi seletsho ngo mpantsho olude pakati kwama
xegokazi, onina : u JIM sele yinto ehamba ngecala kukoyika amehlo
abantu. Siti sakufika kule ndawo sibe lusizi. Siti sakuva ukuba u JIM
ukwele Dayiman ; umshiyile umtshakazi. singa mangaliswa, kuba ufuna
ukumka ezintanjeni, kuba namhla kutiwa kuye kowabo maka “ b'inqe ibayi,”
abuyele emva kumasiko awa ncwatywa kwakudala, kanti yena akawazi ukulele
e Mlungwini apo umfazi apatwa ngolu hlobo. Namhla ke uvukelwe ngulo yise
wayete yiya emfundweni, uye kufunda into zoku kanya kuba tina saposwa
yimfundo. Namhla wenziwa ilolo, aselete naye ukuziyekelela wafana nama
navi la asebenza kwa loliwe. Lipina izwi lako NOZALA lamhla mnene? Ngati
lise ncwabeni.

Kambe besisondele ku JAMES. Make siqondisise induli, masiti gxebe,
intaba ate u SABITYI watintelwa yiyo, eyinto enjenjenje ukubitya. into
engasena ntlahla, eselite ukuruquka kwayo yancama. Kuba ete ngamhla
utile waziceba ; waba uhamba ngendlela, kanti uzifaka konomeva bezalele.
Akatena naye waxelisa u JIM, eba unyusa isihomo somzi wake ngokwenza
into zokukanya, kanti uziposa ezinzitsheni zabafazi ezingena nceba.
Namhla yinto ekutiwa ukutetwa ngayo : “ Uba lendoda uya kuyihlutwa
betu—iyeyake nje “ lento angaxezi mntu, angasabeki nabani! Sele lahle no
“ nina zala, wati nea kule ndoda yake.” Itsho intokazi: “ Wa mntakama,
bubugqi: Lomntwana abantu mabatete “ naye uyemka.” [Esimka iyintonina ?]
Uti lo uxelelwa- yo : “ Kawenze kuhle, mntakama, ndake ndiye kuzibonela
“ ngomso endlwini yake.” Ingomso Iona lifike. Gunqu intokazi enkulu,
umnikazindlu xa mhlaumbi alungisa ibulakufesi. “ Molo! ”—atsho ngentwana
encinane yelizwi, ebleli emnyango pantsi, pofu wumbi ubenikwe isitulo,
wati hayi ndinxamile. Ihlale lentokazi emveni kokuba ibuziwe indaba—pofu
nomnini ndlu ubeke wangena, wateta nenkosikazi yake wabuya wemka. Gqi
lentokazi imke iye kuwabo. lyangena endlwini : “ Mntakama ndivela kona “
koko andihlalanga. Yinto endite ndiya ngena ndafika “ kwinto ete
ukuzibamba intsbiyi zanga zezika YANTOLO.” Ati ngoku futshane: “ Hayi
wetu, ndinqinile yonke into “obuyiteta. Yinyaniso.”

Injalo intlalo yolutsha olutshatileyo, olufundisiweyo, oluti luzama
ukuhlala ekondweni laba fundisi balo empa- tweni yendlu. Ekuti ke Iwaku
gungxulwa kweli kondo Iwaku wexulelwa kwelaba dala, elingazanga Iwali
funda, lube zibiba. Luyibaleke indlu. Lube ngama dasadasa ezweni apa.
Zinto ezincinane ezi ke kuni, bantu bakowetu ; kodwa zihlisa uhlanga.
Niyoyikwa ke nina ngabatsba ngalonto. Taruni mzi wakowetu. Yinina kodwa?
Kuhle. Namhla mazisuswe ezi ntaba endleleni.

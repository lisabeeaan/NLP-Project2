IMPAWANA.

Abantu abavela kwelama Hala nasema Mpondweni bancoma ukuba ubusela,
bugqugqisile. Umbaleli kwipepa lase Qonce ubuza ukuba azi ukuba lento
iyakupela pina. Sede ngomnye umhla yabanga idabi pakati kwa Mabaca nama
Mpondo, kwakufupi nje kubeko idabi pakati kwama Mpondo nama Xesibe.
Bonke abantu esike sitete nabo bati olu “lubo” luhambiseka ngezimini
kulo mimandla ludla ngokwandulela imfazwe. Kwanga kungebe njalo.

Le “ Conference ” yokugqibela yama Wesile yafudusa u Rev. Charles Lwana
kwisikolo sase Palmerton ema Mpondweni, yatumela kulondawo umfundisi u
Rev. R. Walton Lewis omhlope. Kuvakala ukuba Inkosi yama Mpondo ayivumi
ukuba lenguqulo yenziwe, inga u Mr. Lwana angahlala ayingi angasuswa
ngomlungu kuba ibiselingenwe kakulu nguye, ndawo kutiwa ibanga ukuba
angabi nantliziyo yokumamkela u Mr. Walton Lewis kukuba bengavisisani
kakuhle no Rulumeni. Isiko lama Wesile kodwa lenze ukuba u Mr. Lwana
afuduke, inkosi seyigqibe kwelokuba kungahiko m’fundisi umhlope utabata
indawo yake.

Inkosi u Mqikela ike yatyelelela isikolo sase St Andrew’s yaposa amazwi
kwababeko ibakutaza ukuba mabangayekeleli ukufundisa intsapo yabo. Unike
idinga lokuba okwesayo isiqu uya kurola amashumi omabini enkomo, azise
bonke abantu bake ukuba barole abanako ukuba kwakiwe isikolo esikulu.
Utumele isine sabantwana bake esikolweni. Um’fundisi weso sikolo ngu
Rev. C. D Tonkin. Kuvakala ukuba emva kotyelelo lwelo tshawe kutunyelwe
ngapezulu abantwana abamashumi matatu anesihlanu. Olu lupau oluhle:
akwaba bonke abantu bakowetu abase magunyeni bebeya kulandela inkosi
yama Mpondo, ezizizwe zakowetu beziyakude zibe zizizwe eziqinileyo
nezine wonga.

EZAMANYE AMAZWE.

INKOSI YASE RUSSIA kuvakala ukuba iyakutyelela E-England ekuqaleni kuka
May.

ITYALA LIRA TICHBORNE eselineminyaka litetwa selide lagqitywa. Ugwetywe
ukuze abe neshumi elinesine leminyaka etolongweni.

UMLILO.—E-London kuko indlu enkulu ebihlala impahla esand’ ukutsha.
Ixabiso lempahla ezonakeleyo kutiwa lingapezu kwe zigidi ezi tatu
zeponti (£3,000,000).

INDLALA yase India kutiwa ibangele ukuba kupele ukucekisana kwabantu
bakona. Abanye bayazi ukuba kona kuko ukwahluka okukulu kwindidi
ezipantsi nezipakamileyo, kangangokuba abanako nokudla bahlale ndawonye.
Ngoku kutiwa kwezinye indawo basebenza kunye, bancokole, badle ndawonye.

INDLEKO EZINGENISWE kukutetwa kwetyala lika Tichborne zinconywa ubukulu.
Elinye lamapepa apesheya liti, ijura iselifumene £2,600, abateteleli
bakomkulu £14,000. Abateteleli baka Tichborne abaziwa kakuhle into
abayifuineneyo. Kumanqina abeze kummela elinye lizuze £1,000 elinye
£700, elesitatu £500.

INDABA ZASE ENGLAND zokugqibela ziti ngoku umongameli wemicimbi yase
England ngu Desraeli umjodi. Lento ibonisa ukuguquguquka kwezinto.
Ngamaxesha adluleyo E-England Umjodi bekunqabe nokuba azifuyele into
yake. Bebenyatelelwa pantsi, kungeyiyo nto ukubabulala. Namhla
Umongamele wayo yonke imicimbi yakona ngu Mjodi.

UKUTSHATA KO NYANA KA VICTORIA kubekuko ngomhla 21 ka January njengoko
kwakumiselwe ngako. Kunconywa ukuvata abebevate ngako kumacala omabini.
Bute ubucokocoko kwanga kuze kuqayiselwana. Kule Koloni zite zakufika
indaba zokutshata kwake, I-Governor yamisa umhla we 17 ka March ukuze
bonke bazigcobise ngawo bavuyele okwenzekileyo.

OFE KUKUDLA IKATI.—Kutiwa E-Paris kube kuko umfana obeyitanda kunene
inyama yezinja nekati. Kute ngenye imini wadla ikati ebityebe kunene.
Kungekabi mzuzu uve intliziyo ihlamba, waza wahlanza kunene wada wafa.
Kubizwe igqira ukuba lize kukangela into embuleleyo, lafumana ukuba
lekati ebeyidla ibisand’ ukudla impuku ebidle ukudla okunobuhlungu.

UCINGO.—Kuvakala ukuba ngalo nyaka ukoyo kuza kwenziwa ucingo olutsha
olusuka E-England luye E-America lucande elwandle. Aluyi kuba nzima
ngangolu lube luko kade, kanjalo luya kuba nokusetyenzwa kalula
ngapezulu. Kuvakala ukuba Iwakufezwa iyakuba nokuncitshiswa imali
yokutu- mela amazwi ngalo, kubizwe isheleni ngelizwi, endaweni yesheleni
ezine.

E-CALCUTTA, ikomkulu lase India, kusand’ uku- bako intlanganiso
yabamhlope nabemi belozwe, eteta ngalendlala ikoyo kona. Ukumkanikazi
U-Victoria urole iwaka leponti ukuya kuncedisa. Komnye umahlulo izipata
mandla ziti abantu abaya kufuneka ukuxaswa baya kufika esigidini. Abanye
konakele ukudla kwabo emasimini, ukuba kungabi sacolwa nokucolwa.
Imarika ivaliwe. Sekuko abalinga ukudla kube kanye ngemini. Ezona ndawo
zibandezeleke kakulu zezinqabileyo ukufikelelwa. Ukuba I-Govermnent
ibingancedisi ngelilikulu inani labafayo.

UKUSETYENZWA E-ENGLAND.—Umfo otile oha- mbele pesheya esuka kweli lizwe
uti,—“ Ndike ndaya kubona imizana ekutiwa apa yedelekileyo, emincinane.
Ndifike umsinga wabantu ezitilateni usoloko umnye ungapelanga.
Ungumangaliso kumntu ovela E-Koloni. Bonke abantu kona ungafika behamba
ngomsebenzi, benxamile, esuke umntu wati ntsho kulandawo aya kuyo,
ezamela ukufeza lonto ayifunayo. Akuko xesha ake abe elicita
ngokuncokola; akuko bahamba befake izandla ezinxoweni, betwele induku
emagxeni, besayama ngendonga zezindlu. Bonke baxatise emsebenzini.”

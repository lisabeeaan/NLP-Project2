NGOLWE SINE, DECEMBER 1, 1881.

UNYAKA ODLULAYO.

ELI lanamhla ipepa lelo kugqibela kulo nyaka we 1881, asinto ke inge
faneleke ukuke sime, sondele nga semva— ke setyise izinto ezidluleyo
zalo nyaka sesi lindele “ukuba uli kangele uli gqibele”. Lomnyaka we
1881, wa ngena abemi beli lizwe bena matemba nge zinto ezi bekiselele
empilweni; imvula zazi ngezizo nazo; ama simi aye cwayitisa umxelo:
ngeli fupi u SOMBAWO waye ncuma pezu kwetu.

Ngecala le micimbi yo laulo, isibonakalo esabe siko sabe singe siso
esikazimla nje ngesi besi qwalasele kuso. Imfazwe ya Besutu, kwaku kweli
xa ihamb’ apa; kwaku kweli xa igazi lo Mlungu no Msutu liyi mirozo,
imali yabahlauli berafu bale Koloni, ikwa palala kwangohlobo olu njalo.
Kwaku lelixa eyabi bonwa ngaba mbalwa into engati yenziwe ze kubeko uku
nqumama kwe misebenzi eyabi senza aba hlolokazi ne nkedama, kwi mizi
emininzi yeli le South Africa. Ngalo maxesha yabi ngu Mr. SPRIGG naba
desepile bake aba mbalwa, ababe nokuti imfazwe yo tabata inyanga
zontatu, mhlayimbi zontlanu. Ixesha lona la xela eyalo imbali. Siyazi
namhla akutabatanga xesha lide, yangena imfazwe kwa Tato, yatyutya ye
gqamsholo kwa MHLONTLO, ahamba amare, ati nama Mpondo seku ngama
cebetshu angene. Kwalu ndunkunku lwalunye kwelo limele ipesheya kwe
Nciba. Abehle acinywa ama langatye awabe lunge nelama Mpondomse; wacitwa
u MHLONTLO, wa badula ; bazinikela ku Rulumente o MDITSHWA, kwatiwa luko
kwelo uxolo : ondiyalwa baxolelwa, ngapandle ko MHLONTLO owabe netyala
loku ceba uku rodomsha u Mr. HOPE ; yabimi ngokuma e Lusutu.

Kute ngo 22 ku Eebruary, wa galeleka u Mhlekazi u Sir HERCULES GEORGE
ROBERT ROBINSON etunyelwa ngu Mntan’ Omhle, ukuba aze ku laula ngentloko
yake ehluzekileyo imicimbi yeli lizwe ebiseli xake abaniniyo ngoku
xubayela kwayo. Ute esafika wali hlanganisa iqela labenzi miteto, ukuba
lixele uhlobo emazi hanjiswe ngalo izinto. Okwenene lihlangene nge 25 ka
March, lite iqela lamadoda eli bandla elabe liyala ihambiso ye Rulumente
ka Mr. SPRIGG, lamisa u Mr. SCANLEN abe yintloko yalo. Akulahlekanga
xesha; ufumene u Mr. SCANLEN ituba lokuba i Rulumente ka Mr. SPRIGG ati
mayixele ingxelo yobu gosa bayo, ngemisebenzi eyangenisa ezi nxwaleko.
Kute kwakuziwa ekwahluleni amaqela, kwafunyanwa u Mr. SPRIGG evunyelwa
nga madoda a 37; wapuma ne 34 u Mr. SCANLEN. Kute xa kulapo kwavakala
ukuba lowa Mr. SPRIGG wabe hamba ngobu ganga, esiti aka nakupulapula ku
Msutu, ude wayi goba intamo: ucele u Mhlekazi ukuba axolise. Umise u Sir
HERCULES indawo ezitile, ate maba zamkele abe Sutu ze lixole. Emva kwe
nteta eninzi usamkele u LEROTHODI isi gqibo so Mhlekazi. Kwati kwaku
viwa yi Parlamente ukuba u Mr. SPRIGG ude wafuna umxolisi, apitizela
amadoda elo bandla, ati, hleliwe nje ibi ngamazwi ku.

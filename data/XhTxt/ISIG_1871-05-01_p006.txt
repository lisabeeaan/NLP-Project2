PETROS REBE.

UMXOSA OBALA NGE KANTINI,
NEMBOLA, NOMIDUDO.

KUMNANDI ukufumana Isigidimi Samaxosa ngokuba sisiza nemfundiso
ezilungileyo.

Kuseloko ndakolwayo nje ndibona izinto ezine eziwonakalisayo umhlaba
kakubi. Eyokuqala, yimbola; eyesibini, ngozintlombe, nomidudo,
nokwaluka; eyesitatu, yinkantini; eyesine, butywala bamazimba. Ezizinto
zone zifana nenkumbi, nengqakaqa. Inkumbi ziyayidla inch nezityalo
zomhlaba—noko inayo imvula, zikula iziqamo zomhlaba, ihluma kakuhle
inca, inkumbi zifika zitshabalalise konke. Injaloke nengqakaqa. Ibulala
abakulu nabancinane.

Kanjako ezizinto zone ziyawonakalisa umteto ka Rulumente. Ngako oko
nditi kutenina ukuba inkantini, notywala bamazimba, nokwaluka zipate
ubukosi kangaka? Izinto ezivelisa ububi obungaka, zilutshaba luka
Rulumente. Nokuba U-Rulumente uyayazisa imiteto yake ayinakupulapulwa
bani ngetuba lezizinto. Impambano zivela kozinkantini, nomidudo,
nokwaluka—ngokuba ezizinto zivingca nokoyika novalo. Uvalo luyasityela
ukuba U-Tixo ngumdali nomlauli wetu, nokuba sifanele ukuba sikushiye
angakutandiyo siputume oko asiyalele ukuba masikwenze.

Hayi ukuba lusizi uhlanga lwakowetu lweyele kozinkantini. Izidenge!
ezingakwaziyo okulungileyo kokukohlakeleyo ; ezicimeke amehlo nemvo zazo
ngenxa yenkantini. I-Gospel iyanqonqoza imini ngemini, noko akukotuba
lokuba imgwebe, imkokele umntu, ngokuba indlebe nengqondo zabantu
zibanjwe yimidudo, nembola, nenkantini, nomqomboti.

U-Rulumente akana kuzincipisana inkantini nezinye ezi inkohlakalo—kuba
sisazi ukuba kususela kuzo ukubulala, ubusela, nokuyilibala imiteto ka
Rulumente. Ke utywala obu buyajika intliziyo yolungileyo, buyenze
eyokohlakeleyo ukuba ibe sigebenga ngakumbi.

Mna ndibalayo lencwadi ndingunyana wom Xosa—um Gcaleka. Kwaka kwako
pakati kwama Xosa upimiso olutile ekutiwa lisiko. Intliziyo zababequba
elisiko zada zanga seziyinqu yesono. Waqumbake U-Tixo ngenxa yelisiko
walucitacita usapo luka Xosa. Inkantini ndizilinganisela nalonto.

ABEMI BEHLABATI BEBONKE.

INANI labemi behlabati xa baqukiweyo lizizigidi eziliwaka elinamakulu
mabini anamanci asibozo anesibozo (1,288,000,000). Bengaka njeke aba-
ntu, ama Caucasian (abamhlope), azizigidi ezi 360; ama Mongul
(abalubelu), azizigidi ezi 552 ; ama Ethiopian (abamnyama) azizigidi ezi
190; ama Malay (abagwangqa), azizigidi ezi 179; ama American (abantsundu
base America), asisigidi esinye. Intlobo ntlobo zenteto ezikoyo pezu
komhlaba apa zi 3,642. Intlobo zembedesho zili 1,000. Abantu ababuba
nganyaka mnye bazizigidi ezi 33. Oko kukwakunye nokuti ngamini nye
kububa abantu aba 91,554; ngeyure babe 3,730; ngeminete babe 62. Kengoko
okukubeta ibetayo intliziyo kufa umntu. Amadoda azekileyo adla ngokupila
ixesha eli- ngapezu kwelama soka. Ama Kristu azizigidi ezi 336;
abasabambe inkonzo endala yakwa Sirayeli bazizigidi ezi 5 ; abenkonzo
zase Asia bazizigidi ezi 60; abenkonzo ka Mahomet bazizigidi ezi 190;
abasabambe eyezi tixwana, aba kutiwa ngama Pagana, bazizigidi ezi 300.
Kwaba kutiwa ngama Kristu abe yama Roma ityalike, bazizigidi ezi 190;
abe yama Greek ityalike, bazizigidi ezi 75 ; aba kutiwa ngama
Protestant, bazizigidi ezi 80.

IGQIRA ELIMHLOPE NELIMNYAMA.

IMFENGUKAZI ebiqeshwe ngu Mr. Young wase Cuyler Manor, ngase Uitenhage,
ite itunga yazihlaba ngenaliti esihlahleni yapukela. Iye ku Dr. Dyer,
wati akuyi kangela waqonda ukuba yingozi ukuyityanda, waseleyinika iyeza
eliya kuyisebenza kuhle. Iteke kodwa yeva kusitiwa kuko igqira elimnyama
E-Drie Koppen elingayinceda kwa ngoku. lyileke kulo; laza lakuba
liyivisile intlungu ezinkulu, layinika into egqoli- leyo yenaliti, lati
liyirola kuyo, mayize ilikumbule ngayo, ibazi kanjalo nobugcisa balo.
Ibuyileke lemfengukazi; yahla yaqonda kodwa ukuba koti nokuba kuyinene
ukuba i’roliwe zibe zona intlungu zisasele. Zite ezinye izihlobo zayo,
ayikarolwa, yazipendula ngokuzibonisa lenaliti liyizuze egqireni. Kute
kungekabi kade yaza kuti gqi engalweni eyona naliti iyiyo, njengoko waye
exele ngako U-Dr. Dyer. Kwaba kukonake ipolayo ingalo. Olusizana lomfazi
lute ngenxa yobudenge nenkolo ezigwenxa, lwahamba imayile ezipantse
ukufika kumakulu amabini, lwabatala iponti eneshumi lesheleni, kanti
umvuzo wako konke oku uyakuba kukuviswa intlungu ezinkulu, aze akohliswe
libedengu legqira.

IBADA.

UBUSELA obusandukubako apa ngase Qonce kulendlela isinga E-Briedbach,
bukulu. U-Mr. R. Hickey umfo okuteleyo, enomonde, ubevela E-Bayi, esati
upatele usapo lwake iziqamo zemisebenzi yake. Ikaya lise Cambridge,
kodwa umsebenzi wake ugowokuquba inqwelo. Uteke akubon’ ukuba usatshaya
inqawa yake ehleli ngapambili enqweleni, weva selequtyulwa ngumbexeshi
wale nqwelo yake, efuna imali enxoweni. Igama lalo mbexeshi ngu William,
Umxosa. Uzixatiseleke U-Hickey kangangoko abenako koko kufike umkokeli
wanceda kwa umbexeshi. Bamwise pantsi, omnye wambamba ngomqala, wave
omnye erola imali, ama £30 eponti, ne £10 leponti yamapepa imali. Ziteke
ezinkewu zabeka ngase Qonce, waza U-Mr. Hickey akuqabuka waselefuna
indlela zokwazisa U-Birch umpati wamapolisa, oteke wawabiza ukuba
bawafune amasela lawo. Siyavuyake ukuba ngoku selese- tolongweni
omabini. Umkokeli wabanjwa ngakwa Siwani eseneshumi lesheleni, enxibe
ingubo ezin- tsha zodwa. Umqubi ubanjwe selesinga E-Zeleni. Elibedengu
belisenayo inxowa yemali, nenxenye yemali, selitenge nemibalo. Into
ebingeka funyanwa yimali yamapepa. Ezinye impahla abezitengile
zifunyenwe kwesinye isikolo ngase Qonce.

KWELE DIAMOND.

KUKO sela litile lisand’ukungenela itente eyiven- kile ka Mr. Hill no
Mr. Paddon. Bekute ngobo busuku zabiwa ngabo ezonto U-Mr. Paddon
watshixa kwangexa lesibozo. Ute emva kweyure wabuya waya wafika
igqojoziwe itente, kuko nomnqwazi angawaziyo, ezinye impahla zite sa
ezinye zisongiwe sezilungele ukutwalwa. Ubone kanjalo kwakufupi
nalondawo ezinye zisongelwe engubeni yokulala, kubonakala ukuba isela
elo lizishiye lakuva esiza U-Paddon. Akungena wa-fumana ukuba ayiko
nenxowa yemali ebineponti ezi £200, namapepa emali. Usukeke waselekwa-
zisa kuma polisa oko, aza wona kwangobo busuku abamba umfo obene weskoti
entsha inopawu luka Mr. Paddon no Mr. Hill. Ute yena lomfo sele- nyanga
ntatu wayitengayo, nakuba bekumhla ayi- nxibayo. Asikeva ke ukuba kude
kwatinina. Kodwa inxowa yemali ibingekafunyanwa. Kuvakala ukungati
kuranelwa umfo abati ngu James Moore. Kusatiweke makaye kubanjwa.

U-Mr. Brannigan no Mr. Sheehan ababefakwe etolongweni yase Jacobsdal,
ngenxa yokubada indlu yeposi yase Pniel, babalekile kona. Ngoku
sebekweyase Klipdrift.

Imali yenkohliso asiyiyo nayo E-Hebron. Ku­tiwa iponti zayo zivela
kwindlu yokwenza imali yase Sydney E-Australia. Ukungabi nyaniso kwayo
kuqondakala ngokuhlokoma kwayo yakubetwa etafileni.

E-Du Toit’s Pan ziyafunyanwa kanye idiamond, kutiwa kuko
esand’ukufunyanwa ebunzima buzi karat ezi 73. Kwakona apo umfo ontsundu
ugwetyelwe ukuba azuze imivumbo ema 25 ngenxa yokusuka ebe ilitye
elifana ne diamond, ebelibekwe ngabom apo aya kulibona, elingwa ukuba
uyakulibana okwenene.

Idiamond ezi 1414 azidli ngakubonwa. Kodwa zisand’ukubonwa kwa Mr.
Blaine E-Bayi. Bezifi-ke zacwangciswa ngento entle, zenzelwe ukuze umntu
abe nokuzibona kupye. Zigqitagqitene kanye ngobukulu, ezinye
zingangentloko yesipeliti ezinye zingange qanda lencede. Ixabiso lazo xa
ziqukiweyo zonke, lifike kwiponti ezi £15,000. Zanga zingababuyiselela
abaninizo, zize zibengezele iminyaka eliwaka.

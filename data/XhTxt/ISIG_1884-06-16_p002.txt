Ebala ngo 26 May ngezasematyaleni e Bayi wenjenje u “Looker-on”:—Pambi
ko A. O. WYLDE, Esq., C.C. & R.M. U Booy Pantsi ocolwe efile.
Ngolwesi-Hlanu, 23 May 1884, u Benjamin Bower idindala lase Bitsini lati
lihamba leva ivumba ngati lelento efileyo livela ngase Iwandle, laya
kukangela. Labona umfo omde elele pantsi kodonga efile, selenuka. Waye
ambete kupela idlavu le ondubatyi (waistcoat). Laya kumbika ku Sergant
Verrail, owamtabatayo wamncwaba emva kokuba eboniwe ligqira nangumfazi
wake. Ngom-Vulo umfazi wake unike obu bunqina pambi kwe Mantyi:—‘ Igama
lam ngu Makazi: ndihlala e Meveni pesheya kwe cweba. Ndisibonile
isidumbu somntu ocolwe efile, yindoda yam lomntu: Yalahleka ngo-Gqibelo
ongapaya (17 May) ndiyibone selifile ngalo Mgqibelo (24 May). Yayisifa
indoda yam mhla ndahlukana nayo, yayingalele pantsi. Igama layo ngu Booy
Pantsi: Ngumntu ongena ntlalo intle ukuhlala kwake, kanjako uyasela
kakulu. Yonke iveki le idluleyo siyafuna, asiva nomkondo wake: Side seva
nge Slamsi lisiti kuko umntu ocolwe efile, kanti nguye.” I Sajini ite
yaya kukangela kulo ndawo wacolwa kuyo u Booy Pantsi, ukuba akuko mpau
pantsi nakugqusha angaba mhlaimbi ubulewe ngomnye; ayibonanga mpau
namkondo wanto. Umfo lo makabe waye nxilile waza waya kweyela kolo donga
lo Iwandle. O Drink!

Kwango “ Looker-on ” siva ukuba u Mr. Reuben Glover wanikwa ityala ngu
Carl Carlsenidindala lokwapula umteto (Par. 5 Sect 8 Act27 of 1882)
ngokupazamisa idindala emsebenzini walo wokubamba umntu owaye nxilile.
Kubonakele entetweni yamanqena emaninzi ukuba eli dindala bati ngu
Carlsen lafumana um Xosa abati ngu Wm. Pita elele e Tullah Street
enxilile; lamvusa lamfaka izacolo zakomkulu (hand cuffs) lazibekisa emva
ingalo. Lite “Go on now you B y Nigger” latsho lamsunduza nga-

mandla—kwaye kuko iliwa eliyi 18 feet —waya kuwa ezantsi. Uvela apo u
Mr. Glover ati—“ I say, Policeman, if that man gets hurt I’ll report
you.” Lite guqu ipolisa lati “ Hamba uye kuxela kwangoku.” Ute akusuka u
Mr. Glover ukuba aye kuxela laseliziposa kuye lim’bamba. Lizikulule
ibuyi kwesinye isandla som Xosa lo lazifaka ku Mr. Glover, labaquba ke
bobabini bebotshwe ngadyokwe nye. Ute u Mantyi akazanga aweve ama Polisa
axoka kangaka. Lona eli lingu Carlsen lifanelwe ukugxotwa. Lim’bambe u
Mr. Glover lo kupela ngenxa yokuba belisoyika ukuba wolidiza kwesi senzo
salo sibi kangaka sokumkonxa umntu lize limsunduzele eweni. Umntu nokuba
umnyama, nokuba uluhlaza, nokuba unjanina ngebala—NGUMNTU, sisidalwa,
kanjako ngumntu we Nkosazana ofanele naye ukuba akuselwe ngumteto! Yati
yakutsho i Mantyi avakala eduma amatyala.

Xa sikumbula iziganeko eke sizibone ngawetu amehlo— ezingokwaluka
kwentsana pakati kwetu bamnyama kubonakala ukuba lixesha eke amadoda

ukuba lingaselilahlwa as a bad job. Au, masimyeke azitetele um’baleli

NKOSI, Mhleli wepepa elidumekunene: —Kwakona ndicela indawo
ngesisijungqana senteto yam. Kwakona ngokwandiswa kwe Mvo.—ndinendawo
endinga “umzi” ungayiqwalasela, umhlaimbi kungalilungelo oko
ekulandiseni elipepa lawo lidume kangaka, lingatanga apo, kodwa
linoncedo ngendlela zonke kuti balo, ekuyimfanelo ke ngoko kuti
Bantsundu ukuzivelela zonke inkalo zokuba lipuhle ngamacala onke
kubatati balo nase kwandisweni kwalo; enye into besingafanele
kukwaneliswa lipetshana elisitata iyure enye ekulifundeni. Awona maliuga
angeyesenziwa ngu “mzi” obanzi kangaka,—izibonda kunye ne teacher kunye
namadoda anamagama kwizitili azimeleyo abize intlanganiso ezinkulu
ngokwandiswa kwe Mvo — andibashiyi nabafundisi beletu ibala, nabo
mabenze “ Intuku ” ezishushu malunga nalomcimbi. endibatembayo ke ngoko
ukuba banokukwenza oko, bangatsho ukuti umsebenzi ubanzi pambi kwabo “
Owomnqamlezo.” Lixesha lokuba sibe sesinalo ipepa elingapesulu kweli
esityumtyeka pantsi kwalo ukuzibona ubuntu. Nditi nam akufuneki
nakuyicikozela lendawo kuba ingumlala pandle—ndiqinisekile kanjako ukuba
“umzi” uyikangela njengokuba mna ndiyikangele unokuyifunqula ngokulula
ukuba uwafakile amagxalaba ngakunye. Nokuba ke kambe ulutsha inkoliso
yalo ibilitanda elipepa ibingaba yinto entle. ebukekayo, nebalulekileyo
kule South Africa. Anditandabuzi nokutandabuza ukuba belitatwa
nasisiqingata sabo ngelilikulu kangangokuba lizifince izimvo zomzi nje
ngegama lalo, kungabiko nokukuririza simana ukukwenza ngokungafakwa kwe
ncwadi zetu, esiti silahlekwe zinto ezise sineni, lixesha, lipepa, yi
inki, zizitampo, kantike pakati kwezo nxamleko ayikungena, ngesizatu
salendawo yobuncinane bepepa, kungengasizatu so Mhleli. Masimyeke yena
azihlalele akanakuzenza ipepa, ngunotshe. Sekuko ibakala elisibonisayo
elenziwa ngu Mhleli, elibonisa ukuba kulula ukwandiswa kwepepa eli lona,
ukuba bekungeko nkabi zinqenayo zifana nezinye inkabi zenkomo, oti
wakuti “Dyok !” iti ukuba ayincwinanga ikupe ubulongo. Nditi u Mhleli
uyasibonisa ngesi sihlomelo simana ukubonakala kweziveki, ukuba kulula
ukwa­ndiswa kwepepa eli lona, ukuba abamkeli balibatu elinobom.

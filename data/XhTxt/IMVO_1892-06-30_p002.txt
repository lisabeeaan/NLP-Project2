Iwabasebenzi, Seke kwako abati makaputunywe abasebe- nzi kwamanye amazwe
; kodwa yena zininzi izizatu ezimbanga ukuba angayingeni lonto,' kuqala
sinabantu abaninzi abangasenzayo bepetwe kakuhle, kwakona ukuya kwamanye

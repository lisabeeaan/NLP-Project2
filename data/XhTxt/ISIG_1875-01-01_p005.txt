ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 1, 1875. 5

UNOGUMBE.

LOMNYAKA udluleyo usibulise ngemvulakazi enkulu abati nabayi bonayo
eyonaka we 1848, le yanamhla isahleli iyintloko. Sibona emapepeni
amacala ngamacala ukuba izifikelele zonke indawana zala macala ase
mpumalanga, kwezinye yangapezulu nakoko ibikuko apa, noko tina sibe
sikukuza. Ite apa okona kubandezela ukuna kwaba ngosuku Iwe sihlanu
evekini nase nyangeni. Kute kaloku kona ukuza kutshona kwelanga yanga
iya palala yaza ke yavuzisa ngolo hlobo kwade kwasa ngomgqibelo. Iqube
ngalonto noko ite pambi komhla yamana iba neziqabu, inika amatemba okuba
liza kusa. Bate abebesatsho baqonda xa liza kutshona ukuba bebede
bagqibela ukuposisa kukona iseza kuyenza into, kuba ibuye yanga ivuliwe
kwakona imitombo yapezulu yenza into yanye kwade kwasa laya kutshona nge
Sabata ngolwe sixenxe enyangeni, umhla owaba neziganeko apa kuti.
Ukubekela entloko mase sisiti ukumka komhla lomini yadili- ka Itower
yesikolo sentombi, sonakalelwa kakulu yinqolowa, safelwa zigusha nenkomo
nolunye ucuku esingenakulutini. Yakuhlanganiswa into eyonakeleyo apa
E-Lovedale kodwa ifikile kumakulu amatandatu eponti. E-Dikeni kwati
kwizindlu ezimi endleleni akwatandeka. Zaba ntatu ezimke kanye, kunye
nempahla yazo baza nabaninizo basindiswa ebubini. Ezinye zikolise ukuti
zonakale kodwa, nase mpahleni kubuye kubeko into efunyanwayo.

Eyona mini ke yoku konakala kungaka yilomini ye Sabata. Ite noko imvula
iqube kwade kwalolwe Sibini baba abantu sibezuze ituba lokuyi
lungiselela.

Eyalapa imbali yayo singayi gqiba ngelokuti kute emva kwemvula kwenziwa
intlanganiso kwamiswa ikomiti yokwamkela imali yokunceda abonakalelwe
kwati tu, nokuncedisa kule lahlekelo ingaka yase Lovedale. Nokuba
nguwupina ke ongati atumele nokuba yipeni kuwo angaya mkela ngovuyo.
Pakati kwawo kuko U-Rev. W. J. B. Moir, walapa abangati abatandayo
batumele kuye.

Ukuba sibe siya kuzingena zonke izinto esiziva ukuba zonakele kumacala
ngamacala sibe siya kusizalisa Isigidimi sonke ngendaba zika nogumbe
zodwa. Sesingati ke eyona ndawo ibe nezinto ezimbi kakulu kangangoko
sivileyo kuse Queens­town, apo ate amanzi abafikela abantu ebusuku kwafa
isixenxe sabantsundu, wamnye omhlope, impahla yabo yemka kwati tu, kuba
afike amanzi ngohlobo lokuba umntu aselecinga ngobom bake bodwa. Ngase
Monti kumke imikombe emitandatu kwafa umntu wamnye. Into enkulu abakala
ngayo kumacala onke kukufa kwempahla, ezimfutshane nezinde. Kuko
abafelwe zigusha ezide zagqita ewakeni. Kwenye indawo kufikwe inkomo
ezimashumi mahlanu zifele ndawonye zonke, kwezinye kumke inqwelo ezibe
zikufupi nomlambo, indlela zimbi ngokungatetekiyo. Kula macala
angapakati imitantato igqibele ukumka nakonakala. Esiva ukuba imke kwetu
mihlanu, eyonakeleyo kodwa mitatu. Ucingo kude kwapela iveki
lungasebenzi. Bati ukuyi tandabuzela into eyonakeleyo kule koloni iya
kufika esigidini, seponti yakuhlanganiswa. Ezangase Cradock nozibona
kwincwadi yomnye wababalelani betu.

EZIVELA KUBABALELANI.

Kimberley, November 18, 1874.

Nkosi, ndingavuya ukuba uke undifunele indawo kwelopepa lama pakati.
Nditi kuwe maze undincede, kuba andimntu waka wakukataza nganto
kwelipepa labantu. Ke nantsi into endifuna ukuyibalisa, yinto endayibona
kweli lizwe ngacawa itile. Ndite ndakupuma etyalikeni ndemka ndaya
kwenye ityalike kweya kowetu, ekwase Kimberley apa, kwindawo ekutiwa
kuse zintungeleni.

Ndite ndakupuma etyalikeni ndamana ndimi ngapambili kwetyalike
ndinabanye. Seva intsholo yengoma, kodwa noko ezongoma zizezobumnyama,
endite mna ndavakala ndisiti, madoda, makesiye kukangela lanto yenziwa
paya. Site sakufika kona safika kwakiwe isango ngababantu, safika
bezindidi ezine kulento bayenzayo, bepete amaxilongo bonke.

Lamaxilongo ke ngawengcongolo, kuvunywa kwa njengati xana sivuma inoti.
Ndati ndakuyipulapula lonto, ndati, kwabunje, Madoda, kanene lento
yenziwa ngaba bantu bona baya zonwabisa ngezizinto bazenzayo, kanti
abancedi Into. Oku sesifikile nje esisibane, sifikile, asisokubuyela
kumniniso ze, singawufezanga umsebenzi ebesitunyelwe wona.

Ndatsho enkumbulweni yam ndakuyibona lonto yenziwayo ngale mini ye
Nkosi. Kwati kwakuba njalo ndakumbula lamazwi eculo, ati, “
Ziyoyisw’intshaba zako Ngezwi lako Nkosi; Wozalisw’umhlaba wonke

Lizwi lako Nkosi. ”

Ndite ke bazalwana bam, ndakukumbula lomazwi eloculo, ndati, kanene noko
bawapulayo lomhla we Nkosi, abancedi Into. Oku lifikile nje, lifikile,
alisakubuye libuye lingawufezanga umsebenzi walo, ebelitunyelwe wona ngu
mninilo.

Hayi mandipeze hleze mhlaimbi ndiyigqibe indawo. Manditshonele ngeliti,
ningalibali bazalwana bam ukutandazela abapuli bemini ye Nkosi. C.
TUKANI BITYI.

Cradock, December 15, 1874.

Umlambo uzele ngohlobo endingalwaziyo nda- fika onje apa E-Cradock kute
nge 4 Dec. kwada kwangecawa nge 7 December kwati ukumka ko- mhla Iwafika
ucingo Iwase Middelburg lwati lumkani maninzi amanzi azayo ayakufika
E-Cradock ; emva kwexa lesixenxe afika okunene baqala abakufupi nomlambo
bafuduka afika ngamandla, saqala sakupa izitulo zetyalike yamalawu
ebingasemlanjeni kwanemikombe yokushumayela; angena abantu besakupa
ezompahla kwati ngexa leshumi ngokuhlwa yawa ityalike kwati emva kwayo
wemka umcancato wase Cradock, kwati emva kwawo yawa indlu yetola kwemka
nentango zamasimi abelungu ezibe zingase mlanjeni, afika pakati komzi
waba ntsundu atabata ecaleni elisezantsi azifaka pakati ezinye izindlu
awisa eyelawu elihamba neposi yase Middelburg kunye nempahla yalo yonke
eyendlu neyo kunxiba lasala nebulukwe nebatyi, impahla elalizinxibele;
kwanomfazi walo wasala nezozisemzimbeni kuye kwa nosapo Iwabo kanjalo;
kwemka izibonda zocingo zazitandatu Iwaqawuka umgama owemka namanzi
ubungati ukuba uyolulwa ube zimaile ezintatu; kwemka nomcancato wase
Talika inxenye yawo, wona owakuti apa wemka wonke akwasala nento; kwemka
nemfuyo yabantsundu kakulu namasimi abelungu ozinqolowa nozihabile kwati
kucu, lasuka langa lifile kwati ngobo busuku becawa impahla zabantsundu
zafudukela ezigqaqeni amadoda asala evarasha evarashela amanzi awalala
antsundu namhlope, asuke olinganisayo ati ukulala kwake umntu alale
ngamadolo ebeleke umntwana emhlana egone

omnye ecaleni ufike ihleli pantsi emnyango ipete isibane; yande ke lonto
kubo bonke, omhlope nomnyama kwada kwapuma ilanga kunjalo

Enxuba kumke umzi womlungu obehlala kona, kwemka kunye negusha zake
nenkomo kunye ne- zicaka zake zonke, naye nentsapo yake yonke kuba
amanzi afika pakati kobusuku kulelwe ngabo bonke. Ngoku ungebisabona
nalapo umzi wawukona, seliyintlabati yodwa kulondawo.

R. J. PAKATI.

Graham’s Town, November 26, 1874.

Lendawo sasike sayibala ngapambili kekaloku-kubonakala ukuba ayanele
ukuba ingene kube kanye. Nditi kuni bantu bangapandle abamana ukuzisa
izinto zabo ukuza kuzitengisa Edolopini asikuko nokuba niyasibandezela
tina bantu bedolopu ; kuba imali nisuke ningayipati yokuba nizitengele
ukudla, ize niti kanti naleyo niyifumene ngezonto benizise zona
aniyikutonga kutya, niyakusuke nilindele kumninindlu, kanti nalo
mninindlu naye wezakufuna imali njengokuba nani nizekufuna yona. Nditike
yikangeleni lonto ukuba inyamezeleka kanjanina xa niyakufika niligqiza
nize nixaswe nguye ati endaweni yesheleni ezimbini arole isitandatu
ngenxa yokuba nibaninzi. Kuko lento bati inxenye yabantu bedolopu
bangabisavuma nokuba umntu angene endlwini, kuba besazi ukuba ngumtwalo
onzima. Ukugqibelake nditi msa ukuyilibala imali nokuba uyapi nokuba
ungubani. Kubuzeni oko kumzi osendleleni ukuba kubanjani naxa seleruquke
zindwendwe, ukuba bubakona ububele bokuti ngena. Akabi nguhayi akuko
ndawo yinina, umhlayimbi kutiwe umlungu akavumi ukuba kulale abantu apa.
Elozwike liveliswa zintloni kuba bengafuni ukutyila eyonanto iyiyo. Eli
lixesha lemali, msa ukutemba omnye umntu, temba inxowa yako, Ingesi xa
lihamba lipata ingubo yokulala nokudla zonke ezonto zisenxoweni apa,
nendlu yokulala aliyikuyicela mntwini ikwasenxoweni apa.

Enye into yile elisiko lokucela asikuko nokuba lisiko elibi kakulu ;
omnye umntu uti kanti empanje omnye akasampi ngantliziyo ilungileyo,
kupela selesusa uxam emhlana ; aze ati omnye engonako asuke ayekucela
entweni engenako kanye, afumane ayekuba sinyinyimanyevu esinjalo pezu
kwalonto isweleyo. Nditike yiyekeni imifuno edliwa xa kuko indlala
yidlani leyo eyazekayo ukuba iyadliwa nangendyebo.

NTOZINI NTSHONA.

Macfarlane, December 17, 1874.

Nkosi etandekayo Mhleli Wesigidimi Samaxosa ndicela ukuba undifakele
lamazwana ambalwa ombiko. Bazalwana into endinibikela yona nantsi apa
E-Macfarlane siwelwe yindlu yetu yetyalike. Sinicela nonke makowetu
bantsundu nabamhlope abangamakolwa nabangakolwayo lento ize nisivele
ukubanzima kwayo. Kute xa sisakangele ngokufa komfundisi wetu suka qapu
nto yimbi, lento asinto ukubalusizi, endaweni yokuba besisiti nokuba
inkosi iyamtata umfundisi soman’ ukuzitutuzela ngayo lendlu, namhla
iwile. ncedani madoda seyele mandizimele batandwa ndingumhlobo wenu.

MBOVANE M. Q.. RENQWA.

, Quenstown December 19, 1874.

Nkosi yam, Mhleli, indaba apa simke nomlambo kwapantsa ukuhla ingozi
enkulu. Kute pakati kobusuku xa bakobukulu ubutongo abantu wagaleleka
ezindlwini umlambo, bemka nawo abatatu ; abanye abakwabatatu bafe
yinqele.

Enye into ngomhla 7 kufe inkazana etile ngetuba lokufungela igilase
ezisibozo. Ite izigqiba yaba isiwa wapuma umpefumlo ise sesangweni
lenkantini. Lento ikumbuza izwi elitetwe ngoti ukuba umntu akabufuni
ubomi makanxile aze afe. JOSEPH MUTLE.

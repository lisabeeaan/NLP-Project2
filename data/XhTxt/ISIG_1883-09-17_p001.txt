NGOM-VULO, SEPTEMBER 17, 1883.

U-MISS WATERSTON.

KWAKWINTSUKU zale veki izayo elinenekazi liya kuyishiya i Lovedale ukuya
e Kapa apo liya kusebenza kwangamayeza. Kuti bamnyama ukumka kuka Miss
WATERSTON kuyinto ebalulekileyo ; kuba ngalo lonke ixesha abekweli lizwe
intsebenzo yake seloko yaba yeyayamene nati. Yiminyaka elishumi
linesihlanu nambla ezayo kweli lizwe ukuza kuseka esisikula sidumileyo
sentombi sase Lovedale. Ukufika kwake ke kwaba libakala elitsha ebalini
lemfundo yohlanga. Ngapambi kokuza kwake into eyabipapanyelwe yayi
kukufundisa udodana. Ngezomini siti tina imfundo yelo qela ibjdlulisele
nakule yezimini zanamhla : amalati- ne abefundiswa ngomqolo wonke ; i
grike zabe zifundiswa ngomqolo wonke; i kemestri zabe zifunyanwa
ezintlokweni zabamnyama. Singacwangcisa sitinina oko nanamhla imfundiso
ye Botani, ne Zuoloji isazibonakalisa entsebenzweni nasehambeni yabo
baba netamsanqa lokuzifundiswa ezonto. Ewe, imigqwetwana apa bati
zizatifikiti zo Ti. tshala ibingeko: kodwa imfundo yelo xesha siyifumana
ukuba ibingapezulu kweyanamhla kwingxelo ezingena kwele zabahloli
zikolo, zamadoda anjengo MR. O. H. HOGARTH, oti, eteta ngemfundo
yododana lwase Lovedale, imfundo yalo ayingapantsi kwaleyo inikwa
izikolo zetu ezipezulu. Ngubanina ke namhla ongawapimiselayo amazwi
anjengalawo ? Eke watsho kungatiwa ngabo baziyo, ngumfo okalipe kunene.
Yayinjalo imfundo yododana ngezomini lafi- kayo elinenekazi.

Amabandla esiwabulelayo ngokuyiquba imfundo ngoluhlobo, aposisa ngento
enye. Alibala ukuwenzela amalungelo umtinjana. Ngokwenjenjalo alibala
ukuba ati ukuze amanzi omlambo alunge ube umtombo ulungile. Alibala
ukuba abazali bentsapo ngonina, ukuba abaqeqeshi bayo isencinane
ngonina, ukuba abenzi bezimilo zayo ngonina. Umbali wale nteto akayazi
into angaye yiyo namhlanje ukuba wayengena mzalikazi unjengalowo INKOSI
yamnikayo. Indoda ayinampembelelo idlule eyonina elusatsheni. Yiyo lento
namhla ingumzamo wama Ngesi ukuqeqesha onina

bohlanga ezintweni zokukanya, ezintweni ezenza amakaya abe mnandi,
ezintweni ezihlambulula amakaya. Elisebe lomsebenzi wabo balilibala,
okanye balidukisela abafundisi bezo mini. Lide lapuma ilanga. Iboniwe
indawo yokuba ababantu bafundiswe izinto ezipakame kangaka abasakuba
namakosikazi aya kubafanela. Kuba kaloku wonke uyazi ukuba akuko ntlalo
inandipekayo kumntu ofundiswe kakulu nongafundiswanga. Indoda
ingapambili ngomgama omkulu emfazini, ize ke isiqamo soko ibekukuba
umfazi ahlale ayoyikayo indoda yake, ahlale azicingela pantsi, iti
intlalo yake endlwini yake inge yeyesicaka. Oku kuma kwentlalo sibona
sibonile buzihlazisa izindlu zeqela eli- fundileyo. Ungafika indoda idla
yodwa esitebeni sayo umfazi nankuya esingete umntwana ngaseziko,
mhlayimbi ehlamba imbiza zake. Ngaxa limbi nantsiya indoda yonwatyiswe
yinto etile encwadini eyifundayo kuti kuba umfazi akanantliziyo
yokuzinandipa ezonto, kuba engafundiswanga, abonakele selehle umzimba,
ingekuhlekwa yena—nto leyo ke edala izilonda ezininzi, ukungevani,
ukungaqondani, ukungatembani, zeke umfazi ahlele kumgangato opantsi
wokuba akangeleke njengesicaka. Yabonakala apo ke imfuneko yemfundo
yentombi. Sasekwake isikolo. Zafundiswa intombi. Ezokuqala zazipetwe
nguye u Miss WATERSTON IO esavela kwelipesheya kolwandle. Ingqeqesho
yake isabonakala nanamhla kwabo bafazi baka bapantsi kwempato
yake—amangesikazi amnyama ongeze wawakalazela nganto. Yimfanelo ke
namhla emkanje u Miss WATERSTON ukuba sikumbuzane ngalowo wabila esopa
ebeka isiseko, nowasikulisayo isakiwo sokulungiselela intlalo yolutsha
olufundisiweyo. Pezu kolodonga walumisayo namhla sifumana into eninzi
yezikolo zentombi. Asimnqweno wetu namhla ukungena kwindawo yokuba⁻
lenqubo yezimini emfundisweni yentombi yeyona iyakudubula ekulungeni
sinina. Baninzi abatandabuzayo ukuba ozi geography, nozi balo
(arithmetic) baluncedo olukulu ezintombini, abacinga ngokuba eyona nto
inkulu kuzo kukuqonda ukulungisa indlu, nokupeka okuhle, nokulungisa
ingubo zabantwana nezendoda xa zendileyo, zeke pezu kwezi zinto zeleke
Isingesi esihle. Inyaniso yona imi yokuba u Miss WATERSTON weza kweli
lizwe waba luncedo olukulu ukuvelisa oko kwabe kufuneka. Oku wakwenza
ngokuti yena ngokwake ehle esihlalweni sake esipezulu sokuba engu Mlungu
abe mhlabeni mnye nentombi azifundisayo. Ngoluhlobo intombi zakowetu
ezafunda kuye zaba nokuyifunda yonke imo efanelekileyo eyenza iledi
lenyaniso, ingelilo elokubuda.

Emva kweminyaka esixenxe engenisa ukukanya kwintloko zomtinjana u Miss
WATERSTON usinge kwakwela kowabo apo afunde amayeza ewafundela ukuba aze
aye kuwasebenzisa Empakatini we Africa. Amtabate iminyaka emihlanu,
kwati ngo 1879 wasinga apo e Livingstonia; kwelo ubuye ngo 1880 wahlala
kwalapa e Lovedale, enika amayeza kulondlwana ibomvana. Kulondlu ube
luncedo olukulu Iwabamnyama. Amaxesha maninzi sidlula kulondlu, siti
sakubona izihlwele zemilwelwe yakowetu evela kwindawo ezimele lomzi
nezimgama—ema Bofolo, ema Ngqushwa, ema Qonce, nasema Mpofu—sikumbule
ngelo Dike lamanyange ebelisaku pitizeliswa yi ngelosi ye NKOSI.
Isahluko sesokuba kweli umlwelwe nguwona ubufumana unonelelo
olugqitiseleyo kunalowo unokuzipakamisa. Kwindawo yona yobu gqira u Miss
WATERSTON usishiya kwezinkulu intsizi nakwe nkulu inkohla. Uyasishiya
ke—sesisele sigxwala emswaneni. Sivakalisa imigulo yabo bonke abamnyama
xa siti wanga angandlela ntle nakulondawo anyule ukuya kuma kuyo.
Asitandabuzi ukuba nakuyo akasakubalibala abo bantu weza kubo kweli
lizwe kwamhla mnene.

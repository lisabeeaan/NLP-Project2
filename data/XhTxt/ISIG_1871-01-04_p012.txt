ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 4, 1871.

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

UBUGEBENGA NOBUNYANGAZA.—Ama- xosa amatatu U-Moganda, no Makwiba, no
Zokombale, abanjwe afakwa etolongweni yase Bayi, ngokusuka awele
U-Sturman kwa umntu omnyama, ampange nempahla. U-Sturman lo yindoda
enemfuyo, ebesandukufika E-Somerset nen­qwelo yake. Umsebenzi wake ngowo
kuhamba nenqwelo. Ngolwe 17 ku Dec. waye ngakwa Mr. S. White apo kwakuko
iqela lenqwelo zikulule. Wati engakumbulele nto wabona selewelwa
ngamagezana atile, elinye lamhlaba engalweni ngesitshetshe, amanye
ambeta wawapantsi ngenduku. Amkulula ibulukwe yake. Kwakuko iponti
zontandatu kuyo. U- Stunnan uti ababanjwa abo ngabo aba bambetayo.
I-Mantyi isayi pengulula londawo.

UKUTEMBEKA.—Bate abantwana baka Moos Swanepoel, umntu omnyama wase
Colesberg, beye kufuna amahashe bacola inxowa yemali, kuko ete sa
ecaleni kwayo, kuko kanjalo neqaga lecuba. Bayizise yonke lonto ekaya.
Befikile waselezizisa ezonto e-ofisini uyise. Kutandatyuzelwa ukuba
lompahla yeka August into epila ngokucolacola inkuni. Kutiwa ke fanel’
ukuba yawa esacolacola. Akakezi kodwa yena U-August e-ofisini ukuza kuti
zezake ezonto ; kodwa noko kuyakangelwa ukufika kwake. lyonke lomali
iziponti zon­tandatu ezinesheleni ezintatu ezine peni zositoba.

INGOZI EMBI E-BAYI. — Umkonjana obulayishe amadoda omane, kuko
nga­pandle umqubi womkombe ekutiwa yi Bismarck obusiya kule mikombe
mikulu ipakati elwandle, wapenulwa liliza. Umqu­bi mkombe, nompeki,
nomnye ekutiwa ngu Erank, batshona kwa oko. U-Johnson, enye yalomadoda,
asindiswayo yayi seliqawuka. Igqira elalinceda lali ngu Dr.
Dunsterville.

UKUBIWA KWEGUSHA.— U-Mr. W. Jackson, omi kufupi ne Hanglip ngase
Queenstown, ulahlekelwe ngama 34 egusha, ngolwe 10 ku Dec. Uteke wakupa
izicaka zake ukuba ziye kufuna. Zati emva kwexesha elitile zeza nama 30.
Kute ke ngolwe 19 ku Dec. amadoda amnyama amabini azisa intloko
zezimbini, ati azifumene ngase Maxoseni. Lamadoda uyawazi U-Mr. Jackson
lowo. Akukabiko mntu ubanjiweyo ke ngazo, nakuba umkondo kade ulandwa.

UBUSELA.—Ihashana lika Mr. Le Raine wase Beaufort libiwe ngomgqibelo
esitalini. Kute ngomvulo kwabonwa seligaleleka ekaya linomkala. Loti
ukuba aliqawulisanga eseleni apo libe liliwisile.

Leponi ke mayibe inengqondo, ukubuya ibuyele kumniniyo.

OWALE UKUFUNGISWA.—Kwi Mantyi yase Colesberg kusand’ukuziswa Umxosa
ekutiwa ngu Andrew ngenxa yokubeta umfazi wake, oli laukazi. Elilaukazi
belingumfazi wake otshatileyo. Wafika lomfazi kubonakala ukuba
ubetyundyutwa ngesabokwe. Wati akufakwa apo kuma amanqina, xa aza
kufungiswa akavuma. Akabanga nakunika sizatu kwakutiwa makasinike.
Wavuma uku- fungiswa ukuba akalalati izulu kwada kweza kulamazwi ati—“
Tixo ndincede.” Kute kwakufikwa kuwo akavuma ukuwabiza. Wati uyakuxela
inyaniso yonke ngapandle kokufunga. Yayekwa ke lonto kwada kwa ngumhla
olandelayo. Kumanqina abamnyama kumhla ize ibeko into enje.

AMAWELE AMATATU. — Kuko umfazi E-Cape Town ozele abantwana abatatu
ngolwe 9 December 1870.

IDABI.—Kutiwa iqela lama Mfengu lalisela utywala E-Zeleni, kute nge
kwele lama doda atile aselesiwa ezintongeni. Itiwe ntliti kunene enye,
yahlala intsuku zambini yaqawuka.

IMFENGU ENEMBULELO.—Kute kuhla- nganisene intloko ze Hospital yase Bayi,
wati U-Mr. Wormald kwaba beko, imfengu etile ekutiwa ngu Bolmandowa
inika ibaso leponti ezi £5 ukongezelela kwimali exasa londlu. Ngati
lomntu wayenzakele waza wasiwa E-Hospitaleni wanqunyulwa umlenze.
Wapatwa kakuhle kangaka, ngangokude anikele lemali njengomqondiso
wokubulela kwake. Hayi into ifanele ukunconywa ifanele ukuba ngumzekelo
kubantu bonke nokuba ngabalipina ibala.

IHASHE ELIHLATYWE LAFA.— Ute unyana ka Mr. Marran ngamso utile equba
inkabi esuka emzini wake eyisa esilareni, yati yakudlula amancwaba ase
Rini, yakut’ukutukugqiba ukusela, yeguququ, yamkumbula. Imposile yatsho
kunene ehasheni lapants’ukufela kwalapo. Lafa kungekabi mzuzu kodwa emva
koko.

ISICOTO SAMATYE.— Kute ekumkeni kwelanga lomgqibelo ngo 8 ku December
1870 E-Qonce, kwafika isicoto sama­tye sandulelwe kukududuma nokubaneka.
Kunge kuwa izitena pezu kwezindlu. Aye ngalingani ngobukulu lamatye;
amanye aye ngangeqanda ledada, amanye engange leranise. Libete elinye
kwaqekeka isitena, laqekeka lamaqekaza mane lati lilinye langange qanda
lenkuku. Sapule kunene ifesitile, zasinda zona ezezitola kuba zazivaliwe
ngokuba yayi ngumgqibelo. Bate abebesadlala I-Kriket, nabebe sadubula
itekeni, ukufika kwaso

banyatela ukuya kufuna indawo zokusinda. Lutiwe xwi upahla lwendlu etile
obe lulolwentsimbi lwakuposwa mgama. Kodwa site esisipango sahla
sadlula. Kuko nabantu abane ngongoma ngokubetwa ngalomatye.

EZIVELA KWELE DIAMOND.—ISICOTO SEMVULA.—Asikuko nokuba leveki iyodwa
ngenxa yenqwitela nentshabalalo yempa- hla, nokunqumka kwemisebenzi.
Bati abembi ababeko ngelihlobo lidluleyo, akuzange kubeko luqwitela
lunjalo. Kwarazuka ozitente zangamajacu. Kwemka nem­pahla zezindlu
zemiti, zanyibilika izindlu zezitena ezinga tshiswanga. Ukuba Iwa- luke
oluqwitela lwahlala iyure ezimbini nezintatu ngapezu koko Iwahlala
ngako, ngekwakumke into yonke, ngapandle kwe zindlu zentsimbi nezemiti,
nenqwelo ezinzima. Ute omnye umntu esati ulinga ukusindisa isefu zake,
wangena emanzini, akabuye abonwe. Bekungabonwa nto ngapambi kokuqala
koluqwitela, kuviwe kupela isandi sokuvutela komoya. Kuwe kunene amatye
wavutela umoya, laye liduduma kunene. Ite itente ebisitola sika Bernard
Lee yarazuka, zaneta im- fumba ngemfumba zempahla, zite inqwelo ezibe
zikufupi kulo Hotele zanduluka nazo, zisiya kungena kumgonxo owawenziwe
ngamanzi ezantsi. Kuham- biseke izinto zitshabalala ngokunjalo kwada
kwasekutshoneni kwelanga. Zi- sindiswe nzima kanye itente ezisindileyo.
Kudlule iyure ngeyure abantu be bambelele ezibondeni nasezi ntsontelweni
ze tente, aye amatye eseloko ebabeta ezandleni nase zintloko. Ite indawo
eyomileyo yaba yindawo yokusabela abangena- makaya, naba nxweleriweyo.
Kungati kekwamana ukufika izipango ezinje, kungaba yinqaba ukumbiwa kwe
diamond ; kuba kunzima ukuquba inqwelo emigxobozweni; kuba kusiti
umhlaba usambiwe wafunjwa, ufike umlambo ukukulise ube- kise ezantsi
kunye ne diamond njalo. Kuyatenjwa ukuba ezizipango zobuya zipele.

INGOZ’EMBI.—Ute umalusi ka Mr. Daniel Koetser wase Botman ogama lingu
Anthony Lombard um Mozambike eku- mkeni komhla ekwelela inyosi eliweni
wequzu, kahla, wafunyanwa efile ezantsi apuke intamo. Kwaye kuko
umlam­bo obunzulu buma ngamadolo, ufike wanyulwa kuwo lomntu. Kubonakala
ukuba wayesalelwa ukuba angakweli, wa- pika, wawa wafa kunga
lindelekile.

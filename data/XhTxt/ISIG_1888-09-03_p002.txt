KAGASO SAZUZE.

INKOLISO yabafundi betu abange ngawo ama-Lovedale eli
gama lingentl’ apa abayi kulazi ukuba liteta mntu, ligama
amzi, sibizo sinina kwezi Iwimi zininzi zakowetu apa e-
Africa. Kanti ligama lendodana eyaka yalapa e-Lovedale
ize kufunda ivela embindini we Africa, mayela nomlambo
oyi Zambezi. Kugaleleke indaba zokuba ibub’ile ngomhla
lwe 10 ku July kwelakowayo e-Blantyre, apo yaye isel’

ibuyele kona ingumshumayeli welizwi.

Iminyakana edluleyo elishumi elinomvo u-KAGASO SA-
ZUZE waye eyinkwenkwana ebushula ze, ingazi nento le
ihleli ebudengeni obe bubalaula abemi balomlambo we Za-
mbezi. Waye eyinkedama eyabujelwa ngabazali bayo besese
bunyameni. Wacblwa kweso sigama ngu Dr. STEWART nge-
1877 oko way’ese Nyasa. Waza wamposa ngo May
walo nyaka esikuleni sase Blantyre esasifundiswa ngu Mr.
MAPAS NTINTILI, oway’engomnye waba Vangeli base Love-
dale kwelozwe ngelo xesha, osele ngoku ekwa Gcaleka.
Wagaleleka e-Lovedale ngo April 1878 eza kuxuma lomfu-
ndwana selenayo. Ngowesitatu umnyaka elapa waguquka,
wazinikela enkonzweni ye-Nkosi U-IEBU KRISTU, wabapti-
zwa ke ngo 1881. Ngexesha abefunda e-Lovedale ubexaswa
libandla le Established Church of Scotland awatunyelwa
lilo ukuza kufundela ubu-Vangeli e-Lovedale.

Kute ngo October 1883 wayalezwa ukuba agoduke aye
kuncedisa emsebenzini welo lakowabo. Banduluka nga-
mini nye no Mr GEORGE WILLIAMS, no JOSEPH BISMARCK
omnye wakwase Blantyre apo, ombali ikwafana neyomfi lo.
Befikile ekaya babelwe indawo zokuya kufundisa, noku-
shumayela, asel’ eminyaka mihlanu ke esebenza apo.

Ukufa oku uzuzana nako ngokusuka atunywe, ahlise
itnilambo ye Zambezi ne Shire; afumanane nengqele enkulu
kunene kulobambo; impange ezibilinini—kudibene necesini
yelozwe—yonakala ngexesha elifutshane imipunga. Ubu-
be ngoxolo olukulu ngentsasa yombla we 10 ku July nje
ngoko sesitshilo, ezole ngangokuba umkake eci nje yena
ulele kudala umntu waqaukayo.

Iminyaka awayihlalayo apa e-Lovedale, site isimilo sake
sabukeka kakulu, sazixwila ngobuhle baso intliziyo zetitsha-
la zake, nabo bonke ahlalelene nabo. Ebenganele kuzini-
nikela encwadini kupela, ubezama nge nkutalo enkulu
ukuzinikela kuyo yonke into engumsebenzi, sati nesandla
sake sazitambela zonke intlotyana zomsebenzi abezibekela
ekuwufundeni. Ebuyele e-Blantyre uzinikele kwangoku-
njalo emsebenzini awawupatiswayo wokuquba ilizwi, noku-
fundisa amawabo. Ubungqina bencwadi ka Dr. BOWIE bufi-
ke benjenje ukumbika into abeyiyo emsebenzini pakati
kwabo—“ U-KAGASO SAZUZE ushiye isikewu ekuya kuba
“ kukulu ukungabi nakuvingcakala kwaso. Silahlekwe
“ yindoda, ngokukodwa mna abe luncedo kuye emayezeni,
“ nasekuziqondeni incazelo zokupatwa kwawo, nokongiwa,
“ kwa benzakeleyo zizifo ngezifo.”

Kungummangaliso ukuti ngexeshana elifutshane kangaka
abe nako lomginwa—engazekele kufuzeni nzala yazizuku-
Iwana zinokwazi—abe nako ukupumelela kakuhle kangaka,
abe amalungelo afunde pakati kwawo edelekile kunamatuba
azuzwa ngabanye.

Wazeka inkedamazana yakwa kweli lizwe, koko mayela
entla ngecala lase ntshonalanga, egarna lingu JANE BISSET
eyazalelwa e Lagos, West Africa. Babonene e- Lovedale apa,
u Miss BISSET lowo sei’ evela Pesheya apo izihlobo zoyise
bezikona, kuba eli lakowetu lela kulo nina. Abazali bake
bafela e Lagos apo bobabini, waya kuyisekazi Pesheya
obuye wamtumela kwase Africa ukuba aze kuqeqeshwa
kwa kulo nina; wamkelwa ke si Sikula se Ntombi e-Love-
dale. Wanduluka nge 1884 u JANE lowo eya kwendela
ku KAGASO, batshata e-Blantyre; babantwana babini.
Namhla ushiywa engu mhlolokazi, nezonkedamana, kwanti.

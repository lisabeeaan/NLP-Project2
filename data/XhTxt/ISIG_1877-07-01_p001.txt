VoL VII LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, JULY 1, 1877. No. 82-

Isigidimi Samaxosa.

INCWADI KA HON. C. BROWNLEE
NGO LIWANI.

UKUZE abafunda lencwadi ka Mr. Brownlee babe nako ukuyiqonda inteto yayo
kuya kufuneka ukuke senze amazwana okuyi tshayelela. Kula Mamfengu ase
Ngqushwa kwesika Njokweni kwati akububa u-Njo- kweni lowo kwamiselwa ngu
Rulumeni unyana wake u-Fundakubi ukuba abe yi- nkosi. Lo Eundakubi
njengesiko laba ntsundu waenabafazi abafike esibinini. Ngowokuqala
wazala u-Liwani, kwaza seku- mzuzu ezelwe u-Liwani wabuya wazeka u-
Fundakubi, wati lo wamva umfazi wangu- yena kutiwa ngomkulu. Ngalowo ke
umfazi wazala unyana abati ngu Janjala. Akuba ebubile u-Eundakubi nge
1875 u-Captain Hunt Imantyi yase Ngqushwa wabiza intlanganiso yesizwe
ukuba kuze kuxelwa esingatanda esipete. Abazalwana ababini baka
Eundakubi, u-John no Sutu, bati ma- kumiswe u-Liwani, unyana wokuqala,
ote kanjalo wangumfo olungileyo. Uninzi Iwabantu Iwavumelana nalondawo.
Iqutyiwe ko Mkulu uvumile no Rulumeni ukuba kulungile.

Kute ke kulomnyaka udluleyo kwavela u- Xokelelwana, kwa omnye
wabazalwana baka Eundakubi, wati kungatiwanina ukumiswa u-Liwani kanti
uko u-Janjala, unyana woyena mfazi mkulu. Kubonakele ukuba lonto iza
kungenisa izipitipiti isahlule kubini isizwe. Ewe, mayela ku July
odlulileyo besekuke kubetwane ezindywaleni. Ude u-Xokelelwana wabala
incwadi ngo August 15, 1876, ayisingisa ku Mr. Brownlee ati kuyo:
Ndiyabona ukuba umntana womzalwana wam, u-Janjala, upangwa ubukosi bake,
isenzeka lonto ngokusuka Imantyi yetu u- Captain Hunt ingatyilelwa ukuma
kwezinto. Ngesiko letu tina bantsundu oba yinkosi asingozalwa kuqala,
ngozalwa ngumfazi omkulu. Kwaba njalo ngo Sandile owaba yinkosi kanti
u-Maqoma no Tyali bangapambili kuye: kwabanjalo ku Siwane
owaenabakuluwa, u-Mattavas no Qasani: no Jali waba yinkosi enabakuluwa
abaliqela. Ngezindawo ndibonisa ukuba isiko letu asikukumisa unyana
wokuqala, kukumisa owomfazi

omkulu. Umzalwana wam u-Fundakubi wazeka intombi ka Kaulela owaye
kwayinkosi naye. Sasisazi sonke isizwe ukuba nguyena mfazi mkulu nokuba
ngumntana wake oya kuba yinkosi. Emtshatweni wake kwakuko no R. G.
Tainton Esq., namanye amanene. U-Mr. Tainton wada warola netokazi
lenkomo, ukupa umtshakazi. Indawo ke enditeta ngazo bubukosi nenkomo
ebezizezika Eundakubi eziku Liwani ngoku. Nditi ke ngoko mna kwakunye
namapakati akangele u-Janjala, mayi lungiswe lendawo, iyasikataza
isizwe, ingade ivelise nezinto ezimbi.

Lencwadi ka Mr. Brownlee esinga singa- yibeka pambi kwabalesi betu
ipendula lamazwi ka Xokelelwana awabalakunye namadoda amashumi mane
ananye. Masingayishiyi lendawo yokuba lamadoda acela u-Mr. Penn ukuba
awatumelele lencwadi ku Mr. Brown­lee. Uti ke ukupendula ewasingisa
Emantyini amazwi ake ukuze iwaqube ku Xokelelwana:—

“Ngokumalunga nencwadi ka Mr Penn ayitumelele u-Xokelelwana, ndikucela
ukuze ubabizele kuwe bonke abanento yokwenzana nayo, ubazise ukuba kwati
akububa u-Fu­ndakubi, u-Rulumeni wanomnqweno wokumisa umntu endaweni
yake, waza ke wabeka unyana wake u-Liwani, kuba kwakubonakala ukuba
ukufanele ukuba yinkosi.

“ Kubonakala ke ukuba ngoku u-Xokele- Iwana uti makulandelwe isiko
labantsundu, aze u-Liwani ayihlutwe indawo ayinikwe ngu Rulumeni. Uda
akankanye no Sandile ukubonisa ukuba ifanelekile into ayitetayo.

“ U-Xokelelwana kufuneka ukuba akumbu- le ukuba ama Mfengu alusapo Iuka
Rulu- meni, nokuba u-Rulumeni akayise kodwa, uyinkosi yabantwana bake,
ate ama Mfengu ngokukuselwa yiyo, kuleminyaka imashumi mane akula.
Kungummangaliso ke ngoko ukuba u-Xokelelwana asalatise kwimiteto yabantu
abasand’ukuba pantsi kowetu umteto, atete ngezinto ezenzeka abo bantu
bengeka- bi pantsi kwetu.

“ Umnqweno ka Rulumeni ngowokuba usapo Iwake lufunde amasiko ase koloni.
Oko ama Mfengu ebesezidenge engekabi natuba lokufunda okulungileyo,
u-Rulumeni ubengawapazamisi emasikweni awo ; kodwa ngoku wona alusapo,
anencwadi zobukaya. Akufanelekile ke ngoko ukuba alaulwe ngemiteto
eyahlukileyo kweyolunye usapo Iwe

Nkosazana njengokungati angawasemzini nabapambukeli.

“ Ngokowetu umteto umfazi ubamnye endodeni, kulungile ke ngoko ukuba
owoku- qala umfazi abalelwe ukuba nguyena mkulu, oyena unguye ngokwase
mtetweni. U- Rulumeni ke ngoko akanako ukuti ngezimini zanamhla
ambandezele u-Liwani, ampate ngokungati ngumntana wase matyolweni,
ngokumdlula, ati ngu Janjala onguyena nyana ka Fundakubi.

“ Ngokomteto we koloni akuko zinkosi zingamiswanga ngu Rulumeni. Ukufika
kwa Mamfengu kuti sazivuma inkosi zawo; kodwa asilivumi kule koloni
igunya lokuba sendaweni yoyise ebukosini.

“Siyazifuna inkosana zokukangela abantu baka Rulumeni. Yoti yakufa ukuba
inonyana abakufaneleyo ukupata abantu, u- Rulumeni anyule kubo ukuba
babe sendaweni kayise, pambi kokuba akangele kwamanye amacala ; kodwa
ukuba onyana abafanelekile u-Rulumeni uya kunyula kwabanye abantu. Xa ke
unyana wokuqala engumntu ofanelekileyo, u-Rulumeni akanakumona ngokumisa
omncinane pezu kwake, ayenze lonto kuba uyise ezeke omnye umfazi wambiza
ngokuti nguyena mkulu, kanti uko owokuqala. Ukuba wenza into enjalo
u-Rulumeni angaba wapula imiteto yake ngokwake, abe usapo ulukutaza
ukuba Iwenze into enga ingapela.

“ U-Rulumeni umise ukuba u-Liwani abe sendaweni ka Fundakubi. Ekubeni
ekolisa akuko sizatu sokuba ashenxiswe, ngokoke abantu bonke mabazi
ukuba nguye inkosana emiswe ukuba ipate isizwe sika Njokweni,

“ Ngokumalunga nempahla ekankanywa ngu Xokelelwana uze umazise ukuba
njengo- kumisela komteto we 18 womnyaka we 1864, impahla yakokwabo iya
kulunga naye njengoko ayabayo uyise.

“U-Rulumeni akangi angapazamisa ekuyabeni kwake impahla yake, kodwa kona
ukumisela ebukosini kufana nokumisela ebumantyini nakwezinye indawo
zokupata igunya, kuse mandleni ka Rulumeni yedwa."

Siyakolwa ukuba abantu abaninzi abaya kuyi funda lencwadi ka Mr.
Brownlee baya kukolwa yiyo. Into eyoba lusizi kukuba ingati kanti ayi
sayi kufezwa ekwenziweni indawo asingisele kuyo, ibe njengencwadi
esazishicilela kwi Sigidimi sika November we 1875. Inteto yale
ngokufutshane iti

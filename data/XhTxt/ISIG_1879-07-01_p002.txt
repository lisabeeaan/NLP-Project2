abani ebukosini, bamiswe kulendawo bakuyo ngokwazi imiteto nokuqonda
ukuba angaba nguwupina oposisileyo. Ababantu ke ba- mnyama bateta
amatyala, banegunya loku- yigweba nenkosi ukuba iyamona umntu. Nayo
ukuba iti iyoniwa imelwe kukumangala kubo njengokuba bemangalelana
nabapantsi ize ukuba igwetyiwe ibe yoyisiwe. Ukulinganisela ngendawo eya
kukunjulwa ngabafundi betu, singakankanya ityala lika Nehemiya
Msbweshwe. Yena kwakutiwa umangalelwe yinkosaza, yaza ke yamzisa e-Qonce
ukuba litetwe yi Jaji ityala lake. Efikile kona ifike Ijaji yagwebela
u-Nehemiya, yagweba Inkosazana, okunene wapuma entolongweni. Kukwanjalo
nase mpahleni. Inkosi ayinakufumane iyitate. Imelwe kukuke imangale
kubantu abangezizo inkosi ize iti imdla ibe isigwebo isifumene kubo,
ingazigwebelanga ngokwayo.

Indawo yokugqibela ehlanganisela abantu abapantsi kolaulo lwama Ngesi
ngabamisi bemiteto, abakwamisa nerafu eya kubizwa ebantwini. Inkosi ye
Ngesi ukuze irafise imelwe kukuke icele ilizwi kwipalamente, ibe
iparlamente leyo ingamadoda aketwe ngabantu ngokwabo ngokuwazi
nokuwatemba ukuba ayakusibonela asitetelele akuqonda ukuba lento iya
kubacinezela abaya bantu. Ukuba ke ngoko abantu bakala ngokuti
Urulumente impabla yetu uyigqiba ngerafu kungamlungela ukuba yena ati
hayi asindim ngalamadoda nanite nina niyawatemba !

Ezizinto ke zonke zibonisa ukuba ulaulo lwe Ngesi apo luzamela kona
luzamela ukuba umfuyi ahlale esazi ukuba into yake yeyake, aze ngoko abe
nokusebenza ngentliziyo ecacileyo. Okunene sibona zisiti izizwe ezibe
zingenakupumelela pantsi kolaulo lwe- zinye izizwe zinyuke zakuba pantsi
kwe Ngesi.

KWA ZULU.

KULE nyanga ifileyo into engamandla eke yebleka kukufa konyana ka
Napoleon obeze kunceda ama Ngesi. Uhlobo lokufa kwake abafundi betu
bokufumana kwenye indawo yelipepa. Kuvelise usizi olukulu kuyo yonke
lekoni, usizi oluya kuya kupatelela napesheya kolwandle. Kulusizi
nangenxa kanina umhlolokazi okade etwele ububi obukulu, ekubonakala
ukuba uya kude aye kutshona engcwabeni intliziyo yake irara. Abanye boba
basakumbula ukuba kwati emva kokugxotwa kwama French ngama German,
yagxotwa indlu ka Napoleon e-France. Emva kokufa koyise weli Tshawe
kulilelwa lona namhla, unina wahlala nalo e-England, wapatwa ngokufanele
indawo yake. Kekaloku nakuba intliziyo ibisoloko ilila ngokuba engeko
emhlabeni wake beli-

ngapelile itemba lokuba kuya kuda kuvele ituba lokuba unyana wake abe
yinkosi yase France. Kute ke kwakuvela lemfazwe yakwa Zulu, unyana lowo
wanqwenela ukuba aze ekulweni, kanti ke oko sekukusondela kokufa kwake,
nokucima kwegama lika yise kuba kungeko mntwana wumbi wakokwabo.

Imikosi ye Nkosazana ibuye yafunza kwa Zulu, apo ingxamelekona kukomkulu
ekaya. Iqela elipetwe ngu Wood, kuvakala ukuba alise kude kona, nokuba
akasondeli kakulu ama Zulu nakuba ake abonakale engumkosi omkulu
kwezinye indawo. Kunjalo nje kuvakala ukuba u-Cetywayo utumela esand
ukutumela ukuza kuxolisa. Inkoliso yabe Lungu ayi kolwa ukuba okunene
ufuna uxolo, iti uya mfamekisa, ufuna ixesha lokublanganisa imikosi yake
ukuze kubuye kubeko imini enje ngeyase Sandlwana. Nokoke Inkosazana
itumele u-Sir Garnet Wolsely ukuba aze kuyi lungisa imicimbi yakwa Zulu,
ati ukuba u-Cetywayo okunene uyaxolisa alwamkele ololuxolo ukuba
aluwagxekezi ama Ngesi. U-Sir Garnet lowo uselese Natal neqela labapati
mikosi. Kuvakala kanjalo ukuba kuko elinye iqela lamasoldati elizayo
ukuvela pesheya kolwandle.

U-SIR GARNET WOLSELEY.

Abafundi bamapepa endababaya kumkum- bula u-Sir Garnet, owaka weza kuba
yi Go- vernor yase Natal ixeshana elifutshane, tina apa sisapetwe ngu
Sir Henry Barkley. U- Sir Garnet lowo yenye ye general zama Ngesi edume
kunene ngokukalipa nangamaqinga okulwa, esingati lelinye lamatemba
amakulu akoyo ngoku emangesini ngokumalunga nokupata umkosi. Lomfo ke
kumiswe yena ngoku ukuba aye kuba ngumpati wemikosi yenkosazana e-Natal
rase Transvaal, ize kulomazwe yonke imicimbi, nengeyiyo eyokulwa ibe
sesandleni sake, ati u-Sir Bartle Frere akangele eyale koloni imicimbi.
Elinye lamapepa ase Bayi eliteta ngalonto liti: Si- yakolwa ukuba umntu
wonke wase South Africa uya kuvuyiswa kukuva ukuba apa kuza u-Sir
Garnet. Yinto engenakupikwa bani ukuba asisa kutembi ukuqonda kuka
Chelmsford ngokupata imikosi. Kusoloko ite yako lemfazwe yakwa Zulu
akakenzi nto yokuba atenjwe. Imikosi yetu uyi ngenisele amashwa pezu
kwamashwa. Kuyintlungu engenanto yokuyi buyisela ukucinga nge Sandlana,
nge Ntombi, nge Ntlombane nokufa kwetshawe lase France, unyana ka
Napoleon. Nalamaxesha ekuke kuhlaliwe ixesha elide kungeko nto yenziwayo
ibonwa ngababantu akwa yimbandezelo. Ixala esibe sinalo liya ncipa
namhla njengokuba sisiva ukuba kuza u-Sir Garnet Wolseley, yena ungumfo
obuciko ekupateni imikosi bungena kutandatyuzwa bam.

Enye into engakankanywayo ngaye yeyokuba uza enelizwi eliti makaze
njengokuba kuvakala ukuba u-Cetywayo ufuna ukuxolisa, alamkele elozwi
ukuba uyaqonda ukuba okunene livela ku-Cetywayo nokuba ali ngendlela eya
kulwenza lugxekeke uhlanga olumhlope. Liyanda inani pesheya kolwandle
elidiniweyo zimfazwe, elibuzayo ngokukodwa ukuba apa kwa Zulu kubangwa
ntonina. Okunene ke kungaba luvuyo ukuba kuda kufumaneke indlela
yokunqamla eligazi ku- soloko lapalala kulomhlaba wetu.

KWA MOROSI.

Omnye wamadoda ase mkosini kwa Moro- si uti, Sayi shiya ikampuyetu
ngomhla wesitandatu ka April, kwafuneka ukuba umgama omde indlela yetu
siyigabule ngembumbulu, asikuko nokuba unzima umsebenzi esiwenzayo apa,
ulala ubusuku obunye, buze obunye uye kulinda, ongasifumana site nca
ematyeni sixela ikati ilinde impuku, babe ababantu sibalindileyo sibeva
behlekisa ngati entabeni. Ingqele asiyiyo nayo, kube kunjalo nje
kungavunywa nokuba silumeke nenqawa. Okunye sipume ipatuloni yentsuku
ezintatu kanti siya kugqiba ntlanu, ezimbini sazi hlala singase nako
nokudla. Sasiye kungenela inqaba ka Doda emayile zilishumi ukusuka enta-
beni. Sasi ngamadoda amashumi asibozo anamahlanu, sipetwe ngu Colonel
Brabant. Silinge katatu ukuyitabata yasoyisa. Ngemini yokuqala utshaba
lwenzakalise abantu ababini kuti. Owesitatu wenzakaliswe kwangomnye wetu
wamdubula amazwane. Ngelixesha siyirauleyo intaba kukane utshaba lusiza
ebusuku, kusingenela apo sikona. Okokuqala kokubini silugxotile.
Okwesitatu lubulele ipolisa esilifumene linamanxeba alishumi ezikali.
Okwesine luye kungenela i-kampu ebipetwe ngu Chiappini inamadoda
amashumi mane anesibozo. Kute kwakutshona inyanga kanti utshaba lucote
ngamatye nangemiti. Abebesentila bate besotuka lwaselupezu kwabo.
Ngemizuzwana embalwa lungene ezitenteni, abetu abanye base bevuswa lulo
ngezikali. Kulondawo kuti kusale amadoda amatandatu, ingxwelera
zalishumi elinesine, ekungabonakaliyo ukuba ezinye ziya kupumelela. Aba
funyenweyo befile base lutshabeni babe babini, esingaziyo nokuba bangaba
babatwalilena abanye. Emkondweni wabo ibingentwigazi, kwada kwabonakala
namazinyo nobucopo kwenye indawo. Umkosi obuze kungenela lekampu bati
ubukwikulu elinamanci amabini, abanye bati ebefezekile amakulu amabini.
Ukuba inkosana ebipete umkosi wetu ibingenguye umfo okalipileyo
ngekungeko namnye osindileyo. Oke waya apo bekulwelwa kona akangerani
nto.

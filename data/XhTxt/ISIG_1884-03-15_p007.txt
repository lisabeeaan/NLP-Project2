kileyo kanye. Ngokuba nina balapa e Herschel ndivile ngabahlobo bam
ukuba nanitenjiswe ngokuba niyakubuyiselwa inkomo zenu ezonakeleyo
ngemfazwe ka Letsea. Enye into ndivile ukuba natenjiswa kwakudala ukuba
imali yerafu ebiyi £1 endlwini inye awaete u Mntan’ Omhle mcediseni
ngexesha lemfazwe ka Ngcayecibi nisarafa ngayo njalo njalo. Ke mna xa
ningati nindinyule ndibe lilungu lenu e Palamente ndoyihlisa irafu ibe
li 10s njengokuba yayinjalo, kanjalo mna ndomana ndinihambela futi. Into
ebingenziwa ngulo ebenimele, mna ndonilungisela into ezininzi kuba
ndisisityebi kanye. Kulapo zati izidenge zakowetu, nguyena unguye lo.
Amva kaloku esiti uzakuhlisa nerafu. Ati amadoda ambalwa amkela
Isigidimi aka- kolwa kwalomhla ati ubetshilo umfo ka Jabavu ukuba
ningakohliswa kukuteta ngezitembiso abaya kunenzela, abo bati banyuleni.

Emva kweveki ezimbini, yagaleleka into ka Sauer mfondini, inco- mbololo
enkulu, ongati wakuyikangela ubone ukuba isukekile ngenxa yengqondo
ezikuyo. Umfo okangela kakuhle amadoda. Savuya kanye tina malungu ake
ngokuxelelwa ngu Mhleli wepepa letu. Wati niyandazi kakuhle kuba ndikula
nani apa, nakuba sendivile ukuba intlanganiso sezike zako kabini nokuba
kukatatu, ndiva nokuba u Mr. Tamplin, undigxeke kakulu ngokuba
ndingawafezi amadinga amnandi. Ndingumhlobo wenu omkulu, ngokuba e
Palamente zininzi izinto engeba niyalila ngazo ukuba bekungendim owalayo
nabanye belicala lam. Watsho wateta ngemipu, nangotshiso Iwenkomo
kwindawo ngendawo. Wati okunye lendoda anazi nokuba ikulupina ulaulo,
luko oluka Scanlen noluka Sprigg—ndiyazi mna ukuba ngum Sprigg wenene.
Mandiyisonge inteto yam ngokuti umfo ka Sauer wafumana ivoti ezininzi
kuno Tamplin. Oku kuhle ngesizatu se Editor yetu, ngokusazisa
kwangapambili kungekehli oku. Akwaba mfo ka Jabavu ungedinwe ukulwela
uhlanga Iwakowenu ngokulwazisa imfanelo zalo. Sendenze inteto ende
kakade sendoti noko ndisatanda ndiyeke kuba hleze ndigqibe ituba
labanye, kanti Isigidimi asivumi ukuba umntu antyuntye. Owako wenene,

  JOEL M. GUNDWANA.

Herschel, 1 March, 1884.

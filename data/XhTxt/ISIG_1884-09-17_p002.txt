kunene obala ezigubungele ngegama elingu “Tand’ Uhlanga.” Akuko manqaku
singa okwanamhla singawenza ngeli bali liyole kunene, kuba um’balisi

I BLUE-BOOK YABANTSUNDU.

No. 2.
ABASEZIDOLOPINI.

SINOSIZI ukuba abab'aleli be Blue Book yabantsundu bengasiniki kukanya
kucacileyo ngentlalo yabantu abase zidolopini. Ngu Mr. ISAAC WAUCHOPE
yedwa ongati uke wayicinga nokuyicinga lontlalo. Siyam’bulela ke ngoko.
Usincedile, wasinika ituba lokwenza amazwi anamhlanje ngolilobo esikolwa
ukuba aya kuvakala.

Abantu bedolopu bamaqela matatu. Bako abasebe ngabemi bakona; bako
abayela ixesha elifutshane, abaye kufuna imali yokuhlaula irafu,
neyokutenga inkomo zokulobola, nengubo zokutshata, nemisesane yokungeja;
bako esingati liqela lemidungela, amavukaba, into ezifumana zihamba
zilala kungeko ni ezijonge yona. Kunjalo kuzo zonke idolopu. Abasuka
kula mazwe angapandle bakolisa ukundululwa zikweliti ezitile abafuna
ukufeza zona, baze bakuyifumana imali yazo babuye bagoduke. Kekaloku
kuko inxenye eti yakuva ozijinjibiya ise ingena kulempi yamavukaba, ibe
ngamatshafutshafu, amakambi ayakuti nokuba ade agoduka abuye
engasenamongo, efunxwe yi kango nokuncola. Baninzi abantwana betu nobawo
betu abapelele ebutshiveleni bebeke bengabantu ngapambili. Ewe okunene
abam’balwa bemka bengasacingi kubuya kakade; kunjalo ngokukodwa
kwezinkabi zimakosikazi anempondo, eziti zicela incwadi nje zingati ziya
kufuna imali yokurafa kanti zenza iqinga lokuke zipupumise. Ati lomanene
akufika kupolile apo e Bayi angabi sacinga kuza kufumana izilonda
ezitsha, asuke seledlulela e Bobofolo! Noko kunjalo inkoliso yona imka
ngokuya kufuna imali yesixakeko esitile, ize ukuhiliza ikucinge yakufika
kona. Kungoko kusekulixala elikulu kuti bazali xa umntana aza kuya
edolopini. Eloxala lingokukodwa kumntu oyintombazana. Uti emka sibe
sesilandelisa amazwi okumyaleza. Nokude sibavumele bemke kuti kuxakeka.
Ngokuba akuko mntana wangapandle oka abonwe ezidolopini. Soyika ukuba
bengabuyi, bati ababuyayo bafike betshe imilomo yazinto ezibomvu;
sikohlane nabo ngendlela zonke. Wena m‘fo wase Bayi wawukala ngokuti
abantwana betu bayakalazelwa kutiwa abahlali, maze wazi ukuba babangelwa
siti bazali, nati lusapo Iwabo. Ungabaqubela na Belungu bako isizatu
esinaso. Soyikiswa kukuba bebaninzi abantwana betu abati bakuhlala futi
edolopini kanti baya kubuya bengenamali, bengenaso nesimilo. Kuko
idolopu ezisuke zapumelela, ati umntana akusinga kona angabi sahlangana
nabani. Intloko yazo yi Queenstown ne Monti, ezilandelayo li Rini ne
Qonce.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA SEPTEMBER 17, 1884

Nge Bayi kuko indawo esesineminyaka etile siyibona, eyokuba kwezi zigama
zikoma Xesi nama Tyume, aliqela amadodana aya kufuna umsebenzi e Bayi,
emka apa engamaqaba, mhlaumbi engamanxiba ngubo nje kodwa, aze abuye
ekoliwe, ezolile; amanye ese elinganisa nokulesa isipeli. Siya kubulela
wena Bayi ngalo ndawo, nakuba nawe uke wasililisa.

Esinye isizatu esibangela ukuba kungahlalwa kakulu ezidolopini sikwicala
la Belungu. Bati maxa wambi bapelelwe yimisebenzi, aze umntu ose nemo
angabi nako ukunyamezela iveki yonke efumana ehamba pakati komzi, esidla
itikana abese eyiqwebile. Into epilileyo yile yayilingwa ngu Mr.
DORRINGTON e Monti eyokuba kubeko umntu wokubafunela umsebenzi, baze
abantu balale emakaya enkosi zabo. Silila nangokuti abantwana
balahlekiswa kukuba emva komsebenzi kungeko bani uteni nabo; kanti
kakade ixa lokonakala komntu lixa langokuhlwa. Umzamo ka Mr. DORRINGTON
wawunceda abantsundu nabamhlope. Isizatu sokungaxaswa kwawo siti
bantsundu kukuba tina singabantu abati xa into sebeyindwebele singabi
savuma ukusondela kuyo nokuba ise iluhlobo lumbi.

Kweli qela lise zidolopini u Mr. WAUCHOPE uteta nangabanamashishini,
abacweli, abakandi. Wenza amazwi abafanele ukuwetyisa kakuhle bonke
abanamashishini, bakangele inyaniso yawo, Uti, “Inkoliso yabo ayifumani
“ zindawo zibafaneleyo, kunge ngenxa yebala labo, njengoko baba abanye
kunjalo kanti bayaposisa. Oku kusile- “ lelwa kubangelwa kwindawo
yokuqala kukushiyiselwa “ kwabantsundu abasebenzi ngabamhlope xa
kugqatswayo “ kuko umsebenzi onxamisekileyo: Kwindawo yesibini “
kubangelwa kukuba abasebenzi abantsundu bengakucokisanga ukufunda
amashishini abo. Bati besafundiswa “babe bona sebegqibile, bapuma
bengalifezanga ixesha “ lokuqeqeshwa kwabo. Umsebenzi wabo kusoloko
unga- “ vumi ukucikizeka. Kuko abashicileli nabenzi bezihlangu “
endibaziyo abangeke bawuswele umsebenzi noko unqabe “ kangaka nje.
Lomadoda ngabantu abanezimilo, into “ leyo efuneka kumntu wonke
ongumsebenzi.” Utsho ke oka WAUCHOPE. Into esinga nati singabandela kuyo
yeyokucokisa umsebenzi asukuba ewenza umntu, neyokugcina isimilo
esibukekayo. Umntu onezonto zombini, abe ekutele, uya kuwufumana
umsebenzi nakwabangamtandiyo.

Masize kugqiba, siyeke singangenanga kwintlalo yabemi bedolopu. Soyixoxa
nangenye imini. Ekuvaleni masiti, singatanda ukuva koka WAUCHOPE ukuba
lamadungudwane ati ngamadungudwane asel’engoncanyelweni sinina nokuba
kungaba kuseko imizamo eyenziwayo yokuwanceda.

Masisule umlomo ngamazwi ka A. WYLDE, Esq., Imantyi yase Bayi etete
amazwi esoze mhlaimbi senze ingxoxo pezu kwawo. Uti, “ Yinto
epaulekileyo ukuba “ ngoku abantsundu ungababona kwindawo zonke bekuzimo
zokutenjwa, ezitoreni, kololiwe, apo kupuma imikombe, bekangele abanye
xa basebenzayo, bene wotshi “ zokugcina amaxesha. Kwimisebenzi enzima
yezandla “ babakupile abamhlope ; kwimisebenzi enjengokumba “ nokutwala
selingabo. Bafumana isheleni ezintatu ngemini.”

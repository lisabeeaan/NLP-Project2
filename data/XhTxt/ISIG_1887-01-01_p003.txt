UKUCOLACOLA.

IZIMANGA ZALEMINTAKA.—Kuko umfo obale ukuti— Kuko ihangukazi yomntu
ontsundu ezele amatole, ate amabini anezandla nengalo ezifane nezomntu
kwade kweza, kafika ezingqwinibeni. Lawo anjalo azelwe efile omabini.
Lonto yenzeke e Free State ekupeleni kuka November.

INTAPANE YAMAWELE.—Amapepa axokozela ngokuti kuko umntu ozele amawele
amane, kanti kwakona omnye uzele amatatu, Kodwa ke kuvakala ukuba
akapilanga onke, kuko aquzukileyo.

  IMPOSISO ESIMANGA.—-Liti ipepa lase Vryburg, kuko indoda ete iye
  kuzingela, yabona into, yasel’ibamba, “ nzwa- nga" ! Kute kwakuti gabu
  umsi yaya kulento, ite yakuti yo! lihashe layo, itsho yesuka nonxenye
  yomhlati. Ute kanti sel’elilibele. Ute akuti tu limi, wasel’ebamba eba
  yinto yimbi.

  ODLIWE YINGWEKAZI.—Ati amapepa kuko umfo odliwe yingwekazi kufupi
  nefama ka Mr. P. Kropf. Lengwekazi ite yakubonwa yalandwa ngu Mr.
  Kropf nabalusi bake. Bate bakuyifumana, omnye wabalusi uyibete
  ngenduku, watsho wabeka pantsi, kodwa ite ukuvuka kwayo, yamtsibela,
  yazenzela ngenzipo nangamaziyo yakubon’ukuba imlalise pantsi. Kute
  kuba kuxakile ukuyidubula, yasel’ ifunzwa nge zinja, zabehle zayisusa
  emntwini apa zayibulala, nakuba nayo yenze intapane kuzo. Imtyobe
  kunene kule ngalo yasekohlo. Kutembeka ukuba wobehle apole. Unyangwa
  ngu mfundisi u Mr. Beste wase Cumakula.

  UKUMKA NOMLAMBO—Ati amapepa kuko umntu omke nomlambo ezibukweni lo
  Mzimvubu. Isidumbu sake sifunyenwe sekuntsuku nesoniwe kunene
  ngomaramnco, ekwaba ngumsebenzi ukuwagxota emntwini.

ODUTYULWEYO.—Ati amapepa kuko umfo odutyulweyo ngakwa Komani ngu Philip
De Vet i Bulu lase Haasfontein Mond. Kutiwa liboniwe ukumdubula kwalo u
Damon (igama lalomfo), labonwa ngumkuluwe wake. Isizatu esibangele oko
kutiwa beku ke kwako isipitipiti ezibokweni ze Bulu ngosuku
olungapambili. Limdubule wafa. Sitetanje limelwe ngamawaka amabini
eponti £2,000.

UMLILO KWA DALIWE.—Kuke kwehla into ebumanga kulantaba ka Dali wo. Kute
ngabusuku butile, kwabonwa ibaswana entabeni, wati olibonileyo,
fan’ukuba libaso lamasela. Unabo kwa oko ukuya kwi Chief Constable, ete
kwakamsinya, yahamba nelinye idindala ukuya kula mlilo, Suko lamlilo
waya ubamba usisitati, ubekisa pambili. Bawulundele, kwada kwaya kwifama
ka Mr. Barlett. Befikile kwesi sitati—“ Yinile!” Kanti yi Mantyi yakwa
Daliwe, inomhlobo wayo bayazingela.

UKUZIBULALA.—Ati amapepa kuko indodana ye Jelimeni ezidubuleyo e Cala,
ngokusuke yaliwe yintombi eb’iyi filisha, ngexa ebekulungiselwa
umtshato. Suke yaqibika intombi. Yasuke yoj’umnga edingeni layo,
suk’umfana wangenwa yimfuxwa, wasel’ezidubula, wafa kwapela. [Lumkani
zintombi, bapel’abantu benkosi, wabetu! ED. Sigidimi.]

AMASELA.—Ati amapepa, amadodana amabini ase Knapp's Hope (e Xesi lika
Gqadushe) apakule ubusi babanye abantu. Lomadodana ngu Benjamin no
Robert, basemanatsini. Kuyiwe ematyaleni, agwetywa, adliwa ishumi le
sheleni inye (10s). [Lumkani bafana, yekani inyosi zabantu kanti nonyaka
inyosi zibonwa nangumntu oliso linye. Imini zentlondi zadlula lumkelani
into enize- nzayo. Banani betu? ED. Sigidimi.]

AMAYEZA ABANTSUNDU.—Ati amapepa kuko igqira le Mfengu elibulele umntu
ngeyeza lalo. Igama lalomfo ubuleweyo ngu Bokolo ob’efudula elipolisa
Engcobo. Uhlobo Iweyeza asezwe Iona luyele ezi nqwebebeni apa. Lomntu ke
siteta nje ufile kwapela. [Batanda kakulu abantu ukuzibulala ngosiyazi
balemihla, abafane bahambe bedwaba, besimba izinto abangazaziyo nabo,
kuba bona into abayinxameleyo yimali kupela. ED. Sigidimi.]

ILIFA LEZINJA.—Kuka umfo obale kwi Pall Mall Gazette ote—Kuko umhlobo
wake obubileyo, washiya amawaka alishumi eponti £10,000 kwi kaya lezinja
elise Battersea.

INGQAKAQA E TALIKA.—Kuko umfo obale ese Sleep-hock esiti—“ Kuko
abantsundu base Tsomo babini, bafike nengqakaqa, embi kunene.” Utsho ke
ati—Lento yokuba bangagcinwa abantu besitili esisukuba sinesifo
esisulelayo esinje nge ngqakaqa, basuke bayekwe, bamane ilizwe belika
amakenke behamba besulela abanye abantu, imbi kanye.

OWEYELE EMNXUNYENI E BULTFONTEIN.— Ati amapepa kute ngamhla utile
kusasetyenzwa kulominxuma ye Dayimani, tu esidunjini somntu ofileyo,
kanti lomntu uzimke eludongeni ebamba ebusuku engaqondi ukuba usondele
kangako.

KUYASIKWANA E DAYIMANI.—Ati amapepa kute ngamhla utile, kwahlanganyelwa
ndodana itile ngamanye, pambi kwendlu ka Mr. Mier wase Beaconsfield,
abeta induma ezimbi, amenza amunxeba nase siswini, ekude kwavela
namatumbu. Siteta nje use Sibedlelweni (Hospital).

ISIKUNI SIBUYE NOMKWEZELI.—Liti ipepa lase Bofolo kuko indodana
ebibanjiwe yafakwa etolongweni inyanga ezintatu. Lendodana ngumntu
obetanda kunene ukubamba eboleka imalana kubantu ngabantu, isiti inkabi
zayo zibanjiwe. Yade yabolekwa i 7s, ngu Mr. Loveless wase Cookhouse,
ngomqopiso wokuba lonzwana iyakumzisela ikwakwini namaranise (ama lowe
asemlungwini) kanti umduna zonke ezonto akanazo. Kanti nenkabi ezo
azibolekela imali zikwa libongo akanazo.

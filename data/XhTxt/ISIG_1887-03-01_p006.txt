Ukuba kuko incwadi esazeke inqikwe ngezandla ezingca- ngcazelayo zi
Titshala yeye ngxelo yo Mvi-Zikula. Eyale kwota ka December 1886,
ishicilelwe, apo u Mr. Howe Ely acangcisa kona ukuma kwe zikula
abezihambele ukufa komnyaka odluleyo. Ezo zikula afikelele kuzo, zibe
mashumi mahlanu anesitandatu (56) kwizitili zase Dikeni, e Nqushwa, e
Mpofu, nase Qonce. Abafundi ababeko eluviweni ngama 2,443, nakuba be
1,260 kupela abapumeleleyo kwi ziqibi ngeziqibi zemfundo, kwasala 1,183
lisezantsi kakulu. Kwinani labapumeleleyo site ukuze sifikelele elo le
Mission Schools apo zipantsi kwabamnyama, saliketa inani lezikula
ezisixenxe zabamhlope ezipumeze 104, ne Seminari zase Lovedale nase

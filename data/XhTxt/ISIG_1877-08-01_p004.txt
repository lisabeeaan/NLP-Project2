ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1877.

Hayi kute kwakusa ngalomini noko izulu lalinga ti libungqungunqungu
kwako itemba lokungati liza’ kusa, lite lifika ixesha lokuwuqala
umsebenzi laselibalele kamandi ilanga—Bangenile abantu, baza abafundisi
bekokelwe ngabaculi bahlanganisana kwendala benza urozo behamba bevuma
iculo lika David lamashumi mabini anane, ukunyukela kulondlu intsha
—Bate bakufika emnyango kwahlatywa eliculo liti- ‘Hambani ba Kristu ’
kuko nohadi, ukunyuka ukuya endaweni ye Choir, kwavakala kuyinto emandi—
Ligqityiwe u-Rev. C. Taberer umfundisi wase St- Matthews uhambise
imitandazo yokuvulwa kwe Ke- rike kwahanjiswa inkonzo ke ; kwashumayela
u. Venerable Archdeacon White wase Rini ekunyusheIwa ngu Rev. A. J.
Newton wase Gwatyu. Kwat¹ ke emva kwake kwesuka u-Rev. W. H. Turpin,
wase Rini waposa amazwana ambalwa. Kwavunywa ne Anthem nge Singesi
ngexesha lenkongozelo. Ibe ipelile ke apo, inkonzo. Kwakumaqaba, kungeko
sihlalo singenamntu, abamhlope nabantsundu.

Kungene intlanganiso yokunikela. Kuqale u-Vener­able Archdeacon Kitton,
wase Qonce, wateta ngokuqalwa kwale Mission kungekabiko makolwa,
ingabafana aba 12 kupela ababeze ngemfundo, ebonisa ukuhambiseka
komsebenzi ka Tixo kulomzi. Wateta namanye amazwi avuselelayo. Abanye
abake besuka baposa amazwi ngu Venerable Archdeacon White, ngu Mhleli we
Watchman, Mrs. Stent oyi Architect ebekangela, ukwaxiwa kwalendlu
nowatumela umfanekiso wayo isayilwa; ngu Mr. Lonsdale i-Mantyi
yalomandla. Masingalibali ukukankanya ngokubulela uncedo alwenzileyo
ngezandla zake ekuyihombiseni lendlu ; ngu Captain Cherry inkosana yase
Qonce. Onke lamadoda atete ngomsebenzi walendlu. Sibulela unyameko Iwawo
ngokuhambela umsebenzi onjengalo ube uko apa. U-Rev. Mnyakama no Rev.
Nyovane batete amazwana ngamanye okukalipisa nokuvusa abantu. Baqalile
ke abar.tu bagalela ngemali, na- ngenkomo, negusha, nenkuku, neranise,
nokudla aba- nye imali bayirole ngoko abanye batembisa, bonelwe lilanga.
Kute kwakusa yaba kwayilonto, kuqalwe ngenkonzo kwalandela intlanganiso.

Ke kwapunywa ixeshana, ukuyiwa esidlweni, kuba bekuxelwe inkabi zenkomo
ezimbini, kwangenwa kwakona yakwa yilonto, bemana berola ozisheleni,
nozi hafgolweni ngapandle kweyokunikela, neyezitembiso, nozisispenise,
nozitiki—kwada kwati ratya. Upele njalo ke lomsebenzi wezontsuku zombini
esitemba ukuba koba mzuzu ukuze siwulibale. Imali eroliweyo ngezontsuku
zimbini iku £480, kudibene imali nempahla yezitembiso. Ityala lisahleli
lingapantsi kuhle ku £500 kuba ixabiso layo li £1520, imi. nikelo evela
kumacala ngamacala ingabuleleka kakulu. Indlu ubuhle ibugqibile,
asingebi nakubucacisa ngamazwi, kodwa nantso imi ilingqina, iyazitetela.

Masingagqibi singabalanga amazwi embulelo kum- fundisi walomzi,
ngenyameko nemizamo yake, kuba uyiscbenze nangezake izandla;
ndingamshiyi u-Mrs. Taberer inkosikazi yake, no Miss Lucas ogcinayo
amantombazana akwa St Matthew, no Mrs Lonsdale inkosikazi ye Mantyi,
ngezihombiso ezikulondlu, ezi- setyenzwe zizandla zabo. Singasuke siti
ukugqiba, wanga angati umfundisi u-Mr. Taberer ayibone imizamo yake
inesipelo, ngokupela kwetyala ukuze abo nokonwaba.

UKUBEKWA KWELITYE LESISEKO
KWA KAMA.

OLWE Sibini evekini ngomhla 3 ka July ube ungumhla omkulu kwa Kama
wokubekwa kwelitye lesi-

seko sendlu yetyalike eya kuba sisikumbuzo senkoi u-Kama. Pambi kokuteta
ngomsebenzi walomini singake sixelele abangaziyo ukuba kwati yakububa
inkosi u-Kama kwako umnqweno wokuba kubeko into eyenziwayo eya kuhlala
ikumbuza igama lake nezenzo zake ezilungileyo. Kwaveliswa iqinga lokuba
kwenziwe ityalike emali iya kurolwa sisizwe, ati amadoda amakulu akupe
20s inye, amadodana arole 10s—kwabake kusitiwa londlu iya kudla £3,000
Isizwe sayi vuma okunene lonto kwaza ngexeshana elifutshane sarola
£1,000 eselise Bankini. Ubukulu balondlu ngapakati njengoko
ilinganiselwe ngako ngu Mr. Bate wase Qonce iya kuba zinyawo 70 ubude,
ububanzi ibe zinyawo ezi 33. Umsebenzi wamatye uyakudla £1,450, owemiti
udle £1,075 oko kukuti iyonke ibe £2,525 zingaqukwa intwana ezinje
ngozifestile.

Zite ke zakuba zifeziwe izinto zokulungiselela kwamiselwa ukuze ilitye
libekwe ngolwe 3 Iuka July. Lingekafiki ixa lokuqala umsebenzi bamane
ukufika bengamaqela abantsundu. Pambi kwase mini enkulu kungenwe
etyalikeni ebihle yazaliswa ngamakristu antsundu abe kumakulu amane
namahlanu. Kusukwe apo kwayiwa kwindawo ebinelitye. Kute kuba bekuko
nabamhlope iculo laleswa nge Singesi. U- Rev. Ben. Impey utyile uhlobo
ekuya kuhanjiswa ngalo xa kuza kubekwa ilitye. Into yokulibeka
inikezelwe ku Mrs. Impey (inkosikazi ka Rev. W. Impey Umongameli
wezikolo zama Wesile) nomhlolokazi ka Kama, laza lakuhliswa bavakalisa
ukuba libekiwe. U-Mr. Impey, omkulu upendulele Umfazi wake wati asikuko
nokuba kuya ’mvuyisa ukubako kumsebenzi walomini. Usakukumbula oko
ebepakati kweso sizwe kwiminyaka emashumi mahlanu edlulileyo, ukufika
kuka yise u-Rev. William Shaw owaye ngumfundisi wokuqala wabo, awati no
Kama wagu. qukela pantsi kwake. Ubakumbuze ngamatamsanqa afunyenwe
sesosizwe ngokufika kwelizwi lika Tixo, namalungelo esiwazuze ngokuhlala
sikwicala lika Rulumeni. U-William Shaw Kama oyinkosi ngoku usuke
wapendulela unina, kwabonakala ukuba bayapulapula abantu. U-Mr. Bate
utete ngohlobo eya kuma ngalo indlu, walandelwa ngu R. M. B. Fielding
Esq., Imantyi, no Rev. W. Barton wase Nxukwebe no Mr. Joseph Tele.
U-Rev. Ben. Impey ubike ukuba imali eseliroliwe ikwi £1,100, wacela
ukuba abantu beze kubeka iminikelo yabo elityeni. Kunikelwe imali ebe
£36. Emva koko kucitakelwe ngabantu kubonakala ukuba bonke ba-
tonyalalisiwe ngumsebenzi abebekuwo.— Watchman.

UKWAMKELWA KOMFUNDISI E-RAPA.

Kwi Sigidimi sika April kwakuke kwabaliswa nge- siqitana esikwi Pacific
ekutiwa yi Rapa. Abantu belozwe bati ukuze balive ilizwi lika Tixo
baliselwe kwangamawabo alifumana ebehambele kwezinye indawo. Nangoku
kubonakala ukuba akukabiko mfundisi umhlope uhlala pakati kwabo. Bati
ukuzo bambone abe uhambele, alale usuku lube lunye abuye adlule kanti
mhlaumbi kuya kuda kube yiminyaka engabuyanga aye kona. Noko kunjalo
ilizwi lika Tixo kukona lihambela pambili lixaswe ngamakolwa elolizwe
nakukufunda kwawo Izibalo ezingcwele.

U-Rev. A. T. Saville uti ukuya kwake kokugqibela babesiti abanye
makangayi kuba uya kufika kungaseko magqoboka nabantu bakona
bengasatandi kubona mntu umhlope. Babesitsho kukuba kwakusekuminyaka
isixenxe kungazange kuye kuvela bani. Noko ke wati uya kuya kuzibonela
ngokwake. Uti ke: Sati xa sisondelayo kweso siqiti asabona nto ipilileyo

ngapandle kwebokwe zasendle ezabe zisidla emacaleni enduli. Kute kunini
sabona ipenyane lisiza linamadodana asibuza ukuba singobanina. Ate
akusiva ukuba siteta inteto yawo, akuqonda ukuba singabafundisi avuya
into endingena kuyixela. Sawacela ukuba ahambe pambi kwetu asibonise
indlela. Kungekabi mzuzu asishiya kude ngasemva engxamele ukuya kubikela
abanye abantu ukuba ufikile umfundisi. Site singekaqondi sabona unxweme
seluzela ngabantu abaze kusibulisa abanye besahamba benxiba kubonakala
ukuba ababanga nalo ixesha lokufaka ezona bebenga bangeza benazo.
Kubonakele ukuba abanye balungisa indlu esiya kungeniswa kuyo.

Beze kusibulisa bekokelwe yinkosi yabo u-Parima ilandelwa zinkosana
ezipantsi kwayo, nomvangeli ontsundu u-Malabo namadikoni namalungu
eremente Ndaqonda nam ukuba bandamkela ngentliziyo epeleleyo. Ndakugqiba
ukubabamba ngezandla bonke ndasiwa endlwini eyayicokiswe kakuhle. Yazala
lendlu bonke befuna ukuva ngehambo yam, abanye beteta ngobukulu bam.
Ndada ndabacela ukuba bake bandipe ituba lokuhlangana nomvangeli
namadikoni. Ndibuze kubo ngokuhambiseka kwelizwi Bate, umsebenzi wenkosi
usoloko uqubela pambili uhamba kakuhle. Bonke abantu, ngapandle
kwabafayo bayeza etyalikeni. Abantwana bayeza bonke esikolweni se
Sabata. Into abayisweleyo zimpahla zokufundisa maxa wambi ide Ititshere
xa ifundisa ukubala igalele intlabati ecolekileyo pantsi ize yenze
amagama ngentonga! Erementeni kuke kubeko amapike maxa wambi xa kuko
abakutshiweyo.

Ukutshona kwelanga ibuye yazala indlu kwasekufike nabendawo ezikude
abeze nengubo zabo zokulala ku- bonakala ukuba abacingi kubuyela
emakaya. Ndite kuba ndidiniwe ndacela ukuba bandipe ituba ndilale. Bate
nakanye akuyi kulala namhlanje. Ndite ngokuba konakele ntonina; bate
bona ngokuba sekuminyaka isixenxe singabonani nomfundisi, ke akanakuti
akufika alale ; kananjalo siseza kubuza izinto esibe singaziqondi.
Kuqale kwesuka indoda endala ipete incwadi yezi Balo endiqondileyo nam
ukuba iyafundwa futi. Indibuze indawo ebingaziqondi abangazazanga
nabazititshere yakubuza kubo. Ute ezinye ubeseleneminyaka etile
ezigcinile elinde ukuba kude kufike umfundisi. Emva kwalowo kusuke
ixegokazi lipete eyalo incwadi yezibalo lifuna ukutyilelwa. Lite nalo
selineminyaka emininzi lilinde uncedo. Kude kwasa lulurozo lulunye,
amadoda nabafazi, abakulu nabancinane abafun’ ukutyilelwa. Baqalele
kwakweye G-enesis kwada kwaya kweye Sityilelo, bebuza imibuzo—eminye
ilula eminye inzima.

Ndebe ndipete ingxowa izele zincwadi zezi Balo. Bate bakuyibona
bazitabata zonke kwaoko. Kubuya kwaputunywa emkombeni naleyo yapela
ngexeshana elincinane. Isiko letu kweziziqiti lelokuba bonke bazitengele
ngokwabo incwadi zezibalo bangakangeli kupiwa. Ndite ke ngoko
kwabazitabatileyo mabarole imali yazo. Bonke bayirole ngovuyo, ndabona
iseyikona imali yazo ndingekaqondi. Abangabanga nakuzifumana ndiqonde
ukuba balusizi kakulu, ndasenditembisa ukuba ndiya kutumela ezinye
kwakamsinya.

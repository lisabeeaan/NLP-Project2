NGOM-VULO, OCTOBER 2, 1882.

IHLOMELO KUBU FUNDISI OBUZELEYO.

NGOKWESIGQIBO sentlanganiso yaba fundisi base Inde­pendent eyabingo May
e Bayi u MR. T. S. VAN BOOYEN, ungeniswe ebufundisini obuzeleyo ngoku
bekwa kwezandla, ngo 27 September, e Bedford. Aba fundisi ababahlulelwe
lomsebenzi wokubeka izandla ngo REV. EDW. SOLOMON, REV. DR. STEWART,
REV. JAMES READ Sen., no REV. JAMES VAN BOOYEN, umkuluwe wake; siya
kolwa ukuba bekuko nabanye. U REV. TIMOTHY VAN BOOYEN yindodana entsha
eyigqibe apa e Lovedale imfundo yayo. Upumile ezandleni zaba fundisi
balapa iminyaka ngoku mibini ezeleyo. Intsikelelo yo PEZU-KONKE ngamana
yamgubungela. Uza kupata ibandla elimnyama apo e Nyara, ebeli pantsi ko
MR. SOLOMON ixa eli lonke.

AMA BULU NABA MNYAMA.

INTLANGANISO yama Bulu ebise Cradock pakati ko September ifikile, yabuya
yadlula; nokuba ukufunda ingxelo yehambiso zayo makube yinto ebulalayo,
nehlungisayo kumntu omnyama kungabehle kuqashwe. Ingaba yinto elusizi
kunene kuti ukuti lentlanganiso yaba zalwane betu abateta isi Bulu,
ibimenyelwe ukuza kuyila elona qinga lokumhluta ontsundu
okuncinane—kukuncinane kanye betu—acingelwa ukuba unako; kuba kuyo
bekuko abezinxiba-mxaka nabazi ngqondi abanoku funyanwa apa e Koloni;
umnumzana u HONOURABLE J. H. HOFMEYER, M.L.A., no MR. L. H. GOLDSCMIDT,
M.L.A., bebekuyo, masiti kwa sisaqala asifumani zisihla ezintetweni
zabo. Kanti ke noko kungatiwa isininzi sabo batetayo babezimisele ukuba
beze ko ngqu-pantsi bezicaka zabo, bapange i—masiti into engekoyo, kuba
kakade omnyama uze. Yinto esimangalisayo ukufumana abantu abakwi simo
sefama becita into eninzi yexesha ekuxoxeni ngati. Ukuba tina besikule
ndawo ifama zikuyo, besiya kucingela ukuba omnyama ule ngezantsi
kwetu—esingeze sacita into eninzi yengxawa nohlwaya pezu kwake; besiya
kuyiyeka i arme schepsel—njengokuba esitsho—izidlele into zayo,
siziyilele sizilingele, sizihambisele ezona nto zoba luncedo kuti’ Ehla
Lengcamango ayiwa ngenanga ama Bulu, ngango kuba acingele ukuba yeyona
nto afanele ukuyenza kwisimo sawo esipezulu. Ehlele kwi tafa lokuba abe
ecazu-cazulula unwele nomnyama, ngendawo yokuba le tiki mayifunyanwe
ngubanina, ngubanina omakangayi fumani. Ati omnyama

upile ngawo, malungana nokwabiwa kwe mali zokuhlaulela izikolo, esitsho
elibele ukuba omnyama naye uyayirola irafu, ze agqitise xa kukangelwe
oko anako, noko banako aba mhlope. Enye imposiso ate eyela kuyo, engenzi
ngabom, yeyoku cingela ukuba umntu omnyama uyifumana yedwa imali
ebalulwa pantsi kwezikolo ekutiwa zezaba fundisi, (Mission Schools)
elibala ukuba into eninzi kuzo yeyentsapo emhlope. Utyapile ukuba u MR.
HOFMEYER, ayalate le mposiso kunye nezinye engebe zinge nziwanga kuba
beku ngeko mbangi yokuba zide zenziwe.

Asina xesha nandawo kule nyanga lokuba siyicikide ingxoxo; oko
singakwenza mhlaimbi ngelinye elizayo. Ngako konke ukusulungeka siti
besingazi ukuba besiya kude sipikise inteto ezinje zipuma
entlanganisweni yama Bulu, eqalwe ngoku lixesha elingu mnyaka onento nga
pezulu. Waye velana kangaka umbali wale nteto nezonto ekwakusitiwa
yondele kuzo ngelo xesha, ngangokuba nge linye ixesha kwaba cebeshu
ukuba abalele e Free State kuba seki bentlanganiso, enqwenela ukwazi
ukuba omnyama unokungena na. Ngelo xesha kwaku sitiwa iyilelwa ukuba
yenze abantu beli lizwe “imbumba yama nyama;” imanye bonke abanqwenela
lento yenzekile, abaya kusebenza ukuba yenzeke, embumbeni enye. Asi
sanga ke ngoko xa kute sizicingela ngolu hlobo izinto ekondelwe kuzo
yintlanganiso yama Bulu satuswa kukuva inteto zawo e Cradock. Ngo­ku
siqondile ukuba into efunwa ngamanye ama fama liyelenqe loku minxela
tina. Sinosizi ukufumana ukuba kunjalo. Besisiti tina fan’ukuba ngoku
seku cacile kubaninakubani ukuba eyona nto ifanele ukubako kukuvelwana
kubekwane, kakulu ezintweni zokwahluka kwe ntlanga nenteto. Umnqweno
wetu wokuba sanga isimo sefama singamnandi kuzo awugqitwe nangokabani;
singali takazela nge ntliziyo yetu yonke, lonke inyatelo elisingele
ekusondezeni imfundo emigubasini yezindlu zefama; kodwa sicinga ukuba
into enqwenelwa ngabanye bama Afrikander, yokuba kujikwe imali enceda
imfundo yaba mnyama, iye kufundisa usapo lwama Bulu, ayibu yata bunga
pantsi kobo bokuba ati umntu, ngakuba inja yake ilambile, anqumle umsila
wayo ayipe wona.

UKU QWETYWA KWE MPILO.

ISAZISO sika MR. LEWIS L. MICHELL, inene elongamele imicimbi yoku gcinwa
kwe mali, libandla abati ngoku soma yi Standard Bank of British South
Africa, Limited, eso funyanwa kwi bala lezaziso kwipepa letu lanamhla,
siyazi tetela ngo kwaso. Kolo memezo, umncedisi-mpati womsebenzi woku
boleka noku gcina imali, uti ibandla ahambisa umsebenzi walo e Bayi
ligqibe kwelokuti malike lenze amavukela-ntloko, limise i Banki kula
mimandla ingapaya kweli letu, eyaziwa ngoku miwa kakulu ngabe bala letu,
elama Mfengu nama Tato—e Gcuwa nase Mtata. Kunjenjenje ke siya temba
ukuba akusa kubako bani uya kunyusa amagxa, emkise nomoya, abe esiti,
“Into leyo yeyama Ngesi; iziselwe wona apa mna anditeni “nayo. Ingaya
kwa Notanda.” Kwi saziso sake u MR. MICHELL angaba akatanga wamalata
omnyama, wati nditsho kuwe nantsi, kodwa kwa lento yoku tumela isaziso
ku Mcokeli weli pepa lendaba, kunye no myalezo wokuba masi guqulelwe esi
Xoseni sifakwe esi Gidimini, kunye nengxelo yenkolo yebandla apatele
lona edubule ekutyalweni kwe Banki, zonke ezi zinto zalata ekubeni
kutetwa kuwe Mfengu; kutetwa kuwe Mgcaleka; kutetwa nawe Mtembu. Kutiwa,
Gcina ukozo

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 1, 1883.

ISAZISO ESIKULU!

“ISIGIDIMI SAMA-XOSA”

Ekuzameni ukututuzela isikalo esikulu, sokuba “ Isigidimi” sishicilelwe
futi kunoko kunjaio okwangoku, kuyaziswa ukuba emva kwenyanga engu June,
olingwa amavukela-ntloko okusikupa kabini ngenyanga-

Sopuma ekuqaleni kwenyanga, sipume pakati kwayo mayela komihla ye 14,
15, 16 kwakuyo.

Ekutabateni elinyatelo akungakuba abashicileli “ be-Sigidimi ” benziwa
kukuba ipepa lenyanga lite lasikeleleka. Hayi. Kumapepa ama Ngesi
ukukupa ipepa lendaba futi-futj kubangwa kukuti libe ipepa elo
liyaxaswa, linembuyiselo engasakuti umqubi walo imposele ematyaleni.
Okukwenjenje asikutazwa zezonto. Kukuzama ukufuna eyona nto yode
imkolisile umntu omnyama. Makwazeke ku- ye wonke ke ukuba kwenziwa
ilinga nje kodwa, ekoti ukuba alipumelelanga sekuncanywa ke nguye wonke
ukuba ixesha asikabi lilo lokumisela umntu omnyama ipepa.

Emva kokuqwalasela kwindleko zeli linga kufunyenwe ukuba imali ekungaqa-
lwa ngayo ukuze kungabiko lahleko macala onke lishumi lesheleni (10s.)
ngonya- ka ihlaulwa kwasekuqaleni konyaka, xa litunyelwa nge posi, ize
libe zisheleni zositoba (9s.) ngapandle kwayo. Kukululekile nakubani
otandayo, ukuba ahlaule isiqingata selixabiso ehlaulela inyanga
ezintandatu— ukwenza umtwalo lula.

Njengokuba okukushicilela kabini ngenyanga, koqala umnyaka
seluqingatisile, imali yalo yoba zisheleni zosixenxe (7s.) ukusa ku
December.

N. B—Abebesebehlaule 4s.behlau- lela lonyaka ukusa ku December banga
sebe tumela 3s. ukufeza.

Imigudu emikulu iyakwenziwa ukubanga ukuba “ Isigidimi ” sibe lipepa
elimnandi elifundekayo, elifundisayo, ngendlela zonke.

Wonke onomnqweno wokunga ulilanga lunganyuka uyakunjuzwa ukuba kuyakuba
ngokushumayela i-gospeli ye-“ Sigidimi” nge- ngexesha nangapandle
kwexesha esoti sifumane abaxasi sime, side sibe lipepa lemihla ngemihla.

St. Matthew’s,

16 May, 1883.

UMNINI wesi saziso, uyazisa kuwo wonke umtinjana
ukuba ufuna intombi yokuzeka.

  Mayibe yesukileyo egadeni, ibe lusi, ebudala bupakati
  kweminyaka eli 17 na 21 ; ibe nokwazi okute-tyi ezincwadini
  kwane ngqondo yokupata indlu (domestic work).

  Umazisi udiniwe kukudla umngqusho, nezonka ezinga-
  nyukanga. Uyindodana etetyi, ekwi minyaka e 25 ngo
  budala. Ngumfo onomkita, ote jize ngendevu pezu
  komlomo (moustache). Une mpahlana ekwi xabiso le
  £135 : kodwa usikelelwe ngomzimba nengalo ezomeleleyo,
  kunye nengqondo yokusebenzela 'lontombi ingatandayo
  ukuya kwabelana naye ububi nobumnandi bobu bom.

  Abapendula esi saziso banga balela, bafake pakati imifa-
  nekiso yabo, eya kubuya bayibuyiselwe ku

Port Elizabeth.

  N.B. Abafutshane, ipony, zingazikatazi ngokubala.

TSHEHE!!!—BONISANI.

NGO-APRIL IO ugqitileyo ngecawe yokuqala, ndilahle
kelwe zinkomo ezintandatu. Amabala—enye yinka-

  tyana emnyamana tsu, inesilonda ngasekunene emhlubulwe-
  ni, upawu itiwe cipu ecaleni ngokucandwa, ngenye inteto ku-
  tiwa yisineyityi, yashunqulwa enye indlebe, ubudala yiminya-
  ka emine. Enye litole elimnyama eli kuminyaka emitatu,
  likwenziwe isineyityi nalo, nenkonjane kwenye indlebe,
  linopondo olusinqindi. Enye litokazi likwa yiminyaka
  emitatu, upawu kwa isineyityi nenkonjane kwenye indlebe.
  Enye yi jonge oss eminyaka mitatu, erwanqa mnyama,
  inekolo enkulu, upawu yinkonjane kwindlebe yenxele,
  yacandwa kwindlebe yenene. Enye litole eliwaba eliyi
  nkabi, liminyaka mitatu, limnyama, upawu yi half mantyi
  ngapambili kwindlebe yenene, nenkonjane kweye nxele,
  libulunga busonteneyo betshoba. Enye litole eliyinkunzi,
  liyiminyaka emitatu, limnyama nalo, upawu yinkonjane
  kwenye indlebe, kwenye yi half mantyi.

  Nditike ndihlabele lowo mkosi kukangelwe. Umntu
  ozizuzileyo mhlaumbi ondazisileyo ngazo ndomnika iponti
  ezintatu £3. Ndingowako

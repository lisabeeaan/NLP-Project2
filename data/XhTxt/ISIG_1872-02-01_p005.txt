Isigidimi Sama-Xosa.

Vol. II. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, FEBRUARY 1, 1872. No. 17.

Isigidimi Samaxosa

U-REV. JAMES LAING.

U-REV. JAMES LAING umfundisi wecala le Free Church, wase Mkubiso, ubube
nge Cawa umhla wamashumi omabini anesibozo ka January. U-Mkubiso
ubufudula udume ngokuba yindawo apo kwa ncwa- tywa kona U-Ngqika; namhla
uyakuduma ngokuba yindawo apo kuncwatywe kona onga pezu ko
Ngqika—engenkosi pofu, emkulu kodwa ngenxa yomsebenzi alike wasebenza
wona, ezama ukwazisa kwintlanga zakwa Xosa igama lalowo, ugama
liyakuhlala lihleli, ugama lingapezu kwawo onke amagama.

Ziyapela izicaka ze Nkosi,ziyadlula nga- zinye, ziyemka ngokumka.
Lelesitatu incwaba eli namhla lomfundisi wakwa Xosa ngezinyanga
zimbalwa. Safika isigitshimi se Nkosi E-Tutura sabluta owecala le United
Presbyterian. Akuhlutwa owakona umfundisi sati ukulila kwetu, Au kumke
obe ngumlwelwe kade! Kute sisatsho sapinda isigitshimi, sagaleleka
E-Qonce sagodusa ubawo wetu wecala le London. Site ukulila kwetu au
kugoduswa owokugoduswa kuba lomfundisi eba- lupele. Kute sisatsho
sapinda isigitshimi sahluta namhla obe ngemlwelwe, ongalu- pele, loke
ngu Mr Laing we Free Church. Ngubanina namhla ongacingiyo? Zinto
zokucingisa ezi, yimisebenzi yokutusa le. Taruni Mabandla akowetu
cingani; kuko nto itetwayo; kuko ndawo iyifunayo Inkosi ukuba siyiqonde.

U-Mr Laing wazalwa E-Scotland pe- sheya, ute esengumntwana wahlala
ebali- selwa indaba zelilizwe lakwa Xosa, wada wangenwa yintliziyo
yokuba azinikele ukufunda ubufundisi. Kute xa agqibeli- sayo ukufunda
wava kusitiwa ibandla lamakolwa ase Glasgow lifuna umfundisi ukuya ku
Maxosa. Wasele zinikela ngo-

kuti “Nanku ndiko nditumeni.” Wamkelwa ke, watunywa lelo bandla, nge
1830 esengumfana ukuba eze neno.

Wati ukufika kwake waza kugaleleka E-Mkubiso ku Mangqika, wasebenza kona
no Mr. McDiarmid kwada kwa nge 1843. Ngalonyaka kutiwe makakululane no
Mr. Bennie, aye E-Dikeni, wahlala kona iminyaka eshumi linye linamibini;
wabuyela kwa se Mkubiso nge 1855. E- mveni kwe minyaka emihlanu uye
watye- lela E-Scotland inyanga ezishumi nye evuselela abantu bakowabo,
ukuba bawu- kutalele ngakumbi umsebenzi wokuquba ilizwi lika Tixo
kwelilizwe. Ebuyile uqale waka ityalike enkulu entsha, yangum- sebenzi
onzima lowo wokuyibutela imali nokuyikangela ukwakiwa kwayo. Kodwa
Inkosi yamgcina wada wonke lomsebenzi, wawucokisa wawufeza, yavulwa nge
1862 lacinywa lonke ityala layo.

Akabuyanga asuke E-Mkubiso, apelele kona amandla ake, ude waba yingwevu
kona, ububele kona—uncwatywe kona. Ugqibe iminyaka emashumi mane
anamibini efundisa usapo lwakwa Xosa ilizwi lika Tixo. Ubube akufika
kumnyaka wamashumi omatandatu anesibozo.

Ute nge Cawa umhla wamashumi oma- bini ananye ka January washumayela
kabini E-Tyalikeni yake epilile, engenayo nento ayibikayo. Kute ngo
Mvulo wava- kala ebika intloko, wamana esiti ungenwe yingqele noko ke
ebehambahamba ezipu- lapula indawo abebeza ngazo kuye abantu bake.
Ngolwe Sibini akavukanga ; waqala kaloku wabika ihlaba: wayakubizwa U-
Dr. Stewart ngolwe Sitatu, owati akufika wamana empa amayeza. Kodwa
ubuye wagoduka ngolwe Sine esiti U-Mr. Laing unokupilana, akwabiko nto
itusa bani ke kude kwangolwe Sihlanu ngokuhlwa kwabonakala ukuba
umkuhlane uyenyuka; kwabuya kwaya kubjzwa U-Dr. Stewart ote weza no Dr.
Palmer ebusuku ngol­we Sihlanu. Ate ke amagqira lawo abehle abona ukuba
akuko temba, waqala kaloku

U-Mr. Laing ukubuda, kwanjalo kwada kwahlwa ngo Mgqibelo, akasabi
nakuteta kaloku, kwati ke nge Cawa pambi kwesi- tonga wabuba.

Uncwatywe ngolwe Sibini ukumka kwe- langa, ngomhla wamashumi omatatu ka
January, bebeko abafundisi, nenkosi ezizi- mantyi, nabamhlope,
nabamnyama abebe hlanganisene ngosizi olukulu ukubeka ilitye encwabeni
lalondoda ibililungisa Inkonzo bezimbini, eya Mangesi ibenziwe pambi
kwendlu, eya bamnyama encwabeni.

Kunjalo ke mabandla akowetu, akala- langa umfundisi, utiwe xwi kukufa,
qwaka ngexeshana akagulanga, awubikwanga nalomkuhlane, kuba usuke wapala
ukuse- benza kwawo sebetuswa abantu kubikwa kokububa kwake. Situsiwe;
site kokupina oku, akuyizolo na sibe sise Mkubiso nge Jubili sihlangene
sivuya, epilile umfun­disi omelele, engenandawo ayibikayo. Hayi
ukusebenza kwe Nkosi, ngongacebisani nabani, ngondlela zinga fani
nandlela zomntu. Sibesiyakuti tina ngobudenge betu ubenantonina U-Mr.
Laing lento simhlutwayo — unantonina sike simbolekwe, kunantonina
kwakufa izinto ezingenamsebenzi emhlabeni apa, ake ayekwe umntu olunge
kangaka, okiitele kangaka, osebenza kangaka. Nantsi impendulo yomnini
nto zonke “ Ingcinga zenu asingcinga zam!” Hayi ke asikurora
asikukalaza, sosuka silinge ukuti makubenjalo Nkosi, “mayenziwe inta-
ndo yako.”

Asinaxesha lokwenza udaba lwesisicaka se Nkosi, kuba siqutyulwe yinto
sibe sin- gayicingi, amazwi ke okugqibela oba ma- futshane.

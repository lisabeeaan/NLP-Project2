ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 1, 1887.

Yahamba ibalisela usapo lembali entlango, apulapule umfana ade ati ngxa
ulovu xa amadoda amakulu akwa Sirayeli acengceleza eli bali, nezinye
iziganeko zamhla kwapunywa e Jiputa. “ Ngubanina onje ngawe Yehova
pakati ko Tixo, ngubanina onje ngawe obungcwele bungcwele, owoyikekayo
ngodumo, owenza imimangaliso.” Watsho u Sirayeli xa akangele izidumbu
zibetana pezu kwamanzi. “ Akwaba besifele e Jiputa ngesandla sika
Yehova, oko besihlala ecaleni kwembiza zenyama, sisidla izonka sihluta.”
Yini kaloku le? Zana izipango ze Mana ne Zagwityi, xa ipakati inyanga
yesibini bepumile e Jiputa. E Refidime— Yo! yo!! safa linxano. “ Yinina
ukufumane usikupe e Jiputa uze kusibulala kunye nosapo Iwetu nemfuyo
yetu ngenxano? Apuma eweni namhla amanzi. Intonga abekade esimelela
ngayo u Mosesi kwakule ntaba ye Horeb namhla utulula ngayo amanzi eweni.

UKUVULWA KWETYALIKE YASE SMITHVALE.

MHLELI wendaba zamanene, kaundifakele lamazwana ambalwa, ngokuvulwa
kwendlu ye Cawa yalapa e Qitsi. Yakiwe ngumfundisi u Rev. D. D. Young
ngomnyaka we 1884, ayavulwa ngenxa yendlala, nangenxa yengqakaqa,
namhlanje ke uyivulile umfo ka Young, ofike wenza into enkulu, wacela
inkosi, u Matanzima ukuba aye kuvula indlu ye Cawa. Wafika ngom-Vulo,
kwasa ngolwe Sibini ivulwa. Wanikwa isitshixo sokuba ayivule, wasitata
wasifaka elucangweni, ngexesha lokungenwa, efalile ama-Hala,
wati—Ndiyayivula lendlu, ka Tixo wam, ukuze nditandazele kona, nabantu
bam, naba- ntwana babo, watsho wangena, kwangenwa ke. Wahanjiswa
umsebenzi, kwaculwa, kwaleseshwa kwe Yokumkani, isahluko sesibozo.

Washumayela shushu umfo ka Young, ngokukodwa ke awona mazwi esiseko
Nanga—Ubonise ngentlanga ezisuke ziti lakufika kuzo ilizwi lika Tixo
zilicase, zingalivumi lakufika uyazicita u Tixo ezozizwe. Wati—Nati ke
ukuba siyalicasa asilamkeli uyakusicita, watsho wati zinyuleleni eyona
ndlela nitanda yona iko eyokupila, iko eyokufa. Ababeko abamhlope
ngumgcini sihlalo u Rev. D.D. Young, Dr. Weir, Mrs. Young, Messrs. A. G.
Aldum, H. Birch, I. Birch, A. D. Bower, I. Bremer, Arthur Smith, Mrs.
Girdwood, Mrs. Conacher, Mrs. Treasurer, Mrs. Bowen, Mrs. Bremer, Mrs.
Aldum. Ndim umbali,

  P. KAKAZA.

Qitsi of Smithvale

August 30th 1887.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, FEBRUARY 1, 1875

ABABETWE NGUMBANE—Ngomhla 23 ka Janu­ary intombi ka Mhlambiso ibetwe
ngumbane kwa Matole. Kute kanjalo Emampondweni umfundisi abati ngu Mr.
Clement ehamba namadodana amabini antsundu, enye yawo iyeyawufunda apa
E- Lovedale, babetwa ngumbane bawa bonke noko babuya bavuka. Bate nyi
umganyuna wabuya wafika umbane, bawa kwakona kanti akabi savuka
umfundisi.

E-TRANSVAAL umfo abati ngu Marthinus Gouws ufakwe etolongweni. Ityala
lake kutiwa ubeceba ukubulala U-Burgers Umongameli welo- lizwe. Kutiwa
waya ku Mankoroane inkosana yabantsundu wacela ukuba kumenywe U-Burgers
lowo kungati kuza kutetwa naye ngomhlaba kanti uza kubulawa. Kodwa
eloqinga lake livakele lingekenziwa, ngoku nguye osendleleni yokubu-
lawa ukuba butyilekile ubunyaniso balonto.

U-SOBEKWA—Abaleseshi betu bokumbula ukuba kwinyanga ezidluleyo
sasishicilele incwadi evela ku Mr. Girdwood eteta ngokufa kuka Sobekwa,
esiti ucinga ukuba udlisiwe. Zakuyi bona loncwadi izipata mandla
zizamele ukuyi pengulula ubunyaniso bayo. Nangokuke zisawuquba
lomsebenzi. Asikaqondi kakuhle ukuba yopelela pina lonto. Kangangoko
kusavakeleyo ungati kwakungeko sizatu sokuba kutiwe udliswe ngabom.

EZANGASE ASHANTEE—U-Govemor Straban umpati wamazwe akwi Cape Coast
Castle ubene- ntlanganiso yenkosana zakona, ate kuyo wazazisa ukuba
utunywe yi Nkosazana ukuze azazise uku- ba ngoku zipantsi kwayo nje,
maziyeke ukutenga nokutengisa amakoboka. Zide zakuvuma oko okwenene,
noko engazi kukululwa kwangoku asele sebukobokeni. Kutiwe kodwa loti
elike lapatwa kakubi yinkosi yalo libe selikululekile.

IMPAHLA ELAHLEKILEYO—E-Lustu, isandla se government sibe sisand’ ukunika
umteto kwi- nkosana, ukuba zitsho ebantwini bazikupe zonke inkomo
abuzifumene ngokusuke zifike emizini yabo, ezibe zilablekile mhlaumbi
zibiwe, kwatiwa ote wayifihla enayo uya kohlwaywa kanzima. Bate abantu
bawupulapula okwenene lomteto zati inkomo ezikutshiweyo zazalisa
komkulu; baza abebelahlekelwe babuya bayifumana impahla yabo.

KUBABALELANI—Sinosizi ukuba incwadi ka Jonas Hoha singase nakuyi faka
kwesi sanamhla Isigidimi. Iteta ngokuvumelana kwabantsundu E-Bayi nase
Uitenhage, ukuba “ bahlangane ngemiteto, batsale ngamqokozo mnye babe
nolaulo olunye. ” Kwintlanganiso ekuvunyelenwe ngalo- nto kuxoxwe
ikakulu ngesimo abakuso abantsu- ndu ngoku. Singati noko yona ibe
ipakati kwe- ndawo ezimbini ibe isisiqalelo sale kade icelwa ukuba ibe
se Sheshegu abanye beyidukisile.

IBALA ELIMNYAMA.—Umfo otile omhlope uti ukuteta ngokutiywa kwebala
elimnyama ngaba- ntu abatile. “ Ndingalinganisela ngokuti kumzi otile
ati amabulu aka indlu yetyalike akabi nakuyi feza ukuyi hlaulela, ityala
eliseleyo laziponti eziliwaka elinamakulu amahlanu (£1,500). Ate
akunqatyelwa apo angayi fumana kona kwavela umntu omnyama wati uya
kuwaboleka lomali angalindeli nenzala yayo, angakatali nabubude bexesha
lokuyirola kwabo, ukuba baya kumvumela angene etyalikeni kunye nendlu
yake. Ute akakatali nokuba bambeka pambili nokuba kungasemva, nokuba
kuse gumbini nokuba bayamfihla kulondawo, into ayifunayo kupela ukuba
ahlale etyalikeni apulapule ilizwi. Ate amabulu ukupendula, kunokuba
avumele umntu omnyama etyalikeni yawo ungahlala nalomali yake. Elinye
inene elitile lalincokola nebulukazi labuza elinene ukuba umntu omnyama
unokuya na ezulwini, lite elinenekazi bai akanako. Ute omnye ungatinina
ukuba use zulwini waza wabona umntu omnyama engena, lite elinenekazi
ndingase ndipuma ndimka! ”

ABASEBENZI—Ngenxa yokusweleka kwabasebe- nzi apa bekude kwatunyelwa
ukuba baye kufunwa kwele Diamond. Iqela lokuqala elifunyenweyo belifike
kumadoda 120 ukusuka kwawo kona. Umfo opambene nabo bantu xa badlule
I-Beaufort West uti ufike sebe 90, nabo kunzima ukubaquba kobapeteyo.
Isizatu soko ngabezizwe eziblanu, kekaloku bahambe besilwa, abanye
bebesebe dumbe amehlo abanye benxilile, abanye bengavumi kuhamba. Omnye
wabo ute akufumanana nompu wafumana wadubula nje esiqwini noko ate
akacana mntu. Kute kanti aba bavumileyo ukuza yinkunkuma le
ingenamsebenzi nakwelozwe.

Mahlulo ukuba Intlanganiso Yamadoda

Akangela Umahlulo we Victoria East, ngegunya
lesiqendu 38 nesa 39 somteto 9 womnyaka 1858,
ite yamisa ukuba aze wonke umntu arole ipeni
ibenye epontini yemali, kuyo yonke imfuyo yake
engeyiyo ehambayo ekulo Mahlulo, ize ke ibe
yemele umnyaka 1874. Kekaloku lomali kuya
kufuneka ukuba irolwe ibatalwe kwi ofisi yomgci-
ni wemali yalo ntlanganiso apa E-Alice ngomhla
1 ka March, 1875.

W. J. MUGGLETON,
Isekritari Yalontlanganieo.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1870.

UBUGEBENGA.

U-Sanna Peters owaye hlabe elinye ilawukazi ngesitshetshe kwatiwa kuye
njengokuba kumhla azuza ityala ugwetyelwa inyanga ezintatu etolong- weni
asetyenziswe kanzima.

U-Louisa, Um-Xosakazi owaxabela I-Mfengu- kazi ngezembe ngase Fort
England enxamele ukuyibulala kwatiwa knye kuba ebeqaliwe wosele-
gwetyelwa inyanga ezintatu etolongweni kodwa ke alumkele angabuye azuze
ityala elinjalo. Isizatu soku yayili kwele.

EKUBIWA KWENKABI.

U-John Terbane no Sekane, abasebenzi base Philipton banikwa ityala
lokuba bete ngolo 1 ku July E-Skit Fontein ngase Queenstown beba imazi
zenkomo za 2, namatole ama 2, netokazi lenkomo impabla zika Casper la
Roux; beba nemazi zenkomo ezimbini, netokazi, nenkabi zenkomo ezimbini
zika Hendrick la Roux; beba netokazi lenkomo lika Guno indoda esicaka E-
Skit Fontein.

Bagwetyelwa imivumbo ema 25 emnye, nemi- nyaka emibini etolongweni
besebenza kanzima.

INKOHLISO.

Z7- William Thomas Lawson Emett ngapambili ubeyi Mantyi yase Eland’s
Post (oku kukuti Empofu) wati akanatyala kwakutiwa ukohlise I-Ruluneli;
watetelelwa ngu Mr. Barry.

Amaceba ati unetyala kodwa makaze angaba- ndezelwa. Ugwetyelwe unyaka
etolongweni yase Rini.

Ityala lake lalisekubeni ati kwi Ruluneli unemali E-Bankeni kanti
akanayo.

UKUDLWENGULA.

U-Vitshini imfengu yase Kingscote E-Nqushwa (Peddie), wanikwa ityala
lokubamba intombi enxamele ukuyidlwengula; kuba ete ngolwa 29 ku June
wabamba U-Matilda Johanna Foxcroft intombi ka Robert Foxcroft umlimi
wase Salford, wamwisa pantsi wamrwitsha enxamele ukumdlwengula.

Wati yena umbanjwa akanatyala. Waza U- Miss Foxcroft akuba efungisiwe
wakuxela konke okwenzekayo. Kwenzeka oku mayelana nokutshona kwelanga xa
ebevela komnye umlimi, omelene nabo, apo ebehambele kona. Umbanjwa lowo
wamkwaza wati “ Makeme,” wafika wambamba, wamnqula pantsi wambeta
ngentonga, walinga ukumrwitsha, wanxamela ukumpata nje ngoko atanda
ngako. Koko lontombi yalayo kwapela. Wati U-Mr. Forester akuyiva ikala
waya, waza ke lomfo waseleyiyeka. Akufika ekaya U-Miss Foxcroft
wazityela izihlobo zake, kwase kufunwa indlela yokuba abanjwe U-Vitshini
lowo.

U-Mr. Foxcroft no Keyi Danise, itolike yemantyi yase Nqushwa, banika
ubunqina obacacisa into yonke.

Amaceba ati umbanjwa lo unetyala. Wagwutyelwa ukuba azuze imivumbo ema
25, ahlale iminyaka yomitatu etolongweni esebenza kanzima.

UBUGEBENGA.

U-Joyce U-Mxosa ute akanatyala ngokuya wabeta E-Rosendale U-Nxukumeshe
umlimi wase Sheshegu.

Banikwa ubunqina. Ati amaceba unetyala. Wagwetyelwa inyanga zontandatu
aze asebenze kanzima.

UKUBIWA KWEBOKWE.

U- G-eelbooy weli Pezulu Igqukesi (Upper Blink Water), wanikwa ityala
lokuba ebe ibokwe zane zika Antonie Johannes Botha umlimi. Wati yena
umbanjwa akanatyala.

Ekunikeni kwake ubunqina U-Botha wati ulahlekelwe nga 50 ebokwe
nganyanga nye. Kwati kuba ubunqina bunga zalisekanga wanela ukuyalwa
wayekwa.

EKUBIWA KWEGUSHA.

U-Galashe no Aapie banikwa ityala lokuba igusha ezinga 20 zika Henry
MacDonald umlimi ohlala E-Price’s dale ngase Sinqenqeni ngolwe 4 ku
August. Ababanjwa bati abanatyala. Wati U-Mr. William MacDonald
(umtakwabo lowo ubelweyo) nemfengu etile bangama nqina, ababi nako kodwa
ukuti ngalomasela kanye. Ayekwake, anela ukuyalwa ukuze angabuye abe
kona apo.

UKUQEKEZWA KWENDLU NOBUSELA.

U-Hendrick James wanikwa ityala lokuqekeza indlu enxamele ukuba, kuba
ngo 17 ku July wayi qekeza indlu ka Edward Haw E-Rini weba iwatch (oko
kukuti insimbi yamaxesha).

Wagwetyelwa unyaka aze asebenze kanzima. Kodwa ngenxa yesimilo sake
sapambili akabanga sakatswa.

UBUNYANGAZA.

James Hendrick wase Rini wanikwa ityala lokuba wati ngolwe 11 ku August
waqekeza endlwini ka Elizabeth Mary King weba inxowa yemali, inemali
efike kwi £16 15s. 7d.

Wati umbanjwa akanatyala. Akubuzwa ubudala bake wati uli 13 leminyaka.

Umgwebi wabumisa ngento entle ubunqina ebenzela amaceba. Umgwebo
wamaceba waba ngowo kuti unetyala, makaze noko apatwe kuhle ngenxa
yobuncinane.

Umgwebi wavumelana neso sigwebo; kodwa ke wakohlwa ukuba uza kwenza
ntonina ngesela elo. Belingekabi nakubutwala ubunzima betolongo ngenxa
yobuncinane; ke akuko zindlu zokondla nokululeka apa, kuba ebengamsa
kuzo. Into eyaselisenziwa kwaba kukubetwa ngoswazi oluku- lu imivumbo
enga 20 azeke asiwe kunina.

Oku kwaba kupela komsebenzi wamatyala.

UKUBUBA KO MKRISTU

ONTSUNDU E-MKUBISO.

U-Hlangwana, indoda ebise inkulu, ubu- be E-Mkubiso ngolwesine, ngosuku
Iwe 8 ku November we 1870, enalo ukolo netemba elingayo I-Gospile. Ube
elilungu leremente kususela ku September ngonyaka we 1859. Ngalo ufefe
Iuka Tixo abelupiwe, waba nako ukuhamba nje ngokufanele U-Mkristu. Pambi
koku baptizeshwa kwake ngonyaka we 1859 wotuswa kakulu sisimo sake
soboni, esi- nengozi; kodwa wakokelelwa ku Yesu Imvana ka Tixo esusa
izono zelizwe, wafumana uxolo kuye.

Esisifo simtatileyo samqala ngolwama 27 ku October ke ngoko akatatanga
xesha lide ukugula. Ixa lokugula kwake belimnandi kuye, linemfundiso
kwabanye. Kubonakele ke kufanelekile ukuyibala inxenye yamazwi awatetayo
xa seleza kuqauka. Oku sikwenzela ilungelo yaba funda Isigidimi
Samaxosa.

Inxenye yalomazwi yile—“ Bendideliwe ngenxa yobuhlwempu bam elizweni,
kodwa ngoku ndifumene ubutyebi obungasoze

bupele. Ndiyazidlula ngobutyebi zonke izinoni zasemhlabeni ngaye
U-Kristu U- Msindisi wam endimfumeneyo.”

Ute kunyana wake omkulu, “ Ukuba wolandela umzekelo wam utandaze ku
Tixo, wolizuza elolifa lobutyebi ndilifumeneyo, kodwa ukuba akuyi
kutalele inkonzo, loku puluka elo lifa lika Tixo.”

“U-Kristu libaso elinikwe kuti, ke mna ndilifumene.”

Wati kwabanye beremente, “ Lamazwi ndiwatetayo kuni bazalwana alibaso
kulowo unendlebe zokuva. Ndiya kubawo endi msebenzelayo; ndiya kufumana
ukupumla ezulwini.”

“Ukufa koyisiwe. Ndisishiya emhlabeni isono. Ndiya ku Tixo wam. USatana
woyisiwe. Asisakubuya sibonane.”

“ Ilizwi lika Tixo linje ngokudla oku- bekwe endleleni abangati badle
bonke abadlulayo. Inxenye iyadla, kuko inxe- nye engadliyo. Mna ndidlile
ndakolisa andisayi kuza ndilambe.”

“ Iyavuya indoda emkanje ngam nje endlwini yayo. Ndiya zishiya zonke
izinto zelilizwe. Ndandinga zanga nanto em- hlabeni, akuko nto ndimka
nayo. Ndimka ndino Yesu yedwa.”

Iculo xxxvii. 37. “ Gqala ofezekileyo, ukangele olungileyo ngokuba
isipelo sake siluxolo.”

IMINI ZOKUGQIBELA ZIKA

MSHWESHWE INKOSI YA-BASUTU.

LENDODA idumileyo ekade ilaula Elu-Sutu ibube ngalonyaka udluleyo. Ibite
nakuba ibingalicasanga ilizwi, yaba noko ingalamkelanga kwada kwali
xesha lokuza kufa kwayo. Soyinika lembalana ingaye ngamazwi omfundisi
wakona. Uti:—

Ngemini etile akuba umkristu otile ongu Maretta ebemleseshela
U-Mshweshwe kwi Culo Lika David lamashumi omatatu anesibini kulamazwi
ati—“Unetamsanqa umntu ozigqito zixolelweyo” wavakala esiti—Kaunqumame
uwapinde lomazwi. Abuye apindwa ke. Wamangaliswa ngakumbi ngakumbi
akuwacinga lamazwi. Wati kwakutenina lento ndingazanga ndaweva lamazwi
ngapambili? Nanitenina ukuza niwafihle amazwi amnandi kangaka!
“Unetamsanqa umntu ozigqito zixolelweyo.”

Kungekabi mzuzu emva koko U-Mr. Jousse waya kumbona wafika watyila kwese
shumi elinesine ku Johane kulamazwi ati “Endlwini ka Bawo kuko intlalo
ezininzi.” Yavakala selisiti inkosi leyo—

“ Utini! kaubuye uwapinde lomazwi.”

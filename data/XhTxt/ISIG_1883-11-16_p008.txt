  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 16, 1883.

BETA IKWELO MASIZA SILANDELE.

The Merriman Towers, Stutterheim, November 5, 1883.

NKOSI YENDABA.—Makube ukusibonisa kwako inteto ka Mr. Sicina awayenzayo
pambi kwentlanganiso yokunyusa uhlanga, ubusenzela ukuba nabani ake
axovule apendule, endingaziyo nokuba hayi na? Mandibe ngomnye ke u Gqada
Mbekweni ndibe ngoti masipendule.

Ke inteto ka Rev. Masiza ngati kum iyipendule yonke leyo inteto
kulamazwi ati—“ Ukuba umteto ka Rulumente uyalunyanzela uhlanga Iwakuti
kwezinye izinto ezinje ngokuhlutwa kwezixobo, ukukupa imali yemicancato,
imali zendlela, imali zeziza, (House Tax), ezamanzi kwabase
zidolopini.”—Izinto ezo mhla zangeniswa zazibuhlungu, kanti ngoku isizwe
sixolile pantsi kwezi batalana, luzibona kanjalo kuyimfanelo yalo ukuba
luzirole—pofu hayi nganina ukuba le yemfundo into angabi nakusenzela
yona? Makubeko isicelo makowetu sokuba lunyanzelwe uhlanga Iwakuti ukuba
lufundise, lufunde kuba lusebudengeni. Masingamzindli u Rulumente mpi
yakowetu singekateti naye. Danke wena Mr. Masiza ngalomvo. Vela ke ngoku
necebo ukuba masenze ntonina ukuze lento uyitetayo ibe nomqukumbelo.
Ungati xungu ke ngaba nditsho kuba ndoti—

  Xalang’ elintsiba zilusali

  Kuma riwa e Hohita ne Tsojana,

  Esidutyini apa ulalisile Ndokubona ugoduka nge Sabata.

  Sihlangabeze ngoko uyinjengele, Tina bandla apa eligwalileyo; Naku
  site kenu kebevu ngoloyiko, Beta ikwelo ngoko Masiza silandele.

Kumzuzu elipepa lamanene lipupuma ngamacebo alumkileyo amado- dana akuti
afundisiweyo ngento efanele ukwenziwa yokunyusa uhlanga. Kuba ide iti
enye into xa seyilicici ngokuxubushwa Sigidimi ifane ipangalale ipelele
engqitini, ede yati lonto yangenisela ingozi abaninzi ukuba batete
amazwi awacanyangwa sitixo samashwangusha okuba, “ Elipepa lelifileyo.”
Abo batshoyo unge umhlambi wakuti ungebapulapuli kuba kutiwa umlomo
awubekwa siziba. Ubaxolele kuba abayazi into amabenze yona. Kunga
kungafuti kusitiwa,— Hlanganisa intlantsana Sigidimi

Zokukanya kumanene ngamanene,

Pemb’ ibaso lenyaniso ke livute

Zobaleka inunwana zobunyama.

  Yihlanganise le mililo wena Sigidimi,

  Pemba yona bobalek’ onomgogwana

  Nenkolo zobudenge nesihogo,

  Zoya kude elizweni le Niheim.

Ndiya kuvumela nawe Mr. Nella kuba nakuba singateti nje sifana nawe
sonke. Ndingavuya abe u Mr. Kakaza no Mr. Ganya babeke indlebe.
Maligqite ixesha nto zakowetu lengcamango nemibuzo, eli malibe
lelokwenza kade sidwekesha ofoyiya-foco bodwa. Sibizela entlanganisweni
Mr. Masiza ngesaziso kweli pepa, ungadukisi nawe ke Mr. Nomlenzana. Eyo
Mtembu incwadi ipendulwe ngokwaneleyo zi Mpawana ongaba usafuna
ukuyipendula ngamazwi wambi angaba uyayibotola, naye ukuba unendlebe
zokuva kungangabom engevanga. Inga nkosi yam lencwadi yam ingebi
yekukatazileyo ngobude bayo. Nangoku ndiyeka ulwimi lusarauzela. Isicaka
sako esitobekileyo,

M. K. MTAKATI.

ISAZISO SAKWA RULUMENI.—Ese 875, 1883.

  Kwi Ofisi Yompatiswa Mihlaba ne Micimbi,

  EKUBENI enikwe amandla u Rulumeni, sisiqendu sokuqala “ Somteto
  Wemihlaba Engenabaniniyo,” we 3 wo 1879
  ukuba ahambise ngokubona kwake ngokumalungana nemihlaba elahliweyo
  ngoluhlobo lucaziweyo kwesi siqendu

  silandelayo, olu:—

  “ Xa zeke irafu emelwe kuhlaulwa ku Rulumeni we Koloni, ihlaulelwa
  indawo mhlayimbi impahla yakomkulu,
  yakuba ingahlaulwanga kwada kwayiminyaka elishumi, yaba londawo
  mhlayimbi lompahla selilahliwe mhlayimbi ishiyiwe,
  yashiywa ingenamntu, umniniyo mhlayimbi umqeshi, njengokuba kungaba
  njalo mhlayimbi, walondawo mhlayimbi walo-
  mpahla, mhlayimbi ommeleyo ngokomteto engenakufunyanwa, iyakuba
  yimfanelo, ngokomteto, ye Ruluneli ukwazisa
  indawo mhlayimbi impahla esazeke injalo ukuba ayinamniniyo kwi
  Government Gazette (ipepa lakomkulu) nokuba
  kukulipina ipepa angagqiba ekwaziseni kulo, amaxesha angengapantsi
  kwelinye enyanga nganye kwezintatu ezilande-
  lanayo ; kuze kuti ukuba pakati kwezo nyanga zintatu, kususela kusuku
  lokungenisa kwamhlamnene isaziso esisesi-
  balulwe akufiki mninimhlaba mhlayimbi mqeshi, njengokuba kungaba
  njalo, walomhlaba mhlayimbi impahla, akufiki
  nammeli wake ngokomteto, abonise ukuba umhlaba mhlayimbi imfuyo leyo
  yeyake mhlayimbi yeyabo, aze ahlaule
  irafu, ebingasahlaulwa, i Euluneli iyakuti emva kokupela kwezo nyanga
  zintatu zibalulweyo iwutabate ube ngowayo
  kwakona lomhlaba mhlayimbi lompahla ize yenze ekutandayo ngawo
  ngokomteto ekuqutywa ngawo ngelo xesha apa
  kule Koloni: Pantsi kwezimeko kodwa ezinjengokuba ibe kanti lomhlaba
  mhlayimbi lomfuyo, izakupatwa ngokwezi-
  miselo zalo mteto, imi endaweni yetyala (under mortgage), izimiselo
  zesibozo, nesitoba, neshumi Zomteto we 9 wo-
  1844, ezibekiselela entengisweni yomhlaba mhlayimbi yempahla
  elolohlobo, nokusetyenziswa kwemali zawo, ziyakusetye-
  nziswa malungana nayo.”

  Ngenx’enoku ke kuyaziswa ukuba wonke azi ukuba eziziqwenga Zemihlaba
  zisesitilini sase Dikeni (Victoria
  East) ziyakukangelwa njengezingena baninizo, njengoko sekuxeliwe;
  nokuba u Rulumente wozitabatela kwakuye
  ngomhla wokuqala ka March, 1884, idibe ke ngeloxesha kobako babonisayo
  ngobunqina ukuba imihlaba leyo
  banelungelo kuyo, bahlaule imali ezingafinyelelanga, ku Mantyi weso
  sitili, njengoko kumiselweyo kwesi siqendu
  sandulelayo somteto.

  JOHN X. MERRIMAN, Umpatiswa.

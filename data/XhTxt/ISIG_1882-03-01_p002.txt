Uhlanga olu ntsundu kungxanyelwe ukuba lunyuke nge zinto zokanyo.
Kungaba kuku nyuswa kwalo na ke xeshikweni kusayekelelwa kwa kwe zanto
kufuneka kwahlukenwe nazo? Saka sati sixoxa nelinye inene, ngokungavani
kwetu nokuti ontsundu abe nomteto owodwa woku laulwa kwake, ati omhlope
abe nowodwa, lavela nelizwi eli nene esinga tshoyo ukuti ali nabu
nyaniso kwe yalo indawo, lavelisa lendawo yokuzeka izi tembu; lati,
omhlope angati ezeke isi bini saba fazi abe sisulu se tyala, kanti
ontsundu akatintelwa nto, latsho lati, ngalonxa akungebi mposiso nokuba
imiteto yahlukile yezi ntlanga, labambelela kulo mcinga. Ke okunene iko
into leyo, noko kuse kuhleni ukuba isiko loku zeka isi tembu, alimgama
ukuba lingcwatywe, nanamhla elona qela likulu leli bafazi bangabanye,
londawo ngokwayo ngati yeyaneleyo ukuba kulungelelaniswe nabona baninzi.
Asazi nokuba lendawana yebi ngabangela ukuba sekusahlulwa intlantlu ezi
mbini zemiteto. Sitsho singa yazi eyona nto kwenziwa yona ncam,
singatanda noku kanyiselwa koku kufumfuta kwetu ebunyameni. Into
ebangele ukuba sindwebe yile yokohlulwa. Yimvelo yetu ke tina le
yokuyimisela indlebe into etetekayo, ngakumbi xa iza ngolu hlobo.
Sipantsi ko laulo lwe ntloko enye, ulaulo ekutiwa aluketi bala, silusapo
lwa mntu mnye lo MNTAN’ OMHLE. Lwake usapo lwamzi mnye lwa laulwa nge
miteto eyahluka hlukileyo na ke? Ngazwi nye, siti akungebiko ku ri’riza
kwanto, sino mteto omnye, omhlope no ntsundu—Masiqokele, siti, nale
miteto mibini, namitatu, isingisele kwaba ntsundu bodwa, singa kolwa
iguzulwa na namhlanje.

IMPAWANA.

Inani lama dodana afunda ishishini e Mtwaku ngama shumi omatatu.

Namhla sibuye safaka i Grammar yesi Xosa, esitemba abahlobo betu,
abakolwayo yiyo bosi bulela.

N.B.—Abantu abahlaulele Isigidimi ngo Jan. Feb. March, oshicilelwa
amagama abo ngenyanga ezayo.

Incwadi ka Mr. Mpt. Klass Mtakati yobonakala kwi pepa elizayo; ifike
indaba sezi ninzi, yaswela indawo.

Siya vuya sinokuti namhlanje u Somatamsanqa uke wasi fefa nge mvulana
ngomhla we 17 ku February. Umhlaba wona usanxaniwe. Asitandabuzi iyaziwa
lenyaniso Nguye.

Umbali ose Uitenhage usi cela ukuba siritize impi yalo mandla ukuba
kungati kubeko isidlo, malungana ne tyalike eyi St. Anne, ngentsuku zoku
qala zo April. Into efunwayo kuku qweba imali yokuhlaula ityala lalo
ndlu.

Xa sasibika ukuba intlanganiso yaba fundisi aba ntsundu base Rini yoba
se Heald Town ngo November, sati “kutshiwo kambe.” I Methodist efauelwe
kukwazi ngoku gqitileyo, iti intlanganiso leyo iya kuba se Bayi, oko
kukuti; wadana Nxukwebe kwa kona.

Akungakuba sicase ukuba zibonakale incwadi zaba baleli’ esinga zifakanga
kungokuba kupele indawo zinto ebese ziko. Isikalo esingo kupela kwendawo
sopela mhla eli pepa lipuma kabini nge nyanga. Libanjezelwe kodwa
ngabantu bakowetu, aba ngavumiyo ukulamkela.

E Diamond kuko iqela lamanene elike lati maku ngeniswe ama China kwelo
zwe aye kusebenza. U Hon. J. X. Merri­man, kwincwadi emnandi, uyabonisa
ukunga faneleki kwa lonto Kuko nenxenye kwase Kimberly eyicasileyo.
Ngokwazi kwetu loba litamsanqa engezanga ama China.

Asiyazi into ebanga ukuba sibuye aifumane incwadi kubantu abafuna isi
Gidimi zize ezi ncwadi. zihe zingena mali pakati. Sesibaxelele bonke
abamnyarna bakowetu ukuba ipepa aliwokutunyelwa nakubani ongatanga
ahlaule kwase kuqaleni, nokuba izulu selisiwa, nayi gcinwe ke lonto kuba
asi soku pendula.

Kule Parlamente ihlalele uku hlangana kuya kuxoxwa indawo yokuba imali
ehlaulelwa ukutunyelwa kwe ncwadi nge Post ihliswe ; endaweni yese tiki,
incwadi itunyelwe ngese dobilityi zombini, ze amapepa atunyelwe nge
halafu ye peni, nazwinye yonke into ye Post i Government ifuna itotyiwe.
Lonto sonke sivayi takazela.

Kwezi mini indaba. zase Natal ziza kuba yinto enkulu kweli letu, kuba
kuko unxunelo olukulu kudodana olufundileyo uku singa kona. Abo ke,
banjengati, bangxamele ukwenza ikaya labo e Natal, bovuya ukuba uloliwe
osuka e Natal, osinga e Ladysmith uza kubehle aqalwe. Seleko amanene ase
London afuna ukuba embiwe ngemali etile.

Siva ukuba ekuqaleni kwe nyanga le u March, u Rev. John Walton, M.A., no
Rev. Frederick Mason, inkulu zaba fundisi base Wesile, owokuqala base
Rini, nowo kugqibela base Natal, basaza kutyelela pesheya. Umcimbi
kukuxoxa indawo yoku­ba aba fundisi abalapa e South Africa bazi pate ngo
kwabo. Iti i Methodist, esi sicelo soku zilaula, sivela kuba fundisi aba
kweli.

Nge ntengiso mihlaba, exelwa kwi bala leza ziso kwesi Sigidimi, u Rev.
A. Kropf wase Cumakala (Stutterheim) uti singahlebela abo bangxamele
ukunqakula eli lungelo ukuba angaba nceda ngako bonke anako ukuba batete
naye. Akange cebise mntu mnyama u Mr. Kropf ukuba ze afune i agent.
Siyambulela kanye u Mr. Kropf ngokuzinikela kwake kwi lungelo yomntu
omnyama.

I Rulumente imisele ukuba umhla we 15 ku March ibe ngo mabati abe Sutu
ngawo bafeze into ekwati ze kubeko uxolo nje wati u Sir H. Robinson
mabenze yona. Ngu Masupha ongavumi kufeza kanye lomnqopiso. I Rulumente
epesheya inike amandla kule yase Kapa ukuba ize iba pate ngoku tanda
kwayo abe Sutu emva komhla lowo we shumi elinesi hlanu. Kutiwa ute u
Letsea akuyiva lento walila.

Njenge siko, u Mntan’ Omhle ugqibe kwe lokuba awavuze ama pakati ake
awaye lungiselela uku xoliswa kwe Transvaal. I Governor yetu, u Sir H
Robinson, unyulelwe ukuba abe kwi bandla laba cebisi bo Mntan’ Omhle
(Privy Council); ngoku ke waziwa ngeli gama u Mhlekazi.—The Right
Honourable Sir H. G. R. Robinson, G.C.M.G. U Sir Evelyn Wood, V.C.
unyuselwe kwi bandla elibalulwa ngala magama G.C M.G. (Knight Grand
Cross of St. Michael and St. George,) yena no President Brand, G.C.M.G.
wase Orange Free State. U Sir Henry De Velliers, ijaji enkulu yale
Koloni, unikwe lama gama: C.M.G. (Commander of St. Michael and St.
George.) Tina bahlobo babo siyabulela.

Kuko into esingena kuyixela, ebonakala kwezi zikolo zikulu zetu
bamnyama. Yile, udodana olu lufundiswa ama shishini luno moya weratshi,
omana ubonakala ngamatyala angenisa naba fundisi entetweni. Ubutyakala
bama xego akowetu buse bukulu kangangokuba iti into esukuba inje, ibanga
ukuncola ko msebenzi omhle owenzelwa tina ngaba fundisi, yenzeke, ze
ingabi nam buzeli. Ityala lo Samuel Mawonga, Thomas Mjodi, Philip
Majola, no Charles Magoda, besikolo sase Mtwaku, loku kotama, leli
ncinane kuma tyala etu amakulu okunika izisihla ezibi kumsebenzi waba
fnndisi. Inani zi £2, adliwe lona, yimantyi yase Qonce, u Mr. Chalmers,
siyatemba ofunda onke amadodana afunda amashishini, isohlwayo sokuluma
izandla ezisi nika isonka.

Hai aba bantu abe Lungu. Kuko usuku olutile kule nyanga ingu
February—nolo suku ngu 14—ekufike umlisela nomtinjana utumelane incwadi
ezingo tando, nento ezi njalo. Umhla lowo kutiwa ngoka St. Valentine,
esati ngawo, isilumko sasema Ngesini u Shakespeare, zizekana ngawo
intaka; yasukela apo ke into yokuba ibe yintombi ibalele isoka. kuti
lili soka libalele intombi incwadi yeza “fobe.” Mayibe ke lento yeyaba
lumkileyo, kuba ibingeko kowetu kwa Xosa. Ngalomhla kuke kwafika incwadi
zolo hlobo kwi Editor yeli pepa. Asikolwa noko ukuba bezivela kuma Ledi.
Lomfana ngati siyamazi. Into kodwa sivuya ukuba imfundo yetu ifikelele
ekudeni sazi nange Valentine. Akwaba besingazi kakulu ukuzitoba noku
lulama—ezona Valentine zigqitileyo. Asibanga lusizi sakuyi fumana into
leyo, sihlekile kodwa.

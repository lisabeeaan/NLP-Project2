ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 1, 1877.

INKOSI yase Egypt itembise ukuba iyakutumela amasoldati 20,000
ayekuncedisa eyase Turkey kunye nemikombe emine yokulwa.

NGASE Uitenhage kuko umlungu ofunyenwe efile ecaleni kuka Loliwe
kubonakala ukuba ubulewe ngumntu. Kuko nomfo ontsundu obanjiweyo eku-
ranelwa kuye.

“I-CRADOCK REGISTER” iti kwindawo abatiyi Gart" land Kloof bekuko
abantwana ababini ababeshiywe endlwini. Kungekabi mzuzu mde kuviwe
bekala kanti indlu iyatsha, bapelela kona bobabini.

KUVAKALA ukuba i-Germany iyawabiza amasoldati ayo amadala akwamanye
amazwe iti makagoduke nemali yawo yokugoduka ihlaulwa yi Germany ngo-
kwayo. Abaninzi bati iceba ukulwa ne France.

ABAKOHLISI.—E-Uitenhage kuko umlungu obe' ye kutengisa uboya. Kate xa
buse markeni banga buninzi bakubekwa emlingweni, kanti uyakuti akubuqaqa
umntu obutengileyo afumane ilitye nentlabati namatyana amancinane.

INTENGISO—Ngase Somerset kwindawo abati yi Doori Kloof kuya kutengiswa
ngomhla 26 alenyanga igusha ezingapezu kwewaka elinamakulu amatatu,
inkomo namatole 60, amatokazi 35, inkabi zokubo- tshwa nezokuxelwa 100,
amahashe 15, amahashekazi amable 40, ibokwe 4000.

E-DAHOMEY—Inkosi yase Dahomey ebingxamele ukulwa nabe Lungu iti ngoku
ifuna uxolo. Umntu wayo olwaqaleka ngaye ucuku imbambile,
kutandatyuzelwa ukuba iyakumbulala. Icela kodwa ukuba Amangesi
angayihlaulisi kakulu kangangoko ebefun’ ukwenjenjalo, kuba ayingeze
yalifeza elonani.

IPEPA lase Fort Beaufort liti kuko inkosana yakwa Ngqika ebanjiweyo
kona, kutiwe ibe inkomo ezi 13. Liti kanjalo kuko umlungu obezise kwa
Mantyi ama­hashe amabini esiti alike nomfo obezibiza ngo kuti ungu
Stoffel kanti encwadini yake kutiwe ungu Stuurman. Kute kusayiwa kubizwa
umntu abete uyamazi wanga uza kufaka ilahle enqaweni kanti selebaleka.

NGASE KAPA kuko Amakuli ate elambile adla uhlobo olutile Iwentlanzi.
Kube mzuzwana akala ngezisu kanti aza kuqauka kwaoko. Kwenye indawo ute
umntu nomfizi bazifumana ezontlanzi elunxwemeni lolwandle baya kuzidla
ekaya. Ite ingekapeli iyure babasebefile bobabini. Kubonakala ukuba kuko
ixesha emnyakeni eziti ezontlanzi zizale bubuhlungu. Igama lazo bati zi
mussel. Ngebe- zilumkela abazi dlayo.

SINOSIZI ukubika ukububa kuka Mr. Thomas Mer- rington unyana ka Rev. Mr.
Merrington wase Bethelsi dorp Ubeyindodana empilo. intle etembisa
ngokungat uya kuba mhle umsebenziwayo. Ute waruneka umqolo epakamisa
into enzima kanti longozi iya kupelela ekufeni. Ubesalungiselela ukuya
e-Livingstonia ku Dr. Stewart. Asikuko nokuba siyavelana nabazali bake.—
Uitenhage Times.

KWELE DIAMOND indoda abati ngu George Ross ebudala bumashumi mabini
eminyaka ufunyenwe enetyala lokubulala Umkorana. Lomkorana kutiwa
wayehlamba ibotile yaza yapuka amaceba ayo wawa- gibisela elutangweni.
U-George lowo ekubonakala ukungati wayeselile warola ipistoli yake
wadubula kum Korana wambulala. Isigwebo sejuri sibe sesokuba unetyala
kodwa ngelepatwa ngenceba. Ijaji yona ite makaxonywe.

E-BAYI ngomhla wesihlanu ka May bekuko intla- nganiso yama doda akangela
abantu abapata kakubi impahla yabo enje ngozinkomo nomahashe. Ati aku-
mbona oyipatakakubi amse ematyaleni. Kulonyaka upelileyo” babe bahlanu
abantu abasiwe ematyaleni badliwa bobahlanu. Kulamacala etu kufuneka
ukuba kubeko amaqela amadoda ayakubonela izinto ezi- ngenakuzitetela.
Abantu abaninzi abazi ukuba kulityala ukuyipata kakubi into
abayifuyileyo Ukuba bebeyi qonda lento ngelengemaninzi kangaka amahashe
akwelwa imihlana ibolile, nenkabi ezitsha amacala mhla zaqalwa.

PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.—Kute kulenyanga ifileyo u-Ayliff Imantyi yama Mfengu
yeva ukuba kuko inkomo ezibalekiswe zasiwa kwa Feltman nokuba kuko apo
bekusiliwa kona. Kubonakala ukuba kube kudlula inqwelo itwele ibrandi
iyisa kwa Sarili Ite apo ibikulule kona kufupi nase mdeni kweza u-Dala-

sile omnye wonyana baka Sarili no Mehlomane beha- mba namapakati abo.
Basele bade bamnandi. Ute u-Dalasile akubona u-Mehlomane epete ibotile
ezimbini egoduka nazo wazibiza, wala nazo omnye. Kute kwakuba njalo enye
yamadoda ka Dalasile yambeta u-Mehlomane ngentonga. Asuka aselehlangana
aka Dalasile naka Mehlomane zanuka. Ade abaleka aka Dalasile. Ute
u-Mehlomane ngokwazi ukuba ubete inkosi yake waselezibalekisa inkomo
zake ezisa kwela Mamfengu. U-Mr Ayliff unike umteto wokuba zize
zinganikwa mntwini ungekavakali umteto ka Colonel Eustace ngazo.

IPEPA lendaba lase Lusutu liti inani labantu abangena ityalike
zabafundisi ‘base France lifike 20, 000. Inani labakolwayo belohlelo
5000. Abantwana abahamba ezikolweni 2, 000 ; amadodana afunda
e-Simnareni yakona 80. Omnye umfo obala kwakwelo pepa uti:-—Kusand’
ukuvulwa indlu yetyalike ezimbini. Intlanganiso zokuvulwa kwazo zibe
zinkulu zombini abantu abakuzo bekumawaka amabini. Ekuvulweni kwenye
yazo kubaptizwe nenkosi u-Mafa, kunye neshumi elinesibini labantu
abavela ebuhedenini nomnye ozalwa ngabantu abakoliweyo. U-Mafa uzalwa
ngudadebo Mshweshwe owabaptizwa nyakomnye waza wayaleza konyana bake
ukuba bambndele. Ukuluvuma kwake ukolo ukwenzele ekuhleni. Indawo ezinje
ngale yake ziyamkutaza umfundisi zimvuselele emsebenzini wake. Abayeku
Banya ibanduluke ngomhla 16 ka April. Kuhambe u-Rev. E. Coillard no Mrs
Coillard nabavangeli abane kunye nabafazi babo. Amagama amadoda nabafazi
ngu Aser no Lo­nisa, Andreas no Cesarina, Aaron no Kristina, Asaele no
Elizabeth. Inqwelo zabo zintatu. Kuhambe kanjalo namadodana amabini,
u-Charles Machaba no Fredrik Molepo, agodukayo ukuya e-Zoutpansberg,
ayakuti mhlaumbi enziwe abavangeli ngabafundisi be Swiss abase
Valdezia.— Cape Argus

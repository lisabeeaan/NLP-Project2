UDODANA OLUFUNDILEYO.

INTETO ka Rev. P. J. MZIMBA, mhla kwavulwa isikolo sase Lovedale, sayiva
ngodano olukulu. Lati elinene lingumfundisi, like leva ingwevu yakowetu
isiti ngoku yona izimisele ukufundisa intombi zayo kuba abantwana bayo
abangamadodana beyidanisile—imfundo ayibanga nto yakulonto kubo; ifumana
izintombi eziyilulameleyo, zizo ezingabafana bayo. Itsho lemambane
yawahlela azakuya eskoIweni, yayihlela nemali. Ingaba mhlaimbi siteta
nje ezonzwakazi ziseskolweni.

Oludaba lukumbuze u Rev. Dr. STEWART ibalana elibonakele kwipepa lase
London ekutiwa yi Times, ate walibalisa. Ite yakupuma intlanganiso
salifumana ibali elo lingo ABD-EL-KADER inkosana yase Algiers, eyati
iseyindodana yanikwa ihashe elimhlope elalisaziwa ngama Arab ukuba
linexabiso elikulu. Aziyekanga imambane zelo ukumhambela zimnika
amaxabiso onke; konke akwanceda Into. Kwinkita ebimane ukumhambela kuze
netshawe lelozwe, elamcela alayeka kwa eli hashe. Inqaba : akakatsho.
Mhla utile limbone ekwele kweli hashe esel’umoya. Lase- lizinxiba ingubo
zengqiba lisiya kumendo awayeya kubuya ngawo. Ubuyile okwenene u
ABD-EL-KADER, waqubisana nelijacu—into ese ikohlwe nakuhamba. Ubetwe
yinceba wamkwelisa ehasheni elo yena waxolela ukuhamba ngenyawo ukungena
esixekweni. Intoni? Uyilahle kwalapo lontlaka-ntlaka umfo omkulu,
wagqotsa akayeka esimka nehashe. U ABDEL ukwaze ngelirara, esiti
selemnika ihashe elo, maze angatyeli mntu indlela alifumene ngayo, kuba
eyakuti ukuba wenjenjalo abange ukuba into eninzi entliziyo zinenceba
ingabuye itumekelele ekuncedeni abasweleyo.

Ngenqubo yayo into eninzi pakati komlisela yenze lento yenziwa
lelipakati lakohlisayo. Oko umlisela ukwenze kubazali abanjengalo ufike
kwisigqibo sokufundisa intombi namhla, awanele kukwenza kubazali ;
ukwenza nakuba fundisa. Amadodana kaloku afundisiwe. Imibuzo yeyokuba
asebenza ntom’na ngemfundo yawo? Imfundo yawo yenike igama elihle na
kuhlanga, nakuba fundisi? Siqonda tina ezizinto azikafezeki. Udodana
aluncedo luni eluhlangeni, kuba uhlanga lubulawa zinto ezibuhlungu
luhleli nalo oludodana, kungeko nto lunceda kuyo. Alukaniki gama kuba
fundisi kuba inteto, izenzo, intlalo yalo ihlelele- kile. Ngamana Iweza
engqondweni msinya, hleze ababantu babini bebelufundisa bazohlwaye
ngenxa yokuba imali namandla abo bewacitele kokungena xabiso.

Singa amazwi ka ABD-EL-KADER ati, hleze abantliziyo zitumekelelayo
ukunceda banqandeke ukwenza okulungileyo, angavisiswa Xa udodana
lufundiswayo kungeko nto ibonakalayo yokuqubela pambili bayakuqala abo
bebesinceda ukuteta njengo ABD-EL-KADER IO.

Ndawana ibange ukuba singalutabateli pezulu oluda mhla Iwafika kungokuba
bekusekumzuzu zifika indaba zakwa Zulu ze emva kwexeshana zifunyanwe
zibuxoki nengcapukiso yompefumlo. Ekufuneka kekwadlula ixesha ngapambi
kokuba nantonina evela kubabaleli bamapepa ase Natal yamkelwe njengento
enyanisekileyo. Kekaloku kude kwangulo mzuzwana sibala ngawo singavanga
nto ikanyeza ukubulawa kuka CETSHWAYO kwidabi elaliko pakati kwake no
ZIBEBU, e Lundini, ngomhla wamashumii amabini ananye ku July. Emasiti ke
nakuba sitandabuzayo okwenene yenzabele umhlaba into ka MPANDE U
CETSHWAYO, eyati imbongi ngaye ngozito zigoso.

Nakuba abaninzi bebengakulindele ukufa kwake kamsinya kangaka, kodwa
izimiselo zokubuyiselwa kwake kubantu bake zabeta kwamhlope kuye wonke
oyaziyo ihambiso yezinto kwa Zulu ukuba ingxobotshitshi nemfazwe, ziya
kuba ninzi kwelo. Asinantloni ukuti singabanye babo babesiti u CETSHWAYO
magoduswe, nabaposa iminqwazi pezulu ngokuvuya sakuva ukuba ukululwe,
abadanayo kodwa bakuva izimiselo awagoduswa pantsi kwazo ngu LORD
KIMBERLEY. Ezi zimiselo zazezokuba abuyele kwinxenye kodwa yabantu bake.
Into enkulu, etyebileyo yelizwe, ekuyeni ngase Iwandle yanikelwa u JOHN
DUNN ; kwa ubabalala lomhlaba olukwangako ngasentla ekuyeni kuma
Transvaal Iwanikwa u ZIBEBU, waza u Kumkani wanikwa isizityana
esipakati. Umfundi makumbule ukuba u CETSHWAYO akanazwi nelokubika kule
mihlaba yonganyelwe ngo JOHN DUNN no ZIBEBU. Into enje yabonakala ukuba
ayinakubanga ukuba kubeko uxolo nokonwaba. Wayeyibonile u CETSHWAYO into
leyo kwaisabungwa yi Rulumente ya Pesheya, wayixela, yangati imvisisile.
Kodwa ute eseselwandle u CETSHWAYO ebuya—jibilili u LORD KIMBERLEY,
wabuya wawaba umhlaba, wanikela incum yawo ko JOHN DUNN. Oku kuxelwe ngu
SIR HERCULES ROBINSON ku CETSHWAYO, obehle wabona ukuba yimpiti- mpiti
yodwa kwa Zulu, wada, kuba ebeselefuna egodukile, wasayina ezo zimiselo
zolaulo Iwa Pesheya. Wanele ukumiswa egunyeni lake baqala o ZIBEBU
ukuhambisa ezonto kwabe kusoyikelwa ukuba baya kuzenza—ukunqwena,
ukupanga, ukulwa, ukububisa nokubulala.

Ukuba uhlobo awayete u BISHOP COLENSO makabuyiselwe ngalo u CETSHWAYO
kokwake Iwaluhanjisiwe ezizipitipiti ngezingabangako. Elo cebo lika
BISHOP COLENSO lelalamkelwe ngabo bonke ababenga ukumkani angagoduswa.
Lalelokuba ukuxoliswa kwelizwe okwamiselwa ngu SIR GARNET WOLSELY, ngo
1879, kungacitwa; ezo nduna zilishumi linesitatu zingashenxiswa ; kuti
ukuba kunokwenzeka zandiswe, ngendlela emfutshane wonke umhlaba wabiwe
ube ngangalowo wonganyelwe zimantyi e Natal, ze u CETSHWAYO ongamele
zonke ezo nkosana. Lalilelo iqinga lika BISHOP COLENSO ekubonakalayo
ukuba lilo elaliya kulunga. I Rulumente ya Pesheya ayiyibonanga
ngoluhlobo indawo leyo, iqonde ukuba kukumbuyisela pantsi kwezi zimeko
zidubule emashweni namhla. Wonke ozimisele ukuzicedululela olubici
Iwakwa Zulu umelwe kukuzikumbula ezinyaniso, kuba siqonda ukuba baninzi
abafuna ukulibeka igazi elipalazwe ngu ZIBEBU, nabo baquba naye—isizwe
sika MASIPULA, esisemantla o Mhlatuze—pezu kwentloko yomfi u BISHOP
COLENSO, neqela elalisebenza kunye naye elipesheya. Kwi London Times

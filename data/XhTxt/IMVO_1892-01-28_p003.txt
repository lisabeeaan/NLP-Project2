Nditi, sinduluke ngobusuku bo Mgqi-
belo (19 Dec.), ukusinga e Rini, sirolwa
yimpemvu enkulu i injini le. Kwi “ pas-
sengers’’ esasikwele nazo ndobalula
isipompotela segxagxa, into eyayiziqole
kakulu ngo “3 years old,” saza sahlala
kakubi ngokumana ikatazela ekuvuleni
ifestile, siti sakuti, “ Will you please
shut the window sir? ” iti lento : “ Go to
h , I want air, I can’t be suffocated,”

litsho kume ngalo. Samana ukuqaqazela
ubusuku obu sikohlwe yinto esingaye-
nzayo.

  Nditi, site sakufika e Alicedale kwa-
  ngena kule kariji yotu umfo wakwa
  Mahomete, idyongwana ye Silamsi, ete
  isafika, yati kuba lalisaqala igxagxa
  ukuti ncwaba, yalibamba ngesixhanti
  isiti, “ Get up old fellow, are you drunk ?
  this is not your house, its a carriage, get
  up! get up!” Sibehle saqonda ukuba
  kute nqo ingwe yalo ifikile ; noko sihle
  saqonda ukuba i “bhakileyi ” seyikufupi.
  Intoni, like laqobisa ngoku ngati liza
  kulwa, hayi lahle laqonda ukuba oku
  kokunye, lafumana lati ukuncama, “ If
  you feel what I feel, you would no more
  wake me up.” Saqala ukuva ukonwaba
  emva kwemayile azikumashumi asibozo
  sityala nzima.

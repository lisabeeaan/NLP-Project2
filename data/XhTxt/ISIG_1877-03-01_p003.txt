UKUQALA AMAHASHE.

KUNI bakweli mahashe, nimane nizonela inkabi zenu namatokazi enu mhla
nawaqeqesha ngoku wadlongo - zelela, apatwe kakubi ngomsindo,
nangokubetwa oku- kulu. Nakuni bafuya amahashe nitanda ukuqalelwa kuba
ningatandi ukwenzakala. Kuko indlela elula yokuqala amahashe ifuna
kupela umntu oyitandayo impahla yake. Iloluhlobo—Yiti kuqala ihashana
lako liselula umane uliqelisa ukuba lingakoyiki, kamva lakuba likulu
liqelise ukukulekwa. Uti yonke into oyenzayo ulicenge ungake ulinge
uloyikise. Mhlana ke uza kulikwela yiya kulo wedwa xa likulekiweyo.
Ungezi namhlambi wabantu maube wedwa kanye. Woti wakufika umane
ulipatapata, ulipulula, uti wakuqonda ukuba ngoku lonwabile aloyiki,
utyiwule kancinane ube ungenzeli kukwela kwaoko kupe­la ukukangela ukuba
liyakotukana, kwenze oko lide lingotuki. Man’ usenjenjalo ude utyiwule
kakulu ume kwaoko pantsi. Qonda ke lendawo uwedwa, alibanjwanga nto,
kupela likulekiwe, alikabi nasali. Yiti kaloku ukwele ze njalo, utsibele
pantsi kwaoko umane usenjenjalo lide lingotuki. Lakubake lingoyiki,
tabata isali ungeka wufaki umkala, yidlulise

pezu kwentloko ude ke uyibeke emhlana kakuhle. Xa ude walisalisha lema
nalo naxa selude wayifikisa nasemsileni alakaba, zamela kaloku ukulifaka
umkala omnandi ongawutyabuliyo umlomo.

Wakukwela hambisa kuhle. Xa lingahambiyo ulibete ngokubuhlungu kodwa
ungasipakamisanga isabokwe, libeke lisebuhlanti liqonda nalo ukuba
linento elitinteleyo amacala onke. Emveni koko pu­ma nalo uhambe ungama
onobom uze ngokubuya upalise kancinane ubeke umane usihla futi. Ukuba
kuko elinye ihashe elinxulumene nalo lohamba lula ngapezulu.

Maninzi amahashe alungileyo awoniwayo ngabantu ekuqalweni kwawo. Omnye
uliqala liselincinane kakulu kuba esoyika ukuti loti lakude lomelele
lidlokove kakulu ; asuke ke ngoko aliqoboze ibe yinto apa ekubeka kakulu
lide life lingazanga layeka. Wumbi ulinika amagezana, into zonke zenziwe
ngama­ndla, lope umlomo kukutyatyulwa ngumkala, lisuke ke ngoko lingaze
litambe lihlale lemihla lisenza imi- gudu xa lizakufakwa umkala,
inkatazo kumninilo mhlaumbi ngamadoda la makulu kufuneka ukuba ahlale
ezigcinile. Kanti kunjalo nje koti kwakupaliswa lihole nomninilo kuba
kaloku alazinto ilungileyo nemnandi emkaleni. Limbi mhla laqalwa
latyatyulwa lopiswa umzimba ngezabokwe lisuke libe yinto apa oba kuti
tyam kupela ive ngetshoba.

Ezindawana ngati yimfeketo kanti noko zinkulu, maninzi amahashe
awoniwayo mhleni aqalwa. Amanye elungile ngendlela zonke anengozi embi
ngokotuka, kanti lonto ayizuza ngokotuswa mhleni aqalwa. Bekuko inkabi
ezimbini zamahashe, zisukile egadeni zombini. Elinye iyimbaleki
ebendingazi hashe lingalishiyayo, kodwa kuba loniwa mhla laqalwa
belingazange litambe. Xa kungeko mntu emhlana limbuna kakulu akuko hashe
liligqitayo, kodwa kaukwele woyibona nawe into yako! koti kona lakufika
endaweni ehlayo, kuwe minqwazi, kupume zinyembezi, kuvuze zincwe kube
kubi. Elesibini yinkabi apa engawuvayo umsebenzi, woliqonda ukuba
lomelele zakuba mbini iveki, isali isemhlana wofika lisengu- mdunduluza,
iyinqaba ukudinwa, xa usemhlana limbuna kakulu, kodwa ukufaka umkala,
nokukwela yingonzi enkulu, lipata kukaba nokudlokova. Le­nto ke yonke
yabangwa ngumqali.

Yinina ukuba singazisebenzisi ngokutambileyo izi- dalwa esizinikwe ngu
Somandla, kanti nje yena tina zindlandlu usiqeqesha ngokutambileyo
ngezwi lake, ngokusicenga, ngokusibongoza side sitambe.

INKOSANA EDLIWE KUBA IDELE
AMATYALA.

Kute xa u-Mr. Hemming imantyi ibiteta amatyala e-Bolotwa, inkosana engu
Mbundla yangena endlwini yamatyala inxilile yaya kwindawo apo omnye umfo
ebenika ubunqina kwityala ebelitetwa yampazamisa. Ute umantyi noko
ayaziyo lenkosana wazenza ongati akamazi, wabuza ukuba ungubanina. Akuba
exelelwe wati ku Mbundla ndikutele nqa ngalento uyenzayo, hamba upume
apa endlwini yamatyala. Ide exesheni yavuma ukuhlala pantsi, kodwa
akubanga kade izolile, ite kwakona yaya kulomntu unika ubu­nqina yangena
pakati kwake namapolisa yenza isidubedube yaxelela umantyi ukuba yona
iyinkosi, ekubonakala ukuba ibiba ingenza ngokutanda kwayo nalapo.
Umantyi ute kuye nokuba umntu uyinkosi nokuba akayiyo kwindlu yamatyala
bayafana akuketwa, alekele eliti ukuba akuyiyeki lengxobotshane uyenzayo
ndiya kunyanzeleka ukuba ndikugxote apa, kanti ke anditandi ukuba abantu
abamnyama bagxote ngamandla owegazi. Kutiwe ke ngokungapezulu njengokuba
lenkosana inxilile nje mayipume apa. Ite kulonteto inkosana, mantyi
uxela ubuxoki. Kuteke kwaoko basebemkupa ngamandla, xa ati gqi pandle
wati u-Mr. Hemming lento uyenzileyo apa namhla nje ndiyakuyibika kwa
Rulumente. Wayixela ke okwenene, kwabuya ilizwi eliti ebotwe, mayibanjwe
imali yalonkosana iminyaka emibini, oko kukuti idliwe ikulu leponti.
Koko ute umantyi wayicelela itaru ngokuti kumhlana lomntu enza
isipitipiti esinje, wasele dliwa amashumi omahlanu.

zezakusivela emsebenzini wetu.

Umfundisi ute umsebenzi wawuqala ngentshuma- yelo, kwakukov’ukuvunywa
nokutandazwa kulamazwi ati—“Ke nina bazalwana msani ukut’amandla

watyila kuqala umteti wawo, nokuba waye wabekisa kobanina, iyintonina
isizeka bani sokude awatete. Ngendawo yesibini, wabonisa ukuhla kwezinto
ezininzi zomoya nezenyama kumkristu mnye kumzimba mnye, aze ngokungapezu
koko angeti amandla nakomnye walomisebenzi ifunwa ngu-Tixo. Ku­be
nakweyomoya u-Tixo, kumpefumlo mnye efuna ukuba umkristu, makazenze
zonke angeti amandla nakumnye. Ngendawo yesitatu, ukuba kwafuneka
umpostile ukude atete amazwi anje kwibandla lase Tesalonika elincomekayo
ngendawo ezininzi, kukangakananina ukoyikela ukuba iramente zalamaxa
okubanda nokubadikidiki ukuba Sengozini kwazo yokuta amandla
kwimisebenzi elungileyo. Wagqiba ngelizwi alibekise ngokukodwa kumhlambi
wase Dikeni ukulungeselela kwawo izinto zenyama, ngokukutala
em­sebenzini, ngokulima intlobo zonke, ngokunyamekela imfundo; kwizinto
zomoya, ukuyinyamekela imitandazo, isabata, ukupengulula izibalo,
ukushumayeza aboni, ukomeleza amanye amakrestu; nokuhamba intlanganiso,
nokufundisa nokuyala abantwana ngaye u-Yesu Kristu. Niwapumele amaqaba
niwamemela endlwini ka Tixo ukuba aze kuva ilizwi eve ngokufa konyana
kaTixo; eve ngengozi yokuhlala kwizinto ezinika ukufa.

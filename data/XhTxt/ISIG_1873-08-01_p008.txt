8 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1873.

AMADODA asebenza indlela yenqwelo zomlilo epakati kwe Wellington ne
Worcester anga 400.

KUKO UMKOMBE osand’ ukufika E-Algoa Bay unento zokwenza indlela yenqwelo
zomlilo eya E-Bushmans River.

U-WATEBBOEB inkosi yama Griqua usatyelele kumnyeni wentombi yake U-Adam
Koko E- Namaqualand. Kuko amare okuba U-Koko uza kubuyeka ubukosi
abunike U-Waterboer.

ITELEGBAE YAPANTSI KWAMANZI.—Kusekuko ekuvunyelwene naye ukuba alwenze
ucingo olusuka E-Bayi luye E-Aden luhamba pantsi kwa­manzi. Kutiwa lomfo
kuvunyelwene naye ugu Mr Coles. Iqela amele Iona liya kubatalwa £9,500
ngomnyaka.

KWELE GOLIDE.—Ezokugqibela indaba ziti igolide ayiko kakulu. Ukudla
kuswelekile, kute-ngwa ngemali eninzi. Imantyi yase Leydenburg iti
kwincwadi esand’ ukuyibala, abantu ngebenga buduzeli ukuya kona
okwakaloku nje.

INTENGO.—Kwifandesi esand’ ukubako E-Bayi, inkabi zokutsala bezitengiswa
nge £12 5s. Kute- ngiswe zali 132. Erolelwe inani elingapezulu
kwelezinye idle £15 7s. 6d., yaza erolelwe elinci- nane yadla £9 10s.

UKUTWALWA KWEMPAHLA.—Ngoku impahla ezisuka E-Bayi zisiya kwele Diamond,
zirolelwa ne 60s. nge 100 lbs, xa zitwelwe ngenqwelo yondlebe-nde ; iti
ukuba yinqwelo yenkabi kurolwe i nokuba zi 40s. mhlaumbi ezi 45s. nge
100 lbs.

IMALI erolwa yi Government, enikwa kwinko- sana zabantsundu ngomnyaka
izi £7,356.

AMATYALA KWELE DIAMOND kutiwa akulile kanye ngoku. Sekude kwafunwa enye
Imantyi yokuncedisa ukuteta amatyala okuba, nokubetana kwabantu.
Nomsebenzi kona awumninzi nje- ngoko ubunjalo ngapambili.

NATAL.—Kwelo lizwe esisifo senkomo sitsha kutiwa ngamanzi-abomvu, sibete
abantu bakohlwa kukutumela impahla. Ziti eziza kutunyelwa kwele Diamond
zike zize apa E-Monti nokuba kuse Port Alfred. Kuko iqela elifuna
ukuzisa ondlebende ukuba beze kwenza umsebenzi wenkabi. Kutiwa nonyaka
nje iyakuba yindlala ngecala lomti wekofu.

E-TSOMO.

  UYAFUNWA E-TSOMO OTITSHERE bosapo babe batatu nokuba bane. Mababe
  nesimilo esifanelekiyo.

Imali abaya kuqalwa ngayo ziponti ezi ngamashumi ainatatu ngomnyaka
(£30). Lo ofuna lomsebenzi makabale ngokwesi Ngesi kamsinya atumele ku
Bev. J. H. Scott, Tsomo, atume nencwadi yomntu otembekileyo onganqinayo
ngemfundo nangesimilo sake.

P.S.—Kutiwa nga Magqira umoya welilizwe lase Tsomo umfanele kakulu
onesifuba.

J. H. SCOTT.

ISAZISO.

  BAGUQULI be Zibalo Eqonce, bacela kwabo babona inda- wo
  eziposisekileyo, ezincwadini zo Mateyu, ne Zenzo,

ukuba bazitumele kumbaleli wabaguquli, U-BEV. JOHN W. APPLEYARD, MOUNT
COKE, KING WILLIAM’S TOWN, ngapambi komhla wokuqala ka October nonyaka
nje.

Ukutsbo ke siti, makakuxele ukulungiswa kwezondawo ngokwake ukucinga ati
makutiwe ukubalwa.

JOHN W. APPLEYARD, Umbaleli.

King William’s Town, July 24, 1873.

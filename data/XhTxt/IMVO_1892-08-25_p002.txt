UTYELELO PESHEYA KO LWANDLE.

[JOHN KNOX BOKWE.]

Sihambe imihla elishumi elinesibhozo saya kugaleleka e Southampton.
Kodwa pambi kokuba ndilushiye ulwandle ndoke ndiqabaqaba abe mbalwa
endibhaqene nako apo. Uyazi, Mhleli, ukuba ndandi- fana nempukane
etungeni lobisi, ndinde- dwa qha omnyama kulamakulu matatu abantu;
kungeko nomnye endinokuti ndaka ndambona lo. Ngekungenani ukuba ibiyimpi
evela e England isiya e Africa endiwe pakati kwayo, kuba leyo ayiketi
bala. Ndidibene nempi yase Koloni; ubuninzi kodwa bupuma e Dayi- mani,
nase Rautini, into ezikuze zisand’ ukukuza apo u Katile aya kona, nokuba
namhla zihlanjelwa knba zigudlana ama- cala naye, zidle sitebeni sinye
naye! Ndite ngentliziyo, kunjenjenje ungabhe- kisi nelimdaka, sisenzo
into eyakuxela, nesimo sobu gentleman obungenabala bona. Ndinqule
kwiminyanya yakowetu, yaza yalata ukuba “ takata u Nomazaku- zaku lo
ngobuti bakowabo, wokulwela imfazwe zako akupe isihlalo sokonwaba.”
Ndimte jwi amaqosha amabini; ndamti tya ngoshiyi ukuba maze andipe
igumbi elihle elingaxineneyo, nelizolileyo, andi- beke endaweni
endonwaba! Yinto ke mfondini endite kanti ndimgqibile, inqwale, incuma,
inkewu, yandigcina kunye nempahlana yam, yonke indlela, ngangokude
bamangaliswe abamelwana ukuba yinina lento kunje kum? Kube mnandi xa
kaloku ekuhambeni kwexesha abanye bebecezela kude, baye besondela,
ngokubona i Captain namanye amanene e First Class eza kuncokola no
Kahle, ati akungandiboni mhla ndigutywe sisifo solwandle aze
kundilunguza. Ulwandle Iwaluzolile yonke indlela ubuninzi be- mihla.
Noko nditeta ngendlela nje asinto ukoyo umgaqo wayo elwandle, akuko
namihlaba kujongvva kuyo. Isimanga kukuba yaziwa nganina, kuxa linina
emakujikwe ngoku. Ndibuzile, ndalati- swa ilanga ukuba kukangelwa kulo
yonke imihla, nesalatiso apa ekutiwa yi Campass esintloko isinge e Node
esixelayo apo makubhekwe ngakona, nokuba unxweme lolwandle lunxayipina.
Umgca ekutiwa yi Equator uviwa ngobushushu balo elo langa; into oti xa
uhamba kuwo yoba sifutufutu. Isitunzi sako soba pantsi kwenyawo. Tina
singezantsi e South Africa, isitunzi setu sisibona ngasekohlo kuba
ilanga lingentla. Wakuyidlula i Equator usiza pesheya isitunzi siba
ngase kunene.

lenyanga lize kuvula. Ibhongo le kaba yi Tourna­ment, selizixonxile
indlebe ukuva apo ide yaqiniselwa ukuba ibe kona.

INTENETYA.—Kushukuma nabadlali be Ntenetya eseyinonyaka ingadlalwa apa,
nabo bane bhongo lo Mnyhadala ukuba kunokuvunyelwana nezinye indawo u-
kuba ube se Rini nge Krismesi.

INGQADAZA KA MVULA—el igqibileyo ixesha layo engqeqeshweni, yabuliswa
ngemvula yamabhaso ukulishiya kwayo i Rini.

INTO ewuhlalise kakubi umzi apa e Tinara ludodana; lusuke Iwaxela izinja
zasengqina zisukela inyamakazi ukusu- kela kwalo abantwana babantu. Kulo
nyaka utwasileyo wodwa zingamashumi amabini intombi ezineponti enamehlo.

IMIDUNGELA YASE BHAI.—Sekude kwa- ko nemidungela yase Bhai eseyihlal’
apa, ebonakala ukuba ive incomelo ngabalapa abafana; eshoco aba
akubonakali ukuba bayakubuye bemke kuba bondliwa nge bhaka ne helesi
zama dinala ekuvelwa nawo emakitshini zintombi. Ndingaza- nga ndiyibone
nalento yokuti abantwana bepela kungabisabiko noko amnye oka- lavo.

NABAZALI—baxakiwe yilento kuba aba bafana zinto ezingenayo nenja le,
ekubo- nakala ukuba angafanelana selemnya- nzela ukuba atshate abe
uyazipoxa.

UKUTSHATISWA ZIHAGU.—Nakubafana bati abati ngelabo bayabatabata abo
bantwana basuke baneliswe kukutshati- swa zihagu, kuba abade baye
kubafundisi, bati oko batshate kade ezitolofiyeni nase matyolweni, nase
zindongeni, emagqube- ni e ontyi abasakatali. Ukuba bebenoluvo lobuntu
bebengaya kubamba usiba nange ngubo zemini.

  ITIMITI ZE MIGQIBELO.—Ngoku kute gudu umkwana we Timiti ze Migqibelo
  ezenziwa kwimizi ngamine namitandatu ngobusuku ebunye. Kuti kuba kwezi
  timiti akuzelwe kuvunywa miqukumbelo nakupunga zikofu, kuze
  kuzingelwana zezintlobo mbini, kukolise ngokuxatya- nwa ngamadodana,
  nentombazana zilwe zityikilane ilokwana zazo kwezindaka zase Xaba.

  UKUKOHLWA KWE REMENTE.—Asiyazi ke into eyode ilungise obubume bukoyo
  kuba nakuba isibane sisavutayo emnqu- beni wakwa Tixo ngati umanyano
  aluko emkosini wa Komkulu, endinosizi ukuti impembelelo-yabanye
  abapambili ayise- benzi Iona. Nditsho ngento endiyibona isenziwa
  ngumfundisi wase Bhai wase Rabe, onaana ukuza kunika umtendeleko
  kwiqela elalivukele umlundisi walo apa laza lasikwa eburamenteni
  lazimisela elalo ixobongwana. Lite lakuya kucela umfundisi
  entlanganisweni yabafundisi kwaliwa ngeliti, maliye kuxolisana no
  mfundisi walo. Liti lingayenzanga lonto anqamle umfundisi wase Bhai
  aze kule- nzela onke amalungelo oburamente obu- zeleyo. Iramente yase
  Bhai nokuba umfundisi wayo imnike ilungelo loku- gcina i private
  school kuba engenamse- benzi awenzayo, ngeyingamyekeleli u- kuba
  apumele ngapandle kwayo kuba ngoku lento ikangeleka njengokungati
  kusetyenzwa ngenyama akusetyenzwa ngamoya.

IKOWABO.
No. v.

UKWALUSA KWAKE IHANGU.

Waya wanamatela komnye wabemi belo- zwe, wati yena wamtuma emihlabeni
yoke ukuba aye kwalusa ihangu ; Luka xv. 15.

Akusisono ukuba lihlwempu ; nelungisa eli lingaba ngumntu osweleyo
kakulu, ongenampahla, nantoni yase mhlabeni ; zonke izityebi esizibonayo
azimalungisa emhlabeni apa. Asinto yokugxekwa ubuhlwempu kuba baninzi
abantu abangenakubunceda ubuhlwempu babo. Umntu angaba ngumkonzi
engenayo nebokwe le kanti kunjalo nje nguyena usityebi entliziyweni
kunalonkosi isityebi ayikonzayo. Ihlazo lobuhlwempu apo likona kukuti
umntu ebenempahla ayitsha- balalise lompahla abenayo ngobugeza, ngo-
bubedengu. Lomfana kutetwa ngaye apa ngowenjenjalo, usuke wati ubuntu
bake wabulahla ngabom wazihlwempuzisa ngokwake. Alixelwa kulomzekeliso
inani lenkomo awemka nazo mhlenikweni wafudukayo kowabo, asixelelwa
nokuba lempahla abeyibize kuyise ibingakananina. Fanel’ukuba kodwa ibiko
kakulu kuba nanku eyibiza eyibona ukuba inokumanela kwelozwe likude
akumbula kulo, ukuba ibincinane idelekile, ingeyiyo engade imise umzi
ebengeyibiziyo noko. Efudukile nayo waya kwelozwe likude asixelelwa
ukuba uyigcine ixesha elingakananina, indawo esizityilelwayo zezi zokuba
ifike yapela yonke ngangoko ibingako, wati yakuba ipelile waqala
waswela. Ipelile nje ke ufike wavana nento embi, kuba lifike lamkohla
iqinga enza ngalo ukuze angaqauki yindlala ; ipelile impa- hla ifike
yapela nemali akabi nayo ne tiki

le yokutenga nantoni ayinqwenelayo : ubonakele ngoku engasenguye umfo
ovu- tiweyo, zati nezihombo zake zonke omixaka, ozidanga, ozinqwemesha
zakululwa watenga ngazo into etyiwayo. Amakolwane ake abehle abona ukuba
akasenanto anayo, uswele, ukwanjengabo ukulamba ababisamhambela, ati
namahule abefudula epila ngaye akubona ukuba inkomo zipelile nemali
ayiseko akabi samvuma, ati namaqabane ake abeqele ukudla uburara nawo
akabisambulisa nokumbulisa oku. Wakohlwa apo abuta kona, kuba besuke
bati bonke abantu ababesazana naye bambukula namhla bakubona ubuhlwempu
bake. Kunjalo nje ke akabanga nangcinga yakugoduka elilizwe usalitanda
noko linendlala, uyakuke afune amaq'inga okulima kunokuba agoduke
ayekuzibika kuyise. Iratshi lise lonke, alikapeli, lantliziyo abenayo
mhlenikweni wamlahlayo uyise iseko nangoku noko angasenalo neqosha eli
lokuzinceda. Noko alambayo ubesiti ngunakanye ndigoduke ndiye kucela
uxolo ku Bawo ngati ndingumntwana. Usuke waya ngapi ke, watini? Ndifike
ke kwilizwi lokuqala apa nditi.

I.—Kutiwa waya wanamatela komnye wabemi belozwe. Akutiwa uye wafuna mse-
benzi, uye kukonza, uye kubusa, usuke waziti nama kulomnumzana esekeleze
ukuhlutisa isisu sake. Babonakala bupela ubukosi ngokwenjenjalo kwake,
kuba kaloku uyazixela ukuba akasenanto yakuzixasa. Zimpawu zokucitakala
kwake ezi, uye ezeyelisela ngokuzeyelisela entshabalalweni. Ubone
ukonwaba kwalomfo, wabona indyebo yake, wati, make ndincatame kuye,
ndingakuzuza ukupila nalapa; kunokuba ndigoduke masendihlala komnye
wabemi belilizwe. Lomntu waya wangena kuye loluhlobo lwabantu awati
u-Yesu ngabo, “ Hayi ukuba ngenkankulu ukuya kungena kwabo banobutyebi
ebukumkanini buka Tixo!” watsho wati “ Kulula ukuba inkamela ipumele

entunjeni yenaliti, kunokuba isityebi singene ebukumkanini buka Tixo.”
Lomfana uye wazibandakanya kumfo onjalo ongawuxaleleyo umpefumlo wake,
oqondele kupela kwizinto zomhlaba. Nangoku yinto abantu abalolohlobo
nakuti apa, abangati badalelwe kupela ukudla nokusela nokuxamla
ubuncwane bomhlaba ; baninzi abasongwayo ukuba bangaguquki bakubona
ukonwaba nemihlali yababantu abangateni nelizwi lika Tixo. Baninzi
abantu abangeba bayakolwa ku Kristu, abangeba bayalamkela ilizwi lika
Tixo, abangeba bayayiva lendlala yompefumlo babuye ezonweni zabo,
kuseloko basuke banqandwe kubona intlalo ukuba ntle yasemhlabeni yabantu
abange makolwa. Bako abantu abati, nanku unantsi engaxalele nto, ehleli
kamnandi, emzini wake, engakatazeke nganto nakuba engakoliwe, ndingaba
ndenza ntoni ukuti ndifumane ndizibandezele ngomsebenzi
ongayikundivelisela nto, ndingafumane gxebe ndizenze igqobokana
elixwebileyo, ndibuncame ubuntu, ndipume emihlalini yakowetu, ndaye ke
ndingayikufumana lungelo ibonakalayo; amagqoboka ayafa nawo, akanandyebo
igqitiseleyo, ayahlelwa zinxwaleko nawo, kanye masendihlala ndingakolwa
yilento, ndingafumane ndizibulale, ukuba ndite ndafa andisokufa ndedwa
ndofa nabanye, nasesihogweni apo ndohlala, kuba ikwa ngumzi nalowo,
ukwamiwe nawo andisokuba ndodwa kona. Bako abantu abateta indawo
ezinjalo, endike ndabeva nam besitsho, abantu abakangela kupela
kulentlalo yabanye, bati ke banamatele kokukona ezinkohlakalweni zobu
bomi. Wena mntu utshoyo ukuteta yazi okokuba uc'asa u-Tixo ngokokutsho
kwako, seletshilo kade ke ukuti, “ Uhlanga nekomkulu elingavumiyo
ukundizukisa liyakubanga,” ke nawe ukuba u-Tixo akumvumi wofumanana
nomvuzo wako.

UKUMKA KOMHLA.

Kute kwakupindwa ukuhlanganwa ukumka ko­mhla, kwaleseshwa amapepa avela
kubafundisi bamacala ngamacala abonisa ukuba elo nelo litini na
ngokunyanzela abantu ukuba bafundise usapo Iwabo. Kulomapepa kubonakele
ukuba londawo onke amacala ayieingile kakulu, yaza ke ngoko ayabi
saxoxwa kakulu entlanganisweni yabafu- ndisi. Kugqitywe kwelokuti makaze
amacala ngamacala abuye aye kuyixoxa, akangele ukuba wona ati
ngekusitiwanina ? ize ke indawo ayaku- gqiba kuyo iziswe kwelandelayo
intlanganiso.

Emva kwale indawo kulandele into etetwa nga­macala ngamacala
ngenkantini. Kubonakele uku­ba abafundisi balingile ukuyi pengulula into
yokunxila. Ixoxwe londawo kwada kwacitakalwa okwalomini ingafezwanga,
kwase kusitiwa yotatyatwa ngemini elandelayo.

UKUMKA KOMHLA.

Indawo yokuqala emisiweyo ibe kukuba ikomiti enyulelwe ukuba ikangele
into emayenziwe yoku- ncipisa ukunxila, iyakuhlangana nge Wednesday ngo
13 ka August.

Emva koku kubizwe U-Rev. Mr Kropf ukuba aleseshe ipepa eliteta
ngokulobola. Lite laku- gqitywa zake zaxoxwa indawo zalo, intlanganiso
yambulela U-Mr Kropf, kwatiwa malize like liye kumacala ngamacala kuviwe
ukuba wona ati ngekusitiwani na, izeke lonto ibuye iziswe kwi-
ntlanganiso yabafundisi elandelayo.

Kulandele indawo yokwaluka. Amapepa alese- shiweyo ngako abekisele
ekutini bonke abafundisi mabalinge ukukunyelisa, kucelwe nezipata mandla
ukuba zinga kukutazi; ukuba abakweta abafu- nyanwa ezindleleni ezihamba
abantu bohlwaywe, ukutshila kupeliswe.

Ite yakupela leyo U-Dr. Stewart walesesha ipepa elibonisa ukumeka
komsebenzi wabafundisi, kwabonakala ukuba uyahamba.

U-Rev. Mr Holden ubonise ukuba mkulu kom­sebenzi owenziwa ngu Dr.
Stewart ekulungiseni lomapepa abewalesesha, wazake wabulelwa yiyo yonke
intlanganiso ngokuwalungisa kwake.

Intlanganiso elandelayo kumiswe ukuba iyaku- base Qonce nge Wednesday
yokuqala ka March ngexesha lesitoba.

Kumiswe kanjalo ukuba iyure icitelwe emta- ndazweni.

Intlanganiso ivakalise ukuyibulela kwayo ise­kritari nobesesihlalweni
ngenxa yomsebenzi wabo. Umsebenzi uqukunjelwe ngomtandazo.”

UKUMISWA KUKA REV. GWAYI
TYAMZASHE.

U-REV. GWAYI TYAMZASHE umiswe ukuba abe ngumfundisi kwele Diamond ngo 29
ka June nge Cawa. Imbali yokumiswa kwake siyitabata kwi- pepa
elishicilelwa E-New Rush. Liti

“ Ngexesha lesitoba nge Cawa kwahlangana into eninzi yabantu kwindlu
ekutiwa yi Mutual Hall, ukuza kubonela ukumiswa kuka Rev. Gwayi
Tyamzashe. Indlu yazala kwati tu, abantu bekumakulu amatatu. Imali
ehlanganisiweyo yokuncedisa indlu yabantsundu yokubedeshela eza kwakiwa
ibe £18 9s.

“ Lomntu ubemiswa, uncediswe yi Evangelical Voluntary Union emfundweni
yake. Ube sele nenyanga ezilishumi elinesibini esebenza apa kwele
Diamond. Umsebenzi wake unempumelelo ebonakalayo; bonke abamaziyo
bayamncoma bembekile. Kuyabonakala ukuba usifanele isimi- lo ekutiwa
unaso ngu Rev. Dr. Stewart wase Lovedale obe ngumfundisi wake.

“ Emsebenzini wake apa ngumntu onomonde kodwa noko uzolile. Abo
angasebenzi kubo kanye abamazi kakuhle kuba engemntu utanda kuzipa-
kamisa, nokuzivakalisa into ayiyo.

“ Sinosizi ukuba bengabanga baninzi abemi ba- lapa kwele Diamond abeze
kuvela ukuviwa kwake ngu Rev. Mr. Louw welibandla kutiwa yi Dutch
Reformed Church. Impendulo zibatomalalisile kanye abebemva, zabeta
bamangaliswa abebe pulapula, kwabonakala ukuba upendula into ayi-
qondayo, ekwenza oko ngendlela ebingakolisa nabantu abamhlope. Ngokwe
mfundo yake nangokunyamekela kwake Igospile siyakolwa uku­ba U-Rev.
Gwayi Tyamzashe uyakuyimela inkonzo ye Nkosi; siya kolwa kanjalo ukuba
bonke abamvileyo umtandazo wabo ngowokuti wanga U-Tixo anganaye.

“Awonamadoda anempembelelo enkulu kwaba­ntsundu ngabantu abanje ngo Rev.
Tiyo Soga no Rev. Nicholas Goezaar no Rev. Gwayi Tyamzashe. Kuluvuyo ke
ngoko kubo bonke bakubona ama­doda anjalo ezinikela emsebenzini we
Nkosi, nowo kwandisa izwi layo. Akuko nto ebavuyisayo abembi abamhlope
nje ngale, kuba into esukuba ibanyusa abantsundu iyabanceda nabamhlope.
Akwaba baya kuba baninzi abaxasayo umsebenzi onje, bancede ukuba uhambe.

“ Umsebenzi unikelwe ezandleni zake ngu Rev. W. B. Philip, ote
wakankanya nendawo yokuba lowo uze kumiswa ngumntwana wabantu
abakolwayo. Umtandazo wekugqibela wenziwe ngu Rev. James Calvert.”

6 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1875.

Alexandria, June 11, 1875.

Mhleli we Sigidimi Samaxosa, lento ibutywala base Mlungwini iyasigqiba
kanye, seside sanjenga Malawu. Lento utywala ingakwenza ukuba wenze into
obungeze uyenze nxa ungaselanga kanti uyazeyelisela. Make ndikuxelele
umsebenzi wotywala apa kulo Mahlulo wase Mnyameni kumlambo okutiwa yi
Quaggas Flatte. Ngosuku lwesixenxe kule nyanga kwafika Ilawo kutiwa
libulele umntu ngosuku Iwesihlanu kule nyanga kuvakala ukuba
babesenkantini besela kona. Elolawu libulele elinye lalihamba nomkwe
walo nomnye umfo wesosixeko, kunye nomfi lowo ekwakona. Kwati kwakufika
ixesha lokuba mabagoduke wala ukuhamba umbulali basala bobabini nomfi.
Ute ke umbulali nxa etetayo, wayelele enxilile, kwafika umfi wamnyatela
ngonyawo esifubeni, wazake yena wavuka wabaleka wasukelwa ngumfi.
Kuvakala ngati lomsebenzi bawenza benxilile, kuba lomntu ufileyo
wayesele naye, ati ke omnye akuvuka ambete ngenduku nangezililangu. Kuko
namanxeba emela ezintanyeni nasezintloko. Wafa ke lomntu ngemisebenzi
yotywala. Nalo usekoyo ulinde ijaji. Nabo ke ubumnandi botywala. Kade
kusiza into eninzi yamatyala apa ahla ngenxa yotywala. Ofuna ukuba
ahlelwe zingozi uzifumana msinya ngotywala. Abanye banyatelwa nazinqwelo
ngenxa yotywala; abanye abanamilenze ne ngalo ngenxa yotywala.
Ndiyakugqiba ngeliti ize ningakangeli ebuyilweni bam, nditeta into
endiyibonayo ukuba sesifuna ukuba nje ngama- lawu.

W. N. Somngesi.

UMBUZO WENDODANA ENTSHA KWAMANYE.

Uitenhage, August 1, 1875.

Hina madodana amatsha akowetu, ningandipe- ndulana ukuba ndinge ndibuza
umbuzo otile kuni ndikwa yenye indodana entsha?

Ndolinga ukuti lombuzo wam ndiwenze lula ndibe ndisazelelela ingqondo
ukuba azifani eba- ntwini; kanjako ndoti emva kokuba ndiwubuzile lombuzo
ndilinge nokuwupendula, ndibe ke ukwenje njalo oko ndingasongi mpendulo
yamntu.

Nangu ke umbuzo warn. Njengokuba singa- madodana ase zikuleni nje
singaba konzi be Nkosi nje, (xa nditshoyo ndiquka yonke into eyindodana
yase Sikuleni, nokuba lilungu na e Tyalikeni nokuba asililo), ke simazi
nje ukuba u-Tixo nguye ongu Yehova, sitanda nokuba sibizwe nge ngama
lake, kutiwe singamatixo, yiyipina indawo esiposisa kuyo? He nditi
lilipina eli nqanam simana ukubuya ngeneno kwalo, lento siti sakupuma
ukuya kusebenza ezixekweni alike kona apo u-Satana asityikitye; siti
sakuhlala e-makaya sibe kukona simlungeleyo : Ewe, sifanelana
sizilahlela ekuqwebeni imali, asingene ngayo, siti sakufuna amagama
ukuba sidume, sibe zinto zonantsi, angene apo kanye u-Satana. Azade ati
kaloku ukumncamisa ale sakuqweba imfundo. Woyiyeka indodana ifunde, hai;
ayonwabele lonke ixesha abonayo ukuba ayikafuneki, ale yakuqala ukufun’
ukuqabuka nokucubeka atukusuka ayidyobadyobe eludakeni abete ibe yinunu
leyo! Soda siyepina? sozimela pina? Nitinina nina madodana? Usatana
akavumi nokuba sibe zi teacher, sifundise intsapo zobawo emakaya.
Akatandi kanye ukuba sibe ngaba shumayeli belizwi le Nkosi. Akavumi
ukuba sibe ngaba vangeli nokuba sibe ngaba fundisi aba bekekileyo.

Masitinina ke bakuluwa bam, ntanga zam, naba ninawa bam?

Eyam impendulo kulombuzo kwawam yile, Masifune ukwazi Umdali wetu tanci,
siti sakuba simfumene sihlale kuye, woti ke yena andule ukusixbbisa
ngazo izixobo aziyo yena ukuba sombinza ngazo Usatana ; ati kweyokohlo
ingalo asipatise ikaka aziyo yena ukuba soti sakuti

kotso pantsi kwalo, ibe yonke into ayenzayo U- satana kuti yoba lilize.

Ngamana sati kinqi senza umqwebedu nto zakowetu, ukumsaba u-Mtyoli.
(Impendulo ndo- yilendela kwase Sigidimini).

Isaac Williams.

Middleburg, July 10, 1875.

Ndiya buza makowetu ukuba aningeva gama kutiwa ngu Bakise na. Ndibekisa
ngokukodwa kuni base Diamond.

Enye into apa kuko iqeyakazi elibulewe ngama Lau amabini. Wona ngoku
alinde Umgwebi.

Kuko kanjalo no Mxosa obetwe kunene nge­nduku ngu Mlungu.

Petes Maduna.

Graaff Reinet, July 10, 1875.

Siyanazisa usizi olusihleleyo apa. Sishiywe ngu bawo wetu otandekileyo
kakulu umfundisi wetu u-Joseph Kitchingman ngo Mvulo ngokuhlwa ngo
Junengolwama 28, 1875. Isifo amke ngaso waye nento entloko awati
amagqira alinga ukuyipazamisa ngokuyinyanga kwaba kukona ibangakumbi. Ke
simpete esisifo yazinyaga ezi 18 elele. Ekulo msebenzi we Nkosi
uneminyaka ema 26. Ubudala bake ube eminyaka 57 ne ntsuku 28. Kekaloku
wangcwatywa ngolwa 30 ku June. Pambi kokuyiwa kungcwatywa ku- ngenwe
etyalikeni yaza yazala abantu, kwada akwabiko ndawo, ubuninzi babantu
bema ngapandle; kuko nabelungu, amakulu atile, pakati. Kute kanye
ngexesha le 4 wakwela u-Rev. C. Murray esihlalweni sokushumayela watyila
isi qendwana esitile elizwini lika Tixo, esiyelelene nokungewaba.
Kubonakala ukuba upelelwe ngamandla kuba eshiywe ngumzalwana wake, kuba
lawomadoda ebetembana kakulu emanyene ngotando, wabonakala efuna
ukuzomeleza ukulesesha, hai kwanzima. Ubonakele eti nqumama ! elila.
Lonto bazalwana ayinakutelwa nqa, kuba bonke bebelila benkwiniza kungeko
otutuzelayo omnye. Hai ke wada wanga uyomelela kancinane watandaza.
Kwapunywa ukuya emangcwabeni. Abamhlope bodwa bebemakulu mabini,
abantsundu bengenakubalwa. Kute kwakufikwa emangcwabeni kwavunywa iculo
eliyelelene nokuncwaba, waza wafunda indawana etile emveni koko
kwatandaza umfundisi wase Wesileni U- Rev. Mr. Wilson. Kulusizi
bazalwana, sishiywe ngu bawo wetu, tandazelani iremente eshiywe ngu
Mfundisi wayo. Hai yena ngokwake wasishiya neliti ndinishiya emandleni
ka Tixo nase lizwini lamandla ake.

A. Tselanyane.

ECWECWENI EBATENJINI.

Mhleli wendaba ndicela ukuba undifakele la- mazwana epepeni, Yavulwa ke
indlu yase Cwecweni ngo 16 ka June 1875, (Icwecwe sisikolwana esipantsi
komfundisi u-Rev. P. Hargreaves.) Abantu base Mgwali benza into
emangalisayo kuba basuke bati ukuhamba benza lento ifana nokuhlangabeza
inkosi, ingulowo enga angayibona, basuke bati ukushiya amakaya abo,
kwasala umva ngati kusenxuweni, kuba kwasala abantwana bodwa, bashiya
bevuna nokuvuna nempahla ikatazela emasimin , akwabi ndoda inkulu akwabi
mfazazana, akwabi mfana. Ndingasendisiti, umntu ongaba wasala ngongaba
wayesifa, kwasuka nditeta kwati kucu. Into ke kodwa sahamba sitandabuza
kakulu kuba kwaku menywe abantu bezinye indawo, kwasuka kwati ngosuku
lokuba kuza kusa kuhanjwa yana imvula sabona ukuba iyakutintela isininzi
sabantu abamenyiweyo. Enye into lite ixesha lokuvulwa kwayo
lapangelelana nexesha lokuvuna, siti yenye yezinto eya tintela isininzi
sabantu. Kwati kwafika Amandlambe nenkosi zawo ezimbini u-J. Garner Esq.
no

Smith Mhala inkosi ya Mandlambe, ndawonye namanene akowabo. Yabetwa ke
intsimbi lakufu- dumala ilanga, kuba sasi linde ukuti kuyakufika, abanye
mhlaumbi. Bate abafikayo bafika sekungenwe, abase Tsomo, nomfundisi
wabo. Yavulwa ngomtandazo ngu mfundisi u-Rev. P. Hargreaves, isihlalo
sokuyongamela satatyatwa yinkosi u-J. Garner Esq. wati ukusuka waxela
inani lendlu lonke, waxela ke neliseleyo ekufunekayo ukuba lipeliswe
ngoku, watshoke wanikela nge £5. Kwesuka inkosi ya Mandlambe, yati
elityala seliko nje Batembu selifanelwe ukuba lifezwe watsho warola
iponti £3. Sesoqaula amazwi azintloko odwa asiyikuba sapika nakuntyuntya
kwabantu entlanganisweni. Kwesuka u-Mr. Smith Poswa, wati yinina Batembu
yekani imali yinto yabafazi leyo. Nikani kwizinto ezinkulu eziha- mbayo
ezinje nge nkomo, watsho warola inkabi yenkomo, ne £2.11s. Wema umfo
wase Batenjini u-Saulusi Pantshwa wati, siya kudubula nge nkanunu tina,
watsho waxela izinto ezinkulu azirolayo, wanika inkomo nezinye izinto.
Kusuke u- Simone Ngwetyana warola £4. Kwesuka u-K. Madikane warola £5.
usuke u-P. Yenzela wati madoda msani ukulicuntsa ityala malipele
sigoduke, watsho umfo wase Batenjini wati siyakurola tina, warola iponti
£8. Andinakubagqiba, sendinqumla apo kuba naba ndibabalileyo bapindapi-
nda ukunikela pezu kwale bayirolayo. Wasuka wateta umntu ngokuti
malipele ityala lalendlu, wayidiliza ke kwawa zinkomo kwawa mali
endingabanga nakuba ndiyigcine ihlale entlokweni yam. Barola benza into
ende. Bate kaloku emzuzwini ababi sateta asuke umntu ayise, wada,
walenza umntu lalide ilizwi wati, kuhle ke siqonde ukuba kunjanina, wati
akubala umntu wati lipelile sekude kwako, engapandle egqitileyo; basuka
babonakala ukuba abantu abatandi ukugoduka nayo imali, umntu asuke
afumane ayise imali noko sekusitiwa ityala lipelile. Anditeti nto ngo
Njikelana, intloko yaba nga tetanga umfo oqale kwase kuqaleni wesa imali
kwakupela lada lasuka lapela ityala, umfo osuke wati ukukangeleka nxa
awenzayo lomsebenzi wangati uwusongele, ukuba kode kuti kupi kube
kupina. Ngumfo osuke waqumba ngati iyahlabana. Mandingapiki nezinto
ezenzekayo lomini, sendigqiba ilizwi libe linye elisuke lafika
entliziyweni yam, leli lokuba kaloku uhlanga luyazinikela emsebenzini
ongowecala lika Tixo ngokuzalisekileyo. Imali eyayise lityala mhla
kwavulwa yayi £165, yaza ehlanganisiweyo yaba £174. Ityalike yona ibe
idle £320.

P. Matyolo.

OLAHLEKELWEYO.

Gxulu Station, July 25, 1875.

Nkosi yam, Mhleli we Sigidimi ndincede undi- fakele lemigcana epepeni
lamanene. Ndingola- hlekelwe ngamahashe ngosuku lokuqala ku July ngomhla
wesine evekini; alahleke E-Nqamakwe. Lamahashe mabini, yinkab’efosi
enekolo ebunzi. Ukupawulwa kwalo licandwe indlebe, linentsumpa ke pakati
endlebeni. Elinye yinkatyana ebomvana ene kolwana ebunzi. Ubudala balo
buyiminyaka emine. Enye indawana ungati ukuba uligqalile ubone ibalana
elimhlotshana enqineni langasemva.

Kivit Swaartboy.

Rabula, July 6, 1875.

Nkosi yam kwakungovuyo olukulu endati nda- lesesha incwadana yabazalwana
base Kamastone ye Sigidimi sika May. Nditi makowetu ase Kamastone yinina
ukuba nibuye nganeno, nilinani elingaka lokwenza into. Nditi intloko
yento niyiyekile : yile yokuba ngeni fungele ukuba intombi zenu aziyi
kwendela kubantu abalukileyo ; nifungele ukuba niya kuzinika kumntu
ongalukanga.

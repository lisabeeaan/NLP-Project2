UDAVID BLAIN HOOK Inkulu ye Mantyi esabambileyo e Transkei uyazisa

  ukuba njengokuba ngosuku lwamashumi omabini anesixenxe ku August 1884,
  isicelo senziwa kule Ofisi ye Nkulu ye Mantyi, Transkei,
  ngu Mteteleli omele u SOLOMON MONI BABA
  indoda ka MARTHA MONI BABA (ozalwa ngu
  STOFFEL SONTO). Kucelwa umteto onika
  igunya ku SOLOMON MONI BABA ukuba nge
  saziso abize u MARTHA MONI BABA lowo (ozalwa ngu STOFFEL SONTO) ukuba
  abeko ngokwake nokuba ngomnye umntu endaweni
  yake, pambi kwenkundla ye Nkulu ye Mantyi
  ukuba abonise indawo engabanga ukuba isigwebo singawiswa, esiqawula
  umanyano ngo
  mtshato obeluko pakati kwake nendoda yake,
  ngenxa yokuyishiya indoda yake, ngolunya
  emke kuyo; nangenxa yokurexeza.

  Ubizo olunge saziso luya kufakwa kwi
  Sigidimi Sama-Xosa nakwi Kaffrarian Watchman, kulingwe ukuba
  asiboniswe isaziso esiya
  kupela ngomhla we 22 October, 1884.

  Kekaloku ngoku ngenxa yezindawo u
  MARTHA MONI BABA (ozalwa ngu STOFFEL
  SONTO) uyabizwa esaziswa ukuba ngosuku,
  mhlaumbi ngapambili, lwe 22 October, 1884,
  abeko parabi kwe ofisi ye Nkulu ye Mantyi,
  abonise kona imbangi engatintela ukuba isigwebo esiqaula umanyano ngo
  mtshato olube
  luko pakati kwake no SOLOMON MONI BABA,
  singawiswa ngenxa yokumka endodeni yake
  ngolunya, nokurexeza, nokuba u SOLOMON
  MONI BABA angakululeki ukuba abuye atshate
  okwesibini.

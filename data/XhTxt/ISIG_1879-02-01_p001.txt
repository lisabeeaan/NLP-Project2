ISIGIDIMI SAMA-XOSA

Vol. IX. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, FEBRUARY 1, 1879. No. 101.

Isigidimi Samaxosa.

IMICIMBI YAKWAZULU.

Amazwi omhlekazi Umongameli opakami- leyo, umpati wezizwe nentlanga ze
Africa le ise suide, Iruluneli yelizwe lase Kapa, abekiswa kuhlanga
lakwa Zulu, nakwintlanga zonke ezi pantsi kwe sandla negunya lom Hlekazi
lowo.

NGEMINYAKA emashumi matandatu idlulile, ilizwe elitabate elu Tukela
lafikisa Em- zimvubu,litabate elwandle lasa eluKahlambeni, lalakiwe
zizizwe ezininzi, zihleli ngoxolo notandano.

Olu luxolo nolu lutandano lwa’pulwa ngu Tsbaka, owafuna ukuzi tobela
pantsi kwake zonke ezozizwe. Ezininzi wazoyisa ezifaka ezintanjeni
pantsi ko Zulu. Ezinye wazibubisa wazicita, akwabiko sizwe ngapandle ko
Zulu, kuwo wonke lomhlaba upakati ko Tukela no Mzimvubu.

Kute ke kaloku akuba u Tshaka ezicitile izizwe, ewamisile amandla akwa
Zulu, ewa- pakamisile, naye wabulawa ngokayise u Di- ngana, owongeza
ngoku bulala bonke abaninawa bake, washiya u Mpande no Ngqobo kwapela,
zati inkosi ebezi sahleli kwezo zizwe ebezise zingeniswe ngu Tshaka,
wazi bulala nazo, baye bona abantu bazo bepetwe ngobunzima nangobuhlungu
obu kulu, imizi ipela imana ibulawa ngenxa yesono samntu
mnye’otyoliweyo, singabuzwanganokubuzwa.

Kudlule iminyaka emashumi mane, Amabulu egqitile eluKahlambeni angena
kulowomhlaba owaba yintlango ngokwenza kuka Tshaka. Ate ke Amabulu lawo
nangona ebelifumene elo lizwe lingenamntu, kupume amashumi 7 awo apetwe
yinkosi yawo u Betief, aya emGungundlovu, apo beku hlezi u Dingana,
eyaku cela kuye intlango leyo.

U Dingana wamamkela u Betief lowo ne- bandla lake ngobuhlobo, waba nika
isice-

lo sabo, wati kodwa xa beza kunduluka, waba wela ngezikali ngequbuliso
akwasinda noko amnye kubo.

Lomsebenzi wenkohliso kubantu abebe lu- ndwendwe ku Dingana, ebamkele
ngobuhlobo, walandelwa ngowokubujiswa kwabafazi na- bantwana babo,
wapela apo kupele amandla empi yakwaZulu lomsebenzi wobunyama.

Ite impi yakwaZulu ekupeleni, yagxotwa ngamadoda ambalwa, abesinde
kulompi eya- bafikela ebusuku, kwati kwakwazeka okokwa Zulu, wati u
Mpande obehlala ehleli ngokoyika, hlezibe angafa engena tyala, axele
abakayise, ngetuba lodwa lokuba engoka Senzangakona, wabaleka kwa Zulu
ena bantu abaninzi, wafuna usizo kwabo, aba lusapo nabazalwana babo bebe
bulewe ngumkuluwe ka Mpande lowo.

Noko kunjalo nje wamkelwe u Mpande, wapatwa ngobubele, kwati
kwakunqineka innyaniso yake nobuhlobo bake kumabulu, ngokuwanceda empini
ka Dingana, ate amabulu akububa u Dingana, ammisa u’Mpande ebukosini
bakwa Zulu.

Akubanga xesha emveni koko kubalisiweyo, efikile u Bulumente walitabata
elozwe elalipiwe amabulu ngu Dingana, elalite pofu ngeminyaka
engapambili lapiwa amangesi ngu Tshaka, yaza yati ke impatano eyayi
pakati ko ’Mpande namabulu yaguqukela u Rulumente wase Natal, yahlala
ihleli ngobuhlobo, ngalo lonke ixesha oko u’Mpande waye pete u Zulu.
Ezimpawini zobuhlobo buka’ Mpande, kuko isizatu sokuba waesomeleza
abafundisi ukuba baze kwaka pakati kwabantu bake, bafundise u Zulu.

Pambi kokububa kuka Mpande, naye ese- lebetekile budala nakugula, wati u
Cetywayo enxamele ukuba sendaweni kayise, waza wati kuyise makamise
oyakuba yinkosi. Yena ke wadungudelisa ngokuyoyika londawo ngokuba
umnqweno ka Mpande ngokwake, nokunyula kwabantu bakwa Zulu,
bekwahlulelene pakati ko Cetywayo no mBulazi no Mtonga, abafana baka
Mpande; yena ke u Cetywayo wayinqumla londawo ngokuya kulwa nokayise
umBulazi, wabulawa yimpi Ke umBulazi lowo bati abanye onyana baka Mpande
abasindayo babalekela emlungwini waye yena u Cetywayo encinita abantu
baka yise abebeluluhlu luka mBulazi, waqungquluzisa amawaka amatatu,
pezu kokuba bona betaruzisa, besebemvumile no Cetywayo lowo ukuba nguye
inkosi kwa Zulu.

Ngomnyaka opambi kwalowo wokububa kukam Pande, nonyana bake om Mtonga no
Mkungu no Sikota bebalekile njalo, kute u Rulumente wase Natal, watuma u
Somtseu ku Mpande, ukuya kubuza ukuba eyona nkulu ka Mpande yiyipina
kwaba nyana bake. Waye ke u Cetywayo eselepete amandla ne-

gunya ngesake isandla. Umpande ke ubale yena u Cetywayo.

Ngonyaka olandelayo, ububile um Pande. Kute kwakuba nyanga ne ebubile,
kwafika u Mhabalala no Gwayisa no Hlabemncitsha e Natal apa, besiti
batunywe sisizwe sakwa Zulu, ukuba baze kubika ukububa kwenkosi yabo kwi
Bulumente yase Natal, bezakucela ukuba u Somtseu aye kwa Zulu,
ukuyakumisa okuswelekileyo pakati kwabantu bakwa Zulu ngokuba yena
ekwazi konke, besiti u Somtseu lowo umi esikundlezi soyise waba- ntwana
benkosi leyo ibubileyo.

Izitunywa ezo zihambise ngokuti, beziyalwe ukuba zizele okokuba isizwe
sakwaZulu siyafuna ukuba sibe kunye ne Urulumenti yase Natal, nokuba
sambate ngubo ye nayo, ngokoke sicela ukuba u Somtseu aye kumisa obo
bunye ngesiyalo ayakusikupa xa alungisa usapo lwenkosi, aze ati ke
apefumle umpefumlo, esiyakuti isizwe sipatwe ngawo.

Kudlule nyangantatu KwafiKa u-Sidindi no- nKomoyesiwepu, besiti batunywa
kwa Zulu— uKuze baze Kucela UKuba u Somtseu enyuke nabo ukuya kwa Zulu,
aze amise u Cetywayo endaweni kayise, kwatiwa okunye mabati, ngemvelo
yakwa Zulu, u Somtseu ngomi endaweni ka Tshaka, ngako oko ke usendaweni
yo so Cetywayo; ekubeni ke esiya kumisa u Cetywayo njalo, uyaku velisa
obo bunye bu funwayo.

Yona ke Irulumente yase Natal, yawuvuma yawamkela lomcelo wakwa Zulu,
yati ke ngako oko, yamtuma u Somtseu ukuba ayekusifeza eso sicelo.

Kute engeka luweli u Tukela u Somtseu lowo, wasusaizigitshimi ukuba ziye
kwa Zulu ziyekuti ku Cetywayo nakumakosi, ukuba njengalendawo amiswe
kuyo u Somtseu kwa Zulu, umisa ukuba makungabiko nacapazana lagazi,
eliyakucapazela indlela yake, kuze kuti nokuba kuko bani, ote laviwa
ekuhleni ityala lake, wagwetywa ngokwa kwa Zulu, eso sigwebo maze sime,
kungenziwa luto engavumanga yena u Somtseu; nangona ke ukutshiwe njalo
lomteto, naye u Somtseu ese sendleleni, kwati u Masipulu obekonze
ngokuqinisekileyo ku Dingana, naku Mpande, obete naku Cetywayo wabulisa
ngombuliso omkulu wobukosi engekamiswa no Cetywayo, lo Masipula ke
wabulawa ngomteto ka Cetywayo, kunga tetwanga tyala kungamiswanga sono
asenzileyo.

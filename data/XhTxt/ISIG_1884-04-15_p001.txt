NGOLWESI-BINI, APRIL 15, 1884.

umpanga ka Prince Leopold

  NGENDAWO yokuba eletu ipepa litabata ixesha elide ekupumeni, siyaqala
  ukufumana ituba lokuvakalisa umpanga wonyana omnci wo Mntan’omhle,
  obube ngo 28 ku March, 1884. Kwisitoba sabantwana be Nkosazana, u
  VICTORIA, U Prince LEOPOLD GEORGE DUNCAN ngowesibozo, ezelwe ngo 7
  Iuka April 1853. Nakuba u Prince LEOPOLD abengumlwelwe
  kwasebuntwaneni, into ebuhlungu kukuba namhla ubulawa yingozi emhlele
  esemazantsi e France. Ngunyana obebukwa kakulu ngunina, obete
  ekungapilini kakuhle komzimba wazini- kela ezincwadini; ngo 1872
  wapumelela uviwo lokungena kwinkundla yemfundo (Matricula­tion) yase
  Oxford, ngo 1876 wanikwa igama lelo bandla lobu D.C.L. Ngo 1881
  ungeniswe kwimbuto yezinxiba-mxaka za Pesheya ngegama lokuba yi DUKE
  OF ALBANY. Watshata ngo 27 ku April, 1882, no Princess FREDERICA
  AUGUSTA intombi yetshawe lase Waldek. Abantu abapantsi kwe Nkosazana
  kweli lizwe abanakuba bangayiveli elusizini lwayo.

UKULUNGISELELWA KWE MFUNDO.

NGOMNYAKA we 1881, Imbuto yabafundisi bamahlelo ngamahlelo edla
ngokuhlangana e Qonce iminyaka yonke, yanyula iqela labafundisi ukuba
licinge ngezona nto zingenziwayo ukulungiselela ukuhamba kwemfundo
pakati komzi ontsundu. Eligqugula lamisa u Rev. JOHN D. DON, wase Qonce,
ukuba abe ngumlungiseleli walo. Ingxelo yalo ikubone ukukanya kwi
Christian Express eshicilelwe ekuqaleni kwale nyanga siyipeteyo. Singati
okwezetu iziqu sekulixesha sahlukanayo nengxelo ngemicimbi epatelele
kuti enandipeka njengale yanekwe ngu Mr. DON pambi komzi. Siyiqwalasele
sifike sayitanda, nakuba sifumene inye into eswelekileyo kuyo. Ikomiti
yembuto yabafundisi bamahlelo ngamahlelo ite ukuwuquba umsebenzi wayo
yenza imibuzo yayitumela kubo bonke abafundisi. Pezu kwempendulo zeli
bandla yenze lengxelo ipambi kwetu. Oku konke kuhle. Zona izigqibo efike
kuzo siyakolwa zizo, nakuba singaqondi kakuhle ukuba ezinye zinokulingwa
zibe nokuqutywa. Singa ke kweli bala singake siqwalasele kule ngxelo.

Seke sati tina sifumana inye indawo enokohlwaywa ku- lengxelo. Leyo ke
yile yokuba Ikomiti izanelise ngokuhla- nganisa imvo zaba Fundisi
kupela. Into ebiyakusosula tina ibiyeyokuba no Titshala iviwe into
abayeyayo, ngokubekiselele kumsebenzi abawupeteyo. Isiqamo sopicoto
olutabate aba Fundisi bodwa ibekukuba baxele izinto ngokuzibona kwabo
bekum’mango wabo wobufundisi.

Okanye yona ingxelo le yanele. Kulengxelo imvo zaba Fundisi
zahlulahlulwe amaqela amahlanu. I. Ukufaneleka nokungafaneleki kokuba
izikolo zabamnyama zipatwe ngabafundisi; oko kukuti zimiselwe amagqugula
(Boards) ayakunyulwa kwimimandla ngemimandla ukuba agqale izinto
ezipatelele ezikolweni. II. Ukwanda kwezikolo zamahlelo ngamahlelo
pantsi koluhlobo kuqutywa ngalo. III. Ukulungiselelwa ko Titshala. IV.
Ukukuselwa kwezikolo zabamnyama kumazwi amabi atetwa zintshaba ngazo. V.
Ingqomekelela emayelana nemicimbi yemfundo.

Intloko yokuqala, yokuba izikolo zipume ezandleni zaba Fundisi zipatwe
ngamagqugula awanyuliweyo, ayigweba onke lamanene. Ngaba Fundisi
abambalwa abati eliqinga lingake lilingwe, ize kodwa tina singabavisisi
abasateta ngezikolo ezonganyelwe kwangabo kwezo ziqingata zimiselwe i
Bodi. Malungana nale ntloko sinikelwa imvo zamadoda anjengo Rev. J. E.
PARSONSON, wase Komani, Rev. JOHN WALTON M.A., Inkulu yabafundisi bama
Wesile, Rev. E. J. BARRETT, Kamastone, Rev. E. D. HEP­BURN, Emkangiso,
Rev. BRYCE ROSS, Pantsi-kwama- hlati, Rev. A. KROPF, Cumakala, no Rev.
PETER DAVIDSON, wase Kobonqaba—bonke bamxelo mnye, “ Ixesha alikafiki.”
IT Rev. W. M. CAMERON wase Mtata angake azilinge i Bodi ngokuzinika
ukuba zipate amashishini. Obu butshaba kwi Bodi busuka kwindawo yokuba
bengeko abamnyama, kwindawo ezimelwe ngabo, abangaba nokuwuquba
umsebenzi onganikelwa kubo. U Rev Mr. DAVIDSON yena ude ahambisele
pambili ati “ Nama-Ngesi eli lizwe akananonelelo yento ezinjenge “
mfundo ukude i Palamente iwanikele ukuba apate izikolo “ ezinjengezo
bezisakuba pantsi kwaba Fundisi.” Eyetu imvo yeyokuba lenteto yaba
Fundisi ibiyakuba yefaneleke kunene ukuba ibitetwa ngamaxesha angapambi
kwe mfazwe zo NGCAYECIBI, okunene abantu bakowetu oko bebengazikatazi
kakulu ngento zemfundo. Sibabona tina ngezi mini, siziva nokuziva inteto
zabo, bafuna, ukuba kunokwenzeka, bezipatele ngokwabo imvaba. Umxelo
wabo ngowokuba kade bepatelwe. Abahlobo babo ke abangaba Fundisi ngebeke
babatembe. Eyetu ingcinga yeyokuba i Bodi zinokwenziwa pakati kwabantu
bakowetu ukuba abafundisi banemvume yokuzixasa; bengavumi, nati siti
mazingenziwa, kuba kungasifanele tina bamnyama ukuba sipakamise izitende
zetu sikabe aba Fundisi.

Indawo yokumiswa ndaweni ’nye kwezikolo pantsi kwesiko elivumela ukuba
ibe ramente leyo ibe nesikolo sayo icitwe ngamazwi am’balwa ngaba
Fundisi. Bati ukuba umteto uhanjiswa ngokuzeleyo into enjalo ayinakuhla.
Lendawo ukuba ibike yabuzwa ko Titshala nakwabanye abaka baba ngo
Titshala i Komiti ibiyakufumana ukuba umteto lowo uhanjiswa ngolu hlobo
kufumaneka namhla ukuba akuhanjiswa ngalo; kuba kukaninzi izikolo
zivulwa ndawo’nye ngamabandla (sinosizi ukuti into leyo isuka ekweleni),
ze zibe yinkatazo. Ibandla lo “ Manyano “ nge Mfundo ” kukade lisingete
ingxoxo apa esingasafuni nokuyiva igama kutiwa “ Sisalatisa ngamntwana,”
eyabangwa kukuba kusiti kwindawo ate amahlelo lalelo lavula isikolo
salo, ufike inxenye yentsapo ingenwe lutshobo, namhla ikwesi sikolo,
kanti ngomso ikwesinye, ngomsomnye iya kwesinye. Lom’bali akabali zinto
zinjengamapupa aputshwa emini, ubala into ake wayibona ngexesha abe ngu
Titshala iminyaka emihlanu. Asipiki kuba lento inokunxwenywa ngu Dr.

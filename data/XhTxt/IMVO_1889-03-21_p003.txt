IMIDANIS0 E TRANSKEI.

Nkosi yam Mhleli wezimvo,—Sinosizi olukulu ukuba sibuye tina malungu omdaniso wase Transkei, sibhale ngalendawo. Kodwa ngoku kokokugqibela ukubuye sipendule emapepeni ngomdaniso. Inteto ebuye sayibona epepeni ayikunanzwa mntu, iyakuba kujwejweza ngeletu icala, ayikukatalelwa mntu. Umbhaleli wako kwi mpawu ze Mvo ka January 14th, 1889 ukankanya—isizatu sase Hlobo, Edutywa kwa Mqwalaseli, nase Cegcuwana.

Lombhaleli wako masimazise ukuba nanainhlanje asikaguquki ukumbiza ukuba ulixoki. Indawo yase Hlobo ifana kwanaleyo yase Dutywa endixelileyo ngayo kwincwadi yetu ka December nge midaniso ayinayo nento yokwenza no mdaniso nomenzi weso sizatu sase Hlobo asimazi emdanisweni, ukuba iyakuti into eyenziwe ngabantu abake baye emdanisweni kuze kutiwe isizatu ngu kuko nto inga yiyo etetwayo. ngati sazi abatile abenza itimiti zobusuku babonwa benentombi ematunzini, nabashumayeli abatile abahamba emitshatweni, bavume usiso emizini yabo. Susa umqadi esweni lako kuqala, njalo-njalo.

Ngendawo yakwa Mqwalaseli asazi hlazo lake lehla ngo mdaniso owawukona, ngapandle kwe nzilana elalivela edolopini eleza lihluti lafun’ ukubhotola, lakutshwa endlwini.

Akuko mdaniso wake wase Cegcuwana ngokoke asinakupendula kulondawo.

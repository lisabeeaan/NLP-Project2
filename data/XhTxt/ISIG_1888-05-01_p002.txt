ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MAY 1, 1888.

Miss WATERSON—lo tina siti ngu “NOQAKATA”—ngo July 1880, waye ke u Mr.
GOVAN KOBOKA lowo engumncedisi. Ubeteta futi u Miss WATERSON ngokukoleka
kwake emsebenzini lowo, ate naxa sel’emkile igqira elo wasala naye
umsobenzi pantsi kolaulo lomnini-mzi u Dr. STEW­ART. Kubonakala ukuba
lomsebenzi ukuba waye engapaza- manga kuwo ngel’elifikelele iwonga lobu
gqira obuzeleyo ba- wase Mlungwini amayeza. Ubunqina bama gqira abedla
ngo kudibana nawo apa buyamncoma ukwazi abenako, ngo- kukodwa
ekupitikezeni intlobo-ntlobo zamayeza.

Ite indlu yamayeza ngokububa kuka Mr. KOBOKA lowo kwafuneka ivalwa
kubantu bangapandle, ngenxa yokunga. bi nakuwapata yedwa umnini-mzi
walapa zingxakeko zeminye imisebenzi ayipeteyo. Baninzi abaya kulila,
kuba lendlu ibiluncedo kumawaka asingqongileyo.

Imini zokupela kwake zibe nolonwabo olukulu, wazibika futi ngalo uxolo
lompefumlo analo, nokungakoyiki ukufa Ulushiye nelo lifa usapo Iwake.
Intlungu zomzimba abenazo azimbanganga ukuba ahlukane noncumo olwahlala
lum. paula isimo sake, kuba ib’ingenguye nomfo ububele ngokukodwa
kubantwana. Ub’elitanda ngoma ngemini zake, noko ilizwi lake
ubesel’elahlekene nalo. Utande xa seleza kumka emhlabeni ukuvuma
ngokwake, nokuba avunyelwe eliculo.—

Lomhla wafa ngawo Yesu Wati umntu kuwe, Ndikangele namhla Nkosi
Ndikunjulwe nguwe.

  Nam ndingokangela kuwe Ndikumbule, Nkosi Ndikangele ngezibele Ndip’
  ubomi, Nkosi.”

REV. WILLIAM W. GQOBA.

KUKO amaxesha eti iminwe ingavumi ukutobela iminqweno yomniniyo, buti
nobucopo okutiwa bubluza ingcamango zomntu bungavumi ukukufeza oko
kutunywa kona. Elinye lalomaxesha kuxa intliziyo izele lusizi, namalungu
omzimba etanjiswe bubunzima besihlo esazeke sibe senzekile. Lenyanga
ifileyo, intsuku zeveki ezimbini zilandelelana olwe Sine, umhla we 19;
nakwa olo suku Iweveki umhla wama 26 ku April, ibe yembi kunene kulomzi
wase Lovedale. Usuku lokuqala kwanduluka ngokububa kuka Mr. GOVAN KOBOKA
esibalisa ngaye kwesi sanamhla. Ngo- Iwesibini usuku Iwama 26 ku April,
kubb.be u Mr. WILLIAM W. GQOBA osel’eneminyaka encedisa ukucoka elipepa—
Isigidimi Sama-Xosa. Omabini lamadoda ashiye izikewu ekuya kuba nzima
ukuzizalisa kwindawo zabasebenzi bomzi wase Lovedale, nezo zamadodana
afundileyo akowetu,

Ibali lomfo ka GQOBA ngokuzeleyo sicele ukuze libalwe ngumhlobo wetu
olaziyo ngokucokisekileyo, esiyakuti silipakele abafundi batu
sakulifumana kwesizayo Isigidimi. Okwa' namhla sonela ukubika umpanga lo
usihleleyo ngebaqo elingummangaliso.

Ukuqaleka kokungapili kuka Mr. GQOBA lowo kuzekele kuhambo awalenza
ngelangakazi elishushu, elati lamtyafisa kunene ngo March lo udluleyo,
eye kulunguza umkuhlane womkwe wake e Ntsikizini. Wabuya engaziqondi
impilo yake, Iwasuka ulwimi olu Iwafa mnyama ukuncola kwalo, sati nesisu
asalungelelana. Kuye kaloku akakanuka nto ityiwayo, wamana usiti kote
umqala lo enga enga- seloko esela into engamanzi. Ute ngexesha
lentlanganiso yo Manyano nge Mfundo nge 5 ne 6 ku April, kanti
engafikeleli nje kwinxenye ye ngxoxo akonwabile sesi sisu; saza samgcina
ngendlu intsukwana ezitile emva koko, akabi nakuza kufikelela
emsebenzini wake. Kudlule iveki, wati nge sesibini weza emsebenzini,
noko ab’ezibika ehamba. Ebuba

nje oka KOBOKA ukweso simo. Uko naye u Mr. GQOBA encwabeni ngale mini
yencwaba—ngomnye waba celwa ukuquba umtandazo pezu kwedlaka elo. Kanti
koko agqibelisayo; siti esiya kumngunga kwelake kwange ntsukwana
ezingepi.

Ngo Mgqibelo walo veki uye kuviwa isifo sake ligqira, lamxelela ukuba
impilo yake isangene kanye, kufuneka ezilumkele, azonge ngendlela zonke.
Ute kuba ngelo xesha ub’eqinile ngakwicala lokuzituma—engatyi enjalo
nje— kwangena intlanzo ngobusuku bolwe-Sibini, 23 April, wakenyengeka ke
isisu size yabeta lonto yasel’imgqibela. Onke amalinga okumvula oko
kuqina enziwe akwanceda nto; waza nomqala waya uvaleka,—nakuba kodwa
ukupela kokuteta kona kuqale pambi kwo dinala walomhla wama 26, abube
ngawo.

Kubonakala ukuba umfo lowo ub’enendlela yokulufi- hlela usapo lwakowabo
ukuba uyatshona, kuba nezihlobo ezikude akanyamekelanga ukuzibalela
ngokufa kwake. Intombi yake enkulu esesikuleni, naxa sel’icuteka ixesha,
way’engavumi ukuba iye kuxelelwa ukuba uyafa, iye kubizwa; esingaziyo
ukuba ube soyika ukuba luya kotuka sini na, kuba noko sinenkolo yokuba
besingasitelisiwe kanye kuye isazela sokuba ixesha likufupi. Siva ukuba
ngemini yakusasa eyandulela obo busuku bentlanzo, wahle. bela umhlobo
wake, ab’eq'ele ukuncokola ngezimo zemipe- fumlo yabo, ngofifi Iwesi
sazela. U Mr. GQOBA ute kuwabo lowo bebobabini,—“Ndinento endiza
kukuhlebela yona “engaziwa bani kwabakufupi kum. Ndiyafa, andiyi kupila,
“Indawo endiyityilelweyo yeyokuba kwakusondela, ndiya “kuvaleka umqala,
ndikohlwe kukuteta. Ndite ndaku- “ buza ku Tixo ukuba kukutini na ukuba
andenjenjalo akandipendula, koko wandibonisa umbono osimanga.

“ Ndingati ndityilelwe izulu, ndalubona usapo Iuka NTSIKANA luqukene
ndaweni nye kona, lutsho ngeziqaqambileyo ingubo. Bendingamazi nje la
NTSIKANA wembali “namhla ndiyamazi.” Umhlaumbi ingaba abafundi betu
abazi ukuba u Mr GQOBA lo ngumzukulwana ka PEYI omnye wezigidimi abetnma
zona u NTSIKANA lo ateto ngaye, xa abe ebikela u NGQIKA no NDLAMBE
izinto abe- zibona.

Kute ngokuhlwa kolwe-Sitatu elixesha kugqitywa uku- tyiwa,
kulungiselelwa ukulalwa, seva edanduluka ngesi-Ngesi esiti,—“ U KRISTU
ka Tixo.” Site sakusabela wapinda kwa lonteto. Omnye wabafundisi obeko
ngeloxesha, u Mr. GEDDES ubuze ukuba unga angenzelwa ntonina? Ute umfi “
Ndifundele ngesi-Ngesi isahluko sokuqala ku Yohane, iziqendwana
ezilishumi elinambini.” Sifundiwe, wongezelele umfundisi eyake iverse le
iti “ Bona I-MVANA ka Tixo esusa isono sehlabati.” Ucele ukuba
kutandazwe, waza kwakugqitywa, wambulela.

Kwa ngobo busuku xa kulila ezesibini inkuku, esel’ehleli no Dr. STEWART
ngoku, uvakele butshetshetshe,—kuba ilizwi besel’ipela esiti.—“ Igazi
lika YESU KRISTU lihlambu- lula kuso sonke isono.” Ebuziwe into ateta
yona ngokutsho, uxele ukuba kulapo ukolo Iwake alugxumeke kona, nesenzo
esenzekileyo kuye ngobubele be Nkosi, nokuba intliziyo yake yonwatyisiwe
yilondawo.

Kute xa kuti re ukusa akuba ecelwe ukuke abe nelizwi alibekisa kusapo
Iwake, kuba lonke ixesha eli ebengekabinanto ayitetayo kulo. Libe
lifutshane walenjenje : “ Kufuneka umntu wonke ezilungiselele, kuba
ixesha eli lifu- tshane.” Kubuziwe ukuba wena uzilungiselele na? Ute
“Okukokwam ndiba kunjalo.” Uyaleze ukuze lomazwi atunyelwe kunyana wake
e Bayi. Akabanga saba nama- ndla okuteta kuba umqala ubusel’uvingcene.
Ite yonke lomini yolwe-Sine kwanzima, zabikelwa izihlobo, ezikude nge
ngcingo ezikufupi ngezigidimi, kanti kuza kuti xa kuti aatya xa kuza
kubeta intsimbi yesitandatu emaxesheni wa- pume umpefumlo.

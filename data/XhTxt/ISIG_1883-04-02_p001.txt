Isigidimi Sama-Xosa

NGOM-VULO, APRIL 2, 1883.

INGXELO YABACEDULULI MASIKO.

XA nga siboleka inteto yomhlobo wabafundi be Sigidimi, olikonxwa e Kapa
sibala nje, singaba siyakwazi ukuhlinza “ intwakumba ” ukuba sicinge
ngokuyifeza ngaxesha nye ingxelo ebekwe pambi kwe Parliamente ngamanene
awabe nyulelwe ukupicota amasiko nokubonisa ngolona hlobo lolaulo
lunokutomalalisa indimbane zakowetu ezikweli nakweli pesheya kwe Nciba.
Abo, pakati komzi wakowetu, bahleli bezixonxile indlebe ngokusingisele
kwinto zolaula, bokumbula ukuba ngonyaka we 1880 u Governor The Right
Honourable SIR BARTLE ERERE, ejonge ekukoliseni iqela elitile Pesheya
kolwandle, ebelenze into enye limfundekele ngokuti ubapete rabaxa
abamnyama, wanyula igqugula lokukangela ezonanto zoba yilungelo yomntu
omnyama. Ekumisweni kwalo amalungu ayengo:—SIR JACOB D. BARRY, HON.
CHARLES BROWNLEE, MR. W. B. CHALMERS, REV. DR. STEWART, MR. W. E.
STANFORD, umantyi wase Ngcobo, no HON. THOMAS UPINGTON, Q.C. Exesheni
kongezelelwe u MR. JONATHAN AYLIEF. Kwabo bawaziyo lamanene nemvo zawo
ngemicimbi yabantsundu akuzanga kubeko matemba makulu ngeziqamo zolu
cedululo nocebiso. Ayemabini matatu amadoda awaziwayo ukuba
anganobuhlobo kuti. Ute kodwa akupuma u MR. SPRIGG ebotwe kwakungena u
MR. SCANLEN, wahlonyelwa u DR. W. BISSET BERRY, wase Queenstown, no MR.
RICHARD SOLOMON, M.A., ongumteteli ematyaleni e Rini, no MR. EMILE S.
ROLLAND, M.A. Aba banumzana bobatatu ke, ngabaziwayo ngu mntu wonke
owasebenzisayo amehlo, nobucopo bake ukuba ngangena- butshaba kumntu
ontsundu. Ate engena wabe puma u MR. BROWNLEE no MR. CHALMERS. Saqala ke
isininzi sabahlobo bosapo Iuka HAM ukuti, hayi, namhla kuko nto
inokulindelwa kweli qumru. Uhambisekile ke umsebenzi. Aba mhlope naba
mnyama abanoku kanyisa kwizinto ezayamane nabamnyama, baceliwe ukuba
bavakalise imvo zabo malungana nezinto ebezipambi kwaba Picoti. Konke
oku kuqukunjelwe nge Ngxelo ekulunga nebuciko bungena kulityalwa
nangubani oyifundileyo. Ngapambi kokuba sigocagoce izinto
ezinjengamasiko, livakalise imvo zalo ngemo yezinto ekoyo, licebise
ngokwayamene nezinto emazenziwe ukuze abamnyama banyuswe, i-Qumru lite
ngxip inyawo zalo pezu kwalo mhlaba :—

Isigqibo socedululo, nobuninzi bobungqina obamkelwe li-Qumru, bofunyanwa
ukuba bubonisa mhlope ukuba into eninzi yemiteto nama siko ama-Xosa
itungeleke kangaka nemo, namasiko amakulu omzi omnyama, ngokungakumbi
kwimimandla le isand’ukubandakanywa kule Koloni, ngangokuba umzamo
omkulu, ngexesha elinga fanelekileyo, wokuwapula, mhlayimbi
wokuwatshayela, unganengxobotshitshi nengozi ngoku kulu, nangapandle
koko, njengokuba ihambiso yezinto selibonisile, ungacita lonto ungaba
ubujonge ekuyincedeni. Sizindla, ke ngoko, ukuba kungeze kwafaneleka
kanye ukuba sekunyatelelwa pantsi imo yamasiko abamnyama, kumiswe umteto
wo Mlungu njengoko unjalo.

Indawo yokuhanjiswa kwemicimbi enjengeyamatyala, ukumangala, ukuxoxwa
kwawo, inqubo malungana naba gwetyiweyo—elisebe lifumene indawo ebanzi
kwingxe- lo epambi kwetu. “ Ukulanda umkondo,” kubukiwe ngaba Picoti:
bate ke makuqutywe ngobekuqutywa ngako ngaba mnyama kakadeni. Uti ukuba
umkondo uye wangeniswa ekutini, ibe ngumsebenzi wabalomzi ukuwudlulisa.
Nge-

sizatu sokuba ama-Ngesi abengebi nakuwuvisisa lomteto akutshiwe ke wona
kwintsebenzo yawo. Ukuxoma kumatyala okubulala kuko. Aba-Picoti bacinga
kanjalo ukuba ukumsa esiqitini orodomshe omnye kukwalungile pakati
kwetu. Ukatso luko kumatyala amdaka : nasekohlwayeni injubaqa. Isizi
naso sinikwe indawo pakati kwezo hlwayo.

Aba-Picoti, sifuna ukuti isininzi sabo, sicinga ukuba lixa
elifanelekileyo eli ukuba kubeko i-Jaji Enkulu kwimimandla engapaya
kwemida yale Koloni, eyakuhluza izigwebo zo-Mantyi, icedulule igwebe
izibeno ezisakuba zivela kwi nkundla zo-Mantyi. Aba Cedululi kwakona
bacinga ukuba i-Juri mazinyulwe nakwaba mnyama abanokwazi okwaneleyo
ngezinto zolohlobo. Uhlobo abetetwa ngalo amatyala ngokwakowetu
alubasitelanga aba Cedululi kuba kambe sebe luvakalisile olwabo uluvo
ukuba angati u-Mantyi xa atandileyo ameme amadoda atile kwabamnyama
aziwayo ngobugcisa entetweni ukuba amvise etyaleni Nendawo yokuba
inyaniso zihluzwe ngokwesiko letu, aba Picoti bayavana nayo. Kufumaneka
ukuba londlela seli nabayitandayo na Pesheya.

Au, besesitshilo kwasentloko ke mzi wakowetu ukuba lengxelo imnandi
kunene singeyifeze ngamini nye. Amanqaku esinga singawenza pezu kwama
sebe Okwendisa Ezitembu, Amafa, Nawemihlaba, Nawolaulo, Nawebulanti,
Nawamaqaga, njalo njalo, sesoti siwenze umbeko okwana” mhla.

ITYALA LIKA OLIVER LEPPAN.

ELO polisa loyikekayo—umteto—libehle lanyatela ezitendeni zetshitshi
elo, u OLI LAPENI, okumbulala kubi umfazi wesifuba sake, sakubalisela
abafundi besi Gidimi ngenyanga efileyo. Uhlobo esabulawa ngalo eso
sialam lolungena mlinganiso emabalini ama Ngesi—kambe awakowetu
akabaliwe, esingati kukwazi kwetu nakutyelwa ngabadala asikazange sive
ngento enjengale. Kaucinge nawe mfundi—kauzibeke endaweni yale ntokazi
ililelwayo. Masihle isabokwe—esamahashe sona—kumzimba wako ongambete
neqiya embala mayibe yintwaki ukuhla kwaso sibete, sibete, sibete, de
imivumbo ibe kumakulu amabini-— kunganeli, de inyama ibe sixwexwe esinye
ukususa ezinyaweni ukusa entloko. Hayi uyanyanga ; ayizanga ibeko into
enjalo! Yenza kuhle sikutyele, oku kwehla ngo 19 Feb. ngokuhlwa, kwindlu
epakati kweso sitalato sibanzi, sihle, somzana apa mhle kunene,
ngapandle kwezono zawo, bati yi Somerset East. Nyuka isitalato eso
sikangelwe yitolongo wena; dlula ityalike yama Bulu ; yima ke, emva
kokuhamba umganyana; kulondlu inemiti emibini pambi kwayo, abati yi
poplar, kwehla lonto kanye kulukuni kuwe ukuba uyikolwe. U MRS. LEPPAN
wafa ekatswa yindoda yake entliziyo igqite neyesilo ngokuba lukuni.

Ngomhla wesibozo ke ku March usondezwe pambi komgwebi u MR. JUSTICE
SHIPPARD. Kwangapambi kokuba eme pambi komgwebi abateteleli ejajini
ababaluleke ngobuciko babesebeketiwe ngabahlobo bombanjwa ukuba
bamtetelele, ze ati ukuba akasindi mpela, afumane isohlwayo esifunyanwa
ngumntu obulele omnye ngengozi. Lomagqweta makulu u MR. RICHARD SOLOMON,
M.A., no MR. H. LARDNER BURKE, B.A., LL.B., ammela umbanjwa. INKOSAZANA,
eti ngokwasema Ngesini imele icala elonakaliselweyo, yamelwa ngu MR.
FRANCIS BROWN. Ubuciko nendlela awalipata ngayo ityala u MR. BROWN
bunconywa ngabo bonke. Ubuciko nobugcisa buka MR. SOLOMON

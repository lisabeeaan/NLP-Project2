INGOMA ZIKA I. D. SANKEY.

ENYE yendawo abase semva kuzo abantsundu zincwadi zenteto yakowabo.
Ukuba mbalwa kwazo kungangokuba abe nentloni umntu nokuti kuko incwadi
zesixosa; noko ke sise sizipakamisa kuba kungafanelekile ukuyidela imini
yento ezincinane. Abakulangazelelayo ukubona incwadi zamaxosa bayayi
bulela ngentliziyo yonke intwana eseliko ; bayambulela osukuba
esongezelela kuyo nokuba kungokuguqula esingesini nokuba kungokuyiyila
ngobucopo bake. Into esilusizi ukuyi bona kukuba into eninzi yabafundi
ifuna ukuyi cekeca inteto yakowayo, nokuba nehlazo ukuba ifunyanwe
ilesesba incwadi yesixosa, ngokungati kuya kutiwa ayifundanga. Mhlaumbi
yiyo nalendawo bangakutazekiyo nabebengaguqula ezinye. Kanjalo londawo
yenza ukuba simbulele ngokudluleyo oti esazi ukuba akayi kukutazwa
kakulu aze noko enze imizamo yokusipa incwadi esibe singenayo.

Ukuba kuko isikalo sokusweleka kwencwadi zokuleseshwa siya kufika
sidlule esokusweleka kwe ngoma. Tina base zikolweni amaculo esinawo
kupela ngala avunywa ezityalikeni. Sakuhambela kwabamblope sibafumana
benawo—emaninzi kanye—abawavuma etyalikeni; izesakuya ezindlwini zabo
sifike eko ewodwa abadlangokuvuma wona ngokuhlwa—ngokukodwa ngokublwa
kwecawa; aze ke abe wodwa awemigcobo. Tina kuzo zonke ezondawo sivuma la
ase tyalikeni, into leyo ede yenza ukuba angavakali kuti nesibe sifanele
ukuwavuma ngoku ndilekileyo. Kwezi nyanga zidluleyo zonke indawo
zabamhlope—ngokukodwa apo kutetwa Isingesi, abantu bebevuma emakaya abo
ingoma zika Sankey, baze bafumane ukuvuseleleka okukulu emipefumlweni
ngokwenjenjalo kwabo. Tina ke into enjalo sibe singenayo. Siyavuya ke
ngoko sibulela ukuba kuko umfo owenze imizamo yokusiguqulela inxalenye
yamaculo ka Sankey. Simbulela noko singamaziyo igama lake, kuba
akalixeli kuloncwadana. Siti ke wanga angaquba ongezelele baze nabanye
abanako bamlandele, esitembayo kanjalo ukuba baya kuba baninzi abaya
kumkutaza ngokuyitenga, ngokukodwa abase Natal kuba kulapo ishicilelwe
kona e-Durban ngu T. L. Culling- worth, Printer, West Street, (East).

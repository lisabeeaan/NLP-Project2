INCWADI ZABA BALELI.

Ku MHLELI we Sigidimi.—Kuko indawo ku Mabala Engwe eti, Hutshu!” ivuyela
ukupuma komhleli. Andikolwa ukuba abamkeli bayakuluvuyela ololonwabo uti
bakulufumana, kuba bebengamcasile. Kodwa ebeboniswa amatuba
awonakalisayo epepeni, bekusitiwa makangaligqibi ipepa lonke ngentsomi
nembali mazingene ngazinye zingaxini indawo yendaba. Kodwa njengabanye
abantu abangafuniyo ukuboniswa, kodwa betanda okubonwa ngabo; ebe- njalo
Mhleli, esibange Isigidimi ukuba singandileki. Mandipele apo.

Port Elizabeth. J. S. A.

NKOSI MHLELI, kaundifakele lamazwi. Imvo Zabantsundu zilila kuti zifuna
abamkeli abanga 500 ukuze zandiswe. Bangapina ngoku abamkeli bayo ukuba
sifumana bebaninzi malandiswe, ukuba bambalwa masenze imigudu sifake-
abanye, mhlaumbi sinyuse imali, kuqala simcela umpati we ukuba abeke
indleko pambi kwetu (Bill of Costs) zesimbatale singarori, angati kanti
selefuna ityebe mininye. Ngati kungalunga yenziwe lonto.

Yamkela kulencwadi izi tamps zesheleni netiki (1s. 3d.) unditumele
Isigidimi Sama-Xosa ukususela ku January 1887. Ndim,

Hackney, Jan. 5, 1887. MHLOBO WENDABA.

EPENDULA U MR. J. M. VIMBE.

U John Ndlambe waziwa ndim kakulu, wahlal'umnyaka kwa Mabona, wesuk’ apo
wahlala kwa Ngqola, wesuk’ apo waya kwa Vume ukuze umbone nje kwelo,
akubalisele—njengoko utshoyo. Kwakulo manqaku angasentla, umlesi
onengqondo angapaula uluto. Amadoda mna awandibaliselayo,. madala
ngapezu kwake ; ati xana amlandayo, elona gama lake ngu Momile Ndlambe,
owaluka no Mxamli e Ngqwangu, eselezekile kade wona, enye yawo isel’
inomntwami ogama lingu Makazi iyintanga yomkuluwe ka Momile ogama
belingu Mati pambili, ngoku usel’enalimbi, kutiwa ngu Ngubo Ndlambe.
Tina apa kwa Hleke ziseko inkabi zamadoda, nangani sapulukwa lelona ciko
lokubalisa u Notshe. Ke useko u Ngcangwana umfo ka Kwankwa, useko no
Jwarha into ka Rini, into ekade iwela amazibuko, ngazwinye ndiso
zinkabini. Mna nditi ukuze imbali ndiyitumele ukuba ishicilelwe, nditi
ukubalisa komnye ndikutelekise nokomnye njalo-njalo, ndibeke ndifuna
into eyiyo, Ukutshoke, mayela nokumazi kwam u John Ndlambe, ndifuna
ukuba abalesi. bangemkanani nomndilili nangani ndingekupe ngoncolo
yentliziyo yam.

W. K. NTSIKANA.

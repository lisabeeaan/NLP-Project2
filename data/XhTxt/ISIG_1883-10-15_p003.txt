ngamnye mazingeniswe encwadini yamagama (No II,) ze ingxelo leyo

ihambiso yomntwana ngamnye ngomnyaka.

ezizintloko ze Natural Science, kunokuba lidywadi le political
geography.

kodwa, nelipumelele kakuhle.

E-BATENJINI.

UKUBEKWA KWELITYE LENDLU YAMAEAMA ANTSUNDU
E-CALA 20TH SEPTEMBER, 1883.

Kutete u Mongameli u Mr. Levey imantyi yetu esiyibe- ke kakulu
wati:—Lemini yanambla nento esingayo apa iyazeka kuninzi Iwenu, kuba
kukufezeka kwesicelo esenziwa yintlanganiso yamafama antsundu eyayiko
apa kweziveki zidluleyo. Esosiceloke ndasibekisa ku Rulume- nte ukuba
nina nifuna ukwaka indlu ngemali yenu, eniya- kuti nixoxe kuyo izinto
zenu. U-Ruluinente ke utumele ngocingo ku Mr. Eletcher (u-Nocanda) ukuba
mna naye sikangele isiza salendlu. Andazi ke ukuba nonke niyayi- bukana
lento, kodwa ke yindlu yokuqala le iqalwayo namhlanje pakati
kwabamnyama. Ndiyatemba ke ukuba nina madoda makulu noyikutaza lendlu
ukuze abafana benu beze kufunda ubulumko kuyo. Apa bati abafana
abafundileyo bafundise amakowabo indlela yempilo, ukuze ninyuke nibe
luhlunga olulumkileyo. Uqokele wati ngesi- celo esenziweyo u Mr. Levey
uvumile ukulibeka ilitye.

Kusuke unocanda u Mr. Eletcher wati: Ewe ndilufu- mene ucingo luvela
kumpati wonocanda (Surveyor General) lusiti mna no Mr. Levey masikangele
isiza salendlu.

Kusuke umongameli wati into apantse ukuyilibala ku- kuba i title
yalendlu yoba segameni labantsundu igcinw^(r)e ngabo.

Emva koku kusuke u Mrs. Levey wabeka ilitye. Kwati emva koku kwavunywa u
“ God Save the Queen.” Kwa- menyelelwa katatu (u Hurrah kambe) kwi
Nkosazana. Kabini ku Mrs. Levey, kwakabini ku Mongameli u Mr. Levey.

obete umlungu, abati ngegama ngu Mr. Hemmings. Uti u Kleinbooy (igamake
lomxosa lowo) ndite mna xa umlungu ebequba igusha zake ndati kuye makake
erne ndikete ezam, usuke wamangala umlungu wandibuza i pass waqokela
ngokubeta, ukuze nje ndimbete mna ngenduku. Liti elomlungu hayi

ayayinkwinizela lendawo yesisigwebo.

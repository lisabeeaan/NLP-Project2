  nabantsandu impahla yabo isisixakaniso ekudleni kwabantu, bangavuni

  yamadindala ukuya kacela ukuba ancedwe axeielwe igama lake alilibeleyo

  nento le anayo upilile, akanxilanga no kunxila. Ngokunje use hospatala
  apo alinga ukucinga igama lake.

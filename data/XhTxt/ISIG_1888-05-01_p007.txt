UMANYANO.

Nonyaka nje indlu enkulu yama Good Templars (unozala ozele i True
Templars) ukupe amadoda amabini, u

John Geard Esq. wase Bayi no Mr. G. E. Gill wase Willowmore abakwa
ngamalungu eli letu isebe, ukuba baze kuba zindlebe zelo Bandla.

Zintatu indawo ezinkulu anikwe zona lamanene ukuba azingenise kule yetu
indlu:

1.   Simanyane ukuhlaula indleko zehambo yomshumayeli wo Zilo oya
    kuhamba kulo lonke.

2.   Simanyano ekutumeleni izicelo ze Palamente, nangezinyo izinto zo
    Laulo.

3.   Sixuse ipepa labazili ekutiwa yi Templar's Standard ngokuti nati
    situmele kulo izaziso kanjako litatyatwe zi Templars zonke.

4.   Kumiswe amadoda amabini kubazili Beneno (True Templars) abaya
    kupulapulela kulomhlambi indaba kwindlu enkulu ye Good Templars.

Ezindawo zone zinikwe i Komiti engo Rev. H. Kayser, Bros. J. Geard,
Olivier, Wauchope, Harries, Van Vuuren, Fransch, W. Meyer, no L. Joel.
Izigqibo ze Komiti zimi ngoluhlobo.

Indawo yokuqala yamkelwe, kodwa umnqweno ube ngowokuba kuketwe kubantu
ababini umshumayeli wazilo, u Miss Schreiner, mhlayimbi ibe ngumnakwabo
u Mr. Schreiner—amaciko amakulu. Koti ke ukuba aba bavunyiwe yindlu
enkulu ye Good Templars, le yetu yorola £5 ukuncedisa endlekweni
zokuhamba.

Eyesibini yamkelwe mpela.

Eyesitatu yamkelwe, kodwa koke kube nokwenziwa amalinga yi Executive
Committee ukuba ize ikete amadoda amabini aya kuya entlanganisweni
enkulu ye Good Templars eyakuba ngo January 1889.

AMA TEMPILE ANGASE KAPA.

Isicelo esivela ngase Kapa, kwi ngqungqutela ebise Worcester ngo October
1887, ngowona mcimbi ubunzima kuyo yonke. I Komiti yawo ibikwa yile
ikankanyiweyo. Ama Kapa acela indlu enkulu yawo. Ati akude nesazulu
esise Bayi.

Umteto wobu Tempile kweli hlelo letu, otatelwa kwi good Templars uti,—“
Indlu enkulu ayina kumiswa nga- pandle kokuba zibe lishumi itempile
eziya kuba pantsi kwayo, ize naxa imenyiweyo ukuba ihlangane ingabi
naku- kuba msebenzi xa zingapantsi kwesixenxe i Tempile ezi itumela i
delegates [I Tempile maziyipaule lendawo, kuba ibulala umsebenzi, nase
Rafu sipantse ukungafezeki esi sixe- nxe.]

Kugqitywe ekubeni samkelwe eso sicelo pantsi kwezi zimiselo:—

1.   I Tempile ngase Kapa (nokuba kupina apo kufunwa indlu enkulu) ke
    zifike eshumini.

2.   Ingahlangani indlu enkulu ngapandle kwesixenxe se- milomo ye
    Tempile zayo.

3.   “ Ilizwi ” ne Siseko (Charter) ne Ncwadi ne Zimiselo (Constitution)
    zipume kunozala lo wetu.

4.   Zakwanela ukuma indlu ezinkulu le yetu neyakuba se Kapa neyakuba
    pesheya kwe Gqili [Azi yode ibekona eyase Maxoseni?] Yoti leyo
    naleyo inyule amadoda amatatu aya kukangela ukuba kubeko uvisiswano
    nge nqubo kuzo zontatu, lide lifike ixa lokuyilwa kwe Bo- twe
    elikulu eliya kuzongamela zonke, abati yi Hight Worthy Grand Temple.

AMASEBE (BRANCH TEMPLES.)

Kuyilwa ukumiswa amasebe ukwenzela indawo ezise macaleni ekoti
kwakumiswa i Tempile kuzo zingabi nakuma ngenxa yokungabiko bantu
bokuquba umsebenzi, basuke baxakwe zintsokoto nemiteto namasiko amaninzi
obu Tempile obunzulu, kucetywa ukuba indawo ezinjalo zenzeIwe incwadana
ezi kapukapu, zize zinyuke ngoku nyuka zide zifike kwibanga lobu Tempile
obuzeleyo. Licebo langase Kapa ke eli. Lamkelwe yindlu enkulu.

I TEMPILE ZOSAPO (BANDS OF HOPE.)

U Sister Schreiner wayecelwe ukuba enze incwadi (Ritual) yosapo.
Uyibalile ngesi Bulu. Isanikelwe kwi Executive ukuba iyikangele ize
iyishicilele Ikomiti. Ibimiswe e Rafu engo J. S. Oliver, L. Joel, J.
Wauchope, no J. Fransch, ite yaneli siwe kanye yiyo.

INGXELO KA REV. H. KAYSER R. W. T. T.

8 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, OCTOBER 15, 1883.

IS AZISO SAKWA RULUMENI.—Ese 875, 1883.

  Kwi Of si Yompatiswa Mihlaba ne Micimbi,

KUBENI enikwe amandla u Rulumeni, sisiqendu sokuqala “ Somteto Wemihlaba
Engenabaniniyo,” we 3 wo 1879, ukuba ahambise ngokubona kwake
ngokumalungana nemihlaba elahliweyo ngoluhlobo lucaziweyo kwesi siqendu
silandelayo, olu:—

“ Xa zeke irafu emelwe kuhlaulwa ku Rulumeni we Koloni, ihlaulelwa
indawo mhlayimbi impahla yakomkulu, yakuba ingablaulwanga kwada
kwayiminyaka elishumi, yaba londawo mhlayimbi lompahla selilahliwe
mhlayimbi ishiyiwe, yashiywa ingenamntu, umniniyo mhlayimbi umqeshi,
njengokuba kungaba njalo mhlayimbi, walondawo mhlayimbi walo- mpahla,
mhlayimbi ommeleyo ngokomteto engenakufunyanwa, iyakuba yimfanelo,
ngokomteto, ye Ruluneli ukwazisa indawo mhlayimbi impahla esazeke injalo
ukuba ayinamniniyo kwi Government Gazette (ipepa lakomkulu) nokuba
kukulipina ipepa angagqiba ekwaziseni kulo, amaxesha angengapantsi
kwelinye enyanga ngauye kwezintatu ezilande- lanayo ; kuze kuti ukuba
pakati kwezo nyanga zintatu, kususela kusuku lokungenisa kwamhlamnene
isaziso esisesi- balulwe akufiki mninimhlaba mhlayimbi mqeshi,
njengokuba kungaba njalo, walomhlaba mhlayimbi impahla, akufiki nammeli
wake ngokomteto, abonise ukuba umhlaba mhlayimbi imfuyo leyo yeyake
mhlayimbi yeyabo, aze ahlaule irafu, ebingasahlaulwa, i Ruluneli iyakuti
emva kokupela kwezo nyanga zintatu zibalulweyo iwutabate ube ngowayo
kwakona lomhlaba mhlayimbi lompahla ize yenze ekutandayo ngawo
ngokomteto ekuqutywa ngawo ngelo xesha apa kule Koloni: Pantsi kwezimeko
kodwa ezinjengokuba ibe kauti lomhlaba mhlayimbi lomfuyo, izakupatwa
ngokwezi- miselo zalo mteto, imi endaweni yetyala (under mortgage),
izimiselo zesibozo, nesitoba, neshumi Zomteto we 9 wo 1844,
ezibekiselela entengisweni yomhlaba mhlayimbi yempahla elolohlobo,
nokusetyenziswa kwemali zawo, ziyakusetye- nziswa malungana nayo.”

Ngenx’enoku ke kuyaziswa ukuba wonke azi ukuba eziziqwenga Zemihlaba
zisesitilini sase Dikeni (Victoria East) ziyakukangelwa njengezingena
baninizo, njengoko sekuxeliwe; nokuba u Rulumente wozitabatela kwakuye
ngomhla wokuqala ka March, 1884, idibe ke ngeloxesha kobako babonisayo
ngobunq'ina ukuba imihlaba leyo banelungelo kuyo, bahlaule imali
ezingafinyelelanga, ku Mantyi weso sitili, njengoko kumiselweyo kwesi
siqendu sandulelayo somteto.

JOHN X. MERRIMAN, Umpatiswa.

IMPENDULO YO MNTAN’OMHLE.

Bokumbula abafundi betu ukuba kwi Sigidimi senyanga engu April, sabulela
into eyenziwa ngamanye amadodana ohlanga malungana nokubalela
UMNTAN’OMHLE U VICTORIA emkuza malungana nokusinda kwake kwimbumbulu ye
geza elalingxamele ubomi bake. Ngo June sibe nokuxela ukuba incwadi
itunyelwe ku Mhlekazi u Right Honourable Sir Hercules Robinson,
G.C.M.G., i Governor yale Koloni. Malungana kusuku Iwe 15, lo July,
ibuyile impendulo selivela kwi Nkosazana. Asiboni nto ilunge ngapezu
kokuba siyifake njengoko injalo, ze ngezantsi siyi guqulele e Sixoseni.
Inje ke ekufikeni kwayo impendulo:—

  7th July, 1882.

No 103. P.S.

Private Secretary. The Revd. Elijah Makiwane,

Macfarlane, Lovedale Missionary Institution.

13th June, 1882.

  (Signed) KIMBERLEY.

Governor Sir Hercules Robinson, G.C.M.G., &c., &c., &c.

Ukuze abalesi betu abazi ukufunda i Sixosa kupela bavisise nabo
siyiguqulele kulo nteto ;

  Kwindlu ye Ruluneli, e Kapa,

7 July, 1882. Mnumzetu,

Ndiyalelwe ngu Mhlekazi i Ruluneli ukuba ndikwazise ukuba incwadi yako
yomhla 8 May, odlulileyo, uyamkele, nokuba nditumele xa ndipendulayo,
ukuze lazi ibandla cialungiselela incwadi yo Mntan’ Omhle, kunye nabanye
aba mnyama abamele bona, inteto ye ncwadi evela ku Mpatiswa we Koloni ze
komkulu la Pesheya, ibika imbulelo ze Nkosazana ngemvelo, nokuvuya nayo,
kunye nenkoliseko yayo, malungana nesinyaniso sokuyitobela kwabo
neminqweno elungileyo.

  Ndim, Mnumzetu,

  Isicaka esilulamileyo sako, GRAHAM BOWER. Isandla so Mhlekazi.

Rev. Elijah Makiwane

Macfarlane, Lovedale Missionary Institution.

Downing Street,

13 June, 1882. Mnumzetu,

Ndinembeko yokukutyela ukuba inteto, eyabi pakati kwe ncwadi yako 183,
yomhla we 15 May, ibekiwe pambi kwe Nkosazana, nokuba u Mntan’Omhle
uyaba bulela abamnyama base South Africa, abawabalayo amagama abo,
ukuxela imvelo yabo, nokuvuya nayo, ngenxa yokusinda kwake ekugwintweni
kutsha nje, ekolisiwe kakulu sisinyaniso sentobelo neminqweno
elungileyo.

  Ndine, njalo njalo, (Ibalwe ngu) KIMBERLEY.

Kwi Ruluneli u Sir Hercules Robinson, G.C. M.G., njalo njalo, njalo
njalo, njalo njalo.

INTLANGANISO YO TITSHALA E-BOFOLO.

Ngolwesi Tatu, July 12, 1882.

Intlanganiso yeli bandla, ngokwe saziso, ibe se Fort Beaufort. U Jesse
Shaw, Esq., umongameli, esesihlalweni.

Kanye ngo 2.30, Intlanganiso ivulwe ngo mtandazo ngu Rev. Ebenezer
Magaba.

Emveni kokuba umgcini sihlalo ebike ukuba ivuliwe intlanganiso ngama
zwana ambalwa, ucele u Mpati Micimbi ukuba afunde ingxelo yehambiso
yentlanganiso engapambili. Ekute yaku kov’ ukugqitywa yakwamkelwa
kanjako, kwafunwa

I.  INGXELO YE ZIKULA.

u MR. J. KNOX BOKWE (ngesika Miss Ganandana,) ute umsebenzi we sikula
sase Burnshill uhambiseka kakuhle. Yindlu ekubonakala incinane. Imali
yesi kula bemi kwindawo yokuba ihlaulwe kwase kuqaleni kwe Kwota, yabe
yona ngokwayo iyi 2s. 6d. ku mzali ono mntwana esikuleni.

u MR. DUBULA SIHAWU walatise kwingxakeko ezikoyo malu­ngana nokurolwa
kwase kuqaleni kwe ntlaulo ye Titshala kwesase Sheshegu, ezinje ngokuba
lonto igxota abona bantwana abazi hombo zesi kula, ze sihle eluviweni lo
mhloli. Unqwenele ukunga angake acazelwe ntwana malungana noku ngeniswa
kwama gama abantwana encwadini yesi kula, wati ngohlobo emi ngalo
loncwadi zininzi imposiso ezino kwenziwa ekutini amagama abantwana bama
gqiza ngama gqiza angeniswe mhlatini mnye. Ngeku celwa incwadi emi
ngolunye uhlobo.

Malungana nale ndawo o Messrs Gawler, Bokwe, no Tengo-Jabavu banike
incazelo.

u MR. KNOX BOKWE wenze amanqaku atile nge hambiso yase Lovedale, ate ise
yencomekayo.

u REV. E. M AGABA wenze ingxelo nge Heald Town Institution nezi kula
ezibini ezinga pandle ezimelene nayo. Umzuzu ongemkulu odlulileyo,
indawo yokuba intlaulelo yemfundo ibe ko kusapo lwe lali yangeniswa
kwezi zikula, kute nakuba kwaye kuko ngelo xesha inteto zokuba lomali
yoba yintshabalalo yezikula, kwafumaneka zicuma. Ngoku zidlulisele koko
abahambisi bazo bebe kunqwenela.

u MR. ALEX. NJOKWENI unike ingxelo nge sakwa Tini. Ute abantwana
ubafumana ko yise, yabe imali ivela ko nina. Esi simo, ngamanye amaxesha
senza inkatazo. Abantu bale lali akubonakali ukuba bayi nyamekele
imfundo.

Emva kwezi ngxelo kubeko ingxoxo pezu kwe milenga eyasalela kwi
ntlanganiso eyandulelayo, kwaqalwa ngento apa idume kunene—

II. “ISALATISA NGAMNTWANA.”

u MR. JOHN TENGO-JABAVU ute ufumana ukuva nge sigqibo se ntlanganiso
eyandulelayo, beku mele u Dr. Dale ebalelwe malungana neli pepa, woti ke
ngoko acele ukuba zibekwe pezu kwe tafile zonke incwadi ezikoyo ngayo.

u MR. JOHN W. GAWLER ute u Dr. Dale akabalelwanga. Yena ubengazi ukuba,
njengo Mpati Micimbi, umsebenzi wokubala upumle kwawake amagxa.

u MR. TENGO-JABAVU uqokele wati, lengxoxo seloko yaxoxwa oko yati yaqala
le ntlanganiso wonke umntu namhlanje sele capukisekile yile ngxoxo yama
pakade. Eyingena nje yena namhlanje wenziwa ngu mnqweno wokuba igqitywe
kupele. Esi simo sezinto asisesi bonisayo ukuba u Mr. Secretary uwufeza
kakuhle umsebenzi wake.

u MR. KNOX BOKWE ute makaxolelwe u Mr. Secretary nge mposiso yake okwa
namhla.

u MONGAMI ucinge ukuba ipetshana eli shicilelweyo, elingati libe nendawo
ezinga zaliswanga zoku ngenisa igama lo mntwana, oshiya isikula esiya
kwesinye, nembangi yoku sishiya kwake, linga funeka ukuba libe si
“Salatisa ngamntwana.”

u REV. WILLIAM PHILIP ute kokwake ukucinga ipepa elinjalo alifuneki.
Incwadi ehamba no mntwana, esuka kwenye i Titshala isiya kwenye yiyo
into engafunekayo.

u MR. TENGO-JABAVU ute ukuba kuhlale esi kuleni kuko amapepa
ashicilelweyo nje ngala ateta ngawo u Mongami, koba kukukutaza utshobo
entsatsheni, into engatandekiyo leyo. Ucinga ukuba xa kude kwako
imfuneko yokuba umntwana ashiye esi sikula aye kwesinye, incwadi yanele.
Into efunwayo kukuba zonke i titshala zaziswe ukuba abantwana abapuma
ezikuleni zabo besiya kwezinye bahambe nencwadi, ze bangamkelwa
abaposene nokwenjenjalo.

u MR. KNOX BOKWE uvumelene nale nteto.

u REV. W. PHILIP ute maku vunyelwane nge ndawo yokuba: U Dr. Dale
abalelwe ngale nto, ze enze nge ntando yake malungana nohlobo emayi
hanjiswe ngalo.—Kuvunyelwene.

III. u-MONGAMI.

Ngoku bekiselele kwi nteto ka Mr. Paul Xiniwe, eyenziwa umbeko yi
ntlanganiso yase Mgqwakwebe, enqwenela ukuba apume u Monga­mi u J. Shaw,
kunyulwe omnye, kuba engazifikeleli intlanganiso ezi kwindawo ezingelilo
i Fort Beaufort,

U MONGAMI ute kukunga pili okungamnike xesha, nokunga nqinelani noku
hamba, okuyi mbangi yoku. Yabe kanjalo ingumhla ayivayo indawo yokuba
kuke kwako ingxoxo enje ngale ngaye.

Incazelo yo Mongami yabanga inceba kubo bonke.

u MR. KNOX BOKWE ute maku vunyelwane nge ndawo yokuba: “Incazelo yo
Mongami yehlutisayo.”

u REV. E. MAGABA uxasile.

u MR. TENGO-JABAVU ute nalento ibonisa uku yekeleleka kwe zinto
entsebenzweni yo Mpati Micimbi. Akuna kwenzeka ukuba kubeko umsi, ube
ungeko umlilo, ekubonakalayo ke ukuba njengokuba u Mongami engayazi
lento, u Mpati Micimbi, eyindawo yake ukwazisa ngale nto, akawufezanga
umsebenzi wake.

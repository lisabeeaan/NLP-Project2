ezandleni zentshaba ngolwama 26 ku Jan. U Sir Charles Wilson owayesinga

baqekeka akuba kwingxangxasi abati yi Shabluku Cataract, basinda bonke
kodw'a bona ababekubo bapumela esiqitaneni kona apo, apoke bakona.

Owayezibonela ngamehlo ake uti intsha- ba zagaleleka ngesifingo
e-Khartoum ngolwama 25 ku January, atsho ati u-General Gordon,
wadutyulwa xa wayesiya kwindawo awayekuyo omnye umpatiswa. Iqela lika
General Earle lati ngow'e 10 ku February langenela utshaba kumzi oyi
Kerbekan lwagqogqa inqaba eyayi- nqatyisiwe kanye. Kuteke emva kolulo
olukulu kunene olugqibe iyure zone ibambene ngazo, bamkahlela u-General
Earle obepete lompi. Akuba ewile indawo yake itatyatwe ngu Colonel
Bracken- bury. U-Sir Herberet Stewart yena ufele e-Gakdul ngolwe 16 ku
February, ebula- wa ngamanxeba awawafumana mhla ngeduli lase Metammeh.

u-Ru- lamente wa Pesheya, okokuba izaku shiywa yona i-Soudan
ngokwesigqibo sangapambili; kodwa ke kona kugqitywe kwelokuba iye

u-Colonel Brackenbury uya kuke asingise e-Abu Hamed, abugqibe kona

INTLANGANISO E-CALA.

NKOSI.—Kaundincede undifakele lomi- gcana kwelopepa lako lidumileyo
lama­nene, ngendaw^(r)o yokuba kwafika inene elitile emzini wam eligama
lingu Nqose, zada intsuku zambini, koko belimana lifika ndingeko. Ladake
lancama lakuba lingandifumani lemnka. Azake amado­da atuma amanene
amabini ukuza kwazi- sa ukuba intlanganiso iko. Ndazake ekubeni
kuzokutetwa lonto ndamemake amadoda okuba aye kuyo lontlanganiso.
Sifikileke siboniswe umhlaba wase Mnxi nowase Cala osikiweyo ote
wawagqiba onke amatafa ase Cala nowase Mnxi,-—ngo- kunqumla inteto kude
kwango (4 o’clock p.m.) kuxoxwe ngenye elandelayo imini. Kwada kwabizwa
umantyi kweloxesha lika 4 o'clock; kuteke kwelotuba lika 4 kusisa ku 5,
yasuka yonke intlanganiso yabumbana, ngenxa yokuba kwakuxelwa iziroro ku
mantyi. Isiroro esinamandla kukuti u-Rulumeni makasinike lomhla- ba we
Cala no Mnxi, kuba sesiwumi kade, Kuba u-Rulumeni besihlala simbalela
kade ukuba lomhlaba ubengowetu, kwa- bonakala ukuba into endiyifumanayo
kubonakala ukuba nalamda wenziwa yi Komityi ufuna ukufa ; ngelizwi
elinye uma­ntyi wetu wati uyakusisela lomhlaba ku Rulumeni. Yasuka
yapela intlanganiso basuka bona aba Tembu bazicaza zonke iziroro zabo.
Imantyi ebekekileyo yetu yati yakuva iziroro zetu yabulela kakulu
ngoxolo. Lazaliseka ilizwi endilitetileyo lokuba abantu bateta bonke.

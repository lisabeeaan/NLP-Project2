ILITYE LESISEKO SETYALIKE YABA-
NTSUNDU E-QUEENSTOWN.

Ukubekwa kwelitye lesiseko setyalike yabantsu­ndu yama Wesile
E-Queenstown, kube kungomhla we 10 ka September. Babe baninzi kanye
abeyAkubonela ukubekwa kwalo.

Abantsundu baqale bahlangana ngakwendala indlu, bazake baroza ukuya
kwindawo apo iza kuma kona entsha. Kube kuko abavumayo, bepetwe ngumntu
ontsundu; zatandeka zonke izinto.

Kute kwakufikwa kulondawo, kwavunywa iculo, kwenziwa umtandazo, waza ke
U-Rev. W. B. Rayner, wabika ukuba, kute ngenxa kakuba endala indlu ise
yonakele kangaka, kwabonakala ukuba mayitengiswe kwakiwe entshe eyakuba
nkulu. Kekaloku endala itengiswe nge £450. Imali eyakurolelwa eza
kwakiwa iyakuba £688. Kule mali kuya kongezeleleka £25, zokutenga
umhlaba eyakuma kuwo. Kufuneka kanjalo £30 yezinye izinto. Mhlaumbi kuya
kurolwa amakulu amatatu ukongezelelwa kulawo matandatu sesi waka-
nkanyile ukuze zifezeke zonke izinto. Kunxanyelwe ukuze kwakulo mali
kwakiwe indlu yomfundisi wentsapo. Indlu yetyalike yolinganiselwa ukuze
abantu abamakulu matatu benele. Kulo mali ingamakulu omatatu eponti,
angawokongezelela ekwakuvunyelwene ngayo ekuqaleni, aba­ntsundu
batembise ukuba baya kurola £110.

Kute emva kokuba U-Mr. Barrable ebexele imibalo ekoyo kwelo litye,
kwesuka U-Mrs. Weakley ukuya kulibeka ilitye elo, njengokwamasiko
enziwayo xa libekwayo. Emva koko kubeko abaposa amazwi nge Singesi,
nange Sixosa. Bate abebeko bayiposa kunene imali ezityeni zenkongozelo;
abantsundu baposa £36 16s. 6d.

Kucitakelwe ngokuvuma ele Nkosazana, lihla- tyelwa ngumntu ontsundu,
kwazake kwadunywa katatu, kusenzelwa Inkosazana; kwabuya kwadunyelwa no
Mrs. Weakley.

IMFAZWE KWELA MAMPONDO.

KWIPEPA lase Qonce eliyi Kaffrarian Watchman, kuko incwadi ebalwe
ngumntu ose Mampondweni, ati kuyo:—

“ Imfazwe ibuye yavuka apa. Kube kuke kwa­ko ukuzola noko kube kungeko
luxolo lumiyo. Kusand’ ukufika apa inkosana za Mampondo ezihamba
namadoda ama 200 akwele emahasheni, zati zibe zilele ematanga etu,
zaxela ibokwe kona ; sati tina kulungile. Zite ke zacela into yokudla,
sati inkomo aziko ekaya, zigxotelwe endle ngenxa yokusweleka kwenca.
Emva koko zicele ingubo yokulala kuba zisiya kugxota U-Mhlontlo, zazake
zaziuikwa. Site emva koko, mazize zilumkele ivenkile zetu kwa Mhlontlo
zingazonakalisi impahla zetu, zati zona “ Hayi asilwi nabantu abamhlope,
into esiyifunayo kupela kukugxota U-Mhlontlo. ” Kute kodwa endaweni
yokuba zimgxote, wanguye ozigxotaya, watimba inkomo ezi 24, wabulala
indoda nenkwenkwe. Kwenye indawo Amampondo no Mditshwa, batshise into
eninzi yemizi ka Mhlontlo, batimba kunene ezigusheni, uenkomo ezi 40.

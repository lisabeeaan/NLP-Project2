OSINDE EKUNCWATYWENI.

Kute kulenyanga ifileyo Umayeza igqira wabo-
nakala kwakusasa ukungati uqaukile, wada wabo-
tshwa ukuba ayekuncwatywa, nencwaba seligqi-
tyiwe. Ute sekunjalo wavuka. Indaba zake aku-
buzwa ukuba kube kutenina ukuze avuke uti,
ndive kuko nto iteta esibakabakeni isiti Vuka
Mayeza, yatsho kwakabini ndavuka ngokwesitatu;
Ukufa kwam kona andikwazi. Site sisanxamele
ukubuza eminye imibuzo wati yena—Ndipeni
intwana yokutya ndafa lipango. Samnika intwa-
na yemali ukuba atenge isonka.

EZAMATYALA.

Kute kwakulenyanga ifileyo kwalwa into zika
Mbuse zombini, enkulu nencinane, enkulu yapulwa
ingalo, encinane yahlatywa ngebozo emva komko-
no, babanjwa bobabini bagwetywa ukuba babe
nyanga mbini etolongweni besebenza kanzima.
Omkulu akanakusebenza kuba itambo lengalo la-
pukile kodwa omncinane uyasebenza. Ukuze
kuhle lento nje bebenxilile bobabini besele obo
Mlungu. Isisusa kukusuke omkulu abize imali
yake komncinane, aselesuka ekwela ngentonga
yesingqusho. Omncinane uzibiza ngokuti ungu-
mkristu.

Obete walwa nedindala lakufuna ukumbamba
ugwetywe ukuba ahlaule £4 ati engazirolanga
ahlale inyanga eneveki ezimbini etolongweni.

E-Cradock umfazi ugwetywe ukuba abe veki
mbini etolongweni ngenxa yokuba ibatyi nebulu-
kwe ezibela indoda yake.

Ummangaliso okoyo apa, umfazi ka Jonas Lu-
puka Umsutu uzele abantwana abatatu xesha
nye; bonke bapilile. Ubazele ngomhla we 13 ka
August.

P. J. PAKATI.

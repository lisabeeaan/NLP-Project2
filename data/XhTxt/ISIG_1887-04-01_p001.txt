NGOLWESIHLANU, APRIL 1, 1887.

MISS ANTYI MAJIZA.

NGOMHLA wama 28 ku March kusasa, kugaleleke incwadi yocingo evela e
Aliwal North isibikela ukububa komnye wabantwana base Lovedale u ANTYI
MAJIZA. Ngenxa yokungapili wesuka apa ngo January ukuya kulinga umoya
welipezulu ilizwe, kanti koko isifo sesimngene kakulu, nexesha lake
selisondele ukuba agodukele ezulwini. Siti na aba namhlanje bateta ngo
kugodukela ezulwini, ingekuko ukufunisela abesenza ngako amanyange xa
eteta nge “ Nyango ” ekuye ofileyo kuyo, angayaziyo pofu ukuba kukutini
na oko, iyindawo enjaninayona? Ewe, siti. Abaya bantu kwafikwa ngaba
Fundisi besebumnyameni, bengazani naye u-Msindisi ongu YESU KRISTU,
bengalwazi utando Lwake, namhla sebengabo abakanyiseIweyo, benako
ngokungatandabuziyo ukuteta amazwi anje ngawagqibela ngawo kwimiyalezo
ayitumeleyo ngeveki edluleyo u Miss MAJIZA, LO sibala ngaye, ate—“Nakuba
ndise zintlungwini ezinkulu zokungapili komzimba, ndonwabile
empefumlweni, ndibambelele e Nkosini, ndilinde ukugoduka.”

Imbali yake esiya kuyibalisa yeyentlalo yake apa e Lovedale, apo ahleli
iminyaka elishumi elinesihlanu ehleli kona, ezinikele ekuncediseni
kwimisebenzi yalomzi ngokukolisa okukulu, nase kwenzeni oko anako
ukunyusa uhlanga Iwakowabo. Waqala imfundo yake pantsi ko Miss ANTYI
GANANDANA ititshalakazi endala yase Mkubiso, udade boyise, osele ngoku
engu Mrs. GUDULA wase Nxaruni. Wangena esikuleni sentombi e Lovedale nge
1871 wafunda incwadi wamnye unyaka, wangena emsebenzini emibini ukuya ku
1873; wagoduka wonke olandelayo ; waza wabuyela kwalapa ngo 1875,kude
kube ngu December 1886 nje akabanga
sashenxa e Lovedale.

Ib’iyindawo abehlala eyililela ukuba engatanga abe nawo ama lungelo,
ngapaya kwalo mnyakana mnye, okuquba ngapezulu koko abenjalo kwizifundo
zencwadi, kodwa leyo abenayo bambalwa abanaso isipo sokukwazi
ukuyikupela kwabanye nje ngaye. Kuko abantu abazelwe banesipo sokukwazi
ukuqeqesha; baneminwe yokukwazi ukwenza izinto; nentloko eziyitambeleyo
imisebenzi yezinto zentlalo yase Mlungwini,—ngoluhlobo bati abanye
bakowetu
lolwe “ Ncubeko,” kodwa ati wona ama Ngesi yi Civilization Njengokuba
intombi ka

MAJIZA ib’ ikwazi ukulipata igaba, imelane

nabanina ekuhlakuleni indima emasimini, ba- mbalwa abebe ngalingana nayo
ebugciseni bo- kutunga izinxibo zase Mlungwini, nokuhlamba, iyisizile,
iyilungise ingubo nokuba yeyalupina uhlobo kwezamanene namenekazi.
Ekupateni into zendlu asisateti, nangasebugciseni bento ezipekwayo, nje
ngokwe ntlobo-ntlobo zento zasema Ngesini, apo sukuba zifuneka. Inye
imbiza esikolwa ukuba udade lowo ebengakwazi kuyipeka, kuba isimilo sake
ngoncedo Iuka Tixo asimvumelanga ukuba aziqelisele kwelo cala—lombiza
abengayazi ukuyipeka yeyesidudu sotywala. Akwaba bebe baninzi abafana
naye.

Kutiwa ukuba ukolekile kwintwana ezinci- nane, nezidelekileyo, zemfanelo
zako zemihla ngemihla wokoleka nakwezinkulu. Oko kutsho angadela umntu
ukukankanya kwetu ezimpau siti ubenazo u Miss MAJIZA, ati ukubuza—
Kupela na oku? Hayi, mhlobo, asikupela. Isimilo sake sobu Kristu benene
besibalulekile. Ebe yitiyile kanye into eyinkohliso, nokuba
kusentetweni, nokuba kusesenzweni. Ukupilela abanye; ukupata ngobubele
kumhlobo no lutshaba kuwe; inkutalo emsebenzini, ukukonza i-Nkosi
ngomonde nozingiso pakati kwezilingo zaleminyaka kuti lutsha
uzoyise,—nje ngokuba u ANTYI MAJIZA oyisile nje—ngubani na onga kwenzayo
oko ngapandle kokuba ibe bubu Kristu obulaula intliziyo yake ?

Wazalelw’ e Burnshill ngo 1858, iminyaka yake ngoko ibi 29 ubudala.
Uyise, u KLAAS MAJIZA waye ngomnye weqela lama Mfengu elafika apa nge
1848, ogama likankanywayo futi ezincwadini zika Rev. JAMES LAING,
awamkela ilizwi pantsi kwake apa e Lovedale, ukuze ngo 18 ku May 1856
ammisele ebu dikonini beremente yase Mkubiso. Way’ezeke intombi ka
MPINDA, intokazi apa eb’idume ngobubele, nokuba liciko, nangokuyola
Kwelizwi layo. Yabuba kuqala endodeni, ishiya inkedama ezimbini,
encinane ingu ANTYI IO sibalisa ngaye enkulu ingumfikazi u MARGARET,
owayendele ku Rev. E. MAKIWANE, wase Rwarwa. Noyise sele buba naye sel’
efudukel’ e Zagwityi, peshe- ya kwe Nciba.

Hayi obu bom buyinguqu-nguqu. Kodwa isimanga siseza, xa singene kobuya
buzayo apo into zimi unapakade kanapakade zingena ku guqulwa bani. Kobu
bom, hayi ukuba “ ninzi kwezinto ezipitizelisa,- zixakanise o-MARTHA
ngokulungiselela okuninzi kanti kobuya bom base nyangweni “ inye yona
into efunekayo, lowo ke ukete yona ayikususwa kuye.” Wozi- shiya
izihlobo zake zikutaziwe yimisebenzi yake, zonwatyisiwe sisipelo sake.
Lonto inye ke siti isi Balo “ kukuhlala, njengo MARY wakudala,
ezinyaweni zika YESU,” emfundisweni, nasenkonzweni Yake—nokuba uyagxekwa
na, nokuba uyanconywa na.

  WE 1886.

  ntsundu bom mandla

wase Qonce ukuba Irafu yo-
mnyaka we 1886 eli shumi le
sheleni ngendlu kufuneka i-
bhatelwe ngosuku lokuqala ku
January, 1887. Bonke abantu
Abantsundu abazi bhatala ku
Mr. Dick ezabo i Rafu mabazise
kuye; abamele i Xesi bazise ku
Mr. King, abamele u Qoboqobo
bazise ku Mr. Verity.

Umninindlu ngamnye unikwa
ixesha lokuba anga bhatala
kwesi situba singaneno komhla
wa 31 ku March. Kodwa
emva kosuku lwa 31 ku March
bonke aba ngeka bhatali iya
ku tinjwa impahla yabo, ekwe-
nziweni kwa lonto ke bosebe
bhatala ne ndleko ngapandle
kwelo shumi le sheleni.

Ngoko bonke abantu baya
vuswa ngobubele nange nya-
meko ukuba balitatele pezulu
eli lungelo loku vuswa, bazi
bhatale i Rafu zabo zezindlu
nge xesha eli fanelikeleyo
pambi kosuku lwa 31 ku March
ukuze bazi sindise kwi ndleko
ezi nzima ezi bako xa kude
kwa tinjwa impahla yabo.

W. B. CHALMERS,
Imantyi.

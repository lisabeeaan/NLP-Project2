U RULUMENI OMTSHA.

IBANDLA lika Mr. SCANLEN lipumile ebu Rulumenini; kungene elika Mr.
SPRIGG kwakona. Oku kwenzeke ngolwe 7 May, 1884. Soka siziti-gqaba-gqaba
izimeko ezibangele esisihlo situse bonke abamele le Koloni. Isizekabani
ngum’bundane ofunyanwa kumazwe anjengelase France alima imideliya
nolutshaba olungena buyambo lwemideliya. Elangase Kapa, bokumbula abaka
balibona lelilima ideliya njongokuba, ukuba akungapezulu kokuba, eli
lilima umbona namazimba. Abalimi-mideliya ke abakayilibali into eyenziwa
yimofu eyafika ivela Pesheya ezinkomeni zetu ebezingena sifo sinjengeso
ngenx’enga- pambili. Am’bopelela ama Bulu, ngo 1881, u Mr. SPRIGG ukuba
amise umteto olukuni kangangokuba kungabi sangena kweli lizwe “ noswazi
” olu luvela kwamanye amazwe. Ngomhla we 30 March ngalo nyaka u Mr.
SCANLEN ulinge ukuti uxibilikisa iqamesi. Ute noko ezinye izityalo
zingangeniswa xa sazeke zingaveli kwindawo emelene nezo zinemideliya
efayo kudliwa yile mibungu bati yi Phylloxera; wona amagatya emideliya
eminye imihlaba mangake angeniswe. Ngokwenjenjalo esiti yena unceda
batile abanomoya wokulinga imiti yamanye amazwe kweli. Besiba tina akuko
siposo kwesi senzo : kodwa ingxokolo yesikalo sama Bulu amele Intshona
Langa ibe nkulu kangangokuba namadoda angayaziyo nokulinywa kwayo
ideliya asabele kuyo. Kwamhla yavulwa i Palamente u Mr. PHILIP MYBURG,
omele isitili sase Stellenbosch, ubeke inteto eyohlwaya i Rulumeni
eyonganyelwe ngu Mr. SCANLEN ngoluyeketiso, waza u Mr. SPRIGG
ukuyipupumisa indebe erara ka Mr. SCANLEN wati makume kwa eza zimiselo
zilukuni wazimi-

selayo ngo 1881. Ingxoxo ebe mfutshane yashushu ngo 6 May indululwe ngu
Mr. MYBURG osekelwe ngenteto ngo Messrs. A. H. DU TOIT, A. B. DE
VILLIERS, UPINGTON, HOFMEYR, MARAIS, M. J. Louw, no SPRIGG ; i Rulumeni
ikuselwe ngenteto ngo Messrs. SCANLEN, C. T. JONES, omele i Bayi,
LEONARD, RHODES, no VINTCENT. Ubehle wabonakala umoya woninzi ukuba
ungaku Mr. MYBURG ; kuvotiwe elinene livunyelwe ngama 47 u Mr. SCANLEN
ene 10. Waba ke uyapuma. I Ruluneli ibize u Mr. HOFMEYR ukuba amise i
Rulumeni entsha, wamsinda lomtwalo.

Kubizwe ke u Mr. UPINGTON oyinkulu ngoku yehlelo lakwa SPRIGG. Siva
ukuba unyule amanene amane ebandla lake atabate indawo zamadoda
apumileyo. Emi ngolu hlobo:—

bamatyala Hon. T. UPINGTON.

Abaziqwalaseleyo izinto zolaulo Iweli bacinga ukuba u

Mr. SCANLEN wenze eyona nto ib’ifuneka ngokupuma ekubeni sipata mandla.
Ngesi sizatu,—olunyulo lwamadoda e Palamente lube nesixakato sokunyula
intlobo ezintatu zamaqela, angadlulananga kakulu ubutatu bawo, nakuba
elika Mr. SCANLEN belingawadlulayo amanye ngamadoda amabini namatatu.
Amanye amabini ngakokelwa—elinye ngu Mr. UPINGTON no Mr. SPRIGG ; elinye
ngu Mr. HOFMEYR. Kufumaneka ke ukuba la mabini okugqibela ebeselegqibe
kwelokumncinita u Mr. SCANLEN kulomcimbi wokubuyiselwa kwe Pesheya kwe
Nciba kwi NKOSAZANA. ’Nto ke leyo ebekangele kuyo ukuncipisa indleko
zolaulo ezi agula pantsi kwazo u Rulumeni. Ib’ingemnqweno wake ukwaleka
imitwalo yerafu pezu kwabantu ngexesha elibi elinjengeli; into
abekangele ekuyifezeni kukwahlukana ne Pesheya kwe Nciba, ze acite
imikosi efumane yahlala kwelo, akuba emise irafu pezu kwabenzi be
“brandy,” abe ubugqiba. Eyona ’nto iza kwenziwa ngala madoda matsha,
afumene ukuxaswa ngama Bulu kusemsinya ukuba siyixele. Ixesha loxela.

ILIFU ELINGANGE SANDLA.

KWAMHLA yavulwa i Palamente u Mr. JOUTAM JOUBERT, owatunyelwa kulo
nkundla ngama Bulu ase Burghersdorp, unike isaziso sokundulula kamsinya
ingxoxo esibonayo ukuba ipahlwe zingozi ezininzi. Lengxoxo iyakuma pezu
kwenteto eti i Palamente mayizibope ngenteto yokuba “ Umda, awayesiti u
Mr. VAN RENSBURG maube nguwona “ kuvunyelwana ngawo yi Commission
eyayimiselwe uku “lungiselela ukumiwa kwelaseba Tenjini, ngolwe-14 lo “
November, 1882, mawube nguwona umayo kunalo ukoyo “ngoku pakati
kwabamhlope nabamnyama.” Izimeko ezingaba zibange lomoya ushushu ka Mr.
JOUBERT nabo bancedisana naye e Palamente soyekela u Mr. IRVINE, owaye
ngomnye wamanene awayenze elobandla lokumisela ukumiwa kwela Tembu,
azixele. Kuncwadi ayibale esemkombeni oyi Norham Castle—esinga e England
sizindla —uti: “ Siwuqalile umsebenzi wetu [wokulungiselela “ ukumiwa
kwelaba Tembu] sifumene ukuba iqela lama “ satlani, elande ngama Bulu,
selizitabatele ibala elikulu “lomhlaba esasize kuwulungisa. Emva
koqononondelo “ i Commission imise umda owahlula umhlaba lowo kabini,
“ada, emva kodwekesho, ama Yuropa abuyela cala nye “ awayabelwe lona.

olukulu kulomadoda “ amhlope, awayefuna wonke mhlaimbi isiqingata sawo “
wonke unikelwe kuwo. Kodwa tina sikangela icala eli “ nobulungisa,
saquba njengomyalelo esabe siwunikiwe. “Lamadoda acela ku Rulumeni ukuba
angakwamkeli “ ukulungiselela kwetu, kodwa akasizanga Into; atumela “
amadoda kuntlanganiso enkulu yama Bulu eyayise Cra “ dock ngeloxesha,
ecela u Rulumeni ukuba ayandise i “Commission ngokumisa ama Bulu
amabini, Okwenene “unyuliwe u Mr. JOUBERT no Mr. VAN RENSBURG [ukuze “
ke i Commission ibe ngo Messrs. BRADFIELD, BEKKER, “HEMMING, IRVINE,
JOUBERT no VAN RENSBURG.] I “ ngxoxo siyiqale kwasesinqeni, yadubula

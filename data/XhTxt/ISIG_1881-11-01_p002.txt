ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, NOVEMBER 1, 1881.

NGE NDLELANA emfutshane singati ku Mr. Melani Vella ose Cradock,
osingxolisa kuba incwadi azitumelayo zingangeni, zibini izizatu
zokungangeni kwazo. Esinye sesokuba inteto yake asiyivisisi; esinye
incwadi zake asina kuzifunda ngenxa yokubalwa kwazo. Asinguye yedwa ofa
ngezi zizatu; baninzi. Ukuba isijwili sabo besinokuviwa, besingafana
nesika Rakele wase Rama. Akuwedwa ke Mr. Vella baninzi abango Jeremiya
nje ngawe.

Kwi pepa lika October senze inteto eninzi ngentlaulo yemali ye Sigidimi.
Mafupi namhla esizakuwenza. Ngala. Ayakusikwa onke amagama abamkeli be
Sigidimi abanga ubatalanga unyaka we 1882 kwaseku qaleni. Pakati kwalo
nyaka, kungati ukuba imali zonke zihlaulwe i Sigidimi sipume kabini nge
nyanga zonke. Sinikwe igunya ke ukuba siyenze lengxelo
ngokubalulekileyo. Singa ke zihlobo zetu ninga nika indlebe kule nteto.

Kwi sipitipiti sase Bayi i Port Elizabeth Telegraph ifunda esi
sifundo—sokuba imidaka yahlula-hlulwe ngoku e Bayi. Ngekucandwa ilali
ezine, zinga melani, ze iti ilokeshon nganye ibe nabongameli
bayo,bokukangela ukuba izinto zihambe kakuhle. Iti i Telegraph akusoze
kulunge nto xa abantu bentlanga ngentlanga behleli ngohlobo abalulo
ngoku. Sicinga ukuba tina lityala laba mnyama ngokwabo elibanga ukuba
bade benjiwenje libe ibala labo lifana noko.

INENE lase Ireland, u Mr. Parnell, ongenela isi qingata sase Cork e
Parlamente yase England, kufike ucingo lokuba ubanjiwe. Isi zekabani
sesokuba useloko evutuzelisa ama Irish ukuba avukele u Mnta’omhle. Into
ewabeyenza u Mr. Par­nell ibiseyanele ukuba ikataze wonke umntu ocingayo
otanda ukonwaba kwama Irish. Sovuya ukuva ukuba limncedile eli yeza
anikwe lona ngoku ngu Right Hon. W. E. Forster, owongamele imicimbi yase
Ireland.

YINTWANA embana, kanti tina saka sahlala ema Bulwini sesiyi qelile, le
ibaliswa lipepa lase Tinara abati yi Uitenhage Times. Siteta lento
yokuti xa umntu omnyama afuna ukutenga into e markeni (endaweni ye
ntengo) alike aminxelwe. Bati ngu Andries Goodgan um Mbo obepikisene no
Rudman igwangqa nge fama abati yi “Gorie Laagt,” ebiqeshiswa e Ofisini
yase Tinara. Lomhlaba uqeshiswa ngu Rulumente. Benyusene benyusana u
Rudman no Goodgaan wada umhlaba wema noweli gama lokugqibela nge ponti
ezimakulu mabini anamashumi asixenxe ana ponti ’nye (£271). Lendoda u
Good­gaan inconywa isimilo, noku kutala. Amadoda ambalwa amnyama, abeya
kubambelela emsebenzini ebenga lipakamisayo igama lohlanga. O! Ngamana
bande o Goodgaan!

BAFUNDI, kanipulapule umbuzo womfo ka Nzeku u W. Skepe. Uti macaciselwe
ngu Mcokeli lemibuzo:—(a) “ Kulungilena ukuba imidaka itshate? (b)
Unceda ntonina umtshato? (c) Ukuba uko wala ntonina? (d) Ubopa, ukulula,

unqanda ntonina? (e) Ukuba awunazo ezi zinto; kutshatelwa ntonina
yimidaka? Ubuza ati, “Ndingavuya Mhleli undi cacisele undifundise kuba
ndinobudenge kakulu kulonto. Ndingosa kangelelwe eku tshateni ndinga
ndingatshata ndiqondile; andifuni kuxelelwa lomini hleze ndiswele ixa
lokubuza lo mini.” Lombuzo sowuyekela u Mr. I. Williams Wauchope, sisiti
nanko umfana ka Nzeku. Ukuba asiposisi kumqonda, ungumlomo weqela elo
liwusukela umtshato njengama kwenkwe esukela “imiqatu nama badi.” Soka
situle tina marwanqa.

ABALESESHI beli pepa boqonda ukuba kwi ndawo ngendawo zeli lanamhla
izipitipiti zase Bayi simana ukuti zezama nxila. Siyala tina ukuba
kutiwe yimfazwe yama Xosa nesibaxa apa saba Mbo ekutiwa ngama Mfengu.
Siti aba bantu bebesilwa base Bayi, “zidyasi” apa zase Bitsini, abanye
ngabati “molo” kuma kaya abo, abazalise a Maxamba ase Bayi. Eyetu imvo
enyanisekileyo yeyokuba, nabati bangama Xosa nabati bangama Mfengu
bafumana belibiza igama lama Xosa nama Mfengu. Umteto wesitatu kweli
shumi uti: “Akusaku fumana ulibize igama lika Yehova u Tixo wako etc.”
Kula mahilihili ase Bayi siti “Msani uku fumana nilibize igama lama Xosa
nelama Mfengu emiqombotini nasezi brandini zenu. Siyazi bani­nzi, kweli
lase ma Xoseni abahlungiswe intliziyo zizenzo ezi zama hlazo ezenziwa
egameni labo; kodwa ingwevu zakwa Xosa nezase Mbo ezi kweli zinengqondo
ngokwaneleyo ukuba zazi ukuba, ama nxila la zinto ongafika zintyontya
amakwelo ezinkati, zisiti “Mna ndingu Mngqika, into yase Magqwashini; um
Cira, mhlaimbi um Nywabe,” amanye “Mna ndingu Mbo, u Ntsele, mhlaimbi
ndingu Radebe, mhlaimbi u Makaluza, mhlaimbi ndingu Jili, u Masengwa, u
Qabazi.” One ngqondo umntu akateni nezi zibongo kuba zipuma kwi
sihilitwa esashiya ikaya laso. He kungolu hlobo tina apa esiyibonayo
ihambiso yabenzi zipitipiti base Bayi.

INTWANA ehlekisayo kweli dabi lama dakwa ase Bayi ebeliko ngale nyanga
ifileyo, soyi balisela abalesi betu. E Ofisini e Bayi kute ngomnye umhla
kwafakwa ipolisa emkumbini wama ngqina, liza kubuzwa ngezinto zayo
lempitimpiti. Kutiwa i ofisi yasuka yazala tu ngabantu abamnyama. U Mr.
Wylde, umantyi, ufumane wabekisa umbuzo kwi kostawuli elo; wati, Unamntu
umnakanayo na kwaba bakoyo ngoku apa? Libasi ngasingile ikostawuli. Suka
intloko emva kwe ntloko yatoba pantsi; yanika inqentsu umantyi; yapuma.
Bubuso obumhlope obuhlanu obada basala e ofisini, emva kwe five minutes
ubuziwe lombuzo. Esuka awa yintsini ama Ngesi. Efanele.

SINOSIZI ukuva ukuba pakati kwamadabi ase Bayi bekuko nabautu aba ngabe
ramente. Ababantu singati i Nkosi yabo bayi ngcatshile. Njengoko besi
cinga tina lemfazwe igxekise lonke igoma laba ntsundu emehlweni ama
Ngesi; wona awaketi kuba; lo uyi Mfengu lo ngum Xosa. Kwi ntlanganiso
yaba pati micimbi bedolopu lase Bayi, sinosizi ukuti, umhlobo walo lonke
uluntu olumnyama u Mr. John Mackay, M.L.A., acatshukiswe kakulu kuba
amanene onke apikela ukuti “Yiyo leyo ke into ohlala uyifuna xa
utetelela imidaka.” Niyabona na ukuhlaziswa kwaba hlobo betu
ngemisebenzi kwayetu, ebolileyo?

AKUKABI kade kakulu sasite apa kwa Rulumente, ukulwa okudubula entweni
ebonakalayo kokwa mazwi—ukubonisana; okwe nduku, ne minqayi, ne mipu
kuvelisa ukwahlutwa kwemihlaba nentsizi ezilandela abantu abangama
dungudwane. Siyavuya ukubona ukuba ama Mpondo ayiqondile lento. U
Mqikela inkosi yesi sizwe, wabelwa u Rev. Oxley Oxland ukuba abe
ngamehlo ka Rulumente ema Mpondweni. Ekuhambeni kwexesha kufumanekile
ukuba abevani. Ite yakufika le Ruluneli wabalela kuyo u Mqikela,
nencwadi leyo yabalwa ngu Mr. Josiah Jenkins inkosana entsundu
efundileyo. Ukumelana kwalo madoda kucaziwe, saza senziwa isicelo sokuba
akutshwe u Mr. Oxland. Akudlulanga xesha lide senziwe esi sicelo
wakutshwa u Mr. Oxland. Asingeni engxoxweni yokuba waye lungisile
wayenga lungisanga sinina u Mr Oxland; sibonisa kodwa amandla, nelungelo
analo um-Rulumente. Xa kuko into ebakalisayo abantu abantsundu libanzi
ilungelo lokubalela u Mhlekazi i Ruluneli.

“NINGA posi iperule zenu ezi hangwini.” Angade atsho umntu ebekiselele
kwaba mnyama bale Koloni. Inteto yetu nge “Mbumba yama nyama”
akudlulanga nyanga siyi bekisile kusapo olucitakeleyo lwakwa Xosa
nolwase Mbo, sevakusitiwa bebe qekezana intloko e Bayi ngohlobo olubi
ebusweni bezulu uobo mhlaba. Nazo ke iperule ziposiwe zanyatelwa,
zaqwengwa zi “hangu” zase Bayi. Asisokwe tuka tina ukuva ukuba nelicebo,
nokuba ali qwengwanga kodwa linyatelwe zezi “hangu” zibala lifana
neletu. Nantsi ke into. Kuko lentlanganiso yaba Picoti ba Masiko ne
Miteto yaba Ntsundu ehamba-hambayo kweli lakwa Xosa, ezakusa kwela
pesheya kwe Nciba. ifuna ingxelo ngazo zonke izinto zakwa Rulumente
ezibekisele kuti midaka. Ike yati enye indodana kwa fundileyo, bekunga
njanina zite ingwevu zohlanga zanyula amadoda amadala anentloko,
nobuciko, kunye namadodana amatsha akwanjalo, aza atunyelwa kule
ntlanganiso inge micimbi yetu, aya kupalaza imvo yohlanga oluntsundu
pambi kwayo. Lento ibifanele ukwenziwa; imbuzo ngoku yeyokuba ngubanina
oya kuyiqala? Ibinga qalwa ngabantu abahlangeneyo kwi ntlanganiso enje
ngale kutiwa ngoku yeyo Titshala, izakubizwa emzuzwini ngokuba yeyaba
Ntsundu aba Cubekileyo. Asitandabuzi ibiya kwenziwa lento ukuba aba ba
Picoti ba Masiko bebeya kuhlangana zekude kube lixesha lentlanganiso
eyakubase Mgqakwebe mayela kolwe 11 ku January, 1882.

“ABANTSUNDU bafuna indaba ezingapezulu ngenteto yabo,” itshilo enye
indodana yebala letu ku mshicileli otile ohlala e King William’s Town,
xa yabiye kuti mashicilele ipepa elitsha lenteto yesi Xosa. Lendodana
kutiwa isuka apa e Lovedale, yaka nayo yahambisa umsebenzi woku Cokela
indaba ze Sigidimi esi. Le mambane iti ingumlomo wendimbane yaba mnyama.
Asimkanyezi lomhlobo wetu ngento ayitetayo ngenxana lendaba abanalo
abantu bakowetu. Xa kodwa ati eh nxana ulifumene ukuba liko xa waye ngu
Mcokeli we Sigidimi, masiti fanukuba wayese nkungwini ngokuhanjiswa
kweli pepa, kuba ukuba laliko inxana elinjalo ngebabengasi lahlanga
abaninzi i Sigidimi becatshukiswa siso, ngeza hlaulwayo imali zaso.
Asifumani nxano lendaba tina kwinto eninzi yaba mnyama, kuba sasite
kwamzuzu, asali kuli shicilela kabini ngenyanga, nangeveki, ukuba
bayakuti abamkeli beli pepa bahlaule, aze amanani ande kanjako. Kwangesi
sizatu. siyala ukuba lemambane iti ingumlomo waba mnyama. Akuko
mshicileli uyakuke akupe ipepa lama Xosa engadange wabona ukuba
Isigidimi sixaswa kakuhle yimidaka ngu notshe. Singati apa, umshieileli
unike igunya lokuba siti wonke umntu ekufunyenwe ukuba aka sali hlauleli
ipepa lake, alisokubuya litunyelwe kuye, kuba akuna kufunyanwa kube
kugqitywa izi tampo ngokutunyelwa kubantu abangazi niki mizamo nge
Sigidimi.

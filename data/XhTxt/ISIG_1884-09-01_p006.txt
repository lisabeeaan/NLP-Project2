  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1884.

ASIBANGA nakuzifumanela isituba incwadi zala manene —Mr. P. Kakaza,
(Gaga) no Mr. J. T. Gqiba, (Cathcart.)

INGQAKAQA.—Esi sifo sivakala ukuba sesiko kwezi zixeko apa e
Koloni,—Somerset East, Aliwal North, Colesberg, ngase Queenstown :
Esibabambileyo basengabanye, ngababini, nabatatu. Asinyaniso ukuba iko e
Qonce.

AMASELA AMAHASHE.— Lwifama engakwa Komani, kubiwe amahashe amahlanu
kutsha nje. Ifama akutshwe kuyo amahashe la ibibiyelwe ngocingo, kantike
noko abiwe wona kunjalo.

UTYWALA.—E Kapa, sisand’ ukuva ngomfazi otshe wagqibela enxilile. Lide
lafika igqira kuqondakala ukuba akakaqabuki, usanxila. Akahlalanga xesha
lide uqaukele kweso simo.

U REV. MACKENZIE obe kwelama Bechuana, usand’ ukufika e Kapa apo abizwe
kona. Ucingo oluvela pesheya beluxela ngokungazeki kwesizatu kona angaba
ubizelwe osna u Mackenzie.

OHLAHLWE INTLOKO —Enye indoda kulamacala angapakati, ibulewe yenye ebize
kuba ibokwe. Ite indoda leyo ilisela yaqala ngokumgibisela ngelitye
entloko omnye lowo, ateke akuwa yamqekeza intloko ngamatye wada wafa.

KWELAMA SWAZI.—Omnye wama Sheptone usand’ ukubuya kwelama Swazi, apo
wayeye kukangela ukuba akangebi namvumelona yokumba igolide, kwelo lizwe
lityebe kunene yi golide. A kavunyelwanga kodwa ngama Swazi.

E AUSTRALIA lisabalele lenza into yanye. Inkomo, igusha nezinye impahla
ziyapela. Elinye ifama lifelwe nga 100,000 egusha, elinye ngamawaka
asitoba enkomo, elinye ngamakulu amahlanu, elinye ngamakulu amane,
njalo- njalo.

NOKO sekunzima kangakanje ngenxa yendlala nokusweleka komsebenzi, yona
into yokubiwa kwe dayimani nemali ezisukuba zisiya kwindawo ngendawo
ayikabiko. Ngenye imini kwinqwelana yamahashe eyayipuma e Pretoria isiya
e Kimberly kwakuko £12,000.

EZASE BLOEMFONTEIN.—U Samuel Moroko kunyenamahlanu kumapakati ake,
sebeke bangeniswa emkombeni ngama Bulu ukuba bapendule ngokufa kuka
Sepinare. Nokoke asikeva sipelo salo ityala elo, kuba noko alikatetwa
kanye.

IZINTO zo M’BONISO.—Udodana olufunda amashishini ngamashishini, kunye no
mtinjana ofunda kwa amashishini apa e Lov‘dale, lwenze intlobo ntlobo
zezinto eziya em’bonisweni e Kapa. Inxenye yezinto ezenziwe ngalamaqela
omabini iyabukeka kanye.

EZASEMA MPONDWENI.—Amampondo kanye asaquba ubumpi ngaku Mlungu. Siva
ukuba ngenye imini ahluta amahashe o Mlungu owayeya kurweba ngawo kwelo
la Mampondo. Aza okunye abamba u Mlungu emhlabeni wawo, ake amyala
ukum’beta oku.

UKUGQOGQWA KWEZINDLU.—Ngase Bayi sifunda ukuba ade amaxesha alandelelana
amabini, kupikelwe ukugqogqwa izindlu zaba Fundisi. Kweyokuqala indlu
agoqulula efuna imali ade ancama, kweye sibini abonelelwa esagqogqa
abanjwa. Kanye ezi zinto zihla ngexa lenkonzo.

EZASE LUSUTU.—Siva ukuba u Letsea inkosi ya Besutu, inendawo evakala,
njengokuba efile nje u Sepinare, ibanga ukuba lomhlaba ulunge naye kuba
yayingumhlaba owanikwa ku Moroko ngu Mshweshwe. utsho ke elake. Asazike
kodwa ukuba wotananina nama Bulu.

e Monti ukuba akungebiko ndawo ingayifumanayona yokubala. ]fike kwesinye

Ibambe indlela kwaoko indoda leyo kwakuba lapo.

OFE NGEBAQO.—E Rini kute ngomnye umhla inkosikazi abati ngu Mrs. James
iye venkileni itile ihamba nenkwe-nkwana yayo. Ute kumzuzwana wavakala
ectla ukuba ake apiwe isihlalo ahlale uziva engapilile. Ute akuhlala
pantsi kwabonakala ukuba umntu uyafa. Usiwe kwindlu ebikufupi yamayeza
apo ahlale imizuzwana em’balwa waqauka.

OBULELE INGWE.—E Rini u Mxosa usand’ ukubulala ingwe. Ute ebegawula
ehlatini wabona umtinzi ugqita entloko yake, ute akubeka wabona ukuba
yingwe seyilungiselele ukumtsibela. Ute guququ wehlasi izenjana. Kwaoko
izibutile ingwe, wecebu wayeka ngezembe entanyeni, ite kuba seyipantsi
yakupinda ayabi senza mtsi upi, waseleyigqibela kwalapo.

E GQUNUBE.—Kwifama ekulomandla wase Gqunube kusandukubetwa u Mlungukazi
lisela. Yati ingeko indoda inkosikazi yeva kukala inkuku. Ipumile ibone
uxwabiyo lwendoda selungxabalaze pezu kwendlu yenkuku. Lute lwakum’bona
lwaselutanda kuye, lute ngelumgqibele kwalamla izinja ezite zatanda
endodeni leyo, eselisuke yazikusela ngokubaleka.

INGOZI KA LOLIWE.—Sifumana kwindaba ezipuma pesheya e England ukuba
kusand’ ukuhla ingozi enkulu kunene. Wati ngamhla utile uloliwe
owayepuma e Mancehester esinga e London wahlelwa yingozi endleleni. Ute
akubona ukuba kumaxa akaulezayo kanye kwapuka i axle yenye yevili
zenqwelo into ke leyo ebangele ukuba zipambuke inqwelo zibukuqe inxenye,
ezintatu zona zatyumka zaba ngamasuntswana. Kulo ngozi kufe amashumi
omabini abantu, amashumi omahlanu angxwelerwa kodwa.

U MR. HOLDEN WASE SOMERSET, wati kwi Ntlanganiso Yolutsha Yengxoxo
wetupa kwindawo yokuba umtimba ungangeni maxa onke ezintlanganisweni
apo, kuba wosuke ufunde okukulu. Yekake mntundini ite yakuba sengxoxweni
londawo, usazi isapompolo, kwasuka kwalibubu pezu kwentloko yalondoda.
yabeta nayo, sitemba, yazibona uboni. Lento yokucasa amaledi
ezintlanganisweni lupau Lwala maxesha. Ihlazo elagubungela u Mr. Caleb
Mahlutshana kweyokugqibela intlanganiso yo Manyano e Bofolo akungenisa
lendawo laba likulu.

UDODANA OLUQEQESHWAYO.

Ngolwe-Sibini, 26 August, inyatelo elinganqwenelwe nangubani
kwabaqeqesha ulutsha, litatyatwe ngum’fundisi opete isikolo sase Mtwaku.
Amakwenkwe amashumi mabini anesihlanu kufuneke egxotwe mini ’nye
ngokungeva nangokungalulameli abo bapete umzi. Situnyelwe amagama
alentsapo ixwalileyo esiwavakalisa ngetemba lokuba usapo olusezikolweni
loyaleka Iwakubona umhadi opambi kwabangevayo. Kuyatandabuzeka ukuba
impi egxotwe ngesono sokudela abongamele indawo zemfundo ingamkelwa
zezinye izikolo de ibe iza nezimilo kubafundisi bayo. Nanga ke amagama
amadodana andululwe e St Matthew’s:—

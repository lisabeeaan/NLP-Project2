ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JANUARY 1, 1875. 7

MR. CHARLES CLARK umkangeli wezikolo obambele U-Mr. Hogarth ngoku ukula
macala angase Burghersdorp apo awuquba kona lomsebenzi. Siya vuya ukuba
nokuti sekuqinisekile ukuba uza kubehle alike nomniniwo.

UMZI WABANTSUNDU E-Queenstown kutiwa asikuko nokuba uncolile. Igqira
elibe like laya kuwukangela liti alinge mangaliswe ukungcola kwakona
kuvelise isifo esiya kutshayela into eninzi! Asazi nokuba nguwo wodwa
kusinina umzi onjalo.

I-GENESIS—Singa singabakumbuza abaleseshi beta ukuba incwadi ye Genesis
iseligqityiwe uku- shicilelwa. Inokufumancka ngepeni ezisitoba. Siya
temba ukuba bonke abanokulesesha baya kuzamela ukuyi fumana, bakangele
ukuba batini na ngayo, akuko zindawo na kuyo zinteto ifuna ukulungiswa.

ILITYE LOKUSILA—Pakati kwamadoda abeze ku- tabata usapo apa mhla
kwacitakalwa ngabesikolo kube kuko no Mr. Siko Radas, umenzi welitye
lokusila elise Tsojana pesheya kwe Nciba. Sivuyile ukuva ukuba okukuzala
kwemilambo kube kuko akulenzakalisanga kangakanani, nokuba usaqubeka
kakuhle umsebenzi walo.

UMSHICILELI we Sigidimi uti banga bangacu- nyelwa ngulo nyaka bonke
abatabati baso. Uya- bakumbuza abase ngase mva, kwele mali icala, ukuba
unyaka upelile, ngoku singene komtsha. Kuko abase beneminyaka emibini
nemitatu kungafiki nto ivela kubo, kanti eyona ndlela kwakumiswe yona
kwakukuwubatalela usaqala unyaka.

U-BISHOP COLENSO pesheya usand’ ukubala incwadi ebalisa indaba ka
Langalibalele. Ubesand’ ukuya kanjalo ku Lord Carnarvon umkangeli
wemicimbi yekoloni zamangesi, eye kuteta ngalonto. Asikayiva impendulo.;
kodwa asikolwa ukuba uya kupumelela kakuhle. Kuko amadoda atile abale
emapepeni etetelela izipatamandla zase Natal.

ELUSUTU.—Uti umfo otile obala kona—apa abantu bangenelwe yindlala.
Bayazitengisa kunene igusha nenkomo befuna ukuze baye kutenga ukudla
ezivenkileni. Into eninzi ya Besutu iya kwele Diamond. Kanjalo apa
bekusand’ ukutetwa ityala lo Mtembu obehlabe umzalwana wake besilwa.
Kufumaneke ukuba lowo ubuleweyo beluqalwe nguye olucuku; noko omnye
uzuze inyanga 18 etolongweni, wadliwa nokudliwa.

E-BUENOS AYRES kubonakala ukuba kuhleliwe ngentlalo embi kunene. Pambi
kokuba sinike amazwi omfo obala ekufupi nakona, singati I- BUENOS AYRES
lilizwe elise South America. Ukulawulwa kwalo kuti kwakupela iminyaka
etile abantu banyule umntu abamtandayo ukuba makabapate ixesha elitile,
aze lakupela kubuye kunyulwe omnye. Kekaloku ngalo July udluleyo libe
lilixesha lokunyula omnye. Bate ke abantu bahlukana, abanye batanda umfo
abati ngu Mitre, abanye batanda U-Avenelleda. Lite ke kuba lingapezulu
inani elifuna U-Avenelleda kwamiswa yena njengoko linjalo isiko. Ute U-
Mitre endaweni yokuyeka watabata izikali. Amacala ke ahluleleneyo
apantse ukulingana omabini. Kubonakala ke ngoko ukuba koke kube lixesha
kuhlalelwene ngobumpi. Umfo obala ngo November ese Montevedeo kufupi
nase Buenos Ayres uti, “Akuko nto ihambisekayo enje ngomsebenzi kona.
Ukunqaba kwezinto kubangele ukuba abamanye amazwe abantu bacinge
ngokumka. Akuko mini kungeko mntu ubulawayo, oke wati tshe pandle
kwakuti ratya usukuba selencame ubomi. Amapepa endaba angavumelani no
Avenelleda kutiwe makangashicilelwa. Imali asikuko nokusweleka kumacala
omabini. Inkulu ilahlekelo kubantu bomse- benzi. ”

OBETWE NGUMBANE.—Umfo otile ontsundu ubetwe ngumbane ngezimvula zibe
ziko E-Rouxville ngase Aliwal North. Ute akukangelwa kwafikwa inaliti
enkulu yeseyile ibetelekile entloko apo ebebetwe ngumbane, into ke leyo
eyenze ukuba afe kwa oko.

E-MAMPONDWENI.—Siva ukuba U-Sir Henry Barkly ubalele ku Mqikela inkosi
ya Mampondo ecela ukuba imikombe ivunyelwe ukuba ipume ecwebeni lo
Mzimvubu, kuze kanjalo kubeko indlu yokukangela impahla ezipuma kona.
UMqikela ubeseza kuhlanganisa amapakati ake.

UMBULALI.—Kute kwele Diamond kwatunywa ababanjwa abatatu behamba
nedindala elikwa ntsundu ukuba baye kurola imali eyayi biwe yambelwa
ndaweni itile. Bemkile ke kanti sekuvunyelwene ngokuba zekubalekwe nayo.
Bate bakuyimba baxabana ngayo, wati omnye umbanjwa obezalana nedindala
wabaleka nayo, lasuka lona labadubula bobabini abanye, laya kuti
bebebaleka ukuze lenze oko. Kusuke kwase kukulula ukuba kube kuko umntu
obeyivile into ecetyiweyo noko ete eputuma wafika kuse kumva. Ngoku lise
tolongweni idindala.

AMAMPONDO—Umfo otile obala ekula macala ase Mampondweni uti, “ Kwati
zingekabipi intsuku idlule I-Governor ama Mpondo awuwela Umtata atimba
inkomo 30 namahashe amabini kuba Tembu. Kuseloko ke umsebenzi omkulu apa
kukutimbelana. Ngezi ntsuku zidluleyo ama Mpondo ade abonakala ecanda
kulo mhlaba kutetwa ngawo ne government, aya kulwa naba Tembu. Lite
eloqela lama Mpondo xa ligodukayo lasukelwa ngaba Tembu, kwabulawa omnye
Umpondo zabuya zatatyatwa nezo mpahla ebemka nazo. Ute U-Mr. Orpen akuti
yini na ukuba Damas ucande kulo mhlaba, wati omnye andina- kukupendula
kwangoku.”

UKUTAKATA—E-Mtata kutiwa kuko umfo ontsundu obenabafazi abatatu,
kekaloku emtanda kakulu owesibini. Ute omkulu wangenwa likwele, waza ke
wazilalisa ngokungati uyafa. Kubizwe igqira ukuba lize kunyanga. Ute
lomfazi wahlangana nalo ukuze limnuke lo utandwayo. Kwakuvunyiswa
lenjenjalo okunene, lwaza olusizana Iwabanjwa ngamadoda amane ambeta
kunene esiti makade avume ukuba nguye lo wenjenje. Ude wafumana wavuma.
Emva koko bambasele umlilo bambopa izandla nenyawo bamcanwaba bonke
ubusuku. Kusasa bamshiye kulondawo ukuba baya kufuna izipompolo, kwaza
kuse njalo indoda yake yamtabata yaya naye kowabo, apo alike wabonwa
ngumlungu oyena ayihlakazileyo lento. Uti inyama yake ibiseliman’ ukuwa
yodwa, akuko matemba okuba uya kupila.

ASIKUKO apa E-South Africa kodwa apo abafazi bapatiswa okwamakoboka
endaweni yokupatwa nje “ ngomncedisi ofanelene nomntu.” Uti umfo abati
ngu Herbert Barry obala nge Russia, “ Noko zikoyo zininzi indawo
abancomekayo ngazo abantu bendidi ezise zantsi E-Russia, kuya kalazeka
kanye kona ukubapata kwabo abafazi. Yinto ekoyo futi ukuti umfazi
nabantwana beye kubila emsebenzini ibe indoda ikobudala ubutongo emini.
Ukuba ute waqubisana nesoka elidala wabuza isizatu sokuba libe
alikatshati impendulo ongayi fumanayo yeyokuti, Andikayi boni intombi
eyomeleleyo ekuteleyo emsebenzini eya kwenza umsebenzi wam, ndiya
kutshata ndakuyi fumana kona nje, hai.” Isiqamo soku kucinezelwa pantsi
komfazi kukuba nabo bantu abenyuki. Lendawo itetwa ngu Barry isikumbuza
amazwi omnye umfo owati “ Umfazi akadalwanga ngetambo elitatyatwe
entloko, kuba wayengenzelwe kuze alaule ; kanjalo akadalwanga
ngelitatyatwe elunyaweni, kuba wayengenzelwa kuze anyatelelwe pantsi:
koko wadalwa ngobambo ukuze balingane nendoda, lwabe lutatyatwa kufupi
nentliziyo ukuze atandwe, akuselwe, abonelelwe.

UBUSELA.—Umfo otile oyi Mfengu usand’ uku- belwa imali yake E-Bayi.
Ubehlala endlwini ka John Mavuma, Umzulu. Kute ngalevcki idluleyo yaya
kusebenza Imfengu ishiya £10 endlwini. Ite ibuya ngokuhlwa yaba ingaseko
imali, umnini ndlu ehambele E-Bethelsdorp. Akubuya ite le Mfengu
ulisela, imali yam ibiwe nguwe. Uyi pendule lonto ngokusuke abaleke ;
kodwa ngoku selebuye wabanjwa wasiwa apo baya kona abanje ngaye.

INTLANGANISO YABAFUNDISI.—E-Bloemfontein kube kuko intlanganiso
yabafundisi bamahlelo ngamahlelo, enje ngale ibase Qonce iminyaka yonke.
Ibe ingomhla 4 ka Nobember 1874. Indawo ezinkulu ehlanganela zona,
kukufuna ukuba bamanyane ngapezulu abasebenza esitiyeni se Nkosi, nokuze
abafundisi bakutazane emsebenzini bancedane ukuliqubela ilizwi apo
lingekaziwa; baze kanjalo bakangele ukuba akangetina lamasiko akoyo
pakati kwabantsundu bawapate ngaluhlobo lunye bonke, bati ukuba bati
makulotyolwe kube njalo kuwo onke amacala, ukuba bati makungalotyolwa
bavumelane bonke. Abebeko ke abafundisi bebevela kula mahlelo, Dutch
Reform­ed Church, Paris Evangelical Society, Wesleyan Church, Lutheran.
Abangavumanga ukuyi ngena ngabeyase England. Izinto ekube kuxoxwa ngazo
zezi—Ukwaluka, izitembu nokulobola, ijiki ubunxila pakati kwabamhlope
nabantsundu, uba- patizo, ukutshata, ukwamkelelwa erementeni kwa-
bapatizwa bese bancinane, indawo ezifuna abafu- ndisi, umsebenzi
wokulima nokufuya pakati kwa- bantsundu, itikiti, abashumayeli
abantsundu ukuba bafanele ukuti ga pina emfundweni, size isimo sabo sibe
yintonina, baze kanjalo bavuzwe ngantonina abaleseshi bezibalo
nabafundisi bentsapo, nokuba kungatiwanina ukuvuselelwa intlumo
yobulungisa kwabantsundu.

OFUNA ABAFUNDISI BENTSAPO.

Umfundisi wase Qonce U-Rev. J. Harper ufuna abafundisi bentsapo nokuba
babini. Abaufunayo lomsebenzi mebaze baye kuye mhlaumbi batumele
incwadi, bangasilibali isiqiniselo sesimilo nemfundo.

INTENGO EZIZA KUBAKO.

Ngo 21 ka January 1875 kumhlaba ka Africa Isaacs kuya kutengiswa
imihlaba emibini nendlu, nengusha ezi 50, nezinye impahla.

Ngo 5 ka January E-Blinkwater kuya kutengiswa izipani zenkabi 3,
amahashe 20, iqelana lemazi zenkomo, igusha 400, nezinye izinto.

Ngomhla 16 wale nyanga E-Hope Town kuko umhlaba oya kutengiswa neqela
elikulu legusha nenkabi namahashe.

Ngomhla 17 ka Februrry E-Sterkstroom, indawo ekuza kwakiwa umzi omtsha
kuyo ngase. Burghersdorp kuya kutengiswa imihlatyana yezindlu nezitiya
efike kumakulu amane.

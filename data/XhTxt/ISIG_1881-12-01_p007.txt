ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER, 1, 1881,

HON. J. W. SAUER—EMA MPONDWENI,

INTLAULELO YE “SIGIDIMI.”

U MPATISWA Micimbi yaba Ntsundu ugaleleke e Mtata ngo Mgqibelo 12
November, wahlangatyezwa lirola elikulu kwangapandle kwe dolopu.
Kufundwe incwadi ze mibuliso kwakungenwa esixekweni.

Ngo Mvulo 14, uhlangene no Matanzima, neqela elitile labantu bake,
bevela eba Tenjini. Uxele “ubuze bake nentswelo “zake”lo mnumzana, esiti
ngemfazwe le idluleyo yena akalwanga no Rulumente, kanti noko ngenxa
yayo kuko umhlati womhlaba wake obe ngaku bantu base Sidutyini. Ucele
ukuba u Rulumente amnike umhlaba kunye nabantu bake owoba ne pepa
elibalulekileyo. U Mr. Sauer utembise ukuba imbangi yenyembezi zake
yosuswa; nomhlaba wowunikwa, uqiniselwe kuye ukuba wohambisa ngexesha
elizayo nje ngoko wabete wahambisa.

Ukumka komhla u Mpatiswa Micimbi ube nentlanganiso no Ngangelizwe obe
fike nama gora 600. Esake isicelo besisesokuba umhlaba oshiywe ngo
ndiyalwa eba Tenjini unikwe aba Tembu abapantsi kwake, nababe tobele u
Rulumente. Yena ubeseloko etobele u Rulumente, ke xa abambalwa belwe
naye maku ngohlwaywa isininzi ngenxa yabo. Ute amatyala anga eba atetwa
zinkosi ezimnyama abalekiselwa ezi mantyini, lonto akayiqondi. Ute
akafuni umhlaba wake wenziwe ifama. Imali ayifumana ku Rulumente
incinane kakubi. Utembisile u Mr. Sauer ukuba kolekwa £100 eyabi
samkelwa ngu Dalasile, emlahleke ngokulwa kwake no Rulumeate. Uti
umbaleli ngo cingo we Cape Mercury, esikupa kuyo ezi ndatyana, u Mr.
Sauer upose amazwi, ebonisa amashwa ondiyalwa nentsikelelo ka ndilele.
Emva kwamazwi awenziwa zinkosana ezimnyama yaciteka intlanganiso,
bagoduka bebonakala ukuba baya nandipa aba Tembu.

Enye intlanganiso, kwalapo, ibe yeyama Mpondo, esika Nqwiliso, abe
kwamaninzi nawo. Ate ama Mpondo ayakolwa ngu Major Elliott, kodwa
akafuni mntu wumbi pakati kwawo. Ati afuna umda kwicala elinga sentla
uxelwe mhlope. U Mpatiswa waba Ntsundu umbulele u Nqwiliso ngoku ncedisa
kwake ukukulula u Mr. Welsh, wase Tsolo oko wab’ evalelwe. Ute
makawazise poqo ama Mpondo, ukuba i Rulumente izame ukususa izinto
eziwalilisayo, inomnqweno kanjako wokuba balilale ngoxolonawo, iya
kukangela kakulu ukuba imvumelwano eyenziwa ngomnyaka we 1844, pakati
kwama Mpondo no Rulumente, mayi gcinwe kakulu.

U Rili naye uke wacela ituba loku hlangana no Mpatiswa, wab’ esitumele
isicelo sake ngo Bishop Callaway. Ute u Mr. Sauer angeza, aka sakwenziwa
nto: ungqivazele ngenxa yama nwele wacela ukuba u Mr. Sauer ahlangane
naye kunye no Bishop Callaway apo akona. Akayi vuma leyo ke into u
Mpa­tiswa.

Usuke e Mtata u Hon. Mr. Sauer ngo Mgqibelo, esinga e Kokstad.

Ikulu lenkomo ela tinjwa ema Mpondweni langena kumandla wase Qumbu,
kusitiwa zinkomo zama Mpondomise, zibuyiselwe kwaku Nqwiliso.

Kutiwa ayazi ncoma ama Mpondo, ati awafumananga alila ku
Mpatiswa—akufumene abe’ kufuna. Kazi soda sitsho ninina apa e Koloni? Xa
ezi “nkwali” zisiti zingapandiyo, masazi ukuba “akuko nkwali ipandela
enye.”

Pambi kokuba anduluke e Mtata u Mr. Sauer ushiye umyalelo wokuba inkomo
ezi 500 ebezi selugcinweni Iuka Mr. Levy, ezatinjwa kondiyalwa maza
belwe abamnyama aba besilwa lemfazo.

Umda wama Mpondo umisiwe namhla, kwabonakala ukuba u Rulumente ube
nomhlati, amise abantu bake kuwo, obu ngowama Mpondo. Baya kuvuna
izilimo zabo zalo nyaka kodwa ababantu baka Rulumente ze banikwe umhlaba
e Tsolo.

I Rulumente yazisile kuma Tola, amele ama Mpondo, ekwa kutiwe yi
Rulumente ka Mr. Sprigg angama Rulumente, ayisokungena ezi pitipitini
zawo nama Mpondo. Oko kukuti ahlanjiwe ekubeni ngama Rulumente ama Tola
lawo.

Pambi kwe ndimbane enkulu Umpatiswa Micimbi yaba ntsundu ubasele u
Nqwiliso £100 ngoku kulula u Mr. Welsh nabantu base Shawbury, oko babe
valelwe ngama Mpondomse. Oku kwenziwe evenkileni ka Vice kufupi ne
Gungululu. Ute apo u Mr. Sauer kuma Mpondo, zonke izi kalazo zawo namhla
zi cedululwe, zati izinto ezibonakalayo zalungiswa, ke i Rulumente
yolindela ukuba ama Mpondo azalise elawo cala.

ONTSUNDU E PARLAMENTE.

NKOSI,—kwe sika October “ Isigidimi ” sifunda ukuba “ u Mr. I. W.
Wauchope uyakungeniswa e Parlamenteni yimpi entsundu yase Tinara kunye
no Mr. Mackay.” Ke lenteto uti uyibone kwi Eastern Star, ipepa lase
Bini.

Ukuqala manditi nakuba ndingavuyanga yinteto oyenzileyo ngalento kodwa
wona umnqweno ekubonakala sinawo Isigidimi kutsha nje uku. beka pambi
kwetu yonke into eyelele elulaulweni lwakwa Rulumente iyandivuyisa,
itakazelelwa ngabo bonke abantsundu.

Ngokwelixesha bangati abantsundu base Tinara bamtumele e Parlamente u
Mr. Wauchope banga benze into engayi kubanceda luto, engavelisa ububi.
Abantsundu abakafiki kwisimo sokuba batunyelwe e Parlamente, isizwe
sisesemva kakubi kwizinto zokukanya. Nakweli qela lifundileyo pezu
kokuba eko nje amadodana abekekileyo, akuko namnye ofanelwe kukuya e
Parlamente. Ukuteta isi Ngesi kunye nale mfundo anayo ontsundu akwanele
ukuba afanelwe kukuya e Parlame­nte. Kufuneka enomzi, ezipilele kakuhle,
(ndingatsho ukuti u Mr. Wauchope akanamzi) abe kwisimo sokuteta oko
acinga ukuba kulungile, kungeko nkwintshana utolika ukulahlekwa sisonka.
Ke amadodana antsundu akwisimo esingaqondakaliyo—e mkulu u nkwintshana.
Eyile e Parlamente eyona nto ingapulapulwayo kuye kuteta isi Ngesi, ize
lonteto yake ingabi nakuyishukumisa indlu. Umntu ontsundu angalula
kakubi—kanti ke lento ikukubekwa yenye yezinto ekoyiswa ngayo e
Parlamente angalilolo. Kuti manditi ivoti zake zingatengwa ngamadinara
ase Cape Town, ndingasateti ke kona ngamalungelo angasilingo kuye
okumrweba esenziwe yi Government. Ontsundu nomhlope akukadibani kwa
kwintlanganiso ezincinane ezenziwa kwimizi yama Ngesi, noko Isigidimi
sibona ukuba lifikile ixesha lokuba abantsundu batunyelwe e Parlamente
!!

Akuko sizatu sokuba siti makunyulwe ontsundu masilinge tina ukwandisa
inani lama Ngesi asitetelelayo—ati ofanelwe ku vota libalwe igama lake
kwincwadi yabavoti ukuze situmele e Parlament iqela eliya kuba nokwenza
into. Into embi Isigidimi siyiteta lento ngendlela eya kweyelisela
abantu engozini—into engabangela ukuba lincitshiswe neli lungelo analo
ilungelo ontsundu lokuvota. Nezihlobo zabantsundu kwelixesha azingetandi
etunyelwe.

VOTANUMLUNGU.

Colesberg, November 1, 1881.

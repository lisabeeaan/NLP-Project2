ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 16, 1883.

Ishwangusha elibi kunene lehla ngomhla wesitatu ka July emlanjeni wase
Scotland ekutiwa yi Clyde. Indimbane emayela kwikulu elinamashumi mabini
abantu latshona nomkombe owabusand’ ukwenziwa. Amadoda anyuliweyo ukuba
acedulule izizatu ezingokutshona kwalomkombe ati akayiqondi into engaba
yabanga lengozi. Abenzi bezikepe abenza i Daphne (ligama lomkombe elo)
ngamadoda aseleke enza inqanawa ezikumakulu amabini anamashumi asibozo
ezingazanga zabekwa sipako.

Ngolwesi-Tatu ngokuhlwa (8 Aug.) kubeko apa e Lovedale intlanganiso
emnandi. Kulontlanganiso inteto ezamkelekileyo zenziwe ngu Mr. W. G.
Slowan no Rev. G. L. Carstairs, M.A. amadoda amabini kwisitatu
esitunyelwe yiramente yama Presbyterian, kwibandla elikweli. Eyesitatu
linene elingu Mr. Corsar. Entetweni yake u Mr. Slowan wenze amabalana
anandipekayo ngokuhamba kwe Bayibile kulo lonke namaqinga okuyihambisa.
Waza u Mr. Carstairs washiya amacebo kudo- dana ngezinto afanelwe
kuziqwalasela.

U Right Rev. W. W. Jones, D.D., u Bishop wase Kapa siva ukuba uhambele
kwisitili sase Mtata apo aye kungenisa ngo- kubeka izandla u Rev.
Bransby L. Key ekubeni ngu Bishop onceda u Bishop Callaway. Siva ukuba
uhamba no Rev. Canon Lightfoot wakwase Kapa. Apo e Mtata uya kudibana no
Bishop Macrorie wase Maritzburg e Natal. Siqonda ukuba ekubuyeni kwake u
Dr. Jones woka andwendwele indawo ezitile kwesi siqingata se Koloni
sikuso. I Lovedale ikankanyelwa kwindawo ayakuke azihambele.
Simnqwenelela indlela entle.

I Parlament igqibe kwelokuba abamnyama bangatengiselwa iziselo kwindawo
eyakuti i Governor izibalule ukuba kuzo kungatengiswa brandy kumntu
omnyama. Kwingxoxo ebiko pezu kokumiselwa kwalo mteto, sifumana ukuba
umhlobo wabamnyama ibe ngu Hon. Thomas Upington [Lonto ningayi- vakalisi
e Gate!] Ibeyile mambane engayekanga ukuti nabamnyama mabayifumane i
gilasi yabo. Inye indawo esi- yitandileyo entetweni ka Mr. Upington—yile
yokucalula umntu ofundisiweyo nobekekileyo ontsundu. Akwaba ubeya
kuhlala esiqonda esi sahluko. Asitandabuzi, oludaba luyakuba lolumnandi
ko Breeders en Sesters bamabandla abazili.

Into eninzi kubafundi betu izinandipa kakulu indaba ezi- ngemfundo.
Kwabo ke olu lungaludaba :—Kwintlanganiso yamanene aquba imicimbi
yemfundo epakamileyo (University Council) kunyulwe lamanene ukuba ze abe
ngabaya kuzama- nisha abafuna iziqiniselo—namagama emfundo ete-tyi
ngonya- ka ozayo :—Kwisebe le Literature (inteto)—J. H. Brady, Esq.,
B.A., Moderator; Rev. Dr. Cameron; Rev. Dr. Kolbe; Rev. Professor Marais
; Dr. G. W. Van Oordt: Kwisebe le Science— Hon. O. Abercrombie Smith,
M.A., Moderator; W. H. Finlay, Esq., B.A, F.R.A.S.; J. G. Gamble, Esq.,
M.A.; Professor MacOwan, B.A.; Richard Solomon, Esq., M.A. : Kwisebe
lomteto—Judge Smith ; A. W. Cole, Esq.; Kwinteto yesi Jelimani—Rev. C.
H. Hahn, junr.

Siyavuya ukuva ukuba inxenye yododana oluntsundu ingene ekumiseni
ivenkile. Siyatemba ukuba abantu abamnyama abamele ezo venkile
abasakuziyekelela ukuba zibube. Lendawo isivuyisa ngokuba saka sabuzwa
ngumfundisi owayevela e Tshayina wati kukutinina ukuba ibe nanamhla
izinto ezinje- ngokutengela pakati kwenu zisesezandleni zama Ngesi. Tina
e Tshayina, latsho elinene, umsebenzi wama Tshayina wenziwa kwangama
Tshayina. Kakade inkuku yasikwa umlomo. Ukuba sasikwazi esikwaziyo
namhla ngesaba nokumpendula umfundisi lowo, asati sifake imisila pakati
kwemilenze. Okwanamhla asingxamele kuwavakalisa amagama alamadoda siteta
ngawo, kodwa apesheya kwe Nciba. Singati ukuwacebisa, yonke i business e
Mlungwini ihamba kakuhle xa kwaziswa ngayo emapepeni. Siko ke Isigidimi
emabaziswe ngaso abamnyama ngendawo ezinjengezi.

Ipepa lase Scotland, i-Glasgow Herald, litete into esiyiqonda kakuhle,
xa liti, akukonto injengedinala elidliwa buputuputu emva kwexesha
ukupitizelisa uxolo pakati komzi. Lento liyi- balisa ngoluhlobo :
Umninindlu umka kusasa aye kusebenza. Xa agodukayo ke ediniwe sazeke
yena esiti uyakufumana indawo yokuzola. Kanti ke uti akutana nqipu
nobutyututyutu, ukungalungi kamsinyane kwedinala, ze abizelwe kukudla
okupakwe ngobungxamo, ngokungakatali, kakubi, itafile engcolileyo,
isiqingata sezinto ezifunekayo singeko—zonke ezizinto zanele ukucunukisa
unyamezelo Iwe ngilosi. Akuncedi Into ukuba ati u frowu benditunga,
bendite lento mayenziwe zicaka, njalo njalo. Kuhle kanye ukuti hayi
umfazi unezinto ezinkulu ngapezulu kunokucuba itapile, namatswele, kodwa
idinala elungiswe emva kwexesha, engalungiswanga kakuhle ibanga ububi
obukulu emzini wendoda. Imfundiso yentombi ukulungisa indlu nokupeka
sisaza kuyingena ngokuzeleyo kwalapa e-Sigidimini okwanamhla sivakalisa
imvo yomfo wase Scotland,

Ingqweqwe enkulu yawa ngomhla we 9 ku August. Eli
kepu lide lawa nakwindawo elingazanga liwe kuzo—emakaya,
into engaziwayo kweli letu lingapantsi. Kwasa ngalomhla
ingcango zingavuleki yingqele. Kambe izinto ezisakubanje
bezisakuxela umnyaka otyebileyo. Au itemba alidanisi, Sitsho
kambe ku Sambawo.

Ekufeni kwayo lenyanga ifileyo kuhle ingozi embi kumgama
wakwa Daliwe (Cathcart.) Kute ngamvulo utile kanye uku-
mka komhla, kwahla ingozi embi kuloliwe, Kute ekufikeni
kuka loliwe, kwaye kuko itroku (trucks) ebeziye kulayisha
intsimbi kwakufupi emzini, zite ekuzeni kwazo emzini zeza
zikaulezile, babe ngati tipu-tipu zakauleza; kute kuba be-
kuko ama Xosa ndawonye namagxagxa, aseleziposa pantsi
ama Xosa alandela amagxagxa kwangokunjalo, de kwasala
lalinye ezitrokweni, esingaziyo ukuba Iona beliqotyoshwe
yinina. Kute kupi xa avusayo uloliwe zagaleleka itroku,
kanti umqubi ndawonye nomkwezeli abaziboni, zitsho kwakubi
wati umqubi yena okokwake zamnqumla intloko ngohlobo
olubi kuba ziyigabulele kwashiyeka ngezantsi indevu nomlomo,
omnye wenzakaliswe entloko naye, omuye wavikivwa amata-
mbo. Lengozi kwababeko akuko utande ukuyikangela.

E-Dublin, Pesheya, ityendyana abati ngu Mr. Edward
Spring lisand’ukudliwa amawaka omabini eponti (£2000) ngo-
kwapula idinga lomtshato nenzwakazi abati ngu Miss May
Coghlan. Ufilisho Iwaqala ngo 1879. Sifumana ngencwadi
zesoka ezazisiya entombini ukuba Iwalunotando olukulu;
intombi yayifeketiswa ngawo onke amagamana anqabileyo.
Kute kodwa ekuqalekeni kwalonyaka lemambane yapandlwa
yenye inzwakazi, Iwatyafa utando ngaku May. U May yinto-
mbi entloko irelerele, akenzanga into esakwenziwa liqela
elitile lentombi esilaziyo eliti lipange ngokulala isoka zelikwe-
le ke elucocoyini Iwendlu 1wenze umtyangampo ngokukala
lusiti nanga amasoka ezalile. Hayi, u Miss Coghlan ulinde
yada yafika incwadi yesoka eti andisengawe, wayake ematya-
leni, wazifumana indleko zotando oludanisiweyo. Singati
kubafundi be “ Mpawana ” umteto wase Ireland awahlukile
kumteto wase Koloni apa.

Wonke umfundi we “ Mpawana ” wosivumela xa siti imvo ezivakaliswa kule
nteto ilandelayo ziyabuleleka. Lencwadi izitetela ngokwayo—yifundeni ke
:—

MHLELI NKOSI YETU,—Silapa ngokubongoza, ngokucela sisiti, “ Sekulituba
simana sincoma ukuteta kwamadodana akowetu bantsundu, kodwa kutyatyambe
ukuba xa simana ukunqina zesingenzi sikohlisa nje. Ke ngoku siti
ngantliziyo ’nye—Nceda usifake siyavuma, sifuna too ukusitabata
Isigidimi eso. Nkosi yetu akuko nto nto nto inkulu igqita okukuteta kuti
ngenxa yokulangazelela kwentliziyo zetu Isigidimi eso.” Abatshoyo siti
bako.—JOSEPH G. MBOMBELA, Teacher, Wesleyan Mission, Fort Beaufort; and
WILLIAM B. TSHONA, Teacher, Independent, Fort Beaufort.

Impato egadalala awayifumanayo kuma Mfengu apesheya oka Galela ngokusuka
atete inyaniso ebuhlungu, ayikalityalwa siti. Ekuti ke njengokuba
ejokisa nje ke u Mr. Ndwandwa, enga angade alazi nekaya lombaleli owati
ungu “ Fikizolo ” sikumbule ukuba iyawa seliyi “ katsi ka Macwili.”
Incwadi yokugqibela evela kwelozwe lingcwele, ingazanga yapatwa ibotile
zititshala zalo yiyo le :—

MHLELI WE Sigidimi Sama-Xosa ndifakele lamazwi bendicela imibuzo kuwe
wasuka wena Mhleli wandidungudelisa. Sendicela kuwe Mr. Fikizolo ukuba
ndihlambe mhlobo wam, umzi lo wakuti ufuna kucacile ukuba yayingendim.
Ndenzele lento Mr. Fikizolo, linga ukucaza amabala abo bantu waubabona,
enye into ndixelele kakuhle apo uhlala kona ukuze nditi nge Holiday
ezayo ndize kugqita kuwe, ndivisisise ngokunye ngale ndawo. Safa
kukutukwa si Sigidimi kungekonto yenziwe bani. Ndingavuya ingati kanti
olunxilo tina zi Teacher siyahlaziswa kodwa, njengokuba umntu otiye
ititshala engenakuze aposise ekutini yi Titshalana, no Fikizclo lo
makabatyile ababafo angahlazisi eyakwa Feldtman, ayizanga inxile.— THEO.
NDWA- NDWA. Veldtman’s Place, July 17, 1883.

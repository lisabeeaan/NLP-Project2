ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, AUGUST 1, 1585.

  January odluleyo. Kwafunyanwa kulungile ukubaliswa

Isiyalo so Mgcini-Sihlalo.

U Mr. J. TENGO-JABAVU ngenteto enqata, nemfutshane,
uyivule intlanganiso ngokuyaleza isidima, ukutozama, no-
kuzola kule ntlanganiso, ukuze ibe nomdla nesitozela eba-
ntwini. Uyaleze umanyano, nokuvisisana; ukuhlonelana,
nokubekana; ukupulapulana, nokupendulana imibuzo
ngoku ndilekileyo, nange ngqondo, nangomoya wokwazi
ukuba esikuteta apa akupelele kupela pakati kwe ndonga
zendlu esihlangene kuyo, kuya ekugwetyweni ngabanye
abapandle, bentlanga ngentlanga. Uyaleze ukuxaswa kwe
“ Nx'owa ” yentlanganiso, kuba lonto iyenye yentsika
eziyintloko zokuma kwayo.

Itakazele ngamxelo mnye intlanganiso.

Amalungu.

Kubizwe amagama amalungu, kwalandwa izizatuzange-
-koyo. Kute ke, kwangeniswa amagama amalungu amatsha,
-alishumi elinesitandatu (16), amkelwa kwakuba kubuziswe
kuba ngenisi bawo ngezimilo zabo, ukuvumelana nemiteto
yo Manyano. Kwafunyanwa ukuba inani lo Manyano
ngoku lingamalungu ali 110, bamahlelo ngamahlelo. Aba-
mhlope base babini, u Mr. JESSE SHAW, wase Bofolo, no
Attorney R. W. ROSE-INNES, wase Qonce.

Ingxelo yo Mgcini-Ndyebo.

U Mr. JOHN KNOX BOKWE ufunde uhlobo lokuma kwe
mali yentlanganiso, ekubonakele ukuba engenileyo kulo
nyaka ibe ziponti ezi <£5 16s. 3d., zaza indleko zayincipisa

nge ponti ezi <£5 3s. 9d., kwaba kusala esandleni 12s. 6d.
ukuqala omtsha unyaka. Ite yakutazisa londawo. Wati
kodwa wavelisa enye ebe sikwa-silima, yokuba inkoliso
yamalungu asemva ekuyiroleni imali yonyaka ezi 5s. lilinye.
Ngati inani elisaswelekelwe ukurolwa ngamalungu
ukuya kupela ku June 1886, imayela kwi ponti ezi £56.
Utumele ke kuwo incwadi zokuyibuta lomali. Siva ukuba
eseyite yangena kususela kweloxa lokutunyelwa kwe
ncwadi ziponti ezi £8. Ikwa kutaza londawo.

Imimiso ye Ntlanganiso.

Kufundwe i Constitution, oko kukuti imigaqo oluhamba
ngayo u Manyano nge Mfundo, eyashicilelwa ngo 1882.
Yaza emitsha—gxebe ukuhlaziywa kwemidala—eyaxoxwa
e Debe kweka January intlanganiso, kwavunyelwana
ukuba imiswe, ize ke ishicilelwe akubako amalungelo.

  Amapepa Alesiweyo.

Kulawo abelungiselwe le ntlanganiso kufundwe la matatu.

U Mr. CALEB MAHLUTSHANA ubale ngesi Ngesi Imfundo yaba-Ntsundu. Ukolise
ukukubula indima esel’ igqushile. Ibenye indawo entsha ayivelisileyo,
nati yena ngeyilungi- swa; le yokuba, ekuboneni kwake uyonakaliswa
umsebenzi wemfundo kukunikelwa ezandleni zo titshala abangenawo
lamapetshana kutiwa zi Qiniselo-zo-Viwo.

Intlanganiso yalile ukulixoxa ipepa lake engeko; yavu- melana
kwelokuti—Ayizamkeli imvo zake; kodwa iyambu- lela ngenxamleko
yokulungisela into u “Manyano nge Mfundo.”

U Mr. JAMES J. BOVULA ufunde ngesi Ngesi Imbali yo Manyano-nge-Mfundo,
ukuyilwa kwayo iyeye titshala zodwa nge 1876 ngu Mr. JOHN W. GAWLER wase
Mtwaku, noku kwezelwa nge 1877, ngu Mr. M. K. MTAKATI ongaselilo ilungu
ngoku. Ukucelwa kwayo ku Dr. DALE—(asazi ngasizatu sinina)— ummiso wake
wokuba wovuma ukuba ite yonganyelwa ngumntu omhlope, ukuze ke u Mr.
JESSE SHAW wase Bofolo abe kweso sihlalo nje. Oku kona kumiseleka kwayo
nge 1880 nakuba ib’inge kabi namigaqo imiselekileyo. Oko ikufumene nge
1882, kunye nokutwa- buluka, ite kaloku yaligobo elifikisileyo ukuvelela
zonke inkalo zamalungelo ohlanga, esazama kona ngezi imini.

Libulelwe kunene elipepa, kwatiwa kumninilo maze ake apande indawo
ezingaba zisezikewu ebalini lo Manyano, ize ke Iibonelwe iqinga
lokugcinwa kwe mbali esingaziyo ukuba yoba luhlobo lunina kwizizukulwana
ezizayo.

  U Mr PETER TYAMZASHE, ufunde ngesi-Xosa, Isimo

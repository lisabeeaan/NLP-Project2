ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MAY 1, 1876. 5

Akatandanga lomzali ukuba kubeko bani emzini wake ungavuyiyo
ungabuleliyo nambla. Kutetwa bani na ke xa kutshiwoyo? Kutetwa u-Tixo
u-Bawo wetu sonke, nguye lowo unenceba unesisa unobubele ubagcinileyo
abo bangabake, yena ufuna ukuba kuvuye into yonke ayidalileyo ufuna
ukuba umntu wonke amkele adle kuso esisidlo sililizwi lake, ufuna ukuba
abantu bonke bagcobe bonwabe baxolelwe babonakale ukuba bangonyana bake
bazintombi zake, akafuni kubona mntu ehleli elusizini lwezono zake,
akafuni kulilungisa mntu yena, kutsbabalalisa mntu, yena ufuna ukuba
bonke abantu baguquke basindiswe.

V.—Ndifike kwilizwi lesihlanu nditi asimzali ukulunga. Yabona unyana
omkulu akavumanga ukuyingena lendlu kugcotyelwa kuyo, usuke wema
ngapandle walwa kona nezicaka wangxamela ukuwona umsebenzi omhle kunene
wokuxolelana kukayise nonyana wake omnci. Usuke wammisela uyise ityala
wati, akuzanga uudinike netakane eli ukuze ndenze imiblali nezam
izihlobo. Ukutsho uti akuzanga undizuzise nto noko ndisebenze kangaka
leminyaka, ndisapuka ndibila ndilima ndivuna ndisalusa. Uvelise lendawo
lonyana yokuba noko abehleli leminyaka noyise intliziyo yake
ibingonwabile ingaxolile. Eyivile inteto enjalo uyise akasukanga alike
angene endlwini aluvale ucango amsbiye unyana wake oqumbileyo ukuba ade
abuye apole. Hayi ufika amana eteta kancinane nalomntwana wake embonisa
indawo aziposileyo, wati, Mntwanam, wena ungunyana warn, asizanga
sablukana tina, zonke into zam zezako. Bekungeko nto uyisweleyo wena,
nokuba ubufuna ukwenza ntoni, uxele ntoni, ubungexakekiyo wena, musa
ukutetangomvuzo, kangelaapa,bekusifaneleukuba sixolele, bekusifanele
ukuba sisikwe yimfesane, bekusifanele ukuba silubonakalalise
utando,bekusifanele zakugcoba intliziyo zetu; ukuba siwublanganise umzi
upela ube nemihlali wonke, bekusifanele wena nam ukuba namblanje
sivuyisanane.

Asixelelwa apa ukuba lomfo weyiseka yilenteto kayise ilunge kangaka,
masesitemba kodwa ukuba weyiswa wabuya wabona w’angena bagcoba bobabini
abanyana babelablekile, kuba bebelablekile bobabini abanyana, lowa
wemkayo nalo wayehleli ekaya kuba nakuba ebengazanga emke isiqu
intliziyo yona ibingampete kakuhle uyise. Indawo eveliswa ngalamazwi
onke kulunga kukayise. Asikwangu Tixo na lowo ulilungisa ubapeteyo bonke
abantu emblabeni apa ngobulungisa? Kaucinge nawe, noko ulipeteyo kakubi
ilizwi lake, u-Tixo yena ukupete ngolona hlobo ongati ukuba ungumntu
onentliziyo weyiseke.

Ndiyapela ke nambla ukuwucaza kwam lomzekeliso, kunjalo nje zininzi
indawo endingazifikanga, kuba lomzekeliso ungumtombo omanzi angapeliyo,
ongafanelana uke kuwo imibla yonke ungawafinci noko, kusuke kube ngati
kokukona uwuvuleleyo ukuba umpompoze. Ke ndibe ndiwucaza nje ezicawa
kuko luncedo lunina ulufumeneyo? Uyawubonana lomlambo uli Tunxe, baninzi
abantu abawuwelayo lemibla yonke, bebaninzi nje baninzi abawuwelayo
bengawakatalele nganto lomanzi abawela kuwo, baninzi kanjalo abati
bapuze kuwo begqite bahambe indlela zabo, baninzi abaka kuwo bahlambe,
abaka kuwo basebenze imisebenzi yabo, kunjalo nje asimlambo utshayo,
nasemini nasebusuku nasehlotyeni nasebusika, kumi kunjalo indidi
ngendidi zezizukulwana zabantu, lomzekeliso ukwanjengeli Tunxe, ke,
kukade kuseloko watetwayo, baninzi abawavileyo amazwi awo ngendlebe
abawakatalela nganto noko, baninzi abate babamba zwana elo lawo babuya
balilibala, baninzi abate bawamkela onke amazwi awo bancedwa ngawo
emipefumlweni yabo. Ungowolupina uhlobo wena, kwezintlobo zingaka?
Lomzekeliso uteta ntonina kuwe wamkele zwi

linina lawo? Uqonde ndawo nina yawo oti wakuyieinga uve ukuba le ixela
kanye indawo owenze yona wena. Zininzi indawo ozibaliselweyo
wazityilelwa, zifike zateta ntonina kuwe? Zifike zakubonisa ndawo nina
obungayiqondi ungayazi? Ufike wakuxelela ntonina ngo Tixo, wakuxelela
ntonina ngento oyenzileyo wena kuye? Ungomkileyo kwapela na ku Yiblo
ongu Tixo? Usezweni elikude naye na? Uzibubisa kona na? Uqauka yindlala
apo na? Kunjalo nje ke uyakonwaba kude kube ninina kwelozwe okulo?
Ungabuyi njekutenina? Bubone ubutyakala ozeyelisele kubo ubuyele ku Tixo
usiti, Bawo ndonile, ukuze uxolelwe, uvuyiswe entliziyweni. Yabona,
uvulelwe ukuba ubuyele ku Tixo, uxolelwe ngenxa yonyana wake izono zako,
ungafanelana seluziteni ukuzilahlekisa kwako, ungafanelana uzeyelisele
ngalublobo luni ezonweni zonke unokuzixolelwa ngenxa ka Kristu Yesu. Apo
waqala kona lomfana ukubuya wati waziqonda, logama wonke ebengekaziqondi
ebengacingi ngakubuya, uyakuziqonda ninina wena ukuze ubuyele ku Tixo?
Yabona, lonyana mkulu ubezincoma ukulunga ukungamlabli kwake uyise kanti
ebesitsbo nje ebekwamlablile naye umzali wake kuba ebesekeleze inzuzo
yodwa ekumkonzeni kwake uyise, ebengekaziqondi naye into ayiyo kungoko
wateta kakubi kangaka kumzali wake. Into enkulu efunekayo kuwe
kukuziqonda kukuzibona kukuziva into oyiyo, ukuze ke ubenako
ukulikumbula ikowenu, no Tixo ongu Yiblo. Ubuyiswe kuziqonda Lomfana,
wati ke akugoduka, nakuba ebelilablile ikowabo walifumana ikaya, wati
nakuba ebebamba ze wafika wambeswa, wati nakuba ebelambile wakufumana
ukudla. Ubuyele ku Tixo wena koba kwanjalo, womqonda u-Tixo ukuba
ungonentando unenceba unobubele unofefe, wokwamkela ngovuyo olukulu.
Ngubanina apa oya kuzenza isitulu? Ngubanina apa oya kuzenza imfama?
Aniliva na elilizwi linibongozayo ukuba nibuye? Ziyekeni zonke ezondlela
zenu zisingisela ekufeni, nize nambla ku Yesu owanifelayo,
owalililaulayo ityala lezono zenu kuba seletshilo kade ukuti, “Yizani
nonke kum nina nibulalekayo, nisindwayo ngumtwalo ndoninika ukupumla.”
Kwanga kungaba njalo.

ELUSUTU.—Isihlobo setu esitile esise Lusutu siti kwincwadi esiyi bale
ngomhla 22 ka March, “Kule Sabata idluleyo sibe sinentlanganiso emmandi
kunene. Abatunywa abaya nelizwi ku Banyayi bebebulisana neremente
yalapa. Onke amehlo ebezaliswe zinyenibezi, bade abanye ababi
nakuzibamba. Lamadoda mane antsundu aza kuya kuba ngabashumayeli
kubonakala ukuba nga Makristu azinikeleyo. Nabafazi bawo bangamakristu.
Baza kuyihamba lendlela yabo ngovuyo. Omnye wabo, (umfazatia osemcinane)
ute kulontlanganiso wesuka wabekisa lamazwi kwabanye abafazi, ‘Kusoloko
u-Aser, (umyeni wam) abuyayo kwela Banyayi, ndati roqo ukutandaza ukuba
ndibe nomsebenzi endiwenzela Inkosi. Kwati akuti umyeni warn, siza kuya
ku Banyayi ndaqiniseka ukuba Inkosi iwupendule umtandazo wam. Nina
bafazi nditi kuni ize ningakumbuleli ukuti ndihamba kuba ndimelwe
kukulandela umyeni wam. Hayi, ndihamba ngeyam intando, ndiya kuba
ndinomnqweno wokusebenzela Inkosi.’ Ibe njalo inteto yalomfazi. Kule
veki izayo siya e-Leribe apo lamadoda ayakugqibela kona ukubulisana
neremente yalapa.”

E-TSHUNGWANE.—Omnye wababalelani u-Petrus Diwayo wase Tshungwane uti, “
Ndikandanisiwe zindaba ezimnandi. Ilizwi le Nkosi liya hamba
e-Tshungwane. Abantu abaguqukileyo bakumakulu amabini. Siyabona zihlobo
ukuba Ilizwi le Nkosi liya hamba njengoko atshoyo Umninilo ukuti maliye
kuzo zonke intlanga. Ndingakankanya nokuba liya hamba nangapandle. Kona
kuko amagqira amabini, u-Hlohlela no Kamango aguqukileyo. U-Hlohlela
ubenabafazi abane, waza watabata mnye watshata naye wabashiya abanye.
Elinye elo igqira ubemnye kade umfazi walo. Abaguqukayo ibavulcle inkosi
u-Makaula ukuba benze ngokutanda kwabo. Enye indawo ngecala labafazi,
lamasiko emvelo bawalahlile bonke abakristukazi. Elokugqibela nditi
masifezekise ilizwi eliti, ‘ Ndiya ninika umteto omtsha wokuba
nitandane.’ ”

LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, MARCH 3, 1874.

No. 42

Isigidimi Samaxosa

INCWADI ZIKA HON. CHARLES BROWNLEE.

Kwi Ofisi Yompatiswa Wemicimbi Yabantsundu,

Cape Town, 15 ka January, 1874.

LITI elika Rulumente kwinkosi zase Lusutu nabantu bazo abanceda
lomsebenzi wokupelisa imfazwe ka Langalibalele, Urulumente ukoliwe
kakulu lizwi elivele ku Mr. Griffiths, elixela ukufaneleka kwe- mikwa
yenu kurulumente nokunceda kwenu kakuhle ukupelisa imfazwe ka
Langalibalele.

Kuyifanele inkosi esakuba ilungisa ukuti imohlwaye umenzi wento ezimbi.
Kukwayifanele ukuba idumise yomeleze abo bamisebenzi ifanelekileyo.
Kodwake kwezonto zombini eyona immandi yile yokudumisa yomeleze;
kekaloku abenzi bento ezimbi bohlwayi wenje Urulumente ufanelwe kukuti
abulele kwabo benze okulungileyo.

Kwinkosi engu Letsie Urulumente wenza libe linye. Kwezizinto zihlileyo
aka- mdanisanga. Ute kwa oko U-Letsie wayenza ngokutumekelela yonke into
ayitunywayo ngulomfo wakwa Rulumente ( U-Griffiths), wabatintela
abobantu balwa nenkosi ukuba bangangeni kwela Besutu. Kuselokwalomhla
Abesutu baba ngamarulumente kude kube yile mini, umfo wakwa Rulumente
uxela into ezintle ngo Letsie, emtembe kakulu. Mhla kuvele intoke
U-Letsie akabonakalanga ekohlakala; Urulumente ayakubulela kakulu kanye
ukunceda kwake nokufaneleka kwemikwa yake ngakomkulu. Ekonza Urulemente
nje, U-Letsie ngoyena unceda abantu bake.

Ngenyanga ezigqitileyo Urulumente wa­ke weva indaba ezimbi ngo Molapo,
ko­dwake imisebenzi yake elungileyo ngoku lincwaba elinzulu apo
kudityelelwe kona

ezonto zimbi, zezi zilungileyo azenzileyo ngoku. Namhlanje U-Molapo umi
pambi korulumente engumntu onyanisileyo otembekileyo, engumhlobo wabantu
bake, engumfo ka Mshweshwe, kuba U-Mshwe- shwe ubebatanda abantu bake,
ehlala efuna ukusonga into ezimbi nenkatazo kubo.

Kulento ka Langalibalele, U-Molapo usonge into embi ebibazela abantu
bake, wabatobela okulungileyo bona, kwanaye ngokwake. Wavala, yati impi
ayabi na- kungena emhlabeni wake.

Akapelanga apo U-Molapo. Ute kwa- kutshiwo ngumfo wakwa Rulumente, wa-
kupa abantu bayakulwa nalonkosi inonyawo Iwemfene yadubulayo bafa abantu
baka Rulumente besebenza umsebenzi wabo bexolile.

U-Molapo utyapile enjenje. Urulu­mente uyabulelake kuye. Akapulapulanga
kwakuveliswa amaqinga angendawo: usuke webuxe kulandawo wamiswa kuyo
nguyise, yokuba ngumntu ka Victoria; xake ate buxe kuyo kuyakumlungela
kwanabantu bake.

Kwinkosana engu George Mshweshwe, ku Lorothodi, ku Jonathan, ku Joel
Meshoane, nakwabanye bonke abancede umsebenzi wokupelisa lemfazwe,
Urulumente uyabulela. Nakuba wakawati ngelinye ixesha U-Jonathan
wafumana wateta amazwi okunxama, ubonakalisile namhla ukuba intliziyo
yake ilungile, uwanxenxezele ngokugqitisileyo lamazwi obufana
amhlenikweni wateta into angayiqondiyo.

Ekukonzeni Urulumente, Abesutu bazuze inkomo ezininzi, kodwake azinaku-
kankanywa noko zingako, kwakukangelwa lento inkulu bayenzileyo ngokucasa
abo bantu babesilwa nenkosi yabo Urulumente bati ngokwenjenjalo banqanda
izipitipiti zingezi Elusutu.

Imikwa ya Besutu kulento, ibonakala- lisile ukuba babenyanisile
mhlenikweni bati bangama Rulumente inene bona, entlanganisweni enkulu
E-Maseru.

Urulumente utanda ukuba ababone be- ngabantu abanentsikelelo abahleli
kamnandi abanobutyebi. Kungoku bahamba ngendlela ebeka kulontlalo
itandekayo, Urulumente ubatembileke ukuba abayikupambuka kuyo.

  C. BROWNLEE,

Umpatiswa Wemicimbi Yabantsundu.

Kwi Ofisi Yompatiswa Wemicimbi Yabantsundu,

Cape Town, 15 ka January, 1874.

LITI elika Rulumente kwinkosi ezingo Lebana no Lebenya no Zibi,

Urulumente ukoliwe kakulu ukuba ekuvile ngemantyi yenu, U-Mr. Orpen,
ukuba nite akutukuti ukunibiza ngegama lika Rulumente nesuka naxoba naya
kuIwa no Langalibalele lenkosi ilwileyo ne­nkosi yayo; kwalixesha
elikulu nipete intonga; nada nakuqabela ezintabeni zakwa Maluti

Ngeminyaka emashumi matatu egqitile- yo, U-Langalibalele waye njengani
aba ukunxamela ukuyenza into abeyitunywa ngu Rulumente. Ngeloxesha waye
sabe wemka ku Mazulu ayembandezele kakubi kwanesizwe sake. Oko waye
lihlwempu ekohliwe engenawo nokuba ubemnye umpu. Ngeloxa Amahlubi
(isizwe sakowabo) akuvuma ukumtobela U-Rulumente ngendawo zonke. Ateke,
kuba egciniwe ngu Rulumente engenalutshaba ayakuzinqabisa kulo (Amahlubi
lawo) inkosi leyo naba­ntu bayo bakuvuma ukuwutobela umteto ote boke
bavunyelwe ngu Rulumente ukuze babenokutenga imipu, iti nalomipu
bayitengayo ibalwe yenziwe impau ngu Rulumente. Kwakuhamba amaxesha
isi­zwe sakula somelela sanobutyebi. Amadoda amakulu abefudula
ebandezelwe nga Mazulu afa, kwavela amadodana angazaniyo nobubi, akulele
pantsi kogcino Iwalo Rulumente wase Natal, abangawo ami endaweni zoyise
abo. Aqala awapula umteto ngokuti atenge imipu engavunye- Iwanga
ingabalwanga. Ati kwakutiwa

ISIGIDIMI SAMA-XOSA JUNE 2, 1884

IMIHLABA ETENGISWAYO.

E NDENXA (MACLEAR).—Umongameli wonocanda wazisa kwipepa lakomkulu ukuba
kuya kutengiswa, pambi kwe office yemantyi yase Barkly East ngentsimbi
yeshumi, ngomhla wa 25 wo June 1884, iziziba zemihlaba emasbumi mabini,
emokolo (morgen) ziqala ku 468 ziye ku 933.—E DODOLORO.—Pambi kwe office
yomantyi ngomhla wa 27 June 1884, ngexesha leshumi kuyakutengiswa
imihlaba elishumi elinamibini ekwelaba Tembu (Woodehouse) ; emokolo
ziqala 1,055 ziye ku 3,038. E CUMAKALA.—Pambi kwe office yemantyi
ngexesha leshumi ngomhla we 17 July 1884 kuyakutengiswa imihlaba emitatu
; emokolo ziqala esitobeni ziye kwishumi elinambini. Neminye emine
Kwazidenge, emokolo ziqala esixenxeni ziye kwisitoba. Abafuna ukutabata
kule yokugqibela bangake bahambele ku Eev. Mr. Kropf wase Cumakala
oyakuba cacisela izimiselo ngesisa.

I ANNIVERSARI E NXUKWEBE.

Njengokuba sasibikile, eloxesha likulu kunene kubantu abatsha ngokukodwa
labafikela ngomhla we 11 nowe 12 ku May. Lomihla yomibini ifike ise
ilindelwe ngentliziyo ezishushu ngabo bonke, ise ilungiselelwe kunene.
Kambe isaci sayo kukuvuyisa usapo oluhamba isikolo se Sabata kunye no
Titshala baso. Bati ngalomihla abamapandle bahlanganisane kumzi omkulu,
kuze kukutazwane kona, kutetwe amazwi okomeleza.

Owayese Nxukwebe ngalo Sabata wayengababona kwakusasa abantu abavela
emacaleni abeza kubonela. Lomhla we Cawa wacitwa ngoluhlobo : Kwabako
inkonzo ezintatu. Eyokuqala yapatwa ngu Rev. W. Holford, umongameli
wemicimbi ye Lizwi ne Mfundo apo e Nxukwebe. Washumayela kwincwadi yo
Mshumayeli i. 1. wenza amazwi atile ukuqinisa izandla zo Titshala
besikolo se Sabata; amazwi afanele ukubomeleza, nalapo isiqamo
somsebenzi wabo singakauleziyo ukubonakala. Wawateta lamazwi kubantu
abapulapuleyo beliqela elimnandi, nakuba indlu babengayizalisanga.
Ekuhanjisweni kwenkonzo kubeko izituba zokuvuma amaculo ama Ngesi nawaba
Ntsundu. Intshumayelo yona ihanjiswe ngesi Ngesi, yatolikelwa esi Xoseni
ngum’fana esikolwe sagqiba kukusebenza kwake, sakolwa kutyileka kwezwi
lake, kukuzola nokusiqonda kwake isi Ngesi nesi Xosa, umfana esite
sakumkangela equba umsebenzi wake seva sizidla ngaye singamazi pofu.

Kute emva kwe dinala kwangena enye inkonzo, yonganyelwa ngu Mr. Holford,
kodwa umsebenzi wayo wahanjiswa ngu Rev. Grana Kakaza. Kute kaloku
ukuzala kwendlu kwadlula kanye kunokweyokuqala, lati inani labantwana
nabantu abatsha lasimangalisa. Kubonakele kugaleleka nabavela kwindawo
ezigama, abalusapo Iwase Nxukwebe, abasezele izinto zale mini. Ukuqutywa
kwale nkonzo, ute u Mr. Kakaza wamana ebiza abantwana ngezikolo zabo
ukuba babize indawo zokufihlelwa, bacule ingoma ezitile
abebezilungiselele. Incasa abaqube ngayo sikolwa ukuba baya kumana
beyihlwabula futi abaka bayingcamla. Kude kwisikolo sakwa Nobanda kwako
ntwanazana zimbini esite sakuzifaka amehlo sazifunela kwiminyaka
emitandatu nesixenxe ngobudala. Zisuke ezintwanazana zabiza zingaboni “
Ulo Tixo omkulu,” zatsho ngamazwana ayolileyo, zatsho kunye, zacengce-
leza azayeka, zaziteta ngokucacileyo, zada zaya kupuma
zingatintilizanga. Kute ukugqiba kwazo yazidumela indlu yonke noko iyi
Cawa kuse tyalikeni; kwasekuxakeka um’fundisi yedwa, naye sati fanukuba
ukohlwe sisihlalo sake, uyabonga ngapakati. Zite zonke izinto ezibizwa
kule nkonzo zaba zezilunge nelizwi le Nkosi. Indawo esiyipauleyo,
yokulunyukelwa ngabafundisa usapo, kukupakanyiswa kwamazwi ngabanye
ngohlobo lokude bange bayamemeza, babangele ukuba ilizwi lingangqinelani
nesifundo esisakuba sizolile. Enye indawo esiyipaule yavuyisa yona,
ngabantwana abancinane abase mfundweni, abasebequba into epilileyo. Kade
silila ngokuti tina bantsundu sisuka kade kolu gqatso Iwemfundo, size ke
ngoko singaba saya kufika apo sisinga kona. Okunye sibone ukuba
amantombazana ngawona abonakala futi enezifundo zokufihlelwa. Asazi ke
nokuba londawo yeyokunconywa sinina. Umqubi ntlanganiso ude wayigqiba
kumnandi kanye, besapulapula bonke abase ndlwini.

Ngokuhlwa kushumayele u Rev. S. G-udula oza kuya kwa Matole, wa- tyila
kwe 1. Petios iii. 1—3. waxoxa ngokutobela kwabafazi amadoda abo, waza
wabandezela ngokukodwa kwindawo yokuvata—ukutandwa kwako ngabafazi,
ukuhlangana kwabo nentombi zabo babulale indoda xa bafuna izihombo
zentombi, ukutetiswa kokuhomba lilizwi lika Tixo. Uyingene lendawo
shushu umshumayeli wayinyanzela ngamazwi onke abenokuwafumana. Utete
washicilela kumakosikazi; zabonakala zigungqa zikangelana inkabi
zamadoda sati ngentliziyo fanukuba ziteta esom’fo owati exabene
nenkosikazi yakumkulela ngamazwi wacela ukuba kuke kwenziwe umtandazo :
watyila unkabi kwesi siyalo siti, “ Nina bafazi lulamani kwawenu
amadoda,” wati akusifunda wati, “ Uyezwa ke Sala. Masitandaze.” Ati
ukukangelana kwamadoda kwakutiwa ayahlutwa amagunya saqonda ukuba ngoku
ati “ Uyezwa ke Sala.” Sikupaule kodwa kona ukuba akavumi ukukangela
kwelacala lihleli amakosikazi, singazi ukuba oyika ukuti angabuye
afunyanwe sinina! Umshumayeli uyiqube kunene lendawo inqabe kakulu
yezivato esiti fanukuba kova, ‘ Abanendlebe zokuva.” Amakosikazi
namakosazana ahlale ezole tu, amti buxe ngamehlo sakohlwa nokutelekelela
ukuba bayiva njanina lendawo.

Kute ukucula kwangokuhlwa kwadlulisela kokwase mini, kwasiku- mbuza
ilizwi elitile elihlala litetwa ngum’fundisi otile ontsundu, esiti uya
ati akugqiba umsebenzi we Sabata ange umtandazo wangokuhlwa angaya
kuwungena e Nxukwebe, ukuze ati nokuba intshumayelo zimposile, ave
ukucula kwakona. Okwalo mini kubete intliziyo yomntu

opulapulayo kwayi xwila, kwavakala kanye ukuba kuyadunyiswa ukuba
kwenziwa emhlabeni umsebenzi ogcobisa ingelosi emazulwini. Iculo elite
lonakala xa livunywayo ngu “ Ulo Tixo Omkulu.” Usuke umhlabeli
walirolela ingonyana apa engati yeyemihlali yabantwana ; abasuke okunene
abantwana badlala ukuyivuma kwabo, badlala befanele. Kusuke kwashiywa
ukuvunywa kwelo culo, obekudume ukuba kulungiswa e Nxukwebe ngapezulu
kwezinye indawo, ukucula okuncedisana namazwi nokukapela intliziyo
pezulu.

Kusile kusasa, site sakukangela sati namhla abelungu bevenkile baya
kuvuzwa zintokazi zakowetu ezisagqibelelayo ukutenga. Kute kwakubetwa
intsimbi wayi vulela umntu indaba, kwepoko imiqodi kumacala onke,
yayimirozo yameqela ngamaqela. Lifike ixa lokuqala umsebe­nzi izele
londlukazi inkulu yetyalike yase Nxukwebe. Babeliqela elingapezulu
namhla abavela emacaleni abeze kubonela, kwafaneleka ngokukodwa ukungena
kwamadodana azititshala, aye funde kwalapa. Ukuwabona kunge kunkwamla
inqata lobusi. Isizatu soko make sisishiye. Hayike umsebenzi uquty we
kwa ngu Rev. G. Kakaza; kwada kwenziwa into esiyibuka kunene, ukuba
umsebenzi onje xa kukoyo ontsundu onokuwuquba, wenziwe nguye. Kufike
kwahanjiswa kwangohlobo Iwezolo, ukumka komhla. Kodwa namhla kwafu- ndwa
nange ndawo eziteta ngezinto zomhlaba. Amasimnari ade enza into ehlekise
kunene : ukuhlangana kwamadodana angene kwiminyango ekangeleneyo,
ahlangana esitubeni pakati endlwini, angena enga- maxakaxaka angati
avela kude, aqubisana engabahambi. Abuzene indaba sipulapula, apendulana
ngendawo zokuhlekisa; ada kaloku axabana aposana ngamanqindi ebanga
isonka kwalutuli. Abenom’fanana omhlotshana, ityendyana elifanelwa
sihluta salo, lafanelwa kukudinwa kwalo, nobudenge balo, njengokuba
belisiti livela kude apo kungekoyo ukukanya. Bekuko nexaka eliqutu,
isiqukumba apa esifanelwe bubuxakaxaka, into emsindo ulicekwa, pofu
uyifanele ; into ete xa yenza into zayo yafanelwa nakukuqulata imitamo
yesonka, esite kwakufikwa kuso sabona ukuba ama Simnari ngoku
akasalinganiseli, aselesebenza; kwabonakala ukuba abantwana abancinane
abasaqondi ukuba ziya kwanelana ibanisi. Umculo uyole wagqibela, umzi
wapants’ ukukwelwa sikolo sentombazana. Kupunyiwe apo kuyiwe kwamkelwa
ibanisi, ngabo bonke abahambi sikolo se Sabata ; nabangabantu abakulu.
Banikwe amaqekeza amakulu ekeki, atatyatwa zizinto ebezisiti ziyaya
kupuma emnyango sezike zaluma kunene.

Emva koko kuyiwe emidlalweni apo sifike salubona kona usizi; seva
kubuhlungu entliziyweni, sililela abantu bakowetu abatsha. Indawo esibe
sizixoxa entliziyweni ibe pakati kwezinye izezi—“ Kulungile na ukwenza
imigcobo enje ngalo we Anniversari? Lulona hlobo lulungileyo na olu
lokuquba uvuyiso Iwesikolo se Sabata ? Site ke sakudlula apo sakumbula
intshumayelo yapezolo; sati sakubakangela abantu abancinane safika
benxibe kakuhle kakulu, onke amantombazana etsho ngezintsha, eqalele
pezulu eqiyeni aya kuma esihlangwini; sabona ukuba ngulowo uzifikisele.
Ukunyula kwabo izivato sikubuke kunene, ngapandle kwe dresi ezintatu,
ezite zakubona ukuba zinamabala aqaqambileyo, aba engafanelene. Inkoliso
ibitsho ngamabala apakati ekuqaqambeni, sati sakuqwalasela ukucikizeka
kwengubo noko asotuka kakulu. Asazike ke ukuba ngubanina oke iqela
lentombi zakowabo zihombile aze angazidli ngabantu bakowabo ? Singati
tina sibuke kunene nakuba sisiti kube kulusizi. Usizi belulapa. 1.
Lendawo yokuti ngalo mhla, umhla obako yonke iminyaka, aze umntu abe
nezinxibo ezitsha zonke, kusekuhleni ukuba iyimbandezelo etshonisayo. 2.
Akubukekanga kanjalo ukuvata kwentombi zodwa lube udodana olu, ngapandle
kwama semnari no titshala, lutsho ngebatyana apa ezisimilo sikolise
ukumka nelanga. Into entle tina kukusondelelana kwala maqela omabini
kuzo zonke izinto zentlalo yawo. 3. Kube kubi ngokukodwa ukubona ukuba
ezidresi zintle kangaka, zibafanele ngoluhlobo abaninizo, bazitungelwe
ngabanye abantu. Ukuba bebezitungele ngesingasoli nto ngokuhomba kwabo
kubatina sisiti kuhle ukunxiba komntu omncinane. Kodwa intombi
engakwaziyo ukwenza ingubo zayo, siyayoyika ; siyoyikisa kom’fo eseva
ukuba wati ezinye intombi ziti zakub'aka isonka kanti uya kuda usele
ikastor-oil ukuze ulale. Siti intombazana engena kwenza ingubo zayo
yoyikeka njengentombazana engenakupeka, ukuhomba kwayo kuyanyela. 4.
Sicinge nokuti kazi ukuba kwezi nzwakazi engesiba siya zibonga ukuba
singu Hadi, kazi bangapina abantu abahombe ngoluhlobo nase ngqondweni
nase ntliziyweni—abantu abake basibona sona Isigidimi esi sodwa. 5. A-
mandla entlungu abe malunga nemidlalo njengokuba kusitiwa kuziwe
kudlalwa. Kumadodana sibone ibola yodwa; kumantombazana sabo­na umdlalo
abati ngumsino abawugxekayo, esingalaziyo igama labanikaziwo. Owesitatu
ube ngumxube wamadodana nomtinjana, umdlalo abati, “ ngamakwenkwe
nentombi.” Owokuqala ubuye wacitwa zizipata-mzi kuba kusitiwa uyelele
kweyobuhedeni, kwasala ke mibini. Owokugqibela sakukangela kubenzi bawo,
ubuhanjiswa ngobutatasholo bokoyika nokoyikana, kude kwahlwa kungeko nto
yimbi yenziwayo ngaba bantu baze kugcoba. Site sakukumbula ukuba kanene
kuya kuda kube nyakenye kungabuyanga kubeko kuvuyiswa kwabantu abatsha
sagqiba kwelokuba lendawo yemidlalo ike ikangelwe ngabakangeli bezinto.
Umdlalo omnye yonke imini awuyi kukolisa bani. Ukuba imidlalo yabamhlope
ayikasifaneli tina bantsundu kuya ku- nqweneleka ukuba kweyetu kakade
kubeko esike siyivunyelwe; ukuba akwenziwa oko ulutsha luya kurabela;
luze ukuba loyisile kubeko abati belufanele. Sikulilele kunene
ukusweleka kwemidlalo, indawo enobukulu nakuba ngati iyoyikiwa
ngabafundisi abamhlope nabantsundu, nangabapati bendaba zamapepa.

Into emnandi kunene intlanganiso yabantu besikolo. Nakuba babengamakulu
atile ababeko kude kwahlukwana kungaliwanga! Ngokuhlwa kuvakele ukuba
kuza kubako itimiti. Siye ngovuyo olukulu, safika sadla kunene, sati xa
siginya izonka sizitoba nge teya salulibala nosizi Iwase mini. Kube
kuhle ukubona ubuso obungaka bucwayitile. Emva kokudla silindele ukuba
kuza kwenziwa inteto, sike sive into zonantsi ezitetayo, xa ziteta
indaba zeli sikulo, zingashumayeliyo ; site emini kade sibuka umtinjana,
ngoku siseza kuvela amadodana. Site sisajonge apo seva sekuculwa,
sakumbula umfo ka Trollope, owati ndada ndafa ngamaculo. Hayi ke noko
abe mnandi wona, sabuya saxoliswa noko sibe sirana ekuqalweni kwawo.
Bati bakuqukumbela walowo wakumbula emqolombeni wake; yaba idlule
okwanonyaka imini enkulu kubantu bayo.

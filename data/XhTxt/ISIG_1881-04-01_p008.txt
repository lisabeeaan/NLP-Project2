8 ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, APRIL 1, 1881.

IMFUNDO.

UDR. DALE uvelise elinye iq'inga elihle namhla, engxamele ukuba
bafumaneke aba fundisi bentsapo. Kubonakala ukuba uyi nyamekele kanye
imfundo kweli lizwe. Singa singa fundwa, siqondwe ISAZISO sake esikoyo
kwesi Sigidimi. Aba fana, ne ntombi ezi tanda uku zilungiselela
umsebenzi woku fundisa intsapo, zati kodwa azabi natuba, lokuba zizi
xase ngokwazo kwezo zikula ezinga fumana kuzo ituba loku wulungiselela
lomsebenzi, zinalo namhla. Kweli lizwe libanzi ka ngakanana, zininzi
indawo, nendlela abanga nceda ngazo uhlanga, abantu abafundileyo. Into
ke eyo nyusa uhlanga kukuti, sizanywe esi sizukulwana sivelayo ngoku,
siteta ke ngaba balusapo lwetu.

IZIMISO EZILUNGE NABAFUNDISWA UKUFUNDISA,
(Pupil Teachers).

(1.) Abaceli balomcimbi woku fundiswa ukufundisa nokuba ludodana, nokuba
ngu mtinjana na, mabangabi ngapantsi kweminyaka elishumi eline sitatu
(13) ngobudala.

(2.) Abacela lomcimbi, kufuneka ukuba bamanelise Umpatiswa we Micimbi ye
Mfundo, ngendawo ezilunge nesimilo, nokwazi kwabo.

(3.) Abaceli abo, bakuba bamkelwe, baya kungeniswa kwi sikula esiya kuba
sesi manelisayo Umpatiswa we Micimbi ye Mfundo, ngendawo zokuba kuko
amatuba ayakuilungela ulutsha, ukuba lufundiswe kakuble indlela zoku
fundisa, nezo kupata isikula.

(4.) Abafundiswa umcimbi woku fundisa, bakwamkelwa, bozuziswa imali
engengapezu kwe ponti ezilishumi elinambini (£12) ngo mnyaka woku qala.
Ukuba ke umfundiswa lowo, ute wakolisa emsebenzini wake, yonyuswa lomali
ibe zi £18 ngo mnyaka wesibini; le ke iya kuba yimali aya kumana ukuyi
zuza ngo mnyaka, ngalo lonke ixesha loku hlala kwake kulo msebenzi.

(5.) Ixesha aya kulihlala umfundiswa lowo, lodla ngokuba yiminyaka
emitatu, kodwa linga nokolulelwa esihlanwini, ngokwe mvumelo ye
Rulumente.

(6.) Umnqopiso oya kwenziwa, uya kuba pakati komfundiswa lowo, nompati
weso sikula aya kufundiswa kuso, uze ubalwe, kuxelwe nexesha lokubotshwa
ziti nendawo ze miswa nguwo, zibe zezivunywe yi Rulumente.

(7.) Ukuba abapati, abafundisi ngohlobo oluya kumanelisa u Rulumente,
mhlaumbi kufundiswe nangaba ngavunywanga ngu Mpatiswa we Micimbi ye
Mfundo, yoba yimfanelo yo Mpatiswa lowo, enemvumelo ye Rulumente, ukuba
abambe lemali irolelwa abafundiswa aba kweso sikula, boti kodwa abapati
bayi shunyayezwe lonto, inyanga ezintatu, pambi kokuba oku kwenziwe.

(8.) Umfundisi oyintloko kwi sikula apo kufundiswa abafundiswa abo, uya
kubanika izifundiso ezilunge kanye nalo mcimbi wabo, atabate ixesha eli
ngenga pantsi kwe yure (hour) ezine nge veki; ekangele kulendawo yokuba
mababe noku pumela kuviwo, lwabafuna ipepa lesiqiniselo sobu fundisi
(Teacher’s Certificate).

(9.) Umfundisi oyintloko lowo, wozuza £10 ngaye umfundiswa ote
wasifumana isiqiniselo eso; ize yongezwe lemali ibe zi £15, ngaye wonke
umfundiswa osifumeneyo isiqiniselo esi nodumo (with honours); ukuba ke
kodwa u Mpatiswa we Micimbi ye Mfundo, waneliswe ngendawo yokuba
lomfundisi oyintloko, umfundisile umfundiswa lowo ezondawo zibalulweyo,
abe lindelwe ukuba amfundise zona.

ISAZISO.

UMTENGISI wento zonke ezinje nge NKOMO nama HASHE nozi GUSHA into
asakuba umniniyo efuna ukuyi tengisa woyibika kuqala kumtengisi we
fandesi; ize axelelwe ixesha lokutengisa, nemini emaziziswe ngayo ezonto
kwi siqingata sase Nqamakwe kwelama Mfengu.

  Umtengisi, Auctioneer

  JOHN MAZAMISA, Ngqamakwe, 24 January, 1881.

ST. JOHN’S SCHOOL, E MTATA.

KUVULEKELE indawo kwi SIHLANU, 5, sabafana kwesi sikula, ukuba babe zi
Pupil Teachers, oko kukuti abancedisi ekufundiseni, abaya kuti kodwa
baqeqeshelwe umsebenzi woku fundisa, bebotshwe iminyaka emitatu, nje
ngezimiso ezo funyanwa kwesi Sigidimi.

Uviwo luya kwenziwa ngoku ’balwa nangenteto e St. John’s School e Mtata,
kusuku lwo 1st. ne 2nd. ku April, 1881, kwizifundo ezibalulweyo kwi III
ne IV Government Standard, viz :—

Abo balingwa abate emva kwolu viwo bamkelwa ngu Mongameli Wemfundo, baya
kubotshwa ukuba babe zi Pupil Teachers kumpati we sikolo sase Mtata,
iminyaka emitatu ukususela kusuku lwo 1 ku April, 1881, kuya kufuneka
ukuba bawungene lomsebenzi wabo ngako kodwa ukuba lugqitywe uviwo.

Umvuzo wabo uya kuba zi £12 ngomnyaka woku qala, kuze kuti ngowesibini
nowe sitatu ube zi £18 ngomnyaka. Ngapandle kokuba babe ngabeza
esikolweni ngemfundo yodwa, baya kubatala ngomnyaka i £13 kwi
Institution, bebatalela ukuxaswa noku fundiswa kwabo. Akukabiko ndawo
yenziweyo yokuhlala ukuba kwamkelwe abamhlope.

Wonke ozayo eluviweni kufuneka ukuba eze kunye nayo (1) Incwadi
yesimilo, (2) Incwadi yobudala bake, (3) Ipepa elibalwe ngumzali nokuba
ngumgcini wake evumelana nengqesho yake ukuba abe yi Pupil Teacher,
ngokumayela nendawo esezi kankanyiwe kwelipepa.

Bonke abanga balingwa botnmela amagama abo kwa kuqala ku Rev. WM.
CAMERON, e Mtata, baya kulungiselwa indawo endlwini ye sikolo ngexesha
lokuqutywa kwo viwo.

Kungati kubeko indawo zokwamkelwa abafundi abambalwa baba Ntsundu.

Onke amakwenkwe afuna ukungena kufuneka abeko e St. John’s, e Mtata
eluviweni ngosuku lwe 2 ku April, beze nencwadi zesimilo sabo. Kuya
kufuneka behlaule i £5 ngomnyaka, bazifunele ngokwabo incwadi nengubo
zabo.

Uviwo kwabo bafuna ukuba zi Pupil Teachers, nakwaba funa imfundo baya
kuqala ngo 9 wakusasa. I Easter Holidays zipela nge 2 ku April.

Umtata, 14 February, 1881.

“ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA.”

  PEPA lendaba lishicilelwa e-Lovedale—ngenyanga zonke.

Lirolelwa ngomnyaka isheleni ezintatu (3s). Ukuba

  litunyelwa ngeposi (4s); xa lingarolelwanga kwamaqala,—
  zibe zisheleni ezintlanu (5s).

  Ngokutunyelwa kwe mali; kubatabati be Sigidimi kuko abaman’ ukukala
  ngokuti, bengahlauli nje, kungokuba bengenaqinga lokuyi tumela imali.
  Indlela ekunokwenziwa ngazo, zimbini. Enye kukutumela izitampu. Enye
  neyona iqinisekileyo, kukuya kuyinika lomali, kumntu okufupi, ogcina
  incwadi zeposi, aze ke yena anikele ipepa, abati ukulibiza, yimali
  yeposi, (post office order), ukuze ke umntu atumele lona kwi Printing
  Office, e-Lovedale apa. Umgcini posi lowo, uti abize itiki, ukuba
  imali eza kutunyelwa iyi iponti, mhlaumbi inga pantsi kwayo. Indawo
  egqitiseleyi ngokulunga, kolu uhlobo lokutumela imali, kukuba xa
  ilahlekileyo, angabuye umntu aye kufuna elinye ipepa, ngapandle
  kokubuya ahlaule.

Elipepa lishicilelwa E-Lovedale Institution mayela
kolokuqala enyangeni.

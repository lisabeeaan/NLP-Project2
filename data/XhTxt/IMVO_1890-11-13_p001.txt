TOLIBADI.—Umhla we 26 ku August  ngomnye wemihla enobuti obungasa. kwandula kulibaleke e Mgifali (Claikebury) ngokabhubha kwetshawe elingu Samuel Tolibadi into ka Gabaza. Lomfo ka Gabaza ngumfo obekoleke ngendlela zonke, obekumadoda apambili kucala lento zakwa Tixo, osa isandla lula engxovreni yake ngase kuncediseni yonke imisebenzi ka Tixo; ililitemba elikulu lomfundisi nelomzi, ibingomnye wamagosa alomzi kulentlanganiso yabafundisi (District Meeting). Waye ebengepambili kwinto zakwa Tixo zodwa, ube pambili kwizinto zokuqubela pambili ebulungwini, kuba ngumfo obenze izakiwo ezincomekayo, indlu yake abehlala kuyo ibiluxande olubukekayo oluukambile zisixenxe ; waye komnye umzi wake ose Sitebe ebe ake olunye olukwangako. Ube engumfo owake isitali esihle kunene esikulu. Mase sisiti yonke into abeyenza ibiyeyokubhekisa pambili, ube ngokutaza kakulu kwizinto zemfundo ngamazwi nezenzo. Asinamatandabuzo ngokumka kwake, siqinisekele ukuba ubhubhe e e Nkosini, kwa ubomi bake bebumxela njalo, kwaza nasekumkeni kwake waqinisa ukuqondisa ngamazwi ukuba ukufa akakoyiki kuba nya e Nkosini. Imiyalo yake yokugqibela kwintsapo yake wayiyala ngokuze igeine umtandazo. Waza nakwezomhlaba izinto akenza hlazo. Wati onyana bake bobabini wabahlulela ilifa ngokulunge kakulu, waye ebayala ngokuze bahlale kakuhle. Namhlanje kulusizi e Mgwali komkulu nomneikane, kuba ushiyw e yindoda yamadoda. Noko somelezwa ngamazwi atetwa ngumfundisi wetu u Rev, W. S. Davis okuti “ U Tixo uyazazi zonke izinto noko ete wemkisa lecdoda nje angasivusela tnye ekwanje.” Ngokumka kwalo mnumzana kunge kumhla siqondayo ukuba sonke simelwe kukufa, okunene satsho ukuti “ Utnhlaba awoyiki nto oko uvulekile wau.kela umfo ka Gabuza.” 13

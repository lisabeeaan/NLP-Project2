INGXOXO ZASE PARLAMENTE.

U-OBA.

KWINTLANGANISO ye Parliament ebiko ngo 25 August “ u-Mr. Hockley,
wakankanya imali eyi £84 eyamkeliswa inkosi u-Ngonyama wati ngeyinqamka
ngoku lomali kuba londoda ayizipatanga ngohlobo olutembekileyo
nolumtobeleyo u-Rulumente.

Umpatiswa Wemicimbi Yabantsundu ute ukuba u-Mr. Hockley unako, makake
abeke noko sisinye isizatu esimbange ukuba ati lonkosi ayitembekanga.
Wati ingaba yinto elusizi kunene ukuba site sapula umnqopiso esasiwenze
nayo. Ityala lokuba ngundiyalwa alizange linq'ineke ngakuyo.

U-Mr. Irvine ute ingaba kwapulwe umnqo- piso ukuba ite lemali yomamkelo
yanqamka. Akangeyikanyeli indawo yokuba u-Oba uzi- nikele ekuseleni,
kodwa ayingebe mbangi yo- kuba apulukane nemali ayamkelayo leyo.

U-Mr. Brown ute ifama ka Oba ayikude kakulu kwindawo ahlalakuyo. Indawo
anga- yitetayo yena ngalonkosi yile yokuba, nokuba ayidanga iti gqi
mhlope, ibe ngundiyalwa, kodwa ipantse ukuba njalo, akaboni sizatu
sokuba ibatalwe lemali izi £84, angati lomntu enikwa ezi £24 ngonyaka
kube kwanele.

I Colonial Secretary ite nokuba elinani liyacitwana, lonto
ayinakutintela u-Rulumente ekulikupeni, ngokuba liyinxalenye yemali
eyabekwa kudala ngomnqopiso otile, u-Oba akanasenzo sibalulekileyo
esibonisa ukuba ungundiyalwa, nokuba ungaba ulinxila, mhlaumbi engumntu
obutataka. Akuko matandabuzo ngendawo yokuba uvelise inka- tazo enkulu,
noko u-Rulumente akaboni ukuba kungalunga ite yabanjwa imali yake.

U-REV. G. BROWN, M.L.A., ETETA NGOKU-

HLUTWA KWEMIPU.

“ U-MR. BROWN utete e-Parlamente ngoku- hlutwa kwezixobo. Uyakolwa yena
lelo cebo, ubekolwa yindawo enjalo kakade, ucinga oko- kuba kuyafuneka,
ukuze lugcineke uxolo emideni, ukuba abantsundu bahlutwe izixobo. Kuko
umoya okoyo pakati kwabantsundu oti lento umlungu makagxotelwe elwandle,
oku kungenxa yemipu. Ke yena uti umoya onjalo ubungafanele ukuvunyelwa
ubeko. Akuko matandabuzo kuko uburara pakati kwama Mfengu efanel’ukuba
bubangwe kukusuka ahlutwe izixobo zawo, ke ufuna ukwazisa u-Rulumente
ukuba kuko into enjalo. Sokoke kufuneka isandla se Mfengu singabuye
sidibane nompu, ukuba ama Xosa akazi kuyinikwa. Ayitandabuzeki lendawo
yokuba Umxosa ne Mfengu mabapatwe ngokufanayo, kodwa kufanele ukuba
kubeko into eyenziwayo eyakufika idambise (ukuba kunokwenzeka)
lentliziyo icapukayo pakati kwa Mamfengu.”

Kodwa u-Mr. Brown akananto ayenzileyo ngokwake engadambisa lontliziyo,
kanjalo akalifezanga idinga ekutiwa walenza kwaba bantu banga Mamfengu
ase Victoria East (umandla wase Dikeni) mhlenikweni waye- wacela ukuba
amnyule. Kutiwa wawaxelela ukuba uya kuwasebenzela indawo yokuba imipu
yawo ibuyiselwe kwakuwo. Kodwa lenteto yake ixelwe ngapezulu apa
ayilifezi elo dinga.

UMEHLOKAZULU.

ELINYE lamapepa ase Natal liti:—Enye into enkulu esand’ ukwenzeka
kukuzinikela kuka Mehlokazulu omnye wonyana baka Sirayo ababanga ukuba
kuzokubako lemfazwe. Abafundi bendaba bokumbula, ukuba onyana ababini
baka Sirayo bawela u-Tukela beza kubamba abafazi ababebalekele emhlabeni
wama Ngesi, bawelela kwa Cetywayo nabo, baza babulawa abobafazi. Ukuze
ke kuliwe, enye yezinto ekwakutiwe u-Cetywayo makayenze kukurola
abanyana baka Sirayo, waza ke akayenza londawo. Omnye wabo ke, U
mehlokazulu uzinikele ngokwake ku Worth- ington. Uqutywe kwaoko weziswa
e-Natal. Bati endleleni ebonwabe kakulu, wada watengisa ihashe lake
komnye wababantu bebemquba. Efikile e-Natal ufakwe kwaoko etolongweni
akwavunyelwa bani ukuba abonane naye. Akavunyelwanga nomteteleli ukuba
ahlangane naye. Ufike apa ambete ngohlobo abambata ngalo abantu
bakowabo.

Uti yena akazi tyala walenzayo. Okwangoku upetwe ngohlobo abapetwe ngalo
nabanye abase tolongweni. Into ekubuye kwaqondakala ukuba ibingeyiyo
nyaniso yeyokuba uze ekonxiwe.

KWA MOROSI.

LENYANGA ifileyo ipele kungeko zindaba zi- nkulu kwa Morosi. Kwincwadi
ezivela kona kudunyiswa ukuba kwa Baphuti. Ngemini esukuba kumnyama
kakulu babanako ukupuma entabeni, kuba lendawo ilindwe yimikosi yetu
imbi ibanzi. Kekaloku kubonakala ukuba bona banqatyelwe kakulu kukuhla
babuye benyuke betwele ukudla. Maxa wambi banyuka bequba nenkomonempahla
emfutshane. Asikuko nokuba bawa xalisile amafama akufupi nentaba. Ade
ngenye imini apuma alanda, ehamba neqelana lalomkosi ulapo kanti aya
kufumana inxenye yempahla. Ezinye inkomo abaleke nazo amasela zati
zakudinwa azihlaba. azishiya zifile, ezinye zemka kwapela. Noko ke
abebepume lopatuleni bati babulala amadoda amatandatu, akwafa mntu
kwelabo icala.

Kuya bonakala ngoku ukuba akuncedi nto inkulu ukuyi ngqinga intaba, kuta
abalwi bayakufumana ukudla. Abanye bati ngekubuye kufunzwe, intaba
itatwe ngamandla. Mhlaumbi ke kuya kude lupelele kulondawo. Kuluvuyo
ukuva ukuba Isekritari yekoloni u-Hon. Mr. Sprigg uza kuya kulomacala
kwangale nyanga. Unga angapumelela ukulifumana iqinga eliya kuda
lisigqibe esisitukutezi singaka.

IMFAZWE YASE SOUTH AMERICA.

UMKOSI ohlangeneyo wama Peru nama Bolivia elixesha lonke ubungekabi
nakufunza kowama Chili. Into enkulu obuyiswele ibi ’zizixobo. Kekaloku
umkoube obe uye kuzifuna ufike kakuhle. Ngo July ube ulungisela ukuya
kungena emhlabeni wama Chili. Kekaloku wona kwelawo icala azamela
ukufunza ngolwandle, kuba aqinile xa kuliwa emhlabeni. Eminye imikombe
yawo iraule imizana yama Peru ilinde ukuba kungangeni mikombe itwele
ukudla noziruluwa kuyo. Imizi yama Peru ese lunxwemeni ise ihleli
inqatyisiwe ngokulumkela ukuba angangeni ngayo ama Chili. Akukabiko dabi
likulu liliweyo, akukabonakali nelona cala liya kuda loyise.—Empire.

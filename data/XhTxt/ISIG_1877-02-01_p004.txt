INCWADI KA DR. MACKLIN.

ABALESI be Sigidimi bokumbula ukuba ukunyuka kuka Dr. Stewart ukuya
e-Nyassa wahamba no Dr- Macklin oya kuqala esinye isikolo kwapakati
e-Africa Eso sikolo silunge nelihlelo kutiwa yi Established Church yase
Scotland. Apo siya kuma kona kunganeno kwalendawo ine Livingstonia.
U-Mr. Hender­son owayeye kuhlola indawo apo singemakona ubone enganeno
kwezingxangxasi kuti kwakufikwa kuzo zicazululwe neboti zitwalwe
ngabantu. Kwincwadi ebibalwe ngu Dr. Macklin E-Blantyre ngomhla 6 ka
Nov. 1876, uti:—

Seside seza kufika ekupeleni kwehambo yetu kwi- ndawo ezakuba likaya
letu. Asiko kwicibi le Nyassa, sise zintabeni zelama Yanda. Kute kona
u-Mr. Henderson wafumana abantu benomnqweno wokuba kuze umntu omhlope
pakati kwabo, saza saya kuzalisa lomnqweno. Lendawo sikuyo intle kakulu.
Icesine iseyikundifikela katatu. Indishiye sendisitunzela. Olusuku
lolwesibini ndivukile elukukweni, kusangcangcazela nezandla. Ngendinga
bali ukuba bekungengenxa yokugoduka kwabantsundu ababini ababefunelwe
impahla zokuza apa ngabantu base Bayi. Soloko bati bafika apa abazange
bapile. Omnye, ucele ukuba makagoduke, omnye ubesatanda ukuhlala noko
ndite makagoduke hleze ide igqibele ukonakala impilo yake aze kanjalo
abe ngumtwalo kuti. Asi- kuko nokuba ndiya mbulela u-Mr. Kayser ngokusi-
nyamekela kwake, nangemizamo ayenzayo ukusifune- la aba bantsundu babini
nditeta ngabo.

UMSEBENZI WE LOVEDALE.

WOMNYAKA ODLULEYO.

KUYO yonke iminyaka e-Lovedale kumana kubako into ezintsha. Eyalo mnyaka
udluleyo ibisisikalo esivela pakati e-Africa sicela amadodana. Akukabiko
waziyo ukuba siya kupelela pina. Into esiyi kangeleyo yeyokuba
lamadodana aye kuba ngabafundisi bokuqala aya kulandelwa ngamanye,
nokuba siza ku­mana sisiva futi indaba ezivela pakati, kuze mhlaumbi
kubeko amadodana ase Nyassa eza kufunda apa, abone imisebenzi yokukanya
ekode kudlule iminyaka ingekabi nakuqalwa kona.

Ubunyaniso benteto eyayivela kumadodana ngo November we 1875 mhla abuzwa
ngamanye ukuba afuna ukuza apate msebenzi mnina kubonakele ngo May we
1876 lakuti ilizwi ngubanina oya kuya paka­ti e-Africa. Yati yakubuzwa
lonto entlanganisweni akwamangalisa akuvela amadodana avelayo. Inani
elavumayo ukuya laba lishumi elinesine. Abanye kuwo babesebepete
imisebenzi etile abanye bengekabi nayo imfundo ebaneleyo. Kwase kunyulwa
matandatu. Kweso sitandatu abuya atinteleka amabini kwase kunduluka
u-Shadrach Mgunana, William Isaac no Mapas Ntintili. Ababini
kulamadodana asingabo be Free Church. U-Shadrach Mgunana, ngowe U.P.
Mission. Umfundisi wake u-Rev. J. F. Cumming wase Mgwali wati kuya
mvuyisa ukuba ihlelo leremante yake liza kuba nontsundu oye paka­ti
E-Africa. U-Rev. P. J. Paterson we L.M.S. Mis­sion wase Uitenhage
ubefanele ukubulelwa ngokuvumela u-Isaac Williams ukuba azinyulele,
watsho kanti ubesalindele ukuze aye kuncedisana naye akuquba ezifundweni
zake.

Kwintlanganiso yabafundisi abateta kuyo namado-

dana aza kuhamba, kwati kwakutetwa ngemali aya kuyi fumana, u-Shadrach
Mgunana wapendula ngeliti “ Mna bendingakange ndiyicinge nokuyicinga oku
lonto. Ndiya kuyitabata nokuba yintonina enikwayo, nokuba ziponti
ezintlanu.” U-William Koyi wati, “ Ndiyeva ukuba kuyimfanelo yam ukuba
ndiye, ke ndiya kwaneliswa nokuba ndinikwa ntonina.” U Mapas wati “ Nam
bendingakange ndicinge nge­mali. Ukuba ndinikwa into yokwambata nokudla
kuya kuba kwanele.” U-Isaac Williams wati, “ Umsindisi nabantu bake
bandenzele okukulu kangangokuba nam mandizame ukubenzela abanye konke
endinako.”

Amanye amadoda amhlope ayesiya e-Livingstonia ayeselese ndleleni ukuza
kwelilizwe wabake u-Dr. Stewart ecelwe pesheya ukuza awapate awase
Nyas­sa. Kwakunzima ukuba sahlukane naye nangenxa yendlu eyakiwayo apa
neyapesheya kwe Nciba. Noko ke sasivuma ukuba umpakati we Africa ufuna
amadoda ngapezu kwetu, saza ke ngoko sabulela ku Tixo ukuba emtuma kona.
Uya kuba nokunceda okukulu ngokubonisa kwase ntloko indlela ekufane- lwe
ukuhanjwa ngayo.

Kwati ukumka kuka Dr. Stewart kwacelwa u -Mr. Buchanan, owayevela
e-Natal, ukuba ancedise kum- sebenzi wase Lovedale, ongamele
intlanganiso yaba­fundisi balapa. Yamfanela londawo ngenxa yobudala bake
nokuba kukade ewupete umsebenzi. Abafundisi abasebenza kunye naye
bayambulela ngenxa yokuba lukulu koncedo lwake. Unokukuqonda oku­kulu
ukupatwa kwezinto, esitembayo ukuba noko abesuswe kukungapili apo
ebekona, uya kuti lomnyaka ungenayo umcumele kwakwenzayo, ude udlulise
nakokwapambili

Kulomnyaka udluleyo kufike no Rev. D. Young wafika ngexesha abefuneka
kakulu. Uselewupete wonke umsebenzi wake apa—owokushumayela
nowokufundisa, kwiklasi ezise zantsi nezipezulu.

IMFUNDO.

UKUTYILWA KWENTETO EZISINGISELE KU TIXO.

IKLASI yokulungiselela abaza kufunda ngendawo ezi- singisele ku Tixo
ibihambiseka kulomnyaka udluleyo. Okukona kuqiniswa kwayo kuya kuba
ngalo mnyaka ungeneyo. Kodluleyo kube kufundiswa izinto ezisingisele
kobasemhlabeni ubulumko.

Abaklasi zisaqalayo bahlulwahlulwe bamagqiza matatu. Babodwa abonyaka
wokuqala, bako abowesibini, bako abowesitatu. Bafundiswa bona Imbali
yale Koloni, neyamangesi, neyamanye amazwe, ne grammar yamangesi, ne
Geography nokubala nokubala. Ngeziqamo zemfundiso singakankanya ukuba
abafumene iziqiniselo zakwa Rulumeni—kuqukwa nentombi babe 24. Abasi
bozo bafumene ezipakamileyo. Ababini babe ngabokuqala kuyo yonke
ikoloni. Kuko kanjalo abafumene iziqiniselo zeklasi ezipakati —omnye
wabo wafumana esipezulu.

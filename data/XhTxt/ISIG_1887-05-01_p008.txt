ISAZISO SIKA RULUMENTE.—NO. 278, 1887.

Kwi Ofisi yo Mongameli we Mihlaba ya Komkulu,

  EKUBENI U Rulumente enikwe igunya nje ngesigqibo esimalunga ne Siqendu
  so Kuqala so Mteto wemi Hlaba
  Elahliweyo kanye (Derelict Lands) No. 3 we 1879, wokutana qwe,
  nokuyipata yonke imi Hlaba Elahliweyo

  kanye ngolublobo lulandelayo, olunje ngolu :—

  “ Makuti xeshikweni kuko irafu emele ukurolelwa u Rulumente wale
  Koloni, ngendawo nokuba ngumhlaba ka
  Rulumente, esukuba ide yayiminyaka elishumi ilityala, ibe londawo,
  nokuba ngulo mhlaba ite yayekwa, yashiywa,
  yayekwa mpela, kwaza akwabiko nomnini kuyinikwa, nokuba
  ngobeyiqashiselwe, kwaye kunjalonje, kungeko namntu
  bambekileyo esikundleni sabo usendaweni yabo onokufunyanwa, kuyakuba
  semtetweni ukuba i Ruluneli ibale isaziso
  ngawo kwi pepa lezaziso zika Rulumente eliyi Government Gazette,
  nokuba kukulipina ipepa angatanda Iona, umhlaba
  onjalo, nokuba ngumzi oti ushiywe kanye, sishicilelwe inyanga zide
  zibentatu, izekuti ukuba kwezi nyanga ntatu, aku-
  velanga bani, nokuba ngowaye wunikiwe, nokuba ngoqeshileyo, nokuba
  ngobekwe ngabo kwezo ndawo, oyakubanga
  ezondawo, aze ahlaule le rafu inexesha ; i-Ruluneli iyakuti zakupela
  ezo nyanga ntatu, iwutabate lomhlaba nokuba
  ngumzi, iwupate kwangesiko lemihlaba enjalo emisiweyo kule Koloni,
  ukuba akutanga (njengoko kwahlala kunjalo)
  lomhlaba nokuba ngumzi, emaupatwe ngalemiteto ube ub’ubambisele
  ityala, indawo ezikwi ziqendu ze sibozo, nesitoba,
  neseshumi se Simiselo (Ordinance) se 9 se 1844 ngokumayela
  nokutengiswa komhlaba nokuba ngumzi onjalo, yaye
  nokusetyenziswa kwalo mali kuyakuhanjiswa ngokwalomteto.”

  Kuyaziswa kumntu wonke okokuba le Mihlaba icwangciswe ngezants’apa,
  ikwisi Sitili (Division) se Victoria
  East mayibe ngohlobo ilahliwe kanye, nje ngoluhlobo sekuxeliwe ngalo,
  nokokuba u Rulumente uyakuyitabatela kuye
  ayihlute, ngomhla wokuqala (1st) ku October 1887, ngapandle kokuba
  iyakuba sel’ibangiwe yada yahlaulelwa ezo
  rafu zayo bezigqitelwe ngamaxesha, zahlaulwa kwi Mantyi yeso Sitili,
  njengoko kufuneka kwenjiwe njalo kweso siqe-
  ndu salo Mteto kucatshulwe kuwo.

F. SCHERMBRUCKER, Umongameli.

IMIHLABA ESE SITILINI SASE VICTORIA EAST EMAYIBE ILAHLIWE KANYE.

IMPENDULO-

Aliwal North, October 1873.

MANENE,—Impendulo yam ngesicelo senu, esindimema ukuba ngomnye wabamele
nina kwindlu ye Assembly, ndiyayi vuma londawo ngovuyo.

Ndise ndineminyaka ekuma 30 ndikweli cala lase mpumalanga le Koloni,
endite inkoliso yelo xesha ndaba ndixulumene nalo Mahlulo; ndifanele ke
ngoko ukuzazi intswelo zawo. Ndoti ukuba ndinyuliwe ndilinge ukuze
ningadaniswa kwinto eniyicingayo ngam ukuba ndifanele ukunimela.

Kunga amalungu elicala lase Mpumalanga angati kwaku- hlanganwa akuyeke
loku ucuku, akangele okulilungelo.

Ngokumalunga nalomteto uzanyelwa ukumiswa yi Govern­ment, wokuyahlula
ikoloni ibe ziqendu zisixenxe (Seven Circle Bill) endaweni yesibini,
andiboni kufuneka kwawo. Ungati wenzelwa ukuncipisa impembelelo yetu
tina base Mpumalanga. Ukuba andiboniswa zizatu zawo zaneleyo ndiya
kuwucasa.

Enye indawo exoxwayo ngoku yeyokuba zonke iremente zizixase, kungabiko
zifumana uncedo kwi Government (Vol­untary Question). Andisifumani
isizatu esaneleyo sokuba lungabiko oloncedo lube luko ngapambili.
Andikolwa ukuba kuko iremente ezingazuzi lungelo kulo olonoedo. Izindlu
zencwadi, nezitiya, nemiboniso, nezikolo, ziyaneedwa yi Go­vernment,
andisiboni ke isizatu sokuba zingancedwa iremente. Into engelisenziwa
kukuba lomali yahlulahlulwe pakati kwamacala onke, kuze ukuba oko
akulungi kwandule ukutiwa malupele kanye uncedo.

Inteto ezinje ngokwahlukana kwecala lase Ntshona langa nelase
Mpumalanga, nezokushenxiswa kwe komkulu ngezike ziyekwe okwangoku.

Indlela Zenqwelo Zomlilo ngekuzanywa ukuba zikauleziswe Yxokuba zisidla
imali eninzi. Abanye bayazi ukuba zintatu indlela ekutetwa ngazo, ke mna
ndolinga ukuze ningalityalwa xa kude kwako ekunyulwa ukuba kwenziwe
yona.

Masiti kanjalo singazilibaleli indlela nemicancato yokuwela imilambo.
Ukuba kuko owela I-Gqili kulomzi wase Aliwal North, ungaluncedo kulo
Mahlulo nakwe wunqongileyo.

Ucingo ndolinga ukuba lubehle luze kufika nalapa.

I-Government ibe ifanele ukuzamela ukuba amanzi emvula iwatintele
iwabutele kumacibi atile ambiweyo kwindawo ngendawo zekoloni, ukuze abe
noncedo lakubalela.

Bonke bayazi ukuba kuko izikalazo ngemiteto ekoyo kule koloni, nokuba
ayipatwa njenge ndlela yawo. Umsebenzi wazo I-Mantyi uzigqitile. Enye
into bezifanele ukuti pambi kokuba zingene ebumantyini zike zifunde
imiteto. Kulo Mahlulo wase Aliwal North zibe zifanele ukuyihambela
imiza- na enje nge Lady Grey, endaweni yokuba ababanjwa, namadindaia,
nabamangali, yonke lonto ibe yiyo eza ku Mantyi. Imiteto elunge nezicaka
nenkosi zazo ibe ifanele ukulungiswa, yanelise inkosi ngapandle koku
zibandezela izicaka.

JOSEPH GRAHAM.

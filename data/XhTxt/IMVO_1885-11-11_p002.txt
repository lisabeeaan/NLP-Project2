AMAKWELE ETU SODWA.

Komgha, 17 October, 1885.

Elipepa Tmvo alimanga ngaluchuku li- rni ngabafel’ usizi uhlanga
iwetu—fakake lentetwana noko iluchuku yona lokupu- lapulwa noko mhlaumbi
ngumntu.

Ukweyana kwetu bantu babala libufipa kuyakumana ukusikalisa kwatina,
umntu omhlope akanqwanqwi ukuyenza kuti into yokumeya owetu napakati
kwabanye kuba uyayazi lonto yobanga abanye bafumane into
yokuhleka—yenyeke inda- wo yokweyiwa kwetu leyo, nokuba lulu- pina
uhlanga mna kokwam ukuqonda. Into engasikolisiyo njengohlanga ubehle
abone naye omhlope ukuba lento abayifuni ababantu, ndingatetike ngento
esifane sibe ngabangazitandiyo ngobudenge, maxa wambi. Uti owetu oqala
umsebenzi otile bagculele abanye bakuva omhlope edela, iyawakufelemoyeni
lantoke— balibale abetu ukuba kanene ukungapu- meleli kwalonto
beyibonilenje abezenze intlanga yokuba yintlekisa ngohlanga Iwetu.
Uyabona into enjengepepa laba- innyama ivenkile yomnyama, indlu ye-
ndwendwe yomnyama nokuba yiyipina into elungileyo yetu bamnyama
ibitanele ukwazeka ukuba ukuma kwayo kusezingxoweni zabamnyama, omhlope
akanakukatala ulindele ukuhlekisa yena. Anditsho bahlobo bam ukuti
omhlope unjalo wonke kuba baninzi abahlobo abade ukuluvela kwabo uhlanga
Iwetu bagqita nasekuvelaneni kwegazi.

Mna nditi kwakolisa nokuba bake aba- ntu bakowetu baswele kangaka indawo
zokulala kokona koti kwakubako ozame- la ukubenzeia ikaya lendwendwe
bayeke ukugculela batande ukutamsanqelisa lo- wo ukunga angaqubela
pambili kulomse- benzi lande elokuya. Wosinika imbeko omhlope naye
akubona ngoku ukuba siyekile ukujingajinga “ndingamnkelwa na apa, hayi,
asifimi, akukondawo yaba- mnyama apa,” umnke udanile, kuba ubusavete
kuhle uba boba ungumntu nawe. Nenkabi yenqwelo endala ye- nqwelo
wakumana ukuyipekuza ngengu- bo ingade ikuhlabe ibe ngakubaleka, nenja
nantonina wakumana uyijikajika ayivuyiseki yona inja kanti ukuba u'bu-
yeke umzimba wahambela paya ibiyaku- dana. Ziyafuneka ivenkile zetu
sizixhase kwatina sibe nekwele ngazo namakaya endwendwe
njaloke—kwakubanjalo kokona soya sisohdela kwababantu ngokungati
siyabakwelela bosondela basikange- lisise. Amasiko abo amaninzi
osincedisa kuba asikulwa namteto esifuna kona, koko kukuxhaswa ngumteto
sizama ama- lungelo etu. Nditi mna kanjalo, sosinda ekumeni imini yonke
evenkileni xa kusamana ukungena abamhlope uman’ ukutyhalelwa ecaleni
ukatywe mhlaimbi wakuma endleleni. Ivenkilana maziqa- Iwe nge 5s
ngapandle kwesikweliti, soti sakuhlonelana bosihlonela siyeke ukuvu-
yelelana.

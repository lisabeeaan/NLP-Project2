LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, MAY 1, 1878.

Isigidimi Samaxosa.

U-REV. ALEXANDER DUFF, D.D.

KUKO emhlabeni amadoda ampilo inceda uluntu lonke—amadoda angapileli
iziquzawo mhlaumbi isizwe mhlaumbi ibala analo mhlaumbi ihlelo le
remente akulo. Umsebenzi wawo usingisela ekubeni maziti zonke intlanga
zifnmane intsikelelo asukuba wona eyi fumene. Ababaninzi abantu
abanjalo; kodwa ko u-Dr. Duff ubengomnye wabambalwa abampilo iyeyo mntu
wonke, abakufa kufanele ukulilelwa zizo zonke izizwe. Ubukulu bomsebenzi
wake bobu- singisele ekunyuseni intlanga ezise bunya- meni. Ubengomnye
wamadoda ate akubona ukuba wona ubom abufumene ngo Kristu aza akanela
ukuqonda ukuba abangenaye bafile, koko azinikela ekubaboniseni ukuba
okunene u-Kristu uyindlela, ububom, abafanelele ukuba bonke babusukele.
Emehlweni ake isimo sababedeni besifana neso mntu omka nomlambo, ati
ukuhleka ahleka ngako kube kokukona kanye kungenisa intlungu kumntu omi
pezu komlambo ekangele. Ukuzinikela kwake kulo msebenzi wokunceda izizwe
zababedeni bekufana nokwentlambi enentliziyo iputuma okayise embona eza
kuya kwela engxangxasini. Intliziyo yake yaba kusoloko ivuta lutando,
utando oluye lunyuka ngokunyuka lwada lwasuka lwacinywa kukufa ngomhla
12 ka February. Ukwenjenjalo ke sizalisa ilizwi lokuti kufanelwe
ukulilwa ngabo bonke abanako ukusiqonda isimilo somsebenzi wake.

U-Alexander Duff wazalwa ngo 25 ka April 1806 kufupi nomzana wase
Pitlochrie e-England. Kwase buncinaneni bake ubekalipile kakulu kwizinto
zemfundo ; kanjalo ubeti ukuba uke wabona umfanekiso wesitixwana
nobubanzi bamazwe ase nobuhedeni kanti lonto uya kuyi kumbula kude kube
nini. Esikolweni ayibanga ninzi mali ayifumana kuyise yokuhlaulela
imfundo yake. Wagqibela ngamashumi amabini eponti awanikwa eseyi nkwana
eminyaka ilishumi elinesibini. Emva koko wabanokuzixasa ngokwake ngemali
enje nge bursari, ayi fumana ngokukupa abanye eluviweni. Enye

indawo eyamncedayo ekufundeni kwake e-St. Andrews kwaba kukufunda pantsi
kwendoda enje ngo Thomas Chalmers, waza waba nokubonana futi naba

kubo ukuqubeka komsebenzi wabo.

Wabekwa izandla ngomhla 12 ka August 1829 xa amashu.mi mabini anesitatu
ubudala. Kungekabi kade emva koko walishiya ikaya lake ukuya kuba
ngumfundisi e-India, apo aya kugaleleka ngo May 1830—wafika engenazo
nencwadi ngapandle kwe Baibile, engenawo namapepa, nengubo ezi zise
mzimbeni zodwa—enye impahla yake yonke itshone elwandle ; kuba umkombe
aye kuwo wapuka kwada kwakabini.

Wagaleleka kwelozwe ubushushu bunge- nakubaliswa. Eyona ndawo yaba nzima
kuye kwaba kukubona ubuhedeni bakona, ubuhedeni obulumkileyo,
obunezikolo zabo nencwadi zabo. Wafika apa e-Calcutta kungeko sikolo
sama Kristu, ezikoyo zise macaleni. Wahlala inyanga ezimbini ekangela
efunda ubunjani bezinto, kwaza ekupeleni kwazo wagqiba kwelokuba aqale
isikolo kona apo e-Calcutta. Saqala ngabantwana abahlanu. Uninzi
lwabantu lwalusiti uyageza, ukude aqale isikolo kulomzi, lusiti
abantwana abafundiswa nguye baya kupuma bekukanyela ukubako kuka Tixo,
bazinikele enkohlisweni. Nakuba shumayeli abebemana ukuza kushumayela
bevela ema- caleni kwabako abamcasayo. Wati ke noko kuba eyibona yena
imfanelo yake pambi ko Tixo, wasiquba esisikolo. Kungekabi kade saya
sisanda ngamanani nangodumo, kwada kwafuneka ukuba kwakiwe enye indlu.
Kwati mhla saviwa eso sikolo bagoduka bengauhlanganisi—bati abebeze
begxeka be- delile, abanye bebezele ukuze bafumane into yokunqinisa
indelo yabo ngesi sikolo, bemka lomini bengateti. Yati neyona Governor
inkulu yakona yemka yanelisiwe. Emva koko abantu bavela kwindawo ezikude
ukuza kubonela into eyenziwa ngu Dr. Duff ukuze nabo bamlandele.
Wabungenela kaloku ubunyama base India ngendlela ezi- ninzi, wati
efundisa eshumayela waba ebala incwadi. Eso sikolo sake senyuka ngango
kuba sibe ngumtombo ompompoza abafundisi.

Ukwalekela kulowo msebenzi wenziwa umongameli we Komiti ye Eree Church
elungiselela ukutunyelwa kwelizwi kwinda­wo ngendawo. Akupatiswa

Kwizituba anazo ezipakati komsebenzi wake wemihla ubeti ukuba kuko into
angancedisa ngayo nakwezinye iremente enjenjalo. Enye into ebinkulu kuye
ibibubudlelane babako- hvayo. Noko eyona remente yakowabo ibiyi Free
Church ube nobuhlobo ebambana ngezandla nabafundisi bamanye amacala.
Kwinteto yake ayenza ngomnyaka we 1873 ngo May wakala kakulu ngokuba
amakristu alibele kukulwa odwa kanti utshaba lwawo onke alulele lona,
lonakalisa kwindawo ezingapandle nezingapakati.

  ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MAY 1, 1876.

ICAWA ETUNXE.

(Rev. J. A. Chalmers.)

UMZEKELISO WONYANA OWALAHLA IKOWABO.

No. XII.

UMZALI WAKE OLUNGILEYO.

Upumile ke uyise wambongoza. Wapendula ke yena wati kuyise, Uyabona,
leminyaka ingakanana ndiyakukonza, andizanga ndigqite mteto wako; kanti
akuzanga undinike netakane eli, ukuze ndenze imihlali nezam izihlobo;
kodwa zenikweni afikileyo lonyana kako, wayidlayo wayigqiba impilo yako
namahule, umxelele itole eliya lityetyisiweyo. ute ke yena kuye,
mntwanam, wena uhleli unam, nazo zonke into zam zezako. Bekusifanele
ukuba kwenziwe imihlali ke kuvuywe, ngokuba umninawe wako lo ebefile
wabuya wapila; zibelahlekile wafunyanwa. Luka xv. 28, 29, 30, 31, 32.

NAMHLA sifika kumazwi okugqibela alomzekeliso afike asityilele ukulunga
kwalomzali walamadoda mabini sibe sibaliselwa ngawo.

I.  —Ndifike uditi ke asimzali lo ukubatanda abantwana bake: kaukangele
    nawe, ukoniwa kwake ugonyana bake, ufike alahlwe ngomncinane nanko
    esimka esiya kude ezibub'isa kona, ufike atetelwe kakubi ngomkulu
    mhlenikweni intliziyo yake ivuyisiweyo, inemihlali kugoduka
    komntwana wake obelahlekile. Kunjalo nje ke kubonakala utando lodwa
    kwabanyana bake, omncinane akamgxotanga akuba ebuye ze eswele nento
    yokwambata ; komkulu akatshongo ukuti hlala apo ke ungavumi
    kungenanje endlwini, hayi umbongozileunyana wake watetanaye kakuhle
    embonisa ukuba ulungisileukumamkela unyana wake akuza kucela uxolo.
    Ngumzali wokuruquka, wokubaqumbela onyana bake, wokubatetisa
    kabukali angabi sazana nabo. Wenjenjalo ke? Hayi wabatwala bobabini,
    asimzali ukunyamezela! Ati okukoua apatwa kakubi ugonyana bake kube
    kokukona abubonakalalisayo ubunzulu bentando yake. Lomzali ngu Tixo,
    nguye olutando lungako, olunyamezelo lungako, ongabehle aqumbe noko
    oniwayo; kaucinge nawe, njengokuba nawe uzibona ngokwako into oyiyo
    emehlweni ake, njengokuba ungumoni ongazanga ayeke ukuyapula imiteto
    yake uti weua u-Tixo akalilo inyamezela na, akanguye olutando
    lungummangaliso na? Iminyaka ngeminyaka uhleli umdelile, uhleli
    umgxekile, uhleli ungalivumi ilizwi lake, uhleli ungavumi ukuguquka.
    Imihla ngemihla upikelene nokwenza okukohlakeleyo. Ukupete
    ngaluhlobo lunina keyena? Njengokuba ungavumi kuvelisa ziqamo
    zobulungisa ukwenze ntonina? Umntu onomti emyezweni wake
    ongavelisiyo ziqamo leminyaka uyawugaula awote. Ukwenjenjalo na ke
    u-Tixo? Njengokuba wena ufumana wayinto engateni no Tixo ukususile
    na emhlabeni? Hayi, akayekanga kuteta nawe, akayekanga kukubongoza
    ukuba upume kweso similo sikohlakeleyo, akayekanga ukukutumela
    izigitshimi zake zize kuti kuwe “Buya kwezo ndlela zako ziugendawo.”
    U-Tixo ukubonise utando ekubeni wena umenzela ubutshaba. Akuko
    utandwayo njengawe ngu Tixo kanti ke noko olo lutando lwake
    uyalupalaza nje kodwa kuba aluvunywa nguwe wena anga ungaba
    ngumntwana wake inyaniso.

II. —Ndifike kwilizwi lesibini nditi asimzali ubulumko lomzali
    sibaliselwa ngaye kulomzekeliso. Unyana wake omnci akuyibiza impahla
    elunge naye akayibambanga, akufuduka eyiq'uba akamalelanga,
    ezibub'isa ezweni elikude akayanga kumfuna. Ukuba ebemnyanzele
    umntwana wake lowo ukuba ahlale ebengasavumi nje, nto leyo
    yakonakala ityala ebeyakulibeka pezu koyise, alile ngaye ati nguwe
    ke lo wanditintelayo

ndingemki ndiye kuzimisela umzi wam. Emyeka nje umntwana wake enze
ngentando yake kokukona ambeka endleleni yokuba azibonele ngokwake ububi
bezondlela azitandileyo. Akapikanga nonyana wake omkulu akuk'alazelwa
nguye, hayi umbonise kakuhle wamtyilela imfaneleko yalomsebenzi
awenzileyo yokumvuyisa unyana wake omnci ogodukileyo. No Tixo ke
ukwasipata tina ngobulumko, kuko bantu bati, sinantonina singanyanzelwa
ngu Tixo ukuba siguquke inguyena nje onamandla onke, unantonina engu
Tixo nje angenzi ngegunya asiq'ube side sivume, wenza ngobulumko u-Tixo
ngokokungasinyanzeli kwake, akanakusinyanzela entweni esingayivumiyo,
entweni esingayitandiyo, yena ufuna ukuba sizinyulele ngokwetu kuba
sakuzinyulela indlela yobulungisa kokukona sozitandela tina ngokwetu
ukuhamba emkondweni welizwi lake. U-Tixo akanakusisonga endleleni
esesipikelene ukuhamba kuyo, ufuna yena ukuba side siye kufika apo ipela
kona ukuze siti sakuba siruqukile yiyo sibuyele kuye ngokuzitobileyo
sisiti, Bawo ndonile kulo izulu nasemehlweni ako andisafanele kuba
ngzmyana wako.

III. —Asimzali ukuzibeka kwake. Kaukangele uhlobo lokuzipata kwake,
     akuti unyana wake omnci ndinike isahlulo sempahla esilunge nam.
     Mzali wumbi ebeyakuti, usitsho nje ungubanina wena utshoyo?
     Yimpahla oyizeke pina wena le uyibizayo kum? Hayi, usuke wayik'upa
     akwabiko nanye ayigcinayo ayibambayo, umnike yonke. Ebuyile unyana
     wake mhlenikweni wagodukayo, engasenanto, akabuzanga ukuti, ipina
     impahla owemka nayo? Akatanga unyana wake makake enze imbali
     yentlalo yake kwelazwe abeye kulo, akatanga, ewe ndokuxolela ukuba
     ute, wati wati, akamdubulanga unyana wake ngamazwi abukali okuti
     nantso ke into endibe nditeta yona, tshotsho! Hayi usuke lomzali
     wazola tu kuzo zonke ezizinto zalomntwana wake. Kaukangele kanjalo
     unyana wake omkulu umtetele kakubi mhlenikweni kwakusenziwa
     imihlali ngokugoduka konyana omnci, akambizanga umntaka vise ngezwi
     lokuti umninawe wam, ute ukumdubula kwake uyise, xenikweni
     afikileyo lonyana wako wayidlayo wa- yigqiba impilo yako namahule,
     usuke wati uyise wazipendula zonke ezondawo ngoxolo kakuhle
     wamtobela unyana wake efuna ke okokuba kungabi kokukona alahlekayo
     ngumsindo, Mntwanam, bekusifanele wena nam ukuba senze imihlali
     sivuye. Zonke ke ezondawo awenza ngazo lomzali kubonakalalisa
     ukuzibeka kwake. No Tixo ukwanjalo, akapiki yena nobugeza betu,
     neratshi letu, nekw'ele letu, nemisindo yetu, usuke asibonise
     ngelazwi lake okokuba asinakuba nantsikelelo kuso esosimilo
     sokumc'asa sokumgxeka sokumdela.

IV. —Ndifike kwilizwi lesine nditi asimzali inceba, asimzali ububele,
    asimzali isisa, asimzali ukugcina abantu. Kutiwa izicaka zakona zibe
    zidikwa zizonka, zibe zihluta zanele, zibe zipetwe kakuhle. Into
    eyamgodusayo lomfana kukumbula lompato, kukumbula londyebo, ucinge
    ukugcinwa kwabakonzi nguyise ukuze agoduke. Egodukile kuvuye bani
    na? Kuvuye umzi upela, zavuya nezicaka, kwaye ke oko kubonisa inceba
    yalomnini mzi, uye kubizwa umalusi owalusa igusha ezindle paya
    kwatiwa makabuye aze kudla inyama ahlute, obelima emasimini kutiwe
    makazikulule ezonkabi asondele naye aze kulo mdudo,isicakazana
    esibesisebenza msetyenzana waso kutiwe masiwulahle size kugcoba
    naso, iguqulwe yonke into engumkonzi ebusayo kwatiwa unyana omnci
    ugodukile uyise ux'ele itol’ iduna lenkomo elityebileyo mayibuye
    yonke iyi yeke imisebenzi yayo ike ipumle ize kuvuyisa uyise. Okuya
    ebengeko lonyana mnci uyise ebengalikankanyi negama lake eli
    mntwini, ebuyile uhlanganisiwe wonke umzi wake ukuze ube nemihlali.

ngokuba lama Bhulu akulule amanye libonwe ngumntu wonke, kuba lonto
iqelekile. Kudala ke silijongile kabukali elityala lika SKOP, kuba

kungatetwanga nakuba zade zaxelelwa mhlope okokuba

njengesiko lawo, lomapolisa ango RAUBENHEIMEN no SCHUTTE ahamba - hamba
pandle engenasipako ezimilweni zawo. Soke sinixelele ngokufutshane
ngobungqina balomini:—

Bababaninzi abantsundu abati nge Cawa February 22nd umti lowo u Stop

isiduli.

endleleni, lada elinye lafunga okokuba wapangwa ngamapolisa lawo

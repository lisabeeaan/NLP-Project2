E-Dikeni, November 20, 1879.

ISAZISO.

EKUBENI abantsundu abawumiyo lommandla wase Qo-
nce, benga banini mhlaba abanye, abanye bengaba

qeshi, kungati abakuqondi ukuba bamelwe uku rola lemali
yendlela (Road Rates), njengoko benjenjalo bonke aba-
mhlope.

Kuyaziswa namhla, ukuba bonke, abamagama akoyo
kwincwadi exela ukuma kwamaxabiso omhlaba wabo, bame-
lwe ukuba bazirole ezomali zibizwayo, kwi Ofisi Yokulu-
ngisa. Imicimbi Yalommandla, (Divisional Council) ngapa-
ndle kokude kubuye kwenziwe esinye isaziso, kuneso senziwa
ngonyaka, kwipepa lakwa Rulumente (Government Gazette)
ngokomiselo we 9 womnyaka we 1858.

Kusaziswa kwinto yonke, ukuba imali yendlela (Road
Rates), ifuneka ibatelwe ngabanini mhlaba, nangaba qeshi-
leyo, kule Ofisi ikankanyiweyo. Ukuba lomali, kude
kwa yinyanga, kususela kwesi saziso sanamhla, inga rolwa-
nga, kuya kunyanzelwa ngegunya lomteto, kunga buyanga
kwaziswe.

Ngabezi ndawo ke ekufuneka beyi rolile lemali:—Em-
ngqesha, e-G-xulu, e-Zeleni, e-Ramnyiba, e-Qibira, kwa Zi-
denge, e-Ngcobo, e-Rabula, kwa Nakalo, e-Hlovini, e-Zi-
ncuka, kwa Ngwenya (Red Hill), Middle Drift, (kwa
Kama), nakuzo zonke Izikolo, Nezixeko, Nemihlaba Ye-
ngqesho.

  Isandla—(Secretary),

  Kwi Ofisi ye Divisional Council,
  E-Qonce, October 23, 1879.

  ISINQINISO ESITSHA.

Fort White, February 17,1877.

Ku Mr. J. Shaw, Fort Beaufort,—

Nkosi etandekayo.—Umntu otile wandiyalela ukuze ndike ndilinge iyeza
lako loxaxazo mhla ndandipetwe kakubi sisisu. Landinceda kwakamsinyane
kakulu ndaza ndaqgiba kwe- lokuba ndiya kuka ndililinge kwinkwenkwana
yam ebinga- zange ipile soloko yazalwayo. Noko amagqira ndibe ndibamba
kuwo ubesuke umntwana ancedeke intsuku ezimbini nokuba zintatu. Ndilinge
elako iyeza. Ndite ndaku mnika okwesibini ndabon’ ukuba uyancedeka. Ite
ipela ibotile waba selepilile kwakanye umntwana. Ngoku sekupele inyanga
ezilishumi elinesibini noko asikazanga simbuyele isifo. Ndingakankanya
ukuba nabanye abamelwana bam endite ndabayalela eliyeza lako balilinga
kwababo abantwana, nabo bancedekile. Akuko namnye ongati elazi umsebenzi
walo ahlale ngapandle kwalo endlwini yake. Mna andike ndihlale
ndingenalo.

Ndingowako ongu

J. THOMAS.

ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA JULY 01, 1884

IZIMISELO ZENTOLONGO.—Ezi zimiselo zisand’ ukubonakaliswa kwipepa
lakomkulu :—“ Xa um’banjwa angamelwe sisohlwayo sokusebenza nzima,
imantyi inokuti, ngemvumelo yom’patiswa micimbi ye Koloni inike ituba
lokuba lowo obanjiweyo afunde incwadi azitandayo, namapepa, asebenzise
nento zokubala. Unokuvunyelwa kanjako ngu Mantyi kwangalomvumelo ukuba
umntu lowo anxibe ingubo zake zasekaya, adle nokudla angaba
uyakutunyelwa ngabahlobo bake endaweni yokwase ntolongweni.”

E NYARA.—Ngo Mgqibelo (5 July) u Mr George Earr uyakutengisa irikabi
nemazi eziku 40. Zonke zityebile Kunye nenqwelwana yamahashe. Itengiswa
ngenxa yengxakeko zomniniyo.—Emsitweni (market) ngo 23 June ibotolo
ebenexabiso elikulu. Iponti ihambe kwi 2s 6d ne 3s. Ihabile, engentle
kakulu, yafumana 21s ne 22s 6d ngekulu lezitungu. Owayitengayo watabata
izitungu ezi 400. Irasi eluhlaza ihambe kwi 3 1/2d ne 5d ngesitungu.—U
Mr. G. Brink uwe nekari epenulwe lilitye eliqengqelekele endleleni e
Malongwe.

UKULESELA ELUKUKWENI.—Sinosizi (itsho i Telegraph) ukuva ukuba ingozi
embi yehle ngenx’ enelisiko libi. Isicaka esibekeke kunene sase ndlwini
sika Mr. Chase jr. besinesiko lokulesa sesiselukukweni, side ngenxa yoko
safunelwa ilampu eyenziwe ngohlobo lokuba ingafumane itshise izinto,
amane kodwa u Mr. Chase eqondisisa ukuba icinyiwe siuina. Ngo 16 June
emva kokuba zicinyiwe izikanyiso, sibuye salumeka isibane. Kuviwe
ngesijwili, kanti incawa itabate umlilo ebusuku. Ufunyenwe etshe kakulu.

UPAU LWETEMBA EBA TENJINI.

NKOSI YAM kaundifakele kwelopepa lamanene, ndisazisa izizwe zako. wetu
ngengxoxo ezibe zixoxwa yintlanganiso yaba Tembu, kwintlanganiso ebise
Mount Arthur kuxoxwe kuqala: 1. Ngolaulo Iwe Transkei “okokuba ipatwe li
Kapa na nokuba ipatwe pesheya e England?” 2. Makutiwe nina ngonyulo
Iwabantu abantsundu?

3. Lifikilena ixesha lokuba abantu abantsundu bahlale e jurini?
Ezingxoxo ekubeni zixoxwe shushu kwintlanganiso ezimbini, eyokuqala: (1)
South African Native Association, eyesibini, (2) Tembu’s Association.
Kunyulwe i Committee zizo zombini ukuze zihlangane ngomhla wa 21 ku May,
1884, zihluze inteto zezontlanganiso. Eyokuqala inyule o Messrs. D.
Malasi, R. Kawa, Henry Xuku. Eyesibini inyule o Messrs. P. Mahonga, S.
Sigenu, T. Zwedala, J. Vanqa, F. Ntshanga, H. Vanqa. Ingxoxo yokuqala
emveni kokuveliswa kwezizatu ezinje ngokuhlutwa kwemipu, nokunyuswa
kwemali zerafu ezinike ukungazoli kweli lizwe. Kuvunyelwene okokuba
kufanelekile ukuba kutunyelwe isicelo singene ngo Mr. Scanlen kwangoku e
Palamenteni. Indawo yesibini ekubeni ixoxwe yarolelwa izizatu
zokufaneleka konyulo Iwabantsundu. Kuvunyelwene ukuba bonke abanokanyo
bacelwe ukuba bangenise ngako konke abanako amangama abantsundu ukuba
abalwe ezincwadini zokuvota oko kukuti babe zi registered voters. Kuteke
kwakunjuzwana okokuba lo ngunyaka

wokuwafaka amagama ezincwadini zo voto. Indawo yesitatu ekubeni ixoxiwe
kunene kurolwa izizatu zokubonakalisa imipefumlo etshone emangcwabeni
ingena mpindezelelo, kubaliswa nangobulungisa be Judges ngokwazo.
Kutatyatelwe kumatyala anje ngawo Tetani umfo wase Ngqushwa, no Nombewu
umfo wase Tarkastad, otyala latetwa kwa Komani, nabanye eziti i Jaji
ngokwazo zizamele ubulungisa ngapandle kokuketa ibala, ngokwazi ukuba
ziya kupendula ngomhla wokupela, ziti kuba ijuri zizezebala elimhlope
lodwa zingaboni tyala kumntu omhlope. Oh! Africa koda kube ninina
imipefumlo itshona emangcwabeni ingena mteteleli? Isigqibo saba sesokuba
lifikile ixe- sha lokuba abantsundu bahlale e jurini. Kudala amatyala
esoniwa zi juri zabamhlope. Kwavunyelwana ukuba kutunyelwe isicelo ku
Rulumeni sokubiza elilungelo. Pambili luhlanga luntsundu pambili. Ndim
isicaka senu,

H. VANQA.

Mount Arthur, 21 May, 1884.

AZI OKOKUBA INYANISO YIYIPINA?

KOBEKEKILEYO U Mhleli we-Sigidimi. Nceda ufake lemigcana imbalwa
njengoko injalo. Kwisiqanaqu sokuqala se “Mpawana” zelomhla we 16 ku May
kubalwe kakubi nge Concert yase Bayi. Ke ukuyikanyeza kwetu lenteto soti
igwevu ngokutumela okubalwe kwipepa lase Bayi—Telegraph.—Lenjenje Iona
ukuxela ngalomculo : “ NATIVE CON­CERT—The Native Concert alluded to in
our last weekly notes came off at the Wesleyan Native Chapel on Tuesday
evening. The admis­sion was only a shilling yet comparatively few
natives were present but several Europeans patronized the performers we
have already mentioned the names of the natives who took part in the
programme which was very creditably carried out. The best thing of the
evening was the pathetic plantation melody. Gwine to ride up in the
Chariot. It was a fair sample of the old slave refrains so forcibly
narratated in uncle Tom’s Cabin. At all events the concert was
creditable to all the performers. The natives should have encouraged
their efforts to a greater extent. We expected to have seen the building
crowded in place of comparatively a few, and we are sure that both the
young men and women took a great deal of trouble and underwent much
practice in the course of preparation.” Ke sishiya kuwe mbali wolupawana
ukuba usityilele eyona nyaniso, kuba upawana olu lumnyelisa kakubi u Mr.
J. B. Radas. Akuko ne sanarana elincinane lokulunga olukankanywayo. Ke
okokuba kuko inyaniso kulenteto ye Telegraph ndingati ukungxwelerwa
okunjengoku okwenziwe u Mr. Radas ngumbali wolupawana kubi, kungatandeki
nokutandeka, kanjako lento okokuba ayiyiyo njengokuba upawana olo
luyixela njalo Isigidimi asinako okokuba singayalatelwa lento. Ke
ukwenjenje ibangelo lam lelokuba kucace eyona nyaniso, ukuze bangati
abanye Isigidimi fumane sateta ngo Mr. Radas, ze abanye bamdele
bamcekece ngenxa yenteto ye Sigidimi. Kwa no Mr. J. B. Radas kulilungelo
kuye ukuyihlambulula inteto emtoba kangakanana, ukuba ayiyiyo nyaniso.
Mandipele apo.

UMBONISO KA MAGNA.

KUNI manene nani matshawe. Ndite ndafunda kwipepa le 2 June 1884
kwindawo eti “Um’boniso ka Magna.” Ungalindeli nto ndifuna ukutelela
kwinteto yale mambane. He yatata ixesha icinga eyona nto ibalulekileyo
emayenziwe luhlanga oluntsundu. Kuni madodana afundileyo ndifuna ukwazi
ukuba koda kutinina ukuze amadodana afundi- siweyo azibalule kwinkita
ete ayayikatalela lento iyimfundo, kuba uti umntu akuba nentwana
elingene ukucela isonka uve kusitiwa umfo ka nantsi ufundile.
Mandinqumle manene kuba kuteta iyilo into engazanga yakwazi nokutini.
Mfo ka Magna Thomas ngati kum ute ngo­kwazi amagama ala manene wabona
efanele ukwenza into, mna okukokwam mabini matatu angaba anengcinga
enjengaleyo yako yokwenzela into uhlanga, amanye la fumane uwavakalisa
nje. Mna okukokwam nditi ukuba uke uqondisise kakuhle wofumana izinto
ezinkulu zisenziwa ngamadoda angena zitsaba; oko kukuti musa ukubeka
abanye pambili, nawe ngokwako ungayenza into yokunyusa uhlanga, enditi
ke mna ngalo lonke ixesha asati umntu omnyama alindele into komnye
uhlanga alusoze lunyuke, ujonge kwabanye nabo bajonge kuwe, mayivele
imambane yenze into yemisebenzi eyakuxela, ukunyuka kohlanga asimlomo
wodwa zizenzo. Mandishiye manene akowetu node nidikwe. Nindixolele
andinyelisi magama alamanene alapa, nditeta into amanye afumane ab'alwa
kungeko siganeko ake asenza. Malunyuke uhlanga. Ndiyazimela apo, ndim

IMITETO.

Bonke abaya kuzisa impahla ehambayo nokuba kukudla kufuneka beko
eofisini ka Captain Blyth ngolwesitatu usuku pambi kwemini yomboniso ;
baze bazixele into abaya kuzizisa, banikwe itikiti.

Zonkeizinto ezizakuboniswamazize zibekokwi- ndawo yokubonisa kwapambi
kwexa lesitoba, lifike sezilungile.

Zonke izinto eziza kuboniswa mazize zibe zezo- mntu obonisayo, kungabiko
ozisa impahla yomnye.

Ilizwi labagwebi aliyi kupikiswa.

I-PARLAMENT YALE KOLONI.

I-Parlament yale Koloni ivulwe nge Thursday ngo 24 ka April yi Governor.
Ite yenza inteto ende, esesoti sinike izinto etete ngazo.

Itete ngalemfazwe ibiko pakati kuka Rili no Ngangelizwe; ngokuxoliswa
kwayo ngu Mr. Brown­lee ; nokuba noko kungati kuko uxolo pesheya kwe
Nciba akukabiko kuzola. Kanjalo ngekusiti noko, izinto zingenziwa
ngamandla, I-Government inikwe amandla angapezulu pezu kwabantsundu
abapakati kwale Koloni ne Natal ukuze kungabiko zingozi zihlela abantu
nemfuyo yabo.

Inani lamapolisa ngelisandiswa kongezelelwe amadoda abe 150 ; zize
indawo ngendawo zinikwe amandla okumisa amapolisa azo, okubamba amasela.

I-Koloni kubonakala ukuba iyenyuka. Imali ezuzwe yi Government ngalo
nyaka udluleyo ibe ngapezu kweponti eziliwaka.

Indlela ati Ukumkanikazi ungapatwa ngayo umhlaba wakwele Diamond.

Izikalazo ezivela E-Natal zokuba, mininzi ngo- kugqitisileyo imipu
etengiswa kwabantsundu.

Imfaneleko yokuba kukangelwe uhlobo lokufe- zwa kwamatyala, kweli cala
le Koloni lingase mpuma langa.

Ukuvuzwa kwabantu abapete imisebenzi yako- mkulu ; ukunyuswa kwe mali
yabo.

Ukutwalwa kwencwadi eziya pesheya ko lwandle E-England.

Indlela zenqwelo zomlilo—ziyahambiseka noko kukancikane—Ukusweleka
kwabacandi—Intsimbi zokwaka indlela zenqwelo zomlilo azikafiki ukuvela
E-England.

Itelegraf ziyahambiseka ngokutandekayo. Ku- zanyelwa ukwenziwa
eyakuhamba pantsi kwamaanzi iye E-England.

Ukukangelwa kwendawo apo umcancato wase Gqili ufanele ukuba kona.
Ngeusakiwa.

Indlela ekoloni ngezilungiswa.

Abantu bokusebenza indlela zenqwelo zomlilo bazakuvela E-St Helena.
Kufunwa ukulungiselelwa abantu abavela E-Germany.

Zindawo ezizintloko ke ezo etete ngazo I-Governor.

Emva koku kungenwe kwinto zalomnyaka ukoyo, ezinje ngezokuba “ ngubanina
oya kubatala indleko zale hambo ka Mr. Brownlee yokuya kwake kwa Rili ?
”

“Kuko zinto na zenziwayo zokupelisa ubunxila pakati kwabantsundu.”

IZINTO NGEZINTO.

UKUBULALA.—Kute ngale veki idluleyo Umxosa onxilileyo wabulala omnye
E-Carlisle Bridge.

U-SIK BENJAMINS PINE unyulwe ukuba abe yi Governor yase Natal. Wake
wakona ngapambili.

UBUSIKA banonyaka buqaleke kwangapambi kwexesha labo. Inqele ieelike
yalala kwintaba ze Tyume nase Aliwal North.

INGOZI EMBI.—Ite inqwelo ebinabantu abava emtshatweni xa iwela E-Tsomo,
yawa, kwatshona abantu abalishumi elinesitandatu.

OTSHONE EMTONJENI.—Kute E-Bayi umfo otile elinga ukusela kumtombo ongase
ndlwini ka Mr. Blain, weyela watshona kona wafa.

AMAMFENGU amele I-Dike arole iponti ezi £ 120 ngezinyanga zi dluleyo
zintatu, ezirolela ukucadwa komhlaba, nerafu. Nangoku asarola kakulu.

OBETE ABANYE.—E-Fort Beaufort kuko umxosa ote enxilile wabeta abafazi.
Unikwe inyanga ezintatu etolongweni esebenza nzima.

AMAPOLISA.—Amaqela atile amapolisa asand’ ukutunyelwa kwindawo apo kuko
kakulu ubusela, nokungazoli.

IMIKOMBE.—Kusand’ ukufika imikombe emibini le kutiwa ngo stemele, eza
kuhambahamba elu- nxwemeni Iwale koloni. Owesitatu usalindelwe.

INTO ENCOMEKAYO.—Abapatibelibandla kutiwa yi Dutch Reformed Church
E-Bloemfontein banike £100 ku Mawesile azama ukwaka kulomzi indlu
yetyalike yabantsundu.

UBUMENEMENE.—Kuko Amaxosa amatatu a- sand’ ukubanjwa kwa Qoboqobo,
ngokusuke acebe igusha zomnye umntu endle, ati akugqiba ukuzi- ceba
azinqumle imiqala.

UMCANCATO WASE QONCE.—Inkoliso yentsimbi zokwaka lomcancato upumelela
E-Qonce sezifikile kona. Kulindelwe ukuba woba selugqityiwc eku- peleni
kwenyanga ezine.

IPOSI EYA E-NATAL.—Kumiswe ukuba iveki zonke kubeko iposi esuka E-Qonce
iye E-Natal ihamba ngomhlaba, induluke nge Thursday. Incwadi eziya kona
zorolelwa ipeni ezintandatu kwanje ngezihamba ngolwandle.

INGOZI EMBI.—Sinosizi olukulu ukuva ngokwe- nzakala kwenkosikazi yakwa
Rev. Mr. Gordon wase All Saints, ngase Dordrecht. Ubekwele ehasheni waza
wawa wapuka ingalo entla kwe nqwiniba, waselegoduswa etwelwe ngabantu.

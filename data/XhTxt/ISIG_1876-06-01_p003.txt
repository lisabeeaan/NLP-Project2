IHAMBO KA LIEUTENANT CAMERON.

IGAMA lalo mfo ka Cameron sekukafuti simana ukulibeka pambi kwabaleseshi
be Sigidimi, kuba engomnye wabafo, abayenzele into enkulu i-Africa,
ngokusazisaindawo zayo ezibe zingazango zifikwe mntu umhlope ngapambili,
asizisele isimo salomazwe nentlalo yabantu, bakona. Koluhambo lwake
utabate iminyaka emibini enenyanga ezisibozo; ngeloxesha wahamba imayile
ezikumawaka amatatu, ecancla kumahlatikazi amakulu, epumela imigxobozo,
ehlwa- tiza emadotyeni, etwala ubushushu nezinye izinto zokumbandezela.
Izinto azipumeleyo nomsebenzi awufezileyo bezingeze zapunyelwa bani
ongenantliziyo ikalipileyo, nentloko eqabukileyo, namandla okufeza
umsebenzi.

Njengoko baziyo abaleseshi be Sigidimi, ukusuka knka Cameron ekaya
waeyakufuna u-Dr. Livingstone, pakati e-Africa. Ukunduluka kwake
e-Zanzibar amanye amadoda aye naye waengu Dr. Dillon no Mr. Murphey no
Mr. Moffat. Kwasekuqaleni kohambo lwabo bafumana ubunzima obukulu
ekufuneni abantu bokutwala impahla yabo. Noko ke yena no Dr. Dillon bade
banduluka, bake bamshiya ngase mva u-Murphey no Moffat. Endleleni
bafumana inkatazo yokucelwa amabaso amakulu zinkosi zabantsundu abacanda
emhlabeni wazo ; baza mhla baya kungena e-Nyanyembe, ngomhla 5 ku August
1874, bangenelwa ngamabada. U-Murphey wada wafika bekona, baza bonke
bakabalala kunene licesine. Kusenjalo kwafika umfo weqela lika Dr.
Livingstone, oza nezokuti lomntu baya kumfuna selebubile, bayeza abantu
abatwele isidumba sake. Sati sakufika wabuya naso u-Dr. Dillon no Mr.
Murphey basinga kwase Zanzibar, waza yena u- Cameron wagqiba kwelokuba
uya kusinga pambili aye kufeza umsebenzi afe ewenza u-Dr. Livingstone.
Wanduluka ke ngoko u-Cameron wasinga e-Ujiji kwati kodwa engekafiki kona
weva ukuba ububilo u- Dr. Dillon no Mr. Moffat. Ukusuka e-Ujiji waya
kuhlola icibi le Tanganyika waza wabona imilambo emikulu engena kulo
emashumi asitoba anesitandatu, wafumana nomnye optima kulo elocibi,
usingise ngase ntshonalanga. Wati xa asahamba ehlola elocibi kwabiwa
impahla yake, kwaza ngoko kwafuneka ukuba awancipise amadoda aza kuhamba
naye, abe mashumi asixenxe kube kupela.

apo kwilizwe elibantu bazidla kunene ngopoto lwenwele zabo, abangenazi-

kulo—kuba eyonandawo inkulu abeyinxamele ibikukuhlisa Iwamlambo apume

aneminqwazi nebulukwe. Kodwa ke wanqandeka engafikanga ngenxa yokusuka

eyona kutiwa inkulu kwezo zigama, u-Kasanga, yati kuba yayi kude

kuye. Hayi ke waquba apo avela kona nalapo aya kona nokulahleka kwake,
waziquba ezo ndawo elinye iliso lise zityeni. Kwavela ntokazi itile yati
enkwenkweni kaubonise lomntu indlela ayifunayo ubuye ke uze kudla;
yatsho yati kum hamba nalo mntana uza kukwalatisa. Ndapuma ke okunene
noko kwake kwati manditi, Umntana lo ebengasuke adle na ke? Yati
lentwana ukuba ndibe secaleni kwendlu yaxela ngomlomo into endiza
kuyenza, yaya endlwini iqinisile nam ndakohlwa kukuyisola. Ndite
ukupindela ukuya kona into yokuqala endaqubisana nayo yangamadodana
atile ase sikolweni, abafana bo Nantsi endafika “ besele kakulu obo
Mlungu ” sebepambene kwabodwa kukubi. Ndafika nam ndangena ndalamla
yayimigu kule ndawo, ndingasiva nesona sixabano saba bazalwana. Noko ke
kwabako impumelelo. Amagama abo wowafumana ukuba sibuye saqubisana nabo
bekweso simo, nalo ntokazi sokuxelela ukuba ibuye yasalatisa indlela
kungekadliwa.

Kekaloku noko kunjalo lomhlaba wase Hewu salinga ukuwuqonda kakuhle
isimo sawo. Okwetu ukubona sawufumana ungumhlaba obanzi kakulu, waza
wanemilambo eyahlukeneyo ; safumana ngoko kunqabile ukwenza amazwi aya
kulidibanisa kakuhle i Hewu elo. Nokoke asiyi kucazulula, hleze abe
maninzi amazwi etu. Ukuqala ke ngomhlaba. Sati tina ngumhlaba omhle
empahleni ngokukodwa ezigusha, sawubonela ukucuma nase kudleni, noko
malunga nolundi olu luwahlula kowangapantsi, amatye azixinile indawo
zamasimi. Sawufumana ke nawo unale nxwaleko iya iwukulela ngokuwukulela
umhlaba wonke, ukubalelelwa lilanga, wanga ngumhlaba ofanele ukulivela
kakulu. Sagqiba kwelokuba nonyaka nje kona abantu bakona baya kutenga.
Ngokufutshane i Hewu langa ngumhlaba omhle kakulu, ongati noko
uyatshitsha, abaya kuti abantu bawo ukuba abanamijelo namadam, bamelwe
kukucitakala ekuhambeni kweminyaka, ukuba izulu alibuyelanga esimeni
salo sangapambili.

Abantu bezo ndawo sacanda kuzo sabenza maqela mabini. Anga kuti amadoda
aqinileyo, lentanga yendisileyo, ino nyana, yanga kwelozwe yahlukile
konyana. Amadoda lawo sawafumana ingabafo abasebenzayo, ngokukodwa
malunga nezikolo ; safika ingabafo abalilima kanobom izimba le Nkosazana
inqolowa, ingamadoda anenqwelo arweba kunene “ ngemiti , ” sati
sakukangela ezintlantini sabona ukuba kunzinyana, ako wona esabona ukuba
“ afumile ” kanye. Kwabako namanye anemihlaba yawo ayitengayo,
nasatengayo nanamhla ; kwada kwako mfo utile sati sakubona izindlu zake
nentango zamatye ezibiyele amasimi, nemijelo yokuncenceshela sagqiba
kwelokuba ngumsebenzi wo Mlungu lo, kanti hayi ngowomntu ontsu- ndu.
Sati ke ngoko odela i Hewu makake akangelise, angabi mhlaumbi uyana
nomlambo omnye. Indawo esayidelayo tina yaba malunga nododana.
Asiyibonanga eyona nto kanye Iwenza yona ngapandle kokupalisa amahashe
oyise, nokutwala amabekebeke eminqwazi esingaziyo ukuba bazitengele
batengelwe ngonina sinina! Imisebenzi ngoko ekubonakala ukuba babeyiqale
kakuhle oyise, abebefanele ukuyihambisela pambili onyana ibuye ngomva.
Ukulinganisela ngalo mzi safika sawukuza saba ngowo Mlungu. Sati
sakukangela sabona ukuba imijelo leya yamanzi idibekile ayisasebenzi;
ukuba intango zamasimi ziyadilika, azivuswa, ukuba izindlu ziquqekile
azityatyekwa, sabuzisa ukuba kuteta ntonina oku, bati bonke kuteta ukuba
umnini mhlaba wafuduka, umzi wawushiya nonyana, wasuka unya na
akawunyamekela kuwulungisa njengoyise. Site ke sakukangela kweminye
imizi noko ingenazinto zingako zokubonwa, sagqiba kwelokuba, lomfana
weyiswa kukuvusa imijelo eyayise imbiwe akayedwa kwelozwe. Kunge ngati
abafana banxiliswe bubudoda abangabunikwanga ngoyise njengohlobo
lokunikwa kobudoda.

Indawo enye endaka ndayipulapulisa kwelozwe yaba yeyokucandwa komhlaba.
Nokoke sesidlulisile ukuteta namhla sobuya siyingene into esiyipaulayo
malunga nocando.

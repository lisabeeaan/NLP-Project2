IBAKALA ELINCOMEKAYO.

Ibakala elihle elincomekayo, nesinga lingalandelwa zezinye indawo,
litatyatwe e Macfarlane. Intloko apa ka Rev. E. Makiwane, edume
ngokucinga okwendeleyo ifumene iqinga elingati liya kupumelela
lokukutaza intsapo ukuba ifunde, nabazali ukuba bafundise. Ngo 26,
kulenyanga, kwabako uviwo Iwentsapo yezikolo zozitatu—Macfarlan,
Kwezana, Evergreen—azongameleyo. Ukubeva abantwana abo kwamenywa u Rev.
P. J. Mzimba, wase Lovedale, u Rev. S. Ntsiko, wakwa Matole, no Mr. J.
T. Jabavu, wase Lovedale. Uviwo luqale kusasa, Iwafezwa ngentsimbi
ezintatu. Ngesine ibuye yangena endlwini yetyalike intlanganiso ukuva
ingxelo yabazamanishi, nokubonisa ukukutazwa kwabo bakupa abanye.

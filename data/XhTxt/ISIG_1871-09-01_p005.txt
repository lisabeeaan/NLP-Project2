Isigidimi Sama-Xosa

LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1871.

No. 12.

Isigidimi Samaxosa

REV. TIYO SOGA.

U-TIYO ka Soga—umfundisi—ububile. Ingaba ngubanina owakwa Ngqika numhla
ongaliliyo? Ingaba ngubanina owakwa Gcaleka numbla ongaliliyo? Ingaba
ngu- banina owakwa Mlungu olikolwa numhla ongaliliyo? Ingaba ngubanina
owase Mbo numbla ongaliliyo? Taruni ke nonke mabandla akowetu—kanti
kutshiwo ngo- mehlo azele zinyembezi, ngosandla sinca- ncazelayo,
ngontliziyo yopayo.

Esosicaka Senkosi sibube E-Tutura, ngomgqibelo, umhla weshumi linye
lina- mbini ka August, ukumka kwelanga.

U-Mr. Soga wazalwa E-Gwali, nyakana wagxotwa U-Maqoma E-Shokoshele,
uyise ngumfo ka Jotello, ipakati elikulu lika Ngqika—unina ngum
Ntindekazi—inkazana ka Ngayi. Umfundisi wake wokuqala yayingu Mr.
Chalmers owamqubayo emfundweni wada waya wamngenisa esikuleni sase
Dikeni—wakona ke, efundiswa ngu Mr. Govan —Ute yakuhla imfazwe yezembe
wabekela E-Tyeni, apo abafundisi bakowabo baye besabele kona. Kute
imfazwe ingekapeli wakumbula pesheya U-Mr. Govan E-Scotland, wahamba
nonyana baka Mr. Boss ababini, noka Mr. Thomson, yangu Tiyo owesine.
Efikile U-Tiyo pesheya ute wondliwa yindoda elungileyo esityebi U-John
Henderson eyati yamsa eskuleni ekutiwa ye Normal Seminary E-Glasgow.
Ityalike abemana engena kuyo yayiyeka Dr. Anderson, umfundisi odumileyo
kowabo, ngobuciko nangobulumko—wangeniswa pakati kwebandla lika Tixo
yilondoda, esengumfana omncinane.

Ugqibe iminyaka emibini E-Scotland wabuya weza neno. Ebuyile bate
abafundisi makaye kufundisa usapo E-Gxulu.

Wamana ehambisa lomsebenzi kona kwada kwehla imfazwe ka Mlanjeni, awati
wapa- ngwayimpi yakulo Gwelane, wasinda ngo- kungummangaliso. Uhambe
ngobusuku waya wagaleleka E-Gwali. Akuba ekona lemihlawahlalaekatazwa
zinkosi ukuba azi- fundele incwadi ezisukuba zipangwe kube Lungu, kuloko
akavumanga, wati ngenxa yesitukutezi salento wakumbula Eganqeni ku
bafundisi. Ingekapeli lomfazwe ubuye wawela waya E-Scotland no Mr.
Niven, engxamele numhla ukufunda ubufundisi —uhleli konake iminyaka
esixenxe efu- nda, wati akugqiba wamiswa libuto elikulu labafundisi
ukuba abe ngumshumayeli wendaba ezilungileyo zoxolo.

Ubuye kunye nokucitakala kwamawabo ngo Nongqause ufike wagxumeka umquba
Emgwali, wasebenza kona iminyaka eli- shumi, wayakela Inkosi indlu,
wawubi- nqela umsebenzi abewunikelwe ukuba awenze—lavakala ilizwi lake
nasezinkundleni, napi.

Onwabile ke, ehleli Emgwali apo, ite Inkosi yayivula indlela ukuba
kuyokuhanjiswa ilizwi lika Tixo kulo Gojela. Bate ke abafundisi be Free
Church ne United Presbyterian Church behlanganisene, bacela yena ukuba
aye kuba ngumfundisi E-Tutura. Akenzanga mazwi maninzi, usuke wavuma,
waxoba, nanko esinga kwa Gcaleka.

Ugqibe iminyaka emitatu kona, wawaka lomzi wase Tutura. Kute ngenyanga
ka June waya kumisa umshumayeli ngaku Mapassa—wafika inkosi ingeko,
alahleka amahashe kwangolosuku wagaleleka ngalo komkulu apo, wapelelwa
kukudla, wavingcelwa yimvula endlwini engekagqitywa; wangenwa yingqele
emzimbeni. Efunyenwe amahashe, egoduka ngomgqibelo; eputuma icawa
wafunyanwa kwa yimvula, wahamba ngayo imini ipela, waneta waludaka—ufike
ekaya usapo lungeko, efile yingqele, kanti kungenelwa ngumkuhlane

oko, ebengumlwelwe kade—ugqibe icawa ezitile epantsi—kwada kwaya kubizwa
igqi’ra lase Mpongo, elati lakumseza amayeza lavuka itemba, wabonakala
ngati uyacaca. Ngemihla yesitoba neyeshumi ka August ebehamba pandle
eqapula abantu yimihla ke leyo ekwakubanda kunene, lisiwa iliqwa. Wavuka
umkuhlane ukuza kokusa ngolwe sihlanu lweveki 11th August. Ziyandululwa
izigitshimi ukuyakubika, itemba selipelile. Ugaleleke umfundisi wase
Gcuwa U- Mr Longden ngomgqibelo kusasa wafika umzalwana wetu selekohliwe
kuteta. Bayafika O-Mr. Ross emini enkulu sebengabaze kubonela ukupela
kwesicaka Senkosi abo.

Kunjalo ke ukububa kwalomfo wakowe- tu—ndikubika ngokufutshane, pofu
ndibe ndingawagqibayo onke amapepa E-Sigidimi ngokunicokisela. Lento
yenziweyo Yinkosi siyalo, sendiya kutike ndiwamise ngamanye amazwi
okubonisa isiyalo apo sikona.

1.   Kuqala nditi sibujelwe ngumfundisi wokuqala wohlanga lakwa
    Xosa—olukozo belulunye, sihlutwe lonake numhla ; ndiyabuza ke nditi:
    Isitombo esiveliswa kukufa kolukozo siyakude sivele pina sivele
    ninina ? Pendulani—pendulani bazali—pendulani bafundi bafana ndini
    bakowetu. Pendulani lusapo lwakwa. Xosa.

2.   Okunye nditi sibujelwe ngumfo obe- kwazi ukuteta, engazazi pofu
    ukuba uteta kangakanana—umfo obekwazi ukuzicaza izibalo, obekwazi
    ukututuzela—umfo obe- vusa ufefe entliziyweni xa asibaliselayo
    ngotando nenceba lenkosi yake U-Kristu. Niyakuba zitungwa nonke na
    namhla? Sesiyakumana sibaliselwa indaba zovuyo numhla ngamadoda
    olunye uhlanga na, eselesibonisile nje kade umfo ka Soga ukuba nati
    midaka sinokuba ngabavakalisi bendaba zovuyo? Pendulani.

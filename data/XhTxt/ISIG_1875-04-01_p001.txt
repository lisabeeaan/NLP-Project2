Vol. V. LOVEDALE, SOUTH AFRICA, APRIL 1, 1875. No. 55

Isigidimi Samaxosa

ISIKUMBUZO SEGAMA LIKA
DR LIVINGSTONE.

KWINTANGA ezingapambili, sake sakuka- nkanya ukuba kute pakati
kwezikumbuzo ezingagcina igama lika Dr Livingstone, kwaveliswa
nesokumiswa kwesikolo apa E-Africa esiyakubizwa ngokuba yi LIVINGSTONIA.
Eloqinga laqala laveliswa ngu Dr Stewart wase Lovedale. Waliteta
kwintlanganiso enkulu yomnyaka yabafundisi be Free Church, eyayihlangene
ngo May walomnyaka udluleyo. Wati yena kuye akubonakali nto ingade
ikufanele ukugcina igama lalondoda ngapezu kwesikolo esiya kufundisa
abantsundu U-Yesu Kristu, nezinto ezinyusa umntu zokukanya. Isikolo
esingafanela oko sesiya kuti sifundise i-Gospel, nemfundo yencwadi
neyemisebenzi, esiti ke zingati ezonto zifundisiwe, kufe kutshabalale
ukwenzana amakoboka, into eyona wada wafa U-Dr Livingstone enga
angayipelisa.

Enye indawo wati kufanele ukuze isikolo esinjalo singalungi nahlelo
litile kube kupela ; sibe sifanele ukuba sixaswe luhlanga lonke Iwama
Ngesi; kuba lomfo ugama liza kulondolozwa ebe ngumfo wohlanga. Ngendawo
yokumiswa kwaso, wati ubona ukuba eyona ifanele lomsebenzi kukwi
cibilase Nyasa. Eli cibi, njengoko bazi ngako abanye, lingase
Ntshonalanga kwase Mozambique. Kufunwa ukuze umzi wesikolo ube kunxweme
olungase zantsi Iwalo; kuze xa kuyiwa kona kunyukwe nge Zambesi nge
mikonjana umgama ozimaile ezi 350 uti ke ozi maile ezimashumi asixenxe
useluhanjwa ngezinye indlela, impahla zitwalwe ngabantu. Enye indlela
yokuya kona isuka E-Zanzibar ; kodwa ke inde, kanjalo kungafuneka ukuba
impahla itwalwe indlela yonke.

Izizatu ezibangela ukuba isikolo simiswe kulondawo zezi—Ukuqala, kulapo
ake wasebenza kunene U-Dr Livingstone, ekulungileyo ke ngoko ukuba
ligcinwe kona igama lake. Kwindawo yesibini kusesinqeni kanye somsebenzi
wokutenga amakoboka, ekungati unqanyulwe kulondawo ube ugqityiwe amandla
lomsebenzi; kanjalo abantu kona baninzi baxinene, umhlaba mhle, uya
cuma. Kunjalo njeke asi ndawo inasifo kakulu kwabamhlope njengoko
zinjalo indawo ezininzi zapakati E-Africa. Sesitshilo ke ukuti
inokufikelelwa lula ngemikonjana londawo. Noko uti ube nzima umsinga xa
kunyukwayo, awufani nokuhamba ngo- mhlaba ; ube uyakuti xa kuziwa
elunxwe- meni kuhliswa umlambo ube luncedo olu- kulu.

Lati ke lakuba eliqinga livelisiwe ngu Dr. Stewart ngomhla 26 ka May
walomnyaka udluleyo, kwabonakala ukuba okwenene lifanele ukutatyatwa.
Beyivumile ke abafundisi londawo, kufuneke ukuba lilungiselelwe. Kute
kuba liveliswe nguye kwafuneka zibonelwe nguye indawo ezininzi zaso,
ezinje nangemali engafunekayo, nomntu wokusipata.

Kubeko ukubambezeleka okutile ngo- kufunyanwa komntu oza kuya kusipata ;
kuba U-Dr S. engenako ukuya ngokwake ngokunje, ngenxa yokuxakwa yi
Love­dale nesapesheya kwe Nciba isikolo. Ude ke wayifumana indoda abati
ngu Mr E. D. Young. Lomfo bebesazana kwangapambili no Dr. S. Wake naye
waya pakati E-Africa. Ubemazi kanjalo no Dr Livingstone. Nguye lo
wayetunyelwe ngo June 1867 ukuya kukangela ubunyaniso bokuba ufilena
okwenene U-Dr. L. oko izicaka zazibaleke zamshiya etafeni, zafika zati
ubulewe. Ngumfo ke ngoko obuqelileyo ubunzima base Africa; umfo oyi
bekele intliziyo yake ekuluncedeni nase kulunyuseni uhlanga oluntsundu.
Umsebenzi wokuya kusimisa eso sikolo nokusipata iminyaka emibini
nemitatu ushiyelwe ezandleni zalomfo ke.

Ngokwemali engafunekayo ukuya kusi- qala, kutiwa fanukuba anganela
amawaka alishumi, noko abanye besiti ngekucelwa amawaka amashumi matatu
nokuba mane. Ngomhla 18 ka January bekuko E-Glasgow intlanganiso
yokuxoxa ngaso eso sikolo. Kulontlanganiso kubikwe indawo yokuba
amahlelo amabini, i-Free Church, ne Reformed Presbyterian Church,
avumelene ukuba ahlangane kolo msebenzi, kwatiwa ke kodwa kukululekile
nangoku, kohlala kukululekile nokuba kuninina, ukuba bangene abatandayo
nokuba ngabalipina ihlelo. Kukankanywe kanjalo nezinto ekufuneka ukuba
zenziwe ukulungiselela, ezinje ngentlobo zeboti eziya kufuneka, namadoda
aza kuya kona, abafundisi belizwi nabemise- benzi. Imali ekuvakele ukuba
sekuqinisekile ukufumaneka kwayo kutiwa inje ukuma kwayo. U-Mr James
Young, wase Kelly uti urola £1,000, Mr James Stevenson wase Glasgow
£1,000; Mr W. Mackinnon wase Balmakill £500; Mr P. Mackinnon £500; Mr G.
Martin wase Auchendenran £500 ; Mr James White wase Overtoun £500 ; Dr
Joshua Pater­son £100 ; Dr Hugh Miller £100.

Kwavunyelwana kanjalo kulontlanganiso ukuba kumiswe lamadoda siza ku-
wakankanya abe yikomiti yokubuta imali.—Messrs James Young wase Kelly,
James White, James Stevenson, W. Mackinnon, P. Mackinnon, Geo Martin, P.
Playfair, Dr Joshua Paterson, Dr Hugh Paterson, Rev. Dr Buchanan, Rev.
Wells, Rev. R. Howie, nabanye abanga- buye babonwe.

Esinye isixakaniso endleleni ekufuneke ukuba sishenxiswe sesokuba
I-Mozambique ingeko pantsi kwa Mangesi, ipantsi kwe Portugal. Kufuneke
ukuba kuke kubalelwe izipata mandla zakona ukucela amalungelo atile,
nokuba zingancedisana zona ukuqubela pambili lomsebenzi. Asikayiva
impendulo yazo ; kodwa kuko amatemba amakulu okuba iyakufezwa kalula
londawo.

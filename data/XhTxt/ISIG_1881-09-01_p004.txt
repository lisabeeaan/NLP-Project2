ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, SEPTEMBER 1, 1881.

AMAPAKATI abe nyulelwe uku picota ingxowa ye Trans­vaal alike kwisigqibo
sokuba ibuyiselwe kuma Bulu. Siteta nje namhla awaseko pantsi ko Mntan’
Omhle lomadoda. Oyakuba ngu mongameli we Transvaal asikamazi, kodwa ngu
Heer Paul Kruger osa bambe londawo; i Jaji enkulu ngu Chief Justice
Kotze; umbali micimbi ngu Mr. W. Eduard Bok; Umteteleli omkulu wase
matyaleni ngu Dr. Jorrisen; Umpatiswa Micimbi yaba Ntsundu ngu Lieut
Colonel Fereira. Ama Bulu enze amadinga amahle okuba akasaku vusa
butshaba naba mnyama. Siyakolwa ukuba anyanisile kuba yayiyimpato yawo
yaba Ntsundu eya banga ukuba ahlutwe i Transvaal ngu Lord Carnavon.

YINKOHLA silahlekwe yincwadi eyayivela ku Rev. Samuel Ntsiko wase
Uitenhage, abenga singambuzela ku mfana ka Mdliva, u Robert Rate, ukuba
akazanga wama lama na umninawe womfundisi lowo. Igama lake sililibele
okwangoku woti kodwa ukuba umfana lowo akanguye u Henry Ntsiko ibe ngu
Charlie Ntsiko. Wopendula ke Mr. R. Mdliva. Umfana lowo ute shwaka ngexa
lalama benubentsu emfazwe. Impendulo yake ungayitumela kwi Editor ye
Sigidimi. Singati kodwa uku kumbuza okwa ngomso u Rev. Mr. Ntsiko
nabanye abanqwenela ukufaka izaziso, nemibiko, izinto ezinjalo azifakwa
voor niet emapepeni, ngapandle koku batalelwa; njengoko satshoyo kwesika
August ukuti amapepa axaswa zizaziso ezibatalelwayo. Kanifunde isaziso
Somshicileli esi bekwe kwindawo yokuqala, ngasentla kwe nteto enkulu ye
Editor.

XA izinto sezisonakala ze kungabiko uyakulila nge Sigidimi ukuba asixeli
zinto zingekehli kodwa sixela esezihlile. Kwinteto yetu yama “Tuba
empumelelo” eyeyesibini kwe sanamhla, sibekise kwindawo yokuba izipata
mandla zibunga elokuweza indwayi namahlwempu apesheya ziwangenise kule
Koloni. Kuko amadoda amabini u Mr. J. B. Hellier no Mr. Walker ekutiwa
yi Rulumente mawa hamba-hambe eli lasema Xoseni, ahambe eliketa indawo
ezingamanqata, ze kuti kuzo kungeniswe ezindwayi. Ahambele emantla e
Tyume aza kuli gquta-gquta lonke ze axele ku Rulumente ezona ndawo
ayakubona zifanelekile. Kuko intsebelele zokuba ukuba abantsundu
abawusebenzisi umhlaba wonikelwa kwezo ndwayi. Njengoko sesitshilo
kwinteto esesibekiselele kuyo sisapinda nalapa, onenkomo makake azincame
afune umhlaba. Sibona abantsundu bengazikuba nandawo kude kufupi. Ludaba
ke olo.

SIZA kubakataza aba lesi be Sigidimi ngento enye, nalonto kunge gama lo
mcokeli wendaba zeli pepa. Igama elidumileyo leli liti Mhleli. Singani
nqamli, masiti apa, kwa Xosa apa nase Mbo amapepa endaba asinto yaziwayo
kuba ibingeko, kungeko nowaka wayibona nase matongweni. Ukukanya
kufikile ke namhla, kwafika nezinto ezintsha, pakati kwezi zinto kufike
ne Sigidimi, (singasidela ngako konke esinako kodwa kupela kwepepa
lenteto yako wetu). Lifikile elipepa, umntu obelibala ngenteto yako walo
bekusakutiwa, nanamhla kusatiwa yi Editor, oko kukuti umcokeli
Kuyabonakala ke apa ukuba uku hlela into noku cokela akuntonye. Kokwetu
ukucinga eligama u Mhleli, ngokusetyenziswa kwalo ekubekiseleleni
ekubalweni kwe ndaba zeli pepa, liposisekile, lilahlekisa kanjako;
makutiwe ngu Mcokeli, mhlayimbi yi Editor— kusetyenziswe indlela
yakowalo—aze ati ukuba umntu akawafuni lama gama mabini azinqumle umlomo
njenge nkuku. Ukuba kutiwe i Editor ngu Mhleli yimposiso engenziwayi
nkwenkwana yesikolo.

INYANISILE i Cape Times (yase Kapa) ukuti isenzo samanye amacebo akona
sesi ngxwelera intliziyo. Lilo eli ibali lesinye sezenzo zama
hlazo:—Ibandla elipete imisebenzi ye Bitsi (Harbour Board) e Cape Town,
lifumene umhlaba otile kufupi nolwandle wabeke lomhaba beku ngcwatywa
kuwo abantu abamnyama. Lamacebo atande andise umsebenzi wawo,
kwabonakala ukuba mawaxble nakulendawo ingama ngcwaba. Basinikile
isaziso sokuba abo banezihlobo eza ngcwatywa kulo ndawo mabenze msinya
bazikupe ukuba baya tanda. Pakati kwezinye izibonakalo, ezibi sesom
Mozambik obezamana ne ngcwaba lo nina. Ityeya ibisel’ i bolile, nomzimba
sewaba lutuli, amatambo ehamba odwa. Eso si alam siwabutile amatambo,
sawa bopele esizityeni seseyile, sahamba ke ukuya kufuna awodade waso
esasi ngxamele ukuya kuwa ngcwaba ndaweni nye mganyana nalendawo.
Ukunuka bayakuncoma ababekufupi. Sinosizi izenzo zobugqwira ezinje ngezi
zisenziwa kubantu abamnyama ngabantu aba ngabafundisi bezinto zoku
kanya.

ABO, kuba lesi betu, batanda ukuva ngeza pesheya singati zizo ezi
intloko zezo ndaba.—U Mr. Grant-Duff obesisandla sokohlo so Mntan’ Omhle
ekulauleni i Koloni (Under Secretary for the Colonies) wenziwe i
Ruluneli yase Madras e India, yanikwa u Mr. Leonard H. Courtney indawo
yake. Eli nene siyavuya lifakwe kulendawo nje. U Mr. Grant-Duff
sikangela ezintetweni zake ube ngakatali kakulu zintsizi zetu, ibe
ikulelixa u Mr. Courtney abezama ngako konke anako ukuba imidaka ifumane
impato elungileyo pakati kweminyaka embalwa edlulileyo. Kwi ofisi
encinane yemicimbi yase England ne Scotland abekuyo u Mr. L. H. Courtney
kungene inene apa abati ngu Lord Rosebery.—Ugxotiwe kanjako kwi
Parlamente yapesheya u Mr. Bradlaugh omele idolopu abati yi Northampton.
Imbangi yeyokuba ekanyela ubuko buka Tixo, awafuni ke ngoko amanene e
House of Commons ukuba ahlale nendoda enenkolo enjalo.—Ukububa kuka Dean
Stanley umfundisi odume kunene ngobubele bake namandla okushumayela,
yenye yezinto ezinkulu zelozwe.

UNGADINWA Enoch, mfo ka Booy! Ubuza ukuba Ute­nina u Enoch Booy? He,
kaupose amehlo ako kwisituba sababalelani, bati aba Mbo “Indaba yetyela
ayi koli,” zikangelele ke. U Mr. Booy ufuna kucitwe ucumse. Uti ke
makurolwe imali ngabo bonke abatanda benyukile abamnyama ze iye kuqasha
u Capt. Blyth ukuba akatse abantu bakowabo abase sebunyameni! ude akupe
i £1 6s. yokuya kufeza lomsebenzi! Ngenteto emfupi singati, lenteto ka
Mr. Booy ayinakufezeka, ingenakufezeka nje, iyi mposiso ngazo zonke
indlela. U Capt. Blyth sisicaka sika Rulumente akanakwenza nto ngapandle
kwe gunya lake, nokuba seletengwe ngayipi imali. Asiyitandi kanjako tina
into yokuba ati umntu akoyiswa ngama qaba aselewangenela ewatyakatya nge
zabokwe. Singati ku “Enokwe,” imali leyo yinike omnye wabafundisi base
Qonce, uti yeyokuquba ivangeli kumaqaba, ze kupele ucumse. Upikele
ukuyibiza nje inja yakomkulu u Rulumente yosabela yona, ze ike ikufumane
nawe wena uti unxibe ingubo ikupate njenge sela, bangasindi nabafundisi
bakowenu etolongweni. Wamfoka Booy! Hayi, au! yeka u Rulumente alale.

U REV. PETROS MASIZA, “uyibambe inkunzi ngempondo,” njengoko atshoyo ama
Ngesi. Le nkunzi yimi tshato yamagqoboka, nandawo imandla kuyo luduli. U
Mr. Masiza somyeka azitetele ngokwake kwisituba esisinikele ababalelani.
Indawo yoduli sesaka sayicukumisa tina kwipepa elidluleyo mhla sateta
ngoluka Tulu. U Rev. Mr. Masiza selengati wayeko e Healdtown mhla
inqwelo yazaliswa zintombi, nama nkazana, nododana—kanti iya pina?
Umlesi akasokukolwa xa siti kwase Healdtown; imizi—wakulo soka nowa kulo
ntombi—ayikude nange mayile enye! Into enje yenziwa kwi sikolo esidala
nesi dumileyo, awaka wafundisa kuso u Mr. Masiza. Eli siko kubonakala
ukuba lendele kuma kristu antsundu. Izinto ezimbi azibalayo umfundisi lo
ezihlayo eludulini, asikolwa ukuba uzigqitisile koko zinjalo. Singavuya
ite lenteto ka Mr. Masiza ayabuyela kuye, kodwa yawufeze umcimbi anga
angawufeza, nabanga ungafezeka abaninzi, pakati kwabo omtina. Malucitwe
uduli emitshatweni. Kufuneka imitshato yabamnyama yenziwe lula njenge
yama Ngesi, nalento yokauleziswa ngabafundisi ngokwenza lento yenziwe
ngumzalwana wabo u Rev. Petros Masiza.

SIBULELA u Mr. K. Kayo, wase Gqili ngoludaba, olungenisa ukukanya
okuncinane pezu kwe hambiso ka Mr. Sauer, Umpatiswa Micimbi yaba
Ntsundu, e Lusutu Akukabiko uke waba nokukanyisela abantu ngokwenziwa
ngu Mr. Sauer. Abanye bayoyika ngati kusakubuya kuliwe, asikolwa tina
noko kunye nabanye kwa kwaba mhlope, nakuba u Masupha ebungwanyalala.
Kungoluhlobo asixelelayo umbaleli wetu ngenye yezinto ezihle e Lusutu:
“Ute u Lerothodi ngokuhamba eqinisa uxolo wahlangana no Capt. Hook. Ite
lonkosi ikwele pezu kwelitye aba Tembu nabe Sutu benzo isangqa:
pulapulani ndizise inteto ka Letsea no Xoli Lizwe (Mr. Sauer). Ndinovuyo
ukunibuna nonke. Ungabi nantloni Capt. Hook, wulandele umkondo ude uye
pakati kwe sizwe sika Mshweshwe. Nanku ke u Smiti ka Bushula, no
Mosonyane, no Mahlubi, no Kolenye, abazi zandla zam ; mabakuncede
kufezwe ihambo ka Xoli-Lizwe. Ite io nkosi yakupeza, kwesuka u Capt.
Hook, wati, bendisiva kusitiwa uyindoda eliveliti enga pendulwa Mlungu,
kanti hayi, yingqondo into ekuwe. Ukuba uya kufeza lamazwi uwatetileyo
abe Sutu bayakuhlala besi sizwe ngemihla yako.”

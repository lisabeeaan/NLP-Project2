ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, MARCH 1, 1887.

19

UKUCOLACOLA.

KUBABALELI.—Incwadi ezingumbeko,—“ Impindezelo,” “ Inguquko, Umtshato,
no Pehlelelo,”—“ Umfana ovela e Bayi.” Bosixolela abaninizo sipelelwe
situba namhlanje.

ITYALA LOKUBETA.—Iti incwadi evela e .Taggersfontein kudliwe ama Bulu
amabini i £20 lilinyo ngokusuke abete umntu ontsundu, ati ukuba akanayo
lomali ahlale inyanga ezintandatu etolongweni. Ambopelele lomfo
entsimbini ekutiwa yi Vice ambeta apela ngelawo akasayi kuze abuye abe
nampilo ilungileyo.

ISIFO SE GUSHA E SOUTH AMERICA.—Nyakenye si tshabalalise igusha ezininzi
e Australia kwi ncwadi evela e America kubonakala ukuba esisifo sibulele
izigidi zegusha. Kungena impetu emipungeni ziyitshabalalise ziko
nasematunjini. Esisifo sineminyaka etile siko, sibe nzima kanye kubo
ngenxa yokubulala, nokusweleka kwe nca. Ezi impetu zisemipungeni
zibonakala zingafani nezisematunjini. Balinge ukuziqumisela nge Sulphur
akwabiko luncedo lukulu.

U MQIKELA ubale igama lake kwipepa le mvumelwano ababeyenze ngo December
lo udluleyo sakutshwa isaziso sake. Anikwa ama Mpondo i £1,600,
yanduluka ke Imantyi enkulu ngomhla olandelayo ukuya Ecwebeni lo
Mzimvubu no Mqikela nezinye inkosi kuyiwa kwalatwa apo iyakucanda kona
indlela, yagqitywa kakuhle lonto.

IKO kakulu ingqakaqa kwela Mampondo. Kusekufe amakulu, esafa nangoku.
Kubonakala ukuba ayikatalelwe ngama Mpondo.

IMISEBENZI YOTYWALA.—Ipepa lase Kapa eliyi Argus lixela imbali embi
yokubulawa komfazi otile yindoda yake (kuba babenxila bobabini). Kute ke
kuba bebe naba ntwana, bamane behamba bebanqushela emizini ukuze badle,
nakuba bebelala bengadlanga ngezinye imini. Kute ke ngomnye umhla,
kwayawa xabana indoda nomfazi beyawa nxilile, suke namhla indoda ye tasi
isiqosho sokuni, yamnkala ngaso, wade wafa, akwanceda nto ukukala
nokuntantazela kwabantwana.

UMTETO OMHLE.—Liti ipepa lase Natal eliyi Natalian, kuko umteto oqutywa
e Copenhagen, ibotwe lase Denmark, wokuba kuti xa kubonwe umntu epuma
enkantini enxilile aposwe enqwelweni, asiwe ehotele, ize imali yokusiwa
kwake kona irolwe ngumnini nkantini lowo, kuba enxiliswe nguye. [Azi
betu awukuzekelwana lomteto ngulo Rulumente wakowetu? Ngati unganonce-
do olutile kweli lizwe.—ED. Sigidimi.]

UKUBANJWA KOMSHOLOGU.— Liti ipepa eliyi Natalian kwakona, kuke kwako
intlanganiso yabakolwa yimishologu e San Erancisco, U. 8. A., Mission
Street, kwaye kuko namadoda amabini ango Ntamnani (Detectives) angenisi-
weyo ukuze abe nelungelo lokuyiqonda kakuhle eyona nto iyiyo lento
kwenziwa yona. Kute zakutotwa izibane kwati gqi umshologu ngapaya kwe
kuselo (curtain*) wasel’ ubuza ngezwi eloyikekayo usiti—“ Ngobanina
abaze kufun’ ukuqonda lento ? ”

ISIBONAKALO SALOMSHOLOGU.—Sasisoyikeka kanye ngo- kungatetekiyo, wati
kobo bunjalo, wasondela komnye wo Ntamnani. Wasel’ ewuwola ngazo
zozibini izandla, kwavakala isikalo esibukali sisibi, sisoyikeka. Kute
kulo madididi okubambana kwalo ndoda nomshologu, kwe gqi ndoda
kwangapaya kwe kuselo elo, iza kunceda umshologu lo. Yaba yimbuqu
namadididi endlwini apa, ekute kwelo xa yalumeka isibane sayo i Reporter
ebikona, kwa- bonakala ukuba kanti lomshologu yinkazana engum Mexican,
enxibe ngoko Nomanyama.

UKUZITETELELA KWENTLANGANISO YEMISHOLOGU.— Bate akuko nkohliso ibeko
kulento. Into ebekoyo kukuba kute kwakubambana umshologu nale mambane
ingu Ntamnani watsho wajuba umoya womshologu kwase kusala upahla
olungumzimba wobuntu.

UKUMANGALELWANA.—Kuyiwe ematyaleni kumangaleIwe u Ntamnani lowo
ngokutiwa, ubete landoda ikwa ngumshologu, ibiza kunceda Iowa
uyinkazana..

IMIBUZO EXAYWE YITENTO.—Kanene abamoya banakona ukuziguqula bazenze
izimbuku? Ukuba kungaba kunjalo umntu akangembizena umhlobo wake
ongasekoyo, nokuba lutshaba, aze ati akuba ko axeliswe lomshologu
ubanjwe ngu Ntamnani, kupoqe umshologu, kusale isiqu somntu, sihlale
kwanaso kanjalo kwase mhlabeni apa?

UMBUZO KWAKONA.—Gxebe kungade kwenzekena ukuti umoya ube nokuziguqula
uzenze isiqu, nesimbuku? Izi- ntsompoti ezinjalo ekutiwa ziko ngamatamba
alonkolo zininzi, ezinjengokuba bati—Umtshologu lo : unomnye umzimba
Iowa use ncwabeni, ngapandle kwalo uba nawo xa uhambahambayo ezindaweni,
ngapandle kowase zintlanganisweni ezinjengale kubaliswe ngayo kula
manqaku.

UKUBETWA KWENTSIMBI.—Uvakele umfo’mkulu esiti— “Wa Nantsi,” betani
intsimbi siye kwenza ityalike nati, kuba nati sinokuyenza kuba
singamanxila afana kwanamadyakana etu, kwane remente le. Kutiwa yabetwa
okunene intsimbi, yangena ityalike yentlekisa, endlwini yetyalike, kwade
kwa kalima u Titshala walo ndawo akubona ukusa okungaka, kodwa engena
kuyipikisa ingxelo yalo manxila. [Nango ke amasi enu ziramente zakowetu,
ayaniqumbela ngoku.—ED. SIGIDIMI]

LITI ipepa lase Qonce ekutiwa yi Watchman Imantyi enkulu yase Kokstad u
Mr. W. H. Stanford utumele incwadi ku Rev. P. Hargreaves umfundisi wama
Wesile ose Mfundisweni kwelama Mpondo, ebulela kakulu ngoncedo
alwenzileyo ukupazamisa izipitipiti ebezipakati kwo Rulumente wase Kapa
nama Mpondo. Imbulele kakulu Imantyi Enkulu ngomonde, nangexesha
alicitileyo ebonisa ama Mpondo ubunzima abekubo, ewalulekela kweyona
ndlela iyakuwalungela. Utsho wati uyatemba ukuba ubuhlobo buyakubako
ngoku, nje ngokuba ucandiwe nje umda.

UMQALA ONENGOZI.—Izikula ezibini zaba ntsundu e Colesberg zivaliwe okwa
ngoku ngenxa yokwanda kwesisifo kwaba ntsundu. Esisifo siyasulela nakuba
abantu aba ntsundu bengakolwa koko. Sibulele e Graaf-Reinet abantwana
baka Mr. Thomas Taylor, omnye ubudala bake buzinyanga ezisitoba, omnye
ubudala bake yiminyaka emibini nenyanga ezilishumi. Ukubamba kwaso
esisifo uzibika umqala umntu, awuvaxa aginyayo, kungeko bushushu pofu,
kuko amabala amhlope ezinkalakahleni. Kuti emzuzwini lamabala adumbe, i
fever ibeko kuye; kube nzima ukuginya, ube namaqakuva umqala, nentloko
ibe buhlungu, ubushushu bukule, angabi nakonwaba. Kuti ngebaqo kubeko
ukuzekelela ukupefumla, kunga bonakali bunzima pofu xa apefumlayo, aye
epel’ amandla ade apele mpela ngokusuke avingcane umqala. Ub’efanele
ukuti umntu akunakana ukuba uyamgena lomqala angalibazisi ukuya
emagqireni.

INGOZI YOMPU.—Umfana otile ogama lingu Meya wase Bloemfield upulukene
nobomi bake ngokufeketa nge pistol. Wenzakala esandleni, wati ngokuyi
tye-da-tye-da ibinxashiwe yadubula yati imbumbulu yagqwizila edolweni
yati kata etangeni. Kwabizwa i Gqira elite alabona ngozi kuba aliqele
kuhlinza zimbumbulu.—Kute ke emva kwe ntsuku ezisixenxe yati nqi
imihlati wabuba emva kwe ntsuku ezimbini.

NOKULUMKELA INCINIBA.—Kuko ingozi eyenzeke kwelama Bulu e Transvaal,
kufupi ne Pretoria, (litsho ipepa eliyi Independent). Kute xa ifamili
etile ib’ isakwele enqwelwe­ni yama hashe, suke tu ezincinibeni, zimi
kufupi nendlela. Ate ukuti qwememe kwawo asel’ egqotsa, abeta yapenuka
lonqwelana yabakupela pantsi ababe kwele kuyo, benzakala bonke
ngokugqitagqiteneyo, lenzakala kakulu elinye ihashe, ekude kwafuneka
lidutyulwe.

OLUNYWE YINYOKA.—Liti ipepa eliyi V. W. Messenger.— Kuko intombazana
entsundu elunywe yinyoka, idlala endlwini enqukuva e Piquetberg nga
Mgqibelo utile. Yalunywa esandleni, yaqauka kwa oko, kunga bangako
nexesha lukuya kosiyazi. [Azi yinyoka nina le ibuhlungu bungaka?—-ED.
Sigidimi.]

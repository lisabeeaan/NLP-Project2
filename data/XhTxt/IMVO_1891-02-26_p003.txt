3 IMVO ZABANTSUNDU (NATIVE OPINION). FEBRUARY 26, 1891.

UKUNGAQONDI NGOBALO.

KWINTLANGANISO yaba Ntsundu bomandla wase Qonce ebiko nge Cawa edlluleyo
amakowetu abuze ukuba ubalo olu lwezinto zonke asikwalolu bebelumangala
na, kaloku u Rulumente ubenze isigqibo esihle ngo Nobhala; boyikela
ukuba olubalo nolubhalo luvuka pantsi. Umnumzana u Mr. Henry do Smidt
opete ubalo lwabantu uyakuvuyiseka kunene ukwazi okokuba ituba licitelwe
ukushenxisa ubunkungu kucazwa into oluyiyo ubalo; nokulwahlula kolu
lwemantyana zangapandle. Akuko matidala namatandabuzo ke ngoku,
esitembayo ukuba amawetu ayakuluncedisa ngako konke olu luzakubako.

NGOBALO

NGOBALO OLU luzakubako u Rulumente siya mncoma ngenxa yatnacebo
awencileyo pakati kwabantu bakowetu esingenamatidala okokuba uya kuquba
kakuhle. O Mantyi babize intlanganiso zabantu bonke bamane ukuchaza
kakuhle into oluyiyo olu Balo lusayo. Eyona nto ite yasivuyisa ngakumbi
kukuba wonke lomsebenzi wo Balo unikelwe ezandleni zo Titshala
kwanabanye abantsundu abazimfundi ukuba ibe ngabo ababali bempahla
kwanabantu kwimizi ngemizi. Amakowetu wona ahle abe nokurana akubona
abelungu behamba hamba pakati kwabo: esitembayo okokuba kuzo zonke
izitili ekuya kubalwa ngabebala letu kuya kuvela isiqamo esihle
nesibukekayo.

IMPUKANE EMAFUTENI

KIVAKULO olubalo luzakubako sinamanakanibe okubona FUTENI. impukane ise
mafuteni. Sino sizi kakulu ukuva okokuba kwi ndawo ezine Mantyi ezinkulu
pesha kwe Nciba okokuba lamacebo mahle kunene ka Rulumente asuke
agqwatwa kwatiwa lomsebenzi wobalo, mawenziwe ngamapolisa nabamhlope ize
otitshala kwanabanye abafundileyo babe zitoliki kupela. Ekusemhlotsheni
kanye okokuba okoku tunyelwa amapolisa kwanabo bamhlope kanye kucasene
nomnqweno kwanetsingiselo ka Rulumente, kwaye kungeze kwabaleka kakuhle
nxa kwenjinjalo. Siyatembake okokuba u Mr. de Smidt okanye nokuba ngu
mpatiswa we Koloni uyakuyilungisa kakuhle lendawo —yokuba lomsebenzi
unikelwe kubantu abantsundu abanemfundo ukuze olubalo Julia, mbeseke
kakuhle.

ngomcimbi wentengiso yotywala kwa Bantsundu. Lomcimbi utate ixesha elide

pakati kwabo. Lentoto itelelwe ngu Mr. Pitoyi, noko yaqetulwa.

NGELUNGEI.O LOKUVOTA.

acinga ntonina ngokunikwa kwa bantsundu ilungelo lokuvota esiti
ngapandle baninzi abantu abangekoyo emqulwini wabavoti kuba bengenazo
ingxande, lonto ilincipise kakulu inani labavoti abantsundu. Baye ke
ubuninzi babe bengalazi ixabiso lokuba indoda ibe ngumvoti, ngenxa
yalonto kunzima ukuba indoda ichite, itengise ngempahla yayo yake uxande
ibingalufuni ngenxa yokuba ingenalungelo lokuvota ngapandle kokuba ibe
noxande. Bati ke abaninci bahlale baxole nokuba abangabo abavoti
kunokuba bachite impahla zabo.

kuba uti ukulobola kushweshwa. Ukutsho ndiyapika. Nditsho ndisiti,
umfazi olotyolweyo utshatile ngesiko labantsundu, kuba uyise wentombi
mhlana akunikayo uyakuti: ndiyakulahla namhlanje mntwana wam, atsho
alile

inyembezi ezilusizi kunene, kube kuzele abahlobo abayakutsho nabo ukuti,

nditi, ukuba umfazi uyahlukana nendoda yake kuyaketwa inkomo, ke

akunakududwa kungalotyolwanga. Ndiyambulela lomfundisi walileyo, kuba

ukubumba izonkana apa ezimnyamana befuna ukwahlula umtshato. Ndiyapela
ngeliti, tshata nomfazi wako C. T. Tshayangodiza, akalilo ishweshwe.

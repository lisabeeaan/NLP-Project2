UKUCOLACOLA.

IJAJI PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.—Kuvakala ukuba inani le Jaji zase Griqualand
West liza kuncitshiswa, enye yazo iya kutunyelwa pesheya kwe Nciba.

ILINGA ELINGAFEZANGA LUTO.—Ate amakonxwa amabini ase Christiana e
Transvaal, ayila iqinga lokuzimela aku- bulala idindala. Kubonakal’ukuba
latunywa idindala ukugaula amahlahla linamakonxwa amane antsundu. Lite
lakubon’ukuba lisahleli pantsi esebenza amakonxwa leva seliqutyulwa
ngamabini, elinye lingasemva nelinye livele ngapambili alibamba ngenyawo
nangentloko efuna ukumeyelisela esizibeni kunjalo kufike amabini alisiza
idindala, libalekele elinye ibanjwa ematyolweni labanjwa kwa oko lati
elinye laziposa esizibeni latshona lingekayi pi ngenxa yobunzima
bamatyatanga kuba lalinxitywe wona lafa impela.

UMBANE E NQUSHWA.—Kute akubona ukuba usetafileni u Mr. Dorrington
nomkake no sapo Iwake pambi kokuba batye bacula eliculo linamazwi ati “
Yibako Nkosi esitebeni setu” beve ngesiqupe beshwatyaniswa ngumbane.
Belite kwamzuzu labonakala lihloma izulu awe ndudumo amafu. Limfumene u
Mr. Dorrington kwicala lase kohlo latsho akabi nakwenza nto ngengalo
nangomlenze wase kohlo umzuzu otile enjalo. Litsho ku Mrs. Dorrington
kwingalo yase kunene yangati irwaqele. Ibetwe yawa umzuzu intombazana
yabo latsho yoma icala ixeshana elitile. Oyena wenzakele kakulu
yinkwenkwe enkulu yabo. Ite lonto kwabo bebesendlwini yangummangaliso
otile okweloxeshana ukuba batenina ukusinda. Hayi ugcino Iwe Nkosi!

U Mr. Sauer ilungu le Parlamente lesitili sase Aliwal North uqubisene
nengozi ebuhlungu. Ute akubon’ukuba ugqotsile esukela uloliwe, (isiqelo
esazekayo eso,) ukubeke esipunzini waziluma ulwimi Iwapants’ukushunquka.
Akayi kuba nakuteta ixesha elitile ngenxa yalo ngozi kude kube
sekuhlanganeni kwe Parlamente into elusizi ke leyo.

UMFAZI wakwa Komani e Location uzele amawele amatatu ngomhla we 29 ku
December 1886 amabini ngamakwenkwe elinye yintombi. Asaquba kakuhle
lomawele, wenjenjalo ukwongezelela kwake kunyaka omdala. Abazali bala
mawele bebefanele ukungawulibali lonyaka udluleyo.

OZIBULELEYO.—Liti ipepa lase Mtati kubube ivolonti elimhlope lise
zintlungwini ezinkulu. Kubonakala ukuba lomfo ubenentsuku ezilinani
esela kakulu. Kuti lakubon’ ukuba aliko igosa lendlu yamayeza langena
lasela amayeza abangele ukububa kwalo. Kuyiwe kubizwa igqira alanceda
nto. Ute lomfo ekuqaukeni kwake unosizi nge senzo esibi asenzileyo.

UKUBULALA OKUMANYUMNYEZI.—Kwilizwe lapesheya ekutiwa ukubizwa kwalo yi
Kentucky kwisiseko ekutiwa ukubizwa kwaso yi Knox e America bekuko
umlimi ogama lingu Poe enomfazi wake nabantwana abahlanu nama nonokazi
amabini indwendwe zabo, indlu yabo inesitutyana esincinane kweka Worm,
nomkake, nenkwenkwana eminya- ka ilishumi ubudala bayo. Olusapo Iuka
Worm Iwalungena- similo silungileyo ate ke ngoko u Poe walwalela olwake
usapo ukuhambelana nalo. Ivakele ke lonto imqumbise kakulu u Worm
nomkake bacebisana ukuba balubade olu Iuka Poe lonke lupela. Balinda
ituba lokungabiko kwake, kwati akubon’ukuba akako, waya u Worm nomkake
endlwini ka Poe ebusuku. Ulandele unyana ka Worm bakunduluka nomfazi
wake wababona ukungena kwabo kulendlu ka Poe lulele usapo Iwake
nendwendwe ezo. Ubanqumle imiqala ngabanye, ngesitshetshe sendevu, waye
umkake ezifunqula izidumbu zabo ezibeka pantsi emgangatweni wekambile,
wabeka impahla zekoyi ezimbini pezu kwabo. _Emva koko bafake isikuni
kwezo mpahla bezifumbe pezu kwezidumbu nasendlwini. Lute usana kanti
alubonwa Iwafa nalo kuku’raxwa ngumsi nangumlilo. Lwafa lonke ololusapo
ngabusuku bunye ngenzondo ka Worm nomkake.

UBUSELA.—Kulusizi olukulu ukubona emapepeni ukuba luvukile ubiwo
Iwempahla. Isand’ukugweba isela Imantyi yase Qonce eleba e Zeleni igusha
yomntu ontsundu. Itete amazwi alusizi pambi kwokugweba yati akuko sizatu
sikubangele ukuba uyibe legusha kuba unokutya okwa- neleyo. Yatsho yati,
kuzo ezintsuku ibi sand’ ukuncoma abantu abantsundu ngoku ngebi kwabo,
wayonakalisa inteto entle kunene lombanjwa ngobusela bake, engenisa
ngabo igama elibi kulo lonke uhlanga oluntsundu. Ugwe- tyelwe umnyaka
etolongweni esebenza nzima.

KWAKONA ubuye wanikwa inyanga ngokuba izitungu zehabile e Mdikido (Fort
Jackson) zika Mr. Butt. Kutiwe uya kugqiba umnyaka kuqala, uti wakupela
angene kule nyanga.

AMADODA amahlanu antsundu agwetyelwe inyanga ezi ntandatu etolongweni
esebenza nzima ngokuba inkabi ka Mr. Trollip wase Nyara (Bedford).
Impahla yawo etengisiweyo ukubuyisa indleko zika Mr. Trollip ngalenkabi
nangokufuna amagqweta, ibe ngamahashe amatatu, imazi ezintandatu
zenkomo, ibokwe ezima shumi mabini.

IMBALI embi kunene ibaliswa kwa Dalwe, ngokunyamezela intlungu kwabantu
abantsundu.—Iti kwakuko mfo utile owenzakala entloko laqekeka itambo
lokakayi. Ute emnyamekele kakulu umfazi wake ngokumana eluhlamba oluduma
imihla yonke ngosiba, emana epungula ububovu kulo kude kwazi Cawa
ezintatu esenje njalo. Kanti engazi nje yena lento ayikupayo eludumeni
lemihla ngemihla bubucopo. Liti ke igqira ngelesapilile lomfo ukuba
bebungakutshwanga kangaka ubucopo bake.

UBUDENGE BAMA SWAZI.—Kuhlalele ukubako inkatazo kwelama Swazi. U
Mbandini inkosi yawo ulelipantsi inxila epa wonke ubani oze kucela
isiqwenga somhlaba nokuba ungolunge kangakananina, eyakuti eyifumene
lomali ayicitele ebrandini. Siteta nje sekuko ifama ezima 40 abati abemi
kwezo fama ukuwubiza lommandla bakuwo yi “ Klein Vrij Staat.” Ifama zase
Transvaal zizuze imvumelo yokudlisa kwelozwe lama Swazi. Sel’eqalile
ukuwakalazela ama Swazi ukuba ayaziba inkomo zawo. Inkosi ezinkulu
ezingo Joubert no Smit zite ku Mbandini ziyakumnika ama £500 ngonyaka
ukuba uyavuma ukuba pantsi kolaulo Iwase Transvaal. Elase Transvaal
selite nokuba kungayipina nangenjani indlela bayakuwuzuza lomhlaba.
Umhlaba lowo utyebile ngezinto ezinqabileyo ezimbiwa kuwo, uyacuma
ngezilimo. Ayatshabalala yi brandy ama Swazi kuba akuko kwalelwa kwayo.
Elolifa lomhlaba liza kuba lelama Bulu.

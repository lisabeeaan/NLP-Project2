UKUPUMA KUKA MR. LEONARD.

NGOLWESIBINI ngomhla we 3 ku May u Mr. Sprigg wazise ukuba u Mr. Leonard
uzikupile emsebenzini wake wokuba Ligqweta Lakomkulu, uselebambe kodwa
elindele oza kufakwa kulondawo. Ute u Mr. Leonard ekungeneni kwake ebu
Rulumentini ubevumelana no laulo lwabantu aba Ntsundu engenakwazi kunga
pezulu kwolo luntu lonke ; kanti kuko ndawo zivuseleyo, asele eziva
ngoku uzifumene ke ngoku engenako ukuvumelana nalo ngoku. Ute u Mr.
Sprigg way’ emxelele u Mr. Leonard kwasekuqaleni ukuba awugqale kakuhle
lomsebenzi angena kuwo, usuke wati yena akana kubavela abo bacasene
nalo, yena uya kuba ngakwicala lika Rulumente; kekaloku namhla uti uvalo
lwake alumvumele ukuba alandelane nayo indlela elupetwe ngalo ulaulo
lwaba Ntsundu.

UCELO LWO XOLO.

KUHLANGENE ngo 20 April igosa lakwa Rulumente nondiyalwa izolo pantsi
kwentaba ka Lihlati. Bekuko u Lerothode, no Nto obese ndaweni ka Letsea.
Akuba ngako bantu base zikundleni zo Masupa no Joel, nakuba laliko
igqiza lempi ka

Masupha. Uhlobo lokube kwake u Lerothode lwafaneleka. Amapakati
ayemayela e 100 exobile ekwele emahasheni okwawo ukuzipata aye
ngakatali. Kwakuko kunye no Lerothodi u Dr. Casalis no Rev. H. Dyke
(uyise nonyana). Yatabata lontlanganiso iyure ezi 2. Libuze igosa lakwa
Rulumente ku Lerothodi ukuba “ulucela ngenene na uxolo, nokuba ufuna
sahlulwe esi sipitipiti yi Ruluneli na.” Upendule wati “ewe.” Ngendawo
yokunikelwa kwezixobo bate o Lerothodi no Nto abakolwa ukuba baya kuvuma
Abesutu ukuzirola nokuba zibiziwe yi Ruluneli. Kuqubeke inteto emva
kwoko engokumkelwa kwezixobo abanga vananga ngayo ate u Lerothodi
watetelela Abesutu. Ekupeleni u Lerothodi uvumile ukuyindulula imikosi
yondiyalwa nokuzibeka pantsi kwezandla ze Ruluneli. Lite ke igosa lakwa
Rulumente liya kunika umteto kwimikosi yase Koloni ukuba ihlale kwindawo
ekuzo ilinde isigwebo se Ruluneli, liya kuyazisa isipelo sale ngxoxo
ibiko no ndiyalwa. Kwakuko kumgama we mayile enye umhlaumbi ezimbini i
1000 londiyalwa. Kuvakala ukuba ondiyalwa bangxamele oku ukuze bavune
izilimo zabo ezicume kunene. — Grocott's Penny Mail.

INDABA ZAKUTSHA NJE.

Asikuko nokuba silusizi ukuva ukuba Abesutu abalamkelanga icebo loku
xolelanisa kwe Ruluneli. Lento bayenzileyo, bubugeza bobu hilihili
kanye.

KUBA BALELANI.

SIYA zisa ukuba sifumana into eninzi kunene yencwadi ezivela kuba
balelani. Siya bulela ukubona umnqweno okoyo woku balela Isigidimi;
kodwa inkoliso yezi ncwadi zinde ngokuti kuxake; yabe inteto yazo
ikolise ukupinda-pinda nto nye. Sibacela aba balelani betu ukuba balinge
ukuti inteto zabo aba sukuba bezibalela Isigidimi, ukuba kunokuncedwa,
zingolulwa, koko zicube indawo ezizi ntloko, ngamazwi ambalwa,
nacacileyo.

EZIVELA KUBA BALELANI.

Incwadi ezi sukuba zibalelwa ukufakwa e Sigidimini, kufuneka u Mhleli
yena elazi igama lo mtumeli ncwadi, nakuba lingayi ku shicilelwa ukuba
umninilo akalifuni ukuba laziwe luninzi.

Siyazisa kanjako ukuba asilitabateli pezu kwetu ityala lenteto zaba
balelani betu, asitsho nokuti siyavumelana nazo zonke incwadi zabo
esizishicilelayo.

OBULEWEYO.

NKOSI YAM MHLELI,—

Yeha! Nal’ usizi olubi kakulu kubanlu balomzi. Siyalila, zinyembezi,
sishiywe ngumhlobo wetu u Simon Gwama, umfo okulunga ndinge kucazi kuba
ndinga gqiba ipepa. Ngomhla wama 25 ku Feb. ngokuhlwa pakati kwe nqwelo
zase Tsomo ezibe zilele e Qamata, kwesika Matanzima (inkosi yaba Tembu),
kwehla lento xa bakwesika badakazi. Uvakele omnye evusa abanye esiti
vukani niyasivana isitonga esi sompu. Bate abanye hayi. Bate xa bavukayo
wasele lomfo ebanda. Wafunyanwa enenxeba lingene emarinini laza kupuma
ebucotsheni. Ikangelwe ke lengozi ibe ngummangaliso ukuti umntu
odutyulweyo isuke igubeke intloko nobuso lonto ibange ukuba kufane nento
ekandwe ngento enzima. Yati enye into ebe sisimanga kuti isitonga sompu
siviwe mntu mnye ngulo ubelele naye, abe ke lomntu engumntu oseloko
epikelene nomfazi wale ndoda ibuleweyo emrexeza. Ngenxayezi zizatu
lomntu akubonakali ukuba ubulewe ngama Xosa. Londoda yeva isitonga
isentolongweni siteta nje ukuba kude kuqondakale lento.

Tsomo Station,

7 April, 1881.

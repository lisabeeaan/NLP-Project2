NOUN. IGAMA.

I Noun ligama lokubizwa kwento. I Noun ziya hlukahluka zenze ama qela
asi bozo, ahlulwe zi Article ezi lingana nazo. Iqela 1. Umntu. Um Tembu.
Um zuzu.

2.  I hashi. I Tyala. I Zibuko.

Ili zwi. Hi tye. Ui fu.

3.  Im pahla. Im puku.

4.  Isi zwe. Isi punzi. Isi tya.

7.  Ubu so. Ubu hlanti.

8.  Uku dla.

Nanga pandle koku kwahlulwa zi Article kuko ezinye iza hluko ezi koyo.

Ezinye i Noun zi Proper, ngokuba zingama gama alunge nento ezitile.
Ezinye zi Common, ngokuba zinga miselwanga ukubiza into leyo yodwa, zi
biza into ezininzi.

Ezinye izahluko ezi koyo ezi Nouneni, ziyi Gender, eyalilula into ebu
doda kwinto ebu nkazana, ne Numba, eyahlula into enye kwezi ninzi, ne
Case, yona ixela intlalo yento.

Proper Nouns. Amagama angawo.

Besisiti i Noun i lilizwi elibiza into nge gama layo, njengo Mary,
umntu, ihashi, indlu, inkomo, inkosi. Kekaloku eli gama liseku qaleni u
Mary lahlukile kwamanye, lona liyi Pro­per Noun, oko kukuti ligama
elililo ngo kwenene. Igama nokuba lelo mntu, nokuba lelo mzi, nokuba
lelanto nina, ukuba liya hlula into yalo kuzo zonke ezinye, lona liyi
Proper Noun. Ama gama abantu njengo George, Sotshozana, Nowatsha,
Hlakanyana, Mehlomane. Nama gama emizi, nemi lambo, ne ntaba; Africa, i
Rini, i Qonce, i Nxukwebe, u Ndlovukazi, im Vani, i Tsomo, i Nciba, u
Lukanje, Onke lawo magama azi Proper Noun.

I Common Noun ililizwi eli biza into njengo hlobo lwayo; linga hlulanga
lonto inye ukuyi biza yodwa kuzo zonke ezinye ezi fana nayo, njengala
ke: Umlambo, ilitye, umoya, ipupa.

Ukuti liyi Common Noun, igama elinjalo kuku xela ukuba lilungele zonke
izinto ezi lolo hlobo lubizwayo; asi ligama lanto nye yodwa.

Exercise. Ukuze kubonakale ukuba niqondile nakwazi ukwahlula ezi ntlobo
zitetiweyo, mani bale ezi letini zenu la mazwi.

Kuko intaba ezi ntatu, u Qutsa, u Lunda, no Centane.

Kuko imi lambo emi hlanu, um Gwali, um Bashe, um Tata, i Tsitsa, i Tina.

Kuko izi kolo ezi tatu; esase Gcuwa, esase Tsomo, nesa kwa Cofimvaba.

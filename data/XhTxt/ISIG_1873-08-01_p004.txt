UMTETO WEZICAKA NENKOSI ZAZO.

AKUKO mntu uya kunyanzelwa ukuba ahlale endaweni ngapezulu komnyaka,
ngapandle kokuba kube kuvunyelwene ngalondawo pambi kwe Mantyi.

Isicaka nokuba ngumsebenzi oyakufunyanwa apule ezindawo zilandelayo,
uyakudliwa nokuba yimali nina engengapezulu kwe ponti, ati ukuba
akayiroli ahlale etolongweni ixesha ehngengapezulu kwe nyanga, mhlaumbi
ngeloxesha asetyenziswe nzima. Ezondawo zezi:—

1.  Ukuba uyakuti emva komnqopiso angauqali umsebenzi ngexesha
    elimisiweyo, akwenze oko kungeko sizatu sikulu sokuba enjenjalo.

2.  Ukuba uyakuti ngapandle kwemvumelo, emke ngasendlwini yenkosi yake,
    nakwindawo emise- Iwe umsebenzi wake.

3.  Ukuba ute ngexesha lomsebenzi wanxila, mhla­umbi waba-mmandi, wazake
    ngoko akabi naku- wenza umsebenzi ngemfanelo yawo.

4.  Ukuba ute akawenza umsebenzi ebefanele ukuwenza, mhlaumbi wawenza
    ngokunganya- meki, wawugxakaza umsebenzi abefanele uku- wucokisa
    awenze ngenyameko nje ngokom- nqopiso.

5.  Ukuba uyakuti ngapandle kwemvumelo yenkosi yake asebenzise ihashe,
    nokuba yimpahla nina enye eyeyenkosi yake.

6.  Ukuba uyakuti angaupulapuli umyalelo wenkosi yake, mhlaumbi owomntu
    omiswe yiyo, xa ibiyimfanelo yake ukuba awutobele.

7.  Ukuba ute wenza ingxolo nesipazamiso endlwini nokuba kuse mhlabeni
    wenkosi yake, aze anga- pulapuli akutetiswa yinkosi yake, nokuba
    ngu- banina obekwe yinkosi yake esikundleni sayo.

8.  Ukuba uyakuti atete amazwi okutuka mh1aumbi okucapukisa inkosi yake,
    nokuba ngumfazi wayo, nokuba ngumntu omiswe endaweni yenkosi yake;
    ukuba lomazwi aya kubanga ingxabano.

Ukuba kwezindawo zikankanyiweyo kuko aya- pule okwesibini zingekapeli
inyanga ezintandatu angadliwa £3, mhlaumbi abe neveki ezintandatu
etolongweni esebenza nzima.

Umntu osicaka, mhlaumbi ofunda umsebenzi angadliwa imali engengapezulu
kwe ponti ezintatu mhlaumbi afakwe etolongweni ixesha elingenga- pezulu
kwenyanga ezimbini esebenza nzima mhla- umbi kalula, ukuba ute
wafunyanwa enetyala lenye yezindawo :—

1.  Ukuba uyakuti ngokwapula imfanelo, mhla­umbi ngokuyidukisa, nokuba
    kungobunxila, enze into eyakungenisa ilahlekelo, nokonakala nokuba
    kukuba sengozini kwempahla eshiyelwe ezandleni zake yinkosi yake,
    nokuba ngu­mntu omiswe endaweni yayo.

2.  Ukuba ute akavuma, mhlaumbi wadukisa ukwenza imfanelo, nokuba
    kungokunxila, ukuba lomfanelo ibiya kwenza ukuba kugcineke impa- hla
    eshiyelwe ezandleni zake yinkosi yake, nokuba ngumntu osendaweni
    yayo.

3.  Ukuba ute engu malusi akayixela impahla eselugcinweni Iwake
    elahlekileyo nokuba yefi- leyo, ngetuba lokuqala emva kokuba efumene
    ukuba ilahlekile mhlaumbi ifile, nokuba ute akavuma ukuba inkosi
    yake ibe nendawo eyi- bonayo yenyama yento efileyo, xa lonkosi ifuna
    ukuyibona, nokuba kanjalo ziyakuti zingafu- maneki impahla
    ezilahlekileyo, noko kubona- kalayo ukuba ngeziba zifunyenwe ukuba
    zibe zifunwe ngenyameko.

4.  Ukuba kuko impahla elahlekileyo xa esasebe- nza misebenzi yimbi,
    kwaza kwabonakala uku­ba inge inga lahlekanga ukuba ubenge njanga
    njalo.

5.  Ukuba uyakuti kungeko sizatu sifanelekileyo abete, mhlaumbi alinge
    ukubeta inkosi yake nokuba ngumfazi wayo, nokuba sisihlobo sayo,
    mhlaumbi umntu oyihambeleyo, nokuba sesinye isicaka.

6.  Ukuba ute ngapandle kwesizatu waushiya umsebenzi wenkosi yake
    enentliziyo yokuze angabuyi aubuyele.

Ukuba kwezindawo zikankanyiweyo kuko aza­pule okwesibini zingekapeli
inyanga ezintandatu angadliwa £5, mhlaumbi abe setolongweni inya­nga
ezintatu esebenza nzima.

Inkosi mayisise ematyaleni isicaka, ingekapeli inyanga emvakokuba
iyazile into esiyonileyo.

Ukuba inkosi isixelele isicaka ukuze sibe sema- tyaleni ngexesha elitile
saza asabiko, Imantyi ingatumela ukuba siye kubanjwa.

Ukuba inkosi ite ayavuma ukusibatala isicaka noko ibiselifanele
ukwenjenjalo, iyakunyanzelwa ukuba ibatale, ize kanjalo idliwe £5, iti
ukuba ayiziroli ifakwe etolongweni inyanga.

Ukuba inkosi ite isicaka ayasinika kudla, no­kuba yintouina enye
ekunqotshiselwene ngayo kumqopiso obaliweyo iyakudliwa £5, iti ukuba
ayiziroli ifakwe etolongweni inyanga.

Imantyi inegunya lokuwupelisa umnqopiso xa ibona inkosi ingenako
mhlaumbi ingavumi ukuzizalisa indawo ekuvunyelwene ngazo.

Umntu oyinkosi mhlaumbi osisicaka, oyakuzisa isikalazo kungeko sizatu
saneleyo uyakudliwa £5, ati ukuba akaziroli afakwe etolongweni inyanga
ibenye.

INTLANGANISO YABANTSUNDU KWELE DIAMOND.

E-New Rush, ngo July 19,1873.

Kwmhleli we Sigidimi.

NKOSI,—Kambe ungumgcogeli wendatyana zonkenje uyakude uqekeke intloko,
zincwadana zetu eziza zingahluzwanga nokuhluzwa. Ungadinwa nangomso mfo
wapesheya kwamanzi ukusivisa nezakowenu indaba.

Ndiyacelwa ke ngumhlambi onga makowetu

ose Diamond apa, ukuba ndikubalele incwadi yokubulela elibasokazi
Hngakanana, lokwandiswa kwe Sigidimi.

Nantso ke mabandla akowetu into ekade niyi- nqwenela. Namhla indodana
eti ilumkile kanti ayisifundi Isigidimi yoba iyazikohlisa.

Apa indaba zizonke ezimnandi nezimbi. Kodwa ke okwanamhla ndoke
ndikuvandele kwezingati mhlaumbi zibe mnandi kuwe.

Ngomhla we 17 ka July besi nentlanganiswano enkulu kunene ye Tea-meeting
E-Bultfontein, etyalikeni ye Remente epetwe ngu Mr. James Poote. Kwati
kuba umhla lowo wabubikwe kwangenx’ engapambili yazala vingci tu
ityalike pofu inkulu, bada bema ngenyawo abanye, kungenwa ke nge tikiti.

Eyona ndawo inkulu kwakuhlangenwe ngayo yabe ikukuhambiseka komsebenzi
we Nkosi kwizizwe zakowetu ezintsundu, kwano kwanda kwe mfundo pakati
kwazo. Kodwa eyona ndawo inkulu zazisenzelwa yona itikiti ezo, yabe
ikukufaka izandla kwetu kulomsebenzi mkulu sivayo ukuba uyazanywa
kwelase zantsi—wokwandiswa kwe Lovedale Institution.

Yintlanganiso ke leyo ebumnandi busuke bafuna ukufuza ezindala
intlanganiso. Kwati yakubon’ ukuba iko indoda le ingu mongameli
wentlanganiso kwaza kwabuya kwako ndodana ipete usiba imana ukuwagolela
onke lamazwi atetwayo iwabeka epepeni kwada kwanga kuse ndlwini
yamatyala.

Yangena ngexa le 6 ngokuhlwa yapuma ngele 11 ebusuku. Yati imali esele
ezityeni, kundawonye neye bazaar eyayenziwe zintokazi ezitile zase
Bultfontein yaba ziponti ezimashumi mabini (£20). Lomalike itunyelwe
ukunceda lomkosi ubuhlatywa Esigidimini wokwandiswa komzi wemfundo wase
Lovedale, itunyelwa zizo zonke I-Remente zabantsundu ezise Diamond.

Sendonipa intloko zenteto yamadoda alishumi elina mntu mnye zobobusuku.
Kwasuka U- Mongameli (noko angafuniyo ukuba laziwe igama lake) wayicaza
kakuhle into yentlanganiso, wakankanya izindlu zemfundo zabakowetu
nokunqweneleka kokuba abantu bafunde kwanosapo lwabo. Walinganisela nga
Mangesi okokuba enamandla kangaka nje kungenxa yemfundo. Wakankanya umzi
wase Dikeni kwaneziqamo zawo, waza wapela ngelokuti tina Makristu masibe
ngabacombululi bale mbijelane ipinyele abantu E- Diamond apa.

Kubekiswe ke kumfundisi umfo ka Tyamzashe, wateta ngokuti umsebenzi
wanamhla kukuqala kokuzama kwetu ukukasela ngase kwaziweni kwe Nkosi,
kuba akuzanga kubeko ntlanganiso zinje kulomhlaba. Uncome ubulile bale
ntlanganiso yanamhla ingeyiyo yaba nkonzo itile, nasizwe sitile, iyeye
Remente zonke zabantsundu E- Diamond, iyeyentlanga ngentlanga
zabantsundu beli lizwe. Ukankanye izikolo ezikulu zabantsu­ndu
azibonileyo ngokwake—Esase Lusutu esipetwe ngu Rev. Mr Dyke no Rev. A.
Mabille, nesase Dikeni, nesase Nxukwebe, wagqiba ngokuti tina esinga
Besutu na Maxosa ase Diamond sifanele

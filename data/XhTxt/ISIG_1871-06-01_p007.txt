ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, JUNE 1, 1871.

UMBONISO WE KOTON.

KUYA kubako umboniso we Koton E- Qonce emini enkulu ngolwe Sine, ngolwe
10 ku August. Bonke abalimi bekoton E-Koloni bavulelwe ukuze bayise
eyabo.

AMABASO AYAKUNIKWA NGALA :—

1.   Elokuqala loba ziponti ezimashumi matatu (£30). Lonikwa kodlule
    abanye ngokuba ne koton eninzi; engengapantsi kweyase America
    ngobuhle nangokutamba. Iti ukuba selicokisiwe ibe ziponti eziliwaka
    ubunzima bayo (1000lbs) ; ukuba ayi- kacokiswa ibe ziponti ezimawaka
    matatu (30001bs); ziti intlobo ngentlobo zayo zahlulwahlulwe. Kubeko
    kanjalo iziqiniso zokuba okwenene yonke ibityalwe ngulo- mntu.

2.   Elesibini loba ziponti ezimashumi mabini (£20). Lonikwa ko koton
    ilandela eyozuze elokuqala ngobuninzi; ubunzima bayo xa selicokisiwe
    ibe ziponti ezimakulu asixenxe anamashumi mahlanu (7501bs) ; ukuba
    ayikacokiswa ibe ziponti ezimawaka mabini anamakulu mabini
    anamashumi omahlanu (22501bs).

3.   Elesitatu loba ziponti ezilishumi elinesihlanu (£15). Lonikwa
    konentle kakulu yoluhlobo kutiwa yi Sea Island inoboya obude
    butambile. Ingabi ngapantsi kwamakulu amahlanu eponti ngobunzima
    (500lbs) xa iselicokisiwe; ukuba ayikacokiswa ingabi ngapantsi
    kweziliwaka elinamakulu mahlanu (l,5001bs).

4.   Elesine loba yinto ekucokiswa ngayo ikoton, ekutiwa yi koton jin.
    Elibaso linikwe yivenkile ka Peacock no Weir. Ixabiso layo liziponti
    ezilishumi elinesitatu, ezineshumi lesheleni (£13 10s). Lonikelwa
    koneqela elikulu nelona lihle loluhlobo kutiwa yi New Orleans.
    Ngobuninzi ingabi ngapantsi kweyozuze ibaso lesitatu.

5.   Elesihlanu loba kwa yi koton jin, ekwaxabiso linye nale sesike
    sayikankanya. Elibaso linikwe ngabongameli bomboniso. Lonikwa
    konelandelayo ngobukulu nobuhle yoluhlobo kutiwa yi Sea Island.
    Ingabi ngapantsi kwamakulu amabini eponti (2001bs) xa iselicokisiwe.
    Iti ukuba ayikacokiswa ingabi ngapantsi kwamakulu amatandatu eponti
    zobunzi- ma (6001bs).

IDIAMONDI.

IDIAMONDI ezisand’ukutunyelwa E-Lon- don ngomkombe ekutiwa yi Sweden,
zili 198. Ixabiso lazo liziponti ezi £1760.

Idiamondi zisafunyanwa kunene ngabembi bazo. Ngenye iveki kwafunyanwa
ikulu elinamanci matandatu anesine (164), encinane kuzo ingapantsi kwe
karat enye, enkulu izikarat ezimashumi osixenxe ane- sitoba (79). Le
diamondi inkulu yafunyanwa yinkosikazi yakwa Vander Vrede.

Asikuko nokuba likulu ixabiso layo. Bati ukuba baninzi kwabantu abaya
kuyibona, bangangokude ati “ akwaba bendingayifu- mananga.” Kodwa
kobonakala kulembalana ilandelayo ukuba abanamatamsanqa bonke. Omnye
wabasand’ukubuya kona ubalisa ngomnye wabanamashwa. Uti “Il- Mr. Gous
wasePrince Alfred weza kum nge nye imini, kuba ubengembi mgama kulondawo
bendikuyo, warola indaliso wati, ‘sekukupela kwento endinayo le, ukufika
kwam apa ndandinenqwelo, nesipani senkabi, neshumi elinesitandatu
lepcnti (£16) sendinyanga zisitoba ndite qb ndisebenza, noko andizanga
ndazuza nokuba ibenye.’ ” Uti umbalisi walembali, okwenene londoda
yayibinqele pezulu ezonyanga zositoba, noko ingazanga yafumana nto
njalo. Akuko simaziyo uke wahlelwa lilishwa elinje ngelalowo.

KOPUNYULWA ngolwe Sitatu ngolwa 21 ku June; ze kupindwe ukungenwa ngolwa
27 ku July.

Kwesentombi isikolo kuya kwamkelwa ezimbini intombi mhlaumbi ezintatu
kwi- qela elifunda imisebenzi. Ezo zisemseben- zini ziyifumana imfundo
ngokuhlwa ; zize zibatalwe isheleni zone ngenyanga, ngo- nyaka wokuqala,
zizuze nokudla kwazo. Ote wakangela kwi Sigidimi sika December
wozifumana kona ezinye indawo.

KWA MR. AYLIFF wase Beaufort ku- sand’ukubiwa amashumi osibozo egusha.
Nakoka Mr. Gilbert umhlambi kubiwe amashumi omatatu anesibini.

I-LAWO ebelisand’ukufika apa E-Beau- fort, lifuna inkomo ebezibiwe, lite
xa ligodukayo lapangwa ihashe, nomkala, nesali. Belikulule ngase
Bedford, ngoku­hlwa, lalibopelela ihashe emtini kwakufupi, isali
laqamela ngayo. Lite lakupapama yaingaseko isali kunye nehashe. Libuye
ngomva lada leza kufika kwa Ferguson labona apo elinye I-Lawo likwele
kwelihashe lalo. Laselisiti malibanjwe liziswe etolongweni yase
Beaufort.

PESHEYA KWE NCIBA.—I-Watchman, ipepa lendaba lase Qonce
liti:—Kusand’ukuvulwa indlu yetyalike yase Tutura. Mhla yavulwa kwabako
intlanganiso enkulu, abati kuyo abafundisi ababeko benza inteto. Uti
omnye wababeko, yaba yinto entle ukubona abantsundu beposa itiki zabo
ngentliziyo ezicacileyo, lati netyala layo ikakulu lagqitywa yimali
epuma kubo. Bati abamhlope ababeko abazanga babone imali inikwa
ngentliziyo ezinje ukucaca, inikelwa umsebenzi olungileyo. Singa
angaquba kuhle U-Mr. Soga, kucumo nto zonke kuye. Ufanele ukubongwa
ngenxa yokuhambiseka kakuhle kwezinto zonke ngalomini. Owaye
sesihlalweni ngu Rev. Bryce Ross.

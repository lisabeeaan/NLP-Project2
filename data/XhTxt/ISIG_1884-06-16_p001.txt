NGOM-VULO, JUNE 16, 1884.

ILUNGELO LOKU VOTA.

UMNUMZETU, i Ruluneli, inike isaziso sokuba wonke ofuna enelungelo
lokuvota (ukuba nezwi) ekunyulweni kwamalungu e Palamente angalingenisa
igama lake kwi Gosa (Field Cornet) elikum’mandla wake, mhlaimbi ku
Mantyi we Sitili akuso. Lomsebenzi ke maufezwe ngentsuku ezipakati kolwa
26 lo May nolwa 31 July, 1884

Elixesha belilindelwe kangaka ngabantu bakowetu, nga- ngokuba lifikile
nje siyatemba ukuba wonke onayo imfanelo yokungena encwadini
yabanelungelo akasakudenda ukulingenisa igama lake. Ukuba nezwi
ekulungisweni kwesiko ayizange ibe yintwana encinane kwakwimihla
yakudala. Amadoda amakulu ebehlala ebandla imini yonke, ehlalela
ukuqonda icala elisinga kulo isiko Ema Ngesini apa ke yinto enonelelwe
kunene le ikumiswa kwemiteto ; ngakumbi kuba imiswa, mhlaimbi icitwa
yonke leminyaka ihlanganayo i Palamente. Lilungelo elikulu ke lake wonke
opantsi kwe Nkosazana ukuba ayisebenzise imfanelo yake emicimbini
enjengokunyulwa kwamabandla awenza imiteto. Abo ke balidangaleleyo eli
lungelo boseloko besenzelwa ngabalindwebeleyo imiteto, eti maxa wambi
ibe yenga kolisiyo. Akusizi Into ke nokuba ati ubani “ Hai mna “
andisakungena kweli sango kuba kuti lakungenwa kuvele “ okubi;
sendohlala ndikangele lonto indihlelayo : ”—kuba abo balisebenzisayo
ilungelo elo bona baya kuti roqo bali- mise ngokutanda kwabo isiko. Oku
sekubonwe yinto eninzi yempi yakowetu emele ezizitili, sitemba sitandaza
ukuba kwanga kungabonwa ngabo bonke.

Kuninzi esingakutetayo ngokupatelele kweli lungelo; kodwa asixesha eli,
nandawo yokungena nzulu ekuliqwalaseleni, le. Eli lixesha lezenzo.
Esitembayo ke ukuba amadodana ayakuwunceda umzi ngokuwulungiselela kweli
tuba likulu kuzo zonke indawo. Okwangoku singxamele ukuguqulela
kweyakowetu inteto ezona nto zipbkeleyo emazigcinwe ekungenisweni
kwamagama ; kuba ingemntu wonke omaka ngene. Ako amapepa ashicilelweyo
anokufunyanwa ngowafunayo ko Mantyi bendawo ngendawo; kulamapepa onga
anganalo ilungelo lokunyula ungenisa igama lake kwizituba ezishiyelwe
lomsebenzi: ze emva koko alitumele ku Mantyi oya kulikupa igama epepeni
apo alingenise kulencwadi akangelwa kuyo amagama ngomhla wonyulo. Umntu
ofanele ukungeniswa makabe ngonazo zonke ezonto zifunwa ngumteto,
mhlaumbi ongenazo ezo zicaswa kwa nguwo. Singena ngoku ekuguquleni
indawo ezizintloko emtetweni wokungeniswa kwamagama.

Wonke ke ubani ongaba uzifikelele zonke incam ezifunekayo kum" voti
esizilandelisa ngapantsi apa, woba nelungelo lokuvota, ukuba kodwa
uselekwiminyaka emashumi mabini ananye ubudala; nokoke bona abantu
abangamageza, nabaka banamatyala anjengawo kubulala, obudlwengu,
obusela, obupangi nokuba lulupina uhlobo lobumenemene baza bafunyanwa
benetyala, bona bakutshelwa ngapandle. Nanzike impau ekunqweneleka ukuba
onga angangumvoti anganazo :—

1.  Entlalweni nokuba ngozimeleyona nokuba udibene nomnye, kodwa ixesha
    behlangene lingabi ngapantsi konyaka, indlu nokuba yivenkdena nokuba
    yindlu yempahla zokurwebana, nokuba sisitolana, nokuba yi ofisina
    mayingabi yexabiso lingapantsi kwe ponti ezi £25, okoke kukuti umntu
    ongumvoti makabe nexabiso eliziponti ezi £25.

2.  Ukuba ngosebenzayo makabe ngumntu ngonyaka omvuzo ukumashumi
    omahlanu eponti.

3.  Ekuyakuti kananjalo kosukuba ehlala esitya endlwini yomnye kwindawo
    asukuba esebenza kuyo kufuneke kona ngonyaka enalo jxabiso elikwi
    ponti ezi 25.

Xosa sodwa

IMPAWANA.

U Mr. John Booy Radasi, ititshala yesikolo sase Wesile e Bayi, oya
kukunjulwa ngabalesi bezi “ Mpawana ” ngemvumo yentsapo yake esaka senza
mabini matatu ngayo, usibikela ukuba uzakubuya enze enye i Concert
kamsinyane, etembisa ukuba uyakwenza ngokugqitiseleyo kunoko
sakubalisela abalesi betu. Indawo esazibeka amabala siqonda ukuba
uyakuzilungisa. Situnqwenelela impumelelo kweli linga lesibini. Kakade
elinye lamehlo etu liya kubako kulomgidi.

Ngo 4 June kwabako ingxoxo e Palamente engokuba amapolisa e Xesi kwa
Kama makancitshiswe. Inyaniso ezavelayo kule ngxoxo zezokuba kumi
amapolisa alishumi kwesi sitili sakwa Kama imali ahlaulwa ngayo zi £456
ngonyaka. U Mr. John Laing ute lemali mayitotyelwe kwi £228, zekubeko
amapolisa amahlanu endaweni yeshumi. Lendawo ivunyiwe emva kwengxoxo
ende. Isininzi esiyamkeleyo ngama Bulu. Lamapolisa ke azakubehle
ancitshiswe ngokomyalelo we Palame­nte. Lento iya kubakolisa abantu
bakwa Kama kuba tina sisazi ukuba lento ipolisa asinto itandekayo kuti
bamnyama.

Siyatemba ukuba abafundi betu abasakusilwa ngendawo yokufaka udaba
olungati seluludala xa sivakalisa ukububa kuka Sir Bartie Frere ngo May
29 1884. Imbanga yoko Iwafika olodaba selishicilelwe ipepa letu
lokugqibela. U Sir B. Frere wabeyi Ruluneli yeli ukususa ku 1877 ukusa
ku 1880. Ubeyindoda ekuzindila bekubalulekile kwabo bebeke bahlangane
naye ubuso ngobuso. Okwangoku asinqwenele kuteta ngehambiso yake
ekulauleni imicimbi yeli lizwe.—Okunye ukububa okubikwe kwangelo xesha
kokuka Mr. R. Godlonton ingwevu enkulu yase Rini ebaluleke ngokuba
ngomnye wabo- kuqala abamisa isishicilelo kweli cala le Koloni.

U Rev. Dr. Hole, isijoli sebandla elihambisa Izibalo Ezi ngcwele, ohamba
evuselela abemi be Koloni malungana nalo msebenzi, ube nentlanganiso apa
e Lovedale ngo 4 June. Isihlalo sigcinwe ngu Mr. A. Smith, oxele eyona
nto ahamba ngayo u Dr. Hole. Emile ugqira ute eli Bandla asitunywa salo
lihamba nabafundisi. Selizinikele Izibalo Ezingcwele kwabateta Isixosa
nakwabateta Isisutu, nakwezinye inteto. Into eyondelwe lelo Bandla
yeyokuba lenze ngapezu kwako konke oku. Yintonina ixabiso lalo msebenzi
? Lilizwi lika Tixo elifundisa umoni indlela yokuxolelwa kwezono, ukuba
unokutinina u Krestu ukufunyanwa aze abe nguinncedi nomkokeli ukuya
kwilizwe elizayo. Into ebonisa ubunyaniso bezi Zibalo Zingcwele yeyokuba
akukazanga kwabako ncwadi eyaka yaba nentshaba ezininzi njengale, kodwa
onke amacebo okuyi- tshabalalisa asuka aba luncedo lokuyihambisa.
Ubalise ngo- kutshutshiswa kwamakolwa eminini zokuqala kufunwa ukutshi-
tshiswa eli Lizwi. Endaweni yoko lisuke ilizwi latwabuluka. Nasemva
kwalo maxesha kuvuke o Gibbon, no Hume, no Volteri (Voltaire) bazama
ukulitshabisa ilizwi besiti asinto iyakuma. Endaweni yoko zona ezozindlu
babebalela kuzo ezoncwadi zokulitshitshisa zaba zizindlu zabakolwayo,
okanye zatengwa leli Bandla ukuba zibe zezokulisasaza. Ubalise
ngokuhamba kakuhle kwelizwi e France apo like latshutshiswa kunene ;
wabala amanani e Baibile aselekutshiwe leli Bandla. Emva kwenteto ka
Rev. Dr. Hole kutete u Rev. Dr. Stewart no Rev. Mr. Brotherton. U Mr.
Knox Bokwe wenze elokuba imali eyayihlanganiselwe umhla wokuzalwa kwe
Nkosazana engadlalelwanga ngenxa yezulu maseyinikelwa Ibandla loku-
sasaza i Baibile; oku kusekelwe ngu Mr. H. Niekerk kwamkelwa
yintlanganiso. Emva kokuculwa nomtandazo yahlukene intlanganiso emnandi
kunene.

BARTLE EDWARD FRERE,

I-Ruluneli no Mlauli oyintloko wale Colony ye Nkosazana ise Cape of Good
Hope e-South Africa namazwe alunge nale Colony.

Oku kubanje ke kwabo sisohlwayo esibafa- neleyo ngenxa yokulwa kwabo ;
kodwa ke Inkosazana ayingi bangati abantu bayo batshatyalaliswe
xabavumayo ukuyilulamela imiteto ; ikolwa ukuba inkita le ibipume no
Sandile, iwexuliswe zinteto zobuxoki zama- pakati, izibona kaloku
ukuposisa kwayo, ke Uxolelo luyaziswa ngoluhlobo.

1.  Bonke abafuna uxolelo ngenxayokungena kondiyalwa, mabazinikele kwi
    mantyi nokuba kukwinkosi esukuba ipete iqela elitile lemiko- si.

2.  Bakuba ke bezinikele, bazisa nezi xobo zabo, batikitwa, bayakufumana
    iqaga lokuba baxolelwe, eliyakuti lonalibakusele ekoniweni
    xabayakuti nabo kaloku bayilulamele imiteto abangati bayityelwe
    ngulowo wakwa Rulu­mente bate bazinikela kuye.

3.  Kuloke olu Xolelo, bakutshelwa pandle u-Gonya (Edmund) no Matanzima,
    onyana baka Sandile, nabanye onyana, nemizalwana ka Sandile:
    u-Makinana, no Kondile (Na­thaniel) onyana baka Mhala; u-Delima,
    u-Namba, u-Jali, no Dondashe ; onyana bobabini baka Dukwana, onyana
    abatatu baka Nyosi, no Ngojo (wase Mgwali) no John Beck Balfour;
    nawo onke amapakati nenje- ngele ebezitshatshele ukuluxokonxa
    ululo:— nawo onke amapamba abe batalwa ngobubo- nda kwa Rulumente,
    nabo bonke abaranelwa ukuba bebe zinjojeli ekubulaweni kotileshe.

4.  Kuboke abo bahluliweyo akukonto iyimbi iyakuti itenjiswe ngapandle
    kwale yokuba baya kuke balingwe kakuhle endlwini zama- tyala
    kwelizwe lase Colony.

INDABA ezimana ukufika zivela kumacala
onke alomandla wasemdeni, ziyavumelana

ngokuti kupelile ukuti abantu abaxobileyo
balwe nemikosi namapolisa; nokokuba ondi-
yalwa bafe nentliziyo, bacitwacitwa kukuma-
na begxotwa; bapulukene kakulu namadoda
abo okulwa, nenkomo ; nokufa, noku banjwa
kwabapati babo abona baveleleyo ; abasafu-
nyanwa beyimikosi emikulu; abo base nezi-
xobo zabo bazibadubadu ezicitiweyo, eziti
kuzo zonke indawo kwakufika umkosi ka
Rulumente, zibaleke zizimele kwindawo ezi-
nqabileyo, kungekonto yimbi ngapandle kwe-
yokuba zizinqabise ekubulaweni, eziti xa
zifumene ituba zizinikele zifuna inceba.

Kubonakalake ukuba ixesha lifikile lokuba
kubikwe Uxolelo, kwamkelwe ukuzinikela
kwabo bonke abangengabo abapati abavelele-
yo, bengena matyala wambi mabi bawenze
kulemfazwe.

Ke esi Saziso, No. 57, sangoku, sesite sazi-
swa yi Ruluneli, icebisene ne yona inkulu
Intlanganiso Yamacebo (Executive Council);
ke ziyaziswa ukuba ziti zonke Imantyi zikwa-
zise oko kwinto yonke, nge-Singesi, nge-Sibu-
lu, nange-Sixosa.

Zaye kanjalo zinikwa igunya lokubamkela
bonke abazinikela ngoluhlobo selubikiwe.

Bonkeke abo bazinikeleyo, mabanikele
izixobo zabo, batikitwe, babotshwe ixesha
elitile kwa loliwe, nokuba nguwupi omnye
umsebenzi ka Rulumente, babe beya kufuma-
naishumi lesheleni (10s.) ngenyanga, noku-
dla, bekufumana kuny’e nosapo lwabo. Abo
bayakuti budala, mhlaumbi, zezinye intintelo
bangabi nokuya emsebenzini, mabashenxi-
selwe kwindawo abayakuti bafumane indlela
zokupila ngokuzisebenzela, bade babuye babe
nokubuyiselwa kwindawo abaya kubekwa
kuzo, zibe ngumhlaba wabo.

Imantyi ziyaziswa ukubazize,zizaziseindlela
zokuncedisa kwazo kokungaqinisa olu Xolelo,
nokuba ziti apo lonakaliswa kona zikulungise
oko, ize kanjalo zimane ukuti ngeveki zonke
zixele uhlobo lokuhambiseka kwezinto, nezi-
qamo zazo.

  J. GORDON SPRIGG, Colonial Secretary,

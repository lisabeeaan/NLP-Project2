ISIGIDIMI SAMAXOSA, DECEMBER 1, 1881.

  Lovedale Institution, 8 November, 1881.

Ku Hon’ble Thomas C. Scanlen, Esq., M.L.A.

Mnumzetu,—

Kutiwa ihambo yako okwa namhla asiyeyokuba uli tshawe. Woti ke
intlangano yetu nge qubuliso uyikangele njengo mbuliso we ntliziyo,
ngetuba lokufika kwako apa, nokuba lixesha lotyelelo eli fupi, mhlaimbi
elide loku hlola umsebenzi ohanjiswayo e Lovedale.

Akunge fanele, pantsi kwezi zimeko, ukwenza intsingiselo kwi
ngxoxo-ngengxoxo, kodwa sinokuti ngoku bekiselela kwi Micimbi yaba
Ntsundu, eku kuyo umsebenzi wetu uxomekelele, siyakoliseka yimpato oyi
ngenisayo, ebandakanya uxoliso nobu lungisa, sitemba woba noku kufeza
oko selukuqale ngokufaneleke kangaka.

Ngomnqweno wonke wokuba ubuyele e Cape Town ufumene amandla amatsha
nozondelelo,

  Sine mbeko yokuba sibe, zi caka zako ezi lulamileyo, JAMES STEWART.

Egameni labo bonke aba Fundisi—Aba pati bomzi; Intloko zama shishini;
Intsapo—Emhlope ne Ntsundu; Aba funda ama shishini; nabo bonke abase
Lovedale.

U Mr. SCANLEN ute uku pendula: Ku kaninzi esiva nge Lovedale, nakuba
yena kukuqala ukuyibona. Ihambo yake asi yobu mnumzana, uko nje ngomntu
omnyama. Ubabulele ngayo yonke intliziyo yake uhlobo aba mamkele ngalo,
nangemvo zoku yitemba i Rulumente ali lungu layo. Amalungu e Rulumente
onke kuyo yonke into aye nzayo aqutywa ngumoya wokwenza oku fanelekileyo
noku lungileyo kubo bonke, ngapandle koku keta bala, nangqoboko. Into
abo ndele kuyo yeyoku misa oloxolo lumayo nolunga bangiyo. Kuko uxolo,
kubako intsikelelo; kunokulindelwa ukwanda kwezi kolo ezinje ngesi kulo
lonke eli lizwe. Uya temba bonke bosebenza kunye bejonge kweso sipelo.
Ubabulele nge minqweno yokumvela nge mpilo yake.

Emva koku hamba-hamba no Dr. Stewart pakati komzi inene eli libekekileyo
lindulukile lisinga e Fort Beaufort. Lopela nge Cradock.

UMFI U ABRAHAM PAMLA.

Ema Ngesini apa ukufa kwe ndoda endala ne gama lidumileyo—nokuba
lidunyiswe ngo nyana na—aku funyanwa kuyekwe kudlule kunga poswa mazwi.
Xa sifaka eli bali sikwa kulo mkondo. Akuko nosizi esingalwenzela umnobo
wama Pamla, kuba ixego elo liye kwamkela umvuzo walo, lifanele, ku
Mamkelise Opezu konke. Ngum zukulwana u Mr. George Pamla ob'ala
lengxelo, eyibala ese Butter­worth :—

Ububile u Pamla indoda eyazalelwa ngapesheya ko Tukela, Embo yaza
kungcwatyelwa Esikobeni kwelama Mfengu ilizwe. Ubube ngomhla, we 10th
October, wangcwatywa ngo 12th ngu Rev. James Mjila umfundisi wase
Cwecweni, enobudala beminyaka ema shumi ali 90 mhlaumbi 100.

Bonke abayaziyo nabaka bayibona lendoda baya kusingqinela xa siti imkele
enkosini leyo ibizama ukuyikonza. Kuyo ukufa kube njengokulala ubutongo.
Indoda le enditeta ngayo yabe ivelele ebumnyameni, yaza kengoko yangena
kuwo onke amasiko awakohlakeleyo elilizwe likoyo. Yaizeke abafazi
abatatu yaza ke yahlala ngentlalo yokuzonwabisa ngezinto
eziwutshabalalisayo umpefumlo. Ikolwa kuzo izi tixo inikela namadini
kuzo. Ndingati ke ukuze ndingalenz lide ibali lam. Kwati xa kunjalo
lateta ilizwi lika Tixo entliziyweni yayo, yabonakala isiwa ngamadolo
pakati kwaso isitembu sayo eso imfuna u Tixo we zulu no mhlaba waza yena
kuba esiti “Lowo uzayo kum andisayi kumala,” wayihlangabeza indoda leyo
ngofefe lwake. Ndingati yabonakala iqaula itshata nomfazi omnye. Into
enzima zihlobo zam ukusukela kuka Satane ebona ukuba igusba yake
iyamshiya. Manditi ukuze niqonde into endiyitetayo, ndinibalisele idabi
libe linye eyalilwa no Mtyoli.

Kwatandaza yena wati:—

Une tamsanqa umntu ongahambiyo eqingeni labanga lungileyo, nasendleleni

KUMLISELA NO MTINJANA.

P. J. MZIMBA.

1.  UBUHLWEMPU kwindodana litamsanqa asililo lishwa; ndide ndigqitise
    nakwelo nditi, ubunkedama budibene nobuze nobupantsi, asililo lishwa
    kwindodana litamsanqa. Ndifuna ulutsha, ngokukodwa olungu mlisela
    lundivile kakuhle. Ubuhlwempu buzisa itamsanqa ko zazileyo
    eyindodana ukuba ulilo, uyinkedama, uze, upantsi. Ako amahlwempu
    anga madodana angazaziyo, angavumiyo ukukolwa nokuvuma ukuba angawo,
    ngohlobo azipete ngalo. Uhlanga lwakowetu nje ngokuba luzele
    ngamadodana anga mahlwempu, epantsi ngendlela zonke, ngendiba nditi
    anetamsanqa ; ngendiba nditi isizukulwana esizayo sovuyiswa
    zintsikelelo; ndawo inditintelayo yeyokuba amadodana anjalo azipete
    ngohlobo olungavumiyo ukuyibona lonto. Ubuhlwempu bufundisa
    ukusebenza, umntu azi ukuba andisebenzanga ndiyakufa. Andizanga
    ndive ezimbalini nokuba zeza lupina uhlanga ukuba indodana, intsha,
    inesimilo ipilile iyabulawa bubuhlwempu. Ngapezulu koko indawo
    endiziva futi kwintlanga kukupumelela, kwamadododana abuqalele
    pantsi ubuntu.

2.  Indawo endenza buhlungu, lusizi, idanisa kakulu, yile: ulutsha
    lwakowetu lungenelwe sisifo senkomo. Esosifo lutshobo. Sisifo
    esincinane esingaxalisi mfuyi. Kodwa ke site kuba singene ebantwini
    bebatsha siyatshabalalisa. Inkomo xa inotshobo ungafika ipakamise
    itshoba ibuye ipate kulitoba; ipate kuluma etyanini, ibuye ibaleke
    ipata kuma kanjalo; kungabiko nto ode wena ukangeleyo uti ibambelele
    kuyo. Unjalo umlisela kwano mtinjana wohlanga lwakowetu, ungenelwe
    seso sifo senkomo. Nditsho kuba lupata kungena ezimfundweni, uti
    usakangele londlela ilahlwe lomfundo nokuba yeyeshishini lezandla
    nokuba yeyencwadi. Wakubuza isizatu soko. O! hayi! kwatsha ameva,
    yititshare akuko nento le endifundisa yona. Mhlaimbi, yititshare
    ayinditandi, kekaloku indenzele ucuku ndedwa, kanti bako abafana
    abakohlakele ngapezu kwam engabenzi nento bona. Mhlaimbi, ngowetu
    endifunda nabo banomona babone intloko yam itambile bamana ke
    bendihleba kondipeteyo, uyemka ke unyati.

Esenjenje ke nje umfo omkulu namhla akubanga njalo mhla weza kulemfundo.
Wabaxalisa abazali nezihlobo bada bakohlwa nokulala into elalekayo,
nokudla into edlekayo kuba lomfana enxamele imfundo. Wabaqobisa nje
ngomka Samson wakudala efuna amandla endoda yake apo akona.

lomntu pambi kokuba aye kufunda ubengateti nyaniso maxesha onke. Hayi
